4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 10: 履 (Lǚ) — "Treading" (Heaven over Lake). Heaven above, the joyous lake below — the classical image is treading directly on a tiger's tail without being bitten, symbolizing how to move correctly and carefully through a situation that carries real risk, especially in dealings between the strong and the weak, or the high and the low. Success comes not from avoiding the danger, but from proceeding with courtesy, correct conduct, and respect for the natural order of things — the weaker or lower party treads carefully and respectfully behind the stronger, and is not harmed. The counsel: in any delicate or unequal situation, good manners, tact, and staying within one's proper place bring safety and good fortune, while careless or presumptuous conduct invites disaster.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Greenland

  • Location: An Arctic territory situated between the North Atlantic and Arctic Oceans, geographically part of the North American continent but politically an autonomous territory of the Kingdom of Denmark, in Europe.
  • Territory: About 2,166,086 km² — the world's largest island (excluding continental landmasses), roughly four times the size of France and about 60 times the size of Taiwan. Some 80% of its area is covered by the Greenland ice sheet, the second-largest ice body on Earth after Antarctica's.
  • Population: Only around 56,000 people — an extraordinarily low population for its landmass — the great majority ethnically Greenlandic Inuit (Kalaallit), concentrated in coastal towns; roughly a third live in the capital, Nuuk.
  • Religion: Predominantly Evangelical Lutheran Christianity (the Church of Greenland, historically tied to the Church of Denmark), introduced through Danish colonization, alongside a continuing cultural undercurrent of traditional Inuit spiritual beliefs.
  • Economy: Heavily dependent on fishing and seafood exports (shrimp and halibut above all), with a significant annual block grant from Denmark still funding a large share of public spending; growing international interest in Greenland's untapped rare-earth-element and mineral deposits, plus a small but expanding tourism sector, are reshaping its economic outlook.
  • History: Originally settled by Paleo-Inuit and later Inuit peoples over several waves of Arctic migration; Norse settlers led by Erik the Red arrived around the 10th century before their colonies died out; Denmark established colonial control from the 18th century onward. Greenland gained home rule in 1979 and expanded self-government under the 2009 Self-Government Act, which recognizes its right to eventual full independence. It has drawn intense geopolitical attention in the 2020s after repeated US proposals (including renewed pressure in 2025) to acquire the territory, which Greenlandic and Danish leaders have firmly rejected.
  • Current leadership: Premier (Prime Minister) Jens-Frederik Nielsen of the Demokraatit (Democrats) party, in office since March 28, 2025, leading a coalition with Inuit Ataqatigiit, Siumut, and Atassut following that year's general election — held under the shadow of the US pressure campaign, with independence-minded parties gaining ground. Denmark's Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen continues to handle matters reserved to the Danish state (foreign policy, defense, currency).
    Sources: cnbc.com, en.wikipedia.org
  • Language: Greenlandic (Kalaallisut, an Inuit language) is the sole official language; Danish remains widely spoken as a second language, especially in administration, education, and among older generations.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Skibidi Toilet

  • Name: Skibidi Toilet.
  • Origin/history: A surreal animated YouTube short-video series created by Georgian-Russian animator Alexey Gerasimov (channel "DaFuq!?Boom!"), first posted in early 2023. It depicts singing human heads emerging from toilet bowls ("Skibidi Toilets") at war with camera-, TV-, and speaker-headed humanoids.
  • How it became popular: The absurd, wordless, rapidly escalating "lore" spread virally through YouTube Shorts and TikTok, propelled by its addictive nonsense audio hook (built on the song "Skibidi" by Little Big) and endless fan edits, spin-offs, and parody battles between the toilet and camera-head factions. It became one of the defining phenomena of Gen Alpha internet culture from 2023 onward.
  • Meaning/usage: By 2025-2026, "skibidi" had detached from the video series entirely and become general-purpose slang — shorthand for anything weird, chaotic, over-the-top, absurd, or just "meme-coded," used across TikTok comments, gaming voice chat, and youth slang broadly (part of the broader "brainrot" vocabulary alongside terms like "gyatt" and "rizz").
  • Variations/evolution: Countless fan-made spin-off series, an in-development feature film/franchise deal with a Hollywood studio, and heavy commercialization (toys, games) followed its explosive growth — alongside community friction as corporate involvement increased.
  • Current status: Opinion is split — much of the core fan community now declares "Skibidi Toilet is over, it's dead" as the original series winds down its story arc, even as the slang term "skibidi" persists broadly as an ironic, self-aware catch-all in youth internet speech.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, aftermath.site

5) 國家/地區介紹 — 格陵蘭

  • 位置: 位於北大西洋與北極海之間的極地領土,地理上屬於北美大陸的一部分,但政治上是歐洲丹麥王國的自治領地。
  • 領土面積: 約 2,166,086 平方公里,是全世界最大的島嶼(不計算大陸型陸塊),大約是法國的四倍大、台灣的 60 倍大;其中約 80% 的面積被格陵蘭冰蓋覆蓋,是地球上僅次於南極洲的第二大冰體。
  • 人口: 僅約 5.6 萬人,相對其廣大領土而言人口極度稀少,絕大多數為格陵蘭因紐特人(Kalaallit)血統,居住在沿海城鎮,約三分之一集中在首都努克(Nuuk)。
  • 宗教: 以福音路德教會(格陵蘭教會,歷史上與丹麥國教會關係密切)為主流,由丹麥殖民時期傳入;傳統因紐特人的宗教信仰仍以文化底蘊的形式延續至今。
  • 經濟: 高度依賴漁業與海產出口(尤其是蝦與大比目魚),丹麥每年提供的大筆補助款(block grant)仍支撐相當比例的公共支出;國際社會對格陵蘭尚未開發的稀土元素與礦產資源興趣日增,加上規模雖小但持續成長的觀光業,正逐漸重塑其經濟樣貌。
  • 歷史: 最初由多波北極遷徙的原始因紐特人與後來的因紐特人定居;約 10 世紀時「紅髮埃里克」率領的北歐維京人曾在此建立殖民地,後來滅絕消失;丹麥自 18 世紀起建立殖民統治。格陵蘭於 1979 年獲得自治,並依 2009 年《自治法》擴大自治權限,該法也承認格陵蘭最終有權完全獨立。2020 年代,美國多次提議收購格陵蘭(包括 2025 年再度施壓),讓格陵蘭吸引高度地緣政治關注,格陵蘭與丹麥領導人皆堅決拒絕此議。
  • 現任領導人: 總理 Jens-Frederik Nielsen,隸屬「民主黨」(Demokraatit),自 2025 年 3 月 28 日就任,率領與因紐特聯合黨(Inuit Ataqatigiit)、前進黨(Siumut)、聯合黨(Atassut)組成的聯合政府——該屆選舉正值美國施壓收購格陵蘭的爭議陰影下舉行,主張獨立的政黨版圖有所擴大。丹麥總理梅特・弗雷澤里克森(Mette Frederiksen)則持續掌管保留予丹麥中央政府的事務(外交、國防、貨幣等)。

  • 語言: 格陵蘭語(Kalaallisut,因紐特語系)為唯一官方語言;丹麥語仍廣泛作為第二語言使用,尤其常見於行政、教育體系與年長世代之間。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Skibidi Toilet(滑稽馬桶)

  • 名稱: Skibidi Toilet(滑稽馬桶)。
  • 起源/歷史: 由喬治亞裔俄籍動畫師 Alexey Gerasimov(頻道名稱「DaFuq!?Boom!」)創作的超現實 YouTube 動畫短片系列,2023 年初首次上線。內容描述從馬桶裡冒出、會唱歌的人頭「馬桶怪」,與攝影機頭、電視頭、喇叭頭的人形機器人展開荒誕大戰。
  • 如何爆紅: 這種荒謬、幾乎沒有台詞、劇情不斷升級的「世界觀」,靠著洗腦的無意義配樂(改編自 Little Big 樂團歌曲〈Skibidi〉)在 YouTube Shorts 與 TikTok 上迅速病毒式擴散,加上無數粉絲二創、衍生系列與馬桶陣營 vs. 攝影機頭陣營的對戰劇情,使其成為 2023 年起 Alpha 世代網路文化最具代表性的現象之一。
  • 意義與使用情境: 到了 2025-2026 年,「skibidi」一詞已完全脫離原本的影片系列,演變成通用俚語——用來形容任何荒謬、混亂、誇張、莫名其妙、或「很迷因」的事物,廣泛出現在 TikTok 留言、遊戲語音聊天,以及年輕世代的日常用語中,與「gyatt」「rizz」等詞彙同屬更大範疇的「腦腐(brainrot)」網路用語家族。
  • 變體與演變: 隨著爆紅程度提升,衍生出無數粉絲自製的支線系列,甚至傳出好萊塢片商洽談電影/系列作改編授權,並伴隨大量商業化(玩具、遊戲等);隨著企業資本介入程度加深,粉絲社群內部也出現不小的摩擦與反彈聲浪。
  • 目前現況: 社群內部意見分歧——隨著原創系列劇情逐漸步入尾聲,核心粉絲圈許多人已經宣告「Skibidi Toilet 已經過氣、死了」,但「skibidi」這個俚語本身,仍以一種帶有自嘲、反諷意味的萬用詞彙形式,持續存在於年輕世代的網路用語當中。
