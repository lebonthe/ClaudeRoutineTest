3) Spanish Lesson — Day 16 (10 min, builds on Day 15)

Day 15 taught the "backwards" verb gustar and indirect object pronouns for talking about likes. Today's lesson introduces an entirely new tense for the first time: the simple past (pretérito indefinido) for regular -ar/-er/-ir verbs — how to talk about actions that already happened and finished, like "I worked yesterday" or "she wrote a letter last night."

  • Ayer trabajé mucho. (ah-YEHR trah-bah-HEH MOO-choh) — I worked a lot yesterday.
  • ¿Comiste algo rico anoche? (koh-MEES-teh AHL-goh REE-koh ah-NOH-cheh) — Did you eat something delicious last night?
  • Y después le escribí un mensaje a mi amiga. (ee dess-PWESS leh es-kree-BEE oon men-SAH-heh ah mee ah-MEE-gah) — And afterward I wrote a message to my friend.

Grammar note: Regular -ar verbs take -é/-aste/-ó/-amos/-aron (yo: hablé — note the accent on the final é, which distinguishes it from the present-tense hablo); regular -er and -ir verbs share identical preterite endings -í/-iste/-ió/-imos/-ieron (yo: comí, escribí). Unlike the present tense, where -er and -ir verbs diverge slightly, the simple past unifies them into one shared pattern — one set of endings covers both verb families.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Somaliland

  • Location: A self-declared, de facto independent territory in the Horn of Africa, occupying the northwestern portion of Somalia. It borders Djibouti to the northwest, Ethiopia to the west and south, the rest of Somalia (Puntland) to the east, and has a long Gulf of Aden coastline to the north.
  • Territory: Roughly 137,600 km² — about 3.8 times the size of Taiwan (~36,200 km²), a little smaller than Bangladesh, and comparable in area to the US states of Louisiana and Mississippi combined, or to England and Wales combined.
  • Population: Estimated at roughly 5.7–6 million, though no internationally recognized census exists given its unrecognized status; the largest city and capital is Hargeisa.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Sunni Muslim, practiced by essentially the entire population.
  • Economy: A low-income economy centered on livestock exports (chiefly sheep, goats, and camels to Gulf states such as Saudi Arabia — one of the world's most livestock-export-dependent economies), remittances from a large global diaspora, and trade through the port of Berbera. Somaliland issues its own currency (the Somaliland shilling), but its lack of international recognition shuts it out of IMF/World Bank membership, most foreign direct investment, and conventional international banking.
  • History: Colonized as British Somaliland from 1884. It became briefly, fully independent in June 1960, before voluntarily uniting days later with the former Italian Somaliland to form the Somali Republic. As central authority collapsed during Somalia's civil war under dictator Siad Barre, Somaliland unilaterally declared independence again in 1991 and has functioned as a stable, self-governing de facto state ever since — with its own constitution, currency, passport, and multi-party elections — while receiving zero UN member-state recognition to date.
  • Current leader: President Abdirahman Mohamed Abdullahi "Irro," who won Somaliland's November 2024 presidential election and was inaugurated in December 2024, in a peaceful transfer of power frequently cited by Somaliland's supporters as evidence of its democratic credentials relative to the wider region.
  • Language: Somali is the primary and official language; Arabic also holds official status and is widely used in religious and some administrative contexts; English is significant in education, government, and business as a legacy of British colonial rule.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Pepe the Frog

  • Name: Pepe the Frog.
  • Origin/history: Created by cartoonist Matt Furie in 2005 as a laid-back, slacker character in his comic Boy's Club, alongside a cast of anthropomorphic roommates. A single panel of Pepe with his pants pulled down, captioned "feels good man," was posted to MySpace around 2008 and picked up on 4chan shortly after, launching Pepe's life as a reaction image.
  • How it became popular: Through the early-to-mid 2010s, Pepe spread across 4chan, Tumblr, and Reddit as an all-purpose emotional reaction image — "feels good man," "feels bad man" (sad Pepe), and "smug Pepe" variants covered an enormous range of moods. It crossed fully into the mainstream by 2014–2015, with celebrities including Katy Perry and Nicki Minaj posting Pepe images on social media.
  • Meaning/usage: Originally a flexible, apolitical emotional-shorthand image usable in almost any context — contentment, sadness, smugness, frustration — much like an elaborate emoji. Its meaning fractured sharply after 2015 as different communities pulled it in opposing directions.
  • Variations/evolution: Around 2015–2016, "Rare Pepes" emerged — a collectible-card-style subculture treating certain edited Pepe images as valuable and tradeable, eventually formalized on the Counterparty blockchain protocol in 2016 as an early precursor to today's NFTs. Separately and more consequentially, alt-right and white-nationalist communities appropriated Pepe imagery during the 2016 US election cycle, leading the Anti-Defamation League to add Pepe to its hate-symbols database that September (while explicitly noting most uses remained non-hateful). In apparent response, Matt Furie "killed" Pepe in a 2017 comic strip and later pursued legal action (including against Infowars) over unauthorized commercial use of the character. Pepe was also reclaimed as a pro-democracy protest symbol during Hong Kong's 2019 demonstrations, explicitly stripped of any alt-right association in that context, and more recently became a mascot for crypto "meme coins," most notably a token named PEPE launched in 2023.
  • Current status: A genuinely contested cultural artifact — simultaneously a mainstream, largely innocuous reaction-image staple, an early icon of NFT/crypto meme culture, and a symbol multiple subcultures (across the political spectrum and across countries) continue to actively dispute the meaning and ownership of, nearly two decades after its creation.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Peking Opera (京劇)

  • Name: Peking Opera / Beijing Opera (京劇, Jīngjù).
  • Key figures/creators: Emerged from the 1790 arrival in Beijing of the "Four Great Anhui Troupes" (四大徽班), invited for the Qianlong Emperor's 80th birthday celebrations, whose performance style fused Anhui and Han opera traditions with elements of Kunqu and Qinqiang. Cheng Changgeng is credited as a foundational mid-19th-century figure who helped codify the emerging form; Mei Lanfang (1894–1961), the most internationally celebrated performer, was renowned for his dan (female-role) performances and toured the US and Soviet Union in the early-to-mid 20th century, introducing Peking Opera to Western audiences.
  • Era/background: Coalesced into a distinct performance form in Beijing by the early-to-mid 19th century, became closely associated with the Qing imperial court, and reached a peak of popularity in the early 20th century.
  • Content overview: Its repertoire draws heavily on Chinese history, folklore, and classic novels — including Romance of the Three Kingdoms and Journey to the West — typically dramatizing historical battles, court intrigue, romance, and moral fables through highly stylized rather than naturalistic performance.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Built on four fundamental skills — song (唱), speech/recitation (念), dance-acting (做), and combat/acrobatics (打) — performed through four major role types (行當): sheng (生, male roles), dan (旦, female roles), jing (淨, painted-face roles depicting bold warriors or officials), and chou (丑, comic roles). Elaborate, symbolic facial makeup (臉譜), especially for jing roles, uses specific colors to signal character traits (red for loyalty/courage, black for integrity, white for treachery). A small ensemble centered on the jinghu (a small, high-pitched two-string fiddle), plus gongs, drums, and clappers, rhythmically punctuates movement and speech. Highly codified, symbolic gestures substitute for realistic sets or props — a raised whip signifies riding a horse, a specific stylized walk signifies a long journey.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Regarded as a quintessential distillation of centuries of Chinese theatrical tradition and central to 20th-century Chinese cultural identity — both celebrated and, during the Cultural Revolution, tightly controlled and reshaped into ideologically-charged "revolutionary model operas" under Jiang Qing. Inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2010.
  • Influence and legacy: Shaped other regional Chinese opera forms and left a notable mark on international avant-garde theatre — German playwright Bertolt Brecht famously cited Mei Lanfang's performance style as a key inspiration for his theory of "epic theatre" and its alienation/distancing effect (Verfremdungseffekt).
  • Current status: Actively performed today by companies including the China National Peking Opera Company (中國國家京劇院) in Beijing and, in Taiwan, the Kuo Kuang Opera Company (國光劇團), which stage both traditional repertoire and newly commissioned works; also taught at dedicated conservatories such as the National Academy of Chinese Theatre Arts, and regularly performed for domestic and international audiences.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Editing & Montage (Lesson 2)

Lesson 1 taught mise-en-scène: what's deliberately arranged within a single frame. Today's lesson moves to the layer built between frames: editing (montage) — how meaning is manufactured not by any one shot, but by the act of cutting shots together in sequence.

The method: Soviet montage theorists, above all Sergei Eisenstein, argued that placing two shots side by side creates a third meaning that neither shot carries alone — a kind of dialectical collision he called "intellectual montage." Related is the Kuleshov Effect, filmmaker Lev Kuleshov's famous experiment showing that audiences read emotion into an actor's neutral expression purely based on what shot follows it (a bowl of soup, a coffin, a child), not the actor's actual performance. To analyze editing, train yourself to watch for: rhythm/pace — is the cutting fast (urgency, chaos) or slow (calm, dread), and does that pace change as the scene builds? What is being juxtaposed, and why — are two unrelated strands of action intercut to create a comparison or contrast? Continuity vs. disruption — does a cut hide itself (matching action, matching eyelines) so you don't notice it, or does it deliberately jar you? Meaning, in editing, lives in the collision and sequence of shots, not inside any single frame.

Worked example — Battleship Potemkin (dir. Sergei Eisenstein, 1925), the Odessa Steps sequence: Eisenstein crosscuts between three separate strands as Tsarist soldiers massacre civilians on a monumental staircase: the soldiers marching down in a relentless, mechanical rhythm; close-ups of individual terrified faces (an old woman, a mother, a wounded student); and — most famously — a baby carriage that begins bouncing, step by step, down the stairs after its mother is shot. None of these three strands alone would carry the scene's full meaning. As the massacre intensifies, the average shot gets shorter and the cutting rate accelerates — starting with longer establishing shots and building to a rapid, fragmented barrage of close-ups — manufacturing a feeling of overwhelming panic that no single continuous take could produce. The runaway carriage shots are repeatedly intercut with shots of the soldiers' marching boots and leveled rifles: a deliberate juxtaposition of innocence/vulnerability against mechanized violence that produces exactly the "third meaning" Eisenstein described — "state violence crushing the innocent" — emerging from the collision between two otherwise-unrelated image strands, not from any single shot in isolation.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Rhythm as emotional information — track how average shot length shrinks as tension builds; a scene accelerating its cutting rate is manufacturing urgency mechanically, independent of what's happening within any one shot. (2) Parallel intercutting across simultaneous strands, rather than one continuous action — the soldiers, the crowd, and the carriage are three separate lines of action woven together, not filmed and shown as one take. (3) Meaning through juxtaposition — the boots-and-rifles/baby-carriage pairing generates meaning purely from being placed next to each other, the core of montage theory. This builds directly on Lesson 1: mise-en-scène is what's arranged within a shot; editing is how separate shots are stitched together to produce a meaning that no single shot possesses on its own.

4) 易經卦象

第十六卦:豫卦(Yù)——「豫」(上震下坤,雷出地奮)。這一卦緊接著昨天第十五卦(謙)而來:真正做到謙遜之後,自然會迎來一種發自內心、不假造作的暢快與振奮——「豫」這個字同時帶有「愉悅興奮」與「事先準備」兩層含意。卦象描繪雷聲從大地中轟然響起,是一股沉靜蓄積後迸發而出的生命能量。卦辭寫道:「豫:利建侯行師。」〈象傳〉補充道:「雷出地奮,豫;先王以作樂崇德,殷薦之上帝,以配祖考。」古代注疏將豫卦解讀為一堂如何妥善引導強大集體能量(熱情、氣勢)的功課——透過良好的領導、禮樂制度與共同目標,將這股能量導向建設性的方向,而非任其耗散為過度放縱或混亂;卦辭之所以將其與軍事、祭祀等行動(建侯、行師、崇德、薦祖)相連,正是因為這些正是承接、發揮這股能量的恰當出口。

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 索馬利蘭(Somaliland)

  • 位置: 位於非洲之角、索馬利亞西北部的自我宣告獨立、事實上獨立運作的政治實體。西北方與吉布地接壤,西邊與南邊與衣索比亞相鄰,東邊則與索馬利亞其餘地區(邦特蘭)相接,北面濱臨亞丁灣,擁有漫長海岸線。
  • 領土面積: 約 137,600 平方公里——大約是台灣(約 36,200 平方公里)的 3.8 倍,略小於孟加拉,面積與美國路易斯安那州加密西西比州相近,也大致相當於英格蘭加威爾斯的總面積。
  • 人口: 估計約 570 萬至 600 萬人,由於其未受國際承認的地位,並無獲國際認可的正式人口普查數據;最大城市暨首都為哈爾格薩(Hargeisa)。
  • 宗教: 絕大多數人口信奉遜尼派伊斯蘭教,幾乎涵蓋全體居民。
  • 經濟: 屬低所得經濟體,以牲畜出口為經濟核心(主要為綿羊、山羊與駱駝,銷往沙烏地阿拉伯等波斯灣國家——是全球對牲畜出口依賴程度數一數二的經濟體之一),另外仰賴龐大海外僑民匯回的僑匯,以及透過柏培拉港(Berbera)進行的貿易。索馬利蘭發行自己的貨幣(索馬利蘭先令),但由於未獲國際承認,無法加入國際貨幣基金組織(IMF)/世界銀行,也難以吸引外國直接投資或使用一般國際銀行體系。
  • 歷史: 1884 年起成為英屬索馬利蘭殖民地。1960 年 6 月曾短暫完全獨立,數日後便自願與原義屬索馬利蘭合併,組成索馬利共和國。索馬利亞在獨裁者西亞德·巴雷(Siad Barre)統治下爆發內戰、中央政府瓦解後,索馬利蘭於 1991 年再度單方面宣布獨立,此後一直維持穩定的自治政府運作至今——擁有自己的憲法、貨幣、護照與多黨選舉制度——但至今尚未獲得任何聯合國會員國的正式承認。
  • 現任領導人: 總統阿卜迪拉赫曼·穆罕默德·阿卜杜拉希(Abdirahman Mohamed Abdullahi,人稱「Irro」),於 2024 年 11 月贏得索馬利蘭總統選舉,並於同年 12 月就職,這次和平的權力轉移,經常被索馬利蘭的支持者引用,作為其相對於周邊地區更具民主典範性的證據。
  • 語言: 索馬利語為主要官方語言;阿拉伯語同樣具官方地位,廣泛用於宗教與部分行政場合;英語則因英國殖民歷史遺緒,在教育、政府與商業領域佔有重要地位。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Pepe the Frog(佩佩蛙)

  • 名稱: Pepe the Frog(佩佩蛙)。
  • 起源/歷史: 由漫畫家 Matt Furie 於 2005 年創作,是他的漫畫作品《Boy's Club》中一名個性隨性、有點頹廢的角色,與其他擬人化室友同住。2008 年左右,一格畫著佩佩把褲子拉到膝蓋、配文「feels good man」(感覺真好,老兄)的漫畫被貼上 MySpace,隨後很快被 4chan 論壇轉載,自此開啟了佩佩作為「反應圖」(reaction image)的生涯。
  • 如何爆紅: 2010 年代前中期,佩佩蛙在 4chan、Tumblr 與 Reddit 上迅速擴散,成為一種萬用的情緒反應圖——「feels good man」、「feels bad man」(悲傷佩佩)、「得意佩佩」等變體,幾乎涵蓋了各種情緒狀態。到了 2014 至 2015 年間已完全進入主流文化,包括凱蒂·佩芮(Katy Perry)、妮姬·米娜(Nicki Minaj)等名人都曾在社群媒體上發布佩佩蛙圖片。
  • 意義與使用情境: 最初是一種靈活、無關政治立場的情緒速記圖像,幾乎能套用在任何情境——滿足、悲傷、得意、沮喪——功能有點像一個精緻版的表情符號。2015 年後,不同社群開始朝完全相反的方向拉扯這個圖像的意義,使其意涵急遽分裂。
  • 變體與演變: 2015 至 2016 年間出現「Rare Pepe」(稀有佩佩)風潮——一種類似集換卡文化的次文化,將特定經過編輯的佩佩圖像視為具有價值、可交易的收藏品,最終於 2016 年在 Counterparty 區塊鏈協議上正式化,可說是今日 NFT 文化的早期先驅。另一方面、也影響更為深遠的是,2016 年美國總統大選期間,另類右派(alt-right)與白人民族主義社群挪用佩佩蛙圖像作為政治符號,導致反誹謗聯盟(ADL)於同年 9 月將佩佩蛙列入其仇恨符號資料庫(同時也明確註明,絕大多數使用情境仍與仇恨無關)。作為回應,Matt Furie 於 2017 年在漫畫中「殺死」了佩佩這個角色,後續也對未經授權的商業性使用(包括控告極右派媒體 Infowars)提出法律行動。此外,佩佩蛙也曾在 2019 年香港示威活動中被重新賦予「親民主抗爭符號」的意義,在該脈絡下被明確地與另類右派的關聯切割開來;近年則成為加密貨幣「迷因幣」文化的吉祥物,其中最著名的是 2023 年推出、名為 PEPE 的代幣。
  • 目前現況: 這是一個真正充滿爭議的文化產物——它同時是主流、大致上無害的反應圖經典款、早期 NFT/加密貨幣迷因文化的代表圖像,也是一個至今(創作近二十年後)仍持續被跨政治光譜、跨國界的多個次文化群體,爭奪其意義與「所有權」的符號。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 京劇(Peking Opera)

  • 名稱: 京劇(Peking Opera / Beijing Opera)。
  • 關鍵人物: 源起於 1790 年為慶祝乾隆皇帝八十大壽,「四大徽班」奉召進京,其表演風格融合了徽調、漢調,並吸收崑曲與秦腔等元素。程長庚被公認是 19 世紀中葉奠定京劇雛形的關鍵人物;梅蘭芳(1894–1961)則是國際上最負盛名的京劇表演藝術家,以旦角表演聞名於世,並於 20 世紀上半葉赴美國、蘇聯巡演,將京劇藝術介紹給西方觀眾。
  • 時代背景: 於 19 世紀初中葉在北京逐漸發展成獨立的表演藝術形式,與清朝宮廷關係密切,並在 20 世紀初達到鼎盛的流行高峰。
  • 內容概述: 劇目大量取材自中國歷史、民間傳說與古典小說——包括《三國演義》與《西遊記》——通常以高度程式化、而非寫實的表演方式,呈現歷史戰爭、宮廷權謀、愛情故事與道德寓言。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 建立在「唱、念、做、打」四項基本功之上,由「生、旦、淨、丑」四大行當演出:生(男性角色)、旦(女性角色)、淨(俗稱花臉,描繪個性鮮明的武將或官員的塗面角色)、丑(丑角/喜劇角色)。精緻且富有象徵意義的臉譜(尤其是淨角)以特定顏色代表角色性格——紅色代表忠義勇敢、黑色代表剛正不阿、白色則代表奸詐。伴奏樂隊以京胡(一種音色高亢的小型雙弦胡琴)為核心,搭配鑼、鼓、板等打擊樂器,以節奏強化肢體動作與唱唸。高度程式化、具象徵意義的身段手勢取代寫實佈景道具——一根馬鞭代表騎馬,特定的台步則代表長途跋涉。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 被公認是數百年中國戲曲傳統的精粹結晶,也是 20 世紀中國文化認同的核心象徵——既受到推崇,也曾在文化大革命期間,在江青主導下被嚴格管控、改造為政治意識形態濃厚的「革命樣板戲」。2010 年獲聯合國教科文組織列入「人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄」。
  • 影響與傳承: 深刻影響了中國其他地方戲曲劇種,也對國際前衛劇場產生顯著影響——德國劇作家貝托爾特·布萊希特(Bertolt Brecht)曾公開表示,梅蘭芳的表演風格,是啟發他提出「史詩劇場」理論與「間離效果」(Verfremdungseffekt,又譯疏離效果)的重要靈感來源。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 至今仍持續在世界各地演出,例如北京的中國國家京劇院,以及台灣的國光劇團,兩者皆持續搬演傳統經典劇目,也不斷推出全新創作的劇碼;同時也是中國戲曲學院等專業院校的教學核心科目,並定期為國內外觀眾演出。

8) 看片方法 — 剪輯與蒙太奇 Editing & Montage(第二課)

第一課教了場面調度——導演在「單一畫面之內」刻意安排的一切。今天要進入下一層:「畫面與畫面之間」被建構出來的意義——剪輯(蒙太奇,montage)——意義並非來自任何單一鏡頭,而是來自把鏡頭依序剪接在一起的這個動作本身。

方法說明: 蘇聯蒙太奇理論家,尤其是謝爾蓋·艾森斯坦(Sergei Eisenstein),主張把兩個鏡頭並列剪在一起,會創造出第三種、兩個鏡頭單獨都不具備的意義——他稱之為一種辯證式的碰撞,即「智性蒙太奇」(intellectual montage)。與此相關的還有「庫勒雪夫效應」(Kuleshov Effect)——電影工作者列夫·庫勒雪夫著名的實驗證明,觀眾會純粹根據演員面無表情的鏡頭後面接的是什麼畫面(一碗湯、一具棺材、一個小孩),來「腦補」出演員的情緒,而非根據演員真正的表演。想分析剪輯,可以訓練自己留意:節奏/速度——剪接速度快(急迫、混亂)還是慢(平靜、不安),而且這個速度是否隨著劇情推展而改變?什麼被並置在一起、為什麼——是否刻意交叉剪接兩條原本無關的行動線,製造對比或比較?連續 vs. 斷裂——剪接點是否刻意隱藏自己(靠動作接續、視線方向銜接),讓你渾然不覺,還是刻意讓你察覺到剪接的存在、感到突兀?剪輯的意義,存在於鏡頭之間的碰撞與順序之中,而非藏在任何單一畫面裡。

實例示範 ——《波坦金戰艦》(導演:謝爾蓋·艾森斯坦,1925)「奧德薩階梯」一幕: 沙皇軍隊在一座巨大的階梯上屠殺平民時,艾森斯坦交叉剪接了三條敘事線:士兵們以無情、機械式的節奏一步步走下階梯;個別平民驚恐面孔的特寫(一位老婦人、一位母親、一名受傷的學生);以及最著名的畫面——一名母親中彈倒地後,嬰兒車開始一階一階地在階梯上顛簸滾落。這三條線索單獨存在時,都無法承載這場戲完整的意義。隨著屠殺逐漸加劇,平均鏡頭長度越來越短、剪接節奏也隨之加快——從一開始較長的建立鏡頭,逐步堆疊成快速、破碎的特寫鏡頭連發——用純粹的剪輯手法,製造出任何單一連續長鏡頭都無法達到的排山倒海般的恐慌感。失控滾落的嬰兒車鏡頭,反覆與士兵行進的軍靴、平舉的步槍鏡頭交叉剪接:這種「無辜脆弱」對比「機械化暴力」的刻意並置,正是艾森斯坦所說「第三種意義」的實例——「國家暴力輾壓無辜者」的意義,並非來自任何單一鏡頭,而是誕生於這兩條原本互不相關的影像線索被剪接碰撞在一起的瞬間。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)節奏即情緒資訊——留意平均鏡頭長度如何隨著張力升高而縮短;一場戲若刻意加快剪接節奏,就是在用純機械式的手法製造急迫感,與任何單一鏡頭內部發生了什麼無關。(2)跨越多條同時進行的敘事線交叉剪接,而非單一連續的動作——士兵、群眾、嬰兒車是三條被交織在一起的獨立行動線,而非一鏡到底拍下來的單一場景。(3)透過並置產生意義——軍靴與步槍/嬰兒車這組對照,純粹因為被放在一起剪接,而產生了意義,這正是蒙太奇理論的核心。這堂課直接承接第一課:場面調度是「畫面之內」被安排好的一切;剪輯則是如何把獨立的鏡頭縫合在一起,產生任何單一鏡頭都不具備的意義。
