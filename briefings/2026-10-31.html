3) Spanish Lesson — Day 107: Encima de / Debajo de / Al lado de / Enfrente de / Detrás de (location prepositions)

New topic, not covered on Days 1-106: the common words for saying where something is. Most are an adverb plus de (encima de la mesa), de + el becomes del, and the de drops when the word stands alone (Debajo.). delante de is in front, enfrente de is across from, and entre takes no de. Core lines:

  • ¿Dónde está mi teléfono? — Where is my phone?
  • Está encima de la mesa, al lado de las llaves. — It's on top of the table, next to the keys.
  • No, ahí no está. ¿Está debajo del sofá? — No, it's not there. Is it under the sofa?
  • No, mira: está dentro de tu bolsa, detrás de la puerta. — No, look: it's inside your bag, behind the door.

4) Daodejing — Chapter 10: 載營魄抱一 (Zài yíng pò bào yī) — "Embracing the One"

Series note: continues the Daodejing track begun on Oct 22, one chapter per day in order (chapters 1-81), never repeated.

Text (Wang Bi version): 載營魄抱一,能無離乎?專氣致柔,能嬰兒乎?滌除玄覽,能無疵乎?愛民治國,能無為乎?天門開闔,能無雌乎?明白四達,能無知乎?生之、畜之,生而不有,為而不恃,長而不宰,是謂玄德。

Translation (paraphrase): Can you keep body and spirit together, holding to the One, without letting them part? Can you gather your breath and grow soft like a newborn? Can you cleanse and clear your dark mirror until it has no flaw? Can you love the people and govern the state without contrivance? When the gates of heaven open and close, can you take the receptive, female part? Can you understand all within the four directions without relying on knowledge? It bears them and nurtures them; it gives life without possessing, acts without presuming, leads without ruling over. This is called mysterious virtue.

Commentary: After Chapter 9's warning not to push anything to its limit, Chapter 10 turns from advice to a list of practices, put as six challenging questions. The first three are about the person: keeping body and spirit unified, breathing until the body is soft like an infant's (a favorite image of the text), and polishing the "dark mirror" (玄覽) of the mind so that it reflects without distortion. The next three move outward: governing without forcing, taking the receptive role when the "gates of heaven" open and close, and understanding everything without clever scheming. The closing lines are about the Way itself: it produces and nourishes without owning or controlling, which echoes Chapter 2's "give life without possessing" and is repeated almost word for word in Chapter 51. Readers disagree on several details: 營魄 may mean the spirit-soul pair or "the soul in its camp"; 天門 may mean the senses (the nostrils, eyes) or the gate of heaven; and some editions read 能為雌 ("can you play the female part?") or 能無雌 for the fifth line, while the sixth line has variants too. The questions all share the same stance: soft, receptive and uncluttered beats rigid and forced.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — The Floss / "Backpack Kid" (2016-2018)

  • Name: The Floss (also "flossing"), a dance in which the dancer swings both arms across the body in one direction while the hips swing the opposite way, as if flossing teeth. Its best-known performer is "Backpack Kid" (Russell Horning), named for the backpack he wore while dancing.
  • Origin / history: Horning, then a US teenager, posted dance clips in 2016 that were shared widely on social media. The move gained global attention when he danced onstage with Katy Perry on Saturday Night Live in May 2017, performing "Swish Swish" (dates approximate).
  • How it spread: Short clips, school and sports celebrations, and a performance on a high-profile TV show carried it to children and teenagers worldwide. The video game Fortnite added a "Floss" emote in 2018, and millions of players then used it in every match.
  • Meaning / usage: No fixed meaning: it is a playful celebration, a victory move and an easy dance to learn, usually done with a deadpan face. It works as a way to show off or as a joke about being "cool".
  • Variations / evolution: Many people filmed themselves flossing in unlikely places, and sports figures and celebrities copied it. In December 2018, Horning and two other dancers sued the makers of Fortnite over emotes based on their dances; the cases were dropped in 2019 (details approximate). The Floss also ran in parallel with other dance memes such as the Dab.
  • Current status: The craze peaked in 2018 and has faded; it is now remembered as a symbol of that era's kids' culture and school dance floors, and it is often used ironically.
  • Note: General knowledge; dates, venues and legal details were not source-checked today and are approximate.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — The Shining (1980, dir. Stanley Kubrick)

  • Name: The Shining, a psychological horror film of about 144 minutes (the shorter international cut runs about 119 minutes), released in the US in May 1980. A Halloween-weekend pick.
  • Key figures: Directed and co-written by Stanley Kubrick (with novelist Diane Johnson), based on Stephen King's 1977 novel. Jack Nicholson plays Jack Torrance, Shelley Duvall plays Wendy, young Danny Lloyd plays Danny, and Scatman Crothers plays the cook Dick Hallorann. Cinematography by John Alcott; Steadicam operator Garrett Brown; music by Wendy Carlos and Rachel Elkind with pieces by Krzysztof Penderecki, György Ligeti and Béla Bartók.
  • Era / background: Kubrick followed Barry Lyndon (1975) with a horror film built on the Steadicam, a newly invented stabilized camera rig. The Overlook Hotel was built mostly on sets at Elstree Studios in England; the hotel's exterior is Timberline Lodge in Oregon, and the Stanley Hotel in Colorado inspired King (approximate). The shoot was famously long and demanding.
  • Plot overview: Jack Torrance, a failed writer and recovering alcoholic, takes a job as winter caretaker of the isolated Overlook Hotel in the Colorado mountains with his wife Wendy and their young son Danny, who has a psychic gift called "the shining." As snow isolates them, Danny sees visions, and Jack slowly turns violent under the hotel's influence, ending with an axe-wielding chase through the hedge maze.
  • Style and technique: Symmetrical one-point-perspective corridors, long Steadicam shots following Danny's tricycle through empty hallways, bright and ordinary rooms rather than dark gothic ones, and a harsh, dissonant score. Kubrick used spatial confusion on purpose: windows and rooms in the hotel do not fit together logically, which unsettles viewers without a clear cause. The famous "Here's Johnny!" line was improvised by Nicholson.
  • Cultural significance: It moved the haunted-house story away from ghosts toward the question of whether the horror comes from the place or from the man. Its imagery (the twin girls, the elevator of blood, the typed pages) is among the most quoted in film.
  • Influence and legacy: King disliked the adaptation, and it received mixed reviews at first (with Razzie nominations for Kubrick and Duvall), then rose steadily in critical standing. The documentary Room 237 (2012) gathered fan theories; Doctor Sleep (2019) was a sequel; the film is referenced throughout pop culture, including Ready Player One.
  • Current status: Available on home video and streaming (availability varies by region) and a fixture of repertory and Halloween screenings.
  • Note: General knowledge; running times, dates and production details are approximate and were not source-checked today.

8) Film Appreciation Method — Lesson 93: The Flashback (and Temporal Structure)

What it is: A flashback interrupts the present to show an earlier time. The key analytic point is that a flashback is never just a "background explanation": the director chooses what to show, when, and through whose eyes, and the choice changes how we read the present. The question to ask is: why does the film go back here, and what does the past change about this moment?

How to analyze: (1) Entry cue: note what triggers the jump (a song, an object, a face, a line of dialogue, a dissolve) and whether the film signals it clearly or not. (2) Whose memory is it? Is there an owner of the memory, and how reliable is it (compare with Lesson 91 on dream sequences)? (3) How the past looks and sounds different: compare light, colour, lens, music and pace with the present. (4) Information: does the flashback reveal something we did not know, or confirm something we feared, or withhold the key detail on purpose? (5) Effect on the present: after you return, does the same face, line or image mean something new? (6) Exit cue: see where the film comes back, and whether it returns to the same moment or a changed one.

Worked example — Casablanca (1942, dir. Michael Curtiz), the Paris flashback: Late at night, Rick (Humphrey Bogart) sits drunk in his empty café after Ilsa (Ingrid Bergman) has come back into his life. What to look for and why it works: (a) Entry cue. Rick, who had banned the song, drunkenly tells Sam to play "As Time Goes By" (as he had played it for Ilsa), and the film moves to a close-up of Rick's face and a slow dissolve into the past. The song is the trigger, so the music carries us from the present to the memory. (b) Look and mood. The present is a dark, smoky night interior with hard shadows; Paris in June 1940 is bright and romantic, with soft light on Ilsa's face and a mood of freedom, though it is shot on a Hollywood backlot and with rear projection (approximate). The contrast makes us feel what Rick lost. (c) Information. The flashback finally answers what happened between them: they fell in love while the Germans closed in on Paris, and Ilsa did not show up at the train station. It makes Rick's bitterness make sense. (d) Pain in the details. The rain on the station platform smears the ink of Ilsa's farewell letter, a physical image of the relationship dissolving, and the train leaves without her. (e) Exit. The film returns to Rick's face in the empty café, now seen differently: we know why he drinks, why the song hurts, and why his later choice at the airport costs him so much (I describe these cues from memory, so check the exact transitions as you rewatch).

Practice: Watch a film with a flashback and write down the entry cue, one visual difference between past and present, and one thing the flashback changes about how you read a later scene. Then ask whether the film would work if the same information had come in order.

原文(王弼本):載營魄抱一,能無離乎?專氣致柔,能嬰兒乎?滌除玄覽,能無疵乎?愛民治國,能無為乎?天門開闔,能無雌乎?明白四達,能無知乎?生之、畜之,生而不有,為而不恃,長而不宰,是謂玄德。

白話翻譯(意譯):能讓身與心合在一起、守住「一」而不分離嗎?能把氣聚起來,變得像初生的嬰兒那樣柔軟嗎?能把心中那面幽深的鏡子洗淨,不留一點瑕疵嗎?能愛護百姓、治理國家而不刻意造作嗎?天門開合之際,能守住柔順接受的一方嗎?能通達四方,卻不靠機巧與知識嗎?道生養萬物,生了而不占有,做了而不自恃,領導了而不宰制,這就叫做深遠的「玄德」。

解說:第 9 章提醒不要把任何事推到極限,第 10 章則由勸告轉為一連串修練,用六個具挑戰性的問句表達。前三句關於個人:讓身心合一、把呼吸調到像嬰兒那樣柔軟(這部書很愛用的形象)、擦亮心的「玄覽」之鏡,使它映照而不扭曲。後三句往外延伸:不強迫地治理、在「天門」開合時守住接受的位置、不用巧智也能通達一切。最後幾句談的是道本身:它生成與養育萬物,卻不占有也不控制,這與第 2 章「生而不有」相呼應,並在第 51 章幾乎原樣重複。讀者對幾個細節有不同看法:「營魄」可能指魂魄一對,或「居於營舍中的魂」;「天門」可能指感官(鼻孔、眼睛)或天上的門;第五句有些版本作「能為雌」(能不能守雌?)或「能無雌」,第六句也有異文。這些問句有共同的姿態:柔軟、接受、不堆砌,勝過僵硬與強求。

5) 國家/地區介紹 — 羅馬尼亞(Romania)

  • 位置與面積:位於歐洲東南部與中東部,北方與東北方是烏克蘭,東邊是摩爾多瓦,南邊是保加利亞,西南是塞爾維亞,西邊是匈牙利,並擁有黑海海岸。喀爾巴阡山脈彎曲穿過中部,多瑙河在此形成三角洲後入海。面積約 23.84 萬平方公里:約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 6.6 倍,與昨天介紹的寮國幾乎一樣大,比英國略小,也比美國奧勒岡州略小(概數)。首都:布加勒斯特。
  • 人口:約 1,900 萬(2021 年人口普查約 1,900 萬),因移民與低生育率而持續減少,約為台灣人口的 80%(概數)。羅馬尼亞人占絕大多數;匈牙利人(約 6%,集中在特蘭西瓦尼亞)與羅姆人是最大的少數族群。
  • 宗教:以羅馬尼亞東正教為主(2021 年人口普查約占總人口 73%,在表明宗教的人中約 85%),另有天主教、希臘禮天主教、新教與其他社群(概數)。
  • 經濟:使用列伊(leu)貨幣的中高所得經濟體,2004 年加入北約、2007 年加入歐盟,尚未採用歐元。強項包括汽車製造(米奧維尼的達契亞,以及克拉約瓦的福特工廠)、機械、資訊服務與農業;石油與天然氣也很重要。非常大的預算赤字(屬歐盟最高之列)使緊縮政策爭論不斷(概數)。
  • 歷史:達契亞人在西元 106 年被羅馬皇帝圖拉真征服,羅馬尼亞語源自拉丁語。瓦拉幾亞、摩爾達維亞與特蘭西瓦尼亞等中世紀公國先後受奧斯曼、哈布斯堡與俄羅斯影響。15 世紀瓦拉幾亞大公弗拉德三世「穿刺公」是德古拉傳說背後的歷史人物之一。1859 年瓦拉幾亞與摩爾達維亞在庫扎統治下合併;1878 年獨立獲承認;1918 年 12 月 1 日與特蘭西瓦尼亞合併,形成「大羅馬尼亞」(12 月 1 日為國慶日)。二戰後成為共產國家;尼古拉·壽賽斯古在 1965 年起執政,直到 1989 年 12 月革命,並於 1989 年 12 月 25 日被處決。2024 年 11 月憲法法院宣告總統選舉第一輪無效,並於 2025 年 5 月重新投票。
  • 現任領導人:總統尼庫索爾·丹(Nicușor Dan,自 2025 年 5 月 26 日起,今天由搜尋查證)。總理則無法確認:伊利耶·博洛揚(Ilie Bolojan)的政府在 2026 年 5 月的不信任投票中垮台,搜尋結果顯示春夏之間政局動盪;有一則摘要指出盧卡·尼庫列斯庫(Luca Niculescu)自 2026 年 10 月 20 日起擔任總理,但我無法用第二個來源確認。
  • 語言:羅馬尼亞語,是最大的東部羅曼語族語言(源自拉丁語,四周多是斯拉夫、匈牙利與希臘語鄰居);境內也使用匈牙利語與羅姆語,部分社群說德語。
  • 小知識(萬聖節週末):特蘭西瓦尼亞的布朗城堡被行銷為「德古拉城堡」,但它與弗拉德三世的關聯很薄弱,而寫下《德古拉》的布拉姆·斯托克從未到訪過那裡。布加勒斯特還有國會宮,常被稱為世界上最重的建築。備註:總統經今天搜尋查證,其他數字為概數的一般知識。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 甩手舞 The Floss /「背包小子」Backpack Kid(2016-2018)

  • 名稱:The Floss(也叫 flossing),一種舞步:兩臂在身前朝一個方向甩動,同時臀部朝相反方向擺動,像在用牙線清牙。最知名的表演者是「背包小子」(Backpack Kid,本名 Russell Horning),因跳舞時背著背包而得名。
  • 起源與歷史:當時還是美國青少年的 Horning 在 2016 年發布舞蹈短片,在社群媒體上被大量轉傳。2017 年 5 月他在《週六夜現場》與凱蒂·佩芮同台演出〈Swish Swish〉,這個舞步因此獲得全球關注(日期為概數)。
  • 如何流行:短片、校園與運動場上的慶祝動作,加上一場高知名度的電視表演,讓它傳到世界各地的兒童與青少年。電玩《要塞英雄》(Fortnite)在 2018 年加入「Floss」表情動作,之後數百萬名玩家每場比賽都會用到。
  • 意義與使用情境:沒有固定含義:它是好玩的慶祝動作、勝利姿勢,也是容易學的舞步,通常配上面無表情的臉。可以用來炫耀,或當作「很酷」的玩笑。
  • 變體與演變:許多人在意想不到的地方拍下自己甩手舞,運動員與名人也跟著模仿。2018 年 12 月,Horning 與另外兩位舞者控告《要塞英雄》製作公司,指其表情動作取自他們的舞步;這些訴訟於 2019 年撤回(細節為概數)。甩手舞也與 Dab 等其他舞蹈迷因同時流行。
  • 目前狀況:熱潮在 2018 年達到高峰後已經消退;如今被當成那個年代兒童文化與校園舞池的象徵,常以反諷的方式出現。
  • 備註:一般知識;日期、場合與法律細節今天未經來源核對,屬概數。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《鬼店》(The Shining,1980,導演史丹利·庫柏力克)

  • 名稱:《鬼店》(The Shining),心理恐怖片,美國版片長約 144 分鐘(較短的國際版約 119 分鐘),1980 年 5 月在美國上映。萬聖節週末精選。
  • 主要人物:導演兼共同編劇為史丹利·庫柏力克(與小說家黛安·強森合寫),改編自史蒂芬·金 1977 年的小說。傑克·尼克遜飾傑克·托倫斯,雪莉·杜瓦飾溫蒂,年幼的丹尼·洛伊德飾丹尼,史凱特曼·克羅瑟斯飾廚師迪克·哈洛蘭。攝影約翰·奧爾柯特;Steadicam 操作者蓋瑞特·布朗;音樂由溫蒂·卡洛斯與蕾秋·艾爾金德,加上潘德雷茨基、利蓋蒂與巴爾托克的作品。
  • 時代背景:庫柏力克在《巴里·林登》(1975)之後,以剛發明不久的穩定攝影架 Steadicam 拍出這部恐怖片。「全景飯店」大部分是在英國埃爾斯特里製片廠搭的景;飯店外觀是美國奧勒岡州的 Timberline Lodge,啟發史蒂芬·金的則是科羅拉多州的史丹利飯店(概數)。拍攝過程以漫長與嚴苛聞名。
  • 劇情概要:傑克·托倫斯是一位失意的作家、正在戒酒,他接下科羅拉多山區孤立的全景飯店冬季看守人的工作,帶著妻子溫蒂與年幼的兒子丹尼同行,丹尼擁有名為「閃靈」的超能力。大雪將他們困住,丹尼看見各種幻象,傑克在飯店的影響下逐漸變得暴力,最後拿著斧頭在樹籬迷宮裡追殺家人。
  • 風格與技法:對稱的單點透視長廊、Steadicam 長鏡頭跟隨丹尼騎三輪車穿過空蕩的走廊、明亮而平凡的房間(而非陰暗的哥德式場景),以及刺耳不和諧的配樂。庫柏力克刻意製造空間錯亂:飯店裡的窗戶與房間在邏輯上對不起來,讓觀眾不安卻說不出原因。著名的「Here's Johnny!」一句是尼克遜即興說出的。
  • 文化意義:它把鬼屋故事從「鬼」轉向「恐怖究竟來自地方還是來自人」的問題。影片中的畫面(雙胞胎女孩、湧出血的電梯、打字稿頁)是電影史上被引用最多的影像之一。
  • 影響與傳承:史蒂芬·金不喜歡這部改編,影片上映時評價不一(庫柏力克與杜瓦曾獲金酸莓獎提名),之後評價穩定攀升。紀錄片《237 號房》(2012)收集了影迷的各種理論;《睡眠醫生》(2019)是續集;這部電影在流行文化中被廣泛引用,包括《一級玩家》。
  • 目前狀況:可透過家庭影音與串流觀看(供應依地區而異),也是經典影院與萬聖節放映的常客。
  • 備註:一般知識;片長、日期與製作細節為概數,今天未經來源核對。

8) 看片方法 — 第 93 課:倒敘(Flashback)與時間結構

這是什麼:倒敘打斷現在,讓觀眾看到更早的時間。分析時的關鍵是:倒敘從來不只是「補充背景」:導演選擇要展示什麼、何時展示、透過誰的眼睛展示,這個選擇會改變我們怎麼閱讀現在。要問的問題是:影片為什麼在這個時刻回到過去?過去讓這一刻變成了什麼?

如何分析:(1) 進入的提示:注意是什麼觸發跳躍(一首歌、一件物品、一張臉、一句台詞、一個溶接),以及影片是否清楚標示。(2) 這是誰的回憶?回憶有沒有主人?可靠度如何(可與第 91 課夢境片段相比)?(3) 過去的外觀與聲音有何不同:比較光線、色彩、鏡頭、音樂與節奏與現在的差異。(4) 資訊:倒敘是揭露我們不知道的事、證實我們害怕的事,還是刻意保留關鍵細節?(5) 對現在的影響:回到現在之後,同一張臉、同一句話、同一個畫面是否有了新的意義?(6) 離開的提示:觀察影片在哪裡回來,是回到同一個時刻,還是回到一個已經改變的時刻。

示範實例 — 《北非諜影》(Casablanca,1942,導演麥可·柯蒂茲)的巴黎倒敘:深夜,在伊爾莎(英格麗·褒曼)重新走進他的生活之後,瑞克(亨弗萊·鮑嘉)獨自喝醉,坐在空蕩的咖啡館裡。要看什麼、為什麼有效:(a) 進入的提示。原本禁止這首歌的瑞克醉醺醺地要山姆彈奏〈As Time Goes By〉(山姆曾為伊爾莎彈過),接著影片切到瑞克臉部特寫,再慢慢溶入過去。歌曲就是觸發點,音樂把我們從現在帶進回憶。(b) 外觀與氣氛。現在是昏暗、煙霧瀰漫、陰影強硬的夜間室內;1940 年 6 月的巴黎明亮而浪漫,伊爾莎臉上的光線柔和,氣氛自由,不過這是在好萊塢片廠與背景投影拍的(概數)。這個對比讓我們感受到瑞克失去了什麼。(c) 資訊。倒敘終於回答了兩人之間發生過什麼:他們在德軍逼近巴黎時相愛,而伊爾莎沒有出現在火車站。這讓瑞克的苦澀有了道理。(d) 細節裡的痛。月台上的雨水暈開了伊爾莎訣別信的墨跡,是關係消融的具體畫面,火車開走時她不在車上。(e) 離開。影片回到空蕩咖啡館中瑞克的臉,但我們此時看見的已不同:我們知道他為什麼喝酒、這首歌為什麼讓他痛,以及他後來在機場的選擇為何代價如此之大(以上提示憑記憶描述,重看時請自行核對確切的轉場)。

練習:看一部有倒敘的電影,寫下進入的提示、過去與現在的一項視覺差異,以及倒敘如何改變你對後面某一場戲的閱讀。然後想想:如果同樣的資訊是按時間順序給出的,影片還會有效嗎?
