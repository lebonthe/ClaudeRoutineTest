3) Spanish Lesson — Day 57 (10 min, El Se Accidental: Unplanned Occurrences)

Today completes the full "se" family begun on Days 46-48 (reflexive, reciprocal, impersonal/passive) with its fourth and final identity: se + indirect object pronoun (me/te/le/nos/os/les) + verb, used to describe something that happened to a person rather than something they deliberately did — softening or removing blame for an unintentional mishap. Compare the blunt Rompí el vaso ("I broke the glass," owning the action) with the far more common Se me rompió el vaso ("I dropped the glass," literally "the glass broke on me," framing it as an accident that happened to the speaker). The sharpest trap: the verb agrees in number with the grammatical subject — the thing that happened — not with the person affected, so Se me cayó el vaso (singular) becomes Se me cayeron los platos (plural) the moment the affected object is plural, even though "I" (singular) never changes.

  • ¡Ay no! Se me rompió el teléfono. — Oh no! I (accidentally) broke my phone.
  • ¿Otra vez? A mí también se me cayeron las llaves esta mañana. — Again? I also (accidentally) dropped my keys this morning.
  • Qué mala suerte. Y se nos olvidó comprar café también. — What bad luck. And we (accidentally) forgot to buy coffee too.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 57: 巽 (Xùn) — "The Gentle" (The Penetrating, Wind) (Wind over Wind: Xùn 巽 above, Xùn 巽 below — 巽為風, one of only eight hexagrams in the King Wen Sequence formed by doubling a single trigram upon itself, alongside Zhèn/51 and Gèn/52 already featured in this series). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the connection directly from yesterday's hexagram: "旅而无所容,故受之以巽,巽者入也。" ("The wanderer, having no place that will receive him, is therefore followed by Xùn; Xùn means 'to enter.'") Hexagram 56 left the wanderer displaced with nowhere to be accepted; Xùn answers this directly by teaching the only strategy available to someone with no standing of their own — not forceful confrontation, but gentle, gradual penetration, entering a new situation the way wind slips through the smallest gap rather than battering down a door. The Judgment reads: "巽,小亨,利有攸往,利見大人。" ("Xùn. Success in small matters. Favorable to have somewhere to go. Favorable to see the great man.") The "small" success (小亨) deliberately echoes Hexagram 56's own wording: like the wanderer, the gentle wind cannot achieve anything through direct force — its entire power lies in softness, in the capacity to reach places rigidity cannot — so it must proceed only in modest, incremental steps, and it profits from seeking out a capable, great person's guidance, since gentleness alone still needs direction to become effective rather than merely diffuse. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "重巽以申命,剛巽乎中正而志行,柔皆順乎剛,是以小亨,利有攸往,利見大人。" ("Wind doubled upon wind reiterates the command; the firm line is gentle yet resides in the central, correct position, so its will can proceed; the yielding lines all submit to the firm — this is why there is success in small matters, why it is favorable to have somewhere to go, and why it is favorable to see the great man.") The doubled-trigram structure is itself read as an image of repetition — one gust of wind reinforcing the next, one command reiterated by another — while each trigram's structure (a single yielding line beneath two firm lines) shows softness always positioned to submit to and channel firmness above it, never to oppose it outright. The Image commentary reads: "隨風,巽;君子以申命行事。" ("Winds following one upon another: the image of Xùn. Thus the superior man reiterates his commands and carries out his undertakings.") Just as a single gust of wind is sustained and extended by the next gust following it, the superior man does not rely on issuing an order once and assuming compliance; he repeats instructions gently but persistently until they have genuinely penetrated into practice. Read together with Hexagram 56, Xùn offers a direct answer to the wanderer's predicament: displacement is remedied not by force but by learning to move like wind — entering unfamiliar situations gently, repeatedly, and adaptively, accumulating just enough small, modest standing (小亨) to eventually find secure footing once again.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Yemen (葉門/也門)

  • Location: The southwestern tip of the Arabian Peninsula, bordered by Saudi Arabia to the north and Oman to the east, with coastlines on the Red Sea to the west and the Gulf of Aden/Arabian Sea to the south. Yemen controls the Yemeni side of the Bab-el-Mandeb strait, the narrow chokepoint linking the Red Sea to the Indian Ocean that has repeatedly seen Houthi attacks on shipping since late 2023 tied to the Gaza war. Yemen also includes the remote Socotra archipelago in the Arabian Sea, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2008 for its unique endemic biodiversity (including the iconic dragon's blood tree). The constitutional capital, Sana'a, has been under Houthi rebel control since 2014-2015; the internationally recognized government now operates out of the temporary capital, Aden.
  • Territory: About 527,970 km² — roughly 14.6 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), comparable to Thailand (~513,000 km²) and slightly smaller than France (~551,700 km²).
  • Population: Approximately 40-41 million as of the mid-2020s — nearly double Taiwan's roughly 23-24 million, despite Yemen being, even before the current war, one of the poorest countries in the Arab world.
  • Religion: Almost entirely Muslim. Sunni Islam (Shafi'i school), concentrated in the south and east, forms a majority of roughly 65%; Zaidi Shia Islam, concentrated in the northern highlands and the historic base of the Houthi movement, accounts for most of the remainder (~35%) — a distinct Shia branch doctrinally closer to Sunni practice than Twelver Shiism (Iran's dominant branch). Yemen once had a historically significant Jewish community, most of whom emigrated to Israel after 1948 (notably via the 1949-50 "Operation Magic Carpet" airlift); only a small handful remain today.
  • Economy: One of the poorest countries in the Arab world even prior to the war, historically dependent on modest oil/gas exports (severely disrupted by the conflict), remittances from Yemeni workers abroad (especially in Gulf states), and agriculture — including qat, a mild stimulant leaf chewed daily by a large share of the population that consumes a disproportionate share of the country's scarce water and farmland, and coffee, whose historic export port of Mocha lent its name to "mocha" coffee worldwide, with Yemen ranking among the plant's oldest cultivation regions outside Ethiopia. The ongoing civil war has produced what the UN has repeatedly called one of the world's worst humanitarian crises, marked by famine risk, cholera outbreaks, and currency collapse.
  • History: Home to the ancient Sabaean kingdom, traditionally associated with the legendary Queen of Sheba, and other pre-Islamic South Arabian civilizations that controlled lucrative frankincense, myrrh, and spice trade routes. In the modern era, the country split into North Yemen (the Yemen Arab Republic, a republic since a 1962 revolution overthrew the Zaidi imamate) and South Yemen (the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen, the only Marxist-Leninist state ever to exist in the Arab world, independent from British colonial rule over Aden in 1967); the two unified in 1990 as the Republic of Yemen under Ali Abdullah Saleh, North Yemen's former president, who continued ruling the unified state until being ousted in the 2011 Arab Spring uprising. The post-2011 political transition collapsed when Houthi rebels seized Sana'a in 2014-2015, prompting a Saudi Arabia-led military coalition intervention in 2015 to back the internationally recognized government — igniting a civil war now in its second decade. A fragile UN-brokered truce since 2022 has reduced large-scale fighting, though the conflict remains formally unresolved, and the internationally recognized side's own internal alliance fractured further in January 2026.
  • Current leader: Rashad al-Alimi has chaired the internationally recognized Presidential Leadership Council — a collective presidency established in April 2022 to replace long-serving president Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi — since its founding; the council is recognized by the UN, most Arab states, the US, EU, and UK. In January 2026, following the collapse of the Council's alliance with the separatist Southern Transitional Council (STC) — triggered when the STC attempted to seize the country's eastern oil-producing provinces — al-Alimi reshuffled the government, dismissing the defense, transport, and planning ministers along with Aden's governor. Separately, the Houthi movement (Ansar Allah) governs its own parallel administration over Sana'a and the densely populated northern highlands, headed day-to-day by Mahdi al-Mashat as president of its Supreme Political Council, under the overall leadership of Abdul-Malik al-Houthi.
  • Language: Arabic (the Yemeni dialect) is the official and dominant language nationwide. The South Arabian language Soqotri is spoken on Socotra island, alongside other endangered pre-Arabic Modern South Arabian languages (such as Mehri) that survive in isolated eastern regions.
    Sources: aljazeera.com (2026 control map), en.wikipedia.org (al-Alimi), thenationalnews.com (Jan 2026 reshuffle)

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Gru's Plan

  • Name/origin: "Gru's Plan" is a four-panel exploitable comic sourced from Despicable Me (2010, Illumination Entertainment), taken from the scene where villain-turned-hero Gru presents his scheme to steal the moon on a flip-chart storyboard, only to discover an out-of-place, embarrassing drawing hidden among the pages. The meme format itself emerged years later, on Reddit, using cropped screenshots of the storyboard panels as a reusable joke template.
  • How it became popular: The meme's viral breakout traces to March 2018: a Droste-effect (recursive/self-referential) version was posted to r/MemeEconomy on March 12, and a GIF version posted the same day to r/me_irl drew roughly 32,000 upvotes within six hours; two days later, on March 14, YouTuber PewDiePie covered it in the twelfth episode of his "Meme Review" series, which drew millions of views within 24 hours and pushed the format to mainstream visibility well beyond Reddit.
  • Meaning/usage: The classic exploited template runs: panels 1 and 2 present two sequential, escalating steps of a plan; panel 3 — instead of advancing to a genuinely new step — repeats panel 2's step nearly identically, exposing a hidden repetition, hypocrisy, or flaw; panel 4 shows Gru turning back to look at the storyboard in visible confusion, reacting to his own plan's self-sabotage. It's used broadly for jokes about ironic self-owns, unintended consequences, or a repeated mistake dressed up as a "new" step.
  • Variations/evolution: The four-panel structure has been endlessly recaptioned across politics, tech, workplace humor, and relationship jokes; it spawned recursive "Droste effect" versions (a meme about a meme, in the same nested storyboard format) and countless crossovers substituting other characters or reaction images into the fourth panel while preserving the "plan that undermines itself" beat structure.
  • Current status: Still in active, recognizable use nearly a decade after its 2018 breakout, regularly resurfacing in political and pop-culture commentary; its underlying structural joke (an escalating plan that quietly repeats its own flaw) is now recognized as a durable meme "format" independent of the original film, in the same family as this series' previously-covered Drakeposting and Distracted Boyfriend as long-lived template memes.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.meming.world

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Whirling Dervishes / The Mevlevi Sema Ceremony (旋轉苦行僧,土耳其梅夫拉維教團儀式)

  • Name/key figures: The Sema is the ceremonial "turning" meditation of the Mevlevi Sufi order, whose practitioners are known internationally as the Whirling Dervishes. The order traces its spiritual foundation to Jalal ad-Din Rumi (1207-1273), the celebrated Persian mystic poet known in Turkish as Mevlana ("our master"), and was formally established after Rumi's death by his followers — chiefly organized under his son, Sultan Walad — in Konya, in present-day Turkey, around 1273.
  • Era/background: Rumi is said to have developed the turning practice himself as an expression of mystical absorption; his followers systematized it into the structured Sema ceremony that has been transmitted, largely intact, for over seven centuries. Konya, where Rumi lived, taught, and is buried (his tomb, now the Mevlana Museum, remains a major pilgrimage site), has remained the spiritual center of the Mevlevi tradition ever since.
  • Content/structure overview: Dervishes wear a tall conical felt hat (sikke, symbolizing the tombstone of the ego) and a wide white skirt (tennure, symbolizing the ego's burial shroud) over a black cloak, which is removed at the ceremony's start to symbolize spiritual rebirth. The Sema unfolds in four symbolic stages of the mystic's journey toward the Divine: the first represents awakening to knowledge of God, the second the joy of witnessing the splendor of creation, the third the annihilation of the ego into that splendor and full mystical rapture, and the fourth a return to the world with newfound maturity, ready to serve all creation without discrimination. Throughout, dervishes spin counter-clockwise on their left foot, right arm raised (palm up, receiving divine grace) and left arm lowered (palm down, channeling that grace to the earth) — a posture read as connecting heaven and earth through the turning body itself.
  • Artistic style and techniques: The ceremony is inseparable from its live musical accompaniment: the reed flute (ney), whose breathy, keening tone is central to Rumi's own poetry as a symbol of separation and longing for the Divine, joined by kudüm frame drums, the bowed rebab, and vocalists reciting ilahis (devotional hymns) and verses from Rumi's own poetry, all governed by the precise, meditative rhythmic structure of Ottoman-Turkish classical music.
  • Cultural/historical significance: The Sema represents one of the most enduring, continuously transmitted mystical performance traditions in the Islamic world, embodying Sufism's central emphasis on love, spiritual union, and the dissolution of the ego through disciplined, embodied ritual rather than through text or doctrine alone.
  • Influence and legacy: The Mevlevi order and Rumi's poetry (widely translated and read worldwide, with Rumi frequently cited as one of the best-selling poets in the English-speaking world in recent decades) have carried the Sema's imagery and philosophy of "turning" toward the Divine far beyond Turkey, influencing global perceptions of Sufism and Islamic mysticism broadly. The Ottoman state formally banned all Sufi orders, including the Mevlevis, in 1925 as part of Turkey's secularizing reforms; the ban on public Sema performances was later relaxed specifically for cultural/touristic exhibition beginning in the 1950s, a compromise that persists to this day.
  • Current status/where to experience it: UNESCO proclaimed the Mevlevi Sema Ceremony a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity in 2008. Today it is performed both as devotional ritual — most prominently each December in Konya's Şeb-i Arus ("Wedding Night") festival commemorating the anniversary of Rumi's death, understood by Sufis as his union with the Divine — and, more controversially among purists, as a regularly-scheduled cultural performance for tourists in venues across Istanbul and Konya year-round.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, ich.unesco.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — The Wipe Transition (Lesson 43)

A wipe is a transition in which one shot visibly pushes, replaces, or is swept away by the next via a moving line, shape, or pattern travelling across the frame — a deliberately overt, energetic punctuation mark, distinct from Day 24's Ellipsis (which covers ordinary cuts and dissolves smoothing over skipped story time in general, without addressing this specific visible, shape-driven transition device) and from a plain cut or dissolve, both of which aim to be far less noticeable. Because the wipe calls attention to itself as a transition rather than hiding the edit, it carries a distinct set of connotations: an old-fashioned, serialized "chapter break" rhythm, brisk narrative economy (compressing a scene change into a fraction of a second of visible motion), or a knowing genre/nostalgia signal borrowed from the era when the technique was most common. When you spot a wipe, check whether its shape is a plain straight line or something more stylized (a shape, a spin, an iris); whether it's used for a simple, functional scene change or deliberately evokes a specific serial/genre tradition; and whether the film deploys it consistently as a structural signature or only as an occasional punctuation beat.

Worked example: Star Wars (1977, dir. George Lucas) — wipes recur constantly throughout the film's many scene and location changes (Tatooine to the Death Star, and back again), a deliberate homage to two influences Lucas has repeatedly cited: 1930s adventure serials like Flash Gordon, which used wipes and irises constantly to move quickly between short, cliffhanger-driven episodes, and Akira Kurosawa's The Hidden Fortress (1958) — a director whose editing technique this series has already examined via Seven Samurai and Rashomon — which used similarly brisk, overt transitions across its own multi-location adventure narrative. By borrowing the device rather than hiding its edits behind invisible cuts and dissolves, Star Wars deliberately gives its episodic, multi-location space-opera plot the brisk, old-fashioned "chapter break" rhythm of the serials it was consciously designed to evoke.

3)西班牙文課程——第 57 天(10 分鐘,El Se Accidental:無意/意外發生的事)

今天為從第 46 天到第 48 天開始建立的整個「se」家族(反身、相互、無人稱/被動),補上第四個、也是最後一個身分:se + 間接受詞代名詞(me/te/le/nos/os/les)+ 動詞,用來描述某件事「發生在」某人身上,而不是某人「主動去做」了這件事——藉此淡化或撇除意外造成的責任。對比一下直白的「Rompí el vaso」(我打破了杯子,承認是自己造成的),與遠更常見的「Se me rompió el vaso」(我〔不小心〕摔破了杯子,字面上是「杯子在我身上碎了」,把這當成發生在說話者身上的意外)。本課最容易犯錯之處在於:動詞的單複數要依「發生的那件事」(文法主體)變化,而不是依「受影響的人」變化,因此「Se me cayó el vaso」(單數)一旦受影響的物品變成複數,就要改成「Se me cayeron los platos」(複數)——即便「我」(受影響的人)從頭到尾都是單數,完全不變。

  • ¡Ay no! Se me rompió el teléfono. —— 唉呀不!我的手機(不小心)摔破了。
  • ¿Otra vez? A mí también se me cayeron las llaves esta mañana. —— 又來?我今天早上也(不小心)把鑰匙弄掉了。
  • Qué mala suerte. Y se nos olvidó comprar café también. —— 運氣真差。而且我們也(不小心)忘了買咖啡。

4)易經卦象

第 57 卦:巽 (Xùn) ——「巽」(順從、謙遜、風、滲透)(巽為風——上巽下巽:上下卦皆為風〔巽〕,是《易經》六十四卦中僅有的八個「重卦」〔上下卦相同〕之一,與本系列已介紹過的第 51 卦震、第 52 卦艮同屬此類)。《序卦傳》承接前一卦而來:「旅而无所容,故受之以巽,巽者入也。」(旅人漂泊在外,卻找不到任何可以接納自己的地方,因此旅卦之後,接的是巽卦;巽,意指「進入」。)第 56 卦所呈現的,是旅人流離失所、無處可容身的景象;巽卦正面回應了這個困境,教導的正是一個毫無既有地位根基之人,唯一可行的策略——不是強硬對抗、正面衝撞,而是柔順、漸進地滲透進入新的處境,就像風一樣,總能從最細微的縫隙悄然滑入,而非硬闖破門而入。卦辭寫道:「巽,小亨,利有攸往,利見大人。」(巽卦,小有亨通;有所前往為有利,拜見德高望重之人也是有利的。)這裡的「小」亨通,刻意呼應了第 56 卦旅卦本身用過的措辭:正如旅人一般,柔順的風無法透過強硬的力量成就大事——它全部的力量,恰恰來自於自身的柔軟,來自於能夠抵達剛強事物所無法觸及之處的能力——因此只能循序漸進、小規模地行動,而且有利於尋求一位德才兼備的大人物加以指引,因為單靠柔順,若缺乏明確方向,終究仍只是渙散無力,而非真正發揮效用。《彖傳》進一步闡述:「重巽以申命,剛巽乎中正而志行,柔皆順乎剛,是以小亨,利有攸往,利見大人。」(風上加風,象徵反覆申明政令號令;剛健的爻位處於柔順之中,卻居於中正之位,因此其志向得以順利推行;柔順的爻位全都順從剛健的爻位,這正是巽卦之所以能小有亨通、有所前往為有利、拜見大人物也為有利的原因。)這種「重卦」結構本身,便被解讀為一種「反覆」的意象——一陣風接續著另一陣風而持續增強,一道政令又被另一道政令反覆申明;而每個巽卦本身的組成(一柔爻居於兩剛爻之下),也呈現出柔順總是甘居其下、順從並引導上方的剛健之力,而非與之正面對抗的結構意涵。《象傳》寫道:「隨風,巽;君子以申命行事。」(風接續著風而來,這就是巽卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應當反覆申明政令,並據以推行政事。)正如一陣風的效力,總是被緊接而來的下一陣風延續、強化,君子也不會只發布一次命令便以為對方已然遵從;他會以溫和卻堅持不懈的態度,反覆申明指示,直到這些指示真正滲透落實到實際行動之中為止。若與第 56 卦旅卦兩相對照,巽卦恰恰為旅人的困境,提供了一個直接的解答:流離失所之苦,並非能靠蠻力克服,而是要學會像風一樣行動——以柔順、反覆、靈活應變的姿態,漸次滲透進入陌生的處境之中,一點一滴累積起足夠的、雖然微小卻真實的立足之地(小亨),最終終能重新覓得穩固的安身之處。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——葉門(也門,Yemen)

  • 位置:位於阿拉伯半島西南端,北接沙烏地阿拉伯,東鄰阿曼,西側濱臨紅海,南側濱臨亞丁灣/阿拉伯海。葉門控制著曼德海峽(Bab-el-Mandeb)的葉門一側——這是連接紅海與印度洋的狹窄戰略咽喉要道,自 2023 年年底起,因加薩戰爭延伸效應,胡塞武裝多次在此攻擊往來船隻。葉門境內還包括位於阿拉伯海的偏遠索科特拉群島(Socotra),自 2008 年起因獨特的原生生物多樣性(包括著名的龍血樹)被列為聯合國教科文組織世界遺產。憲法上的首都沙那(Sana'a)自 2014 至 2015 年起,已落入胡塞叛軍控制;國際普遍承認的政府,目前則以臨時首都亞丁(Aden)為運作據點。
  • 領土:總面積約 527,970 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 14.6 倍,與泰國(約 513,000 平方公里)相近,略小於法國(約 551,700 平方公里)。
  • 人口:截至 2020 年代中期,人口約 4,000 萬至 4,100 萬人——幾乎是台灣(約 2,300 萬至 2,400 萬人)人口的兩倍,儘管葉門即便在當前戰爭爆發之前,便已是阿拉伯世界最貧窮的國家之一。
  • 宗教:幾乎全民信仰伊斯蘭教。集中於南部與東部的遜尼派(沙斐儀學派)約占多數,比例約 65%;集中於北部高地、也是胡塞運動歷史發源地的宰德派什葉教義(Zaidi Shia),則占剩餘的大部分(約 35%)——這是一支教義上較接近遜尼派、而非伊朗主流十二伊瑪目派什葉教義的獨特什葉分支。葉門歷史上曾有一個具有重要地位的猶太社群,絕大多數已於 1948 年後移民以色列(尤其透過 1949 至 1950 年間的「魔毯行動」空運),如今僅剩極少數留存。
  • 經濟:即便在戰爭爆發之前,葉門便已是阿拉伯世界最貧窮的國家之一,經濟結構歷史上仰賴規模有限的石油/天然氣出口(戰爭期間遭受嚴重衝擊)、旅居波斯灣等地葉門僑工匯回的僑匯,以及農業部門——包括恰特(qat,一種被大量人口每日咀嚼的溫和興奮性植物葉片,消耗了該國本就稀缺的大量水資源與可耕地),以及咖啡——葉門歷史上的咖啡出口港摩卡(Mocha)一名,即為全球「摩卡咖啡」名稱的由來,葉門也被認為是衣索比亞以外,咖啡最古老的種植地區之一。持續延燒的內戰,已造成聯合國多次形容為全球最嚴重人道危機之一的局面,伴隨著飢荒風險、霍亂疫情與貨幣崩潰等問題。
  • 歷史:葉門是古代賽伯伊王國(Sabaean kingdom)的所在地——傳說中與示巴女王(Queen of Sheba)關係密切——以及其他前伊斯蘭時期南阿拉伯文明的發源地,這些文明曾掌控利潤豐厚的乳香、沒藥與香料貿易路線。進入現代,該國分裂為北葉門(葉門阿拉伯共和國,自 1962 年一場革命推翻宰德伊瑪目政權後成為共和國)與南葉門(葉門人民民主共和國,是阿拉伯世界史上唯一存在過的馬克思列寧主義國家,於 1967 年脫離英國對亞丁的殖民統治而獨立);兩者於 1990 年在北葉門前總統阿里·阿卜杜拉·薩利赫(Ali Abdullah Saleh)領導下統一為葉門共和國,薩利赫此後持續統治統一後的葉門,直到 2011 年阿拉伯之春起義中被迫下台。2011 年後的政治轉型進程,隨著胡塞叛軍於 2014 至 2015 年攻占首都沙那而宣告瓦解,促使沙烏地阿拉伯於 2015 年領導多國軍事聯盟介入,支持國際承認的政府——引爆了如今已進入第二個十年的內戰。自 2022 年起,聯合國斡旋的脆弱停火協議雖已大幅降低大規模戰事,但衝突至今仍未正式解決,而國際承認一方內部的政治聯盟,更於 2026 年 1 月進一步出現裂痕。
  • 現任領導人:總理級人物拉沙德·阿萊米(Rashad al-Alimi)自 2022 年 4 月創立以來,始終擔任國際承認的總統領導委員會(Presidential Leadership Council)主席——該委員會是為取代長期在位的總統哈迪(Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi)而設立的集體元首機制,獲得聯合國、多數阿拉伯國家、美國、歐盟與英國承認。2026 年 1 月,該委員會與南方過渡委員會(Southern Transitional Council, STC)的聯盟破裂——起因是 STC 試圖奪取該國東部產油省份——阿萊米隨後改組政府,撤換了國防、交通與規劃部長,以及亞丁省長。另一方面,胡塞運動(安薩安拉,Ansar Allah)則在沙那與人口稠密的北部高地,治理著自身的平行政權體系,由邁赫迪·馬沙特(Mahdi al-Mashat)擔任其最高政治委員會主席,負責日常事務,整體則由胡塞運動領袖阿卜杜勒-馬利克·胡塞(Abdul-Malik al-Houthi)統籌領導。
  • 語言:阿拉伯語(葉門方言)是全國官方且最主要通行的語言。索科特拉島上使用南阿拉伯語系的索科特里語(Soqotri),此外,東部部分偏遠地區,仍留存其他瀕危、早於阿拉伯語傳入的現代南阿拉伯語系語言(如梅赫里語 Mehri)。

6)網路迷因介紹——格魯的計畫(Gru's Plan)

  • 名稱/起源:「Gru's Plan」(格魯的計畫)是一個取材自《神偷奶爸》(Despicable Me,2010,盧米埃娛樂〔Illumination Entertainment〕出品)的四格可編輯漫畫迷因,場景出自片中反派轉正派主角格魯,以翻頁式簡報板向手下展示他偷月球計畫的橋段——結果卻在簡報頁面中,意外發現一張不該出現、令人尷尬的插圖。這個迷因格式本身,是在電影上映數年之後,才在 Reddit 上以裁切自簡報板畫面的截圖為素材,發展成可反覆套用的固定笑話模板。
  • 如何走紅:這個迷因病毒式爆紅的時間點,可追溯至 2018 年 3 月:3 月 12 日,一則採用「俄羅斯套娃效應」(Droste effect,即遞迴自我指涉手法)的版本被貼上 r/MemeEconomy 版;同一天,一則 GIF 版本被貼上 r/me_irl 版,六小時內就獲得約 32,000 個讚;兩天後的 3 月 14 日,YouTuber PewDiePie 在其「Meme Review」系列第十二集中介紹了這則迷因,該影片在 24 小時內累積數百萬次觀看,將這個格式推向遠超出 Reddit 範圍的主流能見度。
  • 意義/使用情境:這個經典的可編輯範本結構如下:第一格與第二格,呈現一項計畫循序漸進、逐步升級的兩個步驟;第三格原本應該推進到真正全新的步驟,卻幾乎一模一樣地重複第二格的內容,藉此揭露某種隱藏的重複行為、虛偽,或缺陷;第四格則呈現格魯轉頭回望簡報板、露出明顯困惑表情的畫面,對自己計畫「自爆」的窘境做出反應。這個格式被廣泛用來製作各種「反諷式自打嘴巴」「意料之外的後果」,或是「把重複犯下的錯誤,包裝成『全新』步驟」之類的笑話。
  • 變體/演化:這套四格結構,已被無數次套用改編至政治、科技、職場幽默與感情話題等各種主題之上;也衍生出遞迴式的「俄羅斯套娃版」(用同樣的簡報板巢狀格式,製作一則「關於這則迷因本身的迷因」),以及大量替換成其他角色或反應圖片、但保留「計畫最終自我瓦解」這套結構性笑點的跨迷因混搭版本。
  • 目前狀態:距離 2018 年爆紅至今已近十年,這則迷因至今仍持續被廣泛使用且容易辨識,經常在政治評論與流行文化相關討論中重新浮現;其底層的結構性笑點(一項不斷升級的計畫,卻悄悄地重蹈自身的覆轍)如今已被視為一種獨立於原電影之外、歷久不衰的迷因「格式」,與本系列先前介紹過的「Drakeposting」及「分心男友」同屬長壽範本型迷因家族。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.meming.world

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——旋轉苦行僧與梅夫拉維薩瑪儀式(Whirling Dervishes, The Mevlevi Sema Ceremony)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:薩瑪(Sema)是梅夫拉維蘇非教團(Mevlevi Order)一套具有儀式性質的「旋轉」冥想修行,其修行者在國際間普遍被稱為「旋轉苦行僧」(Whirling Dervishes)。這套教團的精神根源,可追溯至十三世紀著名的波斯神秘主義詩人賈拉魯丁·魯米(Jalal ad-Din Rumi,1207-1273),在土耳其語中被尊稱為「梅夫拉納」(Mevlana,意為「我們的導師」);魯米辭世後,其信徒——主要由其子蘇丹·瓦拉德(Sultan Walad)領導組織——約於 1273 年,在如今土耳其境內的科尼亞(Konya)正式建立了這個教團。
  • 時代/背景:相傳魯米本人正是這套旋轉修行的創始者,將其作為神秘冥想沉醉狀態的一種表達方式;他的信徒其後將此修行系統化,發展成結構完整的薩瑪儀式,並在超過七個世紀的時間裡,大致完好地代代傳承至今。魯米生前生活、講學,並最終安葬於科尼亞(其陵墓如今已闢為梅夫拉納博物館,仍是重要的朝聖地點),科尼亞至今依然是梅夫拉維教團傳統的精神中心。
  • 內容/結構概述:苦行僧們頭戴高聳的圓錐形毛氈帽(西克〔sikke〕,象徵自我〔ego〕的墓碑),外罩黑色斗篷,內著寬大的白色裙擺(泰努爾〔tennure〕,象徵自我的裹屍布);儀式開始時脫去黑色斗篷,象徵靈性上的重生。薩瑪儀式依序呈現神秘主義者邁向神性旅程的四個象徵階段:第一階段象徵覺醒、初識對真主的認識;第二階段象徵目睹造物榮耀時所感受到的喜悅;第三階段象徵自我徹底消融於那份榮耀之中、達到完全的神秘沉醉狀態;第四階段則象徵懷抱嶄新的靈性成熟,重返塵世,準備一視同仁地服務世間一切造物。整場儀式中,苦行僧以左腳為軸持續逆時針旋轉,右手掌心朝上高舉(承接神恩),左手掌心朝下垂放(將神恩傳遞給大地)——這個姿勢被解讀為透過旋轉的身體本身,連結天與地。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:這套儀式與現場音樂伴奏密不可分:蘆笛(內伊笛,ney)氣音渾厚、略帶哀婉的音色,本身就是魯米詩作中,象徵與神性分離、渴望重新結合的核心意象;伴奏樂器還包括庫杜姆框鼓(kudüm)、拉巴布弓弦樂器(rebab),以及吟唱伊拉希聖歌(ilahi,虔敬讚美詩)與魯米詩作本身的歌者,整場演出皆遵循鄂圖曼土耳其古典音樂精確而具冥想性質的節奏結構。
  • 文化/歷史意義:薩瑪儀式是伊斯蘭世界中,少數歷經數世紀持續傳承、未曾中斷的神秘主義表演傳統之一,體現了蘇非主義的核心精神——透過紀律嚴謹、身體力行的儀式(而非僅憑文字或教條),來實踐愛、與神性合一,以及自我的消融。
  • 影響與傳承:梅夫拉維教團與魯米詩作(其詩作已被廣泛翻譯並流傳全球,近數十年來,魯米經常被列為英語世界最暢銷的詩人之一)已將薩瑪「朝向神性旋轉」的意象與哲學思想,傳播到土耳其境外的廣大世界,深刻影響了全球對蘇非主義與伊斯蘭神秘主義的整體認知。1925 年,土耳其在世俗化改革的一環中,正式明令禁止包括梅夫拉維教團在內的所有蘇非教團;直到 1950 年代起,基於文化與觀光展演目的,這項對公開薩瑪表演的禁令才逐步鬆綁,這項妥協措施延續至今。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:聯合國教科文組織已於 2008 年,將梅夫拉維薩瑪儀式列為「人類口述與非物質遺產傑作」。如今,這套儀式既作為虔誠的宗教儀式演出——最盛大的場合是每年 12 月,於科尼亞舉行的「謝比阿魯斯」(Şeb-i Arus,意為「新婚之夜」)紀念慶典,紀念魯米辭世的忌日,對蘇非信徒而言,此日象徵他與神性的合一——也在虔誠信徒眼中較具爭議地,作為全年定期於伊斯坦堡與科尼亞各地舉行的觀光文化展演節目持續演出。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、ich.unesco.org

8)看片方法——轉場劃像 The Wipe(第 43 課)

劃像轉場(wipe,又稱「劃接」)是指一顆鏡頭,透過一條線條、形狀或圖案在畫面中移動掃過的方式,將前一顆鏡頭「推開」「取代」或「掃除」的轉場手法——這是一種刻意張揚、充滿動感的段落標點,不同於第 24 天所教的「省略」(涵蓋一般性的剪接與溶接,泛指以此手法平滑帶過被跳過的劇情時間,但並未特別探討這種視覺上明顯、以形狀驅動的特定轉場裝置本身),也不同於單純的剪接或溶接——後兩者的目標,恰恰是盡可能不被觀眾察覺。正因為劃像轉場刻意讓觀眾意識到「這是一次轉場」,而非隱藏剪輯痕跡,它因此帶有一組獨特的言外之意:令人聯想起舊時代連載影集「章節分段」般的明快節奏、將場景轉換壓縮進一瞬間可見動態之中的敘事經濟性,或是刻意向這項技法最盛行的年代致敬、帶點懷舊意味的類型訊號。發現劃像轉場時,可以檢視:它的形狀是單純的一條直線,還是更具風格化的設計(某種形狀、旋轉、或光圈式的收放);它是單純功能性的場景轉換,還是刻意呼應特定的連載影集/類型傳統;以及這部片是將其作為貫穿全片的結構性招牌手法持續使用,還是僅偶爾作為點綴性的標點使用。

實例演練:《星際大戰》(Star Wars,1977,導演:喬治·盧卡斯〔George Lucas〕)——全片在眾多場景與地點的轉換之間(從塔圖因星球到死星、再轉回塔圖因),反覆大量使用劃像轉場,這是盧卡斯本人多次公開提及、刻意向兩大靈感來源致敬的手法:其一是 1930 年代的冒險連載影集,如《飛俠哥頓》(Flash Gordon),當年大量使用劃像與光圈式轉場,以便在一集集懸念迭起的短篇冒險之間快速切換;其二則是黑澤明執導的《戰國英豪》(The Hidden Fortress,1958)——本系列先前已透過《七武士》與《羅生門》介紹過這位導演的剪輯手法——該片同樣在自身橫跨多個地點的冒險敘事中,大量運用了同樣明快、毫不掩飾的轉場手法。透過刻意借用這項手法、而非把剪輯痕跡隱藏在不著痕跡的剪接與溶接之後,《星際大戰》有意識地為自身這部集數式、跨越多個地點的太空歌劇劇情,賦予了它刻意想要喚起的、那種舊時代連載影集特有的明快「章節分段」節奏感。
