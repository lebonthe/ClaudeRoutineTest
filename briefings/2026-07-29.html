3) Spanish Lesson — Day 20 (10 min, builds on Day 19)

Days 16-19 covered the simple past tense (pretérito indefinido) in depth — regular endings plus three irregular families (ir/ser/hacer, the u-stem family, and the j-stem family). Today introduces Spanish's other past tense: the imperfect (pretérito imperfecto), used for ongoing, habitual, or background past states rather than single completed events.

  • Cuando era niño, vivía en Madrid. (KWAHN-doh EH-rah NEE-nyoh, vee-VEE-ah ehn mah-DREED) — When I was a kid, I lived in Madrid.
  • ¿Ibas a la escuela cerca de casa? (EE-bahs ah lah ehs-KWEH-lah SEHR-kah deh KAH-sah) — Did your school used to be near home?
  • Sí, y todos los días veía a mis amigos en el parque. (see, ee TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs veh-EE-ah ah mees ah-MEE-gohs ehn ehl PAHR-keh) — Yes, and every day I'd see my friends at the park.
  • ¡Qué bonito! Yo también jugaba mucho de pequeño. (keh boh-NEE-toh! yoh tahm-bee-EHN hoo-GAH-bah MOO-choh deh peh-KEH-nyoh) — How nice! I also used to play a lot as a kid.

Grammar note: regular -ar verbs take -aba, -abas, -aba, -ábamos, -aban; regular -er/-ir verbs share -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -ían. There are only three irregular imperfect verbs in the entire language: ser (era, eras, era, éramos, eran), ir (iba, ibas, iba, íbamos, iban), and ver (veía, veías, veía, veíamos, veían — technically just keeping the archaic stem vowel, not truly irregular endings). The core contrast with the preterite: the imperfect describes an ongoing, habitual, or background state ("cuando era niño, vivía..." — an extended past condition), while the preterite marks a single, bounded completed event — the two often combine in one sentence, e.g. "Cuando llegué (preterite: I arrived, one moment), mis padres veían la tele (imperfect: they were watching TV, ongoing background)."

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 20: 觀 (Guān) — "Contemplation" / "View" (Wind over Earth). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary bridges directly from yesterday's hexagram: "臨者大也,物大然後可觀,故受之以觀" — Lín (Hexagram 19, "Approach") carries the sense of growing great; once a thing has grown truly great, it becomes something worth contemplating and observing, hence Guān ("Contemplation," or "View") follows Lín. The trigram image is wind moving over the earth — reaching every corner, touching all things impartially, evoking both the act of surveying a wide view and of being observed as a model by others. The Judgment reads: "觀:盥而不薦,有孚顒若。" ("CONTEMPLATION. The ablution has been made, but not yet the offering. Full of trust they look up to him.") — likening the ruler's quiet, dignified conduct before a sacrifice, which alone inspires reverent trust, to a superior person's ability to command respect through inner composure rather than active display. The Image adds: "風行地上,觀;先王以省方觀民設教。" ("The wind blows over the earth: the image of CONTEMPLATION. Thus the kings of old visited the regions of the world, contemplated the people, and gave them instruction.") Classical commentary reads this hexagram as being about the two-way nature of "viewing": a leader observes the people closely in order to govern well, while simultaneously being observed by the people as a moral example — making sincere self-cultivation, rather than mere authority, the true source of influence.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Sri Lanka

  • Location: An island nation in South Asia, in the Indian Ocean just southeast of the Indian subcontinent, separated from India by the narrow Palk Strait and the Gulf of Mannar.
  • Territory: About 65,610 km² — roughly 1.8 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), and comparable in area to Ireland or the US state of West Virginia, giving it a modest but geographically varied footprint (central highlands, coastal plains, and a dry northern peninsula).
  • Population: Roughly 22 million (2026), predominantly Sinhalese (~75%) with significant Sri Lankan and Indian Tamil (~15%) and Moor/Muslim (~9%) minorities.
  • Religion: Buddhism (~70%, mostly Theravada, and the majority Sinhalese faith), Hinduism (~12%, mainly among Tamils), Islam (~10%), and Christianity (~7%, a legacy of Portuguese and Dutch colonization).
  • Economy: Historically built on tea (Ceylon tea remains a major export and national brand), garment manufacturing, tourism, and remittances from overseas workers; the economy collapsed into sovereign default and severe shortages of fuel, medicine, and foreign currency in 2022, triggering mass protests (the "Aragalaya") that toppled the sitting president — the country has since been working through an IMF-backed recovery program.
  • History: Home to ancient Sinhalese kingdoms (Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa) dating back over two millennia; colonized in succession by Portugal, the Dutch Republic, and Britain (as the colony of Ceylon); gained independence in 1948; endured a brutal 26-year civil war (1983-2009) between the government and the Tamil Tiger (LTTE) separatist movement; and underwent the 2022 economic collapse and political upheaval described above.
  • Current leader: President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, a former Marxist-leaning JVP party leader who won election in September 2024 on an anti-corruption platform, leading the National People's Power (NPP) coalition; Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya serves alongside him.
  • Language: Sinhala and Tamil are both official languages; English serves as a widely used administrative and "link" language between communities.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org, britannica.com

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — 母湯 (Mǔ Tāng), Taiwan

  • Name: 母湯 (Mǔ Tāng, literally "mother soup" in Mandarin, but meaningless as written — it's a pure phonetic borrowing).
  • Origin/history: Derived from the Taiwanese Hokkien phrase 毋通 (m-thang), meaning "must not" or "no can do." Because standard Mandarin has no native way to write this Hokkien expression, Taiwanese internet users spelled out its sound using unrelated but similarly-pronounced Mandarin characters — 母 (mother) + 湯 (soup) — a common technique in Taiwanese internet Mandarin for rendering spoken Hokkien phrases in text.
  • How it became popular: Spread widely on Taiwanese platforms like PTT, Dcard, and Facebook through the mid-2010s, used as a comment on videos or posts depicting risky, outrageous, or ill-advised behavior — a way of reacting with mock alarm or gentle scolding ("don't do that!").
  • Meaning/usage: Functions as a humorous caution or disapproval, roughly equivalent to "you really shouldn't" or "that's not okay," often used with theatrical exaggeration rather than genuine anger — a staple of Taiwanese comment-section culture reacting to news clips, prank videos, or questionable life decisions.
  • Variations/evolution: Frequently extended to "母湯喔" (with a drawn-out, sing-song "oh"); appears on LINE stickers and in meme image macros; has crossed over into mainstream advertising and even semi-official contexts used humorously/self-aware, and is occasionally borrowed by Mandarin-language internet communities outside Taiwan.
  • Current status: No longer a "new" or trending meme, but fully entrenched as standard colloquial Taiwanese internet (and increasingly spoken) vocabulary — used casually in everyday conversation and text messaging without any sense of novelty.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Nosferatu (1922, dir. F.W. Murnau)

  • Name: Nosferatu: A Symphony of Horror (Nosferatu, eine Symphonie des Grauens), 1922.
  • Key figures/creators: Directed by Friedrich Wilhelm Murnau; screenplay by Henrik Galeen, adapted (without securing the rights) from Bram Stoker's Dracula — renaming characters (Dracula became Count Orlok) in an attempt to dodge copyright, which failed: Stoker's widow sued and won, and a court ordered all prints destroyed. A handful of prints survived internationally, allowing the film to endure.
  • Era/background: Made in Weimar Germany as part of the German Expressionist cinema movement, alongside films like The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari and Murnau's own later Faust.
  • Plot overview: Real-estate clerk Thomas Hutter travels to Transylvania to close a property sale with the mysterious Count Orlok, unwittingly bringing the vampire — and a plague of death — back to his German hometown; his wife Ellen ultimately sacrifices herself, keeping Orlok distracted until sunrise, when the light destroys him.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Unlike Caligari's painted studio sets, Murnau shot largely on real locations, layering in expressionist shadow-play, canted angles, and eerie negative-exposure effects; Orlok (played by Max Schreck) was designed as a rat-like, monstrous figure — bald, clawed, fanged — a sharp departure from the suave aristocratic vampire archetype popularized by later adaptations. The film's iconic shadow-climbing-the-staircase sequence remains one of cinema's most quoted images.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Established several vampire-lore conventions now taken for granted — most notably death by sunlight, which does not appear in Stoker's original novel — and stands as one of the foundational texts of cinematic horror.
  • Influence and legacy: Directly inspired Werner Herzog's 1979 remake Nosferatu the Vampyre and Robert Eggers' 2024 remake; its troubled, tragedy-marked production was dramatized in Shadow of the Vampire (2000); the name "Nosferatu" itself entered popular culture as a byword for vampire.
  • Current status: Long in the public domain in most countries (ironically, thanks to the destruction order making formal copyright renewal impossible), the film is freely viewable via the Internet Archive and other public-domain platforms, with several 4K restorations (Kino Lorber among others) in wide circulation and frequent festival screenings accompanied by live musical scores.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, britannica.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Narrative Structure (Lesson 6)

Lesson 1 covered mise-en-scène, Lesson 2 editing, Lesson 3 cinematography and lighting, Lesson 4 sound design and score, and Lesson 5 colour. Today's lens steps back from any single frame or cut to look at the whole: narrative structure — the order in which a film chooses to tell you its story, which is never neutral.

The method: Ask first whether a film is telling its story in chronological order or not — and if not, what specific effect the reordering produces that a straight timeline couldn't. Non-linear structure can build mystery (withholding causes until after you've seen effects), create ironic or tragic weight (letting you know a character's fate before the story reaches it), or draw thematic connections between distant events by placing them next to each other in viewing order rather than in calendar order. Pay attention to framing devices — a scene the film returns to, bookending everything between it — since a story's beginning and ending, in screen-time rather than story-time, often carries outsized structural meaning: what a film chooses to open and close on is a directorial statement about what the whole thing is "about."

Worked example — Pulp Fiction (1994, dir. Quentin Tarantino), the diner frame: Tarantino tells three loosely connected crime stories completely out of chronological order. The film opens with Pumpkin and Honey Bunny's diner stick-up, then cuts away before it resolves; only in the film's final act does the story return to that same diner scene and finish it — meaning the true chronological ending of the story (in terms of when it happens on the internal timeline) is shown roughly two-thirds of the way through the film, while a scene from the middle of the internal timeline closes it. Because of this reordering, hitman Vincent Vega is shown dying partway through the film (in story-order, this happens last for his character), yet reappears alive and well in the diner scene that closes the film, since that scene happens earlier on the internal timeline than his death. The effect is precisely the point: instead of a linear rise-and-fall arc for any one character, the reordering lets Jules's spiritual "epiphany" in the diner scene — his choice to walk away from violence — land as the film's final note, structurally privileging a moment of grace over the bloodshed the timeline "actually" ends with.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Structure as argument — the order events are shown in, not just the events themselves, tells you what the filmmaker thinks the story is really about; Pulp Fiction's reordering elevates redemption over violence by literally placing it last on screen. (2) The framing device — a scene revisited at both ends of a film compresses everything in between into a single dramatic container, and rewards attention to how the audience's understanding of that opening scene changes once you've seen the rest of the film. (3) Dramatic irony through resequencing — knowing a character's fate before "arriving" at it narratively (as with Vincent) creates a different kind of tension than a straight chronology could, since the suspense shifts from "what happens" to "how it happens" or "what it means." This builds on Lessons 1-5: where mise-en-scène, cinematography, sound, and colour operate scene-by-scene, narrative structure operates at the level of the whole film, governing the order in which every other technique is delivered to you.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第二十天(10 分鐘,銜接第十九天內容)

第十六到十九天,我們深入練了「簡單過去式(pretérito indefinido)」的規則變化,以及三組不規則家族(ir/ser/hacer、u 字根、j 字根)。今天要介紹的是西班牙文另一種過去時態——「過去未完成式(pretérito imperfecto)」,用來描述持續進行中、習慣性、或作為背景鋪陳的過去狀態,而不是單一已完成的事件。

  • Cuando era niño, vivía en Madrid.(昆多 誒拉 尼紐,比比亞 恩 馬德里)—— 我小時候,住在馬德里。
  • ¿Ibas a la escuela cerca de casa?(伊巴斯 阿 拉 誒斯昆拉 些爾卡 貼 卡薩)—— 你以前上的學校離家近嗎?
  • Sí, y todos los días veía a mis amigos en el parque.(西,伊 托多斯 羅斯 迪亞斯 貝伊亞 阿 米斯 阿米勾斯 恩 誒爾 帕爾克)—— 對啊,而且我每天都會在公園看到我朋友。
  • ¡Qué bonito! Yo también jugaba mucho de pequeño.(給 波尼多!喲 譚碧恩 護嘎巴 母秋 貼 貝給紐)—— 好棒喔!我小時候也很常玩。

文法重點:規則 -ar 動詞字尾為 -aba、-abas、-aba、-ábamos、-aban;規則 -er/-ir 動詞共用 -ía、-ías、-ía、-íamos、-ían。全西班牙文只有三個不規則的過去未完成式動詞:ser(era、eras、era、éramos、eran)、ir(iba、ibas、iba、íbamos、iban),以及 ver(veía、veías、veía、veíamos、veían——嚴格來說只是保留古字根母音,字尾仍是規則的)。與簡單過去式的核心差異在於:過去未完成式描述持續進行、習慣性或背景狀態(「cuando era niño, vivía...」——一段延續的過去狀態),簡單過去式則標記單一、有明確起訖的已完成事件——兩者經常在同一句話裡搭配使用,例如「Cuando llegué(簡單過去式:我抵達,單一時刻), mis padres veían la tele(過去未完成式:他們當時正在看電視,持續中的背景畫面)」。

第二十卦:觀卦(Guān)——「觀察 / 瞻仰」(上巽下坤,風行地上)。〈序卦傳〉直接銜接了昨天的卦象:「臨者大也,物大然後可觀,故受之以觀」——臨卦(第十九卦,「臨近」)帶有「壯大」之意;唯有事物真正壯大之後,才值得被仔細觀察與瞻仰,因此觀卦(意涵「觀察」或「瞻仰」)接續臨卦而來。卦象描繪的是風行於大地之上——無所不至、平等地拂及萬物,既象徵著居高臨下、遍覽全局的觀察行為,也象徵著被眾人仰望、作為典範被觀看。卦辭寫道:「觀:盥而不薦,有孚顒若。」(意為:已完成沐浴淨身之禮,尚未獻上祭品,此時肅穆莊重的樣子,已足以令人心懷誠敬、仰望歸心。)以此比喻君王在祭祀之前那份寧靜莊重的舉止,單憑這份內在的沉靜,便足以喚起眾人虔敬的信服,如同君子憑藉內在的修養,而非刻意的展示,便能贏得他人的敬重。〈象傳〉補充道:「風行地上,觀;先王以省方觀民設教。」(風行於大地之上,是觀卦的象徵;因此古代君王藉此巡視四方邦國,觀察民情,並據此設立教化。)古代注疏認為,這一卦談的是「觀看」的雙向性:身居上位者仔細觀察百姓以求善治,同時自身也被百姓所觀察、視為道德典範——真誠的自我修養,而非單純的權威,才是真正影響力的根源。

(第二十次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 斯里蘭卡(Sri Lanka)

  • 位置: 位於南亞、印度洋上的島國,緊鄰印度次大陸東南方,與印度之間隔著狹窄的保克海峽(Palk Strait)與曼納灣(Gulf of Mannar)。
  • 領土面積: 約 65,610 平方公里——約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 1.8 倍,面積與愛爾蘭或美國西維吉尼亞州相當,雖不算大國,但地形相當多元(中央高地、沿海平原,以及北部乾燥半島)。
  • 人口: 約 2,200 萬人(2026 年),以僧伽羅人(Sinhalese,約占 75%)為主,另有相當比例的斯里蘭卡與印度坦米爾人(約 15%)以及摩爾人/穆斯林(約 9%)少數族裔。
  • 宗教: 佛教(約 70%,主要為上座部佛教,也是僧伽羅人多數信仰)、印度教(約 12%,主要為坦米爾人信仰)、伊斯蘭教(約 10%),以及基督教(約 7%,葡萄牙與荷蘭殖民時期遺留的信仰)。
  • 經濟: 傳統上以茶葉(錫蘭紅茶至今仍是重要出口品與國家品牌)、成衣製造、觀光旅遊,以及海外勞工匯款為經濟支柱;2022 年爆發主權債務違約與嚴重的燃料、藥品、外匯短缺危機,引發大規模抗議(「Aragalaya」運動),導致時任總統下台,此後在國際貨幣基金組織(IMF)紓困計畫下逐步復甦。
  • 歷史: 曾孕育擁有兩千多年歷史的古僧伽羅王國(阿努拉德普勒、波隆納魯瓦);先後遭葡萄牙、荷蘭、英國(作為「錫蘭」殖民地)殖民統治;1948 年獨立;1983 至 2009 年間經歷長達 26 年、政府軍與坦米爾之虎(LTTE)分離主義組織之間慘烈的內戰;並於 2022 年經歷上述的經濟崩潰與政治動盪。
  • 現任領導人: 總統阿努拉・庫馬拉・迪薩納亞克(Anura Kumara Dissanayake),為前左翼「人民解放陣線」(JVP)黨領袖,於 2024 年 9 月以反貪腐政見當選,率領「國家人民力量」(NPP)聯盟執政;總理為哈里妮・阿馬拉蘇里亞(Harini Amarasuriya)。
  • 語言: 僧伽羅語與坦米爾語均為官方語言;英語則廣泛作為行政用語與族群間的「連結語言」使用。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 母湯(Mǔ Tāng,台灣)

  • 名稱: 母湯(字面上是「母親的湯」,但這個中文寫法本身沒有意義——純粹是借用發音)。
  • 起源/歷史: 源自台語「毋通」(m-thang),意思是「不可以」、「不行」。由於標準中文沒有現成的文字可以書寫這個台語詞彙,台灣網友便借用發音相近、但意義完全無關的中文字——「母」(母親)+「湯」(湯品)——來拼出這個詞的讀音,這也是台灣網路中文常見的一種「台語諧音借字」寫法。
  • 如何爆紅: 2010 年代中期起,在 PTT、Dcard、Facebook 等台灣網路平台上廣泛流傳,常被用來留言回應影片或貼文中出現的危險、荒唐或不智行為,以誇張、半開玩笑的驚呼或勸阻語氣表達「不要這樣做啦!」。
  • 意義與使用情境: 帶有詼諧的告誡或不贊同意味,大致相當於「你真的不該這樣」或「這樣不行喔」,語氣通常是戲劇化的誇張,而非真正動怒——是台灣網路留言區回應新聞片段、整人影片或各種令人傻眼的人生抉擇時的經典用語。
  • 變體與演變: 常被拉長語氣寫成「母湯喔」;也出現在 LINE 貼圖與各種迷因圖片中;甚至跨界進入主流廣告與帶有自嘲/幽默意味的半官方場合使用,偶爾也被台灣以外的中文網路社群借用。
  • 目前現況: 已不再是「新興」或當紅的流行語,但已完全融入台灣網路(甚至日常口語)慣用詞彙之中,在日常對話與傳訊息時被隨口使用,不再帶有任何新鮮感。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《不死殭屍:恐慄交響曲》Nosferatu(1922,導演:F.W. 穆瑙)

  • 名稱: 《不死殭屍:恐慄交響曲》(Nosferatu, eine Symphonie des Grauens),1922 年上映。
  • 關鍵人物: 導演為弗里德里希・威廉・穆瑙(F.W. Murnau);編劇亨利克・加林(Henrik Galeen)在未取得授權的情況下,改編自布拉姆・史托克(Bram Stoker)的小說《德古拉》(Dracula)——片中將角色改名(德古拉變成奧洛克伯爵 Count Orlok)試圖規避版權問題,但仍以失敗告終:史托克的遺孀提告並勝訴,法院下令銷毀所有拷貝。所幸海外仍有少數拷貝倖存,這部作品才得以流傳至今。
  • 時代背景: 誕生於威瑪德國時期,屬於德國表現主義電影運動的一員,與《卡里加利博士的小屋》以及穆瑙後來的作品《浮士德》同屬一個藝術脈絡。
  • 內容概述: 房地產職員托馬斯・胡特前往外西凡尼亞,與神秘的奧洛克伯爵洽談房產交易,卻在不知情之下把吸血鬼與死亡瘟疫一併帶回德國家鄉;他的妻子艾倫最終犧牲自己,拖延奧洛克直到日出,讓陽光將其消滅。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 與《卡里加利博士的小屋》採用手繪攝影棚布景不同,穆瑙大多在真實外景拍攝,並疊加表現主義式的陰影運用、傾斜構圖與詭異的負片曝光效果;奧洛克(由麥克斯・許瑞克 Max Schreck 飾演)被設計成鼠類般的怪物形象——光頭、爪狀雙手、獠牙——與後來作品中風度翩翩的貴族吸血鬼形象大相逕庭。片中經典的「陰影爬上樓梯」畫面,至今仍是影史最常被引用的畫面之一。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 確立了多項如今被視為理所當然的吸血鬼傳說設定,其中最著名的便是「陽光可以消滅吸血鬼」——這個設定其實並未出現在史托克的原著小說中——本片也被視為電影恐怖類型最重要的奠基之作。
  • 影響與傳承: 直接啟發了荷索(Werner Herzog)1979 年的翻拍版《吸血鬼》(Nosferatu the Vampyre)以及羅伯・艾格斯(Robert Eggers)2024 年的翻拍版;其充滿波折與悲劇色彩的拍攝過程,也被改編為電影《我和殭屍有份合約》(Shadow of the Vampire,2000);「Nosferatu」一詞本身也已成為流行文化中「吸血鬼」的代名詞。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 在多數國家早已進入公共領域(諷刺的是,正因當年的銷毀令使得正式的版權延展手續無法完成),如今可透過網路檔案館(Internet Archive)等公共領域平台免費觀賞,市面上也流通多個 4K 修復版本(包括 Kino Lorber 版),並經常在影展中搭配現場配樂放映。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、britannica.com

8) 看片方法 — 敘事結構 Narrative Structure(第六課)

第一課教了場面調度,第二課教了剪輯,第三課教了攝影與燈光,第四課教了音效與配樂,第五課教了色彩。今天要跳脫單一畫面或剪接點,從整體來看一部片:敘事結構——電影選擇用什麼順序告訴你這個故事,而這個順序從來都不是中立的。

方法說明: 首先判斷一部片是按照時間順序說故事,還是打亂了時序——如果是後者,再進一步問:這種重新排列具體製造了什麼效果,是照著時間軸講故事所做不到的?非線性結構可以用來營造懸疑感(在你看到結果之後才揭露原因)、製造反諷或悲劇性的份量(讓你在劇情走到那裡之前就已經知道某角色的命運),或是把時間上相隔遙遠的事件,以觀影順序(而非故事發生的時間順序)並置在一起,藉此建立主題上的連結。留意「框架裝置」——電影會刻意重複回到、並藉此首尾呼應全片的某個場景——因為以「銀幕時間」而非「故事時間」來看,一部片選擇用什麼畫面開場、用什麼畫面收尾,往往承載著超乎尋常的結構性意義:電影選擇從哪裡開始、在哪裡結束,本身就是導演對「這部片究竟在講什麼」所做出的宣示。

實例示範 ——《黑色追緝令》Pulp Fiction(1994,導演:昆汀・塔倫提諾 Quentin Tarantino),餐廳首尾呼應的場景: 塔倫提諾把三段鬆散連結的犯罪故事,以完全打亂時序的方式說出來。電影一開場是南瓜與蜜糖兔在餐廳持槍搶劫的畫面,但在劇情尚未收尾前就切換到別的場景;一直到電影的最後一幕,劇情才又回到同一個餐廳場景,把這段故事說完——這意味著,若照故事的內部時間軸來看,真正「最後發生」的情節,其實在片長約三分之二處就已經演完,反而是內部時間軸上發生在中段的一場戲,被放在全片的結尾。正因為這樣的重新排列,殺手文生・維加在片中段就已經「死了」(以角色的故事時間軸而言,這是他最後發生的事),卻又活生生地出現在全片結尾的餐廳戲中,因為這場戲在內部時間軸上,發生得比他的死亡更早。這正是整個安排的用意所在:與其讓某個角色走完一條線性的「起與落」故事弧線,這種時序重排讓朱爾斯在餐廳戲中那場「靈性頓悟」——他選擇放下暴力、轉身離開——得以被放在全片的最後一個音符上,在結構上,讓一個恩典時刻,凌駕於照時間軸「本該」收尾的血腥場面之上。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)結構即論述——事件被呈現的順序,而不只是事件本身,透露了創作者真正認為這個故事是關於什麼;《黑色追緝令》的時序重排,把救贖擺在暴力之上,方法就是把它實實在在地放在銀幕的最後。(2)框架裝置——一部片首尾都回到同一個場景,會把中間發生的一切壓縮進單一個戲劇容器裡,也值得留意:當你看完全片之後,再回頭看這個開場場景,你對它的理解會有什麼改變。(3)透過時序重排製造戲劇反諷——在敘事「抵達」某角色的命運之前就先知道結果(如文生的例子),會製造出和照時間順序完全不同的張力,因為懸念從「發生了什麼事」轉移到「事情是怎麼發生的」或「這代表什麼意義」。這堂課承接第一至五課:場面調度、攝影、音效、色彩都是在單一場景層次上運作的技巧,而敘事結構則是在整部電影的層次上運作,決定了其他所有技巧被呈現給你的順序。
