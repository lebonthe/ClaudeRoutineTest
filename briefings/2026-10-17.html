3) Spanish Lesson — Day 93: Qué vs. Cuál ("What" vs. "Which One")

Qué goes directly before a noun (¿Qué libro lees?) or asks for a definition with ser (¿Qué es un volcán?); cuál / cuáles picks one thing out of a known set or asks for identifying information (¿Cuál es tu número?) and does not take a noun directly (use cuál de…). Interrogatives always carry the accent.

  • ¿Qué quieres beber? — What do you want to drink?
  • ¿Cuál prefieres? — Which one do you prefer?
  • ¿Cuál es la capital de España? — What is the capital of Spain?
  • ¿Qué es una paella? — What is a paella?

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 29: 盜跖 (Dào Zhí) — "Robber Zhi"

The seventh of the Miscellaneous Chapters (雜篇, Ch. 29), and the most openly satirical chapter in the book. It stages a confrontation between Confucius and the legendary brigand Zhi (盜跖), whom the text describes as the younger brother of the honest official Liuxia Ji (柳下季), leading nine thousand followers and raiding the states. Liuxia Ji is ashamed of his brother, and Confucius offers to persuade Zhi to reform. Liuxia Ji warns him it is useless, but Confucius goes to Mount Tai, where Zhi is eating minced human liver. He opens with flattery about Zhi's looks and strength and proposes that Zhi be made a ruler of a great city.

Zhi answers with a furious attack. He calls Confucius a hypocrite who "makes up false words and false deeds, deceives the lords of the world and seeks wealth and rank," and says that those the world calls sages, Yao, Shun, Tang and Wu, were themselves violent or unfilial: Yao failed his son, Shun drove away his brother, Tang exiled his lord. He notes that Bi Gan had his heart cut out and Wu Zixu was thrown into the river, so loyalty ended badly, and that Bo Yi starved on a mountain and Wei Sheng drowned holding a bridge pillar for a promise, dying "no better than a dog or a pig" for the sake of a name. He sums up life: even the longest lives are short, and 人上壽百歲,中壽八十,下壽六十,除病瘦死喪憂患,其中開口而笑者,一月之中不過四五日而已矣 (leaving out sickness, death and worry, one laughs openly only four or five days a month). Heaven and earth are endless, a life is brief, so follow your nature rather than a code. Confucius leaves with his color gone, his head down on the carriage rail, unable to speak, and Zhi's parting remark is that going to a tiger to stroke its head and braid its whiskers nearly cost him his life (疾走料虎頭,編虎須,幾不免虎口哉).

The later part of the chapter is a debate between two invented figures (Zi Zhang and Man Gou De) about whether virtue or profit is the true measure of a person, the same argument from another side. Readers have long debated whether this chapter was written by Zhuangzi himself; many scholars think it belongs to a later hand of the Zhuangzi school because its tone is sharper and plainer than the Inner Chapters. Its core move is the one in Chapter 10 (胠篋): the people who build moral systems and the people who break them may be using the same words.

Takeaway: When someone speaks of virtue, ask what they gain from it, and when someone dies for a name, ask whether the name was worth the life. 5) Country / Region Spotlight — Denmark

  • Location & territory size: Northern Europe, on the Jutland peninsula and over 400 islands (including Zealand, Funen and Bornholm), bordering Germany to the south, with the Øresund strait separating it from Sweden. About 43,000 km² for Denmark proper (general knowledge): about 1.2 times the size of Taiwan (36,200 km²), a little larger than the Netherlands (about 41,500 km²) and about 12% of Germany's 357,600 km². The wider Kingdom includes Greenland (2.17 million km², the world's largest island) and the Faroe Islands, both self-governing. The capital is Copenhagen.
  • Population: About 6 million (general knowledge), about a quarter of Taiwan's 23 million. About 1.4 million live in the Copenhagen metropolitan area; Aarhus is the second city.
  • Religion: The Evangelical Lutheran Church (Folkekirken) is the established church and counts roughly 70% of the population as members, though regular attendance is low. Islam is the second largest religion at about 5-6%. Denmark is among the world's most secular societies (approximate, general knowledge).
  • Economy: A rich, high-tax, high-trust welfare state with a GDP of roughly US$450-500 billion (approximate) and one of the highest incomes per person in the world. The labor market model is "flexicurity" (easy hiring and firing paired with generous unemployment support and retraining). Large firms: Novo Nordisk (Ozempic/Wegovy makers), A.P. Moller-Maersk (shipping), Vestas (wind turbines), Ørsted (offshore wind), Carlsberg and LEGO. Novo Nordisk alone has at times been large enough to move the national economy's growth.
  • History: Viking raiders and traders from the 8th-11th centuries; Harald Bluetooth unified the kingdom and adopted Christianity (c. 965). Queen Margrethe I created the Kalmar Union (1397), joining Denmark, Norway and Sweden; Sweden left in 1523, and Denmark-Norway lasted until 1814. Denmark lost Schleswig-Holstein to Prussia and Austria in 1864, a defeat that shaped its small-state outlook. It was neutral in World War I, was occupied by Germany from April 1940 to May 1945 (the Danish resistance and the 1943 rescue of most Danish Jews to Sweden are famous episodes), joined NATO in 1949 and the EEC in 1973, and kept an opt-out from the euro (rejected in a 2000 referendum). A constitutional monarchy since 1849.

After the inconclusive March 24, 2026 election she formed a four-party minority coalition (Social Democrats, Socialist People's Party, Radikale Venstre and the Moderates) in early June 2026, per France 24 and the House of Commons Library. The election followed a crisis over US designs on Greenland.
  • Language: Danish (official; a North Germanic language closely related to Norwegian and Swedish). Greenlandic is official in Greenland, Faroese in the Faroes, and German is a recognized minority language in Southern Jutland. English is very widely spoken.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — The Tide Pod Challenge

  • Name: The "Tide Pod Challenge" (also "Eating Tide Pods"). A dangerous 2017-2018 trend in which people, mostly teenagers, posted videos of themselves biting laundry-detergent pods. Please do not try this: laundry pods are toxic and have caused hospitalizations and deaths.
  • Origin and history (general knowledge, details approximate): Brightly colored single-use detergent pods began selling in the US around 2012 and were soon linked to accidental poisonings of young children. The joke that they "look delicious" came first as an ironic meme on Tumblr, Twitter and Reddit in 2015-2017, with the Procter & Gamble product (Tide) as the stock example; I have not verified a specific first post. It turned into a real "challenge" around December 2017 and January 2018.
  • How it became popular: A feedback loop of ironic jokes, then some people filming actual attempts for views. Media coverage spread it faster than the joke itself. Poison-control centers reported a spike in teen exposures in early January 2018 (reports of several dozen cases in the first weeks of the year, compared with far fewer in the previous full years; exact counts not verified here). Procter & Gamble responded with a public video featuring football player Rob Gronkowski telling people not to do it.
  • Meaning and usage: As a meme it stood for self-mocking, absurd "dares" and for the gap between internet irony and real-world behavior. People used "Tide Pods" as a one-word joke about bad decisions, and "do not eat the Tide Pod" jokes were part of the response.
  • Variations and evolution: Pod-shaped joke images, parody ads and edits of real detergent packaging. The reaction included platform policy changes: YouTube removed videos of the challenge and other platforms restricted related content and searches.
  • Current status: A reference point in discussions of how platforms and brands deal with harmful viral trends, and a classic late-2010s meme. It is mostly retired as a live trend, though still referenced in articles on dangerous social-media challenges. (Meme-history details from general knowledge, not verified today.)

7) Film / Performing-Arts Spotlight — Chungking Express (重慶森林, 1994, dir. Wong Kar-wai)

  • Name and creators: Chungking Express (Chinese title 重慶森林, "Chongqing Forest"), a Hong Kong film written and directed by Wong Kar-wai. Cinematography by Christopher Doyle and Andrew Lau. Cast: Takeshi Kaneshiro, Brigitte Lin, Tony Leung Chiu-wai, Faye Wong. Running time about 102 minutes.
  • Era and background: Released in 1994, in the last years before the 1997 handover of Hong Kong to China, a period of anxiety about time and identity. Wong reportedly made it quickly and cheaply, during a break from the long post-production of his martial arts film Ashes of Time, working with a loose script and improvisation.
  • Plot overview: Two separate stories joined by a Chungking Mansions location and the Midnight Express snack bar. In the first, Cop 223 (Kaneshiro), just left by his girlfriend, buys cans of pineapple that expire on May 1, the date he gives himself to be loved again, and meets a woman in a blonde wig (Lin) involved in a drug-smuggling job. In the second, Cop 663 (Tony Leung) is left by his flight-attendant girlfriend and talks to the objects in his apartment, while Faye (Faye Wong), who works at the snack bar, secretly enters his flat to tidy it and swap the contents of his shelves, to the sound of "California Dreamin'" by The Mamas & the Papas, which she plays on repeat.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Handheld camerawork, step-printing (a slowed, blurred motion look), saturated neon colors, wide-angle lenses, voice-over narration and sudden jumps in tempo. The film treats loneliness as something played out in small, quirky objects and routines (expiry dates, cans, a soap, a toy plane). Pop songs are used like emotions made audible.
  • Cultural and historical significance: It brought Wong Kar-wai international attention and became one of the defining Hong Kong films of the 1990s. It won major Hong Kong Film Awards, reportedly including Best Film and Best Director. In the US, Quentin Tarantino's Rolling Thunder Pictures distributed it in 1996.
  • Influence and legacy: It shaped the look of 1990s and 2000s "urban romance" in Asian cinema and influenced later art-house directors; its mix of dreamy romance, ironic humor and city atmosphere is often compared to the French New Wave. It is a frequent reference in discussions of how to film loneliness in a crowded city.
  • Current status / where to experience it: Restored versions have been released on home video (including through the Criterion Collection), and the film appears in retrospectives and repertory screenings; streaming availability changes, so check current listings. 8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 79: Tone and Tonal Control (How a Film Makes You Feel What It Wants You to Feel)

The method: Tone is the film's attitude toward its material: serious, ironic, tender, menacing, absurd. It is separate from genre and from plot. The same event can be tragic, funny or frightening depending on tone. To analyze it, ask: (1) Content vs. delivery: does the manner of presentation match the subject, or contradict it (a grim subject delivered with politeness or comedy)? (2) Performance pitch: are actors playing it naturalistic, heightened or deadpan? (3) Music and sound: does the score tell you what to feel or does it comment ironically? (4) Camera and editing: is the camera calm and observing, or restless? Is the cutting brisk (comic) or slow (solemn)? (5) Design and lighting: realistic or stylized, clean or oppressive? (6) Shifts: when does the tone change, and what is that change doing: relieving tension, cruelly undermining a comfort, or exposing the story's real subject?

How to analyze it: Choose a moment and write down one word for the tone. Then list the elements pulling toward that word and any pulling against it. A strong film often holds two tones at once (comedy over dread, tenderness inside violence), and the tension between them is its meaning. Then check where the film stops and starts the tension: a tonal shift is usually a turning point in the film's argument.

Worked example — Dr. Strangelove or: How I Learned to Stop Worrying and Love the Bomb (1964, dir. Stanley Kubrick, cinematography Gilbert Taylor, production design Ken Adam), the War Room scenes: The subject is the possible end of the world, yet the film is a satire. (a) Content vs. delivery: American officials argue like office managers about a "Doomsday" machine; President Muffley (Peter Sellers) tries to keep order with "Gentlemen, you can't fight in here! This is the War Room!", a line that is funny because the room exists to manage fighting on a planetary scale. (b) Performance: George C. Scott's General Turgidson is played at a heightened, bullish pitch, while Sellers' Muffley is played flat and exhausted; the gap between the two registers is the comedy. (c) Design: Ken Adam's huge, cavernous War Room, dominated by a circular table under a ring of light, looks like a grand temple of decision and a poker room at once. (d) Camera and light: Taylor shoots it in stark black and white with a documentary plainness, so the absurd is filmed as if it were fact. (e) Shift and ending: The film closes with real nuclear explosions set to Vera Lynn singing "We'll Meet Again," a sweet, sentimental song laid over annihilation. The ironic song makes the horror colder, not softer. Kubrick reportedly shot a custard pie fight for the War Room finale and cut it because it tipped the film from satire into farce, a good example of tonal control by subtraction. What to look for: in any comedy or tragedy, ask which element is pulling against the surface mood, and which would break the film if it were removed. 3) 西班牙文課 — 第 93 天:Qué 與 Cuál(「什麼」與「哪一個」)

Qué 直接放在名詞前(¿Qué libro lees?),或與 ser 連用問定義(¿Qué es un volcán?);cuál / cuáles 是從已知的一組東西中挑出一個,或詢問識別資料(¿Cuál es tu número?),不直接接名詞(要接就用 cuál de…)。疑問詞一律要有重音符號。

  • ¿Cuál prefieres? — 你偏好哪一個?
  • ¿Cuál es la capital de España? — 西班牙的首都是哪裡?
  • ¿Qué es una paella? — 什麼是 paella(西班牙燉飯)?

4) 莊子 — 第 29 篇:盜跖(Dào Zhí)— 「盜跖」

《雜篇》第七篇(雜篇,全書第 29 篇),也是全書最直白的諷刺篇章。它安排了孔子與傳說中的大盜跖正面衝突:跖被描寫為正直官員柳下季的弟弟,帶著九千名徒眾橫行諸侯。柳下季以弟弟為恥,孔子提議去勸跖改過。柳下季警告沒用,孔子仍前往泰山,看到跖正在吃切碎的人肝。孔子先稱讚跖的外貌與勇力,並提議讓他做大城的諸侯。

跖以一番怒斥回應。他罵孔子是偽君子,「矯言偽行,以迷惑天下之主,而欲求富貴」;又說世人稱為聖人的堯、舜、湯、武其實不是暴虐就是不慈:堯對兒子不慈,舜放逐弟弟,湯放逐君主。他舉出比干被剖心、伍子胥被沉江,忠心沒有好下場;伯夷餓死山中、尾生為守約抱橋柱溺死,為了名聲而死,「與磔犬流豕、操瓢而乞者,無異」。他總結人生:最長壽的人也不過百歲,人上壽百歲,中壽八十,下壽六十,除病瘦死喪憂患,其中開口而笑者,一月之中不過四五日而已矣。天地無窮,人生有限,應當順著本性而活,而不是依循一套規範。孔子失色離開,伏在車軾上低頭,說不出話來;跖最後的話是:你去摸老虎的頭、編老虎的鬚,差點就免不了虎口(疾走料虎頭,編虎須,幾不免虎口哉)。

本篇後半是兩位虛構人物(子張與滿苟得)之間的辯論,討論衡量一個人的標準究竟是德行還是利益,是同一個論點的另一面。歷來有人討論本篇是否為莊子本人所作;不少學者認為,它的語氣比內篇更尖銳直白,應是後來莊子學派的手筆。它的核心手法與第 10 篇(胠篋)相近:建立道德制度的人,與破壞它的人,可能用的是同一套詞彙。

5) 國家 / 地區介紹 — 丹麥(Denmark)

  • 位置與面積: 位於北歐,橫跨日德蘭半島與四百多個島嶼(包括西蘭島、菲英島、博恩霍姆島),南邊與德國接壤,隔著厄勒海峽與瑞典相望。丹麥本土約 43,000 平方公里(一般知識):約為台灣(36,200 平方公里)的 1.2 倍,比荷蘭(約 41,500 平方公里)略大,約為德國(357,600 平方公里)的 12%。王國的範圍還包括格陵蘭(217 萬平方公里,世界最大島)與法羅群島,兩者皆為自治領地。首都哥本哈根。
  • 人口: 約 600 萬人(一般知識),約為台灣 2,300 萬人的四分之一。哥本哈根都會區約 140 萬人,第二大城為奧胡斯。
  • 宗教: 福音路德教會(Folkekirken)是國教,約 70% 的人口是教會成員,但定期參加禮拜的人不多。伊斯蘭教是第二大宗教,約 5-6%。丹麥是世界上世俗化程度最高的社會之一(約略數字,一般知識)。
  • 經濟: 富裕、高稅收、高信任度的福利國家,GDP 約 4,500-5,000 億美元(約略),人均所得居世界前列。勞動市場採「彈性安全」(flexicurity)模式:容易僱用與解僱,搭配慷慨的失業給付與再培訓。大型企業:諾和諾德(Ozempic / Wegovy 製造商)、馬士基(航運)、維斯塔斯(風力發電機)、沃旭能源(離岸風電)、嘉士伯與樂高。諾和諾德本身一度大到足以影響全國經濟成長。
  • 歷史: 8-11 世紀的維京人;哈拉爾「藍牙王」統一王國並接受基督教(約 965 年)。瑪格麗特一世建立卡爾馬聯盟(1397 年),結合丹麥、挪威與瑞典;瑞典 1523 年退出,丹麥-挪威則延續到 1814 年。1864 年丹麥輸給普魯士與奧地利,失去石勒蘇益格-荷爾斯泰因,這次戰敗塑造了小國的自我定位。第一次世界大戰中立;1940 年 4 月至 1945 年 5 月被德國占領(丹麥抵抗運動與 1943 年大部分丹麥猶太人被送往瑞典的救援行動很有名);1949 年加入北約,1973 年加入歐洲經濟共同體,並保有歐元的豁免權(2000 年公投否決)。1849 年起為君主立憲制。
  • 現任領導人: 國家元首:國王腓特烈十世(2024 年 1 月 14 日起,瑪格麗特二世退位後)。首相:梅特·弗雷澤里克森(社會民主黨),第三個任期。2026 年 3 月 24 日的選舉沒有明確勝負,她在 2026 年 6 月初組成四黨少數政府(社會民主黨、社會主義人民黨、激進自由黨與溫和黨),見法新社(France 24)與英國下議院圖書館。這次選舉之前,丹麥與美國之間因格陵蘭議題發生了危機。
  • 語言: 丹麥語(官方語言,北日耳曼語,與挪威語、瑞典語相近)。格陵蘭語是格陵蘭的官方語言,法羅語是法羅群島的官方語言,德語是南日德蘭的法定少數語言。英語非常普及。
  • 來源與說明: 領導人資訊來自 France 24、英國下議院圖書館與 AP(News4Jax)。其餘數字為一般知識,屬約略值。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Tide Pod Challenge(洗衣膠囊挑戰)

  • 名稱: 「Tide Pod Challenge」(又稱「Eating Tide Pods」)。2017-2018 年一個危險的風潮:有人(多半是青少年)發布自己咬洗衣膠囊的影片。請千萬不要嘗試:洗衣膠囊有毒,已造成住院與死亡。
  • 起源與歷史(一般知識,細節為約略): 色彩鮮豔的單次用洗衣膠囊約在 2012 年於美國上市,很快就與幼兒誤食中毒事件連在一起。「它們看起來好好吃」的笑話,最先是 2015-2017 年在 Tumblr、Twitter 與 Reddit 上的反諷迷因,以寶僑(P&G)的 Tide 產品作為固定的例子;我沒有查證最早的一則貼文。到了 2017 年 12 月至 2018 年 1 月,它變成真正的「挑戰」。
  • 如何流行: 反諷笑話與一些人為了流量真的拍攝嘗試,兩者互相餵養的循環。媒體報導傳播得比笑話本身更快。毒物防治中心在 2018 年 1 月初通報青少年接觸事件激增(報導是年初幾週有數十例,遠高於前幾個整年;確切數字本文未查證)。寶僑公開發表影片,由美式足球選手羅伯·格隆考斯基呼籲大家不要這麼做。
  • 意義與使用情境: 作為迷因,它象徵自嘲、荒謬的「挑戰」,以及網路反諷與現實行為之間的落差。大家把「Tide Pods」當成一句話來指稱糟糕的決定,「不要吃 Tide Pod」的笑話也成為回應的一部分。
  • 變化與演變: 膠囊形狀的惡搞圖片、仿製廣告,以及改過的真實洗衣膠囊包裝。反應還包括平台政策的變動:YouTube 移除相關挑戰影片,其他平台也限制了相關內容與搜尋。
  • 現況: 它成為討論平台與品牌如何處理有害網路風潮的參考案例,也是 2010 年代後期的經典迷因。作為流行趨勢已大致退場,但仍常被拿來引用於討論社群媒體危險挑戰的文章。(迷因歷史細節出自一般知識,今天未查證。)

7) 電影 / 表演藝術介紹 — 《重慶森林》(Chungking Express,1994,導演王家衛)

  • 名稱與創作者: 《重慶森林》(英文片名 Chungking Express),香港電影,由王家衛編劇與導演。攝影:杜可風與劉偉強。演員:金城武、林青霞、梁朝偉、王菲。片長約 102 分鐘。
  • 年代與背景: 1994 年上映,正是香港 1997 年回歸中國前的最後幾年,社會瀰漫著對時間與身分的焦慮。據說王家衛是在武俠片《東邪西毒》漫長的後製期間抽空,以快速而低成本的方式,靠鬆散的劇本與即興拍出這部片。
  • 劇情概述: 兩個獨立的故事,由重慶大廈這個場景與「午夜快車」小吃店串起來。第一個故事,警察 223(金城武)剛被女友分手,買下一堆 5 月 1 日到期的鳳梨罐頭,把這天當作自己重新被愛的期限,並遇到一位涉及毒品走私的金髮墨鏡女子(林青霞)。第二個故事,警察 663(梁朝偉)被空姐女友離開,對著家裡的物品說話,而在小吃店工作的阿菲(王菲)偷偷進入他的公寓打掃、更換架上的東西,同時反覆播放 The Mamas & the Papas 的〈California Dreamin'〉。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 手持攝影、降格拍攝(動作放慢而模糊的質感)、飽和的霓虹色彩、廣角鏡頭、旁白獨白,以及節奏的突然跳躍。影片把孤獨表現在小小的奇特物件與日常習慣上(到期日、罐頭、肥皂、玩具飛機)。流行歌曲被當成「聽得見的情緒」來使用。
  • 文化與歷史意義: 它讓王家衛受到國際矚目,成為 1990 年代最具代表性的香港電影之一。它在香港電影金像獎獲得多項大獎,據報包括最佳影片與最佳導演。在美國,由昆汀·塔倫提諾的 Rolling Thunder Pictures 於 1996 年發行。
  • 影響與遺產: 它塑造了 1990 年代與 2000 年代亞洲電影「都會愛情」的外觀,並影響了後來的藝術電影導演;夢幻的愛情、反諷的幽默與城市氣氛的混合,常被拿來和法國新浪潮相提並論。討論「如何在擁擠的城市中拍出孤獨」時,它是常被引用的範例。
  • 現況 / 哪裡可以欣賞: 修復版已發行家用影音(包括 Criterion Collection),也會出現在回顧展與經典重映;串流平台上架情況常變動,請查詢最新片單。(細節出自一般知識;今天未對照來源查證。)

8) 看片方法 — 第 79 課:語調與調性控制(影片如何讓你感受它想要的感受)

方法: 調性(tone)是影片對素材的態度:嚴肅、反諷、溫柔、威脅、荒謬。它與類型、情節是分開的。同一個事件,依調性不同,可以是悲劇、喜劇或恐怖。分析時問:(1) 內容與呈現:呈現的方式與主題相符,還是相反(用禮貌或喜劇的方式處理一個陰森的主題)?(2) 表演音高:演員是自然、誇張還是面無表情?(3) 音樂與聲音:配樂是告訴你該感受什麼,還是以反諷的方式評論?(4) 鏡頭與剪接:鏡頭是冷靜觀察,還是躁動?剪接明快(喜劇)還是緩慢(莊重)?(5) 設計與燈光:寫實還是風格化,乾淨還是壓迫?(6) 轉換:調性在何時改變,這個改變做了什麼:緩解緊張、殘酷地破壞安慰,還是揭露故事真正的主題?

如何分析: 選一個片段,寫下一個形容它調性的詞。然後列出所有把它拉向這個詞的元素,以及拉向相反方向的元素。好的電影常同時持有兩種調性(恐懼之上的喜劇、暴力之中的溫柔),兩者之間的張力就是它的意義。接著檢查影片在哪裡製造或放鬆這種張力:調性的轉換通常就是影片論點的轉折點。

實例分析 — 《奇愛博士》(Dr. Strangelove or: How I Learned to Stop Worrying and Love the Bomb,1964,導演史丹利·庫柏力克,攝影吉伯特·泰勒,美術設計肯·亞當)戰情室的場景: 主題是世界可能毀滅,但影片是一部諷刺劇。(a) 內容與呈現:美國官員像辦公室經理一樣爭論一台「末日」機器;穆夫利總統(彼得·謝勒斯)想維持秩序,說出 「先生們,你們不能在這裡打架!這裡是戰情室!」,這句話好笑,是因為這個房間本來就是用來管理全球規模戰爭的。(b) 表演:喬治·C·史考特飾演的塔吉森將軍演得誇張而好鬥,而謝勒斯的穆夫利則演得平淡而疲憊;兩種音高之間的落差就是喜劇。(c) 設計:肯·亞當設計的巨大、空曠的戰情室,以圓桌與一圈燈光為中心,看起來既像決策的神殿,也像撲克牌室。(d) 鏡頭與光線:泰勒以強烈的黑白、近乎紀錄片的平實拍攝,讓荒謬的事彷彿事實一般。(e) 轉換與結尾:影片結束在真實的核爆畫面,配上薇拉·琳演唱的〈We'll Meet Again〉,一首甜美、感傷的歌疊在毀滅之上。反諷的歌曲讓恐怖更冷,而不是更柔和。據說庫柏力克曾為戰情室結局拍過一場奶油派大戰,後來剪掉,因為它會把影片從諷刺推向鬧劇,是「以減法控制調性」的好例子。觀察重點:在任何喜劇或悲劇中,問哪個元素正在與表面的情緒拉扯,以及若拿掉哪一個,影片就會垮掉。(製作細節出自一般知識;今天未對照來源查證。)
