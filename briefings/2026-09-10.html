3) Spanish Lesson — Day 60 (10 min, Two Verbs for "To Know": Saber vs. Conocer)

Steps outside the tense/mood/pronoun/se-family/obligation tracks (Days 46-59) into another foundational everyday-usage gap that English/Chinese speakers collapse into one word: Spanish's two distinct verbs for "to know." Saber (irregular yo-form sé) is used for facts, information, or a que-clause/question-word clause (sé la respuesta, sé que..., sé dónde...), and — critically — saber + infinitive means "to know how to" do something (sé nadar, "I know how to swim"), a structure conocer never takes. Conocer (irregular yo-form conozco) is used for familiarity/acquaintance with a specific person, place, or thing — someone you've met, somewhere you've been, something you've experienced directly — and, when its object is a specific person, obligatorily takes Day 58's personal "a" (conozco a mi vecino). Both verbs also carry the same preterite-vs-imperfect aspectual shift already taught for poder on Day 18: the imperfect (sabía/conocía) marks an already-ongoing state of knowing/being acquainted, while the preterite (supe/conocí) marks the instantaneous event of finding something out or meeting someone for the first time.

  • ¿Sabes la respuesta a esta pregunta? — Do you know the answer to this question?
  • No, pero sé nadar muy bien — eso sí lo sé. — No, but I know how to swim very well — that much I do know.
  • Lo conocí la semana pasada y por fin sé dónde queda. — I met him last week, and now I finally know where it's located.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 60: 節 (Jié) — "Limitation" (Water over Lake) (Kǎn 坎/Water above, Duì 兌/Lake below — 水澤節). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws a direct line from the previous hexagram: "物不可以終離,故受之以節。" ("Things cannot remain scattered/separated forever, therefore Huàn [Hexagram 59, Dispersion] is followed by Jié; Jié means to limit, to regulate.") Where Hexagram 59 addressed how a dispersing group or state of mind could be drawn back together around a shared center, Hexagram 60 addresses what comes next once things are gathered again: the need for boundaries, measure, and restraint to keep a reunited whole from either dissolving again or collapsing under its own unchecked excess. The Judgment reads: "節,亨。苦節不可貞。" ("Jié. Success. But bitter [excessive] restraint should not be persisted in.") The judgment is characteristically double-edged: proper limitation brings success, yet limitation carried too far — restraint that curdles into joyless deprivation or rigid austerity for its own sake — becomes counterproductive and cannot be sustained. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "節亨,剛柔分而剛得中。苦節不可貞,其道窮也。說以行險,當位以節,中正以通。天地節而四時成,節以制度,不傷財,不害民。" ("Jié brings success because the firm and yielding lines are distinct from each other, and the firm line attains the middle position. Bitter restraint cannot be persisted in, because that path leads to exhaustion. [The lower trigram's] joy proceeds through [the upper trigram's] danger; occupying one's proper place through limitation, being centered and correct, one achieves success. Heaven and earth have their own regulation, and thus the four seasons are formed; when limitation is established through institutions and measures, it damages neither resources nor harms the people.") This passage grounds "limitation" in something larger than personal self-denial: just as the natural order of heaven and earth produces the regulated cycle of the four seasons — a limitation that makes abundance possible rather than restricting it — human limitation, properly institutionalized as measured systems rather than imposed as arbitrary harshness, protects both a community's resources and its people rather than depriving them. The Image commentary reads: "澤上有水,節;君子以制數度,議德行。" ("Water above the lake: this is the image of Jié. Thus the superior person establishes numerical standards and measures, and deliberates on virtuous conduct.") A lake can only hold a fixed volume of water — beyond its rim, water simply overflows and is lost — so the lake's very shape is what allows it to hold water at all, turning a boundary into the precondition for capacity rather than a mere restriction upon it. Read against Hexagram 59, the pair traces a complementary cycle: dispersion is answered by reunification around a shared center (Hexagram 59), and once reunified, that gathered whole needs measured limitation — clear standards, proportion, and self-discipline — to avoid either dissolving apart again or overflowing into its own self-destruction, much as a lake's banks are what let it hold water rather than merely contain it.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Thailand (泰國)

  • Location: Mainland Southeast Asia, bordered by Myanmar to the west and northwest, Laos to the north and east, Cambodia to the east and southeast, and Malaysia to the south, with coastlines on both the Gulf of Thailand and the Andaman Sea; its shape includes a long, narrow southern peninsula reaching toward Malaysia.
  • Territory: About 513,000 km² (roughly the 50th-largest country in the world) — about 14 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), and slightly smaller than France (~551,700 km²).
  • Population: Approximately 71.7 million as of 2026 (ranked around 20th in the world) — roughly three times Taiwan's population, with about 54% living in urban areas.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Theravada Buddhist (roughly 93%), with a significant Muslim minority (roughly 5%) concentrated in the southernmost provinces bordering Malaysia, plus small Christian and other minorities; Buddhist temples (wat) and saffron-robed monks are a central part of everyday public life.
  • Economy: Nominal GDP of roughly $580 billion (2026 estimate) with around 2% projected growth; a major regional manufacturing hub for automobiles and auto parts (often called the "Detroit of Asia"), electronics, and agricultural exports (notably rice and rubber, both among the world's largest export volumes); tourism is an outsized contributor to GDP, built around destinations like Bangkok, Phuket, and Chiang Mai.
  • History: The only Southeast Asian nation never formally colonized by a European power, historically known as Siam through the Sukhothai (13th-14th century) and Ayutthaya (14th-18th century) kingdoms before the current Chakri dynasty was founded in 1782 by Rama I in Bangkok. A bloodless 1932 revolution ended centuries of absolute monarchy and established a constitutional monarchy; the country officially renamed itself Thailand in 1939. The 20th and 21st centuries have seen repeated military coups (the most recent in 2014, led by then-army chief Prayut Chan-o-cha) interspersed with periods of civilian democratic government, alongside the exceptionally long and stabilizing reign of King Bhumibol Adulyadej (Rama IX, reigned 1946-2016), succeeded by his son upon his death.
  • Current leader: Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul, the country's 32nd prime minister, who took office September 7, 2025; Head of State is King Maha Vajiralongkorn (Rama X), who has reigned since his father's death in 2016.
  • Language: Thai is the sole official language, written in its own Thai script derived from ancient Khmer script; several regional languages/dialects are also widely spoken, including Isan (closely related to Lao) in the northeast, Northern Thai (Kham Mueang) in the north, and Southern Thai in the south, plus Malay among the Muslim-majority southern border provinces; English is widely used in tourism and business.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (Anutin Charnvirakul), worldometers.info

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Among Us" / "Sus"

  • Name/origin: Among Us is an online multiplayer social-deduction video game developed and published by American indie studio InnerSloth, released quietly in June 2018 for mobile and PC. Its gameplay draws heavily on party games like Mafia/Werewolf and the paranoid crew-versus-infiltrator premise of John Carpenter's film The Thing — players work as "Crewmates" completing tasks aboard a spaceship while one or more secret "Impostors" sabotage the ship and covertly murder Crewmates one by one.
  • How it became popular: The game attracted little attention for nearly two years until mid-2020, when, amid COVID-19 lockdowns, it was picked up by major Twitch and YouTube streamers (including PewDiePie and Corpse Husband, alongside a wave of Brazilian streamers) and exploded into one of the most-played and most-watched games of 2020, reportedly reaching over 100 million monthly active players at its peak — a viral resurgence almost entirely disconnected from its original, largely unnoticed 2018 launch.
  • Meaning/usage: The word "sus" (short for "suspicious"), used constantly in-game to accuse a suspected Impostor, broke out of the game entirely into mainstream youth/internet slang, now used generically to describe anyone or anything behaving oddly or untrustworthily, in contexts with no connection to the game whatsoever. Related in-game vocabulary — "imposter" as a stand-in for any hidden bad actor, and "emergency meeting" as a joking way to call out something suspicious in everyday life — became similarly detached, reusable meme phrases.
  • Variations/evolution: A viral trap-remix song built around in-game voice lines ("when the imposter is sus") spread widely on TikTok and YouTube; the game's simple bean-shaped, colorful "Crewmate" character became an instantly recognizable visual meme reused in fan art, cosplay, and crossover cameos in other games and media; "sus" itself was added to major online dictionaries as recognized slang.
  • Current status: Considered one of the defining phenomena of pandemic-era internet and gaming culture; the game's own mainstream visibility declined considerably after its 2020-2021 peak, and InnerSloth has since released further content updates and a spin-off, but "sus" has outlived the game's peak popularity to remain a permanent fixture of everyday youth slang well beyond its gaming origins.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (sus)

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — A Trip to the Moon (Le Voyage dans la Lune, 1902, dir. Georges Méliès)

  • Name/key figures: A Trip to the Moon (French: Le Voyage dans la Lune), a 1902 French film written, directed, produced by, and starring Georges Méliès — a former stage magician and owner of the Théâtre Robert-Houdin in Paris — who plays the lead astronomer, Professor Barbenfouillis.
  • Era/background: Released in 1902, roughly 14 minutes long, and loosely inspired by Jules Verne's novel From the Earth to the Moon and H.G. Wells's The First Men in the Moon, made at a moment when cinema itself was barely seven years old and still largely used for short actuality footage rather than sustained fictional storytelling.
  • Plot overview: A group of eccentric astronomers builds a bullet-shaped capsule fired from a giant cannon toward the Moon, famously landing directly in the eye of the Man in the Moon's face; the explorers encounter hostile insectoid alien Selenites, are captured, escape, and plunge back to Earth (splashing into the ocean), returning home to a triumphant heroes' welcome.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Méliès pioneered in-camera special effects techniques including the "stop trick" (stopping the camera mid-shot to substitute or remove an object, creating apparent transformations or disappearances), double exposure, dissolves, and elaborately painted theatrical stage sets and backdrops that directly reflect his background as a magician and theater proprietor rather than a documentary-style filmmaker; a hand-colored print (each frame individually tinted by hand) also existed from the film's original release.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as one of the earliest films to use sustained narrative storytelling rather than simply recording an event, and the first widely recognized work of science-fiction cinema; its single image of the cannon-shell capsule lodged in the Moon's eye is among the most reproduced and instantly recognizable images in all of film history.
  • Influence and legacy: Considered foundational to the science-fiction and fantasy film genres and to the development of narrative film editing and special effects generally; a rediscovered hand-colored print, found in Barcelona in 1993, underwent a painstaking decade-long restoration completed in 2011, premiering at the Cannes Film Festival with a new score by the French electronic band Air; Méliès's life and the rediscovery of his work were later dramatized in Martin Scorsese's Hugo (2011).
  • Current status/where to experience it: Available restored on Blu-ray (Flicker Alley) and through various film-archive and streaming platforms; a standard fixture of film history courses covering early cinema, special effects, and the birth of narrative filmmaking.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, filmforum.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — The MacGuffin (Lesson 46)

A MacGuffin is an object, goal, or piece of information that drives a plot forward and motivates characters to act, chase, or scheme — while its own specific nature remains, by design, unimportant to the story's real thematic or emotional interest. Coined into popular use (though not strictly invented) by Alfred Hitchcock, who joked that in a spy film the MacGuffin is often simply "the papers," and the audience never actually needs to know what's written on them for the story to work. This is distinct from Symbolism & Visual Metaphor (Day 8 in Section 7's method track), which requires a recurring image to accumulate and transfer meaning across its appearances, and from Foreshadowing & Chekhov's Gun (also taught earlier in this track), which concerns a planted detail that must later pay off through a meaningful reveal — a MacGuffin needs neither recurrence nor a payoff reveal; it only needs to be desirable enough that characters will act, betray, and risk everything to obtain it. The test to identify one: ask whether the object could be swapped for something else entirely different without changing anything about the story's actual meaning or characters' relationships — if the answer is yes, it's a MacGuffin, and the film is really about something else entirely.

Worked example: The Maltese Falcon (1941, dir. John Huston) — the jewel-encrusted black falcon statuette that private detective Sam Spade and a rotating cast of double-crossing rivals scheme, lie, and kill to obtain throughout the film turns out, in the final scene, to be a worthless plaster forgery; the genuine article is never recovered on screen. The falcon's actual value or appearance is never the point — the film's real interest lies in the shifting alliances, greed, and Spade's own morally ambiguous choices along the way, all of which would play out identically if the desired object were swapped for any other equally valuable prize. Kasper Gutman's closing line calling it "the stuff that dreams are made of" (an ironic misquote of Shakespeare's The Tempest) only underscores that the object's material worth was always beside the point of the story being told.

3)西班牙文課程——第 60 天(10 分鐘,兩種「知道」:Saber 與 Conocer)

今天跳出第 46-59 天的時態/語氣/代名詞/se 家族/義務表達脈絡,補上另一個中英文使用者常常合併成同一個詞、卻在西班牙語裡截然不同的日常用法缺口——西班牙語表達「知道」有兩個不同動詞。Saber(不規則 yo 形式為 sé)用於事實、資訊,或接 que 子句/疑問詞子句(sé la respuesta、sé que...、sé dónde...);而且——這是關鍵——saber + 原形動詞表示「會做」某件事(sé nadar,「我會游泳」),這是 conocer 完全不具備的結構。Conocer(不規則 yo 形式為 conozco)則用於對特定人、地方或事物的熟悉、認識——曾經見過的人、去過的地方、親身體驗過的事物——而且,當受詞是特定的人時,依第 58 天的規則,一定要加上個人受詞 a(conozco a mi vecino)。這兩個動詞也都帶有第 18 天教過、poder 早已示範過的「過去未完了式 vs. 簡單過去式」語意轉變:過去未完了式(sabía/conocía)表示早已持續存在的知道/熟悉狀態,簡單過去式(supe/conocí)則表示瞬間發生的「得知」或「初次見面」事件。

  • ¿Sabes la respuesta a esta pregunta? —— 你知道這題的答案嗎?
  • No, pero sé nadar muy bien — eso sí lo sé. —— 不知道,但我很會游泳——這件事我倒是知道。
  • Lo conocí la semana pasada y por fin sé dónde queda. —— 我上星期認識他/發現這間店的,現在終於知道它在哪裡了。

4)易經卦象

第 60 卦:節 (Jié) ——「節」(水澤節、節制)(水澤節——上坎下兌:上卦為坎〔水〕,下卦為兌〔澤〕。)《序卦傳》承接前一卦而來:「物不可以終離,故受之以節。」(事物不可能永遠處於離散、渙散的狀態,因此渙卦〔第 59 卦,渙散〕之後,接的是節卦;節,意指節制、規範。)第 59 卦處理的是渙散的群體或心境,如何重新凝聚在一個共同核心周圍;第 60 卦則接著處理:當事物重新凝聚之後,接下來該做什麼——需要界限、分寸與節制,才能讓重新凝聚的整體,不至於再度渙散,也不至於因毫無節制的過度膨脹而自我崩潰。卦辭寫道:「節,亨。苦節不可貞。」(節卦,亨通。但過度、令人痛苦的節制,不可長久堅持。)這條卦辭的態度一如既往地帶有雙面性:適當的節制能帶來亨通,但節制若走向極端——那種讓人痛苦、失去喜悅、為節制而節制的僵化苦行——反而會產生反效果,無法長久維持。《彖傳》進一步闡述:「節亨,剛柔分而剛得中。苦節不可貞,其道窮也。說以行險,當位以節,中正以通。天地節而四時成,節以制度,不傷財,不害民。」(節卦之所以亨通,是因為剛健與柔順的爻彼此分明,而剛健的爻居於中位。過度的節制之所以不可長久堅持,是因為那條路終將走向窮困。〔下卦的〕喜悅,透過〔上卦的〕危險前行;各安其位、以節制自持,居中守正,便能通達亨通。天地自有其節制規律,因而形成四季更迭;若節制是透過制度與分寸來建立,便不會損耗財貨,也不會傷害人民。)這段話把「節制」安放在比個人自我克制更宏大的脈絡之中:正如天地的自然秩序,正是靠著節制、規律,才形成了讓萬物得以豐盛生長的四季循環——這種節制成就了豐盛,而非單純限制豐盛——人為的節制若能妥善建立為有分寸的制度,而非強加為毫無道理的嚴苛手段,便能同時保護一個群體的資源與其人民,而非剝奪他們。《象傳》寫道:「澤上有水,節;君子以制數度,議德行。」(水澤之上蓄有水,這就是節卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應當制定數量與制度的標準,並審慎商議自身的德行操守。)一座湖泊,只能容納固定容量的水——一旦超過湖岸邊界,水便會溢出而流失;正是湖岸這道「界限」,才讓湖泊得以蓄水成形——把邊界從單純的限制,轉化為「能夠盛裝、承載」的前提條件。若與第 59 卦對照,兩卦恰好勾勒出一段互補的循環:渙散,靠著圍繞共同核心重新凝聚而獲得解答(第 59 卦);而一旦重新凝聚,這個凝聚而成的整體,便需要有分寸的節制——清楚的標準、恰如其分的比例,以及自律——才能避免再度渙散分裂,或因毫無節制而自我膨脹、自我毀滅,正如湖岸讓湖泊得以「盛裝」水體,而不僅僅是「圍住」水體而已。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——泰國(Thailand)

  • 位置:位於東南亞大陸,西側與西北側與緬甸接壤,北側與東側與寮國相鄰,東側與東南側與柬埔寨接壤,南側與馬來西亞相連,同時濱臨泰國灣與安達曼海;國土形狀包含一條向南延伸、逼近馬來西亞的狹長半島。
  • 領土:總面積約 51.3 萬平方公里(全球面積約排名第 50 大)——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 14 倍,略小於法國(約 551,700 平方公里)。
  • 人口:截至 2026 年,人口約 7,170 萬人(全球排名約第 20 位)——約為台灣人口的三倍,其中約 54% 居住於都市地區。
  • 宗教:絕大多數人口信仰南傳上座部佛教(約占 93%),另有相當比例的穆斯林少數族群(約占 5%),主要集中於鄰近馬來西亞的最南端各府;此外還有少數基督教徒與其他信仰。佛寺(wat)與身披袈裟的僧侶,是泰國日常公共生活中極為核心的一部分。
  • 經濟:名目 GDP 約 5,800 億美元(2026 年預估值),預估成長率約 2%;是東南亞區域重要的汽車與汽車零組件製造重鎮(常被稱為「亞洲底特律」),同時也是電子產品與農產品(尤以稻米與橡膠聞名,出口量皆位居全球數一數二)的主要出口國;以曼谷、普吉島與清邁等地為核心的觀光業,對 GDP 的貢獻比重極高。
  • 歷史:泰國是東南亞唯一從未被歐洲列強正式殖民的國家,歷史上長期被稱為暹羅(Siam),歷經素可泰王朝(13-14 世紀)與大城王朝(14-18 世紀),直至現今的卻克里王朝(Chakri Dynasty)於 1782 年由拉瑪一世(Rama I)在曼谷建立。1932 年一場不流血革命,終結了長達數世紀的絕對君主制,建立起君主立憲制;該國於 1939 年正式更名為泰國(Thailand)。20 世紀至 21 世紀,泰國歷經多次軍事政變(最近一次為 2014 年,由時任陸軍總司令帕拉育〔Prayut Chan-o-cha〕主導),期間穿插文人民主政府執政時期,同時也經歷了在位時間異常長久、發揮穩定作用的蒲美蓬國王(拉瑪九世,1946 至 2016 年在位)統治時期,國王駕崩後由其子繼位。
  • 現任領導人:總理阿努廷·查恩維拉庫(Anutin Charnvirakul),為該國第 32 任總理,於 2025 年 9 月 7 日就任;國家元首為瑪哈·瓦吉拉隆功國王(拉瑪十世),自其父駕崩後,於 2016 年起在位至今。
  • 語言:泰語為唯一官方語言,使用源自古高棉文字的獨立泰文字母書寫;此外境內亦廣泛使用多種地區語言/方言,包括東北部與寮語關係密切的伊善語(Isan)、北部的北泰語(Kham Mueang),以及南部的南泰語,另外在穆斯林人口占多數的南部邊境各府則普遍使用馬來語;英語在觀光與商業領域則廣泛通行。

6)網路迷因介紹——《Among Us》/「Sus」

  • 名稱/起源:《Among Us》是一款線上多人社交推理遊戲,由美國獨立工作室 InnerSloth 開發並發行,於 2018 年 6 月悄悄上線行動裝置與電腦平台。其遊戲玩法大量借鏡狼人殺/黑手黨(Mafia/Werewolf)一類的派對遊戲,以及約翰·卡本特(John Carpenter)電影《突變第三型》(The Thing)那種「船員 vs. 潛伏滲透者」的懸疑設定——玩家扮演「船員」(Crewmate)在太空船上完成任務,同時一名或多名秘密的「冒名頂替者」(Impostor)則暗中破壞太空船,並逐一謀殺船員。
  • 如何走紅:這款遊戲在上線後將近兩年間乏人問津,直到 2020 年年中,適逢新冠疫情封城期間,被多位知名 Twitch 與 YouTube 直播主(包括 PewDiePie、Corpse Husband,以及一波巴西直播主)相繼採用直播,一夕爆紅,成為 2020 年最多人遊玩、也最多人觀看的遊戲之一,據稱高峰時期月活躍玩家一度突破一億人——這波爆紅幾乎與其 2018 年原本乏人問津的上線時期完全脫節。
  • 意義/使用情境:遊戲中用來指控可疑「冒名頂替者」的常用字「sus」(suspicious 的縮寫),完全脫離遊戲本身,成為主流青少年/網路流行語,如今泛指任何行為怪異、令人起疑的人或事,使用情境與遊戲本身毫無關聯。相關的遊戲內詞彙——「imposter」(冒名頂替者)被用來泛指任何隱藏的壞人角色,「emergency meeting」(緊急會議)則被戲謔地用來指出日常生活中任何可疑之事——同樣脫離遊戲本身,成為可重複套用的迷因詞彙。
  • 變體/演化:一首以遊戲內語音台詞混音改編、爆紅的 trap 混音歌曲(「when the imposter is sus」)在 TikTok 與 YouTube 上廣泛流傳;遊戲中造型簡單、色彩繽紛、豆子形狀的「船員」角色,成為一眼可辨的視覺迷因符號,被大量運用於粉絲創作、角色扮演(cosplay),以及其他遊戲與媒體的跨界客串之中;「sus」一詞本身也已被主要線上詞典收錄為正式承認的流行語。
  • 目前狀態:被視為疫情時期網路與遊戲文化最具代表性的現象之一;該遊戲本身在 2020-2021 年高峰過後,主流能見度已大幅下滑,InnerSloth 此後雖持續推出內容更新與衍生作品,但「sus」這個詞的流行程度早已超越遊戲本身的高峰期,至今仍是日常青少年流行語彙中根深蒂固的一部分,早已遠遠脫離其遊戲起源。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《月球旅行記》(Le Voyage dans la Lune / A Trip to the Moon,1902,導演:喬治·梅里葉)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《月球旅行記》(法語:Le Voyage dans la Lune)是一部 1902 年的法國電影,由喬治·梅里葉(Georges Méliès)——一位曾是舞台魔術師、並在巴黎經營羅貝爾-烏丹劇院(Théâtre Robert-Houdin)的人物——一手包辦編劇、導演、製作,並親自主演片中領隊的天文學家巴本弗依教授(Professor Barbenfouillis)。
  • 時代/背景:本片於 1902 年上映,片長約 14 分鐘,鬆散取材自儒勒·凡爾納(Jules Verne)小說《從地球到月球》,以及 H.G. 威爾斯(H.G. Wells)小說《月球上最早的人類》;拍攝當時,電影本身這項媒介誕生還不到七年,絕大多數作品仍停留在單純記錄真實事件的短片,尚未廣泛發展出持續性的虛構敘事。
  • 劇情概述:一群性格古怪的天文學家,建造出一枚子彈狀的太空艙,由一門巨型大砲發射前往月球,以電影史上最著名的畫面之一——太空艙直接命中「月中人」臉上的眼睛——降落月球;探險隊在月球上遭遇具攻擊性、外形似昆蟲的月球外星人「賽勒尼特人」(Selenites),一度遭俘虜後成功逃脫,墜落返回地球(墜入海中),最終以英雄之姿凱旋歸來,受到熱烈歡迎。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:梅里葉開創了多項運用攝影機直接完成的特效技巧,包括「停機再拍」(stop trick,拍攝中途暫停攝影機,替換或移除畫面中的物件,製造出物體瞬間變身或消失的錯覺)、雙重曝光,以及精心繪製、極具劇場感的舞台佈景與背景畫布——這些手法直接反映出他身為魔術師與劇院經營者的背景,而非紀錄片式的拍攝取徑;本片原始發行版本中,也曾存在一份逐格手工上色的拷貝。
  • 文化/歷史意義:普遍被視為最早採用持續性虛構敘事手法、而非單純記錄事件的電影作品之一,也是史上第一部獲得廣泛認可的科幻電影;片中太空艙嵌入月亮眼睛的單一畫面,是整個電影史上被複製、引用次數最多、也最一眼可辨的經典影像之一。
  • 影響與傳承:被視為科幻與奇幻類型電影的奠基之作,也對敘事剪輯與特效技術的整體發展影響深遠;一份於 1993 年在巴塞隆納重新尋獲的手工上色拷貝,歷經長達十年、極其細膩的修復工程,於 2011 年完成,並在坎城影展首映,搭配法國電子音樂團體 Air 全新創作的配樂;梅里葉的生平事蹟,以及他的作品被重新尋獲的過程,後來也被馬丁·史柯西斯(Martin Scorsese)改編為電影《雨果的冒險》(Hugo,2011)。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:目前可透過 Flicker Alley 發行的修復版藍光光碟,以及多個電影資料館與串流平台觀賞;本片至今仍是電影史課程中,探討早期電影、特效技術與敘事電影誕生的標準教材。

8)看片方法——麥高芬 The MacGuffin(第 46 課)

麥高芬(MacGuffin)是指一項物件、目標,或一則資訊,它能推動整部劇情前進,驅使角色為之行動、追逐或算計——但這項物件本身的具體性質,卻在設計上刻意對故事真正的主題或情感核心無關緊要。這個詞由亞佛烈德·希區考克(Alfred Hitchcock)推廣為流行用語(雖非他本人首創),他曾戲稱,在間諜片中,麥高芬經常就只是「那份文件」,而觀眾其實完全不需要知道文件裡到底寫了什麼,故事照樣能夠成立。這不同於第 7 單元方法課程中,第 8 天教過的「象徵與視覺隱喻」(需要一個反覆出現的意象,在每次登場時累積、傳遞意義),也不同於同一課程軌跡稍早教過的「伏筆與契訶夫之槍」(關於一項預先埋下的細節,必須在後續透過有意義的揭曉來兌現)——麥高芬既不需要反覆出現,也不需要透過揭曉來兌現承諾;它只需要「夠誘人」,誘人到足以讓角色們願意為它行動、背叛,並不惜賭上一切去爭奪。判斷一個物件是否為麥高芬的測試方法:問問看,這個物件是否可以被替換成任何一個完全不同的其他物件,而不影響故事真正的意義,或角色之間的關係——如果答案是「可以」,那它就是麥高芬,而這部電影真正要講的,其實是完全另一回事。

實例演練:《梟巢喋血戰》(The Maltese Falcon,1941,導演:約翰·休斯頓〔John Huston〕)——片中,私家偵探山姆·史培德(Sam Spade)與一群不斷互相背叛的對手,全片竭盡心機、說謊,甚至不惜殺人,只為爭奪一尊鑲滿珠寶的黑色獵鷹雕像;然而到了片尾揭曉,這尊雕像其實只是一件毫無價值的石膏贗品,真品自始至終從未在銀幕上被找到。這尊獵鷹雕像的實際價值或外觀從來都不是重點——這部電影真正關注的,是角色們在追逐過程中不斷變換的結盟關係、貪婪,以及史培德本人道德曖昧的種種抉擇,即使把這件被爭奪的目標,換成任何一件同樣珍貴的寶物,這些故事核心也完全不會改變。片中古特曼(Kasper Gutman)最後那句形容獵鷹雕像的台詞「那正是夢想的材質所在」(the stuff that dreams are made of,刻意誤引自莎士比亞《暴風雨》的台詞),恰恰反諷地凸顯出:這件物品的實際物質價值,從頭到尾都不是這個故事真正想說的重點。
