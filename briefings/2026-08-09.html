3) Spanish Lesson — Day 31 (10 min, builds on Day 30)

Days 27 through 30 covered five of the six WEIRDO trigger categories for the present subjunctive: Wishes, Doubt, part of Impersonal expressions, Emotions, and Recommendations/the rest of Impersonal expressions. Today completes the acronym with its final letter: Ojalá, a fixed exclamation (not a conjugating verb) borrowed from Arabic "law shā' allāh" ("if God wills it") via nearly eight centuries of Moorish presence on the Iberian Peninsula, always followed by the subjunctive.

  • Ojalá que llueva mañana. — I hope it rains tomorrow.
  • Ojalá tengas mucha suerte en el examen. — I hope you have a lot of luck on the exam.
  • Ojalá que todos podamos vernos pronto. — I hope we can all see each other again soon.
  • ¡Ojalá! — I hope so! (used standalone, like an interjection)

Grammar note: unlike every other WEIRDO trigger taught so far, Ojalá is not a verb — it never conjugates (no "yo ojalo") and doesn't require two different subjects, so it can express a wish about oneself, about someone else, or about an uncontrollable event like the weather, with "que" optional before the subjunctive clause. It earns its own WEIRDO letter rather than folding into Day 27's wish-verbs because it also carries a further trick not covered today: swapping in the imperfect subjunctive (a more advanced tense) lets the exact same word pivot from "a hopeful wish about the future" (Ojalá llueva) into "a wistful wish about something known to be false" (Ojalá lloviera — I wish it were raining, but it isn't) — a flexibility no other trigger word in the set has. With today's lesson, all six WEIRDO categories (Wishes, Emotions, Impersonal expressions, Recommendations, Doubt, Ojalá) are now complete.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 31: 咸 (Xián) — "Influence" / "Wooing" / "Feeling" (Lake above, Mountain below — Duì over Gèn). This hexagram opens the Lower Canon (下經, hexagrams 31-64), and the Xugua (Sequence) commentary marks the transition with one of the Yijing's most famous cosmological chains of reasoning, explaining why the entire second half of the book begins here: "有天地然後有萬物,有萬物然後有男女,有男女然後有夫婦,有夫婦然後有父子,有父子然後有君臣,有君臣然後有上下,有上下然後禮義有所錯。" ("Once there is Heaven and Earth, only then are there the myriad things; once there are the myriad things, only then are there male and female; once there are male and female, only then is there husband and wife; once there is husband and wife, only then is there father and son; once there is father and son, only then is there ruler and subject; once there is ruler and subject, only then is there high and low; once there is high and low, only then can ritual and righteousness find their proper place.") Where the Upper Canon (1-30) traced cosmic and natural forces, the Lower Canon begins by grounding all human social and political order in the most basic relationship of all — the mutual attraction between man and woman — and Xián, "feeling/influence," is chosen as its opening image. The Judgment reads: "咸,亨,利貞,取女吉。" ("Influence: success. Perseverance furthers. Taking a wife brings good fortune.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) explains the hexagram's structure directly: "咸,感也。柔上而剛下,二氣感應以相與,止而說,男下女,是以亨利貞,取女吉也。天地感而萬物化生,聖人感人心而天下和平,觀其所感,而天下萬物之情可見矣。" ("Xián means to feel, to influence. The soft [trigram] is above and the hard [trigram] is below; the two vital forces stir and respond to each other and thus unite. [Gèn is] Still, and [Duì is] Joyous; the man places himself below the woman — hence success, perseverance, and good fortune in taking a wife. Heaven and Earth feel/influence each other, and the myriad things are transformed and born; the sage moves the hearts of the people, and all under heaven becomes peaceful and harmonious. Observe what it is that stirs feeling, and the true condition of all things under heaven can be seen.") The Image commentary adds the hexagram's ethical instruction: "山上有澤,咸;君子以虛受人。" ("A lake resting atop a mountain: this is Xián. Thus the superior person keeps themselves empty/open, so as to receive others.") The core teaching threads through all three texts: genuine influence — whether between spouses, between ruler and people, or between Heaven and Earth — cannot be forced; it arises only when the receiving party remains humble and receptive (the mountain, still and low, letting the lake's water settle into it) rather than dominant, which is why the hexagram singles out the unusual image of the young man symbolically placing himself "below" the young woman as the model for how influence should properly begin.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Timor-Leste (東帝汶)

  • Location: A Southeast Asian nation occupying the eastern half of the island of Timor in the Lesser Sunda Islands (the western half belongs to Indonesia), plus the Oecusse exclave inside Indonesian West Timor and the small island of Atauro; it lies at the southeastern tip of the Indonesian archipelago, just north of Australia across the Timor Sea.
  • Territory: Approximately 14,900 km² — a little over 40% of Taiwan's land area (~36,193 km²), and roughly comparable in size to Connecticut or the country of Montenegro; the terrain is mountainous and rugged, with Mount Tatamailau (also called Mount Ramelau) reaching about 2,986 meters.
  • Population: Approximately 1.4 million (2023 est.), giving it one of the youngest and fastest-growing populations in Asia, with a median age in the low twenties.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Roman Catholic (around 97%), a legacy of nearly 400 years of Portuguese colonial rule reinforced during the Indonesian occupation, when the Catholic Church became a rallying institution for national identity and resistance rather than a colonial imposition; small Protestant and Muslim minorities exist alongside continuing local animist practices.
  • Economy: Heavily dependent on oil and gas revenue from the Timor Sea (managed through a sovereign wealth fund, the Petroleum Fund), with subsistence agriculture (coffee is the leading non-oil export) still employing most of the workforce; it remains one of Asia's poorest countries by GDP per capita, with the government now pushing to diversify away from a maturing oil-and-gas base and pursuing ASEAN membership.
  • History: A Portuguese colony from the early 16th century until Portugal's 1975 decolonization, when a brief civil war ended with a unilateral declaration of independence by Fretilin, immediately followed by an Indonesian invasion and 24-year occupation (1975-1999) marked by an estimated 100,000-200,000 deaths from conflict, famine, and disease. A UN-supervised referendum in 1999 delivered a landslide vote for independence, followed by a UN transitional administration (UNTAET) that governed the territory until full independence was restored on May 20, 2002, making it the first new sovereign state of the 21st century.
  • Current leader: President José Ramos-Horta (in office since 2022, also a 1996 Nobel Peace Prize laureate for his independence advocacy) and Prime Minister Xanana Gusmão, the former resistance guerrilla leader and the country's first president, who returned to the premiership in 2023 — the two most prominent architects of independence still leading the state together decades later.
  • Language: Two official languages, Portuguese and Tetum (an Austronesian language and the most widely spoken lingua franca), with Indonesian and English designated as "working languages"; over 30 additional local languages are spoken across the country's mountainous regions.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov/the-world-factbook

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Salt Bae

  • Name: "Salt Bae," the internet nickname for Nusret Gökçe, a Turkish chef and owner of the Nusr-Et steakhouse chain.
  • Origin/history: In January 2017, Gökçe posted an Instagram video of himself elegantly carving a tomahawk steak and then theatrically sprinkling salt off the back of his hand and down his forearm before letting it cascade onto the meat, finished with a smug half-smile directly at the camera. The clip's operatic self-confidence over an utterly mundane act — seasoning meat — struck viewers as simultaneously impressive and absurd, and it spread across Twitter and Instagram within days, quickly overtaking the original culinary context entirely.
  • How it became popular: The salt-sprinkling gesture itself became an instantly recognizable, easily imitated physical meme — celebrities, athletes, and everyday users filmed themselves recreating the exact arm angle and flourish over food, drinks, sand, snow, or anything sprinkle-able, while reaction GIFs of the moment circulated as a stand-in for "watch this smoothly, effortlessly executed flex." Gökçe leaned into the fame rather than resisting it, rapidly expanding Nusr-Et into a global steakhouse chain that traded explicitly on his meme-driven celebrity.
  • Meaning/usage: Used to caption or react to any moment of exaggerated, self-satisfied showmanship over a small or ordinary action — the gesture works as shorthand for "performing effortless coolness," and later, as Nusr-Et's prices and celebrity clientele (including gold-leaf-covered steaks costing hundreds of dollars) drew media scrutiny, the meme's tone shifted to also carry a satirical edge about ostentatious wealth and influencer-chef culture.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned the "salt bae" gesture as a standalone emoji-adjacent hand sign, countless parody videos and merchandise, a wave of negative press and viral backlash over Nusr-Et's steep prices and uneven food-safety/service reviews at flagship locations (including in London and Miami), and periodic revival cycles whenever a new "flex" video imitates the original arm motion.
  • Current status: The meme itself peaked in 2017-2018 and now functions mostly as a dated but instantly understood cultural reference; Nusr-Et remains a real, controversy-prone international restaurant business, and Gökçe himself has remained a recognizable celebrity chef whose personal brand is inseparable from the original viral clip.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, knowyourmeme.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Commedia dell'arte

  • Name/creator(s): Commedia dell'arte ("comedy of craft/of professional artists"), an Italian form of improvised popular theatre with no single author or founder — it emerged as a professional tradition practiced by touring troupes (compagnie) rather than a text-based art form written by playwrights.
  • Era/background: Flourished across Italy from roughly the mid-16th century through the 18th century, then spread throughout Europe (especially France, where it heavily cross-pollinated with local farce and later ballet and opera-comique); it arose alongside the rise of professional, paid theatre companies (as opposed to earlier amateur or court-only performance) and is considered the first major European theatrical tradition built around professional, itinerant performers.
  • Plot/content overview: Rather than fixed scripts, troupes worked from a canovaccio — a loose scenario outlining plot points (mistaken identities, thwarted young lovers, scheming servants, a miserly old man) — and improvised the actual dialogue live, night after night, drawing on a shared stock of rehearsed comic routines called lazzi (a servant pretending to faint to steal food, elaborate acrobatic pratfalls, mistaken-letter gags) that could be inserted into almost any scenario.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Built around a fixed roster of stock character types instantly recognizable by their half-masks, costumes, and physical mannerisms — including Arlecchino (Harlequin, the acrobatic, food-obsessed trickster-servant in his diamond-patchwork costume), Pantalone (the miserly old merchant), Il Dottore (the pompous, long-winded fake scholar), Pulcinella (the hunch-backed, scheming Neapolitan servant), and the young lovers (Innamorati) who alone typically performed unmasked; performers trained for years in specific stock roles, developing virtuosic physical comedy, acrobatics, and audience-interactive improvisation rather than memorized recitation.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Established the template for the servant-trickster and the buffoonish authority figure that recurs throughout later Western comedy, and its professionalized, improvisation-based troupe model directly shaped how European theatre companies organized and toured for centuries afterward.
  • Influence and legacy: Its stock characters and masks fed directly into French pantomime and the Pierrot/Columbine tradition, into Molière's comic archetypes, into the British Punch and Judy puppet tradition (Pulcinella becomes "Punch"), and into the silent-film clown tradition of Chaplin and Keaton (the tramp-trickster figure, the physical lazzi-style gag); the visual language of Harlequin's diamond costume and mask remains instantly recognizable in Western popular culture today, including in ballet, opera (I Pagliacci), and Halloween costuming.
  • Current status/where to experience: No longer performed as a continuously living improvised tradition in its original form, but actively studied and revived in theatre schools and conservatories worldwide (mask-work and lazzi training remain a staple of physical-theatre and clowning pedagogy), and periodically staged by specialist companies and festivals across Italy and France; its character archetypes and masks are also widely referenced or directly restaged in opera, ballet, and street theatre.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, britannica.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Continuity Editing & the 180-Degree Rule (Shot/Reverse Shot) (Lesson 17)

Lesson 2 (Editing & Montage) covered how juxtaposed shots generate meaning through rhythm and association. Today narrows the lens to a different, more foundational editing job: how cutting between shots maintains — or deliberately breaks — a coherent sense of physical space, which is a separate question from what meaning a cut produces.

The method: The 180-degree rule holds that once a scene establishes an imaginary line (the "axis of action") running through its subjects — most commonly two people facing each other in conversation — the camera should stay on one side of that line for every subsequent shot. Doing so keeps screen direction consistent (a character looking left stays looking left in every cut) so the audience's mental map of "who is where, facing which way" never has to be rebuilt. The workhorse technique built on this rule is shot/reverse shot: alternating between a shot of Character A (usually favoring their side of the axis, often over Character B's shoulder) and a matching shot of Character B, which lets a dialogue scene cut back and forth without ever losing spatial clarity. A closely related tool is the eyeline match — cutting from a character looking in a direction to a shot of what (or whom) they're looking at, positioned so the angle matches where their eyes were pointed, which is what makes shot/reverse shot conversations feel like two people are truly looking at each other rather than at two disconnected cameras. Because this whole system works by being invisible when followed correctly, the most revealing way to analyze it is to ask: is the axis being respected, and if a film breaks the 180-degree rule or bends the eyeline match, is that a mistake — or a deliberate choice to make the audience feel spatially or psychologically off-balance?

Worked example — The Silence of the Lambs (1991, dir. Jonathan Demme, cinematography by Tak Fujimoto), the Hannibal Lecter interrogation scenes: In Clarice Starling's early conversations with Lecter through the glass of his cell, Demme's reverse shots don't use the normal, slightly offset over-the-shoulder angle; instead, both actors are shot looking almost directly into the lens, as close to a true eyeline match with the camera itself as the shot/reverse-shot convention allows.

What to look for and why it works: (1) The rule is followed structurally but pushed to its most intense edge — Demme keeps the 180-degree axis and the basic shot/reverse-shot rhythm fully intact (we never lose track of who's where), but by aligning each character's eyeline almost exactly with the lens, the technique itself becomes the source of discomfort rather than something invisible; the audience feels looked at, not just shown a conversation. (2) The choice is applied asymmetrically and means something different for each character — Lecter's near-camera stare reads as predatory and invasive, a psychological violation of Clarice (and the viewer) mirroring his character; Clarice's own near-camera looks instead read as vulnerable exposure, since she is younger, less powerful in the scene, and effectively "seen through" by both Lecter and the audience. (3) It demonstrates the core lesson of today's method — because 180-degree, over-the-shoulder shot/reverse shot is such a deeply normalized convention, audiences register the departure from its usual angle as unease before they can consciously name why; recognizing the technique is precisely what lets you explain a feeling the untrained eye can only sense.

  • Ojalá que llueva mañana. — 但願明天會下雨。
  • Ojalá tengas mucha suerte en el examen. — 但願你考試順利。
  • Ojalá que todos podamos vernos pronto. — 但願我們大家能快點再見面。
  • ¡Ojalá! — 但願如此!(單獨使用,像感嘆詞)

文法重點:和目前為止教過的所有其他 WEIRDO 觸發詞不同,Ojalá 不是動詞——它不會變化(沒有「yo ojalo」這種形式),也不需要句子前後有兩個不同主詞,因此可以用來表達對自己的願望、對別人的願望,或是對天氣這類完全無法控制之事的願望,後面的 que 也可以省略不用,直接接虛擬式子句。它之所以能獨立成為 WEIRDO 的專屬一類、而不是併入第二十七天的希望動詞,是因為它還藏著今天沒有深入教授的另一項特殊能力:只要把動詞換成過去未完成虛擬式(更進階的時態),同一個字就能從「對未來懷抱希望的願望」(Ojalá llueva)瞬間切換成「明知不可能成真、帶點惋惜意味的願望」(Ojalá lloviera——但願正在下雨,但事實上沒有)——這種彈性是口訣中其他任何觸發詞都沒有的。學完今天的內容,WEIRDO 六大類別(希望、情感、非人稱表達、建議、懷疑、Ojalá)正式全部集滿。

4) 易經卦象

第三十一卦:咸卦(Xián)——「感應」/「求偶」/「感受」(上澤下山,兌上艮下)。這一卦開啟了《易經》的「下經」(第三十一卦至第六十四卦),〈序卦傳〉以《易經》中最著名的宇宙論式推理鏈,說明了為何全書後半段要從這裡開始:「有天地然後有萬物,有萬物然後有男女,有男女然後有夫婦,有夫婦然後有父子,有父子然後有君臣,有君臣然後有上下,有上下然後禮義有所錯。」(有了天地,才有萬物;有了萬物,才有男女之分;有了男女,才有夫婦;有了夫婦,才有父子;有了父子,才有君臣;有了君臣,才有上下之別;有了上下之別,禮義才有安放之處。)如果說「上經」(第一至三十卦)描繪的是宇宙與自然力量的運行,「下經」則從最基本的人倫關係——男女之間的相互吸引——為一切社會與政治秩序奠定根基,而咸卦(「感應」)正是被選為這段旅程的開篇意象。卦辭寫道:「咸,亨,利貞,取女吉。」(感應之卦:亨通,正固有利,娶妻可獲吉祥。)〈彖傳〉直接解釋了這一卦的結構:「咸,感也。柔上而剛下,二氣感應以相與,止而說,男下女,是以亨利貞,取女吉也。天地感而萬物化生,聖人感人心而天下和平,觀其所感,而天下萬物之情可見矣。」(咸,意即感應。柔爻在上,剛爻在下,陰陽二氣相互感應、彼此契合;下卦為艮(止),上卦為兌(悅),男子謙居於女子之下,因此得以亨通、正固,娶妻可獲吉祥。天地相互感應,萬物因而化育而生;聖人感動人心,天下因而和平安樂。觀察是什麼引發了感應,天下萬物的真實情狀便可由此得見。)〈象傳〉則補上這一卦的德行教誨:「山上有澤,咸;君子以虛受人。」(山上有澤水停駐,這正是咸卦的象徵;因此德行高尚的人,應當讓自己保持謙虛空明,以接納他人。)這三段文字貫穿著同一個核心教誨:真正的感應——無論是夫妻之間、君王與百姓之間,或是天地之間——都無法用強迫的方式達成,唯有當接受的一方保持謙遜、開放(如同靜止而低伏的山,任由澤水自然停駐其上),而非強勢主導,感應才會發生,這也是為何這一卦特別選用「男子謙居於女子之下」這個看似反常的意象,作為感應應當如何開始的典範。

(第三十一次介紹的卦象——也是「下經」(第三十一卦至第六十四卦)的第一卦——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)、第二十一卦 噬嗑(Shì Kè)、第二十二卦 賁(Bì)、第二十三卦 剝(Bō)、第二十四卦 復(Fù)、第二十五卦 无妄(Wú Wàng)、第二十六卦 大畜(Dà Chù)、第二十七卦 頤(Yí)、第二十八卦 大過(Dà Guò)、第二十九卦 坎(Kǎn)、第三十卦 離(Lí)、第三十一卦 咸(Xián)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 東帝汶(Timor-Leste)

  • 位置: 位於東南亞小巽他群島,占據帝汶島東半部(西半部屬印尼領土),另外還擁有位於印尼西帝汶境內的歐庫西飛地(Oecusse),以及外海的阿陶羅島(Atauro);地理位置在印尼群島的東南端,隔帝汶海與澳洲北方相望。
  • 領土面積: 約 14,900 平方公里——略高於台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的四成,面積與美國康乃狄克州或蒙特內哥羅相近;地形以崎嶇山地為主,最高峰塔塔邁勞山(又稱拉梅勞山)海拔約 2,986 公尺。
  • 人口: 約 140 萬人(2023 年估計),是亞洲人口最年輕、成長最快速的國家之一,人口年齡中位數僅二十出頭。
  • 宗教: 絕大多數人口信仰天主教(約 97%),這是近四百年葡萄牙殖民統治留下的遺產,並在印尼占領期間進一步強化——當時天主教會成為凝聚國家認同與反抗運動的核心機構,而非殖民壓迫的象徵;另有少數新教徒與穆斯林,傳統的萬物有靈信仰在部分地區仍持續存在。
  • 經濟: 高度依賴帝汶海的石油與天然氣收入(透過主權基金「石油基金」管理),同時自給自足的農業(咖啡是最主要的非石油出口品)仍是多數勞動人口的謀生方式;以人均 GDP 計算,東帝汶仍是亞洲最貧窮的國家之一,政府正致力於在石油天然氣資源逐漸成熟之際推動經濟多元化,並積極爭取加入東協。
  • 歷史: 自 16 世紀初起為葡萄牙殖民地,直到 1975 年葡萄牙撤出殖民地後爆發短暫內戰,最終由獨立革命陣線(Fretilin)單方面宣布獨立,卻立刻遭印尼入侵,展開長達 24 年的占領統治(1975-1999),期間因戰亂、饑荒與疾病導致估計 10 萬至 20 萬人喪生。1999 年聯合國監督下的公投中,壓倒性多數選民支持獨立,隨後由聯合國過渡行政當局(UNTAET)接管治理,直到 2002 年 5 月 20 日正式恢復完全獨立,使東帝汶成為 21 世紀第一個新誕生的主權國家。
  • 現任領導人: 總統喬瑟・拉莫斯・奧塔(José Ramos-Horta,自 2022 年上任,曾因推動獨立運動於 1996 年獲頒諾貝爾和平獎)與總理夏納納・古斯芒(Xanana Gusmão,曾任游擊反抗軍領袖、東帝汶首任總統,2023 年重返總理職位)——這兩位當年推動獨立建國最重要的關鍵人物,數十年後仍共同領導這個國家。
  • 語言: 官方語言有兩種:葡萄牙語與德頓語(Tetum,一種南島語系語言,也是通行最廣的共通語),印尼語與英語則被列為「工作語言」;境內崎嶇的山區另有超過 30 種當地語言通行。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov/the-world-factbook

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 撒鹽哥(Salt Bae)

  • 名稱: 「撒鹽哥」(Salt Bae),是土耳其主廚努斯瑞特・格克切(Nusret Gökçe)的網路暱稱,他也是牛排連鎖餐廳 Nusr-Et 的創辦人。
  • 起源/歷史: 2017 年 1 月,格克切在 Instagram 上傳一段影片,片中他優雅地切割一塊戰斧牛排,接著戲劇性地將鹽從手背、沿著前臂滑落般灑下,最後直視鏡頭、露出一抹得意的微笑。這段影片對「灑鹽」這種再平凡不過的動作,展現出近乎歌劇般的自信,讓觀眾覺得既厲害又荒謬,短短數天內便在推特與 Instagram 上瘋傳,徹底超越了原本的烹飪情境。
  • 如何爆紅: 灑鹽這個動作本身,迅速變成一個極易辨識、也極易模仿的肢體迷因——名人、運動員與一般網友紛紛拍片重現同樣的手臂角度與灑鹽動作,對象從食物、飲料到沙子、雪花無所不包;這個瞬間的反應 GIF 圖也大量流傳,被當成「展示一種毫不費力、行雲流水般炫技」的代名詞。格克切本人並未抗拒這股爆紅熱潮,反而順勢將 Nusr-Et 迅速擴張成一個全球連鎖牛排品牌,並直接把這股迷因式的知名度轉化為商業資本。
  • 意義與使用情境: 這個哏常被用來為任何一個「小題大作、自信滿滿地完成一件平凡小事」的畫面下標題或作為回應——這個動作可說是「毫不費力耍帥」的代稱;後來隨著 Nusr-Et 高昂的價格與名流顧客(包括要價數百美元、灑上金箔的牛排)引發媒體關注與批評,這個迷因的語氣也逐漸多了一層諷刺炫富與網紅主廚文化的意味。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出獨立成一種近似表情符號的「撒鹽哥」手勢、數不清的模仿影片與周邊商品,以及一波針對 Nusr-Et 旗艦店(包括倫敦、邁阿密分店)高昂價格與參差不齊的食安/服務評價所引發的負面輿論與網路反彈;每當有新的「炫技」影片模仿原始的甩手動作時,這個哏也會週期性地重新翻紅。
  • 目前現況: 這個迷因本身的熱潮高峰落在 2017-2018 年,如今主要作為一個「過氣但一看就懂」的文化參照物存在;Nusr-Et 至今仍是一間真實存在、爭議不斷的國際連鎖餐廳事業,格克切本人也持續維持知名主廚的身分,他的個人品牌與當年那支爆紅影片已密不可分。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 藝術喜劇(Commedia dell'arte)

  • 名稱/創作者: 藝術喜劇(Commedia dell'arte,字面意思是「行家的喜劇」/「專業藝人的喜劇」),一種義大利即興通俗劇場形式,並沒有單一的作者或創始人——它是由巡迴劇團(compagnie)發展出的專業演出傳統,而非由劇作家撰寫劇本的文本藝術形式。
  • 時代背景: 大約從 16 世紀中葉到 18 世紀,盛行於義大利各地,隨後傳播至歐洲各國(尤其是法國,與當地的鬧劇傳統深度交融,後續更影響了芭蕾與喜歌劇);這股風潮伴隨著專業、支薪劇團(有別於先前僅限業餘或宮廷演出)的興起而誕生,並被視為歐洲第一個以專業巡迴演員為核心建立起來的重要劇場傳統。
  • 劇情概述: 藝術喜劇並非採用固定劇本,而是依循一份稱為「canovaccio」的鬆散劇情大綱——概略列出情節走向(身分誤認、遭阻撓的年輕戀人、耍心機的僕人、吝嗇的老頭)——實際台詞則由演員每晚現場即興發揮,並援引一套共享的、事先排練好的固定笑料套路,稱為「lazzi」(例如僕人假裝暈倒藉機偷食物、精心設計的雜技式跌倒橋段、書信誤送的笑料),幾乎可以套用進任何劇情大綱之中。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 建立在一組固定的角色類型上,觀眾光看半罩式面具、服裝與肢體習慣動作就能立刻辨認出角色身分——包括阿爾勒基諾(Arlecchino,即哈勒昆 Harlequin,身穿菱格拼布服、擅長雜技又貪吃的搗蛋僕人)、潘塔隆(Pantalone,吝嗇的老商人)、博士(Il Dottore,自命不凡、滿口空話的假學者)、普欽奈拉(Pulcinella,駝背、心機重的那不勒斯僕人),以及通常不戴面具演出的年輕戀人(Innamorati);演員需要多年訓練才能精通特定的固定角色,培養出高超的肢體喜劇、雜技與即興互動能力,而非單純背誦台詞。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 為後世西方喜劇中反覆出現的「耍心機的僕人」與「滑稽的權威人物」這類角色原型,奠定了最初的模板;其專業化、以即興為核心的劇團運作模式,更直接影響了往後數百年歐洲劇團的組織與巡演方式。
  • 影響與傳承: 其固定角色與面具,直接催生了法國默劇與皮埃羅/科倫比娜(Pierrot/Columbine)的傳統、莫里哀作品中的喜劇角色原型、英國的潘趣與朱蒂木偶劇(Punch and Judy,普欽奈拉演變為「潘趣」),以及卓別林、基頓等人開創的默片喜劇丑角傳統(流浪漢式的搗蛋角色、肢體式的 lazzi 笑料);哈勒昆菱格戲服與面具的視覺語彙,至今仍是西方流行文化中一眼可辨的符號,在芭蕾、歌劇(《丑角》I Pagliacci)乃至萬聖節裝扮中都能見到其身影。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 已不再以原始形式作為持續存在的活態即興傳統演出,但在全球各地的戲劇學校與表演藝術學院中,仍持續被研究與復興(面具表演與 lazzi 訓練,至今仍是肢體劇場與丑角表演教學的必修內容),義大利與法國各地也仍有專門劇團與藝術節不定期搬演;其角色原型與面具,也經常在歌劇、芭蕾與街頭劇場中被引用或直接重現。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、britannica.com

8) 看片方法 — 連戲剪輯與 180 度假想線(正反打)Continuity Editing & the 180-Degree Rule(第十七課)

第二課「剪輯與蒙太奇」談的是並置的鏡頭如何透過節奏與聯想製造意義。今天的分析視角要縮小到剪輯的另一項更基礎的任務:剪接如何維持——或刻意打破——觀眾對物理空間的連貫認知,這和一次剪接「製造了什麼意義」是完全不同的問題。

方法說明: 「180 度假想線」規則指出,一旦一場戲確立了一條貫穿主體之間的想像軸線(「動作軸線」)——最常見的情況是兩人面對面對話——攝影機在後續每一顆鏡頭中都應該維持在這條軸線的同一側。這樣做能確保「銀幕方向」的一致性(一個往左看的角色,在每次剪接後仍然應該持續往左看),觀眾腦中「誰在哪裡、面向哪個方向」的空間地圖就不必一再重建。建立在這條規則之上最常用的技巧,就是「正反打」(shot/reverse shot):在角色 A 的鏡頭(通常偏向軸線的一側,經常透過角色 B 的過肩鏡頭取景)與角色 B 對應的鏡頭之間來回切換,讓一場對話戲可以來回剪接,卻始終不會失去空間上的清晰感。與此密切相關的另一項工具是「視線匹配」(eyeline match)——從角色望向某個方向的鏡頭,剪接到他所望向的對象(人或物)的鏡頭,而且取景角度必須與角色視線所指的方向吻合,這正是讓正反打對話戲「感覺像是兩人真的在對望」、而非「兩台各自獨立的攝影機」的關鍵所在。因為整套系統的運作原理,就是在正確遵守規則時讓觀眾完全「感覺不到」它的存在,因此分析這項技巧最有效的方式,就是問:這條軸線是否被遵守?如果一部片打破了 180 度規則,或刻意扭曲視線匹配,這究竟是失誤,還是刻意讓觀眾在空間感或心理上產生失衡的選擇?

實例示範——《沉默的羔羊》(The Silence of the Lambs,1991,導演強納森・德米 Jonathan Demme,攝影指導塔克・藤本 Tak Fujimoto)中克萊麗絲與漢尼拔隔著玻璃審訊的場景: 在克萊麗絲・史達琳與漢尼拔・萊克特隔著牢房玻璃的早期對話中,德米安排的反打鏡頭,並未採用一般略帶偏移角度的過肩鏡頭,而是讓兩位演員幾乎直視鏡頭本身演出——這已經是正反打慣例所能容許的、最接近「與攝影機本身視線匹配」的極限做法。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)規則在結構上被遵守,卻被推到最極端的邊界——德米完整保留了 180 度軸線與基本的正反打節奏(觀眾始終不會迷失誰在哪裡),但透過讓每個角色的視線幾乎與鏡頭完全對齊,這項技巧本身反而成了不適感的來源,而非原本應該「隱形」的存在;觀眾感覺自己「正在被凝視」,而不只是「被展示」一段對話。(2)這個手法的使用並不對稱,對兩位角色各自帶有不同的意義——萊克特近乎直視鏡頭的凝視,讀來帶有掠食性與侵犯感,像是對克萊麗絲(以及觀眾)進行一場心理層面的侵犯,恰恰呼應了他的角色本質;克萊麗絲自己近乎直視鏡頭的目光,則讀來是一種脆弱的曝露感,因為在這場戲中她較年輕、權力位階較低,實質上被萊克特與觀眾一同「看穿」。(3)這正好示範了今天這套方法的核心教誨——正因為 180 度過肩正反打是一種被觀眾內化到近乎「理所當然」的慣例,一旦手法偏離慣常角度,觀眾會在能夠明確說出原因之前,就先感受到一股不安;而能夠辨識出這項技巧本身,正是讓你有能力解釋「一般觀眾只能隱約感受到、卻說不出所以然」的那種感覺的關鍵。
