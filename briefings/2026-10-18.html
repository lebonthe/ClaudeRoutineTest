3) Spanish Lesson — Day 94: Tardar / Durar / Llevar ("How Long It Takes")

Tardar en + infinitive says how long a person or vehicle takes to do something (Tardo una hora en llegar); durar says how long an event lasts (La película dura dos horas); llevar + time + gerund says how long you have already been doing something (Llevo tres meses estudiando español). Do not use tardar for an event and do not drop en.

  • ¿Cuánto tardas en llegar? — How long does it take you to arrive?
  • La reunión dura una hora. — The meeting lasts an hour.
  • Llevo dos años trabajando aquí. — I have been working here for two years.
  • Llevo una semana sin dormir bien. — I have not slept well for a week.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 30: 說劍 (Shuō Jiàn) — "Discoursing on Swords"

The eighth of the Miscellaneous Chapters (雜篇, Ch. 30). King Wen of Zhao (趙文王) loves swordsmanship so much that more than three thousand swordsmen live as guests in his court and fight each other in front of him, with over a hundred dead or wounded each year. The state declines, and the crown prince, afraid of what neighboring states might do, offers a thousand pieces of gold to anyone who can stop the king. Zhuangzi accepts, refuses the gold, and goes to the palace dressed as a swordsman would expect.

Before the king, Zhuangzi says his sword can "defeat ten thousand men in ten steps", then reveals he has three swords. The Son of Heaven's sword has the whole world as its blade, mountains and seas as its edge and spine, and when raised, it leaves nothing in front of it; "once used, it corrects the feudal lords and brings the world to submit." The feudal lord's sword is made of wise, honest, loyal and brave men and governs a single state. The commoner's sword is the sword of unkempt fighters with glaring eyes, who cut necks and lungs in front of the king "like fighting cocks": "once their lives are cut off, they are of no use to the state." Zhuangzi tells the king that the sword he loves is the commoner's sword, "and I secretly feel ashamed for Your Majesty."

The king, shamed, is led away for a meal and walks around the hall three times without eating. Zhuangzi tells him to calm himself, for he has shown him swords. The king then stays inside his palace for three months, and the swordsmen kill themselves in their quarters, according to the text. It is the one chapter in the book where Zhuangzi acts as a political persuader, in the style of the Warring States strategists, which is why many scholars doubt it was written by Zhuangzi himself.

Takeaway: To change what someone loves, speak in the language of the thing they love and lift it to a higher level; the strongest sword is a ruler who governs well, not one who wins duels. 5) Country / Region Spotlight — Luxembourg

  • Location & territory size: A landlocked country in western Europe, bordered by Belgium (west and north), Germany (east) and France (south). It covers about 2,586 km² (general knowledge): roughly 1/14 of Taiwan (36,200 km²), a little larger than Taichung City (about 2,215 km²), and smaller than the US state of Rhode Island. The longest distance north to south is only about 82 km. It is the only remaining sovereign grand duchy in the world.
  • Population: About 691,000 on January 1, 2026 (Wikipedia's demographics page, citing national statistics), about 3% of Taiwan's 23 million. Around 46-47% of residents are foreign nationals, among the highest shares in Europe; Portuguese are the largest group. The capital, Luxembourg City, has roughly 130,000 residents, and about 200,000 cross-border workers commute in daily from France, Belgium and Germany (general knowledge, approximate).
  • Religion: Historically Roman Catholic, and Catholics are still the largest group, but with a steady decline in practice and a growing non-religious population. There is no state church; the state has funded and recognized several religious communities under agreements signed in 2015 (general knowledge).
  • Economy: One of the highest GDP per person figures in the world (well above US$100,000 nominal, approximate). It is a major financial center, the largest investment-fund center in Europe, with banking, insurance and fund administration central to the economy. Steel (ArcelorMittal's origin) built the 19th-20th century economy. It hosts EU bodies such as the Court of Justice of the EU, the European Investment Bank and the Court of Auditors, and the European headquarters of several tech firms. Since March 1, 2020, public transport nationwide has been free of charge, a first for a country.
  • History: The name comes from the castle Lucilinburhuc, acquired by Count Siegfried in 963. It became a county, then a duchy in 1354, and passed under Burgundian, Spanish, French and Austrian rule. The Congress of Vienna (1815) made it a grand duchy in personal union with the Dutch king; the 1839 Treaty of London gave its western half to Belgium, and the 1867 Treaty of London confirmed independence and neutrality. The personal union with the Netherlands ended in 1890. Germany occupied it in both World Wars (1914-18, 1940-44). Afterward it abandoned neutrality and became a founding member of Benelux, NATO (1949), the European Coal and Steel Community and the EEC (1957); the Schengen Agreement was signed in the Luxembourg village of Schengen in 1985.
  • Current leader: Head of state: Grand Duke Guillaume (since October 3, 2025, when his father Henri abdicated). Prime minister: Luc Frieden (Christian Social People's Party, CSV), heading a CSV-Democratic Party coalition since November 2023. Today's search did not confirm either office-holder, so these come from general knowledge and should be checked against a current source.
  • Language: Three official languages: Luxembourgish (a Moselle Franconian Germanic language, the national language), French (the language of law and administration) and German (press and schooling). English and Portuguese are widely used in daily life.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Change My Mind"

  • Name: "Change My Mind" (also "Steven Crowder Change My Mind").
  • Origin / history: It comes from a photo of conservative commentator Steven Crowder sitting at a small folding table with a coffee mug and a sign, taken during one of his "Change My Mind" campus-debate videos in early 2018 (the photo shows the sign "Male Privilege Is a Myth"; the Texas Christian University location and February 2018 date are from memory and approximate). The video series invites passersby to argue with him over a hot-button claim.
  • How it became popular: Users noticed the image worked as a template. Once the original sign was blanked out, anyone could write a deliberately silly, or absurdly self-assured, claim on it. It spread on Twitter, Reddit and Tumblr through 2018, and became a standard format for jokes of the type "unpopular opinion, prove me wrong."
  • Meaning & usage: The image presents a statement with calm confidence and a dare to refute it. It is used for trivial opinions (food, fandom, tech preferences), for jokes where the claim is obviously false, and for quoting absurd logic. It works because the posed confidence of the man in the photo does the joke on its own, and the caption does only the setup.
  • Variations / evolution: Variants include crossovers (the same table with a cartoon or game character), versions where the sign text is changed to a pun, and ironic uses where nobody wants to change the claim. It also fed the larger "prove me wrong / hot take" template culture. The photo is a favorite of template sites for its easily replaceable sign area.
  • Current status: A well-established, somewhat dated classic. It still appears in discussion threads and as a deliberately retro joke, but it is no longer a trend. The image is also tied to its subject's politics, so many users prefer neutral templates. (Details are from general knowledge and labeled approximate.)

7) Film / Performing-Arts Spotlight — Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (臥虎藏龍, 2000, dir. Ang Lee)

  • Name & key figures: Wò Hǔ Cáng Lóng, directed by Taiwan-born Ang Lee (李安). Starring Chow Yun-fat (Li Mu Bai), Michelle Yeoh (Yu Shu Lien), Zhang Ziyi (Jen Yu) and Chang Chen (Lo, "Dark Cloud"). Action choreography by Yuen Woo-ping; cinematography by Peter Pau; music by Tan Dun with cello solos by Yo-Yo Ma. The screenplay is by James Schamus, Wang Hui-ling and Tsai Kuo-jung, adapted from the 1940s wuxia novel by Wang Dulu (the fourth book of his Crane-Iron Pentalogy).
  • Era / background: Made as a Taiwan-Hong Kong-China-US co-production (Columbia Pictures Asia, Sony Pictures Classics, China Film Co-Production and others) and released in 2000. Ang Lee, then known for Eat Drink Man Woman, Sense and Sensibility and The Ice Storm, said he wanted to make the martial-arts films he dreamed about as a child in Taiwan. Mandarin dialogue; shot largely in China, with scenes in Xinjiang, Anhui and Beijing.
  • Plot overview (light spoilers): In Qing-dynasty China, the retired warrior Li Mu Bai gives his legendary sword, Green Destiny, to Yu Shu Lien, who keeps it in Beijing for the powerful Sir Te. The sword is stolen by a masked thief, who turns out to be Jen, the governor's daughter, who is secretly trained in martial arts by the villain Jade Fox and is trapped between an arranged marriage and her desire for freedom. Jen also has a past with the desert outlaw Lo. Li and Yu, whose feelings for each other have never been spoken, pursue her, and Li hopes to take her as his student. The film ends with a tragedy and with Jen's leap from Wudang mountain, an open ending that Ang Lee has left to interpretation.
  • Artistic style & techniques: It treats wuxia as a drama of repressed feeling, with the duels as an extension of emotions that the characters cannot say aloud. The fights, designed by Yuen Woo-ping, use "wire-fu" (actors on wires) to create flight, most famously the bamboo-forest duel and the rooftop chase. Peter Pau's cinematography moves from the cold, color-controlled interiors of Beijing to the warm desert flashback and the green mountains, and Tan Dun's score mixes Western orchestra, cello and Chinese percussion.
  • Cultural / historical significance: It became the highest-grossing foreign-language film in US history at the time (about US$128 million in the US; roughly US$200 million worldwide, per general knowledge), and it won four Academy Awards at the 73rd ceremony: Best Foreign Language Film, Best Cinematography, Best Art Direction and Best Original Score, with ten nominations in total. It was the first Mandarin-language film to be taken as a mainstream hit in the West, and a source of pride in Taiwan, Hong Kong and China, though some critics in China felt it was made for Western audiences.
  • Influence & legacy: It triggered a wave of prestige wuxia in the 2000s (Hero, House of Flying Daggers, The Banquet) and brought wire work into Hollywood action (the fights of The Matrix, by the same choreographer, had come out a year earlier). A sequel, Sword of Destiny (2016), was released on Netflix.
  • Current status / where to experience it: Widely available on home video and on major streaming services depending on region, and often revived in theaters for anniversaries. A restored 4K release has circulated in recent years. (Figures are from general knowledge and approximate; availability varies by region.)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 80: Reading an Action Sequence (Spatial Clarity, Stakes, Escalation)

The method: An action scene is a story told with bodies and space, and the usual mistake is to judge it by how loud or fast it is. To analyze it, ask three questions in order. (1) Geography: can you tell where everyone is, where the goal is, and which direction each side is moving? Strong action films establish the space first (an establishing shot, a landmark, a consistent screen direction) and then keep orienting you. (2) Stakes and objective: what does each character want in this exact moment, and what will they lose? Without a clear objective, a fight is only motion. (3) Escalation and change: does the scene have a structure (setup, complication, reversal, climax) and does someone have a different position at the end? Then ask how the filmmakers help your eye: framing that keeps the focus in the same part of the screen, shot length long enough to read an action, and cuts that follow cause and effect.

How to analyze it: Watch the scene once for feeling. Then watch with the sound off and sketch a map: positions, direction of travel, a landmark. Count how often you get lost. Write the objective and obstacle of each side in one line, and mark each reversal (an obstacle appears, a plan fails, a tool is lost). Finally, compare average shot length to how well you could follow, because a faster cut does not automatically mean more excitement.

Worked example — Mad Max: Fury Road (2015, dir. George Miller, cinematography John Seale, editing Margaret Sixel), the first chase after Furiosa leaves the Citadel and drives the War Rig into the wasteland: (a) Geography: the scene is built around a simple spatial idea, one long road with a convoy chasing a single truck, so "ahead" and "behind" never change. Miller and his team are known to have kept the key action near the center of the frame from shot to shot, so that, despite the very fast cutting, the eye does not need to search for the subject (this is the director's own stated approach as widely reported; treat it as general knowledge). (b) Stakes and objective: Furiosa wants to reach the Green Place with the five wives; Immortan Joe wants them back; Max, chained to a car, wants to survive and then is dragged into the escape. Each objective is stated in a visual action, such as the opened vault or the wives' escape, before any dialogue explains it. (c) Escalation: the chase moves from pursuit to a sandstorm, a failed gambit, vehicle losses and a change in alliances, so the scene is a chain of reversals rather than one long explosion. Contrast this with a chase shot in dense handheld coverage with unclear screen direction, where we feel chaos but cannot say who is winning; that is a different effect and can be intentional, but it does not give the same sense of authored geography. Practice: pick any chase or duel and ask whether you could draw its map afterward.

4) 莊子 — 第 30 篇:說劍(Shuō Jiàn)—「論劍」

《雜篇》第八篇(雜篇第 30 章)。趙文王酷愛劍術,宮中養了三千多名劍士,日夜在王前比劍,每年死傷上百人,國勢因此衰弱。太子擔心鄰國趁機而動,以千金懸賞能勸止國王的人。莊子接受了委託,拒收千金,穿上劍士預期的裝束入宮。

在國王面前,莊子說他的劍「十步殺一人,千里不留行」,接著說他其實有三把劍。天子之劍以整個天下為鋒刃,山海為鍔與脊,一用之下,「匡諸侯,天下服矣」。諸侯之劍以智勇、清廉、賢良、忠聖、豪傑之士為材,治理一國。庶人之劍是蓬頭突鬢、瞋目語難的鬥士之劍,在王前相擊,「如鬥雞」,「一旦命已絕矣,無所用於國事」。莊子對國王說:您喜歡的是庶人之劍,「臣竊為大王薄之」。

國王感到羞愧,被請去用膳卻繞著廳堂走了三圈而不進食。莊子請他安心,說自己已經把劍講給他聽了。據文中所述,國王此後三個月沒有走出宮門,劍士們都在自己的住所自盡。這是全書中少數由莊子擔任政治說客的篇章,筆法近於戰國縱橫家,因此許多學者懷疑不是莊子本人所作。

今日啟發:想改變一個人所愛的事物,就用他所愛的語言說話,並把它提升到更高的層次;最強的劍是治理得好的國君,而不是決鬥得勝的人。(篇章內容為一般知識並已標明;引文為意譯,請對照原典確認。)

  • 人口:2026 年 1 月 1 日約 691,000 人(維基百科人口統計頁,引用國家統計),約為台灣 2,300 萬人的 3%。居民中約 46-47% 為外國籍,是歐洲比例最高者之一,以葡萄牙人最多。首都盧森堡市約有 13 萬居民;另有約 20 萬名跨境通勤者每天從法國、比利時與德國入境工作(一般知識,為約略數字)。
  • 宗教:歷史上以羅馬天主教為主,至今天主教徒仍是最大宗,但實際參與持續下降,無宗教者增加。沒有國教;國家依 2015 年簽署的協議資助並承認數個宗教團體(一般知識)。
  • 經濟:人均 GDP 位居世界最高之列(名目遠高於 10 萬美元,約略值)。它是主要金融中心,也是歐洲最大的投資基金中心,銀行、保險與基金管理是經濟核心。鋼鐵(安賽樂米塔爾的起源)奠定了 19 至 20 世紀的經濟基礎。境內設有歐盟法院、歐洲投資銀行、歐洲審計院等歐盟機構,以及數家科技公司的歐洲總部。自 2020 年 3 月 1 日起,全國大眾運輸免費,是世界首例。
  • 歷史:國名源自西格弗里德伯爵於 963 年取得的盧西林堡(Lucilinburhuc)。先是伯國,1354 年升為公國,曾先後受勃艮第、西班牙、法國與奧地利統治。1815 年維也納會議使它成為與荷蘭國王共主的大公國;1839 年倫敦條約把西半部劃給比利時;1867 年倫敦條約確認其獨立與中立。與荷蘭的共主關係於 1890 年結束。兩次世界大戰(1914-18、1940-44)皆遭德國佔領。戰後放棄中立,成為比荷盧聯盟、北約(1949)、歐洲煤鋼共同體與歐洲經濟共同體(1957)的創始成員;1985 年《申根協定》就在盧森堡的申根村簽署。
  • 現任領導人:國家元首:紀堯姆大公(2025 年 10 月 3 日起,其父亨利退位)。總理:呂克·弗里登(Luc Frieden,基督教社會人民黨 CSV),自 2023 年 11 月起領導 CSV 與民主黨的聯合政府。今天的搜尋未能確認這兩位現任者,內容來自一般知識,請對照最新來源核實。
  • 語言:三種官方語言:盧森堡語(摩澤爾法蘭克語系的日耳曼語言,國家語言)、法語(法律與行政語言)與德語(報章與學校)。英語與葡萄牙語在日常生活中也廣泛使用。
  • 來源與備註:人口來自維基百科:盧森堡人口統計。其餘數字為一般知識並標明為約略值。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「Change My Mind」(說服我啊)

  • 名稱:"Change My Mind"(也稱 "Steven Crowder Change My Mind")。
  • 起源 / 歷史:源自保守派評論員 Steven Crowder 坐在小折疊桌前、桌上放著咖啡杯與標語牌的照片,拍攝於 2018 年初他的「Change My Mind」校園辯論影片(照片中標語為 "Male Privilege Is a Myth";德州基督教大學這個地點與 2018 年 2 月的時間為憑記憶,屬約略)。該系列影片邀請路人針對一個爭議性主張與他辯論。
  • 如何爆紅:網友發現這張圖很適合當模板。把原本的標語塗掉後,任何人都可以在上面寫一句刻意搞笑、或荒謬地自信的主張。它在 2018 年透過 Twitter、Reddit 與 Tumblr 擴散,成為「不受歡迎的意見,請證明我錯了」這類玩笑的標準格式。
  • 意思與使用情境:這張圖用沉著的自信提出一個陳述,並挑戰別人推翻它。常用於無關緊要的意見(食物、粉絲圈、科技偏好)、明顯錯誤的玩笑,以及引用荒謬邏輯。它之所以有效,是因為照片中男子擺出的自信姿態本身就製造了笑點,說明文字只負責鋪陳。
  • 變化 / 演變:變體包括跨界版(同一張桌子換成卡通或遊戲角色)、把標語改成雙關語的版本,以及沒有人想改變該主張的反諷用法。它也助長了更大範圍的「來證明我錯」/「爭議言論」模板文化。這張照片因標語區域容易替換,是模板網站的常客。
  • 目前狀態:一個已確立、稍微過時的經典。它仍出現在討論串中,也被當作刻意復古的玩笑,但已不是流行趨勢。這張圖也與其主角的政治立場連結,因此許多使用者偏好中立的模板。(細節為一般知識,標明為約略。)

7) 電影 / 表演藝術介紹 — 《臥虎藏龍》(Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon,2000,導演李安)

  • 名稱與主要人物:《臥虎藏龍》,導演為台灣出生的李安。主演周潤發(李慕白)、楊紫瓊(俞秀蓮)、章子怡(玉嬌龍)與張震(羅小虎)。武術指導袁和平;攝影鮑德熹;配樂譚盾,大提琴獨奏為馬友友。編劇為詹姆斯·夏慕斯、王蕙玲與蔡國榮,改編自王度廬 1940 年代的武俠小說(「鶴鐵五部曲」的第四部)。
  • 時代背景:為台灣、香港、中國與美國的合拍片(哥倫比亞影業亞洲公司、新力經典影業、中影合拍等),於 2000 年上映。李安當時以《飲食男女》、《理性與感性》、《冰風暴》聞名,他說想拍出自己兒時在台灣夢想的武俠電影。國語對白;主要在中國拍攝,場景含新疆、安徽與北京。
  • 劇情概述(輕微劇透):清朝,退隱的武者李慕白把傳奇寶劍「青冥劍」交給俞秀蓮,由她帶到北京交給權貴貝勒爺保管。寶劍被一名蒙面賊偷走,賊其實是將軍之女玉嬌龍,她被反派碧眼狐狸暗中傳授武藝,陷在包辦婚姻與對自由的渴望之間。玉嬌龍與沙漠盜匪羅小虎也有一段過去。李慕白與俞秀蓮彼此有情卻從未說破,兩人追查她,李慕白並希望收她為徒。電影以悲劇和玉嬌龍從武當山一躍作結,是李安留給觀眾自行詮釋的開放式結局。
  • 藝術風格與技法:它把武俠當成壓抑情感的戲劇,打鬥是角色說不出口的情緒的延伸。袁和平設計的打鬥採用「威亞功夫」(演員吊鋼絲)營造飛行感,最有名的是竹林對決與屋頂追逐。鮑德熹的攝影從北京冷調、色彩受控的室內,轉到暖色的沙漠回憶與綠意山林,譚盾的配樂則混合西方管弦樂、大提琴與中國打擊樂。
  • 文化與歷史意義:它成為當時美國史上票房最高的外語片(美國約 1.28 億美元,全球約 2 億美元,一般知識),並在第 73 屆奧斯卡獲得四項大獎:最佳外語片、最佳攝影、最佳藝術指導與最佳原創配樂,共獲十項提名。它是第一部被西方視為主流賣座片的華語電影,也是台灣、香港與中國的驕傲,不過中國部分影評人認為它是為西方觀眾而拍。
  • 影響與傳承:它引發了 2000 年代的藝術性武俠熱潮(《英雄》、《十面埋伏》、《夜宴》),並把威亞技法帶進好萊塢動作片(同一位武術指導負責的《駭客任務》早一年上映)。續集《臥虎藏龍:青冥寶劍》(2016)在 Netflix 上映。
  • 現況 / 哪裡可以欣賞:在家庭影音與依地區而異的主要串流平台上都能看到,也常在週年時於戲院重映。近年有修復版 4K 發行。(數字為一般知識的約略值;可看的平台依地區而異。)

8) 看片方法 — 第 80 課:解讀動作場面(空間清晰度、賭注、升級)

方法:動作戲是用身體與空間說的故事,常見的錯誤是只用聲量與速度來評斷。分析時依序問三個問題。(1) 地理:你能不能分辨每個人在哪裡、目標在哪裡、各方往哪個方向移動?優秀的動作片會先建立空間(定場鏡頭、地標、一致的銀幕方向),再持續替觀眾定位。(2) 賭注與目標:此刻每個角色想要什麼、會失去什麼?沒有清楚目標,打鬥只是動作。(3) 升級與改變:這場戲有沒有結構(鋪陳、複雜化、逆轉、高潮),結尾時有人的處境改變了嗎?再看創作者如何幫助你的視線:讓焦點留在畫面同一區域的構圖、長度足夠看清動作的鏡頭,以及依因果關係的剪接。

如何分析:先憑感覺看一遍。再關掉聲音看一遍,畫下地圖:位置、行進方向、一個地標。數一數自己迷路幾次。用一句話寫下每一方的目標與阻礙,並標出每一次逆轉(出現阻礙、計畫失敗、失去工具)。最後比較平均鏡頭長度與你跟得上的程度,因為剪得更快不代表更刺激。

實例 — 《瘋狂麥斯:憤怒道》(2015,導演喬治·米勒,攝影約翰·席爾,剪輯瑪格麗特·西克索)中,芙莉歐莎離開城堡、駕駛戰車開進荒野後的第一場追逐:(a) 地理:這場戲圍繞一個簡單的空間概念:一條長路、一支車隊追著一輛卡車,所以「前」與「後」從不改變。據廣泛報導,米勒團隊讓關鍵動作在鏡頭之間保持在畫面中央附近,因此即使剪接極快,眼睛也不需要尋找主體(這是導演自述的做法,視為一般知識)。(b) 賭注與目標:芙莉歐莎想帶五位妻子抵達綠洲;不死老喬想把她們搶回來;被鎖在車上的麥斯想活下去,隨後被捲入逃亡。每個目標都先用視覺動作交代(打開的保險庫、妻子們的逃離),再由對白解釋。(c) 升級:追逐從追擊升級為沙塵暴、失敗的策略、車輛損失與結盟變化,所以整場戲是一連串逆轉,而不是一次長長的爆炸。對照用密集手持鏡頭、銀幕方向不明的追逐戲:我們感到混亂,卻說不出誰占上風;那是另一種效果,也可能是刻意的,但它不會給人同樣的「設計過的空間」感。練習:挑任何一場追逐或決鬥,問自己看完後能不能畫出它的地圖。
