3) Spanish Lesson — Day 8 (builds on Day 7)

Days 1-7 covered greetings/self-intro, ¿Cómo estás?, farewells/courtesy, ¿De dónde eres? (ser + origin), numbers 0-10, and the days of the week. Today's new topic continues the calendar theme: the twelve months of the year, and asking/telling what month it is.

  • enero, febrero, marzo, abril, mayo, junio, julio, agosto, septiembre, octubre, noviembre, diciembre (Jan-Dec; like the days of the week, month names are never capitalized in Spanish)
  • ¿En qué mes estamos? — What month are we in?
  • Estamos en julio. — We're in July. (uses estar, not ser — the current month is a temporary state, not a fixed identity)

Grammar note: the first few months come from Roman gods (enero=Janus, marzo=Mars, mayo=Maia, junio=Juno), but septiembre-diciembre are fossils of the old Roman calendar, literally meaning "7th-10th month" in Latin (septem, octo, novem, decem) from when the year began in March — the exact same quirk behind English September-December.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 8: 比 (Bǐ) — "Holding Together" / Union (Water over Earth). Water spreads over and soaks into the earth, uniting intimately with it without gaps — the image of people drawing close together in mutual support and alliance. The classical judgment: "Holding Together brings good fortune. Inquire of the oracle once again whether you possess sublimity, constancy, and perseverance; then there is no blame. Those who are uncertain gradually join; whoever comes too late meets with misfortune." The hexagram follows directly from 師 (The Army, hexagram 7): the Sequence commentary explains that once a multitude has been mobilized, it must have something to unite around and adhere to — hence Army is followed by Holding Together. It counsels choosing carefully who or what to unite with, examining one's own sincerity and constancy before joining a cause, and acting promptly, since holding back too long forfeits the benefit of union altogether.

5) Country/Region Spotlight: Mongolia

  • Location & size: A landlocked country in East/Central Asia, sandwiched between Russia to the north and China to the south; about 1,564,116 km² — the world's 18th-largest country by area, roughly 43 times the size of Taiwan (~36,197 km²), about 4 times the size of Japan, and only slightly smaller than the US state of Alaska.
  • Population: About 3.5 million — one of the lowest population densities on Earth (roughly 2 people per km²), with nearly half concentrated in the capital, Ulaanbaatar; the rest is spread across vast steppe, desert (the Gobi), and mountain terrain still home to a significant nomadic-herding population.
  • Religion: Tibetan (Vajrayana) Buddhism is the majority faith, deeply tied to national identity; a large non-religious/atheist share remains from the Soviet-era communist period; indigenous Mongolian shamanism (Tengrism) persists and has seen a revival since 1990, alongside a small Muslim minority among ethnic Kazakhs in the far west.
  • Economy: Resource-driven, centered on copper, coal, and gold (including the massive Oyu Tolgoi copper-gold mine) with heavy trade dependence on neighboring China; traditional nomadic livestock herding (source of the world-famous Mongolian cashmere) remains economically and culturally significant; growth is volatile, closely tracking global commodity cycles since the post-Soviet shift to a market economy in the early 1990s.
  • History: Birthplace of the 13th-century Mongol Empire under Genghis Khan, history's largest contiguous land empire; later came under Qing Chinese rule; declared independence in 1921 with Soviet backing, becoming the Mongolian People's Republic in 1924 — the world's second communist state after the USSR; a peaceful democratic revolution in 1990 ended one-party communist rule and introduced multi-party democracy and a market economy.
  • Current leadership: President Ukhnaagiin Khürelsükh has served since 2021. The premiership has been turbulent: Nyam-Osoryn Uchral became prime minister on Mar 30, 2026, Mongolia's third PM in about a year, after predecessor Gombojavyn Zandanshatar resigned amid political infighting.
  • Language: Khalkha Mongolian is the official language, historically written in the traditional vertical Mongolian script but standardized in Cyrillic since the Soviet era; there are ongoing government efforts to revive wider use of the traditional script.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight: Gangnam Style (South Korea)

  • Name/origin: "Gangnam Style" (강남스타일) is a 2012 K-pop song and music video by South Korean rapper PSY, satirizing the flashy, status-obsessed lifestyle associated with Seoul's wealthy Gangnam district, paired with PSY's exaggerated "invisible horse" dance.
  • How it became popular: The music video spread rapidly on YouTube and Twitter in mid-to-late 2012, picked up by Western celebrities and media, and became the first video ever to reach 1 billion YouTube views (December 2012) — its popularity was so extreme that in December 2014 it literally broke YouTube's 32-bit view counter, forcing Google to rewrite the counter to support 64-bit numbers.
  • Meaning/usage: Used broadly as shorthand for a viral global sensation and for the "invisible horse-riding" dance move itself; it became a byword for South Korea's growing cultural export power and is widely credited as K-pop's first true global crossover breakthrough, years before groups like BTS and Blackpink.
  • Variations: Spawned countless parodies, flash-mob recreations, and cover/tribute videos worldwide, plus follow-up PSY singles ("Gentleman," "Daddy") that tried to replicate the formula; the dance move itself became a stand-alone party/wedding staple independent of the song.
  • Current status: As of 2026 it has long since been surpassed in raw view count by many other videos, but it remains a defining 2010s internet-culture touchstone and is still regularly referenced in retrospectives on K-pop's global rise and on how a single video can go "viral" at unprecedented scale.

第八卦:比卦(Bǐ)——「親比、團結」(上坎下坤,水在地上)。水滲透並覆蓋大地,與大地緊密相合、毫無間隙——象徵眾人彼此靠攏、互相扶持、結為聯盟。卦辭:「比,吉。原筮,元永貞,無咎。不寧方來,後夫凶。」意指:與人團結親比能帶來吉祥,但應再三審視自己是否真正具備崇高、恆久與正直的德性,如此才能無咎;猶豫不決者會逐漸前來歸附,而遲遲不肯加入的人則將招致凶險。此卦緊接在師卦(軍隊,第七卦)之後,〈序卦傳〉說明:眾人被動員起來之後,必然需要有所依附、團結一致,因此師卦之後接著是比卦。此卦也提醒人們:選擇要與誰、與什麼理念結盟時應謹慎審視,加入之前先自問是否真誠、是否能夠恆久堅持,並且應當及時行動——猶豫太久,反而會錯失團結所能帶來的益處。

5) 國家/地區介紹:蒙古

  • 位置與面積: 位於東亞/中亞的內陸國家,北鄰俄羅斯、南接中國;面積約 1,564,116 平方公里,是全球面積第 18 大的國家——約為台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)的 43 倍,約為日本的 4 倍,略小於美國阿拉斯加州。
  • 人口: 約 350 萬人,是全球人口密度最低的國家之一(平均每平方公里僅約 2 人);近半數人口集中於首都烏蘭巴托,其餘則散布在廣袤的草原、戈壁沙漠與山地地區,至今仍有相當比例的游牧畜牧人口。
  • 宗教: 藏傳佛教(金剛乘)為多數信仰,與蒙古民族認同深度相連;蘇聯共產統治時期留下大量無宗教信仰/無神論人口;蒙古本土的薩滿信仰(騰格里信仰,Tengrism)自 1990 年後也逐漸復興,西部的哈薩克族少數族群則多信仰伊斯蘭教。
  • 經濟: 以資源出口為主軸,銅、煤炭與黃金是主力產業(包括大型的奧尤陶勒蓋銅金礦),對鄰國中國的貿易依賴度極高;傳統游牧畜牧業(舉世聞名的蒙古喀什米爾羊絨的來源)在經濟與文化上仍佔重要地位;自 1990 年代初由蘇聯式計畫經濟轉型為市場經濟以來,經濟成長波動劇烈,與全球大宗商品景氣循環緊密連動。
  • 歷史: 13 世紀成吉思汗建立蒙古帝國的發源地,是人類歷史上疆域最大的陸上帝國;其後被納入清朝統治;1921 年在蘇聯支持下宣布獨立,1924 年成立蒙古人民共和國,是繼蘇聯之後全球第二個共產主義國家;1990 年一場和平的民主革命結束一黨專政,轉型為多黨民主與市場經濟體制。
  • 現任領導人: 總統呼日勒蘇赫(Ukhnaagiin Khürelsükh)自 2021 年在任至今。總理職位近期則相當動盪:前總理贊丹沙塔爾(Gombojavyn Zandanshatar)因政治內鬥辭職後,額尼赫巴特(Nyam-Osoryn Uchral)於 2026 年 3 月 30 日就任總理,是蒙古近一年來的第三位總理。

  • 名稱/起源:「江南 Style」(강남스타일)是南韓饒舌歌手 PSY 於 2012 年發行的歌曲與音樂錄影帶,諷刺首爾富裕的江南區居民奢華、講究身分地位的生活方式,搭配 PSY 誇張的「隱形騎馬舞」舞步。
  • 如何爆紅: 這支音樂錄影帶在 2012 年年中至年底於 YouTube 與 Twitter 上快速擴散,受到西方名人與媒體爭相報導,並成為史上第一支觀看次數突破 10 億次的 YouTube 影片(2012 年 12 月);其熱度之驚人,甚至在 2014 年 12 月直接「衝破」YouTube 原本以 32 位元儲存的觀看次數計數器上限,迫使 Google 重寫程式改用 64 位元計數。
  • 意義與使用情境: 廣泛用來泛指「一夕爆紅的全球現象」,也直接用來指稱那個「隱形騎馬舞」的舞步本身;這首歌被普遍視為南韓文化輸出力量崛起的象徵,也是 K-pop 史上第一次真正意義上的全球性突破,比 BTS、Blackpink 等團體的全球爆紅還要早上好幾年。
  • 變體: 衍生出全球數不清的模仿版本、快閃舞蹈重現與翻唱/致敬影片,PSY 本人也推出後續單曲(《紳士》Gentleman、《大叔》Daddy)試圖複製同樣的爆紅公式;騎馬舞本身也脫離歌曲,獨立成為派對、婚禮上常見的表演橋段。
  • 現況: 截至 2026 年,單就觀看次數而言早已被許多後起影片超越,但它依然是 2010 年代網路文化的重要代表作,至今在回顧 K-pop 全球崛起歷程、以及探討單一影片如何以前所未見的規模「爆紅」時,仍經常被提及。
