Topic: one verb, three Chinese meanings — esperar = to wait, to hope, to expect — plus estar deseando for "looking forward to".

🎯 Learning Goal: wait for someone or something (Te espero en la estación), wait until something happens (esperar a que + subjunctive), hope (espero que + subjunctive), say you didn't expect something (No me lo esperaba), and say you're looking forward to something (Estoy deseando…).

Vocabulary: esperar (to wait; to hope; to expect) · ¡Espera! / ¡Espérame! / ¡Espere! (Wait! / Wait for me! / Please wait — formal) · esperar a + person (wait for someone) · esperar a que + subjunctive (wait until…) · esperar que + subjunctive (hope that…) · Espero que sí / Espero que no (I hope so / I hope not) · No me lo esperaba (I didn't see that coming) · estar deseando + infinitive (to be dying to, to look forward to) · la espera / la sala de espera (the wait / waiting room) · estar esperando (un bebé) (to be expecting a baby).

Grammar Point: (1) Wait = esperar + object, with no "por". English says "wait for", so learners say ✗ espero por el autobús. Standard Spanish uses no preposition: Espero el autobús; with a person, add the personal a: Espero a Luis. (Some Latin American speech uses esperar por, but the version without it is safest.) (2) Wait until something happens = esperar a que + subjunctive, because the event hasn't happened yet: Esperamos a que pare la lluvia (We'll wait for the rain to stop). With your own action: esperar a + infinitive (Espera a ver la película). (3) Hope = esperar que + subjunctive when the subjects differ: Espero que tengas un buen viaje; same subject → infinitive: Espero llegar a tiempo. Compared with ojalá (Day 31), espero que is a normal, polite "I hope" — the standard e-mail ending Espero que estés bien. (4) Expect = esperar + noun / lo: No esperaba tanta gente (I didn't expect so many people), ¡No me lo esperaba!, Era de esperar (It was to be expected). (5) Looking forward to = estar deseando + infinitive (or tener muchas ganas de, Day 77). Espero el concierto sounds like "I'm waiting for the concert", not excitement.

Example Sentences:

  • Te estoy esperando en la salida 2. — I'm waiting for you at exit 2.
  • ¡Espérame! El metro va lento. — Wait for me! The metro is slow.
  • Espero llegar en diez minutos. — I hope to arrive in ten minutes.
  • Espero que no empiece a llover antes. — I hope it doesn't start raining before then.
  • Vamos a esperar a que se ponga verde el semáforo. — Let's wait for the light to turn green.
  • ¡Estoy deseando ver el concierto! — I'm really looking forward to the concert!

ENEnglish version

Chapter 27 — 善行無轍跡 (Shàn xíng wú zhé jì), "Good Walking Leaves No Tracks". Heshang Gong's chapter title: 巧用 ("skilful use").

Original text (Wang Bi received text):
善行無轍跡,善言無瑕讁,善數不用籌策,善閉無關楗而不可開,善結無繩約而不可解。是以聖人常善救人,故無棄人;常善救物,故無棄物。是謂襲明。故善人者,不善人之師;不善人者,善人之資。不貴其師,不愛其資,雖智大迷,是謂要妙。

Translation: Good walking leaves no wheel ruts or footprints; good speech has no flaws to blame; good counting needs no counting rods; a good door-closer uses no bolt or bar, yet it cannot be opened; a good knot uses no rope or cord, yet it cannot be untied. Therefore the sage is always good at saving people, so no one is abandoned; always good at saving things, so nothing is abandoned. This is called "following the light" (襲明). So the good person is the teacher of the not-good person; the not-good person is the material (資, resource) of the good person. Not to value one's teacher, not to care for one's material — however clever, this is a great confusion. This is called "the essential mystery" (要妙).

Structure: (1) five images of perfect skill that leaves no trace: walking, speaking, counting, closing, tying; (2) the sage applies this skill to people and things — nobody and nothing is thrown away; (3) the relationship between "good" and "not good" people: teacher and resource, both needed.

Key terms and traditional interpretations:

  • 轍跡 = wheel ruts and footprints. 籌策 = bamboo counting rods used for calculation. 關楗 = a door bar and bolt. 繩約 = ropes and cords. The point: the highest skill does not show its tools.
  • Wang Bi reads every line as "following what things are by nature, not constructing, not imposing" (順自然而行,不造不施). A good door holds not because of a lock but because it fits what it closes; a good "knot" holds because it follows the grain of things.
  • 襲明 is read in two main ways: 襲 = 因循 "to follow, to inherit" (following the natural light/clarity), or 襲 = "to cloak" (a light that is hidden, not displayed). The Mawangdui silk manuscripts have a different character at this point (曳), which supports a reading like "drawn-out, extended clarity" for some scholars. All readings agree that the sage's wisdom is not shown off.
  • 無棄人 — "abandons no one" — is one of the most humane lines in the Daodejing: the bad person is not discarded but becomes a 資, a resource or reason for the good person's work. Heshang Gong reads it as a ruler who uses every person according to their ability.
  • 要妙 = "the essential, subtle point" — the mutual dependence of teacher and learner, which the merely clever overlook.

Historical and philosophical background: the chapter follows Ch. 26 ("heaviness is the root of lightness") and continues the image of the calm, rooted sage. Its craft images (counting rods, door bars) come from everyday Warring States life. The idea of skill without visible effort connects with Zhuangzi's Cook Ding (庖丁解牛), introduced earlier in this series, whose knife never touches bone. Han Feizi's commentary chapters (解老, 喻老) show that Legalists read such lines as advice on hidden statecraft; the Confucian echo is Confucius's "in teaching there should be no distinction of classes" (有教無類), though the Daodejing grounds it in nature, not in ritual.

Philosophical implications: (1) mastery is measured by how little trace it leaves; (2) a system that "throws away" people has failed, not the people; (3) "good" and "not good" are roles in a relationship, not fixed identities — each needs the other.

Modern reflection: good software design follows this chapter: the best interface needs no manual (no "counting rods"), and the best security comes from correct design, not from more locks added later. In teams, a good senior engineer is measured by how the struggling junior grows, not by how often the junior is replaced. And when you meet someone you think is "not good", the chapter asks: what can I learn from this, and what can I teach?

白話翻譯:善於行走的,不留下車轍和腳印;善於說話的,沒有可以挑剔的毛病;善於計算的,不用籌碼;善於關門的,不用門閂,卻打不開;善於打結的,不用繩索,卻解不開。因此聖人總是善於救助人,所以沒有被拋棄的人;總是善於救助物,所以沒有被拋棄的物。這叫做「襲明」。所以善人是不善之人的老師;不善之人是善人的借鏡與資源(資)。不尊重自己的老師,不珍惜自己的資源,即使再聰明也是大糊塗。這叫做「要妙」(精要而微妙的道理)。

結構:(1)五個「完美技藝卻不留痕跡」的意象:行、言、數、閉、結;(2)聖人把這種技藝用在人與物上——沒有人、沒有物被丟棄;(3)「善人」與「不善人」的關係:老師與資源,彼此都需要。

關鍵詞與傳統詮釋:

  • 轍跡=車輪的痕跡和腳印。籌策=古代用來計算的竹籌。關楗=門閂、門栓。繩約=繩索。重點是:最高的技藝不露出工具。
  • 王弼把每一句都讀成「順自然而行,不造不施」。好的門關得牢,不是靠鎖,而是因為合乎它所關閉的東西;好的「結」牢固,是因為順著事物的紋理。
  • 襲明主要有兩種讀法:襲=因循、承襲(順著自然的光明),或襲=覆蓋、掩藏(藏而不露的光明)。馬王堆帛書此處用了不同的字(曳),有些學者據此讀為「延展、綿長的明」。各種讀法都同意:聖人的智慧不炫耀。
  • 無棄人——「不拋棄任何人」——是道德經裡最有人情味的句子之一:不善的人不是被丟掉,而是成為善人的「資」,也就是資源或工作的理由。河上公把它讀成君主依各人能力任用每個人。
  • 要妙=「精要、微妙之處」——老師與學習者互相依存,只會耍聰明的人看不到這一點。

歷史與哲學背景:本章接在第 26 章「重為輕根」之後,延續沉穩、有根的聖人形象。它的工藝意象(籌策、門閂)來自戰國時代的日常生活。「技藝高到看不出用力」的想法,和本系列先前介紹過的莊子〈庖丁解牛〉相通——庖丁的刀從不碰到骨頭。《韓非子》的〈解老〉〈喻老〉顯示,法家把這類句子讀成隱藏的治術;儒家的呼應是孔子的「有教無類」,不過道德經是以自然為根據,而不是以禮為根據。

哲學意涵:(1)衡量高明與否,看的是它留下多少痕跡——越少越好;(2)一個會「丟棄」人的制度,失敗的是制度,不是人;(3)「善」與「不善」是關係中的角色,不是固定身分——彼此需要。

現代反思:好的軟體設計符合這一章:最好的介面不需要說明書(不用「籌策」),最好的資安來自正確的設計,而不是事後加更多的鎖。在團隊裡,資深工程師的好壞,要看那位吃力的新人成長了多少,而不是新人被換掉幾次。當你遇到覺得「不善」的人時,這一章會問你:我能從這裡學到什麼?我又能教什麼?

The state is officially secular and controls religion tightly: the Islamic Renaissance Party was banned in 2015, and a 2024 law restricts clothing considered "alien to national culture", widely reported as targeting the hijab.
  • Languages: Tajik (a variety of Persian, written in Cyrillic) is the state language; the constitution makes Russian the language of inter-ethnic communication. Uzbek and several Pamiri languages are widely spoken.

The Nurek dam (300 m) is among the world's tallest; the Rogun dam, planned to be even taller, is the government's "project of the century". In 2026 the dam body was raised to 1,155 m above sea level, the World Bank approved another $300 million grant on June 30, 2026 (total approved financing $650 million), and the plan is to raise it to 1,185 m in 2027 (Trend, Eurasianet, Times of Central Asia). Critics question its cost and debt.

The Samanid Empire (819–999) is seen as the first Tajik state; its ruler Ismoil Somoni gives his name to the currency (somoni) and the highest peak. The Persian poet Rudaki is a national icon. Russian conquest came in the 19th century; the Tajik SSR was created in 1929, and Samarkand and Bukhara — historic Persian-speaking cities — stayed in Uzbekistan, a lasting grievance. Independence: 9 September 1991. A brutal civil war (1992–1997) killed tens of thousands; the peace agreement was signed in Moscow on 27 June 1997 (now National Unity Day).
  • Leadership and political system: a presidential republic, in practice authoritarian. Emomali Rahmon has led the country since 1992 (head of state from November 1992; president since 1994), one of the longest-serving leaders in the world. A 2016 referendum removed term limits for him as "Leader of the Nation" and lowered the minimum age for president to 30. His son Rustam Emomali has chaired the upper house of parliament since 2020 — constitutionally next in line — and is widely seen as the likely successor. The next presidential election is due in 2027. Opposition parties and independent media are heavily restricted.
  • International relations: member of the CIS, the Russian-led CSTO military alliance and the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation. Russia keeps its 201st military base in Tajikistan. China is a major lender and investor. Tajikistan recognises the People's Republic of China and has no official relations with Taiwan. After deadly border clashes with Kyrgyzstan in 2021 and 2022, the two presidents signed a border treaty in Bishkek on 13 March 2025. The 1,300+ km border with Afghanistan remains a security concern.

Pamiri houses (chid) have five pillars with symbolic meanings. The Pamir Highway attracts adventure travellers.
  • Current challenges: dependence on Russia for jobs and security (after the March 2024 Crocus City Hall attack near Moscow, in which Tajik citizens were charged, Tajik migrants in Russia faced more checks and hostility); poverty and emigration; tension in Gorno-Badakhshan after the 2022 crackdown; succession; and the cost of Rogun.

Unit 1 — Preposition Lesson 4: between vs. among (Stage 1, basic spatial concepts).

  • Today's topic and the usual confusion: Chinese uses 之間 / 中間 for both. Learners often think "between = two, among = three or more" — a school rule that is only partly true.
  • Core concept: between sees things as separate, individual points, with a space or connection from one to the other (two posts, two cities, five countries in a treaty). among sees things as a group or mass with no individual positions — you are inside it, surrounded (a tree among trees, a house among the hills). among also means "one of a group" (Taiwan is among the world's top chip producers) and "shared within a group" (popular among students).
  • Examples: The MRT station is between the bank and the 7-Eleven. / Hsinchu is between Taipei and Taichung. / Don't snack between meals. / The trains are packed between 7 and 9 a.m. / I found my keys among the papers on my desk. / Hiking is very popular among retired people in Taiwan. / Taiwan is among the world's most densely populated places. / The agreement between Taiwan, Japan and the US (three named, separate parties → between).
  • Common mistakes: (1) "the difference among A, B and C" → the difference between A, B and C (differences are always between individual items). (2) "between you and I" → between you and me (after a preposition use the object pronoun). (3) "He is popular between his classmates" → among his classmates (a group, not two points).

Unit 1 — 介系詞第 4 課:between 與 among

1. 今日主題

今天只學一個核心概念:between 和 among 都翻成「之間/中間」,差別在哪?學校常教「between 用在兩個,among 用在三個以上」——這個規則只對一半。真正的差別在於:你把那些東西看成一個一個分開的點,還是一整群。

2. 核心概念

  • between = 分開的、看得出個別位置的點,中間有空間或連線。想像兩根電線桿中間拉一條線,你站在線上:between the two poles。就算是三個、五個,只要每一個都是清楚、各自獨立的(例如條約裡被一一點名的國家、地圖上的三個城市),也用 between。
  • among = 在一群東西「裡面」,被包圍,看不出個別位置。想像一棵樹站在森林裡:a tree among other trees;一間房子藏在山裡:a house among the hills。
  • among 還有兩個常用意思:「是其中之一」(Taiwan is among the world's top chip producers 台灣是全球頂尖晶片生產地之一);「在某群體中普遍」(popular among students 在學生之間很受歡迎)。

心智圖像:between 是「線」(點和點之間),among 是「人群」(被包在裡面)。

3. 比較與例句

between(分開的點)among(一群、被包圍)
位置在兩個(或幾個清楚的)東西中間位置在一群東西裡面
時間:between 7 and 9(兩個時間點之間)不用在時間
差異、比較、關係:the difference between…、a match between…普遍、其中之一:popular among…、among the best
分給兩人或點名的幾人:split it between the two of us分給一群人:shared among the villagers

    捷運站在銀行和 7-Eleven 中間。
  2. Hsinchu is between Taipei and Taichung.
    新竹位在台北和台中之間。
  3. Try not to snack between meals.
    盡量不要在兩餐之間吃零食。
  4. The trains are really crowded between 7 and 9 a.m.
    早上七點到九點之間火車非常擠。
  5. I finally found my keys among the papers on my desk.
    我終於在桌上那堆文件裡找到鑰匙。
  6. Hiking is very popular among retired people in Taiwan.
    在台灣,爬山在退休族之間非常受歡迎。
  7. Taiwan is among the most densely populated places in the world.

  8. There's a trade agreement between Taiwan, Japan and the US.(假設句,示範用法)

換掉介系詞,意思怎麼變?

  • She divided the money between her three children. → 把錢清楚地分給三個(被當成個別的)孩子,每人一份。
    She divided the money among her three children. → 把錢分給「孩子們」這一群,焦點在群體。兩句都對,between 在現代英文裡更常見,語感更「一人一份」。
  • a house between the hills → 房子夾在(兩座)山中間,山的位置很清楚。
    a house among the hills → 房子藏在群山裡,四周都是山。

4. 常見錯誤

  1. ✗ What's the difference among in, on and at? → ✓ What's the difference between in, on and at?
    為什麼會錯:背了「三個以上用 among」。但「差異」一定是在個別項目之間比較,所以永遠用 between。
  2. ✗ This is between you and I. → ✓ This is between you and me.
    為什麼會錯:中文「你和我之間」沒有主格/受格之分;很多母語人士也說錯,以為 I 比較「有禮貌」。介系詞後面要用受格 me。
  3. ✗ He is very popular between his classmates. → ✓ He is very popular among (或 with) his classmates.
    為什麼會錯:中文「在同學之間很受歡迎」用「之間」,就直接翻成 between。同學是一群人,不是兩個點 → among。

Section 7 — Film & Performing Arts: The General (1926)
第 7 段 — 電影與表演藝術:《將軍號》(The General,1926)

ENEnglish version

  • Work: The General, an American silent action-comedy, directed by Buster Keaton and Clyde Bruckman, starring Keaton and Marion Mack; produced by Joseph M. Schenck's company and released by United Artists (first shown at the turn of 1926–27; US release February 1927).
  • Source and background: loosely based on the real Great Locomotive Chase of April 1862, in the American Civil War, when Union volunteers (Andrews' Raiders) stole the locomotive The General in Georgia and were chased by Confederate railwaymen. Keaton's source was William Pittenger's memoir. Keaton told the story from the Confederate side, reportedly because audiences would not laugh at a hero who was the villain of the piece.
  • Plot overview: Johnnie Gray, an engineer of the Western & Atlantic Railroad, loves two things: his engine, the General, and Annabelle Lee. Rejected by the army (he is more useful as an engineer, but nobody tells him), he is called a coward by Annabelle. A year later Union spies steal the General — with Annabelle on board. Johnnie chases them alone on another engine, the Texas, deep behind enemy lines, rescues Annabelle, steals the General back and is chased home in turn. He warns the Confederate army and joins the battle at the Rock River bridge.
  • Artistic style and techniques:
    • Real trains, real danger. Filmed in and around Cottage Grove, Oregon, in 1926, with real locomotives on a real line. Keaton did his own stunts: in one famous shot he sits on the coupling rod of the engine's driving wheels and is lifted up and down as the train starts to move.
    • The most expensive shot of the silent era: the Texas crossing a burning bridge that collapses into the river — one take, a real locomotive. The wreck reportedly stayed in the river for years, until it was salvaged for scrap metal during World War II.
    • Gags built on geometry and cause-and-effect: the mortar cannon slowly lowering until it points at Johnnie — then the track curves and it fires past him; Johnnie clearing railway ties from the track by throwing one tie onto another. Each gag is legible in a wide shot, so the audience sees the danger and the solution in one frame.
    • The "Great Stone Face": Keaton's deadpan expression never begs for sympathy. The comedy comes from a calm, practical man solving huge problems with mechanical logic.
    • Structure: almost the whole film is one chase, out and back — a symmetrical, clear design that later action films still study.
  • Reception and fate: the film was very expensive (often reported at about $750,000) and was a box-office and critical disappointment in 1927; reviewers found it long and not funny enough. The failure helped end Keaton's independence: in 1928 he signed with MGM, a move he later called the biggest mistake of his life.
  • Significance and legacy: rediscovered from the 1950s on, it is now considered one of the greatest films ever made. It was among the first 25 films selected for the US National Film Registry (1989) and placed 34th in the 2012 Sight and Sound critics' poll. Orson Welles is often quoted calling it "the greatest comedy ever made… and perhaps the greatest film ever made." Its practical, geography-clear action is an ancestor of Jackie Chan's stunt films and of Mad Max: Fury Road (introduced in this series on Nov 10) — a vehicle chase out and back.
  • Critical debate (interpretation, not fact): modern critics note that the film makes a Confederate hero sympathetic and leaves slavery entirely out of the picture, in line with the "Lost Cause" view common in 1920s popular culture. Many viewers admire its craft while discussing this blind spot openly.
  • Where to see it today: because of its age it is in the US public domain, so many copies circulate online, often of poor quality. Restored editions (for example from Kino Lorber and the Cohen Film Collection) are on Blu-ray and streaming platforms, and it is a regular at silent-film festivals with live music.

  • 劇情概要:西部與大西洋鐵路的司機約翰尼·葛雷愛兩樣東西:他的火車頭「將軍號」,以及安娜貝爾·李。他被軍隊拒絕(因為當火車司機更有用,但沒有人告訴他),被安娜貝爾罵懦夫。一年後,北軍間諜偷走將軍號——安娜貝爾剛好在車上。約翰尼獨自開著另一部火車頭「德州號」追到敵後深處,救出安娜貝爾、搶回將軍號,反過來被追著一路逃回家。他向南軍示警,並參與了岩石河鐵橋之戰。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:
    • 真火車、真危險。1926 年在奧勒岡州農舍林(Cottage Grove)一帶拍攝,用真的火車頭跑真的鐵路。基頓親自上陣做特技:有一個著名鏡頭,他坐在火車頭動輪的連桿上,火車啟動時被連桿上下帶著走。
    • 默片時代最昂貴的單一鏡頭:德州號開上燃燒的鐵橋,橋垮掉、火車頭墜入河中——只拍一次,用的是真的火車頭。據說殘骸在河裡留了很多年,直到二戰期間才被打撈去當廢鐵。
    • 建立在幾何與因果上的笑點:臼砲的砲口慢慢降下,最後對準約翰尼——接著鐵軌一轉彎,砲彈從他身旁飛過;約翰尼用一根枕木去砸另一根枕木,把擋路的枕木清掉。每個笑點都在遠景中一目了然,觀眾在同一個畫面裡同時看到危險和解法。
    • 「偉大的石頭臉」:基頓面無表情,從不討同情。喜劇來自一個冷靜、務實的人,用機械邏輯解決巨大的麻煩。
    • 結構:幾乎整部片就是一趟追逐,去程與回程——對稱又清楚的設計,至今仍被動作片研究。
  • 當年的評價與命運:這部片成本很高(常見的說法約 75 萬美元),1927 年票房與評論都失利;影評覺得太長、不夠好笑。這次失敗加速了基頓失去獨立製片地位:1928 年他和米高梅簽約,他後來說那是他人生最大的錯誤。
  • 重要性與影響:1950 年代起被重新發現,如今被視為影史最偉大的電影之一。它是 1989 年美國國家影片登記處第一批入選的 25 部片之一,在 2012 年《視與聽》影評人票選中排名第 34。常被引用的是奧森·威爾斯的評語:「有史以來最偉大的喜劇……也許是有史以來最偉大的電影。」它那種實拍、空間關係清楚的動作設計,是成龍特技電影的祖先,也是《瘋狂麥斯:憤怒道》(本系列 11 月 10 日介紹過)的前輩——同樣是一趟去程加回程的載具追逐。
  • 評論爭議(屬於詮釋,不是事實):當代影評指出,這部片讓一位南方邦聯的主角討人喜歡,並把奴隸制完全排除在畫面之外,符合 1920 年代大眾文化中常見的「敗局命定論」(Lost Cause)觀點。許多觀眾一方面欣賞它的技藝,一方面也公開討論這個盲點。
  • 今天去哪裡看:因為年代久遠,它在美國已屬公有領域,所以網路上流傳許多拷貝,畫質常常很差。修復版(例如 Kino Lorber 和 Cohen Film Collection 的版本)可在藍光和串流平台看到,也是默片影展搭配現場配樂的常客。

Section 8 — Film Appreciation & Criticism Method, Lesson 110: Queer Reading, Coding & Subtext

ENEnglish version

1. Today's analytical method

Queer reading is a critical approach that looks at how a film represents — or hides — same-sex desire, gender non-conformity and identities outside the heterosexual "norm". It includes three related ideas: queer coding (signals a film gives a character when censorship or convention forbids saying it openly), subtext (meanings under the surface), and reading against the grain (finding queer meanings an audience can see even if the makers did not plan them). It matters because for decades Hollywood's Production Code (enforced from 1934 to the late 1960s) banned "sex perversion or any inference to it". Queer characters did not disappear; they went under the surface — often as villains, victims or comic figures.

Key thinkers: Vito Russo, The Celluloid Closet (1981; documentary 1995); Richard Dyer, Now You See It (1990); Alexander Doty, Making Things Perfectly Queer (1993) and Flaming Classics (2000), who argued queerness is not a "secret" reading but a normal part of how audiences watch; Patricia White, Uninvited (1999), on lesbian representation in classical Hollywood; B. Ruby Rich, who named "New Queer Cinema" in 1992.

2. Detailed explanation — how to apply it

  1. Know the context of production. What could legally or commercially be shown at that time and place? (Hollywood 1934–68; Taiwan's martial-law-era censorship; today's streaming markets.) Coding makes sense only against what was forbidden.
  2. Look for coding signals: costume and objects that mark a character as "different"; performance style (gesture, voice); a character's intense attachment to someone of the same sex; lines with double meanings; how the camera frames two characters together.
  3. Ask what the plot does to the character. Russo's key finding: coded characters are often punished — they die, go mad or are exposed as villains. A pattern of punishment says something about the culture, not just the character.
  4. Ask who is watching. Queer audiences have long read films "against the grain", finding themselves in stars, friendships and rivalries. Doty's point: these readings are as valid as straight readings.
  5. Separate levels of evidence: (a) on-screen facts (what is shown and said); (b) documented production history (scripts, censorship records, interviews); (c) your interpretation. Say which level you are using.

Common misconceptions: (1) A queer reading is not a claim that a character "really is gay" as a fact — it is an argument about meaning. (2) It is not about guessing the director's or actor's private life. (3) Coding is not always positive: the "sinister queer villain" stereotype did real harm. (4) It does not replace other readings; a film can support several at once.

3. Concrete film example — Rebecca (1940), dir. Alfred Hitchcock

Context: produced by David O. Selznick from Daphne du Maurier's 1938 novel; Joan Fontaine plays the shy new wife (never given a first name), Judith Anderson plays Mrs. Danvers, the housekeeper of Manderley who worshipped the dead first wife, Rebecca. The Production Code shaped the film: the censors also insisted that Maxim not be shown as a murderer, so the cause of Rebecca's death was changed from the novel.

Scene: Mrs. Danvers shows the second Mrs. de Winter Rebecca's bedroom in the west wing. Step by step:

  1. Space as shrine. The room is kept exactly as it was: tall windows, floating sheer curtains, an "R" monogram on objects. Hitchcock lights it like a cathedral. Observation: the room is a preserved space of devotion. Interpretation: Danvers's love for Rebecca is the room's real subject.
  2. Touch and texture. Danvers lifts Rebecca's fur coat and rubs it against her cheek, then against the young wife's. She opens a drawer of underwear "made for her by the nuns of St. Clare's convent" and puts her hand inside a sheer garment: "Look, you can see my hand through it." Observation: the scene is built from intimate touch of clothing. Interpretation: under the Code, desire for a woman could not be spoken, so it moves into objects — a classic coding strategy.
  3. Performance and movement. Hitchcock told François Truffaut that Mrs. Danvers was almost never seen walking; she simply appears beside the heroine. Anderson keeps her face still and her voice soft and intense. This makes her uncanny — but it also links her with the "predatory" lesbian stereotype Russo criticised.
  4. Framing. Danvers often stands behind or above the young wife, close to her shoulder, in two-shots that feel too intimate for a servant and her employer.
  5. What the plot does to her. Danvers ends the film burning Manderley and dying in the flames. Russo-style reading: the coded woman is punished. Patricia White and other critics also point out that Rebecca herself — never seen — is the film's most powerful presence, a woman whose desires escape the husband's control.

Why it works: the film never names anything, yet viewers since 1940 have felt the charge of this scene. That is coding: meaning carried by objects, touch, lighting, performance and framing instead of dialogue.

Objective vs. subjective: objective — the fur, the lingerie line, Danvers's devotion, the Code's restrictions, Hitchcock's comment on her movement. Subjective — that her devotion is erotic, and that her death is a "punishment". Both readings are widely argued, but they remain interpretations.

4. Practical viewing exercise

Watch the opening 15 minutes of Hitchcock's Rope (1948). Note: (a) three coding signals in how Brandon and Phillip are presented (space, dialogue, performance); (b) one fact about the production context you can check (it was made under the Production Code); (c) one sentence that clearly separates your observation from your interpretation. Then compare with an openly queer film, Ang Lee's The Wedding Banquet (1993): what changes when nothing needs to be coded?

5. Key takeaways

  • Coding = meaning moved into objects, touch, performance and framing when words are forbidden.
  • Always check context (what was allowed) and what the plot does to the coded character.
  • A queer reading is an argument about meaning, not a claim about anyone's private life.

6. Suggested further viewing

The Celluloid Closet (1995), dir. Rob Epstein and Jeffrey Friedman — a documentary built from Hollywood clips, based on Russo's book; it shows coding across decades and gives you many short scenes to practise on.

中繁體中文版

1. 今日分析方法

酷兒解讀是一種批評方法,觀察電影如何呈現——或隱藏——同性慾望、不符合性別常規的表現,以及異性戀「常態」以外的身分。它包含三個相關概念:酷兒編碼(當審查或慣例不准明說時,電影給角色的暗示信號)、潛台詞(表面之下的意義),以及逆紋閱讀(觀眾看得出來、即使創作者沒有刻意安排的酷兒意義)。它之所以重要,是因為好萊塢的《製片法典》(1934 年起執行,直到 1960 年代末)禁止「性變態或任何相關暗示」。酷兒角色並沒有消失,而是轉入地下——常以反派、受害者或丑角的形式出現。

重要學者:維托·魯索《賽璐珞衣櫃》(1981;1995 年拍成紀錄片);理查·戴爾《Now You See It》(1990);亞歷山大·多提《Making Things Perfectly Queer》(1993)與《Flaming Classics》(2000),他主張酷兒觀看不是「祕密」讀法,而是觀眾看片的正常方式之一;派翠西亞·懷特《Uninvited》(1999),研究古典好萊塢中的女同志再現;B. 露比·芮奇,1992 年為「新酷兒電影」命名。

2. 詳細說明——怎麼應用

  1. 了解製作背景。當時當地,法律或市場允許拍什麼?(1934–68 年的好萊塢;台灣戒嚴時期的審查;今天的串流市場。)只有對照「什麼被禁止」,編碼才說得通。
  2. 找出編碼信號:標示角色「不一樣」的服裝與物件;表演方式(手勢、聲音);角色對同性者強烈的依戀;雙關台詞;鏡頭如何把兩個角色框在一起。
  3. 問劇情怎麼對待這個角色。魯索的關鍵發現:被編碼的角色常被懲罰——死亡、發瘋或被揭穿是反派。懲罰的模式反映的是文化,而不只是角色。
  4. 問誰在看。酷兒觀眾長期以來「逆紋」閱讀電影,在明星、友誼與對手關係中看見自己。多提的論點是:這些讀法和異性戀讀法一樣成立。
  5. 區分證據層次:(a)銀幕上的事實(演了什麼、說了什麼);(b)有文件記錄的製作史(劇本、審查紀錄、訪談);(c)你的詮釋。說清楚你用的是哪一層。

常見誤解:(1)酷兒解讀不是宣稱某角色「其實是同志」這個事實——它是關於意義的論證。(2)它不是在猜導演或演員的私生活。(3)編碼不一定是正面的:「陰森的酷兒反派」刻板印象造成過真實傷害。(4)它不取代其他讀法;一部電影可以同時支撐好幾種讀法。

3. 具體電影範例——《蝴蝶夢》(Rebecca,1940),希區考克執導

背景:大衛·O·塞茲尼克製作,改編自達芙妮·杜·莫里哀 1938 年的小說;瓊·芳登飾演害羞的新婚妻子(片中從未給她名字),茱蒂絲·安德森飾演曼德利莊園的管家丹佛斯太太——她崇拜已故的第一任妻子蕾貝卡。《製片法典》影響了這部片:審查單位也堅持不能讓麥克辛被呈現為殺人兇手,所以蕾貝卡的死因和小說不同。

場景:丹佛斯太太帶第二任德溫特太太參觀西廂蕾貝卡的臥室。逐步分析:

  1. 空間如聖殿。房間完全保持原樣:高大的窗戶、飄動的薄紗窗簾、物品上的「R」字母花押。希區考克把它打光得像教堂。觀察:這是一個被保存下來、充滿虔敬的空間。詮釋:丹佛斯對蕾貝卡的愛,才是這個房間真正的主題。
  2. 觸感與質地。丹佛斯拿起蕾貝卡的毛皮大衣貼在自己臉頰上,再貼到年輕妻子的臉上。她打開一抽屜「聖克萊爾修道院的修女特別為她做的」內衣,把手伸進一件薄紗衣物裡:「你看,看得到我的手透過去。」觀察:這場戲是由貼身衣物的親密觸碰構成的。詮釋:在法典之下,對女性的慾望不能說出口,於是轉移到物件上——典型的編碼策略。
  3. 表演與移動。希區考克告訴楚浮,丹佛斯太太幾乎從來沒有被拍到走路;她就這樣突然出現在女主角身旁。安德森臉部靜止,聲音輕柔而強烈。這讓她顯得詭異——但也把她和魯索批評的「掠奪型女同志」刻板印象連在一起。
  4. 構圖。丹佛斯常站在年輕妻子的背後或上方、貼近她的肩膀,這些雙人鏡頭對一位管家和女主人來說太過親密。
  5. 劇情怎麼對待她。片尾丹佛斯放火燒了曼德利,死在火中。魯索式讀法:被編碼的女性受到懲罰。派翠西亞·懷特等評論者也指出,從未現身的蕾貝卡本人才是全片最強大的存在——一個慾望逃出丈夫掌控的女人。

為什麼有效:電影從來沒有明說任何事,但從 1940 年以來,觀眾都感受到這場戲的張力。這就是編碼:意義由物件、觸碰、打光、表演和構圖承載,而不是由對白。

客觀與主觀:客觀——毛皮大衣、那句內衣台詞、丹佛斯的崇拜、法典的限制、希區考克對她移動方式的說法。主觀——她的崇拜帶有情慾、她的死是一種「懲罰」。兩種讀法都被廣泛討論,但仍屬詮釋。

4. 實作觀影練習

看希區考克《奪魂索》(Rope,1948)的前 15 分鐘。記下:(a)布蘭登和菲利普被呈現的方式中三個編碼信號(空間、對白、表演);(b)一個你可以查證的製作背景事實(它是在《製片法典》下拍的);(c)一句清楚區分「觀察」與「詮釋」的句子。然後和一部公開的酷兒電影比較——李安《喜宴》(1993):當什麼都不需要編碼時,有什麼不同?

5. 重點整理

  • 編碼=當文字被禁止時,意義轉移到物件、觸碰、表演與構圖上。
  • 一定要查背景(當時允許什麼),以及劇情怎麼對待被編碼的角色。
  • 酷兒解讀是關於意義的論證,不是關於任何人私生活的宣稱。
