3) Spanish Lesson — Day 45 (10 min, combined direct + indirect object pronouns)

Today's lesson combines the two pronoun systems taught separately on Day 15 (indirect object: me/te/le/nos/les) and Day 44 (direct object: lo/la/los/las) into the single sentence pattern Spanish uses whenever both a person and a thing are involved — "I give it to you," "She sends them to us." Two fixed rules govern this: word order always places the indirect object pronoun before the direct object pronoun (never the reverse), e.g. Me lo das (You give it to me), Te la compro (I buy it for you), Nos los envían (They send them to us); and whenever a third-person indirect object pronoun (le or les) would otherwise sit directly in front of a third-person direct object pronoun (lo/la/los/las), le/les is obligatorily replaced by se to avoid the awkward, historically dissimilated "le lo" / "les la" sequence — so Le doy el regalo a él becomes Se lo doy (never "Le lo doy"). Because se erases the distinction between le and les (and even between a él/ella/usted/ellos/ellas/ustedes), a clarifying phrase is added whenever ambiguity matters: Se lo doy a ella. Placement follows the same two positions already learned for single pronouns — before a conjugated verb, or attached as one unit to an infinitive/gerund/affirmative command — except now both pronouns must move together as a pair, never split apart: Te lo voy a dar / Voy a dártelo (both correct), but never "Te voy a lo dar."

  • ¿Me prestas tu bolígrafo? — Will you lend me your pen?
  • Sí, te lo presto. — Yes, I'll lend it to you.
  • ¿Le diste el regalo a tu hermana? — Did you give the gift to your sister?
  • Sí, se lo di ayer. — Yes, I gave it to her yesterday.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 45: 萃 (Cuì) — "Gathering Together" (Lake above, Earth below — Duì over Kūn). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary pivots directly off yesterday's Hexagram 44: "姤者,遇也;物相遇而後聚,故受之以萃;萃者,聚也。" ("Gòu means to meet; once things have met one another, they then gather together, hence it is followed by Cuì [Gathering Together]; Cuì means to gather.") Where 44 depicted a single new force rising to encounter an established order, 45 shows what naturally follows any true meeting: things of the same kind draw together into a mass — water (as a lake) collecting above the receptive earth, just as people, once they have met and recognized common cause, coalesce into a community, a court, or a following. The Judgment reads: "萃,亨。王假有廟,利見大人,亨,利貞。用大牲吉,利有攸往。" ("Gathering Together brings success. The king approaches his ancestral temple. It is favorable to see the great man; this brings success, and it is favorable to persevere in what is correct. Using great sacrificial offerings brings good fortune; it is favorable to have somewhere to go.") The reference to the king at the ancestral temple is deliberate: a true gathering of people needs a unifying center of reverence to hold it together, or it will scatter as easily as it formed. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "萃,聚也;順以說,剛中而應,故聚也。王假有廟,致孝享也。利見大人亨,聚以正也。用大牲吉,利有攸往,順天命也。觀其所聚,而天地萬物之情可見矣。" ("Cuì means gathering — there is docile compliance joined with joyful satisfaction, and firmness centered and responded to, hence a gathering occurs. The king's approach to the ancestral temple carries filial devotion and offering to its utmost. That it is favorable to see the great man and that this brings success means the gathering is held together by correctness. That using great offerings brings good fortune and that it is favorable to have somewhere to go means one is complying with the mandate of Heaven. Observing what a thing gathers around, the true nature of Heaven, Earth, and all beings can be seen.") The Image commentary draws a practical lesson from the hexagram's picture of a lake risen above the earth, ready to overflow: "澤上於地,萃;君子以除戎器,戒不虞。" ("The lake rises above the earth: this is the image of Gathering Together. Thus the superior man renews and repairs his weapons, to guard against the unforeseen.") Read together, 44 and 45 form a natural pair: an unavoidable new encounter (44) is followed by the mass gathering that any successful meeting produces (45) — and the hexagram's warning is that a crowd drawn together by shared purpose is also a store of latent energy that, without a reverent unifying center and vigilant preparedness, can just as easily disperse into chaos or be turned to destructive ends.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Georgia (喬治亞)

  • Location: The South Caucasus, at the crossroads of Eastern Europe and Western Asia, on the eastern shore of the Black Sea; bordered by Russia to the north, Azerbaijan to the east, Armenia and Turkey to the south.
  • Territory: About 69,700 km² — roughly 1.9 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), and comparable to Ireland (~70,273 km²) or the US state of West Virginia (~62,755 km²); this figure includes the two Russian-backed breakaway regions of Abkhazia and South Ossetia, which Georgia's central government does not actually control.
  • Population: Roughly 3.7 million, concentrated heavily in and around the capital, Tbilisi; the population has been shrinking for decades due to low birth rates and sustained emigration.
  • Religion: Predominantly Georgian Orthodox Christianity (around 83%) — Georgia was one of the first nations in the world to adopt Christianity as a state religion, in 326 CE under King Mirian III; Islam accounts for roughly 11% (concentrated in the Adjara region and among ethnic Azerbaijanis), with smaller Armenian Apostolic and other minorities.
  • Economy: A services- and tourism-driven economy with a fast-growing tech/BPO sector, significant remittances from citizens working abroad (especially in Russia and the EU), and a strategic role as a transit corridor for Caspian energy and Silk-Road-revival trade routes (including the Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan pipeline). Georgia is also considered the historical birthplace of winemaking, with an 8,000-year-old tradition of fermenting wine in buried clay qvevri vessels recognized by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage. GDP per capita remains modest by European standards, and the economy is closely tied to EU trade access (a Deep and Comprehensive Free Trade Area has been in place since 2014) alongside growing Chinese investment.
  • History: Home to the ancient kingdoms of Colchis (setting of the Greek myth of the Golden Fleece) and Iberia; reached a medieval "Golden Age" under Queen Tamar in the 12th-13th centuries before Mongol invasions and centuries of contested domination by the Ottoman and Persian empires; annexed by the Russian Empire in the early 19th century. Briefly independent as the Democratic Republic of Georgia (1918-1921) before Soviet annexation; Joseph Stalin was born in the Georgian town of Gori in 1878. Georgia regained independence in 1991, followed by civil conflict and the loss of effective control over Abkhazia and South Ossetia in the early 1990s. The 2003 "Rose Revolution" brought reformist president Mikheil Saakashvili to power; the 2008 Russo-Georgian War over South Ossetia ended with Russia recognizing both breakaway regions as independent (a status virtually no other country accepts). Since 2012 the country has been dominated by the Georgian Dream party, founded by billionaire oligarch Bidzina Ivanishvili, whose disputed October 2024 election win and controversial "foreign agents" legislation triggered sustained pro-EU protests amid accusations of democratic backsliding and a tilt back toward Russia.
  • Current leader: President Mikheil Kavelashvili, a former professional footballer aligned with Georgian Dream, sworn in on December 29, 2024 (the presidency has been a largely ceremonial role since a 2018 constitutional reform shifted executive power to the Prime Minister); Prime Minister Irakli Kobakhidze holds the working executive role, with Ivanishvili widely seen as the power behind the ruling party.
  • Language: Georgian (ქართული), a Kartvelian language unrelated to Indo-European or Turkic language families, written in its own unique alphabet; Abkhaz and other regional languages are spoken in specific areas, and Russian remains widely understood, especially among older generations.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov World Factbook

  • Name: "Spider-Man Pointing at Spider-Man" (also called "Spider-Man Pointing Meme").
  • Origin/history: The source image is a frame from the 1967 "Spider-Man" animated TV series episode "Double Identity," in which the real Spider-Man confronts a Spider-Man impostor (actually the villain Mysterio in disguise), and both point accusingly at each other, each insisting the other is the fake.
  • How it became popular: The screenshot circulated in a small way on forums and early meme sites starting around 2011-2012, but it exploded into mainstream viral popularity around 2018-2019 on Twitter and Reddit as image-macro culture embraced two-panel "identical accuser" templates, becoming one of the most recognizable reaction formats of its era.
  • Meaning/usage: Used to illustrate two people, groups, or things that are functionally identical, equally guilty of the same thing, or indistinguishable from one another, each hypocritically calling out the other — e.g. two companies both accused of the same bad practice, or two nearly identical objects/situations being mutually blamed.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned countless multi-panel edits with more than two "Spider-Men" pointing at each other in a circle (extending the joke to three-, four-, or many-way mutual accusation), and inspired parallel "pointing" templates using other duplicated characters and even real-world duplicate photos/screenshots of near-identical people or objects.
  • Current status: A durable, evergreen reaction-image template that remains in active use across social media for calling out hypocrisy or highlighting uncanny similarity, still regularly resurfacing in "identical situation" jokes years after its peak.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Vertigo (1958, dir. Alfred Hitchcock)

  • Name/key figures: Vertigo, directed by Alfred Hitchcock, starring James Stewart as retired detective John "Scottie" Ferguson and Kim Novak in a dual role as Madeleine Elster/Judy Barton; score by Bernard Herrmann; cinematography by Robert Burks; the hypnotic spiral title sequence and dream sequence were designed by graphic artist Saul Bass and animator John Whitney.
  • Era/background: Released in 1958 by Paramount, set in and shot on location around San Francisco; adapted from the French novel D'entre les morts ("The Living and the Dead") by Boierau and Narcejac, who had written it specifically hoping Hitchcock would adapt it.
  • Plot/content overview: Scottie, forced into early retirement after a rooftop chase leaves him with crippling acrophobia and vertigo, is hired to trail Madeleine, the seemingly possessed wife of an old acquaintance, and falls obsessively in love with her before she apparently dies by falling from a bell tower he cannot climb due to his condition. Later, he encounters Judy, an ordinary woman who resembles Madeleine, and obsessively remakes her in Madeleine's image — only to discover, in a devastating twist, that Judy was Madeleine all along, hired to help stage a murder.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Introduced the "dolly zoom" (simultaneously zooming the lens while dollying the camera in the opposite direction, later nicknamed the "Vertigo effect") to visualize Scottie's disorienting acrophobia; uses a recurring spiral motif (in the title sequence, Madeleine's hair, staircases) as a visual expression of obsessive, downward-pulling desire; a saturated green light bathes key scenes around Judy/Madeleine as a color-coded signal of uncanny transformation and ghostly presence.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Initially met with mixed reviews and modest box office on release, but was dramatically reassessed by critics from the 1970s onward as one of Hitchcock's most personal and psychologically complex films, becoming a foundational text for psychoanalytic film theory (including Laura Mulvey's writing on the "male gaze," illustrated in this routine's own Section 8 lesson on Gaze/Spectatorship Theory).
  • Influence and legacy: Displaced Citizen Kane at the top of the British Film Institute's Sight & Sound decennial critics' poll of the greatest films ever made in 2012 — a symbolic passing of the crown after Kane had held the top spot for fifty years; the dolly zoom effect it pioneered has since been used in countless films (from Jaws to Goodfellas) as shorthand for a character's disorientation or dawning horror; selected for preservation in the US National Film Registry in 1989, among the Registry's first inductees.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Restored and available in high definition via the Criterion Collection and major streaming/home-video platforms; a staple of film-school curricula and critical retrospectives on Hitchcock, obsession, and the cinematic gaze.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, bfi.org.uk Sight & Sound poll

8) Film-Appreciation Method — The Jump Cut (Lesson 31)

A jump cut is an edit that deliberately violates classical continuity by cutting between two shots of the same subject that are too similar in framing/time to read as a smooth continuation, producing a visible, jarring "jump" in the action rather than the invisible continuity a match cut or a standard shot/reverse-shot pair aims for. This is distinct from Day 9's Continuity Editing & the 180-Degree Rule (which is about preserving spatial/temporal coherence across a cut) and Day 11's Match Cut (a *visual rhyme* across two different shots/scenes, not a discontinuity within the same ongoing shot); the jump cut instead foregrounds the cut itself as an authorial interruption, most often used to compress time (skipping ahead within a continuous scene without a dissolve or fade) or to create anxious, unstable rhythm. Three things to check: whether the jump cut is used for narrative economy (visibly skipping dead time the film has no interest in showing) or for a purely psychological/stylistic jolt; whether it occurs within a single ongoing shot of the same subject (the "classic" jarring jump cut) or between distinct but rhythmically similar shots edited in a rapid staccato sequence; and whether the film treats the technique as an error to be smoothed over (as classical Hollywood editing was trained to avoid at all costs) or as a badge of stylistic rebellion against that very tradition.

Worked example: Breathless (À bout de souffle, 1960, dir. Jean-Luc Godard) — the extended car scene where Michel drives Patricia through Paris while they talk; Godard and editor Cécile Decugis cut out chunks of the static, unbroken conversation shot, leaving Patricia's position and the background scenery visibly "jumping" between cuts even though it is clearly the same continuous shot and moment. Originally a practical fix for an overlong first cut, Godard chose to leave the jumps blatantly visible rather than smoothing them with cutaways, turning a budget-driven necessity into one of the French New Wave's defining stylistic signatures — a direct, playful assault on classical Hollywood continuity editing that announced the film's modernism before its plot had even properly begun.

3)西班牙文課程——第 45 天(10 分鐘,直接與間接受詞代名詞合併使用)

今天的課程,把第 15 天教過的間接受詞代名詞(me/te/le/nos/les)與第 44 天教過的直接受詞代名詞(lo/la/los/las)這兩套系統合併起來,對應到西班牙文中「同時牽涉到人與物」時使用的固定句型——例如「我把它給你」、「她把它們寄給我們」。這裡有兩條固定規則:語序上,間接受詞代名詞永遠放在直接受詞代名詞「之前」,絕不會反過來,例如 Me lo das(你把它給我)、Te la compro(我幫你買它)、Nos los envían(他們把它們寄給我們);另外,每當第三人稱間接受詞代名詞(le 或 les)原本應該直接出現在第三人稱直接受詞代名詞(lo/la/los/las)前面時,規則強制將 le/les 改成 se,以避免歷史上因發音相近而被淘汰的彆扭組合「le lo」/「les la」——因此 Le doy el regalo a él 會變成 Se lo doy(絕不能說「Le lo doy」)。由於 se 抹去了 le 與 les 的差異(甚至也抹去了對象是「他/她/您/他們/她們/您們」的區別),當語意可能造成混淆時,會另外加上澄清片語,例如 Se lo doy a ella(我把它給「她」)。代名詞的放置位置規則,與先前單一代名詞時學過的兩個位置完全相同——放在已變化動詞之前,或是黏在原形動詞/現在分詞/肯定命令式字尾當成一個整體——差別在於現在兩個代名詞必須「一起」移動,絕不能被拆開:Te lo voy a dar / Voy a dártelo(兩種寫法皆正確),但絕不能說「Te voy a lo dar」。

  • ¿Me prestas tu bolígrafo? —— 你可以借我你的原子筆嗎?
  • Sí, te lo presto. —— 好,我借給你。
  • ¿Le diste el regalo a tu hermana? —— 你把禮物給你妹妹了嗎?
  • Sí, se lo di ayer. —— 對,我昨天給她了。

4)易經卦象

第 45 卦:萃 (Cuì) ——「聚集」(澤在上,地在下——兌上坤下)。《序卦傳》從昨天的第 44 卦直接接續下來:「姤者,遇也;物相遇而後聚,故受之以萃;萃者,聚也。」(姤就是相遇之意;萬物彼此相遇之後,便會逐漸聚集起來,因此姤卦之後接的是萃卦;「萃」就是聚集之意。)如果說第 44 卦描繪的是一股新生力量崛起、與既有秩序相遇的過程,那麼第 45 卦呈現的,正是任何一場真正的相遇之後自然而然會發生的結果:同類的事物彼此吸引、匯聚成群——正如水匯聚成澤、高懸於柔順的大地之上,也如同人們一旦相遇、認出彼此有共同的理念與目標之後,便會凝聚成一個群體、一個朝廷,或一群追隨者。卦辭寫道:「萃,亨。王假有廟,利見大人,亨,利貞。用大牲吉,利有攸往。」(萃卦:亨通。君王親臨宗廟(以凝聚人心)。適宜求見德高望重的大人物,如此便能亨通,也適宜堅守正道。用豐盛的祭品獻祭則吉祥,適宜有所前往。)卦辭特別提及君王親臨宗廟,用意深刻:一群人真正的聚合,需要一個令眾人虔敬歸向的核心來維繫,否則聚合起來的力量就會像形成時一樣輕易地一哄而散。《彖傳》進一步解釋:「萃,聚也;順以說,剛中而應,故聚也。王假有廟,致孝享也。利見大人亨,聚以正也。用大牲吉,利有攸往,順天命也。觀其所聚,而天地萬物之情可見矣。」(萃,就是聚集之意;柔順配合著喜悅,剛健居中而有所呼應,因此形成聚合。君王親臨宗廟,是為了將孝心與祭祀之誠意發揮到極致。適宜求見大人物、如此便能亨通,是說這樣的聚合是以正道為根基而凝聚起來的。用豐盛祭品而吉祥、適宜有所前往,是說這樣做正是順應天命而行。觀察萬物各自因何而聚集,便能洞見天地萬物運行的真實情理。)《象傳》則從「澤水高懸於地面之上、隨時可能氾濫」這個卦象中,點出實際行事的教訓:「澤上於地,萃;君子以除戎器,戒不虞。」(大澤高懸於大地之上,這就是萃卦的卦象。因此君子藉此整治、修繕兵器,以防範意料之外的變故。)若將第 44 卦與第 45 卦合觀,恰好構成自然而然的一對:一場無可迴避的新相遇(第 44 卦)之後,緊接而來的,正是任何一場成功的相遇必然帶來的群眾聚合(第 45 卦)——而這一卦真正的警示在於:因共同目標而凝聚起來的群眾,同時也是一股蘊藏著巨大潛在能量的力量,若缺少一個令人虔敬歸向的核心、也缺少時刻警戒的準備,這股力量同樣極易在轉瞬間潰散成一片混亂,或被引導、扭轉為破壞性的力量。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——喬治亞(Georgia)

  • 位置:位於南高加索地區,黑海東岸,處於東歐與西亞的交會點;北鄰俄羅斯,東接亞塞拜然,南邊與亞美尼亞、土耳其接壤。
  • 領土:約 69,700 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 1.9 倍,與愛爾蘭(約 70,273 平方公里)或美國西維吉尼亞州(約 62,755 平方公里)相近;此數字包含由俄羅斯支持、實際上並非由喬治亞中央政府控制的兩個分離地區——阿布哈茲與南奧塞提亞。
  • 人口:約 370 萬人,高度集中於首都提比里斯(Tbilisi)周邊;由於出生率偏低與長期人口外移,總人口數已連續數十年呈現下滑趨勢。
  • 宗教:以喬治亞正教會(東正教)為主,約佔 83%——喬治亞是全世界最早將基督教立為國教的國家之一,早在西元 326 年米里安三世(Mirian III)國王在位時便已確立;伊斯蘭教約佔 11%(主要集中於阿扎爾地區及亞塞拜然裔居民之間),另有規模較小的亞美尼亞使徒教會等其他宗教社群。
  • 經濟:以服務業與觀光業為主要支柱,科技/後勤外包(BPO)產業快速成長,並高度仰賴海外(尤其是俄羅斯與歐盟國家)僑民匯回的僑匯收入;喬治亞在裏海能源與絲路復興貿易路線(包括巴庫-提比里斯-傑伊漢輸油管線)上扮演重要的戰略轉運樞紐角色。喬治亞亦被視為釀酒工藝的發源地,擁有長達八千年、將葡萄酒埋入陶製「奎弗利」(qvevri)容器中發酵的傳統釀造技藝,已被聯合國教科文組織列為非物質文化遺產。以歐洲標準而言,喬治亞人均 GDP仍屬偏低,經濟高度仰賴與歐盟的貿易關係(自 2014 年起已生效「深度全面自由貿易區」協定),同時中國的投資規模也持續成長。
  • 歷史:古代分別為科爾基斯王國(希臘神話「金羊毛」故事的發生地)與伊比利亞王國的所在地;12 至 13 世紀塔瑪拉女王(Queen Tamar)在位期間曾達到中世紀「黃金時代」的巔峰,此後歷經蒙古入侵,以及鄂圖曼帝國與波斯帝國數百年爭奪支配權的動盪;19 世紀初被俄羅斯帝國併吞。1918 至 1921 年曾短暫獨立為「喬治亞民主共和國」,隨後遭蘇聯併吞;史達林於 1878 年出生於喬治亞的哥里鎮(Gori)。喬治亞於 1991 年重新獨立,隨即爆發內戰,並在 1990 年代初期實質失去對阿布哈茲與南奧塞提亞的控制權。2003 年「玫瑰革命」將改革派總統薩卡希維利(Mikheil Saakashvili)推上權力核心;2008 年爆發的俄羅斯-喬治亞戰爭(南奧塞提亞戰爭)最終以俄羅斯承認這兩個分離地區為獨立國家收場(此一地位幾乎不被其他任何國家承認)。自 2012 年起,國政由億萬富翁寡頭伊凡尼什維利(Bidzina Ivanishvili)創立的「喬治亞夢想黨」主導執政,該黨在 2024 年 10 月爭議不斷的選舉中勝選,加上備受爭議的「外國代理人法」立法,引爆了持續不斷、訴求親歐盟路線的抗議浪潮,外界並質疑該國正出現民主倒退、重新向俄羅斯靠攏的跡象。
  • 現任領導人:總統米海爾·卡維拉什維利(Mikheil Kavelashvili),前職業足球員,與喬治亞夢想黨關係密切,已於 2024 年 12 月 29 日宣誓就職(自 2018 年修憲將行政實權移交總理之後,總統一職已大致上僅具象徵性質);總理伊拉克利·科巴希澤(Irakli Kobakhidze)實際掌握行政大權,而伊凡尼什維利則普遍被視為執政黨背後真正的權力核心。
  • 語言:官方語言為喬治亞語(ქართული),屬於卡特維利語系,與印歐語系或突厥語系皆無關聯,並使用自成一格、獨一無二的喬治亞字母書寫;阿布哈茲語及其他地區性語言於特定區域使用,俄語則在年長世代中仍相當普及。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov 世界概況(World Factbook)

6)網路迷因介紹——蜘蛛人互指蜘蛛人(Spider-Man Pointing at Spider-Man)

  • 名稱:「蜘蛛人互指蜘蛛人」(Spider-Man Pointing at Spider-Man,亦稱 Spider-Man Pointing Meme)。
  • 起源/歷史:圖片來源為 1967 年美國動畫影集《蜘蛛人》(Spider-Man)其中一集〈雙重身分〉(Double Identity)的畫面:真正的蜘蛛人與一名冒牌蜘蛛人(其實是反派幻影客 Mysterio 假扮)正面對峙,兩人同時舉手互相指向對方,各自堅稱對方才是冒牌貨。
  • 如何走紅:這張截圖大約自 2011 至 2012 年起,便在論壇與早期迷因網站上小範圍流傳,直到 2018 至 2019 年間,隨著「雙格互控」類型的改圖文化興起,在 Twitter 與 Reddit 上爆紅成為全球性的流行迷因,成為該時期最具代表性的反應圖格式之一。
  • 意義/使用情境:用來描繪兩個功能上完全相同、對同一件事同樣有責任,或彼此難以區分的人、群體或事物,卻虛偽地互相指責對方——例如兩家公司同樣被指控有相同的不當行徑,或兩個幾乎一模一樣的物件/情境彼此互相「甩鍋」。
  • 變體/演化:衍生出無數多格改圖版本,將兩名「蜘蛛人」擴展成三、四人甚至更多人圍成一圈互相指來指去(把「互相究責」的笑點無限延伸),也啟發了以其他重複出現的角色,甚至真實世界中幾乎一模一樣的人物/物件照片或截圖,套用相同「互指」邏輯的類似迷因模板。
  • 目前狀態:是一個歷久不衰、長青型的反應圖模板,至今仍在社群媒體上被廣泛用來指出虛偽或凸顯詭異的相似性,即使在爆紅高峰期過後多年,依然經常在「情境完全相同」的笑話中被重新翻出使用。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《迷魂記》Vertigo(1958,導演:亞佛烈德·希區考克)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《迷魂記》(Vertigo),導演為亞佛烈德·希區考克(Alfred Hitchcock),詹姆斯·史都華(James Stewart)飾演退休警探約翰「史考提」弗格森,金·露華(Kim Novak)則一人分飾兩角——瑪德蓮·艾斯特與茱蒂·巴頓;配樂由伯納德·赫爾曼(Bernard Herrmann)創作;攝影指導為羅伯特·柏克斯(Robert Burks);片中令人目眩神迷的螺旋狀片頭與夢境段落,則由平面設計師索爾·巴斯(Saul Bass)與動畫師約翰·惠特尼(John Whitney)共同設計。
  • 時代/背景:1958 年由派拉蒙影業發行,場景設定並實地拍攝於舊金山;改編自法國小說家波瓦洛(Boileau)與納瑟雅克(Narcejac)合著的小說《生者與亡者之間》(D'entre les morts),兩位作者當初撰寫此書時,便是特意希望有朝一日能由希區考克親自改編搬上大銀幕。
  • 內容概述:史考提在一次屋頂追捕行動中意外導致同僚喪命,自此留下嚴重的懼高症與眩暈症(vertigo),被迫提前退休;後受託跟蹤瑪德蓮——一位似乎遭到亡靈附身的舊識之妻,史考提對她產生了近乎執迷的愛戀,直到她似乎從一座他因懼高症而無法攀爬的鐘樓墜樓身亡。日後,史考提遇見了容貌與瑪德蓮神似的平凡女子茱蒂,並執迷地將她一步步改造成瑪德蓮的模樣——直到劇情迎來令人震驚的大逆轉:原來茱蒂自始至終就是瑪德蓮本人,受雇協助策劃了一場謀殺案。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:本片首創「伸縮變焦」(dolly zoom,即攝影機同時向前推軌、鏡頭同時向後拉焦,或反向操作,後世暱稱為「迷魂記效應」Vertigo effect),藉此具象化呈現史考提令人天旋地轉的懼高症體驗;全片反覆運用螺旋意象(片頭字卡、瑪德蓮的髮型、螺旋樓梯)作為執迷慾望不斷向下沉淪、無法自拔的視覺象徵;茱蒂/瑪德蓮出場的關鍵場景,則籠罩在飽和的綠光之中,以顏色作為詭異蛻變與幽靈般存在感的視覺符碼。
  • 文化/歷史意義:上映之初評價兩極、票房也僅屬平平,但自 1970 年代起獲得影評界大幅重新評價,被視為希區考克個人色彩最濃厚、心理層次也最為複雜的作品之一,並成為精神分析電影理論的重要奠基文本之一(包括蘿拉·莫薇〔Laura Mulvey〕論述「男性凝視」的經典文章,亦呼應了本系列第 8 單元「凝視理論/觀影主體性」課程所舉的例子)。
  • 影響與傳承:2012 年英國電影協會(BFI)《視與聽》雜誌(Sight & Sound)十年一度的影評人票選史上最偉大電影調查中,本片擠下蟬聯五十年冠軍寶座的《大國民》(Citizen Kane),登上榜首——象徵著影史「桂冠」的一次具指標意義的交棒;片中首創的「伸縮變焦」技法,此後被無數電影(從《大白鯊》到《四海好傢伙》)沿用,成為表現角色目眩神迷或恐懼油然而生的經典視覺捷徑;1989 年入選美國國家影片登記表,為該登記表最早一批入選的作品之一。
  • 現況/如何欣賞:已透過 Criterion Collection 完成高畫質數位修復,並可於各大串流平台及家庭影音平台觀賞;長年是電影學院課程,以及探討希區考克、執迷心理與電影凝視理論的影評回顧文章中的必備教材。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、bfi.org.uk《視與聽》影史票選

8)看片方法——跳接 The Jump Cut(第 31 課)

「跳接」(jump cut)是一種刻意違反古典連戲原則的剪輯手法:將同一主體、在構圖或時間點上過於相近的兩個鏡頭直接剪在一起,使動作產生一種明顯、突兀的「跳動感」,而非如跳接對位(match cut)或標準正反打鏡頭那樣、追求不著痕跡的連戲流暢感。這與第 9 天教過的「連戲剪輯與 180 度假想線」(著重於維持跨鏡頭的空間/時間連貫性)不同,也與第 11 天的「跳接對位」(match cut,是兩個不同鏡頭/場景之間的「視覺呼應」,而非同一個持續鏡頭內部的不連貫)不同;跳接反而是刻意將剪接本身「前景化」,凸顯為一種創作者主動介入的中斷,最常見的用途是壓縮時間(在一場戲的連續進行中,不透過溶接或淡出、直接跳過一段無關緊要的時間),或是刻意營造焦躁不安、失衡的節奏感。可以檢視三個重點:這個跳接究竟是為了敘事效率(明顯跳過電影不打算呈現的枯燥時間),還是純粹為了製造心理層面或風格上的衝擊感?它是發生在「同一個持續進行中鏡頭」內部(這是最典型、最令人感到突兀的跳接形式),還是發生在多個構圖節奏相近、以急促連續方式剪在一起的不同鏡頭之間?這部電影究竟把這項技巧當成需要被撫平掩蓋的「剪接錯誤」(如同古典好萊塢剪輯體系向來被訓練要極力避免的狀況),還是刻意把它當成一種公然反抗這套傳統的風格宣言?

實例演練:《斷了氣》(À bout de souffle,1960,導演:尚盧·高達)——男主角米歇爾開車載派翠西亞穿越巴黎、兩人在車上持續對話的一長段場景中,高達與剪接師塞西爾·德屈吉(Cécile Decugis)大膽刪去了這段靜態、不間斷對話鏡頭中的多個片段,使得派翠西亞在畫面中的位置與背景街景,在剪接點之間明顯地「跳動」,儘管觀眾清楚知道這其實是同一個持續進行、同一個時間點的鏡頭。這項手法原本只是為了解決初剪版本片長過長的實務問題,但高達刻意選擇不用轉場鏡頭掩蓋這些跳動感、而是讓它們大方地暴露出來,將一項出於預算考量的權宜之計,轉化為法國新浪潮最具代表性的風格印記之一——一次直接、帶著玩心的挑釁,公然向古典好萊塢連戲剪輯體系宣戰,早在劇情正式展開之前,就已經先行宣告了這部電影的現代主義精神。
