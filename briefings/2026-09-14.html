3) Spanish Lesson — Day 64 (10 min, Adverbs of Manner: Los Adverbios de Modo con -mente)

Steps outside the tense/mood track to cover another high-frequency word-formation topic, following on from Day 63's diminutives/augmentatives: forming manner adverbs with the suffix -mente. The rule takes an adjective's feminine singular form (rápido→rápida, lento→lenta) and adds -mente (rápidamente, lentamente); adjectives that already share one form for both genders (normal, fácil, feliz) simply add -mente directly with no change. Any written accent on the base adjective (rápida, fácil) is preserved unchanged in the longer adverb. The lesson's sharpest grammar point is the "chain rule": when two or more -mente adverbs modify the same verb, joined by y/o/pero, only the last one keeps the -mente suffix, and every earlier one reverts to its plain feminine adjective form (habla rápida y claramente, not rápidamente y claramente) — a rule that trips up many learners who assume every adverb in a list needs its own suffix. A closing style note flags that, much like overusing "-ly" adverbs in English, stacking too many -mente words in formal Spanish writing is considered heavy, and native writers often substitute de manera + adjective or con + noun instead.

  • Ella habla español perfectamente. — She speaks Spanish perfectly.
  • Sí, y también escribe rápida y claramente. — Yes, and she also writes quickly and clearly.
  • Normalmente practica dos horas al día. — She normally practices two hours a day.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 64: 未濟 (Wèi Jì) — "Before Completion" (Fire over Water) (火水未濟 — Lí 離/Fire above, Kǎn 坎/Water below). This is the final hexagram in the entire 64-hexagram King Wen sequence, and the Xugua (Sequence) commentary makes that finality explicit: "物不可窮也,故受之以未濟終焉。" ("Things can never be brought to an ultimate, exhaustive end; therefore [the sequence] receives Wèi Jì, and ends there.") The Judgment reads: "未濟,亨。小狐汔濟,濡其尾,無攸利。" ("Wèi Jì: success is possible. [But like] a small fox that has almost finished crossing a stream and then gets its tail wet, nothing [further] would be advantageous.") The image is of a task so nearly finished that only the very last, most careless moment undoes it — a warning that "almost there" is precisely when attention must not lapse. The Image commentary reads: "火在水上,未濟;君子以慎辨物居方。" ("Fire above water: this is Wèi Jì. Thus the superior person is careful to discriminate among things so that each finds its proper place.") Structurally, Wèi Jì's six lines (bottom to top: yin, yang, yin, yang, yin, yang) are the exact mirror-reverse of yesterday's Hexagram 63 (既濟, Jì Jì, "After Completion") — and are, in fact, the only arrangement among all 64 hexagrams in which every single line sits in its formally "incorrect" position (odd positions occupied by yielding/yin lines, even positions by firm/yang lines), the polar opposite of Jì Jì's uniquely all-"correct" arrangement. Fire naturally rises while water naturally sinks, so in Wèi Jì's arrangement the two forces move apart rather than engaging one another — the literal image of incompletion, of a task left permanently, structurally unresolved. That the King Wen sequence deliberately ends not on triumphant completion (Jì Jì) but one step further, on "not yet complete," is among the most-discussed structural decisions in the entire I Ching: read traditionally as insisting that no completion is ever truly final, and that change continues in unbroken cycles rather than stopping at any fixed endpoint. With today's Hexagram 64, this routine's day-by-day, never-repeating journey through the full King Wen sequence — begun with Hexagram 1 (乾, Qián) on 2026-07-08 — is now complete. 5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Croatia

  • Location & territory size: Croatia (Hrvatska) sits in Central/Southeastern Europe along the Adriatic Sea, bordered by Slovenia, Hungary, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Montenegro, with a famously crescent-shaped territory wrapped around Bosnia's narrow Adriatic coastline. At roughly 56,594 km², it is comparable in size to Croatia's neighbor Bosnia and Herzegovina, or to West Virginia in the US, and about 1.6 times the land area of Taiwan (~36,197 km²), though with a coastline (including over a thousand islands) many times longer relative to its size.
  • Population: Approximately 3.8 million people (2026), concentrated in the capital Zagreb and along the Adriatic coast; the population has been gradually declining for decades due to low birth rates and EU-era emigration.
  • Religion: Predominantly Roman Catholic (around 80%+ of the population), reflecting Croatia's long historical association with Central European, Habsburg-influenced Catholic culture, in contrast to the Orthodox Christian and Muslim populations more prevalent among some of its Balkan neighbors.
  • Economy: A high-income, EU/Eurozone/Schengen-member economy heavily driven by tourism (the Dalmatian coast and cities like Dubrovnik and Split draw huge numbers of visitors relative to the country's size), alongside shipbuilding, manufacturing, and IT services; Croatia adopted the euro and joined the Schengen Area on the same day, January 1, 2023, becoming the Eurozone's 20th member.
  • History: A medieval kingdom that entered a personal union with Hungary in 1102, later coming under Habsburg (Austrian) rule for centuries; joined the first Yugoslav state after World War I, then became a socialist republic within Tito's post-WWII Yugoslav federation. Croatia declared independence in 1991, triggering the brutal Croatian War of Independence (1991–1995) against Yugoslav/Serb forces, before joining the EU in 2013 as its most recent member until Croatia's own accession expanded that list.
  • Current leadership: President Zoran Milanović (head of state, since 2020) and Prime Minister Andrej Plenković (head of government, since 2016, leading the HDZ/Croatian Democratic Union) — a frequently cohabitation-style arrangement, since the two have often belonged to opposing political camps.
  • Language: Croatian (a South Slavic language, mutually intelligible to a significant degree with Bosnian, Serbian, and Montenegrin, though written in the Latin rather than Cyrillic alphabet) is the official language; Italian holds co-official status in parts of Istria.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Overly Attached Girlfriend

  • Name & origin: "Overly Attached Girlfriend" originated from a June 2012 YouTube video by Laina Morris (then a college student), submitted as a parody entry to a fan-video contest for Justin Bieber's song "Boyfriend." In the clip, Morris stares unblinkingly into the camera with wide, intense eyes while singing exaggeratedly possessive, controlling lyrics about a boyfriend, framed as if directly confronting the viewer.
  • How it became popular: The video's unsettlingly intense stare and over-the-top "clingy girlfriend" persona resonated instantly as comedy, and it was quickly reposted and remixed across Reddit, Tumblr, and meme-image sites; a still frame of Morris's wide-eyed stare became a standalone reaction/exploitable macro image template, captioned with jealous, controlling, or invasive statements attributed to a hypothetical possessive partner.
  • Meaning & usage context: The meme is used to caricature and poke fun at possessive, jealous, or excessively controlling behavior in relationships — captions typically voice exaggerated demands or accusations ("Who were you texting? I already checked your phone while you were sleeping") for comic effect, functioning as a lighthearted, hyperbolic shorthand for clinginess rather than a serious commentary on any real relationship.
  • Variations & evolution: The format spawned an "Overly Attached Girlfriend" persona that Morris herself leaned into professionally for a period (including a sponsored Mountain Dew campaign and further YouTube content), plus countless remixed image macros, gender-flipped "Overly Attached Boyfriend" variants, and crossovers applying the same wide-eyed-stare template to unrelated captions about obsessive attachment to objects, hobbies, or ideas rather than a person.
  • Current status: The meme's viral peak was firmly 2012–2014, and it no longer actively circulates as a fresh format, but the still-frame image and the phrase itself remain widely recognized as one of the era's defining early-2010s meme templates, regularly referenced in "meme history" retrospectives and nostalgia content in 2026.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari (1920, dir. Robert Wiene)

  • Creator & era: Directed by Robert Wiene, written by Hans Janowitz and Carl Mayer, and released in 1920 in Weimar-era Germany, in the aftermath of World War I; widely regarded as the founding, defining work of German Expressionist cinema.
  • Plot overview: A young man, Francis, recounts a disturbing story to a stranger: a sinister fairground showman, Dr. Caligari, exhibits a somnambulist (sleepwalker), Cesare, who can predict the future — and who, it turns out, is being used by Caligari to commit murders while in a trance. The story builds to a series of twists culminating in a now-famous frame-narrative reveal recontextualizing everything the audience has seen.
  • Artistic style & technique: Defined by its radically non-naturalistic, painted set designs — jagged, distorted buildings, crooked streets and windows, harsh painted shadows and diagonal lines standing in for actual lighting — created on a low budget by Expressionist stage designers rather than through location shooting or naturalistic studio sets, externalizing psychological distortion and unease directly into the physical world of the film.
  • Cultural & historical significance: Widely credited as the origin point of German Expressionist film and an early landmark of horror and psychological-thriller cinema; its visual approach — using distorted, artificial environments to convey a character's or society's inner disturbance rather than realistic space — is considered a foundational precedent for later horror, film noir, and psychological cinema worldwide.
  • Influence & legacy: Its visual vocabulary directly influenced 1930s Universal horror films, American film noir's shadow-and-angle aesthetics, and countless later stylized horror and thriller productions; the film's twist-ending frame narrative is also frequently cited as an early, influential example of an unreliable-narrator structure in cinema.
  • Where to experience it today: Widely available on home video and streaming (including a Kino Lorber/Criterion-adjacent restoration), a public-domain silent classic frequently screened with live musical accompaniment at festivals and a staple of film-school curricula on German Expressionism and early horror.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — The Insert Shot / Cutaway to Significant Detail (Lesson 50)

A new analytical lens distinct from all 49 prior lessons: the insert shot is a close-up cutaway to a specific object or detail — a ticking clock, a dripping faucet, a hand reaching for a weapon — inserted into an otherwise wider scene to direct the audience's attention to something the characters may or may not have noticed themselves. Distinct from Day 8's Depth of Field & Focus (which concerns what stays sharp within one continuous shot) and Day 13's Shot Scale (which just names shot sizes in general), the insert shot is specifically about interrupting a scene's normal coverage to isolate one meaningful detail as its own shot. Check what the camera is asking you to notice that the wide shot alone would let you miss, whether the detail builds suspense (a countdown), foreshadows a later payoff, or supplies information the characters lack, and whether inserts recur as a directorial rhythm or appear as a single, isolated punctuation.

Worked example: Once Upon a Time in the West (1968, dir. Sergio Leone) — the celebrated wordless opening sequence at a remote train station, where three gunmen wait for a train in near-total silence. Leone builds tension almost entirely through a rhythmic sequence of insert shots of mundane environmental details — a dripping water tank splashing onto a hat brim, a fly buzzing around and landing on a man's face, a creaking windmill vane, a telegraph key clicking out a message — each held far longer than the information it conveys strictly requires, forcing the audience to sit inside the men's own agonizing wait alongside them. No dialogue and almost no conventional plot content occurs in nearly ten minutes of screen time; the suspense is constructed purely from the accumulating weight of these inserted, seemingly trivial details, making the sequence one of cinema's most frequently cited demonstrations of the insert shot's power to manufacture dread out of stillness.

3)西班牙文課程——第 64 天(10 分鐘,副詞的構成:Los Adverbios de Modo con -mente)

延續第 63 天的指小詞/擴大詞主題,同樣跳出時態/語氣主軸,處理另一個高頻構詞主題:以字尾 -mente 構成表達「……地」的方式副詞。規則是取形容詞的陰性單數形式(rápido→rápida、lento→lenta)加上 -mente(rápidamente、lentamente);陰陽同形的形容詞(normal、fácil、feliz)則直接加上 -mente,不需任何變化。原形容詞若帶有書寫重音符號(如 rápida、fácil),轉成副詞後重音符號原封不動保留。今天課程最鋒利的文法重點是「省略鏈條規則」:當兩個以上的 -mente 副詞以 y/o/pero 連接、共同修飾同一個動詞時,只有鏈條中最後一個保留 -mente 字尾,前面的副詞一律還原成陰性形容詞形式(habla rápida y claramente,而非 rápidamente y claramente)——許多學習者誤以為清單中每個副詞都得各自保留字尾,這正是最容易踩雷之處。課程最後也提醒:如同英文寫作中「避免濫用 -ly 副詞」的建議,西語正式書面寫作中若連續堆疊過多 -mente 副詞,同樣會顯得笨重累贅,母語作者常會改用「de manera + 形容詞」或「con + 名詞」等替代說法。

  • Ella habla español perfectamente. —— 她西班牙語說得很完美。
  • Sí, y también escribe rápida y claramente. —— 對啊,而且她寫字也寫得又快又清楚。
  • Normalmente practica dos horas al día. —— 她通常每天練習兩小時。

4)易經卦象

第 64 卦:未濟 (Wèi Jì) ——「事尚未完成」(火在水上)(火水未濟——離 Lí/火在上,坎 Kǎn/水在下)。這是整部《易經》64 卦「王本卦序」中的最後一卦,《序卦傳》直接點明了這份終結性:「物不可窮也,故受之以未濟終焉。」(萬事萬物永遠無法被窮盡、帶向絕對的終點;因此[整個卦序]接續以未濟卦作結。)卦辭寫道:「未濟,亨。小狐汔濟,濡其尾,無攸利。」(未濟卦:仍有可能獲得成功。〔但就像〕一隻小狐狸幾乎已經渡過河流,卻在最後關頭弄濕了尾巴,〔接下來〕便無所裨益了。)這個意象描繪的是一件幾乎已經完成的事,卻在最後、最容易鬆懈大意的那一刻功虧一簣——提醒世人「即將完成」的時刻,恰恰正是最不容許分心的時刻。《象傳》寫道:「火在水上,未濟;君子以慎辨物居方。」(火在水之上,這就是未濟之象。因此君子由此體悟,應當謹慎地辨明萬物,使每件事物都能各安其位。)就爻位結構而言,未濟卦的六爻(由下而上依序為:陰、陽、陰、陽、陰、陽)恰好是昨日第 63 卦(既濟,Jì Jì,「事已完成」)的完全鏡像反轉——而且正是全部 64 卦之中,唯一一個每一爻皆恰好落在其「不正位」(奇數位為陰爻、偶數位為陽爻)的卦象,與既濟卦「全部正位」的獨特安排恰成兩極對比。火焰本性向上竄升,水則本性向下滲流,因此在未濟卦的安排中,兩股力量反而彼此背離、無法真正交會——這正是「事尚未完成」的具體寫照,象徵一件被永久懸置、結構上始終無法真正解決的事。整部王本卦序刻意不以既濟(大功告成)作結,而是再往前推進一步、以「尚未完成」畫下句點,是《易經》全書結構安排中最常被討論的設計之一:傳統上被解讀為,堅持任何完成都不曾是真正的終點,變化將以不間斷的循環持續下去,而非停留在任何固定的終點。隨著今日第 64 卦的介紹,本例行公事自 2026 年 7 月 8 日、從第 1 卦(乾,Qián)開始、逐日介紹且絕不重複的完整王本卦序之旅,至此正式完成。依本例行公事既定的規則,自明日起,第 4 項單元將改為每日介紹一篇莊子的散文或詩作,同樣絕不重複。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——克羅埃西亞(Croatia)

  • 地理位置與領土規模:克羅埃西亞(Hrvatska)位於中歐/東南歐交界,沿亞得里亞海分布,與斯洛維尼亞、匈牙利、塞爾維亞、波士尼亞與赫塞哥維納,以及蒙特內哥羅接壤,國土形狀呈現著名的新月形,環繞著波士尼亞狹窄的亞得里亞海岸線。國土面積約 56,594 平方公里,規模與鄰國波士尼亞與赫塞哥維納相當,或大致相當於美國西維吉尼亞州,約為台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)陸地面積的 1.6 倍;不過若以海岸線長度(包含逾千座島嶼)相對於國土面積的比例而言,則遠遠超出多數同等規模的國家。
  • 人口:約 380 萬人(2026 年),人口主要集中於首都札格雷布及亞得里亞海岸沿線;受出生率偏低與加入歐盟後人口外移影響,總人口數十年來持續緩步下滑。
  • 宗教:以羅馬天主教為主(約佔人口 80% 以上),反映克羅埃西亞長久以來與中歐、受哈布斯堡王朝影響的天主教文化之間的深厚淵源,與部分巴爾幹鄰國較普遍的東正教與伊斯蘭教人口結構形成對比。
  • 經濟:屬高所得的歐盟/歐元區/申根區成員國經濟體,高度仰賴觀光業(達爾馬提亞海岸與杜布羅夫尼克、斯普利特等城市,相對於國家規模吸引了龐大的觀光人潮),同時也發展造船、製造業與資訊科技服務業;克羅埃西亞於 2023 年 1 月 1 日同步採用歐元並加入申根區,成為歐元區第 20 個成員國。
  • 歷史:中世紀曾為獨立王國,於 1102 年與匈牙利建立共主邦聯關係,其後長期由哈布斯堡(奧地利)王朝統治;第一次世界大戰後併入第一個南斯拉夫國家,二戰後成為狄托領導下社會主義南斯拉夫聯邦的一個共和國。克羅埃西亞於 1991 年宣布獨立,引爆了慘烈的克羅埃西亞獨立戰爭(1991–1995 年,對抗南斯拉夫/塞爾維亞軍隊),其後於 2013 年加入歐盟,當時是最新加入的成員國,直到後續其他國家加入才改變此一名次。
  • 現任領導人:總統佐蘭·米蘭諾維奇(Zoran Milanović,國家元首,自 2020 年起任職)與總理安德烈·普連科維奇(Andrej Plenković,政府首腦,自 2016 年起任職,領導克羅埃西亞民主聯盟/HDZ)——由於兩人經常分屬不同政治陣營,此一組合時常呈現「左右共治」的局面。
  • 語言:官方語言為克羅埃西亞語(南斯拉夫語族的一支,與波士尼亞語、塞爾維亞語、蒙特內哥羅語有相當程度的互通性,但採用拉丁字母而非西里爾字母書寫);義大利語在伊斯特里亞半島部分地區具有共同官方語言地位。

6)網路迷因介紹——過度依戀女友(Overly Attached Girlfriend)

  • 名稱與起源:「Overly Attached Girlfriend」(過度依戀女友)源自 2012 年 6 月萊娜·莫里斯(Laina Morris,當時仍是大學生)上傳的一支 YouTube 影片,原本是為小賈斯汀(Justin Bieber)歌曲《Boyfriend》的粉絲影片徵稿活動所投的戲仿參賽作品。影片中,莫里斯目不轉睛地直視鏡頭、眼神睜得極大且強烈,一邊誇張地演唱著充滿佔有慾、控制慾的歌詞,彷彿正直接對著觀眾本人「興師問罪」。
  • 如何走紅:影片中令人略感不安的強烈凝視,加上誇張到極致的「黏人女友」人設,瞬間引發喜劇效果,迅速在 Reddit、Tumblr 與各大迷因圖片網站被大量轉發與混剪;莫里斯睜大雙眼凝視鏡頭的靜態截圖,更獨立演變成一個可自由填字的迷因梗圖模板,配上各種假想中佔有慾強、控制慾旺盛、甚至帶有侵犯隱私意味的伴侶發言字幕。
  • 意義與使用情境:這個迷因主要用來誇張地諷刺、戲謔感情關係中佔有慾強、易吃醋或過度控制的行為——字幕內容通常是誇張到近乎荒謬的質問或指控(例如「你剛剛在跟誰傳訊息?你睡著的時候我已經檢查過你的手機了」),純粹用於博君一笑,是一種誇飾、輕鬆看待「黏人」特質的簡便說法,而非針對任何真實感情關係的嚴肅評論。
  • 變體與演進:這個格式曾一度催生出莫里斯本人專業經營的「Overly Attached Girlfriend」人設(包括一支與 Mountain Dew 汽水品牌合作的贊助廣告,以及後續其他 YouTube 內容),也衍生出無數混剪梗圖版本、性別對調的「Overly Attached Boyfriend(過度依戀男友)」變體,以及將同一款睜大雙眼凝視鏡頭的模板,套用在與「人」無關、而是對物品、嗜好或某種想法產生過度執著的無厘頭字幕上。
  • 現況:這個迷因的爆紅高峰穩固落在 2012 年至 2014 年之間,如今已不再是持續活躍、被大量創作的新迷因格式,但其靜態截圖與這句用語本身,至今仍被廣泛公認為 2010 年代初期最具代表性的經典迷因模板之一,在 2026 年的今天依然經常出現在「迷因歷史」回顧與懷舊內容之中。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《卡里加利博士的小屋》(The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari,1920,導演:羅伯特·維內)

  • 創作者與時代背景:由羅伯特·維內(Robert Wiene)執導,漢斯·雅諾維茨(Hans Janowitz)與卡爾·梅耶(Carl Mayer)編劇,於 1920 年、第一次世界大戰結束後的威瑪時期德國上映;普遍被視為德國表現主義電影(German Expressionist cinema)開創性的奠基之作。
  • 劇情概述:年輕男子法蘭西斯向一名陌生人講述一段令人不安的往事:一位陰森的市集雜耍藝人卡里加利博士,展示一名能夠預言未來的夢遊者(夢遊症患者)凱撒——但真相是,凱撒實則是被卡里加利博士操控、在恍惚狀態下犯下多起謀殺案的工具。劇情最終堆疊出一連串驚人轉折,以一段如今已極負盛名的「框架敘事」(frame narrative)結局作結,徹底重新定義觀眾先前所見的一切。
  • 藝術風格與技法:本片最鮮明的特色,是其徹底脫離寫實主義、以手繪佈景構成的視覺風格——扭曲尖銳的建築、傾斜歪扭的街道與窗戶,以及以手繪陰影與斜線取代真實打光效果——由表現主義舞台設計師以低成本製作完成,而非透過實景拍攝或寫實攝影棚佈景,直接將角色內心或整個社會的心理扭曲與不安,外化呈現於電影的物理場景之中。
  • 文化與歷史意義:普遍被公認為德國表現主義電影的起源之作,也是恐怖片與心理驚悚片類型早期的重要里程碑;其視覺手法——以扭曲、人工化的環境來傳達角色或社會內在的騷動不安,而非呈現寫實空間——被視為後續全球恐怖片、film noir(黑色電影)與心理驚悚片類型的重要先例與基石。
  • 影響與傳承:其視覺語彙直接影響了 1930 年代環球影業的恐怖片系列、美國黑色電影對光影與角度的美學運用,以及其後無數風格化的恐怖與驚悚作品;本片的驚奇結局框架敘事手法,也經常被引用為電影史上「不可靠敘事者」結構早期且深具影響力的範例。
  • 今日可觀賞管道:現今可透過家用影碟與串流平台廣泛觀賞(包括 Kino Lorber/近似 Criterion 規格的修復版本),作為公共領域的默片經典,經常在影展中搭配現場配樂放映,也是電影學校講授德國表現主義與早期恐怖片課程的必修經典之一。

8)看片方法——插入鏡頭/切入關鍵細節(第 50 課)

與先前 49 堂課截然不同的全新分析角度:插入鏡頭(insert shot)是指在一場較為寬廣的場景中,刻意切入某個特定物件或細節的特寫鏡頭——一只滴答作響的時鐘、一個滴水的水龍頭、一隻正伸向武器的手——藉此引導觀眾注意到某個角色本身可能注意到、也可能沒注意到的事物。這與第 8 課的「景深與對焦」(關注的是單一連續鏡頭內畫面何者維持清晰)以及第 13 課的「鏡頭尺寸」(僅泛泛命名鏡頭大小種類)皆不相同——插入鏡頭specifically是刻意中斷一場戲原本的正常拍攝涵蓋範圍,將某個有意義的細節獨立成一顆鏡頭。判斷重點包括:這個鏡頭要求觀眾注意到什麼,是單靠遠景鏡頭可能會讓觀眾錯過的;這個細節是在堆疊懸念(例如倒數計時)、埋下後續伏筆,還是提供角色本身並不知情的資訊;以及插入鏡頭是導演反覆使用的敘事節奏手法,還是僅僅出現一次的獨立點綴。

實例演練:《狂沙十萬里》(Once Upon a Time in the West,1968,導演:塞吉歐·李昂尼 Sergio Leone)——片頭那段著名的無對白開場戲,三名槍手在一座偏遠的火車站近乎完全靜默地等待火車到來。李昂尼幾乎完全透過一連串具有節奏感的插入鏡頭,堆疊出整場戲的張力——滴水的蓄水塔水滴落在帽緣上、一隻蒼蠅嗡嗡飛繞並停在男子臉上、吱嘎作響的風車葉片、電報機敲出訊息的按鍵特寫——每個鏡頭停留的時間,都遠遠超過其字面資訊量所需的長度,迫使觀眾與片中角色一同置身於這段令人備感煎熬的等待之中。整整將近十分鐘的片長裡,幾乎沒有任何對白、也幾乎沒有傳統意義上的劇情推進;懸念完全是靠這些看似瑣碎、卻不斷疊加份量的插入鏡頭堆砌而成,使這場戲成為影史上最常被引用、用以示範「插入鏡頭能從靜止之中憑空製造出恐懼與懸念」這項技法威力的經典範例之一。
