3) Spanish Lesson — Day 61 (10 min, Stressed Possessive Pronouns/Adjectives: mío/tuyo/suyo)

Steps outside the tense/mood/pronoun/se-family/obligation/knowledge tracks (Days 46-60) to return to and complete the possessives topic first opened on Day 10: alongside the short, unstressed possessive adjectives already taught (mi/tu/su, always placed before the noun), Spanish has a second, "stressed" set — mío/tuyo/suyo/nuestro/vuestro/suyo (all four gender/number forms: mío/mía/míos/mías, etc.) — which can either follow the noun for emphasis/contrast (un amigo mío, "a friend of mine," implying "one among several," distinct from mi amigo's neutral "my friend") or stand entirely alone as a possessive pronoun replacing the noun outright, typically with a definite article (el mío, la mía, "mine"). The stressed forms agree in gender and number with the thing owned, exactly like Day 10's short forms — never with the owner — and after ser, the article is normally dropped (Este libro es mío, not el mío) unless drawing a specific contrast between options.

  • Este libro es mío, no tuyo. — This book is mine, not yours.
  • Mi coche está en el taller; ¿puedo usar el tuyo? — My car is in the shop; can I use yours?
  • Un amigo mío me contó que la suya es más grande que la nuestra. — A friend of mine told me that his/hers is bigger than ours.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 61: 中孚 (Zhōng Fú) — "Inner Truth" (Wind over Lake) (風澤中孚 — Xùn 巽/Wind above, Duì 兌/Lake below). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the line from the previous hexagram directly: "節而信之,故受之以中孚。" ("Once there is limitation [Jié, Hexagram 60], there is trust in it; therefore Jié is followed by Zhōng Fú.") Where Hexagram 60 established measured boundaries and proper restraint, Hexagram 61 addresses what those boundaries make possible once genuinely internalized: a limitation freely accepted and lived by, rather than merely imposed, becomes the ground for authentic mutual trust — "inner truth," the name itself pointing to sincerity that originates from the center (the hexagram's own structure, with two broken/yielding lines nestled at its very center between solid lines above and below, is traditionally read as a visual image of an empty, receptive heart at the middle of true confidence). The Judgment reads: "中孚,豚魚吉,利涉大川,利貞。" ("Inner Truth. [Trust that reaches] pigs and fish is auspicious; it is favorable to cross the great river; it is favorable to be steadfast and correct.") Pigs and fish are traditionally read as the most obtuse, difficult-to-move creatures imaginable — if one's sincerity is genuine enough to move even them, it can move anyone; crossing "the great river" (a recurring Yijing metaphor for a great, risky undertaking) becomes safe precisely because inner truth, not external force, is what carries a person through danger. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "中孚,柔在內而剛得中,說而巽,孚乃化邦也。豚魚吉,信及豚魚也。利涉大川,乘木舟虛也。中孚以利貞,乃應乎天也。" ("In Zhōng Fú, the yielding lines are within while the firm lines attain the center; there is joy [the lower trigram, Duì] combined with gentle penetration [the upper trigram, Xùn] — through this, sincerity transforms an entire state. 'Auspicious even for pigs and fish' means that trust extends even to pigs and fish. 'Favorable to cross the great river' means riding a wooden boat that is empty [light, and so does not sink]. Inner Truth being favorable through steadfastness corresponds to the way of Heaven itself.") The image of an empty wooden boat crossing safely is the hexagram's central teaching in miniature: a boat loaded down with cargo (equivalent to a heart cluttered with self-interest, calculation, or hidden agenda) risks sinking, while an empty boat — a mind and heart genuinely open, holding nothing back and concealing nothing — rides safely over danger precisely because it carries no excess weight to capsize it. The Image commentary reads: "澤上有風,中孚;君子以議獄緩死。" ("Wind over the lake: this is the image of Zhōng Fú. Thus the superior person deliberates carefully over legal cases and delays [the carrying-out of] death sentences.") Wind moving invisibly over water, stirring its entire surface without any single visible point of contact, is read as an image of how genuine inner sincerity likewise moves and influences others invisibly, without coercion or visible mechanism — and because judging another's guilt with total certainty is inherently difficult, the superior person facing a capital case extends the same quality of patient, searching sincerity to the accused that Zhōng Fú as a whole describes, favoring careful deliberation and delay over hasty finality. Read against Hexagram 60, the pair traces a complete arc: measured limitation, properly established (Hexagram 60), becomes — once genuinely internalized rather than merely imposed from outside — the wellspring of the deep, contagious sincerity that can move even the most unlikely hearts and carry a person safely through any great undertaking (Hexagram 61).

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Morocco (摩洛哥)

  • Location: Northwest Africa, occupying the northwestern corner of the Maghreb, bordered by Algeria to the east/southeast, the disputed territory of Western Sahara to the south (which Morocco administers as its own southern provinces, a claim not internationally universally recognized), the Atlantic Ocean to the west, and the Mediterranean Sea to the north, separated from Spain by only the roughly 13km-wide Strait of Gibraltar; also unusually hosts two Spanish exclave cities, Ceuta and Melilla, on its own Mediterranean coast.
  • Territory: About 446,550 km² excluding Western Sahara (or roughly 710,850 km² including the disputed territory it administers) — roughly 12 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), and somewhat larger than Spain (~505,990 km²) if Western Sahara is included, or slightly smaller than Spain if not.
  • Population: Approximately 38 million as of 2026, concentrated heavily along the Atlantic coastal plain and in cities like Casablanca (the largest, a major economic hub) and the capital Rabat — roughly 1.6 times Taiwan's population.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Sunni Muslim (roughly 99%), predominantly of the Maliki school; the King holds the religious title "Commander of the Faithful" (Amir al-Mu'minin), giving the monarchy a religious as well as political dimension; small historic Jewish and Christian minorities remain, and Morocco retains one of the oldest continuous Jewish communities in the Arab world, though much diminished from its mid-20th-century size through emigration.
  • Economy: Nominal GDP of roughly $160 billion (2026 estimate); a diversified economy built on phosphate mining and exports (Morocco holds the world's largest known phosphate reserves, critical to global fertilizer supply), a fast-growing automotive manufacturing sector (now a major European-market export hub, with plants for Renault and Stellantis), textiles, agriculture, and a large tourism sector centered on cities like Marrakesh, Fez, and Chefchaouen; remittances from a large diaspora in France and elsewhere in Europe are also economically significant.
  • History: Home to some of the earliest known Homo sapiens fossil evidence found anywhere (Jebel Irhoud, roughly 300,000 years old); historically shaped by successive Berber and Arab dynasties (Idrisid, Almoravid, Almohad, Marinid) from the 8th century onward, with the Alaouite dynasty — the current ruling family — established in the mid-17th century, making it one of the world's longest-reigning royal houses. Morocco became a French protectorate in 1912 (with a separate Spanish protectorate zone in the north) and regained full independence in 1956 under King Mohammed V. The "Green March" of 1975 saw Morocco assert control over Western Sahara following Spain's withdrawal, a territorial dispute with the Polisario Front independence movement that remains unresolved to this day.
  • Current leader: King Mohammed VI, head of state since 1999 and holder of extensive executive authority under the constitution; Prime Minister Aziz Akhannouch has headed the elected government since 2021, though the monarchy retains the dominant role in foreign policy, religious affairs, and the military.
  • Language: Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic and the distinct Moroccan Arabic dialect, Darija) and Tamazight (Berber) are both official languages, the latter elevated to official status in the 2011 constitution; French remains widely used in business, government, and education as a legacy of the protectorate era, and Spanish retains a regional presence in the north.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (Mohammed VI), worldometers.info

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — The Ice Bucket Challenge

  • Name/origin: The Ice Bucket Challenge (originally circulating in mid-2014 among golfers and athletes as a generic charity/dare stunt with no fixed cause attached) was adopted and reshaped in late July 2014 by the ALS Association and patient advocates — notably golfer Chris Kennedy and, pivotally, former Boston College baseball captain Pete Frates, who was living with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS, also known as Lou Gehrig's disease) — into a dedicated fundraising and awareness campaign for ALS research.
  • How it became popular: The format was simple and inherently shareable: a person films themselves being doused with a bucket of ice water, then challenges specific named friends (via social media) to do the same within 24 hours or donate to ALS research instead — a built-in nomination chain that functioned like a chain letter with video proof. Celebrities, athletes, and eventually political and business leaders (including Bill Gates, Mark Zuckerberg, and Oprah Winfrey) participated and nominated others on camera, and the challenge spread virally across Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube through August 2014.
  • Meaning/usage: Functioned simultaneously as a feel-good social dare, a public declaration of participation in a cause, and a direct fundraising mechanism; participation and donation were framed as complementary rather than substitutes (many participants did both), and the visible, personalized nomination structure was widely credited as the key innovation distinguishing it from earlier, less successful "donate to X" awareness campaigns.
  • Variations/evolution: Countless parody and localized versions followed (using other liquids, cold-weather variants, and adaptations for other causes/charities entirely disconnected from ALS); the challenge is frequently cited in marketing and nonprofit-fundraising literature as a landmark case study in viral, peer-to-peer digital fundraising design.
  • Current status: The 2014 wave raised over $115 million for the ALS Association in about eight weeks (compared to roughly $2.8 million in the same period the prior year) and is widely credited with funding research that contributed to the 2016 discovery of a new ALS-linked gene (NEK1); the ALS Association has since revived the challenge periodically (including an official "Every August Until There's A Cure" campaign), though none of the later revivals matched the original 2014 wave's viral scale — it remains one of the most frequently cited examples of a single meme format directly translating social virality into large-scale real-world charitable funding.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, als.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Lawrence of Arabia (1962, dir. David Lean)

  • Name/key figures: Lawrence of Arabia, a 1962 British epic film directed by David Lean, produced by Sam Spiegel, with a screenplay by Robert Bolt and Michael Wilson, cinematography by Freddie Young, and a score by Maurice Jarre; Peter O'Toole stars as T.E. Lawrence, with Alec Guinness, Anthony Quinn, Omar Sharif (in his breakout international role), Jack Hawkins, and Claude Rains in supporting roles.
  • Era/background: Based loosely on the real life of British Army officer T.E. Lawrence and his own memoir Seven Pillars of Wisdom, dramatizing his role advising and fighting alongside Arab tribal forces during the Arab Revolt against the Ottoman Empire in the First World War (1916-1918); shot largely on real desert locations in Jordan, Morocco, and Spain rather than studio sets, at what was then one of the largest production budgets in film history.
  • Plot overview: Follows Lawrence from an eccentric, unconventional British Army lieutenant assigned to liaise with Arab rebel forces, through his growing identification with and leadership of Bedouin tribesmen (including the daring capture of the port of Aqaba via a surprise overland attack from the desert), to his increasing psychological unraveling under the strain of violence, divided loyalty between Britain and the Arab cause, and his own uncertain, myth-making self-image, ending with the fractious postwar Arab National Council in Damascus and Lawrence's own disillusioned return home.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Renowned for its extreme use of the 70mm widescreen frame and desert landscape as a nearly abstract, painterly canvas (epitomized by the celebrated multi-minute long-shot introducing Sharif's character as a distant, wavering mirage-speck that gradually resolves into a rider approaching across the desert); makes minimal use of dialogue in favor of pure landscape, scale, and composition to carry meaning, and is built on Lean's characteristic patient, deliberate pacing and epic sense of physical space.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential films ever made and a high-water mark of the mid-century "epic" genre; won seven Academy Awards including Best Picture and Best Director, and has been credited (alongside subsequent restoration efforts) with helping preserve serious critical appreciation of large-format widescreen cinematography as an art form in its own right rather than mere spectacle.
  • Influence and legacy: Cited as a direct influence by numerous major directors, including Steven Spielberg (who has called it formative to his own visual education) and George Lucas, whose Star Wars borrows staging and desert-vista compositional techniques traceable to this film; its extended, patient long-shot approach to landscape and scale remains a standard reference point in film-school cinematography instruction.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Restored in the 1980s under the supervision of Lean himself (recovering cut theatrical footage to something close to his original intended length) and again in 4K in the 2010s; widely available on physical media and major streaming/rental platforms, and still regularly programmed as a theatrical 70mm revival screening at major film festivals and specialty cinemas.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, bfi.org.uk

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Acousmatic Sound / Off-Screen Sound Source (Lesson 47)

Acousmatic sound (a term formalized by film-sound theorist Michel Chion, building on earlier work by Pierre Schaeffer) is any sound heard by the audience whose visual source is deliberately withheld from the frame — a sound whose cause the viewer must infer, imagine, or dread, rather than see confirmed on screen. This is distinct from Day 4's broader Sound Design & Score lesson, which addressed diegetic/non-diegetic sound and score-as-emotional-cue generally without isolating this specific case of a hidden sound source, and from Day 23's Sound Bridge (J-Cut & L-Cut), which concerns audio overlapping a picture cut across two already-visible scenes rather than a sound whose originating source is never shown at all. Withholding the visual source does specific narrative work an on-screen reveal cannot: it forces the audience's imagination to supply (and usually exaggerate) the unseen cause, sustains suspense precisely because the threat's exact position, scale, or nature remains uncertain, and — the key test to apply — the moment the sound's source is finally revealed on screen (what Chion calls "de-acousmatization"), the sound normally loses much of its power, since a visible, bounded source is inherently less frightening than an unbounded, unlocated one the mind is free to imagine as worse than any prop or effect could show.

Worked example: Jurassic Park (1993, dir. Steven Spielberg) — the Tyrannosaurus rex paddock-breakout sequence: before the animal is ever shown on screen, its approach is signaled entirely acousmatically, first through a distant, rhythmic low-frequency thump felt more than heard, then through the now-iconic image of concentric ripples shuddering across the surface of a glass of water on the car's dashboard in time with each unseen footfall — sound (and its physical vibration effect) standing in completely for a visual the film deliberately withholds for as long as possible. When the T. rex is finally revealed moments later, the sequence's terror shifts register from dread of the unknown to visceral, embodied action-spectacle — precisely the de-acousmatization effect the lesson describes, and a big part of why Spielberg (and editor Michael Kahn) held the reveal back for so long in the first place.

3)西班牙文課程——第 61 天(10 分鐘,重讀所有格代名詞/形容詞:mío/tuyo/suyo)

今天跳出第 46-60 天的時態/語氣/代名詞/se 家族/義務/知曉動詞脈絡,回頭補完第 10 天首次開啟、但尚未完整教過的所有格主題:除了已教過的簡短、非重讀所有格形容詞(mi/tu/su,固定放在名詞之前)之外,西班牙語還有第二組「重讀」所有格形式——mío/tuyo/suyo/nuestro/vuestro/suyo(各自都有陰陽性、單複數四種變化:mío/mía/míos/mías 等)——這組形式可以放在名詞之後,用來強調或做出對比(un amigo mío,「我的一位朋友」,隱含「眾多朋友中的其中一位」,不同於 mi amigo 中性、平鋪直敘的「我朋友」),也可以完全獨立使用,直接取代名詞本身,成為所有格代名詞,通常搭配定冠詞(el mío、la mía,「我的那個」)。這組重讀形式和第 10 天教過的簡短形式一樣,都是依「被擁有的東西」的陰陽性/單複數變化,而非依「擁有者」本身變化;在 ser 動詞之後,通常會省略冠詞(Este libro es mío,而非 el mío),除非要特別強調「是這一個、不是另一個」的對比語氣。

  • Este libro es mío, no tuyo. —— 這本書是我的,不是你的。
  • Mi coche está en el taller; ¿puedo usar el tuyo? —— 我的車在修車廠;我可以用你的(車)嗎?
  • Un amigo mío me contó que la suya es más grande que la nuestra. —— 我的一位朋友跟我說,他/她的(那個)比我們的還大。

4)易經卦象

第 61 卦:中孚 (Zhōng Fú) ——「中孚」(風澤中孚、內心的真誠)(風澤中孚——上巽下兌:上卦為巽〔風〕,下卦為兌〔澤〕。)《序卦傳》承接前一卦而來:「節而信之,故受之以中孚。」(有了節制,人才會對其產生信任,因此節卦〔第 60 卦〕之後,接的是中孚卦。)第 60 卦建立了有分寸的界限與適當的節制;第 61 卦接著處理:當這些界限被真正內化、發自內心接受(而非僅是外在強加)之後,會帶來什麼——一種發自內心、而非勉強遵守的節制,便成為真正互信的根基,也就是「中孚」——這個卦名本身,便指向一種源自「中心」的真誠(此卦的卦象結構,是兩條陰爻〔柔爻〕深藏於卦體最中央、上下皆被陽爻〔剛爻〕環繞,傳統上被解讀為「內心空靈、虛懷若谷」正是真正信任得以立足的中心意象)。卦辭寫道:「中孚,豚魚吉,利涉大川,利貞。」(中孚卦。〔真誠信實之心,若能感化〕豬與魚一類遲鈍的動物,也是吉祥的;適宜渡過大河;適宜堅守正道。)豬與魚,傳統上被視為最遲鈍、最難以被感動或說服的生物——如果一個人的真誠,足以感動連豬、魚都能被打動,那便足以感動任何人;「渡過大河」(易經中反覆出現、象徵重大且充滿風險之行動的譬喻),之所以能夠安然渡過,正是因為靠的是發自內心的真誠,而非外在的強力,才能帶領人穿越危險。《彖傳》進一步闡述:「中孚,柔在內而剛得中,說而巽,孚乃化邦也。豚魚吉,信及豚魚也。利涉大川,乘木舟虛也。中孚以利貞,乃應乎天也。」(中孚卦,柔順的爻居於卦體之內,而剛健的爻居於中位;〔下卦的〕喜悅,配合〔上卦的〕柔順滲透——透過這樣的結構,真誠信實得以感化整個邦國。「連豬魚都覺得吉祥」,意思是真誠信實之心,連豬與魚都能感應觸及。「適宜渡過大河」,意思就像乘坐一艘中空、輕盈的木舟〔因此不會沉沒〕。中孚之所以適宜堅守正道,是因為這正呼應了上天運行的法則。)這艘中空木舟得以安然渡河的意象,正是整卦教誨的縮影:一艘裝滿貨物的船(對應一顆充滿私心、算計或隱藏心機的心),反而容易因負重而沉沒;而一艘中空的木舟——一顆真正敞開、毫無保留、也毫無隱瞞的心——正因為不承載多餘的重量,才能安然渡過危險,不致翻覆。《象傳》寫道:「澤上有風,中孚;君子以議獄緩死。」(澤水之上有風吹拂,這就是中孚卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應當審慎議斷刑獄案件,並延緩死刑的執行。)風在水面上無形吹拂,擾動整片水面,卻找不到任何單一、具體的接觸點——這正是「真誠信實之心,同樣也能在無形中感化、影響他人,不假強制、也不見任何外顯機制」的意象;而正因為要百分之百確定他人有罪,本質上極為困難,君子面對死刑案件時,便應當以中孚卦所描述的那種耐心、深入探究的真誠態度,同等地對待被告,寧可審慎議斷、延緩定案,也不倉促了結。若與第 60 卦對照,兩卦恰好勾勒出一段完整的歷程:有分寸的節制若能被妥善建立(第 60 卦),一旦真正被內化、而非僅是外在強加,便會成為深刻、足以感染他人的真誠信實之心的源頭——這種真誠,連最遲鈍的心也能打動,也能帶領一個人安然渡過任何重大的考驗(第 61 卦)。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——摩洛哥(Morocco)

  • 位置:位於西北非洲,占據馬格里布地區的西北角,東側/東南側與阿爾及利亞接壤,南側與具爭議的西撒哈拉領土相鄰(摩洛哥將其作為自身南部各省管轄,但此主張並未獲得國際社會普遍承認),西側濱臨大西洋,北側濱臨地中海,與西班牙之間僅隔著寬約 13 公里的直布羅陀海峽;摩洛哥境內還罕見地存在兩座西班牙的海外飛地城市——休達(Ceuta)與梅利利亞(Melilla),位於摩洛哥自身的地中海沿岸。
  • 領土:不含西撒哈拉,面積約 446,550 平方公里(若計入其實際管轄的西撒哈拉爭議領土,則約達 710,850 平方公里)——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 12 倍;若計入西撒哈拉,面積略大於西班牙(約 505,990 平方公里),若不計入則略小於西班牙。
  • 人口:截至 2026 年,人口約 3,800 萬人,主要集中於大西洋沿岸平原地帶,以及卡薩布蘭卡(最大城市,重要經濟樞紐)與首都拉巴特等城市——約為台灣人口的 1.6 倍。
  • 宗教:絕大多數人口信仰遜尼派伊斯蘭教(約占 99%),以馬立克學派(Maliki)為主;摩洛哥國王擁有「信士的統帥」(Amir al-Mu'minin)這項宗教頭銜,使得摩洛哥王室兼具政治與宗教雙重地位;境內仍留有規模較小、歷史悠久的猶太教與基督教社群,摩洛哥更保有阿拉伯世界歷史最悠久、延續至今的猶太社群之一,惟規模已因移民潮而較 20 世紀中葉大幅縮減。
  • 經濟:名目 GDP 約 1,600 億美元(2026 年預估值);屬多元化經濟體,建立在磷酸鹽開採與出口(摩洛哥擁有全球已知最大的磷酸鹽儲量,對全球肥料供應至關重要)、快速成長的汽車製造業(現已成為面向歐洲市場的重要出口樞紐,雷諾與 Stellantis 皆在當地設廠)、紡織業、農業,以及以馬拉喀什、費茲與雪夫沙萬等城市為核心的龐大觀光產業之上;來自旅居法國及歐洲其他地區龐大僑民的僑匯,對經濟亦具重要意義。
  • 歷史:摩洛哥境內發現了目前已知全球最早的現代智人化石證據之一(傑貝爾伊爾胡德〔Jebel Irhoud〕遺址,約 30 萬年前);自西元 8 世紀起,歷史上先後由多個柏柏爾人與阿拉伯人王朝統治(伊德里斯王朝、穆拉比特王朝、穆瓦希德王朝、馬林王朝),現今統治王室——阿拉維王朝(Alaouite dynasty)——則建立於 17 世紀中葉,使其成為全球在位時間最悠久的王室之一。摩洛哥於 1912 年淪為法國保護國(北部另設有獨立的西班牙保護區),並於 1956 年在穆罕默德五世國王領導下,重獲完整獨立。1975 年的「綠色進軍」(Green March)行動,則是摩洛哥在西班牙撤出西撒哈拉後,趁勢主張對該地區的控制權,由此引發與波利薩里奧陣線(獨立運動組織)之間的領土爭端,至今仍未解決。
  • 現任領導人:國王穆罕默德六世,自 1999 年起擔任國家元首,依憲法擁有廣泛的行政權力;總理阿齊茲·阿汗努什(Aziz Akhannouch)自 2021 年起領導民選政府,惟王室在外交政策、宗教事務與軍事領域,仍居主導地位。
  • 語言:阿拉伯語(現代標準阿拉伯語,以及當地特有的摩洛哥阿拉伯語方言「達里雅」〔Darija〕)與塔馬齊格特語(柏柏爾語)皆為官方語言,後者於 2011 年憲法修訂後正式取得官方地位;法語因保護國時期的歷史遺緒,至今仍廣泛使用於商業、政府與教育領域;西班牙語則在北部地區仍保有一定的區域使用度。

6)網路迷因介紹——冰桶挑戰(Ice Bucket Challenge)

  • 名稱/起源:冰桶挑戰最初於 2014 年年中,在高爾夫球員與運動員之間流傳,原本只是一種沒有固定訴求對象、單純的慈善/挑戰性質小活動;直到 2014 年 7 月底,才被美國肌萎縮性側索硬化症協會(ALS Association)與病友倡議人士——特別是高爾夫球員克里斯·甘迺迪(Chris Kennedy),以及最關鍵的推手、時任波士頓學院棒球隊隊長、當時正與肌萎縮性側索硬化症(ALS,又稱漸凍人症、路·賈里格症)搏鬥的皮特·弗雷茲(Pete Frates)——採用並改造,轉型為一項專門為 ALS 研究募款、提升病症意識的公益活動。
  • 如何走紅:活動形式簡單且天生具備高度傳播性:參與者自拍被一整桶冰水淋濕的過程,接著在社群媒體上指名挑戰特定朋友,要求對方在 24 小時內比照辦理,否則就得改為捐款支持 ALS 研究——這種內建的「指名接力」機制,運作方式類似附有影片證明的連鎖信。從名人、運動員,到後來的政治與商界領袖(包括比爾·蓋茲、馬克·祖克柏與歐普拉·溫芙蕾),都紛紛入鏡參與並指名接力,活動於 2014 年 8 月間,透過 Facebook、Twitter 與 YouTube 迅速病毒式擴散。
  • 意義/使用情境:這項活動同時具備多重功能——既是一種令人心情愉悅的社交挑戰、一種公開表態參與公益的宣示行為,也是一種直接的募款機制;參與挑戰與實際捐款被視為互補、而非互相取代的關係(許多參與者兩者兼行),而這種公開、個人化的指名接力結構,普遍被認為是它相較於先前效果較差的「請捐款給某某議題」宣導活動,關鍵的創新之處。
  • 變體/演化:此後衍生出無數惡搞版本與在地化改編(改用其他液體、寒冷氣候版本,以及完全與 ALS 無關、改為支持其他議題/慈善機構的改編版本);這項挑戰也經常被行銷與非營利募款相關文獻,引用為病毒式、點對點數位募款設計的經典指標案例。
  • 目前狀態:2014 年那波熱潮,在約八週內為 ALS Association 募得超過 1.15 億美元(相較於前一年同期僅約 280 萬美元),普遍被認為其募得資金,對 2016 年發現一項與 ALS 相關的新基因(NEK1)研究,做出了實質貢獻;ALS Association 此後也曾定期重啟這項挑戰(包括官方發起的「每年八月直到找到解方為止」〔Every August Until There's A Cure〕活動),但後續幾波重啟,皆未能重現 2014 年原始那波的病毒式擴散規模——它至今仍是「單一迷因形式,直接將社群病毒式傳播,轉化為大規模真實世界慈善募款成果」最常被引用的經典案例之一。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、als.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《阿拉伯的勞倫斯》(Lawrence of Arabia,1962,導演:大衛·連)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《阿拉伯的勞倫斯》是一部 1962 年英國史詩電影,由大衛·連(David Lean)執導,山姆·史匹格(Sam Spiegel)製片,劇本由羅伯特·鮑特(Robert Bolt)與麥可·威爾森(Michael Wilson)共同撰寫,攝影指導為佛烈迪·楊(Freddie Young),配樂由莫里斯·賈爾(Maurice Jarre)創作;彼得·奧圖(Peter O'Toole)主演 T.E. 勞倫斯,亞歷·堅尼斯(Alec Guinness)、安東尼·昆(Anthony Quinn)、奧瑪·雪瑞夫(Omar Sharif,憑本片一舉躍上國際影壇)、傑克·霍金斯(Jack Hawkins)與克勞德·雷恩斯(Claude Rains)擔任要角。
  • 時代/背景:本片鬆散取材自英國陸軍軍官 T.E. 勞倫斯的真實生平,以及他本人所著回憶錄《智慧七柱》(Seven Pillars of Wisdom),戲劇化呈現他在第一次世界大戰期間(1916-1918 年),協助並率領阿拉伯部族軍隊,對抗鄂圖曼帝國統治的「阿拉伯大起義」中所扮演的角色;本片絕大部分場景皆在約旦、摩洛哥與西班牙的真實沙漠實地拍攝,而非搭建攝影棚布景,製作預算在當時堪稱電影史上數一數二龐大。
  • 劇情概述:劇情描繪勞倫斯從一名性格古怪、不拘一格,奉命與阿拉伯反抗軍聯繫的英國陸軍中尉,逐漸與貝都因部族戰士產生認同、進而領導他們(包括那場經典的、從沙漠內陸出其不意突襲、奪下亞喀巴港的大膽行動),到他在暴力、對英國與阿拉伯理念之間分裂的忠誠拉扯,以及自身不確定、近乎自我造神的形象壓力下,心理逐漸崩解的過程,最終以戰後大馬士革紛亂不休的阿拉伯民族議會,以及勞倫斯本人心灰意冷返鄉作結。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:本片以極致運用 70 毫米超寬銀幕畫面、將沙漠景觀轉化為近乎抽象、如畫作一般的畫布而聞名(其中最為人稱道的,是那個長達數分鐘、以長鏡頭呈現奧瑪·雪瑞夫飾演角色首次登場的畫面——遠方一個隨熱浪扭曲、彷彿海市蜃樓般的微小光點,逐漸清晰化為一名騎士穿越沙漠緩緩接近);全片大量減少對白的使用,轉而以純粹的地景、尺度與構圖來承載意義,並建立在大衛·連一貫耐心、從容不迫的敘事節奏,以及對實體空間宏大尺度的掌握之上。
  • 文化/歷史意義:普遍被公認為史上最偉大、影響力最深遠的電影之一,也是中世紀「史詩電影」類型的高峰之作;榮獲七項奧斯卡金像獎,包括最佳影片與最佳導演,並(連同後續多次修復工程)被認為對於保存「大畫幅寬銀幕攝影本身作為一門獨立藝術形式、而非單純視覺奇觀」的嚴肅評論傳統,發揮了重要貢獻。
  • 影響與傳承:被多位重量級導演直接引為創作養分,包括史蒂芬·史匹柏(他曾表示本片是形塑自身視覺教育的重要作品)與喬治·盧卡斯——《星際大戰》中,可明確追溯至本片的場面調度與沙漠景觀構圖手法,即為一例;本片對地景與尺度耐心、綿長的長鏡頭處理方式,至今仍是電影學院攝影課程教學的標準參考範例。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:本片曾於 1980 年代由大衛·連本人親自監督修復(找回遭剪去的院線畫面,盡量還原至接近他本人原始設想的完整片長),並於 2010 年代再次進行 4K 修復;目前廣泛可於實體媒體與各大串流/租片平台觀賞,並仍經常在各大影展與特色影院,以 70 毫米院線重映形式定期放映。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、bfi.org.uk

8)看片方法——聲源不現形/離軸音(Acousmatic Sound,第 47 課)

「離軸音」(acousmatic sound,由電影聲音理論家米歇爾·希翁〔Michel Chion〕正式定名,承襲皮耶·薛費爾〔Pierre Schaeffer〕的早期研究)指的是任何觀眾聽得到、但畫面上刻意不揭露其視覺來源的聲音——觀眾必須自行推測、想像,甚至心生恐懼,去猜測這個聲音的成因,而非直接在銀幕上獲得確認。這不同於第 4 天教過、範圍較廣的「聲音設計與配樂」課程(當時處理的是「音源音/畫外音」與「配樂作為情緒提示」的一般性原則,並未特別聚焦於「聲源刻意隱藏不現形」這種特定情境),也不同於第 23 天教過的「聲橋(J-cut 與 L-cut)」(那堂課處理的是聲音跨越剪接點、與兩個「已經在畫面上出現過」的場景重疊,而非聲音的源頭自始至終從未在畫面上現身)。刻意隱藏視覺來源,能達成畫面直接揭露永遠做不到的特定敘事效果:它逼迫觀眾自行用想像力補全(而且通常會誇大)這個看不見的成因,也正因為威脅的確切位置、規模或本質仍不明朗,才得以持續維持懸疑張力——而判斷這堂課的關鍵測試方法是:一旦聲音的來源終於在畫面上揭曉(希翁稱之為「去離軸化」〔de-acousmatization〕),這個聲音通常會立刻失去大部分的威力,因為一個看得見、有明確邊界的聲源,本質上遠不如一個「無邊無界、無法定位」、任憑心智自行想像成比任何道具或特效都更可怕的聲源那樣令人恐懼。

實例演練:《侏羅紀公園》(Jurassic Park,1993,導演:史蒂芬·史匹柏)——暴龍衝出圍欄的那場戲:在暴龍第一次出現在畫面上之前,牠的接近過程完全透過離軸音來傳達,先是一陣與其說「聽見」不如說「感受到」的遙遠、規律低頻悶響,接著是如今已成經典畫面的一幕——汽車儀表板上一杯水的表面,隨著每一次看不見的腳步聲,泛起一圈圈同心圓漣漪——聲音(以及它引發的物理震動效果),完全取代了電影刻意盡可能延後揭露的視覺畫面。當暴龍終於在片刻之後現形,整場戲的恐怖感,便從「對未知的恐懼」轉為「具體、身歷其境的動作奇觀」——這正是本課所描述的「去離軸化」效果,也是史匹柏(與剪輯師麥可·卡恩〔Michael Kahn〕)之所以將這個揭曉時刻,刻意延後這麼久的重要原因之一。
