4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 4: 蒙 (Méng) — "Youthful Folly" (Mountain over Water). A spring bubbling up at the foot of a mountain, not yet channeled into a clear stream — the image of untrained potential and inexperience. The classical judgment holds that it is not the teacher who seeks out the student, but the student who must approach the teacher with sincerity; a question asked once in earnest deserves a clear answer, but repeated, insincere questioning only "muddies the water" and gets none. Favorable for humble learning, patience with beginners, and building good habits early.

4) 易經卦象

第四卦:蒙卦(Méng)——「蒙昧」(上艮下坎)。象徵山下湧出泉水,尚未匯聚成河流的狀態——比喻未經教化的潛力與蒙昧無知。卦辭指出:並非老師該去找學生,而是學生應誠心求教於老師;誠心求問一次就該獲得明確解答,但若一再輕慢、反覆試探地發問,則如攪濁泉水,不會得到答案。此卦有利於謙虛學習、耐心對待初學者,並及早養成良好習慣。
