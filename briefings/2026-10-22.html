3) Spanish Lesson — Day 98: Recordar / Acordarse de / Olvidar / Olvidarse de ("Remembering and Forgetting")

Recordar + object means "to remember" and takes no preposition: Recuerdo tu cara. Acordarse de means the same but is reflexive and needs de: Me acuerdo de tu cara. Both change o→ue (recuerdo, me acuerdo). Memory trick: recordar is not reflexive and has no de; acordarse is reflexive and has de. Recordar also means "to remind": Recuérdame llamar a mamá. Olvidar and olvidarse de both mean "to forget" (Olvidé las llaves = Me olvidé de las llaves), and se me olvidó (the accidental se) says it slipped away without intent, with the forgotten thing as subject: Se me olvidaron las entradas. Reminders use No te olvides de + infinitive.

  • ¿Te acuerdas de mi cumpleaños? — Do you remember my birthday?
  • ¡Claro que me acuerdo! Recuerdo que es el doce de marzo. — Of course I do! I remember it's March 12th.
  • ¿Y ya compraste el regalo? — And have you bought the gift yet?
  • Ay, se me olvidó comprarlo. — Oh, I forgot to buy it.
  • Entonces no te olvides de llamarme mañana. — Then don't forget to call me tomorrow.

Series note (new track): all 64 hexagrams and all 33 Zhuangzi chapters have now been used, so Section 4 has no unrepeated content left under its original rule. As suggested in yesterday's notification, and with no instruction from the user otherwise, this routine starts a new track today: one chapter of the Daodejing (道德經) per day, in order, chapters 1 to 81, never repeated. The user can change this at any time.

Text: 道可道,非常道;名可名,非常名。無名,天地之始;有名,萬物之母。故常無欲,以觀其妙;常有欲,以觀其徼。此兩者,同出而異名,同謂之玄。玄之又玄,眾妙之門。

Plain-English rendering (paraphrase): A Way that can be spoken is not the constant Way; a name that can be named is not the constant name. The nameless is the beginning of heaven and earth; the named is the mother of the ten thousand things. So, always without desire, one sees its subtlety; always with desire, one sees only its outer edge. These two come from the same source but have different names; together they are called the mystery (玄), mystery upon mystery, the gate of all subtleties.

Notes: The book opens by admitting the limits of language: the Way (道) is not an object that words can fix. The chapter sets up two "views" of the same reality, the nameless origin and the named world of things, and two attitudes, being free of desire (seeing the subtle) and being driven by desire (seeing only the boundary or result). Punctuation of some lines is debated (for example, reading 常無/常有 as "constant non-being / constant being" instead of "always without / with desire"), and the Mawangdui and Guodian manuscripts differ in places. It pairs well with Zhuangzi's "words are for catching meaning; once you have the meaning, forget the words". The text and translation are from general knowledge, not verified against an edition today.

Takeaway: Take every definition lightly, including this one: the first lesson of the book is to hold your names loosely.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Latvia

  • Location & territory size: The middle one of the three Baltic states in north-eastern Europe, on the Baltic Sea and the Gulf of Riga, bordering Estonia (north), Lithuania (south), Russia and Belarus (east). Area about 64,600 km², roughly 1.8 times Taiwan (about 36,200 km²), a little smaller than Lithuania and larger than Estonia.
  • Population: About 1.9 million (approximate), fewer than the city of Taipei (about 2.5 million). The capital Riga has roughly 600,000 residents, about a third of the country.
  • Religion: Historically Lutheran in the west and north, Roman Catholic in the eastern Latgale region, Orthodox among many Russian speakers; a large share of people are not actively religious. Exact percentages vary by survey.
  • Economy: EU and NATO member since 2004 and eurozone member since January 1, 2014. A small, open services economy with timber and wood products, food processing, transit through the ports of Riga and Ventspils, and a growing IT and fintech sector. Rejoined the world economy from the Soviet system in the 1990s and has grown quickly since.
  • History: Baltic and Finnic peoples settled the coast early; German crusaders arrived in the 13th century and Bishop Albert founded Riga in 1201, which later joined the Hanseatic League. Latvian lands were then ruled by the Livonian Order, Poland-Lithuania, Sweden and the Russian Empire. Independence was declared on November 18, 1918; after Soviet occupation in 1940 (following the Molotov-Ribbentrop pact), Nazi occupation (1941-44) and a second Soviet occupation, Latvia became a Soviet republic. On August 23, 1989, about two million people in the three Baltic states joined hands in the 600 km "Baltic Way" human chain; Latvia declared the restoration of independence on May 4, 1990, and it was recognized after the failed Moscow coup in August 1991.
  • Current leader: President Edgars Rinkēvičs (largely ceremonial head of state, in office since July 2023). Prime Minister Andris Kulbergs (United List) was confirmed on May 28, 2026 with 66 votes, leading a coalition after former PM Evika Siliņa resigned in a dispute over the response to a drone incursion. In the parliamentary election on October 3, 2026 the United List won about 35.8% and roughly 41 seats of 100 (sources say 41 or 42), a record for any party since independence, short of a majority; the next government's formation was not confirmed in the sources found today.
  • Language: Latvian, a Baltic language of the Indo-European family, closest to Lithuanian (neither Slavic nor Finnic), written in the Latin alphabet with diacritics (ā, č, ē, ģ, ī, ķ, ļ, ņ, š, ū, ž). Latgalian is recognized as a historical variety, and Russian is widely spoken by a large minority.
  • Culture & trivia: Riga's city center has one of Europe's largest collections of Art Nouveau buildings (UNESCO World Heritage). The Baltic song and dance festivals, with roots in 1873, are a UNESCO-recognized tradition; Midsummer (Jāņi / Līgo) is celebrated with wreaths, bonfires and caraway cheese; Rīgas Melnais balzams is the national herbal liqueur; and Latvia won its first-ever men's ice hockey World Championship medal (bronze) in 2023.
  • Sources & notes: Election results from LSM and OSW analysis; government background from Wikipedia: Andris Kulbergs and Bloomberg. Other figures are general knowledge, rounded and not verified today.

7) Film / Performing-Arts Spotlight — Pina Bausch and Tanztheater Wuppertal (dance theatre)

  • Name & key figures: Pina Bausch (1940-2009), German choreographer and director of the Tanztheater Wuppertal, the company that became the model for "dance theatre" (Tanztheater). Born in Solingen, she trained at the Folkwang School in Essen under Kurt Jooss and then in New York.
  • Era / background: In 1973 she took over the dance branch of the Wuppertal opera house and renamed it Tanztheater Wuppertal, which she led until she died on June 30, 2009, only days after a cancer diagnosis. She emerged from German postwar expressionist dance (Ausdruckstanz) and reshaped it.
  • Content overview (key works): Le Sacre du printemps (1975, to Stravinsky, danced on a stage covered with peat); Café Müller (1978, a stage of tangled chairs and tables, with Bausch herself dancing with eyes closed, to Purcell); Kontakthof (1978); Nelken ("Carnations", 1982, a stage covered with thousands of carnations); and many later pieces made after visiting cities (for example Palermo Palermo, 1989).
  • Artistic style & techniques: She replaced linear plots with collages of scenes; asked her dancers questions ("Tell me about a moment of tenderness") and built pieces from their answers; used repetition of gestures until they take on new meanings; mixed everyday movement, speech, song and sheer physical exhaustion; and used heavy, tactile stage designs (earth, water, flowers) as characters. Her recurring subject is love, loneliness, desire and the violence in relationships between men and women.
  • Cultural / historical significance: She made dance a way to ask "not how people move, but what moves them" (a line commonly attributed to her), and broke the border between dance, theatre and everyday behavior.
  • Influence & legacy: Her approach shaped contemporary choreographers and film-makers worldwide; Pedro Almodóvar opened Talk to Her (2002) with a scene from Café Müller, and Wim Wenders made the documentary Pina (2011, in 3D, nominated for the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature). The Pina Bausch Foundation preserves her archive.
  • Current status / where to experience it: The Tanztheater Wuppertal Pina Bausch continues to perform her repertoire in Wuppertal and on world tours (check its official site for current dates and artistic leadership, which I have not verified today). Wenders's Pina is the best entry point on screen.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 84: Film Stock, Grain and Texture (What the Image Surface Feels Like)

What it is: Besides what the camera shows, the physical or digital material that records it gives the image a texture: the grain of celluloid, its colour response, its contrast, versus the clean, sharp, noise-free look of many digital cameras, or the harsh, video-like look of low-resolution digital. Format (16mm, 35mm, 65mm/IMAX, digital) changes how much detail and grain the picture carries. Filmmakers choose a stock or format to make the image feel nostalgic, documentary-like, dreamlike, cold or immersive. (This is different from the earlier aspect-ratio lesson (2026-08-10), which is the shape of the frame, and from colour, which is the palette.)

How to analyze: (1) Ask what the surface looks like: coarse grain or smooth, soft or razor sharp, warm or cool, deep blacks or milky ones. (2) Ask whether the texture is consistent or changes between scenes, and what each change marks (memory versus present, subjective versus objective, one character's world versus another's). (3) Ask what the choice suggests about the film's relation to the past, to realism and to the audience's attention. (4) Compare with the same story imagined in a different look.

Worked example — Oppenheimer (2023, dir. Christopher Nolan): Nolan shot the film on large-format film (IMAX 65mm and 5-perf 65mm). He alternated colour scenes, which are tied to Oppenheimer's subjective point of view, with black-and-white scenes, which follow Lewis Strauss and the more "objective" political record, and the black-and-white sequences were shot on a new black-and-white IMAX film stock that Kodak prepared for the project. Watch the Trinity test sequence: the huge, finely grained film image makes the desert flash feel physical, and the film was made with real practical explosions rather than computer-generated imagery for the blast, according to the filmmakers. Compare with 28 Days Later (2002), shot on consumer digital video, whose rough, flickering texture makes the story feel like raw footage from a collapsing present. (Details from general knowledge; not verified today.)

Try it: In your next film, name the texture in one word within the first five minutes ("grainy", "glassy", "milky"). Then watch for the moment the texture changes and ask what the film wants you to feel about that moment.

3) 西班牙文課程 — 第 98 天:Recordar / Acordarse de / Olvidar / Olvidarse de(記得與忘記)

Recordar + 受詞是「記得」,後面不接介系詞:Recuerdo tu cara。Acordarse de 意思相同,但是反身動詞,且要加 de:Me acuerdo de tu cara。兩者都有 o→ue 變化(recuerdo, me acuerdo)。記訣:recordar 不反身、不加 de;acordarse 要反身、要加 de。Recordar 還有「提醒」的意思:Recuérdame llamar a mamá。Olvidar 與 olvidarse de 都是「忘記」(Olvidé las llaves = Me olvidé de las llaves),而 se me olvidó(無意之 se)表示「不是故意的,事情自己溜走了」,忘掉的東西是主詞:Se me olvidaron las entradas。提醒別人用 No te olvides de + 原形。

4) 道德經 — 第 1 章:道可道 (Dào kě dào) —「可以說出口的道」

系列說明(新的軌道):64 卦與《莊子》33 篇都已經用完,依原本規則第 4 項已沒有未重複的內容。依昨日通知中的建議,且使用者沒有另外指示,本排程今天起開啟新軌道:每天介紹《道德經》一章,依序從第 1 章到第 81 章,不重複。使用者可以隨時更改。

原文:道可道,非常道;名可名,非常名。無名,天地之始;有名,萬物之母。故常無欲,以觀其妙;常有欲,以觀其徼。此兩者,同出而異名,同謂之玄。玄之又玄,眾妙之門。

白話意譯:可以說出口的道,就不是恆常的道;可以叫得出的名,就不是恆常的名。沒有名,是天地的開端;有了名,是萬物的母親。所以常保持無欲,就能看見它的精妙;常帶著欲求,就只看見它的邊界與結果。這兩者出自同一個根源,卻有不同的名稱,合起來叫做「玄」,玄之又玄,是一切精妙的門。

說明:全書一開頭先承認語言的限度:道不是文字能固定住的對象。這一章為同一個實在設下兩種「看法」(無名的本源與有名的萬物世界)和兩種態度(無欲而見其妙,有欲而只見其徼)。部分句讀有爭議(例如把「常無/常有」讀成「恆常的無/恆常的有」,而不是「常無欲/常有欲」),馬王堆帛書與郭店楚簡的字句也有些差異。可與《莊子》「得意而忘言」並讀。原文與翻譯憑一般知識,今天沒有對照版本查證。

小結:對任何定義都保持輕巧,包括這一個:這本書的第一課,就是對自己的名稱不要抓得太緊。

5) 國家 / 地區 / 勢力介紹 — 拉脫維亞(Latvia)

  • 位置與國土面積:東北歐波羅的海三小國的中間一個,瀕臨波羅的海與里加灣,北接愛沙尼亞,南接立陶宛,東接俄羅斯與白俄羅斯。面積約 64,600 平方公里,約為台灣(約 36,200 平方公里)的 1.8 倍,比立陶宛略小,比愛沙尼亞大。
  • 人口:約 190 萬(概數),比台北市(約 250 萬)還少。首都里加約 60 萬人,約占全國三分之一。
  • 宗教:歷史上西部與北部以路德宗為主,東部拉特加萊(Latgale)地區以天主教為主,許多說俄語的居民信東正教;相當比例的人不積極參與宗教活動。確切比例依調查而異。
  • 經濟:2004 年加入歐盟與北約,2014 年 1 月 1 日加入歐元區。是小而開放的服務業經濟,產業有木材與木製品、食品加工、經由里加與文茨皮爾斯(Ventspils)港口的轉運,以及成長中的資訊科技與金融科技。1990 年代脫離蘇聯體系後重新融入世界經濟,成長快速。
  • 歷史:波羅的與芬蘭語系民族很早就定居沿海;13 世紀德國十字軍到來,1201 年阿爾伯特主教建立里加,後來里加加入漢薩同盟。拉脫維亞地區先後受利沃尼亞騎士團、波蘭-立陶宛、瑞典與俄羅斯帝國統治。1918 年 11 月 18 日宣布獨立;1940 年(莫洛托夫-里賓特洛甫條約後)被蘇聯占領,1941–44 年納粹占領,之後再次被蘇聯占領並成為蘇聯加盟共和國。1989 年 8 月 23 日,波羅的海三國約兩百萬人手牽手組成 600 公里長的「波羅的海之路」人鏈;拉脫維亞於 1990 年 5 月 4 日宣布恢復獨立,並在 1991 年 8 月莫斯科政變失敗後獲得承認。
  • 現任領導人:總統埃德加斯·林克維奇斯(Edgars Rinkēvičs,以禮儀性質為主的國家元首,2023 年 7 月就任)。總理安德里斯·庫爾別爾格斯(Andris Kulbergs,聯合名單黨)在前總理伊薇卡·西利尼亞(Evika Siliņa)因無人機入侵應對爭議辭職後,於 2026 年 5 月 28 日以 66 票獲國會確認,領導聯合政府。2026 年 10 月 3 日國會選舉中,聯合名單黨得票約 35.8%,拿下 100 席中約 41 席(來源稱 41 或 42 席),為獨立以來單一政黨最佳成績,但未過半;下一屆政府組成情形,今天找到的來源中尚未確認。
  • 語言:拉脫維亞語,屬印歐語系的波羅的語族,與立陶宛語最接近(既非斯拉夫語也非芬蘭語系),使用帶變音符號的拉丁字母(ā、č、ē、ģ、ī、ķ、ļ、ņ、š、ū、ž)。拉特加萊語被承認為歷史上的方言,俄語在大量少數族群中通行。
  • 文化與趣聞:里加市中心擁有歐洲最大規模之一的新藝術風格(Art Nouveau)建築群(聯合國教科文組織世界遺產)。起源可追溯到 1873 年的波羅的海歌舞節是教科文組織認可的傳統;仲夏節(Jāņi / Līgo)以花環、營火與香芹籽乳酪慶祝;黑巴爾薩姆(Rīgas Melnais balzams)是國民草本利口酒;2023 年拉脫維亞首次拿下男子冰球世界錦標賽獎牌(銅牌)。
  • 來源與說明:選舉結果來自 LSM 與 OSW 分析;政府背景來自 維基百科:Andris Kulbergs 與 Bloomberg。其他數字憑一般知識,為四捨五入概數,今天未逐項查證。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「To Be Continued」(《JoJo 的奇妙冒險》/「Roundabout」)

  • 名稱:「To Be Continued」(也稱「To Be Continued… Roundabout」、「JoJo 未完待續」)。
  • 起源與歷史:出自荒木飛呂彥漫畫《JoJo 的奇妙冒險》(漫畫自 1987 年起;電視動畫自 2012 年起)的日本動畫改編。當一集停在懸念處時,畫面會定格、轉為褐色(懷舊色調),疊上「TO BE CONTINUED」的箭頭字樣,並響起英國前衛搖滾樂團 Yes 的歌曲〈Roundabout〉(1971)。確切是哪幾集、第一次何時使用,憑記憶,今天未查證。
  • 如何流行:約 2015–2016 年,YouTube、Vine 與 Twitter 的使用者開始把這個格式套到真實影片上:快要跌倒的人、快要撞上東西的車、快要得手的惡作劇。定格、褐色濾鏡與歌曲開頭的貝斯旋律,成了「下一秒就要出大事」的喜劇暗號。
  • 意義與使用情境:一種冷面的笑點:影片剛好在後果發生前結束,讓觀眾自己補上結局。用在失誤、險些出事與「等一下就知道」的場合,也拿來調侃電影與遊戲的懸念結尾。
  • 變化與演變:套用在歷史照片、電影片段與遊戲畫面的剪輯;從同一個粉絲圈衍生出的「這是 JoJo 梗嗎?」玩笑;以及反過來讓定格後出現意想不到的內容。這首歌本身也因迷因而重新受到串流關注。
  • 現況:屬於「格式模板」類迷因中的長壽經典。已不是新潮流,但短影音中需要「懸念笑點」時,仍然會用到這個一眼就能認出的暗號。
  • 說明:細節憑一般知識,今天未對照來源查證。

7) 電影 / 表演藝術介紹 — 皮娜·鮑許與烏帕塔舞蹈劇場(舞蹈劇場)

  • 名稱與關鍵人物:皮娜·鮑許(Pina Bausch,1940–2009),德國編舞家與烏帕塔舞蹈劇場(Tanztheater Wuppertal)的藝術總監,該團成為「舞蹈劇場」(Tanztheater)的典範。出生於索林根,在埃森的福克旺學院(Folkwang)師從庫爾特·尤斯(Kurt Jooss),之後赴紐約進修。
  • 年代與背景:1973 年她接掌烏帕塔歌劇院的舞蹈部門並更名為烏帕塔舞蹈劇場,領導至 2009 年 6 月 30 日辭世,距離診斷出癌症只有數天。她出身於德國戰後的表現主義舞蹈(Ausdruckstanz)傳統,並重塑了它。
  • 內容概述(主要作品):《春之祭》(Le Sacre du printemps,1975,配史特拉汶斯基音樂,舞台鋪滿泥炭土);《穆勒咖啡館》(Café Müller,1978,舞台上滿是糾纏的桌椅,鮑許本人閉眼起舞,配普賽爾音樂);《接觸之庭》(Kontakthof,1978);《康乃馨》(Nelken,1982,舞台鋪滿數千朵康乃馨);以及許多以造訪城市為靈感的後期作品(例如《巴勒莫巴勒莫》,1989)。
  • 藝術風格與技法:她以場景拼貼取代線性劇情;向舞者提問(「告訴我一個溫柔的時刻」)並用他們的回答建構作品;重複某個手勢,直到它產生新的含義;混合日常動作、說話、歌唱與純粹的體力耗竭;以厚重、可觸摸的舞台設計(泥土、水、花)當作角色。反覆出現的主題是愛、孤獨、慾望,以及男女關係中的暴力。
  • 文化與歷史意義:她讓舞蹈成為追問「不是人如何動,而是什麼讓人動」(常被歸於她的話)的方式,並打破舞蹈、戲劇與日常行為之間的界線。
  • 影響與傳承:她的方法影響了世界各地的當代編舞家與電影人;佩德羅·阿莫多瓦在《悄悄告訴她》(2002)開場放入《穆勒咖啡館》的片段,文·溫德斯則拍攝紀錄片《皮娜》(2011,3D,入圍奧斯卡最佳紀錄長片)。皮娜·鮑許基金會負責保存她的檔案。
  • 現況與哪裡可以接觸:烏帕塔皮娜·鮑許舞蹈劇場持續在烏帕塔與世界巡演她的劇目(最新演出日期與藝術領導請查官方網站,我今天沒有查證)。溫德斯的《皮娜》是銀幕上最好的入門。
  • 說明:所有細節皆憑一般知識與記憶寫成,今天未對照來源查證。

8) 看片方法 — 第 84 課:底片、顆粒與質感(影像表面摸起來像什麼)

它是什麼:除了鏡頭拍到什麼,記錄影像的物質或數位媒介也會給畫面一種質感:賽璐珞底片的顆粒、它的色彩反應與反差,相對於許多數位攝影機乾淨、銳利、無雜訊的外觀,或低解析度數位影像那種粗糙的錄影帶感。畫幅與格式(16mm、35mm、65mm/IMAX、數位)決定畫面含有多少細節與顆粒。電影人選擇底片或格式,是要讓畫面感覺懷舊、像紀錄片、像夢、冷冽,或有沉浸感。(這和先前(2026-08-10)的畫面比例課不同,畫面比例講的是畫框形狀;也和色彩不同,色彩講的是調色盤。)

如何分析:(1) 問畫面表面看起來如何:顆粒粗或細、柔或銳利、暖或冷、黑色深沉或發灰。(2) 問質感是一致的,還是在場景間改變,每次改變標示著什麼(記憶與當下、主觀與客觀、某個角色的世界與另一個角色的世界)。(3) 問這個選擇暗示電影與過去、與寫實、與觀眾注意力之間的關係。(4) 想像同一個故事換成另一種外觀來比較。

實例分析 —《奧本海默》(Oppenheimer,2023,導演克里斯多福·諾蘭):諾蘭以大畫幅底片(IMAX 65mm 與 5 孔 65mm)拍攝。他讓彩色場景與奧本海默的主觀視角連在一起,黑白場景則跟著路易斯·史特勞斯與較「客觀」的政治紀錄,而黑白段落是用柯達為這部片準備的新式黑白 IMAX 底片拍攝。看「三位一體」(Trinity)試爆那一段:巨大且細緻顆粒的底片影像讓沙漠中的閃光有了實體感;據製作團隊所說,片中的爆炸是用實際爆破拍攝,而非電腦生成。對照《28 天毀滅倒數》(2002),用消費級數位攝影機拍攝,粗糙、閃爍的質感讓故事像是從崩潰的當下傳回來的原始影像。(細節憑一般知識,今天未查證。)

試試看:下次看電影時,在前五分鐘內用一個詞說出畫面質感(「顆粒感」、「玻璃般」、「霧白」),然後留意質感改變的那一刻,問電影想讓你對那一刻有什麼感覺。
