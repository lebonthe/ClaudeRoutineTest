3) Spanish Lesson — Day 73 (10 min, More "Backwards Verbs": Doler / Faltar / Interesar / Importar)

Extends Day 15's foundational gustar pattern to four more everyday "backwards verbs" that share the exact same structure: an indirect object pronoun (me/te/le/nos/os/les) plus a verb conjugated to agree with whatever is causing the sensation — not the person experiencing it — plus that causing thing as the sentence's true grammatical subject. Doler ("to hurt/cause pain," an o→ue stem-changing verb like Day 18's poder) gives duele/duelen; faltar ("to be lacking/missing," also usable for "how much longer until") gives falta/faltan; interesar ("to interest") gives interesa/interesan; importar ("to matter/to care about") gives importa/importan. The lesson's sharpest trap, specific to doler: Spanish never adds a possessive adjective ("my") before a body part in these constructions — Me duele la cabeza ("My head hurts"), never "Me duele mi cabeza" — because the indirect pronoun me already establishes whose head it is, making a possessive redundant, and idiomatically wrong on top of that. Faltar's dual use is also flagged: beyond "lacking" an object (Nos falta tiempo, "We're short on time"), it names a remaining duration (Nos faltan dos horas, "We have two hours left"), with the time quantity itself deciding singular vs. plural agreement.

  • Me duele mucho la cabeza. — My head hurts a lot.
  • ¿Te duelen los pies? — Do your feet hurt?
  • Nos faltan solo dos horas antes del tren. — We only have two hours left before the train.
  • Le interesan mucho los idiomas. — He/She is very interested in languages.
  • No nos importa el precio. — We don't care about the price.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 9: 馬蹄 (Mǎ Tí) — "Horses' Hooves"

The second chapter of the Outer Chapters (外篇), continuing directly from Essay 8's "Webbed Toes" (駢拇) and its polemical "Primitivist" strand, per A.C. Graham's classification of the Outer/Miscellaneous Chapters as later "Zhuangzi school" writing distinct from the Inner Chapters' more exploratory parables. The essay opens by describing a horse's true, unspoiled nature (真性): hooves that can tread frost and snow, a coat that withstands wind and cold, grazing grass, drinking water, and prancing freely with raised hooves — the complete, self-sufficient life of a horse left alone. Into this scene enters Bo Le (伯樂), history's legendary master horse-trainer, declaring "I am skilled at managing horses" (我善治馬) — and Zhuangzi immediately inverts the reputation Bo Le has carried for centuries: his methods are branding the horses, clipping their manes, paring their hooves, fitting halters and hobbles, penning them in stables, all in the name of "management," and the essay states bluntly that two or three horses out of every ten die from this treatment alone before training even truly begins, with still more dying under the whip and spur that follow. Zhuangzi extends the same critique in parallel to the potter who claims mastery over clay ("I am skilled at handling clay — round pieces obey the compass, square pieces obey the carpenter's square") and the carpenter who claims mastery over wood — in every case, an "expert's" imposed standard is really a violence done to the material's own nature, achieving conformity to an external ideal only by destroying what made the material itself alive and whole. The essay's governing political metaphor is then made explicit: sages and rulers who "manage" the people through benevolence and righteousness (仁義) — exactly the same target Essay 8 attacked — are Bo Le to the common people's horses, and the essay's remedy is government by non-action (無為而治): a ruler who does not impose forced standards but lets people graze, drink, and prance according to their own given nature, exactly as the essay's opening image of the unmanaged horse describes.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Ghana (加納)

  • Location & territory size: Ghana sits on West Africa's Gulf of Guinea coast, bordered by Côte d'Ivoire to the west, Burkina Faso to the north, and Togo to the east. At roughly 240,000 km², it is close in size to the United Kingdom (243,610 km²) and about 6.6 times the size of Taiwan (~36,197 km²), spanning coastal plains, the forested Ashanti heartland, and savanna in the north around Lake Volta, the world's largest reservoir by surface area.
  • Population: Approximately 34-35 million (estimates vary slightly by source for 2026), with the capital Accra and the Ashanti regional capital Kumasi as the two largest urban centers; a historically young, fast-urbanizing population.
  • Religion: Predominantly Christian (roughly seven in ten Ghanaians, split across Pentecostal/Charismatic, Protestant, and Catholic traditions), with a significant Muslim minority (concentrated especially in the northern regions, roughly one in five nationally) and small communities practicing traditional African religions or syncretic blends alongside Christianity or Islam.
  • Economy: Gold, cocoa, and crude oil form the traditional "big three" of Ghana's export economy, with gold currently dominant — gold exports alone reportedly reached around US$12.5 billion in the first half of 2026 amid strong global prices. GDP is estimated around US$118 billion in 2026 (GDP per capita roughly US$3,300), with real growth around 4.8% and inflation cooling to roughly 5.8% from much higher levels earlier in the decade; government debt remains elevated at roughly 53% of GDP, a legacy of Ghana's 2022 sovereign default and subsequent IMF-backed restructuring program. Persistent challenges include youth unemployment, illegal small-scale gold mining ("galamsey," blamed for major environmental and water-pollution damage), and corruption concerns.
  • History: Home to the pre-colonial Ashanti (Asante) Empire, one of West Africa's most powerful states, built on gold and the trans-Saharan and later Atlantic trade before British colonization as the Gold Coast. On March 6, 1957, Ghana became the first Sub-Saharan African nation to gain independence from European colonial rule, under founding president and pan-Africanist icon Kwame Nkrumah — a milestone that made Ghana a symbolic leader of Africa's broader decolonization wave. Nkrumah was overthrown in a 1966 coup, ushering in decades of alternating civilian and military rule (including Jerry Rawlings' periods of military and later elected civilian leadership) before a return to stable multi-party democracy formalized in the 1992 constitution.
  • Current leadership: John Dramani Mahama, of the National Democratic Congress (NDC), won the December 2024 election and was sworn in for a new term in January 2025, marking a return to the presidency he previously held from 2012 to 2017 — an unusual "comeback" in Ghanaian politics, brought in partly on promises to reset the economy after the debt crisis.
  • Language: English is the official language, a legacy of British colonial rule, used in government, education, and media; Akan (particularly its Twi dialect) is the most widely spoken indigenous language, alongside Ewe, Ga, Dagbani, and dozens of other local languages reflecting Ghana's considerable ethnic diversity.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — 工具人 (Tool Man, Taiwan/Chinese-Language Internet Culture)

  • Name & origin: 工具人 (gōngjùrén), literally "tool person" or "tool man," is a piece of Chinese-language internet slang widely used across Taiwan's online forums and social media, describing someone reduced to being useful only for their function rather than valued as a person in a relationship. Its precise forum-of-origin and first-coined date are not cleanly documented in available sources, but it is consistently associated with Taiwan's PTT bulletin board's dating/relationship discussion boards, where the concept of being exploited for one's usefulness in an unrequited romantic pursuit became a recurring, named complaint.
  • How it became popular: The term spread because it names a widely-recognized, previously unlabeled social dynamic with instant, sympathetic recognizability — a single compact label for a common frustration, which made it easy to apply conversationally, in memes, in advice columns, and eventually in mainstream entertainment and news commentary across Chinese-language internet culture generally, not only in Taiwan.
  • Meaning & usage context: Most typically describes a man (though the concept can apply to any gender) who dotes on a romantic interest — running errands, providing money or gifts (jokingly labeled becoming her "ATM"), providing transportation (becoming her "chauffeur"), and offering constant emotional support — without romantic interest ever being reciprocated, while the object of his affection neither accepts nor firmly rejects him, keeping him available and useful indefinitely. It is used both as a warning ("don't be a 工具人") and as a rueful self-description by people who recognize the pattern in their own behavior.
  • Variations & evolution: Common intensified/derivative forms include 老工具人 ("veteran/longtime tool man," used for someone who has played this role for a long time or repeatedly), and situational sub-labels for specific "tool" functions (ATM, chauffeur, "emotional trash can" 情緒垃圾桶); the underlying concept has also cross-pollinated with broader East Asian "nice guy"/"simp"-adjacent discourse, though 工具人 predates and is linguistically distinct from the English "simp" meme.
  • Current status: Still in everyday active use across Taiwanese and broader Chinese-language internet/social conversation as a stable, non-faddish piece of relationship vocabulary rather than a fast-burning viral moment — closer in lifespan to an adopted slang term than to a one-off image-macro meme.

Sources: en.wiktionary.org, hinative.com, hinative.com (老工具人了)

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans (1927, dir. F.W. Murnau)

  • Name & key background: Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans (1927) was German director F.W. Murnau's first American feature, made after Fox Studios' producer William Fox brought him to Hollywood and, unusually for the studio system, gave him near-total creative control — a freedom later echoed by Orson Welles' arrangement for Citizen Kane at RKO. Murnau brought with him the visual language he had developed across 17 prior German films, chiefly the German Expressionist tradition's emphasis on mood-driven, symbolically-charged lighting and set design.
  • Content & overview: The story is deliberately told through archetypes rather than named, individuated characters — credited only as The Man, The Wife, and The Woman from the City — following a farmer seduced by a city temptress into a plot to murder his own wife, his crisis of conscience before the act, and the couple's fragile, hard-won reconciliation over the course of a single day that takes them from the countryside into the city and back again.
  • Artistic style & technique: Murnau used chiaroscuro lighting and elaborate forced-perspective sets (an artificially receding cityscape and swamp built to exaggerate depth) to visually encode the characters' shifting inner states, blending German Expressionist stylization with a more naturalistic, fluid camera mobility that was considered startlingly advanced for 1927 — extensive dolly and crane work let the camera travel with characters through built environments in ways few contemporary Hollywood productions attempted. Released the same year as the part-talkie The Jazz Singer, Sunrise sits at the exact hinge point between silent and sound cinema, using the new Movietone sound-on-film technology for a synchronized musical score and effects track while remaining performed and structured as a silent film with intertitles.
  • Cultural & historical significance: Despite Murnau's total creative freedom and the film's now-legendary critical standing, Sunrise was a commercial disappointment on its initial 1927 release. It nonetheless won the only competitive "Unique and Artistic Picture" award ever given, at the first Academy Awards ceremony in 1929 (a category discontinued thereafter and folded into Best Picture), alongside a Best Cinematography win for Charles Rosher and Karl Struss.
  • Influence & legacy: Sunrise is now routinely ranked among the greatest films ever made in critics' polls (including repeated top-10 placements in Sight & Sound's decennial critics' poll) and is cited as a foundational influence on the development of expressive camera movement, mood-driven lighting design, and the visual possibilities of the silent-to-sound transition period more broadly.
  • Where to experience it today: Preserved and restored by the George Eastman Museum, and widely available today via major physical/streaming classic-film releases (including a Criterion Collection edition) and regular retrospective screenings at silent-film festivals such as the San Francisco Silent Film Festival.

Sources: sensesofcinema.com, bfi.org.uk, tcm.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Foley Artistry & Diegetic Sound-Effects Design (Lesson 59)

A craft-focused sound lens distinct from every prior sound-related lesson in this track: Day 4's Sound Design & Score covered diegetic/non-diegetic sound and score-as-emotional-cue broadly; Day 47's Acousmatic Sound concerned a sound whose visual source is deliberately hidden; Day 45's Needle Drop concerned pre-existing, previously-recorded licensed music. Foley (named for sound-effects pioneer Jack Foley) is the specific craft of performing and recording new sound effects — footsteps, cloth rustle, punches, object handling — synchronized frame-accurately to on-screen physical action in post-production, since most on-set production audio is too poor, too contaminated by crew noise, or simply absent (for silent-era films or heavily VFX-driven modern shots) to use directly. Because Foley is manufactured rather than simply captured, it is inherently a design choice, not a neutral record of "what really happened" on set: a Foley artist chooses which sounds to emphasize, exaggerate, soften, or omit entirely, shaping how weighty, threatening, or intimate an action feels. When analysing a scene, check which everyday physical sounds are unnaturally clear/foregrounded relative to how they would actually sound at that distance, whether the absence of expected sound (or of any score at all) is itself doing dramatic work, and whether Foley choices are calibrated for pure realism or for a more heightened, stylized emotional effect.

Worked example: No Country for Old Men (2007, dir. Joel & Ethan Coen, sound by supervising sound editor/re-recording mixer Skip Lievsay) builds nearly the entire back half of its suspense on meticulous Foley and diegetic sound design rather than a conventional score — the Coens reportedly concluded after watching their first rough cut that the film "didn't need any" music at all, a deliberate choice Lievsay called "quite a remarkable experiment" specifically because removing score "removes the safety net that lets the audience feel like they know what's going to happen." The film's most analyzed sequences — Anton Chigurh's captive-bolt cattle gun used as a murder weapon, and the extended Eagle Hotel corridor stalking sequence — rely on isolated, carefully sculpted Foley (footsteps on carpet versus tile, the mechanical hiss-and-thunk of the bolt gun, boots on gravel, wind across open prairie) mixed without a musical cushion, forcing the audience to track danger purely through the specificity of manufactured sound rather than being cued by a score's rising tension — exactly the "lost comfort zone" effect Lievsay described, and a key factor in the film's two Academy Award sound nominations.

3)西班牙文課程——第 73 天(10 分鐘,更多「反向動詞」:Doler / Faltar / Interesar / Importar)

從第 15 天學過的 gustar 基本句型延伸出四個同構、同樣日常實用的「反向動詞」:間接受詞代名詞(me/te/le/nos/os/les)加上依「造成該感受的東西」(而非感受到它的人)變化的動詞,再加上那個造成感受的東西本身,做為句子真正的文法主詞。Doler(「使疼痛」,與第 18 天的 poder 一樣屬於 o→ue 詞根變化動詞)變化為 duele/duelen;faltar(「缺少」,也可用來表達「還差多久」)變化為 falta/faltan;interesar(「使感興趣」)變化為 interesa/interesan;importar(「使在意/要緊」)變化為 importa/importan。本課最尖銳的陷阱專屬於 doler:這類句型中,西班牙文絕不會在身體部位前面加上所有格「我的」——只會說 Me duele la cabeza(「我頭痛」),絕不說「Me duele mi cabeza」——因為間接受詞代名詞 me 早已表明這顆頭是誰的,再加所有格不僅多餘,慣用法上更是錯誤。本課也特別提醒 faltar 的雙重用法:除了表達「缺少」某項東西(Nos falta tiempo,「我們時間不夠」)之外,也能用來表達「還剩多久」(Nos faltan dos horas,「我們還有兩小時」),此時決定動詞單複數的,正是那段時間量詞本身。

  • Me duele mucho la cabeza. —— 我頭很痛。
  • ¿Te duelen los pies? —— 你腳會痛嗎?
  • Nos faltan solo dos horas antes del tren. —— 距離發車我們只剩兩小時了。
  • Le interesan mucho los idiomas. —— 他/她對語言很感興趣。
  • No nos importa el precio. —— 我們不在乎價錢。

4)莊子——第 9 篇:馬蹄(Mǎ Tí)——「馬的蹄子」

《莊子》外篇的第二篇,緊接第 8 篇〈駢拇〉而來,延續同一支葛瑞漢(A.C. Graham)歸類為「原始主義」的論戰性脈絡——葛瑞漢將外篇與雜篇整體歸為較晚期「莊子學派」門人所寫,論述風格與內篇那種較為鬆散、探索性的寓言筆法明顯不同。本篇開篇先描繪馬未經干預的真性:馬蹄可以踐踏霜雪,皮毛可以抵禦風寒,渴則飲水,飢則食草,興致一來便揚蹄奔騰——這是一匹馬獨立自足、不受拘束時的完整生命樣態。接著,歷史上傳說中的相馬與馴馬大師伯樂登場,自稱「我善治馬」——莊子隨即將這位流傳千古的美名徹底翻轉:伯樂的所謂「治理」手法,包括在馬身上烙印、剪毛、削蹄、套上籠頭與絆索,再將馬圈養於馬廄之中,文中直言,光是這些手段,十匹馬當中便有兩三匹因此喪命,而後續鞭策驅使之下,死去的馬匹數量還會更多。莊子將同樣的批判邏輯,平行套用到自稱擅長處理陶土的陶匠(「我善治埴,圓者中規,方者中矩」)以及自稱擅長處理木材的木匠身上——每一個案例中,所謂「專家」強加的標準,實質上都是對材料本身天性的一種暴力,唯有透過摧毀材料原本鮮活完整的樣貌,才能換來對外在理想標準的服從。本篇最終將這套統攝全文的政治隱喻明白揭示:那些以仁義治理百姓的聖人與統治者——正是第 8 篇所批判的同一個對象——之於百姓,正如伯樂之於馬匹;本篇開出的藥方是「無為而治」:統治者不強加人為標準,而是任由百姓依循自身與生俱來的本性自在覓食、飲水、奔騰,一如本篇開篇那匹未受馴治的馬。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——加納(Ghana)

  • 地理位置與領土規模:加納位於西非幾內亞灣沿岸,西與象牙海岸接壤,北鄰布吉納法索,東接多哥。國土面積約 24 萬平方公里,與英國(243,610 平方公里)相近,約為台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)的 6.6 倍,境內涵蓋沿海平原、阿散蒂(Ashanti)森林核心地帶,以及北部圍繞伏塔湖(Lake Volta,全球表面積最大的水庫)的莽原地形。
  • 人口:約 3,400 萬至 3,500 萬人(2026 年各方估計數字略有出入),首都阿克拉(Accra)與阿散蒂區首府庫馬西(Kumasi)為兩大主要都市中心;人口結構歷來偏年輕,都市化速度快。
  • 宗教:以基督教為主(約占全國人口十分之七,涵蓋五旬節/靈恩派、新教與天主教等不同支派),另有相當規模的穆斯林少數族群(尤其集中於北部地區,全國占比約五分之一),此外也有小規模社群奉行傳統非洲宗教,或與基督教/伊斯蘭教並存的融合信仰。
  • 經濟:黃金、可可與原油向來是加納出口經濟的「三大支柱」,其中黃金目前居首——據報導,僅 2026 年上半年,黃金出口額便在全球金價走高的帶動下達到約 125 億美元。2026 年 GDP 估計約 1,180 億美元(人均 GDP 約 3,300 美元),實質成長率約 4.8%,通膨率已從十年前段的高點降溫至約 5.8%;政府債務占 GDP 比重仍偏高,約達 53%,這是加納 2022 年主權債務違約、以及其後 IMF 主導債務重整計畫所遺留的後果。持續存在的挑戰包括青年失業、非法小型採金活動(「加拉姆西」galamsey,被指控造成重大環境與水污染破壞),以及貪腐疑慮。
  • 歷史:加納曾是殖民時期以前西非最強大的國家之一——阿散蒂帝國(Asante Empire)的所在地,該帝國以黃金貿易及跨撒哈拉、後來的大西洋貿易為根基崛起,其後淪為英國殖民地「黃金海岸」(Gold Coast)。1957 年 3 月 6 日,加納在建國總統暨泛非主義象徵人物克瓦米·恩克魯瑪(Kwame Nkrumah)的帶領下,成為撒哈拉以南非洲第一個脫離歐洲殖民統治而獨立的國家,這項里程碑也使加納成為非洲整體去殖民化浪潮的象徵性領頭者。恩克魯瑪於 1966 年遭政變推翻,此後數十年間,文人政權與軍事統治交替更迭(包括傑瑞·羅林斯〔Jerry Rawlings〕歷經軍事統治、後轉為民選文人領導的時期),直到 1992 年憲法正式確立,加納才恢復穩定的多黨民主體制。
  • 現任領導人:國家民主大會黨(NDC)籍的約翰·德拉馬尼·馬哈馬(John Dramani Mahama),於 2024 年 12 月大選勝出,並於 2025 年 1 月宣誓就任新任期——這是他繼 2012 至 2017 年首度擔任總統之後的「回鍋」,在加納政壇實屬罕見案例,部分原因即是選民期望他能在債務危機之後為經濟帶來重新開機的契機。
  • 語言:英語為官方語言,係英國殖民統治遺緒,通用於政府、教育與媒體;阿坎語(Akan,尤其是其分支特威語 Twi)為使用人口最多的本土語言,此外還有埃維語(Ewe)、加語(Ga)、達戈姆巴語(Dagbani)等數十種在地語言,反映加納相當多元的族群組成。

6)網路迷因介紹——工具人(Tool Man,台灣/華文網路文化)

  • 名稱與起源:工具人(gōngjùrén),字面意思為「工具般的人」,是廣泛流通於台灣論壇與社群媒體的華文網路用語,用來形容一個人在感情關係中,僅因「有用」而被利用,卻未被真正當作一個完整的人來對待。其確切最早出現的論壇來源與確切創詞時間,在現有可查資料中並無明確記載,但普遍與台灣 PTT 電子佈告欄的感情/交友討論板連結在一起——在那裡,「為單方追求付出、卻遭利用其有用性」這種現象逐漸被賦予這個固定稱呼,成為反覆出現的抱怨主題。
  • 如何走紅:這個詞之所以能夠廣泛流傳,是因為它為一種原本廣泛存在、卻缺乏統一稱呼的社交互動模式,賦予了一個立即引發共鳴、易於辨識的名稱——這種簡潔有力的標籤,使其很容易被套用於日常對話、迷因圖、感情專欄,乃至後來的主流娛樂與新聞評論之中,不僅限於台灣,更擴及整個華文網路文化圈。
  • 意義與使用情境:最典型的用法,是描述一名男性(儘管此概念也可套用於任何性別)長期討好心儀對象——跑腿辦事、提供金錢或禮物(戲稱淪為對方的「提款機」)、提供交通接送(淪為「司機」),以及提供源源不絕的情緒支持——卻始終未能換得對方回應的愛意,而對方既不明確接受、也不明確拒絕,任由他持續處於「隨傳隨到、有用即可」的狀態。此詞既可用作警語(「不要當工具人」),也常被人用來自嘲,承認自己在感情中確實落入了這種模式。
  • 變體與演進:常見的加強/衍生用法包括「老工具人」(用來形容長期或反覆扮演此角色的人),以及針對特定「工具」功能而生的次分類稱呼(提款機、司機、「情緒垃圾桶」);此一底層概念,也與更廣泛的東亞「好人卡」/「舔狗」相關論述互相交融,惟工具人一詞的出現時間更早,且在語言脈絡上與英文「simp」迷因並不相同。
  • 現況:至今仍持續活躍於台灣及廣大華文網路/社群日常對話之中,已成為一個穩定、非速食型的感情關係慣用詞彙,而非曇花一現的病毒式爆紅現象——其生命週期更接近一個被日常收編吸收的俚語,而非單一圖片哏式迷因。

資料來源:en.wiktionary.org、hinative.com、hinative.com(老工具人了)

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《日出》(Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans,1927,導演:F.W. 穆瑙)

  • 名稱與背景概述:《日出:兩個人的歌》(1927)是德國導演 F.W. 穆瑙(F.W. Murnau)的首部美國作品,由福斯電影公司製片人威廉·福斯(William Fox)將他延攬至好萊塢,並罕見地給予他近乎完全的創作自主權——這種安排後來也被奧森·威爾斯在 RKO 拍攝《大國民》時所複製。穆瑙將自己在此前 17 部德國作品中發展出的視覺語彙一併帶入,主要承襲德國表現主義傳統中,以情緒為導向、充滿象徵意涵的燈光與場景設計手法。
  • 內容概述:本片刻意以原型角色、而非具名的個體角色來說故事——片中角色僅標示為「男人」、「妻子」與「城裡來的女人」——描述一位農夫受城裡誘惑者引誘,策劃謀殺自己妻子的陰謀、犯案前良心的掙扎,以及這對夫妻歷經一整天(從鄉間前往城市、再返回鄉間)後,得來不易且脆弱的和解過程。
  • 藝術風格與技法:穆瑙運用明暗對比強烈的燈光,以及精心打造的強迫透視布景(刻意建造出視覺上向遠方延伸的誇張城市街景與沼澤場景以強化空間深度),將角色內心狀態的轉變以視覺化方式呈現,融合德國表現主義的風格化手法,與當時被視為異常先進、流動自如的攝影機運動——大量的推軌與升降鏡頭,讓攝影機得以跟隨角色穿梭於搭建場景之中,這種手法在同期好萊塢作品中極為罕見。本片與有聲片先驅《爵士歌手》(The Jazz Singer)同年上映,正好座落於默片與有聲片交替的關鍵時刻:片中採用了新問世的 Movietone 光學錄音技術,同步播放配樂與音效軌,但整體表演與敘事結構仍依循默片傳統,搭配字卡呈現對白。
  • 文化與歷史意義:儘管穆瑙獲得完全的創作自主,本片如今更享有傳奇般的評論地位,但《日出》於 1927 年首度上映時,票房表現其實令人失望。儘管如此,本片仍於 1929 年首屆奧斯卡金像獎頒獎典禮上,獲頒史上唯一一次頒發的「最獨特藝術影片」獎(此獎項此後即遭廢除,相關精神併入最佳影片獎項),同時查爾斯·羅雪(Charles Rosher)與卡爾·斯特魯斯(Karl Struss)也以本片獲得最佳攝影獎。
  • 影響與傳承:《日出》如今在各項影評人票選的影史最偉大電影名單中經常名列前茅(包括在《視與聽》〔Sight & Sound〕每十年一次的影評人票選中屢次擠進前十名),並被公認為對「富有表現力的攝影機運動」、「以情緒為導向的燈光設計」,以及默片邁向有聲片過渡時期視覺可能性的發展,具有奠基性的深遠影響。
  • 今日可觀賞管道:本片已由喬治伊士曼博物館(George Eastman Museum)完整保存並修復,如今可透過主要實體/串流經典電影發行管道觀賞(包括標準收藏〔Criterion Collection〕版本),舊金山默片影展(San Francisco Silent Film Festival)等默片影展也經常安排本片的回顧放映場次。

8)看片方法——擬音藝術與現場音效設計(Foley,第 59 課)

一項聚焦於「工藝製作」層面的聲音分析角度,與本系列先前所有聲音相關課程截然不同:第 4 天的「聲音設計與配樂」廣泛涵蓋了現場音/畫外音,以及配樂作為情緒提示的手法;第 47 天的「無聲源音」(Acousmatic Sound)關注的是刻意隱藏視覺來源的聲音;第 45 天的「情懷金曲置入」(Needle Drop)關注的則是既有、事先錄製並取得授權的音樂。擬音(Foley,得名自音效先驅傑克·佛利〔Jack Foley〕)是一項特定的專業工藝:在後製階段,由專人重新演出並錄製新的音效——腳步聲、衣物摩擦聲、拳擊聲、物品操作聲——並逐格精確同步至畫面中的實際動作,因為現場拍攝時錄下的原始聲音,絕大多數品質太差、混雜過多工作人員雜音,或根本不存在(例如默片時代的作品,或大量仰賴視覺特效鏡頭的現代電影),無法直接使用。正因為擬音是「人工製作」而非單純「現場錄下」的聲音,它本質上是一項設計選擇,而非對片場「真實發生了什麼」的中立紀錄:擬音師會主動選擇要強調、誇大、柔化,或乾脆完全省略哪些聲音,藉此形塑一個動作在觀眾感受中的份量、威脅感或親密程度。分析一段場景時,請留意:哪些日常的物理聲響,相對於實際拍攝距離該有的音量而言,顯得不自然地清晰、被刻意放大;預期中應該出現的聲音(或甚至整段配樂)的缺席,本身是否正在發揮戲劇效果;以及擬音的選擇,究竟是為了追求純粹的寫實,還是為了營造更為誇張、風格化的情緒效果。

實例演練:《險路勿近》(No Country for Old Men,2007,導演:柯恩兄弟〔Joel & Ethan Coen〕,音效總監暨混音師:史基普·利夫塞〔Skip Lievsay〕)幾乎整部電影後半段的懸疑感,都建立在極其精細的擬音與現場音效設計之上,而非仰賴傳統配樂——據報導,柯恩兄弟在看完影片初剪版本後便認定「根本不需要」任何配樂,這項刻意的選擇,被利夫塞形容為「相當不凡的一次實驗」,原因正是拿掉配樂之後,「等於拿掉了讓觀眾自以為知道接下來會發生什麼事的那張安全網」。片中最常被分析討論的幾場戲——安東·奇哥(Anton Chigurh)以牲畜擊昏槍作為殺人凶器的橋段,以及鷹旅館(Eagle Hotel)那段延伸的走廊跟蹤戲——皆仰賴獨立、經過精心雕琢的擬音效果(地毯與磁磚上截然不同的腳步聲、擊昏槍機械式的嘶嘶聲與撞擊聲、靴子踩踏碎石的聲響、掠過大草原的風聲),混音時刻意不搭配任何音樂緩衝,迫使觀眾必須完全仰賴人工製造的聲音細節本身去追蹤危險的逼近,而非靠配樂漸強來獲得提示——這正是利夫塞所描述「失去安全感」的效果,也是本片獲得兩項奧斯卡金像獎音效類提名的關鍵原因之一。
