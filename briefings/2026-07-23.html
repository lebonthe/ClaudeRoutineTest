3) Spanish Lesson — Day 14 (10 min, builds on Day 13)

Day 13 introduced ir a + infinitivo for talking about the near future. Today's new concept is the present progressive: estar (conjugated) + gerundio — used to describe an action happening right now, at the moment of speaking.

  • ¿Qué estás haciendo? (keh ehs-TAHS ah-see-EHN-doh) — What are you doing (right now)?
  • Estoy estudiando español ahora mismo. (ehs-TOY ehs-too-dee-AHN-doh ehs-pahn-YOHL ah-OH-rah MEES-moh) — I'm studying Spanish right now.
  • Ella está durmiendo. ¡Todavía es muy temprano! (EH-yah ehs-TAH door-mee-EHN-doh. toh-dah-BEE-ah ehs mwee tehm-PRAH-noh) — She's sleeping. It's still very early!

Grammar note: the gerund is formed by dropping the infinitive ending and adding -ando (for -ar verbs) or -iendo (for -er/-ir verbs) — hablar → hablando, comer → comiendo, escribir → escribiendo — and it never changes form regardless of subject; only estar conjugates. A handful of common verbs have irregular gerunds worth memorizing: dormir → durmiendo, leer → leyendo, decir → diciendo.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 14: 大有 (Dà Yǒu) — "Possession in Great Measure" (Fire over Heaven). This is the direct inversion of yesterday's Hexagram 13 (同人, Fellowship): where Fellowship placed Heaven above Fire, Great Possession places Fire above Heaven — the sun blazing at the zenith of the sky, its light reaching and illuminating everything below without exception. The Judgment reads simply: "Possession in Great Measure. Supreme success." The Image adds: "Fire in heaven above: the image of Possession in Great Measure. Thus the superior man curbs evil and furthers good, and thereby obeys the benevolent will of heaven." The teaching follows naturally from yesterday's hexagram: genuine, open fellowship among people (13) is what makes truly great possession — of resources, of influence, of abundance (14) — possible and sustainable, rather than something hoarded or seized. But great possession is also a test: the superior person holds abundance not for private indulgence, but as a trust to be administered with clarity and moral discernment, actively rewarding good and restraining wrongdoing.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Andorra

  • Location: A landlocked micro-state tucked in the eastern Pyrenees mountains of southwestern Europe, wedged directly between France to the north and Spain to the south.
  • Territory: About 468 km² — one of Europe's smallest countries, roughly 1/77th the size of Taiwan (~36,200 km²), or a little over twice the size of Washington, D.C. (~177 km²).
  • Population: Approximately 80,000 people, concentrated mostly in and around the capital, Andorra la Vella (Europe's highest-elevation capital city).
  • Religion: Predominantly Roman Catholic (around 90%), though the constitution guarantees freedom of religion and the state has no official religion.
  • Economy: Overwhelmingly driven by tourism — winter skiing, hiking, and duty-free shopping account for roughly 80% of GDP — supplemented by a well-developed banking/financial-services sector built on its historic status as a low-tax haven; Andorra only introduced a personal income tax in 2015, largely under EU pressure to increase fiscal transparency.
  • History: Legend attributes its founding to a charter granted by Charlemagne in the 8th century. Since 1278, Andorra has been governed as a unique "co-principality" under a feudal pareage agreement, jointly headed by two co-princes — making it the world's only co-principality and one of Europe's oldest continuously surviving political arrangements. It adopted its first written constitution and joined the United Nations only in 1993.
  • Current leader(s): Co-Princes Emmanuel Macron (President of France, ex officio) and Bishop Joan-Enric Vives i Sicília (Bishop of Urgell, Spain, ex officio) serve as joint heads of state; day-to-day government is run by Cap de Govern (Head of Government) Xavier Espot Zamora, in office since 2019.
  • Language: Catalan is the sole official language, though Spanish, French, and Portuguese are all widely spoken given the large immigrant workforce.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Versailles Literature" (凡爾賽文學, Fán'ěrsài Wénxué)

  • Name: "Versailles Literature," Chinese: 凡爾賽文學 (fán'ěrsài wénxué), often shortened to just "凡爾賽" (Versailles) as an adjective or verb.
  • Origin/history: Coined in 2020 by a Weibo blogger known as "小奶球" (Xiǎo Nǎiqiú), who began cataloguing and mocking a genre of humble-bragging posts, naming it after the opulent French palace of Versailles and, more specifically, the classic anime "The Rose of Versailles" (凡爾賽玫瑰), whose aristocratic characters casually flaunt extravagant wealth as a matter of course.
  • How it became popular: The blogger's "textbook examples" and mock "appreciation classes" (凡爾賽文學鑑賞班) for the genre spread rapidly across Weibo and Douyin through 2020-2021, as netizens started competitively identifying and parodying real posts that fit the pattern — turning a niche observation into a nationwide shorthand almost overnight.
  • Meaning/usage: Describes a specific rhetorical formula for humble-bragging: 先抑後揚 (build up a complaint first, then reveal the enviable truth), 自問自答 (ask yourself a rhetorical question and answer it), and a studied understatement ("其實還好啦" — "it's really not a big deal") — e.g., complaining that a new Birkin bag "doesn't really match" the color of one's private jet. Used both to describe real humble-braggers and, jokingly, to call out a friend's obviously boastful "complaint."
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned parody "appreciation class" accounts dissecting new examples, turned "凡爾賽" into a standalone adjective/verb ("你又在凡爾賽什麼"), and is frequently compared in English-language coverage to Instagram-style "humble-bragging" or "flexing" culture, though commentators note the Chinese version is more self-consciously literary and formulaic.
  • Current status: Less of a daily trending topic today than at its 2020-2021 peak, but the term has stuck as a permanent fixture of Chinese internet vocabulary, still reached for whenever a new "subtle flex" post goes viral.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第十四天(10 分鐘,銜接第十三天內容)

第十三天學了 ir a + 原形動詞,用來表達近未來的計畫。今天的新內容是現在進行式:estar(變化後)+ 現在分詞——用來描述「說話當下正在發生」的動作。

  • ¿Qué estás haciendo?(克誒 誒斯達斯 阿誰恩多)—— 你現在在做什麼?
  • Estoy estudiando español ahora mismo.(誒斯多伊 誒斯度迪安多 誒斯巴紐爾 阿歐拉 米斯莫)—— 我現在正在讀西班牙文。
  • Ella está durmiendo. ¡Todavía es muy temprano!(誒亞 誒斯達 都爾米安多。多達比亞 誒斯 母伊 天普拉諾)—— 她正在睡覺。現在還很早呢!

文法重點:現在分詞的變化方式是去掉原形動詞字尾,加上 -ando(-AR 動詞)或 -iendo(-ER/-IR 動詞)——hablar → hablando、comer → comiendo、escribir → escribiendo——而且不論主詞是誰,現在分詞本身永遠不變化,真正變化的只有 estar。有幾個常用動詞的現在分詞是不規則的,值得特別記住:dormir → durmiendo、leer → leyendo、decir → diciendo。

4) 易經卦象

第十四卦:大有卦(Dà Yǒu)——「大有」(上離下乾,火在天上)。這一卦正好是昨天第十三卦(同人)的顛倒:同人卦是天在上、火在下;大有卦則是火在上、天在下——太陽高懸天空之巔,光芒普照大地,無所遺漏。卦辭僅簡單寫道:「大有:元亨。」〈象傳〉補充道:「火在天上,大有;君子以遏惡揚善,順天休命。」這一卦的道理緊接著昨天而來:唯有像同人卦那樣真誠、開放的團結合作,才能孕育出真正龐大且可長可久的擁有——無論是資源、影響力或財富(大有)——而非靠囤積或奪取而來。然而,「大有」同時也是一種考驗:君子擁有豐盛之時,不應據為己有、放縱私欲,而應將其視為一種託付,以清明的判斷力積極獎善懲惡,善加治理。

5) 國家/地區介紹 — 安道爾(Andorra)

  • 位置: 位於歐洲西南部庇里牛斯山脈(Pyrenees)東段的內陸微型國家,恰好夾在北邊的法國與南邊的西班牙之間。
  • 領土面積: 約 468 平方公里——是歐洲面積最小的國家之一,大約只有台灣(約 36,200 平方公里)的 1/77,約為美國華盛頓特區(約 177 平方公里)的兩倍多。
  • 人口: 約 8 萬人,絕大多數集中在首都安道爾城(Andorra la Vella)及周邊地區——安道爾城也是歐洲海拔最高的首都。
  • 宗教: 以羅馬天主教為主(約占 90%),不過憲法保障宗教自由,國家並無官方宗教。
  • 經濟: 高度依賴觀光業——冬季滑雪、健行與免稅購物合計約占 GDP 的 80%——另有發展成熟的銀行/金融服務業,建立在安道爾長期作為低稅天堂的歷史地位之上;安道爾直到 2015 年才在歐盟壓力下首次開徵個人所得稅,以提升財政透明度。
  • 歷史: 傳說其建國可追溯至 8 世紀查理曼大帝頒發的特許狀。自 1278 年起,安道爾一直以獨特的「雙公國」(co-principality)封建協議(pareage)形式治理,由兩位共治親王共同擔任元首——使其成為全世界唯一的雙公國,也是歐洲現存最古老的政治體制安排之一。安道爾遲至 1993 年才制定第一部成文憲法並加入聯合國。
  • 現任領導人: 兩位共治親王分別為法國總統馬克宏(Emmanuel Macron,依職權自動兼任)與西班牙烏赫爾主教文森斯(Joan-Enric Vives i Sicília,依職權自動兼任),共同擔任國家元首;日常政府運作則由政府首長(Cap de Govern)哈維爾·埃斯波特·薩摩拉(Xavier Espot Zamora)負責,自 2019 年上任至今。
  • 語言: 加泰隆尼亞語(Catalan)為唯一官方語言,但因為有大量外籍移工,西班牙語、法語與葡萄牙語也十分普遍。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、en.wikipedia.org

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「凡爾賽文學」(Fán'ěrsài Wénxué)

  • 名稱: 「凡爾賽文學」(fán'ěrsài wénxué),常直接簡稱為「凡爾賽」,作為形容詞或動詞使用。
  • 起源/歷史: 由微博部落客「小奶球」於 2020 年首創並開始整理、嘲諷一種炫富式的抱怨貼文,並以法國凡爾賽宮的奢華意象命名,更具體地說是取材自經典動畫《凡爾賽玫瑰》——劇中貴族角色總是理所當然地炫耀誇張的財富與享受。
  • 如何爆紅: 這位部落客整理的「教科書級範例」與戲仿的「凡爾賽文學鑑賞班」內容,在 2020 至 2021 年間迅速於微博、抖音上瘋傳,網友們開始競相辨認並模仿符合這種套路的真實貼文——一個小眾的觀察幾乎在一夕之間變成全民皆知的流行用語。
  • 意義與使用情境: 形容一種特定的炫富修辭公式:「先抑後揚」(先抱怨、鋪陳,再揭露令人稱羨的真相)、「自問自答」,以及刻意輕描淡寫的語氣(「其實還好啦」)——例如抱怨新買的柏金包「顏色跟我的私人飛機不太搭」。這個詞既可以用來形容真正的炫富者,也常被朋友之間開玩笑地用來吐槽對方明顯是在「抱怨式炫耀」。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出戲仿的「鑑賞班」帳號,持續解析新出現的範例;「凡爾賽」也演變成獨立的形容詞/動詞用法(如「你又在凡爾賽什麼」);英語媒體報導時也常將其類比為 Instagram 式的「炫耀」(flexing)文化,不過評論者指出中文版本更講究修辭套路、更具「文學性」。
  • 目前現況: 雖然熱度已不如 2020-2021 年高峰期那樣天天登上熱搜,但這個詞已成為中文網路詞彙中固定的一部分,每當有新的「不經意炫耀」貼文爆紅時,仍會被拿出來使用。
