4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 2: 坤 (Kūn) — "The Receptive" (Earth over Earth). Pure yin, the perfect complement to Hexagram 1 (乾, The Creative). It represents devoted, nurturing, adaptive strength — the power of the Earth that carries and supports all things by yielding rather than by force. The Judgment reads roughly: "The Receptive brings supreme success, furthering through the perseverance of a mare. If the superior person tries to lead, he goes astray; but if he follows, he finds guidance." Favorable for supportive, receptive action and letting go of the need to initiate — success comes through following a well-founded lead rather than forcing one's own.

5) Country / Region Introduction — Bhutan

  • Location: A landlocked kingdom in the Eastern Himalayas, sandwiched between China (Tibet Autonomous Region) to the north and India to the south, east, and west.
  • Size: About 38,394 km² — roughly the same size as Switzerland (41,285 km²) or the Netherlands, and slightly larger than Taiwan (~36,197 km²).
  • Population: Approximately 787,000 (one of the least populous countries in Asia).
  • Religion: Vajrayana Buddhism of the Drukpa Kagyu school is the state religion (~75% of the population); a Hindu minority (~23%), mostly of Nepali ethnic descent, practices Hinduism.
  • Economy: Small (GDP roughly US$3 billion), driven mainly by hydropower exports to India and high-value, low-volume tourism (visitors pay a Sustainable Development Fee). Bhutan is globally known for prioritizing a "Gross National Happiness" index over GDP in policymaking.
  • History: Unified as a single state in 1616 by the Tibetan lama Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal; became a hereditary monarchy in 1907 under Ugyen Wangchuck; transitioned to a constitutional monarchy in 2008 under King Jigme Singye Wangchuck.
  • Current leader: King (Druk Gyalpo) Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck (reigning since December 2006) as head of state; Prime Minister Tshering Tobgay (since January 2024) as head of government.
  • Language: Dzongkha is the official national language; numerous regional languages are spoken (e.g., Tshangla/Sharchopkha, Nepali), and English is widely used in schools and government.

6) Internet Meme — Doge

  • Name: "Doge" — a deliberately misspelled version of "dog."
  • Origin: Traces back to a 2010 blog photo of Kabosu, a female Shiba Inu adopted in 2008 by Japanese kindergarten teacher Atsuko Sato. A 2013 blog post captioned with fragmented, colorful phrases ("such amaze," "very wow") became the template.
  • How it became popular: Spread rapidly via Tumblr and Reddit's r/doge community in 2013, with the format standardized as the dog's photo surrounded by short, ungrammatical captions in multicolored Comic Sans font.
  • Meaning / usage: The captions represent the dog's fragmented "internal monologue," used to express naive excitement or wonder in an ironic, self-aware way; became a shorthand for internet-native, absurdist humor.
  • Variations / evolution: Directly inspired Dogecoin, the satirical cryptocurrency launched in December 2013 by Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer, which later became a major (and Elon Musk-associated) cryptocurrency in its own right. The 2020 "Swole Doge vs. Cheems" format split the character into a buff, articulate "classic Doge" contrasted with a small, degraded "Cheems" dog, used to compare an idealized past against a diminished present.
  • Current status: Kabosu, the original dog, died on May 24, 2024 at age 18 after battling leukemia and liver disease. The Doge meme itself is now considered a legacy/nostalgia meme rather than an actively trending format, but it remains a permanent fixture of internet culture and stays culturally relevant through Dogecoin's continued (and volatile) existence.

4) 易經卦象

第二卦:坤卦(Kūn)——「地」(上坤下坤)。純陰之卦,與第一卦(乾,創造)互為完美對應。象徵柔順、承載、滋養與順應的力量——大地承載萬物,不靠強力對抗,而是以柔順、包容成就一切。卦辭大意為:「坤,元亨,利牝馬之貞。君子有攸往,先迷後得主,利。」意即:若強行主導、搶先領導,容易迷失方向;若順勢而為、追隨正確的引導,反而能找到方向並獲得利益。今日宜以輔佐、包容、順應的態度行事,不強求主導,順勢而為方能成功。

5) 國家╱地區介紹 — 不丹

  • 地理位置: 位於東喜馬拉雅山區的內陸王國,北鄰中國西藏自治區,東、西、南三面與印度接壤。
  • 國土面積: 約 38,394 平方公里,大致與瑞士(41,285 平方公里)或荷蘭相近,略大於台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)。
  • 人口: 約 78.7 萬人,是亞洲人口最稀少的國家之一。
  • 宗教: 藏傳佛教竹巴噶舉派為國教,信眾約占人口 75%;約 23% 為印度教徒,多為尼泊爾裔少數族群。
  • 經濟: 經濟規模很小(GDP 約 30 億美元),主要仰賴對印度出口水力發電,以及「高價值、低流量」的觀光政策(旅客需繳交永續發展費)。不丹以「國民幸福總值(GNH)」聞名於世,施政以此取代單純追求 GDP 成長。
  • 歷史: 1616 年由西藏喇嘛夏尊·阿旺南嘉(Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal)統一建國;1907 年在烏顏·旺楚克(Ugyen Wangchuck)之下成為世襲君主國;2008 年在第四任國王吉美·辛格·旺楚克主導下,轉型為君主立憲制。
  • 現任領導人: 國王(竹嘉波)吉美·凱薩爾·南嘉·旺楚克(Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck,自 2006 年 12 月在位)為國家元首;總理策林·托傑(Tshering Tobgay,自 2024 年 1 月上任)為政府首腦。
  • 語言: 官方語言為宗卡語(Dzongkha);境內也通行多種地方語言(如夏喬卡語、尼泊爾語等),英語則廣泛使用於教育與政府體系。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Doge(狗狗幣哏圖 / 神犬迷因)

  • 名稱:「Doge」是英文 "dog"(狗)故意拼錯的寫法。
  • 起源: 源自 2010 年一篇部落格貼文中的照片,主角是柴犬「Kabosu」(かぼす),由日本幼稚園老師佐藤敦子(Atsuko Sato)於 2008 年領養。2013 年一篇部落格貼文以破碎、色彩繽紛的英文短語(如 "such amaze"、"very wow")為照片配文,成為迷因的雛形範本。
  • 如何爆紅: 2013 年透過 Tumblr 與 Reddit 的 r/doge 社群迅速擴散,格式逐漸固定為:柴犬照片周圍環繞著色彩繽紛的 Comic Sans 字型短句,句子刻意不合文法。
  • 意義與使用情境: 這些短句象徵狗狗腦中破碎的「內心獨白」,用來以自嘲、反諷的方式表達天真的興奮或驚嘆之情,逐漸成為網路原生荒謬幽默的代名詞。
  • 變體與演變: 直接催生了「狗狗幣」(Dogecoin)——由 Billy Markus 與 Jackson Palmer 於 2013 年 12 月創立、原本帶有諷刺意味的加密貨幣,後來卻發展成真正具影響力的加密貨幣(並與馬斯克產生強烈連結)。2020 年出現「壯碩 Doge vs. Cheems」變體,將角色一分為二:健壯、談吐得體的「經典 Doge」,對比矮小、行為退化的「Cheems」犬,常用來對比「理想化的過去」與「墮落的現在」。
  • 現況: 原型犬 Kabosu 於 2024 年 5 月 24 日因白血病與肝病去世,享壽 18 歲。Doge 迷因本身如今已被視為懷舊、傳承性質的「經典迷因」,而非仍在快速擴散的當紅哏,但它已成為網路文化中不可抹滅的一部分,並持續透過狗狗幣(Dogecoin)的存在(儘管幣值波動劇烈)維持文化能見度。
