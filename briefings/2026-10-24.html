3) Spanish Lesson — Day 100: Dar / Darse cuenta de / Dar igual / Dar ganas de

Day 100! Dar (to give) is irregular only in the yo form of the present: doy, das, da, damos, dais, dan. In the preterite it takes -er/-ir endings with no accents: di, diste, dio, dimos, disteis, dieron. Four everyday expressions: darse cuenta de (to realize; reflexive, always with de; not realizar, a false friend), dar igual (to not matter; works like gustar: Me da igual), dar ganas de + infinitive (to make you feel like: Me da ganas de celebrar) and dar las gracias (to thank).

  • ¿Te diste cuenta de que hoy es el día cien? — Did you realize today is day one hundred?
  • ¡No me di cuenta! Me da ganas de celebrar. — I didn't notice! I feel like celebrating.
  • ¿Vamos a un restaurante o cocinamos en casa? — Restaurant, or do we cook at home?
  • Me da igual. Lo que tú prefieras. — I don't mind. Whatever you prefer.
  • Entonces te doy las gracias por esta compañía. — Then I thank you for this company.

4) Daodejing — Chapter 3: 不尚賢 (Bù shàng xián) — "Do Not Exalt the Worthy"

Series note: this continues the Daodejing track begun on Oct 22, one chapter per day in order (chapters 1-81), never repeated.

Text: 不尚賢,使民不爭;不貴難得之貨,使民不為盜;不見可欲,使民心不亂。是以聖人之治,虛其心,實其腹,弱其志,強其骨。常使民無知無欲,使夫智者不敢為也。為無為,則無不治。

Plain-English rendering (paraphrase): If you do not exalt the "worthy", the people will not compete; if you do not prize goods that are hard to get, the people will not turn to theft; if you do not display what can be desired, the people's hearts will not be disturbed. Therefore the sage governs by emptying their hearts and filling their bellies, weakening their ambitions and strengthening their bones. He keeps the people without cunning knowledge and without craving, so the clever dare not meddle. Act through non-action, and nothing is left ungoverned.

Notes: Chapter 2 showed that naming creates opposites; Chapter 3 applies this to society. Ranking people as "worthy" creates a contest for rank, and treasuring rare goods creates theft; the problem is the artificial desire the display stirs up, not the people. "Empty the heart, fill the belly" is often read as satisfying real needs while quieting manufactured wants, not as keeping people ignorant, though some critics read the lines about "no knowledge, no desire" as authoritarian, and commentators disagree. The closing 為無為 (act through non-action) means acting without forcing, the thread running through Chapters 1-3.

Takeaway: Much of our restlessness is manufactured by what society displays and rewards; reducing the display reduces the craving.

  • Location & territory size: An island country in the central Mediterranean, about 93 km south of Sicily and roughly 290 km north of Libya. About 316 km² across the main islands (Malta, Gozo, Comino), roughly 1.2 times the area of Taipei City and under half the size of Singapore (approximate).

  • Economy: A high-income, services-based economy using the euro (since 2008): tourism, financial services, online gaming (iGaming), shipping and maritime services, and aviation maintenance. Growth has been among the fastest in the EU in recent years (approximate). Malta was on the FATF "grey list" from 2021 to 2022.
  • History: The Ġgantija temples on Gozo (c. 3600 BC) are among the oldest free-standing stone structures in the world. Later rulers included Phoenicians, Carthaginians and Romans (St. Paul's shipwreck is dated to about AD 60), Arabs (from 870), Normans (1091) and the Knights of St. John (1530-1798), who withstood the Great Siege of 1565 and built Valletta. Napoleon took the islands in 1798; Britain held them from 1800. Malta was heavily bombed in WWII and the people were awarded the George Cross (1942). Independence came on 21 September 1964; it became a republic on 13 December 1974 and joined the EU in 2004.
  • Current leader: President Myriam Spiteri Debono (since April 2024, a largely ceremonial role) and Prime Minister Robert Abela (Labour, since January 2020). Status not verified against a source today; an election is due by 2027, so check for changes.
  • Language: Maltese and English are both official. Maltese is the only Semitic language written in the Latin alphabet, descended from Siculo-Arabic with many Italian and English loanwords.
  • Culture & trivia: Valletta and the Hypogeum of Ħal-Saflieni are UNESCO World Heritage sites, and the Knights' legacy gives the flag its George Cross. Malta drives on the left, a British legacy.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Barbenheimer

  • Name: "Barbenheimer", a portmanteau of Barbie and Oppenheimer.
  • Origin / history: Warner Bros.' Barbie (dir. Greta Gerwig) and Universal's Oppenheimer (dir. Christopher Nolan) were scheduled for the same day, July 21, 2023. Online film fans started joking about the "counter-programming" months earlier, when the dates were set, since the films had opposite moods: a bright pink comedy versus a dark three-hour historical drama about the atomic bomb.
  • How it became popular: Social media users made mashup posters and memes, and film fans pledged to see both films on the same day as a double feature, usually Oppenheimer first and Barbie second. Both studios and many cinemas leaned into the joke instead of fighting it. The opening weekend was huge: about US$162 million for Barbie and about US$82 million for Oppenheimer in the US (approximate), one of the biggest combined openings in recent years.
  • Meaning / usage: It means seeing two very different films back to back, and more broadly any pairing of contrasting big releases that boosts both. People use it as a hashtag, a costume mashup (pink dresses with fedoras) and a shorthand for a shared cultural event.
  • Variations / evolution: The mashup spawned fan art, merchandise and parody trailers. The pattern was reused later, most notably "Glicked" for Wicked and Gladiator II in November 2024. Rival studios now often try to engineer such pairings.
  • Results: Barbie grossed roughly US$1.4 billion worldwide and Oppenheimer roughly US$950 million to US$1 billion (approximate). At the March 2024 Oscars, Oppenheimer won seven awards including Best Picture, Director and Actor; Barbie won Best Original Song.
  • Current status: A settled piece of pop-culture history. It is rarely a fresh joke now, but it is the standard example of how a meme can turn a scheduling clash into a marketing win.

7) Film / Performing-Arts Spotlight — Bizet's Carmen (1875)

  • Name: Carmen, an opéra comique in four acts by Georges Bizet.
  • Creators / key figures: Music by Georges Bizet (1838-1875); libretto by Henri Meilhac and Ludovic Halévy, based on Prosper Mérimée's 1845 novella. The recitatives used in most modern stagings were added after Bizet's death by his friend Ernest Guiraud for the Vienna production.
  • Era / background: It premiered at the Opéra-Comique in Paris on 3 March 1875. The first reception was mixed; some critics and the audience were shocked by a lead character who is a working-class, free-willed woman and by an onstage murder, in a house known for family audiences. Bizet died on 3 June 1875, aged 36, believing it a failure, before it became a worldwide hit.
  • Plot: In Seville around 1820, the soldier Don José is drawn to Carmen, a Roma cigarette-factory worker who sings the "Habanera" ("L'amour est un oiseau rebelle"). He abandons his gentle fiancée Micaëla and his career, joins Carmen's smugglers, and is humiliated when she turns to the bullfighter Escamillo (whose "Toreador Song" is famous). Outside the bullring, as the crowd cheers Escamillo, José kills Carmen after she refuses to return to him.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Vivid Spanish colour (the Habanera is adapted from a song by Sebastián Iradier; there are seguidillas and gypsy dances), fast, theatrical orchestration, and unusually direct, realistic characters instead of gods and kings, which foreshadowed Italian verismo. Carmen is traditionally sung by a mezzo-soprano, and recurring "fate" motifs warn of the ending.
  • Cultural / historical significance: It helped move opera toward realism and put a free, unrepentant woman at the centre of the story, which is still debated for how it treats gender and violence. Tchaikovsky and Nietzsche were early admirers. It is one of the most performed operas in the world.
  • Influence and legacy: Oscar Hammerstein II's all-Black-cast Carmen Jones (1943 stage musical; 1954 film dir. Otto Preminger); Peter Brook's La Tragédie de Carmen (1981); Carlos Saura's flamenco film Carmen (1983); Rodion Shchedrin's Carmen Suite ballet (1967); Matthew Bourne's The Car Man (2000).
  • Current status / where to experience: Staged each season by major houses such as the Metropolitan Opera, the Paris Opéra and many regional companies, and often streamed in cinemas and online (e.g. Met Opera on Demand). Details of current productions were not checked today.
  • Notes: General knowledge, not verified against a source today.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 86: Negative Space, Headroom & Look Room

What it is: Composition is not only about what is in the frame but how much empty space surrounds it and where that space sits. Negative space is the empty area around a subject. Headroom is the gap above a head; look room (or "lead room") is the space in front of a character in the direction they face or move. Standard practice leaves comfortable room ahead of the subject; withholding it (a character jammed against the frame's edge, cut-off heads, tiny figures in a vast void) creates unease, vulnerability or dread, and a big empty area behind or beside a subject can suggest absence, loneliness or something about to enter.

How to analyze: (1) Pause on a shot and divide the frame mentally: how much is subject, how much is empty? (2) Note where the empty space sits relative to the character's gaze and movement: in front (comfortable, open future) or behind (pursuit, looking back) or tightly cropped (trapped). (3) Ask what the emptiness is "about": is it room for something to enter, a sign of isolation, or time stretched out? (4) Check whether the emptiness changes when the situation changes. (5) Remember emptiness is paired with sound: silence or small noises often fill the void.

Worked example — Once Upon a Time in the West (1968, dir. Sergio Leone, cinematography Tonino Delli Colli, music Ennio Morricone), the Cattle Corner station opening: Three gunmen (played by Jack Elam, Al Mulock and Woody Strode) wait at a rural train station for a man who is due on the next train. Leone holds the scene for roughly ten minutes with almost no dialogue. Wide shots show small figures dwarfed by the bare platform and sky, and extreme close-ups of faces and details (a windmill's creak, water dripping onto a hat, a fly buzzing around a gun barrel) alternate with them. The emptiness is the tension: the screen is full of space where something must happen. When the train leaves, the man they are waiting for (Harmonica, Charles Bronson) is revealed standing alone in the open space across the tracks, and the gunfight is over in seconds. Leone uses the huge negative space to stretch time, and he reverses expectations by placing the threat in the empty part of the frame, where we did not expect it. Notice that the scene works almost without score: the sounds of the place do the work.

Try it: In your next film, pick one conversation and check the look room of each character: who is given space ahead of them and who is cramped against the frame edge? Then find one shot where the empty area clearly matters and say what it is "waiting" for.

4) 道德經 — 第 3 章:不尚賢 (Bù shàng xián) —「不推崇賢能」

系列說明:延續 10/22 開始的道德經單元,每天依序一章(共 81 章),不重複。

原文:不尚賢,使民不爭;不貴難得之貨,使民不為盜;不見可欲,使民心不亂。是以聖人之治,虛其心,實其腹,弱其志,強其骨。常使民無知無欲,使夫智者不敢為也。為無為,則無不治。

白話翻譯(意譯):不推崇所謂「賢能」,人民就不會互相爭奪;不看重稀有難得的財貨,人民就不會去偷盜;不展示那些引人慾望的東西,人民的心就不會被擾亂。所以聖人治理,是讓人民心思清虛、肚子吃飽,削弱爭強的志向、強健筋骨。讓人民沒有機巧的知識與過多的慾望,使聰明人也不敢妄為。以「無為」的方式去做,就沒有治理不好的事。

說明:第 2 章說明「命名會製造對立」,第 3 章把這個道理用在社會上:把人排成「賢」與「不賢」,就會引發爭名;珍藏稀有財貨,就會引發偷盜;問題在於展示所激起的人為慾望,而不是人民本身。「虛其心,實其腹」常被解讀為滿足真正的需要、平息被製造出來的慾望,而不是要人民愚昧;不過「無知無欲」這幾句也有人批評帶有威權色彩,注釋家看法不一。結尾的「為無為」是指不強求地行動,是第 1 至 3 章一貫的主線。

一句話帶走:我們很多躁動是被社會的展示與獎勵製造出來的;減少展示,就能減少渴求。

  • 歷史:戈佐島的吉干提亞(Ġgantija)神廟(約西元前 3600 年)是世上最古老的獨立石造建築之一。後來歷經腓尼基人、迦太基人、羅馬人(聖保羅的船難約在西元 60 年)、阿拉伯人(870 年起)、諾曼人(1091 年)與聖約翰騎士團(1530-1798 年)統治;騎士團撐過 1565 年的大圍城並興建瓦萊塔。1798 年拿破崙占領,1800 年起由英國統治。二戰期間遭到猛烈轟炸,全體人民獲頒喬治十字勳章(1942 年)。1964 年 9 月 21 日獨立,1974 年 12 月 13 日成為共和國,2004 年加入歐盟。
  • 現任領導人:總統米莉安·斯皮泰里·德博諾(Myriam Spiteri Debono,2024 年 4 月就任,職務以象徵性為主)與總理羅伯特·阿貝拉(Robert Abela,工黨,2020 年 1 月起)。今日未以來源驗證;2027 年前須舉行大選,請留意是否有變動。
  • 語言:馬爾他語與英語皆為官方語言。馬爾他語是唯一以拉丁字母書寫的閃米特語,源自西西里阿拉伯語,並有大量義大利語與英語借詞。
  • 文化與小知識:瓦萊塔與哈薩夫利尼地下墓穴(Ħal-Saflieni Hypogeum)是聯合國教科文組織世界遺產,喬治十字勳章也出現在國旗上。馬爾他靠左行駛,這是英國留下的習慣。
  • 備註:以上皆為一般常識,今日未以來源查證;政治與人口資料尤其可能已有變動。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Barbenheimer(芭比海默)

  • 名稱:「Barbenheimer」,由 Barbie(芭比)與 Oppenheimer(奧本海默)合成的混成詞。
  • 起源 / 歷史:華納兄弟的《芭比》(導演葛莉塔·潔薇)與環球影業的《奧本海默》(導演克里斯多福·諾蘭)被安排在同一天上映:2023 年 7 月 21 日。早在檔期公布的數個月前,網路影迷就開始拿這場「對打」開玩笑,因為兩部片氣氛完全相反:一部是鮮亮粉紅的喜劇,一部是黑暗、三小時長的原子彈歷史劇。
  • 如何爆紅:網友製作合成海報與迷因,影迷相約同一天連看兩部,通常先看《奧本海默》再看《芭比》。兩家片商與許多戲院都順勢配合,而不是對抗。首週末開出巨大成績:美國國內《芭比》約 1.62 億美元、《奧本海默》約 8,200 萬美元(概略),是近年最大的合計首週末之一。
  • 意義 / 使用情境:指連續看兩部風格迥異的電影,更廣義指兩部對比強烈的大片同期上映、彼此帶動買氣。常見用法包括 hashtag、角色混搭造型(粉紅洋裝配軟呢帽)以及「共同文化事件」的代稱。
  • 變體 / 演變:這股風潮衍生出同人畫、周邊與惡搞預告片。後來的例子是 2024 年 11 月《魔法壞女巫》與《神鬼戰士 II》的「Glicked」。如今片商也常試圖刻意製造這類配對。
  • 成果:《芭比》全球票房約 14 億美元,《奧本海默》約 9.5 億至 10 億美元(概略)。2024 年 3 月奧斯卡,《奧本海默》拿下七項大獎,包括最佳影片、導演與男主角;《芭比》拿下最佳原創歌曲。
  • 現況:已成為流行文化史的一頁。如今很少再當新鮮梗使用,但它仍是「檔期撞期如何變成行銷勝利」的標準範例。
  • 備註:票房與獎項數字來自一般常識,今日未以來源查證。

7) 電影 / 表演藝術介紹 — 比才的《卡門》(1875)

  • 名稱:《卡門》(Carmen),喬治·比才(Georges Bizet)作曲的四幕喜歌劇。
  • 創作者 / 關鍵人物:作曲者比才(1838-1875);劇本由亨利·梅亞克(Henri Meilhac)與呂多維克·阿萊維(Ludovic Halévy)改編自普羅斯佩·梅里美 1845 年的中篇小說。現代多數製作使用的宣敘調,是比才去世後由好友歐內斯特·吉羅(Ernest Guiraud)為維也納版本所補寫。
  • 時代背景:1875 年 3 月 3 日在巴黎喜歌劇院(Opéra-Comique)首演。初期評價褒貶不一:男主角之外的女主角是個勞動階層、意志自由的女子,又在以家庭觀眾聞名的劇院裡上演殺人情節,讓部分評論與觀眾震驚。比才在 1875 年 6 月 3 日去世,享年 36 歲,他到死都以為這是一次失敗,沒看到它後來紅遍全球。
  • 劇情:約 1820 年的塞維亞,士兵唐荷西被在捲菸工廠工作的羅姆女子卡門吸引,她唱出〈哈巴奈拉〉(「愛情是隻叛逆的鳥」)。他拋下溫柔的未婚妻米凱拉與軍旅生涯,加入卡門的走私集團,而當卡門轉向鬥牛士艾斯卡米里歐(著名的〈鬥牛士之歌〉)時,他備受屈辱。在鬥牛場外,群眾為艾斯卡米里歐歡呼之際,卡門拒絕回到他身邊,荷西將她殺死。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:鮮明的西班牙色彩(〈哈巴奈拉〉改編自塞巴斯蒂安·伊拉迪耶的歌曲;另有塞吉迪亞舞曲與吉普賽舞曲)、輕快而戲劇化的配器,以及不寫神與國王而寫直接、寫實的人物,預告了義大利真實主義(verismo)。卡門傳統上由次女高音演唱,反覆出現的「命運」動機預告了結局。
  • 文化與歷史意義:它推動歌劇走向寫實,並把一位自由、不悔改的女子放在故事中心,其對性別與暴力的處理至今仍有爭論。柴可夫斯基與尼采是早期的愛好者。它是全球演出次數最多的歌劇之一。
  • 影響與傳承:漢默斯坦二世的全黑人演員《卡門·瓊斯》(1943 年舞台劇;1954 年電影,導演奧托·普雷明格);彼得·布魯克的《卡門的悲劇》(1981);卡洛斯·紹拉的佛朗明哥電影《卡門》(1983);羅季翁·謝德林的《卡門組曲》芭蕾(1967);馬修·伯恩的《The Car Man》(2000)。
  • 現況 / 哪裡可以欣賞:大都會歌劇院、巴黎歌劇院與許多地方劇團每季都有製作,也常在電影院與線上轉播(例如 Met Opera on Demand)。今日未查證各製作的最新檔期。
  • 備註:一般常識,今日未以來源查證。

8) 看片方法 — 第 86 課:留白、頭上空間與視線空間

這是什麼:構圖不只看畫面裡有什麼,也要看周圍留了多少空白、空白在哪一側。留白(negative space)是主體周圍的空白區域;頭上空間(headroom)是人物頭頂上方的空隙;視線空間(look room / lead room)是人物面向或移動方向前方的空間。一般做法是在主體前方留足空間;刻意不給(人物被擠到畫面邊緣、頭被切掉、廣大虛空裡的小人影)會製造不安、脆弱或恐懼,而主體後方或旁邊的一大片空白則可能暗示缺席、孤獨,或是有什麼即將進入畫面。

怎麼分析:(1) 暫停在一個鏡頭,在腦中把畫面分區:主體占多少、空白占多少?(2) 看空白相對於人物視線與動作的位置:在前方(舒適、未來開闊)、在後方(被追趕、回頭望),還是被緊緊裁切(被困住)?(3) 問這片空白「在說什麼」:是留給某事物進入的空間、孤立感,還是被拉長的時間?(4) 注意情勢變化時,空白是否也跟著改變。(5) 別忘了空白常搭配聲音:靜默或細小的聲響會填滿空白。

實例 —《狂沙十萬里》(Once Upon a Time in the West,1968,導演塞吉歐·李昂尼,攝影托尼諾·德利·柯利,配樂恩尼歐·莫利克奈),牛欄角車站開場:三名槍手(傑克·艾蘭、艾爾·穆洛克、伍迪·斯特羅德飾演)在偏遠的火車站等待搭下一班火車而來的男人。李昂尼把這場戲拉長到約十分鐘,幾乎沒有對白。寬鏡頭裡,小小的人影被空曠的月台與天空壓得很渺小,並與臉部與細節的極端特寫交替出現(風車的嘎吱聲、水滴在帽子上、蒼蠅繞著槍管嗡嗡飛)。留白就是張力:畫面裡充滿「某事必然發生」的空間。火車離開後,他們等的人(口琴客,查理斯·布朗遜飾)被揭示獨自站在鐵軌對面的空地上,而槍戰在幾秒內結束。李昂尼用龐大的留白拉長時間,並把威脅放在畫面空白處,在我們沒預期的地方來顛覆期待。請注意這場戲幾乎沒有配樂:場景的聲響本身在發揮作用。

練習:下次看電影時,挑一段對話,檢查每個角色的視線空間:誰在前方被給了空間、誰被擠到畫面邊緣?再找一個空白明顯有意義的鏡頭,說說它「在等」什麼。
