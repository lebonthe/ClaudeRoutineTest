3) Spanish Lesson — Day 62 (10 min, Reported Speech: El Estilo Indirecto)

Steps outside the tense/mood/pronoun/se-family/obligation/knowledge/possessive tracks (Days 46-61) into a capstone topic that deliberately reuses nearly every tense already taught: converting a direct quotation (Estilo Directo, "Juan dice: 'Estudio español.'") into reported speech (Estilo Indirecto, "Juan dijo que estudiaba español"). The central rule is the secuencia de tiempos (sequence of tenses): when the reporting verb (decir, contar, preguntar, explicar...) is itself in a past tense, every tense inside the quoted material must "shift back" one step — Presente → Imperfecto; Pretérito Indefinido/Perfecto → Pluscuamperfecto (Day 22); Futuro Simple → Condicional Simple (Day 24); and an Imperativo (Days 25-26) becomes a Subjuntivo Imperfecto (Day 35) clause. Reported yes/no questions add "si" ("¿Vienes?" → preguntó si vendría); reported information questions keep their question word but drop the inversion/question marks ("¿Qué haces?" → preguntó qué hacía). A parallel set of deictic words must also shift to match the new, later vantage point of the retelling: aquí→allí, este→ese, hoy→ese día, mañana→al día siguiente, ayer→el día anterior.

  • Juan dice: "Estudio español." → Juan dijo que estudiaba español. — Juan said (that) he was studying Spanish.
  • María preguntó: "¿Vienes mañana?" → María preguntó si vendría al día siguiente. — María asked if I would come the next day.
  • El profesor dijo: "Hagan la tarea." → El profesor dijo que hiciéramos la tarea. — The teacher said (that) we should do the homework.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 62: 小過 (Xiǎo Guò) — "Preponderance of the Small" (Thunder over Mountain) (雷山小過 — Zhèn 震/Thunder above, Gèn 艮/Mountain below). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the line directly from the previous hexagram: "有其信者必行之,故受之以小過。" ("One who possesses [Hexagram 61's] inner truth must inevitably act upon it; therefore Zhōng Fú is followed by Xiǎo Guò.") Genuine sincerity, once fully internalized, cannot remain merely internal — it must be carried into action; but the action that follows directly from such deep, quiet conviction is necessarily modest and small in scale, not a grand or ambitious undertaking, which is exactly what "the small exceeding" (a small excess, a slight overstepping, permissible only in small affairs) names. Structurally, the hexagram's six lines (from bottom to top: yin, yin, yang, yang, yin, yin) form a shape traditionally read as a flying bird in outline — two firm (yang) lines as the body flanked by yielding (yin) lines as the outstretched wings above and below — which is why the Judgment invokes exactly that image: "小過,亨,利貞,可小事,不可大事。飛鳥遺之音,不宜上,宜下,大吉。" ("Xiǎo Guò. Success. Favorable to be steadfast and correct. Small affairs may be undertaken; great affairs may not. The flying bird leaves behind only its sound [it has nowhere solid to land or build]; it is not fitting to soar upward, but fitting to descend — great good fortune.") A bird that keeps climbing exhausts itself in open air with nothing to show for the ascent but a fading sound; a bird that comes down to earth finds safety and rest. The Image commentary reads: "山上有雷,小過;君子以行過乎恭,喪過乎哀,用過乎儉。" ("Thunder above the mountain: this is Xiǎo Guò. Thus the superior person, in conduct, leans toward excessive respectfulness; in mourning, leans toward excessive grief; in expenditure, leans toward excessive frugality.") In each of these three cases, deliberately erring slightly on the humble, restrained side of a virtue is itself the correct, careful response to a time defined by smallness and caution — not a flaw to correct, but the precise character of right action when circumstances call for staying low and modest rather than reaching for the great and lofty. Read against Hexagram 61, the pair traces a complete arc: an inner truth so genuine it moves even the most unlikely hearts (Hexagram 61) must be acted upon — but the right action for such a moment is deliberately small, humble, and grounded, favoring descent over ascent (Hexagram 62).

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Poland (波蘭)

  • Location: Central Europe, bordered by Germany to the west, the Czech Republic and Slovakia to the south, Ukraine and Belarus to the east, Lithuania and Russia's Kaliningrad exclave to the northeast, with a Baltic Sea coastline to the north.
  • Territory: About 312,696 km² — roughly 8.6 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), comparable in size to Italy (~301,340 km²) or the US state of New Mexico (~314,915 km²), making it the sixth-largest country in the European Union by area.
  • Population: Approximately 37.6 million as of 2026 — about 1.6 times Taiwan's population — concentrated in the capital Warsaw and major cities including Kraków, Wrocław, and Gdańsk.
  • Religion: Predominantly Roman Catholic (roughly 85% nominal affiliation), though regular church attendance, especially among younger Poles, has declined markedly in recent years; small Orthodox and Protestant minorities remain.
  • Economy: The EU's sixth-largest economy by nominal GDP and one of its fastest-growing major economies over the past two decades, built on a strong manufacturing, automotive-parts, and electronics export base alongside a large services sector; a major net beneficiary of EU structural funds since 2004 accession; still uses its own currency, the Polish złoty, rather than the euro.
  • History: Traces statehood to the Piast dynasty (10th century) and later formed the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth (16th-18th centuries), at its peak one of Europe's largest and most populous states with an unusually early elective-monarchy/parliamentary tradition; partitioned three times among Russia, Prussia, and Austria in the late 18th century, erasing Poland from the map for 123 years; independence was restored in 1918. Nazi Germany's 1939 invasion (jointly with the Soviet Union) triggered the Second World War in Europe; Poland suffered roughly six million deaths, including the near-total destruction of its Jewish population in the Holocaust. Postwar Poland fell under Soviet-aligned communist rule; the Solidarność (Solidarity) trade-union movement led by Lech Wałęsa in the 1980s became the first independent labor union in the Eastern Bloc and a driving force behind the 1989 collapse of Polish communism, a pivotal event that helped trigger the wider fall of communism across Eastern Europe. Poland joined NATO in 1999 and the European Union in 2004.
  • Current leader: President Karol Nawrocki, an independent backed by the Law and Justice (PiS) party, in office since August 2025; Prime Minister Donald Tusk of the Civic Coalition (KO), heading a coalition government since December 2023 — the two hold differing political alignments, a "cohabitation" dynamic that has shaped several recent domestic policy disputes.
  • Language: Polish, a West Slavic language written in the Latin alphabet with a distinctive set of nasal vowels and consonant clusters.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (Karol Nawrocki), en.wikipedia.org (Donald Tusk)

  • Variations/evolution: The alternating-case "mocking text" formatting style took on a life independent of the SpongeBob image itself — often called "sPoNgEbOb CaSe" or "mOcKiNg tExT" — and is now used across countless platforms and contexts even without the accompanying picture, including dedicated online text-generator tools that convert any typed sentence into the format automatically.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Kecak (Balinese "Ramayana Monkey Chant")

  • Name/key figures: Kecak (also spelled "Ketjak," widely nicknamed the "Ramayana Monkey Chant" for English-speaking audiences), a form of Balinese dance-drama distinguished by an all-vocal chanted accompaniment with no gamelan instruments at all; developed in its present theatrical form in the early 1930s by Balinese dancer and choreographer Wayan Limbak, working in close collaboration with German painter and musician Walter Spies, a key figure in shaping mid-20th-century Western artistic engagement with Bali.
  • Era/background: Adapted from the older Sanghyang, a Balinese trance-ritual chant traditionally used for spiritual exorcism and communal purification; in the early 1930s, Limbak and Spies reworked the Sanghyang's distinctive vocal "cak" chant into a new, staged narrative performance built around the Hindu epic Ramayana, deliberately shaped to be legible and appealing to a broader audience, including the foreign visitors then beginning to arrive in Bali in growing numbers.
  • Plot overview: Depicts episodes from the Ramayana centered on Prince Rama, his wife Sita, his brother Lakshmana, the loyal monkey general Hanuman, and the demon king Ravana, who abducts Sita; performances typically build toward Hanuman's fire ordeal and Sita's eventual rescue.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Dozens to well over a hundred bare-chested male performers sit in tight concentric circles, chanting the syllable "cak" in rapid, interlocking polyrhythmic patterns that function collectively as a substitute for an entire gamelan orchestra, while swaying their raised arms and torsos in unison; a small group of costumed dancers within the circle enacts the principal Ramayana characters. Many staged versions append a trance-derived "fire dance" finale, in which a performer in trance walks on or kicks apart a pile of burning coconut husks, drawing directly on the original Sanghyang ritual's exorcistic core.
  • Cultural/historical significance: One of Bali's most internationally recognizable performing arts and a frequently cited case study in the complicated, mutually-shaping relationship between "traditional" performing arts and 20th-century Western tourism and patronage — a genuinely old ritual chant reworked, with real Western involvement, into a new staged form that nonetheless became a durable and deeply-rooted part of contemporary Balinese cultural identity; recognized as part of Indonesia's intangible cultural heritage.
  • Influence and legacy: Its stripped-down, purely vocal and physical circular staging — no instruments, no sets, only massed human voices and bodies — has been cited as an influence on later international theatrical and choreographic works seeking a comparably minimal, ensemble-driven aesthetic.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Performed daily for visitors across Bali, most famously at sunset on the clifftop grounds of Uluwatu Temple, where the Indian Ocean horizon behind the performers is itself part of the staging, as well as regularly in Ubud and at other cultural venues across the island.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (Walter Spies)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Axial Cutting / "The Kubrick Cut" (Lesson 48)

Axial cutting is a cut between two or more shots of the same subject, framed from the same camera angle along the same sightline (axis) but at a different distance or focal length, producing an abrupt jump toward or away from the subject rather than a smooth, continuous camera movement. This deliberately violates the conventional continuity-editing rule of thumb that successive shots of the same subject should change camera angle by a meaningful amount (often cited as at least roughly 30 degrees) to avoid reading as an accidental, jarring "mistake" cut. Axial cutting is distinct from Day 11's Match Cut & Graphic Match, which links two different subjects or shots via a shared graphic shape rather than jumping distance on one unchanging subject and axis; from Day 36's Dolly Zoom/"Vertigo Effect," which achieves a related but different disorienting effect through one continuous shot with simultaneous, opposed dolly-and-zoom movement rather than a hard cut at all; from Day 24's Jump Cut, which exposes a discontinuity in time within an otherwise static, single-angle shot rather than deliberately changing distance/scale along a fixed sightline; and from Day 13's Shot Scale, which simply names different shot sizes as vocabulary without addressing this specific same-axis cutting technique. When analyzing a scene for axial cuts, check whether the cuts punch in toward the subject or pull back away from it, whether the camera stays exactly on-axis or drifts slightly between cuts, how many successive axial cuts are chained together and whether they accelerate in pace, and what psychological effect the sudden, unsmoothed proximity produces compared to what an equivalent, gradual dolly-in covering the same physical distance would feel like instead.

Worked example: The Shining (1980, dir. Stanley Kubrick) — a director whose recurring use of this technique across his filmography has made "the Kubrick cut" a common shorthand name for it. In the scene where young Danny Torrance, riding his tricycle down the Overlook Hotel's corridors, rounds a corner to find the ghostly Grady twins standing motionless at the far end of the hallway, Kubrick cuts in a rapid series of axial shots straight down that same corridor toward the twins, each cut bringing them noticeably nearer to camera without any dolly or zoom bridging the visible gap between positions. The jaggedness of the jump itself — rather than a smooth, creeping push-in that would let the audience adjust gradually — is what manufactures the moment's dread, turning what could have been an ordinary "get closer to the scary thing" camera move into a series of small, mechanical intrusions on the frame that feel closer to a shock than a reveal.

3)西班牙文課程——第 62 天(10 分鐘,轉述句/間接引語:El Estilo Indirecto)

今天跳出第 46-61 天的時態/語氣/代名詞/se 家族/義務/知曉動詞/所有格脈絡,進入一個刻意重新運用幾乎所有已教過時態的「總複習」主題:如何把直接引語(Estilo Directo,「Juan dice: 'Estudio español.'」)轉換成間接引語(Estilo Indirecto,「Juan dijo que estudiaba español」)。核心規則是「時態序列」(secuencia de tiempos):當轉述動詞(decir、contar、preguntar、explicar 等)本身是過去式時,被引述內容裡的每個時態都必須「往後退一格」——現在式 → 過去未完成式;簡單過去式/現在完成式 → 過去完成式(第 22 天);未來簡單式 → 條件式簡單式(第 24 天);祈使語氣(第 25-26 天)則轉為虛擬式過去未完了式(第 35 天)子句。轉述「是/否」問句時要加上「si」(「¿Vienes?」→ preguntó si vendría);轉述疑問詞問句時保留疑問詞本身,但拿掉倒裝語序與問號(「¿Qué haces?」→ preguntó qué hacía)。與此同時,一整組指示詞/時間副詞也必須跟著轉換,以配合轉述當下、時間點已經往後推移的新視角:aquí(這裡)→ allí(那裡)、este(這個)→ ese(那個)、hoy(今天)→ ese día(那一天)、mañana(明天)→ al día siguiente(隔天)、ayer(昨天)→ el día anterior(前一天)。

  • Juan dice: "Estudio español." → Juan dijo que estudiaba español. —— 璜安說他在學西班牙文。
  • María preguntó: "¿Vienes mañana?" → María preguntó si vendría al día siguiente. —— 瑪麗亞問我隔天是否會來。
  • El profesor dijo: "Hagan la tarea." → El profesor dijo que hiciéramos la tarea. —— 老師說我們該做作業。

4)易經卦象

第 62 卦:小過 (Xiǎo Guò) ——「小的過度」(雷山小過)(雷山小過——上震下艮:上卦為震〔雷〕,下卦為艮〔山〕。)《序卦傳》承接前一卦而來:「有其信者必行之,故受之以小過。」(擁有〔第 61 卦所言的〕內心真誠的人,必然會將其付諸行動,因此中孚卦之後,接的是小過卦。)真正的真誠,一旦被徹底內化,便無法只停留在內心層面——必須付諸行動;但緊接著如此深沉、內斂的信念而來的行動,必然是規模小、態度謙遜的,而非宏大或野心勃勃的舉措,這正是「小過」(小小的過度、輕微的越界,僅適用於小事而非大事)這個卦名所要傳達的。從結構上來看,此卦六爻(由下至上依序為:陰、陰、陽、陽、陰、陰)組成的形狀,傳統上被解讀為一隻展翅飛行中的鳥的輪廓——中間兩條陽爻(剛爻)構成鳥的身軀,上下各由陰爻(柔爻)構成展開的雙翼——這正是卦辭之所以直接援引「飛鳥」意象的原因:「小過,亨,利貞,可小事,不可大事。飛鳥遺之音,不宜上,宜下,大吉。」(小過卦。亨通。適宜堅守正道。適合處理小事,不適合處理大事。飛鳥飛翔時只留下聲音〔牠沒有堅實之處可以降落或築巢〕;不宜向上高飛,適宜向下棲息——大吉。)一隻不斷向上攀升的鳥,只會在空中耗盡體力,除了漸漸消逝的鳴叫聲外一無所獲;而一隻願意降落地面的鳥,則能覓得安穩與棲息之地。《象傳》寫道:「山上有雷,小過;君子以行過乎恭,喪過乎哀,用過乎儉。」(山上響起雷聲,這就是小過卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,在行為舉止上,寧可偏向過度恭敬;在喪禮之事上,寧可偏向過度哀傷;在日常用度上,寧可偏向過度節儉。)在這三種情況中,刻意在某項美德上略微偏向謙遜、節制的一方,本身正是面對「小」與「謹慎」這個時代主題時,最為正確且審慎的應對之道——這並非需要被糾正的缺點,而正是當外在環境要求保持低調、謙遜,而非追求宏大崇高之時,「正確行動」該有的樣貌。若與第 61 卦對照,兩卦恰好勾勒出一段完整的歷程:一種真摯到足以感動最遲鈍人心的內在真誠(第 61 卦),必然要付諸行動——但適合這個時刻的行動,卻是刻意保持小巧、謙遜、腳踏實地,寧可向下棲息,也不強求向上高飛(第 62 卦)。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——波蘭(Poland)

  • 位置:位於中歐,西鄰德國,南接捷克與斯洛伐克,東鄰烏克蘭與白俄羅斯,東北方與立陶宛及俄羅斯的飛地加里寧格勒接壤,北側濱臨波羅的海。
  • 領土:面積約 312,696 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 8.6 倍,與義大利(約 301,340 平方公里)或美國新墨西哥州(約 314,915 平方公里)大致相當,以面積計為歐盟第六大國。
  • 人口:截至 2026 年,人口約 3,760 萬人——約為台灣人口的 1.6 倍——主要集中於首都華沙,以及克拉科夫、弗羅茨瓦夫、格但斯克等主要城市。
  • 宗教:以羅馬天主教為絕對主流(名義上信仰人口約占 85%),惟近年來、尤其是年輕世代的實際上教堂頻率已明顯下滑;境內另有規模較小的東正教與新教社群。
  • 經濟:以名目 GDP 計為歐盟第六大經濟體,過去二十年間也是歐盟成長最快的主要經濟體之一,建立在強大的製造業、汽車零組件與電子產品出口基礎,以及龐大的服務業之上;自 2004 年加入歐盟以來,長期是歐盟結構性基金的主要淨受益國之一;目前仍使用自身貨幣波蘭茲羅提(złoty),尚未採用歐元。
  • 歷史:波蘭的立國歷史可追溯至皮雅斯特王朝(10 世紀),其後形成的波蘭立陶宛聯邦(16 至 18 世紀),鼎盛時期曾是歐洲面積最大、人口最多的國家之一,並擁有罕見的早期選王制/議會傳統;18 世紀末,波蘭遭俄羅斯、普魯士與奧地利三度瓜分,自世界地圖上消失長達 123 年,直到 1918 年才重獲獨立。1939 年納粹德國(與蘇聯聯手)入侵波蘭,揭開歐洲戰場的第二次世界大戰序幕;波蘭在戰爭中損失約 600 萬人,猶太人口更幾乎在大屠殺中遭到滅絕。二戰後,波蘭淪為蘇聯陣營下的共產政權;1980 年代由萊赫·華勒沙(Lech Wałęsa)領導的「團結工聯」(Solidarność)運動,成為東方集團第一個獨立工會,並成為推動 1989 年波蘭共產政權垮台的關鍵力量,這一事件更進一步促成了整個東歐共產陣營的骨牌式瓦解。波蘭於 1999 年加入北約,並於 2004 年加入歐盟。
  • 現任領導人:總統卡羅爾·納夫羅茨基(Karol Nawrocki),獨立人士、獲法律與公正黨(PiS)支持,自 2025 年 8 月起就任;總理唐納德·圖斯克(Donald Tusk),來自公民聯盟(KO),自 2023 年 12 月起領導聯合政府——兩人政治立場並不一致,形成的「共治」(cohabitation)格局,也塑造了近期多項國內政策爭議的樣貌。
  • 語言:波蘭語,屬西斯拉夫語族,以拉丁字母書寫,具有獨特的鼻化母音與子音群結構。

6)網路迷因介紹——嘲諷海綿寶寶(Mocking SpongeBob)

  • 名稱/起源:「嘲諷海綿寶寶」迷因,取自卡通《海綿寶寶》第 8 季(2012 年)〈小黃書〉("Little Yellow Book")一集中的單一截圖:畫面中海綿寶寶彎腰駝背、伸長舌頭、雙臂彎曲擺出誇張姿勢,正在用嘲弄的語氣,學章魚哥用尖細嗓音朗讀自己日記的模樣。
  • 如何走紅:這張截圖大約於 2017 年在 Tumblr 與 Twitter 上重新被挖出,並搭配以「大小寫交錯」方式呈現的文字(「StUpId TeXt LiKe THIS」),以視覺方式呈現一種嘲弄、拉高尾音模仿他人說話的語氣。此迷因於 2017 年間迅速在 Twitter、Reddit 與 4chan 上擴散,成為當年最具辨識度的反應圖迷因之一。
  • 意義/使用情境:用來以嘲諷、輕蔑的語氣貶低某個說法——典型用法是在回覆貼文中,引用對方的論點並以「大小寫交錯」的嘲諷文字格式重新呈現,暗示對方的說法愚蠢、無病呻吟,不值得認真反駁;是網路論戰與政治/社群媒體討論中常見的固定招式。
  • 變體/演化:這種「大小寫交錯嘲諷文字」的格式,後來發展出獨立於海綿寶寶圖片之外的生命力——常被稱作「sPoNgEbOb CaSe」或「mOcKiNg tExT」——如今即使不搭配原始圖片,也廣泛應用於各種平台與情境,甚至出現專門的線上文字產生工具,可自動將任何輸入的句子轉換成這種格式。
  • 目前狀態:距首次爆紅已近十年,至今仍被廣泛且頻繁使用,是網路史上最歷久不衰的反應圖/文字格式迷因之一;偶爾也會被各大品牌、甚至《海綿寶寶》官方社群媒體帳號,以一種心照不宣的方式主動引用。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——克差舞(Kecak,峇里島「羅摩衍那猴子頌」)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:克差舞(Kecak,亦拼作 Ketjak,英語世界常暱稱為「羅摩衍那猴子頌」〔Ramayana Monkey Chant〕),是一種峇里島特有的舞蹈戲劇形式,最大特色在於完全以人聲吟唱伴奏,不使用任何甘美朗樂器;其現今的劇場形式,於 1930 年代初由峇里島舞者暨編舞家瓦揚·林巴克(Wayan Limbak),與德國畫家暨音樂家華特·史畢斯(Walter Spies)密切合作發展而成——史畢斯本人,正是形塑二十世紀中葉西方藝術界如何看待、理解峇里島文化的關鍵人物之一。
  • 時代/背景:克差舞改編自峇里島更古老的「桑揚」(Sanghyang)儀式吟唱傳統,原本是用於驅邪、集體淨化的宗教儀式;1930 年代初,林巴克與史畢斯將桑揚儀式中獨特的「差」(cak)人聲吟唱,改編成一齣以印度教史詩《羅摩衍那》為主軸、全新編排的敘事性舞台表演,刻意讓內容更易於理解、也更能吸引廣大觀眾——包括當時開始逐漸增多、造訪峇里島的外國旅客。
  • 劇情概述:劇情取材自《羅摩衍那》史詩中的段落,核心人物包括羅摩王子、其妻悉多、其弟羅什曼那、忠心耿耿的猴王哈奴曼,以及擄走悉多的魔王羅波那;表演通常會鋪陳至哈奴曼經歷火焰考驗、悉多最終獲救的高潮段落。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:數十名、甚至上百名赤裸上身的男性表演者,以緊密的同心圓隊形圍坐,以急促、環環相扣的複節奏方式,齊聲吟唱「差」(cak)這個音節,整體效果宛如取代了一整支甘美朗樂團,同時整齊劃一地擺動高舉的手臂與軀幹;圈內則有少數身著戲服的舞者,扮演《羅摩衍那》的主要角色。許多舞台版本,還會在結尾加上源自桑揚儀式的「火舞」橋段,由一名進入出神狀態的表演者,踩踏或踢散一堆燃燒中的椰殼,直接呼應原始桑揚儀式驅邪淨化的核心精神。
  • 文化/歷史意義:是峇里島國際知名度最高的表演藝術形式之一,也經常被引用為「傳統」表演藝術,如何與二十世紀西方觀光業及藝文贊助相互形塑、彼此纏繞的經典案例——一項貨真價實、歷史悠久的宗教吟唱儀式,在西方人士的實質參與下被改編為全新的舞台形式,卻依然穩固地融入、成為當代峇里島文化認同不可或缺的一部分;現已被列為印尼的無形文化資產。
  • 影響與傳承:其去除樂器、僅靠大量人聲與肢體構成的極簡圓形舞台調度方式,曾被引為後續多部國際劇場與編舞作品的靈感來源,這些作品同樣追求類似的極簡、以群體演出為核心的美學風格。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:目前每日皆有為遊客安排的演出,其中最負盛名的,是在峇里島南端海崖上的烏魯瓦圖神殿(Uluwatu Temple),於日落時分登場的場次——演出者身後印度洋的海天景色,本身即成為舞台調度的一部分;此外,烏布(Ubud)與島上其他文化場館,也經常固定演出。

8)看片方法——軸線剪接/「庫柏力克式跳接」(Axial Cutting,第 48 課)

「軸線剪接」(axial cutting)指的是針對同一個拍攝主體、以完全相同的機位角度(同一條視線軸線)、但不同距離或焦段所拍攝的兩個(或以上)鏡頭之間的剪接方式,產生的效果是朝主體猛然「跳近」或「拉遠」,而非透過推軌或伸縮鏡頭達成的平滑、連續運鏡。這種手法刻意違反了連戲剪接(continuity editing)的傳統經驗法則——同一主體的相鄰鏡頭之間,機位角度通常應有足夠幅度的變化(常見說法為至少約 30 度),以避免被誤認為是不小心接錯、造成生硬「跳接」的失誤。軸線剪接不同於第 11 天教過的「match cut 與圖形匹配剪接」(那項技巧是透過共同的圖形形狀,連結兩個不同主體或不同鏡頭,而非針對同一主體、沿同一軸線改變距離);也不同於第 36 天教過的「伸縮變焦鏡頭/『暈眩效果』」(那是透過同一個連續鏡頭內,同時進行方向相反的推軌與變焦所達成的相關但不同的暈眩效果,並非剪接);也不同於第 24 天教過的「跳接」(那是在原本靜態、單一機位的鏡頭內,刻意暴露時間上的不連續性,而非沿著固定視線軸線刻意改變距離/尺度);也不同於第 13 天教過的「鏡頭尺度」(那只是為不同景別大小命名的基礎詞彙,並未處理這種特定的同軸剪接技巧)。分析一場戲是否運用軸線剪接時,可檢視:剪接是朝主體逼近,還是向後拉遠;機位是否精準維持在同一軸線上,還是有些微偏移;連續使用了幾次軸線剪接、節奏是否逐漸加快;以及這種突兀、未經平滑處理的「貼近感」,相較於以同等距離變化、但採平滑推軌拍攝所能達到的效果,兩者帶給觀眾的心理感受有何不同。

實例演練:《鬼店》(The Shining,1980,導演:史丹利·庫柏力克)——庫柏力克在其創作生涯中反覆運用這項技巧,以至於「庫柏力克式剪接」(the Kubrick cut)已成為這種手法的通稱之一。片中,小男孩丹尼·托倫斯騎著三輪車,在歐弗魯克飯店的長廊中穿梭,轉過轉角後,發現走廊盡頭站著兩名一動不動、如幽靈般的葛雷迪雙胞胎姊妹;庫柏力克此時連續剪接了一系列沿著同一條走廊、直直朝雙胞胎逼近的軸線鏡頭,每一次剪接都讓她們明顯更靠近鏡頭,鏡頭位置之間卻沒有任何推軌或變焦畫面加以銜接。正是這種跳躍本身的生硬感——而非讓觀眾得以逐漸適應的平滑推近運鏡——才營造出這一刻的恐懼感,將原本可能只是「鏡頭逐漸靠近恐怖事物」的尋常運鏡手法,轉化為一連串近乎機械式、對畫面造成的小型突兀侵入感,帶來的感受更接近驚嚇,而非單純的揭露。
