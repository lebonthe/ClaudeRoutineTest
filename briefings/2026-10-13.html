3) Spanish Lesson — Day 89: Preterite vs. Imperfect (Indefinido vs. Imperfecto)

Think of the past as a film: the Imperfecto paints the background (description, age, time, weather, habits, actions in progress), while the Indefinido moves the plot (completed events, sequences, bounded periods, interruptions). The classic pattern is Imperfecto + cuando + Indefinido: Cocinaba cuando sonó el timbre — "I was cooking when the doorbell rang." Signal words: ayer, anoche, de repente, una vez (Indefinido) vs. siempre, todos los días, mientras, de niño (Imperfecto). Some verbs change meaning: sabía (knew) / supe (found out); conocía (was acquainted with) / conocí (met); quería (wanted) / no quise (refused); podía (could) / pude (managed to).

  • ¿Qué hacías anoche cuando te llamé? — What were you doing last night when I called you?
  • Cocinaba cuando sonó el timbre. Era mi vecino. — I was cooking when the doorbell rang. It was my neighbor.
  • Quería azúcar. Le di un poco y volví a la cocina. — He wanted sugar. I gave him some and went back to the kitchen.
  • Ayer la conocí; estaba muy contenta. — I met her yesterday; she was very happy.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 25: 則陽 (Zéyáng) — "Zeyang"

The third chapter of the Miscellaneous Chapters (雜篇, Ch. 25), named after its opening figure Zeyang, an ambitious wanderer who seeks a position at the court of Chu. The chapter is a patchwork of dialogues on the limits of human viewpoints. Its most famous passage is the "war on the snail's horns" (蝸角之爭): Dai Jinren, a sage, asks King Ying of Wei, who wants to attack Qi, whether space has an end. The king says no. Dai then says that in the middle of that boundless space lies the "central states", and in them the kingdom of Wei, and in Wei the king himself: is there any difference between the king and a creature in the boundless void? Then he tells of a kingdom on the snail's left horn (the Touch clan) and another on its right horn (the Man clan) that fight over territory, leaving tens of thousands dead. The king admits there is no real difference; he is left lost when Dai leaves. The idiom 蝸角之爭 still means a quarrel over trivial gains.

Other threads: the border warden of Changwu tells Zilao not to govern or live carelessly, since a field plowed deeply and weeded well yields a rich harvest (the source of the idiom 鹵莽滅裂, "crude and sloppy"); Qu Boyu "changed with each of his sixty years", never clinging to what he had called right the year before; and a dialogue on the "words of the village" (丘里之言), where many names and surnames combine into a custom, as the many things combine in the Dao, which is only a borrowed name for the whole.

Takeaway: Zoom out. Wars over a border look like a fight between two snail horns from the scale of the cosmos; do not take any viewpoint, even yesterday's "right", as final. (Chapter details are from general knowledge of the text, not from today's search.)

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Cloud Gate Dance Theatre 雲門舞集 (Taiwan, founded 1973)

  • Key figures: Founded in 1973 by choreographer Lin Hwai-min (林懷民), who led it for 46 years and retired as artistic director at the end of 2019. His successor is Cheng Tsung-lung (鄭宗龍), artistic director since 2020 and still in the post in mid-2026 according to a July 2026 SCMP report. A younger-choreographer sister troupe, Cloud Gate 2 (雲門2), was founded in 1999.
  • Era / background: Taiwan's first professional contemporary dance company, and the first in the Chinese-speaking world, founded in Taipei during an era of political isolation when many Taiwanese turned to their own culture. The name comes from Yunmen (雲門), described in ancient texts as the oldest known dance in China, said to date to the Yellow Emperor. Lin, who was a writer before he was a choreographer, trained in the US (including the Martha Graham school).
  • Works & content: Legacy (薪傳, 1978), about the ancestors who crossed the Taiwan Strait to settle the island; Songs of the Wanderers (流浪者之歌, 1994), in which a dancer sits in stillness while tons of rice fall on the stage to Georgian chants; Moon Water (水月, 1998), set to Bach's cello suites on a mirror-like stage; the Cursive trilogy (行草, 2001-05), based on calligraphy; Rice (稻禾, 2013), inspired by the rice fields of Chishang; and, under Cheng, Lunar Halo, his first full-length work, performed in Hong Kong on July 10-12, 2026 (per SCMP). (Earlier works: general knowledge.)
  • Artistic style and techniques: A blend of Western modern dance (Graham technique, ballet) and Asian movement training: Peking-opera and martial-arts forms, tai chi, qigong, meditation and calligraphy. The body's breath and center of gravity, slow sustained movement, and strong stage images of rice, water and ink are signatures.
  • Cultural / historical significance: It gave Taiwan a distinctive contemporary-dance identity and made "Taiwan" a name on international dance stages, while turning local history and landscape into a shared artistic language. Its image of a cultural identity not borrowed from the mainland or the West is often cited in discussions of Taiwanese culture.
  • Influence and legacy: Established the professional dance scene in Taiwan, trained generations of choreographers (many of whom founded their own companies), and made Taiwan a regular stop for world tours. Lin has received international lifetime honors (general knowledge).
  • Where to experience it today: The company performs at home in Taiwan every year, with a theatre and studio in Bali, New Taipei City, as well as national venues in Taipei, and has a tradition of free outdoor performances. It tours internationally (for example Hong Kong in July 2026); check cloudgate.org.tw for current dates. Recordings and excerpts exist online. (Where-to-see details are general knowledge, not rechecked.)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 75: Point of Audition (Subjective Sound)

The method: We all ask "where is the camera?" but good viewers also ask "whose ears are we in?" The French sound theorist Michel Chion called this the point of audition (point d'écoute). A film can present sound objectively (as an ideal neutral listener would hear the scene) or subjectively (as a particular character hears it): muffled, filtered, ringing, with their heartbeat and breathing in the foreground and the world pushed back. Shifting between the two lets a director put the audience inside a character's body, often at the moment of shock, injury, fear or isolation.

How to analyze it: (1) Ask whose ears we are in at each moment, and notice the cue that changes it (a blast, a dive underwater, a closing door). (2) Note what is filtered out (high frequencies, dialogue, background) and what is added (ringing, heartbeat, breath, bass). (3) Compare sound with image: does the picture also go "subjective" (blur, slow motion), or does the sound alone do the work? (4) Watch the return: how, and how fast, does normal sound come back, and what does that say about the character's recovery? (5) Ask what would be lost with an objective mix.

Worked example — Saving Private Ryan (1998, dir. Steven Spielberg), the Omaha Beach landing: Early in the landing, the roar of machine-gun fire and shouting is cut off as soldiers who jump from the landing craft go under the water: the sound collapses to a muffled underwater rumble, with bullets only audible as faint streaks. Later, after a shell explodes near Captain Miller (Tom Hanks), the soundtrack drops to a high ringing and muffled, distant noise while the picture shows soldiers still shouting and falling in near-silence; as he regains his bearings, the full noise of battle returns. What to look for: (a) the cue (explosion, submersion) that flips the sound to Miller's ears; (b) the loss of detail, making us feel the shock rather than watch it; (c) the way the noise returning marks the moment he can act again. Contrast: in Sound of Metal (2019, dir. Darius Marder), the audience is repeatedly placed inside the hearing loss of the drummer Ruben, so that the muffled, buzzing sound becomes the whole experience of the film and the objective mix feels like what he has lost. (Film details are from general knowledge, not from today's search.)

  • 位置與面積:位於南歐,在地中海上形似長靴的半島,加上西西里島與薩丁尼亞島。與法國、瑞士、奧地利、斯洛維尼亞接壤,境內包圍著聖馬利諾與梵蒂岡兩個飛地國家。面積約 30.1 萬平方公里,約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 8 倍,與菲律賓(約 30 萬平方公里)差不多,比美國亞利桑那州(約 29.5 萬平方公里)略大,比日本(約 37.8 萬平方公里)小。
  • 人口:約 5,900 萬(一個次級來源給出 2025 年為 5,925 萬;未與義大利國家統計局 ISTAT 核對)。是歐洲人口最老、縮減最快的國家之一,生育率約 1.2(約略數字)。
  • 宗教:羅馬天主教是最大的宗教傳統(絕大多數居民自認天主教徒,但定期上教堂的比例很低);教宗居住在位於羅馬城內的獨立國家梵蒂岡。依 1984 年的協約,國家在制度上是世俗的;少數包括東正教徒、穆斯林、新教徒與猶太人。(約略數字。)
  • 經濟:歐元區第三大經濟體,GDP 約 2.3–2.5 兆美元(約略數字),以製造業(機械、汽車含法拉利、時尚、食品與葡萄酒)、家族式中小企業與旅遊業為基礎。公共債務在歐元區名列前茅(約占 GDP 135%,約略數字)。ISTAT 7 月 30 日的發布顯示第二季成長 0.2%,並把 2026 年預測上調到 0.8%;政府在 4 月預測為 0.6%。
  • 歷史:羅馬從城邦發展為共和國、再成為稱霸地中海的帝國;西羅馬帝國於 476 年滅亡。中世紀與文藝復興時期的義大利是城邦(威尼斯、佛羅倫斯、熱那亞、米蘭)與教皇國拼成的馬賽克,是銀行業、藝術與科學的中心。統一運動(復興運動 Risorgimento,加富爾、加里波第與國王維托里奧·埃馬努埃萊二世)在 1861 年建立王國,1871 年定都羅馬。墨索里尼的法西斯政權統治 1922–1943 年;1946 年公投廢除君主制成為共和國;義大利是歐盟前身的創始成員國(1957 年羅馬條約)。
  • 政體與現任領導人:議會共和國。總統為塞爾吉奧·馬塔雷拉(2015 年起任職,2022 年連任;一般知識,今天未重新查核)。總理為喬治亞·梅洛尼(義大利兄弟黨),2022 年 10 月起任職;今天的搜尋結果仍稱她為總理。
  • 語言:官方語言為義大利語;區域與少數語言包括薩丁尼亞語、弗留利語、拉丁語(Ladin)、南蒂羅爾的德語、奧斯塔谷的法語與的里雅斯特附近的斯洛維尼亞語;方言眾多。
  • 與台灣的關係:沒有正式外交關係;由非官方機構處理經貿與文化(駐義大利台北代表處、義大利經濟貿易文化推廣辦事處駐台北)。義大利電影(見昨天第 7 節《新天堂樂園》)與美食在台灣很受歡迎。(一般知識。)

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「雙彩虹」大叔(Double Rainbow All the Way)

  • 名稱:「Double Rainbow」/「雙彩虹大叔」,出自 YouTube 影片「Yosemitebear Mountain Double Rainbow 1-8-10」。
  • 起源/歷史:2010 年 1 月 8 日,保羅「熊」瓦斯奎茲(Paul "Bear" Vasquez)在加州優勝美地國家公園附近自家土地上,拍下一道鮮明的雙彩虹並隨後上傳 YouTube。隨著彩虹愈來愈亮,他開始喘氣、啜泣並大喊:「雙彩虹貫穿天際!我的天……這是什麼意思啊?」(細節為一般知識,今天未查核。)
  • 如何流行:這支影片放了好幾個月都沒什麼點閱。2010 年 7 月,電視主持人吉米·金莫(Jimmy Kimmel)在推特上分享後,透過部落格、推特與脫口秀擴散,幾天內累積數百萬次觀看。隨後出現自動調音(Auto-Tune)「歌曲化」的混音、深夜節目的模仿,以及找他代言的廣告。
  • 意義與用法:「double rainbow」成為「對自然、簡單的事物感到無比驚嘆或狂喜」的簡稱,常帶一點調侃;「What does it mean?」則用來表達對沒有明顯意義的事物誇張的敬畏。
  • 變體/演變:混音、惡搞、T 恤與模仿;「double rainbow」也出現在其他關於迷幻式驚嘆的笑話中。它屬於早期 YouTube「病毒影片」時代,與《Charlie Bit My Finger》同期。
  • 現況:2010 年的經典;影片仍在網路上,常被引為業餘者製作的早期病毒影片案例,但現在很少用來開新玩笑。我沒有查證創作者的近況。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 雲門舞集 Cloud Gate Dance Theatre(台灣,1973 年創立)

  • 關鍵人物:1973 年由編舞家林懷民創立,他擔任藝術總監 46 年,於 2019 年底卸任。接任者為鄭宗龍,自 2020 年起擔任藝術總監,據 2026 年 7 月南華早報報導,到 2026 年中仍在任。1999 年另成立培育年輕編舞家的姊妹舞團「雲門2」。
  • 時代背景:台灣第一個職業現代舞團,也是華人世界第一個,在台北創立於政治上相對孤立、許多台灣人轉向本土文化的年代。名稱來自「雲門」,古籍記載為中國最古老的舞蹈,相傳可追溯到黃帝時代。林懷民在成為編舞家之前是作家,曾赴美學習(包括瑪莎·葛蘭姆舞蹈學校)。
  • 作品與內容:《薪傳》(1978),講述渡過台灣海峽來島上開墾的先民;《流浪者之歌》(1994),一名舞者靜坐,在喬治亞聖歌聲中數噸稻米從天而降;《水月》(1998),配合巴哈無伴奏大提琴組曲、在鏡面般的舞台上演出;書法為本的《行草》三部曲(2001–05);靈感來自池上稻田的《稻禾》(2013);以及鄭宗龍的首部完整長度作品《暈》(Lunar Halo),2026 年 7 月 10–12 日在香港演出(據南華早報)。(早期作品為一般知識。)
  • 藝術風格與技巧:融合西方現代舞(葛蘭姆技巧、芭蕾)與亞洲的身體訓練:京劇與武術的身段、太極、氣功、靜坐與書法。呼吸與重心、緩慢而綿長的動作,以及稻米、水、墨等強烈的舞台意象,是它的標誌。
  • 文化與歷史意義:替台灣建立了獨特的現代舞身分,讓「台灣」成為國際舞台上的名字,並把本地歷史與風景變成共同的藝術語言。它所呈現的「既不是向大陸也不是向西方借來」的文化認同,常在討論台灣文化時被引用。
  • 影響與傳承:奠定了台灣的職業舞蹈生態,培養出許多後來自創舞團的編舞家,也讓台灣成為世界巡演的常客。林懷民獲得多項國際終身成就榮譽(一般知識)。
  • 今天哪裡可以看到:舞團每年在台灣本地演出,在新北市八里設有劇場與排練場,也在台北的國家級場館演出,並有免費戶外演出的傳統;也定期國際巡演(例如 2026 年 7 月的香港)。最新場次請查看 cloudgate.org.tw;網路上也有紀錄片與片段。(演出地點的細節為一般知識,未重新查核。)

8) 看片方法 — 第 75 課:聆聽點(主觀聲音)

方法:大家都會問「攝影機在哪裡?」,但好的觀眾也會問「我們在誰的耳朵裡?」法國聲音理論家米歇爾·希翁(Michel Chion)稱之為聆聽點(point d'écoute)。電影可以客觀地呈現聲音(像理想的中立聽者所聽到的場景),也可以主觀地呈現(像某個角色聽到的):悶住、被過濾、耳鳴、他自己的心跳與呼吸被放在前景,而外界被推遠。在兩者間切換,讓導演把觀眾放進角色的身體裡,常用在震驚、受傷、恐懼或孤立的時刻。

怎麼分析:(1) 在每一刻問自己:我們在誰的耳朵裡?並注意切換的提示(爆炸、潛入水中、關上門)。(2) 記下什麼被濾掉(高頻、對白、背景),什麼被加進來(耳鳴、心跳、呼吸、低頻)。(3) 對照畫面:畫面是否也變成「主觀」(模糊、慢動作),還是只有聲音在做事?(4) 觀察回歸:正常聲音如何、多快回來,這對角色的恢復說明了什麼?(5) 問自己:如果換成客觀混音,會失去什麼?

實例分析——《搶救雷恩大兵》(Saving Private Ryan,1998,導演史蒂芬·史匹柏)奧馬哈海灘登陸:登陸初期,士兵從登陸艇跳入水中,機槍與吶喊的轟鳴驟然被切斷:聲音塌縮成悶悶的水下隆隆聲,子彈只剩下微弱的劃過聲。稍後,一枚砲彈在米勒上尉(湯姆·漢克斯)附近爆炸,聲軌降成高頻耳鳴與悶、遠的雜訊,畫面中的士兵仍在吶喊、倒下,卻近乎無聲;待他重新找回方向,戰場的全部噪音才回來。觀察重點:(a) 把聲音切到米勒耳朵裡的提示(爆炸、入水);(b) 細節的流失讓我們「感受」震驚而不只是「看」;(c) 噪音回來的瞬間,標示著他又能行動的時刻。對照:《Sound of Metal》(2019,導演達流士·馬德)一再把觀眾放進鼓手魯本逐漸失聰的聽覺裡,於是悶住、嗡嗡的聲音成為整部電影的體驗,而客觀混音聽起來就像他失去的東西。(影片細節來自一般知識,而非今日搜尋。)
