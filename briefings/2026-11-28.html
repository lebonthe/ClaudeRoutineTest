ENEnglish version

🎯 Learning Goal: after yesterday's review (Day 130), today's new topic is how to say that something is broken, has stopped working, or needs to be fixed — very practical in a hotel, a rented flat or a phone shop. Chinese uses one word, 壞了, for all of these; Spanish chooses the verb by the kind of damage.

Vocabulary: romper (to break, tear — past participle irregular: roto); romperse (to get broken); estropearse (Spain: to stop working, to go off — food); descomponerse (Latin America: to break down; participle descompuesto); averiarse (machines and cars; noun la avería); funcionar (to work, function); arreglar (to fix; also to tidy); reparar (to repair, more formal); el técnico (repair person); la pantalla (screen).

Grammar Point: (1) Physically broken (glass, a screen, a cup, paper) → romper / está roto. (2) Doesn't work but is not visibly broken (the Wi-Fi, a washing machine, the air conditioning) → no funciona, or se estropeó (Spain) / se descompuso (Latin America). (3) Accidental se from Day 57 removes blame: Se me rompió el móvil = "My phone broke (on me)" vs. Rompí el móvil = "I broke the phone." (4) estar + participle from Day 50 for the resulting state: La ventana está rota. La lavadora está estropeada. (5) Fixing: arreglar is the everyday verb; to say you get someone else to fix it, use llevar a arreglar or mandar a arreglar.

Example Sentences: Se me rompió la pantalla del móvil. (My phone screen broke.) · El aire acondicionado no funciona. (The air conditioning isn't working.) · Se estropeó la lavadora. (The washing machine broke down.) · ¿Me lo pueden arreglar hoy? (Can you fix it for me today?) · Llevé el portátil a arreglar. (I took the laptop in to be repaired.)

Practice Prompt: tell a hotel receptionist in Spanish that the shower is not working and the lamp is broken, and ask whether someone can fix it today. Answers are on the lesson page below a divider.

ENEnglish version

Original text (common received text, based on Wang Bi)

大道氾兮,其可左右。萬物恃之而生而不辭,功成不名有。衣養萬物而不為主,常無欲,可名於小;萬物歸焉而不為主,可名為大。以其終不自為大,故能成其大。

Translation: "The great Way overflows everywhere; it can go left or right. The ten thousand things rely on it for life, and it does not refuse them. It completes its work but does not claim to own it. It clothes and nourishes all things but does not act as their master. Always without desire, it can be called small. All things return to it, yet it does not act as their master; it can be called great. Because it never makes itself great, it can achieve its greatness."

Key terms and textual notes

  • 氾 (fàn): overflowing like flood water — reaching every side. Wang Bi explains that the Way spreads everywhere and "can go left, right, up and down", so there is nowhere it does not reach.
  • Variants: editions differ — 恃之而生 / 恃之以生; 衣養 (Wang Bi) / 衣被 / 愛養 (Heshang Gong tradition). The Mawangdui silk texts, as usually cited, give a shorter chapter without "萬物恃之而生而不辭" and end with a sentence about the sage: 是以聖人之能成大也,以其不為大也,故能成大. This chapter is not among those found on the Guodian bamboo slips.
  • Small and great at the same time: the Way is "small" because it has no desire and does not show itself; it is "great" because everything returns to it. The paradox is the point: the same quality — not dominating — makes it both invisible and all-embracing.
  • Echoes: Ch. 2 (萬物作焉而不辭, 功成而弗居); Ch. 10 and 51 (生而不有, 為而不恃, 長而不宰); Ch. 32 yesterday-but-one (樸雖小, 天下莫能臣); Ch. 63 repeats the last line almost exactly (是以聖人終不為大,故能成其大).

Historical and philosophical background

Many Warring States thinkers argued about what makes a ruler great: Legalists stressed control and visible authority, and Confucians stressed moral example. This chapter answers from the Daoist side: real greatness looks like water that supports everything without claiming credit. Heshang Gong's commentary reads it partly as advice on rulership and long life; Wang Bi reads it as a description of how the Way works without acting. Both readings are traditional interpretations, not facts about the author's intention.

Practical philosophical implications

  • Support without ownership: giving help without turning it into control or a claim.
  • Greatness as a by-product: aiming directly at "being great" (自為大) tends to block it; doing the supporting work well produces it.
  • Invisible infrastructure: the most important things are often the ones nobody notices until they fail.

Modern reflection

Think of good infrastructure — a reliable power grid, an open-source library everyone depends on, or a senior engineer who unblocks others. They are "small" because they rarely get credit, and "great" because so much returns to them. Today's market news gives a contrast: when one company becomes the master of a supply chain, a single pause (Nvidia's shipments) affects everyone. The chapter's ideal is power that nourishes without becoming a choke point. This is a reflection, not a claim about the original meaning.

原文(通行本,以王弼本為主)

大道氾兮,其可左右。萬物恃之而生而不辭,功成不名有。衣養萬物而不為主,常無欲,可名於小;萬物歸焉而不為主,可名為大。以其終不自為大,故能成其大。

白話翻譯:「大道像大水一樣漫溢四方,可左可右。萬物依靠它而生長,它從不推辭;成就了功業,卻不自認擁有。它覆蓋、養育萬物,卻不當它們的主宰。它永遠沒有私欲,可以稱為『小』;萬物都歸附它,它卻不當主宰,可以稱為『大』。正因為它始終不自以為大,所以能成就它的大。」

關鍵字與版本說明

  • 氾:像洪水一樣氾濫、漫溢,四面八方都到得了。王弼注解說,道無所不到,「可左右上下周旋而用」,沒有它到不了的地方。
  • 版本差異:各本文字不同——「恃之而生」/「恃之以生」;「衣養」(王弼本)/「衣被」/「愛養」(河上公系統)。依一般引述,馬王堆帛書本這章較短,沒有「萬物恃之而生而不辭」一句,結尾講聖人:「是以聖人之能成大也,以其不為大也,故能成大」。郭店竹簡中沒有這一章。
  • 同時是小也是大:道沒有欲望、不顯露自己,所以「小」;萬物都歸向它,所以「大」。重點就在這個弔詭:同一個特質——不主宰——讓它既看不見,又包容一切。
  • 呼應:第 2 章(萬物作焉而不辭,功成而弗居);第 10、51 章(生而不有,為而不恃,長而不宰);前天的第 32 章(樸雖小,天下莫能臣);第 63 章幾乎原句重複最後一句(是以聖人終不為大,故能成其大)。

歷史與哲學背景

戰國時代許多思想家爭論:君主的「大」從何而來?法家強調控制與看得見的權威,儒家強調道德典範。這一章從道家的角度回答:真正的大,像水一樣支撐萬物而不居功。河上公注部分把它讀成治國與養生之道;王弼則讀成「道如何無為而運作」的描述。兩者都是傳統詮釋,不是作者原意的事實。

實際的哲學啟示

  • 支持而不佔有:給予幫助,但不把它變成控制或索取。
  • 「大」是副產品:直接追求「當老大」(自為大)反而會阻礙它;把支持的工作做好,大自然就成了。
  • 看不見的基礎建設:最重要的東西,往往要等它出問題,大家才注意到。

現代反思

想想好的基礎建設——穩定的電網、大家都依賴的開源函式庫,或是一位總在幫別人解決卡關問題的資深工程師。他們很「小」,因為很少得到功勞;他們又很「大」,因為那麼多東西都歸向他們。今天的市場新聞提供了一個對比:當一家公司成為整條供應鏈的「主」,一次停貨(輝達出貨暫停)就會影響所有人。這一章的理想,是養育萬物而不成為咽喉要道的力量。這是反思,不是對原意的主張。

Section 4 — Historical Faction: The Kingdom of Tungning (東寧王國, 1661–1683) — the Zheng regime in Taiwan
第 4 段 — 歷史政權:東寧王國(1661–1683)— 明鄭在台灣

ENEnglish version

This is a historical regime that no longer exists, so this section describes its period, leaders, territory, goals and fate instead of present-day data. Population figures for the 17th century are scholarly estimates and vary widely.

Name and location

"Kingdom of Tungning" is the name usually used in English for the regime that the Zheng family ran in southwestern Taiwan from 1661/62 to 1683; Chinese-language histories often call it Ming Zheng (明鄭) or the Zheng regime. Its rulers did not call themselves kings of an independent country: they held the Southern Ming title Prince of Yanping (延平王) and kept using the Ming Yongli reign calendar. English East India Company records referred to the ruler as the "King of Tywan". Koxinga first named the territory Dongdu (東都, "Eastern Capital"); his son Zheng Jing renamed it Dongning (東寧, Tungning, "Eastern Peace") in 1664.

Territory and scale

The regime controlled the western plains around today's Tainan (its capital, Chengtian Prefecture 承天府), spreading south toward Kaohsiung and north into parts of central Taiwan, plus the Penghu islands. During the 1670s it also held coastal footholds in Fujian, including Xiamen and Kinmen, until 1680. It did not control Taiwan's mountains or east coast, which remained Indigenous lands. Effective control was therefore only a fraction of Taiwan's roughly 36,000 km² — very roughly comparable to a few of today's western counties.

Population

Koxinga's invasion force numbered about 25,000 soldiers, and many soldiers' families and settlers followed. Estimates of the Han Chinese population under Zheng rule range from about 100,000 to 200,000, alongside a larger Indigenous population not under its control. These are estimates, not census data.

History

  • Background: Zheng Chenggong (鄭成功, 1624–1662), known in Europe as Koxinga (國姓爺, "Lord of the Imperial Surname"), was born in Hirado, Japan, to the Chinese merchant-admiral Zheng Zhilong and a Japanese mother (Tagawa Matsu). After the Qing conquest of Beijing (1644) he fought for the Southern Ming from bases at Xiamen and Kinmen. His failed attack on Nanjing in 1659 pushed him to look for a safer base.
  • 1661–1662, the siege of Fort Zeelandia: in April 1661 his fleet entered the lagoon at Luermen (鹿耳門) and besieged the Dutch East India Company's Fort Zeelandia (熱蘭遮城, in today's Anping). After about nine months, Governor Frederick Coyett surrendered in February 1662, ending 38 years of Dutch rule in southwestern Taiwan. Koxinga died a few months later, in June 1662.
  • Zheng Jing (鄭經, ruled 1662–1681): after a succession dispute, Koxinga's son took power. With his adviser Chen Yonghua (陳永華) he built a state: military farming colonies (屯田), salt fields, sugar production, and in 1666 the Confucian Temple in Tainan, still known as "the first school of all Taiwan" (全臺首學), with a civil examination system.
  • Trade: the regime lived from maritime trade — deer skins and sugar to Japan, trade with Southeast Asia, and an English East India Company trading post in Tainan in the 1670s.
  • Revolt of the Three Feudatories (1674–1680): Zheng Jing joined the revolt against the Qing and occupied parts of coastal Fujian and Guangdong, but lost them by 1680 and withdrew to Taiwan.
  • Fall (1681–1683): after Zheng Jing's death in 1681, a court faction led by Feng Xifan (馮錫範) killed the designated heir Zheng Kezang and installed the boy Zheng Keshuang (鄭克塽). In July 1683 the Qing admiral Shi Lang (施琅), a former Zheng officer, destroyed the Zheng fleet under Liu Guoxuan at the Battle of Penghu. Zheng Keshuang surrendered later that year, and in 1684 the Qing made Taiwan a prefecture of Fujian Province.

Leaders and political system

Hereditary military rule by the Zheng family (Zheng Chenggong → Zheng Jing → Zheng Keshuang), with Ming-style ministries and prefectures. Its stated political goal was to restore the Ming dynasty. In practice, during negotiations with the Qing, Zheng Jing at times asked for a status like Korea's — a tributary state that kept its own customs — which historians debate as a sign of a growing separate identity or as a bargaining position.

Religion and culture

The settlers brought southern Fujian folk religion, Buddhism and Confucian education; many old temples in Tainan trace their founding to this period. Koxinga himself later became a deity: the Koxinga Shrine (延平郡王祠) in Tainan and many local temples worship him. He is also the hero of Chikamatsu Monzaemon's 1715 Bunraku play The Battles of Coxinga (國性爺合戰) in Japan.

Legacy and debates

  • Place names in southern Taiwan still recall the military colonies: Zuoying (左營) — the HSR terminus — Xinying (新營) and Linfengying (林鳳營) all contain 營, "camp".
  • The regime is remembered very differently: as the first Han Chinese government in Taiwan; as a Ming-loyalist hero story (emphasised in both ROC and PRC narratives); and, from an Indigenous perspective, as a period of land seizure and violent campaigns, such as attacks on plains Indigenous communities in central Taiwan (for example, the Shalu 沙轆 community).
  • Sites you can visit today: Anping Fort (remains of Fort Zeelandia), Chihkan Tower (赤崁樓, the Dutch Fort Provintia used by the Zheng), the Tainan Confucian Temple and the Koxinga Shrine.

中繁體中文版

這是已經不存在的歷史政權,所以本段介紹它的時代、領導人、領土、目標與結局,而不是現今的資料。17 世紀的人口數字是學者估計,差距很大。

名稱與位置

「東寧王國」(Kingdom of Tungning)是英文常用的名稱,指鄭氏家族在 1661/62 到 1683 年間統治台灣西南部的政權;中文史書多稱為明鄭或鄭氏政權。統治者並沒有自稱獨立國家的國王:他們使用南明封的延平王爵位,並繼續使用明朝永曆年號。英國東印度公司的紀錄稱統治者為「King of Tywan(台灣王)」。鄭成功最初把這塊地方命名為東都;他的兒子鄭經在 1664 年改名為東寧。

領土與規模

政權控制今天台南周圍的西部平原(首府承天府),往南延伸到高雄一帶、往北到中部部分地區,再加上澎湖。1670 年代它還一度佔有福建沿海據點,包括廈門與金門,直到 1680 年。它沒有控制台灣的山區與東海岸,那裡仍是原住民的土地。因此實際控制範圍只佔台灣約 36,000 平方公里的一小部分——大致相當於今天西部幾個縣市的大小。

人口

鄭成功攻台的部隊約 25,000 人,之後許多官兵眷屬與移民跟著來台。明鄭時期漢人人口的估計從約 10 萬到 20 萬不等,另外還有更多不在其控制下的原住民人口。這些是估計,不是人口普查資料。

歷史

  • 背景:鄭成功(1624–1662),歐洲人稱為 Koxinga(國姓爺),生於日本平戶,父親是華人海商兼水師將領鄭芝龍,母親是日本人(田川松)。清軍 1644 年入關後,他以廈門、金門為基地為南明作戰。1659 年攻打南京失敗後,他開始尋找更安全的根據地。
  • 1661–1662 年,圍攻熱蘭遮城:1661 年 4 月,他的艦隊駛進鹿耳門內海,包圍荷蘭東印度公司的熱蘭遮城(今安平)。約九個月後,荷蘭長官揆一(Frederick Coyett)於 1662 年 2 月投降,結束荷蘭在台灣西南部 38 年的統治。鄭成功數個月後、1662 年 6 月去世。
  • 鄭經(在位 1662–1681):經過一場繼承爭議後,鄭成功的兒子掌權。他和謀臣陳永華一起建立國家體制:軍屯、鹽田、製糖,並在 1666 年建立台南孔廟,至今仍被稱為「全臺首學」,也設立科舉考試。
  • 貿易:政權靠海上貿易維生——把鹿皮和糖賣到日本、與東南亞貿易,1670 年代英國東印度公司還在台南設有商館。
  • 三藩之亂(1674–1680):鄭經加入反清行動,一度佔領福建、廣東沿海部分地區,但到 1680 年全部失去,退回台灣。
  • 覆亡(1681–1683):鄭經 1681 年去世後,以馮錫範為首的朝中派系殺死原定繼承人鄭克臧,擁立年幼的鄭克塽。1683 年 7 月,曾是鄭氏部將的清朝水師提督施琅在澎湖海戰中擊潰劉國軒率領的鄭軍艦隊。鄭克塽同年稍後投降;1684 年清朝把台灣設為福建省轄下的一府。

領導人與政治體制

鄭氏家族世襲的軍事統治(鄭成功 → 鄭經 → 鄭克塽),仿明制設六官與府縣。官方的政治目標是恢復明朝。實際上,鄭經在與清朝談判時,曾一度要求比照朝鮮——作為保留自身習俗的朝貢國——史學家對此有不同看法:是逐漸形成獨立認同的跡象,還是談判籌碼。

宗教與文化

移民帶來閩南民間信仰、佛教與儒家教育;台南許多老廟的創建都追溯到這個時期。鄭成功本人後來也成為神明:台南的延平郡王祠和許多地方廟宇都祭祀他。在日本,他也是近松門左衛門 1715 年文樂劇《國性爺合戰》的主角。

遺產與爭議

  • 南台灣的地名仍保留軍屯的痕跡:左營(高鐵終點站)、新營、林鳳營都有「營」字。
  • 這個政權被以非常不同的方式記憶:台灣第一個漢人政權;明朝忠臣的英雄故事(中華民國與中華人民共和國的敘事都強調這一點);以及從原住民的角度,是土地被奪、遭受武力征伐的時期,例如對中部平埔族社群的攻擊(像是沙轆社)。
  • 今天可以造訪的地點:安平古堡(熱蘭遮城遺跡)、赤崁樓(荷蘭人的普羅民遮城,明鄭時期沿用)、台南孔廟與延平郡王祠。

Section 5 — Internet Meme: "Mr. Incredible Becoming Uncanny" (and "Becoming Canny")
第 5 段 — 網路迷因:「超人特攻隊爸爸越來越詭異」(Mr. Incredible Becoming Uncanny,以及反向的 Becoming Canny)

ENEnglish version

Name and variations

"Mr. Incredible Becoming Uncanny" (also "Traumatized Mr. Incredible" or "Uncanny phases"), and its opposite, "Mr. Incredible Becoming Canny". Spin-offs use the same structure with other characters or with different moods ("becoming old", "becoming idiot", "becoming rich").

Origin and history

The images come from Pixar's The Incredibles (2004, dir. Brad Bird), whose hero Bob Parr is "Mr. Incredible". The meme format took shape around 2021 on YouTube and TikTok and spread widely in 2021–2022, according to meme databases such as Know Your Meme. The exact first post is not clearly documented, so treat the start date as approximate.

How it works

A video or image series shows "phases". Each phase pairs a statement with a picture of Mr. Incredible. As the statements become more disturbing, the face becomes darker and more distorted — first a worried look, then a pale, traumatised stare, then black-and-white edits, finally a skull or an empty black screen — usually with tense music. In the "Canny" version, the statements become nicer and the picture becomes brighter, happier and finally glowing gold.

Why it became popular

  • Easy template: anyone can rank facts from "fine" to "horrifying" with the same images.
  • Dark humour and "creepy facts" culture: it fits the internet taste for unsettling trivia (deep-sea animals, space, history).
  • Gradual escalation: the joke is the build-up — you know the next phase will be worse, and you wait for it.

Meaning and typical usage

"Uncanny" here means "disturbing, creepy" (from the "uncanny valley"). "Canny" is used as an invented opposite meaning "nice, comforting". Note: in Scottish and northern English, canny really means "nice" or "clever" — but most users simply treat it as the antonym. Typical uses: "Your phone at 5% / 3% / 1% / 0% while you're lost in a foreign city" (uncanny); "Things that happen when you finish work early" (canny). Users in many countries adapt it to local topics, such as housing prices or exam results — the template works in any language because the pictures carry the emotion.

Evolution and current status

The format peaked around 2022 and still appears as a recognisable template, especially on YouTube Shorts and TikTok, but I found no evidence that it is trending now. It is best described as an established, slightly older template rather than a current viral trend.

Cultural note

The humour comes from contrast: a family-friendly Pixar character is turned into a horror mask. The meme is about the feeling of "this gets worse the more you think about it", not about the film's story.

中繁體中文版

名稱與變化

「Mr. Incredible Becoming Uncanny」(也叫「Traumatized Mr. Incredible」或「Uncanny 階段圖」),以及反過來的「Mr. Incredible Becoming Canny」。衍生版用同樣的結構換成其他角色或其他情緒(「越來越老」「越來越笨」「越來越有錢」)。

起源與歷史

圖片來自皮克斯動畫《超人特攻隊》(The Incredibles,2004,布萊德·博德導演),主角鮑伯·帕爾就是「超能先生」(Mr. Incredible)。依 Know Your Meme 等迷因資料庫,這個格式大約在 2021 年於 YouTube 與 TikTok 成形,2021–2022 年大量流傳。最早的貼文沒有明確紀錄,所以起始時間請視為大約。

怎麼玩

一段影片或一組圖片分成好幾個「階段」。每個階段搭配一句陳述和一張超能先生的臉。陳述越來越令人不安,臉也越來越暗、越扭曲——先是擔心,再來是蒼白、受到驚嚇的眼神,接著是黑白修圖,最後變成骷髏或全黑畫面——通常配上緊張的音樂。「Canny」版本則相反:陳述越來越美好,圖片越來越亮、越開心,最後發出金光。

為什麼會紅

  • 模板簡單:任何人都能用同一組圖,把事實從「還好」排到「好恐怖」。
  • 黑色幽默與「毛骨悚然冷知識」文化:符合網路上對讓人不安的冷知識(深海生物、宇宙、歷史)的喜好。
  • 逐步升級:笑點在鋪陳——你知道下一階段會更糟,於是一直等。

意思與常見用法

這裡的「uncanny」意思是「令人不安、毛毛的」(來自「恐怖谷」uncanny valley)。「canny」被當成自創的反義詞,意思是「美好、讓人安心」。補充:在蘇格蘭與英格蘭北部,canny 本來就有「好、聰明」的意思——但大多數使用者只是把它當成反義詞。常見用法:「你在國外迷路時手機電量 5%/3%/1%/0%」(uncanny);「提早下班會發生的事」(canny)。各國網友都會把它改成在地主題,例如房價或考試成績——因為情緒由圖片承載,這個模板在任何語言都用得上。

演變與目前地位

這個格式大約在 2022 年達到高峰,現在仍是大家認得的模板,特別是在 YouTube Shorts 和 TikTok,但我沒有找到它目前正在爆紅的證據。比較準確的說法是:一個已經定型、稍舊的模板,而不是當下的爆紅趨勢。

文化說明

笑點來自反差:適合全家觀賞的皮克斯角色被改成恐怖面具。這個迷因表達的是「越想越不對勁」的感覺,和電影劇情無關。

Unit 1 — Preposition Lesson 11: into / out of / onto / off (Stage 3, movement in and out).

  • Today's topic and the usual confusion: Chinese says 上車、下車、進去、出來 for every vehicle and every space. English chooses between into / out of and onto (on) / off depending on whether the thing is a container or a surface/platform. Learners say "get down the bus" or "get on the taxi".
  • Core concept — the same pictures as Lesson 1, now moving: Lesson 1 said in = inside a container and on = on a surface. Today adds movement: into = from outside to inside a container; out of = from inside to outside; onto = moving up onto a surface; off = moving away from a surface.
  • Vehicles: small vehicles you sit in and can't stand up in (car, taxi, small boat) are containers → get into / get in, get out of. Big vehicles you can walk around in, and things you sit on top of (bus, train, MRT, plane, ship, scooter, bike, horse) are platforms → get on, get off.
  • into vs. in: after verbs of movement, into shows the change of place (She jumped into the pool). In everyday speech in is often used too (jump in the pool, put it in the box), especially in American English — both are acceptable; into is clearer. After "be", only in works: She is in the pool, not "into the pool".
  • Examples: Get into the taxi — it's raining. / We got off the MRT at Taipei Main Station. / The cat jumped onto the table. / He fell off his scooter but wasn't hurt. / Take the cake out of the fridge. / Please take your shoes off.
  • Common mistakes: (1) "get down the bus" (from 下車) → get off the bus. (2) "get on the taxi" → get in(to) the taxi. (3) "enter into the room" → enter the room (enter already means "go into"; "enter into" is used only for agreements or discussions: enter into a contract).

  • Key takeaways: container → into / out of; surface or big vehicle → on(to) / off. Car and taxi = in/out; bus, train, MRT, plane, scooter = on/off. "Enter" takes no preposition.

Unit 1 — 介系詞第 11 課:into / out of / onto / off(第三階段:進出與上下)

1. 今日主題
中文不管什麼交通工具、什麼空間,都說「上車、下車、進去、出來」。英文卻要看那個東西是容器還是平台/表面,在 into / out of 和 onto(on)/ off 之間選一個。最常見的錯誤:✗ get down the bus、✗ get on the taxi。

2. 核心概念:第 1 課的圖,現在動起來

  • 第 1 課說過:in = 在容器裡,on = 在表面上。今天加上「移動」:
  • into = 從外面移動到容器裡面(箭頭穿過牆壁進去)。
  • out of = 從容器裡面移動到外面。
  • onto = 移動到一個表面上面(常常是往上跳、往上爬)。
  • off = 從表面上離開(下來、掉下來、脫下來)。
  • 交通工具的判斷法:小到只能坐、不能站起來走動的(汽車、計程車、小船)= 容器 → get into / get in、get out of。大到可以在裡面走動的,或是「坐在上面」的(公車、火車、捷運、飛機、大船、機車、腳踏車、馬)= 平台 → get on、get off。

Section 7 — Film & Performing Arts: Meshes of the Afternoon (1943, Maya Deren & Alexander Hammid)
第 7 段 — 電影與表演藝術:《午後的迷惘》(Meshes of the Afternoon,1943,瑪雅·黛倫與亞歷山大·漢米德)

ENEnglish version

The work and its creators

Meshes of the Afternoon is a short experimental film of about 14 minutes, shot on 16mm in black and white in Los Angeles in 1943. It was made by Maya Deren (born Eleonora Derenkowska in Kyiv in 1917, died 1961) and her then-husband, the Czech-born cinematographer Alexander Hammid. Deren plays the woman at the centre of the film; Hammid plays the man. The film was originally silent; in 1959 Deren added a score by Teiji Ito, the Japanese-American composer who later became her husband.

Historical and cultural background

In 1943 American cinema meant the Hollywood studio system. Deren made the film at home, with almost no budget, a few years before the post-war American avant-garde grew. She later called this kind of work "chamber films" — small, personal works, like chamber music compared with an orchestra. She promoted it herself: in 1946 she rented the Provincetown Playhouse in New York to screen her films, and that year she became the first filmmaker to receive a Guggenheim Fellowship for creative work in film.

Content overview

A woman walks home, sees a flower on the path and a figure disappearing round a corner, lets herself in with a key, and falls asleep in a chair. What follows repeats and changes: a hooded figure in black with a mirror for a face walks away from her; a knife appears in a loaf of bread and then in the bed; a telephone is off the hook; a key falls and turns into a knife. The woman sees other versions of herself — at one point three of her sit at the table and draw the key to decide who will act. In the end a man comes home and finds her dead in the chair, covered with broken mirror and seaweed. Whether it is a dream, a suicide or a symbolic murder is left open.

Artistic style and techniques

  • Dream logic through editing: match cuts carry her step from sand to grass to pavement to a carpet — one continuous movement across different places.
  • Repetition with variation: the same objects (key, knife, flower, phone, record player) return in changed contexts, so they become charged symbols.
  • Subjective camera and tilted framing: handheld point-of-view shots and a camera that tilts as she climbs the stairs make the house unstable.
  • Multiplication of the self: multiple Derens on screen show inner conflict without dialogue.

Significance and influence

It is widely considered a founding work of the American avant-garde and of the "trance film". It influenced later experimental filmmakers such as Kenneth Anger and Stan Brakhage, and critics often compare it with the looping, identity-splitting structure of David Lynch's films (for example Lost Highway) — that is a critical comparison, not a documented influence claim. It was added to the US National Film Registry in 1990, and in the 2022 Sight and Sound critics' poll it ranked 16th, the highest-placed short film. Feminist critics read it as a portrait of a woman's sense of entrapment in the home; that is an interpretation, not a statement from the makers.

Where to see it today

It is often screened in museum and film-school programmes and is available in home-video collections of Deren's films and on some streaming services; availability differs by country, so check local services.

中繁體中文版

作品與創作者

《午後的迷惘》是一部約 14 分鐘的實驗短片,1943 年在洛杉磯以 16 釐米黑白底片拍攝。由瑪雅·黛倫(Maya Deren,1917 年生於基輔,本名 Eleonora Derenkowska,1961 年過世)與她當時的丈夫、捷克出身的攝影師亞歷山大·漢米德(Alexander Hammid)共同完成。黛倫飾演片中的女子,漢米德飾演男子。影片原本是默片;1959 年黛倫加入伊藤貞司(Teiji Ito,日裔美籍作曲家,後來成為她的丈夫)的配樂。

歷史與文化背景

1943 年的美國電影幾乎就等於好萊塢片廠制度。黛倫在自己家裡、幾乎零預算拍出這部片,比戰後美國前衛電影的興起早了幾年。她後來把這類作品稱為「室內電影」(chamber films)——小型、個人化的作品,就像室內樂之於管弦樂團。她親自推廣:1946 年她租下紐約的普羅溫斯敦劇場(Provincetown Playhouse)放映自己的作品;同年她成為第一位獲得古根漢獎助、從事電影創作的電影人。

內容概述

一名女子走路回家,看到小路上的一朵花,以及一個在轉角消失的身影;她用鑰匙開門進屋,坐在椅子上睡著了。接下來的情節不斷重複又變化:一個穿黑色斗篷、臉是一面鏡子的人影從她面前走開;刀子先出現在麵包上,接著出現在床上;電話話筒沒掛好;鑰匙掉落,變成一把刀。女子看見其他版本的自己——有一幕,三個她坐在桌邊,抽鑰匙決定誰要採取行動。最後一個男人回家,發現她死在椅子上,身上覆蓋著碎鏡片與海草。這到底是夢、自殺,還是象徵性的謀殺,電影沒有給答案。

藝術風格與技巧

  • 用剪接製造夢的邏輯:匹配剪接讓她的一步從沙灘、草地、人行道走到地毯上——一個連續動作跨越不同地方。
  • 重複與變奏:同樣的物件(鑰匙、刀、花、電話、唱機)在不同情境中重複出現,於是變成充滿意味的象徵。
  • 主觀鏡頭與傾斜構圖:手持的觀點鏡頭,以及她上樓時跟著傾斜的攝影機,讓房子變得不穩定。
  • 自我的分裂與複製:畫面上同時出現好幾個黛倫,不靠對白就表現出內心衝突。

重要性與影響

這部片普遍被視為美國前衛電影與「出神電影」(trance film)的奠基作品之一。它影響了後來的實驗電影人,例如肯尼斯·安格(Kenneth Anger)與史坦·布雷克基(Stan Brakhage);影評人也常把它和大衛·林區電影中循環、身分分裂的結構相比(例如《驚狂》Lost Highway)——這是評論上的比較,不是有文獻證明的影響關係。它在 1990 年列入美國國家影片登記表;在 2022 年《視與聽》(Sight and Sound)影評人票選中排名第 16,是排名最高的短片。女性主義評論者把它解讀為女性在家庭中受困感的寫照;這是詮釋,不是創作者本人的說法。

今天在哪裡看得到

它常在美術館與電影學校的節目中放映,也收錄在黛倫作品的影音合輯中,部分串流平台可以觀看;各國上架情況不同,請查詢當地平台。

Section 8 — Film Analysis Method, Lesson 117: Neoformalism & Defamiliarization — How Films Make the Familiar Strange

ENEnglish version

1. Today's analytical method

Neoformalist analysis and defamiliarization. The idea comes from the Russian Formalist critic Viktor Shklovsky, whose 1917 essay "Art as Device" argued that habit makes us stop really seeing things, and that art exists to make them strange again — ostranenie, "defamiliarization" or "making strange". The film scholar Kristin Thompson turned this into a method for film in Breaking the Glass Armor: Neoformalist Film Analysis (1988). It matters because it gives you a precise question to ask of any film: what habit of perception is this film breaking, and how?

2. Detailed explanation

  • Devices and functions: a "device" is any element — a cut, a colour, a sound, a gag, a plot twist. Neoformalism asks what function each device has in the whole film, not what it "means" in isolation.
  • Motivation: Thompson lists four reasons a device can be justified: compositional (the story needs it), realistic (it seems true to life), transtextual (it is a genre convention — people burst into song in a musical), and artistic (it is there mainly to be noticed as form). Defamiliarization is strongest when artistic motivation dominates.
  • Background and norms: strangeness is always relative to what viewers expect — Hollywood continuity, genre rules, or the film's own earlier patterns. So you must name the norm being broken.
  • The dominant: the main formal principle that organises a film (for example, a film whose dominant is spatial play rather than plot).
  • What to watch for: moments where you notice the form itself — a held shot that goes on too long, a sound that doesn't match, a familiar action shown from an unfamiliar angle.
  • Common misconceptions: (1) Neoformalism does not say content doesn't matter; it studies how form shapes perception, including of social life. (2) "Strange" doesn't mean "weird for its own sake" — the test is whether the strangeness makes you see something familiar freshly. (3) It is not the same as Brecht's alienation effect (taught through Section 7's Brecht entry): both break habits, but Brecht's aim is political distance, while Shklovsky's is renewed perception.

3. Concrete film example — Play Time (1967, dir. Jacques Tati)

Film: Play Time, directed by Jacques Tati, shot in 70mm on a huge purpose-built set of glass-and-steel buildings outside Paris, nicknamed "Tativille". Thompson devotes a chapter of Breaking the Glass Armor to it.

Scene: the Royal Garden restaurant sequence in the second half — especially the moment when Monsieur Hulot accidentally shatters the restaurant's glass front door.

  1. What to look for first: the glass. The whole film is full of transparent doors and walls that people bump into or misjudge. By the time we reach the restaurant, we have learned that glass is both invisible and a barrier.
  2. The device: the door breaks, but the doorman keeps holding the large brass handle that is left and keeps "opening" the empty space for arriving guests, who politely walk through the door that no longer exists.
  3. What it does to perception: we usually don't notice the ritual of a door — opening, waiting, bowing. Without the glass, only the ritual is left, so we suddenly see it as a social performance. That is defamiliarization in a single gag.
  4. Framing choices: Tati shows most gags in wide, deep shots with almost no close-ups, and often several gags at once in different parts of the frame. The film doesn't tell you where to look, so you have to search the image — another broken habit (Hollywood guides attention with close-ups and cuts).
  5. Sound: the restaurant is full of overlapping chatter and small mechanical noises, and dialogue is mostly background. Sound doesn't single out a "main" line, which supports the same democratic scanning.
  6. Deeper interpretation (subjective): the film can be read as a gentle critique of modern design that makes people behave like parts of a machine — and as a celebration of how people turn rigid spaces into play as the night goes on and the restaurant falls apart. This is an interpretation; the observation is the broken door and the doorman's continued ritual.

4. Practical viewing exercise

Choose a film you know well and find one moment where an everyday action (eating, using a phone, opening a door, a conversation) is shown in a way that makes you notice it. Write: (a) the everyday norm it breaks, (b) the device used (framing, duration, sound, editing, performance), (c) its motivation (compositional, realistic, transtextual or artistic), and (d) what you now see that you did not see before.

5. Key takeaways

  • Ask what habit of seeing a film breaks, and by which device.
  • Name the norm (Hollywood style, genre, or the film's own pattern) — strangeness only exists against a background.
  • Separate observation (what the device is) from interpretation (what the renewed perception might mean).

6. Suggested further viewing

Songs from the Second Floor (2000, dir. Roy Andersson) — static, long, carefully composed tableaux of ordinary office and street life. Practise naming which everyday rituals become strange, and how duration and stillness do it.

中繁體中文版

1. 今日分析方法

新形式主義分析與「陌生化」。這個概念來自俄國形式主義評論家什克洛夫斯基(Viktor Shklovsky):他 1917 年的文章〈作為手法的藝術〉主張,習慣讓我們不再真正「看見」事物,而藝術存在的目的,就是讓它們重新變得陌生——ostranenie,也就是「陌生化」。電影學者克莉絲汀·湯普遜(Kristin Thompson)在 1988 年的《打破玻璃盔甲:新形式主義電影分析》(Breaking the Glass Armor)中,把它發展成分析電影的方法。它重要的原因是:它給你一個可以問任何電影的精確問題——這部電影打破了哪一種觀看習慣?怎麼打破的?

2. 詳細說明

  • 手法與功能:「手法」(device)是任何元素——一個剪接、一個顏色、一個聲音、一個笑點、一個劇情轉折。新形式主義問的是每個手法在整部片中有什麼功能,而不是單獨看它「代表什麼」。
  • 動機:湯普遜列出手法成立的四種理由:結構動機(故事需要)、寫實動機(看起來像真實生活)、跨文本動機(類型慣例——歌舞片裡大家突然開始唱歌)、藝術動機(主要是為了讓人注意到形式本身)。當藝術動機佔主導時,陌生化最強。
  • 背景與常規:「陌生」永遠是相對於觀眾的預期——好萊塢連戲剪接、類型規則,或這部片自己前面建立的模式。所以你必須說出被打破的是哪一種常規。
  • 主導要素(the dominant):組織整部片的主要形式原則(例如一部片的主導要素是空間的遊戲,而不是情節)。
  • 要注意什麼:那些讓你意識到「形式本身」的時刻——一個停留太久的鏡頭、一個對不上的聲音、從陌生角度呈現的熟悉動作。
  • 常見誤解:(1)新形式主義不是說內容不重要;它研究形式如何塑造知覺,包括我們對社會生活的知覺。(2)「陌生」不等於「為怪而怪」——檢驗標準是:這種陌生感有沒有讓你重新看見熟悉的事物。(3)它和布萊希特的「疏離效果」(第 7 段曾介紹布萊希特)不同:兩者都打破習慣,但布萊希特的目標是政治上的距離,什克洛夫斯基的目標是更新知覺。

3. 具體電影範例 —《遊戲時間》(Play Time,1967,賈克·大地導演)

電影:《遊戲時間》,賈克·大地(Jacques Tati)導演,以 70 釐米底片,在巴黎郊外專門搭建、外號「大地城」(Tativille)的巨大玻璃鋼骨建築場景中拍攝。湯普遜在《打破玻璃盔甲》中用一整章討論這部片。

段落:後半部的「皇家花園」(Royal Garden)餐廳段落——特別是于勒先生(Monsieur Hulot)不小心撞碎餐廳玻璃大門的那一刻。

  1. 先看什麼:玻璃。整部片充滿透明的門和牆,人們一直撞上去或判斷錯誤。到了餐廳這段,我們已經學會:玻璃既看不見,又是一道障礙。
  2. 手法:門碎了,但門房繼續握著剩下的那個大銅把手,不斷為抵達的客人「打開」那片空無一物的空間,客人也很有禮貌地從那扇已經不存在的門走進來。
  3. 它對知覺做了什麼:我們平常不會注意到「門的儀式」——開門、等待、鞠躬。少了玻璃,只剩下儀式,於是我們突然看見它是一場社交表演。這就是用一個笑點完成的陌生化。
  4. 構圖選擇:大地用廣角、深景深的遠景呈現大部分笑點,幾乎不用特寫,而且常常在畫面不同角落同時發生好幾個笑點。電影不告訴你該看哪裡,你必須自己在畫面中搜尋——又打破了一個習慣(好萊塢用特寫和剪接引導注意力)。
  5. 聲音:餐廳裡充滿重疊的閒聊和細小的機械聲,對白大多是背景。聲音沒有挑出一條「主線」,支持同樣「平等掃視」的觀看方式。
  6. 更深的詮釋(主觀):這部片可以讀成對現代設計的溫和批評——現代設計讓人像機器零件一樣行動;也可以讀成一種讚頌:隨著夜晚推進、餐廳逐漸崩壞,人們把僵硬的空間變成遊戲。這是詮釋;觀察的部分是碎掉的門,以及門房繼續進行的儀式。

4. 實作練習

挑一部你很熟的電影,找一個把日常動作(吃飯、用手機、開門、聊天)拍得讓你「注意到它」的時刻。寫下:(a)它打破了哪一種日常常規;(b)用了什麼手法(構圖、時長、聲音、剪接、表演);(c)它的動機(結構、寫實、跨文本或藝術);(d)你現在看見了什麼以前沒看見的東西。

5. 重點整理

  • 問電影打破了哪一種觀看習慣,用的是哪一種手法。
  • 說出常規(好萊塢風格、類型,或電影自己的模式)——陌生只存在於背景之上。
  • 區分觀察(手法是什麼)與詮釋(重新看見的東西可能代表什麼)。

6. 延伸觀看

《二樓傳來的歌聲》(Songs from the Second Floor,2000,洛伊·安德森導演)——靜止、冗長、精心構圖的辦公室與街頭日常場景。練習說出哪些日常儀式變得陌生,以及時長與靜止是如何做到的。
