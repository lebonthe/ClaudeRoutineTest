3) Spanish Lesson — Day 27 (10 min, builds on Day 26)

Days 25-26 taught the imperative mood (affirmative and negative commands) and showed that every negative command — and every regular "usted" affirmative command — actually borrows a form from a mood we hadn't named yet. Today names it: the present subjunctive itself, its general formation, and its very first everyday use — expressing a wish about what someone else does.

  • Quiero que vengas a la fiesta. — I want you to come to the party.
  • Espero que todo salga bien. — I hope everything turns out fine.
  • Mis padres quieren que estudie medicina. — My parents want me to study medicine.
  • ¿Quieres que te ayude? — Do you want me to help you?

Grammar note: the present subjunctive is formed with the same "opposite vowel" rule already seen in commands — regular -ar verbs take -e/-es/-e/-emos/-en, regular -er/-ir verbs take -a/-as/-a/-amos/-an — and, critically, the yo and él/ella forms are always identical in the subjunctive (unlike the indicative present, where they differ). Any verb with an irregular yo-stem in the indicative (tener→tengo, hacer→hago) carries that same irregular stem through every subjunctive form (tenga, tengas, tenga, tengamos, tengan). The trigger pattern is: [Subject A] + a verb of wish/desire (querer, esperar, necesitar) + que + [Subject B, a different person] + subjunctive. When the wisher and the doer are the same person, Spanish drops "que" entirely and uses a plain infinitive instead — "Quiero estudiar" (I want to study, myself) vs. "Quiero que estudies" (I want you to study) — mixing these up is one of the most common learner errors.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 27: 頤 (Yí) — "Nourishment" / "The Corners of the Mouth" (Mountain above, Thunder below). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary bridges directly from yesterday: "物畜然後可養,故受之以頤,頤者養也。" ("Once things have been accumulated, they must be nourished/cared for — hence Dà Chù is succeeded by Yí, Nourishment; Yí means nourishing.") This is a direct continuation of yesterday's logic: the great store of strength and resources built up in Hexagram 26 is only valuable if it is properly nourished, sustained, and put to use — accumulation without ongoing nourishment simply decays. The Judgment reads: "頤:貞吉。觀頤,自求口實。" ("Nourishment: perseverance brings good fortune. Observe the providing of nourishment, and how each being seeks its own means of sustenance.") The Commentary on the Judgment (Tuanzhuan) expands: "天地養萬物,聖人養賢以及萬民,頤之時義大矣哉!" ("Heaven and earth nourish all beings; the sage nourishes the worthy, and through them the myriad people — great indeed is the timely significance of Nourishment!") The Image adds: "山下有雷,頤;君子以慎言語,節飲食。" ("Thunder at the foot of the mountain: the image of Nourishment. Thus the superior person is careful in speech and moderate in eating and drinking.") The hexagram's image — a mouth-like shape formed by one solid line at the top, one at the bottom, and four broken lines in between, echoed by the trigram picture of a still mountain sitting above stirring thunder — grounds its teaching in the most literal act of nourishment (eating and speaking, both functions of the mouth) before extending outward to the broader principle that anything of value, from a body to a state, survives only by being continually and appropriately fed.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Armenia (亞美尼亞)

  • Location: A landlocked country in the South Caucasus, bordered by Georgia (north), Azerbaijan (east, plus Azerbaijan's Nakhchivan exclave to the southwest), Iran (south), and Turkey (west).
  • Territory: Approximately 29,743 km² — roughly the size of Belgium, and slightly smaller than Taiwan (~36,193 km²); a highly mountainous country with an average elevation of about 1,800 m, home to Lake Sevan, one of the largest high-altitude lakes in the world.
  • Population: An estimated ~2.96 million (2025) live within Armenia itself, but the global Armenian diaspora is estimated at 5-7 million or more — over double the resident population, a legacy in large part of the mass displacement following the 1915 Armenian Genocide.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Armenian Apostolic Christian (~95%). Armenia is traditionally credited as the first nation in the world to adopt Christianity as its official state religion, in 301 CE under King Tiridates III — decades before the Roman Empire did.
  • Economy: An upper-middle-income economy (~US$7,810 average annual income); mining (copper, zinc, gold, molybdenum) is a major export sector, alongside a fast-growing IT/tech industry centered on Yerevan (sometimes branded the "Silicon Mountains" of the Caucasus) and significant remittances from the diaspora. Armenia is also one of the world's oldest wine-producing regions — a 6,100-year-old winery was excavated at the Areni-1 cave.
  • History: Traces its origins to the Iron Age Kingdom of Urartu (9th century BCE) and the Orontid-dynasty Kingdom of Armenia (6th century BCE); its unique 36-letter alphabet was created by the scholar-monk Mesrop Mashtots in 405 CE. Centuries of Persian, Byzantine, and Ottoman rule followed. The 1915 Armenian Genocide, carried out by the Ottoman Empire and killing an estimated 1-1.5 million Armenians, remains central to modern Armenian national identity and is formally recognized by dozens of countries. Armenia was a Soviet republic from the 1920s until independence in 1991. The decades-long Nagorno-Karabakh conflict with Azerbaijan over the ethnic-Armenian-majority enclave ended in September 2023, when Azerbaijan retook full control of the territory, triggering the near-total exodus of its roughly 120,000-person ethnic Armenian population.
  • Current leader(s): Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan (head of government, in office since May 2018, having come to power via that year's "Velvet Revolution" protest movement); President Vahagn Khachaturyan (a largely ceremonial head of state, in office since March 2022).
  • Language: Armenian (official), written in its own unique alphabet; Russian is widely spoken as a second language.
    Sources: theworldfactbook.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Karen"

  • Name: "Karen" — a common American first name repurposed as a pejorative stereotype label.
  • Origin/history: Precursors trace to 2014-era Reddit jokes (the "I'd like to speak to your manager" haircut, and an early "Nintendo Switch Karen" viral clip), but the name-as-stereotype usage solidified around 2017-2018, sharpened by high-profile viral incidents like 2018's "BBQ Becky" (a white woman calling police on a Black family barbecuing in a park), which cemented the racial-entitlement dimension of the character.
  • How it became popular: The label exploded into mainstream use during the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic, as videos of women refusing masks or confronting store employees over safety rules were captioned and shared en masse — usage across social media reportedly rose roughly 75% that year, pushing "Karen" from niche meme into everyday vocabulary.
  • Meaning/usage: Describes a specific caricature — usually a middle-class, middle-aged white woman exhibiting entitled, confrontational behavior, frequently invoking managerial authority ("I want to speak to your manager"), calling police over minor or racially charged non-issues, or refusing to follow shared social/health rules.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned a male counterpart ("Ken"), a younger-entitled-man variant ("Kevin"), and countless localized/national riffs; also inspired real-world "Karen"-themed haircut and sunglasses aesthetics used as visual shorthand even without the name being said.
  • Current status: Fully mainstream and still in everyday use, but increasingly contested — commentators and advocacy voices have argued the term has become sexist and ageist against women (and, by extension, against anyone actually named Karen), and some outlets and platforms have called for retiring it even as it remains one of the 2020s' most durable social-behavior meme labels.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, time.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — The Battle of Algiers (La battaglia di Algeri, 1966, dir. Gillo Pontecorvo)

  • Name/creator: The Battle of Algiers, an Italian-Algerian co-production directed by Gillo Pontecorvo, with a landmark score co-composed by Ennio Morricone and Pontecorvo himself.
  • Era/background: Made just four years after Algeria won independence from France, dramatizing the 1954-1957 phase of the Algerian War of Independence and the FLN's urban guerrilla campaign against French colonial forces in the city of Algiers.
  • Plot/content overview: Structured largely as flashback from a French paratrooper commander's interrogation of a captured FLN leader, the film reconstructs the escalating cycle of terrorist bombings by the FLN and brutal counter-insurgency (including systematic torture) by French forces, refusing to cast either side as simple heroes or villains.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Shot in grainy, high-contrast black-and-white to mimic newsreel footage, using entirely non-professional actors (many actual veterans of the real events), multiple cameras to inflate the apparent size of street crowds, and deliberately repeated reshoots — sometimes 20 takes per scene — so performers would look authentically fatigued and battle-worn; no archival footage was actually used despite the documentary illusion.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as one of the most influential political films ever made, and unusual for being embraced simultaneously by anti-colonial revolutionary movements as a training/inspiration text and by military institutions (including, reportedly, the Pentagon, which screened it in 2003 while planning counter-insurgency strategy for Iraq) as a case study in urban guerrilla warfare and torture's moral and strategic costs.
  • Influence and legacy: Its handheld, newsreel-style "pseudo-documentary" aesthetic and use of non-actors directly influenced later filmmakers including Paul Greengrass, Spike Lee, Ken Loach, Steven Soderbergh, Oliver Stone, and Christopher Nolan, and it helped define a template later liberation movements and colonial powers alike have studied.
  • Current status/where to experience: Preserved and distributed by the Criterion Collection (available on the Criterion Channel and home video), regularly programmed in film-history and political-cinema courses, and still screened at retrospectives on anti-colonial and war cinema worldwide.
    Sources: bfi.org.uk, criterion.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Genre & Convention Analysis (Lesson 13)

Lessons 1-12 covered mise-en-scène, editing/montage, cinematography/lighting, sound, colour, narrative structure, blocking/staging, framing/composition, symbolism, auteur theory, the long take, and performance analysis — all lenses for reading a single film closely. Today's lens works differently: it reads a film against the whole body of films that came before it in its genre.

The method: Every genre (the Western, film noir, the horror film, the musical...) accumulates a shared grammar of expected iconography, character types, plot beats, and moral logic through repetition across many films — audiences arrive already knowing, roughly, "how a Western behaves." Genre analysis asks: (1) What conventions does this film invoke? Identify the specific iconography/tropes being deployed (the gunslinger's showdown, the femme fatale, the final girl) and name the genre expectation each one is designed to trigger. (2) Does the film satisfy that expectation, subvert it, or hybridize it with another genre entirely? A film can lean fully into convention for comfort/catharsis, invert a convention for critique or irony, or graft one genre's grammar onto another's setting for new meaning. (3) What ideology is smuggled in by the convention itself? Genre conventions are rarely neutral — the classical Western's "righteous violence" convention, for instance, encodes a specific mythology about frontier justice; noticing when a film keeps, softens, or dismantles that encoded ideology is the payoff of this lens. (4) What does the audience's genre literacy let the film skip? Because viewers already know the rules, a genre film can compress exposition and instead spend its runtime on variation — the interesting move is usually in the gap between the convention and what the film actually delivers.

Worked example — Unforgiven (1992, dir. Clint Eastwood), the final showdown at Skinny's saloon: Classical Westerns (many starring Eastwood himself decades earlier) built the showdown as a clean, sunlit, almost ceremonial contest that resolves the plot and confirms the hero's righteousness. Unforgiven invokes every iconographic beat of that convention — the aging gunslinger returning to violence, the walk into a saloon, the drawn pistol — but stages it at night, indoors, in flickering lamplight and driving rain, and lets William Munny's actual killing be graceless, cold-blooded, and morally ugly rather than triumphant: he shoots an unarmed man, threatens a room of bystanders, and the "victory" leaves no one, including the audience, feeling cleansed. The film is explicitly in conversation with the mythology Eastwood's own earlier Westerns helped build, using the audience's genre literacy about how a showdown is "supposed" to feel in order to make its absence land as a deliberate indictment.

What to look for and why it works: (1) The convention is quoted precisely so its violation is legible — Eastwood stages every beat an experienced Western viewer expects, which is what makes the tonal wrongness (darkness, rain, cruelty) register as meaningful rather than merely different. (2) Genre subversion here does ideological work, not just stylistic work — by refusing the showdown's usual catharsis, the film argues that the Western's traditional "righteous gunfighter" myth was always sanitizing something brutal. (3) The lens only works if you know the genre's baseline first — without dozens of prior Westerns' clean, heroic showdowns as a reference point, Unforgiven's ending reads as merely grim rather than as a pointed rebuttal of an entire genre's mythology.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第二十七天(10 分鐘,銜接第二十六天內容)

第二十五、二十六天教了命令式語氣(肯定與否定命令),也發現每一個否定命令、以及規則動詞 usted 的肯定命令,其實都借用了一個當時還沒正式命名的語氣形態。今天就要正式命名它:虛擬式現在式(Presente de Subjuntivo)本身、它的一般形成規則,以及它最基本、最日常的第一個用法——表達對「別人」所做的事的期望或願望。

  • Quiero que vengas a la fiesta. — 我希望你來參加派對。
  • Espero que todo salga bien. — 我希望一切順利。
  • Mis padres quieren que estudie medicina. — 我父母希望我念醫學。
  • ¿Quieres que te ayude? — 你想要我幫你嗎?

文法重點:虛擬式現在式的形成規則,和命令式已經學過的「母音對調」規則完全相同——規則 -ar 動詞字尾改成 -e/-es/-e/-emos/-en;規則 -er/-ir 動詞字尾改成 -a/-as/-a/-amos/-an;而且非常關鍵的一點是,虛擬式中 yo 和 él/ella 的形態永遠一模一樣(不像現在式直陳式中,這兩者通常不同)。任何在直陳式現在式中 yo 字根不規則的動詞(tener→tengo、hacer→hago),在虛擬式的每一個人稱中,都會延續同一個不規則字根(tenga、tengas、tenga、tengamos、tengan)。觸發虛擬式的固定句型是:「[主詞 A] + 表達願望/期望的動詞(querer、esperar、necesitar)+ que + [主詞 B,與 A 不同的人] + 虛擬式動詞」。當「希望的人」和「做這件事的人」是同一個人時,西班牙語會直接省略 que,改用原形動詞——例如「Quiero estudiar」(我想自己念書)對比「Quiero que estudies」(我希望你去念書)——這兩者的混淆,是初學者最常犯的錯誤之一。

第二十七卦:頤卦(Yí)——「養育之道」(上艮下震,山下有雷)。〈序卦傳〉直接銜接了昨天的卦象:「物畜然後可養,故受之以頤,頤者養也。」(萬物先經過積蓄,之後才談得上養育,因此大畜卦之後,便是頤卦;頤,意即養育。)這正是昨天邏輯的直接延續:昨天大畜卦所積蓄下來的龐大力量與資源,唯有經過持續的養育、涵養與善加運用,才真正具有價值——單純積蓄而不加以養育,終將逐漸衰敗。卦辭寫道:「頤:貞吉。觀頤,自求口實。」(頤卦象徵:守持正道,吉祥。觀察頤養之道,便是觀察每個生命如何自行尋求維持生存所需的食物。)〈彖傳〉進一步闡述:「天地養萬物,聖人養賢以及萬民,頤之時義大矣哉!」(天地養育萬物,聖人養育賢能之士,並藉此惠及天下百姓——頤養之道所蘊含、順應時勢的深遠意義,是多麼重大啊!)〈象傳〉補充道:「山下有雷,頤;君子以慎言語,節飲食。」(雷聲在山腳下震動,這正是頤卦的象徵;因此君子應當謹言慎語,節制飲食。)這一卦的卦象——上下各一條陽爻、中間夾著四條陰爻,形似一個張開的口——呼應了「山靜、雷動」的卦象組合,將教誨立基於最直接的養育行為(飲食與言語,恰好都是口的功能)之上,再進一步推展至更廣泛的原則:無論是一個身體、還是一個國家,任何有價值之物,唯有持續、適當地被滋養,才能長久存續。

(第二十七次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)、第二十一卦 噬嗑(Shì Kè)、第二十二卦 賁(Bì)、第二十三卦 剝(Bō)、第二十四卦 復(Fù)、第二十五卦 无妄(Wú Wàng)、第二十六卦 大畜(Dà Chù)、第二十七卦 頤(Yí)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 亞美尼亞(Armenia)

  • 位置: 位於南高加索地區的內陸國,北接喬治亞、東接亞塞拜然(西南側還與亞塞拜然的納希契凡飛地相鄰)、南接伊朗、西接土耳其。
  • 領土面積: 約 29,743 平方公里——大致相當於比利時的面積,略小於台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里);國土高度多山,平均海拔約 1,800 公尺,境內的塞凡湖(Lake Sevan)是全世界最大的高海拔湖泊之一。
  • 人口: 亞美尼亞境內人口估計約 296 萬人(2025 年),但全球亞美尼亞僑民人數估計達 500 萬至 700 萬人以上——是境內人口的兩倍以上,這在很大程度上是 1915 年亞美尼亞大屠殺造成大規模流離失所所留下的歷史遺產。
  • 宗教: 絕大多數信奉亞美尼亞使徒教會(Armenian Apostolic Church,約占 95%)。傳統上,亞美尼亞被視為全世界第一個將基督教立為國教的國家,時間可追溯至西元 301 年,由國王梯里達底三世(Tiridates III)宣布——比羅馬帝國正式立基督教為國教還早了數十年。
  • 經濟: 屬於中高收入經濟體(平均年收入約 7,810 美元);礦業(銅、鋅、金、鉬)是主要出口產業,以首都葉里溫(Yerevan)為核心的資訊科技產業也快速成長(有時被稱為高加索地區的「矽山」),僑民匯款同樣是重要的經濟來源。亞美尼亞也是全世界最古老的葡萄酒產區之一——在阿雷尼一號洞穴(Areni-1 cave)曾出土一座距今 6,100 年的古代酒莊遺址。
  • 歷史: 其源頭可追溯至西元前 9 世紀鐵器時代的烏拉爾圖王國(Kingdom of Urartu),以及西元前 6 世紀奧龍特王朝統治下的亞美尼亞王國;獨特的 36 字母亞美尼亞字母,則是由學者兼修士梅斯羅普・馬什托茨(Mesrop Mashtots)於西元 405 年所創制。此後歷經波斯、拜占庭與奧斯曼帝國數百年的統治。1915 年由奧斯曼帝國所犯下的「亞美尼亞大屠殺」,造成估計 100 萬至 150 萬亞美尼亞人死亡,至今仍是現代亞美尼亞民族認同的核心事件,並已獲數十個國家正式承認。亞美尼亞自 1920 年代起成為蘇聯加盟共和國,直到 1991 年才正式獨立。與亞塞拜然之間曠日持久的納戈爾諾-卡拉巴赫(Nagorno-Karabakh)衝突,則於 2023 年 9 月落幕——亞塞拜然重新完全控制該地區,導致當地約 12 萬亞美尼亞裔居民幾乎全數出走。
  • 現任領導人: 總理尼科爾・帕辛揚(Nikol Pashinyan,政府首腦,自 2018 年 5 月就任,透過當年的「絲絨革命」抗議運動上台);總統瓦哈格恩・哈查圖良(Vahagn Khachaturyan,大致為虛位國家元首,自 2022 年 3 月就任)。
  • 語言: 官方語言為亞美尼亞語,使用其獨有的專屬字母書寫;俄語則作為第二語言廣泛使用。
    資料來源:theworldfactbook.org、en.wikipedia.org

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「Karen」(凱倫哏)

  • 名稱: 「Karen」——一個常見的美國女性名字,被挪用為一種刻板印象的貶義標籤。
  • 起源/歷史: 其前身可追溯至 2014 年前後 Reddit 上的哏圖笑話(「我要找你的經理」髮型哏,以及一支早期爆紅的「Nintendo Switch Karen」短片),但真正把「Karen」這個名字定型為刻板印象標籤,是在 2017 至 2018 年間逐漸成形,並因 2018 年「BBQ Becky」事件(一名白人女性因黑人家庭在公園烤肉而報警)等高關注度爆紅事件,進一步強化了其中的種族特權意涵。
  • 如何爆紅: 2020 年新冠疫情期間,這個標籤徹底進入主流用語——大量女性拒戴口罩、或因防疫規定與店員起衝突的影片被大量配上「Karen」字幕並廣為轉發,據報當年此詞在社群媒體上的使用量成長約 75%,讓「Karen」從小眾哏圖一舉躍升為日常詞彙。
  • 意義與使用情境: 用來描述一種特定的刻板形象——通常是中產階級、中年白人女性,表現出自恃特權、好與人衝突的行為,經常動輒訴諸「經理權威」(要求「我要找你的經理」)、因輕微或帶有種族色彩的小事報警,或拒絕遵守公共防疫/社會規範。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出男性對照版本「Ken」,以及較年輕、自恃特權男性的變體「Kevin」,還有無數在地化/各國版本;也催生出即使不直接說出這個名字、僅靠特定髮型與墨鏡造型就能作為視覺代稱的「Karen 風格」。
  • 目前現況: 至今仍是主流且日常使用的詞彙,但爭議也與日俱增——部分評論者與倡議團體認為,這個詞已經演變成對女性(以及,連帶對真正名叫 Karen 的人)帶有性別歧視與年齡歧視意味,部分媒體與平台甚至呼籲應該停用此詞,即便如此,它仍是 2020 年代最歷久不衰的社會行為類哏圖標籤之一。
    資料來源:knowyourmeme.com、time.com

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《大戰阿爾及爾》(La battaglia di Algeri,1966,導演吉洛・彭特科沃 Gillo Pontecorvo)

  • 名稱/創作者: 《大戰阿爾及爾》,義大利與阿爾及利亞合資製作,由吉洛・彭特科沃執導,由顏尼歐・莫利克奈(Ennio Morricone)與彭特科沃本人共同創作的經典配樂,同樣是本片的重要標誌。
  • 時代背景: 在阿爾及利亞脫離法國獨立僅四年後拍攝完成,劇情重現 1954 至 1957 年阿爾及利亞獨立戰爭階段、民族解放陣線(FLN)在阿爾及爾市內對法國殖民軍發動的城市游擊戰。
  • 劇情概述: 全片主要以法國空降部隊指揮官對一名被俘 FLN 領袖的訊問場景為敘事框架,以倒敘手法重建 FLN 一連串升級中的恐怖爆炸行動,以及法軍(包括系統性刑求)所展開的殘酷鎮壓行動,刻意拒絕將任一方簡化為單純的英雄或惡人。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 以粗顆粒、高對比的黑白畫面拍攝,刻意模擬新聞紀錄片質感;全片演員清一色為非職業演員(其中不少人正是當年真實事件的參與者);運用多台攝影機拉大街頭群眾場面的視覺規模;並刻意反覆重拍——某些場景甚至重拍達 20 次之多——只為讓演員呈現出真實的疲憊與飽經戰火的神態;儘管營造出強烈的紀錄片假象,片中實際上並未使用任何真實檔案影像。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 普遍被視為影史上最具影響力的政治電影之一,罕見地同時受到反殖民革命運動(視其為訓練與精神啟發教材)與軍事機構(據報美國國防部曾於 2003 年,在規劃伊拉克反叛亂戰略時放映此片)雙方的重視,將其視為城市游擊戰,以及刑求所付出的道德與戰略代價的經典案例研究。
  • 影響與傳承: 其手持攝影、新聞紀錄片式的「偽紀錄片」美學,以及大量運用非職業演員的手法,直接影響了後世導演,包括保羅・葛林葛拉斯(Paul Greengrass)、史派克・李(Spike Lee)、肯・洛區(Ken Loach)、史蒂芬・索德柏(Steven Soderbergh)、奧利佛・史東(Oliver Stone)與克里斯多福・諾蘭(Christopher Nolan),並協助建立了一套後世解放運動與殖民政權雙方都持續研究的範式。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 由 Criterion Collection 負責修復與發行(可透過 Criterion Channel 串流平台或實體影碟觀賞),經常被列入電影史與政治電影相關課程的教材,至今世界各地探討反殖民與戰爭電影的回顧影展仍持續放映此片。
    資料來源:bfi.org.uk、criterion.com

8) 看片方法 — 類型與慣例分析 Genre & Convention Analysis(第十三課)

第一到十二課教了場面調度、剪輯與蒙太奇、攝影與燈光、音效、色彩、敘事結構、走位與空間調度、取景與構圖、象徵與視覺隱喻、作者論、長鏡頭,以及表演/演技分析——這些都是深入解讀「單一部電影」的視角。今天的分析視角則不同:它要把一部電影,放進「這個類型過去所有作品」所構成的整體脈絡中來閱讀。

方法說明: 每一種電影類型(西部片、黑色電影、恐怖片、音樂劇⋯⋯)都會透過大量作品的不斷重複,逐漸累積出一套觀眾共享的圖像語言、角色類型、劇情節拍與道德邏輯——觀眾在進場前,大致就已經知道「一部西部片『應該』怎麼進行」。類型分析要問的是:(1)這部片援用了哪些慣例? 找出片中具體運用了哪些圖像/套路(槍手對決、蛇蠍美人、活到最後的女主角),並指出每一個套路原本是設計來觸發觀眾哪一種類型期待。(2)這部片是滿足了這個期待、顛覆了它,還是把它和另一個類型混血、雜交在一起? 一部電影可以完全順著慣例走以求安穩感/情感宣洩,也可以刻意反轉某個慣例來達到批判或諷刺效果,或是把一個類型的語法,嫁接到另一個類型的場景設定上,創造出全新意義。(3)這個慣例本身,悄悄夾帶了什麼意識形態? 類型慣例幾乎從來不是中立的——例如經典西部片「正義暴力」的慣例,本身就編碼了一整套關於邊疆正義的特定神話;留意一部電影究竟是保留、軟化,還是徹底拆解了這套內建的意識形態,正是這個分析視角最有價值的收穫。(4)觀眾的「類型識讀能力」,讓這部片可以省略掉什麼? 正因為觀眾早已熟知這套規則,類型片才能大幅壓縮鋪陳、把篇幅留給「變化」本身——真正有趣的地方,通常就藏在「慣例」與「這部片實際端出什麼」之間的落差裡。

實例示範——《殺無赦》(Unforgiven,1992,導演克林・伊斯威特 Clint Eastwood)在斯基尼酒館(Skinny's saloon)的結局槍戰: 經典西部片(其中不少正是克林・伊斯威特本人數十年前主演的作品)向來把最終對決處理成一場乾淨、陽光明亮、近乎儀式性的較量,用來收束劇情、並確認英雄的正當性。《殺無赦》援用了這個慣例的每一個圖像元素——重出江湖的年邁槍手、走進酒館的畫面、拔槍的動作——卻刻意將整場戲安排在夜晚、室內、搭配閃爍的燈光與傾盆大雨中進行,並讓比爾・莫尼(William Munny)真正的殺戮顯得毫無風度、冷血且道德上令人不安,而非凱旋式的勝利:他槍殺一名沒有武裝的男子,以武力威嚇滿屋子的旁觀者,而這場「勝利」無論對片中人物還是觀眾而言,都完全談不上任何淨化或救贖的感受。這部片明顯是在與伊斯威特自己早年主演的西部片所共同建立的那套神話進行對話,刻意利用觀眾對於「對決『應該』有什麼感覺」的類型識讀能力,讓這種缺席的宣洩感,轉化為一種刻意為之、直指整個類型的控訴。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)慣例被精確地「引用」出來,才能讓對它的違反被看懂——伊斯威特把一個熟悉西部片的觀眾所預期的每一個節拍都完整搬演出來,這正是為什麼那種調性上的「不對」(黑暗、下雨、殘酷)會顯得意義深遠,而不只是單純的與眾不同。(2)這裡的類型顛覆,做的是意識形態層面的工作,而不只是風格層面的工作——透過拒絕給予對決通常帶來的情感宣洩,這部片等於在論證:西部片傳統上「正義槍手」的神話,其實一直都在美化某種本質上殘暴的東西。(3)這個分析視角,唯有先掌握該類型的「基準線」才能成立——如果沒有數十部先前西部片中乾淨、英雄式對決作為參照基準,《殺無赦》的結局,讀起來就只會是單純的陰暗,而不會被理解為一次對整個類型神話的精準反駁。
