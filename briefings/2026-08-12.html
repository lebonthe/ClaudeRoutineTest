3) Spanish Lesson — Day 34 (10 min, builds on Day 33)

Day 33 covered adjective (relative) clauses, where subjunctive vs. indicative depends on whether the antecedent noun is known to exist. Today opens a fourth, equally independent trigger category: adverbial clauses of purpose and concession. Purpose clauses with para que always take the subjunctive, but only when the two clauses have different subjects — same-subject purpose uses para + infinitive instead. Concession with aunque is the trickiest case in the whole lesson series so far: the exact same word takes the subjunctive when the conceded fact is still hypothetical/unconfirmed (Voy a salir aunque llueva — "even if it turns out to rain"), but the indicative when the speaker already knows the fact to be true (Voy a salir aunque llueve — "even though it is, in fact, raining right now"). Finally, a menos que, con tal de que, and sin que behave like Day 32's antes de que: they take only the subjunctive, in every case, with no indicative alternative, because what they describe is inherently a condition or exclusion that hasn't been established as fact.

  • Te presto mi coche para que puedas ir al aeropuerto. — I'll lend you my car so that you can go to the airport.
  • Voy a salir aunque llueva. — I'll go out even if it rains. (not yet known whether it will rain)
  • Voy a salir aunque llueve. — I'll go out even though it's raining. (it is, in fact, raining right now)
  • No puedes entrar sin que te vean. — You can't get in without being seen.

Grammar note: para que + subjunctive requires the purpose clause's subject to differ from the main clause's subject (Estudio para aprobar el examen uses para + infinitive instead, since the studier and the passer are the same "I"). Aunque is the one connector in this category that can take either mood, and the mood alone signals whether the conceded circumstance is a live fact or a mere hypothetical — arguably the single most commonly tested "trap" in the whole subjunctive system. A menos que, con tal de que, and sin que never take the indicative at all, regardless of how likely the described condition actually is.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 34: 大壯 (Dà Zhuàng) — "The Power of the Great" (Thunder above, Heaven below — Zhèn over Qián). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the line directly from yesterday's Hexagram 33: "物不可以終遯,故受之以大壯。" ("Things cannot end in retreat forever; hence Dùn [Retreat] is followed by Dà Zhuàng [The Power of the Great].") Where Hexagram 33 counseled a timely, dignified withdrawal from unfavorable conditions, Dà Zhuàng marks the turn of the cycle: retreat is not permanent, and the ascending, vigorous (yang) energy that had been in retreat regains the upper hand and grows powerful once more. The Judgment reads: "大壯,利貞。" ("The Power of the Great: perseverance in righteousness furthers.") — deliberately terse, and deliberately conditional: great strength itself is presented as neither inherently good nor bad, valuable only when yoked to correctness. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "大壯,大者壯也,剛以動,故壯。大壯利貞,大者正也。正大而天地之情可見矣。" ("Da Zhuang means: the great one is strong. It is strong because it moves with vigor. 'Perseverance in righteousness furthers' means the great must also be correct. Once greatness and correctness are united, the true nature of Heaven and Earth becomes visible.") The image being read is four strong (yang) lines massed at the base of the hexagram, pushing vigorously upward beneath two yielding (yin) lines above — the mirror image of yesterday's Dùn, where yin was quietly rising to displace yang; here it is yang's turn to surge, and the hexagram's warning is precisely about the temptation of that surge: raw strength unaccompanied by discipline curdles into recklessness. The Image commentary supplies the ethical corrective: "雷在天上,大壯;君子以非禮弗履。" ("Thunder above Heaven: this is the image of Da Zhuang. Thus the superior person does not tread on any path that is contrary to propriety.") Read together, the three texts insist that true greatness is self-limiting rather than self-indulgent: possessing great power is precisely the moment that demands the most scrupulous self-restraint, since strength that oversteps propriety — however forceful in the moment — is not the "great strength" the hexagram actually endorses, only its degraded counterfeit.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Lesotho (賴索托)

  • Location: A landlocked mountain kingdom entirely enclosed within the borders of South Africa, located in southern Africa within the Maloti and Drakensberg mountain ranges — one of only three sovereign "enclave" nations on Earth completely surrounded by a single other country (the others being Vatican City and San Marino, both fully enclosed by Italy).
  • Territory: About 30,355 km² — remarkably close in size to Belgium (30,528 km²), and roughly 84% of Taiwan's land area (~36,193 km²). Uniquely, Lesotho is the only country in the world whose entire territory lies above 1,000 meters elevation, earning it the nickname "The Kingdom in the Sky" — even its lowest point (at the junction of the Orange and Makhaleng rivers, about 1,400m) sits higher than the highest point of many other nations.
  • Population: Approximately 2.3 million (2023 est.).
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Christian (roughly 90%), split mainly between Roman Catholic and Protestant denominations (notably the Lesotho Evangelical Church, descended from 19th-century French Protestant missionary work), with indigenous Basotho beliefs and ancestor veneration still practiced alongside, especially in rural areas.
  • Economy: Heavily dependent on textile and garment manufacturing exports (Lesotho is one of Africa's largest apparel exporters to the US under the African Growth and Opportunity Act, AGOA), remittances from Basotho men employed in South African mines, and — a distinctive feature — direct export revenue from selling fresh mountain water to South Africa via the Lesotho Highlands Water Project, one of the largest water-transfer engineering schemes in the world. Lesotho belongs to the Southern African Customs Union (SACU), and its currency, the loti, is pegged 1:1 to and freely interchangeable with the South African rand.
  • History: United in the early 19th century by King Moshoeshoe I, who gathered scattered Basotho refugee groups atop defensible mountain plateaus during the violent Difaqane upheavals sweeping southern Africa; sought British protection in 1868 (becoming the protectorate of Basutoland) specifically to avoid annexation by expanding Boer/Afrikaner republics; gained full independence as the Kingdom of Lesotho in 1966; experienced a coup in 1986 and a brief SADC-led military intervention in 1998 to restore order after a contested election.
  • Current leader: King Letsie III, a constitutional monarch serving as ceremonial head of state since 1996 (his second reign, having briefly held the throne 1990-1995 before his father Moshoeshoe II was reinstated); Prime Minister Sam Matekane, a businessman-turned-politician who became PM in 2022 after his newly formed Revolution for Prosperity (RFP) party won the general election.
  • Language: Sesotho and English, both official.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov/the-world-factbook

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Bad Luck Brian"

  • Name: "Bad Luck Brian," one of the defining "advice animal" image-macro memes of the early 2010s.
  • Origin/history: The photo is a 2005 high-school yearbook picture of Ohio teenager Ian Davis — wearing a plaid sweater vest, braces, and an awkward crooked grin — which his friend Kyle Craven scanned and submitted to Reddit's r/AdviceAnimals in January 2012 with the caption "Takes SATs... Sets curve for lowest possible score."
  • How it became popular: The image-macro format (an ironic caption describing an absurd, exaggerated string of terrible luck, paired with Brian's sheepishly cheerful yearbook grin) hit Reddit's front page within a day and spread rapidly to Facebook, Quickmeme, and Tumblr, generating an enormous wave of user-submitted captions almost overnight — a defining example of the "advice animal" genre that dominated meme culture in 2011-2013.
  • Meaning/usage: Captions describe increasingly absurd, often deliberately impossible strings of misfortune (e.g., "Finally beats cancer... Dies in car accident on the way home from hospital"), with the humor coming from the mismatch between the outlandish disaster described and Brian's mild, oblivious grin — distinct from genuinely relatable "bad day" humor.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned thousands of user-generated captioned images, spin-off merchandise, and media appearances by the real Ian Davis (including on Tosh.0 and The Today Show); occasionally paired in "crossover" images against other advice-animal characters like Success Kid for ironic contrast.
  • Current status: Now a dated but historically foundational meme, widely referenced as emblematic of the early-2010s Reddit "advice animal"/rage-comic image-macro era; still recognized and occasionally reused nostalgically rather than actively circulating as fresh content.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Rashomon (羅生門, 1950, dir. Akira Kurosawa)

  • Name/creators: Directed by Akira Kurosawa, adapted by Kurosawa and Shinobu Hashimoto from two short stories by Ryūnosuke Akutagawa — "Rashōmon" (supplying the ruined-gate frame and title) and "In a Grove" (supplying the central multi-perspective plot); cinematography by Kazuo Miyagawa; starring Toshiro Mifune, Machiko Kyō, and Masayuki Mori. Produced by Daiei Film.
  • Era/background: Made in 1950, during the Allied Occupation of postwar Japan, at a moment when Japanese cinema was still largely unknown outside Japan.
  • Plot/content overview: A woodcutter, a priest, and a commoner shelter from a downpour beneath the ruined Rashomon gate, recounting the trial that followed the rape of a samurai's wife and the samurai's death in a forest grove. Four participants — the bandit Tajōmaru, the wife, the dead samurai (speaking through a medium), and the woodcutter himself — each give an account of the same event that is self-serving and mutually irreconcilable with the others, leaving the audience with no single reliable version of what actually happened.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Pioneered pointing the camera directly into the sun through tree branches, letting dappled light flicker unpredictably across the actors' faces; fluid, extended tracking shots follow characters through the forest; and the structural device of contradictory subjective flashbacks — rather than one omniscient narrative — became so influential it gave rise to the now-universal term "the Rashomon effect," describing any situation where multiple witnesses to the same event give incompatible, self-interested accounts.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Won the Golden Lion at the 1951 Venice Film Festival and an honorary Academy Award, and is widely credited with single-handedly opening the door for Japanese — and more broadly Asian — cinema to international arthouse audiences for the first time.
  • Influence and legacy: "The Rashomon effect" entered everyday usage across law, journalism, and psychology to describe conflicting eyewitness testimony; the film's multi-perspective structure directly influenced countless later works, from Courage Under Fire to Vantage Point to numerous television episodes built around the same narrative device.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Restored and distributed internationally by the Criterion Collection; regularly screened in film-society retrospectives and taught as a canonical text in film studies programs worldwide.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, criterion.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Costume Design & Wardrobe as Characterization/Worldbuilding (Lesson 20)

Costume design does narrative work that dialogue and plot don't have to: a character's silhouette, color, texture, and how it changes (or doesn't) across a story can index internal state, social status, or a character arc without a line being spoken. When analyzing costume, look for three things: whether a costume evolves as a character changes internally (signaling growth or corruption visually before the plot confirms it), whether costume is being used as worldbuilding shorthand — communicating an invented culture's social structure, technology level, or history through clothing alone rather than exposition — and whether a deliberate anachronism or exaggeration in costume is functioning as authorial commentary rather than simple production design. This is distinct from Day 3's cinematography/color-grading lesson (which concerns light and the camera's palette) and Day 8's aspect-ratio lesson (which concerns frame shape): costume analysis concerns what is worn inside the frame, independent of how it is lit or shaped.

Worked example: Black Panther (2018, dir. Ryan Coogler, costume design by Ruth Carter) — the costuming of Wakanda's five tribes fuses real African textile and dress traditions (Basotho blanket patterns for the Border Tribe — the same Sesotho weaving tradition rooted in today's Lesotho spotlight above — Maasai beadwork motifs, Tuareg garments for the Merchant Tribe, and Dogon architecture-inspired silhouettes for the royal family) with futuristic vibranium-tech materials, so that costume alone instantly communicates Wakanda's fictional five-tribe political structure and its Afrofuturist synthesis of ancient tradition with advanced technology, without a single line of dialogue explaining any of it. Ruth Carter became the first Black costume designer to win the Academy Award for Costume Design for this work, a recognition of costume functioning here as genuine worldbuilding rather than decoration.

3)西班牙文課程 — 第 34 天(10 分鐘,承接第 33 天)

第 33 天教的是形容詞(關係)子句,虛擬式或直陳式的選擇,取決於先行詞名詞是否確定存在。今天要開啟第四個、同樣獨立的觸發類別:目的與讓步副詞子句。用 para que 表達目的時,恆用虛擬式,但前提是前後子句主詞不同——若主詞相同,則改用「para + 原形動詞」。而 aunque 表達讓步時,是目前為止整個課程系列裡最棘手的一個情況:同一個字,當讓步的事實仍屬假設、尚未確定時接虛擬式(Voy a salir aunque llueva——就算「結果真的」下雨,我還是要出門),但當說話者此刻已經確知這件事為真時,則接直陳式(Voy a salir aunque llueve——雖然此刻「真的」正在下雨,我還是要出門)。最後,a menos que、con tal de que、sin que 的行為模式,和第 32 天教過的 antes de que 一樣:不論任何情況,都只能接虛擬式,沒有對應的直陳式版本,因為這幾個連接詞所描述的,本質上就是一種尚未被確立為事實的條件或排除狀況。

  • Te presto mi coche para que puedas ir al aeropuerto. — 我把車借給你,好讓你能去機場。
  • Voy a salir aunque llueva. — 就算會下雨,我還是要出門。(還不確定會不會下雨)
  • Voy a salir aunque llueve. — 雖然現在正在下雨,我還是要出門。(此刻雨真的正在下)
  • No puedes entrar sin que te vean. — 你沒辦法在不被看到的情況下進去。

文法筆記:para que + 虛擬式這個結構,要求「目的子句」的主詞必須和「主要子句」的主詞不同(如果主詞相同,例如 Estudio para aprobar el examen,讀書的人和通過考試的人是同一個「我」,就要改用 para + 原形動詞,而非 para que)。aunque 是這個類別中唯一能同時接兩種語氣的連接詞,單靠語氣的切換,就能標記「讓步的狀況究竟是既成事實、還是純屬假設」——這可以說是整個虛擬式系統中最常被考、也最容易混淆的一個「陷阱」。而 a menos que、con tal de que、sin que 則完全不接直陳式,不論句子所描述的條件在現實中發生的機率有多高都一樣。

4)易經卦象

第 34 卦:大壯 (Dà Zhuàng) ——「大者昌盛」(震上乾下,雷在上、天在下)。〈序卦傳〉直接承接昨天的第 33 卦而來:「物不可以終遯,故受之以大壯。」(事物不可能永遠停留在退避的狀態,所以〈遯〉之後接著是〈大壯〉。)昨天的遯卦教導的是「在局勢不利時,及時、有尊嚴地退避」;大壯卦則標誌著局勢的轉折點:退避不會是永遠的,原本節節敗退的陽剛之氣,終究會重新占上風、再度壯盛起來。〈卦辭〉說:「大壯,利貞。」(大者昌盛:堅守正道會有利。)——這句話刻意簡短,也刻意帶著限定條件:強大的力量本身,被呈現為既非絕對的善、也非絕對的惡,唯有與「正道」相結合時才有價值。〈彖傳〉進一步闡述:「大壯,大者壯也,剛以動,故壯。大壯利貞,大者正也。正大而天地之情可見矣。」(大壯,是指「大者」變得強盛;因為陽剛以奮發的姿態行動,所以稱為「壯」。「大壯,利貞」是指強大者也必須同時保持中正;一旦「盛大」與「中正」合而為一,天地的真實本質便由此顯現。)這裡所讀出的卦象,是四條陽爻齊聚在卦的底部,正奮力向上推進,而上方只剩兩條陰爻——恰好與昨天遯卦的卦象互為鏡像:遯卦是陰柔悄悄上升、逐步侵蝕陽剛;大壯卦則反過來,是陽剛正在奮起反攻。而這一卦真正的警示,恰恰就在這股「奮起」本身所帶來的誘惑:若強盛的力量缺乏節制與紀律,就會腐化成有勇無謀的躁進。〈象傳〉給出的道德修正是:「雷在天上,大壯;君子以非禮弗履。」(雷在天之上:這就是大壯卦的象。因此君子不會踏上任何違背禮節、有失分寸的道路。)三段文字合在一起看,強調的是:真正的「強大」是一種自我節制,而不是自我放縱——擁有強大力量的那一刻,恰恰正是最需要謹慎自持的時刻,因為一旦逾越了禮節分寸,無論當下看起來多麼有力,那都已經不是這一卦真正肯定的「大壯」,而只是它墮落、變質後的贗品。

(第 34 個易經卦象,已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 以避免重複。目前已介紹:1 乾 Qián、2 坤 Kūn、3 屯 Zhūn、4 蒙 Méng、5 需 Xū、6 訟 Sòng、7 師 Shī、8 比 Bǐ、9 小畜 Xiǎo Chù、10 履 Lǚ、11 泰 Tài、12 否 Pǐ、13 同人 Tóng Rén、14 大有 Dà Yǒu、15 謙 Qiān、16 豫 Yù、17 隨 Suí、18 蠱 Gǔ、19 臨 Lín、20 觀 Guān、21 噬嗑 Shì Kè、22 賁 Bì、23 剝 Bō、24 復 Fù、25 无妄 Wú Wàng、26 大畜 Dà Chù、27 頤 Yí、28 大過 Dà Guò、29 坎 Kǎn、30 離 Lí、31 咸 Xián、32 恆 Héng、33 遯 Dùn、34 大壯 Dà Zhuàng。)

5)國家/地區/勢力介紹 — 賴索托(Lesotho)

  • 位置:賴索托是一個完全被南非國土包圍的內陸山地王國,位於南部非洲的馬洛蒂山脈與德拉肯斯山脈之間——是全世界僅有三個完全被單一國家包圍的「內飛地」主權國之一(另外兩個是同樣完全被義大利包圍的梵蒂岡與聖馬利諾)。
  • 領土規模:國土總面積約 30,355 平方公里——與比利時(30,528 平方公里)的面積驚人地接近,約為台灣陸地面積(約 36,193 平方公里)的 84%。賴索托還有一項獨特之處:它是全世界唯一一個「全國領土都位於海拔 1,000 公尺以上」的國家,因此有「天空王國」之稱——就連該國海拔最低的地點(位於橘河與馬卡朗河交會處,約 1,400 公尺)都比許多其他國家的最高峰還要高。
  • 人口:約 230 萬人(2023 年估計)。
  • 宗教:絕大多數信奉基督教(約 90%),主要分為羅馬天主教與新教教派(其中包括源自 19 世紀法國新教傳教活動的賴索托福音教會),而傳統巴索托信仰與祖先崇拜,至今仍在鄉間地區與基督信仰並行流傳。
  • 經濟:高度依賴紡織與成衣製造出口(賴索托是非洲根據《非洲成長與機會法案》(AGOA)對美出口成衣規模最大的國家之一)、在南非礦場工作的巴索托男性勞工匯回的僑匯,以及一項獨特的經濟特色——透過「賴索托高地水利計畫」(全世界規模數一數二的跨境調水工程)直接出售山區淡水給南非所賺取的收入。賴索托是南部非洲關稅同盟(SACU)成員,其貨幣洛蒂(loti)與南非蘭特採 1:1 固定匯率,且可自由互通使用。
  • 歷史:19 世紀初,由摩舒舒一世國王(King Moshoeshoe I)統一建國——他在席捲南部非洲、動盪血腥的「姆菲卡尼」(Difaqane)戰亂時期,將四散逃難的巴索托族群聚集於易守難攻的山地高原上;1868 年為了避免被不斷擴張的波耳人/阿非利卡人共和國併吞,主動尋求英國保護,成為英屬巴蘇陀蘭保護地;1966 年以賴索托王國之名正式獨立;1986 年曾發生政變,1998 年一場爭議選舉後,南部非洲發展共同體(SADC)曾短暫出兵介入以恢復秩序。
  • 現任領導人:國王萊齊三世(King Letsie III),自 1996 年起擔任禮儀性虛位元首的君主立憲國王(這是他的第二次在位——1990 至 1995 年間他曾短暫繼位,之後其父摩舒舒二世一度復位);總理薩姆·馬特坎(Sam Matekane),原為企業家、後轉戰政壇,2022 年隨其新成立的「繁榮革命黨」(RFP)贏得大選後出任總理。
  • 語言:塞索托語(Sesotho)與英語,兩者皆為官方語言。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov/the-world-factbook

6)網路迷因介紹 —「衰爺 Brian」(Bad Luck Brian)

  • 名稱:「Bad Luck Brian」(衰爺 Brian),是 2010 年代初期最具代表性的「建議動物」(advice animal)類圖片迷因之一。
  • 起源/歷史:這張照片是 2005 年美國俄亥俄州高中生 Ian Davis 的畢業紀念冊照片——他穿著格紋毛衣背心、戴著牙套,臉上掛著一抹尷尬彆扭的笑容——2012 年 1 月,他的朋友 Kyle Craven 將這張照片掃描後上傳到 Reddit 的 r/AdviceAnimals 版,並配上文字:「參加 SAT 考試……以全場最低分刷新了計分標準曲線。」
  • 如何走紅:這種「圖片迷因」格式——用一句反諷的文字,描述一連串誇張、離譜的悲慘遭遇,搭配 Brian 那張憨厚、渾然不覺的畢業照笑容——在短短一天內就衝上 Reddit 首頁,並迅速擴散到 Facebook、Quickmeme 與 Tumblr,幾乎是一夜之間就湧現出大量使用者自製的配文版本——是 2011 至 2013 年間主宰迷因文化的「建議動物」類型的代表案例之一。
  • 意義/使用情境:配文通常描述越來越荒謬、甚至刻意不合邏輯的一連串厄運(例如:「終於戰勝癌症……在出院回家路上發生車禍身亡」),笑點就來自於「誇張到極點的悲劇」與 Brian 那張溫和、渾然不知情笑容之間的強烈反差——這與真正貼近生活、令人感同身受的「衰事」幽默完全不同。
  • 變體/演變:衍生出數千張使用者自製的配文圖片、周邊商品,本尊 Ian Davis 也曾多次登上媒體節目(包括 Tosh.0 與 Today Show);偶爾也會被剪輯成與其他「建議動物」角色(例如「成功寶寶」Success Kid)的「跨界對比」圖,製造反諷式的落差效果。
  • 現況:如今是一個已顯過時、但在迷因史上具有奠基意義的經典案例,經常被引用來代表 2010 年代初期 Reddit「建議動物」/怒漫圖迷因時代的典型樣貌;目前仍被廣泛認得,但多半是懷舊式的重新引用,而非仍在持續產生新內容的活躍迷因。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹 —《羅生門》(1950,導演:黑澤明)

  • 名稱/創作者:由黑澤明執導,與橋本忍共同改編自芥川龍之介的兩篇短篇小說——〈羅生門〉(提供了破敗城門的敘事框架與片名)與〈竹藪中〉(提供了核心的多重視角劇情);攝影師為宮川一夫;由三船敏郎、京町子與森雅之主演;大映株式會社製作。
  • 時代背景:拍攝於 1950 年,正值盟軍占領下的戰後日本,當時日本電影在國際上幾乎不為人知。
  • 劇情概述:一名樵夫、一名僧侶與一名路人,在破敗的羅生門城樓下躲雨,講述一樁發生在樹林深處的審判案件——一名武士的妻子遭到侵犯,武士本人也隨後死亡。四位相關人士——強盜多襄丸、武士的妻子、透過靈媒發言的亡魂武士本人,以及樵夫自己——分別對同一事件提出各自的說法,每個版本都對自己有利,彼此之間卻互相矛盾、無法調和,讓觀眾始終無法得知一個確定可信的「真相」版本。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:開創性地將攝影機直接對著穿透樹梢的陽光拍攝,讓斑駁的光影不規則地在演員臉上閃爍跳動;鏡頭以流暢的長距離跟拍,穿梭於樹林之間;而「以互相矛盾的主觀回憶取代單一全知敘事觀點」這種結構手法,影響力之大,甚至催生出「羅生門效應」(the Rashomon effect)這個至今仍被廣泛使用的術語,用來形容多位目擊者對同一事件各自提出、彼此矛盾又各自有利的說法。
  • 文化/歷史意義:榮獲 1951 年威尼斯影展金獅獎與奧斯卡榮譽獎,普遍被認為是「單獨一部作品」首次為日本電影、乃至於更廣泛的亞洲電影,打開國際藝術電影市場大門的關鍵之作。
  • 影響與傳承:「羅生門效應」一詞如今已廣泛用於法律、新聞學與心理學領域,用來描述互相矛盾的目擊證詞;本片的多重視角敘事結構,直接影響了後世無數作品,從《英雄本色》(Courage Under Fire)、《人命關天》(Vantage Point),到眾多以同一種敘事手法為結構核心的電視劇集。
  • 現況/觀賞方式:已由 Criterion Collection 進行修復並於國際發行;經常在影展與經典回顧展中放映,也是全球電影研究課程中的經典教材。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、criterion.com

8)電影賞析方法 — 服裝設計與造型作為角色塑造/世界觀建構(第 20 課)

服裝設計能完成對白與劇情本身做不到的敘事工作:一個角色的服裝輪廓、顏色、材質,以及它在故事中是否隨時間演變,都能在不說出一句台詞的情況下,標示出角色的內在狀態、社會地位,或人物的成長弧線。分析服裝時,要留意三件事:服裝是否隨著角色內心的轉變而演變(在劇情明確點出之前,先透過視覺暗示成長或墮落);服裝是否被當作「世界觀建構」的捷徑——單靠衣著本身,就傳達出一個虛構文化的社會結構、科技水準或歷史脈絡,而不必依賴口白說明;以及服裝上刻意的時代錯置或誇張化處理,是否是一種作者刻意安排的評論手法,而不只是單純的美術設計。這與第 3 課的攝影/調色課程(關注的是光線與鏡頭本身的色彩)、以及第 8 課的畫面比例課程(關注的是畫框的形狀)都不同:服裝分析關注的是「畫面之中所穿的衣服」本身,獨立於燈光或畫框如何呈現它。

實例演練:《黑豹》(Black Panther,2018,導演:萊恩·庫格勒 Ryan Coogler,服裝設計:露絲·卡特 Ruth Carter)——瓦干達五大部族的服裝設計,融合了真實存在的非洲織品與服飾傳統(邊境部族採用巴索托毛毯的圖紋——正是本篇上方賴索托介紹中提到、根植於今日賴索托的塞索托織布傳統;馬賽族的串珠工藝圖案;商人部族的圖阿雷格式服裝;皇室成員則採用受多貢族建築啟發的輪廓造型),再結合未來感十足的振金科技材質——單靠服裝本身,就足以立即傳達出瓦干達虛構的五大部族政治結構,以及其「非洲未來主義」式古老傳統與先進科技融合的世界觀,完全不需要任何一句台詞說明。露絲·卡特也因這部作品,成為史上第一位獲得奧斯卡最佳服裝設計獎的黑人服裝設計師——這正是對「服裝在此發揮真正世界觀建構功能、而不只是裝飾」的肯定。
