ENEnglish version

Topic: "turn on", "turn off", "open", "close", "turn up", "turn down" — encender / prender, apagar, abrir, cerrar, subir, bajar. Chinese uses 開 and 關 for almost everything (開燈、開門、開冷氣、開電視), but Spanish chooses the verb by the kind of object.

🎯 Learning Goal: switch lights and devices on and off (Enciende la luz / Apaga la tele), open and close doors, windows, taps and shops (Cierra la ventana / ¿A qué hora abren?), turn the volume or temperature up and down (Sube el volumen / Baja la temperatura), and say that something went off by itself (Se fue la luz / Se me apagó el móvil). The key test: things that light up or run on power → encender/prender and apagar; things with a door, lid or opening → abrir and cerrar. So 開燈 is encender la luz, never ✗ abrir la luz.

Vocabulary: encender (e→ie: enciendo, enciendes, enciende, encendemos; to switch on, to light) · prender (regular; "switch on" in much of Latin America, e.g. Mexico and Colombia) · apagar (switch off, put out; past apagué, g→gu; negative command no apagues) · abrir (open; irregular past participle abierto) · cerrar (e→ie: cierro, cierras; close) · subir / bajar (turn up / down; also go up / down, Day 108) · la luz (light), la tele (TV), el aire (acondicionado) (air conditioning), la calefacción (heating), el grifo (tap, Spain) / la llave or la canilla (tap, Latin America), el volumen, la persiana (roller blind) · enchufar / desenchufar (plug in / unplug) · cargar el móvil / el celular (charge the phone) · Se fue la luz / un apagón (the power went out / a blackout).

Grammar Point: (1) Choose the verb by the object. Lights, TVs, computers, ovens, fires and cigarettes: encender/prender and apagar. Doors, windows, boxes, bottles, taps, apps, shops: abrir and cerrar. A tap is "opened" in Spanish (abre el grifo = 開水龍頭), but a light is "lit" (enciende la luz). (2) Regional choice. Encender is understood everywhere and is standard in Spain; prender is the everyday word in much of Latin America (Prende la tele). In Spain prender sounds more like "to set fire to" or "to catch" (prender fuego). For radios and TVs, poner also works (Pon la tele, Day 126). (3) Spelling: apagar keeps its hard g sound before e by writing gu: apagué, no apagues (like busqué, Day 128). (4) Up and down: subir / bajar el volumen, la temperatura, la persiana — Spanish says "raise / lower", not "open bigger". (5) When it happens by itself: use se: Se apagó la luz (the light went off); with the accidental se (Day 57): Se me apagó el móvil (my phone died on me). "There's a power cut" is usually Se fue la luz or Hay un apagón. (6) Commands with pronouns: Enciéndela (turn it on — note the written accent), Apágalo, but No la apagues (pronoun before the verb in negative commands, Day 26).

Example Sentences:

  • ¿Puedes encender la luz? — Can you turn on the light?
  • Claro. ¿Apago el aire? — Sure. Shall I turn off the air conditioning?
  • No, pero sube la temperatura, por favor. Hace frío. — No, but turn the temperature up, please. It's cold.
  • Vale. Y cierra la ventana, que entra el ruido. — OK. And close the window — the noise is coming in.
  • Prende la tele, ya empieza el partido. — Turn on the TV, the match is starting. (Latin America)
  • Abre el grifo un momento, que no sale agua caliente. — Turn on the tap for a moment — no hot water is coming out.
  • Baja el volumen, que el bebé está durmiendo. — Turn the volume down, the baby is sleeping.
  • Anoche se fue la luz dos horas. — Last night the power went out for two hours.
  • Se me apagó el móvil; ¿me dejas tu cargador? — My phone died; can I borrow your charger?
  • ¿A qué hora cierran? — What time do you close? (shops are "closed", not "switched off")

Chapter 32 — 道常無名 (Dào cháng wú míng) — "The Way Is Always Nameless"

Text (Wang Bi received version):
道常無名,樸雖小,天下莫能臣也。侯王若能守之,萬物將自賓。天地相合,以降甘露,民莫之令而自均。始制有名,名亦既有,夫亦將知止,知止所以不殆。譬道之在天下,猶川谷之於江海。

Translation: "The Way is always nameless. The uncarved block, though small, cannot be made a subject by anyone in the world. If lords and kings could hold on to it, the ten thousand things would come as guests of their own accord. Heaven and earth unite and send down sweet dew; no one commands the people, yet they become even by themselves. When cutting begins, there are names. Once there are names, one must also know where to stop; knowing where to stop is how one avoids danger. The Way's presence in the world is like the way streams in the valleys flow to the rivers and the sea."

Structure and key ideas:

  • Nameless and small, yet nobody's subject. "樸" (pǔ) is the uncarved block of wood — raw, simple, undivided — an image used for the Dao since Chapter 15 and Chapter 28. It is "small" (simple, unimpressive), yet "天下莫能臣" — nothing in the world can make it a servant. The paradox: what has no name, rank or size cannot be ranked under anyone.
  • Rulers who keep to it. "萬物將自賓" — things will come "as guests" (賓, to submit willingly, like a visitor coming to a host) without being forced. This repeats a theme of Chapter 37 (侯王若能守之,萬物將自化) and connects to Chapter 30's warning against force.
  • Sweet dew and natural evenness. "天地相合,以降甘露" — sweet dew was a traditional auspicious sign of good government. "民莫之令而自均" — nobody orders the people, yet things are distributed evenly by themselves. Dew falls on everything without choosing; the line imagines order without command.
  • Cutting the block creates names — and the need to stop. "始制有名" links directly to Chapter 28: "樸散則為器,聖人用之則為官長" (when the block is broken up it becomes vessels; the sage uses them as officials). Once you cut (制, to tailor, to institute), you have names: offices, ranks, categories. Laozi does not say names are evil; he says that once names exist, "夫亦將知止" — one must know where to stop. "知止所以不殆" — knowing the limit keeps one out of danger (compare Chapter 44: 知足不辱,知止不殆).
  • Streams and the sea. The closing image says the Dao relates to the world as rivers and seas relate to valley streams: water gathers there naturally because they are low. The same image returns in Chapter 66 (江海所以能為百谷王者,以其善下之).

Traditional interpretations and text variants:

  • Wang Bi (226–249) reads "始制" as the moment when the block is broken up and officials and leaders are first set up; names and ranks then become necessary to fix high and low, but if one goes further, people will fight over trivial gains — so one must know where to stop. He also reads the final image as streams seeking the rivers and the sea not because the sea calls them, but because they return of themselves.
  • Heshang Gong's text reads "天下不敢臣" (no one dares to make it a subject) and "知止可以不殆" (knowing where to stop, one can avoid danger), small differences from Wang Bi's "莫能臣" and "所以不殆".
  • Excavated texts: the Mawangdui silk manuscripts (2nd century BCE) begin "道恆無名" — "恆" rather than "常", because "常" replaced "恆" later to avoid the taboo on the name of Emperor Wen of Han (Liu Heng). Material corresponding to this chapter also appears in the Guodian bamboo slips (c. 300 BCE, Laozi A), which, as usually transcribed, read "天地弗敢臣" — "heaven and earth dare not make it a subject" — a stronger claim than "no one in the world".

Historical and philosophical background: the chapter belongs to the Warring States debate about names (名). Confucians wanted to "rectify names" (正名, Analects 13.3) so that rulers, ministers, fathers and sons each match their roles; the Mohists and the School of Names analysed how names fit things. Laozi's position is different: names are a necessary tool once society is organised, but they are a step away from the original wholeness, so their use must be limited. This becomes a lasting theme in Chinese political thought: institutions are needed, but over-regulation breeds competition and conflict.

Practical philosophical implications: (1) Structure is useful but has a cost: every category you create is something people can compete over or game. (2) The real skill is not creating more rules but knowing when to stop adding them. (3) Influence that does not force — like dew or low ground — is often more lasting than command.

Modern reflection: think of a software team. A new project starts as an "uncarved block": a few people, no titles, no process. As it grows, it must be "cut": roles, approval steps, naming conventions, dashboards. That is "始制有名", and it is necessary. But each new rule and metric also creates something to argue about or optimise for its own sake — the "fight over trivial gains" Wang Bi warned about. "知止" asks: which processes actually help, and where should we stop? Yesterday's and today's markets offer a second picture: labels such as "AI winner" were attached to companies, and when people tried to rank everything by that one name, prices swung far beyond what the underlying businesses changed in two days. Knowing where to stop — in naming, in rules, in speculation — is how one "avoids danger".

原文(王弼通行本):
道常無名,樸雖小,天下莫能臣也。侯王若能守之,萬物將自賓。天地相合,以降甘露,民莫之令而自均。始制有名,名亦既有,夫亦將知止,知止所以不殆。譬道之在天下,猶川谷之於江海。

白話翻譯:「道永遠沒有名字。『樸』雖然看起來微小,天下卻沒有人能使它臣服。侯王如果能守住它,萬物就會自然來歸附,像賓客一樣。天地之氣相合,降下甘露;沒有人命令百姓,他們卻自然均平。開始裁製時就有了名稱;名稱既然有了,也就要懂得適可而止;懂得適可而止,就能避免危險。道在天下的位置,就像山谷裡的溪流流向江河大海一樣。」

結構與重點:

  • 無名而小,卻不臣服於任何人。「樸」是還沒雕刻的原木——質樸、簡單、未分割——從第 15 章、第 28 章起就被用來比喻道。它「小」(樸素、不起眼),卻「天下莫能臣」——天下沒有任何東西能讓它當僕人。矛盾之處就在這裡:沒有名稱、沒有等級、沒有大小的東西,反而無法被排在任何人底下。
  • 守住它的君主。「萬物將自賓」——萬物會像賓客拜訪主人一樣,心甘情願地前來,而不是被強迫。這和第 37 章「侯王若能守之,萬物將自化」相呼應,也接續第 30 章反對用武力的警告。
  • 甘露與自然的均平。「天地相合,以降甘露」——甘露在傳統上是政治清明的祥瑞。「民莫之令而自均」——沒有人下命令,百姓之間卻自然分配均勻。露水落在萬物上,不挑對象;這句話想像的是一種沒有命令的秩序。
  • 剖開原木就有了名稱——也就需要知止。「始制有名」直接連到第 28 章:「樸散則為器,聖人用之則為官長」。一旦開始裁製(制,裁剪、制定),就有了名稱:官職、等級、分類。老子並沒有說名稱是壞的;他說名稱既然存在,「夫亦將知止」——就必須知道在哪裡停下來。「知止所以不殆」——知道界限,就能遠離危險(比較第 44 章:「知足不辱,知止不殆」)。
  • 溪流與大海。結尾的比喻說,道和天下的關係,就像江海和山谷溪流的關係:水自然匯聚到那裡,因為江海處在低處。同一個意象在第 66 章再次出現(「江海所以能為百谷王者,以其善下之」)。

傳統詮釋與版本差異:

  • 王弼(226–249)把「始制」解釋為原木被剖開、開始設立官長的時候;這時必須建立名分來確定尊卑,但如果再往下走,人們就會爭奪細微的利益——所以要知止。他也把結尾的比喻理解為:溪流歸向江海,不是江海召喚它們,而是不召不求、自然回歸。
  • 河上公本作「天下不敢臣」(沒有人敢使它臣服)和「知止可以不殆」(懂得適可而止就可以避免危險),和王弼本的「莫能臣」「所以不殆」略有不同。
  • 出土文獻:馬王堆帛書(西元前 2 世紀)開頭作「道恆無名」——用「恆」而不是「常」,因為後來為了避漢文帝劉恆的名諱,才把「恆」改成「常」。郭店楚簡(約西元前 300 年,《老子》甲本)也有相當於本章的內容,一般的釋文讀作「天地弗敢臣」——「天地都不敢使它臣服」,說法比「天下莫能臣」更強烈。

歷史與哲學背景:本章屬於戰國時代關於「名」的論辯。儒家主張「正名」(《論語・子路》13.3),讓君臣父子各自符合名分;墨家與名家則分析名稱如何對應事物。老子的立場不同:社會一旦組織起來,名稱是必要的工具,但它也是離開原初整體的一步,所以使用必須有限度。這成為中國政治思想中長久的主題:制度是需要的,但過度的規範會引發競爭與衝突。

實際的哲學意涵:(1)結構有用,但有代價:你建立的每一個分類,都可能變成別人爭奪或鑽漏洞的對象。(2)真正的功夫不在於訂更多規則,而在於知道什麼時候停止增加規則。(3)不靠強迫的影響力——像露水、像低處——往往比命令更長久。

現代省思:想想一個軟體團隊。新專案一開始是「樸」:幾個人,沒有職稱,沒有流程。專案長大後,就必須「制」:角色分工、審核步驟、命名規範、儀表板。這就是「始制有名」,而且是必要的。但每一條新規則、每一個新指標,也同時製造出可以爭論、或為了指標本身而去優化的東西——就是王弼警告的「爭錐刀之末」。「知止」要問的是:哪些流程真的有幫助?該在哪裡停下來?昨天和今天的股市提供了另一個畫面:「AI 贏家」這類標籤被貼到公司身上,當大家想用這一個名稱來替所有東西排名時,股價在兩天內的擺盪,遠遠超過公司本身實際的改變。在命名、在規則、在投機上懂得知止,就是「不殆」之道。

Name and location: the Republic of Guatemala (República de Guatemala) is in northern Central America. It borders Mexico to the north and west, Belize to the north-east (introduced on Nov 23), Honduras and El Salvador to the south-east, and has coasts on both the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean (the Gulf of Honduras). The capital is Guatemala City (Ciudad de Guatemala), the largest city in Central America.

Size: about 108,900 km² — roughly three times the size of Taiwan (about 36,000 km²), and almost exactly the size of Cuba or a little smaller than Bulgaria. The land runs from the lowland rainforest of Petén in the north, through a volcanic highland chain (Tajumulco, about 4,220 m, is the highest peak in Central America), down to the Pacific plain. Lake Atitlán, surrounded by three volcanoes, is its best-known landscape.

Population: the 2018 census counted about 14.9 million; official projections now put the population at about 18 million, the largest in Central America. In the 2018 census about 44% identified as Indigenous, mostly Maya, plus small Xinka and Garífuna communities; most of the rest identify as Ladino (mixed or Spanish-speaking).

Religion: Christianity dominates, but the balance has changed. Surveys usually find Catholics and evangelical Protestants each at roughly 40% of the population — one of the highest Protestant shares in Latin America — with Maya spiritual practice often mixed into Catholic ritual (for example, the folk saint Maximón in Santiago Atitlán).

Languages: Spanish is the official language. The state also recognises 22 Mayan languages (K'iche', Q'eqchi', Kaqchikel and Mam are the largest), plus Xinka and Garífuna. Many rural Guatemalans speak a Mayan language at home and Spanish as a second language.

Economy: Guatemala has the largest economy in Central America and a long record of low inflation and a stable currency, the quetzal (named after the national bird). Key exports include clothing, bananas, coffee, sugar and cardamom (Guatemala is among the world's largest cardamom exporters). Remittances from Guatemalans abroad, mostly in the US, are enormous — around a fifth of GDP in recent years — so US migration policy is also an economic issue. Poverty and child malnutrition remain high, especially in Indigenous rural areas, and tax revenue is among the lowest in the region.

History — key events:

  • Maya civilisation: the Classic-period city of Tikal in Petén (UNESCO World Heritage since 1979) was one of the greatest Maya centres; the K'iche' Maya text Popol Vuh survives as a key source of Maya mythology.
  • Spanish conquest: Pedro de Alvarado began the conquest in 1524. Santiago de los Caballeros — today's Antigua Guatemala (UNESCO, 1979) — became the colonial capital; after the 1773 earthquakes the capital moved to the present Guatemala City.
  • Independence: 15 September 1821 from Spain; briefly part of Mexico and then of the Federal Republic of Central America, before becoming a separate republic in the 1840s.
  • The "Ten Years of Spring" (1944–1954): the October Revolution of 1944 led to President Juan José Arévalo (1945–51) and then Jacobo Árbenz, whose 1952 land reform expropriated unused land, including that of the US-owned United Fruit Company. In 1954 a CIA-backed coup overthrew Árbenz.
  • Civil war (1960–1996): about 200,000 people were killed or disappeared. The UN-backed truth commission (CEH, report 1999) attributed the great majority of violations to state forces and concluded that acts of genocide were committed against Maya communities in the early 1980s. Peace accords were signed on 29 December 1996. Rigoberta Menchú, a K'iche' activist, received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1992; the novelist Miguel Ángel Asturias won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1967.
  • Anti-corruption struggle: the UN-backed commission CICIG (2007–2019) helped expose corruption that led to President Otto Pérez Molina's resignation in 2015; its mandate was not renewed in 2019.

Current leader and political system: Guatemala is a presidential republic with a single four-year presidential term and a one-chamber Congress of 160 members. President Bernardo Arévalo (Movimiento Semilla), son of Juan José Arévalo, won the 2023 run-off on an anti-corruption platform and took office on 15 January 2024 despite repeated legal attempts by the Attorney General's office under Consuelo Porras to block him. In May 2026, when Porras's term ended, Arévalo appointed Gabriel García Luna as Attorney General for 2026–2030; he has promised to dismantle what he calls his predecessor's "repressive" legacy (AP via WTOP, WLRN).

International relations — and Taiwan: Guatemala is one of the 12 states that recognise Taiwan (the Republic of China) and has no diplomatic relations with Beijing. Ties go back to the 1930s; in September 2026 Guatemala's ambassador in Taipei said the two countries would mark 92 years of uninterrupted relations in December (Taipei Times). Arévalo visited Taiwan in 2025 and has said ties will continue, although his government has also discussed expanding trade with China. Relations with the United States centre on migration, security and trade.

Culture: handwoven Maya clothing — the huipil blouse, whose patterns often identify a town — is the most visible marker of identity. Antigua's Holy Week processions walk over huge carpets (alfombras) of dyed sawdust and flowers. On 1 November, the town of Sumpango flies giant kites (barriletes gigantes) to honour the dead. The marimba is the national instrument, and Guatemala sits on the "Ring of Fire": the eruption of Volcán de Fuego on 3 June 2018 left hundreds dead or missing.

Current developments and challenges (as of late November 2026):

  • Security: on 6 November a member of Congress and three police officers were killed in an armed attack in the eastern department of Zacapa (Al Jazeera). The government declared a 30-day state of exception against drug-related violence, and by mid-November security operations were under way in departments bordering Honduras (Washington Post).
  • Budget: Congress must vote on the 2027 budget by 30 November; a committee has backed cutting the government's proposal of about Q192 billion (about US$25 billion) by around 10% (Rio Times, a secondary source).
  • Elections: the electoral tribunal has set the first round of the next general election for 27 June 2027 (president, all 160 seats in Congress and local offices); Arévalo cannot run again (Prensa Latina).
  • Belize dispute: Guatemala's long-standing territorial claim against Belize is before the International Court of Justice, with hearings scheduled for February 2027 (see the Nov 23 briefing).

ENEnglish version

Name and common variations: "Ah sh*t, here we go again" — also written "Ah shit here we go again", often misquoted as "Oh shit, here we go again". Variants include "Here we go again", a cleaner "Ah, here we go again", and remixes with other characters.

Origin: the line comes from the opening of Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (Rockstar Games, 2004). The protagonist, Carl "CJ" Johnson, returns home to Los Santos (the game's version of Los Angeles) after his mother is killed. Corrupt police officers stop him, take his money and drop him in the territory of a rival gang, the Ballas. As he walks off, CJ mutters: "Ah sh*t, here we go again" — and, according to Know Your Meme's transcript, continues by complaining about being back in Ballas country. The joke is in the tone: he is not surprised, just tired. He knows exactly what kind of trouble is waiting, because he has lived it before.

How and why it became popular: the scene was well known among gamers for years, but it became a mainstream meme in 2019. Know Your Meme and the Daily Dot trace the boom to April 2019, when a Twitter user posted a green-screen version of the clip so that anyone could paste CJ into any background. People then dropped him into film scenes, historical photos and news events (Know Your Meme, Daily Dot; Kotaku also covered the trend in 2019). It spread because it fits a universal feeling: something bad that has happened before is starting again.

Meaning and usage: it expresses weary recognition of a repeating problem — not panic, not anger, but "I've seen this before". Typical uses: Monday morning; exam season; a bug that was "fixed" appearing again in production; a family argument at every holiday dinner; a sports team losing in the same way every year; a second "once-in-a-century" event in a few years. The image macro usually shows CJ standing in the street with the caption under him, placed after a set-up like "Me when the printer jams again:".

Variations and evolution: history jokes (CJ placed at the start of World War II, "here we go again" after World War I); "loop" versions where the clip restarts endlessly; the related "Damn, here we go again" walking meme; and the cleaned-up "here we go again" used in workplaces. The 2021 GTA: The Trilogy – Definitive Edition remaster brought the scene back to attention.

Current status: peak popularity was around 2019–2020. Today it is an evergreen reaction meme: widely recognised, often used in comments and chat, but not a currently trending format. No evidence was found of a new 2026 revival.

Cultural notes: the humour depends on two things a literal translation loses: (1) the casual swear word, which makes the line sound like real tired speech rather than drama; and (2) "again" — the meme is always about repetition. Because it contains profanity, use the cleaner "Here we go again" in work settings. It should not be confused with Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again (2018) or the Whitesnake song "Here I Go Again".

Unit 1 — Preposition Lesson 9: by / until / before / after (Stage 2, time).

  • Today's topic and the usual confusion: Chinese uses 之前 and 到 for several different ideas: 週五前交 (a deadline), 等到六點 (a state that continues), 兩點才睡 (something that does not happen until a point). Learners often say "Please finish it until Friday" when they mean "by Friday", or "I slept until 2 a.m." when they mean "I didn't go to bed until 2 a.m."
  • Core concept — a line and a deadline: until draws a continuous line: a state or activity keeps going and stops at a point (I'll be in the office until six). by marks a deadline: the action happens at some moment no later than that point (I'll finish the report by six). before and after simply put events in order around a point or another event (before lunch, after the meeting, before leaving, after I got home). not … until = 直到……才: I didn't go to bed until 2 a.m.
  • Comparison: "You can use the room until Friday" = from now continuously up to Friday, then stop. "Please return the key by Friday" = one action, any time up to Friday. "Please return the key before Friday" = strictly earlier than Friday (Thursday at the latest) — in everyday speech many people treat it like "by", so if the deadline matters, say "by Friday" or "no later than Thursday".
  • Examples: The night market is open until midnight. / Can you send me the slides by Thursday? / We waited until the rain stopped. / By the time we got to the station, the last train had left. / Wash your hands before eating. / After the typhoon, the streets were covered in leaves. / I didn't start learning English until I was twelve.
  • Common mistakes: (1) "Please finish it until Friday" → by Friday (a one-time action needs a deadline, not a line). (2) "I slept until 2 a.m." when you mean 兩點才睡 → I didn't go to bed until 2 a.m. ("slept until 2" means you woke up at 2). (3) "After, we went to a café" → After that / Afterwards, we went to a café (after alone at the start of a sentence needs an object or a clause).

Unit 1 — 介系詞第 9 課:by / until / before / after(第二階段:時間)

1. 今日主題

今天只學一個核心概念:until 是「一條線撐到某個時間點」,by 是「最晚在某個時間點之前完成」;before / after 只負責排順序。中文的「之前」「到」「才」常常一個字包辦好幾種意思:「週五前交」(截止期限)、「等到六點」(狀態持續)、「兩點才睡」(到某時間點才發生)。所以常聽到 ✗ Please finish it until Friday(其實要說 by Friday),或是把「兩點才睡」說成 ✗ I slept until 2 a.m.(這句其實是「睡到兩點才起床」)。

2. 核心概念:一條線 vs. 一道截止線

  • until(till)= 一條持續的線。某個狀態或動作一直進行,到那個時間點才停。想像一條從現在畫到右邊的線,線的盡頭就是 until 後面的時間:I'll be in the office until six.(我會在辦公室待到六點。)until 前面的動詞通常是「可以持續」的:stay、wait、work、be open、live。
  • by = 一道截止線。動作只發生一次,但必須在那個時間點(含)以前完成,什麼時候做都可以:I'll finish the report by six.(我六點前會把報告寫完。)想像一道紅線:紅線前任何時候完成都 OK,過了就遲了。by 前面通常是「完成型」的動作:finish、send、arrive、pay、hand in、decide。
  • before / after = 排順序。只是說明哪件事先、哪件事後,不談持續或期限:before lunch、after the meeting。後面可以接名詞、動名詞(before leaving)或子句(after I got home)。
  • not … until = 直到……才。英文用否定 + until 表達「到那個時間點才發生」:I didn't go to bed until 2 a.m.(我兩點才睡。)意思是:在兩點以前「沒睡」這個狀態一直持續,到兩點才結束。
  • by the time + 子句 = 等到……的時候(已經)。By the time we got to the station, the last train had left.(等我們到車站時,末班車已經開走了。)

4. 常見錯誤

  • 錯誤 1:✗ Please finish the report until Friday. → ✔ Please finish the report by Friday.
    為什麼會錯:中文「週五前完成」「到週五要完成」都有「到」或「前」,很容易想到 until。但「完成」是一次性的動作,不能「持續完成到週五」,所以要用截止線 by。
  • 錯誤 2:把「我昨天兩點才睡」說成 ✗ I slept until 2 a.m. yesterday. → ✔ I didn't go to bed until 2 a.m. 或 I went to bed at 2 a.m.
    為什麼會錯:中文的「才」在英文裡沒有單一對應的字。I slept until 2 a.m. 的意思是「我睡到兩點(才醒)」,剛好相反。英文要用「否定 + until」表達「到那時候才發生」。
  • 錯誤 3:✗ After, we went to a café. → ✔ After that, we went to a café. / Afterwards, we went to a café.
    為什麼會錯:中文「之後,我們去了咖啡廳」可以單獨用「之後」。英文的 after 是介系詞或連接詞,後面要接東西(after lunch、after we left);單獨放在句首時,要改成 after that、afterwards 或 then。

Section 7 — Film & Performing Arts: Dogme 95 and Festen (The Celebration, 1998)
第 7 段 — 電影與表演藝術:逗馬 95(Dogme 95)與《那一個晚上》(Festen,1998)

ENEnglish version

What it is: Dogme 95 was a Danish film movement — part artistic programme, part provocation — launched by directors Lars von Trier and Thomas Vinterberg. Its first and most admired film is Vinterberg's Festen (The Celebration, 1998), officially "Dogme #1".

Key figures: von Trier and Vinterberg wrote the manifesto in Copenhagen in March 1995; they were soon joined by Kristian Levring and Søren Kragh-Jacobsen, and the four were known as the "Dogme brothers". Cinematographer Anthony Dod Mantle shot Festen and became closely associated with the movement's look.

Historical background: von Trier announced the manifesto on 20 March 1995 in Paris, at a conference marking the centenary of cinema, by throwing red leaflets into the audience. The mid-1990s were dominated by expensive, effects-heavy Hollywood films; at the same time, cheap digital video cameras were arriving. Dogme turned these facts into a challenge: what happens if you take away every tool that makes a film look "produced"?

The "Vow of Chastity" (ten rules, summarised):

  1. Shooting must be done on location; no props or sets may be brought in.
  2. Sound must never be produced apart from the images (no added music unless it is played in the scene).
  3. The camera must be hand-held.
  4. The film must be in colour; no special lighting.
  5. No optical work or filters.
  6. No superficial action (murders, weapons, etc. must not occur).
  7. No temporal or geographical alienation — the film takes place here and now.
  8. No genre films.
  9. The final format must be Academy 35 mm.
  10. The director must not be credited.

The directors also promised to give up personal taste and "aesthetic" choices in favour of truth. In practice the rules were bent — the directors themselves confessed to small "sins" — and critics have long debated whether the manifesto was a serious aesthetic, a marketing stunt, or both. That debate is part of its significance.

Content of Festen: a wealthy family gathers at a country hotel for the 60th birthday of the patriarch, Helge (Henning Moritzen). During dinner, his eldest son Christian (Ulrich Thomsen) stands to give a toast and calmly reveals that Helge sexually abused him and his twin sister, Linda, who recently took her own life. The guests at first carry on as if nothing has been said; the evening becomes a long struggle between politeness and truth. Other key performers include Thomas Bo Larsen as the volatile brother Michael and Paprika Steen as the sister Helene.

Artistic style and techniques:

  • Consumer camcorder images: Festen was shot largely on a small Sony consumer digital camcorder and transferred to 35 mm film. The result is grainy, blurred in low light and colour-shifted — and that roughness reads as evidence, like a home video of a family event.
  • Hand-held movement and unusual angles: the small camera could be hidden in corners, placed low, or moved among guests, giving the feeling that we are eavesdropping.
  • Only diegetic sound and available light: no score tells us how to feel; songs are sung by the characters, and the darkness of the hotel at night is real darkness.
  • Jump cuts and broken continuity: editing ignores smooth continuity rules, so the film feels nervous and unstable — like the family.

Cultural and historical significance: Festen won the Jury Prize at Cannes in 1998 and was praised internationally; von Trier's The Idiots (Dogme #2) competed at Cannes the same year. Later Dogme films included Kragh-Jacobsen's Mifune's Last Song (#3), Levring's The King Is Alive (#4), Harmony Korine's American julien donkey-boy and Lone Scherfig's Italian for Beginners, which won a Silver Bear in Berlin. The movement proved that a small country's low-budget films could become a global event and that digital video could be cinema.

Influence and legacy: Dogme helped legitimise digital and hand-held filmmaking before it became normal; its spirit is visible in later low-budget independent films and in the shaky, "found" realism of many TV dramas. Anthony Dod Mantle went on to win the Academy Award for cinematography for Slumdog Millionaire (2008). Vinterberg later directed The Hunt (2012) and Another Round (2020), which won the Academy Award for Best International Feature Film. The founders stopped certifying films in the early 2000s, so the official movement ended, but "Dogme" is still used as shorthand for stripped-down filmmaking.

Where to experience it today: Festen is available on disc and on some streaming services, depending on region. It has also been adapted for the stage (David Eldridge's English-language version premiered at the Almeida Theatre in London in 2004). When watching, notice how often you forget the poor image quality — and ask why.

Facts vs. interpretation: the dates, rules, prizes and credits above are historical facts. The claims that rough images "read as evidence" or that the editing mirrors the family's instability are critical interpretations.

中繁體中文版

它是什麼:逗馬 95(Dogme 95)是丹麥的一個電影運動——一半是藝術綱領,一半是挑釁——由導演拉斯・馮・提爾(Lars von Trier)和湯瑪斯・凡提柏格(Thomas Vinterberg)發起。它第一部、也是最受推崇的作品,是凡提柏格的《那一個晚上》(Festen,英文片名 The Celebration,1998),正式編號「Dogme #1」。

關鍵人物:馮・提爾和凡提柏格在 1995 年 3 月於哥本哈根寫下宣言;不久後克里斯蒂安・勒夫林(Kristian Levring)和索倫・克拉-雅克布森(Søren Kragh-Jacobsen)加入,四人被稱為「逗馬兄弟」。攝影師安東尼・道德・曼托(Anthony Dod Mantle)拍攝了《那一個晚上》,也成為這個運動影像風格的代表人物。

歷史背景:1995 年 3 月 20 日,馮・提爾在巴黎一場慶祝電影誕生百年的研討會上發表宣言,把紅色傳單撒向觀眾。1990 年代中期,電影市場由昂貴、特效密集的好萊塢片主導;同時,便宜的數位攝影機正在出現。逗馬把這兩件事變成一個挑戰:如果拿掉所有讓電影看起來「被製作過」的工具,會發生什麼事?

「貞潔誓約」(十條規則,摘要):

  1. 必須在實景拍攝,不得帶入道具或搭景。
  2. 聲音不能和影像分開製作(除非音樂是在場景中實際演奏,否則不能加配樂)。
  3. 攝影機必須手持。
  4. 必須是彩色片;不得使用特殊燈光。
  5. 不得使用光學後製或濾鏡。
  6. 不得有表面化的動作(不能出現謀殺、武器等)。
  7. 不得在時間或地理上疏離——故事發生在此時此地。
  8. 不得拍類型片。
  9. 最終格式必須是學院比例 35 釐米底片。
  10. 導演不得掛名。

導演們也承諾放棄個人品味與「美學」選擇,以追求真實。實際上規則經常被打破——導演自己也承認犯了一些小「罪」——評論界長期爭論這份宣言究竟是嚴肅的美學主張、行銷噱頭,還是兩者都是。這場爭論本身就是它的重要性之一。

《那一個晚上》的內容:一個富裕家族在鄉間旅館為大家長赫格(Helge,亨寧・莫里森 Henning Moritzen 飾)慶祝六十大壽。晚宴上,長子克里斯欽(Christian,烏利奇・湯姆森 Ulrich Thomsen 飾)起身致詞,平靜地揭露赫格曾經性侵他和他的雙胞胎妹妹琳達——琳達不久前自殺身亡。賓客一開始照常吃喝,彷彿什麼都沒聽到;整個晚上變成禮貌與真相之間的漫長拉鋸。其他重要演員包括飾演暴躁弟弟麥可的湯瑪斯・波・拉森(Thomas Bo Larsen),以及飾演妹妹海倫的帕布莉卡・斯汀(Paprika Steen)。

藝術風格與技巧:

  • 家用攝影機的畫面:《那一個晚上》大部分用一台小型 Sony 家用數位攝影機拍攝,再轉成 35 釐米底片。結果是顆粒粗、低光時模糊、色彩偏移——而這種粗糙感讀起來像證據,就像家族聚會的家庭錄影帶。
  • 手持運鏡與不尋常的角度:小攝影機可以藏在角落、放得很低,或在賓客之間移動,讓觀眾覺得自己在偷聽。
  • 只用場景內的聲音和現場光:沒有配樂告訴觀眾該有什麼感受;歌是角色自己唱的,旅館夜裡的黑暗就是真正的黑暗。
  • 跳接與破碎的連戲:剪接不理會流暢的連戲規則,讓整部片感覺緊張、不穩定——就像這個家族。

文化與歷史意義:《那一個晚上》獲得 1998 年坎城影展評審團獎,在國際上備受讚譽;馮・提爾的《白痴》(The Idiots,Dogme #2)同一年也入圍坎城競賽。之後的逗馬作品包括克拉-雅克布森的《敏郎的最後一首歌》(Mifune's Last Song,#3)、勒夫林的《國王不死》(The King Is Alive,#4)、美國導演哈莫尼・柯林(Harmony Korine)的《驢孩朱利安》(julien donkey-boy),以及隆恩・雪菲(Lone Scherfig)獲得柏林影展銀熊獎的《義大利初級課程》(Italian for Beginners)。這個運動證明了小國家的低成本電影也能成為全球事件,數位影像也可以是電影。

影響與遺產:逗馬讓數位與手持拍攝在成為常態之前就取得了正當性;後來許多低成本獨立電影,以及不少電視劇搖晃、「現場撿到」般的寫實風格,都看得到它的精神。安東尼・道德・曼托後來以《貧民百萬富翁》(Slumdog Millionaire,2008)獲得奧斯卡最佳攝影。凡提柏格之後執導了《謊言的烙印》(The Hunt,2012)和《醉好的時光》(Another Round,2020),後者獲得奧斯卡最佳國際影片。創始人在 2000 年代初期停止認證作品,官方運動就此結束,但「逗馬」至今仍被用來指稱極簡、去裝飾的拍片方式。

今天在哪裡可以體驗:《那一個晚上》有光碟版,也可在部分地區的串流平台觀看。它也被改編成舞台劇(大衛・艾德里奇 David Eldridge 的英文版 2004 年在倫敦阿爾梅達劇院首演)。觀賞時,留意你有多常忘記畫質很差——然後問問自己為什麼。

事實與詮釋:上面的日期、規則、獎項與演職員是歷史事實。「粗糙畫面讀起來像證據」、「剪接反映家族的不穩定」則是評論上的詮釋。

Section 8 — Film Appreciation & Criticism Method, Lesson 115: Comparative Remake Analysis — Reading a Film Through Its Remake
第 8 段 — 電影欣賞與評論方法第 115 課:翻拍比較分析——透過翻拍版來讀一部電影

ENEnglish version

1. Today's Analytical Method

Comparative remake analysis means placing an original film and its remake side by side and asking: what stayed the same, what changed, and why? Because the basic story is held constant, every difference becomes visible as a choice — shaped by culture, industry, era, audience, censorship or the filmmakers' own interests. It is different from adaptation analysis (Lesson 23), which compares a film with a source in another medium such as a novel; here both texts are films, so we can compare shots, performances, sound and structure directly.

2. Detailed Explanation

  • Step 1 — Separate the story from the telling. List the shared skeleton: characters' functions, key events, the order of reveals. This is what the remake "bought".
  • Step 2 — Make four lists: kept, cut, added, changed. Characters merged or invented, scenes moved, a new ending, a different setting, a changed genre emphasis.
  • Step 3 — Explain each change with context. Ask which pressure best explains it: cultural (religion, family values, ideas of justice), industrial (budget, running time, star casting), historical (what was happening when it was made), regulatory (censorship rules), authorial (the director's recurring themes, Lesson 10).
  • Step 4 — Compare one scene beat by beat. Choose a scene both versions contain and compare framing, performance, music, editing rhythm and dialogue. Small differences here often reveal the larger change in meaning.
  • Step 5 — Judge each film on its own terms. The aim is not to decide which is "better" but what each version is about.

Common misconceptions: (1) "The original is always better" — sometimes the remake is the more famous or more accomplished work (Hitchcock remade his own The Man Who Knew Too Much). (2) "Remakes are just copies" — a remake is an interpretation, like a new production of a play. (3) "Every change is deliberate meaning" — some changes are practical (budget, a location that was unavailable); say so when that is the likely reason.

3. Concrete Film Example — Infernal Affairs (2002) and The Departed (2006)

Films: Infernal Affairs (無間道), directed by Andrew Lau and Alan Mak, written by Alan Mak and Felix Chong (Hong Kong, 2002). The Departed, directed by Martin Scorsese, screenplay by William Monahan (USA, 2006); it won the Academy Awards for Best Picture, Director, Adapted Screenplay and Film Editing (Thelma Schoonmaker).

Shared skeleton: a police officer goes undercover inside a criminal gang, while a gang member secretly works inside the police; each side discovers it has a mole, and each mole must find the other before being found.

What to look at, step by step:

  1. The frame around the story. Infernal Affairs opens with a Buddhist epigraph about Avici, the "continuous" hell (無間地獄) whose suffering never stops — the Chinese title means "the endless way". The Departed opens with Frank Costello (Jack Nicholson) in voice-over over images of Boston's Irish neighbourhood: "I don't want to be a product of my environment. I want my environment to be a product of me." One frames the story as spiritual punishment; the other as a social story about class, ethnicity and a city.
  2. The rooftop confrontation (both films). In Infernal Affairs, Chan Wing-yan (Tony Leung) holds a gun to Lau Kin-ming (Andy Lau) on a bright, empty rooftop high above Hong Kong. The staging is spare: two men, wide sky, long pauses. Lau says he wants to be a good person (我想做個好人); Chan answers that he should say that to the judge. In The Departed, Billy Costigan (Leonardo DiCaprio) arrests Colin Sullivan (Matt Damon) on a rooftop too, but the scene is faster, louder and angrier; the dialogue is profane, and DiCaprio plays Billy as close to breaking. Look at framing and rhythm: the Hong Kong film holds on faces and silence; the remake cuts more and lets the performances explode.
  3. The lift. In both films the undercover cop is shot dead in a lift by another hidden mole, and the surviving mole kills that man to protect himself. The beat is kept almost exactly — a sign the remake recognised it as the story's core shock.
  4. The ending — the biggest change. In the Hong Kong release of Infernal Affairs, Lau survives and keeps his secret: he is free, but must live as a "good cop" he is not — the endless hell of the epigraph. (A separate version prepared for mainland China changed the ending so that he is arrested, reflecting rules there that criminals must not go unpunished.) In The Departed, Sergeant Dignam (Mark Wahlberg), who has disappeared from the story, returns and shoots Colin dead in his apartment; the final shot shows a rat on the balcony rail, with the Massachusetts State House dome behind it.
  5. Why these choices work. The Hong Kong ending makes survival the punishment — an inner, Buddhist idea of retribution. Scorsese's ending gives a violent, external settling of accounts, consistent with his long interest in Catholic guilt and violence (Mean Streets, Goodfellas), and the rat turns the theme into a blunt visual joke about informers in the heart of government.
  6. Deeper meaning (interpretation). Comparing the endings shows two ideas of justice: one where the guilty are trapped inside themselves, and one where somebody has to pay. The mainland ending shows a third pressure — the state's rules about what stories may say.

Observation vs. interpretation: the credits, awards, epigraph, plot events, final rat shot and the existence of a different mainland ending are verifiable observations. Readings about "Buddhist inner punishment" versus "Catholic external reckoning" are interpretations — strong ones, but still arguments.

4. Practical Viewing Exercise

Watch the videotape sequence and the ending of Ringu (Hideo Nakata, 1998) and of its American remake The Ring (Gore Verbinski, 2002). Make the four lists (kept / cut / added / changed) for these two sequences only, then write two sentences: one observation ("The remake's tape contains …") and one interpretation ("This change suggests …"). Which pressures — culture, industry, era, censorship or author — best explain the differences?

5. Key Takeaways

  • Hold the story constant; every difference becomes a choice you can read.
  • Explain changes with context: culture, industry, era, censorship, author — and admit when a change is just practical.
  • Compare one shared scene shot by shot; the endings often reveal the deepest difference.
  • Ask what each version is about, not which is "better".

6. Suggested Further Viewing

Yojimbo (Akira Kurosawa, 1961) and its unofficial Western remake A Fistful of Dollars (Sergio Leone, 1964). Compare the opening walk into town and the hero's first fight: what does the samurai's world become when it moves to the American frontier?

中繁體中文版

1. 今日分析方法

翻拍比較分析是把原版電影和翻拍版並排比較,問:什麼保留了、什麼改變了、為什麼?因為基本故事保持不變,每一個差異都會清楚顯示為一個選擇——受到文化、產業、時代、觀眾、審查或創作者本身興趣的影響。它和改編分析(第 23 課)不同:改編分析比較電影和另一種媒介的原著(例如小說);這裡兩邊都是電影,所以可以直接比較鏡頭、表演、聲音和結構。

2. 詳細說明

  • 步驟一——把故事和講法分開。列出共同骨架:角色的功能、關鍵事件、揭露的順序。這是翻拍版「買下」的東西。
  • 步驟二——列四張清單:保留、刪除、新增、改變。角色被合併或新創、場景被移動、新的結局、不同的背景、類型重點的轉移。
  • 步驟三——用脈絡解釋每個改變。問哪一種壓力最能解釋它:文化(宗教、家庭價值、正義觀)、產業(預算、片長、明星選角)、歷史(拍攝當時發生了什麼)、法規(審查規定)、作者(導演一再出現的主題,第 10 課)。
  • 步驟四——逐拍比較一場戲。選一場兩個版本都有的戲,比較取景、表演、音樂、剪接節奏和對白。這裡的小差異常常揭露整體意義的大改變。
  • 步驟五——用各自的標準評價。目的不是判斷哪一部「比較好」,而是看每個版本各自「在講什麼」。

常見誤解:(1)「原版一定比較好」——有時翻拍版反而更有名或更成熟(希區考克就翻拍過自己的《擒凶記》)。(2)「翻拍只是複製」——翻拍是一種詮釋,就像同一齣戲的新製作版本。(3)「每個改變都有刻意的意義」——有些改變是現實考量(預算、某個場景借不到);如果那可能才是原因,就要直說。

3. 具體電影範例——《無間道》(2002)與《神鬼無間》(The Departed,2006)

電影:《無間道》,導演劉偉強、麥兆輝,編劇麥兆輝、莊文強(香港,2002)。《神鬼無間》,導演馬丁・史柯西斯,編劇威廉・莫納漢(William Monahan)(美國,2006);獲得奧斯卡最佳影片、最佳導演、最佳改編劇本和最佳剪接(瑟爾瑪・斯昆梅克 Thelma Schoonmaker)。

共同骨架:一名警察臥底進入黑幫,同時一名黑幫成員秘密潛伏在警隊;雙方都發現自己內部有臥底,兩個臥底都必須在被揪出來之前先找到對方。

逐步觀察重點:

  1. 故事外面的框架。《無間道》開場是一段佛經引文,談無間地獄——受苦永不間斷的地獄,中文片名就是由此而來。《神鬼無間》開場則是法蘭克・卡斯提洛(傑克・尼克遜飾)的旁白,配上波士頓愛爾蘭社區的畫面:「我不想當環境的產物,我要環境成為我的產物。」前者把故事框成心靈上的報應;後者框成關於階級、族裔與一座城市的社會故事。
  2. 天台對峙(兩部都有)。《無間道》裡,陳永仁(梁朝偉)在香港高空、明亮空曠的天台上用槍指著劉建明(劉德華)。場面調度很簡單:兩個男人、遼闊的天空、長長的停頓。劉建明說「我想做個好人」;陳永仁回答他,這種話去跟法官說。《神鬼無間》裡,比利・寇斯提根(李奧納多・狄卡皮歐)也在天台上逮捕柯林・蘇利文(麥特・戴蒙),但節奏更快、更吵、更憤怒;對白滿是髒話,狄卡皮歐把比利演得瀕臨崩潰。注意取景和節奏:香港版停留在臉和沉默上;翻拍版剪得更多,讓表演爆發。
  3. 電梯。兩部片中,臥底警察都在電梯裡被另一名潛伏的臥底槍殺,活下來的臥底再殺掉那個人自保。這個段落幾乎原封不動地保留——表示翻拍版認定這是整個故事最核心的震撼。
  4. 結局——最大的改變。《無間道》的香港上映版中,劉建明活了下來並守住秘密:他自由了,卻必須一直扮演一個他根本不是的「好警察」——正是開場引文說的無間地獄。(為中國大陸市場準備的另一個版本修改了結局,讓他被逮捕,反映當地「壞人不能逍遙法外」的規定。)《神鬼無間》裡,早已從故事中消失的狄南警佐(馬克・華伯格飾)回來,在柯林的公寓裡開槍打死他;最後一個鏡頭是陽台欄杆上的一隻老鼠,背後是麻州州議會大廈的圓頂。
  5. 為什麼這些選擇有效。香港版的結局讓「活著」本身就是懲罰——一種內在的、佛教式的報應觀。史柯西斯的結局則是暴力的、外在的清算,符合他長期關注的天主教罪疚與暴力(《殘酷大街》、《四海好傢伙》);那隻老鼠則把主題變成一個直白的視覺玩笑:告密者(rat)就在政府的心臟地帶。
  6. 更深的意義(詮釋)。比較兩個結局,可以看到兩種正義觀:一種是罪人被困在自己心裡;一種是總得有人付出代價。中國大陸版的結局則顯示第三種壓力——國家對故事能說什麼的規定。

觀察與詮釋:演職員、獎項、開場引文、情節事件、結尾的老鼠鏡頭,以及另有一個大陸版結局,都是可以查證的觀察。「佛教式內在懲罰」對比「天主教式外在清算」的解讀則是詮釋——很有說服力,但仍然是論點。

4. 實際觀影練習

看《七夜怪談》(Ringu,中田秀夫,1998)和美國翻拍版《七夜怪談西洋篇》(The Ring,高爾・范瑞賓斯基,2002)的錄影帶段落和結局。只針對這兩段列出四張清單(保留/刪除/新增/改變),然後寫兩句話:一句觀察(「翻拍版的錄影帶裡有……」),一句詮釋(「這個改變暗示……」)。文化、產業、時代、審查、作者,哪一種壓力最能解釋這些差異?

5. 重點整理

  • 讓故事保持不變,每一個差異就變成可以解讀的選擇。
  • 用脈絡解釋改變:文化、產業、時代、審查、作者——如果只是現實考量,也要承認。
  • 逐鏡比較一場共同的戲;結局往往揭露最深的差異。
  • 問每個版本在講什麼,而不是哪一部「比較好」。

6. 延伸觀賞推薦

《大鏢客》(Yojimbo,黑澤明,1961)和它的非正式西部片翻拍版《荒野大鏢客》(A Fistful of Dollars,塞吉歐・李昂尼,1964)。比較主角走進小鎮的開場和第一場打鬥:武士的世界搬到美國邊疆之後,變成了什麼?
