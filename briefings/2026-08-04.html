3) Spanish Lesson — Day 26 (10 min, builds on Day 25)

Yesterday covered the affirmative imperative (telling someone to do something). Today covers its mirror image: the negative imperative (telling someone not to do something) — which, surprisingly, borrows an entirely different set of verb forms than yesterday's affirmative commands.

  • ¡No hables tan alto, por favor! — Please don't talk so loud!
  • ¡No coma tanto pan, por favor! — Please don't eat so much bread (formal)!
  • ¡No te preocupes, todo va a salir bien! — Don't worry, everything will be fine!
  • ¡No lo toques, está muy caliente! — Don't touch it, it's very hot!

Grammar note: unlike affirmative commands, every negative command — tú and usted alike — borrows the present subjunctive form. Regular usted negative commands look identical to yesterday's affirmative usted commands (just add "no": no hable, no coma, no escriba), but regular tú negative commands are brand new forms that must NOT reuse yesterday's affirmative tú commands: no hables, no comas, no escribas (not "no habla/come/escribe"). The eight irregular affirmative tú commands from yesterday (di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven) are likewise replaced by subjunctive forms built from the irregular yo-stem: no digas, no hagas, no pongas, no salgas, no tengas, no vengas — with ir and ser as the two true exceptions (no vayas, no seas). Object and reflexive pronouns also flip position: they attach after the verb in affirmative commands (Tócalo!) but move in front of the verb in negative ones (¡No lo toques!).

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 26: 大畜 (Dà Chù) — "The Taming Power of the Great" (Mountain above, Heaven below). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary bridges directly from yesterday: "无妄然後可畜,故受之以大畜。" ("Only once one has attained innocence/freedom from waywardness can great accumulation follow — hence Wú Wàng is succeeded by Dà Chù, the Taming Power of the Great.") This continues yesterday's logic: genuine, uncalculating correctness (无妄) is the necessary precondition for the great store of strength, virtue, or resources this hexagram describes — accumulation built on anything less solid cannot be sustained. The Judgment reads: "大畜:利貞,不家食,吉,利涉大川。" ("The Taming Power of the Great: perseverance furthers. Not eating at home brings good fortune. It furthers one to cross the great water.") "Not eating at home" traditionally signals a worthy person called out of private life into public service, where their accumulated capability can be put to use — while "crossing the great water" signals that this reserve of strength is sufficient to attempt a major, otherwise risky undertaking. The Image adds: "天在山中,大畜;君子以多識前言往行,以畜其德。" ("Heaven within the mountain: the image of the Taming Power of the Great. Thus the superior person acquaints themselves with many sayings and deeds of the past, in order to build up their character thereby.") The image of heaven's vast power being contained and held firm within a still mountain models the hexagram's core teaching: true strength is not merely forceful energy but energy that has been deliberately restrained, stored, and cultivated — much as a person builds character over time by studying accumulated precedent rather than acting purely on impulse.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Ethiopia (衣索比亞)

  • Location: The Horn of Africa; landlocked since Eritrea's 1993 independence, bordered by Eritrea (north), Djibouti (northeast), Somalia (east/southeast), Kenya (south), and South Sudan and Sudan (west/northwest).
  • Territory: Approximately 1,104,300 km² — about 30 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), and roughly comparable to France and Spain combined, making it Africa's 10th-largest country by area.
  • Population: An estimated ~130 million (2026), Africa's second-most populous country after Nigeria and the world's most populous landlocked country; over 80 recognized ethnic groups, with the Oromo (~34%) and Amhara (~27%) the two largest.
  • Religion: A near-even split between Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Christianity (~43%) and Islam (~34%, predominantly Sunni), with a fast-growing Protestant/Evangelical minority known locally as P'ent'ay (~22%) and small numbers of traditional and other faiths.
  • Economy: Historically one of Africa's fastest-growing economies (though strained by 2020-2022's Tigray War), still heavily agriculture-based and the birthplace of coffee (Coffea arabica, native to the Kaffa region). The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD) on the Blue Nile — Africa's largest hydroelectric dam — has become both a national development symbol and a persistent diplomatic flashpoint with downstream Egypt and Sudan over Nile water rights. Ethiopian Airlines, Africa's largest airline group, runs a major long-haul hub through Addis Ababa's Bole International Airport, and government-backed industrial parks (e.g. Hawassa) anchor a growing textile and garment export sector.
  • History: One of the world's oldest continuously recognized states, home to the ancient Kingdom of Aksum (1st-7th century CE, once ranked among the great powers of its era) and to "Lucy," the 3.2-million-year-old Australopithecus afarensis fossil discovered in 1974. Ethiopia's Solomonic dynasty traditionally claimed descent from King Solomon and the Queen of Sheba via the Kebra Nagast, and the country is Africa's only one never formally colonized — Italy's 1896 invasion was decisively repelled at the Battle of Adwa, though a brief Italian occupation did follow from 1936 to 1941. Emperor Haile Selassie ruled from 1930 until the 1974 Derg military coup; the ensuing communist Derg regime's brutal "Red Terror" and civil war ended in 1991, after which Eritrea formally seceded in 1993 (followed by a 1998-2000 border war). Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's 2018 peace deal with Eritrea won him the 2019 Nobel Peace Prize, but the devastating 2020-2022 Tigray War — ended by the Pretoria peace agreement — remains the country's most consequential recent event.
  • Current leader(s): Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed (head of government, in office since April 2018); President Taye Atske Selassie (ceremonial head of state, in office since October 2024).
  • Language: Amharic, written in the Ge'ez script, is the official federal working language; Oromo, Tigrinya, Somali, and Afar hold regional official status, and well over 80 languages are spoken nationwide, with English widely used in business, diplomacy, and higher education.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov (World Factbook)

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Stonks

  • Name: "Stonks" — a deliberate misspelling of "stocks."
  • Origin/history: Built around a 2018 stock-photo asset showing a crudely 3D-rendered, blank-eyed businessman in a suit gesturing at a rising stock chart, purchased from a stock-image marketplace and originally intended as generic corporate clip art with no comedic purpose at all.
  • How it became popular: Adopted by Reddit's r/MemeEconomy community and Facebook meme pages starting around June 2019, where the deliberately "off," soulless CGI aesthetic of the figure became the joke in itself; it spread rapidly across Reddit, Twitter, and Instagram through mid-to-late 2019.
  • Meaning/usage: Captions an ostensibly clever financial or business move that is actually irrational, naive, or reckless — framing a bad decision (or a genuinely lucky windfall) with mock-serious pride, as if profoundly savvy. Also used earnestly and self-deprecatingly by retail investors to celebrate (or ruefully joke about) real stock and crypto price movements.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned a mirrored "Not stonks" counterpart (the same figure with a distressed expression and a falling chart, used for losses), countless edited crossovers with other meme templates, and a surge of renewed relevance during the 2021 GameStop/AMC "meme stock" retail-trading frenzy, when the format became a recurring shorthand across trading-focused online communities.
  • Current status: A settled, evergreen finance-meme template from the 2019 era, still regularly reused whenever retail traders discuss market gains (or losses) online, and widely recognized as one of the internet's defining "so bad/blank it's funny" corporate-stock-photo memes.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Butoh (舞踏)

  • Name: Butoh, more fully "Ankoku Butoh" (暗黒舞踏, "the dance of darkness"), a Japanese avant-garde performance-dance form.
  • Key figures/creators: Co-founded by dancer-choreographer Tatsumi Hijikata (土方巽) and dancer Kazuo Ohno (大野一雄), whose 1959 premiere of "Kinjiki" (禁色, "Forbidden Colors") — based on a Yukio Mishima novella and featuring taboo homoerotic and violent imagery — caused a scandal that got Hijikata expelled from the dance association that had sponsored the performance, and is generally treated as butoh's founding moment.
  • Era/background: Emerged in post-World War II Japan as a radical rejection of both imported Western modern dance/ballet and Japan's own codified traditional forms (Noh, Kabuki), widely read as a visceral artistic response to the trauma of the atomic bombings, wartime devastation, and the disorientation of American occupation.
  • Content overview: Largely non-narrative, exploring the grotesque, the taboo, and the body's "darkness" — decay, metamorphosis, madness, eroticism, and the boundary between human and non-human (insects, the elderly, spirits, corpses) — rather than telling a conventional story.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Performers typically shave their heads and cover their bodies in white rice-powder paint, moving with extreme, hyper-controlled slowness through contorted, often unnatural postures. Choreography is frequently guided not by fixed steps but by "butoh-fu" (舞踏譜) — poetic, image-based verbal scores (e.g., "become a decaying flower") that performers interpret through improvisation rather than execute literally.
  • Cultural/historical significance: A defining rupture in postwar Japanese cultural identity, positioning itself as neither Western nor traditionally Japanese but as something new born from the body's raw, unbeautified truth — directly challenging the refined aesthetic conventions of Noh and Kabuki covered in earlier spotlights.
  • Influence and legacy: Spread internationally through companies such as Sankai Juku (山海塾) and Dairakudakan (大駱駝艦), profoundly influencing global avant-garde dance, physical theatre, and performance art; it remains one of the most widely studied and imitated Japanese contributions to 20th-century performing arts outside Japan.
  • Current status/where to experience: Actively performed and taught today — Sankai Juku continues to tour internationally with signature works like "Unetsu," dedicated butoh festivals and workshops run in Japan (Tokyo, Kyoto) and abroad, and second- and third-generation practitioners (including some trained directly under Ohno before his 2010 death) keep the form evolving.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, britannica.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Performance / Acting Analysis (Lesson 12)

Lessons 1-11 covered mise-en-scène, editing/montage, cinematography/lighting, sound, colour, narrative structure, blocking/staging, framing/composition, symbolism, auteur theory, and the long take. Today's lens turns to something often taken for granted: how to actually analyze a film performance, rather than simply judging it "good" or "bad."

The method: Move past a gut reaction and ask concrete questions: (1) Naturalism vs. stylization — is the performance aiming for lifelike, understated behavior, or a heightened, theatrical register, and does that register match the film's own visual style? (2) Internal vs. external technique — is the actor working from the inside out (Method-style, drawing on real emotional memory to generate a feeling, then letting behavior follow) or from the outside in (building a character through externally observed physical/vocal choices first, as in the classical British tradition)? (3) Micro-behavior — what do the eyes, breath, and stillness (versus movement) communicate in moments with no dialogue at all? A performance's most revealing choices are often what an actor does when they are not required to do anything. (4) Vocal control and its breakdown — track how pitch, pace, and volume shift across a scene, especially the moment control is deliberately lost. (5) Physical transformation — note deliberate changes in weight, posture, accent, or physicality undertaken for the role, and ask whether the transformation serves the character or merely calls attention to the actor's effort. (6) Camera relationship — is the performance scaled for an intimate close-up (which can register the smallest facial shift) or a wide shot (which demands the performance read through body language and blocking alone)?

Worked example — Daniel Day-Lewis in There Will Be Blood (2007, dir. Paul Thomas Anderson), the bowling-alley finale: The scene opens with Day-Lewis's oil tycoon Daniel Plainview affecting a controlled, almost lazy menace — quiet, unhurried, needling his rival Eli Sunday with the now-famous "I drink your milkshake" monologue delivered at a measured, mocking pace. As the confrontation escalates, his vocal control audibly disintegrates: measured mockery gives way to genuine, guttural rage, his volume and pace spiraling upward in real time rather than jumping straight to a "big" outburst. Physically, he dominates the space, chasing Eli around the alley and eventually using a bowling pin as a weapon — his body language shifting from a seated, contained posture to full, uncontained physical aggression. Anderson largely holds on Day-Lewis in extended, minimally interrupted takes (echoing Lesson 11's long take), which forces the performance itself — not editing — to carry the scene's entire escalation.

What to look for and why it works: (1) The performance's arc is the scene's structure — the escalation from contained menace to complete unraveling is built entirely through Day-Lewis's vocal and physical choices, not through cutting between shots. (2) The transformation reads as earned because it is gradual and internally logical: the film has spent its entire runtime building Plainview's contempt and isolation, so this eruption plays as inevitable rather than as a sudden performative "big moment." (3) The uninterrupted camera holds the actor accountable — with few cuts to hide behind, every vocal crack and physical choice is fully visible and sustained in real time, which is precisely why analyzing performance requires watching for behavior across a whole take, not just an isolated line reading.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第二十六天(10 分鐘,銜接第二十五天內容)

昨天學了肯定命令式(叫某人「去做」某件事)。今天則要學它的鏡像對照:否定命令式(叫某人「不要做」某件事)——令人意外的是,否定命令式借用的動詞形態,竟然和昨天的肯定命令式完全不同。

  • ¡No hables tan alto, por favor! — 請不要說話那麼大聲!
  • ¡No coma tanto pan, por favor! — 請不要吃那麼多麵包!(正式用法)
  • ¡No te preocupes, todo va a salir bien! — 別擔心,一切都會順利的!
  • ¡No lo toques, está muy caliente! — 不要碰它,很燙!

文法重點:與肯定命令式不同,不論 tú 或 usted,否定命令式一律借用虛擬式現在式的形態。規則 usted 否定命令式和昨天學的肯定 usted 命令式長得一模一樣(只是前面加上 no:no hable、no coma、no escriba);但規則 tú 否定命令式卻是全新的形態,絕對不能沿用昨天的肯定 tú 命令式:no hables、no comas、no escribas(不能寫成「no habla/come/escribe」)。昨天背過的八個不規則肯定 tú 命令式(di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven),在否定句中同樣要換成從「yo 現在式不規則字根」推導出的虛擬式形態:no digas、no hagas、no pongas、no salgas、no tengas、no vengas——只有 ir 與 ser 是真正的例外(no vayas、no seas)。受詞代名詞與反身代名詞的位置也會跟著翻轉:肯定命令式時代名詞接在動詞後面(¡Tócalo!),否定命令式時則要移到動詞前面(¡No lo toques!)。

4) 易經卦象

第二十六卦:大畜卦(Dà Chù)——「大者積蓄之力量」(上艮下乾,山中藏天)。〈序卦傳〉直接銜接了昨天的卦象:「无妄然後可畜,故受之以大畜。」(唯有先達到无妄——不虛妄躁動的境界——之後,才能真正談得上大規模的積蓄,因此无妄卦之後,便是大畜卦。)這正延續了昨天的邏輯:唯有出於本然、毫不造作的正確(无妄),才是這一卦所描述的巨大力量、德行或資源得以積蓄的必要前提——建立在其他基礎之上的積蓄,終究難以長久維持。卦辭寫道:「大畜:利貞,不家食,吉,利涉大川。」(大畜卦象徵:利於守持正道。不在家中自行飲食(而出仕於朝廷),吉祥;利於渡越大江大河。)「不家食」在傳統上,象徵一位德才兼備之人被召喚走出私人生活、投身公共事務,讓自己積蓄已久的才能得以發揮;而「利涉大川」則意味著,這股積蓄的力量已然充足,足以嘗試一項原本充滿風險的重大行動。〈象傳〉補充道:「天在山中,大畜;君子以多識前言往行,以畜其德。」(天(的浩大能量)蘊藏於山之中,這正是大畜卦的象徵;因此君子應當廣泛認識前人的言論與往昔的事蹟,藉此積蓄、培養自身的德行。)天之浩大能量被沉穩的高山所容納、蘊藏的意象,正呼應了這一卦的核心教誨:真正的力量,並非單純外放的蠻力,而是經過刻意節制、積蓄與涵養的能量——正如一個人若要累積德行,靠的是長期研讀前人累積下來的智慧與經驗,而非僅憑一時衝動行事。

(第二十六次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)、第二十一卦 噬嗑(Shì Kè)、第二十二卦 賁(Bì)、第二十三卦 剝(Bō)、第二十四卦 復(Fù)、第二十五卦 无妄(Wú Wàng)、第二十六卦 大畜(Dà Chù)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 衣索比亞(Ethiopia)

  • 位置: 位於非洲之角(Horn of Africa);自 1993 年厄利垂亞獨立後成為內陸國,北接厄利垂亞、東北接吉布地、東與東南接索馬利亞、南接肯亞、西與西北則與南蘇丹及蘇丹接壤。
  • 領土面積: 約 1,104,300 平方公里——約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 30 倍,規模大致相當於法國與西班牙國土面積之總和,是非洲面積第 10 大的國家。
  • 人口: 估計約 1 億 3,000 萬人(2026 年),是繼奈及利亞之後非洲人口第二多的國家,也是全世界人口最多的內陸國;境內擁有超過 80 個受承認的民族,其中歐羅莫族(Oromo,約占 34%)與阿姆哈拉族(Amhara,約占 27%)為兩大主要民族。
  • 宗教: 衣索比亞正教會(Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Christianity,約占 43%)與伊斯蘭教(約占 34%,以遜尼派為主)幾乎勢均力敵,另有一支成長迅速、當地稱為「P'ent'ay」的新教/福音派少數群體(約占 22%),以及少數傳統信仰與其他宗教信眾。
  • 經濟: 過去曾是非洲經濟成長最快的國家之一(儘管 2020 至 2022 年的提格雷戰爭對經濟造成相當程度的損傷),經濟結構仍高度仰賴農業,更是咖啡的原生發源地(阿拉比卡咖啡 Coffea arabica,原生於衣索比亞卡法 Kaffa 地區)。位於藍尼羅河上的「衣索比亞復興大壩」(GERD)是非洲規模最大的水力發電設施,既是國家發展的象徵,也持續是與下游埃及、蘇丹之間,因尼羅河水權問題而產生的重要外交爭議焦點。非洲規模最大的航空集團衣索比亞航空,以阿迪斯阿貝巴博萊國際機場為樞紐經營長程航線;政府支持設立的工業園區(如哈瓦薩 Hawassa)則帶動了日漸成長的紡織與成衣出口產業。
  • 歷史: 是全世界持續獲得承認最悠久的國家之一,境內曾存在古代的阿克蘇姆王國(Kingdom of Aksum,約西元 1 至 7 世紀,曾被列為當時世界主要強權之一),1974 年在此發現的「露西」(Lucy)化石——一具距今約 320 萬年的南方古猿化石——也舉世聞名。衣索比亞的所羅門王朝,傳統上依《光榮之書》(Kebra Nagast)所載,自稱是所羅門王與示巴女王的後裔;衣索比亞也是非洲唯一從未被正式殖民的國家——1896 年義大利入侵時,在阿杜瓦戰役(Battle of Adwa)中遭到衣索比亞決定性擊退,不過 1936 至 1941 年間仍曾遭義大利短暫占領。海爾・塞拉西皇帝(Haile Selassie)自 1930 年統治至 1974 年,因「德爾格」(Derg)軍事政變而遭推翻;隨後而來的共產政權德爾格血腥的「紅色恐怖」與內戰,直到 1991 年才告終,厄利垂亞則於 1993 年正式獨立(1998 至 2000 年間雙方並曾爆發邊境戰爭)。總理阿比・艾哈邁德(Abiy Ahmed)於 2018 年與厄利垂亞達成和平協議,因此獲頒 2019 年諾貝爾和平獎,但 2020 至 2022 年間造成慘重人道災難的提格雷戰爭(最終以《普勒托利亞和平協議》告終),仍是該國近年來影響最為深遠的重大事件。
  • 現任領導人: 總理阿比・艾哈邁德(Abiy Ahmed,政府首腦,自 2018 年 4 月就任);總統塔耶・阿茨科・塞拉西(Taye Atske Selassie,虛位的國家元首,自 2024 年 10 月就任)。
  • 語言: 使用吉茲字母(Ge'ez script)書寫的阿姆哈拉語(Amharic)為聯邦官方工作語言;歐羅莫語、提格利尼亞語、索馬利語與阿法爾語則在各自地區具有官方地位,全國通行的語言總數超過 80 種,英語則廣泛使用於商業、外交與高等教育場域。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov(World Factbook)

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Stonks(股神哏圖)

  • 名稱: 「Stonks」——刻意把英文「stocks」(股票)拼錯的寫法。
  • 起源/歷史: 源自一張 2018 年的素材庫圖庫照片,畫面是一位以粗糙 3D 技術繪製、表情空洞的西裝男子,正指著一條上揚的股價走勢圖;這張圖原本是從素材庫網站購入,單純作為通用商業素材使用,完全沒有任何幽默或哏圖的意圖。
  • 如何爆紅: 約在 2019 年 6 月左右,開始被 Reddit 的 r/MemeEconomy 社群與 Facebook 哏圖粉專廣泛採用,這個人物刻意呈現出的「詭異」、缺乏靈魂感的 CGI 美學風格,本身就成了笑點所在;隨後在 2019 年中後段,迅速在 Reddit、Twitter 與 Instagram 上擴散開來。
  • 意義與使用情境: 用來搭配一項表面看似高明、實際上卻魯莽、天真或不合邏輯的財務或商業決定——用一種煞有其事、假裝高深莫測的口吻,來包裝一個爛決定(或單純只是運氣好的意外之財)。同時也常被散戶投資人拿來自嘲或真心慶祝股票、加密貨幣的漲跌。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出鏡像對照版本「Not stonks」(同一人物換上沮喪表情,搭配下跌走勢圖,用於虧損情境),也出現大量與其他哏圖模板混搭的改圖版本;2021 年 GameStop、AMC 等「迷因股」散戶交易狂潮期間,這個哏圖再度爆紅,成為當時各大交易討論社群中反覆出現的固定用語。
  • 目前現況: 已成為源自 2019 年、歷久不衰的經典財經哏圖模板,至今每當散戶在網路上討論股市漲跌時仍經常被重新使用,並被公認是網路上「爛到很好笑」商業素材庫哏圖的代表作之一。
    資料來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 舞踏(Butoh)

  • 名稱: 舞踏,正式全名為「暗黑舞踏」(Ankoku Butoh),是一種源自日本的前衛表演舞蹈形式。
  • 關鍵人物: 由舞者暨編舞家土方巽與舞者大野一雄共同創立;1959 年首演的《禁色》(改編自三島由紀夫的同名小說,內容涉及禁忌的同性情慾與暴力意象)引發軒然大波,土方巽也因此被原本贊助該演出的舞蹈協會除名——這場演出一般被視為舞踏誕生的起點。
  • 時代背景: 誕生於第二次世界大戰後的日本,是對西方引進的現代舞/芭蕾,以及日本自身高度規範化的傳統表演形式(能劇、歌舞伎)所提出的激進反叛,普遍被解讀為對原爆創傷、戰爭浩劫,以及美軍占領時期集體迷惘感受的一種強烈藝術回應。
  • 內容概述: 大多不採用傳統敘事,而是探索怪誕、禁忌與身體「黑暗面」的主題——腐朽、變形、瘋狂、情慾,以及人與非人(昆蟲、老者、幽靈、屍體)之間的界線——而非講述一個完整的故事情節。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 表演者通常剃光頭髮,全身塗滿白色米粉顏料,以極度、近乎超現實的緩慢速度,擺出扭曲、往往違反自然的姿態移動。編舞經常不依循固定舞步,而是透過「舞踏譜」(butoh-fu)——以詩意、意象為基礎的口述指令(例如「化身為一朵逐漸腐爛的花」)——由表演者以即興方式詮釋,而非機械式地照本宣科。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 是戰後日本文化認同上的一次重大斷裂,標榜自己既非西方、也非傳統日本文化,而是源自身體最原始、未經美化的真實所誕生的一種全新表演形式——直接挑戰了先前介紹過的能劇與歌舞伎所代表的精緻美學傳統。
  • 影響與傳承: 透過山海塾、大駱駝艦等舞團的國際巡演而傳播至世界各地,對全球前衛舞蹈、肢體劇場與行為藝術產生深遠影響;至今仍是日本對 20 世紀表演藝術貢獻中,在海外被研究與模仿最廣泛的形式之一。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 至今仍持續活躍地演出與傳承——山海塾持續以《卯猝》(Unetsu)等代表作在國際間巡演,日本國內(東京、京都)與海外皆設有專屬舞踏藝術節與工作坊,曾直接受教於大野一雄(2010 年辭世)的第二、第三代傳人,也持續讓這項藝術形式不斷演化。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、britannica.com

8) 看片方法 — 表演/演技分析 Performance & Acting Analysis(第十二課)

第一到十一課教了場面調度、剪輯與蒙太奇、攝影與燈光、音效、色彩、敘事結構、走位與空間調度、取景與構圖、象徵與視覺隱喻、作者論,以及長鏡頭。今天的分析視角,則轉向一個經常被視為理所當然的面向:該如何真正分析一段演技,而不只是單純評論「演得好」或「演得差」。

方法說明: 先跳脫直覺反應,問自己幾個具體問題:(1)自然寫實 vs. 風格化——這段表演追求的是生活化、內斂的行為模式,還是刻意誇張、戲劇化的表演調性?這樣的調性,是否與影片本身的視覺風格相互呼應?(2)由內而外 vs. 由外而內的表演方法——演員是從「內在」出發(方法演技〔Method acting〕式做法,先喚起真實的情感記憶來生成情緒,再讓外顯行為自然跟上),還是從「外在」出發(先透過外部觀察到的肢體、聲音選擇來建構角色,如英式古典表演傳統)?(3)細微行為——在完全沒有台詞的時刻,演員的眼神、呼吸與靜止(相對於動作)透露了什麼訊息?一段表演中最具啟發性的選擇,往往正是演員「不需要做任何事」時所做的選擇。(4)聲音控制及其崩解——留意整場戲中,音高、節奏與音量如何隨劇情演變,尤其是控制力被刻意「放手」瓦解的那一刻。(5)身體上的轉變——留意演員為了角色所刻意做出的體重、體態、口音或身體特徵改變,並思考這種轉變究竟是服務於角色本身,還是只是在炫耀演員付出的努力。(6)與攝影機的關係——這段表演是為近距特寫設計(能捕捉最細微的臉部變化),還是為遠景鏡頭設計(必須完全靠肢體語言與走位調度來傳達)?

實例示範——丹尼爾・戴-路易斯(Daniel Day-Lewis)在《黑金企業》(There Will Be Blood,2007,導演保羅・湯瑪斯・安德森〔Paul Thomas Anderson〕)保齡球館結局戲的演出: 這場戲一開場,戴-路易斯飾演的石油大亨丹尼爾・普蘭維(Daniel Plainview),刻意展現出一種收斂、近乎懶洋洋的威脅感——語氣平靜、不急不徐,以現已成經典台詞的「我喝了你的奶昔」獨白,用一種節制而帶著嘲弄意味的步調來挑釁對手伊萊・桑迪(Eli Sunday)。隨著衝突不斷升級,他的聲音控制力也隨之逐漸瓦解:節制的嘲諷,轉為真實、發自喉頭的狂怒,音量與節奏在真實時間中逐步攀升,而非直接跳到一次「爆發式」的高潮。在肢體層面,他徹底主宰了整個空間,追著伊萊在保齡球館裡跑,最終甚至拿起一根保齡球瓶當作武器——他的肢體語言,從原本坐姿、內斂的狀態,轉變為完全不受控制的肢體攻擊。導演安德森大量採用長時間、極少剪接的鏡頭固定拍攝戴-路易斯的表演(與第十一課的長鏡頭手法相呼應),讓「表演本身」——而非剪接——承擔起這場戲整段情緒升級的全部重量。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)表演的弧線,本身就是這場戲的結構——從內斂的威脅感到徹底崩潰的情緒升級,完全是透過戴-路易斯的聲音與肢體選擇所建構出來的,而非依靠鏡頭之間的剪接。(2)這樣的轉變之所以令人信服,是因為它是漸進、且邏輯上站得住腳的——整部電影從頭到尾都在鋪陳普蘭維的輕蔑與孤立感,因此這場爆發顯得理所當然、水到渠成,而非一次為了表演而表演的「大場面」。(3)不間斷的鏡頭讓演員的表演完全無所遁形——由於幾乎沒有剪接可以掩飾失誤,每一次聲音的裂痕與每一個肢體上的選擇,都必須在真實時間裡完整且持續地呈現出來,這正是為什麼分析演技,必須觀察演員在「整顆鏡頭」中的行為變化,而不能只看單一句台詞的表現。
