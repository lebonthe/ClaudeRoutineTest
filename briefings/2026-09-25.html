3) Spanish Lesson — Day 72 (10 min, Pero vs. Sino / Sino Que)

Steps outside the "lo neutro" topic just completed on Day 71 into a classic, high-value contrast: two Spanish words that both translate as English "but," used in mutually exclusive situations. Pero is the default, general-purpose "but," usable after either an affirmative or a negative statement, and it always adds a qualifying or contrasting idea without erasing what came before (Es caro, pero me gusta — "It's expensive, but I like it" — both facts stand side by side). Sino (and its clause-length partner sino que) is a "corrective but," usable only when the first part of the sentence is explicitly negated and the second part directly replaces/contradicts it, rather than merely adding an unrelated qualification — effectively "not X, but rather Y," where Y cancels X out entirely (No es alto, sino bajo — "He's not tall, but rather short" — the second idea replaces the first). The clearest test: if the second clause could be introduced with "but rather" in English without sounding strange, use sino; if it reads more naturally as a simple "but," use pero — compare No es alto, sino bajo (correction: sino) against No es alto, pero es simpático (addition of an unrelated quality: pero). When the corrective second part contains its own conjugated verb rather than a single noun/adjective/phrase, que must be inserted: No fue a la fiesta, sino que se quedó en casa ("She didn't go to the party, but rather stayed home") — dropping que there (No fue a la fiesta, sino se quedó...) is ungrammatical, since bare sino can only join two matching non-clausal elements of the same grammatical category (noun with noun, adjective with adjective, adverb with adverb).

  • Es caro, pero me gusta. — It's expensive, but I like it.
  • No es alto, sino bajo. — He's not tall, but rather short.
  • No quiero café, sino té. — I don't want coffee, but rather tea.
  • No fue a la fiesta, sino que se quedó en casa. — She didn't go to the party, but rather stayed home.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 8: 駢拇 (Pián Mǔ) — "Webbed Toes"

The opening chapter of the Outer Chapters (外篇), begun now that the Inner Chapters (內篇, traditionally attributed to Zhuangzi himself) concluded with Essay 7's Hundun parable; scholars including A.C. Graham generally treat the Outer and Miscellaneous Chapters as the work of a broader "Zhuangzi school" writing in various later hands and rhetorical styles rather than a single author, and this chapter in particular is often grouped by Graham with a distinct "Primitivist" strand of the text, more polemical and single-mindedly argued than the Inner Chapters' looser, more exploratory parables. The essay opens with its governing image: webbed toes (駢拇) and an extra, sixth finger (枝指) are, biologically, natural growths from a person's own body — yet everyone agrees they are "excessive" (侈) relative to the standard human form, and no one would think to amputate them without doing the person harm, since removing them means cutting away living flesh rather than trimming away something foreign. From this the essay pivots directly to its real target: benevolence and righteousness (仁義), as embraced and promoted by Confucian moralists, are argued to be exactly this kind of "webbed toe" — an artificial, forcibly grafted-on excess, external standards imposed onto human nature from the outside rather than growing organically out of it, however admirable they may look and however much praise their possessors "webbed" with extra virtue may receive. The chapter's most memorable passage extends this logic to a deliberately provocative pairing: Bo Yi (伯夷), the legendary paragon of moral integrity who starved to death atop Mount Shouyang rather than serve the Zhou dynasty he considered illegitimate, and Robber Zhi (盜跖), the archetypal bandit chieftain who died in pursuit of plunder atop Mount Dongling — "the two men died for different things, but in equally destroying their lives and injuring their inborn nature, they were exactly the same" (伯夷死名於首陽之下,盜跖死利於東陵之上,二人者,所死不同,其於殘生傷性均也). The point is deliberately shocking: it is not that virtue and banditry are morally equivalent in the ordinary sense, but that both men alike sacrificed their given nature (性命之情) to an external goal outside themselves — one to reputation, one to profit — and from the standpoint of preserving one's natural allotment intact, chasing an admired external standard is no less a self-inflicted mutilation than chasing a base one. The essay's closing image restates the Inner Chapters' recurring theme of natural sufficiency in a new, physical register: a duck's legs are short, but lengthening them would only cause it pain; a crane's legs are long, but shortening them would only cause it grief (是故凫脛雖短,續之則憂;鶴脛雖長,斷之則悲) — true contentment lies not in conforming to any imposed standard, benevolent or otherwise, but in resting undisturbed within one's own given nature (安其性命之情), letting webbed toes be webbed toes and long legs be long legs.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Argentina (阿根廷)

  • Location & territory size: Occupying most of the southern half of South America's Southern Cone, Argentina borders Chile along the Andes to the west, and Bolivia, Paraguay, Brazil, and Uruguay to the north and east, with a long Atlantic coastline running down to Tierra del Fuego near Antarctica. At roughly 2,780,400 km², it is the world's 8th-largest country by area — about 76.8 times the size of Taiwan (~36,197 km²), more than five times the size of France, and just under a third the size of the entire United States — spanning the fertile Pampas grasslands, the Andes mountains, Patagonian steppe, and a share of the Gran Chaco in the north.
  • Population: Approximately 46 million, heavily concentrated in and around the capital, Buenos Aires, whose greater metropolitan area alone holds roughly a third of the national population; one of Latin America's most urbanized and, per capita, historically wealthiest societies, though with deep regional inequality between the capital and the interior provinces.
  • Religion: Historically overwhelmingly Roman Catholic (Pope Francis, elected 2013 and who died in 2025, was Argentine-born), with the Catholic share now estimated around 60-65% and declining; a growing Evangelical/Protestant minority and a rapidly rising religiously unaffiliated population, particularly among younger urban Argentines, alongside small but historically significant Jewish and Muslim communities (Argentina is home to Latin America's largest Jewish population).
  • Economy: One of Latin America's largest economies and a major global exporter of soybeans, beef, wheat, and other agricultural commodities, alongside a growing energy sector centered on the Vaca Muerta shale formation, which helped push oil production to a record roughly 861,000 barrels per day and turn Argentina into a net energy exporter for the first time in decades. The economy is in the midst of a closely watched, controversial "shock therapy" program under President Javier Milei: monthly inflation has fallen from roughly 12.8% (November 2023) to around 2% by mid-2026 (annualized down from over 300% to roughly 28-34%), the fiscal accounts have moved into surplus, Fitch upgraded Argentina's sovereign credit rating from CCC+ to B-, and country-risk spreads have fallen sharply — achieved alongside real economic pain, including a 1.7% GDP contraction in 2024 before a 4.4% rebound in 2025 and an IMF-projected ~3.5% growth in 2026, plus a reported 28.2% poverty rate (second half of 2025) and widespread household debt.
  • History: Colonized by Spain from the 16th century as part of the Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata, Argentina declared independence in 1816 under leaders including José de San Martín. The 20th century was marked by recurring cycles of civilian and military rule, the populist Peronist movement founded by Juan Perón and Eva "Evita" Perón in the 1940s-50s, a brutal 1976-1983 military dictatorship (the "Dirty War," in which thousands of dissidents were "disappeared"), the 1982 Falklands War with the United Kingdom, a return to democracy in 1983, and a severe economic/sovereign-debt crisis in 2001-2002 that wiped out much of the middle class's savings — a trauma still widely cited as the backdrop to the country's current appetite for radical economic change.
  • Current leadership: Javier Milei, a libertarian economist and political outsider, was elected president and took office on December 10, 2023, and remains in office through 2026 (his term runs until December 2027); he has pursued sweeping deregulation, privatization, and austerity under the slogan "there is no money" (no hay plata), governing with a small party bloc in Congress and relying heavily on negotiated coalitions and executive decrees.
  • Language: Spanish is the official and dominant national language, spoken in a distinctive Rioplatense dialect featuring "voseo" (using vos instead of tú) and a characteristic sh-like pronunciation of "ll"/"y"; Italian immigrant influence (Argentina received one of the largest waves of Italian immigration outside Italy) is also strongly reflected in vocabulary, intonation, and cultural life, and several indigenous languages (including Quechua, Guaraní, and Mapudungun) remain spoken by minority communities.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — PPAP (Pen-Pineapple-Apple-Pen)

  • Name & origin: "PPAP" (an acronym for "Pen-Pineapple-Apple-Pen") is a roughly 45-second novelty song and video uploaded to YouTube in August 2016 by Japanese comedian and singer Kazuhito Kosaka, performing under the stage persona Piko Taro (ピコ太郎). Dressed in a leopard-print-and-zebra-print outfit, Piko Taro performs a simple, repetitive dance while singing a nonsense lyric in which he mimes combining a pen and a pineapple into "Pen-Pineapple," a pen and an apple into "Pen-Apple," and then the two combined phrases into "Pen-Pineapple-Apple-Pen."
  • How it became popular: The video's absurdist simplicity, extremely short length, and instantly imitable dance made it ideal for rapid cross-platform sharing; it received a major international boost in September 2016 when singer Justin Bieber tweeted a link calling it "the catchiest video I've ever seen," instantly exposing it to Bieber's global fanbase, and further mainstream attention followed via a Jimmy Fallon-hosted performance and a Justin Timberlake shout-out.
  • Meaning & usage context: The song has no real semantic content beyond its own wordplay premise; its usage context is almost entirely performative and referential — quoting or re-performing the dance/lyric as pure recognizable novelty, invoking it as a byword for a certain kind of gleefully absurd, low-production-value internet virality, or referencing it in discussions of Japanese-to-global meme crossover.
  • Variations & evolution: PPAP inspired an enormous number of parody, remix, and translated-lyric versions across many languages, official merchandising and a mobile game, and a formal recognition milestone: in December 2016, Guinness World Records certified PPAP as the shortest song ever to chart on the US Billboard Hot 100 (peaking at #77), officially cementing its status beyond ordinary viral ephemera.
  • Current status: Ten years on, PPAP remains a widely recognized reference point for Japanese internet-culture crossover into global meme consciousness and is regularly cited alongside earlier Japan-originated viral hits in retrospectives of 2010s internet culture; Piko Taro has continued performing and releasing PPAP-adjacent novelty material in the years since, though never again at the same scale as the original 2016 wave.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Khon (โขน, Thai Classical Masked Dance-Drama)

  • Name & key background: Khon is Thailand's classical masked dance-drama, traditionally performed at the royal court and now recognized by UNESCO (inscribed on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2018) as a national treasure of Thai performing arts; its origins are traced to court entertainments of the Ayutthaya Kingdom (14th-18th centuries), drawing together elements of shadow-puppet theatre (nang yai), martial performance traditions, and classical Thai dance.
  • Content & overview: Khon performances are drawn almost exclusively from the Ramakien, Thailand's own national epic adaptation of the Indian Ramayana, retelling the story of Prince Rama, his wife Sita, his loyal ally the monkey-general Hanuman, and his battle against the demon king Ravana (Thotsakan in the Thai telling) to rescue Sita from captivity — a narrative reworked over centuries to reflect distinctly Thai court values, cosmology, and kingship ideals rather than a literal translation of the Sanskrit original.
  • Artistic style & technique: Performers wear large, ornately gilded and painted full-head masks (khon masks) representing specific characters — demons, monkeys, and certain other roles are always masked, while the principal human roles (Rama, Sita) traditionally perform bare-faced with elaborate crown-like headdresses instead; because the masks conceal the performer's face entirely, emotion and characterization are conveyed almost entirely through a codified, highly precise vocabulary of body posture, hand gestures (mudra-like), and stylized combat choreography (particularly for the demon and monkey battle scenes), accompanied by a piphat ensemble (xylophones, gongs, oboe-like pi, and drums) and sung/narrated verse rather than the performers' own spoken dialogue.
  • Cultural & historical significance: As a court art form historically restricted to royal patronage and elite training lineages, Khon functioned for centuries as a direct expression and reinforcement of Thai kingship and cosmological order, with its Ramakien source material itself closely tied to the traditional titles and symbolic identity of Thai monarchs (each of whom has historically been associated with Rama); its 2018 UNESCO inscription was pursued explicitly as a national preservation priority amid concerns about a shrinking pool of masters capable of training new performers in its exacting technique.
  • Influence & legacy: Khon has directly shaped Thai classical dance more broadly and remains a reference point for national identity in Thai education and cultural diplomacy; its stylized demon and monkey masks are among the most internationally recognizable single images of Thai culture, reproduced constantly in tourism, decorative art, and popular design.
  • Where to experience it today: Regularly staged at Bangkok's Thailand Cultural Centre and the Sala Chalermkrung Royal Theatre (which specializes in Khon performances for general audiences), with an annual royally-patronized production also staged for the wider public; shorter demonstration performances are also a staple of Thai cultural tourism programming.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — High-Angle vs. Low-Angle Shots: Camera Angle & Power Dynamics (Lesson 58)

A foundational compositional variable distinct from every lens/lighting/framing lesson taught so far in this track (Day 22's Lens Choice & Focal Length, Day 3's Cinematography & Lighting, Day 22/2026-09-22's High-Key vs. Low-Key Lighting, Day 31's Framing & Composition): where the camera is positioned vertically relative to its subject's eyeline, independent of angle-of-rotation (Day 26's Dutch Angle, a roll/tilt around the lens axis, is a different variable entirely and can be combined with either a high or low angle). A low-angle shot places the camera below the subject's eyeline, looking up — the subject appears to loom, tower, or dominate the frame, conventionally read as an index of power, authority, threat, or grandeur, since it replicates the physical vantage point of someone smaller looking up at someone or something larger. A high-angle shot places the camera above the subject's eyeline, looking down — the subject appears diminished, vulnerable, or subordinate within the frame, replicating the vantage point of looking down at someone or something smaller or lower in status. Neither convention is an absolute rule (a director can and does deliberately invert the expected reading for irony or surprise), but the baseline association is strong and consistent enough across film history that a departure from it reads as a meaningful choice in itself. When analysing a scene, check where the camera sits relative to each character's eyeline in a given shot, whether that placement is held consistently for a character across a scene or shifted as the power balance within the scene itself shifts, and whether the angle is reinforcing the dialogue/blocking's power dynamic or deliberately working against it.

Worked example: Citizen Kane (1941, dir. Orson Welles, cinematography by Gregg Toland) uses camera angle as one of its most legible visual signatures of Charles Foster Kane's rise and fall. At the height of his political ambition, Kane is repeatedly shot from a pronounced low angle — most famously in the campaign-rally sequence, where Welles positions the camera near floor level looking sharply up at Kane addressing a crowd beneath a towering banner bearing his own giant painted face, fusing the low angle with the literal, oversized scale of his self-image to produce a portrait of a man at his most outsized and commanding. Toland and Welles pursued these extreme low angles partly for a purely technical reason that became a celebrated stylistic signature in its own right: shooting upward at actors' faces required building visible ceilings into the sets (a near-unprecedented choice at the time, since a low camera would otherwise expose the lighting rigs above a conventional open-topped soundstage set), reinforcing the sense of characters boxed in beneath real, oppressive architecture rather than floating in a generic studio void. By contrast, in the film's final act, the aged, friendless Kane is shown wandering alone amid the vast, crated-up interior of Xanadu, increasingly filmed from higher, more distant, more detached camera positions that shrink him within the frame's own cavernous scale — the same directorial toolkit used to build him up at the film's start is run in reverse to stage his isolation and diminishment at its end.

3)西班牙文課程——第 72 天(10 分鐘,Pero 與 Sino / Sino Que 的用法區分)

從第 71 天剛學完的「中性 lo」主線跳脫出來,進入一組經典且實用價值極高的對比:兩個都能翻譯成英文「but」的西班牙文單字,卻分別用在互斥的情境中。Pero 是預設、通用型的「但是」,不論前一句是肯定句或否定句皆可使用,且它永遠是在「補充」一個對比或限定的想法,而不會抹去前面已成立的事實(Es caro, pero me gusta. ——「它很貴,但我喜歡。」——兩件事同時成立,並存不悖)。Sino(及其接完整子句時的搭檔 sino que)則是一種「修正式的但是」,只能用在前半句被明確否定、且後半句直接取代/推翻前半句內容之時——而非僅僅補充一個不相關的限定——實質上等於「不是 X,而是 Y」,其中 Y 完全抵銷了 X(No es alto, sino bajo. ——「他不高,而是矮。」——後面的說法直接取代了前面的說法)。最清楚的判斷測試:如果後半句可以用英文「but rather」(而是……)自然接上而不顯得奇怪,就該用 sino;如果讀起來更像單純的「但是」,就該用 pero——比較 No es alto, sino bajo(屬於修正:用 sino)與 No es alto, pero es simpático(補充一項不相關的特質:用 pero)。當修正用的後半部分本身帶有自己的變位動詞、而非單一名詞/形容詞/片語時,必須插入 que:No fue a la fiesta, sino que se quedó en casa.(「她沒去派對,而是留在家裡。」)——若省略 que(誤寫成 No fue a la fiesta, sino se quedó...)則不合文法,因為單純的 sino 只能連接兩個屬於同一詞類、非完整子句的對等成分(名詞對名詞、形容詞對形容詞、副詞對副詞)。

  • Es caro, pero me gusta. —— 它很貴,但我喜歡。
  • No es alto, sino bajo. —— 他不高,而是矮。
  • No quiero café, sino té. —— 我不要咖啡,而是要茶。
  • No fue a la fiesta, sino que se quedó en casa. —— 她沒去派對,而是留在家裡。

4)莊子——第 8 篇:駢拇(Pián Mǔ)——「並生的腳趾」

《莊子》外篇的開篇之作,始於內篇(傳統上認為出自莊子本人手筆)以第七篇渾沌寓言作結之後;包括葛瑞漢(A.C. Graham)在內的學者,普遍認為外篇與雜篇是「莊子學派」在不同時期、以不同筆法寫成的作品集,而非單一作者所著,本篇尤其常被葛瑞漢歸入一支立場鮮明的「原始主義」(Primitivist)脈絡,論述風格更具論戰性、單一目的性,不同於內篇那種較為鬆散、探索性的寓言筆法。本篇開篇即拋出貫穿全文的核心意象:駢拇(腳趾併生)與枝指(多長出的第六根手指),就生理而言,都是從自己身體上自然長出的組織——然而人人皆認為這相對於標準人形而言屬於「多餘」(侈),卻也沒有人會想要將其切除而不傷及當事人,因為切除它們等於是割去活生生的血肉,而非修剪掉某種外來異物。由此,本篇直接將論述焦點轉向真正的批判對象:儒家道德家所提倡、奉行的仁義,正是這樣一種「駢拇」——一種人為、被強行嫁接上去的多餘之物,是從外部強加於人性之上的標準,而非從人性內部自然生長出來的東西,無論這些標準表面上看起來多麼令人稱道,無論那些被仁義「駢生」出額外美德之人獲得多少讚譽,皆是如此。本篇最令人印象深刻的段落,將這套邏輯延伸至一組刻意挑釁的對比:伯夷,傳說中堅守道義的典範,寧可餓死於首陽山下也不願臣服於他認為不具正當性的周朝;盜跖,典型的盜賊頭目,為爭奪財貨而死於東陵山上——「伯夷死名於首陽之下,盜跖死利於東陵之上,二人者,所死不同,其於殘生傷性均也。」這個論點刻意營造出強烈的衝擊感:並非說德行與盜匪在一般道德意義上等同,而是說兩人同樣都為了自身之外的某個目標而犧牲了與生俱來的本性(性命之情)——一人為名,一人為利——而從保全自身天賦本性完整無缺的角度而言,追逐一項受人稱頌的外在標準,與追逐一項卑下的外在標準相比,同樣都是自我造成的戕害,並無二致。本篇的結尾意象,以一種更為具體的生理化語彙,重申了內篇反覆出現的「自然自足」主題:野鴨的腿雖短,若強行接長,牠只會因此痛苦;仙鶴的腿雖長,若強行截短,牠只會因此悲傷(是故凫脛雖短,續之則憂;鶴脛雖長,斷之則悲)——真正的安適自在,不在於順從任何強加的標準(無論那標準是否出於仁義),而在於安然停留於自身與生俱來的本性之中(安其性命之情),讓併生的腳趾就是併生的腳趾,讓長腿就是長腿。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——阿根廷(Argentina)

  • 地理位置與領土規模:阿根廷占據南美洲「南錐體」的大半部分,西側沿安地斯山脈與智利接壤,北側與東側則與玻利維亞、巴拉圭、巴西及烏拉圭相鄰,擁有一條綿延至靠近南極洲的火地島的漫長大西洋海岸線。國土面積約 278 萬 400 平方公里,為全球面積第 8 大國家——約為台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)的 76.8 倍,超過法國國土面積的五倍,略低於整個美國國土面積的三分之一;境內涵蓋肥沃的彭巴草原、安地斯山脈、巴塔哥尼亞草原,以及北部一部分的大廈谷(Gran Chaco)地區。
  • 人口:約 4,600 萬人,人口高度集中於首都布宜諾斯艾利斯及其周邊,光是大布宜諾斯艾利斯都會區便占全國人口約三分之一;是拉丁美洲都市化程度最高、人均而言歷史上也最富裕的社會之一,惟首都與內陸各省之間存在顯著的區域不平等。
  • 宗教:歷史上以羅馬天主教占絕對多數(已故教宗方濟各〔2013 年當選,已於 2025 年辭世〕即出身阿根廷),目前天主教徒比例估計約 60-65% 且持續下滑;新教/福音派人口正逐漸成長,無宗教信仰人口也快速上升,尤以都會區年輕世代為甚,此外境內也有規模雖小、但歷史意義重大的猶太與穆斯林社群(阿根廷擁有拉丁美洲最大的猶太人口)。
  • 經濟:為拉丁美洲最大經濟體之一,也是全球大豆、牛肉、小麥等農產品出口大國,同時以「殭屍死牛」(Vaca Muerta)頁岩油氣區為核心,能源產業快速成長,原油產量已衝上每日約 86.1 萬桶的歷史新高,使阿根廷數十年來首度轉為能源淨出口國。目前經濟正處於總統哈維爾·米雷伊(Javier Milei)備受矚目、也備受爭議的「震盪療法」改革之中:月通膨率已從 2023 年 11 月的約 12.8% 降至 2026 年年中約 2%(年化通膨率則從逾 300% 降至約 28-34%),財政帳已轉為盈餘,惠譽(Fitch)已將阿根廷主權信用評等由 CCC+ 調升至 B-,國家風險利差也大幅收斂——但這些成果也伴隨著實質的經濟陣痛:2024 年 GDP 一度萎縮 1.7%,其後 2025 年反彈成長 4.4%,IMF 預估 2026 年成長率約 3.5%,同時據報 2025 年下半年貧窮率達 28.2%,家庭普遍背負債務。
  • 歷史:16 世紀起由西班牙殖民,隸屬拉普拉塔總督轄區;1816 年在包括荷西·德·聖馬丁(José de San Martín)在內的領導人帶領下宣布獨立。20 世紀阿根廷歷經文人與軍事政權反覆更迭:1940-50 年代由胡安·裴隆(Juan Perón)與艾薇塔·裴隆(Eva "Evita" Perón)創立的裴隆主義民粹運動;1976 至 1983 年殘酷的軍事獨裁統治(「骯髒戰爭」,數千名異議人士遭「失蹤」處決);1982 年與英國爆發的福克蘭群島戰爭;1983 年恢復民主;以及 2001 至 2002 年嚴重的經濟與主權債務危機,幾乎摧毀了中產階級的儲蓄——這場創傷至今仍被廣泛視為該國如今願意接受激進經濟改革的背景成因。
  • 現任領導人:自由意志派經濟學者、政治素人哈維爾·米雷伊,於 2023 年 12 月 10 日當選總統並就任,2026 年目前仍在任(任期至 2027 年 12 月屆滿);他以「沒有錢」(no hay plata)為口號,大力推動法規鬆綁、國營事業私有化與財政撙節,並在國會小黨團的支持下,高度仰賴協商組成的執政聯盟與行政命令來推動施政。
  • 語言:西班牙語為官方及主要通行語言,當地使用獨具特色的拉普拉塔口音(Rioplatense),特徵包括以 vos 取代 tú 的「voseo」用法,以及將「ll」/「y」發成類似英文「sh」音的獨特發音;由於阿根廷曾接收全球規模數一數二、義大利本土以外最大的義大利移民潮,義大利文化影響也深深滲入當地詞彙、語調與生活文化之中;此外,包括蓋丘亞語(Quechua)、瓜拉尼語(Guaraní)與馬普切語(Mapudungun)在內的多種原住民語言,至今仍在少數族群社群中通行。

6)網路迷因介紹——PPAP(Pen-Pineapple-Apple-Pen)

  • 名稱與起源:「PPAP」(「Pen-Pineapple-Apple-Pen」的縮寫)是一支長度約 45 秒的搞笑歌曲暨影片,於 2016 年 8 月由日本藝人小坂一茂(Kazuhito Kosaka)以藝名「Piko太郎」(ピコ太郎)上傳至 YouTube。他身穿豹紋搭配斑馬紋的誇張服裝,一邊唱著毫無實質意義的歌詞,一邊做出簡單而不斷重複的舞蹈動作,比手劃腳地將一支筆與一顆鳳梨「組合」成「Pen-Pineapple」,再將一支筆與一顆蘋果「組合」成「Pen-Apple」,最後再將這兩個組合詞合而為一,唱出「Pen-Pineapple-Apple-Pen」。
  • 如何走紅:影片本身荒謬簡單、片長極短、舞步又極易模仿,使其非常適合跨平台快速傳播;2016 年 9 月,歌手小賈斯汀(Justin Bieber)在推特上轉貼此片連結,稱其為「我看過最洗腦的影片」,一舉將其曝光給自己龐大的全球粉絲群,隨後又透過吉米·法倫(Jimmy Fallon)主持節目上的演出,以及賈斯汀·提姆布萊克(Justin Timberlake)的公開推薦,進一步獲得主流媒體關注。
  • 意義與使用情境:這首歌除了自身文字遊戲的前提之外,並無任何實質語意內容;其使用情境幾乎完全是「表演性」與「引用性」的——單純引用或重新演出這段舞蹈/歌詞,作為一種純粹、可辨識的搞笑哏,或用來指稱某種帶著歡快荒謬感、低製作成本的網路爆紅現象,又或是在討論「日本迷因跨國走紅」此類主題時被引用。
  • 變體與演進:PPAP 衍生出大量惡搞、混音,以及各語言版本的翻唱歌詞,還催生了官方周邊商品與一款手機遊戲;更有一項正式的紀錄里程碑:2016 年 12 月,金氏世界紀錄正式認證 PPAP 為登上美國告示牌百強單曲榜(Billboard Hot 100)史上最短的歌曲(最高名次第 77 名),正式確立其超越一般短暫網路爆紅現象的地位。
  • 現況:時隔十年,PPAP 至今仍是「日本網路文化跨國走紅為全球性迷因」的重要參照點,經常在回顧 2010 年代網路文化的文章中,與其他更早期、同樣源自日本的爆紅內容並列提及;Piko太郎本人在此後多年間持續演出並發行與 PPAP 相關的搞笑作品,惟規模始終未能再現 2016 年原始熱潮的盛況。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——孔劇(โขน,泰國古典面具舞劇)

  • 名稱與背景概述:孔劇(Khon)是泰國古典的面具舞劇形式,傳統上於泰國王室宮廷中演出,現已獲聯合國教科文組織列為人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄(2018 年登錄),被視為泰國表演藝術的國家瑰寶;其起源可追溯至大城王朝(14 至 18 世紀)的宮廷娛樂表演,融合了皮影戲(nang yai)、武術表演傳統與古典泰國舞蹈等元素。
  • 內容概述:孔劇的劇目幾乎全部取材自《拉瑪堅》(Ramakien),即泰國版本的印度史詩《羅摩衍那》(Ramayana)民族化改編作品,講述羅摩王子(Rama)與其妻悉多(Sita)、忠心耿耿的猴王將軍哈奴曼(Hanuman),對抗惡魔之王羅波那(在泰國版本中稱為 Thotsakan)、營救被擄的悉多的故事——這則敘事歷經數百年在地化改編,反映的是泰國宮廷特有的價值觀、宇宙觀與王權理念,而非梵文原典的逐字翻譯。
  • 藝術風格與技法:表演者頭戴大型、金碧輝煌且彩繪精細的整頭面具(孔劇面具)以代表特定角色——惡魔、猴類及部分其他角色一律戴面具演出,而羅摩、悉多等主要人類角色,傳統上則以真面目演出,改戴類似王冠的華麗頭飾。由於面具完全遮住表演者的臉孔,情感與角色刻劃幾乎完全仰賴一套高度精確、程式化的肢體姿態、手勢語彙(類似手印),以及風格化的戰鬥編舞(尤以惡魔與猴族的戰鬥場面最具代表性)來傳達,並搭配皮帕特樂團(piphat,包含木琴、鑼、類似嗩吶的畢〔pi〕,以及鼓類樂器)伴奏,以吟唱/念誦的詩文取代表演者本人的台詞對白。
  • 文化與歷史意義:孔劇作為一種歷史上長期僅限於王室贊助與菁英傳承體系的宮廷藝術形式,數百年來直接體現並強化了泰國的王權與宇宙秩序觀,其取材來源《拉瑪堅》本身,更與泰國歷代君主的傳統封號與象徵身分緊密相連(歷代泰王皆與羅摩王子的形象有所連結);2018 年申請列入聯合國教科文組織名錄,正是出於對能夠以嚴謹技法訓練新一代表演者的大師人才日漸凋零的擔憂,而將其列為國家優先保存的文化資產。
  • 影響與傳承:孔劇直接形塑了更廣義的泰國古典舞蹈發展,至今仍是泰國教育體系與文化外交中國族認同的重要參照點;其風格化的惡魔與猴族面具,是泰國文化在國際上最容易被辨識的單一意象之一,在觀光紀念品、裝飾藝術與流行設計中被大量複製運用。
  • 今日可觀賞管道:曼谷的泰國文化中心(Thailand Cultural Centre),以及專門為一般觀眾製作孔劇演出的「詩麗吉皇家劇院」(Sala Chalermkrung Royal Theatre)皆定期上演孔劇;每年也會有一場由王室贊助、面向社會大眾公演的年度大戲;較短篇幅的示範性演出,也是泰國文化觀光行程中的常見節目。

8)看片方法——仰角與俯角鏡位:攝影機角度與權力關係(第 58 課)

一項與本系列先前所有鏡頭/燈光/取景課程(第 22 天的鏡頭選擇與焦距壓縮、第 3 天的攝影與燈光、2026-09-22 的高調/低調打光、第 31 天的取景與構圖)截然不同的基礎構圖變因:攝影機相對於拍攝主體視線水平所在的垂直位置,與旋轉角度(第 26 天學過的傾斜鏡位,那是繞鏡頭軸心的翻滾/傾斜,屬於完全不同的變因,且可與仰角或俯角自由組合)無關。仰角鏡頭將攝影機放置在拍攝主體視線之下、由下往上拍攝——主體在畫面中顯得高聳、龐大、具支配感,依慣例通常被解讀為權力、權威、威脅或宏偉氣勢的視覺標誌,因為這重現了一個較矮小的人抬頭仰望一個較高大的人或物的真實視角。俯角鏡頭則將攝影機放置在拍攝主體視線之上、由上往下拍攝——主體在畫面中顯得渺小、脆弱或處於從屬地位,重現的是俯視一個地位較低、身形較小的人或物的視角。這兩項慣例並非絕對鐵律(導演完全可以、也經常刻意反轉這種預期的解讀方式以製造反諷或意外效果),但這種基本聯想在整個電影史中如此強烈且一致,以至於任何偏離這項慣例的做法,本身便足以構成一項具有意義的選擇。分析一段場景時,請留意某個鏡頭中攝影機相對於各角色視線的位置;這個位置在整場戲中是否針對某個角色維持一致,或隨著戲中權力關係的消長而改變;以及這個角度究竟是強化了台詞/走位所呈現的權力關係,還是刻意與之背道而馳。

實例演練:《大國民》(Citizen Kane,1941,導演:奧森·威爾斯〔Orson Welles〕,攝影:格雷格·托蘭〔Gregg Toland〕)將攝影機角度運用為凱恩(Charles Foster Kane)由盛轉衰的最清晰可辨識的視覺標誌之一。在凱恩政治野心的巔峰時期,他一再以明顯的仰角鏡頭拍攝——最著名的莫過於競選集會那場戲:威爾斯將攝影機幾乎貼近地面,由下往上、以陡峭的仰角拍攝凱恩在群眾面前發表演說,背景則是一幅懸掛著凱恩本人巨幅畫像的高聳布條,將仰角鏡頭與他自我形象本身的誇張尺度融為一體,共同塑造出這位人物一生中最為壯大、最具支配氣勢的一幅肖像。托蘭與威爾斯採用這類極端仰角鏡頭,部分原因其實出於純粹的技術考量,卻反而成為一項備受推崇、自成一格的風格標誌:由下往上拍攝演員面孔,勢必需要在片場搭建起真實可見的天花板(在當時幾乎是前所未見的做法,因為若採低機位拍攝一般敞頂式攝影棚場景,勢必會將棚頂上的燈光設備一併入鏡),這也強化了片中人物彷彿被禁錮於真實、具壓迫感的建築結構之下,而非漂浮在毫無特徵的攝影棚虛空之中的觀感。相對地,在本片最後一幕,年邁、眾叛親離的凱恩獨自徘徊於堆滿箱籠的仙那度(Xanadu)豪宅內部,攝影機位置也逐漸轉為更高、更遠、更疏離的視角,將他的身形在畫面本身空曠浩大的尺度中不斷縮小——片頭用以將他形象逐步壯大的同一套導演手法,在片尾則被反向運用,用以呈現他的孤立與渺小。
