5) Country Spotlight — Ecuador (厄瓜多)

  • Location & size: northwest South America, bordered by Colombia (north) and Peru (south and east), with the Pacific to the west; it straddles the equator, which gives the country its name. Area about 283,560 km² including the Galápagos Islands, roughly 7.9 times Taiwan (about 36,200 km²), larger than the United Kingdom (about 243,600 km²) and a little bigger than Colorado.
  • Population: about 18 million (UN-based estimates put it near 18.4 million in mid-2026; the World Bank's 2024 figure was about 18.1 million). Capital Quito (about 2,850 m above sea level, one of the highest capitals in the world); largest city Guayaquil.
  • Religion: Roman Catholicism is the largest faith (roughly 70% by recent estimates, approximate), with a sizeable evangelical Protestant minority; the state is secular.
  • Languages: Spanish (official); Kichwa and Shuar are official languages of intercultural relations, and there are about a dozen other Indigenous languages. Parts of the Andean highlands use some voseo, linking today's Spanish lesson.
  • Economy: upper-middle-income; IMF projections put 2026 nominal GDP near US$138 billion (the World Bank's 2024 figure was about US$125 billion) and GDP per capita near US$7,600. It has used the US dollar as its currency since 2000, after a banking crisis. Main exports are crude oil, bananas (it is among the world's largest exporters), shrimp, cut flowers, cocoa and fish. Growth is forecast at about 2.5% (IMF).
  • History: The Quito region came under the Inca empire in the late 15th century. Spanish conquerors founded Quito in 1534, and it became the Royal Audiencia of Quito. After the Battle of Pichincha on 24 May 1822 (Antonio José de Sucre) it joined Gran Colombia and became a separate republic in 1830. A long border dispute with Peru led to a 1941 war, the 1942 Rio Protocol and a final peace treaty in 1998. Politics in the 2000s were shaped by Rafael Correa (2007-2017). Since 2023 the country has faced a severe security crisis tied to organised crime and drug trafficking.
  • Current leader: President Daniel Noboa (took office in November 2023 after a snap election; won a full term in the April 2025 runoff and was inaugurated on 24 May 2025, term to 2029). A report dated Sept 2026 still describes him as president. Voters rejected all four proposals in his Nov 16, 2025 referendum (per a search summary). I did not find reporting dated Nov 2026, so any leadership change in recent weeks is unverified.
  • Fun facts: The Galápagos Islands, about 1,000 km offshore, inspired Darwin's thinking on evolution and are a UNESCO World Heritage Site; the "Mitad del Mundo" monument marks the equator near Quito; Ecuador was the first country to grant constitutional rights to nature (2008).
  • Sources/notes: Population/GDP from IMF- and UN-based search summaries (StatisticsTimes, Worldometers); leader from a general search (La República). The rest comes from general knowledge; the religion percentage and historical details are approximate.

6) Internet Meme — The Wednesday Dance (Wednesday Addams, Nov 2022)

  • Name: "The Wednesday Dance" (also the "Wednesday Addams dance" or "Goo Goo Muck dance").
  • Origin: In Netflix's series Wednesday (premiered Nov 23, 2022; created by Alfred Gough and Miles Millar, directed in part by Tim Burton), Jenna Ortega's Wednesday Addams performs a stiff, jerky solo dance at the school's Rave'N dance in episode 4. Ortega has said she choreographed it herself, drawing on 1980s goth and post-punk dancing (including Siouxsie Sioux and The Cramps' Lux Interior) and her own movements rather than the choreography she was offered. The song in the scene is The Cramps' "Goo Goo Muck" (1981).
  • How it spread: Within days, people on TikTok recreated the dance, and then a mash-up with Lady Gaga's "Bloody Mary" (sped up) became the viral soundtrack, sending Gaga's 2011 song back up the charts. Recreations came from fans, celebrities and brands, and the show itself became one of Netflix's most-watched English-language series.
  • Meaning & usage: A shorthand for deadpan goth awkwardness and for owning an odd, off-beat style; people post it as a costume or Halloween trend, as a "my face when…" reaction, and in cover videos. It signals a mix of gloom, dry humour and unapologetic weirdness.
  • Variations & evolution: The "Bloody Mary" version, parodies in other sitcoms and kids' shows, lip-sync and cosplay versions, and recreations in Taiwan and across Asia during Halloween seasons.
  • Current status: Past its 2022-2023 peak, but it returns every Halloween and with each new season of the show. (Dates and attribution are from general knowledge; no live source was checked today.)

7) Film / Performing Arts — Ugetsu (雨月物語, 1953, dir. Kenji Mizoguchi)

  • Name & creators: Ugetsu (also Ugetsu Monogatari, "Tales of Moonlight and Rain"), produced by Daiei; directed by Kenji Mizoguchi; screenplay by Yoshikata Yoda and Matsutarō Kawaguchi; cinematography by Kazuo Miyagawa. The cast includes Masayuki Mori (Genjūrō), Machiko Kyō (Lady Wakasa), Kinuyo Tanaka (Miyagi) and Sakae Ozawa (Tōbei).
  • Era & background: Made in 1953 in a Japan still recovering from the war, set in the civil-war era of the late 16th century. It adapts two ghost stories from Ueda Akinari's 1776 collection Ugetsu Monogatari and borrows an episode from a Guy de Maupassant short story. It won the Silver Lion at the 1953 Venice Film Festival, helping open Western eyes to Japanese cinema.
  • Plot overview: Two peasant couples live by a lake. The potter Genjūrō wants to sell his pots for profit, and his brother-in-law Tōbei dreams of becoming a samurai. Both leave their wives and sons behind as war nears. Genjūrō is seduced by the aristocratic Lady Wakasa, who turns out to be a ghost, while Tōbei buys his way into the army and becomes a hollow hero. When Genjūrō finally returns home, his wife Miyagi seems to welcome him, but the next morning he learns she died during his absence. The film ends with Genjūrō's son at her grave and Tōbei returning to his wife.
  • Style & technique: Mizoguchi's famous long takes and slow, scroll-like camera movements that glide through scenes and unroll like a painted scroll; the boat crossing in mist on Lake Biwa; the way the camera moves from the living world to the ghost world without a cut; the delicate, ink-painting lighting from Miyagawa; and a traditional Japanese score mixing noh-style drum and flute.
  • Significance & legacy: Regularly named among the great Japanese films and a regular on critics' lists such as Sight & Sound; it shows how a ghost story can carry a humane criticism of war, greed and ambition and of their cost to women. Its flowing camera and ethereal atmosphere influenced later directors in Japan and beyond.
  • Where to experience it: Restored editions have been released by the Criterion Collection; it also appears in retrospectives at repertory cinemas. Check current streaming availability in your region. (From general knowledge; no live source checked.)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 98: Ambient Soundscape & Sound Perspective

  • What it is: Beyond music and dialogue, every scene has a soundscape: the layers of ambient sound (wind, traffic, machinery, insects, room hum) that tell us where we are and how it feels. Sound designers choose which layers are loud, quiet or missing, and where the "microphone" sits relative to the character (close and intimate, or far and distant). That is sound perspective. This lesson differs from Lesson 67 (silence/absence of score), Lesson 73 (overlapping dialogue) and Lesson 81 (anempathetic sound).
  • How to analyse: (1) Close your eyes for one minute and list every sound layer you hear. (2) Name each layer: natural (wind, water), mechanical (engines, fans), human (crowds, voices), and ask what each says about the place. (3) Check the mix: which layer dominates, and does it change when the character's mood changes? (4) Check perspective: does the sound match what the camera shows, or does it place us inside the character's head? (5) Look for transformations: does one sound become another (a "sound match"), the way a visual match cut works? (6) Ask what the sound tells us that the picture does not.
  • Worked example — Apocalypse Now (1979, dir. Francis Ford Coppola; sound design by Walter Murch), the opening: Over a jungle shot, the thudding of helicopter blades is heard before we see anything. The sound then merges with the whirr of a ceiling fan in a Saigon hotel room, where Captain Willard lies on a bed, and The Doors' "The End" plays. (a) Layers: the helicopter is a memory layer, the fan is the present, and the two are mixed so we can't tell them apart. (b) Perspective: the sound comes from inside Willard's head, not from the room we see. (c) Transformation: blades become fan, so the war follows him into the room, and the film tells us he cannot leave it. (d) Why it works: the soundscape tells us his psychological state before any dialogue, and it sets the film's rule that sound will often show us what the mind hears rather than what the eye sees.
  • Practice: Pick any scene and watch it with your eyes closed. Write down the layers you hear, then rewatch with the picture and note any sound that is not explained by what is on screen.
  • Note: Sections 6-8 are from general knowledge and were not verified against live sources today; sound-design credits and details are as commonly reported.

4) 道德經 — 第 15 章:古之善為士者(Gǔ zhī shàn wéi shì zhě)

  • 背景:易經與莊子已全部用完,本單元依序介紹道德經。昨天是第 14 章。
  • 原文(王弼本):古之善為士者,微妙玄通,深不可識。夫唯不可識,故強為之容:豫兮若冬涉川;猶兮若畏四鄰;儼兮其若客;渙兮若冰之將釋;敦兮其若樸;曠兮其若谷;混兮其若濁。孰能濁以靜之徐清?孰能安以久動之徐生?保此道者不欲盈。夫唯不盈,故能蔽不新成。
  • 大意:古代善於行道的人,微妙、玄遠、通達,深得讓人無法完全理解。正因為無法理解,只能勉強描述他的樣子:謹慎得像冬天涉過河川;戒備得像害怕四鄰;莊重得像做客的人;渙散(隨和)得像將要融化的冰;敦厚得像未雕的原木;空曠得像山谷;混同得像渾濁的水。誰能讓渾水靜下來慢慢變清?誰能讓安靜的事物慢慢動起來而生出生機?持守這個道的人不想盈滿。正因為不盈滿,所以能夠破舊而更新。(最後一句各本不同:王弼本作「蔽不新成」,有些版本作「蔽而新成」,後者在現代注解中較常見。)
  • 與前面章節的關係:第 14 章說道超越感官;第 15 章則描寫依循道的人是什麼樣子:一連七個關於謹慎、開放與耐心的形象。「不欲盈」呼應第 11 章「空」的用處。(以上為依一般常識的詮釋;各版本文字略有差異。)

  • 歷史:基多地區在 15 世紀末被印加帝國納入;西班牙征服者在 1534 年建立基多,成為基多皇家檢審庭。1822 年 5 月 24 日皮欽查戰役(安東尼奧·何塞·德蘇克雷)後加入大哥倫比亞,1830 年成為獨立共和國。與秘魯長期的邊界爭端導致 1941 年戰爭、1942 年里約議定書,以及 1998 年最終和平條約。2000 年代的政治由拉斐爾·科雷亞(2007-2017)主導。2023 年起,國家面臨與有組織犯罪和毒品販運相關的嚴重治安危機。
  • 現任領導人:總統丹尼爾·諾沃亞(Daniel Noboa;2023 年 11 月在提前大選後就任,2025 年 4 月決選贏得完整任期,於 2025 年 5 月 24 日就職,任期至 2029 年)。2026 年 9 月的報導仍稱他為總統。依搜尋摘要,選民在 2025 年 11 月 16 日的公投中否決了他提出的全部四項提案。我沒有找到 2026 年 11 月的報導,所以近幾週是否有領導層變動未經查證。
  • 趣聞:離岸約 1,000 公里的加拉巴哥群島啟發了達爾文的演化思想,並列為聯合國教科文組織世界遺產;基多附近的「世界中心」紀念碑標示赤道位置;厄瓜多是第一個在憲法中賦予自然權利的國家(2008 年)。
  • 來源/附註:人口與 GDP 來自以 IMF 和聯合國為基礎的搜尋摘要(StatisticsTimes、Worldometers);領導人來自一般搜尋(La República)。其餘為一般常識;宗教比例與歷史細節為約略值。

6) 網路迷因 — 星期三之舞(Wednesday Addams,2022 年 11 月)

  • 名稱:「The Wednesday Dance」(星期三之舞,也稱「星期三·亞當斯之舞」或「Goo Goo Muck 舞」)。
  • 起源:在 Netflix 影集《星期三》(2022 年 11 月 23 日首播;由阿爾弗雷德·高夫與邁爾斯·米勒創作,提姆·波頓執導部分集數)中,珍娜·奧爾蒂加飾演的星期三·亞當斯在第 4 集學校的 Rave'N 舞會上跳了一段僵硬、抽動的獨舞。奧爾蒂加表示舞蹈是她自己編的,取材自 1980 年代哥德與後龐克舞蹈(包括 Siouxsie Sioux 與 The Cramps 的 Lux Interior)以及她自己的動作,而不是別人給她的編舞。場景中的歌曲是 The Cramps 的〈Goo Goo Muck〉(1981)。
  • 如何流行:幾天內,TikTok 上就有人重現這段舞,隨後加速版的 Lady Gaga〈Bloody Mary〉搭配成為爆紅的配樂,讓 Gaga 2011 年的歌曲重新登上榜單。粉絲、名人與品牌都推出了重現版本,影集本身也成為 Netflix 收看量最高的英語影集之一。
  • 意義與使用情境:象徵冷面的哥德式尷尬,以及擁抱怪異、不合拍風格的態度;大家把它當作萬聖節造型或趨勢、「我的表情就是……」的反應,以及翻跳影片。它傳達出陰鬱、冷幽默與毫不掩飾的怪異。
  • 變體與演變:「Bloody Mary」版本、其他情境喜劇與兒童節目的惡搞、對嘴與角色扮演版本,以及在台灣與亞洲各地萬聖節期間的重現。
  • 目前狀況:已過了 2022-2023 年的高峰,但每年萬聖節與影集每一季新推出時都會回潮。(日期與說法來自一般常識,今日未查證即時來源。)

7) 電影/表演藝術 — 《雨月物語》(Ugetsu,1953,導演溝口健二)

  • 名稱與創作者:《雨月物語》(Ugetsu / Ugetsu Monogatari,「月光與雨的故事」),大映出品;導演溝口健二;劇本依田義賢與川口松太郎;攝影宮川一夫。演員包括森雅之(源十郎)、京町子(若狹夫人)、田中絹代(宮木)與小澤榮(藤兵衛)。
  • 時代背景:1953 年拍攝,當時日本仍在戰後復原,故事設定在 16 世紀末的戰亂年代。影片改編自上田秋成 1776 年短篇集《雨月物語》中的兩個鬼故事,並借用了莫泊桑一篇短篇小說的情節。它獲得 1953 年威尼斯影展銀獅獎,幫助西方觀眾打開對日本電影的眼界。
  • 劇情概述:兩對農民夫妻住在湖邊。陶工源十郎想賣陶器賺錢,妹夫藤兵衛夢想成為武士。戰亂逼近時,兩人都拋下妻兒離開。源十郎被貴族若狹夫人迷惑,她其實是鬼魂;藤兵衛則花錢買到從軍的機會,成了空洞的英雄。源十郎最後回到家,妻子宮木似乎歡迎他,但隔天早上他才知道她在他離開期間就已去世。影片以源十郎的兒子在墳前、藤兵衛回到妻子身邊作結。
  • 風格與技法:溝口著名的長鏡頭與緩慢、如畫卷般展開的攝影機運動;琵琶湖霧中的行船場景;攝影機不經剪接就從生者的世界移動到鬼魂的世界;宮川一夫如水墨畫般細緻的打光;以及融合能樂式鼓與笛的日本傳統配樂。
  • 意義與影響:經常被列為日本電影的傑作,也是《Sight & Sound》等影評人榜單的常客;它展示了鬼故事如何承載對戰爭、貪婪與野心及其對女性代價的人道批判。其流動的鏡頭與空靈氛圍影響了日本與海外後來的導演。
  • 在哪裡體驗:Criterion Collection 已推出修復版;也會出現在重映戲院的回顧展中。請查詢你所在地區目前的串流資訊。(來自一般常識;今日未查證即時來源。)

8) 看片方法 — 第 98 課:環境音景與聲音透視

  • 是什麼:除了音樂與對白,每個場景都有一層音景:環境聲(風、車流、機器、昆蟲、房間的嗡嗡聲)的層次,告訴我們身處何處與感受如何。聲音設計師決定哪些層次大聲、小聲或消失,以及「麥克風」相對於角色的位置(貼近而親密,或遙遠而疏離),這就是聲音透視。本課不同於第 67 課(靜默/無配樂)、第 73 課(重疊對白)與第 81 課(反共情音效)。
  • 如何分析:(1) 閉上眼睛一分鐘,列出你聽到的每一層聲音。(2) 為每一層命名:自然的(風、水)、機械的(引擎、風扇)、人聲的(人群、說話聲),並問它對這個地方說了什麼。(3) 檢查混音:哪一層最主導?角色心情改變時它是否也改變?(4) 檢查透視:聲音與鏡頭所見是否吻合,還是把我們放進角色的腦中?(5) 找轉化:一種聲音是否變成另一種(「聲音匹配」),就像視覺的匹配剪接?(6) 問聲音告訴了我們什麼是畫面沒有告訴我們的。
  • 實例分析 —《現代啟示錄》(Apocalypse Now,1979,導演法蘭西斯·福特·柯波拉;聲音設計華特·默奇),開場:在叢林畫面上,我們還沒看到任何東西就先聽見直升機旋翼的撞擊聲。隨後聲音與西貢旅館房間裡吊扇的轉動聲融合,威拉德上尉躺在床上,門戶合唱團的〈The End〉響起。(a) 層次:直升機是記憶層,吊扇是現在,兩者混在一起讓我們無法分辨。(b) 透視:聲音來自威拉德的腦海,而不是我們看到的房間。(c) 轉化:旋翼變成風扇,戰爭跟著他進入房間,影片告訴我們他無法離開。(d) 為什麼有效:音景在任何對白之前就告訴我們他的心理狀態,並確立了影片的規則:聲音往往呈現心靈聽到的而不是眼睛看到的。
  • 練習:挑任何一個場景,閉眼聽一遍。寫下你聽到的層次,然後再看畫面重看一次,記下任何畫面上沒有解釋的聲音。
  • 附註:第 6-8 節來自一般常識,今日未查證即時來源;聲音設計的人員與細節為一般流傳的說法。
