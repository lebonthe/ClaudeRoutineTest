3) Spanish Lesson — Day 17 (10 min, builds on Day 16)

Day 16 taught the regular simple past (pretérito indefinido) for -ar/-er/-ir verbs. Today tackles the two most common irregular preterite verbs you'll need immediately: ir (to go) and ser (to be) — which share the exact same irregular conjugation — plus hacer (to do/make).

  • ¿Adónde fuiste ayer? (ah-DOHN-deh FWEES-teh ah-YEHR) — Where did you go yesterday?
  • Fui al cine con mi hermano. Fue una peli buenísima. (fwee ahl SEE-neh kohn mee ehr-MAH-noh. fweh OO-nah PEH-lee bweh-NEE-see-mah) — I went to the movies with my brother. It was a really good film.
  • ¿Qué hiciste tú anoche? (keh ee-SEES-teh too ah-NOH-cheh) — What did you do last night?
  • Hice la cena para toda mi familia. (EE-seh lah SEH-nah PAH-rah TOH-dah mee fah-MEE-lyah) — I made dinner for my whole family.

Grammar note: ir and ser are completely irregular in the preterite and, unusually, share one identical conjugation (fui/fuiste/fue/fuimos/fueron) — context alone tells you which verb is meant (fue una peli = "it was," from ser; fui al cine = "I went," from ir). Hacer has an irregular stem (hic-) with its own quirk: the third-person singular is hizo, not "hico" — the spelling changes c→z before o purely to preserve the soft "s" sound, a common Spanish spelling rule, not a new sound.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 17: 隨 (Suí) — "Following" (Lake over Thunder). The classical Xugua (Sequence) commentary explains this hexagram's position directly: "豫必有隨" — where there is genuine enthusiasm (Hexagram 16, 豫), others will naturally follow, hence "Following" comes next. The trigram image is thunder hidden within a lake — movement stirring quietly beneath stillness, or, read another way, the older/stronger trigram (thunder) yielding its place to move in step with the younger/gentler one (lake), modeling the correct relationship between a leader and those who follow. The Judgment reads: "FOLLOWING has supreme success. Perseverance furthers. No blame." The Image adds: "Thunder in the middle of the lake: the image of FOLLOWING. Thus the superior man at nightfall goes indoors for rest and recuperation." Classical commentary reads this as counsel on the art of following well and being worth following: true following requires adapting sincerely to the time and to legitimate authority — not blind submission, and not stubborn independence — while the Image's instruction to rest at nightfall suggests that even a leader who inspires following must know when to yield the stage and allow natural rhythms (of rest, of season, of authority) to take their course.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Indonesia

  • Location: Southeast Asia's largest nation and the world's largest archipelagic state, spanning roughly 17,000 islands straddling the equator between the Indian and Pacific Oceans. It shares land borders with Malaysia (on Borneo), Papua New Guinea (on New Guinea), and Timor-Leste (on Timor island), with maritime neighbors including Singapore, the Philippines, and Australia.
  • Territory: About 1.905 million km² of land — roughly 53 times the size of Taiwan (~36,200 km²) and the world's 14th-largest country by land area, comparable in size to Mexico; including its vast archipelagic waters, its total sovereign territory is considerably larger still. Its capital is being relocated from the overcrowded, sinking megacity of Jakarta (on Java) to a purpose-built new capital, Nusantara, in East Kalimantan on Borneo.
  • Population: Roughly 287 million (2026 estimate) — the world's 4th most populous country, and home to the largest Muslim population of any nation on Earth.
  • Religion: The state officially recognizes six religions. Islam is the majority faith at roughly 87% of the population (the largest national Muslim population in the world); Christianity accounts for about 10% (split between Protestant and Catholic); Hinduism remains dominant on the island of Bali specifically; smaller Buddhist and Confucian communities also exist, concentrated among ethnic-Chinese Indonesians.
  • Economy: Southeast Asia's largest economy and a G20 member, with 2026 nominal GDP estimated around US$1.54 trillion (roughly 17th-largest in the world) and growth targeted above 5%. Indonesia is the world's dominant nickel producer — about 2.6 million of the world's 3.9 million tonnes in 2025 — a resource central to EV-battery supply chains; mining contributes close to 9% of GDP and 17% of exports, and the Prabowo government has pushed to centralize state control over the sector and mandate domestic ore processing rather than raw exports. Palm oil and coal are its other major export commodities.
  • History: Home to ancient Hindu-Buddhist kingdoms (notably Srivijaya and Majapahit) before Islam spread widely from the 13th century onward via maritime trade. Dutch colonization as the "Dutch East Indies" lasted roughly 350 years from the early 1600s, interrupted by Japanese occupation during World War II. Sukarno declared independence on August 17, 1945; a subsequent war against Dutch reoccupation ended with the Netherlands recognizing Indonesian sovereignty in 1949. General Suharto's authoritarian "New Order" ruled from 1966 until the 1998 Reformasi movement, triggered partly by the Asian Financial Crisis, ushered in Indonesia's current democratic era.
  • Current leader: President Prabowo Subianto, sworn in October 2024 succeeding Joko Widodo, elected on a populist platform emphasizing faster economic growth and expanded social spending (including a major free-meal program), while continuing his predecessor's nickel-downstreaming and new-capital projects.
  • Language: Bahasa Indonesia, a standardized form of Malay, is the official language and unifying lingua franca across a nation with more than 700 distinct regional languages and ethnic groups; Javanese, spoken by the largest single ethnic group, is the most widely spoken regional/home language even though it is not the national official language.
    Sources: fulcrum.sg, geofactbook.com, en.wikipedia.org

  • How it became popular: In May 2019, Twitter user @MISSINGEGIRL combined the two images into the now-iconic side-by-side format.   • Meaning/usage: The format depicts a lopsided argument: an accusing, emotionally overwrought figure (the "yelling woman" panel) confronted by a second party who reacts with total, comic indifference or confusion (the "cat at the dinner table" panel). It's typically captioned to stage an absurd, one-sided dispute between two unrelated ideas or forces.
  • Variations/evolution: Countless recaptioned versions substitute different characters, brands, or concepts for either panel while preserving the "accuser vs. oblivious responder" structure. The meme also carries a more serious footnote: Taylor Armstrong's original outburst stemmed from real domestic abuse she experienced during that period of her life, which she has since spoken about publicly, noting she has made peace with her past.
  • Current status: Now regarded as a defining, slightly dated meme template of 2019 — still widely recognized and occasionally reused (often self-consciously, as a "throwback" format), rather than in active daily circulation the way it was at its 2019 peak.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Kabuki (歌舞伎)

  • Name: Kabuki (歌舞伎), classical Japanese theatre combining music, dance, and highly stylized drama.
  • Key figures/creators: Traditionally credited to Izumo no Okuni, a female shrine attendant (miko) associated with Izumo Taisha, who began performing an innovative, often gender-bending dance-drama style on the dry riverbeds of Kyoto starting in 1603 — reportedly appearing in male attire, which was part of what made her performances so novel.
  • Era/background: Originated at the very start of the Edo period (1603). Okuni's troupes of female performers were hugely popular but were banned by the Tokugawa shogunate in 1629 over morality concerns (the performances, and the performers themselves, were closely associated with prostitution); this forced a transition first to young male performers (wakashu-kabuki), also later restricted, and finally to the all-adult-male performance tradition (yaro-kabuki) that has defined Kabuki ever since, with female roles (onnagata) performed by specially trained male actors.
  • Content overview: The repertoire spans historical/period dramas (jidaimono, often depicting samurai-era conflicts and legendary events), domestic dramas of ordinary townspeople (sewamono, frequently tragic love stories), and dance-based pieces (shosagoto) — many plots centering on themes of loyalty, revenge, duty versus personal feeling (giri-ninjō), and doomed love.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Marked by bold, exaggerated stage makeup (kumadori) using colored lines to signal a character's nature (red for righteous passion or heroism, blue/black for villainy or malevolence, for example); a rotating stage (mawari-butai) and a runway (hanamichi) extending through the audience for dramatic entrances and exits; stylized vocal delivery and dramatic frozen poses (mie) held at emotional climaxes to punctuate key moments; and a distinctive triangular wooden-clapper (tsuke) sound effect emphasizing dramatic movement.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Developed as a popular, accessible theatre form for Edo-period commoners in contrast to the more austere, aristocratic Noh theatre, Kabuki became a central pillar of Edo urban culture and a major influence on woodblock print art (many ukiyo-e prints depict famous Kabuki actors and scenes). It was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2005 (proclaimed) / formally inscribed 2008.
  • Influence and legacy: Kabuki acting families (such as the Ichikawa and Nakamura lines) have passed down signature roles and performance styles across many generations via a hereditary system of stage names, shaping Japanese performing arts broadly and influencing later Japanese cinema's sense of stylized gesture and dramatic timing.
  • Current status: Actively performed today, principally at the Kabuki-za theatre in Tokyo's Ginza district (capacity around 1,600) and the National Theatre of Japan, with regular programs aimed at both devoted fans and first-time/tourist audiences (including English-language earphone guides), keeping Kabuki a living, continuously performed tradition rather than a museum piece.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, newworldencyclopedia.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Cinematography & Lighting (Lesson 3)

Lesson 1 covered mise-en-scène (what's arranged within the frame) and Lesson 2 covered editing (how meaning is built between frames). Today's lesson looks at the layer that shapes how each individual frame actually looks and feels: cinematography and lighting — the choices a director of photography makes about light, color, lens, and camera movement.

The method: Cinematography is not neutral documentation of a scene — it's an argument made through light. To analyze it, ask: What is the quality and direction of the light? Hard light (sharp shadows, high contrast) tends to read as harsh, dangerous, or exposing; soft, diffused light reads as gentle, intimate, or nostalgic. Light from below a face ("underlighting") traditionally signals menace; light from a single motivated source within the story world (a lamp, a window, a fire) grounds a scene's mood in something the characters themselves could plausibly see. What is the color palette doing? A director of photography rarely lights "neutral" — a dominant color temperature (warm amber vs. cold blue, for instance) functions like a mood-setting filter over the entire scene, and shifts in that palette across a film usually track shifts in emotional or narrative state. How does the camera move, or choose not to? A static, locked-off frame implies control, stillness, or entrapment; a handheld or searching camera implies chaos, urgency, or subjective uncertainty. Cinematography's job is to make you feel a scene's emotional temperature before you've consciously registered a single plot detail.

Worked example — Blade Runner 2049 (dir. Denis Villeneuve, 2017; cinematography by Roger Deakins), the irradiated Las Vegas sequence: When K arrives in the abandoned, dust-choked ruins of Las Vegas searching for Deckard, Deakins bathes the entire sequence in a thick, all-consuming orange-red haze — achieved largely in-camera using a custom Tiffen filter pack combining Lee lighting gels (including "Moroccan Pink," "Orange," and two shades of amber). There is effectively no neutral light anywhere in this sequence; every surface, every shadow, is tinted the same sickly amber, evoking both the dust-storm photography that visually inspired the look and the color's traditional association with danger, decay, and uncertainty. Giant crumbling gold statues loom out of the haze as half-glimpsed silhouettes rather than crisply lit figures, because Deakins deliberately keeps detail suppressed within the orange fog — withholding visual clarity to match the disorientation K himself feels entering this dead city.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Total color saturation as a mood-lock — when literally every element on screen shares one color cast, the palette itself becomes the scene's emotional register (here: decayed, poisoned, uncertain), not just a background detail. (2) Withheld clarity as a deliberate choice — Deakins could have lit the statues to be crisply visible; keeping them as haze-shrouded silhouettes is a lighting decision that manufactures disorientation to match the character's subjective experience. (3) Light with an in-world justification — the amber tone reads as "environmental" (dust, decay, irradiation) rather than an artificial mood filter slapped on top, which is what makes it feel diegetically earned rather than stylistically arbitrary. This builds on Lessons 1 and 2: mise-en-scène is what's arranged in the frame, editing is how frames are sequenced, and cinematography/lighting is what those frames are made to look and feel like before any cut or arrangement even registers.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第十七天(10 分鐘,銜接第十六天內容)

第十六天學了規則 -ar/-er/-ir 動詞的簡單過去式。今天要挑戰兩個你會立刻用到的最常見不規則過去式動詞:ir(去)與 ser(是)——這兩個動詞在簡單過去式裡完全共用同一套變化——外加 hacer(做/製作)。

  • ¿Adónde fuiste ayer?(阿東貼 夫伊斯貼 阿伊誒爾)—— 你昨天去了哪裡?
  • Fui al cine con mi hermano. Fue una peli buenísima.(夫伊 阿爾 西內 昆 米 誒兒馬諾。夫誒 烏娜 貝里 布誒尼西馬)—— 我跟我哥哥去看了電影。超好看的。
  • ¿Qué hiciste tú anoche?(給 伊西斯貼 圖 阿諾切)—— 你昨晚做了什麼?
  • Hice la cena para toda mi familia.(伊誒 拉 塞納 帕拉 托達 米 法米莉亞)—— 我幫全家人做了晚餐。

文法重點:ir 跟 ser 在簡單過去式裡完全不規則,而且很特別地共用同一套變化(fui/fuiste/fue/fuimos/fueron)——要靠上下文判斷究竟是哪個動詞(fue una peli 是「是」,來自 ser;fui al cine 是「去了」,來自 ir)。hacer 則有不規則字根(hic-),還有個小陷阱:第三人稱單數是 hizo,不是「hico」——c 在 o 前面改成 z,純粹是為了保留原本的「s」音,是西班牙文常見的拼字規則,不是新的發音。

4) 易經卦象

第十七卦:隨卦(Suí)——「隨」(上兌下震,澤中有雷)。〈序卦傳〉直接說明了這一卦的排序邏輯:「豫必有隨」——當上位者展現出真誠的熱忱(第十六卦「豫」)時,眾人自然會樂於追隨,因此接下來便是「隨」卦。卦象描繪雷藏於澤中——寧靜表象下暗自蘊藏著律動;另一種解讀是,原本剛健的雷(震)甘願退讓位置,配合較為柔順的澤(兌)一同行動,示範了領導者與追隨者之間應有的正確關係。卦辭寫道:「隨:元亨,利貞,無咎。」〈象傳〉補充道:「澤中有雷,隨;君子以嚮晦入宴息。」古代注疏將此卦解讀為一堂關於「如何善於追隨、也值得被追隨」的功課:真正的「隨」需要真誠地順應時勢與正當的權威——既不是盲從,也不是頑固地一意孤行;而〈象傳〉「入夜便回房休息」的提示則說明,即便是能感召眾人追隨的領導者,也必須懂得適時退場,讓休息、時節、權威等自然的節奏各安其位。

(第十七次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 印尼(Indonesia)

  • 位置: 東南亞面積最大的國家,也是全世界最大的群島國家,橫跨赤道、由約 17,000 座島嶼組成,位於印度洋與太平洋之間。陸地上與馬來西亞(在婆羅洲)、巴布亞紐幾內亞(在新幾內亞島)、東帝汶(在帝汶島)接壤,海上鄰國則包括新加坡、菲律賓與澳洲。
  • 領土面積: 陸地面積約 190.5 萬平方公里——大約是台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 53 倍,是全球陸地面積第 14 大的國家,大小與墨西哥相近;若計入其廣大的群島海域,主權領土範圍則更為龐大。首都目前正從人口過度密集、地層持續下陷的爪哇島大城雅加達,遷往婆羅洲東加里曼丹省一座全新規劃的首都「努山塔拉」(Nusantara)。
  • 人口: 約 2.87 億人(2026 年估計)——世界第四人口大國,也是全球穆斯林人口最多的國家。
  • 宗教: 官方承認六種宗教。伊斯蘭教為多數信仰,約占人口 87%(是全球穆斯林人口最多的國家);基督宗教約占 10%(新教與天主教皆有);印度教則在峇里島特別居於主導地位;另外還有規模較小、主要集中於印尼華人社群中的佛教與儒教信仰者。
  • 經濟: 東南亞最大經濟體,同時是 G20 成員國,2026 年名目 GDP 估計約 1.54 兆美元(全球排名約第 17 位),經濟成長目標設定在 5% 以上。印尼是全球最大的鎳生產國——2025 年全球 390 萬噸鎳產量中,印尼便占了約 260 萬噸——鎳是電動車電池供應鏈的關鍵資源;礦業貢獻了將近 9% 的 GDP 與 17% 的出口值,普拉伯沃政府也持續推動將礦業收歸國家集中管控,並要求礦石須在國內加工後才能出口,而非直接出口原礦。棕櫚油與煤炭則是另外兩項主要出口商品。
  • 歷史: 曾孕育出古老的印度教-佛教王國(尤以室利佛逝與滿者伯夷最為著名),伊斯蘭教自 13 世紀起透過海上貿易廣泛傳播。荷蘭以「荷屬東印度」之名殖民統治約 350 年(始於 17 世紀初),其間曾因二次大戰遭日本占領而中斷。蘇卡諾於 1945 年 8 月 17 日宣布獨立;隨後與企圖重返殖民統治的荷蘭爆發戰爭,直到 1949 年荷蘭正式承認印尼主權為止。蘇哈托將軍主導的威權「新秩序」政權統治印尼自 1966 年起,直到 1998 年、部分肇因於亞洲金融風暴所引發的「改革」(Reformasi)運動,才開啟了印尼現今的民主時代。
  • 現任領導人: 總統普拉伯沃·蘇比安托(Prabowo Subianto),於 2024 年 10 月就職,接替佐科威(Joko Widodo);他以強調加速經濟成長、擴大社會福利支出(包括大規模的免費營養餐計畫)的民粹路線當選,同時延續前任政府的鎳礦下游化與新首都建設計畫。
  • 語言: 印尼語(Bahasa Indonesia,標準化的馬來語形式)是官方語言,也是這個擁有超過 700 種地方語言與族群的國家的共同通用語;爪哇語是人口最多的單一族群所使用的語言,雖非國家官方語言,卻是使用人數最多的地方/母語。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Woman Yelling at a Cat(對貓咆哮的女人)

  • 名稱: Woman Yelling at a Cat(對貓咆哮的女人),又稱 Confused Cat at Dinner(晚餐桌上一臉困惑的貓)。
  • 起源/歷史: 這是一張結合兩個完全不相干來源的雙格迷因圖。左格取自 2011 年美國實境節目《比佛利嬌妻》(The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills)其中一集「Malibu Beach Party from Hell」的畫面,顯示泰勒·阿姆斯壯(Taylor Armstrong)一邊哭泣、一邊指著對方,被凱爾·理查茲(Kyle Richards)攔住的畫面。右格則是一隻名叫 Smudge 的白貓照片,牠早已因為在 Tumblr、Instagram 上一副「萬年臭臉、看誰都不順眼」的表情而小有名氣——這張照片正是牠坐在一盤自己完全沒興趣吃的沙拉前面的畫面。
  • 如何爆紅: 2019 年 5 月,Twitter 用戶 @MISSINGEGIRL 將這兩張圖片並排組合,成為如今經典的雙格迷因格式。這個迷因在 2019 年 6 月中旬,透過 Twitter 與 Facebook 迅速擴散,很快突破次文化圈層,成為當年度被使用最廣泛的反應圖格式之一。
  • 意義與使用情境: 這個格式呈現的是一場「一面倒」的爭吵:一方情緒激動、指責對方(「咆哮的女人」畫面),另一方則以徹底、滑稽的無動於衷或困惑回應(「餐桌上的貓」畫面)。通常用來配上文字,呈現兩個原本毫不相干的概念之間,一場荒謬的「單方面爭執」。
  • 變體與演變: 出現過無數重新配文的版本,將雙格中的角色替換成不同人物、品牌或概念,但始終保留「指控者 vs. 無動於衷的回應者」這個基本結構。這個迷因也帶有較為沉重的背景註記:泰勒·阿姆斯壯當年畫面中的情緒爆發,源自她當時實際遭受的家庭暴力經歷,她後來公開談論此事,表示自己已經與那段過去和解。
  • 目前現況: 如今被視為 2019 年具代表性、但已稍嫌「過時」的經典迷因範本——仍廣為人知,偶爾會被重新拿出來使用(通常帶點「懷舊致敬」的自覺意味),但已不像 2019 年顛峰時期那樣天天被大量使用。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 歌舞伎(Kabuki)

  • 名稱: 歌舞伎(Kabuki),結合音樂、舞蹈與高度程式化戲劇表演的日本古典戲劇形式。
  • 關鍵人物: 傳統上將歌舞伎的創始歸功於出雲阿國(Izumo no Okuni),她是一位與出雲大社淵源深厚的女性巫女(みこ),自 1603 年起在京都乾涸的河床上,展開一種創新、常帶有性別扮演意味的舞蹈戲劇表演——據傳她會身著男裝演出,這正是她表演形式之所以新穎獨特的原因之一。
  • 時代背景: 誕生於江戶時代之初(1603 年)。阿國所率領的女性劇團極受歡迎,卻在 1629 年因風化問題(這些表演與表演者本身,當時與賣淫行業關係密切)遭德川幕府下令禁演;此後表演形式先轉為由年輕男性演出(若眾歌舞伎),隨後同樣遭到限制,最終演變為延續至今、全由成年男性演出的傳統(野郎歌舞伎),女性角色(女形)則由經過專門訓練的男演員擔綱。
  • 內容概述: 劇目涵蓋歷史時代劇(時代物,常描繪武士時代的衝突與傳說事蹟)、描寫市井小民生活的世話物(常為悲劇性的愛情故事),以及以舞蹈為主的所作事(舞蹈劇目)——許多劇情圍繞忠義、復仇、義理與人情之間的衝突,以及注定破滅的愛情等主題展開。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 以誇張大膽的舞台化妝「隈取」(くまどり)著稱,以不同顏色線條代表角色性格(例如紅色代表正義凜然或英雄氣概,藍色/黑色則代表奸邪或惡意);設有可旋轉的舞台「迴り舞台」,以及延伸穿越觀眾席、供角色戲劇性登場退場的花道(はなみち);表演方式高度程式化的唸白唱腔,以及在情緒最高潮時刻定格擺出誇張姿勢的「見得」(みえ),用以強調關鍵時刻;還有以特殊三角形木製拍子板「附け」(つけ)敲擊、用來強化戲劇性動作的獨特音效。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 歌舞伎的發展,是為了讓江戶時代的平民百姓也能親近戲劇,相對於較為肅穆、貴族氣息濃厚的能劇,歌舞伎因而成為江戶都市文化的重要支柱,同時也深刻影響了浮世繪版畫藝術(許多浮世繪作品皆以知名歌舞伎演員與經典場景為題材)。2005 年獲聯合國教科文組織宣告、2008 年正式列入「人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄」。
  • 影響與傳承: 市川家、中村家等著名歌舞伎世家,透過代代相傳的「襲名」制度,將招牌角色與表演風格延續數個世代之久,深刻形塑了日本表演藝術的整體樣貌,也影響了後來日本電影中程式化肢體動作與戲劇性節奏的美學表現。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 至今仍持續公演,主要場地為東京銀座的歌舞伎座(座位數約 1,600 席)與日本國立劇場,並固定安排針對資深戲迷與初次接觸的觀光客(包含提供英語耳機導覽)的節目,使歌舞伎維持為一項活生生、持續演出的傳統,而非博物館裡的靜態展品。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、newworldencyclopedia.org

8) 看片方法 — 攝影與燈光 Cinematography & Lighting(第三課)

第一課教了場面調度(畫面之內安排了什麼),第二課教了剪輯(畫面與畫面之間如何建構意義)。今天要看的是決定「每一格畫面本身看起來、感覺起來是什麼樣子」的那一層:攝影與燈光——攝影指導對光線、色彩、鏡頭與運鏡所做的每一個選擇。

方法說明: 攝影從來不是中立地記錄一個場景——而是一種透過光線表達的論述。分析時可以問:光線的性質與方向是什麼? 硬光(陰影銳利、對比強烈)通常讓人感受到嚴酷、危險或無所遁形;柔和、漫射的光則給人溫柔、親密或懷舊的感覺。從臉部下方打光(「底光」)在傳統上暗示著威脅感;若光源是劇情世界裡角色本身也看得見的「有動機光源」(一盞燈、一扇窗、一團火),則能讓場景氛圍更扎根於角色所身處的真實環境。色調在做什麼? 攝影指導幾乎不會選擇「中性」打光——一種主導性的色溫(例如暖橘色 vs. 冷藍色)往往就像罩在整個場景上的情緒濾鏡,而色調在片中的轉變,通常也對應著情緒或劇情狀態的轉折。攝影機如何運動、或選擇不運動? 固定不動的畫面暗示著掌控、靜止或受困;手持或游移搜尋的鏡頭則暗示混亂、急迫或主觀的不確定感。攝影的任務,就是在你意識到任何一個劇情細節之前,先讓你「感受」到這場戲的情緒溫度。

實例示範 ——《銀翼殺手 2049》(導演:丹尼·維勒納夫 Denis Villeneuve,2017;攝影:羅傑·狄金斯 Roger Deakins)拉斯維加斯輻射廢墟一幕: 當 K 進入被塵埃籠罩、荒廢的拉斯維加斯廢墟尋找戴克(Deckard)時,狄金斯讓整場戲籠罩在濃厚、揮之不去的橘紅色霧霾之中——這個效果主要透過相機前加裝一組特製 Tiffen 濾鏡(結合多款 Lee 燈光色片,包括「摩洛哥粉」、「橘色」與兩種不同深淺的琥珀色)在拍攝現場直接完成。這場戲裡幾乎找不到任何一處中性色調的光線——每一個表面、每一道陰影,全都被染上同一種病態的琥珀色,既呼應了啟發這個畫面風格的沙塵暴攝影作品,也運用了這個顏色在傳統上與危險、衰敗、不確定感的連結。巨大傾頹的金色雕像從霧霾中若隱若現,呈現半剪影狀態,而非被清晰打光的完整形體——因為狄金斯刻意在橘色霧霾中壓抑細節,刻意保留的視覺模糊感,正好對應 K 踏入這座死城時所感受到的迷失方向感。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)整體色彩飽和度即情緒鎖定——當畫面上幾乎每個元素都染上同一種色調時,這個色調本身就成了整場戲的情緒基調(在此處:衰敗、中毒、不確定),而不只是背景細節。(2)刻意保留的模糊感是一種選擇——狄金斯明明可以把雕像打得清晰可見;選擇讓它們維持在霧霾籠罩的剪影狀態,是一個刻意用來製造迷失感、呼應角色主觀體驗的燈光決定。(3)有劇情世界內在依據的光源——這種琥珀色調讀起來像是「環境本身」的顏色(塵埃、衰敗、輻射),而非硬套上去的風格化情緒濾鏡,這正是它之所以感覺「劇情內在真實」而非「風格上刻意為之」的原因。這堂課承接第一、二課:場面調度是畫面之內安排了什麼,剪輯是畫面如何被排列剪接,而攝影與燈光,則是在任何剪接或畫面安排被意識到之前,先決定了這些畫面「看起來、感覺起來」是什麼樣子。
