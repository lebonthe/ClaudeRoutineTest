3) Spanish Lesson — Day 43 (10 min, the absolute superlative)

Day 42 taught the relative superlative (el/la más...de, "the most ... of/in"), which ranks one item against a specific group. Today's lesson teaches the absolute superlative — a way to say something is "extremely" or "insanely" X with no comparison group at all, built with the suffix -ísimo/-ísima instead of the word muy ("very"). To form it, drop the adjective's final vowel and add -ísimo (masculine) or -ísima (feminine): rico → riquísimo, cansado → cansadísimo, fácil → facilísimo. Three adjective families require a spelling change before -ísimo to preserve the original consonant sound: -co/-ca → -quísimo (rico → riquísimo), -go/-ga → -guísimo (largo → larguísimo), and -z → -císimo (feliz → felicísimo).

  • Este pastel está riquísimo. — This cake is incredibly delicious.
  • Estoy cansadísimo después del viaje. — I'm absolutely exhausted after the trip.
  • La película fue larguísima. — The movie was extremely long.

Grammar note: -ísimo and muy + adjective both intensify without naming a comparison group, but they are not identical in register — muy cansado is neutral everyday intensification, while cansadísimo is more emphatic/colloquial, closer to English "insanely tired" than plain "very tired," and the two are not normally stacked together (avoid muy riquísimo). This is a different axis entirely from Day 42's relative superlative: el/la más...de ranks one member of a named group (la más inteligente de la clase), while -ísimo intensifies an adjective on its own with no group or comparison implied at all.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 43: 夬 (Guài) — "Breakthrough / Resoluteness" (Lake above, Heaven below — Duì over Qián). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary pivots directly off yesterday's Hexagram 42: "益而不已必決,故受之以夬;夬者,決也。" ("Increase that does not stop must eventually break through; hence it is followed by Guài [Breakthrough]. Guài means to resolutely decide/break away.") The hexagram's structure makes the teaching visible at a glance: five solid (yang) lines have risen from the bottom and now press against a single remaining broken (yin) line at the very top — the image of a single small, entrenched evil finally being resolutely displaced by an overwhelming, unified force of the good, but only barely, and only if handled correctly. The Judgment reads: "夬,揚于王庭,孚號有厲,告自邑,不利即戎,利有攸往。" ("Breakthrough: it must be announced publicly at the king's court; proclaim it with sincerity, yet danger remains; report first to one's own city, for it is not favorable to resort to arms directly — it is favorable to have somewhere to go.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "夬,決也,剛決柔也。健而說,決而和。揚于王庭,柔乘五剛也。孚號有厲,其危乃光也。告自邑,不利即戎,所尚乃窮也。利有攸往,剛長乃終也。" ("Guài means resolute decision — the firm decisively displacing the yielding. It is strong yet joyous, resolute yet harmonious. That it must be announced at the king's court is because the one yielding line still rides atop the five firm ones. That sincere proclamation still carries danger is precisely what makes the danger itself become manifest and clear. That reporting to one's own city is stressed while resorting to arms is discouraged shows that reliance on force alone would exhaust its own cause. That it is favorable to have somewhere to go means the growth of the firm lines is about to reach its completion.") The Image commentary draws the lesson for conduct: "澤上於天,夬;君子以施祿及下,居德則忌。" ("Lake risen above Heaven: this is the image of Breakthrough. Thus the superior person distributes benefits down to those below, yet is wary of resting complacently on virtue already accumulated.") Read against Hexagram 42's teaching that increase flows from the willingness of those above to give way to those below, Hexagram 43 supplies the necessary complement: even a nearly complete moral or political victory must still be carried out openly, sincerely, and without complacent reliance on sheer force, since the very last, smallest resistance is often the most dangerous to underestimate.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Djibouti (吉布地)

  • Location: Horn of Africa, on the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait at the southern entrance to the Red Sea; bordered by Eritrea to the north, Ethiopia to the west and south, and Somalia to the southeast, with a coastline on the Gulf of Aden and the Gulf of Tadjoura.
  • Territory: About 23,200 km² — roughly 0.64 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km², i.e. noticeably smaller), close in area to Wales (~20,779 km²) or the US state of New Jersey (~22,591 km²).
  • Population: Roughly 1.1 million (2026 estimate), heavily concentrated in the capital, Djibouti City.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Sunni Islam (around 94%), with a small Christian minority (mostly among foreign residents and a historic community tied to the colonial era).
  • Economy: Built almost entirely around its strategic location rather than natural resources: Djibouti's ports (operated in part through the Djibouti Ports and Free Zones Authority) serve as the primary maritime gateway for landlocked Ethiopia's trade, and the country earns substantial rent by hosting multiple foreign military bases at the Bab-el-Mandeb chokepoint, including France's largest African base, the US's Camp Lemonnier, and China's first-ever overseas military base, plus Japanese and Italian facilities. Despite this logistics and rent income, unemployment and poverty remain high and the economy depends heavily on continued foreign investment in port and rail infrastructure (including the Addis Ababa-Djibouti railway).
  • History: Colonized by France as French Somaliland from the late 19th century, later French Territory of the Afars and Issas; gained independence in 1977. Post-independence politics were shaped by tension between the two main ethnic groups, the Afar and the Issa Somali, culminating in a civil war from 1991 to 1994 that ended with a peace accord bringing Afar representation into government; the country has remained largely stable since.
  • Current leader: President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh, in power continuously since 1999 and re-elected multiple times after constitutional term-limit changes; his long tenure has drawn criticism over restricted political competition, even as it has coincided with the country's rise as a major logistics and basing hub.
  • Language: French and Arabic are the official languages (a legacy of the colonial period and Djibouti's Arab League membership), while Somali and Afar are the most widely spoken languages day-to-day among the two main ethnic communities.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov World Factbook

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Ugandan Knuckles ("Do You Know Da Wae?")

  • Name: "Ugandan Knuckles," also known by its catchphrase "Do you know da wae?"
  • Origin/history: Emerged in early 2018 within the social VR platform VRChat, built around a crude, low-poly fan redesign of Sonic the Hedgehog's character Knuckles, originally created for a "Metaverse" YouTube channel avatar contest. The character's exaggerated pidgin-English chanting was directly inspired by the cast of "Who Killed Captain Alex?", a 2010 Ugandan action film ("Wakaliwood") whose absurdist low-budget style and VJ narration had already gone viral online for its unintentional comedy.
  • How it became popular: VRChat users mass-adopted the Ugandan Knuckles avatar, roleplaying as tribal hordes that swarmed newcomers to the platform chanting "do you know da wae" and "spitting" on them as a running joke; clips and screen recordings spread rapidly to YouTube, Twitter, and Reddit through 2018, turning a niche VR in-joke into a mainstream meme almost overnight.
  • Meaning/usage: Used to imitate an exaggerated, broken-English tribal chant, typically deployed as absurdist pack-mentality humor — a crowd of identical avatars repeating the same nonsensical phrase at a bewildered target — applied to unrelated situations purely for its chaotic, swarming comic effect.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned numerous VRChat avatar variants (including crossovers with other characters), remix/parody songs built around the "da wae" chant, and extensive discussion across gaming media; it also became a widely cited case study of a meme whose exaggerated mock-African-accent humor drew sustained criticism as racially insensitive, prompting real moderation debates on VRChat and other platforms.
  • Current status: Effectively retired as an actively used meme; VRChat and related communities moved to restrict or informally discourage the avatar amid harassment complaints and the racism criticism noted above. It persists mainly as a reference point in retrospectives on 2018 internet culture and in discussions of how platform-native, avatar-based memes can spread and burn out unusually fast.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Argentine Tango (探戈)

  • Name/key figures: Tango, specifically Argentine Tango, as both a social dance and a musical genre. No single inventor; it crystallized among the working-class immigrant neighborhoods of Buenos Aires and Montevideo. Its two most influential later figures are Carlos Gardel (1890-1935), the genre-defining singer/composer often called "El Zorzal Criollo" whose recordings and films carried tango to global audiences, and Ástor Piazzolla (1921-1992), whose "Tango Nuevo" fused traditional tango with jazz harmony and classical composition techniques, radically expanding the form decades later.
  • Era/background: Emerged in the 1880s-1890s in the poorer port districts (arrabales) of Buenos Aires and Montevideo, in a melting pot of Spanish and Italian immigrant laborers, Afro-Argentine communities (whose candombe drumming rhythms fed into its early musical roots), and displaced gauchos. Originally performed in brothels and lower-class dance halls and considered disreputable by Argentina's own elite, it was unexpectedly embraced by Parisian high society around 1913 ("tangomanía"), and this European vogue then reflected back to make tango respectable within Argentina itself.
  • Plot/content overview: As a social dance, tango is danced in a close embrace between two partners, built around sharp, staccato steps, sudden pauses, and largely improvised, lead-and-follow movement rather than fixed choreography; as a musical genre, tango songs (letras) are overwhelmingly melancholic and nostalgic, dwelling on themes of lost love, betrayal, homesickness, and the immigrant's longing for a better life.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Music is traditionally played by an orquesta típica centered on the bandoneón (a German button-concertina instrument that became inseparable from tango's mournful sound), plus violin, piano, and double bass. Core dance vocabulary includes the caminata (the basic walk), the ocho (a figure-eight pivot), the gancho (a hooking leg movement), and the sacada (a displacing step); related, closely tied genres include the faster milonga and the vals criollo.
  • Cultural/historical significance: A foundational symbol of Argentine (and Uruguayan) national identity, so closely tied to Buenos Aires that the dance and the city are often treated as inseparable in the popular imagination; recognized jointly by Argentina and Uruguay in a successful 2009 UNESCO inscription on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
  • Influence and legacy: Shaped the codified "International Tango" ballroom-dance style that later diverged from the improvisational Argentine original; Gardel's international film and recording career made tango one of the first Latin American popular art forms with true global reach; Piazzolla's Tango Nuevo directly influenced jazz and contemporary classical composers and remains a staple of concert repertoire worldwide.
  • Current status/where to experience it: A living, actively practiced tradition rather than a historical artifact — danced nightly at milongas (social tango dance events) in Buenos Aires and in tango communities in cities worldwide, with Buenos Aires hosting a dedicated annual Tango Festival and World Cup (Mundial de Tango) that draws competitive and social dancers internationally.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, ich.unesco.org (UNESCO)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lens Choice & Focal Length: Wide-Angle vs. Telephoto Compression (Lesson 29)

Every shot is filmed through a lens of some specific focal length, and that choice shapes how the audience perceives space long before any editing or lighting decision comes into play. A wide-angle lens exaggerates the apparent distance between foreground and background — objects close to the lens loom disproportionately large while the background recedes and appears smaller and farther away, producing a sense of depth, vastness, or (used differently) claustrophobic distortion at close range. A telephoto lens does the opposite: it compresses space, flattening the visual distance between foreground and background so that objects at very different real-world distances appear crowded unnaturally close together on screen. This is a distinct analytical axis from Day 8's Depth of Field & Focus (which concerns what stays sharp versus blurred, not spatial compression) and from Day 3's Cinematography & Lighting (light and color, not lens geometry) and Day 15's Camera Movement (physical travel of the camera, not the optical property of a fixed lens). Three questions to ask: what focal length is evidently being used and how confidently can you tell from the image alone; is the resulting spatial distortion motivated by the scene's emotional or narrative content, or does it feel like an unmotivated technical choice; and does a shift in typical lens choice partway through a film mark a deliberate change in a character's psychological state or the story's stakes.

Worked example: The Graduate (1967, dir. Mike Nichols, cinematography by Robert Surtees) — the famous shot of Benjamin Braddock sprinting toward the camera down a street and later across a churchyard, shot on an extreme telephoto lens. The lens's severe compression flattens the sense of forward progress so completely that although Benjamin is visibly running at a full sprint, he appears barely to close any distance toward the frame at all — a purely optical effect (no trick editing, no slow-motion) that conveys his exhausting, seemingly futile race against time more directly than any line of dialogue could, and that remains one of the most frequently cited demonstrations of telephoto compression as a storytelling device in film-school curricula.

3)西班牙文課程——第 43 天(10 分鐘,絕對最高級)

第 42 天教的是「相對最高級」(el/la más...de,「在……之中最……的」),用來將某項事物與一個特定群體做排序比較。今天的課程要教的是「絕對最高級」——一種完全不需要比較群體、直接表達「極度」或「超級」怎樣怎樣的說法,做法是在形容詞字尾加上「-ísimo/-ísima」,而不是用 muy(很)這個字。變化方式是:去掉形容詞字尾的母音,加上 -ísimo(陽性)或 -ísima(陰性):rico(好吃)→ riquísimo(超級好吃)、cansado(累)→ cansadísimo(累翻了)、fácil(簡單)→ facilísimo(超簡單)。有三類形容詞在加 -ísimo 前需要調整拼寫,以維持原本子音的發音:字尾 -co/-ca → -quísimo(rico → riquísimo)、字尾 -go/-ga → -guísimo(largo → larguísimo)、字尾 -z → -císimo(feliz → felicísimo)。

  • Este pastel está riquísimo. —— 這個蛋糕超級好吃。
  • Estoy cansadísimo después del viaje. —— 這趟旅行後我累翻了。
  • La película fue larguísima. —— 這部電影超級長。

文法筆記:「-ísimo」和「muy + 形容詞」兩者都是在不指定比較群體的情況下加強語氣,但兩者的語感並不完全相同——muy cansado(很累)是中性、日常的加強說法,而 cansadísimo(累翻了)語氣更強烈、也更口語,比較接近中文「累爆了」而不只是「很累」,而且這兩種加強方式通常不會疊加使用(避免說 muy riquísimo)。這與第 42 天教的相對最高級完全是不同的文法軸線:el/la más...de 是在一個「有名字的群體」裡排序其中一員(la más inteligente de la clase,班上最聰明的),而「-ísimo」則是單純加強形容詞本身的語氣,完全不涉及群體或比較的概念。

4)易經卦象

第 43 卦:夬 (Guài) ——「決斷、突破」(澤在上,天在下——兌上乾下)。《序卦傳》從昨天的第 42 卦直接接續下來:「益而不已必決,故受之以夬;夬者,決也。」(增益若持續不止,終將導致決斷、突破,因此益卦之後接的是夬卦;「夬」就是決斷、果決割捨之意。)這一卦的卦象結構一眼就能看出其教誨:五條陽爻已從下方層層而起,如今直逼最上方僅存的最後一條陰爻——象徵一個微小卻根深柢固的邪惡,終於在強大而團結的善的力量面前,被果斷排除,但過程仍相當驚險,唯有處置得當方能成功。卦辭寫道:「夬,揚于王庭,孚號有厲,告自邑,不利即戎,利有攸往。」(夬卦:必須在君王的朝廷上公開宣告此事;以誠信呼籲眾人,但危險依然存在;應先向自己的封邑報告,不宜直接訴諸武力;適合有所前往。)《彖傳》進一步解釋:「夬,決也,剛決柔也。健而說,決而和。揚于王庭,柔乘五剛也。孚號有厲,其危乃光也。告自邑,不利即戎,所尚乃窮也。利有攸往,剛長乃終也。」(夬,就是決斷之意,是剛強果決地排除柔弱。它剛健而又帶著喜悅,果決而又能保持和諧。之所以要在朝廷上公開宣告,是因為那唯一的柔弱之爻,仍然凌駕於五條剛強之爻的最上方。誠信呼籲眾人卻仍存有危險,正是因為這樣的態度,才能讓潛藏的危險真正被看清、彰顯出來。強調應先報告自己的封邑、不宜直接訴諸武力,是因為若只依恃武力,終將使自己所崇尚的力量走向窮盡。適合有所前往,則是說明剛強之爻的成長,即將邁向完成的階段。)《象傳》則點出實際行事的教訓:「澤上於天,夬;君子以施祿及下,居德則忌。」(湖澤高懸於天之上,這就是夬卦的卦象。因此君子看見夬卦,便懂得將俸祿、恩澤廣施給地位在下者,同時也對安於已積累的德行、因而心生自滿有所戒懼。)若將第 42 卦「增益源自上位者甘願向下位者讓步」的教誨,與第 43 卦合觀,便能看出兩者彼此互補的關係:即使一場道義或政治上的勝利已幾近完成,也仍必須以公開、誠懇的方式進行,不能自滿地僅僅依恃武力,因為往往正是那最後、看似最微不足道的一絲抵抗,才是最容易被低估、也最為危險的部分。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——吉布地(Djibouti)

  • 位置:位於非洲之角,坐落於紅海南端出口的曼德海峽(Bab-el-Mandeb Strait)畔;北鄰厄利垂亞,西邊與南邊皆與衣索比亞接壤,東南方則與索馬利亞相鄰,並擁有面向亞丁灣與塔朱拉灣的海岸線。
  • 領土:約 23,200 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 0.64 倍,面積明顯小於台灣;與英國威爾斯(約 20,779 平方公里)或美國紐澤西州(約 22,591 平方公里)相近。
  • 人口:約 110 萬人(2026 年估計),高度集中於首都吉布地市。
  • 宗教:絕大多數信仰遜尼派伊斯蘭教(約佔 94%),另有少數基督徒(多為外籍居民及與殖民時期相關的歷史社群)。
  • 經濟:幾乎完全建立在其地理戰略位置之上,而非天然資源:吉布地的港口(部分由吉布地港口與自由貿易區管理局經營)是內陸國衣索比亞對外貿易最主要的海運門戶;此外,該國因位處曼德海峽這個戰略要衝,同時容納多國軍事基地而獲得可觀的租金收入,包括法國在非洲最大的海外基地、美國的雷蒙尼爾營(Camp Lemonnier)、中國首座海外軍事基地,以及日本、義大利的駐軍設施。儘管有物流與租金收入挹注,失業率與貧窮問題依然嚴峻,經濟高度仰賴港口與鐵路基礎建設(包括亞的斯亞貝巴—吉布地鐵路)持續獲得外國投資。
  • 歷史:19 世紀末起淪為法國殖民地「法屬索馬利蘭」,其後改稱「法屬阿法爾與伊薩領地」;1977 年獨立。獨立後政局深受阿法爾族與伊薩索馬利族兩大族群間的緊張關係影響,並於 1991 年至 1994 年間爆發內戰,最終以和平協議收場,讓阿法爾族在政府中獲得代表席次;此後該國大致維持穩定。
  • 現任領導人:總統伊斯梅爾·奧馬爾·蓋萊(Ismaïl Omar Guelleh),自 1999 年起連續執政至今,並在憲法任期限制多次修改後屢次連任;其長期執政雖招致政治競爭受限的批評,但同時也伴隨著吉布地崛起成為重要物流與軍事基地樞紐的過程。
  • 語言:法語與阿拉伯語為官方語言(此為殖民歷史及吉布地加入阿拉伯國家聯盟的遺緒),而索馬利語與阿法爾語則是兩大主要族群日常生活中最普遍使用的語言。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov 世界概況(World Factbook)

6)網路迷因介紹——烏干達指節(Ugandan Knuckles / "Do You Know Da Wae?")

  • 名稱:「Ugandan Knuckles」(烏干達指節),以其台詞「Do you know da wae?」(你知道那條路(哪威)嗎?)聞名。
  • 起源/歷史:於 2018 年初起源自社交 VR 平台 VRChat,角色造型是以音速小子(Sonic the Hedgehog)角色「Knuckles」為基礎、粗糙簡陋的低多邊形二次創作,原本是為一個名為「Metaverse」的 YouTube 頻道所舉辦的虛擬化身競賽而製作。這個角色誇張、破碎的洋涇濱英語腔調,直接來自 2010 年烏干達動作片《誰殺了亞歷克斯隊長?》(Who Killed Captain Alex?,即「瓦卡萊塢」Wakaliwood 代表作)中的角色形象——這部低成本、風格荒誕的電影,早已因其無心插柳的喜感與現場 VJ 口白解說而在網路上爆紅。
  • 如何走紅:VRChat 使用者大量採用「烏干達指節」虛擬化身,集體扮演部落式的「族人」,成群圍繞新加入平台的使用者,一邊高喊「do you know da wae」、一邊對其「吐口水」作為一種玩笑;相關片段與螢幕錄影迅速於 2018 年間傳播至 YouTube、Twitter 與 Reddit,讓這個原本僅限於小眾 VR 圈內的玩笑,幾乎在一夕之間躍升為主流網路迷因。
  • 意義/使用情境:用來刻意模仿誇張、破碎的「部落式」洋涇濱英語呼喊腔調,通常被用作一種荒誕的「群體起鬨」式幽默——一群長相一致的虛擬化身,對著一位不知所措的目標反覆喊著同一句無意義的台詞——並被套用到各種不相關的情境中,單純追求那種混亂、群起圍攻式的喜劇效果。
  • 變體/演化:衍生出大量 VRChat 虛擬化身變體(包括與其他角色的混搭創作)、以「da wae」呼喊聲為基礎的混音/惡搞歌曲,並在遊戲媒體圈引發廣泛討論;同時它也成為一個經常被引用的重要案例——用以說明一個因誇張模仿非洲口音而涉及種族刻板印象的迷因,如何招致持續不斷的批評聲浪,並在 VRChat 與其他平台引發實質的內容審核爭議。
  • 目前狀態:作為一個被積極使用的迷因,如今已大致退場;VRChat 及相關社群後續已針對騷擾投訴與前述種族議題爭議,採取限制措施或非正式勸阻使用此虛擬化身。如今它主要作為回顧 2018 年網路文化時經常被提及的參考案例,以及探討「平台原生、以虛擬化身為載體的迷因」如何異常快速地爆紅又迅速退燒的重要討論素材。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——阿根廷探戈(Argentine Tango)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:探戈(Tango),特指阿根廷探戈,兼具社交舞蹈與音樂類型的雙重身分,並非由單一人物發明,而是在布宜諾斯艾利斯與蒙特維多兩地的勞工移民社區中逐漸成形。其後兩位對探戈影響最深遠的關鍵人物,分別是卡洛斯·加德爾(Carlos Gardel,1890-1935)——奠定探戈演唱與創作典範的傳奇歌手/作曲家,人稱「本土畫眉」(El Zorzal Criollo),透過唱片與電影將探戈帶向全球觀眾;以及阿斯托爾·皮亞佐拉(Ástor Piazzolla,1921-1992)——其「新探戈」(Tango Nuevo)將傳統探戈與爵士和聲、古典作曲技法融合,在數十年後大幅拓展了這個曲種的邊界。
  • 時代/背景:興起於 1880 至 1890 年代,發源自布宜諾斯艾利斯與蒙特維多較貧困的港區(arrabales),當地聚集了來自西班牙、義大利的移民勞工、非裔阿根廷社群(其坎東貝鼓樂節奏 candombe 融入了探戈早期的音樂根源),以及流離失所的高卓人(gauchos)。探戈最初在妓院與底層舞廳中演出,曾被阿根廷本地上流社會視為不入流的產物,卻意外於 1913 年前後在巴黎上流社會蔚為風潮(「探戈熱」tangomanía),而這股歐洲熱潮又反過來提升了探戈在阿根廷本土的社會地位。
  • 內容概述:作為一種社交舞蹈,探戈由兩位舞伴以緊密相擁的姿態共舞,動作特色是俐落、頓挫分明的步伐、突如其來的停頓,以及大量即興的「引導與跟隨」互動,而非固定編排的舞步;作為一種音樂類型,探戈歌詞(letras)絕大多數瀰漫著憂鬱、懷舊的氛圍,反覆描寫失落的愛情、背叛、思鄉之情,以及移民對美好生活的渴望。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:傳統上由一支以班多鈕手風琴(bandoneón,一種德製鈕扣式手風琴,其哀愁音色已與探戈密不可分)為核心的典型樂團(orquesta típica)演奏,搭配小提琴、鋼琴與低音提琴。核心舞步詞彙包括基本走步(caminata)、八字形旋轉步(ocho)、勾腿動作(gancho)與位移步(sacada);與探戈關係密切的相關曲種還包括節奏較快的米隆加(milonga)與克里奧爾華爾滋(vals criollo)。
  • 文化/歷史意義:探戈是阿根廷(與烏拉圭)國族認同的重要象徵,與布宜諾斯艾利斯這座城市的意象緊密相連,在大眾想像中兩者幾乎密不可分;2009 年由阿根廷與烏拉圭共同申請,成功列入聯合國教科文組織(UNESCO)人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄。
  • 影響與傳承:探戈後來衍生出經過系統化編排的「國際標準探戈」(International Tango)舞廳舞蹈流派,與即興性強烈的阿根廷原生探戈逐漸分道揚鑣;加德爾透過國際電影與唱片事業的發展,使探戈成為最早真正走向世界舞台的拉丁美洲通俗藝術形式之一;皮亞佐拉的新探戈則直接影響了爵士樂與當代古典音樂作曲家,至今仍是全球音樂會曲目中的常見經典之作。
  • 現況/如何欣賞:探戈至今仍是活生生、持續被實踐的傳統,而非僅存於歷史紀錄中的文化遺跡——布宜諾斯艾利斯與全球各地的探戈社群,每晚都有稱為「米隆加」(milonga)的社交探戈舞會持續進行,布宜諾斯艾利斯更主辦一年一度的探戈藝術節暨探戈世界盃(Mundial de Tango),吸引來自世界各地的競技與社交舞者共襄盛舉。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、ich.unesco.org(聯合國教科文組織)

8)看片方法——鏡頭選擇與焦距:廣角壓縮 vs. 長焦壓縮 Lens Choice & Focal Length(第 29 課)

每一個鏡頭畫面,都是透過某支特定焦距的鏡頭拍攝而成,而這個選擇早在任何剪輯或燈光決定介入之前,就已經決定了觀眾對空間的感知方式。廣角鏡頭會誇大前景與背景之間的視覺距離感——靠近鏡頭的物體會顯得不成比例地巨大,而背景則相對縮小、顯得更遙遠,藉此營造出深邃、遼闊的空間感,或者反過來,在極近距離拍攝時營造出令人壓迫的變形感。長焦鏡頭的效果則恰恰相反:它會壓縮空間,將前景與背景之間的視覺距離「壓平」,使得實際上相距甚遠的物體,在畫面中看起來不自然地擁擠、靠得極近。這與第 8 天教過的「景深與焦點控制」(關注的是畫面何處清晰、何處模糊,而非空間壓縮)、第 3 天的「攝影與燈光」(處理光線與色彩,而非鏡頭的幾何特性),以及第 15 天的「運鏡」(鏡頭本身的物理移動,而非固定鏡頭的光學特性)是完全不同的分析角度。可以問自己三個問題:從畫面本身,你能多有把握地判斷出這是哪一種焦距的鏡頭?這種空間變形效果,是否呼應了這場戲的情緒或敘事內容,還是純粹是一個看不出動機的技術性選擇?一部電影中途若切換慣用的鏡頭焦距,是否標誌著角色心理狀態,或劇情張力層級,出現了刻意的轉變?

實例演練:《畢業生》(The Graduate,1967,導演:麥克·尼可斯,攝影:羅伯特·瑟提斯)——片中班傑明·布拉德克狂奔於街道上、隨後衝過教堂庭院朝鏡頭跑來的知名畫面,即以極端長焦鏡頭拍攝。長焦鏡頭強烈的空間壓縮效果,徹底扁平化了「向前推進」的視覺感受,以至於儘管班傑明明顯正在全力衝刺,他看起來卻幾乎沒有拉近與畫面之間的距離——這是一種純粹透過光學原理達成的效果(沒有任何剪輯花招,也沒有慢動作處理),比任何一句台詞都更直接地傳達出他這場令人心力交瘁、彷彿注定徒勞的時間賽跑,如今仍是電影學校課堂上,最常被引用來示範「長焦壓縮作為敘事手法」的經典範例之一。
