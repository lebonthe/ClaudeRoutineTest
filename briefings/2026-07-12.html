3) Spanish Lesson — Day 5 (10 min, builds on Day 4)

Days 1–4 covered greetings/self-intro, ¿Cómo estás? (estar), más o menos / un poco cansado(a), and farewells (Adiós/Hasta luego). Today introduces another "to be" verb, ser, used for asking and answering where someone is from:

  • ¿De dónde eres? (deh DOHN-deh EH-rehs) — Where are you from?
  • Soy de Taiwán (soy deh tai-WAHN) — I'm from Taiwan
  • Soy español / española (soy ess-pahn-YOHL / ess-pahn-YOH-lah) — I'm Spanish (nationality adjective, gender-agreeing)

Grammar note: ser (soy/eres/es) is for permanent identity — origin, nationality — unlike estar (learned Day 2), which is for temporary states.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 5: 需 (Xū) — "Waiting" (Water over Heaven). Clouds rising into the sky, not yet fallen as rain — the image of confident, sustaining waiting. Represents patience grounded in sincerity and integrity rather than passive delay, like clouds gathering strength before the rain that will nourish the fields. The classical judgment favors waiting for the right moment with quiet confidence, nourishing oneself and one's resources, rather than forcing premature action — a major undertaking succeeds when timed well.
