3) Spanish Lesson — Day 25 (10 min, builds on Day 24)

Days 23-24 covered the future and conditional simple tenses. Today shifts from tense to mood: el imperativo (the imperative), starting with affirmative commands for tú and usted — how to tell someone directly to do something.

  • ¡Habla más despacio, por favor! — Speak more slowly, please!
  • ¡Coma usted primero! — Please eat first (formal)!
  • ¡Ven aquí ahora mismo! — Come here right now!
  • ¡Ten cuidado con el escalón! — Be careful of the step!

Grammar note: the regular affirmative tú command borrows the él/ella present-tense form (habla, come, escribe) — no new conjugation to learn. The regular affirmative usted command instead uses the "opposite vowel" pattern (-ar verbs end in -e: hable; -er/-ir verbs end in -a: coma, escriba) — the same pattern the present subjunctive uses. Eight verbs have irregular tú commands that must be memorized as a set: di (decir), haz (hacer), ve (ir), pon (poner), sal (salir), sé (ser), ten (tener), ven (venir).

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 25: 无妄 (Wú Wàng) — "Innocence" (The Unexpected) (Heaven above, Thunder below). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary bridges directly from yesterday's hexagram: "復則不妄矣,故受之以无妄。" ("Having returned [to the right path], one is no longer wayward or deluded — hence it is followed by Wú Wàng, Innocence.") This is the natural consequence of yesterday's Fù: once the single yang line has genuinely returned and renewal is underway, the correct state that follows is one of unforced, uncalculating naturalness — action free of ulterior motive or artificial scheming. The Judgment reads: "无妄:元亨,利貞。其匪正有眚,不利有攸往。" ("Innocence: supreme success, furthering through correctness. If someone is not correct, they will have misfortune, and it will not further them to undertake anything.") This hexagram carries a rare warning embedded in an otherwise auspicious reading — the good fortune of innocence is conditional on genuine correctness, not merely on good intentions; a wayward or self-deceiving actor gains nothing from claiming innocence. The Image adds: "天下雷行,物與无妄;先王以茂對時,育萬物。" ("Under heaven, thunder rolls: all things are stirred into their innocent, natural state. Thus the kings of antiquity, rich in virtue and in harmony with the time, nourished and cared for all beings.") The counsel is to act spontaneously and correctly according to one's nature and the moment, trusting that unforced right action succeeds — while resisting any temptation to manufacture outcomes through scheming or excessive planning, since Wú Wàng's entire power comes from its freedom from calculation.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Kurdistan (庫德斯坦)

  • Location: A mountainous cultural region spanning parts of four sovereign states — southeastern Turkey, northern Iraq, northwestern Iran, and northern Syria — rather than a single recognized country; this is the first Section 5 entry that is a stateless nation/region rather than a sovereign state, chosen deliberately for that contrast.
  • Territory: Commonly cited at roughly 390,000-450,000 km² across all four host states combined (estimates vary since there is no official border) — around 11-12 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), comparable in scale to Sweden or Germany, though the region is fragmented rather than contiguous under one administration.
  • Population: An estimated 30-45 million Kurds, making them the largest stateless ethnic group in the world; roughly 15-20 million in Turkey, 8-10 million in Iran, 5-6 million in Iraq, and 2-3 million in Syria.
  • Religion: Predominantly Sunni Islam of the Shafi'i school (distinct from the Hanafi majority among Turks and Arabs), with significant minorities practicing Alevism, Yazidism (concentrated around Sinjar, Iraq), Yarsanism (Ahl-e Haqq, mainly in Iran), Shia Islam, and Christianity.
  • Economy: Highly uneven by host country — the Kurdistan Region of Iraq runs a semi-autonomous, oil-export-driven economy centered on Erbil, with its own pipeline arrangements to Turkey; Syria's Kurdish-led Autonomous Administration of North and East Syria (Rojava) relies on agriculture and modest oil fields; Kurdish areas of Turkey and Iran are generally less developed than each country's national average, with periodic state investment and long-standing underdevelopment grievances both factors.
  • History: Kurdish identity traces to ancient peoples of the Zagros/Taurus mountain region; post-WWI, the 1920 Treaty of Sèvres briefly promised an independent Kurdish state, but the 1923 Treaty of Lausanne divided Kurdish-majority lands among Turkey, Iraq, Iran, and Syria with no such state created. The 20th century saw repeated autonomy movements: the short-lived Republic of Mahabad in Iran (1946); decades of Iraqi Kurdish peshmerga-led autonomy struggles under the Barzani family, including the devastating 1988 Halabja chemical attack under Saddam Hussein; a US/UK-enforced no-fly zone after the 1991 Gulf War that let Iraqi Kurdish autonomy take root, formalized in Iraq's 2005 constitution as the Kurdistan Region; Syrian Kurdish YPG/SDF forces became a key US partner against ISIS (2014-2019), establishing the Rojava autonomous administration; and Turkey's PKK insurgency, active since 1984, has seen recent (2024-2025) disarmament and peace-process developments.
  • Current leader(s): No single unified Kurdish leader exists. The Kurdistan Region of Iraq is led by President Nechirvan Barzani and Prime Minister Masrour Barzani (both KDP); Syria's Autonomous Administration of North and East Syria is associated with SDF commander Mazloum Abdi and co-chair figures such as Ilham Ahmed; Kurdish political representation in Turkey runs mainly through the pro-Kurdish DEM Party; Iranian Kurdish areas have no formal autonomous government.
  • Language: Kurdish, split mainly between the Kurmanji dialect (Turkey, Syria, and parts of Iraq) and Sorani (Iraq and Iran), plus widespread bilingualism in the majority language of each host state (Turkish, Arabic, Persian).
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Success Kid

  • Name: Success Kid (originally captioned "I Hate Sandcastles").
  • Origin/history: A photo taken in August 2007 by Laney Griner of her 11-month-old son, Sammy Griner, clenching a determined fist on a beach in Jacksonville, Florida — originally uploaded to Flickr simply because Laney thought the expression was funny, with no meme intent at all.
  • How it became popular: The image was picked up in 2008 as an "I Hate Sandcastles" rage-comic-style caption joke, then spread through the "advice animal"/image-macro boom of 2010-2012 on Reddit, 4chan, and Tumblr, where it became one of the era's most reliably recognizable reaction templates.
  • Meaning/usage: Captions a small, often trivial personal victory or triumphant moment — parking on the first try, correctly guessing a coworker's coffee order — paired with the baby's disproportionately fierce, determined expression for comic contrast.
  • Variations/evolution: Became part of the broader "advice animal" template family; used commercially (a UK price-comparison site paid to license Sammy's image for an ad campaign); and, notably, became the center of a real-world fundraising story in 2015 when Sammy's father needed a kidney transplant — a GoFundMe campaign leaning on the meme's fame raised well over $100,000. Periodic "meme grows up" viral posts have shown Sammy recreating the pose as he's aged.
  • Current status: A settled, nostalgic landmark of the 2010-2012 advice-animal/rage-comic era, still instantly recognized and occasionally revived for "then vs. now" viral posts; regarded as one of the defining reaction images of that period of meme history.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Kathakali (कथकली)

  • Name: Kathakali, a classical dance-drama form from Kerala, southwestern India; the name combines "katha" (story) and "kali" (play/performance).
  • Key figures/creators: Traditionally credited to the 17th-century ruler Kottarakkara Thampuran, who created the Ramanattam cycle of plays that evolved into Kathakali's present form; later Kerala princely patrons, including the Kottayam Thampuran and Vettath Raja, further shaped its repertoire and technique. Unlike Noh's Zeami, Kathakali has no single codifying author — it developed across generations of temple and court patronage.
  • Era/background: Emerged around the 17th century in Kerala, synthesizing older Sanskrit temple theatre (Kutiyattam), regional dance traditions, and the physical training system of Kalaripayattu, Kerala's martial art.
  • Content overview: Draws its stories from the Hindu epics — the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, and the Puranas — traditionally performed as night-long temple festival events, opening with ritual invocations (Kelikottu, Thodayam) before the scripted narrative episodes (attakatha) begin.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Built on elaborate, symbolically coded face makeup and costume (vesham) using natural pigments — green (pacha) for noble/heroic characters, red-and-white (kathi) for the proud or villainous, white-bearded (thadi) roles, black (kari) for demonic figures, and a softer style (minukku) for refined or feminine characters. Performers never speak; the sung narrative and dialogue come from vocalists (accompanied by chenda and maddalam drums) while performers convey the entire story through codified hand gestures (mudras, drawn from the Hastalakshana Deepika treatise), highly trained facial expressions built on the navarasa (nine classical emotions), and precise eye movements, all underpinned by years of Kalaripayattu-based physical conditioning.
  • Cultural/historical significance: A signature Kerala art form fusing devotion, athletic discipline, and total-body storytelling; widely considered one of the most physically demanding classical performance traditions in the world.
  • Influence and legacy: Studied and admired internationally by theatre practitioners exploring non-Western, non-naturalistic performance ("total theatre" and physical-theatre movements); the Kerala Kalamandalam institute, founded in 1930 by the poet Vallathol Narayana Menon, professionalized and helped preserve the tradition, training generations of performers.
  • Current status/where to experience: Actively performed today at Kerala temple festivals, at the Kerala Kalamandalam in Cheruthuruthy, and at tourist-oriented cultural centers in Kochi and elsewhere that offer shortened performances with pre-show makeup demonstrations; touring troupes also present it at international festivals.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, britannica.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — The Long Take / Plan-Séquence & Camera Choreography (Lesson 11)

Lessons 1-10 covered mise-en-scène, editing/montage, cinematography/lighting, sound, colour, narrative structure, blocking/staging, framing/composition, symbolism, and auteur theory. Today's lens looks at the opposite pole from editing: the long take (plan-séquence) — a single, unbroken shot sustained across an entire scene, or even multiple scenes, with no cuts at all.

The method: Where montage (Lesson 2) generates meaning by juxtaposing separate shots, the long take generates meaning by refusing to cut — forcing the camera itself to do the work editing would otherwise do, through reframing, movement, and choreography within a single continuous take. When analyzing a long take, ask: (1) How does the camera move between points of interest — does it pan, dolly, or crane to a new focal point rather than cutting to it, effectively "editing within the frame"? (2) How precisely must the actors' blocking and timing be choreographed, since there's no cut to hide a missed mark or paper over a mistake? (3) What emotional effect does the absence of cuts produce — often heightened tension, immersion, or a sense of real, unmanipulated time, since the audience gets no cutaway "relief" from the scene's duration? (4) What is the practical stake of the technique — a long take is inherently high-risk, since a single error late in a multi-minute shot can force a total restart, so its use itself signals directorial and cast confidence.

Worked example — Children of Men (2006, dir. Alfonso Cuarón, cinematography by Emmanuel Lubezki), the car ambush sequence: A specially rigged camera mounted inside the car rotates a full 360 degrees around the vehicle's interior and exterior in real time as an ambush erupts around it, with no visible cuts across several minutes of escalating chaos. Because the shot never breaks, the geography of the attack — where each assailant comes from, how close they get, which window shatters when — plays out in continuous, legible real space and time, rather than being assembled after the fact through cutting. The actors' terror must be sustained continuously rather than performed in short, editable bursts, and the camera's own choreographed rotation becomes a kind of blocking in itself, revealing new threats the instant it turns to face them — exactly like a character would experience the ambush, with no editorial "cut away" granting the audience a breath the characters themselves don't get.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Camera movement replaces the cut as the scene's structuring device — every reframe is a directorial choice about what the audience sees next, carrying the same weight a cut would in a conventionally edited scene. (2) Unbroken duration builds a specific, non-substitutable kind of tension — because a cut can't rescue a bad moment or skip past a lull, the sustained real-time pressure the long take creates cannot be replicated by clever editing of shorter shots. (3) Verisimilitude through continuity — audiences implicitly trust that what a long take shows happened as shown, in the order shown, without editorial rearrangement, giving the technique a documentary-like credibility even in a scripted, technically engineered sequence. This lesson pairs directly against Lesson 2 (editing and montage): where montage builds meaning by cutting between shots, the long take builds meaning specifically by refusing to.

4) 易經卦象

第二十五卦:无妄卦(Wú Wàng)——「無妄／不期而遇」(上乾下震,天下雷行)。〈序卦傳〉直接銜接了昨天的卦象:「復則不妄矣,故受之以无妄。」(既已回復正道,便不再虛妄躁動——因此接著便是无妄卦,無妄之意。)這正是昨天復卦的自然延續:當那根陽爻真正回復、新生之勢已然展開之後,接下來正確的狀態,便是一種不假造作、毫無算計的自然而然——行事出於本然,而非別有用心或刻意謀劃。卦辭寫道:「无妄:元亨,利貞。其匪正有眚,不利有攸往。」(无妄卦象徵:至為亨通,利於守持正道。若有人行事不正,便會招致災禍,此時不利於有所前往。)這一卦罕見地在整體吉利的卦辭中,埋藏了一項警示——无妄之所以吉祥,前提是真正的「正」,而非僅僅出於自認為良善的動機;一個心存偏邪或自欺之人,即便自稱無妄,也無從因此獲益。〈象傳〉補充道:「天下雷行,物與无妄;先王以茂對時,育萬物。」(雷聲行於天下,萬物皆被激盪回歸其無妄自然的本然狀態;因此古代聖王德行豐盛,順應天時,養育撫育萬物。)這裡的告誡是:應當依循自身本性與時機,自然而然、正直地行事,相信不假造作的正確行為自然能夠成功——同時也要抗拒藉由算計或過度謀劃來刻意製造結果的誘惑,因為无妄卦全部的力量,正來自於它擺脫算計、渾然天成的本質。

(第二十五次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)、第二十一卦 噬嗑(Shì Kè)、第二十二卦 賁(Bì)、第二十三卦 剝(Bō)、第二十四卦 復(Fù)、第二十五卦 无妄(Wú Wàng)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 庫德斯坦(Kurdistan)

  • 位置: 一個橫跨四個主權國家的山地文化區域——土耳其東南部、伊拉克北部、伊朗西北部與敘利亞北部——而非單一受承認的國家;這是本欄目第一次介紹「無國家地位的民族/地區」,而非主權國家,刻意選擇以形成對比。
  • 領土面積: 若將四個國家境內的庫德人聚居區域合併估算,一般約在 39 萬至 45 萬平方公里之間(由於並無官方邊界,估計數字略有出入)——約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 11 至 12 倍,規模上大致相當於瑞典或德國,不過整個區域是分散於四國之下、並非由單一行政體統一管轄。
  • 人口: 估計約有 3,000 萬至 4,500 萬庫德人,使其成為全世界人口最多、卻沒有自己國家的民族;土耳其境內約 1,500 萬至 2,000 萬人,伊朗約 800 萬至 1,000 萬人,伊拉克約 500 萬至 600 萬人,敘利亞約 200 萬至 300 萬人。
  • 宗教: 以沙斐儀學派(Shafi'i)遜尼派伊斯蘭教為主(有別於土耳其人與阿拉伯人多數信奉的哈乃菲學派),另有相當比例信奉阿列維派(Alevism)、雅茲迪教(Yazidism,主要集中於伊拉克辛賈爾地區)、亞爾桑教(Yarsanism/Ahl-e Haqq,主要在伊朗)、什葉派伊斯蘭教,以及基督教。
  • 經濟: 依所在國家不同而落差甚大——伊拉克庫德斯坦地區擁有半自治、以石油出口為核心的經濟體系,以埃爾比勒(Erbil)為中心,並自有輸往土耳其的輸油管線安排;敘利亞由庫德人主導的「敘利亞北部與東部自治政府」(羅賈瓦,Rojava)則仰賴農業與規模有限的油田;土耳其與伊朗境內的庫德人地區,發展程度普遍低於各自國家的全國平均水準,長期以來的低度開發問題與國家投資不足,兩者皆是重要因素。
  • 歷史: 庫德民族的認同可追溯至札格羅斯山脈/托魯斯山脈一帶的古代民族;第一次世界大戰後,1920 年《色佛爾條約》一度承諾建立獨立的庫德國家,但 1923 年的《洛桑條約》卻將庫德人聚居地區分割歸屬於土耳其、伊拉克、伊朗與敘利亞,並未真正建國。20 世紀間,庫德人多次發起自治運動:1946 年伊朗境內短命的馬哈巴德共和國(Republic of Mahabad);數十年來由巴爾札尼(Barzani)家族領導、以自由鬥士(peshmerga)為主力的伊拉克庫德自治抗爭運動,期間包括 1988 年薩達姆・海珊政權對哈拉卜賈(Halabja)發動的毀滅性化學武器攻擊;1991 年波灣戰爭後由美英執行的禁航區政策,讓伊拉克庫德自治得以生根茁壯,並於 2005 年伊拉克憲法中正式確立為「庫德斯坦地區」;敘利亞庫德人的人民保衛軍/敘利亞民主軍(YPG/SDF)在 2014 至 2019 年間,成為美國對抗伊斯蘭國(ISIS)的重要夥伴,進而建立起羅賈瓦自治政府;而土耳其境內自 1984 年以來持續不斷的庫德工人黨(PKK)叛亂,近期(2024至2025年間)也出現了解除武裝與和平進程的相關進展。
  • 現任領導人: 並不存在單一、統一的庫德領導人。伊拉克庫德斯坦地區由總統內契爾凡・巴爾札尼(Nechirvan Barzani)與總理馬斯魯爾・巴爾札尼(Masrour Barzani,兩人皆屬庫德斯坦民主黨 KDP)領導;敘利亞北部與東部自治政府則與敘利亞民主軍指揮官馬茲盧姆・阿卜迪(Mazloum Abdi),以及共同主席伊爾罕・艾哈邁德(Ilham Ahmed)等人物有關;土耳其境內的庫德人政治代表,主要透過親庫德的「人民平等與民主黨」(DEM Party)發聲;伊朗境內的庫德人地區則沒有正式的自治政府。
  • 語言: 庫德語,主要分為庫爾曼吉方言(Kurmanji,通行於土耳其、敘利亞及伊拉克部分地區)與索拉尼方言(Sorani,通行於伊拉克與伊朗),此外庫德人普遍能雙語使用所在國家的主要語言(土耳其語、阿拉伯語、波斯語)。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、en.wikipedia.org

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 成功寶寶(Success Kid)

  • 名稱: Success Kid(成功寶寶),原本的配文標題是「I Hate Sandcastles」(我討厭沙堡)。
  • 起源/歷史: 這張照片是 2007 年 8 月,由蘭妮・葛蘭納(Laney Griner)在美國佛羅里達州傑克遜維爾的海灘上,拍下她當時 11 個月大的兒子山米・葛蘭納(Sammy Griner)緊握拳頭、神情堅定的模樣;原本只是單純上傳到 Flickr,因為蘭妮覺得這個表情很好笑,完全沒有想過會變成迷因。
  • 如何爆紅: 這張照片在 2008 年被拿來當作「I Hate Sandcastles」風格的「憤怒漫畫」式配文哏圖使用,隨後在 2010 至 2012 年間 Reddit、4chan 與 Tumblr 上掀起的「建議動物(advice animal)」/圖片哏圖熱潮中廣泛流傳,成為那個時代最容易被辨識的反應圖範本之一。
  • 意義與使用情境: 用來搭配一個微小、甚至微不足道的個人「小勝利」時刻——例如一次就成功停好車、或正確猜中同事的咖啡口味——搭配這個寶寶與情境完全不成比例、卻異常堅毅的表情,製造出反差幽默效果。
  • 變體與演變: 成為更廣義「建議動物」哏圖家族的一員;曾被商業運用(英國一家比價網站付費取得山米肖像授權,用於廣告行銷活動);更值得一提的是,2015 年這張迷因照片催生了一則真實的募款故事——山米的父親需要接受腎臟移植手術,一項借助這張迷因知名度的 GoFundMe 募款活動,最終募得超過 10 萬美元。往後也不時出現「迷因主角長大了」的病毒式貼文,展示山米隨著年齡增長重現當年那個經典姿勢。
  • 目前現況: 已是 2010 至 2012 年「建議動物」/憤怒漫畫時代公認的懷舊經典地標,至今仍能被立即辨識,並不時被拿來製作「當年 vs. 現在」的對比病毒式貼文;被公認為那個時代最具代表性的反應圖之一。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 卡達卡利舞劇(Kathakali)

  • 名稱: Kathakali(卡達卡利),源自印度西南部喀拉拉邦(Kerala)的古典舞劇形式;名稱由「katha」(故事)與「kali」(演出/戲劇)組合而成。
  • 關鍵人物: 傳統上將其起源歸功於 17 世紀的統治者科塔拉卡拉・坦布蘭(Kottarakkara Thampuran),他創作了「羅摩衍那劇」(Ramanattam)系列劇作,後來演變為今日所見的卡達卡利形式;此後喀拉拉邦其他土邦統治者的贊助,包括科塔亞姆・坦布蘭(Kottayam Thampuran)與韋塔特・拉賈(Vettath Raja),也進一步形塑了它的劇目與表演技法。與能劇的世阿彌不同,卡達卡利並沒有單一的奠基人物,而是歷經數代寺廟與宮廷的贊助逐步發展而成。
  • 時代背景: 大約於 17 世紀在喀拉拉邦興起,融合了更早期的梵語寺廟戲劇(古提亞坦,Kutiyattam)、當地舞蹈傳統,以及喀拉拉邦武術「卡拉里帕亞圖」(Kalaripayattu)的身體訓練體系。
  • 內容概述: 取材自印度教史詩——《羅摩衍那》、《摩訶婆羅多》,以及往世書(Puranas)中的故事;傳統上以徹夜演出的寺廟祭典形式呈現,先以儀式性的開場(Kelikottu、Thodayam)揭開序幕,而後才進入劇本化的敘事段落(attakatha)。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 建立在極其繁複、具有象徵編碼意義的臉部化妝與服裝(vesham)之上,使用天然顏料——綠色(pacha)代表高貴/英雄角色,紅白相間(kathi)代表驕傲或反派角色,白鬍造型(thadi)代表各類角色,黑色(kari)代表魔怪角色,而較柔和的化妝風格(minukku)則用於溫婉或女性角色。演員全程不發一語;歌唱敘事與對白由歌者(搭配 chenda 與 maddalam 兩種鼓樂伴奏)負責演唱,演員則完全透過一套規範化的手勢語言(手印,mudras,源自《手相手冊》Hastalakshana Deepika 一書)、以「九種情味」(navarasa)為基礎、經過高度訓練的臉部表情,以及精準的眼神移動來傳達整個故事,而這一切都建立在多年卡拉里帕亞圖武術訓練所打下的體能基礎之上。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 是喀拉拉邦融合宗教虔誠、體能鍛鍊與全身敘事於一體的代表性藝術形式;普遍被認為是全世界對體能要求最嚴苛的古典表演藝術傳統之一。
  • 影響與傳承: 受到全球研究非西方、非寫實主義表演形式的劇場工作者高度重視與研究(啟發了「整體劇場」與肢體劇場等相關運動);1930 年由詩人瓦拉托爾・納拉亞納・梅農(Vallathol Narayana Menon)創立的喀拉拉卡拉曼達蘭學院(Kerala Kalamandalam),將這項傳統加以專業化並妥善保存,培育了一代又一代的表演者。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 至今仍活躍於喀拉拉邦各地的寺廟祭典演出、位於切魯圖魯蒂(Cheruthuruthy)的喀拉拉卡拉曼達蘭學院,以及柯欽(Kochi)等地面向觀光客推出、附有上妝過程展示的精簡版演出場所;各地巡演團隊也會將其帶到國際藝術節上演出。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、britannica.com

8) 看片方法 — 長鏡頭與運鏡調度 The Long Take / Plan-Séquence(第十一課)

第一到十課教了場面調度、剪輯與蒙太奇、攝影與燈光、音效、色彩、敘事結構、走位與空間調度、取景與構圖、象徵與視覺隱喻,以及作者論。今天的分析視角則站在剪輯的對立面:長鏡頭(plan-séquence)——整場戲、甚至跨越多個場景,都以單一、不間斷的鏡頭一鏡到底,完全沒有剪接。

方法說明: 蒙太奇(第二課)透過並置不同鏡頭來產生意義;長鏡頭則反其道而行,透過「拒絕剪接」來產生意義——迫使攝影機本身,在單一連續鏡頭之內,透過重新構圖、運鏡與走位調度,去完成原本該由剪接負責的工作。分析長鏡頭時,可以這樣提問:(1)攝影機如何在不同關注焦點之間移動——是透過平移、推軌或吊臂運鏡「移向」新的焦點,而非直接剪接過去,等於是「在畫面之內進行剪輯」?(2)演員的走位與時間點,必須經過多麼精密的排練與調度,因為沒有剪接可以掩蓋走位失誤或表演失準?(3)不剪接所帶來的情緒效果是什麼——通常是更強烈的張力、沉浸感,或一種「真實、未經操弄的時間感」,因為觀眾在這段時間裡完全得不到剪接帶來的喘息空間?(4)這項技巧在實務上承擔的風險是什麼——長鏡頭天生就是高風險的拍攝方式,因為一顆長達數分鐘的鏡頭,只要在後段出一次差錯,整顆鏡頭往往就得整個重來,因此使用長鏡頭本身,就是導演與演員對彼此信心的展現。

實例示範——《人類之子》(Children of Men,2006,導演艾方索・柯朗〔Alfonso Cuarón〕,攝影艾曼紐・盧貝茲基〔Emmanuel Lubezki〕)車輛遭伏擊的段落: 一台特別加裝的攝影機固定在車廂內部,在伏擊爆發的過程中,以真實時間、完整 360 度環繞拍攝車內與車外空間,長達數分鐘的混亂場面之中完全沒有可見的剪接點。正因為這顆鏡頭從未中斷,整場伏擊的空間關係——攻擊者從哪個方向出現、逼近到多近、哪一扇車窗會在什麼時刻被打破——是在連續、清晰可辨的真實空間與時間之中展開,而非事後透過剪接拼湊而成。演員的恐懼情緒必須連貫地持續演出,而非拆成數個可供剪接的短促片段;攝影機本身經過編排的旋轉軌跡,也成為一種調度手法本身——鏡頭一轉向哪裡,新的威脅便隨即顯現,如同劇中角色親身經歷這場伏擊時的視角一般,完全沒有任何剪接上的「喘息空間」讓觀眾得以脫身,而劇中角色自己同樣無處可逃。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)運鏡取代剪接,成為場景的結構性手法——每一次重新構圖,都是導演對「觀眾接下來該看什麼」所做的決定,其份量等同於傳統剪接場景中的一次剪接點。(2)不間斷的持續時間,製造出一種無可替代的張力——因為沒有剪接可以挽救失誤的瞬間、也無法跳過場面中的低潮片段,長鏡頭所建立的持續性真實壓力,是任何再高明的短鏡頭剪接組合都無法複製的。(3)透過連續性建立的真實可信度——觀眾會不自覺地相信,長鏡頭所呈現的一切,正是依照畫面所示的順序、如實發生,沒有經過剪接重新編排,這使得這項技巧即便用在一場經過精密工程設計、完全按劇本執行的場景中,依然能帶來近似紀錄片式的真實可信度。這一課恰好與第二課(剪輯與蒙太奇)形成正面對照:蒙太奇透過剪接鏡頭之間的關係來建立意義,而長鏡頭恰恰是透過「拒絕剪接」來建立意義。
