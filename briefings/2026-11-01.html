3) Spanish Lesson — Day 108: Transport and ways of getting there (ir en / a pie / tomar / coger / llegar)

New topic, not covered on Days 1-107: the means of transport and the fixed phrases for how you travel. Use ir en + vehicle with no article (voy en metro), the exception ir a pie (on foot), tomar or coger + article for "to take" (avoid coger in much of Latin America, where it is vulgar), llegar a for arrival, and bajarse del / en for getting off.

  • ¿Cuánto tarda? — Tarda unos veinte minutos. — How long does it take? — About twenty minutes.

4) Daodejing — Chapter 11: 三十輻共一轂 (Sānshí fú gòng yī gū) — "Thirty Spokes"

Series note: continues the Daodejing track begun on Oct 22, one chapter per day in order (chapters 1-81), never repeated.

Text (Wang Bi version): 三十輻共一轂,當其無,有車之用。埏埴以為器,當其無,有器之用。鑿戶牖以為室,當其無,有室之用。故有之以為利,無之以為用。

Translation (paraphrase): Thirty spokes meet at one hub, and it is the empty space at the center that makes the wheel useful. Clay is shaped into a vessel, and it is the emptiness inside that makes the vessel useful. Doors and windows are cut to make a room, and it is the empty space that makes the room useful. So what exists gives advantage, and what is not there gives use.

Commentary: This is one of the most quoted chapters because its argument is concrete: three everyday objects (wheel, pot, room), each defined by what is absent. The spokes, the clay and the walls are the "having" (you, 有) that we can see and sell; the hole in the hub, the hollow of the bowl and the space in the room are the "not-having" (wu, 無) in which the thing actually works. The last line states the principle: "having" provides the benefit, "not-having" provides the use. After Chapter 10's practices of softness and non-contrivance, Chapter 11 explains why emptiness is not a lack. A modern reading: a good design leaves room (white space in a layout, rest in music, margin in a schedule), and a mind with some space in it can receive something new.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Czechia (the Czech Republic)

  • Location & size: A landlocked country in Central Europe, bordered by Germany (west and northwest), Poland (northeast), Slovakia (east) and Austria (south). It has three historic lands: Bohemia, Moravia and Czech Silesia. Area about 78,900 km², roughly 2.2 times Taiwan (about 36,000 km²) (approximate comparison).
  • Population: About 10.9 million, roughly 46% of Taiwan's population (approximate). Czechs are the large majority; Slovaks, Ukrainians and Vietnamese are among the larger minorities. The capital is Prague (about 1.4 million).
  • Religion: One of the least religious countries in Europe. In the 2021 census, a large share of people said they had no religion or did not answer; the Catholic Church is the largest single denomination but a small minority.
  • Economy: A high-income, industrial, export-oriented economy in the EU (since 2004) that still uses the koruna (Czech crown, CZK) rather than the euro. Cars are central (Škoda in Mladá Boleslav, plus Toyota and Hyundai plants), with machinery, electronics and engineering; it also has a strong beer tradition (Pilsner Urquell, 1842) and one of the world's highest per-person beer consumption rates.
  • History: The Kingdom of Bohemia became a major European power; Charles IV made Prague an imperial capital in the 14th century and founded Charles University (1348). The Hussite wars followed the burning of Jan Hus (1415). The Habsburgs ruled from 1526 until 1918, when Czechoslovakia was founded. Nazi Germany occupied it after the Munich Agreement (1938-1939); the Communist Party took power in 1948; the Prague Spring reforms were crushed by a Warsaw Pact invasion in August 1968; the Velvet Revolution of November 1989 ended Communist rule; and Czechoslovakia split peacefully on Jan 1, 1993 (the "Velvet Divorce"). Czechia joined NATO in 1999 and the EU in 2004.
  • Current leaders: President Petr Pavel (elected 2023; a former NATO general). Prime Minister Andrej Babiš (ANO), whose coalition government with SPD and the Motorists was sworn in on Dec 15, 2025 (checked by search). I could not confirm today whether anything changed in 2026: search results mention a parliamentary no-confidence vote in early 2026 amid a dispute with the president, but the outcome was not found.
  • Language: Czech, a West Slavic language closely related to Slovak; the word "robot" comes from Czech (robota, forced labor), introduced by Karel Čapek's play R.U.R. (1920).
  • Fun fact: Prague Castle is often called the largest ancient castle complex in the world; the Charles Bridge dates to 1357; and the Prague astronomical clock (1410) is the oldest still working.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Trololo" / Mr. Trololo (Eduard Khil, 1976 song, viral 2009)

  • Name: "Trololo" (also "Mr. Trololo", "Trololo Man"), the nickname of a 1976 Soviet recording by baritone Eduard Khil, officially titled "Я очень рад, ведь я, наконец, возвращаюсь домой" ("I Am Very Glad, Because I'm Finally Returning Home").
  • Origin / history: Khil (1934-2012) was a popular Soviet singer. The song, with music by Arkady Ostrovsky, was recorded in 1976. Its original lyrics are said to have been rejected by Soviet censors, so Khil performed it as wordless vocalizing ("la-la-la" / "tro-lo-lo"). A television performance from the late 1970s or early 1980s, showing Khil in a brown suit, is the clip that spread.
  • How it spread: The clip was uploaded to the internet in 2009 and exploded on YouTube and forums in late 2009 and 2010, reaching tens of millions of views. English-speaking viewers named it "Trololo". It became a prank and "bait" video, a cousin of the Rickroll (the song's sheer cheerfulness was the joke).
  • Meaning / usage: No lyrics, so it carries no fixed meaning: it is used as a prank link, a symbol of absurd, unstoppable good cheer, or a reaction to a ridiculous situation. Remixes and covers use its recognizable "oh-oh-oh" melody.
  • Variations / evolution: Countless parodies, remixes and covers, including versions by other singers; Khil took the fame in good humor, re-recorded the song and performed it in later years. He died in January 2012.
  • Current status: A "classic" early-YouTube meme, remembered fondly; the original clip is still online.
  • Note: General knowledge; dates, the lyric-censorship story and view counts are approximate and were not source-checked today.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — West Side Story (stage 1957; film 1961)

  • Name: West Side Story, an American musical that retells Romeo and Juliet in 1950s New York.
  • Key figures: Conceived and choreographed by Jerome Robbins; book by Arthur Laurents; music by Leonard Bernstein; lyrics by Stephen Sondheim (his first Broadway credit as a lyricist). The 1961 film was directed by Robert Wise and Robbins.
  • Era / background: It opened on Broadway on September 26, 1957 (Winter Garden Theatre). Robbins first imagined it as a story of Jews and Catholics in a conflict in New York; it was changed to the Jets (white working-class teenagers) and the Sharks (Puerto Rican immigrants), reflecting immigration and gang tensions in the Upper West Side of the time.
  • Plot overview: Tony, a former Jet, falls in love with Maria, the sister of the Sharks' leader Bernardo. A rumble leaves Bernardo and Tony's friend Riff dead; Tony kills Bernardo and the lovers' hopes collapse into tragedy. Unlike Shakespeare, the ending is not a double suicide: Tony is shot, and the two gangs are left confronting what they have done.
  • Style and technique: It fuses ballet, jazz and Latin rhythms (including mambo and the use of the tritone in "Maria"), and tells much of the story through dance rather than dialogue, with the "Prologue" and the "Dance at the Gym" as landmarks. Songs include "Tonight", "Maria", "America", "Cool", "I Feel Pretty" and "Somewhere".
  • Cultural significance: It raised the artistic ambition of the Broadway musical, showing that dance and tragedy could drive a story; it also placed immigrant and racial tension at the center of American popular theatre.
  • Influence and legacy: The 1961 film won 10 Academy Awards, including Best Picture, with Rita Moreno and George Chakiris winning the supporting-acting awards (Marni Nixon dubbed Natalie Wood's singing). Steven Spielberg's 2021 film won Ariana DeBose the supporting-actress Oscar. Critics today also discuss the original's casting of non-Latino actors in Latino roles and its stereotypes.
  • Current status: Constantly staged by professional, school and community groups around the world; both films are available on home video and streaming (availability varies by region).
  • Note: General knowledge; dates and awards are from memory and were not source-checked today.

8) Film Appreciation Method — Lesson 94: The Cold Open (Prologue)

What it is: A cold open (or prologue) is a scene that comes before the title and the main story begins. A good one is a small complete movie: it promises a tone, shows the film's rules, and plants the question the rest of the film will answer. The question to ask is: "What is this scene teaching me about how to watch the next two hours?"

How to analyze: (1) The promise: what genre, tone and level of risk does it set? (2) A mini-story: does it have its own beginning, middle and end, with a reversal? (3) Rules and character: what do we learn about the world's logic and about the person or force the film is built around? (4) The seam: how does the film mark the move into the main story (a title card, a music cue, a cut to a different place and time)? (5) The rhyme: does the ending return to the opening's situation, image or theme? (compare with the earlier lesson on opening and closing image pairs).

Worked example — Inglourious Basterds (2009, dir. Quentin Tarantino), Chapter One, the farmhouse scene: In 1941 occupied France, a dairy farmer (Perrier LaPadite) is chopping wood when SS Colonel Hans Landa (Christoph Waltz) arrives to question him about missing Jewish families. What to look for and why it works: (a) Promise. The scene is almost all conversation at a kitchen table, yet it is as tense as a thriller: the film says that talk will be its weapon. (b) A mini-story. Politeness (a glass of milk, praise for the farm) turns, step by step, into a confession, and then into a massacre; there is a clear reversal when Landa switches from French to English so the hidden family under the floorboards cannot follow. (c) Character. We learn exactly who Landa is: charming, patient, and terrifying; the later film depends on this. (d) Cinematic craft. The camera cuts between close-ups of the two men and, at the key moment, looks down through the floor at the hidden family, so the audience knows what the characters at the table are pretending not to know (dramatic irony). (e) The seam. The one survivor, Shosanna, runs across the field, and then the title card "Chapter One" sets up the film's novel-like structure. The scene works because it is self-contained but leaves a loose end (Shosanna's escape) that pays off later.

Practice: Watch only the first five minutes of a film you know well, then write down the tone it promises, one rule of the world it teaches, and the exact moment it "begins" the main story. Then check whether the final scene answers or echoes the opening.

4) 道德經 — 第 11 章:三十輻共一轂 (Sānshí fú gòng yī gū) —「三十根輻條」

系列說明:延續 10 月 22 日開始的道德經系列,每天依序一章(第 1-81 章),不重複。

原文(王弼本):三十輻共一轂,當其無,有車之用。埏埴以為器,當其無,有器之用。鑿戶牖以為室,當其無,有室之用。故有之以為利,無之以為用。

白話翻譯(意譯):三十根輻條集中到一個車轂,正是中間那個空的地方,車子才有用處。揉合黏土做成器皿,正是器皿中空的地方,才有器皿的用處。開鑿門窗蓋成房間,正是房間空的地方,才有房間的用處。所以「有」帶來便利,「無」發揮作用。

解說:這章是《道德經》最常被引用的章節之一,因為論證非常具體:三樣日常物品(車輪、陶器、房間),各自由「不存在的部分」所定義。輻條、黏土與牆壁是看得見、買得到的「有」;車轂的孔、碗的中空、房間的空間則是東西真正發揮功能的「無」。最後一句點出原則:「有」提供利益,「無」提供用處。前一章談柔軟與無為的修練,這一章則說明「空」不是缺少。現代的讀法:好設計要留白(版面的空白、音樂的休止、行程的緩衝),心裡留有空間,才容得下新的東西。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「Trololo」/ Trololo 大叔(愛德華·希爾,1976 年歌曲,2009 年爆紅)

  • 名稱:「Trololo」(又稱「Mr. Trololo」、「Trololo Man」),這是蘇聯男中音愛德華·希爾(Eduard Khil)1976 年一首錄音的綽號,正式歌名為〈Я очень рад, ведь я, наконец, возвращаюсь домой〉(「我非常高興,因為我終於要回家了」)。
  • 起源/歷史:希爾(1934-2012)是知名的蘇聯歌手。這首歌由阿爾卡迪·奧斯特羅夫斯基作曲,1976 年錄製。據說原本的歌詞被蘇聯審查單位拒絕,於是希爾改以無歌詞的哼唱(「啦啦啦」/「tro-lo-lo」)演出。後來流傳的是一段 1970 年代末或 1980 年代初的電視演出,片中希爾穿著棕色西裝。
  • 如何爆紅:這段影片 2009 年被上傳到網路,在 2009 年底至 2010 年間於 YouTube 與論壇爆紅,觀看數達數千萬。英語網友把它叫做「Trololo」。它成為惡作劇與「誘餌」影片,是瑞克搖(Rickroll)的表親(歌曲過度開朗本身就是笑點)。
  • 意義/使用情境:沒有歌詞,因此沒有固定意義:用作惡作劇連結、荒謬而停不下來的好心情的象徵,或對荒唐情境的反應。混音與翻唱都用它辨識度很高的「喔喔喔」旋律。
  • 變化/演變:有無數的惡搞、混音與翻唱,包括其他歌手的版本;希爾以幽默的態度面對成名,後來重新錄製並在晚年多次演出。他於 2012 年 1 月去世。
  • 目前狀況:屬於早期 YouTube 的「經典」迷因,被人懷念;原始影片仍在網路上。
  • 備註:為一般性知識;日期、歌詞審查的說法與觀看數都是概數,今天未經來源查證。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《西城故事》West Side Story(舞台劇 1957;電影 1961)

  • 名稱:《西城故事》(West Side Story),一部把《羅密歐與茱麗葉》搬到 1950 年代紐約的美國音樂劇。
  • 主要人物:傑羅姆·羅賓斯(Jerome Robbins)構想並編舞;亞瑟·勞倫茲(Arthur Laurents)編劇;里奧納德·伯恩斯坦(Leonard Bernstein)作曲;史蒂芬·桑坦(Stephen Sondheim)作詞(他首次以作詞人身分登上百老匯)。1961 年電影由勞勃·懷斯(Robert Wise)與羅賓斯共同執導。
  • 時代/背景:1957 年 9 月 26 日在百老匯的冬園劇院(Winter Garden Theatre)首演。羅賓斯最初構想是紐約猶太人與天主教徒的衝突故事,後來改成噴射機幫(Jets,白人勞工階級青少年)與鯊魚幫(Sharks,波多黎各移民),反映當時上西城的移民與幫派緊張關係。
  • 劇情概要:前噴射機幫成員東尼愛上鯊魚幫首領貝爾納多的妹妹瑪麗亞。一場械鬥造成貝爾納多與東尼的朋友瑞夫死亡;東尼殺了貝爾納多,戀人的希望崩潰成悲劇。與莎士比亞不同,結局不是雙雙自殺:東尼中槍身亡,兩個幫派被迫面對自己造成的後果。
  • 風格與技法:融合芭蕾、爵士與拉丁節奏(包括曼波,以及〈Maria〉使用的三全音),大量透過舞蹈而非對白敘事,「序幕」與「體育館舞會」是經典段落。歌曲包括〈Tonight〉、〈Maria〉、〈America〉、〈Cool〉、〈I Feel Pretty〉與〈Somewhere〉。
  • 文化意義:它提高了百老匯音樂劇的藝術企圖,證明舞蹈與悲劇可以推動故事;也把移民與種族緊張關係放到美國大眾戲劇的中心。
  • 影響與遺產:1961 年電影贏得 10 項奧斯卡獎,包括最佳影片,麗塔·莫雷諾與喬治·查基里斯拿下最佳配角(瑪妮·尼克森替娜塔莉·伍德代唱)。史蒂芬·史匹柏 2021 年的電影讓艾莉安娜·狄波絲奪得最佳女配角。今天的評論也討論原版由非拉丁裔演員飾演拉丁裔角色,以及其中的刻板印象。
  • 目前狀況:世界各地的專業、學校與社區團體不斷重新製作演出;兩部電影都有家庭影音與串流版本(各地提供情況不同)。
  • 備註:為一般性知識;日期與獎項憑記憶寫成,今天未經來源查證。

8) 看片方法 — 第 94 課:冷開場(序幕)The Cold Open

它是什麼:冷開場(或序幕)是在片名與正片開始之前的一場戲。好的冷開場是一部完整的小電影:它承諾一種調性、展示這部電影的規則,並埋下整部電影要回答的問題。要問的是:「這場戲在教我接下來兩個小時該怎麼看?」

如何分析:(1)承諾:它設定了什麼類型、調性與風險等級?(2)小故事:它是否有自己的開頭、中段與結尾,並有一個逆轉?(3)規則與角色:我們得知這個世界的邏輯,以及整部電影圍繞的人物或力量是什麼?(4)接縫:電影如何標示進入正片(字卡、音樂提示、切到另一個地點與時間)?(5)呼應:結尾是否回到開場的情境、影像或主題?(可與前面「首尾呼應」那一課對照。)

範例 — 《決戰時刻》(Inglourious Basterds,2009,昆汀·塔倫提諾導演)第一章「農舍」那場:1941 年被占領的法國,一位酪農(佩里耶·拉帕迪特)正在劈柴,黨衛軍上校漢斯·蘭達(克里斯托夫·華茲飾)前來盤問失蹤猶太家庭的下落。看什麼、為什麼成功:(a)承諾。整場戲幾乎都是廚房餐桌邊的對話,卻比驚悚片還緊張:電影宣告「談話」就是它的武器。(b)小故事。客氣(一杯牛奶、稱讚農場)一步步變成自白,再變成屠殺;蘭達改用英語、讓藏在地板下的一家人聽不懂,是明確的逆轉。(c)角色。我們準確地認識了蘭達:迷人、耐心、令人恐懼;後面的整部電影都靠這個。(d)電影技法。鏡頭在兩人的特寫之間切換,並在關鍵時刻穿過地板往下看藏匿的一家人,讓觀眾知道餐桌邊的人假裝不知道的事(戲劇性反諷)。(e)接縫。唯一的倖存者蕭莎娜跑過田野,接著出現「第一章」字卡,確立電影如小說般的章節結構。這場戲之所以有效,在於它自成一體,卻留下一個懸而未決的線頭(蕭莎娜的逃脫),之後得到回收。

練習:挑一部你很熟的電影,只看開頭五分鐘,寫下它承諾的調性、它教你的一條世界規則,以及它「正式開始」正片的確切時刻。然後檢查最後一場戲是回答還是呼應了開場。
