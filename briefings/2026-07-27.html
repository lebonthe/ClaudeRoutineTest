3) Spanish Lesson — Day 18 (10 min, builds on Day 17)

Day 17 covered the fully irregular preterites of ir, ser, and hacer. Today introduces a whole family of common verbs that share one irregular pattern in the simple past — the "u-stem" preterites: tener (to have), estar (to be/be located), and poder (to be able to).

  • ¿Tuviste tiempo de hacer la tarea? (too-VEES-teh tee-EHM-poh deh ah-SEHR lah tah-REH-ah) — Did you have time to do the homework?
  • No tuve tiempo porque estuve todo el día en el trabajo. (noh TOO-veh tee-EHM-poh POHR-keh ehs-TOO-veh TOH-doh el DEE-ah en el trah-BAH-hoh) — I didn't have time because I was at work all day.
  • ¿Pudiste terminar el proyecto? (poo-DEES-teh tehr-mee-NAHR el proh-YEHK-toh) — Were you able to finish the project?
  • Sí, pude terminarlo anoche. (see POO-deh tehr-mee-NAHR-loh ah-NOH-cheh) — Yes, I managed to finish it last night.

Grammar note: tener, estar, and poder all shift to an irregular stem containing "u" in the preterite (tuv-, estuv-, pud-) and share one set of endings: -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -ieron — notably with no written accents on the yo/él forms, unlike regular preterites (hablé, habló). Poder in the preterite carries a special nuance: pude/pudo often means "managed to (succeed at)," while the negative no pude/no pudo means "failed to (despite trying)" — a sharper, more event-specific meaning than English "couldn't," setting up a future contrast with the imperfect podía (was generally able to, regardless of whether it was attempted).

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 18: 蠱 (Gǔ) — "Work on What Has Been Spoiled" (Mountain over Wind). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary explains the transition directly: "以喜隨人者必有事,故受之以蠱" — one who follows others merely out of pleasure (Hexagram 17, 隨, Following) will inevitably run into matters that need managing, hence Gǔ follows Suí; the character 蠱 itself depicts insects/parasites corrupting a vessel of food, symbolizing decay or corruption that has set in and now must be actively remedied. The trigram image is wind trapped low beneath a mountain — stagnant air that cannot circulate, letting rot take hold. The Judgment reads: "WORK ON WHAT HAS BEEN SPOILED has supreme success. It furthers one to cross the great water. Before the starting point, three days. After the starting point, three days." The Image adds: "The wind blows low on the mountain: the image of DECAY. Thus the superior man stirs up the people and strengthens their spirit." Classical commentary reads this hexagram as fundamentally hopeful despite its subject: decay, once recognized, is entirely reparable through decisive, well-timed work — the "three days before, three days after" refrain counsels careful planning before acting and follow-through after, rather than either reckless haste or paralysis in the face of accumulated corruption.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Kazakhstan

  • Location: A landlocked Central Asian nation bordering Russia to the north, China to the east, and Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, and Turkmenistan to the south, with a long coastline on the (also landlocked) Caspian Sea to the west.
  • Territory: About 2.725 million km² — the world's largest landlocked country and the 9th-largest country overall, roughly 75 times the size of Taiwan (~36,200 km²) and comparable in scale to Western Europe. Its capital is Astana (renamed from Akmola in 1998; briefly called Nur-Sultan from 2019-2022 in honor of former president Nazarbayev before reverting to Astana).
  • Population: Roughly 21.08 million (2026) — the world's 65th most populous country, about 65% urban and rising; ethnic Kazakhs make up just over 60% of the population, with ethnic Russians constituting roughly a quarter.
  • Religion: Predominantly Sunni Islam (around 70%), with a substantial Russian Orthodox Christian minority (roughly 26%) reflecting the large ethnic-Russian population; the state is constitutionally secular, a legacy of its Soviet past.
  • Economy: Oil and gas dominate, accounting for roughly 35% of GDP and 75% of exports; nominal GDP is estimated at about US$360 billion for 2026 (48th largest nominal economy, 36th by PPP). Kazakhstan is also the world's largest uranium producer and a major wheat exporter, though inflation accelerated sharply to 12.3% by December 2025 amid rising import costs and utility-tariff hikes.
  • History: Historically home to nomadic Turkic and Mongol steppe confederations along the Silk Road; gradually absorbed into the Russian Empire through the 18th and 19th centuries. Forced Soviet collectivization in the 1930s caused a catastrophic famine (the Asharshylyk) that killed a large share of the ethnic Kazakh population; the territory also hosted the Semipalatinsk Soviet nuclear-testing site. Kazakhstan declared independence in December 1991 as the USSR dissolved, and was led for nearly three decades by Nursultan Nazarbayev, whose political dominance was decisively curtailed after violent nationwide unrest in January 2022 ("Bloody January").
  • Current leader: President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, in office since March 2019, who consolidated power in the wake of the 2022 unrest and has pursued modest political and economic reforms since.
  • Language: Kazakh (a Turkic language) is the official state language, while Russian retains official status as the language of interethnic communication and remains dominant in business, government, and much of daily urban life; Kazakhstan is also gradually transitioning Kazakh's writing system from Cyrillic to Latin script.
    Sources: worldpopulationreview.com, en.wikipedia.org, usnews.com

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Harlem Shake

  • Name: "Harlem Shake" (meme) — notably unrelated to the actual Harlem Shake, a street dance that originated in 1980s Harlem, New York City.
  • Origin/history: Named after producer Baauer's 2012 electronic track "Harlem Shake." In January 2013, YouTube personality Filthy Frank (George Miller) posted a comedy sketch of himself and friends dancing outrageously in costumes to the track's bass drop — that video became the meme's template.
  • How it became popular: Parody videos following Filthy Frank's format went viral in early February 2013; the trend exploded so fast that, according to YouTube Trends, over 4,000 Harlem Shake videos were being uploaded per day at its peak on February 10, 2013.
  • Meaning/usage: A roughly 30-second clip in which one person (often masked or helmeted) dances alone amid a group standing stock-still; after about 15 seconds the bass drops, and everyone suddenly erupts into wild, costumed dancing simultaneously.
  • Variations/evolution: The format's short length and loose rules made it endlessly replicable — sports teams, offices, schools, and military units all produced their own versions (some participants got into real trouble for filming them, including workers disciplined for on-the-job videos).
  • Current status: Regarded as the archetypal flash-in-the-pan viral craze of the early social-video era — its explosive rise and equally fast fade have made it a textbook example in discussions of viral-meme lifecycle, occasionally revived today only as nostalgic throwback content.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Seven Samurai (1954, dir. Akira Kurosawa)

  • Name: Seven Samurai (七人の侍, Shichinin no Samurai, 1954).
  • Key figures/creators: Directed by Akira Kurosawa, co-written with Shinobu Hashimoto and Hideo Oguni; starring Toshiro Mifune as the wild, unpolished Kikuchiyo and Takashi Shimura as the veteran ronin leader Kambei.
  • Era/background: Made in postwar Japan specifically to revive jidaigeki (period film), a genre Kurosawa felt had gone creatively stagnant; set at the very end of Japan's turbulent Sengoku ("Warring States") period, around 1586.
  • Plot/content overview: A poor farming village, repeatedly raided by bandits, recruits seven masterless samurai (ronin) — paying them only in food, since the village has no money — to train the farmers and defend the village. The film builds patiently through recruitment and training before culminating in an extended, rain-drenched climactic battle.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Kurosawa used telephoto lenses and multiple cameras shooting simultaneously to capture the battle sequences with unprecedented immediacy and geographic clarity; he famously wrote a detailed individual dossier — covering background, clothing, and even diet — for every speaking character, however minor, an approach later adopted by directors including Michael Mann and Quentin Tarantino. Weather (driving rain, wind, mud) is used throughout as an active dramatic force rather than mere atmosphere.
  • Cultural/historical significance: At three and a half hours, its epic scale was unprecedented for the period-film genre; the story's portrait of farmers and samurai uniting to defend their own land resonated deeply with a Japan still processing wartime devastation and postwar class tension.
  • Influence and legacy: Widely cited as the archetype of the "team assembled for one mission" plot structure that recurs across countless later films; directly remade as the Hollywood Western The Magnificent Seven (1960); a major influence on the spaghetti Western subgenre; and a well-documented inspiration for George Lucas's Star Wars, particularly its ensemble of specialists banding together against a greater threat.
  • Current status: Restored and distributed by the Criterion Collection, and consistently ranked among the greatest films ever made in critics' polls such as Sight & Sound; regularly screened at retrospectives and repertory cinemas worldwide.
    Sources: criterion.com, theconversation.com, asianstudies.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Sound Design & Score (Lesson 4)

Lesson 1 covered mise-en-scène, Lesson 2 covered editing, and Lesson 3 covered cinematography and lighting — all visual layers. Today's lesson turns to the layer most viewers feel most strongly but consciously analyze least: sound design and score.

The method: Start by separating diegetic sound (sound that exists within the story world — dialogue, footsteps, ambient noise, a radio playing) from non-diegetic sound (score, narration — sound the characters themselves can't hear) — and notice when a film deliberately blurs that line for effect. Then ask what the score is doing that the image alone isn't: is it telling you how to feel before you've consciously registered why (a classic score's job), foreshadowing danger the visuals haven't revealed yet, or working in ironic counterpoint against what's on screen? Pay close attention to silence — a sudden absence of score or ambient sound is frequently a more aggressive, manipulative choice than adding music would be. Finally, notice what the sound mix privileges in a given moment: a single frequency pushed to the front, a recurring leitmotif, or a mundane diegetic prop sound (a ticking clock, a dripping tap) deliberately raised far louder than realism would ever require.

Worked example — Psycho (1960, dir. Alfred Hitchcock; score by Bernard Herrmann), the shower scene: Marion Crane's murder is, famously, almost bloodless and anatomically vague on screen — rapid, fragmented editing implies the stabbing far more than it explicitly shows it. What sells the scene as visceral, shocking violence is Herrmann's cue: a shrieking, high-register string ostinato of stabbing, dissonant glissandi, timed almost exactly to the visual thrusts of the knife. Herrmann scored the entire film using only strings — an unusual, budget-driven constraint — but that timbral austerity is precisely why the shower cue's violent shriek lands so much harder against the sparse, restrained scoring everywhere else in the film. Hitchcock originally wanted the scene to play with no music at all; Herrmann insisted on writing the cue anyway, and Hitchcock later credited it directly, reportedly estimating that "33 percent of the effect of Psycho was due to the music."

What to look for and why it works: (1) Sound substituting for withheld visual information — the score supplies the visceral violence that the edit and camera deliberately never show outright, doing narrative work images alone don't. (2) Timbral restriction as a form of discipline — an all-strings score is a self-imposed constraint that makes the one moment it breaks from quiet restraint into shrieking dissonance land far harder by contrast. (3) Sound design as authorship, not decoration — a director changing his own creative intention (dropping his "no music" plan) because of what a composer's cue actually accomplished is proof that score isn't added on top of a finished scene, but can be as authorial as any visual choice. This builds on Lessons 1-3: mise-en-scène is what's arranged in frame, editing is how frames are sequenced, cinematography/lighting is what frames look like, and sound/score is what a scene is made to feel like beneath and beyond what you actually see.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第十八天(10 分鐘,銜接第十七天內容)

第十七天學了 ir、ser、hacer 這三個各自完全不規則的過去式動詞。今天要介紹的是另一整個家族——共用同一套不規則變化模式的常用動詞,也就是「u 字根」過去式:tener(擁有)、estar(在/是某狀態),以及 poder(能夠)。

  • ¿Tuviste tiempo de hacer la tarea?(兔比斯貼 提恩波 貼 阿誒塞爾 拉 塔誒亞)—— 你有時間做功課嗎?
  • No tuve tiempo porque estuve todo el día en el trabajo.(諾 兔誒 提恩波 波爾給 誒斯兔誒 托多 誒爾 弟亞 恩 誒爾 特拉巴後)—— 沒有,因為我一整天都在上班。
  • ¿Pudiste terminar el proyecto?(布迪斯貼 貼爾米那爾 誒爾 波羅耶克多)—— 你有辦法完成那個專案嗎?
  • Sí, pude terminarlo anoche.(西,布誒 貼爾米那爾羅 阿諾切)—— 有,我昨晚完成了。

文法重點:tener、estar、poder 在簡單過去式裡字根都變成帶「u」的不規則形式(tuv-、estuv-、pud-),並共用同一套字尾:-e、-iste、-o、-imos、-ieron——特別注意 yo/él 形態完全不需要重音符號,這點和規則過去式(hablé、habló)不同。poder 在過去式裡帶有特殊語感:pude/pudo 常表示「(當下)成功做到了」,否定形式 no pude/no pudo 則表示「(嘗試過但)沒能做到」,語意比英文的 "couldn't" 更明確指向一次具體的嘗試結果——這也預告了未來會學到的未完成過去式 podía(泛指當時普遍有沒有能力,不一定真的嘗試過)之間的對比。

4) 易經卦象

第十八卦:蠱卦(Gǔ)——「腐敗待整」(上艮下巽,山下有風)。〈序卦傳〉直接說明了這一卦的排序邏輯:「以喜隨人者必有事,故受之以蠱」——單純因為喜悅而追隨他人(第十七卦「隨」),必然會招致需要處理的事務,因此接下來便是「蠱」卦;「蠱」這個字本身描繪的正是器皿中滋生蟲害、腐壞食物的意象,象徵已經產生的腐敗或弊病,如今需要積極整治。卦象描繪的是風被困於山下——空氣無法流通,才讓腐壞得以滋長。卦辭寫道:「蠱:元亨,利涉大川。先甲三日,後甲三日。」〈象傳〉補充道:「山下有風,蠱;君子以振民育德。」古代注疏認為,這一卦儘管主題是「腐敗」,骨子裡卻相當樂觀:腐敗一旦被察覺,只要透過果斷、掌握時機的整治工作,便完全可以修復——「先甲三日,後甲三日」這句話,提醒人們在行動之前要審慎規劃,行動之後也要徹底落實,既不可魯莽躁進,也不可在積弊之前癱瘓不作為。

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 哈薩克(Kazakhstan)

  • 位置: 位於中亞的內陸國家,北接俄羅斯,東鄰中國,南邊與吉爾吉斯、烏茲別克、土庫曼接壤,西側則有一段瀕臨(同樣是內陸水域的)裏海長長的海岸線。
  • 領土面積: 約 272.5 萬平方公里——是全世界面積最大的內陸國家,也是全球第 9 大國,大約是台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 75 倍,規模與整個西歐相當。首都為阿斯塔納(Astana,1998 年由阿克莫拉更名而來;2019 至 2022 年間曾為紀念前總統納扎爾巴耶夫短暫改名為努爾蘇丹,後又改回阿斯塔納)。
  • 人口: 約 2,108 萬人(2026 年)——全球人口排名第 65 位,都市化比例約 65% 且持續上升;哈薩克族占人口略高於 60%,俄羅斯族約占四分之一。
  • 宗教: 以遜尼派伊斯蘭教為主(約占 70%),另有相當比例的俄羅斯東正教信徒(約 26%),反映境內龐大的俄羅斯族群;受蘇聯時期歷史影響,國家憲法定位為世俗國家。
  • 經濟: 石油與天然氣主導經濟,約占 GDP 的 35%、出口的 75%;2026 年名目 GDP 估計約 3,600 億美元(名目排名全球第 48 位,以購買力平價計算則為第 36 位)。哈薩克同時是全球最大的鈾生產國與重要小麥出口國,但截至 2025 年 12 月,通膨已因進口成本上升與公用事業費率調漲而急升至 12.3%。
  • 歷史: 歷史上曾是絲路沿線遊牧突厥與蒙古部族聯盟的故鄉;18、19 世紀逐漸被併入俄羅斯帝國版圖。1930 年代蘇聯強制推行集體化政策,引發了造成大量哈薩克族人口死亡的大饑荒(Asharshylyk);境內也曾設有蘇聯的塞米巴拉金斯克核子試驗場。哈薩克於 1991 年 12 月蘇聯解體之際宣布獨立,此後近三十年由納扎爾巴耶夫(Nursultan Nazarbayev)主政,直到 2022 年 1 月爆發全國性暴力動亂(「血腥一月」)後,其政治影響力才明顯遭到削弱。
  • 現任領導人: 總統卡瑟姆若馬特·托卡耶夫(Kassym-Jomart Tokayev),自 2019 年 3 月就任,在 2022 年動亂後鞏固了權力,此後推動了一系列溫和的政治與經濟改革。
  • 語言: 哈薩克語(一種突厥語系語言)為官方國家語言,俄語則保有作為族群間溝通官方語言的地位,在商業、政府部門與都市日常生活中仍占主導地位;哈薩克目前也正逐步將哈薩克語的書寫系統,從西里爾字母轉換為拉丁字母。
    資料來源:worldpopulationreview.com、en.wikipedia.org、usnews.com

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Harlem Shake(哈林搖)

  • 名稱: "Harlem Shake"(哈林搖迷因)——值得注意的是,它與 1980 年代起源於紐約哈林區的真正街舞「哈林搖」其實毫無關聯。
  • 起源/歷史: 名稱來自製作人 Baauer 於 2012 年發表的電子音樂單曲《Harlem Shake》。2013 年 1 月,YouTube 網紅 Filthy Frank(本名 George Miller)上傳了一段搞笑短片,他與朋友們身穿誇張服裝、隨著歌曲的重低音鼓點瘋狂熱舞——這支影片後來成為整個迷因的範本格式。
  • 如何爆紅: 依循 Filthy Frank 格式拍攝的模仿影片,在 2013 年 2 月初迅速爆紅;根據 YouTube Trends 統計,這股風潮在 2 月 10 日達到高峰時,每天上傳的 Harlem Shake 影片超過 4,000 支,擴散速度極快。
  • 意義與使用情境: 影片格式大約 30 秒:一開始由一人(通常戴著頭盔或面具)獨自跳舞,周圍其他人則站著一動也不動;約 15 秒後重低音鼓點一下,所有人瞬間同時爆發,穿著誇張服裝一起瘋狂熱舞。
  • 變體與演變: 影片格式短小、規則寬鬆,幾乎可以無限複製套用——運動隊伍、辦公室、學校、軍隊單位都拍過自己的版本(部分參與者甚至因拍攝影片惹上真正的麻煩,包括曾有員工因在工作時間拍攝而遭到懲處)。
  • 目前現況: 如今被視為早期社群影音時代「爆紅又迅速退燒」的經典教科書範例——其爆炸性崛起與同樣迅速的退燒速度,常被拿來當作討論迷因生命週期的典型案例,如今僅偶爾以懷舊致敬的方式被重新提起。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《七武士》Seven Samurai(1954,導演:黑澤明)

  • 名稱: 《七武士》(七人の侍,Shichinin no Samurai,1954 年)。
  • 關鍵人物: 由黑澤明執導,與橋本忍、小國英雄共同編劇;三船敏郎飾演狂野不羈的菊千代,志村喬飾演資深浪人領袖勘兵衛。
  • 時代背景: 拍攝於戰後日本,黑澤明特意藉此片重振他認為已陷入創作停滯的時代劇(period film)類型;故事設定於日本動盪的戰國時代末期,約 1586 年前後。
  • 內容概述: 一座屢遭盜賊侵擾的貧困農村,雇用了七名無主浪人——由於村子根本沒有錢,只能以食物作為酬勞——來訓練農民、保衛村莊。影片以耐心的節奏鋪陳招募與訓練過程,最終在一場大雨滂沱中的高潮戰役畫下句點。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 黑澤明採用長焦鏡頭與多台攝影機同步拍攝戰鬥場面,呈現出前所未見的臨場感與空間清晰度;他也以為片中每一位有台詞的角色——即使是極小的配角——都撰寫詳細的人物背景檔案(涵蓋出身、服裝、甚至飲食習慣)聞名於世,這種做法後來也被麥可·曼恩(Michael Mann)、昆汀·塔倫提諾(Quentin Tarantino)等導演所採用。片中天候(傾盆大雨、狂風、泥濘)自始至終都被當作主動的戲劇力量運用,而不只是營造氣氛的背景元素。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 長達三個半小時的片長,在當時的時代劇類型中規模空前;農民與武士攜手保衛自身土地的故事,深刻呼應了戰後日本社會仍在消化戰爭創傷、階級張力猶存的時代氛圍。
  • 影響與傳承: 被廣泛視為「一群人為了單一任務集結行動」這種敘事結構的原型,深刻影響了後世無數電影;曾被直接翻拍為好萊塢西部片《豪勇七蛟龍》(The Magnificent Seven,1960);對義大利式西部片(spaghetti Western)次類型影響深遠;喬治·盧卡斯(George Lucas)也曾公開表示,《星際大戰》中一群各具專長的角色聯手對抗更強大敵人的設定,深受本片啟發。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 目前由標準收藏(Criterion Collection)進行修復與發行,並持續在《視與聲》(Sight & Sound)等影評票選中,穩居「影史最偉大電影」之列;至今仍在世界各地的影展與經典重映戲院持續放映。
    資料來源:criterion.com、theconversation.com、asianstudies.org

8) 看片方法 — 音效與配樂 Sound Design & Score(第四課)

第一課教了場面調度,第二課教了剪輯,第三課教了攝影與燈光——這三課都屬於「視覺」層面。今天要看的是觀眾感受最強烈、卻最少被有意識分析的層面:音效與配樂。

方法說明: 首先要區分劇情內音效(diegetic sound)(存在於故事世界之內的聲音——對白、腳步聲、環境噪音、角色能聽見的收音機聲)與劇情外音效(non-diegetic sound)(配樂、旁白——角色本身聽不見的聲音),並留意電影何時刻意模糊這條界線以製造效果。接著問:配樂在做什麼、是畫面本身做不到的?它是在你意識到原因之前,先告訴你該有什麼感受(這是傳統電影配樂的核心任務)?是在預告畫面尚未揭露的危險?還是與畫面內容形成反諷式的對位?也要特別留意「靜默」的運用——突然抽掉配樂或環境音,往往比加入音樂更具侵略性、更具操控力。最後,留意混音在某個時刻選擇凸顯什麼:某個被特別放大的頻率、一個反覆出現的主導動機(leitmotif),或是一個原本平凡無奇的劇情內道具聲(滴答的時鐘、滴水的水龍頭),被刻意放大到遠超寫實所需的音量。

實例示範 ——《驚魂記》Psycho(1960,導演:亞佛烈德·希區考克 Alfred Hitchcock;配樂:伯納德·赫曼 Bernard Herrmann)浴室謀殺一幕: 瑪莉安·克萊恩(Marion Crane)遇害的過程,畫面上其實幾乎不見血、身體部位也刻意模糊——快速、破碎的剪輯手法,暗示刺殺動作的成分,遠遠多過真正明確展示的部分。真正讓這場戲感覺猙獰、令人震驚的,是赫曼的配樂:一段尖銳的高音域弦樂顫音,伴隨刺耳、不和諧的滑音,幾乎精準對應著刀子每一次刺下的視覺動作。赫曼為整部電影只使用弦樂配器——這是一項出於預算考量的特殊限制——但正是這種音色上的克制,才讓浴室這一幕突如其來的尖叫聲,在全片其餘部分節制、稀疏的配樂襯托之下,顯得格外猛烈。希區考克原本打算讓這場戲完全不配樂;赫曼堅持寫下這段配樂,而希區考克後來也直接肯定了它的效果,據傳他曾表示「《驚魂記》的效果有 33% 要歸功於音樂」。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)用聲音補足畫面刻意保留的資訊——配樂提供了剪輯與鏡頭刻意不直接展示出來的血腥暴力感,完成了畫面本身做不到的敘事工作。(2)音色上的自我限制是一種紀律——全弦樂配器是一種自我設限,正因為如此,當它從節制、安靜的狀態,一舉轉為尖銳刺耳的不和諧音時,對比效果才格外強烈。(3)音效設計是一種作者性,而非裝飾——一位導演因為作曲家配樂實際達成的效果,而改變自己原本的創作意圖(放棄「不配樂」的想法),正證明了配樂並非事後加在完成畫面上的裝飾,而可以和任何視覺選擇一樣具有作者性。這堂課承接第一至三課:場面調度是畫面之內安排了什麼,剪輯是畫面如何被排列剪接,攝影與燈光決定畫面看起來、感覺起來是什麼樣子,而音效與配樂,則是在你實際「看到」的畫面之下與之外,決定了整場戲「感覺起來」是什麼樣子。
