3) Spanish Lesson — Day 48 (10 min, impersonal & passive "se")

Today's lesson gives the pronoun se its third and final identity, distinct from Day 46's reflexive se (subject acting on itself) and Day 47's reciprocal se (multiple subjects acting on each other): a construction with no identifiable agent at all. This splits into two related patterns spelled identically but governed by opposite agreement rules. Impersonal se pairs with a verb that has no concrete noun available to serve as grammatical subject (Se vive bien aquí, "One lives well here"; Se dice que..., "It's said that..."), and the verb is always fixed in the third-person singular no matter how many people are actually implied. Passive se (voz pasiva refleja), by contrast, promotes the thing being acted upon into the grammatical subject, so the verb must agree with it in number: Se vende casa ("House for sale") vs. Se venden casas ("Houses for sale"). A genuinely tricky edge case arises when the object sought is a specific person marked with the personal "a" (Se busca a los voluntarios) — since a personal-a-marked noun cannot itself be a grammatical subject, this technically falls under impersonal se plus a direct object, and prescriptive usage often keeps the verb singular even when the implied people are plural, though colloquial usage varies.

  • Aquí se habla español. — Spanish is spoken here.
  • Se venden casas nuevas en este barrio. — New houses are for sale in this neighborhood.
  • Se dice que va a llover mañana. — It's said that it's going to rain tomorrow.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 48: 井 (Jǐng) — "The Well" (Water above, Wind/Wood below — Kǎn over Xùn: the image of a wooden well-mechanism drawing water up from below). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary explains the transition directly from yesterday's Hexagram 47: "困乎上者必反下,故受之以井。" ("One who is exhausted at the heights must inevitably turn back and return below; therefore Kùn [Oppression] is followed by Jǐng [the Well].") Where Hexagram 47 depicted resources visibly draining away under pressure at the top, Hexagram 48 answers that exhaustion by returning to the deepest, most basic thing of all — the communal well — a resource that, unlike a person's fortunes, cannot itself be exhausted by use. The Judgment reads: "井,改邑不改井,無喪無得,往來井井。汔至,亦未繘井,羸其瓶,凶。" ("THE WELL. The town may be changed, but the well cannot be changed. It neither decreases nor increases; people come and go and draw from it. If one gets down almost to the water and the rope does not go all the way, or the jug breaks, it brings misfortune.") A town (邑) can be relocated, rebuilt, or abandoned, but the well beneath it endures unchanged — a symbol of a constancy that outlasts every surface-level social or political rearrangement. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "巽乎水而上水,井。井養而不窮也。改邑不改井,乃以剛中也。汔至亦未繘井,未有功也。羸其瓶,是以凶也。" ("[The lower trigram] penetrates into the water and draws it upward — this is the Well. The Well nourishes, and is never exhausted. 'The town may be changed but the well cannot be changed' — because the ruling lines are firm and centered. 'One gets down almost to the water yet the rope does not go all the way' means no achievement has yet been completed. 'The jug breaks' — this is why it is inauspicious.") The Image commentary draws the hexagram's practical lesson from the trigram picture itself: "木上有水,井;君子以勞民勸相。" ("Wood carries water above it: this is the image of the Well. Thus the superior man exhorts the people to labor, and encourages them to help one another.") The hexagram's final warning is deliberately pointed: even an inexhaustible resource yields nothing to someone who is careless at the last step — the rope cut short, the jug shattered just before the water reaches the surface. Read together with Hexagram 47, the pair completes a small arc: exhaustion at the top (47) is answered not by argument or waiting, but by returning to what is most fundamental and constant (48) — yet even that return demands care and follow-through to actually bear fruit.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — North Korea (北韓)

  • Location: Northeast Asia, occupying the northern half of the Korean Peninsula. Borders China along the Yalu and Tumen rivers (~1,400 km) and a short ~17 km stretch of Russia in the far northeast, also along the Tumen River; borders South Korea along the heavily fortified Demilitarized Zone (DMZ), roughly following the 38th parallel since the 1953 armistice. Coastline on the Yellow Sea to the west and the Sea of Japan/East Sea to the east. Capital: Pyongyang.
  • Territory: About 120,538 km² — roughly 3.3 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), comparable to the US state of Mississippi (~125,400 km²), and about 21% larger than South Korea (~100,210 km²).
  • Population: Around 26.6 million (2026 estimate, derived largely from UN modeling since North Korea does not regularly publish independent census data); growth is very slow, with UN projections showing a peak around 2032 before a gradual decline.
  • Religion: Officially an atheist state with near-total suppression of independent religious life in practice; Juche (self-reliance ideology) and the state-orchestrated cult of personality around the Kim family function as a de facto political religion, complete with ritual practices like portrait bowing and loyalty pledges. Small, largely state-supervised communities of Buddhism (historically the most tolerated, ~5%), Christianity (under 2%, mostly practiced underground), and homegrown Cheondoism persist; a handful of showcase churches/temples exist in Pyongyang mainly for external presentation.
  • Economy: Centrally planned, isolated, and heavily sanctioned over its nuclear and missile programs; outside estimates (chiefly South Korea's central bank) put GDP around $26 billion, though official data is essentially unavailable ("a data black hole"). Several 2025-2026 reports describe an unexpected upswing — growth near 3.5% — driven substantially by revenue from military support to Russia (troops, munitions, missiles for the Ukraine war, reportedly worth on the order of $14 billion) plus expanded Chinese trade and aid. Key sectors include mining, munitions/arms exports, textiles, and chronically strained agriculture; the informal jangmadang market system, which emerged from the 1990s famine, now handles most everyday consumer trade alongside the formal planned economy.
  • History: Korea was divided at the 38th parallel in 1945 after Japan's WWII defeat, with Soviet forces occupying the north and US forces the south. The Korean War (1950-1953) followed the North's invasion of the South and ended in an armistice, not a formal peace treaty, leaving the peninsula divided roughly where it began. Kim Il Sung founded and led the DPRK from 1948 until his death in 1994, establishing Juche ideology and a one-party personality-cult state; Kim Jong Il succeeded him (1994-2011), formalizing "Songun" (military-first) policy especially after the severe 1990s famine. The nuclear weapons program, rooted in Kim Il Sung-era development, produced test detonations from 2006 through 2017, and North Korea continues developing ICBM-class ballistic missiles.
  • Current leader: Kim Jong Un, General Secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea and de facto head of state, in power since December 2011 (Kim Il Sung's grandson). He was re-elected General Secretary at the Ninth Party Congress around February 2026, and 2026 constitutional amendments have reportedly further consolidated his "monolithic leadership." Recent developments include deepened military cooperation with Russia (troops and munitions for the Ukraine war, plus a mutual defense pact with Putin), a June 2026 meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping in Pyongyang, and South Korean intelligence speculation that Kim may be positioning his daughter, Kim Ju Ae, for a future leadership role, though this remains unconfirmed.
  • Language: Korean (Chosŏnŏ), written in the Chosŏn'gŭl script (North Korea's term for Hangul, created in the 15th century under King Sejong); decades of separate development have given North Korean usage some distinct vocabulary, spelling, and stylistic differences from South Korean Hangul.
    Sources: worldometers.info, en.wikipedia.org, forbes.com, 38north.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Gigachad"

  • Name/origin: "Gigachad" is built from a real photograph of Russian fitness/bodybuilding model Ernest Khalimov, taken as part of a heavily stylized black-and-white photo series called "Sleek'N'Tears" by Russian photographer Krista Sudmalis in the mid-2010s. A link to the project was posted to Reddit's r/bodybuilding on October 15, 2017; the very next day, October 16, 2017, one of the images was posted to 4chan's /pol/ board captioned describing the man as "Gigachad. The perfect human specimen destined to lead us against the reptilians" — the first documented use of the name.
  • How it became popular: The image spread from 4chan's /pol/ into wider 4chan boards, then Reddit meme communities, then Twitter/X and eventually TikTok, following a slow-burn-then-explosion trajectory typical of 2017-2019 meme culture. Its core association is with the pre-existing "Virgin vs. Chad" comparison format (an insecure "virgin" contrasted against an overly confident, masculine "Chad"); Gigachad became the exaggerated, maximal endpoint of the "Chad" side, spawning the popular "Average Fan vs. Average Enjoyer" sub-format, which typically pairs Khalimov's image against a comedic "fan" figure, often set to a specific viral audio clip.
  • Meaning/usage: Represents an exaggerated, stoic, hyper-competent masculine ideal — the "perfect human specimen" — used both sincerely (as praise or a reaction image for "based" takes) and ironically/self-deprecatingly (mocking exaggerated masculinity, or humorously contrasting one's own mundane achievement against a "Gigachad" standard).
  • Variations/evolution: Common derivatives include face-swap edits (other people's faces photoshopped onto the Gigachad body), the "Virgin vs. Chad"/"Average Fan vs. Average Enjoyer" comparison sub-genre (now a thriving short-video format on TikTok/YouTube Shorts), crossovers with soyjak/wojak-adjacent meme ecosystems where Gigachad serves as the "chad" pole, and even commercial spinoffs like a "Gigachad"-branded cryptocurrency token.
  • Current status: Still highly active in 2026, with continued creative remixing rather than decline; "gigachad" has become fully normalized internet vocabulary, used as a casual adjective/noun in everyday online slang well beyond its original meme-format context, and is documented as an established term in Wiktionary.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Cantonese Opera (粵劇)

  • Name/key figures: Cantonese Opera (Yueju, 粵劇), a Chinese traditional performing art distinct from Peking Opera, Kunqu, and Taiwanese Opera/Gezaixi already covered in this series. Landmark performers include Hung Sin Nui (紅線女, 1924-2013), a revered "national treasure"-level actress and singer; Yam Kim-fai (任劍輝, 1913-1989), famed for her low-register voice that let her convincingly play male leads; and Pak Suet-sin (白雪仙, b. 1928), a top-tier "hua dan" (female-role) performer whose decades-long artistic partnership with Yam Kim-fai is considered one of the most legendary duos in the art form's history.
  • Era/background: Roots trace to the Ming dynasty (1368-1644), coalescing more formally in Guangzhou and the Pearl River Delta especially under the Jiajing Emperor (r. 1522-1566) and continuing through the Qing dynasty. It formed as touring troupes brought Kunqu (elegant arias, poetic librettos from Suzhou), plus the coarser Yiyang, Bangzi, and Huizhou opera styles, which local Lingnan performers fused with Cantonese-dialect vernacular music and folk ballads to create a distinct regional form, historically performed at village temple and clan rituals.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Performed entirely in Cantonese dialect (unlike Mandarin-based Peking Opera or Wu-dialect-based Kunqu), led musically by the gaohu (高胡) — a bright-toned bowed string instrument developed from the erhu in the 1920s specifically for Cantonese music — alongside erhu, yehu, yangqin, pipa, dizi, and percussion including the wooden ban clapper that governs rhythm. Uses the standard Chinese-opera five-role system (Sheng/male, Dan/female, Jing/painted-face, Mo/martial, Chou/clown) but with unique regional subtypes not found in Peking or Kunqu opera, such as Er Hua Mian (between Jing and Chou) and Gong Jiao (an elderly-male Sheng subtype), plus martial-arts-influenced stage combat and elaborate regional headdresses and costumes.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2009 (as "Yueju Opera"), jointly nominated by Guangdong, Hong Kong, and Macao — reflecting its shared role as a marker of identity across the Cantonese-speaking Lingnan region and its global diaspora, historically tied to temple worship, clan unity, and community festival life.
  • Influence and legacy / current status: Remains a living, actively performed tradition rather than a museum piece. Hong Kong's Xiqu Centre (West Kowloon, opened 2019) is the flagship modern venue: its 1,000-plus-seat Grand Theatre hosts large-scale productions, while its intimate 200-seat Tea House Theatre runs the Tea House Rising Stars Troupe, pairing a 90-minute performance with traditional tea and dim sum service. Recent programming includes the 54th Hong Kong Arts Festival's "300 Years of Cantonese Opera: The Transition" (March 2026, led by veteran performer Yuen Siu-fai) and the Black Box Chinese Opera Festival (October 2026), which pairs Cantonese and Kunqu works for direct comparison.
    Sources: ich.unesco.org, westk.hk, hk.artsfestival.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — The Dolly Zoom / "Vertigo Effect" (Lesson 34)

The dolly zoom (also called a "zolly" or contra-zoom) is produced by physically moving the camera on a dolly toward or away from a subject while simultaneously zooming the lens in the opposite direction, keeping the subject's size constant in the frame while the background dramatically stretches or compresses around it. This is distinct from Day 15's general Camera Movement lesson, which named "zoom" as one movement among several in a broad taxonomy but did not address this specific, simultaneous combination of an optical zoom against a physical dolly move, nor the disorienting perceptual effect the combination alone produces — a warping of spatial depth that no single technique (a plain zoom or a plain dolly) can achieve on its own. When you spot a dolly zoom, check what psychological state it's externalizing (vertigo, dread, sudden realization), whether the background stretches away (subject stays put while depth "drops out" behind them) or compresses toward the viewer, and whether it's deployed once as a single shock beat or used more than once as a structural motif.

Worked example: Jaws (1975, dir. Steven Spielberg, cinematography by Bill Butler) — the beach scene where police chief Martin Brody (Roy Scheider), sitting in a beach chair scanning the water, realizes in real time that a shark is attacking a swimmer. As his shock registers, Spielberg dollies the camera toward Brody while simultaneously zooming the lens out, holding his face at a constant size while the beach and crowd behind him appear to lurch and stretch away — externalizing, without a single line of dialogue or a cut to the attack itself, the exact sensation of the ground dropping out from under Brody's sense of control. Deliberately borrowed from Alfred Hitchcock's Vertigo (1958) — its own use of the technique for Scottie's fear of heights gave the effect its popular name — Spielberg's version is often cited as the second-most famous dolly zoom in film history, proof that a technique invented for one specific psychological state (vertigo) can be repurposed for an entirely different one (dawning horror) as long as the core sensation, a suddenly unstable relationship between a fixed subject and a warping world, still fits.

3)西班牙文課程——第 48 天(10 分鐘,無人稱與被動 se)

今天的課程,為代名詞 se 補上第三個、也是最後一個身分——與第 46 天的反身 se(主詞對自己做動作)、第 47 天的相互 se(多個主詞彼此互相做動作)都不同,這次的句型完全沒有任何可辨識的施動者。這又細分成兩種拼法相同、但一致性規則完全相反的句型。無人稱 se 搭配的動詞,句子裡沒有任何具體名詞可以拿來當文法主詞(Se vive bien aquí,「這裡過得很不錯」;Se dice que…,「據說……」),此時動詞永遠固定用第三人稱單數,無論實際上指涉多少人都不會改變。相對地,被動 se(voz pasiva refleja)則是把「被動作影響的東西」拉來當文法主詞,因此動詞必須依這個主詞的單複數變化:Se vende casa(房屋出售)vs. Se venden casas(房屋出售,複數)。真正棘手的例外情況,發生在「被尋找/被需要的對象」是某個特定的人、因而必須加上人稱受詞介係詞 a 的時候(例如 Se busca a los voluntarios,「尋找志工」)——由於帶有人稱 a 的名詞本身已不能再擔任文法主詞,嚴格來說這時應歸類為「無人稱 se + 直接受詞」的結構,正式書面用法上動詞經常因此維持單數,即便實際上指涉的是多數人,不過口語中的實際用法其實相當分歧。

  • Aquí se habla español. —— 這裡說西班牙語。
  • Se venden casas nuevas en este barrio. —— 這個社區有新成屋在賣。
  • Se dice que va a llover mañana. —— 據說明天會下雨。

4)易經卦象

第 48 卦:井 (Jǐng) ——「水井」(水在上,風/木在下——坎上巽下:卦象呈現以木製汲水器具,將水從底下汲取而上)。《序卦傳》直接接續昨天的第 47 卦而來:「困乎上者必反下,故受之以井。」(在高處陷入困頓窮盡的人,終究必然回歸到最底層、最根本的地方;因此困卦之後,接的是井卦。)如果說第 47 卦描繪的是資源在高處承受壓力、眼睜睜流失殆盡的景象,那麼第 48 卦回應這種困頓的方式,是回歸到一切事物中最根本、最基礎的東西——村落共用的水井——這是一種與個人際遇不同、不會因為被汲取使用而枯竭的資源。卦辭寫道:「井,改邑不改井,无喪无得,往來井井。汔至,亦未繘井,羸其瓶,凶。」(井卦:城邑可以遷移改建,水井卻無法被取代改變。井水既不會減少,也不會增加;人們來來去去,都到井邊汲水。若汲水汲到快接近水面,汲水繩卻還沒放到底,或是水瓶就在此時破損,那便是凶兆。)一座城邑(邑)可以搬遷、重建,甚至荒廢,但城邑底下的水井卻始終如一,不受影響——象徵著一種比任何表面的社會或政治變動都更為恆久不變的常道。《彖傳》進一步解釋:「巽乎水而上水,井。井養而不窮也。改邑不改井,乃以剛中也。汔至亦未繘井,未有功也。羸其瓶,是以凶也。」(下卦深入水中,將水汲取向上,這就是井卦。水井滋養萬物,卻永不枯竭窮盡。「城邑可以遷移改建,水井卻無法被取代改變」,是因為主導此卦的爻剛健而居中不偏。「汲水汲到快接近水面,汲水繩卻還沒放到底」,意味著此事尚未真正完成、尚未建立功業。「水瓶破損」,正是造成凶險的原因。)《象傳》則直接從卦象本身,點出這一卦最核心的實踐教訓:「木上有水,井;君子以勞民勸相。」(木製器具之上承載著水,這就是井卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應勉勵百姓勤奮勞動,並鼓勵彼此互相幫助。)這一卦結尾的警示格外值得留意:即便是永不枯竭的資源,若在最後一步鬆懈大意——汲水繩放得不夠長,或水瓶就在水面即將觸及之際破裂——依然會一無所獲。把第 47 卦與第 48 卦放在一起看,恰好完成了一段小小的完整弧線:高處的困頓窮盡(第 47 卦),不是靠辯解或消極等待來化解,而是要回歸到最根本、最恆常不變的事物(第 48 卦)——然而即便如此,這樣的回歸依然需要謹慎與貫徹到底,才能真正結出果實。

(第四十八個卦象登場——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 以避免重複。目前已介紹:1 乾 Qián、2 坤 Kūn、3 屯 Zhūn、4 蒙 Méng、5 需 Xū、6 訟 Sòng、7 師 Shī、8 比 Bǐ、9 小畜 Xiǎo Chù、10 履 Lǚ、11 泰 Tài、12 否 Pǐ、13 同人 Tóng Rén、14 大有 Dà Yǒu、15 謙 Qiān、16 豫 Yù、17 隨 Suí、18 蠱 Gǔ、19 臨 Lín、20 觀 Guān、21 噬嗑 Shì Kè、22 賁 Bì、23 剝 Bō、24 復 Fù、25 无妄 Wú Wàng、26 大畜 Dà Chù、27 頤 Yí、28 大過 Dà Guò、29 坎 Kǎn、30 離 Lí、31 咸 Xián、32 恆 Héng、33 遯 Dùn、34 大壯 Dà Zhuàng、35 晉 Jìn、36 明夷 Míng Yí、37 家人 Jiā Rén、38 睽 Kuí、39 蹇 Jiǎn、40 解 Xiè、41 損 Sǔn、42 益 Yì、43 夬 Guài、44 姤 Gòu、45 萃 Cuì、46 升 Shēng、47 困 Kùn、48 井 Jǐng。)

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——北韓(North Korea)

  • 位置:位於東北亞,佔據朝鮮半島北半部。與中國沿鴨綠江與圖們江接壤(約 1,400 公里),東北角則有一段約 17 公里長、同樣沿圖們江劃定的俄羅斯邊界;南邊則沿著戒備森嚴的非軍事區(DMZ)與南韓相鄰,大致沿北緯 38 度線劃分,此一分界自 1953 年停戰協定簽署後延續至今。西臨黃海,東臨日本海(東海)。首都:平壤。
  • 領土:約 120,538 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 3.3 倍大,與美國密西西比州(約 125,400 平方公里)面積相近,也比南韓(約 100,210 平方公里)大約 21%。
  • 人口:約 2,660 萬人(2026 年估計值,主要根據聯合國人口推估模型計算,因北韓並未定期公布獨立的人口普查數據);人口成長速度非常緩慢,聯合國推估將於 2032 年左右達到高峰,其後逐步下滑。
  • 宗教:官方立場為無神論國家,實務上幾乎全面壓制獨立宗教活動;主體思想(Juche,自力更生意識形態)以及環繞金氏家族打造的國家級個人崇拜,實質上扮演著一種政治化宗教的角色,配合鞠躬敬拜肖像、效忠宣誓等儀式性活動運作。境內仍存在少數、多受國家監管的宗教社群,包括佛教(歷史上最受容忍,約佔 5%)、基督教(不到 2%,多數以地下形式活動),以及本土的天道教;平壤市內另設有少數展示性質的教堂與寺廟,主要用於對外宣傳。
  • 經濟:屬於高度中央計畫經濟,長期因核武與飛彈計畫遭受國際孤立與嚴厲制裁;外界估計(主要來自南韓央行)其 GDP 約為 260 億美元左右,但官方數據幾乎完全無法取得,常被形容為「數據黑洞」。2025 至 2026 年間多篇報導指出,北韓經濟出現意外回溫——成長率接近 3.5%——主要來自對俄羅斯提供軍事支援(派遣部隊、彈藥與飛彈援助俄烏戰爭,估計總值約達 140 億美元左右)所獲得的收入,以及與中國擴大貿易與援助往來。主要產業包括礦業、軍火/武器出口、紡織業,以及長期處於緊張狀態的農業;源自 1990 年代大饑荒時期而興起的非正式「場馬當」(jangmadang)市場體系,如今已與官方計畫經濟並行,承擔了絕大多數日常民生消費交易。
  • 歷史:1945 年日本於二戰戰敗後,朝鮮半島沿北緯 38 度線一分為二,蘇聯軍隊佔領北部,美軍則佔領南部。1950 至 1953 年爆發的韓戰,源於北韓入侵南韓,最終僅以停戰協定收場,並未簽署正式和平條約,半島分裂局面大致維持在戰爭爆發前的分界線上。金日成自 1948 年建國起領導北韓,直到 1994 年去世為止,確立了主體思想與一黨專政的個人崇拜體制;其子金正日繼位(1994-2011),尤其在 1990 年代嚴重饑荒之後,正式確立「先軍政治」(軍事優先)路線。源自金日成時代發展的核武計畫,自 2006 年至 2017 年間陸續進行多次核試爆,北韓目前仍持續研發洲際彈道飛彈等級的武器。
  • 現任領導人:金正恩,朝鮮勞動黨總書記,實質國家元首,自 2011 年 12 月掌權至今(金日成之孫)。他已於 2026 年 2 月左右召開的第九次黨代表大會上獲得連任總書記,而 2026 年的修憲內容據報進一步鞏固了他的「一元化領導」體制。近期動態包括:與俄羅斯深化軍事合作(派遣部隊與提供彈藥支援俄烏戰爭,並與普丁簽署共同防禦協定)、2026 年 6 月於平壤與中國國家主席習近平會晤,以及南韓情報單位推測金正恩可能正為女兒金主愛安排未來接班之路——不過此點目前仍屬臆測,尚未獲得證實。
  • 語言:韓語(朝鮮語,Chosŏnŏ),使用「朝鮮文」書寫(北韓對諺文的稱呼,由 15 世紀朝鮮世宗大王創制);歷經數十年各自獨立發展,北韓的用語在詞彙、拼寫與文體風格上,與南韓的諺文用法已出現若干差異。
    來源:worldometers.info、en.wikipedia.org、forbes.com、38north.org

6)網路迷因介紹——「Gigachad」

  • 名稱/起源:「Gigachad」源自一張真實照片,主角是俄羅斯健身/健美模特兒厄尼斯特·哈利莫夫(Ernest Khalimov),照片出自俄羅斯攝影師克莉絲塔·蘇德馬利斯(Krista Sudmalis)於 2010 年代中期拍攝、風格高度渲染的黑白攝影系列作品「Sleek'N'Tears」。2017 年 10 月 15 日,該系列作品的連結被貼上 Reddit 的 r/bodybuilding 版;隔天,也就是 2017 年 10 月 16 日,其中一張照片被貼上 4chan 的 /pol/ 版,並附上文字描述這名男子為「Gigachad。命中注定要帶領我們對抗爬蟲人的完美人類標本」——這是目前有紀錄可查、「Gigachad」這個名稱最早的使用案例。
  • 如何走紅:這張圖片從 4chan 的 /pol/ 版擴散到 4chan 其他版面,接著傳入 Reddit 各大迷因社群,再擴散到 Twitter/X,最終蔓延至 TikTok,走的是 2017 至 2019 年間迷因文化典型的「先緩慢醞釀、後爆發式擴散」路線。它的核心關聯,是先前就已存在的「Virgin vs. Chad」(魯蛇 vs. 型男)對比迷因格式——將缺乏自信的「魯蛇」與過度自信、陽剛味十足的「型男(Chad)」相互對比;Gigachad 則成為「型男」一端被誇大到極致的終極版本,並衍生出廣受歡迎的「Average Fan vs. Average Enjoyer」(一般粉絲 vs. 一般愛好者)子格式,通常將哈利莫夫的照片,與某位搞笑「粉絲」角色相互對比,並經常搭配某段特定的爆紅音訊片段使用。
  • 意義/使用情境:代表一種被誇大、沉著冷靜、能力超群到近乎不真實的陽剛理想形象——「完美人類標本」——使用時可以是真心的(用來稱讚或作為「有夠中肯」發言的反應圖),也可以是反諷/自嘲式的(嘲諷過度誇張的陽剛氣質,或用來幽默地拿自己平凡無奇的成就,對比「Gigachad 等級」的成就)。
  • 變體/演化:常見衍生用法包括:把其他人的臉換到 Gigachad 的身體上(換臉迷因)、「Virgin vs. Chad」/「Average Fan vs. Average Enjoyer」對比子類型(如今已是 TikTok/YouTube Shorts 上蓬勃發展的短影音格式)、與 soyjak/wojak 系迷因生態圈的交叉運用(Gigachad 在其中扮演「型男」一端的代表角色),甚至衍生出商業化的周邊產品,例如以「Gigachad」為名的加密貨幣代幣。
  • 目前狀態:截至 2026 年仍相當活躍,持續有創意十足的新變體產生,而非逐漸退燒;「gigachad」一詞如今已完全融入日常網路用語,常被當作口語形容詞/名詞使用,用法早已超出原本的迷因圖片格式本身,並已被 Wiktionary 收錄為正式詞條。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、dailydot.com、en.wiktionary.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——粵劇(Cantonese Opera)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:粵劇(Yueju),是中國傳統表演藝術的一支,與本系列已介紹過的京劇、崑曲、台灣歌仔戲皆屬不同體系。代表性藝人包括紅線女(1924-2013),備受推崇、堪稱「國寶級」的粵劇名伶與歌唱家;任劍輝(1913-1989),以其低音域嗓音聞名,能極具說服力地反串男性主角;以及白雪仙(1928 年生),頂尖的「花旦」(女性角色)演員,她與任劍輝長達數十年的藝術搭檔關係,被視為粵劇史上最傳奇的搭檔組合之一。
  • 時代/背景:粵劇的根源可追溯至明代(1368-1644),並在嘉靖皇帝(在位 1522-1566)年間於廣州與珠江三角洲一帶逐漸成形,發展歷程延續至清代。其形成過程,源於外地巡演戲班將崑曲(源自蘇州,以優雅唱腔與詩化劇本著稱)、以及較為粗獷的弋陽腔、梆子腔與徽劇等戲曲風格帶入嶺南地區,當地藝人再將這些外來風格,與粵語方言民間音樂及民謠融合,發展出獨具地方特色的戲曲形式,歷史上常於村落廟會與宗族祭祀場合演出。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:全劇以粵語方言演出(有別於以官話為基礎的京劇,或以吳語為基礎的崑曲),音樂上以高胡為主奏樂器——這是一種 1920 年代專為粵樂改良自二胡、音色明亮的拉弦樂器——並搭配二胡、椰胡、揚琴、琵琶、笛子,以及包括木製板在內的打擊樂器負責掌控節奏。角色行當沿用中國戲曲通行的「生旦淨末丑」五大類型(生:男性角色、旦:女性角色、淨:花臉角色、末:武行角色、丑:丑角),但另有京劇與崑曲所沒有的獨特地方分支,例如介於淨與丑之間的「二花面」,以及生行中的年長男性分支「公腳」,並融入受武術影響的舞台武打身段,以及華麗繁複的地方特色頭飾與戲服。
  • 文化/歷史意義:2009 年以「粵劇」(Yueju Opera)之名,由廣東、香港與澳門三地聯合申報,獲聯合國教科文組織列入「人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄」——反映出粵劇在整個粵語通行的嶺南地區,以及遍佈全球的粵籍海外僑民社群之中,長期作為共同文化認同象徵的角色,歷史上與廟宇祭祀、宗族凝聚及社區節慶生活密不可分。
  • 影響與傳承/現況:至今仍是活躍演出的傳統藝術,而非僅存於博物館中的展示品。香港西九文化區的戲曲中心(2019 年啟用)是當代旗艦級演出場館:擁有逾千席座位的大劇院可容納大型製作演出,而僅約 200 席、氣氛親密的茶館劇場,則由「茶館新星劇團」常態演出,將 90 分鐘的粵劇演出,搭配傳統茶點與港式點心一併呈現。近期節目包括第 54 屆香港藝術節推出的《粵劇三百年:蛻變》(2026 年 3 月,由資深藝人阮兆輝領銜,從藝生涯橫跨七十年),以及黑盒中國戲曲節(2026 年 10 月),將粵劇與崑曲作品同台並陳,提供直接比較兩種戲曲形式的機會。
    來源:ich.unesco.org、westk.hk、hk.artsfestival.org

8)看片方法——推軌變焦/「暈眩效應」The Dolly Zoom(第 34 課)

推軌變焦(dolly zoom,又稱 zolly 或反向變焦)的做法,是讓攝影機實際透過推軌車朝主體靠近或遠離,同時鏡頭本身則朝相反方向變焦——使畫面中主體的大小維持不變,而主體周圍的背景卻劇烈地被拉伸或壓縮。這與第 15 天教過的「攝影機運動」概論不同:當時的課程只是在一份較廣泛的分類清單中,把「變焦(zoom)」列為眾多攝影機運動方式之一,並未特別處理「光學變焦」與「實體推軌」同時、反向並行這種特殊組合手法,也沒有談到唯有這種組合才能製造出的、令人暈眩的空間深度扭曲知覺效果——這是單獨使用變焦或單獨使用推軌,都無法達成的效果。發現推軌變焦時,可以檢視:這個手法外化的是哪一種心理狀態(暈眩、恐懼,或是猛然醒悟的瞬間認知)?背景是朝遠方被拉伸而去(主體待在原地不動,背後的景深彷彿「驟然塌陷」),還是朝觀眾方向被壓縮而來?這個手法是作為單一次的驚嚇強調時刻使用,還是在片中反覆出現、成為一種結構性的母題?

實例演練:《大白鯊》(Jaws,1975,導演:史蒂芬·史匹柏,攝影:比爾·巴特勒)——沙灘場景中,坐在海灘椅上、正望向海面的警長馬丁·布洛迪(羅伊·施奈德飾),在瞬間目睹一名泳客遭鯊魚攻擊,驚駭之情當場浮現。就在他震驚神情浮現的同時,史匹柏讓攝影機以推軌方式朝布洛迪靠近,鏡頭卻同步向外變焦拉遠,讓他的臉部大小在畫面中維持不變,而他身後的沙灘與人群,卻彷彿猛然傾斜、被向外拉伸而去——完全不需要一句台詞、也不需要剪接到鯊魚攻擊的畫面本身,便精準外化出布洛迪原本掌控一切的安全感,在那一瞬間徹底崩塌瓦解的確切感受。這項手法刻意向亞佛烈德·希區考克的《迷魂記》(Vertigo,1958)致敬——正是該片用這項技巧表現主角史考堤懼高症的畫面,讓這個效果從此以「暈眩效應(Vertigo Effect)」之名廣為人知——史匹柏的這個版本,經常被譽為影史上第二有名的推軌變焦鏡頭,證明了一項原本為特定心理狀態(暈眩)而發明的技巧,只要核心的知覺感受(固定不動的主體,與周遭世界忽然變得不穩定扭曲之間的落差)依然成立,便同樣能被挪用來表現截然不同的另一種心理狀態(驟然湧現的恐懼)。
