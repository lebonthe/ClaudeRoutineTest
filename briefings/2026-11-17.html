Topic: talking about people you like or dislike — caer bien / caer mal, llevarse bien / mal con, and why me gustas is risky.

🎯 Learning Goal: say whether you like someone as a person (Me cae bien), describe how you get on with people (Nos llevamos bien), talk about first impressions in the past (Al principio me cayó mal), and avoid accidentally confessing romantic interest with me gustas.

Vocabulary: caer bien (to like someone, literally "to fall well on") · caer mal / fatal (to dislike / to really dislike) · caer genial (to like a lot) · llevarse bien / mal con (to get along / not get along with) · el jefe / la jefa (boss) · el compañero / la compañera de piso (flatmate) · el compañero de trabajo (colleague) · simpático/a (nice, friendly) · antipático/a (unfriendly) · al principio (at first) · la primera impresión (first impression).

Grammar Point: (1) caer bien/mal works like gustar (Day 15): the person who is liked is the grammatical subject and the person who feels it is the indirect object. Me cae bien Marta (= Marta "falls well" on me); with a plural subject the verb becomes plural: Me caen bien tus padres. With a third party, add the clarifying a: A mi madre le cae mal mi novio. (2) caer is irregular: present caigo, caes, cae, caemos, caéis, caen; preterite caí, caíste, cayó, caímos, caísteis, cayeron (the i becomes y between vowels). Use the preterite for a first impression: Me cayó bien = "I liked him when I met him"; the present describes your current feeling. (3) llevarse bien/mal con is a pronominal (reflexive-form) verb with the person as subject: Me llevo bien con mi hermana; for a mutual relationship use the plural: Nos llevamos fatal. (4) gustar with a person usually means physical or romantic attraction: Me gustas = "I fancy you". To say a colleague is nice, use me cae bien.

Example Sentences:

  • —¿Qué tal tu nuevo jefe? —Me cae muy bien. Es simpático y nos llevamos bien. — "How's your new boss?" "I really like him. He's nice and we get on well."
  • Mi compañera de piso me cae fatal. — I can't stand my flatmate.
  • Pero al principio te cayó bien, ¿no? — But you liked her at first, didn't you?
  • A mis padres les cae genial tu novio. — My parents really like your boyfriend.
  • Me llevo mal con mi cuñado, pero sus hijos me caen bien. — I don't get on with my brother-in-law, but I like his kids.

Chapter 25 — 有物混成 (Yǒu wù hùn chéng), "There Is a Thing Formed in Chaos"

Original text: 有物混成,先天地生。寂兮寥兮,獨立而不改,周行而不殆,可以為天下母。吾不知其名,字之曰道,強為之名曰大。大曰逝,逝曰遠,遠曰反。故道大,天大,地大,王亦大。域中有四大,而王居其一焉。人法地,地法天,天法道,道法自然。

Translation: There is a thing formed out of chaos, born before Heaven and Earth. Silent! Empty! It stands alone and does not change; it moves everywhere and is never exhausted. It could be the mother of all under heaven. I do not know its name; I give it the courtesy name "the Way", and if forced to name it, I call it "Great". Great means going on; going on means going far; going far means returning. So the Way is great, Heaven is great, Earth is great, and the king is also great. In the realm there are four great things, and the king is one of them. Humans take Earth as their model, Earth takes Heaven, Heaven takes the Way, and the Way takes as its model what is so of itself (自然, zìrán).

Key ideas and traditional readings:

  • Something before Heaven and Earth: this is the Daodejing's clearest statement that the Way is prior to the cosmos. 混成 ("formed in a blend") describes something without distinct parts — the "uncarved" state that Chapters 14 and 21 also approached.
  • 字 vs. 名: in ancient China a person had a given name (名) and a courtesy name (字) used by others. Laozi says he can only give the Way a courtesy name — a polite label, not its true name — echoing Chapter 1, "the name that can be named is not the constant name".
  • 大 → 逝 → 遠 → 反: greatness flows outward, goes far, and comes back. 反 (return/reversal) is the movement of the Way, stated directly in Chapter 40: 反者道之動 ("returning is the motion of the Way"). Chapter 16's 歸根 ("return to the root") is the same idea.
  • The four great things: the received Wang Bi text and the Mawangdui silk manuscripts read 王 (king); some later editions read 人 (human beings). Either way, humans are placed inside the cosmos, not above it.
  • 道法自然: the famous last line does not mean "the Way follows Nature" as if Nature were a fifth, higher thing. 自然 means "so of itself / spontaneous". Wang Bi (226–249) explained: "to model oneself on ziran is, in a square, to follow the square; in a circle, to follow the circle" — the Way simply does not go against how things are.
  • Textual note: according to standard comparisons of the excavated texts, the line 周行而不殆 does not appear in the Guodian bamboo slips or the Mawangdui silk manuscripts; it is found in the received text.

Historical and philosophical background: Warring States thinkers argued about what rulers should model themselves on: Confucians pointed to the sage kings of antiquity, Mohists to the will of Heaven. This chapter answers with a chain — humans → Earth → Heaven → the Way → self-so — that puts even Heaven under something prior and impersonal. Later Neo-Daoist (Xuanxue) and Chinese Buddhist thinkers used this chapter to discuss whether "being" or "non-being" is the root of things.

Practical philosophical implications: (1) Labels are tools, not the thing itself. (2) Growth naturally turns back; expecting endless expansion goes against the pattern. (3) Good models come from below and around us — the ground we stand on, the seasons — before abstract ideals.

Modern reflection: markets this week show 大曰逝,逝曰遠,遠曰反 vividly: a rally that "goes far" (Taiwan's +10% week) turns back sharply. For daily life, "the Way follows what is so of itself" suggests asking, before forcing a plan, "What is this situation already doing by itself?" — and working with it, like water in a square or round vessel.

原文:有物混成,先天地生。寂兮寥兮,獨立而不改,周行而不殆,可以為天下母。吾不知其名,字之曰道,強為之名曰大。大曰逝,逝曰遠,遠曰反。故道大,天大,地大,王亦大。域中有四大,而王居其一焉。人法地,地法天,天法道,道法自然。

白話翻譯:有一個東西渾然而成,在天地之前就已存在。它無聲啊、空廓啊,獨立存在而不改變,周流各處而不會衰竭,可以說是天下萬物的母親。我不知道它的名字,替它取個「字」叫做「道」,勉強給它命名,就叫「大」。大就是流行不息,流行不息就會到達遙遠,到達遙遠就會返回。所以道大、天大、地大、王也大。宇宙之中有四大,而王是其中之一。人以地為法則,地以天為法則,天以道為法則,道以「自然」(自己如此)為法則。

重點與傳統詮釋:

  • 先於天地的東西:這是《道德經》最清楚說明「道先於宇宙」的一章。「混成」是指沒有分化出各個部分的渾然狀態——第 14 章、第 21 章也曾接近描寫這種狀態。
  • 「字」與「名」:古代中國人有本名(名)和供他人稱呼的表字(字)。老子說他只能給道取個「字」——一個客氣的稱呼,不是它真正的名字——呼應第 1 章「名可名,非常名」。
  • 大 → 逝 → 遠 → 反:「大」向外流行、走得很遠,然後回返。「反」(返回/反轉)是道的運動方式,第 40 章直接說「反者道之動」;第 16 章的「歸根」也是同一個概念。
  • 四大:通行的王弼本與馬王堆帛書作「王」;部分後世版本作「人」。不論哪一種,人都被放在宇宙「之中」,而不是凌駕其上。
  • 道法自然:這句名言不是說「道效法大自然」,好像大自然是比道更高的第五樣東西。「自然」是「自己如此、本來這樣」。王弼(226–249)的注解說:「法自然者,在方而法方,在圓而法圓,於自然無所違也」——道只是不違背事物本來的樣子。
  • 版本說明:依一般出土文獻比對研究,「周行而不殆」一句不見於郭店竹簡與馬王堆帛書,只見於傳世本。

歷史與哲學背景:戰國思想家爭論統治者應該效法什麼:儒家舉古代聖王,墨家講天志。這一章給出一條鏈:人 → 地 → 天 → 道 → 自然,連「天」都被放在一個更先、更不具人格的東西之下。後來的魏晉玄學與中國佛教思想家,都用這一章討論萬物的根本是「有」還是「無」。

實際的哲學意涵:(1)名稱是工具,不是事物本身。(2)成長自然會回返;期待無止盡的擴張,是違背這個模式的。(3)好的典範先來自腳下與周遭——我們站立的土地、四季的變化——然後才是抽象的理想。

現代省思:本週的股市把「大曰逝,逝曰遠,遠曰反」演得很清楚:一段「走得很遠」的漲勢(台股一週漲 10%)急轉回頭。在日常生活中,「道法自然」提醒我們,在硬推計畫之前先問:「這個情況本身已經在往哪裡走?」——然後順著它做事,就像水在方的容器就方、在圓的容器就圓。

Section 4 — Historical Faction: The Hanseatic League (Die Hanse)
第 4 段 — 歷史勢力:漢薩同盟(Die Hanse)

A historical commercial league that no longer exists as a political body, so this entry covers its period, leadership, territory, goals and fate rather than present-day data — plus its living legacy, relevant to the German-friend theme in Section 6.

ENEnglish version

  • What it was: a network of merchant associations and trading towns in northern Europe, centred on the Baltic and North Seas, active from roughly the mid-12th century to the 17th century. "Hanse" is an old German word for a group or company of merchants.
  • Location and reach: its core lay along today's northern German coast (Lübeck, Hamburg, Bremen, Rostock, Stralsund, Lüneburg) and Baltic ports such as Danzig (Gdańsk), Riga, Reval (Tallinn) and Visby on Gotland. It ran four major foreign trading posts (Kontore): the Steelyard in London, Bruges in Flanders, Bergen in Norway (Bryggen wharf), and the Peterhof in Novgorod, Russia. London to Novgorod is about 2,100 km in a straight line — roughly the distance from Taipei to Tokyo, or more than five times the length of Taiwan.
  • Size: it had no fixed territory and no exact membership list. At its height (14th–15th centuries) around 200 towns took part at one time or another, though far fewer attended its assemblies. Lübeck, the leading city, probably had about 20,000–25,000 inhabitants around 1400 (historians' estimates vary).
  • Government and leaders: there was no king, president or standing army. Decisions were made at the Hansetag (Hanseatic Diet), an assembly of city delegates usually held in Lübeck; the first general Diet is usually dated to 1356. Lübeck, called the "Queen of the Hanse", led in practice, and Lübeck law was adopted by many Baltic towns.
  • Political goals: to protect merchants' privileges abroad (lower tolls, legal protection, their own courts), keep trade routes safe from pirates, and secure monopolies on key goods. It used trade embargoes as its main weapon and, when needed, temporary war fleets.
  • Economy: it moved goods between east and west: grain, timber, furs, wax, amber and pitch from the Baltic and Russia; dried cod (stockfish) from Bergen; herring from the Øresund region, preserved with salt from Lüneburg; cloth from Flanders and England; wine from the Rhineland. Its typical ship was the cog (Kogge), a broad cargo ship.
  • Key events: war with King Valdemar IV of Denmark ended in the Peace of Stralsund (1370), which gave the Hanse control of fortresses on the Scanian coast for 15 years — the high point of its power. Around 1400 it fought the pirate "Victual Brothers"; their leader Klaus Störtebeker was, according to tradition, executed in Hamburg around 1401.
  • Religion and language: medieval members were Catholic Christian; after the Reformation (from the 1520s–1530s) most Hanseatic towns, including Lübeck, became Lutheran. The common business language was Middle Low German, with Latin for many documents; it left many loanwords in Scandinavian languages.
  • Decline and fate: stronger territorial states (Denmark, Sweden, Poland-Lithuania, Muscovy, England), Dutch shipping competition, and the shift of trade to the Atlantic after 1500 weakened it. Ivan III closed the Novgorod Kontor in 1494; Elizabeth I closed the London Steelyard in 1598. The last Diet met in 1669 with only a handful of cities. Lübeck, Hamburg and Bremen kept the Hanseatic title.
  • Legacy today: Hamburg's official name is Freie und Hansestadt Hamburg and Bremen's is Freie Hansestadt Bremen; German car plates such as HH (Hansestadt Hamburg) and HL (Hansestadt Lübeck) keep the "H". Lübeck's old town (1987) and Bryggen in Bergen (1979) are UNESCO World Heritage Sites, and the airline name Lufthansa contains the word. A modern city network, "The Hanse" (Hanseatic League of New Times), founded in Zwolle in 1980, links towns in more than a dozen countries. In EU politics, a 2018 group of fiscally cautious northern finance ministers was nicknamed the "New Hanseatic League".
  • Why it matters: it shows that a powerful international actor can be a network of towns and merchants rather than a state — an early form of a trade bloc. For a German friend from the north, "hanseatisch" still suggests a self-image of being reserved, reliable and businesslike.

  • 宗教與語言:中世紀的成員信仰天主教;宗教改革後(1520–1530 年代起),包括呂貝克在內的多數漢薩城市改信路德宗。共同的商業語言是中古低地德語,許多文件用拉丁文;它在斯堪地那維亞語言中留下大量借詞。
  • 衰落與結局:更強大的領土國家(丹麥、瑞典、波蘭-立陶宛、莫斯科大公國、英格蘭)崛起、荷蘭航運的競爭,以及 1500 年後貿易重心移往大西洋,使它逐漸衰弱。伊凡三世於 1494 年關閉諾夫哥羅德商館;伊莉莎白一世於 1598 年關閉倫敦鋼院。最後一次議會在 1669 年召開,只剩少數城市出席。呂貝克、漢堡、不來梅保留了「漢薩城市」的稱號。
  • 今日的遺產:漢堡的正式名稱是「漢薩自由市漢堡」(Freie und Hansestadt Hamburg),不來梅是「不來梅漢薩自由市」(Freie Hansestadt Bremen);德國車牌 HH(漢薩城市漢堡)、HL(漢薩城市呂貝克)中的「H」就是漢薩。呂貝克舊城(1987 年)與卑爾根的布呂根(1979 年)都是聯合國教科文組織世界遺產,航空公司 Lufthansa(漢莎航空)的名字裡也有這個字。1980 年在荷蘭茲沃勒成立的現代城市網絡「新漢薩」(The Hanse),連結十多個國家的城鎮。在歐盟政治中,2018 年一群主張財政紀律的北歐、西北歐財政部長被稱為「新漢薩同盟」。
  • 為什麼重要:它說明一個強大的國際行動者,可以是城市與商人組成的網絡,而不一定是國家——是早期的貿易集團。對來自德國北部的朋友來說,「hanseatisch」(漢薩式的)至今仍帶有「含蓄、可靠、講究生意信用」的自我形象。

Section 5 — Internet Meme: "Loss" (| || || |_)
第 5 段 — 網路迷因:「Loss」(| || || |_)

ENEnglish version

  • Name and variations: "Loss", "Is this Loss?", "| || || |_" (the minimalist line version), "lossedits".
  • Origin: a four-panel strip of the gaming webcomic Ctrl+Alt+Del by Tim Buckley, published on June 2, 2008. Almost wordless, it shows the main character Ethan hurrying into a hospital, speaking to a receptionist, seeing a doctor, and finally kneeling beside his girlfriend Lilah in a hospital bed — she has had a miscarriage. The strip's title was "Loss".
  • Why it spread: the comic was normally a light gaming joke strip, and readers found the sudden turn to a serious subject clumsy and tonally jarring. Communities such as 4chan and Something Awful mocked it, then began redrawing it in other styles. The four-panel layout — one figure; two figures; two figures; one standing and one lying down — turned out to be easy to abstract.
  • Meaning and usage: the joke is recognition: people hide the panel layout in unrelated images (photos, charts, logos, game screenshots, even rows of furniture) and others reply "Is this Loss?". Its most extreme form is just vertical lines: | || || |_. The humour is not about the original subject; it is about how a famously awkward comic became a pattern people can't stop seeing.
  • Evolution: late 2000s: parodies and redraws; 2010s: increasingly abstract versions, Reddit's r/lossedits, and "Loss" hidden in memes as an easter egg. It is often cited as one of the longest-lived "pattern recognition" memes.
  • Current status: a well-known niche in-joke among long-time internet users, not a current trend; it resurfaces when someone notices the pattern somewhere new. Many casual users do not know it.
  • Cultural note and caution: because the original is about pregnancy loss, joking about "Loss" can upset people who don't know the meme's history. Among people who know it, the target is the comic's execution, not miscarriage itself. Avoid using it in workplaces or with people who may be grieving.

Unit 1 — Preposition Lesson 2: above / below vs. over / under (Stage 1, basic spatial concepts).

  • Today's topic and the usual confusion: Chinese uses 在……上面/上方 and 在……下面/下方 for all four words, so learners pick at random. Typical errors: "walk under the rain", "jump above the fence", "I live under my parents".
  • Core concept: above / below = a level: one thing is higher or lower than another, like floors in a building or marks on a thermometer; they don't touch, and it need not be directly on top. over / under = a direct vertical relationship: directly on top of or beneath, often covering or touching, and over is also used for movement across something. For numbers: ages and amounts usually take over/under (children under 12); measurements on a scale usually take above/below (below zero, above sea level).
  • Examples: Our apartment is above a convenience store. / There's a lamp over the dining table. / She put a blanket over the sleeping baby. / The cat is hiding under the bed. / It was three degrees below zero on Hehuanshan. / The plane flew above the clouds. / The kids ran over the bridge. / Children under 6 ride the MRT for free.
  • Common mistakes: (1) "We walked under the rain" → in the rain (rain surrounds you; English treats weather as a space you are in). (2) "He jumped above the fence" → over the fence (movement across = over). Unit 1 — 介系詞第 2 課:above / below 與 over / under

1. 今日主題

今天只學一個核心概念:垂直方向的「上面/下面」該用 above/below 還是 over/under。中文全都說「在……上面(上方)」「在……下面(下方)」,所以很多人隨便挑一個。最常見的混淆:「走在雨中」說成 under the rain、「跳過圍欄」說成 jump above the fence、「我住我爸媽樓下」說成 I live under my parents。

2. 核心概念

  • above / below = 高度「層級」。想像一棟大樓的樓層或一支溫度計的刻度:某樣東西比另一樣「高一層」或「低一層」。兩者不接觸,而且不一定要正上方、正下方。The Wangs live above us(王家住我們樓上)。
  • over / under = 「正上方/正下方」的直接關係,常帶有覆蓋或接觸的感覺:雨傘「在頭上」、毯子「蓋在身上」、貓「躲在床底下」。另外 over 還有越過、橫跨的動態意思:walk over a bridge、jump over a wall。
  • 數字:年齡、數量多用 over/under(children under 12、over 100 people);刻度上的量測多用 above/below(below zero、above sea level、above average)。

重點:很多時候兩組都可以,差別在畫面。The lamp is above the table(燈在桌子上方的高度,客觀描述位置)和 The lamp hangs over the table(燈正對著桌子垂掛著,有「罩著」的感覺)都對。但只要牽涉「覆蓋」或「越過」,就要用 over;只要是「刻度、樓層的高低」,above/below 最自然。

  • Key figures: director Wim Wenders; screenplay by Wenders with the Austrian writer Peter Handke (who wrote the recurring poem "Song of Childhood", Lied vom Kindsein); cinematographer Henri Alekan, then in his late seventies, famous for Jean Cocteau's Beauty and the Beast (1946). Damiel falls in love with Marion, who is lonely after her circus closes, and decides to give up eternity to become human — to feel cold, taste coffee and bleed. Peter Falk, in Berlin to shoot a film, turns out to be a former angel who made the same choice.

  • Influence and legacy: a sequel, Faraway, So Close! (In weiter Ferne, so nah!, 1993), won the Grand Prix at Cannes. Hollywood remade it as City of Angels (1998, Brad Silberling, with Nicolas Cage and Meg Ryan), moved to Los Angeles. Its image of angels standing on the Victory Column (Siegessäule) and its black-and-white/colour split have been quoted by many films and music videos.

  • Fact vs. interpretation: the dates, cast and awards above are facts; readings of the film as a story about German history or about the value of mortality are critical interpretations.

Section 8 — Film Appreciation & Criticism Method, Lesson 108: Structuralist Criticism & Binary Oppositions
第 8 段 — 看片方法第 108 課:結構主義批評與二元對立

Progression: Lessons 102 (ideological criticism) and 107 (psychoanalytic criticism) began the "critical theory" stage; this lesson adds a third lens. 進度:第 102 課(意識形態批評)與第 107 課(精神分析批評)開啟了「批評理論」階段,本課加入第三種視角。

ENEnglish version

1. Today's analytical method
Structuralist criticism reads a film as a system of binary oppositions — paired opposites such as nature/culture, wilderness/civilisation, individual/community, past/future — and asks how the story arranges, tests and tries to resolve them. It comes from the anthropologist Claude Lévi-Strauss ("The Structural Study of Myth", 1955), who argued that myths work through contradictions a culture cannot easily settle. In film studies it was applied by Peter Wollen (Signs and Meaning in the Cinema, 1969, on John Ford), Jim Kitses (Horizons West, 1969, a table of wilderness/civilisation contrasts in the Western) and Will Wright (Sixguns and Society, 1975). It matters because it shows why a story feels meaningful beyond its plot: characters, places and objects carry the weight of larger ideas.

2. Detailed explanation — how to apply it

  • Step 1 — List the pairs: note places, costumes, objects and people that are clearly contrasted (inside/outside, light/dark, farm/town, soft/hard).
  • Step 2 — Group them into columns: put related terms together (e.g. "garden, family, law, future" vs. "wilderness, loner, gun, past"). The columns reveal the film's underlying opposition.
  • Step 3 — Find the mediator: look for a character, place or object that sits between the columns. Lévi-Strauss saw mediators as the key to how myths handle contradictions.
  • Step 4 — Track the ending: does the film resolve the opposition (one side wins), merge it, or leave it painfully open? The ending tells you what the story "decides".
  • Effect on the viewer: oppositions make meaning feel natural and inevitable; when a character crosses from one column to the other, we feel it as a big event even if the action is small.
  • Misconceptions: (1) It is not just "good vs. evil" — the best oppositions are values the film finds both attractive (the wild loner can be admirable). (2) It does not look at the director's intentions or psychology; it looks at the pattern on screen. (3) Don't force everything into pairs; note what doesn't fit — that is often where a film is most interesting. (4) Distinguish observation ("Shane changes from buckskins into denim") from interpretation ("this means he tries to join civilisation").

3. Concrete film example — Shane (1953), dir. George Stevens

Wyoming, around 1889, during a range war: homesteaders led by Joe Starrett (Van Heflin) want to fence and farm; cattleman Rufus Ryker wants the open range. A mysterious gunfighter, Shane (Alan Ladd), arrives, works for the Starretts, and is admired by their son Joey (Brandon deWilde). Cinematographer Loyal Griggs won the Oscar for colour cinematography; the Grand Teton mountains frame the valley.

  • Scene A — the tree stump: early on, Shane and Joe attack a huge stump Joe has fought for years. They chop and push together until it rolls out. Look for: two men in work clothes, sweat, cooperation, the land being cleared. Opposition: cultivated land vs. raw wilderness; the stump is the wilderness itself, and removing it is "civilisation" winning — through cooperation, not violence.
  • Scene B — Grafton's store and saloon: Grafton's building has a general store on one side and a saloon on the other, joined by a doorway. Shane buys work clothes in the store, then steps through the door into the saloon to buy soda pop for Joey; Ryker's man Chris Calloway mocks him and throws whiskey on his new shirt, and Shane walks away. Look for: the doorway as a border; the change from his buckskins to store-bought denim. Opposition: family/commerce/law (store) vs. male violence/drink (saloon). Shane is literally standing on the line between columns — he is the mediator.
  • Scene C — the ending: to save Joe, Shane puts his buckskins and gun back on, kills the hired gunman Jack Wilson (Jack Palance, dressed in black) and the Ryker brothers in the saloon, then rides away toward the mountains while Joey calls, "Shane! Come back!" Why it works: the community can only be secured by violence, but the violent man cannot belong to the community he saves. The opposition is "resolved" by removing the mediator.
  • Deeper interpretation (subjective): the Western as an American myth about founding a society: civilisation needs the gun at its birth, then must exile it. Will Wright classed Shane as the "classical plot" of the Western; Kitses' columns fit it closely.

4. Practical viewing exercise
Choose a film you know well (for example Parasite, 2019, or Spirited Away, 2001). Make two columns and list at least five contrasted places, objects or characters. Then answer: (1) Who or what is the mediator? (2) Does the ending choose one column, merge them, or leave the conflict open? Mark which of your statements are observations and which are interpretations.

5. Key takeaways

  • Look for paired opposites and group them into columns.
  • Find the mediator; watch what happens to it at the end.
  • Good oppositions are tensions between two values the film finds attractive, not simply good vs. evil.

6. Suggested further viewing
The Man Who Shot Liberty Valance (1962, dir. John Ford): the lawyer Ransom Stoddard (James Stewart) and the gunman Tom Doniphon (John Wayne) embody law vs. the gun; watch how the desert cactus rose and the burned-down house complete the opposition.

中繁體中文版

1. 今日分析方法
結構主義批評把電影看成一套二元對立的系統——成對的相反概念,例如自然/文化、荒野/文明、個人/群體、過去/未來——並追問故事如何安排、考驗並試圖化解它們。這個方法來自人類學家李維史陀(Claude Lévi-Strauss,〈神話的結構研究〉,1955),他認為神話是透過一個文化難以輕易解決的矛盾在運作。在電影研究中,彼得·沃倫(Signs and Meaning in the Cinema,1969,分析約翰·福特)、吉姆·基茨(Horizons West,1969,列出西部片中荒野/文明的對照表)與威爾·萊特(Sixguns and Society,1975)都運用了它。它重要的原因是:它能說明為什麼一個故事的意義超過情節本身——角色、地點、物件都承載著更大的觀念。

2. 詳細說明——如何應用

  • 步驟 1——列出對立組:記下明顯形成對比的地點、服裝、物件與人物(室內/室外、明/暗、農場/城鎮、柔/硬)。
  • 步驟 2——分成兩欄:把相關的詞放在一起(例如「田園、家庭、法律、未來」對「荒野、獨行者、槍、過去」)。兩欄會顯示出電影底層的對立。
  • 步驟 3——找出中介者:找一個位在兩欄「之間」的角色、地點或物件。李維史陀認為中介者是神話處理矛盾的關鍵。
  • 步驟 4——追蹤結局:電影是化解了對立(一方獲勝)、融合了它,還是痛苦地留下懸而未決?結局告訴你故事最後「決定」了什麼。
  • 對觀眾的影響:二元對立會讓意義顯得自然、理所當然;當角色從一欄跨到另一欄,即使動作很小,我們也會覺得是大事。
  • 常見誤解:(1)它不只是「善與惡」——最好的對立是電影認為兩邊都有吸引力的價值(狂野的獨行者也可能令人欽佩)。(2)它不看導演的意圖或心理,而是看銀幕上的模式。(3)不要硬把所有東西塞進對立組;記下不合的地方——那往往是電影最有意思的地方。(4)區分觀察(「謝恩把鹿皮衣換成丹寧工作服」)與詮釋(「這代表他想加入文明社會」)。

3. 具體電影實例——《原野奇俠》(Shane,1953),導演喬治·史蒂文斯

懷俄明,約 1889 年的牧場戰爭:以喬·史塔瑞特(范·赫夫林飾)為首的墾荒農民想圍籬耕種;牧牛大亨魯弗斯·萊克想保留開放牧場。一位神祕的槍手謝恩(亞倫·賴德飾)來到此地,替史塔瑞特一家工作,受到他們的兒子喬伊(布蘭登·德懷爾德飾)崇拜。攝影師洛伊·葛瑞格斯以本片獲得奧斯卡彩色攝影獎;大提頓山脈框住了整個山谷。

  • 場景 A——樹樁:片子前段,謝恩和喬一起對付一個喬奮戰多年的巨大樹樁。兩人一起砍、一起推,直到樹樁翻倒出土。要注意:兩個穿著工作服、滿身大汗的男人,合作,土地被清理開來。對立:開墾的土地 vs. 原始荒野;樹樁就是荒野本身,把它移除就是「文明」獲勝——靠的是合作,而不是暴力。
  • 場景 B——葛拉夫頓的雜貨店與酒館:葛拉夫頓的建築一邊是雜貨店,另一邊是酒館,中間以一道門相連。謝恩在雜貨店買了工作服,接著穿過那道門進入酒館替喬伊買汽水;萊克的手下克里斯·卡洛威嘲笑他,把威士忌潑在他的新襯衫上,謝恩轉身離開。要注意:那道門就是一條邊界;他從鹿皮衣換成店裡買的丹寧布衣服。對立:家庭/商業/法律(雜貨店)vs. 男性暴力/酒精(酒館)。謝恩實際上就站在兩欄之間的那條線上——他是中介者。
  • 場景 C——結局:為了救喬,謝恩重新穿上鹿皮衣、配上槍,在酒館裡殺了受僱槍手傑克·威爾森(傑克·帕蘭斯飾,一身黑衣)和萊克兄弟,然後騎馬往山的方向離去,喬伊在後面大喊:「謝恩!回來!」為什麼有效:社群只有靠暴力才能得到保障,但使用暴力的人卻無法屬於他拯救的社群。對立透過「移除中介者」而得到「化解」。
  • 更深層的詮釋(主觀):西部片是一則關於建立社會的美國神話:文明誕生時需要槍,之後卻必須把槍放逐。威爾·萊特把《原野奇俠》歸為西部片的「古典情節」;基茨的兩欄對照表與它非常吻合。

4. 實作看片練習
選一部你很熟的電影(例如《寄生上流》,2019,或《神隱少女》,2001)。畫出兩欄,列出至少五個形成對比的地點、物件或角色。接著回答:(1)誰或什麼是中介者?(2)結局選了其中一欄、把兩欄融合,還是讓衝突懸而未決?請標出你的句子哪些是觀察、哪些是詮釋。

5. 重點整理

  • 找出成對的相反概念,並分成兩欄。
  • 找出中介者,看它在結局時發生了什麼事。
  • 好的對立是電影認為兩種都有吸引力的價值之間的拉扯,而不只是善與惡。
