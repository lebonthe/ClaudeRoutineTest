3) Spanish Lesson — Day 54 (10 min, Verbs of Change: ponerse/volverse/hacerse/llegar a ser/convertirse en)

Today tackles a famous Spanish learners' sticking point: five distinct verbs where English and Chinese both make do with a single "become." Ponerse + adjective marks a short-lived, involuntary emotional/physical change (ponerse pálido, "to go pale"); volverse marks a more lasting, often sudden personality/state shift (volverse desconfiado, "to become distrustful"); hacerse marks a change achieved through effort or choice, often social status or belief (hacerse abogada, "to become a lawyer"); while llegar a ser (a more formal "to finally become," emphasizing a long process) and convertirse en (reserved for nouns, marking a change in fundamental nature) round out the set.

  • Cuando vio la nota, se puso pálido. — When he saw the grade, he went pale.
  • Después del divorcio, se volvió muy desconfiado. — After the divorce, he became very distrustful.
  • Trabajó duro durante años y se hizo abogada. — She worked hard for years and became a lawyer.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 54: 歸妹 (Guī Mèi) — "The Marrying Maiden" (Thunder over Lake: Zhèn 震 above, Duì 兌 below — 雷澤歸妹). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws a direct line from yesterday's hexagram: "進必有所歸,故受之以歸妹。" ("What advances must have somewhere to return to; therefore Jiàn is followed by Guī Mèi.") Hexagram 53 pictured correct advance through a bride's properly-sequenced marriage journey — proposal, gifts, a chosen date, the procession itself, each stage completed before the next began. Guī Mèi completes that image's logic by supplying the destination an advance must eventually reach, while showing what happens when the same marriage imagery unfolds in the wrong order instead. The name itself literally means "the return[ing] of the younger sister" — classically read as a maiden married off as a secondary or subordinate match (historically, an attendant bride accompanying the primary bride) rather than as the principal wife, an arrangement already carrying a built-in imbalance of position. The Judgment is unusually blunt for the Sequence: "歸妹,征凶,無攸利。" ("The Marrying Maiden. To press forward brings misfortune. Nothing is furthering.") Where Hexagram 53 promised good fortune for gradual, correctly-ordered advance, this hexagram warns that advance itself — pressing the situation further — brings misfortune here, because the underlying position is already wrong. The trigram structure explains why: Duì (lake, joy, read as the youngest daughter) sits below, with Zhèn (thunder, arousing movement, read as the eldest son) above it — a union kindled by momentary joy and attraction (Duì) that provokes impulsive movement (Zhèn), rather than the patient, properly-staged order Hexagram 53 just modeled. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "歸妹,天地之大義也。天地不交,而萬物不興;歸妹,人之終始也。說以動,所歸妹也。征凶,位不當也。無攸利,柔乘剛也。" ("The marrying of a young woman is a great principle of Heaven and Earth: if Heaven and Earth did not unite, the myriad creatures would not flourish; marriage marks the beginning and the end of human life. Joy [Duì] provoking movement [Zhèn] is what this hexagram, the Marrying Maiden, describes. 'To press forward brings misfortune' — because the positions [of its lines] are inappropriate; 'nothing is furthering' — because a yielding line rides above a firm one.") The commentary is careful not to condemn marriage itself — quite the opposite, calling it one of Heaven and Earth's great governing principles, the very beginning and end of human life — but to condemn a marriage/advance entered into out of impulsive attraction, with its lines sitting in the wrong positions and a weak element inappropriately placed above a strong one. The Image commentary reads: "澤上有雷,歸妹;君子以永終知敝。" ("Thunder above the lake: the image of the Marrying Maiden. Thus the superior man, contemplating the eternity of the proper end, recognizes what is transitory and deficient in it.") A lake's still joy stirred by thunder above it captures a passing excitement rather than a lasting bond; the superior man's response is to keep the far end — the eventual, lasting outcome — in view, so as to recognize where a present arrangement, however pleasant now, is built on a flaw that time will expose. Read together, Hexagrams 53 and 54 form a deliberate teaching pair: both use marriage as their central image, both concern how something "arrives" or "advances," yet one is patient, correctly staged, and rewarded, while the other is impulsive, wrongly positioned, and warned against — the Sequence's way of showing that the same act (union, advance, arrival) can be either auspicious or inauspicious entirely depending on whether its underlying order and position are correct.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Ukraine (烏克蘭)

  • Location: A large country in Eastern Europe, bordered by Russia to the east and northeast, Belarus to the north, Poland, Slovakia, and Hungary to the west, Romania and Moldova to the southwest, and the Black Sea and Sea of Azov to the south. Capital: Kyiv, on the Dnipro River.
  • Territory: About 603,700 km² — the largest country lying entirely within Europe (excluding Russia's European portion), comparable in size to France (~551,695 km²) or the US state of Texas (~695,662 km²), and roughly 16-17 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²).
  • Population: Roughly 36-38 million residents as of 2026 by most estimates, though this figure carries unusual uncertainty: the pre-war (2021) population was around 41-44 million, and the full-scale Russian invasion since February 2022 has driven millions abroad as refugees (chiefly to Poland, Germany, and other EU countries) and displaced millions more internally, alongside territory under Russian occupation not fully counted in government figures.
  • Religion: Predominantly Eastern Orthodox Christianity, split mainly between the Orthodox Church of Ukraine (OCU, granted autocephaly — full independence — by the Ecumenical Patriarchate in 2019) and the historically Moscow-linked Ukrainian Orthodox Church (UOC-MP), whose standing has become increasingly contested since 2022; a Ukrainian Greek Catholic (Uniate) minority is concentrated in the western regions, alongside smaller Protestant, Jewish, and Crimean Tatar Muslim communities.
  • Economy: Historically one of Europe's great agricultural powerhouses — often called a "breadbasket of Europe" for its exports of wheat, corn, barley, and sunflower oil (a major global supplier, whose wartime export disruptions rattled global food markets after 2022) — alongside significant heavy industry, steel, and a fast-growing IT/software outsourcing sector before the war. The full-scale invasion has caused massive economic damage: GDP contracted sharply in 2022 before stabilizing at a lower base, infrastructure and energy systems have suffered repeated missile/drone damage, and the economy now runs substantially on international financial aid, reconstruction funding, and wartime state spending.
  • History: Traces cultural roots to Kyivan Rus' (9th-13th centuries), a medieval East Slavic state centered on Kyiv; later periods saw the region divided among the Grand Duchy of Lithuania, the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, the Cossack Hetmanate, the Russian Empire, and the Austro-Hungarian Empire (western Galicia). Ukraine briefly gained independence after 1917 before becoming a Soviet republic; the 1932-33 Holodomor, a man-made famine under Stalin's collectivization policies, killed millions and is officially recognized by Ukraine and many other states as genocide. Ukraine regained independence in 1991 upon the Soviet Union's dissolution. Post-independence politics were shaped by the 2004 Orange Revolution (protesting a disputed, Russia-favored election) and the 2014 Euromaidan/Revolution of Dignity (which ousted a pro-Russian president after he rejected an EU association agreement), followed immediately by Russia's annexation of Crimea and a Russian-backed war in the Donbas region. Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, triggering the largest war in Europe since World War II, still ongoing as of this briefing.
  • Current leader: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, a former actor and comedian elected in 2019, has led the country throughout the full-scale war; scheduled elections have been postponed under martial law, which Ukrainian law suspends during, a point of ongoing domestic and international debate.
  • Language: Ukrainian is the sole official language; Russian remains widely spoken, especially historically in eastern and southern regions and major cities, though its everyday use has visibly declined since 2014 and especially since 2022 as part of a broader wartime shift in linguistic and cultural identity away from Russian-language association.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (demographics), en.wikipedia.org (economy)

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "One Does Not Simply" (Boromir Meme)

  • Name/origin: "One Does Not Simply" (also known as the Boromir Meme) is an image macro built from a screenshot of actor Sean Bean as Boromir in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (2001, dir. Peter Jackson), captured mid-sentence during his line "One does not simply walk into Mordor." The meme format emerged around 2011 on meme-generator sites.
  • How it became popular: It spread rapidly through meme-generator platforms like Quickmeme and Memegenerator, and communities like Reddit and 4chan, in the early 2010s "advice animal"/image-macro era; the format's simple top-line-setup, bottom-line-punchline structure and instantly recognizable, slightly pained facial expression made it effortless to caption with any new "impossible task."
  • Meaning/usage: The meme humorously asserts that some task — usually a mundane, genuinely easy one — is secretly fraught with hidden difficulty, playing on the ironic mismatch between Boromir's original life-or-death warning about a perilous fictional quest and the comparatively trivial real-world tasks (fixing a printer, quitting sugar, understanding a tax form) the format gets applied to.
  • Variations/evolution: Countless "One does not simply [X]" captions have been produced across nearly every subject imaginable (programming bugs, dieting, dating, corporate jargon); the meme helped popularize the broader "reaction image macro with ironic mismatch" genre, and inspired other Lord of the Rings-derived reaction images (e.g., "And My Axe!", Gandalf's "You Shall Not Pass").
  • Current status: Past its early-2010s viral peak but remains one of the most widely recognized foundational image macros of the "meme generator era," still occasionally revived nostalgically or ironically, and frequently cited in retrospectives on early internet meme culture as a defining example of the format.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — The Rules of the Game (La Règle du jeu, 1939, dir. Jean Renoir)

  • Name/key figures: La Règle du jeu ("The Rules of the Game"), written and directed by Jean Renoir, who also plays a supporting role as the affable outsider Octave; the ensemble cast includes Nora Gregor as Christine, Marcel Dalio as the Marquis Robert de la Chesnaye, Mila Parély, Roland Toutain, and Paulette Dubost.
  • Era/background: Produced and released in France in 1939, mere months before the outbreak of World War II in Europe, at the tail end of France's "poetic realism" film movement; Renoir conceived it explicitly as a satirical comedy of manners skewering the moral hollowness of the French upper class on the eve of catastrophe.
  • Plot/content overview: A weekend house party at the country chateau of La Colinière brings together aristocrats and their servants; overlapping romantic entanglements — the Marquis's affair, his wife Christine's flirtations, and a parallel set of romantic rivalries among the household's servants that mirror and satirize their masters' behavior — build toward a chaotic hunting-party sequence and a final, tragic shooting during a costume-party performance, presented by the household as a hushed-up "accident."
  • Artistic style and techniques: Celebrated above all for its innovative use of deep-focus cinematography and elaborate long takes with intricate, choreographed blocking across multiple planes of the frame simultaneously — actors move in complex patterns through foreground, midground, and background within single unbroken shots, letting the audience's eye roam and discover relationships rather than being directed shot-by-shot; the notorious rabbit-and-pheasant hunting sequence intercuts the literal slaughter of game with the "hunting" of romantic partners at the party, a directly stated visual metaphor.
  • Cultural/historical significance: A critical and commercial failure on release — audiences reportedly walked out and even tried to burn a cinema screen in protest — its unflattering portrait of the French elite was seen by some as demoralizing on the eve of war; French censors cut it repeatedly, and the original negative was later destroyed in an Allied bombing raid during the war. A painstaking reconstruction from surviving fragments and prints was completed in 1959, restoring it close to Renoir's original cut.
  • Influence and legacy: Film critic and theorist André Bazin's influential writings on deep focus and mise-en-scène used this film as a central case study, shaping decades of film criticism's vocabulary for discussing staging-in-depth; its ensemble, overlapping-dialogue style directly influenced later filmmakers such as Robert Altman, and it is a standing fixture near the top of critics' "greatest films ever made" polls (including Sight & Sound's decennial critics' poll) since its 1959 restoration.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Available today via the Criterion Collection's restored home-video edition and regularly programmed in film-school curricula and retrospective repertory screenings worldwide as a foundational text for studying mise-en-scène and deep-focus staging.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, criterion.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Eyeline Match (Lesson 40)

An eyeline match cuts from a shot of a character looking off-screen in a particular direction to a second shot revealing what (or whom) they are looking at, creating a purely directional, editing-constructed spatial connection between two shots that may never have been filmed anywhere near each other — distinct from Day 9's Continuity Editing/180-Degree Rule (Shot/Reverse Shot), which specifically alternates between two facing speakers across a dialogue exchange while preserving a consistent camera axis, and from Day 19's Subjective/POV Camera, which places the lens directly at a character's own eye position rather than showing both the act of looking and a separate reaction/object shot from outside that character. An eyeline match asks the audience to do a small piece of unconscious inference — "this shot must be what that character just saw" — purely from directional cues (where the eyes point, matched roughly to where the following shot's subject sits in the frame), making it one of editing's most economical tools for building a coherent fictional space out of shots that share no actual physical proximity. When you spot one, check: is it used for simple scene-establishing orientation, for delayed-reveal suspense (withholding what's seen a beat longer than the look itself), or to construct an entirely fabricated spatial or emotional relationship between people, places, or objects that were never physically together; and whether the chain of look-and-reveal is a single beat or a sustained structural device running through a whole sequence.

Worked example: Rear Window (1954, dir. Alfred Hitchcock) — built almost entirely on repeated eyeline-match chains: a shot of the wheelchair-bound Jefferies looking out his rear window, cut to what he sees across the courtyard (a neighbor's apartment, an ambiguous gesture, a suspicious absence), cut back to his reaction. None of it requires that the actor (James Stewart) and the elaborate courtyard set actually occupy real, continuous space together in front of the camera at once — the eyeline match alone manufactures the illusion that he is truly watching those specific people in that specific building. This look-cut-to-object-cut-back-to-reaction chain is the film's entire narrative engine: Hitchcock (echoing the earlier Soviet Kuleshov Effect experiments on directional editing) uses nothing but eyeline matches, repeated and varied across the runtime, to build both the courtyard's fictional geography and the mounting suspicion that drives the plot, without ever needing a single master shot containing Jefferies and the crime scene together.

3)西班牙文課程——第 54 天(10 分鐘,變化動詞:ponerse/volverse/hacerse/llegar a ser/convertirse en)

今天要處理西語學習者惡名昭彰的一大難點:英文與中文都只用一個「become/變成」打發過去,西語卻細分成五個各具意義的動詞。Ponerse + 形容詞,標記短暫、非自願的情緒/生理變化(ponerse pálido,「臉色變得蒼白」);volverse 標記較持久、常帶突然性質的性格/狀態轉變(volverse desconfiado,「變得多疑」);hacerse 標記透過努力或選擇達成的轉變,常涉及社會身份或信仰(hacerse abogada,「成為律師」);而 llegar a ser(較正式的「終於成為」,強調漫長過程)與 convertirse en(僅接名詞,標記本質上的根本轉變),則補齊了這組動詞家族的最後兩員。

  • Cuando vio la nota, se puso pálido. —— 他看到成績時,臉色瞬間變得蒼白。
  • Después del divorcio, se volvió muy desconfiado. —— 離婚之後,他變得非常多疑。
  • Trabajó duro durante años y se hizo abogada. —— 她多年努力不懈,最終成為了一名律師。

4)易經卦象

第 54 卦:歸妹 (Guī Mèi) ——「歸妹」(雷澤歸妹——震上兌下:上卦為雷〔震〕,下卦為澤〔兌〕)。《序卦傳》直接點出與前一卦的連結:「進必有所歸,故受之以歸妹。」(凡是向前推進的事物,終究必須有所歸宿、有所抵達,因此漸卦之後,接的是歸妹卦。)第 53 卦透過女子按部就班出嫁的歷程——提親、聘禮、擇定吉日、迎娶儀式本身,每個階段依序完成才能進入下一階段——來描繪正確的推進之道。歸妹卦則進一步補完這個意象背後的邏輯:推進終究要抵達某個終點,而本卦要呈現的,正是當同樣的婚嫁意象、以錯誤的順序展開時,會發生什麼事。「歸妹」二字字面意思是「妹妹出嫁、有所歸屬」——古代解讀為女子以陪嫁、側室的身分出嫁(歷史上常指陪伴正室一同出嫁的隨嫁女子),而非正室、正妻的身分,這樣的安排本身便帶有一種先天的地位失衡。卦辭在整套序卦當中顯得格外直白:「歸妹,征凶,無攸利。」(歸妹卦。若貿然向前推進,將招致凶險;沒有任何一方會因此獲利。)第 53 卦承諾循序漸進、依正確順序推進終將帶來吉祥,本卦卻警告:在此處,一味推進反而招致凶險,原因就在於根本的位置本身已然不當。卦象的組成結構說明了原因:兌卦(澤、喜悅,象徵最年幼的女兒)居下,震卦(雷、躁動的振動,象徵最年長的兒子)居上——這是一段由一時的喜悅與吸引力(兌)所點燃、進而引發衝動躁進(震)的結合,而非第 53 卦所示範的那種耐心、依序鋪陳的正確步驟。《彖傳》進一步闡述:「歸妹,天地之大義也。天地不交,而萬物不興;歸妹,人之終始也。說以動,所歸妹也。征凶,位不當也。無攸利,柔乘剛也。」(女子出嫁,是天地間極重要的大道理:倘若天地陰陽不相交合,萬物便無從興盛繁衍;婚嫁,正是人類生命循環終而復始的起點。因喜悅〔兌〕而引發躁動〔震〕,正是本卦「歸妹」所要描述的情境。「貿然推進招致凶險」,是因為〔各爻所居的〕位置本身並不恰當;「沒有任何一方獲利」,則是因為陰柔之爻凌駕於陽剛之爻之上。)這段文字並非否定婚姻本身——恰恰相反,它稱婚姻為天地間的重要法則之一,是人類生命終而復始的起點——但它所要譴責的,是那種出於一時衝動、各爻位置本身錯亂、陰柔者不當地凌駕於陽剛者之上的結合或推進。《象傳》寫道:「澤上有雷,歸妹;君子以永終知敝。」(澤水之上響著雷聲,這就是歸妹卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應當放眼長遠、著眼於最終真正圓滿的結局,方能看清眼前這段關係當中轉瞬即逝、有所欠缺之處。)平靜的澤水被上方的雷聲所激盪,象徵的是一時的興奮激情,而非長久穩固的情感連結;君子的因應之道,便是把眼光放在最終、長久的結果之上,才能看清眼前這段看似美好的安排,其實建立在終將隨時間浮現的缺陷之上。將第 53 卦與第 54 卦合併來看,兩者構成了一組刻意設計的教學對照:兩卦皆以婚嫁作為核心意象,皆探討事物如何「抵達」或「推進」,但一卦耐心、依序鋪陳、終獲吉祥,另一卦則衝動躁進、位置失當、遭到警告——《序卦傳》藉此說明:同樣一件事(結合、推進、抵達),究竟是吉是凶,完全取決於其背後的順序與位置是否得當。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——烏克蘭(Ukraine)

  • 位置:位於東歐的大型國家,東側與東北側與俄羅斯接壤,北側與白俄羅斯為鄰,西側與波蘭、斯洛伐克、匈牙利相接,西南側與羅馬尼亞、摩爾多瓦交界,南側則濱臨黑海與亞速海。首都:基輔(Kyiv),坐落於第聶伯河(Dnipro River)畔。
  • 領土:總面積約 603,700 平方公里——是完全位於歐洲境內(不含俄羅斯歐洲部分領土)面積最大的國家,大致與法國(約 551,695 平方公里)相當,或與美國德州(約 695,662 平方公里)相近,約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 16 至 17 倍。
  • 人口:截至 2026 年,多數估計約在 3,600 萬至 3,800 萬人之間,不過這項數字帶有異於一般國家的高度不確定性:戰前(2021 年)人口約為 4,100 萬至 4,400 萬人,自 2022 年 2 月俄羅斯發動全面入侵以來,已有數百萬人以難民身分逃往國外(主要前往波蘭、德國及其他歐盟國家),另有數百萬人在國內流離失所,加上部分遭俄羅斯佔領的領土,其人口並未完整納入政府統計數字之中。
  • 宗教:以東正教為主要信仰,主要分為兩大體系:烏克蘭東正教會(OCU,於 2019 年獲君士坦丁堡普世牧首正式授予「自主教會」〔autocephaly〕地位,即完全獨立地位),以及歷史上與莫斯科關係密切的烏克蘭正教會(莫斯科宗主教區轄下,UOC-MP),後者的地位自 2022 年以來爭議日益加劇;西部地區則聚集著一定比例的烏克蘭希臘禮天主教(東儀天主教)少數族群,另有規模較小的新教、猶太教與克里米亞韃靼穆斯林社群。
  • 經濟:歷史上一直是歐洲舉足輕重的農業大國——常被稱為「歐洲糧倉」,是小麥、玉米、大麥與葵花油的重要出口國(在全球市場舉足輕重,2022 年後因戰爭導致出口中斷,一度撼動全球糧食市場);戰前該國同時擁有可觀的重工業、鋼鐵產業,以及快速成長的資訊科技/軟體外包產業。俄羅斯全面入侵已造成該國經濟遭受重創:2022 年 GDP 大幅萎縮,其後雖在較低基期上趨於穩定,但能源與基礎設施系統持續遭受飛彈與無人機攻擊破壞,目前該國經濟運作,已高度仰賴國際財政援助、重建資金與戰時政府支出來維持。
  • 歷史:文化根源可追溯至 9 至 13 世紀的基輔羅斯(Kyivan Rus')——一個以基輔為中心的中世紀東斯拉夫國家;其後該地區歷經立陶宛大公國、波蘭立陶宛聯邦、哥薩克酋長國(Cossack Hetmanate)、俄羅斯帝國,以及奧匈帝國(西部加利西亞地區)等勢力先後統治瓜分。1917 年後烏克蘭曾短暫獨立,其後成為蘇聯加盟共和國之一;1932 至 1933 年間,史達林推行集體化政策所導致的人為大饑荒「霍洛多莫爾」(Holodomor)奪走數百萬人性命,烏克蘭及許多其他國家已正式將其認定為種族滅絕事件。烏克蘭於 1991 年蘇聯解體之際重新獲得獨立。獨立後的政治發展,深受 2004 年「橙色革命」(抗議一場遭質疑、有利於親俄候選人的選舉舞弊)與 2014 年「歐洲廣場革命/尊嚴革命」(在時任親俄總統拒絕簽署歐盟聯繫協定後,將其罷黜下台)所形塑,緊接著俄羅斯隨即併吞克里米亞,並在頓巴斯地區扶植親俄勢力發動戰爭。俄羅斯已於 2022 年 2 月對烏克蘭發動全面入侵,引爆二戰以來歐洲規模最大的戰爭,截至本篇簡報發布時仍持續進行中。
  • 現任領導人:總統弗拉迪米爾·澤倫斯基(Volodymyr Zelenskyy),曾任演員與喜劇演員,於 2019 年當選總統,自全面戰爭爆發以來持續領導該國;依烏克蘭法律規定,戒嚴狀態下應暫停選舉,原訂選舉因而多次延後,此舉在該國國內與國際社會均引發持續爭論。
  • 語言:烏克蘭語為唯一官方語言;俄語在歷史上、尤其在東部與南部地區及各大城市中仍廣泛使用,但自 2014 年、尤其是 2022 年戰爭爆發以來,俄語的日常使用比例已明顯下滑,反映出戰時該國語言與文化認同,正加速擺脫與俄語之間的連結。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、en.wikipedia.org(人口)、en.wikipedia.org(經濟)

6)網路迷因介紹——「哪有這麼簡單」(One Does Not Simply,波羅莫迷因)

  • 名稱/起源:「One Does Not Simply」(直譯「事情才沒有這麼簡單」,又稱「波羅莫迷因」Boromir Meme)是一張取材自電影《魔戒首部曲:魔戒現身》(The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring,2001,導演:彼得·傑克森)的截圖迷因——擷取自演員西恩·賓(Sean Bean)飾演的波羅莫,說出「One does not simply walk into Mordor.」(想直接走進魔多,可沒那麼簡單)這句台詞時,話說到一半的表情瞬間。此迷因格式大約於 2011 年,在各大迷因產生器網站上興起。
  • 如何走紅:此迷因透過 Quickmeme、Memegenerator 等迷因產生器平台,以及 Reddit、4chan 等社群迅速擴散,正值 2010 年代初期「建議動物」(advice animal)/圖像迷因盛行的年代;其格式結構簡單——上方鋪陳情境、下方揭曉吐槽笑點,再搭配波羅莫那個瞬間、略帶痛苦掙扎的辨識度極高的表情,讓任何人都能輕鬆為各種「看似不可能的任務」自行配上文字。
  • 意義/使用情境:這則迷因幽默地主張某件事——通常是件再平凡、再簡單不過的日常瑣事——其實暗藏著不為人知的重重困難,巧妙運用波羅莫原本那句攸關生死、警告眾人踏上凶險虛構征途的台詞,與被套用進來的、相對瑣碎的現實任務(修印表機、戒糖、看懂一張報稅表)之間的荒謬落差,製造反諷笑點。
  • 變體/演化:「One does not simply [某件事]」的文字接龍幾乎被套用在任何想像得到的主題上(程式除錯、減肥、約會、辦公室術語);這則迷因也帶動了更廣泛的「反諷落差型反應圖迷因」類型的流行,並進一步啟發了其他取材自《魔戒》的反應圖迷因(例如「And My Axe!」〔還有我的斧頭!〕,以及甘道夫的「You Shall Not Pass」〔你休想通過!〕)。
  • 目前狀態:雖已過了 2010 年代初期病毒式擴散的高峰期,但至今仍是「迷因產生器時代」最廣為人知的經典圖像迷因之一,偶爾仍會被懷舊或反諷性地重新翻出使用,也經常在回顧早期網路迷因文化的文章中,被引用作為該類型格式的代表性範例。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《遊戲規則》(La Règle du jeu, 1939,導演:尚·雷諾)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《遊戲規則》(La Règle du jeu)由尚·雷諾(Jean Renoir)編劇並執導,雷諾本人亦在片中飾演一位討喜的局外人角色屋大維(Octave);其餘主要演員陣容包括飾演克莉絲汀(Christine)的諾拉·葛雷格爾(Nora Gregor)、飾演侯爵羅伯·德拉切奈(Robert de la Chesnaye)的馬歇爾·達里歐(Marcel Dalio),以及米拉·帕雷利(Mila Parély)、羅蘭·圖塔恩(Roland Toutain)與寶蕾特·杜博斯特(Paulette Dubost)。
  • 時代/背景:本片於 1939 年在法國拍攝並上映,距離歐洲爆發第二次世界大戰僅有數月之遙,正值法國「詩意寫實主義」電影運動的尾聲;雷諾刻意將本片構思成一部諷刺喜劇,藉由描繪風俗世態,直指法國上流社會在浩劫來臨前夕道德上的空洞虛偽。
  • 劇情/內容概述:故事發生在鄉間城堡「拉科利尼耶」(La Colinière)舉辦的一場週末派對,聚集了貴族與其僕役;劇情圍繞著層層交錯的感情糾葛展開——侯爵的婚外情、妻子克莉絲汀的曖昧調情,以及僕役之間一組平行發展、恰恰映照並諷刺著主人們行徑的感情競逐——最終在一場混亂的狩獵場景,以及一場化裝舞會表演中發生的悲劇槍擊事件中達到高潮,而這起槍擊事件,最終被這戶人家刻意淡化、掩飾成一場「意外」。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:本片最為人稱道之處,在於其創新運用深焦攝影(deep-focus cinematography),搭配精心設計、長時間不中斷的長鏡頭,同時在畫面的多個景深層次上,編排出極為繁複精細的走位調度——演員在同一個不間斷的鏡頭裡,於前景、中景與背景之間,以複雜的動線交錯移動,讓觀眾得以自行以目光在畫面中游移、主動發現角色之間的關係,而非被鏡頭剪接一步步牽著走;片中惡名昭彰的獵兔與獵雉場景,將真實獵殺獵物的畫面,與派對上「獵取」感情對象的行徑交叉剪接呈現,是一則直白明示的視覺隱喻。
  • 文化/歷史意義:本片上映之初,評論與票房均慘遭滑鐵盧——據傳觀眾當場憤而離席,甚至一度企圖縱火燒毀戲院銀幕以表抗議——部分人士認為,片中對法國上流階級毫不留情的刻畫,在戰爭一觸即發的時刻顯得過於打擊士氣;法國電檢單位屢次刪剪本片,原始底片後來更在戰爭期間的盟軍轟炸中付之一炬。直到 1959 年,團隊才透過現存的殘片與拷貝,煞費苦心地完成修復重建工程,使本片得以盡可能貼近雷諾原始剪輯版本的樣貌重現於世。
  • 影響與傳承:知名影評人暨電影理論家安德烈·巴贊(André Bazin)論述深焦攝影與場面調度(mise-en-scène)的重要著作,即以本片作為核心案例研究,深刻形塑了後世數十年間,電影評論界討論「景深調度」時所使用的詞彙與思考框架;本片群戲式、對白交疊的敘事風格,更直接影響了後世導演如勞勃·阿特曼(Robert Altman);自 1959 年修復重現以來,本片始終穩居各大影評人「影史最偉大電影」票選榜單前列(包括《視與聽》〔Sight & Sound〕雜誌每十年一次的影評人票選)。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:目前可透過標準收藏(Criterion Collection)發行的修復版家用影音版本觀賞,並經常被列入全球各大電影學校的課程教材,以及世界各地影展/資料館的回顧放映片單之中,被視為研習「場面調度」與「深焦長鏡頭」調度技法的經典入門教材。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、criterion.com

8)看片方法——視線對接剪輯 Eyeline Match(第 40 課)

視線對接剪輯(eyeline match)是指:先拍攝一位角色望向畫面外某個方向的鏡頭,接著剪接至第二個鏡頭,揭曉該角色究竟在看什麼(或看誰)——藉此在兩個實際拍攝時可能相隔甚遠、毫無關聯的鏡頭之間,純粹透過剪接手法,建構出一種方向性的空間連結。這項技巧不同於第 9 天所教的「連戲剪輯與 180 度線規則」(正反打鏡頭),後者專門用於對話場景中,在維持一致攝影機軸線的前提下,於兩位面對面說話的角色之間交替剪接;也不同於第 19 天的「主觀鏡頭/POV 攝影機」,後者是將鏡頭直接置於角色本人的眼睛位置,而非分別呈現「角色觀看的動作」與「從角色之外拍攝、獨立呈現的反應/被看對象鏡頭」這兩個部分。視線對接剪輯要求觀眾在無意識間完成一個小小的推論——「這個鏡頭,想必就是剛才那位角色所看到的畫面」——完全僅憑方向性線索(眼神望向的方位,大致對應下一個鏡頭中主體在畫面裡的相對位置)來達成,使它成為剪接手法中,用最經濟的方式,將彼此毫無實際空間鄰近性的鏡頭,建構成一個前後連貫的虛構空間的最有效工具之一。發現這項手法時,可以檢視:它是單純用來建立場景方位的基本功能性用法,還是刻意延遲揭曉觀看內容以營造懸疑感(讓「所見為何」比「觀看動作本身」晚一拍才揭曉);還是被用來建構一段人物、地點或物件之間,實際上從未真正處於同一個物理空間、卻被剪接刻意「捏造」出來的空間或情感關係;以及這種「觀看—揭曉—反應」的鏈結,究竟只是單一場景中的一次運用,還是貫穿整段場景、甚至全片的結構性手法。

實例演練:《後窗》(Rear Window,1954,導演:亞佛烈德·希區考克〔Alfred Hitchcock〕)——幾乎整部電影都建立在反覆運用的視線對接剪輯鏈結之上:先是坐著輪椅、行動不便的傑佛瑞斯(Jefferies)望向後窗外的鏡頭,接著剪接至他所看到的中庭景象(某位鄰居的公寓、一個含糊不清的動作、某人可疑地不在場),再剪回他臉上的反應鏡頭。這整套鏈結完全不需要演員(詹姆斯·史都華〔James Stewart〕)與那座精心搭建的中庭場景,在攝影機前真正同時處於同一個連續的實體空間之中——單靠視線對接剪輯,便足以營造出他正真實注視著那棟特定建築物裡那些特定人物的錯覺。這一套「觀看—剪接至被看對象—剪回反應」的鏈結,正是整部電影的敘事引擎:希區考克(呼應更早期蘇聯庫勒雪夫效應〔Kuleshov Effect〕針對方向性剪輯的實驗)全片幾乎僅憑視線對接剪輯,透過反覆變化運用,同時建構出中庭這個虛構空間的地理樣貌,以及推動全片劇情發展、逐漸升高的懷疑猜忌之情,自始至終,完全不需要任何一個將傑佛瑞斯與犯罪現場同時收進畫面裡的主鏡頭(master shot)。
