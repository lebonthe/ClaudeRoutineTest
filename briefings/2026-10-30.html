4) Daodejing — Chapter 9: 持而盈之 (Chí ér yíng zhī) — "Holding It Full"

Series note: continues the Daodejing track begun on Oct 22, one chapter per day in order (chapters 1-81), never repeated.

Text: 持而盈之,不如其已;揣而銳之,不可長保。金玉滿堂,莫之能守;富貴而驕,自遺其咎。功遂身退,天之道。

Translation (paraphrase): Keep a vessel filled to the brim and it would be better to have stopped; hammer a blade to a sharp edge and it cannot stay sharp for long. When gold and jade fill the hall, no one can guard them; wealth and rank joined to arrogance bring on one's own downfall. When the work is done, withdraw: this is the Way of heaven.

Commentary: Chapter 8 praised water for settling low and not contending; Chapter 9 gives the warning that goes with it: do not push anything to its limit. The first image is a bowl filled until it spills, the second a blade honed until it chips. Both show that the maximum is where reversal begins, a theme the text returns to many times. The third image applies the idea to wealth: a hall full of treasure invites theft and anxiety, and pride attached to success sows its own trouble. The closing line, 功遂身退, is often read as "withdraw once the task is done", the same stance the sage takes in Chapter 2 and Chapter 7, who works without claiming credit. A common modern reading is practical: stop at enough, and leave before the moment of peak position becomes a trap. Note that readers differ on whether 揣 means "to hammer" or "to measure"; the sharpening image is the more usual one.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Laos (Lao People's Democratic Republic)

  • Location & size: The only landlocked country in Southeast Asia, bordered by Myanmar and China (north), Vietnam (east), Cambodia (south) and Thailand (west and southwest), with the Mekong River forming much of the Thai border. About 236,800 sq km: roughly 6.5 times Taiwan (about 36,200 sq km), a little smaller than the United Kingdom, and close to the size of Oregon (approximate). Capital: Vientiane.
  • Population: About 7.5-7.8 million, roughly a third of Taiwan's population on a land area six times larger, so it is among the least densely populated countries in the region (approximate). The Lao are the largest group, alongside dozens of recognised ethnic groups including the Khmu and Hmong.
  • Religion: Theravada Buddhism is the main religion (around two thirds of the population), mixed with spirit worship (phi); there are small Christian and other communities (approximate). The golden stupa That Luang in Vientiane is the national symbol.
  • Economy: A one-party state with a lower-middle-income economy. Hydropower exports have earned it the nickname "battery of Southeast Asia", and mining, agriculture and tourism matter too. The China-Laos Railway, opened in December 2021, links Vientiane to Kunming. Heavy public debt, much of it to China, and high inflation with a weak kip have been persistent problems (approximate).
  • History: The Lan Xang ("Land of a Million Elephants") kingdom was founded in 1353 by Fa Ngum and united the Lao lands for centuries before splitting into rival kingdoms and falling under Siamese influence. France made Laos part of French Indochina in 1893. Independence came in 1953, followed by a civil war between royalists and the communist Pathet Lao linked to the Vietnam War. From 1964 to 1973 the United States dropped more than two million tons of bombs on Laos, which is often cited as the heaviest bombing per capita in history; unexploded ordnance still kills and injures people. The monarchy was abolished and the Lao PDR was proclaimed in December 1975.
  • Current leaders: President Thongloun Sisoulith (re-elected by the 10th National Assembly in March 2026). The National Assembly elected Saleumxay Kommasith as prime minister in early October 2026, replacing Sonexay Siphandone, who moved to be President of the National Assembly (per search today; the Vietnamese and Lao reports differ by a couple of days on the exact date). Real power sits with the Lao People's Revolutionary Party.
  • Language: Lao, a tonal Tai-Kadai language closely related to Thai, written in its own script; French remains in some official and older-generation use, and many minority languages are spoken.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Ievan Polkka" / Loituma Girl / Leekspin (2006)

  • Name: "Loituma Girl" or "Leekspin": a looping Flash animation of an anime girl twirling a spring onion (leek) to the Finnish folk song "Ievan Polkka" ("Eva's Polka") by the group Loituma.
  • Origin / history: The animation used a clip of the character Orihime Inoue from the Japanese anime Bleach, set to a 1995 recording of the song by Loituma, a Finnish folk quartet. The song is older, a polka whose lyrics are attributed to Eino Kettunen, a singer from Finland's Karelian region, in the 1930s (approximate). The loop surfaced on imageboards and Flash-sharing sites in spring 2006; its exact creator is not firmly established.
  • How it spread: The short, hypnotic loop circulated on forums, message boards and Flash portals, then reached a mainstream audience through blogs and video sites. Its spread shows how fan-edited anime clips and obscure international music could become an in-joke across the English-language internet.
  • Meaning / usage: A pure nonsense meme with no deeper meaning; people post it as an absurd distraction or as a way of saying "this is random". The fun is in the contrast between a cheerful Finnish polka and a girl spinning a vegetable.
  • Variations / evolution: Countless remixes and parodies swapped in other characters or objects; the loop became an early example of looped-clip culture, which later moved to GIFs and short videos. The song also led many listeners to discover Finnish folk music, and Loituma's tracks gained attention outside Finland.
  • Current status: The 2006-2008 wave is over, but it is remembered as a classic of the early meme era and shows up in nostalgia compilations; the song remains available on streaming services.
  • Note: General knowledge; dates, creators and details were not source-checked today and are approximate.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Tampopo (1985, dir. Juzo Itami)

  • Name: Tampopo (タンポポ, "Dandelion"), a Japanese comedy about the search for the perfect bowl of ramen, about 114 minutes, released in Japan in November 1985. It is often called the first "ramen western".
  • Key figures: Written and directed by Juzo Itami, a former actor and essayist who turned to directing in his fifties. The cast includes Tsutomu Yamazaki as the truck driver Goro, Nobuko Miyamoto (Itami's wife) as the widow Tampopo, Koji Yakusho as the man in the white suit, and a very young Ken Watanabe as Goro's companion Gun. Music by Kunihiko Murai.
  • Era / background: Japan's bubble-era mid-1980s, when gourmet culture, dining out and "gurume" food boom became a national obsession. Itami had made his directing debut the year before with The Funeral (1984).
  • Plot overview: A truck driver who stops at a struggling ramen shop helps its owner turn it into a success, learning to make noodles and broth from an assembled team of experts, including a retired chef and a homeless connoisseur. Woven around this story are short, unrelated sketches about food and desire: a gangster in a white suit and his lover pass an egg yolk between their mouths, a mother cooks one last meal before she dies, and a salaryman's meeting turns into a lesson on how to eat spaghetti.
  • Style and technique: It borrows the structure of an American western (the stranger who rides in to help the widow; the Shane-like hero), but treats ramen with the seriousness of a duel. The film mixes parody, melodrama and food photography, with close-ups of steaming broth and noodles. The vignettes break the main plot, giving the film an episodic, essay-like rhythm.
  • Cultural significance: It treats food as a source of pleasure, memory and social ritual, and made Japanese ramen culture visible abroad. It is often cited as a landmark of "food cinema".
  • Influence and legacy: It helped inspire later food films and ramen-obsessed pop culture, and it is frequently referenced in discussion of films about eating. Itami went on to make several more satirical films before his death in 1997.
  • Current status: Available on home video, including a restored edition; streaming availability varies by region, and repertory cinemas still screen it.
  • Note: General knowledge; running time, release dates and cast details are approximate and were not source-checked today.

8) Film Appreciation Method — Lesson 92: Confined Space and Single-Location Staging (the "Bottle Film")

What it is: Some films keep nearly all their action inside one room, one vehicle or one building. That limit is a creative choice: with nowhere to go, the film has to create variety, tension and meaning from the same space. The analytic question is: how does the director make a fixed space feel different from minute to minute, and how does the space itself pressure the characters?

How to analyze: (1) Map the geography: note where doors, windows, tables and exits are, and who sits or stands where. (2) Track changes in the same space: look for shifts in light, heat, noise, camera height and lens, which keep the room from becoming flat. (3) Blocking as argument: see who is allowed to move and who is stuck, who stands over whom, and how groups form and break. (4) The way out: notice what the room offers as an exit, such as a window, a door or the end of the day, and what that exit means. (5) Pressure: ask whether the walls feel like they are closing in, and which technical choices cause that feeling.

Worked example — 12 Angry Men (1957, dir. Sidney Lumet, cinematography Boris Kaufman): Twelve jurors deliberate a murder case almost entirely in one jury room, and the film runs in close to real time. What to look for and why it works: (a) Camera height and lenses change over the film. By Lumet's own later account, the first part is shot from above eye level with wider lenses, the middle at eye level, and the last third from below eye level with longer lenses, so that the ceiling appears in the frame and the room seems to shrink as the pressure rises (the exact lens figures are approximate). (b) Blocking as power. When Juror 8 (Henry Fonda) is outnumbered at the start, he is often alone at the window or the edge of the table; later the camera keeps him in the middle of the group as the vote turns. (c) Heat and weather. The broken fan, the sweat on shirts and the storm that finally breaks make the room's discomfort visible. (d) Objects as turning points. The switchblade knife that Juror 8 sticks into the table, and the way the older juror's reading of the witness changes the discussion, give the story clear turning points inside one space. (e) The exit. The final shots move to the courthouse steps in daylight, so the viewer feels the room as a pressure chamber that has just been opened.

Practice: Pick a film set mostly in one place (a car, an elevator, a house). Draw a quick floor plan, then list three ways the film changes the feeling of the space over time (light, camera, blocking) and what the exit means.

4) 道德經 — 第 9 章:持而盈之 (Chí ér yíng zhī) —「持盈保泰」

系列說明:延續 10 月 22 日開始的道德經系列,每天依序一章(第 1-81 章),不重複。

原文:持而盈之,不如其已;揣而銳之,不可長保。金玉滿堂,莫之能守;富貴而驕,自遺其咎。功遂身退,天之道。

白話翻譯(意譯):把容器一直加滿,不如適時停手;把刀刃磨到極尖,不可能長久保持鋒利。金玉堆滿廳堂,沒有人守得住;富貴之後又驕傲,等於自己替自己招來災禍。功成之後就退下,這是天的道理。

解說:第 8 章稱讚水處在低處、與世無爭;第 9 章接著提醒:任何事都不要推到極限。第一個畫面是一只斟到溢出的碗,第二個是磨到崩缺的刀。兩者都說明「到了頂點,就是反轉的開始」,這個主題在全書反覆出現。第三個畫面把同一道理放到財富上:滿屋寶物會招來盜賊與焦慮,成功之後又帶著驕傲,等於替自己種下禍根。最後一句「功遂身退」常被解讀為「任務完成就退下」,與第 2 章、第 7 章聖人做了事而不居功是同一個姿態。現代常見的實用解讀是:知道什麼叫「夠了」就停,並在位置達到頂峰、變成陷阱之前離開。附帶一提:「揣」字有人解作「捶打」,有人解作「度量」,磨利刃的說法比較常見。

  • 歷史:瀾滄王國(Lan Xang,「百萬大象之國」)由法昂於 1353 年建立,統一寮族地區數百年,之後分裂成互相競爭的王國,並受暹羅影響。1893 年法國將寮國納入法屬印度支那。1953 年獨立,隨後在保皇派與共產勢力巴特寮之間爆發與越戰相連的內戰。1964 至 1973 年間,美國對寮國投下超過 200 萬噸炸彈,常被認為是歷史上人均受轟炸最嚴重的國家,至今未爆彈仍造成傷亡。1975 年 12 月廢除君主制,宣布成立寮人民民主共和國。
  • 現任領導人:總統通倫·西蘇里(Thongloun Sisoulith,2026 年 3 月由第 10 屆國會再度選出)。2026 年 10 月初,國會選出賽魯賽·貢瑪西(Saleumxay Kommasith)為總理,接替改任國會議長的宋賽·西潘敦(Sonexay Siphandone)(今天由搜尋查證;越南與寮國的報導在確切日期上差幾天)。實權掌握在寮國人民革命黨手中。
  • 語言:寮語,屬於侗台語系的聲調語言,與泰語關係密切,有自己的文字;法語在部分官方場合與年長世代中仍有使用,境內還有許多少數民族語言。
  • 小知識:川壙(Xiangkhouang)的石缸平原有數千個古代石缸,是聯合國教科文組織世界遺產。備註:領導人經今天搜尋查證,其他數字為概數的一般知識。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「Ievan Polkka」/ Loituma Girl / Leekspin(蔥舞,2006)

  • 名稱:「Loituma Girl」或「Leekspin」(甩蔥舞):一段循環播放的 Flash 動畫,一個動漫女孩隨著芬蘭民謠〈Ievan Polkka〉(「伊娃的波爾卡」)旋轉一根蔥,歌曲由 Loituma 樂團演唱。
  • 起源與歷史:動畫使用日本動漫《死神 BLEACH》角色井上織姬的片段,配上芬蘭民謠四人組 Loituma 在 1995 年的錄音。這首歌本身更老,是一首波爾卡,歌詞一般歸於 1930 年代來自芬蘭卡累利阿地區的歌手艾諾·凱圖寧(Eino Kettunen)(概數)。這段循環動畫在 2006 年春天出現在圖片論壇與 Flash 分享網站,確切作者並未確定。
  • 如何流行:這個簡短、令人催眠的循環先在論壇、留言板與 Flash 入口網站流傳,後來透過部落格與影音網站進入大眾視野。它的傳播顯示,粉絲剪輯的動漫片段與冷門的外國音樂,可以變成整個英語網路圈的內部梗。
  • 意義與使用情境:純粹的無厘頭迷因,沒有更深的含義;人們把它當成荒謬的轉移注意力,或用來表示「這很隨機」。好笑的地方在於歡快的芬蘭波爾卡與一個轉蔬菜的女孩之間的反差。
  • 變體與演變:無數混音與惡搞把角色或物品換成別的;這個循環是早期「循環片段文化」的例子,後來轉移到 GIF 與短影音。這首歌也讓許多聽眾認識了芬蘭民謠,Loituma 的曲子因此在芬蘭以外受到關注。
  • 目前狀況:2006 至 2008 年的熱潮已過,但它被視為早期迷因時代的經典,仍出現在懷舊合輯中;歌曲在串流平台上仍可聽到。
  • 備註:一般知識;日期、作者與細節今天未經來源核對,屬概數。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《蒲公英》(Tampopo,1985,導演伊丹十三)

  • 名稱:《蒲公英》(タンポポ),日本喜劇,講述尋找一碗完美拉麵的故事,片長約 114 分鐘,1985 年 11 月在日本上映,常被稱為第一部「拉麵西部片」。
  • 主要人物:編劇兼導演為伊丹十三,他原本是演員與散文作家,五十多歲才轉任導演。演員包括山崎努(飾卡車司機郷)、宮本信子(伊丹的妻子,飾寡婦蒲公英)、役所廣司(飾白西裝男子),以及非常年輕的渡邊謙(飾郷的同伴 Gun)。音樂為村井邦彥。
  • 時代背景:1980 年代中期泡沫經濟時代的日本,美食與外食風氣盛行,「グルメ」成為全民熱潮。伊丹在前一年以《葬禮》(1984)首次執導。
  • 劇情概要:一位卡車司機在一家苦苦經營的拉麵店停留,並幫老闆把店做起來,他請來一組專家教她做麵與熬湯,其中包括退休廚師與一位流浪的美食家。這條主線的周圍穿插著關於食物與欲望的短篇小故事:穿白西裝的黑道與他的情人在口中傳遞一顆蛋黃,一位母親在臨終前做最後一餐,一位上班族的會議變成教人如何吃義大利麵的課。
  • 風格與技法:它借用美國西部片的結構(陌生人來幫助寡婦、像《萬夫莫敵》的英雄),但以決鬥般的認真態度對待拉麵。影片混合戲仿、通俗劇與美食攝影,包括冒著熱氣的湯與麵條的特寫。這些短篇小故事打斷主線,使影片有片段式、像散文的節奏。
  • 文化意義:它把食物當成快樂、記憶與社交儀式的來源,並讓日本拉麵文化在國外被看見。常被視為「美食電影」的里程碑。
  • 影響與傳承:它啟發了後來的美食電影與痴迷拉麵的流行文化,討論「吃」的電影時經常被提起。伊丹之後又拍了幾部諷刺電影,1997 年去世。
  • 目前狀況:可透過家庭影音(包括修復版)觀看;串流供應依地區而異,經典影院仍會放映。
  • 備註:一般知識;片長、上映日期與演員細節為概數,今天未經來源核對。

8) 看片方法 — 第 92 課:封閉空間與單一場景調度(「瓶中電影」)

這是什麼:有些電影把幾乎所有的戲都放在同一個房間、一輛車或一棟建築裡。這個限制是創作上的選擇:無處可去,影片就必須從同一個空間創造出變化、張力與意義。分析時要問的是:導演如何讓固定的空間在每一分鐘看起來都不一樣?空間本身又如何對角色施加壓力?

如何分析:(1) 畫出空間地圖:記下門、窗、桌子與出口的位置,以及誰坐在或站在哪裡。(2) 追蹤同一空間中的變化:留意光線、溫度、噪音、攝影機高度與鏡頭的轉變,它們讓房間不會變得平板。(3) 走位就是論點:看誰可以移動、誰被困住、誰站在誰上方,以及群體如何形成又瓦解。(4) 出路:注意空間提供什麼出口,例如窗、門或一天的結束,以及那個出口代表什麼。(5) 壓力:問問牆壁是否像在逼近,以及哪些技術選擇造成這種感覺。

示範實例 — 《十二怒漢》(12 Angry Men,1957,導演西德尼·盧梅,攝影鮑里斯·考夫曼):十二位陪審員幾乎全程在同一間陪審室裡討論一樁謀殺案,影片近乎即時進行。要看什麼、為什麼有效:(a) 攝影機高度與鏡頭隨影片改變。依盧梅後來自己的說法,前段以高於視線的角度與較廣的鏡頭拍攝,中段與視線同高,最後三分之一從低於視線的角度、用較長的鏡頭拍攝,使天花板進入畫面,隨著壓力升高,房間彷彿越縮越小(確切的鏡頭數字為概數)。(b) 走位就是權力。一開始,八號陪審員(亨利·方達)寡不敵眾,常獨自站在窗邊或桌子邊緣;後來隨著投票逆轉,鏡頭讓他留在群體中央。(c) 悶熱與天氣。壞掉的電風扇、襯衫上的汗水,以及最後終於落下的暴雨,讓房間的不適看得見。(d) 物件即轉折點。八號陪審員把彈簧刀插在桌上,以及年長陪審員對證人的觀察如何改變討論,讓故事在同一個空間內有清楚的轉折。(e) 出口。最後的畫面移到白天的法院台階,讓觀眾感到那個房間是一個剛被打開的壓力艙。

練習:選一部主要在單一地點(車內、電梯、一間屋子)發生的電影。畫一張簡單的平面圖,然後列出影片隨時間改變空間感受的三種方式(光線、攝影機、走位),並說明出口的意義。
