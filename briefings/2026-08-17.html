3) Spanish Lesson — Day 38 (10 min, completes the three-type "Si" conditional system)

Days 36 and 37 built the two most common conditional types — the "contrary-to-present-fact" Type 2 (si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional simple) and the "real/likely" Type 1 (si + present indicative + present/future/imperative). Today delivers the third and final type: the "contrary-to-past-fact" Type 3 conditional, used to imagine a different outcome for something that already happened and cannot be changed — reusing Day 21-22's haber-based compound-tense machinery and Day 35's imperfect-subjunctive stem, now combined into two new compound tenses.

  • Si hubiera estudiado más, habría aprobado el examen. — If I had studied more, I would have passed the exam.
  • Si hubieras llegado a tiempo, habrías visto el espectáculo. — If you had arrived on time, you would have seen the show.
  • Si no hubiera llovido, habríamos ido a la playa. — If it hadn't rained, we would have gone to the beach.

Grammar note: the si-clause takes the pluperfect subjunctive (pluscuamperfecto de subjuntivo) — hubiera/hubieras/hubiera/hubiéramos/hubieran (Day 35's imperfect-subjunctive conjugation of haber) plus a past participle (Day 21's -ado/-ido forms and irregulars) — while the result clause takes the conditional perfect (condicional compuesto) — habría/habrías/habría/habríamos/habrían (Day 24's conditional-simple conjugation of haber) plus the same past participle. Both clauses describe an event already finished and unchangeable; the whole sentence is a look backward at a fork already closed, distinct from Type 2's present-tense hypothetical and Type 1's open-future possibility.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 38: 睽 (Kuí) — "Opposition" (Estrangement) (Fire above, Lake below — Lí over Duì). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws a direct, almost mirror-image line from yesterday's Hexagram 37: "家道窮必乖,故受之以睽;睽者,乖也。" ("When the way of the family is carried to its limit, estrangement must follow; hence Jiā Rén [The Family] is followed by Kuí [Opposition]. Kuí means estrangement.") Where Hexagram 37 showed the household as the foundation from which all order radiates outward, Hexagram 38 picks up the shadow side of that same structure: even the most tightly bound unit eventually produces friction and divergence of will. The Judgment is brief: "睽,小事吉。" ("Opposition: in small matters, there is good fortune.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) explains the image through the trigrams' natural motion and the Bagua family relationships: "睽,火動而上,澤動而下;二女同居,其志不同行。說而麗乎明,柔進而上行,得中而應乎剛,是以小事吉。天地睽而其事同也,男女睽而其志通也,萬物睽而其事類也,睽之時用大矣哉!" ("In Opposition, fire moves upward while the lake moves downward; two daughters dwell in the same house, yet their wills do not move in the same direction [Lí is the middle daughter, Duì the youngest daughter in the Bagua family scheme — sisters under one roof, but each with her own separate direction]. [Duì's] joy adheres to [Lí's] clarity; the yielding line advances and moves upward, attains the middle position and responds to the firm line below — hence in small matters there is good fortune. Heaven and earth are in opposition [high and low, separate in position], yet their work is the same [together they generate all things]; man and woman are in opposition [distinct in body and role], yet their wills are in communication [together they form a family]; the ten thousand creatures are in opposition [utterly varied in form], yet their affairs are of the same kind [all subject to the same natural principles]. Great indeed is the function of the time of Opposition!") The Image commentary draws the practical lesson for conduct: "上火下澤,睽,君子以同而異。" ("Fire above, the lake below: this is the image of Opposition. Thus the superior person, amid agreement, still preserves distinctions.") Rather than treating "opposition" as a problem to be eliminated, the hexagram reframes it as a structural fact of any complex system — heaven/earth, man/woman, and the ten thousand creatures are all fundamentally different from one another, yet that very difference is what lets them combine into a working whole; the task is not to erase distinction but to find the "small matters" of common ground (說而麗乎明, joy adhering to clarity) that let genuinely different wills still cooperate. Read together with Hexagram 37, the pair traces a complete arc: an ordered household radiating outward into the world (37), and then that very order's own tendency, once matured, to generate internal divergence that must be managed rather than denied (38) — with the Image commentary's closing phrase, "同而異" (unity that still permits difference), standing as one of the Yijing's more understated pieces of social wisdom.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Nauru (諾魯)

  • Location: A single isolated coral atoll island in the central Pacific Ocean (Micronesia), with no land border with any country; its nearest neighbor is Banaba Island (Kiribati), roughly 300 km away.
  • Territory: About 21 km² — the world's smallest island nation and the third-smallest country of any kind (after Vatican City and Monaco). It is roughly 0.06% the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²) — Taiwan is over 1,700 times larger — and only about a third the area of Manhattan Island.
  • Population: Roughly 10,000-12,000, making Nauru the world's third-least-populous sovereign state (after Vatican City and Tuvalu).
  • Religion: Predominantly Christian, historically anchored by the Nauru Congregational Church (now the Nauru Independent Church) alongside a significant Roman Catholic community and other Protestant denominations.
  • Economy: A dramatic boom-bust-recovery story. Phosphate mining (guano deposits) made Nauru briefly one of the richest countries on Earth per capita in the mid-1970s — rivaling Saudi Arabia — but decades of extraction stripped roughly 80% of the island's interior ("Topside") into uninhabitable moonscape, and mismanaged sovereign-wealth investments (including a notorious failed West End musical) triggered a fiscal collapse in the 1990s-2000s that left GDP down more than 90% and unemployment near 90% at its worst. Today's economy leans heavily on fishing-license revenue from its exclusive economic zone, remaining secondary phosphate deposits, foreign aid, and — most controversially — fees from hosting an Australian-run offshore immigration detention center for asylum seekers, an arrangement that has been a major, recurring source of government revenue since the early 2000s.
  • History: Settled by Micronesian and Polynesian peoples for roughly 3,000 years before European contact; annexed by Germany in 1888, then administered by Australia (under a UK/Australia/New Zealand League of Nations mandate) after WWI; occupied by Japan in WWII, which deported much of the population to forced labor on Chuuk, killing many; returned to Australian-administered trusteeship post-war and gained full independence in 1968 under founding President Hammer DeRoburt. Diplomatically, Nauru's recognition has swung between Taipei and Beijing several times — it recognized Taiwan, switched to China in 2002, restored ties with Taiwan in 2005, and switched again to recognize the People's Republic of China in January 2024, ending its most recent stretch of formal relations with Taiwan.
  • Current leader: President David W.R. Adeang, re-elected for a consecutive term in October 2025; Lionel Aingimea was sworn in as Nauru's first-ever Vice President in April 2026 following a constitutional reform creating the office.
  • Language: Nauruan (a distinct Micronesian language) is the official/national language; English is widely used in government, business, and education.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, devpolicy.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — LOLcats / "I Can Has Cheezburger?" (貓咪好笑話 / 貓咪對話體)

  • Name: "LOLcats," with its single most iconic example being the "I Can Has Cheezburger?" image macro.
  • Origin/history: The tradition of posting humorous captioned cat photos traces to the imageboard 4chan's "Caturday" ritual around 2005, itself building on the earlier "Happy Cat" image (a British Shorthair) that had circulated on the Something Awful forums since 2003. The specific "I Can Has Cheezburger?" photo — the same wide-eyed British Shorthair captioned in deliberately broken English asking for a cheeseburger — was posted to 4chan on January 11, 2007, by Eric Nakagawa (posting under the name "Cheezburger").
  • How it became popular: Later that same month, Nakagawa and his girlfriend Kari Unebasami launched a dedicated site, icanhascheezburger.com, for users to submit their own captioned cat photos; it drew millions of page views within months, was profiled by Time Magazine in June 2007 (by which point Google already returned 3.3 million results for "lolcat"), and the site was acquired by investors for roughly $2 million in September 2007, spinning off into the broader "Cheezburger Network" of humor sites.
  • Meaning/usage: A photo of a cat (or other animal) captioned in "lolspeak" — a deliberately misspelled, ungrammatical dialect ("I can has...", "Ceiling Cat is watching you") — used purely for comedic, cute-aggression value, with no political or satirical target; the humor comes from the mismatch between the animal's expression and the invented "voice" given to it.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned an entire lolspeak micro-dialect and mythology (Ceiling Cat, Basement Cat, "invisible [object]"), plus the related "O RLY?" owl macro; the Cheezburger Network expanded far beyond cats into sites like FAILblog and Memebase. Most significantly, LOLcats is widely credited as one of the direct ancestors of the modern "image macro" format broadly — the captioned-photo template later adapted into Advice Animals, Rage Comics, and countless other formats, distinct in mechanism from the comparison-panel format of Drakeposting (Day 37) or the reaction-photo format of Surprised Pikachu (Day 36).
  • Current status: No longer at the center of mainstream internet culture the way it was at its 2007-2012 peak, but still widely recognized as a foundational, nostalgic touchstone of early "Web 2.0" meme culture; the Cheezburger media company continues to operate, and the underlying "captioned animal photo" template it popularized remains one of the internet's most enduring content genres nearly two decades later.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Apocalypse Now (現代啟示錄, 1979, dir. Francis Ford Coppola)

  • Name/key figures: Apocalypse Now (1979), directed by Francis Ford Coppola; screenplay by John Milius and Coppola, freely adapting Joseph Conrad's 1899 novella Heart of Darkness by relocating its Congo River journey to the Vietnam War; starring Martin Sheen (Capt. Willard), Marlon Brando (Col. Kurtz), Robert Duvall (Lt. Col. Kilgore), with cinematography by Vittorio Storaro.
  • Era/background: Shot in 1976-77 in the Philippines through Coppola's own American Zoetrope studio during the New Hollywood era, on a production so famously chaotic — a typhoon destroyed the main sets, star Martin Sheen suffered a near-fatal heart attack, Brando arrived overweight and having not read the source novella, and Philippine military helicopters loaned for filming were sometimes recalled mid-shoot to fight actual local insurgents — that it was documented in its own 1991 feature, "Hearts of Darkness: A Filmmaker's Apocalypse," directed by Coppola's wife Eleanor Coppola.
  • Plot/content overview: Captain Benjamin Willard is sent up a river into Cambodia during the Vietnam War on a covert mission to find and "terminate with extreme prejudice" Colonel Walter Kurtz, a decorated Green Beret who has gone rogue and established his own cult-like kingdom deep in the jungle; the journey upriver — through the chaotic Do Long Bridge outpost, a USO Playboy Playmates show that dissolves into a riot, and Lt. Col. Kilgore's helicopter air-cavalry assault staged to Wagner's "Ride of the Valkyries" — becomes less a military mission than a slow descent into the moral derangement of war itself.
  • Artistic style and techniques: A hallucinatory, expressionistic visual style built on Storaro's saturated colour and chiaroscuro lighting; groundbreaking layered sound design that blends helicopter rotor noise directly into The Doors' music; extensive practical pyrotechnics and real explosions in place of effects work; and Marlon Brando's largely improvised, deliberately shadow-obscured monologues as Kurtz, shot to hide his refusal to lose weight for the role.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely ranked among the greatest war films and greatest films of any genre, valued precisely for abandoning conventional war-narrative structure in favor of an allegorical, Conrad-inflected descent into imperialism's darkness; Coppola's own famous Cannes remark — "My film is not about Vietnam. My film IS Vietnam" — captured how the production's real chaos became inseparable from the film's thematic content.
  • Influence and legacy: The film's theatrical sound mix directly pioneered multi-channel "Split Surround" theatrical audio, a technical lineage that led toward the modern 5.1 surround standard; Kilgore's lines "I love the smell of napalm in the morning" and "Charlie don't surf" remain among cinema's most quoted; its portrait of war-as-moral-collapse influenced later films from Full Metal Jacket to The Hurt Locker, and the 2001 "Apocalypse Now Redux" restored roughly 49 minutes of previously cut footage.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Preserved in the US National Film Registry (2000) as culturally and historically significant; available today via streaming rental, Blu-ray/4K UHD (including Coppola's re-edited 2019 "Final Cut"), and periodic theatrical anniversary re-releases.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, loc.gov (National Film Registry)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Title Sequences as Thesis Statement (Lesson 24)

A film's main title sequence is its first act of authorship, arriving before a single line of story dialogue — treating it as mere administrative name-listing misses that a well-designed title sequence can encode tone, genre, and even a character's psychology in advance, functioning as a compressed short film that primes everything the audience is about to watch. Four things to check: first, whether the typography and material texture themselves carry meaning (a handwritten scrawl signals something very different from a clean corporate sans-serif); second, whether the images shown are diegetic (belonging concretely to the story world, such as a character's actual notebook or possessions) or purely symbolic/abstract; third, how the chosen music or sound design relates to — or deliberately clashes with — those images; and fourth, whether the sequence is designed to stand alone as a self-contained short film rather than simply rolling over establishing footage.

Worked example: Se7en (1995, dir. David Fincher), title sequence designed by Kyle Cooper. The titles show extreme-close-up, degraded, hand-scratched footage of a pair of hands — implicitly serial killer John Doe's — meticulously preparing obsessive notebooks: razor-blading photographs, sanding down fingertips, sewing pages, all shot on high-contrast Kodalith film stock that Cooper physically scratched, shook, and light-fogged frame by frame (a technique consciously echoing experimental filmmaker Stan Brakhage's hand-processed work), intercut with a hooded silhouette and scored to a discordant industrial remix of Nine Inch Nails' "Closer." No narrative dialogue has occurred yet, but the sequence has already fully established the killer's disturbing obsessiveness and the film's oppressive tone; contemporary reports describe test audiences actually applauding once the titles ended, an almost unheard-of reaction to a main title sequence. The sequence is now widely credited with reviving Hollywood's interest in title design as a serious art form in its own right (distinct from the earlier, more playful Saul Bass tradition of graphic titles), and its influence can be traced directly into later credit sequences for shows like Dexter, True Detective, and Mindhunter.

3)西班牙文課程——第 38 天(10 分鐘,完成三種類型的「Si」條件句系統)

第 36、37 天已建立最常見的兩種條件句類型——「與現在事實相反」的第二類條件句(si + 虛擬式過去未完了式 + 條件式簡單式)與「真實、有可能發生」的第一類條件句(si + 直陳式現在式 + 現在式/未來式/命令式)。今天要教授第三種、也是最後一種類型:「與過去事實相反」的第三類條件句,用來想像已經發生、且無法改變的事情若有不同結果會是如何——重新運用第 21、22 天學過的 haber 複合時態架構,以及第 35 天的虛擬式過去未完了式詞幹,組合成兩種全新的複合時態。

  • Si hubiera estudiado más, habría aprobado el examen. —— 如果我當初多讀一點書,就會通過考試了。
  • Si hubieras llegado a tiempo, habrías visto el espectáculo. —— 如果你當初準時到,就會看到那場表演了。
  • Si no hubiera llovido, habríamos ido a la playa. —— 如果當初沒下雨,我們就會去海邊了。

文法筆記:Si 子句要用虛擬式過去完成式(pluscuamperfecto de subjuntivo)——hubiera/hubieras/hubiera/hubiéramos/hubieran(第 35 天教過的 haber 虛擬式過去未完了式變化)加上過去分詞(第 21 天教過的 -ado/-ido 規則變化與不規則詞形);結果子句則要用條件完成式(condicional compuesto)——habría/habrías/habría/habríamos/habrían(第 24 天教過的 haber 條件式簡單式變化)加上同一個過去分詞。兩個子句描述的都是已經結束、無法改變的事件;整句話是回頭凝視一條早已關閉的岔路,這一點和第二類條件句的「現在式假設」、第一類條件句的「開放中的未來可能性」都不相同。

4)易經卦象

第 38 卦:睽 (Kuí) ——「乖離」(睽違)(火在上,澤在下——離上兌下)。《序卦傳》從昨天的第 37 卦直接接續下來:「家道窮必乖,故受之以睽;睽者,乖也。」(當一家之道發展到極致,必然產生乖離、疏離;因此家人卦之後接的是睽卦。睽,就是乖離的意思。)如果說第 37 卦展現的是一個秩序井然的家庭如何向外輻射出整個世界的秩序,那麼第 38 卦所呈現的,正是同一個結構必然浮現的陰影面:即使是最緊密結合的單位,終究也會產生摩擦與意志上的分歧。卦辭簡短:「睽,小事吉。」(睽卦:在小事上,還算吉利。)《彖傳》透過卦象自身的運動方向與八卦家族關係來解釋:「睽,火動而上,澤動而下;二女同居,其志不同行。說而麗乎明,柔進而上行,得中而應乎剛,是以小事吉。天地睽而其事同也,男女睽而其志通也,萬物睽而其事類也,睽之時用大矣哉!」(睽卦之中,火向上運動,澤向下運動;兩位女兒同住一個屋簷下,志向卻不朝同一個方向前進〔離為中女、兌為少女,是同住一家卻各有各自方向的兩姊妹〕。〔兌之〕喜悅依附於〔離之〕光明;柔順的爻向上前進,獲得中位並與下方的剛爻相應——因此在小事上還算吉利。天與地彼此乖離〔位置一高一低,各自分離〕,但運作的事業卻是相同的〔共同生成萬物〕;男與女彼此乖離〔形體、角色各不相同〕,但意志卻是相通的〔共同組成一個家庭〕;萬物彼此乖離〔形態千差萬別〕,但所行之事卻屬同一類〔都遵循相同的自然法則〕。睽卦所象徵的這個時機與作用,實在是太重大了!)《象傳》則點出實際行事的教訓:「上火下澤,睽,君子以同而異。」(火在上,澤在下,這就是睽卦的卦象。因此君子在追求共通、和諧之際,仍會保留彼此的差異。)這一卦並不把「乖離」當成需要消除的問題,而是將它重新定義為任何複雜系統中本就存在的結構性事實——天與地、男與女、萬物彼此之間本來就根本不同,但正是這份差異,才讓它們得以結合成一個可以運作的整體;真正的任務不是抹除差異,而是找出「說而麗乎明」(喜悅依附於光明)這樣的「小事」共通點,讓真正不同的意志仍能彼此合作。與第 37 卦合觀,這兩卦勾勒出一段完整的弧線:一個秩序井然的家庭向外輻射出整個世界的秩序(第 37 卦),而這份秩序一旦成熟,自身也必然會產生需要被妥善面對、而非一味否認的內部分歧(第 38 卦)——《象傳》結尾的「同而異」三字,正是《易經》裡最含蓄深刻的社會智慧之一。

(第三十八個卦象登場——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 以避免重複。目前已介紹:1 乾 Qián、2 坤 Kūn、3 屯 Zhūn、4 蒙 Méng、5 需 Xū、6 訟 Sòng、7 師 Shī、8 比 Bǐ、9 小畜 Xiǎo Chù、10 履 Lǚ、11 泰 Tài、12 否 Pǐ、13 同人 Tóng Rén、14 大有 Dà Yǒu、15 謙 Qiān、16 豫 Yù、17 隨 Suí、18 蠱 Gǔ、19 臨 Lín、20 觀 Guān、21 噬嗑 Shì Kè、22 賁 Bì、23 剝 Bō、24 復 Fù、25 无妄 Wú Wàng、26 大畜 Dà Chù、27 頤 Yí、28 大過 Dà Guò、29 坎 Kǎn、30 離 Lí、31 咸 Xián、32 恆 Héng、33 遯 Dùn、34 大壯 Dà Zhuàng、35 晉 Jìn、36 明夷 Míng Yí、37 家人 Jiā Rén、38 睽 Kuí。)

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——諾魯(Nauru)

  • 位置:位於中太平洋(密克羅尼西亞)的一座孤立珊瑚環礁島嶼,不與任何國家接壤;最近的鄰居是吉里巴斯的巴納巴島(Banaba),距離約 300 公里。
  • 領土:約 21 平方公里——全世界最小的島國,也是全球第三小的國家(僅次於梵蒂岡與摩納哥)。面積約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 0.06%,換算下來台灣比諾魯大上超過 1,700 倍;面積也僅約曼哈頓島的三分之一。
  • 人口:約 1 萬至 1.2 萬人,是全世界人口第三少的主權國家(僅次於梵蒂岡與吐瓦魯)。
  • 宗教:以基督宗教為主,歷史上以諾魯公理會(現稱諾魯獨立教會)為主要教派,另有相當規模的天主教徒與其他新教教派信眾。
  • 經濟:一段戲劇性的「暴富—破產—重建」故事。磷酸鹽(鳥糞石)開採曾在 1970 年代中期讓諾魯短暫躋身全球人均最富有的國家之一,足以與沙烏地阿拉伯匹敵;但數十年的開採將該島約八成內陸地區(「Topside」)變成無法居住的月球表面般的荒地,加上主權財富基金投資嚴重失當(包括一齣著名失敗的倫敦西區音樂劇投資案),於 1990 至 2000 年代引爆財政崩潰,國內生產毛額一度暴跌超過九成,失業率最嚴重時逼近九成。如今的經濟則高度仰賴其專屬經濟海域的漁業執照收入、剩餘的次級磷酸鹽礦藏、外國援助,以及——最具爭議性的——出租土地供澳洲設置境外難民收容中心所收取的費用,自 2000 年代初期起,這項安排一直是諾魯政府反覆倚賴的重要財政收入來源。
  • 歷史:在歐洲人抵達之前,已有密克羅尼西亞與玻里尼西亞民族在此定居約三千年;1888 年被德國兼併,一戰後改由澳洲代表英國、澳洲、紐西蘭的國際聯盟託管制度管理;二戰期間遭日本佔領,島上多數居民被強制遷往楚克島(Chuuk)充當勞工,許多人因此喪生;戰後恢復由澳洲託管,並於 1968 年在開國總統哈默·德羅布爾特(Hammer DeRoburt)領導下完全獨立。在外交承認上,諾魯多次在台北與北京之間搖擺——曾承認台灣、2002 年轉向承認中國、2005 年恢復與台灣的邦交,又於 2024 年 1 月再度轉向承認中華人民共和國,結束了與台灣最近一段的正式邦交關係。
  • 現任領導人:總統大衛·阿德昂(David W.R. Adeang),於 2025 年 10 月連任;萊昂內爾·安金梅亞(Lionel Aingimea)則於 2026 年 4 月依修憲新設的副總統職位宣誓就任,成為諾魯史上首位副總統。
  • 語言:諾魯語(一種獨特的密克羅尼西亞語言)為官方/國家語言;英語則廣泛使用於政府、商業與教育場合。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、devpolicy.org

6)網路迷因介紹——LOLcats /「I Can Has Cheezburger?」(貓咪蹩腳英語對話迷因)

  • 名稱:「LOLcats」,其中最具代表性的單一範例是「I Can Has Cheezburger?」這張迷因圖。
  • 起源/歷史:張貼詼諧配文貓咪照片的傳統,可追溯至圖片論壇 4chan 約 2005 年開始的「Caturday」(週六貓咪日)慣例,而這又是建立在更早、自 2003 年起就在 Something Awful 論壇流傳的「Happy Cat」(一隻英國短毛貓)圖片之上。真正那張「I Can Has Cheezburger?」照片——同一隻睜大眼睛的英國短毛貓,配上刻意寫錯文法、要求吃起司漢堡的破英文字幕——是由 Eric Nakagawa(以「Cheezburger」為帳號名)於 2007 年 1 月 11 日張貼到 4chan 上的。
  • 如何走紅:就在同一個月稍晚,Nakagawa 與女友 Kari Unebasami 創立了專屬網站 icanhascheezburger.com,供使用者上傳自己配文的貓咪照片;短短數月內就吸引數百萬次瀏覽量,2007 年 6 月獲《時代》雜誌專文報導(當時 Google 搜尋「lolcat」已有 330 萬筆結果),該網站更於 2007 年 9 月被投資人以約 200 萬美元收購,進而衍生出更龐大的「Cheezburger Network」幽默網站集團。
  • 意義/使用情境:將一張貓咪(或其他動物)的照片,配上「lolspeak」(刻意拼錯字、不合文法的方言,例如「I can has...」、「Ceiling Cat is watching you」)風格的字幕,純粹用於博君一笑、賣萌逗趣,不帶任何政治或諷刺意圖;笑點來自動物本身的表情,與被賦予的這種「話語聲音」之間的反差。
  • 變體/演化:衍生出一整套 lolspeak 迷你方言與神話體系(Ceiling Cat 天花板貓神、Basement Cat 地下室貓神、「invisible〔物品〕」句型),以及相關的「O RLY?」貓頭鷹迷因圖;Cheezburger Network 也從貓咪擴展到 FAILblog、Memebase 等更多網站。最重要的是,LOLcats 普遍被認為是現代「image macro」(配文迷因圖)這整個格式的直系源頭之一——這種配文照片模板後來演變出 Advice Animals、Rage Comics 等無數格式,其運作機制與第 37 天介紹的 Drakeposting 對比式版面、或第 36 天介紹的驚訝皮卡丘反應圖截然不同。
  • 目前狀態:雖已不再處於主流網路文化的中心地位(其巔峰約落在 2007 至 2012 年間),但仍普遍被視為早期「Web 2.0」迷因文化中一個具有懷舊代表性的基石;Cheezburger 這家媒體公司至今仍在營運,而它所帶動流行的「配文動物照片」這一模板,近二十年後依然是網路上最歷久不衰的內容類型之一。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《現代啟示錄》(Apocalypse Now,1979,導演:法蘭西斯·福特·柯波拉)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《現代啟示錄》(1979),由法蘭西斯·福特·柯波拉(Francis Ford Coppola)執導;劇本由約翰·米利厄斯(John Milius)與柯波拉共同撰寫,自由改編自約瑟夫·康拉德(Joseph Conrad)1899 年的中篇小說《黑暗之心》,將故事場景從剛果河改到越戰戰場;主演為馬丁·辛(Martin Sheen,飾演威拉德上尉)、馬龍·白蘭度(Marlon Brando,飾演寇茲上校)、勞勃·杜瓦(Robert Duvall,飾演基爾戈中校),攝影指導為維多里奧·斯托拉羅(Vittorio Storaro)。
  • 時代/背景:本片於 1976 至 1977 年間,由柯波拉自己的美國西洋鏡製片廠(American Zoetrope)在菲律賓拍攝,正值「新好萊塢」時期;拍攝過程之混亂堪稱電影史傳奇——一場颱風摧毀了主要場景、男主角馬丁·辛在拍攝期間心臟病發、命危一線、白蘭度抵達片場時體重過重且根本沒讀過原著小說,而借來拍片的菲律賓軍方直升機,有時甚至會在拍攝途中被緊急召回去鎮壓當地真實的叛亂份子——這段拍攝過程之離奇,後來甚至被柯波拉的妻子伊蓮諾·柯波拉(Eleanor Coppola)拍成 1991 年的紀錄片《黑暗之心:一個電影人的啟示錄》。
  • 劇情/內容概述:班傑明·威拉德上尉在越戰期間奉命沿河深入柬埔寨,執行一項秘密任務:找到並「以極端手段終結」戰功彪炳卻已叛離體制的綠扁帽上校華特·寇茲——此人在叢林深處建立起自己近乎邪教般的王國。這趟溯河之旅——途經混亂不堪的多隆橋前哨站、一場最終演變成暴動的美國勞軍聯合會花花公子女郎秀,以及基爾戈中校配著華格納《女武神的騎行》展開的直升機空騎突擊——與其說是一場軍事任務,不如說是一段緩緩沉入戰爭本身道德失序深淵的旅程。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:建立在斯托拉羅飽和色彩與明暗對比強烈用光之上的迷幻、表現主義視覺風格;開創性地將直升機旋翼聲直接融入「大門」樂團(The Doors)音樂之中的多層次聲音設計;大量使用實體煙火與真實爆破取代特效;以及馬龍·白蘭度大幅即興、刻意以陰影遮蔽拍攝的寇茲獨白場景——這種拍法同時也巧妙掩蓋了他拒絕為角色減重的事實。
  • 文化/歷史意義:普遍被列為影史上最偉大的戰爭片之一,乃至所有類型中最偉大的電影之一,其價值恰恰在於捨棄傳統戰爭敘事結構,轉而以帶有康拉德色彩的寓言方式,呈現一段沉入帝國主義黑暗的旅程;柯波拉本人在坎城影展著名的發言——「我的電影不是關於越戰。我的電影就是越戰」——精準捕捉了拍攝過程中的真實混亂,如何與影片本身的主題內容密不可分。
  • 影響與傳承:本片的戲院混音技術直接開創了多聲道「Split Surround」劇院音效系統,這項技術脈絡後來演變成現今的 5.1 環繞音效標準;基爾戈的台詞「我愛死清晨的凝固汽油彈味道了」與「查理不會衝浪」至今仍是影史上被引用次數最多的台詞之一;其「戰爭即道德崩壞」的描繪,影響了後續從《金甲部隊》到《危機倒數》等眾多戰爭片,而 2001 年推出的《現代啟示錄:重生版》(Apocalypse Now Redux)則修復了約 49 分鐘先前被刪減的片段。
  • 現況/如何欣賞:2000 年獲美國國家影片保護局(National Film Registry)列為具文化、歷史重要性的保存片單;如今可透過串流租借、藍光/4K UHD(包括柯波拉 2019 年重新剪輯的「最終版」)觀賞,並不時有戲院週年紀念重映場次。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、loc.gov(美國國家影片保護局)

8)看片方法——片頭字幕作為「主題宣言」(第 24 課)

一部電影的片頭字幕,是電影導演在任何一句劇情台詞出現之前,最早的一次「作者宣示」——如果只把它當成單純列出工作人員名單的行政程序,就會錯過一個設計精良的片頭字幕其實能夠預先埋入整部片的基調、類型,甚至某個角色的心理狀態,如同一部濃縮的短片,預先為觀眾即將看到的一切定調。有四件事值得檢視:第一,字體與材質本身是否承載意義(手寫刮痕般的字跡,和乾淨俐落的企業無襯線字體,傳達的訊息截然不同);第二,畫面中出現的影像究竟是「劇情內」的(切實屬於故事世界,例如角色本人真實擁有的筆記本或物品),還是純粹象徵性/抽象的;第三,所選用的音樂或聲音設計,與這些畫面之間是彼此呼應,還是刻意形成衝突;第四,這段字幕是否被設計成一段能夠獨立成篇的短片,而不只是隨意疊放在建立場景的畫面之上。

實例演練:《火線追緝令》(Se7en,1995,導演:大衛·芬奇),片頭字幕由凱爾·庫柏(Kyle Cooper)設計。片頭以極端特寫、畫質刻意做舊、佈滿刮痕的鏡頭,呈現一雙手——暗示正是連續殺人犯約翰·杜(John Doe)——正一絲不苟地製作著充滿偏執狂色彩的筆記本:用刀片裁切照片、磨平指尖、縫裝書頁,全程拍攝在高反差的柯達立(Kodalith)底片上,並由庫柏一格一格親手刮傷、搖晃、曝光處理(這項技法有意識地呼應了實驗電影工作者史丹·布萊克哈奇〔Stan Brakhage〕手工沖印膠捲的技術),中間穿插一個戴著兜帽的剪影,配樂則是「九吋釘」樂團(Nine Inch Nails)歌曲《Closer》一段刺耳不諧調的工業風混音版本。此時劇情台詞尚未展開,但這段字幕已經完整建立起殺手令人不安的偏執性格,以及全片壓迫性的基調;當年試映時,據報導觀眾在片頭字幕結束時竟然真的鼓掌——這對一段片頭字幕而言,是極為罕見的反應。這段字幕如今普遍被認為重新喚起了好萊塢對「片頭設計」作為一門嚴肅藝術形式的重視(有別於早期索爾·巴斯〔Saul Bass〕那種較為輕快活潑的圖像式片頭傳統),其影響力也直接延伸到後來《嗜血法醫》、《無間警探》、《破案神探》等影集的片頭字幕設計之中。
