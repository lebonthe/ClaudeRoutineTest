3) Spanish Lesson — Day 41 (10 min, "as if" comparisons reuse both subjunctive time-layers)

Day 40 completed the subjunctive mood's own simple/compound pair (presente de subjuntivo ↔ perfecto de subjuntivo). Today introduces a new fixed trigger phrase that puts both of those layers to work side by side: como si ("as if"), which always takes the subjunctive — never the indicative — because by definition it compares reality to something admittedly untrue. Unlike Day 32-34's trigger categories (time, adjective clauses, purpose/concession), como si is a single frozen idiom rather than a clause type, and it picks between two already-taught subjunctive tenses depending on timing: the imperfect subjunctive (Day 35) for an "as if" comparison happening at the same time as the main verb, and the pluperfect subjunctive (the hubiera-form introduced inside Day 38's Type 3 conditionals) for a comparison about something supposedly already finished.

  • Habla español como si fuera nativo. — He speaks Spanish as if he were a native speaker.
  • Me miró como si no me conociera. — She looked at me as if she didn't know me.
  • Actúa como si nada hubiera pasado. — He acts as if nothing had happened.

Grammar note: como si never takes the indicative, no matter how mundane the comparison sounds — "habla como si fuera nativo" does not mean the speaker doubts reality in the WEIRDO sense, it simply states, as a fact, that the comparison itself is counterfactual (he is, in fact, not a native speaker). Choosing the tense is purely about timing relative to the main verb: fuera/conociera (Day 35's imperfect subjunctive) for a same-time unreal comparison, hubiera pasado (Day 38's pluperfect subjunctive) for a comparison about something that would have had to already be finished. This reuses two forms you already know and simply teaches a new context that demands them.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 41: 損 (Sǔn) — "Decrease" (Mountain above, Lake below — Gèn over Duì). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary pivots directly off yesterday's Hexagram 40: "緩必有所失,故受之以損。" ("What has been loosened/relaxed must inevitably suffer some loss; hence it is followed by Sǔn [Decrease].") Where Hexagram 40 described the relief of finally breaking free from difficulty, Hexagram 41 observes that relaxation itself always costs something — nothing stays gained without a corresponding decrease elsewhere. The Judgment reads: "損,有孚,元吉,無咎,可貞,利有攸往。曷之用?二簋可用享。" ("Decrease, if it possesses sincerity, brings supreme good fortune, no blame; it can be persevered in, and it is favorable to have somewhere to go. What is to be used for it? Two small bowls of grain may be used for the offering.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) explains the mechanism: "損,損下益上,其道上行。損而有孚,元吉,無咎,可貞,利有攸往。曷之用,二簋可用享,二簋應有時,損剛益柔有時,損益盈虛,與時偕行。" ("Decrease means decreasing what is below to increase what is above; its way moves upward. If decrease is carried out with sincerity, it brings supreme good fortune, no blame, can be persevered in, and is favorable to have somewhere to go. As for what is to be used, two bowls may serve for the offering — even two bowls have their proper occasion. Decreasing the firm and increasing the yielding also has its proper time; decrease and increase, fullness and emptiness, must move together with the times.") The Image commentary draws the lesson for conduct: "山下有澤,損;君子以懲忿窒欲。" ("Below the mountain is a lake: this is the image of Decrease. Thus the superior person restrains anger and blocks desire.") Read as a pair, Hexagrams 40 and 41 trace release into its necessary cost: 40 (thunder breaking free of water below) shows danger giving way to liberating movement, while 41 (a lake hollowed out beneath a mountain, its substance rising to nourish the peak) shows that every such gain draws down a reserve somewhere else — the two hexagrams together teaching that renewal is never free, and that willingly accepting a small, sincere loss below is what actually purchases a greater gain above.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — South Sudan (南蘇丹)

  • Location: Northeastern Africa, landlocked; bordered by Sudan to the north, Ethiopia to the east, Kenya, Uganda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo to the south, and the Central African Republic to the west.
  • Territory: About 644,329 km² — roughly 18 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), somewhat larger than France (~551,700 km²), and comparable in scale to the US state of Texas (~695,700 km²).
  • Population: Roughly 11.6 million (2026 estimate); the world's newest sovereign state, having gained independence in 2011.
  • Religion: Predominantly Christian (roughly 60%, mostly Catholic and Anglican/Episcopal), with a substantial share of the population practicing traditional African religions or blending them with Christianity, and a small Muslim minority (roughly 6-10%) concentrated in border and urban areas.
  • Economy: Almost entirely dependent on crude oil, which accounts for the large majority of government revenue and export earnings; ranks among the world's poorest countries by GDP per capita, with subsistence agriculture and pastoralism supporting most livelihoods outside the oil sector, and the economy repeatedly devastated by civil conflict, currency instability, and heavy reliance on foreign humanitarian aid.
  • History: Administered as part of Sudan under the Anglo-Egyptian condominium until Sudanese independence in 1956; the mostly Christian/animist south then fought two long civil wars against the Arab, Muslim-majority north (1955-1972 and 1983-2005), the second alone killing an estimated two million people. The 2005 Comprehensive Peace Agreement ended the war and granted the south autonomy; a 2011 independence referendum passed with 98.83% support, making South Sudan the world's newest country on July 9, 2011. Independence was followed almost immediately by its own devastating civil war (2013-2020) between forces loyal to President Salva Kiir and former Vice President Riek Machar, formally ended by a 2018 peace agreement and a unity government formed in 2020, though tensions between the two camps have flared again in recent years.
  • Current leader: President Salva Kiir Mayardit, in power continuously since independence in 2011, governing in an uneasy power-sharing arrangement with First Vice President Riek Machar under the 2018 peace framework.
  • Language: English is the official language; Juba Arabic serves as a widely spoken lingua franca, alongside dozens of indigenous languages including Dinka, Nuer, Bari, Zande, and Shilluk.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov World Factbook

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — All Your Base Are Belong to Us

  • Name: "All Your Base Are Belong to Us" (often abbreviated AYBABTU), a deliberately broken-English phrase.
  • Origin/history: Taken from the opening cutscene of Zero Wing, a 1989 Sega Mega Drive shoot-'em-up by Toaplan, whose 1992 European release carried a notoriously poor Japanese-to-English translation. In it, the villain CATS declares "All your base are belong to us" while seizing control of the player's space station.
  • How it became popular: The line sat largely unnoticed for nearly a decade until around 2000-2001, when an anonymous Flash animation set the phrase (and the cutscene's other broken dialogue, like "Somebody set up us the bomb") to a techno track and photoshopped the text into real-world signs, billboards, and landmarks. The video spread virally across early internet forums and IRC channels, becoming one of the first true viral-video sensations of the pre-YouTube internet, and was covered by mainstream outlets including CNN and Wired as an early case study of internet culture crossing into the mainstream.
  • Meaning/usage: Used to jokingly declare total ownership, victory, or takeover of something — "all your [X] are belong to us" — often satirizing awkward machine-style or non-native translations ("Engrish") while also functioning as a tongue-in-cheek boast.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned an enormous number of "all your X are belong to us" snowclone variants applied to unrelated topics in tech, business, and gaming communities; referenced in later video games, in the animated series Futurama, and even name-checked in press coverage of real corporate acquisitions; widely considered one of the founding examples of the "snowclone" meme format itself.
  • Current status: Rarely used actively today, but preserved as foundational digital folklore — a fixture in retrospectives on early internet/meme history and frequently cited as the meme that proved a joke could spread globally through nothing but forwarded video files, years before social media existed.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — 8½ (1963, dir. Federico Fellini)

  • Name/key figures: 8½ (Otto e mezzo), written and directed by Federico Fellini, starring Marcello Mastroianni as director Guido Anselmi, with cinematography by Gianni Di Venanzo and score by longtime Fellini collaborator Nino Rota.
  • Era/background: Released in 1963 as Italian cinema moved away from postwar neorealism toward introspective, stylized arthouse filmmaking; the title itself is autobiographical arithmetic — Fellini counted his six prior features, two shorts (each worth a "half"), and one aborted co-directed project to arrive at his "eighth-and-a-half" film, made while he was genuinely blocked on what that film should be about.
  • Plot/content overview: Guido, a celebrated film director, retreats to a spa town supposedly to plan his next film but is instead paralyzed by creative block, besieged by producers, critics, his wife, and his mistress, while an enormous, half-built rocket-ship set sits waiting for a story he cannot summon. The film freely dissolves between his present crisis, childhood memories, and fantasy sequences — most famously an imagined harem where every woman in his life submits to him — with no formal markers separating memory, dream, and reality.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Pioneered a fluid, unannounced blending of dream, memory, fantasy, and present-tense reality within a single continuous visual register; expressionistic black-and-white photography; direct address to the camera; and pervasive self-reflexive metafiction, since the film's subject is explicitly a director's inability to make the very film the audience is watching.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as cinema's definitive portrait of creative block and the artist's crisis of confidence, and a landmark in legitimizing stream-of-consciousness, psychologically subjective narrative structure within mainstream film discourse rather than confining it to experimental cinema.
  • Influence and legacy: Directly inspired a lineage of "filmmaker's block" meta-films, including All That Jazz, Stardust Memories, and Adaptation, as well as the stage musical and film Nine; won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film and Best Costume Design (Black-and-White) in 1964, and consistently ranks among the greatest films ever made in critics' polls such as Sight & Sound's decennial survey.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Restored and distributed by the Criterion Collection; screened regularly at film festivals, retrospectives, and film schools as a foundational text for the study of auteur cinema and narrative subjectivity.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, criterion.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Foreshadowing & Chekhov's Gun (Lesson 27)

Foreshadowing is a narrative or visual detail deliberately planted early — introduced with just enough weight to register, but not so much that its later payoff is telegraphed — that returns to carry significance the audience could not have fully grasped on first appearance. The strictest version of this principle is named for Anton Chekhov's dictum for the stage: "If you say in the first chapter that there is a rifle hanging on the wall, in the second or third chapter it absolutely must go off." This is distinct from Day 6's Narrative Structure (which addresses the overall shape/sequencing of a plot, linear or not) and Day 17's Title Sequence lesson (a compressed thesis statement delivered before the story proper begins), because Chekhov's Gun concerns a single planted detail's delayed activation inside whatever structure the film already has. Four things to check: whether the plant is introduced casually enough to avoid telegraphing itself, yet clearly enough to register on a second viewing; whether the film honors the strict Chekhov's Gun principle (every planted object eventually fires) or deliberately violates it (a red herring, or a conspicuously unfired "gun" used to comment on the audience's own genre expectations); whether the foreshadowing is delivered visually (an object, a gesture, a background detail) or verbally (a line of dialogue easy to miss the first time); and how recognizing the plant on a rewatch retroactively changes the meaning of the scene where it was first introduced.

Worked example: Parasite (2019, dir. Bong Joon-ho) — the ornamental "scholar's rock" (수석, suseok) that the Kims' wealthy friend Min gives the family early in the film as a gift meant to bring prosperity. Introduced almost comically, as a slightly absurd token of upward-mobility fantasy, the rock recurs at increasingly weighted moments: Ki-woo instinctively carries it with him into the rising sewage floodwater rather than saving anything else, revealing exactly how much symbolic hope the family has invested in the object, and it ultimately becomes an actual weapon in the film's violent climax, wielded by Ki-woo before being turned against him. Bong plants the rock purely as a decorative symbol of aspiration in an early scene played partly for comedy, and its final appearance as a literal instrument of violence retroactively reframes that earlier hope as always having carried the seed of harm within it — a textbook Chekhov's Gun that doubles as the film's darkest thematic statement about what aspirational objects curdle into under enough pressure.

3)西班牙文課程——第 41 天(10 分鐘,「彷彿」句同時動用兩層虛擬式時態)

第 40 天補完了虛擬式自己的簡單式/複合式配對(虛擬式現在式 ↔ 虛擬式現在完成式)。今天要介紹一個全新的固定觸發語——「como si」(彷彿、好像),它會同時動用這兩層時態。「como si」永遠只接虛擬式,絕不接直陳式,因為它的定義本身就是「拿現實去比較一件明知不是真的事」。與第 32~34 天教過的觸發類別(時間子句、形容詞子句、目的/讓步子句)不同,「como si」不是一種子句類型,而是一句固定成語;它會依據時間點,從你已經學過的兩種虛擬式時態裡二選一:與主要動詞同時發生的比較,用第 35 天教過的虛擬式過去未完了式;比較的內容是「早已發生完畢的事」,則用第 38 天「與過去事實相反」條件句裡出現過的虛擬式過去完成式(hubiera 形式)。

  • Habla español como si fuera nativo. —— 他說西班牙文,彷彿他是母語人士一樣。
  • Me miró como si no me conociera. —— 她看著我,彷彿不認識我一樣。
  • Actúa como si nada hubiera pasado. —— 他表現得,彷彿什麼事都沒發生過一樣。

文法筆記:「como si」永遠不接直陳式,不管這句比較聽起來多麼稀鬆平常——「habla como si fuera nativo」並不是在 WEIRDO 那種「懷疑現實」的意義上使用虛擬式,而是直接陳述一個事實:這個比較本身就是與事實相反的(說話者其實並不是母語人士)。真正需要判斷的,只是「相對於主要動詞,時間點在哪裡」:fuera/conociera(第 35 天教過的虛擬式過去未完了式)用於「同一時間點」的不實比較;hubiera pasado(第 38 天教過的虛擬式過去完成式)則用於「比較的內容早該已經結束」的情況。這堂課並沒有教新的動詞變化,而是教你一個全新的情境,逼著你動用兩個你早已學會的形式。

第 41 卦:損 (Sǔn) ——「減損」(山在上,澤在下——艮上兌下)。《序卦傳》從昨天的第 40 卦直接接續下來:「緩必有所失,故受之以損。」(凡是鬆懈下來的事物,必然會有所損失,因此解卦之後接的是損卦。)如果說第 40 卦描述的是終於掙脫困境所帶來的釋放感,那麼第 41 卦則指出:鬆懈本身必然要付出代價——沒有任何獲得,是不需要在別處相應付出減損的。卦辭寫道:「損,有孚,元吉,無咎,可貞,利有攸往。曷之用?二簋可用享。」(損卦:若心懷誠信,便能帶來至高的吉祥,沒有災咎,可以持續奉行下去,適合有所前往。那該用什麼來實踐它呢?即使只用兩簋淺薄的食物,也足以用來獻祭。)《彖傳》進一步解釋其中的道理:「損,損下益上,其道上行。損而有孚,元吉,無咎,可貞,利有攸往。曷之用,二簋可用享,二簋應有時,損剛益柔有時,損益盈虛,與時偕行。」(損卦,是減損下方以增益上方,它的道理是向上運行的。只要減損的同時心懷誠信,就能帶來至高的吉祥,沒有災咎,可以持續奉行,適合有所前往。至於該用什麼來實踐,即使兩簋菲薄的祭品也能奉獻——但即使是兩簋,也要合乎時宜;減損剛強、增益柔順,同樣要合乎時宜;減損與增益、盈滿與空虛,都必須隨著時勢的變化而同步調整。)《象傳》則點出實際行事的教訓:「山下有澤,損;君子以懲忿窒欲。」(山的下方有一片澤水,這就是損卦的卦象。因此君子看見損卦,便會藉此克制憤怒、堵住過度的欲望。)將第 40 卦與第 41 卦合觀,兩卦勾勒出「釋放」之後必然伴隨的代價:第 40 卦(雷掙脫下方水勢而出)呈現的是危險讓位給解放的行動,而第 41 卦(山下的澤水,將自身的內涵向上輸送、滋養山峰)則顯示:每一次這樣的獲得,都是從別處的存量中支取出來的——兩卦合起來教導的是,更新從來不是沒有代價的;甘願在下方承受一次微小而真誠的損失,正是換取上方更大獲得的必要條件。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——南蘇丹(South Sudan)

  • 位置:位於東北非,為內陸國;北鄰蘇丹,東接衣索比亞,南邊是肯亞、烏干達與剛果民主共和國,西邊是中非共和國。
  • 領土:約 644,329 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 18 倍大,略大於法國(約 551,700 平方公里),規模上與美國德州(約 695,700 平方公里)相當。
  • 人口:約 1,160 萬人(2026 年估計);是全球最年輕的主權國家,於 2011 年才正式獨立建國。
  • 宗教:以基督教為主(約 60%,多屬天主教與聖公會/英國國教派),另有相當比例人口信奉非洲傳統宗教、或將傳統信仰與基督教融合實踐,穆斯林人口占少數(約 6%~10%),主要集中在邊境與都市地區。
  • 經濟:幾乎完全仰賴原油,原油出口占政府歲入與出口收入的絕大部分;以人均 GDP 而言,是全球最貧窮的國家之一,油業以外的多數民生仰賴自給農業與遊牧畜牧業維持;經濟屢遭內戰、貨幣不穩定重創,高度仰賴國際人道援助。
  • 歷史:在英埃共管時期以蘇丹一部分的身分被統治,直到 1956 年蘇丹獨立;此後,以基督教/傳統信仰為主的南方,與以阿拉伯穆斯林為主的北方,爆發了兩次曠日持久的內戰(1955-1972 年、1983-2005 年),第二次內戰估計奪走約兩百萬條人命。2005 年《全面和平協議》結束戰爭,賦予南方自治權;2011 年獨立公投以 98.83% 的高支持率通過,南蘇丹於 2011 年 7 月 9 日成為全球最年輕的獨立國家。獨立後幾乎立刻爆發了自己的內戰(2013-2020 年),交戰雙方為效忠總統薩爾瓦·基爾(Salva Kiir)的勢力,以及前副總統里克·馬查爾(Riek Machar)的勢力,2018 年簽署和平協議正式結束戰爭,2020 年組成團結政府,但雙方陣營近年來緊張情勢仍時有再起。
  • 現任領導人:總統薩爾瓦·基爾·馬亞爾迪特(Salva Kiir Mayardit),自 2011 年獨立以來持續在位,依 2018 年和平框架,與第一副總統里克·馬查爾維持一種並不穩固的權力分享關係。
  • 語言:英語為官方語言;朱巴阿拉伯語(Juba Arabic)是廣泛使用的通用語,境內另有數十種本土語言,包括丁卡語、努爾語、巴里語、贊德語與希魯克語等。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov 世界概況(World Factbook)

6)網路迷因介紹——All Your Base Are Belong to Us(你的一切基地都屬於我們)

  • 名稱:「All Your Base Are Belong to Us」(常縮寫為 AYBABTU),一句刻意呈現破碎文法的英文句子。
  • 起源/歷史:出自 1989 年 Toaplan 開發、Sega Mega Drive 平台的射擊遊戲《Zero Wing》開場過場動畫,其 1992 年歐洲版由日文翻譯成英文時,翻譯品質極差、廣為人知。動畫中反派角色 CATS 一邊奪取玩家太空站的控制權,一邊宣告:「All your base are belong to us.」
  • 如何走紅:這句台詞幾乎沉寂了近十年,直到 2000 年至 2001 年左右,有位匿名創作者製作了一支 Flash 動畫,將這句台詞(以及動畫中其他破碎的對白,例如「Somebody set up us the bomb」)配上電子舞曲,並將文字合成進真實世界的招牌、看板與地標照片中。這支影片在早期網路論壇與 IRC 頻道間瘋狂轉傳,成為 YouTube 出現以前、真正意義上第一批病毒式爆紅影片之一,甚至獲得 CNN、Wired 等主流媒體報導,視為網路文化打入主流視野的早期經典案例。
  • 意義/使用情境:用來半開玩笑地宣告對某件事物的全面掌控、勝利或接管——套用句型「all your [X] are belong to us」——經常用來諷刺笨拙的機器式或非母語翻譯(即「Engrish」),同時也帶有一種戲謔式炫耀的意味。
  • 變體/演化:衍生出大量「all your X are belong to us」的句型變體,套用在科技、商業、遊戲等各種不相關領域;後續也被電玩遊戲、動畫影集《飛出個未來》(Futurama)引用,甚至出現在真實企業併購的媒體報導標題中;普遍被視為「句型迷因」(snowclone)這種迷因形式的奠基範例之一。
  • 目前狀態:如今已鮮少被主動使用,但作為數位民俗學的重要一頁被保留下來——在早期網路/迷因歷史的回顧文章中經常被提及,也常被引用來證明:早在社群媒體出現的許多年前,單靠轉傳影片檔案,一個笑話就足以在全球範圍內散播開來。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《8又1/2》8½(1963,導演:費德里柯·費里尼)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《8又1/2》(義大利文原名 Otto e mezzo),由費德里柯·費里尼(Federico Fellini)編劇並執導,馬切洛·馬斯楚安尼(Marcello Mastroianni)飾演導演主角圭多·安塞爾米(Guido Anselmi),攝影由詹尼·迪·維南佐(Gianni Di Venanzo)掌鏡,配樂則由費里尼長年合作夥伴尼諾·羅塔(Nino Rota)操刀。
  • 時代/背景:發行於 1963 年,正值義大利電影從戰後新寫實主義,轉向內省、風格化藝術電影的年代;片名本身就是一道自傳式的算術題——費里尼將自己先前執導的六部長片、兩部短片(各算「半部」),加上一部未完成的聯合執導作品,合計算出這是他的「第八又二分之一部」電影,而拍攝當時,他正陷於不知道這部電影該講什麼的真實創作瓶頸之中。
  • 內容概述:圭多是一位知名電影導演,原本前往溫泉小鎮規劃下一部電影,卻反而陷入創作癱瘓,被製片人、影評人、妻子與情婦四面包圍,一座龐大、尚未完工的火箭太空船佈景,靜靜等待一個他遲遲想不出來的故事。全片在他當下的危機、童年回憶與幻想場景之間自由流動、不做區隔——其中最著名的是一段幻想中的後宮場景,他生命中的每一位女性都對他俯首聽命——記憶、夢境與現實之間完全沒有正式的分界標記。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:開創了一種流暢、不加預告地在夢境、記憶、幻想與當下現實之間自由切換的敘事手法,全部收攏在同一種連貫的視覺語彙之中;搭配富有表現主義色彩的黑白攝影;直接對鏡頭說話的表現方式;以及貫穿全片、高度自我指涉的後設敘事——因為這部電影本身的主題,正是一位導演無法拍出「觀眾此刻正在觀看的這部電影」。
  • 文化/歷史意義:被廣泛視為電影史上對「創作瓶頸」與「藝術家自我信心危機」最具代表性的刻劃之作,也是將意識流、心理主觀式敘事結構,從實驗電影的邊緣位置,正式帶入主流電影論述核心的重要里程碑。
  • 影響與傳承:直接啟發了一整個系譜的「導演創作瓶頸」後設電影,包括《爵士春秋》(All That Jazz)、伍迪·艾倫的《星塵往事》(Stardust Memories),以及《蘭花賊》(Adaptation),也催生了同名百老匯音樂劇與電影《華麗年代》(Nine);本片在 1964 年獲得奧斯卡最佳外語片與最佳黑白服裝設計獎,並持續在《視與聽》(Sight & Sound)雜誌等權威影史十年一度的影史最佳影片票選中名列前茅。
  • 現況/如何欣賞:目前已由 Criterion Collection 完成數位修復並發行;在世界各地的影展、回顧展與電影學校中,仍是研究作者論電影與敘事主觀性時經常放映的經典教材。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、criterion.com

8)看片方法——伏筆與「契訶夫之槍」Foreshadowing & Chekhov's Gun(第 27 課)

伏筆(foreshadowing)是一種刻意在早期埋下的敘事或視覺細節——分量拿捏得剛好足以讓觀眾留下印象,卻又不至於直接洩漏後續發展——這個細節會在後段回收,承載觀眾第一次看到時,不可能完全理解的意義。這項原則最嚴格的版本,得名自契訶夫(Anton Chekhov)對舞台劇的名言:「如果你在第一幕提到牆上掛著一把槍,那麼到了第二幕或第三幕,這把槍就絕對必須擊發。」這與第 6 天教過的敘事結構(處理整體劇情的排列順序,無論是否線性)不同,也與第 17 天教過的片頭字幕課(在正式故事開始前,就先端出一份壓縮過的主題宣言)不同,因為「契訶夫之槍」關注的是:在電影既有的任何結構之中,單一伏筆本身如何延遲引爆。判斷時可檢查四件事:這個伏筆的埋設,是否隨性到不至於暴露自己,卻又清楚到能在二刷時被辨認出來;這部電影是嚴格遵守「契訶夫之槍」原則(每一把埋下的槍最終都會擊發),還是刻意違反它(當作一個誤導觀眾的紅鯡魚,或是刻意留一把「沒有擊發的槍」,藉此評論觀眾自身的類型期待);這個伏筆是透過視覺呈現(一件物品、一個動作、背景中的一個細節),還是透過台詞呈現(一句第一次很容易被忽略的對白);以及,當觀眾在重看時認出這個伏筆,會如何回頭改變它第一次出現那場戲的意義。

實例演練:《寄生上流》(Parasite,2019,導演:奉俊昊)——片中富有的朋友敏赫,在電影早段送給金家一顆據說能帶來財運的裝飾性「水石」(수석,scholar's rock)。這顆石頭一開始的登場近乎帶點喜感,像是一個關於階級向上流動幻想、略顯荒謬的象徵物;但它在後續愈趨沉重的時刻反覆出現——當地下室淹水、污水暴漲時,基宇本能地選擇搶救這顆石頭,而不是其他任何東西,揭示這家人究竟在這件物品上寄託了多少象徵性的希望;而在電影結尾那場暴力高潮戲中,這顆石頭最終真的化為一件實體凶器,先被基宇用來行凶,隨後又反過來被用在他身上。奉俊昊一開始只是把這顆石頭種下,作為一段部分帶著喜劇色彩戲份中、單純裝飾性的欲望象徵物,而它最終以實體暴力工具之姿再度登場,回頭將先前那份希望重新定義為——其中始終潛藏著暴力的種子——這是一次教科書等級的「契訶夫之槍」演練,同時也是全片對「向上流動的欲望物件,在足夠的壓力之下會如何質變」這個最陰暗主題陳述的具體體現。
