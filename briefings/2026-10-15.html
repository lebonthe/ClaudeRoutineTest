3) Spanish Lesson — Day 91: Mientras / Mientras que / Mientras tanto

One small word with three faces. Mientras + verb means "while" (two actions at the same time) and, with the subjunctive, "as long as" for future or hypothetical situations; mientras que means "whereas" and contrasts two facts; mientras tanto means "meanwhile". The colloquial mientras más…, más… means "the more… the more…". Rule of thumb: durante + noun, mientras + clause with a verb.

  • Mientras tú cocinas, yo pongo la mesa. — While you cook, I set the table.
  • Mientras tanto, los niños ven la tele. — Meanwhile, the kids watch TV.
  • Yo trabajo mucho, mientras que tú descansas. — I work a lot, whereas you rest.
  • Mientras vivas aquí, ayudarás en casa. — As long as you live here, you'll help at home.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 27: 寓言 (Yùyán) — "Imputed Words" / "Allegory"

The fifth of the Miscellaneous Chapters (雜篇, Ch. 27) and one of the chapters in which Zhuangzi explains his own way of speaking. It opens with three kinds of speech: 寓言 "lodged words" (allegory, nine parts in ten: arguing through someone else's mouth because a father does not praise his own son, since praise from outside is more believable), 重言 "weighty words" (the words of elders, seven in ten, which carry authority though age alone is not wisdom), and 卮言 "goblet words" (words that pour out freely like a tilting goblet, day after day, harmonized by the "heavenly whetstone" 天倪, so that one can speak through the years without being exhausted). A key line: 言無言。終身言,未嘗言;終身不言,未嘗不言 — "Speak words that say nothing: speak your whole life and you have never spoken; stay silent your whole life and you have never not spoken."

The chapter then moves into parables. Zhuangzi says Confucius "changed at sixty, with every year" (行年六十而六十化): what he held right at first he later set aside as wrong, so one can never be sure that today's "right" is not tomorrow's "wrong". Zengzi's two official posts show how a person's feeling is shaped by circumstance: with a small salary of three fu he was happy because he could feed his parents; later with three thousand zhong he was sad because they were gone and could not share it. In 罔兩問影, the Penumbra asks the Shadow why it moves and stops, sits and rises without a will of its own; the Shadow answers that it depends on something else, which depends on something else in turn (the snake's scales, the cicada's wings), so "how could I know why it is so, or why it is not?" Yangzi Ju's meeting with Lao Dan (大白若辱,盛德若不足, "the purest white looks soiled, great virtue looks lacking") shows that once he dropped his pride, the guests at the inn who had earlier hurried out of his way now fought him for a seat. The chapter also lists nine stages by which Yan Chengzi Youyi (a pupil of Dongguo Zi Qi) describes the ripening of understanding, from "rustic" to "complete in heaven" and finally "great wonder".

Takeaway: Be wary of treating any single position as final, speak in ways that let others hear without arguing, and note how much of "the self" depends on circumstances beyond oneself. (Chapter details are from general knowledge and labeled as such; not rechecked against a text today.)

  • Culture note: Birthplace of ska, rocksteady, reggae and dancehall; Bob Marley is the best-known figure, and sprinter Usain Bolt made the island famous in athletics. Jamaica has diplomatic relations with Beijing, not Taipei (general knowledge).

Cast: Dharmendra (Veeru), Amitabh Bachchan (Jai), Sanjeev Kumar (Thakur Baldev Singh), Hema Malini (Basanti), Jaya Bhaduri (Radha) and Amjad Khan as the bandit Gabbar Singh.
  • Era / background: Released on 15 August 1975 in the middle of India's period of Emergency, when angry-young-man stories were popular. It was made as a large-scale "masala" film, shot in 70mm widescreen with stereophonic sound. A large rocky set was built near Ramanagara, Karnataka, for the fictional village of Ramgarh.
  • Plot overview: A retired police officer, Thakur, hires two small-time crooks, Jai and Veeru, whom he once arrested, to capture the dreaded dacoit Gabbar Singh, who killed his family and cut off his arms. The pair become friends of the village, with a love story for Veeru and Basanti and a quiet one for Jai and the widowed Radha. The story ends with Jai's death and Gabbar's defeat. The censors required the original ending, in which Thakur kills Gabbar, to be replaced by one where the police arrive; the original cut was restored for later releases (general knowledge).
  • Artistic style and techniques: Often called a "curry western", it mixes the structure of Sergio Leone spaghetti westerns and Kurosawa's Seven Samurai (a village protected by hired fighters) with Indian melodrama, songs ("Yeh Dosti", "Mehbooba Mehbooba"), comedy and tragedy. Action sequences (the train robbery, the horse-and-motorbike chase) were staged with wide scope compositions; Gabbar Singh's introduction and his lines ("Kitne aadmi the?") were designed to be remembered.
  • Cultural / historical significance: The most celebrated Hindi blockbuster: it ran for five years or more in a single Mumbai cinema, and its dialogues were sold on records and cassettes before the film's video release. The British Film Institute's 2004 poll named it the best Indian film of all time (general knowledge). Gabbar became the model of the Bollywood villain.
  • Influence and legacy: Its tropes (the buddy duo, the charismatic villain, quotable dialogue, the song-and-action balance) shaped decades of Indian cinema. It has been parodied constantly, and the 2014 3D version and the restored 4K version have brought it to new audiences.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 77: Exposition — Showing vs. Telling (How a Film Delivers Information)

The method: Every film has to tell the audience things: who the hero is, what happened before, what the rules of the world are. Exposition is how it delivers that information. The question to ask while watching is: "How do I know this, and what did the film make me do to learn it?" The delivery can be divided into several channels: (1) action: the character does something that reveals who they are; (2) image and design: costume, props, location, light; (3) reaction: what other characters do or say about someone; (4) dialogue; (5) voice-over or titles; and (6) performance: an actor's face and body. Strong exposition usually uses the earlier channels and keeps explicit dialogue for moments when characters actually need to say it.

How to analyze it: (1) In the first ten minutes, write down five things you now know and, for each, the channel that taught you. (2) Check whether the information arrives with conflict or need (a character who wants something forces the fact out) or arrives as a lecture where two characters tell each other what both already know (the "As you know, Bob" problem). (3) Look at timing: is information given only when the audience needs it, or dumped up front? (4) Ask what is withheld, since withholding is also exposition: a mystery is built from what the film chooses not to show yet. (5) Ask whether the film trusts you to infer, and what that says about the film's attitude to its audience.

Worked example — Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981, dir. Steven Spielberg, written by Lawrence Kasdan, cinematography by Douglas Slocombe), the Peruvian temple opening: In roughly the first ten minutes, with almost no explanatory dialogue, the film teaches us who Indiana Jones is. (a) Image and design: he is first seen from behind and in shadow, the hat and silhouette arriving before the face, so we learn he is a figure of legend before we learn his name. His costume (fedora, leather jacket, whip, satchel) becomes a visual shorthand that will return in every scene. (b) Action: he does not simply run in; he tests the ground, notices the spiderwebs and the dust, and uses a sandbag to try to fool the idol's trap. We learn he is clever, careful and prepared, but also that his plan fails, so he is fallible and must improvise. (c) Reaction: his guide Satipo's betrayal and the way the local people respond to him show that he is trusted and distrusted. (d) Dialogue only where it pays: the rival Belloq's line when he takes the idol ("Once again, Jones, what was rightfully mine becomes yours" is the film's rival-and-mirror idea in one sentence, and it arrives with conflict, as a taunt, not as a lecture). (e) Withholding: his history with Belloq is hinted at but never explained, so the audience is left curious and the film can pay it off later. Compare: in Jaws (1975), Quint's USS Indianapolis monologue is pure telling, yet it works because it is staged as a drunken confession in a scene about comparing scars, with a motive, an audience on screen, and a performance that makes the images in our mind. Why it works: information that arrives through action or conflict feels discovered, not delivered, so the audience stays active. Try this: on your next film, stop at the first plot-heavy scene and ask whether the same information could have been shown through something a character does.

3) 西班牙文課程 — 第 91 天:Mientras / Mientras que / Mientras tanto

一個小字,三種面貌。Mientras + 動詞是「當……的時候」(兩個動作同時進行),搭配虛擬式則表示未來或假設的「只要……就」;mientras que 是「然而、而……卻……」,用來對比兩個事實;mientras tanto 是「與此同時」。口語的 mientras más…, más… 是「越……越……」。口訣:durante 後面接名詞,mientras 後面接有動詞的子句。

4) 莊子 — 第 27 篇:寓言(Yùyán)

這是雜篇的第五章(雜篇第 27 章),也是莊子自己解釋說話方式的篇章之一。開頭點出三種言說:寓言(藉他人之口立論,十分之九;因為父親不自誇兒子,由外人來稱讚才更可信)、重言(長者之言,十分之七,有權威,但光靠年紀不等於智慧)、卮言(像傾斜的酒器一樣日日流出的話,以「天倪」調和,因此可以說上多年而不窮盡)。關鍵一句是言無言。終身言,未嘗言;終身不言,未嘗不言——「說那無所言說的話:說了一輩子,等於沒說;一輩子不說,等於沒有不說。」

接著進入一連串寓言。莊子說孔子「行年六十而六十化」:起初認為對的,後來又否定,所以你永遠不能確定今天的「是」不是明天的「非」。曾子兩度出仕,說明人的感受如何被處境塑造:他第一次領俸祿只有三釜,因為能奉養父母而快樂;後來領了三千鍾,卻因為父母已不在而悲傷。在罔兩問影裡,影子外圍的微影(罔兩)問影子:為什麼你一會兒動、一會兒停、一會兒坐、一會兒起,毫無主見?影子回答說,它是有所依待而這樣的,而它所依待的東西又有所依待(像蛇的蛻皮與蟬的翼),所以「我怎麼知道為什麼這樣?又怎麼知道為什麼不這樣?」陽子居見老聃(大白若辱,盛德若不足,最潔白的好像有污點,盛大的德行好像不足),他放下驕傲之後,原先在旅店裡紛紛避讓他的住客,如今反而與他爭席位。本篇還列出顏成子游(東郭子綦的學生)描述領悟逐步成熟的九個階段,從「野」(質樸)到「天成」,最後是「大妙」。

重點:不要把任何單一立場當成最終答案;用讓人聽得進去、不引起爭辯的方式說話;並留意所謂「自我」有多少是由自身以外的條件所決定。(篇章細節來自一般知識,今天未對照原文重新查核。)

  • 宗教:以基督教為主(多為新教:五旬節派、浸信會、基督復臨安息日會、聖公會等,另有天主教少數);拉斯塔法里(Rastafari)信仰於 1930 年代在牙買加興起,人數雖少卻舉世聞名;也有源自非洲的 Revival 與 Obeah 傳統(約略,一般知識)。
  • 經濟:小型開放的中高收入經濟體,GDP 約 200–250 億美元(一般知識)。主要產業:旅遊業(最大外匯來源)、僑匯、鋁土礦與氧化鋁開採、農業(甘蔗、香蕉、藍山咖啡)與商務流程外包。高額公共債務在過去十年大幅降低。島上容易受颶風影響;2025 年 10 月下旬,災難性的五級颶風「梅莉莎」(Melissa)襲擊牙買加西部(一般知識,復原情況今天未查核)。
  • 歷史:哥倫布於 1494 年到達之前,島上住著泰諾人。西班牙統治至 1655 年被英國攻占;成為由被奴役的非洲人耕作的大型蔗糖殖民地,也出現著名的馬龍人(逃亡奴隸)社群。1834 年廢除奴隸制(1838 年完全自由);1865 年莫蘭特灣叛亂後改為英國王室直接統治。馬科斯·加維(Marcus Garvey)以及後來的諾曼·曼利與亞歷山大·布斯塔曼特等勞工運動領袖,推動了自治之路;牙買加於 1962 年 8 月 6 日獨立,仍為大英國協王國。
  • 政體與現任領導人:議會制君主立憲,君主為查爾斯三世國王,由總督派崔克·艾倫爵士代表。總理為牙買加工黨的安德魯·霍尼斯(Andrew Holness),自 2016 年起任職(2011–12 年也曾短暫任職),2025 年 9 月再度勝選(一般知識;未對照最新新聞)。
  • 語言:官方語言為英語;牙買加方言(Jamaican Patois / Jamaican Creole,帶有西非影響的英語克里奧爾語)是大多數人日常使用的語言。
  • 文化備註:斯卡(ska)、搖滾穩定(rocksteady)、雷鬼(reggae)與舞廳(dancehall)音樂的發源地;鮑勃·馬利(Bob Marley)是最知名的人物,短跑選手尤塞恩·博爾特(Usain Bolt)也讓這座島在田徑界聞名。牙買加與北京有外交關係,與台北沒有(一般知識)。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — Roll Safe(「你不可能……,只要你……」)

  • 名稱:「Roll Safe」(也被稱為「聰明黑人」或「思考男」),是一張男子笑著用手指敲太陽穴的截圖,配字格式通常是「You can't [壞結果] if you [鑽漏洞]」。
  • 起源/歷史:圖片出自英國喜劇演員 Kayode Ewumi 的網路影集 Hood Documentary(2015),他在其中飾演 Roll Safe,一個自信滿滿但街頭智慧有問題的角色。「敲太陽穴」的畫面取自該角色的影片片段。確切日期與最早的迷因用法今天未重新查核。
  • 如何流行:約 2016–2017 年透過推特與 Reddit 擴散,網友為它加上歪理式的文字,例如「You can't be broke if you don't check your bank balance」(不查餘額就不會沒錢),也以動態 GIF 流傳。好笑之處在於圖像看起來像得意的天才,推理卻明顯有漏洞。
  • 意義與使用情境:用來表現荒謬的「生活小撇步」邏輯、逃避問題而不是解決問題,或刻意的愚蠢自信。常用來自嘲(「不去考試就不會不及格」)。
  • 變化/演變:裁切版本、各國語言的版本,以及被品牌與體育帳號沿用的「鑽漏洞」句型模板。這個角色一般被解讀為對過度自信的街頭建議的戲仿。
  • 目前狀況:仍偶爾被當成反應 GIF 使用,但主要流行期在 2017–2018 年。(一般知識;今天未驗證。)

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《火焰》Sholay(1975)

  • 名稱與主要人物:Sholay(意為「餘燼/火焰」),印地語電影,導演 Ramesh Sippy,製片 G. P. Sippy,編劇 Salim-Javed(Salim Khan 與 Javed Akhtar),音樂 R. D. Burman,攝影 Dwarka Divecha。演員:Dharmendra(Veeru)、Amitabh Bachchan(Jai)、Sanjeev Kumar(Thakur Baldev Singh)、Hema Malini(Basanti)、Jaya Bhaduri(Radha),以及飾演土匪 Gabbar Singh 的 Amjad Khan。
  • 時代與背景:1975 年 8 月 15 日上映,正值印度「緊急狀態」時期,「憤怒青年」故事當道。它被打造成大規模的「馬薩拉」(綜合類型)電影,以 70 毫米寬銀幕與立體聲拍攝;劇組在卡納塔克邦拉馬納加拉(Ramanagara)附近搭建了大型岩石場景,作為虛構的拉姆格爾(Ramgarh)村莊。
  • 劇情概述:退休警官 Thakur 雇用曾被他逮捕的兩個小混混 Jai 與 Veeru,去抓捕殺了他全家、還砍斷他雙臂的可怕土匪 Gabbar Singh。兩人成為村民的朋友,Veeru 與 Basanti 展開愛情,Jai 則與寡婦 Radha 有一段含蓄的感情。故事以 Jai 之死與 Gabbar 被擊敗作結。審查單位要求把原本由 Thakur 親手殺死 Gabbar 的結局,改為警察趕到;原版後來在重新發行時恢復(一般知識)。
  • 藝術風格與技法:常被稱為「咖哩西部片」,把李昂尼(Sergio Leone)義大利式西部片與黑澤明《七武士》(僱用戰士保護村莊)的結構,和印度式情節劇、歌舞(〈Yeh Dosti〉、〈Mehbooba Mehbooba〉)、喜劇與悲劇混合。動作場面(劫火車、馬與摩托車追逐)以寬廣構圖呈現;Gabbar Singh 的登場與台詞(「Kitne aadmi the?」,「有幾個人?」)被設計成令人難忘。
  • 文化與歷史意義:最受推崇的印地語賣座片:在孟買單一戲院連映五年以上,台詞在影片發行錄影帶之前就以唱片與錄音帶販售。英國電影協會(BFI)2004 年的票選將它評為史上最佳印度電影(一般知識)。Gabbar 成為寶萊塢反派的典範。
  • 影響與傳承:它的套路(搭檔二人組、有魅力的反派、可引用的台詞、歌舞與動作的平衡)形塑了數十年的印度電影。它不斷被惡搞,2014 年的 3D 版本與修復的 4K 版本也讓新觀眾接觸到它。
  • 今天可以在哪裡欣賞:修復版曾在影展與重新上映中放映;各地區通常可在串流平台與家庭影音上找到(請確認當前上架情況)。今天未查核。

8) 看片方法 — 第 77 課:資訊鋪陳(Exposition)——「演出來」還是「說出來」

方法:每部電影都必須告訴觀眾一些事:主角是誰、之前發生過什麼、這個世界的規則是什麼。資訊鋪陳(exposition)就是電影傳遞這些資訊的方式。看片時要問的問題是:「我是怎麼知道這件事的?電影讓我做了什麼才讓我知道?」傳遞的管道可以分成幾種:(1) 動作:角色做了什麼,透露了他是誰;(2) 影像與設計:服裝、道具、場景、光線;(3) 反應:其他角色對某人的舉動或說法;(4) 對白;(5) 旁白或字幕;(6) 表演:演員的臉與身體。好的資訊鋪陳通常用前面的管道,把直接的對白留給角色真正需要說的時刻。

分析步驟:(1) 在開頭十分鐘寫下你現在知道的五件事,並為每一件標出教你的管道。(2) 檢查資訊是帶著衝突或需求出現(有目標的角色逼出事實),還是變成一場講課,讓兩個角色互相講述雙方都已經知道的事(「As you know, Bob」問題)。(3) 看時機:資訊是在觀眾需要時才給,還是一開始就傾倒出來?(4) 問什麼被保留,因為保留也是一種鋪陳:懸疑來自電影選擇暫時不展示的東西。(5) 問電影是否信任你自己去推論,這又說明了電影對觀眾的態度。

實例分析 — 《法櫃奇兵》(Raiders of the Lost Ark,1981,導演史蒂芬·史匹柏,編劇勞倫斯·卡斯丹,攝影道格拉斯·史洛孔)開場的秘魯神殿片段:在大約前十分鐘裡,幾乎沒有說明性對白,影片就教會我們印第安納·瓊斯是誰。(a) 影像與設計:他最初是從背後、在陰影中出現,帽子與剪影比臉先到,所以我們在知道他的名字之前,就先知道他是個傳奇人物。他的服裝(軟呢帽、皮夾克、長鞭、背包)成為每場戲都會再出現的視覺簡寫。(b) 動作:他不是直接衝進去;他測試地面、注意蜘蛛網與灰塵,並用沙袋試圖欺騙神像底座的機關。我們知道他聰明、謹慎、有準備,但他的計畫失敗了,所以他會犯錯,也必須臨場應變。(c) 反應:嚮導薩提波的背叛,以及當地人對他的反應,顯示他既受信任也被懷疑。(d) 只在值得的地方用對白:對手貝洛克拿走神像時的台詞(「Once again, Jones, what was rightfully mine becomes yours」——「瓊斯,又一次,本該屬於我的東西落入你手裡」),用一句話點出影片「對手即鏡像」的概念,而且它是帶著衝突、以嘲諷的方式出現,不是演講。(e) 保留:他與貝洛克的過往只被暗示、從不解釋,觀眾因此保持好奇,影片也能留待之後再收回這條線。對照:《大白鯊》(1975)中,昆特講述印第安納波利斯號的獨白是純粹的「說出來」,卻之所以有效,是因為它被安排成一場醉酒告白,出現在比較傷疤的戲裡,有動機、有在場的聽眾,並由表演讓畫面在我們腦中出現。為什麼有效:透過動作或衝突抵達的資訊讓人感覺是自己「發現」的,而不是被「交付」的,所以觀眾保持主動。試試看:下一部電影看到第一場資訊量很大的戲時,暫停一下,問自己:同樣的資訊能否改由角色做的某件事來呈現?
