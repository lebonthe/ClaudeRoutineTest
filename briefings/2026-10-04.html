3) Spanish Lesson — Day 80 (10 min, Quedar vs. Quedarse)

Yesterday's "leaving" verbs have a mirror image: quedarse (reflexive) means to stay put, while non-reflexive quedar means to arrange to meet, to be left over, to be located, or to fit/suit. Trap: me quedo en casa = I'm staying home; quedo en casa = I'll meet you at home.

  • ¿Quedamos mañana a las siete? — Shall we meet tomorrow at seven?
  • Me quedo en la oficina hasta las seis. — I'm staying at the office until six.
  • Solo quedan diez minutos. — Only ten minutes are left.
  • Esa chaqueta te queda genial. — That jacket looks great on you.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 16: 繕性 (Shàn Xìng) — "Mending the Inborn Nature"

The ninth chapter of the Outer Chapters (外篇), following "Constrained in Will." It argues that people who try to repair their original nature through learning, and to cultivate their desires through cleverness, are "benighted": they have started from the wrong end. In the age of perfect unity, tranquility nourished knowledge without knowledge being used to manage anything; harmony arose of itself. As the world declined, rulers added rules and ornaments, and people drowned their nature in conventions. The chapter closes by saying the ancient hermits did not hide out of cowardice: their times and fates simply did not allow the Way to be put into practice, so they waited quietly. 5) Country / Region Spotlight — Hungary (匈牙利)

  • Location & territory size: A landlocked country in Central Europe, straddling the middle Danube and the Carpathian Basin, bordering Slovakia, Ukraine, Romania, Serbia, Croatia, Slovenia and Austria. Area about 93,000 km², roughly 2.6 times Taiwan, about the size of Portugal or the US state of Indiana.
  • Population: About 9.6 million (2025); Budapest holds roughly 1.7 million, nearly a fifth of the country.
  • Religion: Historically Christian. Catholics are the largest group (roughly 40% in recent censuses), followed by Reformed (Calvinist) and Lutheran churches, with a large and growing share who report no religion or no answer; there is also a small Jewish community.
  • Language: Hungarian (Magyar), a Uralic language unrelated to its Slavic, Germanic and Romance neighbors, distantly related to Finnish and Estonian.
  • Economy: GDP about US$223 billion in 2024, about US$23,300 per person. Growth was weak in 2025 (about 0.7% expected). Strong in car manufacturing (Audi in Győr, Mercedes in Kecskemét, BMW in Debrecen) and battery plants; EU member since 2004, but it still uses its own currency, the forint, not the euro.
  • History: Magyar tribes entered the Carpathian Basin around 895; Stephen I was crowned around 1000-1001 and made the kingdom Christian. The Mongol invasion of 1241 devastated it; the defeat at Mohács in 1526 led to about 150 years of Ottoman rule, then Habsburg control. The 1867 compromise created Austria-Hungary. After World War I the Treaty of Trianon (1920) stripped it of about two thirds of its territory, a wound still central to national memory. It was a communist state after World War II; the 1956 uprising was crushed by Soviet forces; in 1989 it opened its border with Austria, helping end the Iron Curtain. It joined NATO in 1999.
  • Current leader: Prime Minister Péter Magyar (leader of the Tisza party), who took office in May 2026 after Tisza defeated Viktor Orbán's Fidesz, about 52% to 40%, in the April 12 election, winning a two-thirds majority (137 of 199 seats). Orbán had governed since 2010.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Ermahgerd" (the Goosebumps "Berks" Girl)

  • Origin: A photo of a young woman clutching Goosebumps books and grinning with braces, taken for a joke photo years earlier, was posted to Reddit in January 2012 with the caption "Ermahgerd! Gersberms! Mah fravrit berks!" (Oh my god! Goosebumps! My favorite books!).
  • How it spread: Reddit, Tumblr and Facebook users made image macros at once; the key idea was a mock "accent" that drops consonants and mangles vowels, so it was easy for anyone to imitate. Websites that auto-translated any sentence into "Ermahgerd-speak" appeared within days.
  • Meaning & usage: Shorthand for exaggerated, childlike excitement about something you love, usually with a pinch of mockery. Typical use: captioning food, pets or fandom photos.
  • Variations: Endless re-captions ("Ermahgerd, Cherstmers"), the translator tools, Halloween costumes, and a wave of imitations in other languages.
  • Status: A peak-2012 classic now used mostly nostalgically; it shows how a pronunciation joke can travel without any text knowledge. (Written from general knowledge; details of the original post are approximate.)

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Taxi Driver (1976, dir. Martin Scorsese)

  • Key figures: Director Martin Scorsese; screenwriter Paul Schrader; star Robert De Niro as Travis Bickle, with Jodie Foster, Cybill Shepherd, Harvey Keitel and Albert Brooks; cinematography by Michael Chapman; score by Bernard Herrmann, his last, completed shortly before his death in December 1975.
  • Background: Made in a New York City of the mid-1970s marked by fiscal crisis, crime and post-Vietnam disillusion, within the "New Hollywood" wave of director-driven films. Schrader wrote it from a period of his own isolation.
  • Plot: Bickle, a lonely Vietnam veteran, takes a night job driving a cab. Insomnia, disgust at the city and a failed attempt at romance push him toward violence, and he fixes on a plan to rescue a teenage sex worker (Foster) from her pimp.
  • Style & technique: Handheld street shooting, neon-lit rain on windshields, a slow overhead pan away from the pay phone during a painful call, and Herrmann's jazz-and-menace score. The point of view stays close to Bickle's skewed perception, so the audience is unsure how far to trust it. De Niro's famous "You talkin' to me?" mirror scene was largely improvised.
  • Significance: Won the Palme d'Or at Cannes in 1976 and was nominated for four Academy Awards including Best Picture. It is a touchstone for films about alienated men, and it was controversial for its violence.
  • Influence & today: Echoes in Joker (2019) and countless urban-loner films. Restored versions circulate on disc and streaming, and repertory cinemas screen it regularly. (From general knowledge.)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 66: Opening and Closing Image Pairs (Bookending)

The method: Many films open and close with the same image, location or gesture, changed in one telling way. Compare the first and last shots side by side: what is identical (framing, setting, action), what differs (who is in the frame, light, sound, who is inside or outside), and what that difference says the story has changed. A strong bookend answers the film's central question in pure image. Ask: does the end image resolve, complete, or ironically reverse the beginning?

Worked example — The Searchers (1956, dir. John Ford, cinematography by Winton C. Hoch): The film opens from inside a dark Texas homestead. A woman opens the door and the camera looks out at bright Monument Valley as a rider approaches: Ethan Edwards, coming home. In the final shot, the camera is again inside the dark home, looking out at the brilliant landscape through the same doorway. The family walks in and the door's frame holds Ethan outside; he hesitates, turns and walks away as the door closes on him. Identical camera position, opposite meaning: at the start the doorway welcomes a wanderer into the family's world; at the end the home is safe for others, but the man whose violence and obsession made the rescue possible cannot enter it. The frame-within-a-frame (Lesson on diegetic framing) and the light contrast do the work without dialogue.

Try it: Next time you watch any film, pause on the first and last minute and write down three things that changed.

8) 看片方法課 — 第 66 課:開場與結尾的對照畫面(首尾呼應)

方法:許多電影以同一個畫面、地點或動作開場與收尾,只在一個關鍵處做改變。把第一個鏡頭與最後一個鏡頭並排比較:什麼相同(構圖、場景、動作)、什麼不同(誰在畫面內、光線、聲音、誰在室內或室外),以及這個差異說明故事改變了什麼。好的首尾呼應能用純影像回答電影的核心問題。問自己:結尾畫面是解答、完成,還是反諷地翻轉了開頭?

實例 — 《搜索者》(The Searchers,1956,約翰·福特導演,攝影 Winton C. Hoch):電影從德州農舍的黑暗室內開始,一名女子推開門,鏡頭望向戶外明亮的紀念碑谷,一名騎士漸近:是返家的伊森·愛德華茲。最後一個鏡頭,攝影機再度位於昏暗屋內,透過同一道門框望向耀眼的原野。家人走進屋裡,門框把伊森留在門外;他遲疑、轉身離開,門在他身後關上。同樣的機位、相反的意義:開頭,門迎接流浪者進入家人的世界;結尾,家對其他人而言安全了,但以暴力與執念促成救援的這個人,卻無法踏入。畫面中的框中框與光線對比,不靠一句台詞就完成了敘事。

練習:下次看任何電影,暫停在第一分鐘與最後一分鐘,寫下三個變化。
