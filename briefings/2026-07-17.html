3) Spanish Lesson — Day 9 (builds on Day 8)

Days 1-8 covered greetings/self-intro, ¿Cómo estás?, farewells/courtesy, ¿De dónde eres? (ser + origin), numbers 0-10, the days of the week, and the months of the year. Today leaves the calendar theme and moves into telling time: asking and answering "what time is it," and asking when an event happens.

  • ¿Qué hora es? — What time is it? / Es la una. — It's one o'clock. / Son las tres y media de la tarde. — It's 3:30pm.
  • ¿A qué hora es la reunión? — What time is the meeting? / Es a las cinco en punto. — It's at five o'clock sharp.

Grammar note: only "one o'clock" uses the singular Es la una; every other hour uses the plural Son las..., because Spanish time-telling is really an elided "hour(s)" (hora, feminine), hence the feminine articles la/las. Minutes past the hour use y cuarto (quarter past) / y media (half past); past the half-hour, Spanish switches to counting down from the next hour with menos cuarto (quarter to) rather than saying "forty-five."

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 9: 小畜 (Xiǎo Chù) — "The Taming Power of the Small" (Wind over Heaven). A single yielding (yin) line, in the fourth position, is all that stands between the five strong (yang) lines that make up the rest of the hexagram — a small, gentle force attempting to restrain and channel something vastly more powerful. The classical judgment: "The Taming Power of the Small has success. Dense clouds, no rain from our western region." The clouds gather but do not yet break into rain — the image of an accumulation of gentle influence that has not yet grown large enough to produce a visible result; the time calls for patience, quiet cultivation of inner strength and small restraining measures, rather than open confrontation or forceful action. The Sequence of Hexagrams explains its place directly after 比 (Holding Together, hexagram 8): once things or people are united in fellowship, that union must in turn be gathered and accumulated, or "tamed," into something with lasting shape — hence Holding Together is followed by the Taming Power of the Small. It counsels patient, gentle persistence: small, consistent efforts of restraint and cultivation, repeated over time, are what eventually allow the great to be guided, even though no immediate, dramatic breakthrough (the "rain") has yet arrived.

5) Country/Region Spotlight: Iceland

  • Location & size: An island nation in the North Atlantic, just south of the Arctic Circle, roughly midway between Greenland and Norway; about 103,000 km² — around 2.8 times the size of Taiwan (~36,197 km²), somewhat larger than South Korea, and comparable in area to the US state of Kentucky.
  • Population: Roughly 400,000 people — one of the lowest population densities in Europe, with nearly two-thirds concentrated in the greater Reykjavík capital area; the interior highlands are almost uninhabited, covered by glaciers, lava fields, and volcanic terrain.
  • Religion: The Evangelical Lutheran Church of Iceland remains the largest denomination (roughly 60% of the population, though the share has been steadily declining), with a growing unaffiliated/secular population; small Catholic and other Christian minorities exist, alongside a notable modern revival of Old Norse paganism (Ásatrú), which is an officially state-recognized religion with a growing following.
  • Economy: A small, highly developed, high-income economy historically built on fishing and seafood exports, energy-intensive aluminum smelting powered by cheap geothermal and hydroelectric electricity, and — since the 2010s — a tourism boom that turned visitor numbers into a multiple of the resident population; Iceland is also a global reference case for a 2008 banking collapse, when its three major banks failed and the króna crashed, followed by one of the more studied financial recoveries of the past two decades.
  • History: Settled by Norse Vikings around 874 AD, Iceland founded the Althing in 930 AD, often cited as the world's oldest surviving parliament; it came under Norwegian and later Danish rule for centuries, gained home rule in 1904, and became a fully independent republic in 1944 while Denmark was under German occupation. It joined NATO as a founding member in 1949 despite maintaining no standing army, and fought a series of 20th-century "Cod Wars" fishing disputes with the United Kingdom over its territorial waters.
  • Current leadership: Halla Tómasdóttir has served as the (largely ceremonial) President since August 2024. Executive governance sits with Prime Minister Kristrún Frostadóttir, an economist who took office in December 2024 at age 36 as Iceland's youngest-ever prime minister, leading a coalition of the Social Democratic Alliance, the Liberal Reform Party, and the People's Party.
  • Language: Icelandic, a North Germanic language known for being unusually conservative — modern Icelandic speakers can still largely read medieval Old Norse sagas — and for a strong linguistic purism policy that favors coining new native compound words over adopting foreign loanwords for new technology and concepts.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight: Doge

  • Name/origin: "Doge" (a deliberately misspelled "dog," traced back to a 2005 Homestar Runner reference popularized further by a Sealab 2021 clip) became attached to photos of Kabosu, a female Shiba Inu owned by Japanese kindergarten teacher Atsuko Sato, which Sato first posted to her blog in 2010.
  • How it became popular: Around 2013, the now-classic format emerged on Tumblr, Reddit, and 4chan: a Kabosu photo overlaid with multicolored Comic Sans snippets in broken English representing the dog's exaggerated inner monologue ("such wow," "very intimidate," "much scare"). The meme reached a much wider audience in December 2013, when developers Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer created "Dogecoin" as a joking parody cryptocurrency built on the meme — which unexpectedly developed a genuine, enthusiastic community, funding the Jamaican bobsled team's trip to the 2014 Winter Olympics and sponsoring a NASCAR driver.
  • Meaning/usage: The "such X, very Y, much Z, wow" phrasing is used to express ironic, broken-English wonder or enthusiasm about mundane or absurd things, and "doge" more broadly became shorthand for the wholesome-but-goofy Shiba Inu meme archetype and for early-2010s internet absurdist humor generally.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned the "Cheems" derivative (a rounder, "buffer" Shiba Inu with intentionally poor-grammar captions, popularized from roughly 2019 onward) and the paired "Swole Doge vs. Cheems" contrast format; Dogecoin itself grew into a real, actively traded cryptocurrency, repeatedly boosted by attention from figures like Elon Musk over the following decade.
  • Current status: Kabosu, the original dog, died peacefully at home in Japan in May 2024 at age 18, an event marked by tribute monuments already in place in her hometown of Sakura, Chiba (a park-bench statue from 2023 and a manhole cover from 2024). As of 2026, the original "such wow" caption style reads as a nostalgic, foundational "classic meme" largely superseded by newer formats like Cheems, while Dogecoin remains one of the better-known and most actively traded cryptocurrencies to have originated directly from an internet joke.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第九天(銜接第八天內容)

第一到第八天學了問候自我介紹、¿Cómo estás?、道別與禮貌用語、¿De dónde eres?(ser 與國籍)、數字 0-10、一週七天,以及一年十二個月。今天離開日曆主題,進入「報時」:如何問答「現在幾點」,以及某件事是幾點發生。

  • ¿Qué hora es? —— 現在幾點? / Es la una. —— 現在一點。 / Son las tres y media de la tarde. —— 現在是下午三點半。
  • ¿A qué hora es la reunión? —— 會議是幾點? / Es a las cinco en punto. —— 準時五點。

文法重點:只有「一點」用單數 Es la una;其餘所有整點都用複數 Son las...——因為西班牙文報時其實是省略了陰性名詞「小時」(hora)的講法,所以固定搭配陰性冠詞 la/las。分鐘的說法上,過整點後用 y cuarto(過一刻)、y media(過半);但一旦超過半點,西語就會改用「用下一個整點往回減一刻」的 menos cuarto(差一刻)來表示,而不是直接說「四十五分」。

4) 易經卦象

第九卦:小畜卦(Xiǎo Chù)——「以小蓄大、小有積蓄」(上巽下乾,風行天上)。全卦五陽爻之中僅有一個柔順的陰爻居於第四爻,象徵一股微小、柔和的力量,試圖去節制、引導遠比自己強大許多的力量。卦辭:「小畜,亨。密雲不雨,自我西郊。」意指烏雲已經密布,卻尚未降下甘霖——象徵柔和的積蓄力量雖已逐漸凝聚,但尚未壯大到足以產生明顯可見的成果;此時應當耐心蓄養內在實力,以溫和節制的小方法逐步累積,而非正面衝突或強力行動。〈序卦傳〉說明此卦緊接在比卦(親比團結,第八卦)之後的道理:眾人一旦透過親比而團結起來,接下來就必須將這股力量加以凝聚、蓄積,使其成形持久,因此比卦之後接著是小畜卦。此卦提醒人們:耐心而溫和地堅持——透過長時間反覆的小幅節制與自我涵養——終將能夠引導、駕馭巨大的力量,即使眼前還沒有出現「降雨」般立即而戲劇性的突破。

5) 國家/地區介紹:冰島

  • 位置與面積: 位於北大西洋、北極圈以南不遠處的島國,大約介於格陵蘭與挪威之間;面積約 103,000 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)的 2.8 倍,略大於南韓,與美國肯塔基州面積相近。
  • 人口: 約 40 萬人,是歐洲人口密度最低的國家之一;近三分之二人口集中在首都雷克雅維克(Reykjavík)的大都會區,內陸高地幾乎無人居住,多為冰川、熔岩地形與火山地貌覆蓋。
  • 宗教: 冰島福音路德教會(Church of Iceland)仍是最大教派,約佔人口 60%(比例持續下滑中),無宗教信仰/世俗人口則逐漸增加;另有少數天主教與其他基督教派,以及近年逐漸復興、且已獲得官方正式承認為宗教組織的古北歐異教信仰(Ásatrú),追隨者持續成長。
  • 經濟: 屬於規模小但高度發展的高所得經濟體,傳統上以漁業與海鮮出口、仰賴廉價地熱與水力發電的高耗能鋁業冶煉為主軸;自 2010 年代起觀光業蓬勃發展,遊客人數一度達到本國人口的數倍之多。冰島也是全球研究 2008 年銀行業崩潰的經典案例——當年三大銀行相繼倒閉、克朗貨幣重挫,此後展開的金融重建過程也成為近二十年來廣受討論的復甦範例之一。
  • 歷史: 約西元 874 年由北歐維京人移居建國,930 年設立「阿爾庭」(Althing)國會,常被視為現存最古老的國會制度;其後歷經數百年挪威、丹麥統治,1904 年取得自治地位,並於 1944 年丹麥仍受德國佔領期間正式獨立建國。冰島於 1949 年以創始會員國身分加入北約,雖不維持常備軍隊;20 世紀期間也曾與英國因領海漁權爆發多次「鱈魚戰爭」(Cod Wars)。
  • 現任領導人: 總統哈拉・托馬斯多蒂爾(Halla Tómasdóttir)自 2024 年 8 月就任,職權以象徵性為主。實際行政權則由總理克里斯特倫・弗羅斯塔多蒂爾(Kristrún Frostadóttir)掌理,她是一位經濟學家,於 2024 年 12 月以 36 歲之齡就任,成為冰島史上最年輕的總理,目前領導由社會民主聯盟、自由改革黨與人民黨組成的聯合政府。
  • 語言: 官方語言為冰島語,屬於北日耳曼語族,以罕見的語言保守性著稱——現代冰島人仍大致能直接閱讀中世紀的古北歐薩迦(saga)原文;冰島也長期奉行強烈的語言純化政策,面對新科技與新概念時傾向自創本土複合詞,而非直接採用外來借詞。

6) 網路迷因介紹:Doge(狗狗迷因)

  • 名稱/起源:「Doge」是刻意拼錯的「dog」(源自 2005 年 Homestar Runner 的一次用語,後因 Sealab 2021 動畫片段而更廣為流傳),後來被套用在日本幼兒園老師佐藤敦子(Atsuko Sato)所飼養的柴犬「Kabosu」的照片上——佐藤最早於 2010 年將這些照片發布在自己的部落格。
  • 如何爆紅: 約在 2013 年,現今經典的迷因格式在 Tumblr、Reddit 與 4chan 上成形:在 Kabosu 的照片上疊加多種顏色的 Comic Sans 字體,以刻意破碎的英文表達這隻狗誇張的內心獨白(例如「such wow」、「very intimidate」、「much scare」)。這個迷因在 2013 年 12 月觸及更廣大的族群——當時軟體工程師 Billy Markus 與 Jackson Palmer 以這個迷因為基礎,開玩笑推出了戲仿性質的加密貨幣「狗狗幣」(Dogecoin),沒想到卻意外凝聚出一個真誠而熱情的社群,不僅募資讓牙買加雪橇隊得以參加 2014 年冬季奧運,還贊助了一位 NASCAR 賽車手。
  • 意義與使用情境:「such X, very Y, much Z, wow」這種句型,常被用來以刻意破碎的英文語法,表達對平凡甚至荒謬事物的諷刺性驚嘆或熱情;「doge」一詞後來也更廣泛地成為「憨萌柴犬」這類迷因原型、以及 2010 年代初期網路無厘頭幽默文化的代名詞。
  • 變體: 衍生出「Cheems」這個變體角色(一隻造型更圓潤、看起來「更壯」的柴犬,搭配刻意文法不通的字幕,約自 2019 年起開始流行),以及將兩者並列對比的「Swole Doge vs. Cheems」迷因格式;狗狗幣本身則逐漸發展成真實、交易活躍的加密貨幣,往後十年間也多次因馬斯克(Elon Musk)等名人關注而再度爆紅。
  • 現況: 原型犬 Kabosu 已於 2024 年 5 月在日本家中安詳離世,享壽 18 歲;牠的故鄉千葉縣佐倉市已設有紀念設施,包括 2023 年設立的公園長椅雕像,以及 2024 年設置的紀念人孔蓋。截至 2026 年,原始的「such wow」字幕風格已被視為懷舊、具開創意義的「經典迷因」,在流行程度上大致已被 Cheems 等後起格式取代;但狗狗幣仍是少數幾個直接源自網路笑話、卻發展成知名且交易活躍的加密貨幣之一。
