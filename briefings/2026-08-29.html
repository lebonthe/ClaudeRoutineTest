3) Spanish Lesson — Day 49 (10 min, the true passive voice with "ser")

Today's lesson delivers the classic "textbook" passive voice that Day 48's se-passive (voz pasiva refleja) was always standing in contrast to: ser (conjugated in whatever tense the sentence needs) + a past participle that must agree in gender and number with the subject, optionally followed by por + the agent who performed the action. This is the one structural power the se-passive can never offer: se-passive sentences (Se vende la casa) cannot name who is doing the selling at all, while the ser-passive can spell it out explicitly (La casa fue vendida por un banco extranjero, "The house was sold by a foreign bank"). Because Spanish speakers overwhelmingly prefer the active voice or Day 48's se-passive in everyday conversation, the ser-passive is comparatively rare in speech and concentrated instead in formal writing, news reporting, and historical/legal narration — exactly the registers where naming a specific agent (a company, an author, a government) actually matters. A second key detail distinguishes it from every haber-based compound tense taught so far (Days 21, 22, 38, 39, 40): whereas haber's past participle never changes form (siempre "ha comido," never "ha comida"), the ser-passive's past participle behaves like an adjective and must agree with the subject in both gender and number — fue construido (masc. sing.), fue construida (fem. sing.), fueron construidos (masc./mixed pl.), fueron construidas (fem. pl.).

  • El puente fue diseñado por un ingeniero famoso. — The bridge was designed by a famous engineer.
  • Las cartas fueron enviadas ayer. — The letters were sent yesterday.
  • Este libro fue escrito por Gabriel García Márquez. — This book was written by Gabriel García Márquez.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 49: 革 (Gé) — "Revolution" (Molting) (Lake above, Fire below — Duì over Lí: fire burning beneath a lake's water, the two elements locked in mutual extinction/transformation rather than harmony). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws a direct, almost architectural line from yesterday's hexagram: "井道不可不革,故受之以革。" ("The way of the Well cannot go forever without renewal; therefore Jǐng [the Well] is followed by Gé [Revolution].") Where Hexagram 48 celebrated the well's tireless constancy — a resource that never changes and never runs dry — Hexagram 49 answers with the opposite half of the same truth: even the most enduring institution eventually silts up, ages, or becomes unfit for its purpose, and at that point renewal, not more patience, is what integrity actually demands. The Judgment reads: "革,己日乃孚,元亨利貞,悔亡。" ("REVOLUTION. On one's own day, one is believed. Supreme success; furthering through perseverance. Remorse disappears.") The phrase "on one's own day" (己日) signals that a revolution is only trusted once it has already proven itself over time, rather than on the strength of promises alone — premature or unproven change invites exactly the distrust that a well-timed one dissolves. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "革,水火相息,二女同居,其志不相得,曰革。己日乃孚,革而信之。文明以說,大亨以正。革而當,其悔乃亡。天地革而四時成,湯武革命,順乎天而應乎人。革之時大矣哉!" ("Water and fire extinguish each other; [as trigrams] two women dwell together, yet their wills do not agree — this is called Revolution. 'On one's own day, one is believed' — the revolution is trusted once it is enacted. [The lower trigram's] clarity and [the upper trigram's] joy bring great success through correctness. When the revolution is fitting, its remorse disappears. Heaven and earth undergo revolution and the four seasons are thereby completed; [the ancient sage-kings] Tang and Wu revolutionized [their mandates] in accordance with Heaven and in response to mankind. Great indeed is the timeliness of Revolution!") The reference to Tang and Wu — the legendary founders who overthrew the corrupt last rulers of the Xia and Shang dynasties — grounds "revolution" in its most literal, political sense: the hexagram's name (革) is the very term later used for "dynastic revolution" (革命, gémìng) in classical Chinese political thought. The Image commentary then draws a calmer, more civic lesson from the same trigram picture: "澤中有火,革;君子以治歷明時。" ("Fire within the lake: this is the image of Revolution. Thus the superior man sets the calendar in order and makes the seasons clear.") Even the changing of seasons — the sun's apparent motion through the year, which a calendar exists to track — is itself a form of orderly, recurring revolution; the superior man's task is not to fear change but to measure and order it correctly, so that renewal serves stability rather than chaos. Read together, Hexagrams 48 and 49 form one of the Sequence's clearest paired lessons: yesterday's constancy (the well that never changes) and today's revolution (the courage to change what has outlived its usefulness) are not opposites but two disciplines that only work when each is applied to the right situation — clinging to a well past its use is Hexagram 47's exhaustion, but revolutionizing something that still serves its purpose is mere disorder.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Chad (查德)

  • Location: North-Central Africa, landlocked, straddling the Sahara, Sahel, and Sudanian savanna zones. Borders Libya to the north, Sudan to the east, the Central African Republic to the south, Cameroon and Nigeria to the southwest (meeting at Lake Chad), and Niger to the west. Capital: N'Djamena.
  • Territory: About 1,284,000 km² — roughly 35 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), a little over twice the size of France (~551,695 km²), and comparable in scale to South Africa plus Lesotho combined. It is Africa's fifth-largest country and one of the largest landlocked countries in the world.
  • Population: Around 19-20 million (2026 estimate); one of the youngest and fastest-growing populations globally, with a median age in the mid-teens and a high total fertility rate.
  • Religion: Predominantly Muslim (roughly mid-50s percent, concentrated in the north and center) and Christian (roughly low-40s percent, mostly Catholic and Protestant, concentrated in the south), with a small remaining minority practicing indigenous animist traditions; the north-south religious divide has historically tracked closely with regional political and ethnic tension.
  • Economy: One of the world's poorest countries by GDP per capita despite significant oil reserves; crude oil (pumped mainly from the Doba Basin since 2003 and exported via a pipeline through Cameroon to the Atlantic) dominates export revenue, but the wider economy remains overwhelmingly agrarian and pastoral (cotton, livestock, gum arabic, subsistence farming), constrained by landlocked logistics, chronic underinvestment, and recurring Sahel-region security crises, including Boko Haram-linked violence around Lake Chad and hosting large refugee populations fleeing conflicts in Sudan and the Central African Republic.
  • History: Part of French Equatorial Africa under colonial rule until independence in 1960; decades of civil war and instability followed, including the brutal 1980s rule of Hissène Habré (later convicted of crimes against humanity by an international tribunal in Senegal) and Idriss Déby's three-decade rule from 1990. Déby was killed in April 2021 while visiting troops fighting rebels near the northern front, prompting a military transitional council led by his son, Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno, to take power outside the constitutional order of succession.
  • Current leader: President Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno, who led the post-2021 transitional government and then won a widely criticized May 2024 presidential election against a field that excluded most serious opposition figures, formalizing a father-to-son succession that consolidated Déby family rule into its fourth decade.
  • Language: French and (Chadian) Arabic are the two official languages; more than 100 additional local languages and dialects are spoken across Chad's roughly 200 ethnic groups, with Chadian Arabic serving as a widespread lingua franca alongside Sara-language cluster tongues in the south.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov World Factbook, worldbank.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Tung Tung Tung Sahur"

  • Name/origin: "Tung Tung Tung Sahur" is an AI-generated character — a wooden log/drum figure with a carved, googly-eyed face and human legs, its name built from "tung tung tung" (onomatopoeia for the beat of a bedug or kentungan, the drums traditionally used in Indonesia) plus "sahur" (the pre-dawn meal eaten before the daily fast during Ramadan, which in Indonesian tradition is announced by people going door-to-door banging drums to wake the neighborhood). The character emerged out of Indonesia in early-to-mid 2025 as part of a wider wave of surreal, AI-generated "brainrot" mascot characters — built with AI image and short-video generation tools rather than traditional animation — that also produced viral figures like Bombardiro Crocodilo and Ballerina Cappuccina.
  • How it became popular: Short AI-generated clips of the character, often paired with a simple droning chant of its own name and surreal, dreamlike "lore" (fighting other brainrot characters, absurd backstories), spread rapidly through TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels via algorithmic recommendation, following the same explosive, remix-driven distribution pattern as the broader 2025 AI-brainrot character wave; its distinctly Indonesian cultural reference (the Ramadan sahur drum-call) gave it a regional identity that helped it stand out within that crowded new meme ecosystem before spreading to a global, largely younger audience.
  • Meaning/usage: Functions less as a joke with a punchline and more as a piece of purely absorbing, low-narrative "brainrot" content consumed (especially by children and younger teens) for its repetitive audio hook and uncanny visual design; among older internet users and media commentators, the character (and the "Italian/Indonesian brainrot" genre it belongs to) has also become shorthand for concern about algorithmically optimized, nonsensical AI-generated content flooding young viewers' video feeds.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned an entire loosely connected "extended universe" of similarly random-noun-combination AI brainrot characters, crossover battle/lore videos pitting Tung Tung Tung Sahur against other brainrot figures, fan-made plush toys and merchandise, mobile games, and countless remix/parody audio tracks built around its chant-like name.
  • Current status: Still widely circulating and commercially merchandised as of 2026, though it now shares attention with newer entries in the same brainrot-character wave; it has simultaneously become a reference point in ongoing media, parental, and platform-policy debates about AI-generated "slop" content and children's video consumption, rather than fading quietly the way many single-format memes do.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Singin' in the Rain (1952)

  • Name/key figures: Singin' in the Rain (1952), co-directed by and starring Gene Kelly (also credited as choreographer alongside Kelly himself), co-directed by Stanley Donen, produced by Arthur Freed for MGM's famed "Freed Unit," with a screenplay by Betty Comden and Adolph Green and songs largely by Nacio Herb Brown (music) and Arthur Freed (lyrics), most originally written for earlier MGM films of the late 1920s and 1930s and repurposed here. Co-stars Debbie Reynolds, Donald O'Connor, and Jean Hagen round out the principal cast.
  • Era/background: Made at the creative peak of MGM's Freed Unit, the studio division responsible for many of Hollywood's most celebrated integrated musicals of the late 1940s-1950s. Its own story looks back roughly a quarter-century, to Hollywood's chaotic, career-upending transition from silent film to synchronized-sound "talkies" around 1927-1929 (the era inaugurated by The Jazz Singer), making it a musical about the birth of a new kind of movie musical.
  • Plot overview: Silent-era star Don Lockwood (Kelly) and his vain, shrill-voiced leading lady Lina Lamont (Hagen) find their careers upended as studios scramble to convert to sound film; Don falls for aspiring stage actress Kathy Selden (Reynolds), and together with his comic sidekick Cosmo Brown (O'Connor) hatches a plan to dub the unwatchable Lina's dialogue and singing with Kathy's voice for Lina's new sound picture — a scheme that ultimately exposes Lina's fraud publicly and launches Kathy's own stardom.
  • Artistic style and techniques: A textbook "integrated musical," where musical numbers advance character and plot rather than pausing the story for a spectacle; combines tap, ballet, and vaudeville-inflected slapstick within the same choreographic vocabulary. "Moses Supposes" and "Good Morning" showcase tightly synchronized ensemble choreography; "Make 'Em Laugh" is a solo acrobatic-comedy showcase for Donald O'Connor; the celebrated title number was shot almost entirely with practical, in-camera effects — real rain machines, genuinely soaked pavement, and precisely engineered puddles — rather than trick photography, reportedly filmed while Kelly had a 103°F fever.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Consistently ranked among the greatest films and musicals ever made, topping the American Film Institute's list of the best American movie musicals; the umbrella-twirling, lamppost-swinging title sequence is one of the single most quoted and referenced images in all of cinema. Its "Broadway Melody Ballet" sequence is a landmark screen realization of the "dream ballet," a device popularized on stage a decade earlier by Agnes de Mille's choreography for Oklahoma! and here fully absorbed into film-musical grammar.
  • Influence and legacy: Shaped the staging vocabulary of movie musicals for decades afterward and remains a constant homage target — most visibly in Damien Chazelle's La La Land (2016), whose own opening and closing sequences directly echo it — while the title number itself has been endlessly parodied and quoted across film, television, and advertising, including its unsettlingly ironic reuse by Malcolm McDowell's character in Stanley Kubrick's A Clockwork Orange (1971).
  • Current status/where to experience it: Preserved in the US Library of Congress's National Film Registry as part of its inaugural 1989 class of selections; still in wide theatrical revival and re-release rotation, available on major streaming and home-video platforms, and a standard fixture of film-school curricula on the history and grammar of the American movie musical.
    Sources: loc.gov National Film Registry, en.wikipedia.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Ellipsis / Editing for Compressed Time (Lesson 35)

Ellipsis is a cut, dissolve, or short montage sequence deliberately used to skip over a stretch of story time without leaving the audience confused about how much time has actually passed. This is distinct from Day 24's Jump Cut, which cuts between two too-similar shots within what is meant to be one continuous moment specifically to produce a jarring, visible discontinuity, and from Day 18's Cross-Cutting/Parallel Editing, which alternates between genuinely simultaneous actions in different places rather than compressing time within a single ongoing action. Where the jump cut exposes the edit as a disruptive event, ellipsis smooths the gap over, typically signaled by a dissolve, a recurring visual/musical motif, a calendar or headline insert, or a costume and prop change that silently confirms time has moved on. When you spot one, check how much time is being skipped, what specific cue marks the jump, whether the missing time is genuinely irrelevant to the story or is being deliberately withheld for a later reveal, and whether the technique is compressing years into a life-spanning montage or just hours or days into a simple dissolve.

Worked example: Up (2009, dir. Pete Docter, co-dir. Bob Peterson) — the wordless "Married Life" montage that opens the film. A rapid sequence of dissolves and vignettes — the wedding, painting their house together, a picnic interrupted by rain, a devastating implied miscarriage shown only through a single shot of an empty nursery, a coin jar labeled "Paradise Falls" that keeps getting emptied out by life's unglamorous emergencies, and the slow physical aging of both characters — compresses roughly forty-five years of Carl and Ellie's entire marriage into about four minutes with no dialogue at all. A single recurring musical theme by Michael Giacchino and recurring match-cut transitions (the same objects and gestures recurring, subtly aged, across each dissolve) bind every time-leap into one continuous emotional throughline, so that when Ellie eventually dies moments later, the loss lands with the accumulated weight of an entire unseen lifetime rather than needing every year of it shown scene by scene.

3)西班牙文課程——第 49 天(10 分鐘,以 ser 構成的真正被動語態)

今天的課程,要正式介紹那個第 48 天的「反身被動 se」(voz pasiva refleja)始終在與之對照的「教科書式」經典被動語態:以 ser(依句意變化成所需時態)加上必須依主詞陰陽性與單複數變化的過去分詞,後面可選擇性再加上 por(由……)加上真正執行動作的施動者。這正是被動 se 句型永遠做不到的一件事:被動 se 句子(Se vende la casa,房屋出售)完全無法點出「是誰」在賣這棟房子,而 ser 被動語態卻可以清楚地講明白(La casa fue vendida por un banco extranjero,「這棟房子是被一家外國銀行賣掉的」)。正因為西語母語者在日常口語中,壓倒性地偏好使用主動語態或第 48 天教過的被動 se,ser 被動語態在口語中反而相對少見,主要集中出現在正式書面文體、新聞報導與歷史/法律敘事之中——恰好就是那些真正需要點名具體施動者(某間公司、某位作者、某個政府)的場合。第二個必須留意的關鍵細節,是它與先前教過的所有 haber 系複合時態(第 21、22、38、39、40 天)之間的根本差異:haber 構成的過去分詞永遠不會變化(永遠是 ha comido,絕不會變成 ha comida),但 ser 被動語態中的過去分詞,行為模式其實更像形容詞,必須依主詞的陰陽性與單複數確實變化——fue construido(陽性單數)、fue construida(陰性單數)、fueron construidos(陽性/混合複數)、fueron construidas(陰性複數)。

  • El puente fue diseñado por un ingeniero famoso. —— 這座橋是由一位知名工程師設計的。
  • Las cartas fueron enviadas ayer. —— 這些信是昨天寄出的。
  • Este libro fue escrito por Gabriel García Márquez. —— 這本書是加布列·賈西亞·馬奎斯寫的。

4)易經卦象

第 49 卦:革 (Gé) ——「變革」(澤在上,火在下——兌上離下:火在澤水之下燃燒,水火二者本質相剋、彼此消長,而非彼此和諧)。《序卦傳》從昨日的卦象直接、幾乎是理所當然地承接而來:「井道不可不革,故受之以革。」(水井之道不能永遠不加以更新變革;因此井卦之後,接的是革卦。)如果說第 48 卦頌揚的是水井那種歷久不衰的恆常性——一種永不改變、也永不枯竭的資源——那麼第 49 卦回應的,正是同一項真理的另一面:即便是最經久不衰的體制,終究也會淤積、老化,或不再適用於原本的目的,而到了那個時刻,真正符合道理的做法,已不再是繼續耐心等待,而是進行更新變革。卦辭寫道:「革,己日乃孚,元亨利貞,悔亡。」(革卦:在自己的日子裡,才會被人信任。至為亨通,利於堅守正道。懊悔將會消失。)「己日」一詞,點出一項變革唯有在已經以時間證明自身之後,才會真正被信任,而非僅憑一句空口承諾就能取信於人——過早或尚未被證實的變革,反而只會招致猜疑,而時機恰當的變革,才能真正化解猜疑。《彖傳》進一步闡述:「革,水火相息,二女同居,其志不相得,曰革。己日乃孚,革而信之。文明以說,大亨以正。革而當,其悔乃亡。天地革而四時成,湯武革命,順乎天而應乎人。革之時大矣哉!」(水與火彼此消滅對方;〔以卦象而言〕兩個女子同住一處,心志卻彼此不合——這就叫做「革」。「在自己的日子裡才被信任」,意味著變革唯有真正付諸實行之後,才會取信於人。〔下卦〕光明而〔上卦〕喜悅,以正道行事而獲致大亨通。當變革恰如其分、合乎時宜,懊悔便隨之消失。天地經歷變革,四季方得以順利運行;〔古代聖王〕湯與武王順應天命、回應民心而進行革命。變革所講究的時機,意義何其重大啊!)這裡提到的湯與武王——推翻夏、商兩代腐敗末代君主的傳說中聖王——把「革」字扎根在最直接、最政治性的意涵之中:這一卦的卦名「革」,正是後世中國古典政治思想中「革命」(gémìng)一詞的直接源頭。《象傳》則從同一組卦象中,引申出一則更為平和、更貼近日常治理的教訓:「澤中有火,革;君子以治歷明時。」(澤水之中蘊藏著火,這就是革卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應當整治曆法,使四時節令清楚分明。)即便是四季更迭本身——太陽在一年之中看似運行的軌跡,而曆法的存在正是為了掌握這種規律——本身也是一種井然有序、周而復始的「變革」;君子的任務,並非畏懼變化本身,而是要正確地測度並整頓變化,使更新真正服務於穩定,而非淪為混亂。將第 48 卦與第 49 卦合在一起看,恰好構成《序卦傳》中最清楚的一組對照教訓:昨日的恆常(永不改變的水井)與今日的變革(有勇氣改變已經不再有用之物),二者並非互相對立,而是唯有各自用對場合,才能真正發揮作用的兩種修養——執著於一口早已失去效用的枯井,是第 47 卦所描述的困頓窮盡;但若去變革一個明明仍在發揮作用的事物,那便只是徒生亂象而已。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——查德(Chad)

  • 位置:位於非洲中北部的內陸國,橫跨撒哈拉沙漠、薩赫爾地帶與蘇丹式莽原三種地理帶。北接利比亞,東接蘇丹,南接中非共和國,西南方與喀麥隆、奈及利亞相鄰(交會於查德湖一帶),西邊則與尼日相接。首都:恩將美納(N'Djamena)。
  • 領土:約 1,284,000 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 35 倍大,略高於法國(約 551,695 平方公里)面積的兩倍,規模大致相當於南非加上賴索托的面積總和。查德是非洲面積第五大的國家,也是全球面積最大的內陸國家之一。
  • 人口:約 1,900 萬至 2,000 萬人(2026 年估計值);屬全球人口結構最年輕、成長速度最快的國家之一,年齡中位數僅約十幾歲,總生育率偏高。
  • 宗教:以伊斯蘭教為主(約佔五成出頭,主要集中於北部與中部地區),基督教次之(約佔四成出頭,以天主教與新教為主,主要集中於南部);另有少數人口仍信奉本土泛靈信仰。歷史上,國內南北之間的宗教分野,長期與地區政治及族群緊張情勢高度重疊。
  • 經濟:儘管擁有可觀的石油蘊藏,查德按人均 GDP 計算仍是全球最貧窮的國家之一;原油(主要來自 2003 年起開採的多巴盆地油田,並經由一條穿越喀麥隆通往大西洋的管線出口)主導了絕大部分出口收入,但整體經濟仍以農牧業為主(棉花、畜牧業、阿拉伯膠與自給農業),並受制於內陸國先天的物流劣勢、長期投資不足,以及薩赫爾地區反覆爆發的安全危機,包括查德湖周邊與「博科聖地」相關的暴力衝突,同時也收容了大量因蘇丹與中非共和國衝突而逃離的難民。
  • 歷史:殖民時期隸屬法屬赤道非洲,直到 1960 年獨立建國;此後數十年陷入內戰與動盪,包括 1980 年代伊森·哈布雷(Hissène Habré)殘暴統治時期(他後來在塞內加爾一個國際法庭上,被判犯有危害人類罪),以及伊德里斯·代比(Idriss Déby)自 1990 年起長達三十年的統治。2021 年 4 月,代比在北部前線視察對抗叛軍的部隊時陣亡,隨後由其子馬哈馬特·伊德里斯·代比·伊特諾(Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno)領導的軍事過渡委員會,在憲政繼承秩序之外接掌政權。
  • 現任領導人:總統馬哈馬特·伊德里斯·代比·伊特諾,他在 2021 年後領導過渡政府,隨後於 2024 年 5 月贏得一場廣受外界批評、幾乎排除所有主要反對派人物參選的總統大選,正式將代比家族的統治延續至第四個十年,完成了一場父傳子式的權力繼承。
  • 語言:法語與查德阿拉伯語為兩種官方語言;境內約 200 個族群另使用超過 100 種當地語言與方言,查德阿拉伯語在全境作為廣泛使用的共通語,南部地區則以薩拉語系諸語言為主。

6)網路迷因介紹——「Tung Tung Tung Sahur」

  • 名稱/起源:「Tung Tung Tung Sahur」是一個由 AI 生成的角色——一根雕刻著呆萌大眼、卻長著一雙人類雙腿的木製鼓形/木棍角色,名稱組合自「tung tung tung」(模擬印尼傳統上用來敲擊 bedug 或 kentungan 木鼓的擬聲詞)與「sahur」(齋戒月期間,穆斯林在每日齋戒開始前所吃的封齋前用餐;在印尼傳統中,人們會挨家挨戶敲鼓喚醒鄰居準備享用封齋飯)。這個角色於 2025 年上半年左右起源自印尼,是當時一波超現實、由 AI 生成的「腦腐(brainrot)」吉祥物角色浪潮的一部分——這些角色是透過 AI 圖像與短影音生成工具、而非傳統動畫技術製作而成,同一波浪潮還誕生了「炸彈鱷魚」(Bombardiro Crocodilo)、「芭蕾卡布奇諾」(Ballerina Cappuccina)等爆紅角色。
  • 如何走紅:各種以該角色為主角的簡短 AI 生成影片,通常搭配單調反覆吟唱其名稱的旋律,以及超現實、夢境般的「設定」(與其他腦腐角色互相打鬥、荒誕不經的背景故事),透過演算法推薦,在 TikTok、YouTube Shorts 與 Instagram Reels 上快速擴散,走的是與整體 2025 年 AI 腦腐角色浪潮相同、爆發式且高度仰賴混剪再製的傳播模式;其鮮明的印尼在地文化指涉(齋戒月封齋飯敲鼓喚醒習俗),讓它在這波擁擠的新興迷因生態中,得以先建立起一種鮮明的地域識別度,而後才逐漸擴散至全球、以較年輕族群為主的觀眾群。
  • 意義/使用情境:與其說是一個帶有明確笑點的笑話,不如說它更接近一種純粹用來吸引注意力、敘事成分極低的「腦腐」內容,特別受兒童與青少年族群喜愛,主要靠重複洗腦的音效鉤子與詭異的視覺設計來維持吸引力;而在較年長的網路使用者與媒體評論者之間,這個角色(以及它所屬的「義大利/印尼腦腐」類型)也逐漸成為一種代名詞,用來表達對演算法過度優化、內容荒誕無稽的 AI 生成影片大量湧入年輕觀眾影音資訊流的擔憂。
  • 變體/演化:衍生出一整套鬆散相連的「擴展宇宙」,由各種隨機名詞組合而成的類似 AI 腦腐角色構成;出現了讓 Tung Tung Tung Sahur 與其他腦腐角色互相對戰、鋪陳「設定」劇情的交叉影片、粉絲自製的絨毛玩偶與周邊商品、手機遊戲,以及無數以其吟唱式名稱為基礎製作的混音/戲仿音軌。
  • 目前狀態:截至 2026 年,這個角色仍廣泛流傳並持續有商業周邊產品推出,不過如今也必須與同一波腦腐角色浪潮中更新推出的角色分食關注度;與此同時,它也成為媒體、家長與平台政策圈,持續討論 AI 生成「內容垃圾(slop)」與兒童影音消費議題時,經常被引用的參考案例,而不像許多單一格式的迷因那樣悄然退燒。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《萬花嬉春》Singin' in the Rain(1952)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《萬花嬉春》(Singin' in the Rain,1952),由金·凱利(Gene Kelly,同時身兼主演與編舞)與史丹利·杜寧(Stanley Donen)共同執導,由亞瑟·佛里德(Arthur Freed)為 MGM 旗下著名的「佛里德製作單位」監製,編劇為貝蒂·康登(Betty Comden)與阿道夫·葛林(Adolph Green),歌曲多由納西歐·賀伯·布朗(Nacio Herb Brown,作曲)與亞瑟·佛里德(作詞)創作,絕大部分原是 1920 年代末至 1930 年代為 MGM 早期電影所寫、此次重新沿用。主要卡司還包括黛比·雷諾(Debbie Reynolds)、唐納德·奧康納(Donald O'Connor)與珍·哈根(Jean Hagen)。
  • 時代/背景:本片誕生於 MGM「佛里德製作單位」創作力最鼎盛的時期,該製作部門在 1940 年代末至 1950 年代間,催生出許多好萊塢最受推崇的整合式歌舞片。劇情本身則回望約四分之一世紀前,好萊塢從默片轉型為同步錄音「有聲片」那段動盪不安、足以斷送演員生涯的過渡期(約 1927 至 1929 年,由《爵士歌手》開啟的年代)——換言之,這是一部講述「新型態電影歌舞片如何誕生」的電影歌舞片。
  • 劇情概述:默片時代巨星唐·洛克伍德(金·凱利飾)與他那位虛榮、嗓音尖銳刺耳的女主角麗娜·拉蒙特(珍·哈根飾),在片廠倉促轉向有聲電影製作的過程中,發現自己的演藝事業岌岌可危;唐愛上了懷抱舞台演員夢想的凱西·賽爾登(黛比·雷諾飾),並與搞笑好友柯斯莫·布朗(唐納德·奧康納飾)一同策劃,由凱西為完全無法勝任有聲演出的麗娜配音、代唱,好讓麗娜的新片得以順利上映——這項計謀最終當眾揭穿了麗娜的騙局,同時也成就了凱西自己的星途。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:堪稱「整合式歌舞片」的教科書範例——歌舞段落用來推展角色性格與劇情,而非讓故事暫停下來欣賞一場單純的歌舞奇觀;融合踢踏舞、芭蕾與帶有雜耍喜劇色彩的動作語彙於同一套編舞語言之中。〈Moses Supposes〉與〈Good Morning〉展現高度同步的群舞編排;〈Make 'Em Laugh〉則是唐納德·奧康納一展個人特技喜劇功力的獨舞段落;片名同名歌舞段落幾乎全採實景手法拍攝——真正的降雨機、確實被打濕的路面,以及精心設計的積水——而非任何特效合成技術,據傳拍攝當時金·凱利其實正發著高達 39.4°C(103°F)的高燒。
  • 文化/歷史意義:始終位居影史最偉大電影與歌舞片之列,曾登上美國電影學會(AFI)「最偉大美國歌舞片」名單榜首;片中撐傘轉圈、攀掛路燈柱的經典畫面,是整個電影史上被引用與致敬次數最多的畫面之一。片中〈百老匯旋律芭蕾〉(Broadway Melody Ballet)段落,則是「夢境芭蕾」(dream ballet)這種手法在銀幕上的里程碑式呈現——這項手法最早由艾格妮絲·德米爾(Agnes de Mille)十年前為舞台劇《奧克拉荷馬!》編舞而推廣開來,如今在本片中被完整吸收、融入電影歌舞片的敘事語彙之中。
  • 影響與傳承:此後數十年間,深刻形塑了電影歌舞片的場面調度語彙,至今仍是不斷被致敬的對象——最明顯的例子,是戴米恩·查澤雷執導的《樂來越愛你》(La La Land,2016),其開場與結尾段落皆直接呼應本片;而本片同名歌舞段落本身,更在電影、電視與廣告中被無數次戲仿與引用,其中最具反諷意味的一次,是史丹利·庫柏力克《發條橘子》(A Clockwork Orange,1971)中,由馬爾坎·麥道威爾飾演的角色以令人不安的方式重新演繹了這段旋律。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:本片已獲美國國會圖書館列入「國家影片登記表」(National Film Registry)1989 年首批入選名單之中;至今仍持續於戲院重映與再發行放映,並可在各大串流平台與家庭影音平台上觀賞,同時也是電影學院講授美國電影歌舞片歷史與敘事語彙時的必修經典教材。
    來源:loc.gov 美國國家影片登記表、en.wikipedia.org

8)看片方法——省略剪輯/壓縮時間的剪接 Ellipsis(第 35 課)

「省略剪輯」(ellipsis)指的是刻意利用一個剪接點、疊化(dissolve),或一小段蒙太奇段落,跳過故事中某一段時間,同時又不讓觀眾對「究竟過了多久」感到困惑。這與第 24 天教過的「跳接」(Jump Cut)不同——跳接是在原本應為同一連續時刻之中,剪接兩個過於相似的鏡頭,刻意製造出明顯、令人不適的斷裂感;也與第 18 天教過的「交叉剪接/平行蒙太奇」不同——那是在不同地點、真正同時發生的動作之間來回切換,而非在單一連續動作之中壓縮時間。跳接是刻意讓剪接點的存在「暴露」出來、成為一種擾動,而省略剪輯則正好相反,是要把時間跳躍的痕跡「撫平」,通常會透過疊化、某個反覆出現的視覺或音樂母題、月曆或新聞標題的插入畫面,或是服裝與道具的更換,不著痕跡地確認時間已然流逝。發現這項手法時,可以檢視:究竟跳過了多長的時間?是透過哪一項具體線索標示出這次跳躍?被省略的這段時間,對故事而言真的無關緊要,還是刻意保留、留待日後才揭曉?這項手法壓縮的,是長達數年、足以構成一段完整人生的蒙太奇,還是僅僅幾個小時或幾天的簡單疊化過場?

實例演練:《天外奇蹟》(Up,2009,導演:彼特·達克特,共同導演:鮑勃·彼德森)——開場那段完全沒有台詞的〈婚姻生活〉(Married Life)蒙太奇。一連串快速的疊化與生活片段——婚禮、一起粉刷房子、被大雨打斷的野餐、僅以一顆空蕩蕩育嬰房鏡頭暗示的、令人心碎的流產經歷、貼著「天堂瀑布」標籤、卻總是被生活中各種不浪漫的意外開銷掏空的存錢罐,以及兩人一同緩慢老去的身影——將卡爾與艾莉長達約四十五年的整段婚姻生活,壓縮進大約四分鐘、完全沒有對白的畫面之中。麥可·吉亞奇諾(Michael Giacchino)譜寫的單一反覆主題旋律,搭配反覆出現的圖形匹配剪輯轉場(相同的物件與動作,在每次疊化之間悄悄地一次次老去),將每一次時間跳躍全都串連成同一條連貫的情感主線,使得片刻之後艾莉終究離世的那一刻,承載的是這整段未曾被逐一呈現、卻真實存在過的一生份量,而不必透過逐場戲展示才能累積出這股重量。
