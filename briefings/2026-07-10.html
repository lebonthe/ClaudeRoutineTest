4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 3: 屯 (Zhūn) — "Difficulty at the Beginning" (Water over Thunder). The image of a seed struggling to sprout through hard ground, or clouds and thunder gathering before rain finally falls — representing the chaos and difficulty of any new beginning at its earliest, most vulnerable stage. The classical judgment favors perseverance and seeking capable help rather than acting alone; it warns against rushing forward impulsively, but equally against giving up. Small, careful steps and gathering good allies carry one through the initial turbulence toward eventual order.
