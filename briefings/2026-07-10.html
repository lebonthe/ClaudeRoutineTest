3) Spanish Lesson — Day 2 (10 min, complete beginner)

Yesterday covered greetings and self-introductions (¡Hola!, ¿Cómo te llamas?, Me llamo..., Mucho gusto). Today builds on that with the natural next step — ending a conversation politely: farewells and basic courtesy words.

  • Hasta luego (AH-sta LWEH-go) — See you later (the everyday, casual farewell)
  • Adiós (ah-DYOHS) — Goodbye (more formal / more final-sounding)
  • Tengo que irme (TEN-go keh EER-meh) — I have to go (tener que + reflexive irse)
  • Fue un placer conocerte (fweh oon plah-SEHR koh-noh-SEHR-teh) — It was a pleasure to meet you
  • Gracias / De nada (GRAH-syahs / deh NAH-dah) — Thank you / You're welcome
  • Que tengas un buen día (keh TEN-gahs oon bwen DEE-ah) — Have a good day (a subjunctive wish form, previewed for later)

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 2: 坤 (Kūn) — "The Receptive" (Earth over Earth). Pure yin, the natural complement to yesterday's Hexagram 1 (乾, The Creative). It represents receptivity, devotion, nurturing, and adaptability — strength expressed through yielding rather than initiating. Interpretation: favorable for consolidating and supporting rather than leading — like a mare that carries steadily and follows a good path without complaint. Success comes from patience, cooperation, and letting things develop in their proper time, not from forcing new ventures.

4) 易經卦象

第二卦:坤卦(Kūn)——「地」(上坤下坤)。純陰之氣,是昨天第一卦(乾,創造之力)的自然對應。象徵接納、包容、滋養與順應——透過柔順而非主動來展現力量。解讀:此卦適合鞏固既有成果、支持他人的行動,而非主動開創新局,就像溫順的母馬,穩健承載、依循正道而不抱怨。成功來自耐心、合作,以及順應時機發展,而非強求。
