3) Spanish Lesson — Day 65 (10 min, Ser vs. Estar with Adjectives that Change Meaning)

Steps outside the word-formation topics of Days 63-64 to return to and formally contrast ser and estar — the two "to be" verbs used separately since Day 2 (estar, temporary feelings) and Day 5 (ser, permanent identity/nationality) but never directly set side by side until now. The core logic: ser describes a definitional, relatively stable "what kind of thing/person is this," while estar describes a "how are things right now" state or situation. The sharpest, most memorable demonstration of that logic is a small set of adjectives whose English/Chinese translation changes entirely depending on which verb is used: aburrido (ser = boring as a permanent trait, estar = bored right now), listo (ser = clever/smart as a personality trait, estar = ready in this moment), malo (ser = bad/of poor character, estar = sick, or of spoiled food), rico (ser = wealthy as a lasting status, estar = delicious as an in-the-moment sensory judgment), verde (ser = green in color, or inexperienced as a lasting trait; estar = unripe as a temporary growth stage), and vivo (ser = sharp/cunning as a personality trait, estar = alive as a life-state). These are not arbitrary exceptions to memorize by rote but the clearest possible proof that the ser/estar distinction is really about "essence" vs. "state," not a fixed pairing between specific words and specific verbs.

  • Es muy aburrido, habla sin parar. Pero hoy estoy aburrido porque no dormí bien. — He's a boring person, he talks non-stop. But today I'm bored because I didn't sleep well.
  • ¿Ya estás lista para el examen? — Are you ready for the exam yet?
  • Sí, y además soy muy lista, así que no me preocupo mucho. — Yes, and besides I'm smart, so I don't worry much.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 1: 逍遙遊 (Xiāoyáo Yóu) — "Free and Easy Wandering"

With Hexagram 64 completing the full King Wen sequence yesterday, this section now begins introducing one essay or poem of Zhuangzi (莊子, c. 4th century BCE, Warring States period), one per day, never repeated, starting with the natural first choice: Xiāoyáo Yóu ("Free and Easy Wandering"), the opening chapter of the Inner Chapters (內篇) — the core seven chapters most confidently attributed to Zhuangzi himself — and by far the most famous single piece in the entire text. The essay opens with a cosmic image of scale: in the northern darkness there is a fish named Kun (鯤), so enormous no one knows how many thousand li it measures; it transforms into a bird named Peng (鵬), whose back alone spans who-knows-how-many thousand li, and which, when it rises, beats its wings across 3,000 li of water and climbs 90,000 li into the sky on a six-month wind, journeying to the Southern Darkness. A cicada and a young dove laugh at Peng, saying they themselves fly only as high as an elm tree before dropping back to the ground — why would anyone need to travel 90,000 li? Zhuangzi's reply is not that the small creatures are wrong to laugh, but that their laughter simply reveals the limits of their own perspective: "small knowledge cannot conceive of great knowledge; short life cannot conceive of long life" (小知不及大知,小年不及大年). The essay proceeds through further escalating examples — a morning mushroom that never sees a full day, a cicada that never sees a year, contrasted with the legendary tortoise and tree that measure life in millennia — building toward its famous conclusion: even Liezi (列子), who could ride the wind and travel effortlessly for fifteen days, still "depended on something" (猶有所待者也) — the wind itself. True freedom, Zhuangzi argues, belongs only to one who "rides on the truth of Heaven and Earth, and drives the transformations of the six vital breaths, wandering in the boundless" (乘天地之正,而御六氣之辯,以遊無窮者) and who therefore depends on nothing at all — summarized in the essay's single most quoted line: "the ultimate person has no self; the spiritual person has no achievement; the sage has no name" (至人無己,神人無功,聖人無名). Read across more than two thousand years, the chapter is less a narrative than an extended argument, by escalating example, that most of what we take as "big" or "impressive" is only relatively so, and that genuine freedom means shedding dependence on any fixed external measure altogether — a foundational text of philosophical Daoism whose imagery (the Kun-Peng transformation, the small creatures' laughter, the "nothing to depend on") remains among the most widely quoted in Chinese literature and thought to this day.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Serbia

  • Location & territory size: Serbia is a landlocked country in the Balkans/Southeast Europe, bordered by Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Kosovo (status disputed — Serbia does not recognize its 2008 declaration of independence), Montenegro, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Croatia. Serbia proper (excluding Kosovo) covers roughly 77,474 km², making it somewhat larger than the Czech Republic or South Carolina, and a little over twice the land area of Taiwan (~36,197 km²); including the territory of Kosovo as claimed under the Serbian constitution, the figure rises to roughly 88,499 km².
  • Population: Approximately 6.6-6.7 million (2026, excluding Kosovo), concentrated around the capital and largest city, Belgrade (Beograd); like much of the Balkans, Serbia's population has been gradually declining due to low birth rates and continued emigration, particularly of younger, skilled workers to Western Europe.
  • Religion: Predominantly Eastern Orthodox Christian (around 85% of the population), specifically adherents of the Serbian Orthodox Church, which has historically played a central role in Serbian national identity, distinguishing Serbia religiously from its Catholic-majority neighbor Croatia and its Muslim-majority neighbor Bosnia's Bosniak population.
  • Economy: An upper-middle-income, EU-candidate economy (accession talks ongoing since 2014, with progress widely seen as stalled) built on manufacturing (automotive parts, machinery), agriculture, IT/services outsourcing, and significant Chinese and European foreign investment in mining and heavy industry; not a Eurozone or Schengen member, using its own currency, the Serbian dinar.
  • History: Heir to a medieval kingdom and empire that peaked under Emperor Dušan in the 14th century before falling to the Ottoman Empire after the 1389 Battle of Kosovo — an event still central to Serbian national memory; regained independence in stages through the 19th century, became the core of the first Yugoslav state after World War I, endured Nazi occupation and brutal internal conflict in World War II, and was the dominant republic within Tito's post-war socialist Yugoslav federation. The 1990s Yugoslav wars (including the Bosnian War and the 1999 Kosovo War, which triggered NATO's bombing campaign against Serbia) and the still-unresolved status of Kosovo's 2008 independence declaration remain the defining modern fault lines in Serbian politics and foreign relations.

As of this briefing, Serbia is in the middle of a live political story: a snap parliamentary election has been called for October 25, 2026, after Vučić's ruling SNS party nominated him as its candidate for prime minister on September 5 — meaning Serbia's sitting president may move to the premiership if his party wins, a shift closely watched given the scale of the anti-government protest movement over the past two years.
  • Language: Serbian (a South Slavic language, mutually intelligible to a large degree with Croatian, Bosnian, and Montenegrin, and — distinctively among that group — written using both the Cyrillic script, which holds official constitutional status, and the Latin alphabet in everyday use).

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — The Bed Intruder Song / Antoine Dodson

  • Name & origin: In late July 2010, local Huntsville, Alabama news station WAFF interviewed Antoine Dodson, a young man whose sister had just survived an attempted sexual assault by an intruder who broke into their public-housing apartment. Dodson's raw, animated, quotable on-camera reaction — delivered directly to the camera in a distinctive, expressive style, including the lines "He's climbing in your windows, he's snatching your people up" and "Hide your kids, hide your wife" — was clipped and began spreading online within days.
  • How it became popular: The comedy music duo The Gregory Brothers (creators of the "Auto-Tune the News" series) set Dodson's actual spoken words to a melody using Auto-Tune pitch-correction software, releasing "The Bed Intruder Song" on YouTube on July 31, 2010. The video went viral almost immediately, and the song — built entirely from a real news interview rather than newly written lyrics — was released commercially on iTunes, charting on the Billboard Hot 100 (reaching the mid-90s on the chart), an unusual feat for an unscripted local-news clip turned song.
  • Meaning & usage context: The meme functions on two layers that have drawn lasting scholarly and cultural commentary: as pure comedic virality (the exaggerated cadence and quotable lines were repeated, remixed, and referenced across the internet for years), and as an early, prominent example of a real person's genuine, unscripted moment of distress being transformed into internet entertainment — raising questions, discussed at the time and since, about the ethics of "viral fame" built from someone else's real trauma, particularly given Dodson's identity as a Black, openly gay man whose mannerisms some critics argued were being mocked rather than simply enjoyed.
  • Variations & evolution: Beyond the song itself, the meme spawned countless remixes, ringtones, parody covers, and the phrase "hide your kids, hide your wife" became a stand-alone catchphrase applied as an exaggerated warning in unrelated contexts; Dodson himself leaned into the unexpected fame for a period (media appearances, a brief attempt at a broader media career) before later stepping back from public life and, in later interviews, offering a more complicated, mixed assessment of what the experience had been like for him personally.
  • Current status: Firmly a historical/nostalgia-tier meme by 2026, regularly cited in "2010s internet history" retrospectives as one of the first news-clip-to-viral-song crossovers and an early case study in the still-unresolved ethical debate over turning real people's unscripted personal moments into internet content without their prior consent.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Ran (亂, 1985, dir. Akira Kurosawa)

  • Creator & era: Directed by Akira Kurosawa, written by Kurosawa with Hideo Oguni and Masato Ide, released in 1985 — a Japanese-French co-production made near the end of Kurosawa's career, at age 75, after years of struggling to secure financing (French producer Serge Silberman ultimately helped fund the film, and it remains one of the most expensive Japanese films made up to that time).
  • Plot overview: An aging warlord, Hidetora Ichimonji, decides to divide his kingdom among his three sons rather than continue ruling alone; his youngest son, Saburo, warns that the plan will end in disaster and is banished for his honesty, while the two elder sons, Taro and Jiro, quickly turn against their father and each other in a spiraling war for total control, ultimately reducing Hidetora — and the kingdom — to ruin and madness. The story reworks Shakespeare's King Lear through the lens of Japanese Sengoku-era (Warring States) history and the legend of the historical warlord Mōri Motonari, who was said to have taught his three sons the strength of unity through the famous "bundle of arrows" parable — a lesson Kurosawa's film inverts into tragedy.
  • Artistic style & technique: Renowned for its meticulously choreographed large-scale battle sequences (the assault on the Third Castle is often singled out as one of cinema's great sustained battle set-pieces), its use of color-coded armies and costumes (each son's forces dressed in a single dominant color — yellow, red, blue — making the escalating three-way conflict instantly legible even in wide, chaotic battle shots), and Emi Wada's Academy Award-winning costume design; Kurosawa, who was going blind while making the film, reportedly painted detailed storyboards in advance to communicate his exact visual intentions to the crew.
  • Cultural & historical significance: Widely regarded as one of Kurosawa's supreme late-career masterpieces and among the greatest films ever made about the futility and self-destructiveness of war and power; frequently discussed alongside Kurosawa's earlier Shakespeare adaptation Throne of Blood (based on Macbeth) as evidence of how thoroughly Japanese jidaigeki (period-drama) conventions and Shakespearean tragedy could be fused into something distinct from either tradition alone.
  • Influence & legacy: A major influence on subsequent large-scale historical epics' approach to legible, color-coded battle staging, and frequently cited by later filmmakers (including George Lucas and Francis Ford Coppola, both of whom helped secure the film's international backing) as a defining influence on how to stage tragedy at an epic historical scale; nominated for four Academy Awards, winning Best Costume Design.
  • Where to experience it today: Available in a Criterion Collection restoration on home video and streaming, and a staple of retrospective theatrical screenings and Kurosawa/Japanese-cinema film-school curricula worldwide.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Frame Narrative / Nested Diegesis (Story-within-a-Story) (Lesson 51)

A new analytical lens distinct from all 50 prior lessons, including Day 6's Narrative Structure (which addresses achronological/non-linear sequencing of a single story) and Day 17's Title Sequence lesson (a compressed thesis delivered before the story proper begins): frame narrative concerns how many distinct diegetic levels — layers of storytelling nested inside one another — a film constructs, and what happens at the boundary where one level tells, remembers, or frames another. Check who is narrating each level and to whom, whether the outer frame is a brief bookend or a continuously returned-to presence, whether the levels differ in reliability or visual/tonal treatment, and — the sharpest version of the technique — whether the film uses the relationship between levels (which one is "true," which is a construction) as its own thematic payload rather than mere structural bookkeeping.

Worked example: Life of Pi (2012, dir. Ang Lee) — the film frames its entire fantastical shipwreck-and-tiger survival story as adult Pi Patel recounting his youth to a novelist seeking material, establishing from the outset that we are watching a remembered, told story rather than a directly witnessed one. Deep in the outer frame's final act, adult Pi offers the novelist (and the audience) a second, brutally mundane alternate version of the same events — one with no tiger, no floating island, only desperate human survival and violence — and asks which story the novelist prefers. The novelist chooses the tiger story; Pi replies, "And so it goes with God," reframing the entire preceding two hours not as a simple test of the audience's credulity but as a direct argument, staged through the nested-narrative structure itself, about why humans choose meaningful stories over bare, brutal fact. The technique here does exactly what Day 6's Narrative Structure lesson does not: it is not about when events are shown relative to each other, but about how many layers of telling stand between the audience and the "actual" events, and what is gained or lost crossing each layer's boundary.

3)西班牙文課程——第 65 天(10 分鐘,Ser 與 Estar 的用法辨析:意義隨動詞改變的形容詞)

跳出第 63~64 天的構詞主題,回頭正式對照 ser 與 estar——這兩個「to be」動詞,自第 2 天(estar,暫時心情)與第 5 天(ser,永久身分/國籍)起便分別各自使用,卻直到今天才第一次被正式並排比較。核心邏輯是:ser 描述一種定義性、相對穩定的「這是什麼樣的人/事物」,estar 描述的則是「現在情況如何」的暫時狀態或情境。而最鋒利、也最容易讓人牢牢記住這條邏輯的示範,正是一小組「換了動詞、中文翻譯就完全不同」的形容詞:aburrido(ser =本質上無聊的人格特質,estar =此刻感到無聊)、listo(ser =聰明機靈的人格特質,estar =此刻準備好了)、malo(ser =品行不端、本質上的壞,estar =身體不舒服,或食物已經壞掉)、rico(ser =長期身分上的富有,estar =此刻嚐到的味道很美味)、verde(ser =顏色是綠色的,或比喻上還嫩、沒經驗的長期特質;estar =水果還沒成熟的暫時生長階段)、以及 vivo(ser =腦筋動得快、機靈狡猾的人格特質,estar =活著的生命狀態)。這些並非需要死背的隨機例外,而是最清楚的證明:ser/estar 的區別,關鍵始終在「本質」與「狀態」之分,而不是某個字被硬性綁定某個動詞。

  • Es muy aburrido, habla sin parar. Pero hoy estoy aburrido porque no dormí bien. —— 他這個人很無聊,講話講個不停。不過我今天很想睡,因為昨晚沒睡好。
  • ¿Ya estás lista para el examen? —— 你準備好考試了嗎?
  • Sí, y además soy muy lista, así que no me preocupo mucho. —— 是啊,而且我本來就很聰明,所以也不太擔心。

4)莊子——第 1 篇:逍遙遊(Xiāoyáo Yóu)——「無拘無束、自在悠遊」

隨著昨日第 64 卦完成整部王本卦序,本單元自今日起改為每日介紹一篇莊子(約西元前 4 世紀、戰國時代)的散文或詩作,絕不重複,並以最自然的第一篇作為開場:〈逍遙遊〉,《莊子》內篇(學界普遍認為最可能出自莊周本人手筆的核心七篇)的開篇之作,也是整部《莊子》中最負盛名的單篇文章。文章開頭便是氣勢磅礴的宏大意象:北方幽暗之海中有一條魚,名叫鯤,體型龐大到無人知曉牠究竟有幾千里長;牠化身為一隻名叫鵬的大鳥,光是背部就不知有幾千里寬,當牠奮起高飛時,拍打水面激起三千里的浪花,乘著六月的大風盤旋而上、直上九萬里高空,飛往南方的大海。一隻蟬與一隻斑鳩因此嘲笑大鵬,說牠們自己不過飛到榆樹、枋樹的高度,有時飛不到甚至還會落回地面,又何必大費周章飛上九萬里呢?莊子的回應並非指責這些小動物嘲笑得不對,而是點出牠們的笑聲恰恰暴露了自身見識的侷限:「小知不及大知,小年不及大年。」文章接著透過層層遞進的例子持續推展——朝生暮死、看不到一整天的菌類,活不過一年的寒蟬,對比壽命動輒以千年計算的傳說神龜與大椿樹——最終推向全文最為人稱道的結論:即便是能夠御風而行、逍遙飛行十五天的列子,依然「猶有所待者也」——他終究還是得倚賴風的存在。莊子主張,真正的自由,只屬於那個能夠「乘天地之正,而御六氣之辯,以遊無窮者」、因此再無任何倚賴的人——並凝縮成全文被引用次數最多的一句話作結:「至人無己,神人無功,聖人無名。」歷經兩千餘年後重讀此篇,與其說它是一則敘事,不如說它是一場層層遞進的論證:我們平日視為「宏大」或「了不起」的事物,往往只是相對而言如此;真正的自由,意味著徹底擺脫對任何固定外在標準的倚賴——這正是道家哲學的奠基之作,其意象(鯤鵬之變、小動物的嘲笑、「無所待」)至今仍是中國文學與思想中被引用最頻繁的段落之一。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——塞爾維亞(Serbia)

  • 地理位置與領土規模:塞爾維亞是位於巴爾幹半島/東南歐的內陸國家,與匈牙利、羅馬尼亞、保加利亞、北馬其頓、科索沃(地位存在爭議——塞爾維亞不承認科索沃 2008 年單方面宣布獨立)、蒙特內哥羅、波士尼亞與赫塞哥維納,以及克羅埃西亞接壤。塞爾維亞本土(不含科索沃)國土面積約 77,474 平方公里,略大於捷克或美國南卡羅來納州,約為台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)陸地面積的兩倍多;若依塞爾維亞憲法主張、將科索沃領土一併計入,面積則增至約 88,499 平方公里。
  • 人口:約 660 萬至 670 萬人(2026 年,不含科索沃),主要集中於首都暨最大城市貝爾格勒(Beograd);與巴爾幹地區多數國家相似,受出生率偏低與年輕技術人才持續外移西歐影響,塞爾維亞總人口正緩步下滑。
  • 宗教:以東正教為主(約佔人口 85%),具體而言為塞爾維亞正教會信徒——該教會在歷史上,對塞爾維亞的民族認同扮演核心角色,也在宗教信仰上,將塞爾維亞與以天主教為主的鄰國克羅埃西亞、以及以波士尼亞克(Bosniak)穆斯林人口為主的鄰國波士尼亞,清楚區隔開來。
  • 經濟:屬中高所得、歐盟候選國經濟體(入盟談判自 2014 年展開,但普遍認為進展已陷入停滯),產業以製造業(汽車零組件、機械)、農業、資訊科技/服務業委外,以及中國與歐洲對礦業、重工業的大量外資投入為主;並非歐元區或申根區成員國,使用自有貨幣塞爾維亞第納爾(dinar)。
  • 歷史:承襲一個中世紀王國與帝國的歷史遺產,該王國在 14 世紀杜尚皇帝(Emperor Dušan)治下達到鼎盛,其後於 1389 年科索沃戰役後淪陷於鄂圖曼帝國統治——這場戰役至今仍是塞爾維亞民族記憶的核心事件之一;19 世紀起分階段重獲獨立,第一次世界大戰後成為第一個南斯拉夫國家的核心,二戰期間歷經納粹佔領與慘烈的內部衝突,戰後則是狄托領導下社會主義南斯拉夫聯邦中最具主導地位的加盟共和國。1990 年代的南斯拉夫內戰(包括波士尼亞戰爭,以及引發北約對塞爾維亞展開轟炸行動的 1999 年科索沃戰爭),以及科索沃 2008 年宣布獨立至今仍未解決的地位爭議,至今仍是塞爾維亞現代政治與對外關係中最核心的分歧點。
  • 現任領導人與一則正在發生的政治新聞:總統亞歷山大·武契奇(Aleksandar Vučić,自 2017 年起任職,先前曾擔任總理)仍為國家元首,總理一職自 2025 年 4 月起由久羅·馬庫特(Đuro Macut)擔任——馬庫特的上任,緊接在前總理米洛什·武切維奇(Miloš Vučević)因 2024 年 11 月諾維薩德(Novi Sad)火車站雨棚倒塌致死事件、引發全國持續大規模抗議後辭職之後。截至本篇簡報為止,塞爾維亞正處於一則進行式的政治新聞核心:執政的塞爾維亞前進黨(SNS)已於 9 月 5 日提名武契奇作為總理候選人,隨後國會提前選舉已訂於 2026 年 10 月 25 日舉行——意味著若其政黨勝選,現任總統可能轉任總理一職,考量到過去兩年來反政府抗議運動的規模,此一發展備受各界密切關注。
  • 語言:官方語言為塞爾維亞語(南斯拉夫語族的一支,與克羅埃西亞語、波士尼亞語、蒙特內哥羅語有高度的互通性;與這組語言中的其他成員相比,塞爾維亞語的獨特之處,在於同時使用具憲法官方地位的西里爾字母,以及日常生活中普遍使用的拉丁字母)。

6)網路迷因介紹——床上入侵者之歌(The Bed Intruder Song)與安東尼·多德森(Antoine Dodson)

  • 名稱與起源:2010 年 7 月下旬,阿拉巴馬州杭茨維爾(Huntsville)當地新聞台 WAFF 採訪了安東尼·多德森(Antoine Dodson)——他的姊姊剛剛逃過一名闖入其公共住宅公寓的入侵者的性侵未遂攻擊。多德森在鏡頭前情緒激動、極具戲劇張力且金句連發的即席發言——以獨樹一格、極富表現力的方式直接對著鏡頭喊話,包括「他爬進你家窗戶,把你家人抓走」(He's climbing in your windows, he's snatching your people up)以及「藏好你的小孩,藏好你老婆」(Hide your kids, hide your wife)等經典台詞——被剪輯後短短幾天內便在網路上迅速擴散。
  • 如何走紅:喜劇音樂組合「格雷戈里兄弟」(The Gregory Brothers,「Auto-Tune the News」系列的創作者)將多德森原始的真實發言,以 Auto-Tune 自動音準修正軟體加工譜成旋律,於 2010 年 7 月 31 日在 YouTube 發布〈The Bed Intruder Song〉(床上入侵者之歌)。影片幾乎立刻爆紅,而這首完全取材自真實新聞採訪、而非重新填詞創作的歌曲,更正式在 iTunes 上架發行,並打入告示牌百大單曲榜(Billboard Hot 100,名次落在 90 名左右)——對一支未經編劇的地方新聞片段改編而成的歌曲而言,是相當罕見的成就。
  • 意義與使用情境:這個迷因同時運作在兩個層面上,至今仍引發持續的學術與文化討論:其一是純粹的喜劇病毒式擴散(誇張的語調與金句台詞,多年來持續在網路上被反覆引用、混剪與致敬);其二則是一個極早期、極具代表性的案例,說明一個真實人物在真實痛苦、毫無劇本的即席時刻,如何被轉化為網路娛樂內容——當時與其後皆有評論者提出質疑,討論建立在他人真實創傷之上的「病毒式爆紅」是否符合倫理,尤其考量到多德森身為一名黑人、公開出櫃的同志男性,部分評論者認為他當時的言行舉止,遭到的其實更像是嘲弄、而非單純的欣賞。
  • 變體與演進:除了歌曲本身之外,這個迷因也催生出無數混音版本、手機鈴聲、戲仿翻唱,而「藏好你的小孩,藏好你老婆」這句台詞更獨立演變為一句固定用語,被套用在與原始情境完全無關的各種場合中,作為誇張的警告用語;多德森本人有一段時期也順勢擁抱了這份意外而來的名氣(接受媒體採訪、短暫嘗試跨足更廣泛的媒體事業),但其後逐漸淡出公眾視野,並在後續訪談中,對這段經歷給出更為複雜、褒貶不一的個人評價。
  • 現況:截至 2026 年,這已明確是一個「歷史/懷舊等級」的迷因,經常在「2010 年代網路歷史」回顧內容中被提及,被視為新聞片段跨界成為爆紅歌曲的最早案例之一,也是「未經當事人同意、將真實人物的即席個人時刻轉化為網路內容」這場至今仍未有定論的倫理爭辯中,一個經常被引用的早期案例。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《亂》(Ran,1985,導演:黑澤明)

  • 創作者與時代背景:由黑澤明執導,並與小國英雄、井手雅人共同編劇,於 1985 年上映——這是一部日法合資製作的電影,誕生於黑澤明創作生涯接近尾聲、時年 75 歲之際,歷經多年資金籌措困境後才得以完成(法國製片人賽爾吉·席爾伯曼〔Serge Silberman〕最終協助促成本片的資金到位),至今仍是當時日本電影史上製作成本最高昂的作品之一。
  • 劇情概述:年邁的軍閥一文字秀虎,決定將自己的領地分封給三個兒子,不再獨自統治;么子三郎直言此舉終將釀成大禍,卻因這番誠實進言而遭到放逐,兩位兄長太郎與次郎則迅速反目成仇、彼此鬥爭,一場爭奪絕對統治權的內戰隨之螺旋式升級,最終將秀虎——以及整個領地——一同拖向毀滅與瘋狂的深淵。劇情透過日本戰國時代的歷史脈絡,以及戰國大名毛利元就的傳說——相傳毛利元就曾以著名的「一支箭易折,三支箭難斷」寓言,教導三個兒子團結的重要性——重新演繹了莎士比亞的《李爾王》,而黑澤明的電影則將這則寓言徹底翻轉為一齣悲劇。
  • 藝術風格與技法:本片以精心編排、場面浩大的戰爭場景聞名於世(對「三の城」〔第三座城〕的攻城戲,經常被單獨列為影史上最偉大的長篇戰爭場面之一),並運用色彩區分各方陣營服裝與旗幟(三個兒子的軍隊分別以單一主色——黃、紅、藍——作為識別),即便在混亂寬廣的戰爭鏡頭中,也能讓觀眾一眼辨明三方勢力的消長;和田惠美更以本片服裝設計榮獲奧斯卡金像獎。黑澤明在拍攝本片期間視力已幾近全盲,據傳事先繪製了極為詳盡的分鏡手繪稿,藉此向劇組人員精確傳達他心目中的視覺構想。
  • 文化與歷史意義:普遍被公認為黑澤明晚期創作生涯中最卓越的傑作之一,也是描繪戰爭與權力徒勞、自我毀滅本質的影史經典之作;經常與黑澤明先前改編自《馬克白》的莎劇作品《蜘蛛巢城》相提並論,共同證明日本時代劇(jidaigeki)傳統與莎士比亞悲劇,能夠徹底融合成一種既非純粹日本傳統、也非純粹莎劇傳統的全新藝術形式。
  • 影響與傳承:本片對後續大型歷史史詩電影「以色彩清楚區分陣營」的戰爭調度手法,影響深遠,並經常被後輩導演(包括喬治·盧卡斯與法蘭西斯·柯波拉,兩人皆曾協助促成本片獲得國際資金挹注)引用為「如何在史詩級歷史規模上呈現悲劇」的重要典範;本片共獲四項奧斯卡提名,最終奪下最佳服裝設計獎。
  • 今日可觀賞管道:現今可透過 Criterion Collection 的修復版本,在家用影碟與串流平台上觀賞,也是全球各地回顧影展,以及黑澤明/日本電影相關電影學校課程的必修經典之一。

8)看片方法——框架敘事/嵌套敘事層(故事中的故事)(第 51 課)

與先前 50 堂課截然不同的全新分析角度,包括第 6 課的「敘事結構」(處理單一故事內部的非線性/去時序重新排列)以及第 17 課的「片頭字幕」課程(在正式故事開始前,以壓縮形式先行揭示全片主旨)皆與本課不同:框架敘事關注的是,一部電影建構了多少層彼此嵌套的「敘事層級」(diegetic levels),以及當某一層講述、回憶或框住另一層時,這個邊界上究竟發生了什麼。判斷重點包括:每一層是由誰向誰講述的;外層框架是短暫的頭尾點綴,還是持續反覆出現的存在;各層之間在可信度或視覺/情緒調性上是否有所差異;以及——本技法最鋒利的運用形式——電影是否直接把「各層之間的關係」(哪一層是「真實」,哪一層是「建構出來的」)本身,當作全片的核心主題來運用,而不僅僅是單純的結構安排。

實例演練:《少年 Pi 的奇幻漂流》(Life of Pi,2012,導演:李安)——全片將整段充滿奇幻色彩的海難與老虎生存故事,框架設定為成年後的 Pi·帕特爾(Pi Patel),向一位正在尋找寫作題材的小說家講述自己的少年往事,從一開始便清楚建立起「我們正在觀看的,是一段被回憶、被講述出來的故事,而非直接目睹的真實事件」這個前提。在外層框架接近尾聲之際,成年 Pi 向這位小說家(同時也是向觀眾)提出了同一段事件的第二個版本——一個殘酷寫實、毫無奇幻色彩的版本,沒有老虎、沒有漂浮小島,只有絕望的人類求生與暴力——並詢問小說家比較喜歡哪一個版本。小說家選擇了有老虎的那個版本;Pi 回答:「上帝也是這樣。」這句話瞬間將前面兩個小時的內容,重新定義為不只是單純考驗觀眾輕信與否的把戲,而是透過嵌套敘事結構本身所提出的一項直接論證:探討人類為何總是選擇有意義的故事,而非赤裸裸的殘酷事實。這項技法所做的,恰恰是第 6 課「敘事結構」課程未曾處理的面向——它關注的不是事件相對彼此以何種順序呈現,而是觀眾與「實際」發生的事件之間,究竟隔著多少層講述,以及跨越每一層邊界時,究竟得到了什麼、又失去了什麼。
