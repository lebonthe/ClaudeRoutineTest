3) Spanish Lesson — Day 99: Intentar / Tratar de / Lograr / Conseguir ("Trying" vs. "Succeeding")

Intentar + infinitive means "to try" with no preposition: Intento dormir. Tratar de + infinitive means the same but always needs de: Trato de dormir. Neither promises success. Lograr means "to manage to / achieve" (no preposition: Logré terminar), and conseguir (e→i: consigo, consigues, consigue) means "to manage to" with an infinitive and "to get / obtain" with a noun: Conseguí un trabajo. Rule of thumb: intentar has no de, tratar has de; "I tried" and "I did it" are different verbs.

  • ¿Conseguiste las entradas para el concierto? — Did you get the tickets for the concert?
  • Lo intenté toda la mañana, pero no logré entrar a la página. — I tried all morning, but I couldn't get onto the page.
  • Trata de llamar a la taquilla. — Try calling the box office.
  • ¡Al final conseguí dos! — In the end I got two!
  • ¡Qué bien! Entonces trato de conseguir un día libre. — Great! Then I'll try to get a day off.

4) Daodejing — Chapter 2: 天下皆知美之為美 (Tiānxià jiē zhī měi zhī wéi měi) — "When Everyone Knows Beauty as Beauty"

Series note: this continues the Daodejing track begun on Oct 22, one chapter per day in order (chapters 1-81), never repeated.

Text: 天下皆知美之為美,斯惡已;皆知善之為善,斯不善已。故有無相生,難易相成,長短相較,高下相傾,音聲相和,前後相隨。是以聖人處無為之事,行不言之教;萬物作焉而不辭,生而不有,為而不恃,功成而弗居。夫唯弗居,是以不去。

Plain-English rendering (paraphrase): When everyone in the world knows beauty as beauty, ugliness has already appeared; when everyone knows good as good, the not-good has already appeared. So being and non-being give birth to each other, difficult and easy complete each other, long and short measure each other, high and low lean on each other, tone and voice harmonize, before and after follow each other. Therefore the sage handles affairs through non-action and carries out teaching without words. The myriad things arise and he does not turn them away; he gives life but does not possess, acts but does not rely on it, completes the work but does not dwell in it. Precisely because he does not dwell in it, it does not leave him.

Notes: After Chapter 1's warning about naming, Chapter 2 shows what naming does: every concept comes bundled with its opposite, so fixing one pole (beauty, good) creates the other (ugliness, not-good). The six paired opposites are presented as mutually generating rather than as enemies. The sage's response is 無為 (non-action, meaning not forcing) and 不言之教 (teaching without words), and the closing lines describe an attitude of non-possession: do the work, give life, and do not cling to the credit. Some editions read 較 as 形 in "長短相較/形"; the sense is the same.

Takeaway: Praise and blame are two sides of one coin; do the work and let go of the credit.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Mali

  • Location & territory size: A landlocked country in West Africa, in the Sahel and Sahara, bordering Algeria (north), Niger (east), Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire and Guinea (south), and Senegal and Mauritania (west). Its area is about 1.24 million square kilometers, roughly 34 times Taiwan, about 3.3 times Japan, and a bit under twice the size of Texas. Most of the north is desert, while most people live in the south along the Niger River.
  • Population: About 23 million (approximate), concentrated in the south; the capital Bamako is the largest city, with a few million residents. One of the youngest populations in the world.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Muslim (commonly cited at over 90%, mostly Sunni with a strong Sufi tradition), with small Christian communities and some traditional African religious practice. Exact percentages vary by source.
  • Economy: Among the poorest countries in the world by income per person (roughly US$1,000 per year, approximate). Agriculture (cotton, millet, rice, livestock) employs most people, and gold is the main export: Mali is one of Africa's largest gold producers. It uses the West African CFA franc, and has been affected by conflict, sanctions and fuel shortages in recent years.
  • History: The region hosted the Ghana Empire, then the Mali Empire, founded around 1235 by Sundiata Keita; its ruler Mansa Musa made a famous pilgrimage to Mecca in 1324 and is often described as one of the wealthiest people in history. Timbuktu became a center of trade and Islamic scholarship, and the Songhai Empire followed. France colonized the area as French Sudan from the late 19th century; Mali became independent in 1960 under Modibo Keïta after the short-lived Mali Federation with Senegal broke up. A 1968 coup brought Moussa Traoré; a 1991 uprising led to multiparty democracy. In 2012 a Tuareg rebellion and jihadist groups seized the north, prompting French intervention in 2013. Military coups followed in August 2020 and May 2021. French and UN forces left in 2022-2023, and in 2025 the junta dissolved political parties and gave its leader a renewable five-year term.
  • Current leader: General Assimi Goïta, head of the military-led transitional government since 2021 (the status of the government today was not verified).
  • Language: French is the official language; Bambara (Bamanankan) is the most widely spoken lingua franca, and a dozen or more other national languages (Fula, Songhai, Soninke, Tamasheq and others) are used.
  • Culture & trivia: The Great Mosque of Djenné is the largest mud-brick building in the world and is rebuilt each year in a communal plastering festival; Timbuktu's old manuscripts, Dogon cliff villages and the music of artists such as Ali Farka Touré and Salif Keita are all world-famous. Mali withdrew from ECOWAS in 2025 together with Burkina Faso and Niger, forming the Alliance of Sahel States.

7) Film / Performing-Arts Spotlight — Bertolt Brecht and Epic Theatre (Berliner Ensemble)

  • Name & key figures: Epic Theatre (episches Theater), developed by the German playwright and director Bertolt Brecht (1898-1956), with the composer Kurt Weill, the actress and company leader Helene Weigel, and the director Erwin Piscator as an earlier influence. Its central concept is the Verfremdungseffekt (V-effekt, "alienation" or "distancing" effect).
  • Era / background: It grew out of Weimar-era Berlin in the 1920s, when Brecht was a Marxist writing for a theatre he thought lulled audiences into emotion. He went into exile when the Nazis took power in 1933, wrote his major plays abroad (Denmark, Sweden, Finland, the US), and returned to East Berlin in 1948-49, where he and Weigel founded the Berliner Ensemble in 1949. The company settled at the Theater am Schiffbauerdamm in 1954.
  • Content overview (key works): The Threepenny Opera (1928, with Weill, adapted from John Gay's The Beggar's Opera, including "Mack the Knife"); Mother Courage and Her Children (written 1939, set in the Thirty Years' War, about a canteen woman who profits from the war that destroys her children); The Life of Galileo; The Good Person of Szechwan; and The Caucasian Chalk Circle.
  • Artistic style & techniques: Instead of making the audience forget they are watching a play and cry with the hero (what Brecht called "Aristotelian" theatre), Epic Theatre keeps them thinking. Techniques include actors who "show" a character rather than become it, direct address to the audience, narrators and captions, songs that interrupt the action, exposed stage lights and bare sets, episodic scenes with titles, and gestus, a gesture or attitude that shows social relationships. The aim is to make familiar social conditions look strange so they can be questioned and changed.
  • Cultural / historical significance: It reframed theatre as a place for political reasoning, not only emotion. Brecht's 1935 viewing of the Peking Opera star Mei Lanfang in Moscow influenced his essay on alienation effects in Chinese acting, an early example of Western theatre borrowing from Chinese stage conventions.
  • Influence & legacy: A major influence on postwar theatre and film: Jean-Luc Godard, Rainer Werner Fassbinder and Lars von Trier's Dogville (bare chalk-outline set, narrator) use Brechtian devices, as do the Theatre of the Oppressed (Augusto Boal) and playwrights such as Tony Kushner. The terms "Brechtian" and "distancing" are now standard in drama and film criticism.
  • Current status / where to experience it: The Berliner Ensemble still performs in Berlin, and Brecht's plays are staged around the world; many are also available as published scripts, recordings and some filmed productions. Check the company's official site for current programming, which was not verified today.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 85: Transitions as Punctuation (Cut, Fade, Dissolve)

What it is: Every film must get from one shot to the next, and the way it does so is a choice. A hard cut is a plain full stop; a fade to or from black is a heavier paragraph break (it can mean ending, death, a time jump or the start of a story); a dissolve (one image melting into another) suggests time passing or a link between two things. The transition is a piece of the film's grammar, and a director who uses an unusual one is telling you something.

How to analyze: (1) Notice the default: most films use hard cuts almost all the time, so any fade or dissolve is marked. (2) For each unusual transition ask what it separates (time, place, state of mind) and how long it lasts; a slow fade feels like a deep breath or a solemn pause. (3) Ask what the transition says that a plain cut would not. (4) Look at how the film's first and last transitions frame the whole story, and notice whether a film that is full of fades suddenly switches to hard cuts (or the reverse) at a key moment.

Worked example — The Godfather (1972, dir. Francis Ford Coppola, cinematography Gordon Willis), the opening: The film begins on a black screen with a man's voice saying, "I believe in America." The image only fades in afterward, on a close-up of the undertaker Bonasera asking Don Corleone for justice, and the camera then slowly pulls back to reveal the Don's back. Watch what the transition does: the voice reaches us before any picture, so we are in the position of someone listening to a confession, and the slow emergence from darkness matches the film's low-key lighting and its theme of power working in shadow. Then compare it with the hard cut to the sunlit wedding outdoors, which contrasts the closed, dark office with the bright public party and shows the family's two faces. A plain cut at the start would have shown us a man talking; the fade makes us feel we are being let into a secret. (Described from general knowledge, not checked against the film today.)

Try it: In your next film, count the first five transitions and name each one (cut, fade, dissolve, wipe), then ask why the director picked it. The first time you see a fade or dissolve, write down what it separates.

4) 道德經 — 第 2 章:天下皆知美之為美 (Tiānxià jiē zhī měi zhī wéi měi) —「當天下都知道美是美」

系列說明:本章延續 10 月 22 日開始的道德經軌道,每天依序一章(第 1–81 章),不重複。

原文:天下皆知美之為美,斯惡已;皆知善之為善,斯不善已。故有無相生,難易相成,長短相較,高下相傾,音聲相和,前後相隨。是以聖人處無為之事,行不言之教;萬物作焉而不辭,生而不有,為而不恃,功成而弗居。夫唯弗居,是以不去。

白話意譯:當天下人都知道美之所以為美,醜就已經出現了;都知道善之所以為善,不善就已經出現了。所以有和無互相生成,難和易互相成就,長和短互相比較,高和下互相依靠,音和聲互相應和,前和後互相跟隨。因此聖人用不強求(無為)的方式處事,用不說教的方式教導;萬物興起,他不推辭;生養萬物卻不占為己有,有所作為卻不倚仗,功成了也不居功。正因為不居功,功勞才不會離開他。

說明:第 1 章提醒「命名有限度」,第 2 章就示範命名的後果:每一個概念都帶著它的反面,一旦固定了一端(美、善),另一端(醜、不善)就跟著出現。六組對立被說成互相生成,而不是互相敵對。聖人的回應是無為(不強求)與不言之教,結尾描述一種不占有的態度:做事、養育,但不執著於功勞。有些版本把「長短相較」寫成「長短相形」,意思相同。

小結:讚美與批評是一體兩面;把事情做好,然後放下功勞。

5) 國家 / 地區 / 勢力介紹 — 馬利(Mali)

  • 位置與國土面積:西非內陸國,位於薩赫爾與撒哈拉地帶,北接阿爾及利亞,東接尼日,南接布吉納法索、象牙海岸與幾內亞,西接塞內加爾與茅利塔尼亞。面積約 124 萬平方公里,約為台灣的 34 倍、日本的 3.3 倍,比德州的兩倍稍小。北部大多是沙漠,多數人口住在南部的尼日河沿岸。
  • 人口:約 2,300 萬(概數),集中在南部;首都巴馬科是最大城市,居民數百萬。是世界上最年輕的人口結構之一。
  • 宗教:壓倒性多數是穆斯林(常見說法為 90% 以上,多為遜尼派,蘇菲傳統深厚),另有少數基督徒與一些傳統非洲信仰。確切比例各來源不同。
  • 經濟:按人均所得是世界上最貧窮的國家之一(約每年 1,000 美元,概數)。農業(棉花、小米、稻米、畜牧)雇用多數人口,黃金是最主要的出口品:馬利是非洲最大的產金國之一。使用西非 CFA 法郎,近年受衝突、制裁與燃料短缺影響。
  • 歷史:這個地區先後出現迦納帝國與馬利帝國;馬利帝國約於 1235 年由松迪亞塔·凱塔(Sundiata Keita)建立,國王曼薩·穆薩(Mansa Musa)在 1324 年前往麥加朝聖而聞名,常被形容為史上最富有的人之一。廷巴克圖成為貿易與伊斯蘭學術中心,後來是桑海帝國。法國自 19 世紀後期把這裡殖民為法屬蘇丹;1960 年在莫迪博·凱塔(Modibo Keïta)領導下獨立,此前與塞內加爾組成的馬利聯邦很快解體。1968 年政變由穆薩·特拉奧雷上台,1991 年的人民起義帶來多黨民主。2012 年圖阿雷格人叛亂與聖戰團體占領北部,促成法國 2013 年出兵。2020 年 8 月與 2021 年 5 月接連發生軍事政變。法國與聯合國部隊於 2022–2023 年撤離,2025 年軍政府解散政黨,並給予領導人可續任的五年任期。
  • 現任領導人:阿西米·戈伊塔(Assimi Goïta)將軍,自 2021 年起擔任軍方主導的過渡政府領導人(今日政府的現況未經查證)。
  • 語言:官方語言是法語;班巴拉語(Bambara,Bamanankan)是使用最廣的通用語,另有十幾種以上的國家語言(富拉語、桑海語、索寧克語、塔馬謝克語等)。
  • 文化與趣聞:傑內大清真寺(Great Mosque of Djenné)是世界上最大的泥磚建築,每年全社區一起參與抹泥節進行重新修繕;廷巴克圖的古老手抄本、多貢人的懸崖村落,以及阿里·法爾卡·杜爾(Ali Farka Touré)、薩利夫·凱塔(Salif Keita)等音樂人都舉世聞名。2025 年馬利與布吉納法索、尼日一同退出西非國家經濟共同體(ECOWAS),組成薩赫爾國家聯盟。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「Press F to Pay Respects」(按 F 致敬)

  • 名稱:「Press F to Pay Respects」(也稱「F」、「F in the chat」)。
  • 起源與歷史:出自 2014 年的第一人稱射擊遊戲《決勝時刻:先進戰爭》(Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare,Sledgehammer Games 開發)。遊戲中有一場葬禮,玩家走向棺木時畫面會出現提示「Press [按鍵] to pay respects」(在 PC 上是 F 鍵)。玩家嘲笑遊戲把哀悼變成按一個鍵。
  • 如何流行:這一幕的影片在 2014 年底與 2015 年於 YouTube 和 Reddit 流傳,笑話隨後進入遊戲實況、Twitch 與 Discord 聊天室:當實況主或角色遇到死亡、失敗或壞消息時,觀眾就在聊天室打「F」。
  • 意義與使用情境:表達諷刺或真心同情的簡寫:「F」代表「向你致敬」。用在遊戲角色死亡、考試失利、手機掉了、產品停產或名人遇到壞消息時,通常帶著半開玩笑的語氣。
  • 變化與演變:「F in the chat」、實況裡一次洗出一大串「F」、其他遊戲與迷因把「Press F」提示加進去當內梗,還有拉長的「FFFF」。這個說法也以翻譯的形式傳到中文與其他語言社群。
  • 現況:是遊戲與實況文化中的長壽常用語,至今仍天天出現在聊天室,但已不是新潮流。

7) 電影 / 表演藝術介紹 — 布萊希特與史詩劇場(柏林劇團)

  • 名稱與關鍵人物:史詩劇場(Episches Theater,Epic Theatre),由德國劇作家與導演貝托爾特·布萊希特(Bertolt Brecht,1898–1956)發展,合作者有作曲家庫爾特·魏爾(Kurt Weill)、演員暨劇團領導人海倫娜·威格爾(Helene Weigel),早期影響來自導演艾爾文·皮斯卡托(Erwin Piscator)。其核心概念是「疏離效果」(Verfremdungseffekt,V 效果)。
  • 年代與背景:它從 1920 年代威瑪時期的柏林長出來,當時身為馬克思主義者的布萊希特認為傳統劇場只是用情緒麻醉觀眾。1933 年納粹上台後他流亡,在丹麥、瑞典、芬蘭與美國等地寫出主要劇作,1948–49 年回到東柏林,與威格爾於 1949 年創立柏林劇團(Berliner Ensemble),1954 年劇團進駐席夫鮑爾達姆劇院(Theater am Schiffbauerdamm)。
  • 內容概述(主要作品):《三便士歌劇》(1928,與魏爾合作,改編自約翰·蓋伊的《乞丐歌劇》,含〈刀客麥基〉);《大膽媽媽和她的孩子們》(1939 年寫成,背景是三十年戰爭,講一個靠戰爭做生意的隨軍商販,最後戰爭奪走了她的孩子);《伽利略傳》、《四川好人》與《高加索灰闌記》。
  • 藝術風格與技法:傳統劇場(布萊希特稱之為「亞里斯多德式」戲劇)讓觀眾忘了自己在看戲,跟著主角哭;史詩劇場則要讓觀眾保持思考。技法包括:演員「展示」角色而不是變成角色、直接對觀眾說話、旁白與標語牌、打斷劇情的歌曲、外露的燈光與簡單布景、有標題的片段式場景,以及「姿態」(Gestus,一個顯示社會關係的動作或態度)。目的是讓熟悉的社會狀況變得陌生,才能被質疑與改變。
  • 文化與歷史意義:它把劇場重新定義為政治思考的場所,而不只是情緒的場所。1935 年布萊希特在莫斯科看了京劇名伶梅蘭芳的演出,後來寫下談中國戲曲表演中疏離效果的文章,是西方劇場借鑑中國舞台慣例的早期例子。
  • 影響與傳承:對戰後戲劇與電影影響深遠:尚盧·高達、法斯賓達,以及拉斯·馮·提爾的《厄夜變奏曲》(Dogville,粉筆線條的簡單布景、旁白)都使用布萊希特式手法,波瓦的「被壓迫者劇場」與東尼·庫許納等劇作家也受影響。「布萊希特式」與「疏離」已成為戲劇與電影評論的標準用語。
  • 現況與哪裡可以接觸:柏林劇團至今仍在柏林演出,布萊希特的劇作在世界各地上演;許多劇作也有出版劇本、錄音與部分拍成影像的製作。最新節目請查劇團官方網站,我今天沒有查證。
  • 說明:所有細節皆憑一般知識與記憶寫成,今天未對照來源查證。

8) 看片方法 — 第 85 課:轉場即標點符號(硬切、淡出入、疊化)

它是什麼:每部電影都得從一個鏡頭走到下一個鏡頭,而怎麼走是一種選擇。硬切是普通的句點;淡出到黑或從黑淡入是更重的分段(可以代表結束、死亡、時間跳躍或故事開始);疊化(一個畫面融進另一個)暗示時間流逝或兩件事之間的連結。轉場是電影文法的一部分,導演使用不尋常的轉場,就是在告訴你某件事。

如何分析:(1) 先看預設值:大多數電影幾乎都用硬切,所以任何淡入淡出或疊化都是「有標記」的。(2) 對每個不尋常的轉場問:它隔開了什麼(時間、地點、心理狀態)、持續多久;緩慢的淡入像深呼吸或莊嚴的停頓。(3) 問這個轉場說了硬切說不出的什麼。(4) 看電影第一個與最後一個轉場如何框住整個故事,並留意一部滿是淡入淡出的片子,是否在關鍵時刻突然改用硬切(或相反)。

實例分析 —《教父》(The Godfather,1972,導演法蘭西斯·福特·柯波拉,攝影戈登·威利斯)開場:電影從一片黑畫面開始,一個男人的聲音說:「I believe in America。」之後畫面才淡入,出現殯儀館老闆博納塞拉(Bonasera)向柯里昂教父求取正義的特寫,接著鏡頭緩緩後拉,露出教父的背影。看這個轉場做了什麼:聲音比畫面先到,所以我們的位置像是在聽一場告解;緩慢從黑暗中浮現,呼應全片的低調打光與「權力在陰影中運作」的主題。再對比接下來硬切到戶外陽光下的婚禮:封閉、昏暗的辦公室與明亮的公開派對形成對比,顯示這個家族的兩張臉。如果開場用硬切,我們看到的只是一個男人在說話;淡入讓我們覺得自己被帶進一個祕密。(依一般知識描述,今天未對照影片查證。)

試試看:下次看電影時,數出前五個轉場並為每一個命名(硬切、淡入淡出、疊化、劃接),然後問導演為什麼選它。第一次看到淡入淡出或疊化時,寫下它隔開了什麼。
