3) Spanish Lesson — Day 6 (10 min, builds on Day 5)

Days 1–5 covered greetings/self-intro, ¿Cómo estás?, farewells/courtesy, and ¿De dónde eres? (ser + origin). Today's new concept: the numbers 0-10, plus the fixed idiom for stating age.

  • cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez (0–10)
  • ¿Cuántos años tienes? (KWAHN-tohs AHN-yohs TYEH-nehs) — How old are you?
  • Tengo diez años. (TEN-go dyess AHN-yohs) — I'm ten years old.

Grammar note: Spanish states age with tener (to have) + number + años ("years") — literally "I have ten years" — not with ser or estar.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 6: 訟 (Sòng) — "Conflict" (Heaven over Water). Heaven's motion rises upward while water flows downward — two forces moving in opposite directions, the image of irreconcilable positions leading to dispute. Represents conflict and the danger of pursuing a grievance too far, even when one is in the right. The classical judgment cautions against carrying a dispute through to its bitter end — seeking mediation and compromise early brings good fortune, while stubbornly pressing a conflict to a final "victory" brings continued misfortune. The wise plan carefully at the outset of any undertaking to avoid disputes arising in the first place.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第六天(10 分鐘,銜接第五天內容)

第一到第五天學了問候自我介紹、¿Cómo estás?、道別與禮貌用語、以及 ¿De dónde eres?(ser 與國籍)。今天的新概念是:數字 0 到 10,以及表達年齡的固定句型。

  • cero、uno、dos、tres、cuatro、cinco、seis、siete、ocho、nueve、diez(0–10)
  • ¿Cuántos años tienes?(光多斯 阿紐斯 提誒內斯)—— 你幾歲?
  • Tengo diez años.(天勾 蒂耶斯 阿紐斯)—— 我十歲。

文法重點:西班牙文表達年齡用 tener(擁有)+ 數字 + años(歲)這個固定句型——字面「我擁有十年」——不是用 ser 或 estar。
