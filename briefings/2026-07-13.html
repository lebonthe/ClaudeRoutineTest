3) Spanish Lesson — Day 2 (10 min, complete beginner)

Building on Day 1 (greetings, "¿Cómo te llamas?" / "Me llamo...", Mucho gusto), today's new concept is asking and answering "How are you?" using the verb estar (temporary state) — as opposed to ser (permanent identity):

  • ¿Cómo estás? (KOH-moh ess-TAHS) — How are you?
  • Estoy muy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú? (ess-TOY mwee byen, GRAH-syahs. ee too?) — I'm very well, thanks. And you?
  • Muy bien también. ¡Hasta luego! (mwee byen tahm-byén. AHS-tah LWEH-goh!) — Very well too. See you later!

Key point: estar is used because how you feel is temporary — saying "Eres bien" (using ser) would sound like a statement about your permanent nature, not your current mood.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 2: 坤 (Kūn) — "The Receptive" (Earth over Earth, all six lines yin). The natural complement to Hexagram 1 (乾, The Creative): where Qián is pure creative, initiating yang force, Kūn is pure receptive, nurturing yin force — the power of the earth that supports and completes what heaven initiates.

Interpretation: This is a hexagram of yielding strength, not weakness — devotion, adaptability, and quiet endurance carry great power when aligned with the right leader or moment. The classical judgment favors "the firmness of a mare": gentle but tireless. It cautions that one who tries to lead/initiate here goes astray, while one who follows and supports finds guidance and lasting good fortune. A favorable time to nurture, cooperate, and build a strong foundation rather than force new initiatives.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第二天(10 分鐘,完全新手)

延續第一天內容(問候語、「¿Cómo te llamas?」/「Me llamo...」、Mucho gusto),今天的新概念是用動詞 estar(暫時狀態)來問候並回答「你好嗎」——與表示永久身份的 ser 不同:

  • ¿Cómo estás?(摳摸 耶斯大斯?)—— 你好嗎?
  • Estoy muy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?(耶斯多伊 母伊 比恩,格拉西亞斯。伊 兔?)—— 我很好,謝謝。你呢?
  • Muy bien también. ¡Hasta luego!(母伊 比恩 談比恩。阿斯大 魯耶溝!)—— 我也很好。回頭見!

重點:之所以用 estar,是因為心情、狀態是暫時的;如果用 ser 說成「Eres bien」,聽起來會像在描述一個人永久不變的本質,而不是當下的感受。

4) 易經卦象

第 2 卦:坤卦(Kūn)——「地」/ The Receptive(坤上坤下,六爻皆為陰爻)。是第 1 卦(乾,The Creative)的天然對應卦:乾為純陽、代表創造與開創的力量;坤則為純陰、代表承載與滋養的力量——是大地承接、成就天所開創之事的力量。

解讀: 這一卦所講的柔順,並非軟弱,而是一種內斂而強大的力量——順從、包容、堅忍不拔,若能配合對的時機或對的領導者,便能發揮極大的作用。卦辭以「牝馬之貞」為喻:溫和卻堅韌不懈。此卦提醒:此時若強行主導、率先行動,容易迷失方向;反之,若能順勢而為、輔佐配合,則能得到指引,獲得長久的吉祥。這是適合滋養根基、與人合作、穩固基礎,而非躁進開創新局的時機。
