3) Spanish Lesson — Day 11 (10 min, builds on Day 10)

Days 1–10 covered greetings, ¿Cómo estás?, farewells, ¿De dónde eres?, numbers/age, days of the week, months, telling time, and family + possessives (mi/tu/su). Today's new concept: the present tense of regular -ar verbs — the single biggest, most useful pattern in Spanish grammar, covering verbs like hablar (to speak), trabajar (to work), and estudiar (to study).

  • ¿Dónde trabajas? (DOHN-deh trah-BAH-hahs) — Where do you work?
  • Trabajo en Taipéi. También estudio español. (trah-BAH-hoh ehn tai-PEI. tahm-byEHN ehs-TOO-dyoh ehs-pahn-YOHL) — I work in Taipei. I'm also studying Spanish.
  • Mi hermana habla español muy bien. (mee ehr-MAH-nah AH-blah ehs-pahn-YOHL mwee byehn) — My sister speaks Spanish very well.

Grammar note: drop the -ar ending to get the stem (habl-, trabaj-, estudi-), then add -o (yo), -as (tú), or -a (él/ella) — the same subject-marking-via-ending pattern seen with estar back on Day 2.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 11: 泰 (Tài) — "Peace" (Earth over Heaven). Unusually, earth sits above heaven here — but rather than being a contradiction, this represents heaven's energy rising and earth's energy descending to meet it, so the two interpenetrate fully and communicate freely. This is the image of true harmony between high and low, ruler and subject, heaven and earth: a time of peace, prosperity, and free exchange, when what is small (weak, base, incorrect) retreats and what is great (strong, noble, correct) advances. The counsel: in a time of peace and abundance, do not become complacent — cultivate the conditions of harmony deliberately, keep communication open between those above and those below, and remain mindful that ease can turn to difficulty if vigilance lapses.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Vatican City

  • Location: A sovereign city-state entirely enclosed within the city of Rome, Italy — the world's only country contained wholly inside another city.
  • Territory: About 0.49 km², making it by far the smallest sovereign state on Earth — roughly the size of a large golf course, and about 73,000 times smaller than Taiwan in land area.
  • Population: Only around 800 people hold Vatican citizenship, nearly all clergy, Swiss Guard members, or Holy See officials — citizenship is tied to office and residence rather than birthright, so there is essentially no ordinary civilian population.
  • Religion: The Vatican is the spiritual and administrative center of the worldwide Roman Catholic Church — the religion is effectively the state's entire reason for existing.
  • Economy: Not a conventional market economy — funded through worldwide Catholic donations (including the annual "Peter's Pence" collection), investment income, and revenue from the Vatican Museums, publishing, and postage stamps; there is no taxation or GDP in the usual sense.
  • History: The Pope ruled the much larger Papal States for over a thousand years until Italian unification absorbed them in 1870, leaving the papacy without sovereign territory for nearly six decades (the "Roman Question"). Vatican City as a fully independent sovereign state was created by the 1929 Lateran Treaty between the Holy See and the Kingdom of Italy under Mussolini, resolving that dispute.
  • Current leadership: Pope Leo XIV, elected May 8, 2025 — the first pope born in the United States (Chicago), serving as absolute monarch of Vatican City and spiritual leader of over a billion Catholics worldwide.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, americamagazine.org
  • Language: Italian is used for day-to-day administration, while Latin remains the Vatican's official language for law and formal ecclesiastical documents.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Ohio" / "Only in Ohio"

  • Name: The "Ohio" meme, often phrased as "Only in Ohio."
  • Origin/history: Building on a long-running internet joke tradition portraying the US state of Ohio as bizarre, cursed, or dimension-bending (in the same lineage as "Florida Man" humor), the meme crystallized into its modern form on TikTok and Twitter/X around 2023, frequently paired with surreal AI-generated or absurdist imagery and captions claiming strange events "only" happen there.
  • How it became popular: It spread rapidly through TikTok's short-form video format as a flexible caption/reaction template, merging with the broader wave of Gen Alpha "brainrot" internet humor (alongside Skibidi Toilet, "rizz," and "gyatt") that dominated youth internet culture from 2023 onward, becoming an easy punchline for anything nonsensical regardless of actual connection to Ohio.
  • Meaning/usage: Used as a catch-all descriptor for anything weird, cursed, embarrassing, or inexplicable — "that's so Ohio" or "get out, Ohio" function as generic reactions to absurdity, similar in grammatical role to how "skibidi" is used, and often deployed with self-aware irony rather than literal geographic mockery.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned the "Ohio Final Boss" image format (an exaggeratedly monstrous character supposedly native to the state), frequent crossovers with other brainrot memes and formats, and endless copycat "state" memes that never achieved the same traction.
  • Current status: Like Skibidi Toilet, the Ohio meme has been widely commercialized and adopted by brands/marketers trying to reach Gen Z audiences, which has made parts of the core youth audience consider it "cringe" or played out — but the underlying "so Ohio" usage pattern persists as an entrenched piece of mid-2020s internet slang.

5) 國家/地區介紹 — 梵蒂岡

  • 位置: 完全被包圍在義大利羅馬市之內的主權城邦國家——是全世界唯一整個國土都位於另一座城市內部的國家。
  • 領土面積: 約 0.49 平方公里,是全世界面積最小的主權國家,大約只有一座大型高爾夫球場那麼大,面積約為台灣的七萬三千分之一。
  • 人口: 僅約 800 人持有梵蒂岡國籍,幾乎全為神職人員、瑞士衛隊成員或教廷官員——國籍與職務、居住身分綁定,而非出生即取得,因此實質上沒有一般平民人口。
  • 宗教: 梵蒂岡是全球天主教會的精神與行政中心——宗教本身幾乎就是這個國家存在的全部理由。
  • 經濟: 並非傳統市場經濟型態——資金來源包括全球天主教徒的捐款(如每年的「伯多祿獻金」)、投資收益,以及梵蒂岡博物館、出版品與郵票的收入;沒有一般意義上的課稅或 GDP 概念。
  • 歷史: 教宗曾統治遠比今日梵蒂岡龐大的「教宗國」長達千年以上,直到 1870 年義大利統一運動將其併吞,教廷因而近 60 年間沒有主權領土(史稱「羅馬問題」)。1929 年,教廷與墨索里尼領導的義大利王國簽署《拉特朗條約》,解決此一爭議,梵蒂岡城國從此成為完全獨立的主權國家。
  • 現任領導人: 教宗良十四世(Pope Leo XIV),2025 年 5 月 8 日當選——首位出生於美國(芝加哥)的教宗,身兼梵蒂岡城國的絕對君主與全球逾十億天主教徒的精神領袖。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、americamagazine.org
  • 語言: 日常行政使用義大利語;拉丁文則仍是梵蒂岡法律與正式教會文件所使用的官方語言。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「Ohio」/「Only in Ohio」

  • 名稱: 「Ohio」迷因,常見用語為「Only in Ohio」(只有在俄亥俄州才會發生)。
  • 起源/歷史: 這個迷因延續了長期以來把美國俄亥俄州描繪成怪異、被詛咒、彷彿扭曲異次元的網路笑話傳統(與「Florida Man」系列笑話系出同源),並在 2023 年前後於 TikTok 與 Twitter/X 上定型為現今的樣貌,經常搭配超現實的 AI 生成圖像或荒誕文案,聲稱某些怪事「只有在俄亥俄州」才會發生。
  • 如何爆紅: 這個迷因作為彈性極高的字幕/反應模板,透過 TikTok 短影音格式迅速擴散,並與 2023 年後席捲年輕世代網路文化的「腦腐(brainrot)」風潮(與 Skibidi Toilet、「rizz」、「gyatt」等詞彙同屬一個現象)相互交融,成為形容任何無厘頭事物的簡便哏,早已與俄亥俄州本身是否真的有關聯脫鉤。
  • 意義與使用情境: 作為形容「怪異、被詛咒、尷尬、莫名其妙」事物的萬用詞——「that's so Ohio」或「get out, Ohio」被用來對荒謬事物做出反應,文法角色與「skibidi」相似,使用時通常帶有自嘲、反諷意味,而非真的在嘲笑該州本身。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出「Ohio Final Boss」(誇張、怪物化、聲稱是該州「原生種」的角色)圖像格式,經常與其他腦腐迷因、格式互相混搭,也催生大量模仿其他「州」的迷因,但都未能複製同等聲量。
  • 目前現況: 與 Skibidi Toilet 類似,Ohio 迷因已被廣泛商業化、被品牌行銷用來討好 Z 世代客群,這讓部分核心年輕族群開始覺得它「土」、過氣;但「so Ohio」這種用法模式,已成為 2020 年代中期網路用語根深蒂固的一部分,持續存在。
