3) Spanish Lesson — Day 39 (10 min, completes the "haber + participle" compound-tense family)

Days 21, 22, and 38 built three corners of the "haber + past participle" compound-tense family (present perfect, pluperfect, and conditional perfect). Today delivers the fourth and final corner: the future perfect (futuro perfecto), formed from haber's own future-simple stem (habr-, one of Day 23's twelve irregular future stems) plus Day 21's past participle — used both to mark an action that will already be finished by a given future point, and to speculate about something that has probably just happened.

  • Para las ocho, ya habré terminado el informe. — By eight o'clock, I will have already finished the report.
  • Cuando llegues, habremos preparado la cena. — By the time you arrive, we will have prepared dinner.
  • ¿Habrás llamado a tus padres antes del viaje? — Will you have called your parents before the trip?

Grammar note: habré/habrás/habrá/habremos/habrán + past participle marks either (1) a future-anterior action — completed before a specific future reference point, often paired with para + time or with a Day-32 cuando + present-subjunctive clause — or (2) a conjecture about something already completed (¿Habrá llegado ya? — "He's probably already arrived?"), extending the conjecture pattern seen in Day 23's future simple (present conjecture) and Day 24's conditional simple (past conjecture) one step further into completed actions. This closes out the full haber-compound square: present perfect (Day 21) / pluperfect (Day 22) on the indicative side, conditional perfect (Day 38) / future perfect (Day 39, today) on the "later tense" side.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 39: 蹇 (Jiǎn) — "Obstruction" (Limping) (Water above, Mountain below — Kǎn over Gèn). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary pivots directly off yesterday's Hexagram 38: "乖必有難,故受之以蹇;蹇者,難也。" ("Estrangement must inevitably produce difficulty; hence Kuí [Opposition] is followed by Jiǎn [Obstruction]. Jiǎn means difficulty.") Where Hexagram 38 diagnosed the friction that arises once unity permits difference, Hexagram 39 shows what that friction concretely becomes: a hard, physical-feeling obstacle blocking forward movement. The Judgment reads: "蹇,利西南,不利東北。利見大人,貞吉。" ("Obstruction: favorable in the southwest, unfavorable in the northeast. It is favorable to see a great person; steadfastness brings good fortune.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) explains the image through the trigrams' positions: "蹇,難也,險在前也。見險而能止,知矣哉!蹇,利西南,往得中也;不利東北,其道窮也。利見大人,往有功也;當位貞吉,以正邦也。蹇之時用大矣哉!" ("Jiǎn means difficulty — danger lies ahead [Kǎn, the Abysmal/Water, sits above, blocking the way forward]. To see danger and be able to stop is true wisdom! 'Favorable in the southwest' means that going there attains the middle position [a level, accessible direction]; 'unfavorable in the northeast' means that the way there is exhausted [a direction of further obstruction]. 'Favorable to see a great person' means that going forward will achieve merit; occupying one's rightful position with steadfastness brings good fortune, thereby setting the state in order. Great indeed is the function of the time of Obstruction!") The Image commentary draws the lesson for conduct: "山上有水,蹇,君子以反身修德。" ("Water above the mountain: this is the image of Obstruction. Thus the superior person turns inward to cultivate their own character.") Unlike hexagrams that counsel forcing a path through difficulty, Jiǎn's core teaching is the wisdom of recognizing an obstacle honestly and choosing not to advance blindly into it — the mountain (Gèn) is already still and immovable beneath the danger (Kǎn) pooling above it, modeling the correct response: stop, do not panic, and use the enforced pause to look inward and strengthen one's own virtue rather than waste effort pushing against terrain that cannot yet be crossed. Read together with Hexagram 38, the pair traces cause and effect: difference matured into friction (38), and friction, left unaddressed, hardens into a real obstacle that demands patience and self-cultivation rather than force (39).

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Bangladesh (孟加拉)

  • Location: South Asia, occupying the vast, low-lying Ganges-Brahmaputra-Meghna river delta; bordered almost entirely by India (to the west, north, and east) with a short border with Myanmar in the southeast, and the Bay of Bengal to the south.
  • Territory: About 148,460 km² — roughly 4.1 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), yet one of the most densely populated large countries on Earth given its population.
  • Population: Roughly 175 million (2026 estimate), making it the world's 8th most populous country, packed into an area smaller than the US state of Iowa — one of the highest population densities of any sizable nation.
  • Religion: Predominantly Muslim (around 91%, mostly Sunni), with a significant Hindu minority (about 8%) and small Buddhist and Christian communities.
  • Economy: Historically one of the world's fastest-growing economies through the 2010s-early 2020s, powered above all by its ready-made garment (RMG) export industry — the world's second-largest garment exporter after China, supplying major Western retailers. Growth slowed and volatility rose around the 2024 political upheaval; the country remains highly exposed to climate risk (cyclones, river flooding, and sea-level rise across its delta geography threaten to displace millions in coming decades) and depends heavily on remittances from its large overseas migrant-worker population in the Gulf states and elsewhere.
  • History: Part of British India until the 1947 partition created it as East Pakistan; decades of political and economic marginalization by West Pakistan culminated in the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War, in which East Pakistan won independence (with Indian military support) under founding leader Sheikh Mujibur Rahman. His daughter, Sheikh Hasina, dominated politics for roughly 15 years (2009-2024) until a student-led uprising (the "Monsoon Revolution") forced her from power and into exile in August 2024; Nobel laureate Muhammad Yunus then led an interim government from August 2024 until February 2026, overseeing Bangladesh's first post-uprising parliamentary election.
  • Current leader: Prime Minister Tarique Rahman, leader of the Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP), was sworn in on February 17, 2026, after the BNP's landslide win (210 of 300 directly elected seats) in the February 12, 2026 election — the first male prime minister in 35 years, ending what had been dubbed the "two begums" era of Sheikh Hasina and Khaleda Zia (Rahman's mother). Mohammed Shahabuddin continues as the largely ceremonial President.
  • Language: Bengali (Bangla) is the official and national language, one of the world's most-spoken languages; English is widely used in higher education, business, and government administration.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, aljazeera.com

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Charlie Bit My Finger (查理咬我的手指)

  • Name: "Charlie Bit My Finger – Again!" (often shortened to just "Charlie Bit My Finger" or "Charlie Bit Me").
  • Origin/history: A home video uploaded to YouTube on May 22, 2007, by British father Howard Davies-Carr, showing his young sons Harry (about 3 years old) holding his baby brother Charlie's hand near his mouth; Charlie playfully bites down on Harry's finger, prompting Harry's now-legendary indignant cry "Charlie bit me!... and that really hurt, Charlie" while a mischievously grinning Charlie looks unrepentant. It was originally uploaded privately, simply so a family member in the US could see the boys.
  • How it became popular: The video escaped its intended private audience and spread organically across the still-young YouTube platform, becoming one of the first ordinary home videos (rather than a professionally made clip) to go massively viral; it eventually accumulated well over 880 million views, at one point ranking among the most-watched videos in YouTube's entire history and, for a long stretch, the most-viewed non-music-video on the platform.
  • Meaning/usage: Used as a shorthand reference point for unstaged, wholesome, purely-by-accident viral fame — the canonical example that genuine, unedited home footage of ordinary family life could outperform professionally produced content in the platform's early "Web 2.0" era; often invoked in conversations about internet virality itself as the go-to historical touchstone.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned countless remixes (including an Auto-Tuned song version), parody reaction and reenactment videos, and extensive media coverage of the Davies-Carr family; most notably, the family sold the original video as an NFT in a 2021 charity auction for roughly $760,999 and took the original down from YouTube as part of that sale, triggering a wave of backlash and a broader public debate over who "owns" a viral video and what NFT sales mean for shared internet culture — the video was later restored to YouTube.
  • Current status: The original video remains viewable on YouTube today; Harry and Charlie, now grown, have given periodic retrospective interviews around the video's anniversaries, and it continues to be cited as a foundational case study in discussions of early viral-video culture and, more recently, of internet-content ownership economics.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, knowyourmeme.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — The Seventh Seal (第七封印, Det sjunde inseglet, 1957, dir. Ingmar Bergman)

  • Name/key figures: The Seventh Seal (1957), written and directed by Ingmar Bergman; starring Max von Sydow as the knight Antonius Block, Bengt Ekerot as Death, and Gunnar Björnstrand as the knight's cynical squire Jöns; cinematography by Gunnar Fischer.
  • Era/background: Made in Sweden on a modest budget over roughly 35 days of shooting, part of the wave of 1950s European arthouse cinema that established Bergman's international reputation; set in medieval Sweden during a devastating outbreak of the Black Death.
  • Plot/content overview: A knight, Antonius Block, returns from the Crusades disillusioned and spiritually exhausted, only to be confronted on a desolate beach by Death itself, personified as a pale, black-robed figure. Block challenges Death to a game of chess, buying himself time to search for some proof of God's existence or life's meaning before the game — and his life — must end; his journey home, accompanied by his squire Jöns and a traveling troupe of actors (Jof, Mia, and their infant son), becomes an extended meditation on faith, doubt, and mortality as plague ravages the countryside around them.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Stark, high-contrast black-and-white cinematography built around some of cinema's most iconic single images — the chess match against Death on an empty shoreline, and the closing "Dance of Death" silhouette of the doomed characters led away across a hilltop; a spare, allegorical narrative structure that externalizes internal spiritual crisis as literal encounters with personified Death, drawing on medieval morality-play and danse macabre traditions.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Won the Jury Prize (Prix du Jury) at the 1957 Cannes Film Festival and is widely regarded as one of the defining works of European arthouse cinema and existentialist filmmaking, its themes shaped in part by Bergman's own lifelong wrestling with the religious doubt instilled by his strict Lutheran-pastor father.
  • Influence and legacy: The "chess with Death" image has become one of the most referenced and parodied in all of film — from Woody Allen's Love and Death to Bill & Ted's Bogus Journey to countless cartoons and sketch-comedy send-ups — cementing the film's imagery in mainstream pop culture far beyond arthouse audiences; it remains a fixture of virtually every "greatest films ever made" list.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Restored and distributed by the Criterion Collection on Blu-ray and its streaming Criterion Channel, and periodically screened at film festivals and retrospectives worldwide.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, criterion.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Cross-Cutting / Parallel Editing for Simultaneity (Lesson 25)

Cross-cutting (parallel editing) intercuts between two or more lines of action happening at the same time in different places, using the alternation itself — rather than the juxtaposition of unrelated ideas (Day 2's Soviet montage) or a scrambled chronology (Day 6's non-linear narrative structure) — to build meaning. Four things to check: whether the intercut lines are genuinely simultaneous or only implied to be; whether the rhythm of alternation accelerates as the sequence builds, using shorter and shorter segments to raise tension; whether the crosscutting produces ironic contrast (pairing a sacred or celebratory line of action against a violent or transgressive one) or straightforward suspense (two lines of action converging toward a single outcome); and whether continuous music or sound bridges the separate locations into one unified emotional experience, since without that bridge the "simultaneity" can read as arbitrary alternation rather than one connected event.

Worked example: The Godfather (1972, dir. Francis Ford Coppola) — the baptism sequence near the film's end. Michael Corleone stands as godfather at his nephew's christening, solemnly reciting the Latin renunciation of Satan, while the film intercuts to the coordinated, simultaneous assassinations of the heads of the rival Five Families that Michael has secretly ordered — a bound henchman garroted, a boss shot in an elevator, another gunned down in a hotel massage chair. Nino Rota's church organ score plays continuously across every cut, binding the sacred ritual and the profane violence into a single unbroken emotional unit rather than two separate scenes stitched together. The irony lands entirely through the editing structure itself — Michael literally renouncing Satan in the same breath as he consummates his own turn toward damnation as the new Don — without a single line of dialogue needing to state the connection.

3)西班牙文課程——第 39 天(10 分鐘,補完「haber + 過去分詞」複合時態家族)

第 21、22、38 天已經建立起「haber + 過去分詞」複合時態家族的三個角落(現在完成式、過去完成式、條件完成式)。今天要教的是第四個、也是最後一個角落:未來完成式(futuro perfecto)——由 haber 自身的未來簡單式詞幹(habr-,第 23 天教過的十二個不規則未來詞幹之一)加上第 21 天教過的過去分詞組成,可以用來標記「到未來某個時間點為止,某個動作已經完成」,也可以用來推測一件可能剛剛發生完的事。

  • Para las ocho, ya habré terminado el informe. —— 到八點的時候,我應該已經把報告寫完了。
  • Cuando llegues, habremos preparado la cena. —— 等你到的時候,我們應該已經把晚餐準備好了。
  • ¿Habrás llamado a tus padres antes del viaje? —— 你出發前會先打電話給你父母嗎(你應該會先打了吧)?

文法筆記:habré/habrás/habrá/habremos/habrán + 過去分詞,可以表達兩種用法:(1)「未來完成」動作——在某個未來參照時間點之前已經完成,常搭配 para + 時間點,或第 32 天教過的 cuando + 虛擬式現在式子句;(2)對一件「可能已經完成」的事表達推測(¿Habrá llegado ya? ——「他現在應該已經到了吧?」),延續第 23 天未來簡單式表達「對現在的推測」、第 24 天條件式簡單式表達「對過去的推測」這條脈絡,再往前推進一步、用來推測已完成的動作。這也補完了整組 haber 複合時態:直陳式一側的現在完成式(第 21 天)/過去完成式(第 22 天),以及「較晚時態」一側的條件完成式(第 38 天)/未來完成式(第 39 天,今天)。

4)易經卦象

第 39 卦:蹇 (Jiǎn) ——「阻難」(跛足難行)(水在上,山在下——坎上艮下)。《序卦傳》從昨天的第 38 卦直接接續下來:「乖必有難,故受之以蹇;蹇者,難也。」(乖離、疏離發展到極致,必然產生困難;因此睽卦之後接的是蹇卦。蹇,就是困難的意思。)如果說第 38 卦診斷的是「差異一旦被允許存在,便會產生摩擦」這件事,那麼第 39 卦所呈現的,正是這股摩擦具體化之後的樣子:一個堅實、彷彿可以觸摸到的障礙,擋住了前進的道路。卦辭寫道:「蹇,利西南,不利東北。利見大人,貞吉。」(蹇卦:往西南方向有利,往東北方向不利。適宜求見德高望重之人,守持正道則吉利。)《彖傳》透過卦象所處的位置來解釋:「蹇,難也,險在前也。見險而能止,知矣哉!蹇,利西南,往得中也;不利東北,其道窮也。利見大人,往有功也;當位貞吉,以正邦也。蹇之時用大矣哉!」(蹇,就是困難的意思——危險就在前方〔坎,代表險難的水,居於卦象上方,擋住了前進之路〕。見到危險而能夠停下腳步,這才是真正的智慧啊!「往西南方向有利」,是因為往那個方向前進能夠獲得中正之位〔一個平坦、可以到達的方向〕;「往東北方向不利」,是因為往那個方向去,道路已經窮盡〔是一個會帶來更多阻礙的方向〕。「適宜求見德高望重之人」,是因為前去求見必能有所成就;身處正位並守持正道,便能帶來吉利,藉此使國家安定下來。蹇卦所象徵的這個時機與作用,實在是太重大了!)《象傳》則點出實際行事的教訓:「山上有水,蹇,君子以反身修德。」(山的上方有水,這就是蹇卦的卦象。因此君子遇到阻難時,會轉而向內反省,修養自身的品德。)蹇卦的核心教誨,並不是教人如何強行突破困難,而是教人誠實辨認出障礙、並選擇不盲目向前硬闖的智慧——山(艮)本身安穩不動,靜靜承受著上方匯聚而成的危險(坎),正好示範了正確的應對之道:停下來,不要驚慌,並利用這個被迫暫停的時機向內看,修養自身的德行,而不是把力氣浪費在硬闖一片目前還無法穿越的地形上。與第 38 卦合觀,這兩卦勾勒出一條因果鏈:差異成熟後化為摩擦(第 38 卦),而摩擦若未被妥善處理,便會硬化成一個真正的障礙,需要耐心與自我修養來面對,而不是一味用力硬拚(第 39 卦)。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——孟加拉(Bangladesh)

  • 位置:位於南亞,坐落在廣闊、地勢低平的恆河—布拉馬普特拉河—梅克納河三角洲上;幾乎完全被印度環繞(西、北、東三面),東南方與緬甸有一小段邊界,南臨孟加拉灣。
  • 領土:約 148,460 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 4.1 倍大,但以其人口規模而言,卻是全球人口密度最高的大型國家之一。
  • 人口:約 1.75 億人(2026 年估計),是全世界人口第 8 多的國家,擠在一塊比美國愛荷華州還小的土地上——是所有大型國家中人口密度數一數二高的國家。
  • 宗教:以伊斯蘭教為主(約 91%,多數為遜尼派),另有相當規模的印度教少數族群(約 8%),以及少量佛教與基督教社群。
  • 經濟:從 2010 年代到 2020 年代初期,曾是全球經濟成長最快的國家之一,主要動力來自成衣出口(RMG)產業——孟加拉是全球第二大成衣出口國(僅次於中國),供貨給多家西方大型零售商。2024 年政局動盪前後,經濟成長趨緩、波動加劇;該國同時高度暴露於氣候風險之下(氣旋、河川氾濫,以及三角洲地形所面臨的海平面上升威脅,未來數十年可能迫使數百萬人遷離),並高度依賴其龐大海外移工(主要在海灣國家等地工作)寄回的僑匯收入。
  • 歷史:1947 年印巴分治前為英屬印度的一部分,分治後成為「東巴基斯坦」;數十年來遭西巴基斯坦在政治與經濟上邊緣化,終於在 1971 年爆發孟加拉獨立戰爭,東巴基斯坦在印度軍事支援下贏得獨立,由開國領袖穆吉布爾·拉赫曼(Sheikh Mujibur Rahman)領導建國。他的女兒謝赫·哈西娜(Sheikh Hasina)主導孟加拉政壇長達約 15 年(2009-2024),直到 2024 年 8 月一場由學生主導的起義(「季風革命」)迫使她下台並流亡海外;隨後由諾貝爾獎得主穆罕默德·尤努斯(Muhammad Yunus)領導看守政府,自 2024 年 8 月執政至 2026 年 2 月,期間監督了起義後的第一次國會選舉。
  • 現任領導人:孟加拉民族主義黨(BNP)黨魁塔里克·拉赫曼(Tarique Rahman)於 2026 年 2 月 17 日宣誓就任總理,此前該黨在 2026 年 2 月 12 日的選舉中取得壓倒性勝利(在 300 個直選席次中拿下 210 席)——他是 35 年來首位男性總理,結束了外界稱為謝赫·哈西娜與卡莉達·齊亞(Khaleda Zia,拉赫曼之母)「兩位女士(begum)」輪政的時代。穆罕默德·沙哈布丁(Mohammed Shahabuddin)則繼續擔任職權多屬象徵性的總統。
  • 語言:孟加拉語(Bangla)為官方與國家語言,是全球使用人數最多的語言之一;英語則廣泛使用於高等教育、商業與政府行政事務。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、aljazeera.com

6)網路迷因介紹——查理咬我的手指(Charlie Bit My Finger)

  • 名稱:「Charlie Bit My Finger – Again!」(常簡稱為「Charlie Bit My Finger」或「Charlie Bit Me」)。
  • 起源/歷史:2007 年 5 月 22 日,英國父親霍華德·戴維斯-卡爾(Howard Davies-Carr)將一段家庭影片上傳到 YouTube,畫面中他年幼的兒子哈利(Harry,約 3 歲)把手指靠近弟弟查理(Charlie)的嘴邊,查理調皮地咬了下去,哈利隨即發出如今已成經典的憤怒喊聲:「Charlie bit me!……而且真的很痛,Charlie。」而咬人的查理則一臉頑皮、毫無悔意地笑著。這段影片原本只是私下上傳,單純是為了讓住在美國的親戚也能看到兩個孩子的模樣。
  • 如何走紅:這段影片脫離了原本設定的私人觀眾範圍,在當時仍相對年輕的 YouTube 平台上自然擴散開來,成為最早一批「純粹的家庭生活影片」(而非專業製作內容)在網路上大規模爆紅的案例之一;累計觀看次數最終超過 8.8 億次,一度躋身 YouTube 史上觀看次數最高的影片之列,並在相當長一段時間內,是該平台上觀看次數最高的「非音樂類」影片。
  • 意義/使用情境:常被用作「未經安排、純屬意外、老少咸宜」病毒式爆紅的代表性參照案例——是「Web 2.0」早期,真實、未經剪輯的家庭日常影片竟能超越專業製作內容」這一現象的經典範例;在談論網路病毒式傳播這個議題本身時,經常被拿出來當作歷史性的參照點。
  • 變體/演化:衍生出無數remix版本(包括一個 Auto-Tune 修音歌曲版本)、大量惡搞反應影片與重演影片,以及媒體對戴維斯-卡爾家族的廣泛報導;最值得一提的是,這家人在 2021 年一場慈善拍賣中,將原始影片以 NFT 形式出售,成交價約 76 萬 999 美元,並依拍賣條件將原始影片從 YouTube 上下架,此舉引發一波輿論反彈,並掀起一場關於「病毒式影片的所有權歸誰」以及 NFT 交易對共享網路文化意味著什麼的公共討論——該影片後來仍被重新上架回 YouTube。
  • 目前狀態:原始影片如今仍可在 YouTube 上觀看;哈利與查理如今已經成年,偶爾會在影片週年紀念時接受回顧性採訪,而這段影片持續被視為研究早期病毒式影片文化、以及近年網路內容所有權經濟學議題時的一個重要經典案例。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、knowyourmeme.com

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《第七封印》(The Seventh Seal,Det sjunde inseglet,1957,導演:英格瑪·柏格曼)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《第七封印》(1957),由英格瑪·柏格曼(Ingmar Bergman)編劇並執導;麥斯·馮·西度(Max von Sydow)飾演騎士安東尼奧斯·布洛克(Antonius Block),本特·艾克羅特(Bengt Ekerot)飾演死神,古納爾·布耶恩施特蘭德(Gunnar Björnstrand)飾演騎士那位憤世嫉俗的隨從約恩斯(Jöns);攝影指導為古納爾·費舍爾(Gunnar Fischer)。
  • 時代/背景:在瑞典以有限預算拍攝,拍攝期僅約 35 天,屬於 1950 年代確立柏格曼國際聲望的歐洲藝術電影浪潮之一;故事背景設定在黑死病肆虐的中世紀瑞典。
  • 劇情/內容概述:騎士安東尼奧斯·布洛克從十字軍東征歸來,對信仰與人生已心灰意冷、精神耗竭,卻在一片荒涼的海灘上迎來死神本人——化身為一個面色蒼白、身披黑袍的形象。布洛克向死神提出下一局棋的挑戰,為自己爭取時間,好在這局棋(以及自己的性命)結束之前,找到上帝存在或人生意義的某種證明;他與隨從約恩斯,以及一團流浪演員(約夫、米亞,和他們的嬰兒)一同踏上返家之路,這段旅程成為一場關於信仰、懷疑與死亡的漫長沉思,而瘟疫正在他們周遭的鄉間肆虐蔓延。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:強烈對比的黑白攝影風格,塑造出影史上數一數二具代表性的畫面——在空無一人的海岸邊與死神對弈的棋局,以及結尾那群註定赴死的角色被死神引領著、走過山丘剪影的「死亡之舞」場景;簡潔而寓言化的敘事結構,將內在的精神危機外化為與擬人化死神的具體交鋒,取材自中世紀道德劇與「死亡之舞」(danse macabre)的傳統。
  • 文化/歷史意義:榮獲 1957 年坎城影展評審團獎(Prix du Jury),普遍被視為歐洲藝術電影與存在主義電影最具代表性的作品之一,其主題部分源自柏格曼一生對信仰的懷疑與掙扎——這份懷疑,正是他嚴格路德教派牧師父親從小灌輸給他的。
  • 影響與傳承:「與死神下棋」這一畫面,已成為整個電影史上最常被引用、也最常被戲仿的畫面之一——從伍迪·艾倫的《愛與死》,到《比爾與泰德的冒險 2》,再到無數卡通與短劇喜劇的致敬與惡搞——讓這部電影的意象遠遠超出藝術電影觀眾的範圍,深植於主流流行文化之中;它至今仍是幾乎所有「影史最偉大電影」片單上的常客。
  • 現況/如何欣賞:由 Criterion Collection 修復並發行藍光光碟,亦收錄於其串流平台 Criterion Channel,並持續在世界各地的影展與回顧展中不時放映。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、criterion.com

8)看片方法——交叉剪接與平行蒙太奇的「同步性」(第 25 課)

交叉剪接(平行蒙太奇)是在兩條或多條「同時」發生於不同地點的動作線之間來回切換,靠這種「交替」本身——而不是像第 2 天教過的蘇聯蒙太奇那樣,靠拼接彼此無關的意象來產生意義,也不是像第 6 天教過的非線性敘事那樣,靠打亂時間順序來製造效果——來建立意義。有四件事值得檢視:第一,這些交叉剪接的動作線,究竟是真正同時發生,還是只是被「暗示」為同時發生;第二,隨著段落推進,交替的節奏是否逐漸加快,靠越來越短的片段來拉高張力;第三,這樣的交叉剪接,製造出來的是一種「反諷式對比」(把神聖或歡慶的動作線,與暴力或逾越常規的動作線並置),還是單純的「懸疑感」(兩條動作線逐漸匯聚向同一個結果);第四,是否有連續不斷的音樂或聲音,將這幾個不同的地點縫合成同一個統一的情感事件——如果缺少這條聲音的縫線,「同時性」就可能顯得只是隨意的交替剪接,而不是一個真正連貫一體的事件。

實例演練:《教父》(The Godfather,1972,導演:法蘭西斯·福特·柯波拉),片尾附近的受洗場景。麥克·柯里昂站在外甥的受洗儀式上擔任教父,以拉丁文肅穆地誦讀著棄絕撒旦的誓詞,而電影同時交叉剪接到他暗中下令、同步進行的一連串刺殺行動——敵對五大家族的首腦分別遭到勒殺、在電梯裡被槍擊、在飯店按摩椅上被擊斃。尼諾·羅塔(Nino Rota)的教堂管風琴配樂,連續貫穿每一次剪接,將這場神聖儀式與那場褻瀆般的暴力,縫合成一個不可分割的整體情感事件,而不是兩段各自獨立、被硬湊在一起的場景。這份反諷完全透過剪接結構本身呈現出來——麥克在同一口氣裡一邊誦讀棄絕撒旦的誓言,一邊親手完成了自己邁向「新任教父」、也邁向自身墮落的轉變——完全不需要任何一句台詞把這層關聯講明白。
