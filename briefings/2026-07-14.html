5) Country/Region Spotlight: Bhutan

  • Location & size: A landlocked Himalayan kingdom between India and China (Tibet), about 38,394 km² — roughly the same size as Switzerland, and slightly larger than Taiwan (~36,197 km²).
  • Population: About 780,000-800,000, one of the least densely populated countries in Asia.
  • Religion: Vajrayana Buddhism is the state religion (~75% of the population); a Hindu minority (~23%), mostly of Nepali descent, lives mainly in the south.
  • Economy: Small and hydropower-driven — Bhutan exports hydroelectricity to India, its largest trading partner; tourism follows a "high value, low volume" policy (a daily Sustainable Development Fee for visitors); the government also tracks a Gross National Happiness (GNH) index alongside GDP.
  • History: Unified as a state in 1616 by the Tibetan lama Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal; became a hereditary monarchy in 1907 under the Wangchuck dynasty; peacefully transitioned to a constitutional monarchy with an elected parliament in 2008.
  • Current leadership: Head of state is King (Druk Gyalpo) Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck (reigning since 2006); head of government is Prime Minister Tshering Tobgay of the People's Democratic Party, in office since January 2024 (his second term, having also served 2013-2018).
  • Language: Dzongkha is the national language; Sharchopkha and Nepali (Lhotshamkha) are also widely spoken regionally.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight: Rickroll

  • Name/format: A "rickroll" is a bait-and-switch prank where a link or video promising one thing instead plays Rick Astley's 1987 music video "Never Gonna Give You Up."
  • Origin: Emerged on 4chan in May 2007, evolving from an earlier prank called "duckrolling" (linking to a picture of a duck on wheels).
  • How it spread: It went mainstream after a widely shared 2007 prank swapped a "GTA IV trailer" link for the video; it snowballed through forums, YouTube, and news coverage, cemented by Rick Astley himself surprise-performing the song as a live "rickroll" float at the 2008 Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.
  • Meaning/usage: Used whenever someone disguises a link as something desirable (news, a download, a "leaked" video) to trick people into watching the Astley video instead — a lighthearted, victimless practical joke.
  • Variations: Video-in-video rickrolls, QR-code rickrolls placed in real-world posters, ARG/puzzle rickrolls, and a famous 2021 stunt where YouTube "renamed" itself Rickroll for April Fools' Day, officially retitling the platform's own logo video.
  • Current status: Still active nearly two decades later — one of the internet's longest-running in-jokes, now recognized across multiple generations as shorthand for "you got tricked (harmlessly)."

6) 網路迷因介紹:Rickroll(瑞克搖)

  • 名稱/形式: 「Rickroll」是一種誘餌式惡搞:點擊一個聲稱是別的內容的連結或影片,結果卻播放瑞克·艾斯特利(Rick Astley)1987 年的音樂錄影帶《Never Gonna Give You Up》。
  • 起源: 2007 年 5 月出現於 4chan 論壇,前身是更早的惡搞手法「duckrolling」(連結導向一張裝有輪子的鴨子圖片)。
  • 如何爆紅: 2007 年一起廣傳的惡搞事件——將原本標榜「GTA IV 預告片」的連結換成瑞克搖影片——使其開始擴散,並透過論壇、YouTube 與新聞報導迅速走紅;瑞克·艾斯特利本人於 2008 年梅西感恩節遊行上驚喜現場演出該曲,更讓這個迷因深植大眾文化。
  • 意義與使用情境: 凡是把連結偽裝成吸引人的內容(新聞、下載檔、「外流」影片等)藉此誘騙他人點擊、實際上導向瑞克搖影片,都稱為 rickroll——是一種無害、輕鬆逗趣的整人玩笑。
  • 變體: 影片中藏影片的 rickroll、貼在現實世界海報上的 QR code rickroll、密室解謎/ARG 式 rickroll,以及 2021 年愚人節 YouTube 官方將自己的品牌影片「改名」為 Rickroll 的知名行銷噱頭。
  • 現況: 歷經近二十年仍然活躍,是網路上壽命最長的迷因之一,如今橫跨多個世代,已成為「(無傷大雅地)被騙了」的通用代名詞。
