3) Spanish Lesson — Day 58 (10 min, La A Personal: The Personal "A")

Steps outside the tense/mood/pronoun/se-family tracks entirely into a distinctively Spanish grammatical marker with no English or Chinese equivalent: the personal "a", required immediately before a direct object when that object is a specific, identifiable person (e.g. Veo a mi hermana, "I see my sister") but omitted entirely when the direct object is a thing (Veo el coche, "I see the car"). It carries no translatable meaning of its own — it is a pure grammatical signal telling the listener "a person is coming, not another subject." A handy self-test: if a sentence could naturally be questioned with ¿A quién...? ("Whom...?"), its direct object needs the personal "a." The lesson directly cross-references Day 55's indefinite people-words (alguien/nadie), which always take the personal "a" when they're a direct object (¿Conoces a alguien? / No conozco a nadie), and Day 44's direct object pronouns (lo/la/los/las), before which the "a" disappears once the noun is replaced (Veo a mi hermana → La veo).

  • ¿Ves a tu hermana en la foto? — Do you see your sister in the photo?
  • No, pero veo el perro que está a su lado. — No, but I see the dog next to her.
  • ¿Conoces a alguien más en la foto? No, no conozco a nadie más, solo a tu hermana. — Do you know anyone else in the photo? No, I don't know anyone else, only your sister.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 58: 兌 (Duì) — "The Joyous" (Lake, Pleasure) (Lake over Lake: Duì 兌 above, Duì 兌 below — 兌為澤, the last of only eight hexagrams in the King Wen Sequence formed by doubling a single trigram upon itself, completing the full set alongside Qián/1, Kūn/2, Kǎn/29, Lí/30, Zhèn/51, Gèn/52, and Xùn/57, all already featured in this series). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the connection directly from yesterday's hexagram: "巽者,入也;入而後說之,故受之以兌;兌者,說也。" ("Xùn means to enter; having entered, one then delights in it — therefore Xùn is followed by Duì; Duì means joy.") Where Hexagram 57 taught the only viable strategy for someone with no standing of their own — gentle, gradual penetration, entering the way wind slips through the smallest gap — Hexagram 58 shows the natural result once that gentle entering succeeds: genuine joy. The Judgment reads: "兌,亨,利貞。" ("Duì. Success. Favorable to be firm and correct.") The brevity of the judgment carries a deliberate warning alongside the promise: joy is auspicious only when grounded in correctness, not when pursued as flattery or indulgence for its own sake. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "兌,說也。剛中而柔外,說以利貞,是以順乎天而應乎人。說以先民,民忘其勞;說以犯難,民忘其死。說之大,民勸矣哉!" ("Duì means joy. The firm lines occupy the center while the yielding line rests outside — joy achieved through inner firmness and outer correctness — and so it accords with Heaven above and responds to humanity below. When a leader inspires joy in the people before calling on their labor, they forget their toil; when a leader inspires joy in the people before leading them into hardship, they forget even the danger of death. Great indeed is the power of joy in moving people to willing effort!") The structural reading matters here: each Duì trigram places one yielding (yin) line above two firm (yang) lines, meaning the outward face is soft and pleasing while the inner substance remains firm and correct — the Tuan's warning that genuine joy must be "firm within, gentle without," never the reverse (a hollow, merely flattering exterior masking no real substance). The Image commentary reads: "麗澤,兌;君子以朋友講習。" ("Two lakes joined together: this is the image of Duì. Thus the superior man gathers with friends to discuss and practice together.") Two adjoining lakes replenish and nourish each other through their connection, exactly as friends who gather to discuss ideas and rehearse skills together enrich one another's understanding — a mutual, ongoing exchange rather than one party simply entertaining or flattering the other. Read together with Hexagram 57, the pair completes a small teaching arc: 57 shows how someone with no standing must gently, repeatedly penetrate a new situation to gain any foothold at all; 58 shows the reward once that gentle work succeeds — not conquest or domination, but shared, well-grounded joy, sustained (like two connected lakes) only for as long as the exchange remains mutual and correct.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Egypt (埃及)

  • Location: Northeastern Africa, spanning into Asia via the Sinai Peninsula, making Egypt one of the few transcontinental countries on Earth. Bordered by Libya to the west, Sudan to the south, and Israel/Gaza to the northeast, with coastlines on the Mediterranean Sea (north) and the Red Sea (east). The Nile River runs the country's full north-south length; over 95% of Egypt's population is packed into the narrow Nile Valley and Delta, since the remaining land is Sahara/Eastern Desert. The Suez Canal, connecting the Mediterranean to the Red Sea across the Sinai land bridge, is one of the world's most important shipping chokepoints.
  • Territory: About 1,010,000 km² — roughly 28 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), about 1.8 times the size of France (~551,700 km²), and about 1.45 times the size of Texas.
  • Population: Approximately 120.2 million as of 2026 — roughly five times Taiwan's population, making Egypt one of the most populous countries in both Africa (third, after Nigeria and Ethiopia) and the Arab world.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Muslim (Sunni Islam), roughly 90% of the population, with a Coptic Orthodox Christian minority of roughly 10% — one of the oldest continuous Christian communities in the world, tracing its founding to St. Mark the Evangelist's mission to Alexandria around 42 AD, predating Islam by nearly six centuries.
  • Economy: Anchored by Suez Canal transit revenue (roughly 12% of global maritime trade passes through it), tourism (the Pyramids of Giza, Luxor's temples, and Red Sea resort diving), Nile-irrigated agriculture (cotton historically, now diversified cereals/produce), natural gas exports, and remittances from millions of Egyptians working abroad, especially in Gulf states. The economy has faced persistent strain from high public debt, repeated Egyptian pound devaluations, and multiple IMF-backed reform programs in recent years.
  • History: Home to one of the world's oldest civilizations, unified under Pharaoh Narmer around 3100 BC and famed for the pyramids, pharaonic dynasties, and Nile-centered agriculture; later ruled successively by Persians, Greeks (the Ptolemaic dynasty, ending with Cleopatra), Romans, and Byzantines, before the Arab Muslim conquest (639-646 AD) introduced Islam and the Arabic language that define Egypt today. Ottoman rule from 1517 gave way to British occupation from 1882; the 1952 Free Officers' coup (led by Gamal Abdel Nasser) overthrew the monarchy and established a republic, followed by Nasser's pan-Arabist era and the 1956 Suez Crisis, Anwar Sadat's landmark 1979 peace treaty with Israel, Hosni Mubarak's three-decade rule ended by the 2011 Arab Spring uprising, a brief Muslim Brotherhood presidency under Mohamed Morsi (2012-2013), and the 2013 military-led ouster that brought Abdel Fattah el-Sisi to power.
  • Current leader: President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi, in power since 2014 and re-elected most recently in December 2023 with 89.6% of the vote for a new six-year term, under a 2019 constitutional amendment extending presidential terms.
  • Language: Arabic is official; the colloquial Egyptian Arabic dialect, thanks to Egypt's historically dominant film and television industry, is the most widely understood Arabic dialect across the entire Arab world. Modern Standard Arabic is used for formal writing and media, the Coptic language survives liturgically within the Coptic Orthodox Church, and English/French are widely used in business, higher education, and among the professional classes.
    Sources: geofactbook.com, sis.gov.eg (presidency), kurdistan24.net

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Disaster Girl

  • Name/origin: "Disaster Girl" is built on a photograph of four-year-old Zoë Roth smirking directly at the camera while a house burns in the background — a real controlled-burn training exercise for the local fire department in Mebane, North Carolina, photographed by her father, Dave Roth, in January 2005 and uploaded to his Flickr account in 2007.
  • How it became popular: Dave Roth submitted the photo to JPG Magazine's "Dominating Blow" photo contest in 2008, where it won. It was then posted to BuzzFeed on October 27, 2008, with a follow-up post the next day showcasing early fan-made derivatives, and quickly spread further via Digg, TrendHunter, eBaum's World Forum, and Best Week Ever.
  • Meaning/usage: The meme is an exploitable Photoshop template: Zoë's smirking face is composited in front of unrelated disaster scenes (explosions, collapsing buildings, natural catastrophes), implying she caused — or is smugly delighted by — the calamity behind her. It's used broadly as a reaction image for gleeful mischief, unrepentant chaos-causing, or ironic satisfaction at disorder.
  • Variations/evolution: Countless disaster backdrops have been photoshopped in over nearly two decades; the format also crossed into GIF and short-video remixes and helped popularize a broader "smug kid amid chaos" reaction-image subgenre. In a striking coda to its meme history, Dave Roth sold the original photograph as an NFT in 2021 for 180 ETH (roughly US$486,716), turning the meme into a widely cited case study in monetizing internet-culture ephemera.
  • Current status: Still instantly recognizable nearly two decades after the original 2005 photo, regularly cited in "greatest memes of all time" retrospectives alongside other foundational 2008-era image-macro templates; Zoë Roth, now an adult, has spoken publicly in interviews about growing up as the internet's most famous accidental supervillain.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, knowyourmeme.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Chinatown (1974, dir. Roman Polanski)

  • Name/key figures: Chinatown (1974), directed by Roman Polanski from an original screenplay by Robert Towne — widely considered one of the greatest screenplays ever written — starring Jack Nicholson as private investigator J.J. "Jake" Gittes and Faye Dunaway as Evelyn Mulwray, produced by Robert Evans for Paramount Pictures.
  • Era/background: Made during Hollywood's early-to-mid-1970s "New Hollywood" era, deliberately styled as a throwback to 1930s-40s film noir — the story is explicitly set in 1937 Los Angeles — while exploiting the post-Production-Code freedom of the 1970s to depict violence, sexuality, and moral bleakness the original noir cycle could never have shown directly.
  • Plot overview: Gittes is hired to investigate a suspected affair involving the chief engineer of the Los Angeles Department of Water and Power, and the case spirals into uncovering a vast municipal water-rights conspiracy loosely inspired by Los Angeles's real early-20th-century "California Water Wars" over the Owens Valley aqueduct, and further into a buried incest secret within the Mulwray family. The investigation costs Gittes his moral footing and ends in a tragedy he proves powerless to prevent.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Muted, sun-bleached amber and brown cinematography (by John A. Alonzo) evokes both period nostalgia and the moral corrosion lurking beneath California's sunny surface. Polanski's classical, restrained direction lets Towne's dense, information-heavy dialogue and Nicholson's increasingly battered performance (his character spends much of the film with a bandaged, slashed nose) carry the tension rather than relying on overt stylistic flourishes. Jerry Goldsmith's melancholic, trumpet-led score — composed in just ten days after the studio rejected an earlier score — became as iconic as the film itself.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely considered the definitive neo-noir, reviving the classical noir detective template for a post-Watergate, post-Vietnam American audience deeply skeptical of institutional power and no longer expecting a "just" resolution; Towne's screenplay remains a staple text in screenwriting courses for its structural precision.
  • Influence and legacy: Regularly ranked among the greatest American films and screenplays (including on AFI's lists); its closing line — "Forget it, Jake. It's Chinatown." — became American cultural shorthand for institutional corruption too vast for any one person to fix, and it directly shaped later neo-noir films (from L.A. Confidential onward) that likewise favor a bleak, conspiracy-driven ending over a triumphant one. A belated sequel, The Two Jakes (1990), was written by Towne and directed by and starring Nicholson himself, to a far cooler reception.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Widely available on major streaming and rental platforms and in a Criterion Collection restoration; remains a fixture of American film-school curricula and "greatest screenplays ever written" lists half a century after its release.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, criterion.com

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Frame Within a Frame / Diegetic Framing Devices (Lesson 44)

This lens examines how a director uses an in-scene, diegetic aperture — a window, doorway, archway, mirror, or other physical opening actually present within the story world — to construct a secondary frame around a character inside the camera's own rectangular frame. It is distinct from Day 8's Framing & Composition (which addresses symmetry, one-point perspective, and negative space within an already-fixed camera frame, without addressing a specific in-scene architectural device doing the framing) and from Day 6's The Gaze/Spectatorship Theory (a structural question of whose eyes the camera aligns with, not a concrete object physically enclosing a character on screen). When you spot a frame-within-a-frame, check what the enclosing object actually is (a window, doorway, mirror, or something else); whether it isolates, imprisons, or lets someone secretly observe the character it encloses; whether it appears as a single punctuation moment or recurs as a structural motif tracking a character's psychological state across the film; and whether the "frame" itself stays stable or grows more unstable and threatening as the scene develops.

Worked example: Alien (1979, dir. Ridley Scott, cinematography by Derek Vanlint) — the Nostromo's narrow corridors, airlock hatches, and doorway thresholds repeatedly enclose Ripley and her crewmates within tight secondary frames as the Xenomorph stalks the ship's interior, turning ordinary industrial architecture into a sustained visual metaphor for entrapment and unseen surveillance. The device intensifies as the crew is picked off one by one: the enclosing frames grow progressively tighter and more claustrophobic precisely as the characters' options for escape keep shrinking, so the ship's own architecture visually tracks the narrowing of hope scene by scene.

3)西班牙文課程——第 58 天(10 分鐘,個人受詞 A:La A Personal)

今天完全跳出時態、語氣、代名詞、se 家族的脈絡,介紹一個西班牙語獨有、中英文都沒有對應概念的文法標記:個人受詞 a(la a personal)——當直接受詞是一個特定、可辨識的人時,必須緊接在動詞之後、受詞之前加上這個「a」(例如 Veo a mi hermana,「我看到我姊姊」);但如果受詞是事物,則完全不加(Veo el coche,「我看到那台車」)。這個「a」本身沒有任何可翻譯的意義——它純粹是一個文法訊號,提醒聽者「接下來出現的是一個人,不是另一個主詞」。一個實用的自我檢查法:如果一句話可以自然地用「¿A quién...?」(問誰)來提問其受詞,那麼這句話的直接受詞前面就該加上「a」。本課也直接與第 55 天教過的指人不定代名詞(alguien/nadie)串聯——這兩者作為直接受詞時一律要加 a(¿Conoces a alguien? / No conozco a nadie)——並與第 44 天的直接受詞代名詞(lo/la/los/las)銜接:受詞一旦被代名詞取代,「a」就會跟著消失(Veo a mi hermana → La veo)。

  • ¿Ves a tu hermana en la foto? —— 你在照片裡看到你姊姊了嗎?
  • No, pero veo el perro que está a su lado. —— 沒有,但我看到在她旁邊的那隻狗了。
  • ¿Conoces a alguien más en la foto? No, no conozco a nadie más, solo a tu hermana. —— 你在照片裡還認識其他人嗎?不,除了你姊姊,我誰都不認識。

4)易經卦象

第 58 卦:兌 (Duì) ——「兌」(喜悅、澤、口舌)(兌為澤——上兌下兌:上下卦皆為澤〔兌〕,是《易經》六十四卦中僅有的八個「重卦」〔上下卦相同〕之一,也是最後一個——與本系列已介紹過的第 1 卦乾、第 2 卦坤、第 29 卦坎、第 30 卦離、第 51 卦震、第 52 卦艮、第 57 卦巽一同,湊齊全部八個重卦。)《序卦傳》承接前一卦而來:「巽者,入也;入而後說之,故受之以兌;兌者,說也。」(巽,意指「進入」;進入之後便會心生喜悅,因此巽卦之後,接的是兌卦;兌,意指「喜悅」。)第 57 卦教導的是毫無既有地位根基之人,唯一可行的策略——柔順、漸進地滲透進入,就像風從最細微的縫隙悄然滑入;第 58 卦則呈現,一旦這種柔順滲透成功之後,自然會迎來的結果:真正的喜悅。卦辭寫道:「兌,亨,利貞。」(兌卦,亨通;守持正固為有利。)這句卦辭雖然簡短,卻同時帶有一項刻意的警示:唯有建立在正固之上的喜悅,才是吉祥的,若只是為了討好或放縱享樂而追求喜悅,則未必有利。《彖傳》進一步闡述:「兌,說也。剛中而柔外,說以利貞,是以順乎天而應乎人。說以先民,民忘其勞;說以犯難,民忘其死。說之大,民勸矣哉!」(兌,意指喜悅。剛健的爻位居於中位,柔順的爻位則居於外;透過內在剛健、外在守持正固而生的喜悅,因此能上順天道、下應人心。若當政者能先讓百姓心生喜悅,再役使其勞力,百姓便會忘卻辛勞;若能先讓百姓心生喜悅,再率領其涉險犯難,百姓便會忘卻生死的危險。喜悅所能發揮的力量之大,足以讓百姓心甘情願、自動自發!)這裡的卦象結構值得留意:兌卦每一個組成的「兌」卦,都是一柔(陰)爻居於兩剛(陽)爻之上,象徵外表柔和悅人,內裡實質卻仍剛健、守持正固——《彖傳》的警示正在於此:真正的喜悅必須「內剛外柔」,絕不能反過來,變成內裡空洞、僅剩討好表象、毫無實質內涵的虛假喜悅。《象傳》寫道:「麗澤,兌;君子以朋友講習。」(兩澤相連相依,這就是兌卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應當與朋友相聚,一同講述道理、切磋學習。)兩片相鄰的湖澤,透過彼此相連而互相滋潤、豐沛彼此的水量,正如朋友相聚討論道理、共同切磋練習,也能彼此豐富、增進理解——這是一種雙向、持續進行的交流,而非單方面取悅或奉承對方。若與第 57 卦兩相對照,這兩卦恰恰構成一段小小的教誨脈絡:第 57 卦說明,毫無既有地位根基之人,必須柔順、反覆地滲透進入新的處境,才能爭取到任何立足之地;第 58 卦則呈現,一旦這番柔順的努力獲得成功之後應得的回報——不是征服或支配,而是共同分享、根基穩固的喜悅,而這份喜悅要能持續維繫下去(就像兩片相連的湖澤一般),前提是這場交流必須始終保持雙向,並且守持正固。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——埃及(Egypt)

  • 位置:位於非洲東北角,並透過西奈半島延伸至亞洲,使埃及成為地球上少數橫跨兩大洲的國家之一。西鄰利比亞,南接蘇丹,東北方向與以色列/加薩相鄰,北側濱臨地中海,東側濱臨紅海。尼羅河貫穿全國南北,超過 95% 的埃及人口,集中生活在狹長的尼羅河谷與尼羅河三角洲地帶,其餘國土則多為撒哈拉沙漠/東部沙漠。連接地中海與紅海、橫跨西奈陸橋的蘇伊士運河,是全球最重要的海運戰略咽喉要道之一。
  • 領土:總面積約 101 萬平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 28 倍,約為法國(約 551,700 平方公里)的 1.8 倍,也約為美國德州面積的 1.45 倍。
  • 人口:截至 2026 年,人口約 1 億 2,020 萬人——約為台灣人口的五倍,使埃及成為非洲(僅次於奈及利亞與衣索比亞,排名第三)與阿拉伯世界人口最多的國家之一。
  • 宗教:絕大多數人口信仰伊斯蘭教(遜尼派),比例約占 90%;科普特正教(Coptic Orthodox)基督徒少數族群約占 10%——這是全球歷史最悠久、延續至今未曾中斷的基督教社群之一,其創教歷史可追溯至約西元 42 年,由聖馬可(St. Mark the Evangelist)在亞歷山大港傳教而立,時間比伊斯蘭教的出現還早了將近六個世紀。
  • 經濟:經濟命脈以蘇伊士運河通行費收入為核心(全球約 12% 的海運貿易經此通過)、觀光業(吉薩金字塔、路克索神廟群,以及紅海度假潛水勝地)、依賴尼羅河灌溉的農業(歷史上以棉花為主,如今已多元發展至各類穀物與農產品)、天然氣出口,以及旅居海外(尤其是波斯灣國家)的數百萬埃及僑工匯回的僑匯。近年來,埃及經濟持續承受高額公共債務、埃及鎊多次貶值,以及多輪國際貨幣基金組織(IMF)紓困改革方案的壓力。
  • 歷史:埃及是全球歷史最悠久的文明發源地之一,約於西元前 3100 年由法老那爾邁(Narmer)統一,以金字塔、法老王朝與尼羅河為中心的農業聞名於世;其後相繼被波斯人、希臘人(托勒密王朝,終於克麗奧佩脫拉〔埃及豔后〕)、羅馬人與拜占庭人統治,直到阿拉伯穆斯林征服(西元 639 至 646 年)引進了伊斯蘭教與阿拉伯語,奠定今日埃及的文化基礎。1517 年起的鄂圖曼統治,於 1882 年讓位給英國佔領;1952 年由賈邁勒·阿卜杜勒·納賽爾(Gamal Abdel Nasser)領導的「自由軍官組織」政變推翻君主制,建立共和國,隨後進入納賽爾主導的泛阿拉伯主義時代與 1956 年蘇伊士運河危機,接著是安瓦爾·薩達特(Anwar Sadat)於 1979 年與以色列簽署具里程碑意義的和平條約,再到胡斯尼·穆巴拉克(Hosni Mubarak)長達三十年的統治,最終於 2011 年阿拉伯之春起義中終結;其後短暫由穆斯林兄弟會的穆罕默德·穆爾西(Mohamed Morsi)出任總統(2012 至 2013 年),再經 2013 年軍方主導的政變,由阿卜杜勒-法塔赫·塞西(Abdel Fattah el-Sisi)掌權至今。
  • 現任領導人:總統阿卜杜勒-法塔赫·塞西(Abdel Fattah el-Sisi)自 2014 年起執政,最近一次於 2023 年 12 月以 89.6% 得票率成功連任,依 2019 年憲法修正案延長後的任期,展開新一任六年任期。
  • 語言:官方語言為阿拉伯語;由於埃及電影與電視產業長期居於阿拉伯世界主導地位,埃及方言口語(Egyptian Arabic)已成為整個阿拉伯世界最普遍被理解的阿拉伯語方言。標準現代阿拉伯語則用於正式書寫與媒體;科普特語仍在科普特正教會的禮拜儀式中留存使用;英語與法語則廣泛通行於商業、高等教育與專業人士階層之間。

6)網路迷因介紹——災難女孩(Disaster Girl)

  • 名稱/起源:「Disaster Girl」(災難女孩)的迷因,源自一張四歲女孩柔伊·羅斯(Zoë Roth)直視鏡頭、露出得意笑容,背景卻是一棟正在燃燒的房屋的照片——這其實是北卡羅來納州梅本市(Mebane)當地消防隊的一場真實控制性燃燒訓練演習,由她的父親戴夫·羅斯(Dave Roth)於 2005 年 1 月拍攝,並於 2007 年上傳至他的 Flickr 帳號。
  • 如何走紅:戴夫·羅斯於 2008 年將這張照片投稿至《JPG 雜誌》舉辦的「壓倒性打擊」(Dominating Blow)攝影比賽,並獲獎。這張照片隨後於 2008 年 10 月 27 日被刊登於 BuzzFeed,隔日又刊出一篇彙整早期網友惡搞版本的追蹤報導,並迅速透過 Digg、TrendHunter、eBaum's World 論壇,以及 Best Week Ever 等平台進一步擴散。
  • 意義/使用情境:這是一個可自由編輯的 Photoshop 迷因模板:將柔伊得意的笑臉,合成到各種與原照無關的災難場景前方(爆炸、建築物倒塌、天災現場等),暗示她是造成這場災難的元兇——或至少對眼前的災難感到得意洋洋、幸災樂禍。這個迷因被廣泛用作反應圖,用來表達「幸災樂禍的惡作劇」「毫不悔改地製造混亂」,或是「對混亂局面感到諷刺式滿足」等情境。
  • 變體/演化:近二十年來,已有無數種災難背景被合成置入這張照片中;這個格式也跨界演變出 GIF 與短影音改編版本,並帶動了更廣泛的「混亂中得意小孩」反應圖次類型走紅。這則迷因的故事還有一段令人玩味的後續:戴夫·羅斯於 2021 年將這張原始照片,以 NFT 形式售出,售價高達 180 顆以太幣(約合 48 萬 6,716 美元),使這則迷因成為網路文化如何被「變現」的知名案例研究對象。
  • 目前狀態:距離 2005 年原始照片拍攝至今已近二十年,這張照片依然一眼可辨,經常與其他 2008 年前後崛起的經典圖片迷因模板一同,出現在「史上最偉大迷因」的回顧盤點之中;如今已成年的柔伊·羅斯,也曾多次公開受訪,分享自己作為網路上「最著名的無心大反派」的成長經歷。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《唐人街》(Chinatown,1974,導演:羅曼·波蘭斯基)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《唐人街》(Chinatown,1974)由羅曼·波蘭斯基(Roman Polanski)執導,劇本出自羅伯特·湯恩(Robert Towne)的原創劇本——普遍被譽為史上最偉大的電影劇本之一——由傑克·尼克遜(Jack Nicholson)飾演私家偵探傑克·吉茨(J.J. "Jake" Gittes),費·唐娜薇(Faye Dunaway)飾演伊芙琳·馬爾雷(Evelyn Mulwray),由羅伯特·埃文斯(Robert Evans)為派拉蒙影業製作。
  • 時代/背景:本片拍攝於 1970 年代初中期的「新好萊塢」時期,刻意仿效 1930 至 40 年代黑色電影(film noir)的風格——劇情明確設定於 1937 年的洛杉磯——同時充分利用 1970 年代「電影製作法規」(Production Code)廢除後的創作自由,直接呈現暴力、性愛與道德黑暗面等原始黑色電影年代因審查制度而無法直接表現的內容。
  • 劇情概述:吉茨受雇調查洛杉磯水電局總工程師疑似外遇的案件,案情卻逐步演變成揭發一場龐大的市政水權陰謀——其靈感鬆散取材自洛杉磯 20 世紀初真實發生的「加州水資源戰爭」(圍繞歐文斯谷輸水渠道爭議),並進一步牽扯出馬爾雷家族深埋已久的亂倫秘密。這場調查最終耗盡了吉茨的道德立足點,並以一場他無力阻止的悲劇收場。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:攝影師約翰·A·阿隆索(John A. Alonzo)以低飽和、日曬泛黃的琥珀棕色調攝影風格,同時喚起時代懷舊氛圍,以及潛藏於加州陽光表象之下的道德腐蝕感。波蘭斯基採取古典、克制的導演手法,讓湯恩資訊量龐大、緊湊細密的對白,以及尼克遜逐漸傷痕累累的表演(劇中角色大半時間都纏著繃帶、鼻子遭割傷)自行撐起全片張力,而非仰賴過度張揚的風格化手法。傑瑞·戈德史密斯(Jerry Goldsmith)以小號主奏、憂鬱哀傷的配樂——在原配樂遭片廠否決後,僅花十天緊急重新譜寫而成——最終與電影本身同樣成為經典。
  • 文化/歷史意義:普遍被視為「新黑色電影」(neo-noir)的定義之作,為後水門事件、後越戰時代、對體制權力深感懷疑、不再期待「正義終將伸張」的美國觀眾,重新復興並改造了經典黑色電影偵探敘事模板;湯恩的劇本至今仍是編劇課程中,以結構精確著稱的必讀教材。
  • 影響與傳承:經常被列入美國最偉大電影與最偉大劇本排行榜(包括美國電影學會〔AFI〕的各項榜單);片尾經典台詞「算了吧,傑克,這裡是唐人街。」("Forget it, Jake. It's Chinatown.")已成為美國文化中,用來形容「體制腐敗龐大到無人能夠獨力撼動」的代名詞,並直接影響了此後以《鐵面特警隊》(L.A. Confidential)為代表的一系列新黑色電影,同樣偏好以陰鬱、陰謀導向的結局取代皆大歡喜的收場。遲來的續集《陰謀的天空》(The Two Jakes,1990)由湯恩編劇、尼克遜自導自演,但反應遠不如前作熱烈。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:目前廣泛可於各大串流平台與租片服務中觀賞,並有標準收藏(Criterion Collection)修復版本;上映至今已超過半世紀,依然是美國電影學院課程與「史上最偉大劇本」排行榜上的常客。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、criterion.com

8)看片方法——畫中畫框景手法 Frame Within a Frame(第 44 課)

這項分析角度,關注導演如何利用場景中真實存在、屬於劇情世界一部分的「開口」——窗戶、門框、拱門、鏡子,或其他實體開口——在攝影機本身的矩形畫面之中,為角色再構築出一層次要的框景。這不同於第 8 天教過的「框景與構圖」(探討的是已經固定的攝影機畫面內部的對稱性、單點透視與留白/負空間,並未特別聚焦於場景中某個具體、實際負責「框住」角色的建築裝置),也不同於第 6 天教過的「凝視/觀影理論」(那是一個關於攝影機究竟借用了誰的視角這種結構性問題,而非畫面中實際框住角色的具體物件)。發現「畫中畫」手法時,可以檢視:這個包夾角色的開口實際上是什麼(窗戶、門框、鏡子,或其他物件);它是讓角色顯得孤立、被囚禁,還是暗示有人正在偷偷觀察被框住的角色;它是單一次的點綴性瞬間,還是貫穿全片、用來追蹤角色心理狀態變化的結構性母題;以及這個「框」本身,隨著劇情推進,是維持穩定不變,還是逐漸變得更加不穩、更具威脅性。

實例演練:《異形》(Alien,1979,導演:雷利·史考特〔Ridley Scott〕,攝影:德瑞克·凡蘭特〔Derek Vanlint〕)——諾斯特羅莫號太空船狹窄的走道、氣密艙門與門框通道,在異形潛伏於船艙內部、伺機獵殺蕾普莉(Ripley)與船員的過程中,反覆將他們框限於緊縮的次要框景之中,把再普通不過的工業建築結構,轉化為貫穿全片、象徵「受困」與「遭到暗中窺視」的持續性視覺隱喻。這項手法隨著船員一個接一個遭到殺害而愈演愈烈:正當角色逃脫的可能性不斷縮小之際,包夾住他們的畫面框景也隨之愈收愈緊、愈發令人窒息,使太空船自身的建築結構,逐場逐景地,視覺化地追蹤著希望不斷收窄的過程。
