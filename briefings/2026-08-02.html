3) Spanish Lesson — Day 24 (10 min, builds on Day 23)

Day 23 introduced the futuro simple (simple future) and its twelve irregular stems. Today's condicional simple (simple conditional) is its "twin" tense: regular verbs take the exact same full-infinitive formation, and irregular verbs reuse the identical twelve stems from yesterday — only the endings change.

  • Me gustaría viajar a Chile algún día. — I would like to travel to Chile someday.
  • ¿Podrías ayudarme mañana? — Could you help me tomorrow?
  • Yo, en tu lugar, hablaría con ella. — If I were you, I would talk to her.
  • Dijo que vendría a la fiesta. — He said he would come to the party.

Grammar note: regular conditional endings (-ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -ían) attach to the full infinitive across all three verb families (hablar→hablaría, comer→comería, vivir→viviría), and every irregular verb from Day 23's future tense (poder→podr-, saber→sabr-, querer→querr-, haber→habr-, tener→tendr-, poner→pondr-, salir→saldr-, venir→vendr-, hacer→har-, decir→dir-) keeps the exact same irregular stem here — just swap yesterday's future endings for today's conditional endings. Four core uses: hypothetical statements ("would do"), polite requests (¿Podrías...?, Me gustaría...), reported "future" inside past-tense speech (dijo que vendría), and conjecture about the past (¿Qué hora sería?).

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Chile

  • Location: South America's western edge, a narrow ribbon running along the Pacific coast between the Andes and the ocean; borders Peru to the north, Bolivia to the northeast, and Argentina along nearly its entire eastern length via the Andes mountains.
  • Territory: About 756,000 km² on the mainland — roughly 21 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²) — but famous above all for its shape: it stretches about 4,300 km from north to south (comparable to the entire length of the continental United States) while averaging only about 180 km east to west, making it one of the longest, narrowest countries on Earth; it also claims a slice of Antarctica and includes Easter Island (Rapa Nui) far out in the Pacific.
  • Population: Roughly 19.5-20 million (2026 estimate), heavily concentrated in and around the capital, Santiago.
  • Religion: Historically majority Roman Catholic (now roughly half the population), a growing Evangelical/Protestant minority (around 15%), and a rapidly rising share of religiously unaffiliated Chileans (over a fifth of the population, especially among younger generations).
  • Economy: A high-income, OECD-member economy and the world's largest copper producer (supplying close to a quarter of global output), also a top global lithium producer thanks to the Atacama Desert's salt flats; wine and salmon exports round out a trade profile long considered one of Latin America's most open and market-oriented.
  • History: Home to the Mapuche and other Indigenous peoples, notably in the south, who were never fully subdued by either the Inca Empire or early Spanish colonization; a Spanish colony from the 16th century until independence in 1818, led by Bernardo O'Higgins and José de San Martín. The War of the Pacific (1879-1883) won Chile its mineral-rich northern territories from Peru and Bolivia. Decades of relatively stable democracy ended in the 1973 coup that installed General Augusto Pinochet, whose military dictatorship (1973-1990) was marked by severe human-rights abuses before a return to democracy in 1990. Mass protests in 2019-2020 over inequality ("el estallido social") triggered a constitutional rewrite process, though voters rejected both a left-leaning 2022 draft and a right-leaning 2023 draft, leaving the amended Pinochet-era 1980 constitution in force.
  • Current leader: José Antonio Kast, who won the December 2025 runoff election with just over 58% of the vote and was inaugurated on March 11, 2026 — the first radical-right president since Chile's return to democracy, elected on concerns over public safety, crime, irregular immigration, and the economy, succeeding outgoing president Gabriel Boric.
  • Language: Spanish (Chilean Spanish, noted for its rapid pace and distinctive slang), plus Indigenous languages including Mapudungun (spoken by the Mapuche) and Rapa Nui on Easter Island.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, aljazeera.com

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Trollface

  • Name: Trollface (briefly also known as "Coolface" during an early rebranding attempt).
  • Origin/history: Drawn in Microsoft Paint on September 19, 2008 by 18-year-old Oakland art student Carlos Ramirez (DeviantArt handle "Whynne"), as a throwaway character in a webcomic mocking pointless trolling on 4chan's video-games board; Ramirez has said he was actually trying to draw an unrelated character called "Rape Rodent" when the now-iconic mischievous grin emerged.
  • How it became popular: The image quickly became 4chan's go-to visual shorthand for a triumphant internet troll, then exploded alongside the "rage comics" boom of 2009-2012 on Reddit's /r/f7u12 and elsewhere — crude four-panel MS-Paint comics ending on a trolling "gotcha" punchline, with Trollface as the genre's signature closing panel.
  • Meaning/usage: Signals gleeful satisfaction after successfully deceiving, pranking, or winding someone up online; posting the face alone is shorthand for "you just got trolled."
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned an entire cast of companion rage-comic faces (Forever Alone, Me Gusta, Y U No, Poker Face), a long-running mobile puzzle-game franchise ("Trollface Quest"), countless redraws and edits, and recurring cameo status as an easter egg and reaction image well past the rage-comic era's peak.
  • Current status: A settled, nostalgic landmark of the 2008-2012 rage-comics era rather than an active trend — still instantly recognized as the definitive "you got trolled" symbol and periodically revived ironically; Ramirez formally copyrighted the image in 2010 and has earned significant licensing income from it since.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Noh Theatre (能)

  • Name: Noh (能, Nō), classical Japanese musical drama.
  • Key figures/creators: Codified in its present form in the 14th century by the actor-playwright Kan'ami and, above all, his son Zeami Motokiyo, under the patronage of the Ashikaga shogunate; Zeami wrote over 100 plays (roughly 50 survive) and some twenty theoretical treatises, most famously "Fūshikaden" ("Transmitting the Flower Through Effects and Attitudes").
  • Era/background: Emerged from earlier ritual and folk performance traditions (including sarugaku and dengaku) and was elevated into a refined court art form in the 14th century, making it one of the world's oldest continuously performed theatrical traditions.
  • Content overview: Plays are typically built around a principal masked actor (shite) who is often revealed partway through to be a ghost, deity, demon, or other supernatural being reliving or resolving an unfinished passion from life, accompanied by a secondary actor (waki, usually an ordinary human, frequently a traveling priest), a chorus, and a small ensemble of flute and drums.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Extremely stylized and deliberately slow, built on Zeami's central aesthetic ideal of yūgen — a profound, mysterious, understated beauty suggested rather than shown outright; the shite's carved cypress-wood mask (representing women, elders, spirits, or demons) is designed so that subtle tilts of the head shift its perceived expression between light and shadow, while minimal gesture and near-motionless stretches of stage time carry as much weight as movement itself.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Established the template — mask, chorus, minimalist symbolic staging, sung/chanted verse — that later influenced Kabuki and Bunraku, and shaped Japanese aesthetic concepts (yūgen, ma/negative space, suggestion over depiction) far beyond the stage itself.
  • Influence and legacy: A recognized influence on 20th-century Western theatre and film artists seeking alternatives to Western realism (including Bertolt Brecht and Akira Kurosawa, who drew on Noh's stylized performance conventions in films such as Throne of Blood); UNESCO proclaimed Noh a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity in 2001.
  • Current status/where to experience: Still actively performed today by professional schools (ryū) at dedicated Noh theatres across Japan, notably the National Noh Theatre in Tokyo, with a living repertoire of Zeami-era and later plays maintained largely unchanged for over 600 years.
    Sources: britannica.com, worldhistory.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Auteur Theory / Director's Signature Analysis (Lesson 10)

Lessons 1-9 covered mise-en-scène, editing, cinematography/lighting, sound, colour, narrative structure, blocking/staging, framing/composition, and symbolism — all methods for reading a single scene or film. Today's lens, auteur theory, zooms out one level further: it reads a recurring visual and thematic signature across an entire filmography, treating a director as a film's true "author" whose personal fingerprint should be identifiable from work to work, much like a novelist's prose style.

The method: To apply auteur analysis, don't judge one film in isolation — instead, compare at least two or three films by the same director and ask what recurs: a visual habit (a favored lens, framing style, or blocking pattern), a recurring collaborator (composer, cinematographer, actor), a thematic obsession (a type of protagonist, moral conflict, or setting the director keeps returning to), or a consistent formal device (how they open/close films, how they use music or narration). A true auteur signature must be consistent (present across multiple, otherwise unrelated projects), distinctive (identifiable even with the director's name hidden), and meaningful (tied to a worldview or preoccupation, not just a technical tic).

Worked example — Wes Anderson's visual signature, focused through The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014): Across Bottle Rocket, Rushmore, The Royal Tenenbaums, Moonrise Kingdom, and The Grand Budapest Hotel, Anderson repeats an unmistakable set of habits: perfectly centered, symmetrical compositions with the camera locked dead-on to a scene's axis; flat, "dollhouse" blocking where actors face the camera directly rather than each other; snap 90-degree whip-pans instead of conventional cuts between speakers; a meticulously art-directed, storybook-flat production design (often literal handcrafted miniatures, as in the Grand Budapest Hotel's exterior model and its sled-chase-with-the-Mendl's-pastry-box sequence); a nested, chapter-book narration structure (the 2014 film frames its story through four layers — a girl reading a book, its author as an old man, the author as a young man, and Zero's own told-to-the-author memory); and a recurring, tonally deadpan ensemble delivering high emotion in a flat, formal register (M. Gustave's rapid-fire manners and courtly diction amid slapstick chaos). No single one of these traits is exclusive to Anderson, but their consistent co-occurrence, film after film, is what makes a shot recognizably "his" even with no directing credit shown.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Consistency across otherwise unrelated stories proves it's a signature, not a coincidence — the same symmetrical, dead-center framing appears whether Anderson is filming a prep-school play (Rushmore), a fractured family reunion (The Royal Tenenbaums), or a fictional Alpine hotel, meaning the visual choice reflects the director's worldview rather than any single story's demands. (2) Formal devices carry thematic weight — Grand Budapest's nested narration (story within story within story) visually enacts its own themes of memory, nostalgia, and the loss of a more civilized world receding further with each narrative layer, so the "trick" of the structure is doing emotional work, not just showing off. (3) Deadpan tone as directorial control — by having actors deliver even violent or heartbreaking moments in the same flat, mannered register as small talk, Anderson asserts total authorial command over tone, refusing to let any single scene's emotion overwhelm the film's controlled aesthetic universe. This lesson sits one level above Lessons 1-9: where mise-en-scène or framing analyzes what one shot is doing, auteur analysis asks what a director's shots keep doing, film after film.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第二十四天(10 分鐘,銜接第二十三天內容)

第二十三天介紹了未來簡單式(futuro simple)與其十二個不規則字根。今天的條件式簡單式(condicional simple)是它的「雙胞胎時態」:規則動詞用的是完全相同的完整原形動詞變化方式,不規則動詞也直接沿用昨天那十二個字根——只有字尾換了一組。

  • Me gustaría viajar a Chile algún día. — 我希望有一天能去智利旅行。
  • ¿Podrías ayudarme mañana? — 你明天能不能幫我?
  • Yo, en tu lugar, hablaría con ella. — 如果我是你,我會跟她談。
  • Dijo que vendría a la fiesta. — 他說過他會來派對。

文法重點:規則的條件式字尾(-ía、-ías、-ía、-íamos、-ían)一律接在完整的原形動詞後面,三種動詞家族共用同一組(hablar→hablaría,comer→comería,vivir→viviría);而第二十三天未來式所有的不規則動詞(poder→podr-、saber→sabr-、querer→querr-、haber→habr-、tener→tendr-、poner→pondr-、salir→saldr-、venir→vendr-、hacer→har-、decir→dir-),在條件式裡完全沿用同一個不規則字根——只需要把昨天的未來式字尾換成今天的條件式字尾即可。條件式主要有四種用法:假設語氣的「將會」、禮貌請求(¿Podrías...?、Me gustaría...)、過去式脈絡下引述的「未來」(dijo que vendría)、以及對過去的推測(¿Qué hora sería?)。

4) 易經卦象

第二十四卦:復卦(Fù)——「回復」(上坤下震,雷在地中)。〈序卦傳〉直接銜接了昨天的卦象:「物不可以終盡剝,窮上反下,故受之以復。」(萬物不可能永遠處於剝落之中而窮盡殆盡;當衰敗之勢在頂端走到盡頭,便會反轉,重新從底部復生——因此接著便是復卦,回復之意。)這正是昨天解讀剝卦時所預示的卦象:剝卦頂端那根碩果僅存的陽爻終於被完全消耗殆盡,而一根嶄新的陽爻,則在六爻的最底端重新生成——雷聲此刻被封藏於承載萬物的大地之中,宛如深冬時節,新生命正在地底悄悄萌動的第一絲跡象。卦辭寫道:「復:亨。出入無疾,朋來無咎。反復其道,七日來復,利有攸往。」(復卦象徵回復,亨通。往來出入皆無災咎,朋友前來相聚也無過失。往復循環,依循其道,七日之後便迎來轉折之機,此時利於有所前往。)「七日」傳統上並非按字面理解,而是象徵一個完整週期的完成,之後成長之勢才會穩固地重新確立。〈象傳〉補充道:「雷在地中,復;先王以至日閉關,不行商旅,后不省之方。」(雷聲潛藏於大地之中,是復卦的象徵;因此古代聖王會在冬至這一天關閉關隘,商旅停止往來,君王也不巡視四方邦國。)這裡的告誡在於:在轉折之機來臨的當下,應當有意識地保持靜默——新生的成長雖然真實存在,卻依然十分脆弱,因此面對初萌的復甦,正確的回應是保護性的靜守,而非立即積極躁動的作為,如此才能讓這根剛剛復生的陽爻,有足夠的空間在不受干擾之下逐漸茁壯,直到世界得以恢復其正常的運行。

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 智利(Chile)

  • 位置: 位於南美洲最西側,沿著太平洋海岸,夾在安地斯山脈與大海之間形成一條狹長帶狀國土;北與秘魯接壤,東北與玻利維亞相鄰,東側則幾乎沿著整條安地斯山脈與阿根廷交界。
  • 領土面積: 本土面積約 756,000 平方公里——約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 21 倍——但真正著名之處在於它的形狀:南北狹長約 4,300 公里(約略等同於美國本土的整體長度),東西平均寬度卻僅約 180 公里,是全世界最狹長的國家之一;此外,智利也主張擁有南極洲的一部分領土,並轄有位於太平洋深處的復活節島(拉帕努伊,Rapa Nui)。
  • 人口: 約 1,950 萬至 2,000 萬人(2026 年估計),人口高度集中於首都聖地牙哥及其周邊地區。
  • 宗教: 歷史上以羅馬天主教為多數信仰(目前約佔人口一半),福音派/新教徒比例正持續成長(約佔 15%),無宗教信仰人口的比例也快速攀升(超過五分之一,尤其在年輕世代中更為明顯)。
  • 經濟: 屬於高所得的 OECD 成員國經濟體,也是全球最大的銅礦生產國(產量接近全球總產量的四分之一),同時受惠於阿他加馬沙漠(Atacama Desert)的鹽湖資源,也是全球主要的鋰礦生產國之一;葡萄酒與鮭魚出口則進一步豐富了智利的貿易結構,長期以來被視為拉丁美洲市場最開放、最自由化的經濟體之一。
  • 歷史: 馬普切人(Mapuche)等原住民族在此定居已久,尤其分布於南部地區,無論印加帝國或早期西班牙殖民勢力都未能將其完全征服;智利自 16 世紀起淪為西班牙殖民地,直到 1818 年在貝爾納多・奧希金斯(Bernardo O'Higgins)與荷西・德・聖馬丁(José de San Martín)率領下獨立建國。1879 至 1883 年的「太平洋戰爭」讓智利從秘魯與玻利維亞手中,贏得了礦產資源豐富的北部領土。數十年相對穩定的民主體制,在 1973 年政變中畫下句點,奧古斯圖・皮諾契特(Augusto Pinochet)將軍就此掌權,其軍事獨裁統治(1973 至 1990 年)期間爆發嚴重的侵犯人權事件,直到 1990 年才重返民主。2019 至 2020 年間因貧富不均引發的大規模抗議(「社會爆發」,el estallido social),促成了修憲程序的啟動,然而選民先後否決了 2022 年偏左派與 2023 年偏右派的兩份修憲草案,使得歷經修訂的皮諾契特時代 1980 年憲法至今仍然有效。
  • 現任領導人: 荷西・安東尼奧・卡斯特(José Antonio Kast),他在 2025 年 12 月的第二輪投票中,以超過 58% 的得票率勝選,並於 2026 年 3 月 11 日正式就任——這是智利自重返民主以來,首位當選的激進右派總統,選民主要考量公共安全、犯罪問題、非法移民與經濟情勢等議題,接替了卸任的前總統加布列爾・博里奇(Gabriel Boric)。
  • 語言: 西班牙語(智利式西班牙語,以說話速度快與獨特俚語聞名),此外還有包括馬普切人所使用的馬普切語(Mapudungun),以及復活節島上使用的拉帕努伊語等原住民語言。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、aljazeera.com

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 酸民臉(Trollface)

  • 名稱: Trollface(酸民臉/巨魔臉),早期曾短暫改名嘗試稱作「Coolface」。
  • 起源/歷史: 這張圖是 2008 年 9 月 19 日,由當時 18 歲、就讀奧克蘭藝術學院的卡洛斯・拉米雷茲(Carlos Ramirez,DeviantArt 帳號「Whynne」)用小畫家隨手畫出的角色,原本只是一部嘲諷 4chan 電玩討論版上「無意義酸民行為」的網路漫畫中的配角;拉米雷茲後來表示,他當時其實是想畫另一個毫不相干、名叫「Rape Rodent」的角色,卻意外畫出了這個如今家喻戶曉、帶著壞笑的表情。
  • 如何爆紅: 這張圖很快成為 4chan 上代表「酸民得逞」的通用視覺符號,隨後隨著 2009 至 2012 年間 Reddit「/r/f7u12」等版面掀起的「憤怒漫畫(rage comics)」熱潮而爆炸性流行——這類粗糙的四格小畫家漫畫,通常以酸民成功整到人的「翻轉笑點」作結,而 Trollface 正是這個類型招牌式的結尾格。
  • 意義與使用情境: 用來表達成功欺騙、惡搞或吊人胃口之後的得意心情;單獨貼出這張臉,就是「你被酸民耍了」的視覺簡稱。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出一整組「憤怒漫畫」班底角色(Forever Alone、Me Gusta、Y U No、Poker Face)、一款長銷至今的手機解謎遊戲系列《Trollface Quest》,無數的改繪與二創版本,以及即便在「憤怒漫畫」熱潮退燒許久之後,仍持續以彩蛋與反應圖形式客串登場。
  • 目前現況: 已是 2008 至 2012 年「憤怒漫畫」時代公認的懷舊經典地標,而非仍在發燒的流行趨勢——至今仍被立即辨識為「你被酸民耍了」的代表性符號,並不時被拿出來反諷式重溫;拉米雷茲已於 2010 年正式為這張圖登記著作權,並自此獲得可觀的授權收益。
    資料來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 能劇(能,Noh)

  • 名稱: 能劇(能,日文讀作 Nō),日本古典音樂戲劇形式。
  • 關鍵人物: 由演員兼劇作家觀阿彌(Kan'ami),以及更重要的是其子世阿彌元清(Zeami Motokiyo),在足利幕府的庇護支持下,於 14 世紀確立了如今我們所見的形式;世阿彌一生創作超過百部劇作(現存約五十部),並撰有約二十篇理論著作,其中最著名的即是《風姿花傳》("Fūshikaden",意為「傳授風姿之花」)。
  • 時代背景: 能劇脫胎自更早期的祭典與民間表演傳統(包括猿樂與田樂),於 14 世紀被提升、精煉為宮廷等級的精緻藝術形式,使其成為世界上少數歷經數百年仍持續不間斷演出的戲劇傳統之一。
  • 內容概述: 劇作通常圍繞著一位戴著面具的主角(仕手,shite)展開,劇情往往在中途揭曉,這位主角其實是幽靈、神祇、惡鬼,或某種超自然存在,正在重新經歷、或試圖了結生前未竟的執念;伴演的還有一位次要角色(脅,waki,通常是凡人,常設定為雲遊僧侶)、合唱團(地謠),以及一支由笛與鼓組成的小型伴奏樂隊。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 極度風格化且刻意放緩節奏,其核心美學理念來自世阿彌所提出的「幽玄」(yūgen)——一種深邃、神秘、含蓄內斂之美,講求「暗示」而非「直接展現」；仕手所戴的檜木雕刻面具(用以representing女性、老者、精怪或鬼魂),其設計精妙之處,在於演員只需些微傾斜頭部角度,便能透過光影變化,讓面具呈現出截然不同的表情;極簡的肢體動作,以及舞台上近乎靜止不動的大段時間,其份量往往與動作本身同等重要。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 能劇建立了一整套後世沿用的表演範式——面具、合唱、極簡而具象徵性的舞台調度、吟唱式的詩詞台詞——這套範式深深影響了後來的歌舞伎與文樂,並形塑出「幽玄」「間」(ma,留白/空間感)、「以暗示取代直陳」等日本美學核心概念,其影響力早已遠遠超出舞台本身。
  • 影響與傳承: 能劇公認影響了 20 世紀西方尋求跳脫寫實主義框架的劇場與電影創作者(包括布萊希特〔Bertolt Brecht〕,以及在《蜘蛛巢城》等作品中大量汲取能劇風格化表演成規的黑澤明);2001 年,聯合國教科文組織將能劇列為「人類口述與非物質文化遺產代表作」。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 至今仍由專業的能劇流派(流,ryū)在日本各地專門的能劇劇場積極演出,東京的國立能樂堂尤其著名;世阿彌時代及其後的劇目,至今仍以歷經六百多年、幾乎原封不動的形式持續傳承演出。
    資料來源:britannica.com、worldhistory.org

8) 看片方法 — 作者論／導演簽名式分析 Auteur Theory(第十課)

第一到九課教了場面調度、剪輯、攝影與燈光、音效、色彩、敘事結構、走位與空間調度、取景與構圖,以及象徵與視覺隱喻——這些方法都聚焦在解讀單一場景或單一部電影。今天的分析視角「作者論(auteur theory)」則再往上拉高一層視野:它解讀的是貫穿整個導演作品序列反覆出現的視覺與主題簽名,將導演視為電影真正的「作者」,認為其個人風格印記應當能像小說家的文筆一樣,在一部又一部作品中被辨識出來。

方法說明: 運用作者論分析時,不要只孤立地評斷單一一部電影——而是應該比較同一位導演至少兩到三部作品,並追問:哪些元素不斷重複出現?可能是視覺上的習慣(慣用的鏡頭、構圖風格,或走位模式)、固定合作的班底(配樂家、攝影師、演員)、反覆出現的主題執念(某一類主角、道德衝突,或導演一再回頭處理的場景設定),或是一貫的形式手法(如何開場/收尾、如何運用音樂或旁白)。真正的「作者簽名」必須具備三項特質:一致性(在多部彼此無關的作品中都能觀察到)、辨識度(即使遮住導演姓名,依然能被認出)、意義性(與某種世界觀或核心關懷相連,而非僅止於一項技術上的小習慣)。

實例示範——魏斯・安德森(Wes Anderson)的視覺簽名,以《歡迎來到布達佩斯大飯店》(The Grand Budapest Hotel,2014)為核心: 綜觀《英雄不流淚》(Bottle Rocket)、《都是愛情惹的禍》(Rushmore)、《天才一族》(The Royal Tenenbaums)、《月昇冒險王國》(Moonrise Kingdom)到《歡迎來到布達佩斯大飯店》,安德森反覆使用一套極具辨識度的手法:攝影機牢牢鎖定畫面中軸、完全置中對稱的構圖;演員直接面對鏡頭而非彼此對望的「扁平、玩具屋式」走位調度;以俐落的 90 度快速橫搖(whip-pan)取代傳統的對話正反打剪接;極度講究、如繪本般平面化的美術設計(經常直接使用手工模型,例如布達佩斯大飯店的建築外觀模型,以及攜帶「梅道爾」〔Mendl's〕糕點盒滑雪橇追逐那場戲);層層嵌套、如章回小說般的敘事結構(2014 年這部電影透過四層敘事框架講故事——閱讀書本的女孩、年邁的作者本人、年輕時的作者,以及零自己講述給作者聽的親身回憶);以及班底演員一貫以不動聲色、形式化的口吻,詮釋強烈情緒(古斯塔夫先生在鬧劇般的混亂之中,仍維持連珠炮般彬彬有禮的宮廷式措辭)。這些手法沒有任何一項是安德森獨創或獨有,但它們在一部又一部作品中如此一致地同時出現,正是即使畫面上完全沒有標示導演名字,觀眾依然能一眼認出「這是他的鏡頭」的關鍵所在。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)在彼此毫不相干的故事中維持一致性,證明這是「簽名」而非巧合——無論安德森拍的是預科學校的校園話劇(《都是愛情惹的禍》)、支離破碎的家族重聚(《天才一族》),還是一間虛構的阿爾卑斯山飯店,同樣置中對稱的構圖手法始終如一地出現,意味著這項視覺選擇反映的是導演本人的世界觀,而非任何單一故事本身的敘事需求。(2)形式手法承載主題重量——布達佩斯大飯店層層嵌套的敘事結構(故事中的故事中的故事),在視覺與結構上親自演繹了影片自身「記憶」「懷舊」以及「一個更文明的世界隨著每一層敘事的推進而漸行漸遠、逐漸消逝」的核心主題,因此這個結構上的「花招」承擔的是情感上的實質功能,而非單純炫技。(3)不動聲色的口吻,即是導演的作者掌控力——藉由讓演員以與閒話家常完全相同、平板而形式化的口吻,詮釋即便是暴力或令人心碎的時刻,安德森藉此宣示了對整部電影情緒基調的絕對主導權,拒絕讓任何單一場景的情緒,凌駕於他精心掌控的美學宇宙之上。這堂課的視角,比第一到九課更高出一個層次:場面調度或取景構圖分析的是「單一鏡頭正在做什麼」,而作者論分析的,則是一位導演的鏡頭,在一部又一部作品中,持續不斷地在做什麼。
