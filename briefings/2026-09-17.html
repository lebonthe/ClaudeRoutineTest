3) Spanish Lesson — Day 67 (10 min, Numbers 11-100: Los Números del 11 al 100)

Steps back to fill a foundational gap left open since Day 6's 0-10: the full number system from 11 to 100, essential for prices, ages, phone numbers, and quantities. Numbers 11-15 (once, doce, trece, catorce, quince) are irregular and must be memorized individually, with no derivable pattern from 0-10. Numbers 16-19 and 21-29 follow a predictable but fused pattern — diez + i + digit (dieciséis, diecisiete, diecicho, diecinueve) and veinti- + digit (veintiuno through veintinueve) — each written as a single word, with a scattered subset (dieciséis; veintidós, veintitrés, veintiséis) requiring a written accent. From 30 onward, the fusion rule stops entirely: decades (treinta, cuarenta, cincuenta, sesenta, setenta, ochenta, noventa) combine with units via a mandatory "y" as three separate words (treinta y uno, cuarenta y dos — never a fused "treintaiuno"). Two further traps: uno apocopates to un before a masculine noun and una before a feminine noun even inside a compound number (veintiún años, treinta y una personas), and cien (used alone or directly before a noun for exactly 100) gives way to ciento inside any 101-199 compound (cien dólares vs. ciento diez dólares), with no "y" ever separating cien/ciento from a following ten.

  • Somos catorce en total, con primos y todo. — We're fourteen in total, counting cousins and everyone.
  • Tiene ochenta y siete años, pero todavía camina cinco kilómetros cada día. — She's eighty-seven, but she still walks five kilometers every day.
  • Cuesta cien pesos exactos. — It costs exactly one hundred pesos.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 3: 養生主 (Yǎngshēng Zhǔ) — "The Secret of Caring for Life" / "The Principle Governing the Nurture of Life"

The third of the Inner Chapters (內篇), following Essay 1 (逍遙遊) and Essay 2 (齊物論), and one of the shortest yet most concretely illustrated chapters in the entire text. It opens with a warning against two opposite excesses: pursuing knowledge without limit exhausts a finite life chasing an infinite goal, while chasing fame or avoiding all blame equally distorts a life meant to follow its own natural, unforced course — the guiding principle instead is yuan du yi wei jing (緣督以為經), "follow the central meridian as your constant," a metaphor for staying to the natural middle path rather than either extreme. The chapter's centerpiece, and the single most famous story in the Zhuangzi after the butterfly dream, is Cook Ding's Butchering of an Ox (庖丁解牛): summoned to demonstrate his craft before Lord Wenhui, the cook carves the animal with such rhythmic grace — his hands, shoulder, foot, and knee moving in what the ruler compares to a dance and a symphony — that Wenhui asks how mere skill could reach such heights. Cook Ding explains that he no longer sees the whole ox with his eyes at all, only perceives it with his spirit (神遇而不以目視), guiding his blade through the natural gaps and hollow spaces already present in the joints (依乎天理, 因其固然) rather than hacking through bone and sinew; because his knife's edge never actually strikes solid resistance, it has stayed razor-sharp for nineteen years and thousands of oxen, as if newly honed from the whetstone, while an ordinary cook's knife dulls within a month from hacking, and a good cook's within a year from cutting. Even so, Cook Ding notes, whenever he reaches a genuinely complex knot of tissue, he slows his movements, narrows his focus, and proceeds with the utmost caution until the difficulty falls away like clumps of earth crumbling to the ground — the story's deeper point being not effortless mastery in the sense of never encountering difficulty, but the cultivated art of following a thing's own inherent structure so completely that even its hardest parts eventually yield without force or self-injury, offered as a working metaphor for how to nurture and preserve one's own life (養生) inside a difficult world. The chapter closes with two shorter parables reinforcing the same theme — a one-legged commander whose mutilation Gongwen Xuan assumes must be Heaven's doing rather than man's, since accepting one's circumstances as natural preserves inner peace where resentment would not; and a swamp pheasant that must forage far and wide for every mouthful of food and drink, yet, Zhuangzi notes, would never wish to be caged and fed with ease, since a caged bird's body may thrive while its spirit does not — and the closing image comparing the body to firewood and the spirit/life-force to the fire it carries: individual logs are used up one by one, but the fire itself passes on and never comes to an end (指窮於為薪, 火傳也, 不知其盡也), a compact statement of Zhuangzi's view that what most needs preserving is not the finite physical vessel but the vital principle that outlives and transmits beyond any single body.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — The Netherlands

  • Location & territory size: A small, low-lying country in Northwestern Europe on the North Sea, bordered by Germany to the east and Belgium to the south, with roughly a quarter of its land area below sea level and protected by an extensive system of dikes, dunes, and pumping stations (a reality embedded in the country's own name — "the Low Countries"). Total land area is approximately 41,850 km², making it almost exactly the size of Switzerland (~41,285 km²) and slightly larger than Taiwan (~36,197 km²), despite the Netherlands packing a population more than 3.5 times Taiwan's onto comparable land, giving it one of the highest population densities in Europe.
  • Population: Approximately 18 million (2026 estimate), among the most densely populated countries in the world outside city-states, concentrated overwhelmingly in the "Randstad" conurbation ringing Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague, and Utrecht.
  • Religion: Increasingly secular — those identifying with no religion form the largest single group (roughly half the population), with Roman Catholicism (concentrated in the southern provinces) at around a fifth and Protestantism (historically Dutch Reformed/Calvinist, the faith of the 16th-century revolt against Catholic Spain) at roughly 15%, alongside a growing Muslim minority of about 5%, chiefly with Moroccan and Turkish immigrant roots.
  • Economy: A highly developed, trade-dependent economy built around the Port of Rotterdam (Europe's largest seaport), a founding Eurozone/EU member with the euro as currency, the world's second-largest exporter of food and agricultural products by value despite its small size (intensive greenhouse horticulture and dairy), and home to ASML, the sole global supplier of the extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography machines essential to manufacturing the most advanced semiconductor chips worldwide — giving this small country an outsized, strategically pivotal role in the global chip supply chain.
  • History: The seven northern provinces revolted against Catholic Habsburg Spain in the Eighty Years' War, formally uniting via the 1579 Union of Utrecht and gaining internationally recognized independence at the 1648 Peace of Münster (part of the wider Peace of Westphalia). The 17th century "Dutch Golden Age" that followed made the Netherlands a leading global maritime, trading, and artistic power (Rembrandt, Vermeer), driven substantially by the Dutch East India Company (VOC) — often cited as the world's first major multinational corporation and the first to issue tradable shares — which built a vast colonial trading empire, most consequentially over the Indonesian archipelago, ruled as the Dutch East Indies until Indonesian independence in 1949. Germany's occupation during World War II (1940-1945) devastated the country and its Jewish population; the postwar Netherlands became a founding member of NATO, the European Coal and Steel Community, and subsequently the EU, anchoring its modern identity in European integration alongside its Benelux neighbors.
  • Current leadership: King Willem-Alexander remains the ceremonial head of state. Prime Minister Rob Jetten, leader of the centrist/progressive-liberal D66 party, was sworn in on Feb 23, 2026 as the Netherlands' youngest-ever prime minister, heading a centre-to-centre-right minority coalition of D66, VVD, and CDA — the first Dutch minority government in decades, holding only 66 of 150 seats and thus requiring case-by-case negotiation with opposition parties to pass legislation — with Dilan Yeşilgöz and Bart van den Brink serving as deputy prime ministers.
  • Language: Dutch is the sole nationwide official language; West Frisian holds co-official status in the northern province of Friesland; English proficiency is exceptionally high and widely used in business, higher education, and daily public life.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Chocolate Rain

  • Name & origin: "Chocolate Rain" is a 2007 original song written and performed by American singer-songwriter Tay Zonday (born Adam Bahner), uploaded to YouTube on April 22, 2007, as a simple webcam recording of Zonday singing and playing keyboard alone in what appears to be a bedroom.
  • How it became popular: The video went viral almost entirely on the strength of the jarring mismatch between Zonday's youthful appearance and his unexpectedly deep, resonant bass-baritone singing voice, compounded by his distinctive performance mannerisms — a repeated backward head-tilt away from the microphone between phrases, deadpan expression, and minimalist single-take staging — which struck early YouTube audiences as simultaneously bizarre and mesmerizing at a moment (2007) when YouTube itself was barely two years old and "viral video" was still a novel cultural category.
  • Meaning & usage context: Despite its comedic viral packaging, the song's actual lyrics address institutional racism and social inequality in America ("Chocolate rain / Some stay dry and others feel the pain," a metaphor Zonday has explained represents unequal exposure to society's hardships) — a serious message largely lost on the audience that made it famous, who fixated almost entirely on the voice and visual mannerisms rather than the lyrical content, making it an early, oft-cited case study in how internet virality can strip a work's original intent entirely from its viral afterlife.
  • Variations & evolution: The video spawned a wave of parody and remix videos, including a widely-viewed "Auto-Tune the News"-style remix, comedic sung tributes/parodies by other YouTubers, and a real 2007 late-night television performance by Zonday on Jimmy Kimmel Live, which further cemented the song's crossover from pure internet artifact into mainstream pop-culture recognition.
  • Current status: Tay Zonday remains an active YouTuber and internet personality nearly two decades later, frequently discussing his own experience of unwanted viral fame with a self-aware, good-humored perspective; "Chocolate Rain" itself is now most commonly cited in retrospectives on the first wave of pre-2010 YouTube viral videos, alongside contemporaries like Numa Numa and Leave Britney Alone, as a foundational artifact of the platform's earliest viral-video era.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — The Passion of Joan of Arc (La Passion de Jeanne d'Arc, 1928, dir. Carl Theodor Dreyer)

  • Creator & era: A French silent film directed by Danish filmmaker Carl Theodor Dreyer, based directly on the surviving transcripts of Joan of Arc's actual 1431 heresy trial, starring stage actress Renée Jeanne Falconetti — in what became her only significant film role — as Joan.
  • Plot overview: The film compresses Joan's trial, interrogation, recantation under duress, retraction of that recantation, and execution by burning at the stake into a single day's dramatic action, staying almost entirely within the claustrophobic confines of the interrogation chamber and courtyard, focused overwhelmingly on faces rather than any external battlefield action from her military career.
  • Artistic style & technique: Dreyer builds the entire film from an unrelenting sequence of extreme close-ups — Falconetti performed almost entirely without makeup under harsh, unflattering lighting to capture raw, unmediated emotional anguish, while her clergical interrogators are shot in disorienting, often canted or off-axis angles that violate classical Hollywood composition norms of the era. Dreyer had many of the elaborate sets built in full, three-dimensional, white-painted detail specifically so the harsh diffuse studio light would fall naturally and evenly across every face, even though the vast majority of that architectural detail never appears within any single close-up frame.
  • Cultural & historical significance: Long considered one of the towering achievements of silent-era cinema and one of the greatest films ever made in any era, its original camera negative was destroyed in a 1928 fire at the UFA film studio in Berlin; a second version assembled by Dreyer from alternate takes was itself believed lost after a subsequent fire, and the film survived for decades only in significantly altered, censored, or lower-quality prints, until a complete, undamaged print of Dreyer's original cut was discovered by chance in 1981 in a janitor's closet at a Norwegian mental institution — one of film history's most celebrated archival rediscoveries.
  • Influence & legacy: Routinely ranked on critics' lists of the greatest films ever made, and credited with demonstrating, more thoroughly than any film before it, the close-up's capacity to function as its own independent expressive language rather than a mere framing choice — directly relevant to this routine's own Day 13 (Shot Scale / Size) film-analysis lesson, and an acknowledged influence on subsequent directors' use of the sustained facial close-up as a vehicle for interior, unspoken emotion.
  • Where to experience it today: Available via the Criterion Collection in its fully restored 1981-print form, frequently presented with a live or recorded musical score (several have been commissioned over the decades, since the film's original intended score was itself lost); a continued staple of silent-film retrospectives and film-school curricula worldwide.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Match Dissolve / Superimposed Cross-Dissolve as Transition (Lesson 53)

A new analytical lens distinct from Day 11's Match Cut & Graphic Match (an instantaneous hard cut joining two shots via a shared graphic shape, with no overlap between outgoing and incoming images) and Day 29's Ellipsis (a cut or dissolve that smooths over skipped story time, without requiring any positional/compositional rhyme between the two shots during the transition itself): this method asks whether a film uses a sustained cross-dissolve — the outgoing shot's image gradually fading out while the incoming shot's image simultaneously fades in, overlapping and superimposed for an extended beat rather than instantaneously replaced — to let a specific compositional element (a shape, a silhouette, a figure's position in frame) remain visually anchored and continuous across the transition even as everything else in the frame transforms around it. Check what element stays visually anchored through the overlap, whether the dissolve marks a simple scene change or is doing meaningful representational work (compressing time, marking a symbolic transformation, linking two otherwise-unconnected ideas), and whether the visible overlap period is brief and functional or extended and foregrounded as a directorial statement in its own right.

Worked example: The Wizard of Oz (1939, dir. Victor Fleming) — the film's most celebrated single transition, where young Dorothy Gale opens the door of her sepia-toned, black-and-white Kansas farmhouse interior and steps out into the vividly Technicolor world of Munchkinland. The transition is executed as an extended cross-dissolve rather than a hard cut: a sepia-toned stand-in double, framed in the doorway, is gradually dissolved out while Judy Garland, in full Technicolor, is simultaneously dissolved in at the same framed position, so the doorway's silhouette and Dorothy's central position within it remain the one constant, continuously-held anchor throughout the entire color transformation happening around her. The extended, deliberately lingered-on overlap — rather than an instantaneous switch — externalizes the sense of gradual, wondrous transition from the ordinary into the fantastic purely through the dissolve's own technical execution, with no dialogue needed to announce that Dorothy (and the audience) has left Kansas behind.

3)西班牙文課程——第 67 天(10 分鐘,數字 11 到 100:Los Números del 11 al 100)

回頭補上自第 6 天(0-10)之後就一直懸而未教的基礎缺口:11 到 100 完整的數字系統,是報價錢、講年齡、講電話號碼、講數量時不可或缺的內容。11-15(once、doce、trece、catorce、quince)完全不規則,必須個別背誦,無法從 0-10 推導。16-19 與 21-29 遵循可預測、但仍融合成一個字的規則——diez + i + 個位數(dieciséis、diecisiete、dieciocho、diecinueve)以及 veinti- + 個位數(veintiuno 到 veintinueve)——其中一小部分(dieciséis;veintidós、veintitrés、veintiséis)需要加上書寫重音符號。從 30 開始,融合規則完全終止:十位數(treinta、cuarenta、cincuenta、sesenta、setenta、ochenta、noventa)與個位數之間必須用「y」連接,寫成三個獨立的字(treinta y uno、cuarenta y dos——絕不融合成 "treintaiuno")。另外兩個陷阱:uno 即使在複合數字中,遇到陽性名詞仍會省尾音變成 un、遇到陰性名詞變成 una(veintiún años、treinta y una personas);而 cien(單獨使用,或直接接名詞表示剛好 100 時使用)在 101-199 之間的任何複合數字中,都必須改用 ciento(cien dólares 對比 ciento diez dólares),且 cien/ciento 與後面的十位數之間絕不會用「y」連接。

  • Somos catorce en total, con primos y todo. —— 我們家總共十四個人,包括表兄弟姊妹。
  • Tiene ochenta y siete años, pero todavía camina cinco kilómetros cada día. —— 她八十七歲了,但每天還是走五公里。
  • Cuesta cien pesos exactos. —— 剛好一百披索。

4)莊子——第 3 篇:養生主(Yǎngshēng Zhǔ)——「養生的根本原則」

《莊子》內篇第三篇,接續第 1 篇〈逍遙遊〉與第 2 篇〈齊物論〉之後,是全書篇幅最短、卻以最具體故事呈現核心思想的一篇。開篇即警告兩種相反的極端:無止盡追求知識,是以有限之生命追逐無窮之目標,終將徒勞而危;而一味追逐名聲或刻意迴避一切責難,同樣扭曲了原本應當順其自然、不受外力強加的人生——莊子提出的準則是「緣督以為經」,意即依循身體中央、居中不偏的脈絡作為恆常的準繩,比喻遵循自然中道,而非奔向任何一端的極端。本篇的核心、也是全書繼「莊周夢蝶」之後最著名的單一故事,是「庖丁解牛」:庖丁受召在文惠君面前展示技藝,他分解牛隻的動作節奏優美流暢——雙手、肩膀、雙腳、雙膝的動作,被文惠君形容宛如舞蹈、宛如音樂——文惠君不禁問道,單憑技術怎能達到如此境界?庖丁回答,他早已不再用眼睛去看整頭牛,而是「以神遇而不以目視」,順著牛體本身既有的天然紋理與空隙下刀(依乎天理,因其固然),而非硬生生劈砍骨頭與筋腱;正因為刀刃從未真正撞上堅硬的阻礙,這把刀已使用十九年、宰殺數千頭牛,依然鋒利如新磨——相較之下,一般廚師的刀一個月就會砍鈍,技術好一點的廚師的刀,一年也會因切割而變鈍。即便如此,庖丁也坦言,每當遇到真正盤根錯節、難以下手之處,他仍會放慢動作、集中注意力、極度謹慎地處理,直到那個難處如同土塊般崩解落地——這則故事更深一層的意涵,並非「毫不費力的絕對精熟」、彷彿從不遇上任何困難,而是一種培養出來的技藝:徹底順應事物本身固有的結構,即便是最艱難的部分,最終也能不靠蠻力、不傷及自身地迎刃而解,並以此作為在艱難世道中如何「養生」的實用比喻。本篇最後以兩則較短的寓言收尾,呼應同一主題——一位獨腳的將領,公文軒見狀,認定這必是天意所致,而非人為所致,因為坦然接受自身處境為自然天定,方能保有內心的平靜,怨懟反而做不到這一點;以及一隻澤雉,必須四處奔波才能覓得每一口飲食,但莊子指出,牠終究不會希望被關在籠子裡輕鬆餵養,因為籠中之鳥或許身體無虞,精神卻未必安適——篇末以「薪」與「火」作結:個別的木柴一根根燒盡,但火本身卻代代相傳,永不熄滅(指窮於為薪,火傳也,不知其盡也)——這句精煉的收束,道出莊子的核心觀點:真正需要保全的,並非有限的肉身軀殼,而是那超越、並傳遞於任何單一軀體之外的生命根本原理。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——荷蘭(The Netherlands)

  • 地理位置與領土規模:荷蘭是位於西北歐、瀕臨北海的低地小國,東與德國、南與比利時接壤,國土約有四分之一低於海平面,靠著綿密的堤防、沙丘與抽水站系統加以保護(這項事實正體現在其國名本身——「低地國」)。國土總面積約 41,850 平方公里,幾乎與瑞士(約 41,285 平方公里)完全相同,也略大於台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)——儘管面積相近,荷蘭人口卻是台灣的 3.5 倍以上,使其成為歐洲人口密度最高的國家之一。
  • 人口:約 1,800 萬人(2026 年估計值),是全球除城市國家外人口密度最高的國家之一,人口高度集中於環繞阿姆斯特丹、鹿特丹、海牙與烏特勒支的「蘭斯塔德」(Randstad)城市群。
  • 宗教:世俗化程度日益提高——無宗教信仰者是單一佔比最高的族群(約佔總人口一半),羅馬天主教(集中於南部各省)約佔五分之一,新教(歷史上源自 16 世紀對抗天主教西班牙統治的荷蘭歸正宗/喀爾文教派)約佔 15%,此外還有約 5% 、主要源自摩洛哥與土耳其移民背景的穆斯林少數族群,比例持續成長。
  • 經濟:屬高度已開發、高度依賴貿易的經濟體,以歐洲最大海港——鹿特丹港為核心,是歐元區/歐盟創始會員國,使用歐元;儘管國土面積不大,依出口值計算卻是全球第二大農產品出口國(高度集約化的溫室園藝與乳製品產業),同時也是艾司摩爾(ASML)的所在地——這家公司是全球唯一能製造「極紫外光」(EUV)微影設備的供應商,而這正是製造全球最先進半導體晶片不可或缺的關鍵設備,使這個小國在全球晶片供應鏈中,扮演著遠超其國土規模的關鍵戰略角色。
  • 歷史:荷蘭北部七省在「八十年戰爭」中反抗信奉天主教的哈布斯堡西班牙統治,於 1579 年簽署《烏特勒支同盟》正式結盟,並在 1648 年《明斯特和約》(《西發里亞和約》的一部分)中獲得國際承認的獨立地位。隨後的 17 世紀「荷蘭黃金時代」,使荷蘭一躍成為全球領先的海上強權、貿易強國與藝術重鎮(林布蘭、維梅爾),這段榮景很大程度上由荷蘭東印度公司(VOC)所驅動——這家公司常被視為全球第一家真正意義上的跨國企業,也是史上第一家發行可交易股票的公司——並建立起龐大的殖民貿易帝國,其中影響最深遠的,是對印尼群島的統治,以「荷屬東印度」之名統治,直到 1949 年印尼獨立為止。二次大戰期間(1940-1945)德國的佔領,重創了這個國家及其猶太人口;戰後荷蘭成為北約、歐洲煤鋼共同體,以及其後歐盟的創始會員國,與比荷盧鄰國一同,將自身現代身分深植於歐洲整合進程之中。
  • 現任領導人:威廉-亞歷山大國王(King Willem-Alexander)仍為國家名義上的元首。總理羅布・葉騰(Rob Jetten),中間偏進步自由派 D66 黨黨魁,已於 2026 年 2 月 23 日宣誓就職,成為荷蘭史上最年輕的總理,領導由 D66、VVD(自由民主人民黨)與 CDA(基督教民主呼籲黨)組成的中間偏中右少數執政聯盟——這是荷蘭數十年來首次出現的少數政府,在 150 席國會中僅掌握 66 席,因此每項法案都必須與在野黨個別協商才能通過;迪蘭・耶希爾格茲(Dilan Yeşilgöz)與巴特・范登布林克(Bart van den Brink)分別擔任副總理。
  • 語言:荷蘭語是全國唯一的官方語言;西弗里斯蘭語在北部弗里斯蘭省享有共同官方語言地位;英語能力極高,廣泛使用於商業、高等教育與日常公共生活中。

6)網路迷因介紹——巧克力雨(Chocolate Rain)

  • 名稱與起源:「Chocolate Rain」是美國創作歌手 Tay Zonday(本名 Adam Bahner)於 2007 年創作並演唱的原創歌曲,於 2007 年 4 月 22 日上傳至 YouTube,影片內容僅是他獨自一人在看似臥室的空間裡,對著網路攝影機彈奏鍵盤並演唱的簡單錄影。
  • 如何走紅:這支影片幾乎完全靠著 Zonday 年輕的外表,與他出乎意料、渾厚低沉的男低音歌聲之間強烈的反差而爆紅,再加上他獨特的表演習慣——在每個樂句之間,反覆將頭往後仰、遠離麥克風的動作、面無表情的神態,以及極簡的一鏡到底拍攝方式——這些元素,在 YouTube 本身才成立不到兩年、「病毒式影片」這個文化類別仍屬新奇事物的 2007 年,讓早期的觀眾覺得既古怪又深深著迷。
  • 意義與使用情境:儘管這支影片以喜劇式的病毒傳播形式廣為流傳,歌詞本身其實探討的是美國社會中的制度性種族歧視與社會不平等(「巧克力雨/有人得以倖免,有人卻承受苦痛」,Zonday 曾解釋這是比喻社會苦難分配不均的隱喻)——這個嚴肅的訊息,幾乎完全被讓這首歌爆紅的觀眾群所忽略,他們幾乎只聚焦在歌聲與視覺上的怪異舉止,而非歌詞內容本身,使這支影片成為網路病毒式傳播如何徹底剝離作品原始創作意圖、留下與原意脫節的病毒式「餘生」的早期、經常被引用的案例。
  • 變體與演進:這支影片催生了一波惡搞與混音重製影片,包括一支廣受觀看、風格類似「Auto-Tune the News」的自動調音混音版本、其他 YouTuber 的喜劇式翻唱致敬/惡搞版本,以及 Zonday 本人於 2007 年在深夜電視節目《吉米・金摩直播秀》(Jimmy Kimmel Live)上的真實現場演出——這場演出進一步鞏固了這首歌從單純的網路現象,跨越到主流流行文化認知範疇的地位。
  • 現況:將近二十年後的今天,Tay Zonday 依然活躍於 YouTube、持續擔任網路知名人物,經常以帶著自我調侃、幽默豁達的角度,談論自己這段非自願爆紅經驗;「Chocolate Rain」本身,如今最常被引用於回顧 2010 年以前 YouTube 第一波病毒式影片浪潮的討論之中,與 Numa Numa、Leave Britney Alone 等同期案例並列,被視為這個平台最早期病毒式影片時代的奠基性代表作之一。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《聖女貞德受難記》(La Passion de Jeanne d'Arc,1928,導演:卡爾・西奧多・德萊葉 Carl Theodor Dreyer)

  • 創作者與時代背景:一部法國默片,由丹麥籍導演卡爾・西奧多・德萊葉執導,直接取材自貞德 1431 年異端審判現存的法庭紀錄逐字稿,由舞台劇演員蕾妮・珍妮・法爾康涅蒂(Renée Jeanne Falconetti)飾演貞德——這也是她唯一一部具代表性的電影作品。
  • 劇情概述:本片將貞德的審判、審訊、遭脅迫下的悔罪認錯、隨後撤回悔罪,以及最終被綁上火刑柱處決的過程,壓縮呈現為單一一天內發生的戲劇性事件,場景幾乎完全侷限在令人窒息的審訊室與庭院之內,鏡頭絕大部分聚焦於人物臉部特寫,而非她軍事生涯中的任何戰場場面。
  • 藝術風格與技法:德萊葉以一連串毫不間斷的極端特寫鏡頭,構築起全片的視覺骨幹——法爾康涅蒂幾乎素顏演出,在刺眼、毫不修飾美化的燈光下,捕捉最原始、未經修飾的情緒煎熬,而審訊她的神職人員們,則多以令人不安、時常傾斜或偏離軸線的角度拍攝,刻意違反當時古典好萊塢構圖的既定規範。德萊葉刻意將許多精緻的場景,以完整立體、漆成純白的細節搭建出來,目的是讓工作室裡刺眼而漫射的燈光,能自然均勻地灑落在每一張臉孔上——即使這些建築細節絕大多數,從未真正出現在任何一個特寫鏡頭的畫面之中。
  • 文化與歷史意義:長期以來被公認是默片時代最偉大的成就之一,也是各個時代電影史上數一數二的傑作;本片原始底片,已於 1928 年在柏林 UFA 電影製片廠的一場大火中焚毀;德萊葉本人以其他備用鏡次重新剪輯的第二個版本,後來也一度被認為在另一場大火中佚失,本片有數十年的時間,僅以經過大幅改動、審查刪剪或畫質低劣的版本流傳於世,直到 1981 年,一份完整無損、德萊葉原始剪輯版的拷貝,意外在一間挪威精神病院的工友儲藏室中被發現——這是電影史上最為人稱道的檔案重新發現案例之一。
  • 影響與傳承:本片經常被列入影評人評選的影史最偉大電影名單之中,並被認為是史上第一部如此徹底地展現出「特寫鏡頭本身足以構成一套獨立表達語言,而非僅僅是一種取景選擇」的電影——這與本例行公事第 13 天(景別:特寫與遠景)的看片方法課程直接相關,也公認影響了後世導演,將長時間持續的臉部特寫,作為傳達內在、未言明情緒的表現手法。
  • 今日可觀賞管道:目前可透過 Criterion Collection 觀賞其 1981 年拷貝的完整修復版本,通常搭配現場或錄製配樂演出(由於本片原本預定的配樂本身也已佚失,數十年來已有多個委託創作的配樂版本問世);至今仍是全球默片回顧展與電影學校課程中的常見片單。

8)看片方法——疊化轉場/疊影交融剪輯(Match Dissolve / Superimposed Cross-Dissolve)(第 53 課)

與第 11 課的「跳接對位與圖形匹配剪輯」(一種瞬間完成的硬剪接,透過共享的圖形形狀連接兩個鏡頭,前後畫面之間沒有重疊)以及第 29 課的「省略剪輯」(一種剪接或疊化手法,用來平滑帶過故事中被省略的時間,但轉場當下不要求兩個鏡頭之間有任何位置或構圖上的呼應)皆截然不同的全新分析角度:這項技法探討的是,一部電影是否使用了持續一段時間的「疊化」(cross-dissolve)——前一個鏡頭的畫面逐漸淡出,同時後一個鏡頭的畫面同步淡入,兩者在畫面中重疊、共存一段可被察覺的時間,而非瞬間切換取代——藉此讓某個特定的構圖元素(某個形狀、某個剪影、某個人物在畫面中的位置)在整個轉場過程中,持續維持視覺上的定錨與連續性,即便畫面中其他一切都在同時產生變化。判斷重點包括:哪個元素在疊化重疊期間持續維持視覺定錨;這次疊化只是單純的場景轉換,還是承載了具體的敘事意涵(壓縮時間、標記某種象徵性的轉變、連結兩個原本互不相關的概念);以及這段可見的重疊時間,是簡短而功能性的,還是被刻意拉長、當作導演自身的一種風格宣示。

實例演練:《綠野仙蹤》(The Wizard of Oz,1939,導演:維克多・弗萊明)——本片最著名的單一轉場鏡頭:小女孩桃樂絲・蓋爾打開她那間色調呈棕褐色、黑白畫面呈現的堪薩斯農舍室內大門,踏入色彩鮮豔的技術彩色(Technicolor)芒奇金國度世界。這段轉場並非以硬剪接完成,而是以一段拉長的疊化處理:一位身穿棕褐色調服裝、站在門框位置的替身演員,畫面逐漸淡出,與此同時,身穿全彩技術彩色戲服的茱蒂・嘉蘭(Judy Garland),在完全相同的取景位置同步淡入——使得整個門框的剪影輪廓,以及桃樂絲本人在畫面中央的位置,成為周遭色彩徹底轉變過程中,唯一持續不變、貫穿始終的視覺定錨點。這段刻意拉長、讓觀眾清楚察覺的重疊過程,而非瞬間切換,單靠疊化這項技術本身的執行方式,便外化了從平凡世界逐步過渡到奇幻世界那種漸進、充滿驚奇的感受——完全不需要任何台詞,便向觀眾(以及桃樂絲本人)宣告:堪薩斯,已經被拋在身後了。
