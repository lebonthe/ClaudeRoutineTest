3) Spanish Lesson — Day 55 (10 min, Negative & Indefinite Words: algo/nada, alguien/nadie, alguno/ninguno, siempre/nunca, también/tampoco)

Today steps outside verb tenses/moods entirely to cover five paired indefinite/negative words Spanish learners need from day one but rarely get taught systematically: algo/nada (something/nothing, for things), alguien/nadie (someone/no one, for people), alguno/ninguno (some/none, shortened to algún/ningún before a masculine singular noun), siempre/nunca (always/never), and también/tampoco (also/neither, echoing an affirmative vs. negative statement). The headline grammar point is Spanish's mandatory double negation, the opposite of English's "two negatives make a positive": a negative word placed after the verb requires "no" before the verb (No veo a nadie), while a negative word placed before the verb takes no additional "no" (Nadie viene) — the two never combine, and a clause is never left with zero negative marking before the verb either.

  • ¿Hay alguien en casa? — Is anyone home?
  • No, no hay nadie. Y tampoco hay nada en la nevera. — No, there's no one. And there's nothing in the fridge either.
  • No tengo ningún problema con eso. — I have no problem with that at all.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 55: 豐 (Fēng) — "Abundance" (Fullness) (Thunder over Fire: Zhèn 震 above, Lí 離 below — 雷火豐). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the connecting thread from yesterday's hexagram: "得其所歸者必大,故受之以豐;豐者,大也。" ("One who has found where they properly belong must become great; therefore Guī Mèi is followed by Fēng. Fēng means greatness/abundance.") Hexagram 54 warned against a union or advance entered into from impulsive attraction, with its elements badly out of position; having now examined what happens when the underlying order is correct instead — a "return" or "arrival" properly and legitimately reached — the Sequence moves naturally to the state that follows: having arrived at one's rightful place, one can now flourish, expand, and reach a peak of greatness. The Judgment is a striking blend of promise and warning: "豐,亨,王假之,勿憂,宜日中。" ("Abundance has success. The king approaches it. Be not sad; you should be like the sun at midday.") A king (or ruler) is uniquely positioned to "reach" or sustain a state of abundance, since only someone with sufficient resources, authority, and clarity of vision can hold such a peak without being overwhelmed by it; the comparison to the sun at noon captures abundance as a moment of maximum brightness and power. But the trigram structure already signals what happens next: Lí (fire/lightning, clarity/intelligence) sits below, with Zhèn (thunder, arousing movement) above it — clarity in the ruler paired with vigorous action produces the condition of greatness, yet a sun at its zenith has nowhere to go from that exact point but down. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) makes this explicit: "豐,大也。明以動,故豐。王假之,尚大也。勿憂,宜日中,宜照天下也。日中則昃,月盈則食,天地盈虛,與時消息,而況於人乎?況於鬼神乎?" ("Fēng means greatness. Clarity joined with movement is what produces abundance. 'The king approaches it' — because he values greatness. 'Be not sad; you should be like the sun at midday' — one should shine upon all under heaven. Yet when the sun reaches its zenith, it begins to decline; when the moon becomes full, it begins to wane. The fullness and emptiness of Heaven and Earth wax and wane together with time — how much more so, then, for human affairs? How much more so for spirits and gods?") This is the Yijing's classic teaching on the impermanence of peak states: the very moment a thing reaches its fullest greatness is also the moment decline necessarily begins, a pattern the commentary insists applies universally — to nature, to human fortune, and even to unseen forces. The Image commentary reads: "雷電皆至,豐;君子以折獄致刑。" ("Thunder and lightning both arrive together: the image of Abundance. Thus the superior man decides lawsuits and carries out punishments.") Thunder (decisive action) and lightning/fire (clear illumination) arriving together model the two qualities a ruler needs most at a moment of peak power and visibility: the clarity to see a matter fully, and the decisiveness to act on it firmly — here applied specifically to the administration of justice, where both swift judgment and illuminating insight are required together, neither alone being sufficient. Read against Hexagram 54's warning about impulsive, wrongly-positioned advance, Fēng completes the pair by showing the opposite case brought to its logical peak — but immediately tempers that success with the Yijing's characteristic caution that greatness itself is never a stable resting point, only a phase in an ongoing cycle of waxing and waning.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Uzbekistan (烏茲別克)

  • Location: A Central Asian country and one of only two "doubly landlocked" countries in the world (a landlocked country surrounded entirely by other landlocked countries; the other is Liechtenstein, already featured in this series). Borders Kazakhstan to the north and northwest, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan to the east and southeast, Afghanistan to the south, and Turkmenistan to the southwest. Capital: Tashkent.
  • Territory: About 447,400 km² — roughly the size of Sweden (~450,300 km²), somewhat larger than California (~424,000 km²), and roughly 12 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²). The most populous of the five Central Asian "-stan" republics.
  • Population: Roughly 37-38 million as of the mid-2020s, the largest population of any Central Asian state and one of the youngest and fastest-growing populations in the region.
  • Religion: Overwhelmingly Sunni Muslim (well over 90% of the population), reflecting the region's centuries as a hub of Islamic scholarship along the Silk Road; the state constitution is officially secular, and a small Russian Orthodox Christian minority remains, concentrated among the ethnic Russian population.
  • Economy: Historically built around Soviet-era cotton monoculture (once dubbed the region's "white gold"), the economy today is diversifying into natural gas, gold mining, textiles, and a rapidly growing tourism sector centered on the ancient Silk Road cities of Samarkand, Bukhara, and Khiva. Since 2016, sweeping economic reforms — currency liberalization, reduced forced-labor practices in the cotton sector, and new openness to foreign investment — have marked a significant break from the more closed, state-controlled economic model of the earlier post-independence decades.
  • History: A crossroads of the ancient Silk Road for over two millennia, with Samarkand and Bukhara ranking among Central Asia's oldest continuously inhabited cities; conquered by Alexander the Great's forces (as part of ancient Sogdiana) and later ruled by a succession of Persianate and Turkic empires. Its golden age arrived under Timur (Tamerlane) in the 14th century, whose Timurid Empire, centered on Samarkand, became a renowned hub of art, architecture, and science (Timur's grandson Ulugh Beg built one of the era's great astronomical observatories there). The Russian Empire conquered the region in the 19th century; it became the Uzbek Soviet Socialist Republic in 1924 and gained independence in 1991 upon the USSR's dissolution. Independent Uzbekistan was ruled for a quarter-century under the authoritarian leadership of Islam Karimov (1991-2016); his death in 2016 opened the way for the current period of gradual political and economic opening.
  • Current leader: President Shavkat Mirziyoyev, in office since 2016, has pursued a program of economic liberalization and cautious diplomatic re-engagement with neighboring Central Asian states, while maintaining a political system critics still describe as authoritarian with limited genuine electoral competition.
  • Language: Uzbek, a Turkic language of the Karluk branch (closely related to Uyghur), is the sole official language, written in a Latin-based script since a post-independence transition away from Cyrillic. Russian remains widely spoken as a lingua franca in business, government, and urban life, and Tajik (a Persian language) is spoken by significant minorities, especially in and around Samarkand and Bukhara.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (Mirziyoyev)

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Crying Jordan (Michael Jordan Crying Meme)

  • Name/origin: "Crying Jordan" is a reaction-image meme built from a Getty Images photograph (taken by photographer Jonathan Daniel) of basketball legend Michael Jordan crying during his emotional 2009 speech at his induction into the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame — a moment of pride and joy, not distress, at the time it was captured.
  • How it became popular: Around 2012-2015, internet users began Photoshopping Jordan's distinctive tear-streaked, scrunched-up face onto photos of athletes, teams, and public figures experiencing embarrassing failures or losses. The format exploded in visibility during the 2015 NFL and NBA offseasons, when sports media outlets and Twitter/Instagram accounts (some dedicated entirely to "Crying Jordan" edits) turned it into an informal running joke — dubbed "Crying Jordan Season" — applied to virtually every notable sports collapse of the year.
  • Meaning/usage: The meme mocks a person, team, or entity after a failure, blunder, or humiliating defeat by superimposing Jordan's crying face onto their image, casting them as "the ultimate loser." Its comedic power rests partly on a deliberate irony: the original photo captured a moment of triumph and emotional pride, not sadness, so the meme's entire negative meaning is a complete inversion of the source image's real context — a pattern shared with other iconic reappropriated reaction images.
  • Variations/evolution: Countless Photoshop templates circulated pasting the face onto athletes' bodies, jerseys, and magazine covers; informal "Crying Jordan Czar" rankings and brackets became an annual tradition each NFL/NBA offseason to crown the year's most embarrassing sports moment; usage eventually spread beyond sports into general schadenfreude-reaction contexts online.
  • Current status: Past its 2015-2016 viral peak but still a widely recognized fixture of 2010s sports-internet culture, occasionally revived nostalgically during major sports collapses or in retrospectives on the "golden age" of Photoshop reaction memes.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Pansori (판소리, Korean Narrative Singing)

  • Name/key figures: Pansori is a traditional Korean genre of musical storytelling performed by a single vocalist (sorikkun) accompanied by one drummer (gosu) playing a barrel-shaped drum called the buk. The genre was significantly codified and preserved in the 19th century by the scholar-patron Sin Jae-hyo, who is credited with editing and standardizing the surviving repertoire into its canonical written form.
  • Era/background: Emerged among commoners in Korea's southwestern Jeolla region around the 17th century, rooted in local shamanistic ritual singing and oral folk-narrative traditions; originally performed in open-air markets for common audiences, pansori was later also cultivated as refined entertainment for the yangban aristocracy, giving it a rare cultural reach across Joseon Dynasty class lines.
  • Plot/content overview: Of the many pansori narratives once performed, only five complete madang (repertoire pieces) survive today: Chunhyangga (a love story testing class-crossing fidelity between a nobleman's son and a lower-status woman's daughter), Simcheongga, Heungbuga, Sugungga, and Jeokbyeokga. Each is an extended narrative — a single performance can run several hours — blending spoken narration (aniri), sung passages (chang), and stylized expressive gesture and fan-work (ballim).
  • Artistic style and techniques: Defined by a uniquely rough, husky vocal technique called tongseong, traditionally cultivated through years of grueling vocal training (legendarily including singing for hours beside waterfalls) to deliberately roughen and deepen the voice into a resonant, emotionally weighted tone. The drummer sets the piece's rhythmic cycle (jangdan) and punctuates the performance with vocal interjections of encouragement (chuimsae, such as "eoulssigu!" or "jota!"), actively shaping the emotional pacing alongside the singer rather than merely keeping time.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as one of the purest expressions of han — the distinctively Korean aesthetic of deep, often unresolved sorrow and longing — pansori is also notable as a genuinely cross-class performance tradition, moving fluidly between marketplace and court audiences in a way few other traditional Korean art forms did.
  • Influence and legacy: Pansori is the direct ancestor of changgeuk, a multi-performer operatic adaptation of pansori stories developed in the early 20th century, and has influenced modern Korean cinema and music — most famously Im Kwon-taek's acclaimed 1993 film Sopyonje, centered on a family of itinerant pansori performers. UNESCO proclaimed pansori a "Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity" in 2003, later formalized on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2008.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Actively preserved and taught today through South Korea's National Gugak Center and dedicated pansori schools; master performers (myeongchang) can be officially recognized as holders of Important Intangible Cultural Heritage status (Korea's "Living National Treasure" designation), and pansori remains regularly performed in both traditional and contemporary/experimental staged productions across South Korea.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, ich.unesco.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Match on Action (Lesson 41)

Match on action is a cut made in the middle of a continuous physical movement, timed so precisely against that movement's momentum that the action appears to carry on seamlessly across the cut — even though the two shots may differ in angle, distance, or lens, and may even come from entirely separate takes, camera setups, or stunt performers stitched together as one fluid gesture. It is distinct from Day 11's Match Cut & Graphic Match, which joins two shots via a shared graphic shape or motion vector specifically to foreground the edit itself as a deliberate visual rhyme (match on action instead tries to hide the cut inside the motion, making the edit as invisible as possible); and distinct from Day 9's Continuity Editing & the 180-Degree Rule, which addresses maintaining a consistent camera axis and screen direction across a dialogue exchange between facing speakers, rather than cutting mid-motion within a single continuous action. When you spot a match-on-action cut, check whether it lines up frame-accurately (a true match) or leaves a small, jarring discontinuity (which becomes a jump cut instead, a distinct device already covered); whether it's used mainly for coverage — varying shot scale or angle mid-action purely to keep a sequence visually dynamic without breaking its flow — or for a more expressive purpose, such as compressing or intensifying the perceived speed and urgency of the action by trimming a few frames at the cut point; and whether the "single continuous action" being shown was in fact filmed as one uninterrupted event, or is an illusion carefully assembled from separate shots/stunts that only becomes one fluid movement in the edit.

Worked example: Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981, dir. Steven Spielberg) — the giant-boulder chase in Peru. As Indiana Jones sprints from the rolling boulder, the sequence cuts rapidly among wide shots of the boulder itself, medium shots of Indy's full-body sprint, and low, ground-level angles — dozens of cuts in total, each timed to match his running stride and forward momentum precisely at the cut point, so his sprint reads as one unbroken continuous action despite the constantly shifting camera position and distance. The technique here serves two purposes at once: it provides the dynamic, ever-changing coverage needed to sell the boulder's massive scale and immediate danger from multiple vantage points, while the frame-accurate matching on his stride keeps the escape's kinetic energy and mounting urgency completely uninterrupted — proof that varying the camera constantly and preserving a feeling of continuous, escalating motion are not in tension, so long as the cuts are matched precisely on the action itself.

3)西班牙文課程——第 55 天(10 分鐘,否定詞與不定詞:algo/nada, alguien/nadie, alguno/ninguno, siempre/nunca, también/tampoco)

今天完全跳出動詞時態/語氣的主線,補齊西語學習者從第一天起就會用到、卻很少被系統性教過的五組不定詞/否定詞對照:algo/nada(某事物/什麼都沒有,用於事物)、alguien/nadie(某人/沒有人,用於人)、alguno/ninguno(某個/沒有任何一個,放在陽性單數名詞前分別縮寫為 algún/ningún)、siempre/nunca(總是/從不),以及también/tampoco(也是/也不是,分別呼應肯定句與否定句)。本課的重點文法,是西語強制規定的「雙重否定」規則——恰與英文「兩個否定等於肯定」的直覺相反:否定詞若放在動詞之後,動詞前必須加上 no(No veo a nadie);否定詞若放在動詞之前,則不可再加 no(Nadie viene)——兩者絕不會同時出現在動詞前,動詞前也絕不會完全沒有任何否定標記。

  • ¿Hay alguien en casa? —— 家裡有人在嗎?
  • No, no hay nadie. Y tampoco hay nada en la nevera. —— 沒有,一個人都沒有。而且冰箱裡也什麼都沒有。
  • No tengo ningún problema con eso. —— 我對這件事完全沒有任何意見。

4)易經卦象

第 55 卦:豐 (Fēng) ——「豐」(豐盛、盛大)(雷火豐——震上離下:上卦為雷〔震〕,下卦為火〔離〕)。《序卦傳》承接前一卦而來:「得其所歸者必大,故受之以豐;豐者,大也。」(凡是找到了自己應有歸宿的事物,必然會走向壯大興盛,因此歸妹卦之後,接的是豐卦;豐,就是壯大、豐盛的意思。)第 54 卦警告的是出於一時衝動、各爻位置錯亂而貿然推進的結合;如今探討的則是相反的情況——當「歸宿」或「抵達」是以正當、合宜的方式達成時,順著《序卦傳》的邏輯自然會走向下一個階段:既已抵達應有的歸屬之處,便能進一步興盛、擴展,達到壯大豐盛的頂點。卦辭同時交織著期許與警示,語氣格外鮮明:「豐,亨,王假之,勿憂,宜日中。」(豐卦,亨通。君王能夠成就、保有這樣的盛況。不必憂慮,應當如日正當中一般〔光明普照〕。)唯有君王(或居於統治地位者)才具備足夠的資源、權威與清明的眼光,能夠成就並維繫豐盛之局而不被其反噬;以「如日正中天」為喻,正捕捉了豐盛作為光明與力量都達到頂點那一刻的意象。然而卦象的組成結構,早已暗示了接下來會發生的事:離卦(火、光明、智慧)居下,震卦(雷、躁動的振動)居上——君王的清明智慧,搭配積極果決的行動,共同造就了壯盛之局;然而太陽一旦行至正中天,自此刻開始便只會逐漸西斜下沉,別無他途。《彖傳》將這一點說得更加明白:「豐,大也。明以動,故豐。王假之,尚大也。勿憂,宜日中,宜照天下也。日中則昃,月盈則食,天地盈虛,與時消息,而況於人乎?況於鬼神乎?」(豐,就是壯大的意思。清明的智慧搭配積極的行動,才造就了豐盛壯大之局。「君王能夠成就這樣的盛況」,是因為他重視、崇尚壯大興盛;「不必憂慮,應當如日正當中」,是因為理應普照天下萬物。然而太陽一旦行至正中,便開始西斜;月亮一旦盈滿,便開始虧缺;天地萬物的盈盛與虧虛,總是隨著時序推移而此消彼長——何況是人世間的際遇呢?又何況是鬼神之事呢?)這正是《易經》對「顛峰狀態必然無法長存」這一經典教誨的具體闡述:事物達到最壯盛豐盈的那一刻,恰恰也正是衰退必然開始的那一刻,而這段文字強調,這個規律普遍適用——無論是自然萬物、人世際遇,乃至於幽冥鬼神之事,概莫能外。《象傳》寫道:「雷電皆至,豐;君子以折獄致刑。」(雷聲與電光〔火光〕同時降臨,這就是豐卦的卦象。因此君子藉此領悟,應當以此態度來裁決訟案、施行刑罰。)雷(果決的行動)與電、火(清晰的照明)同時出現,恰恰示範了身處權力與能見度都達到頂點之際的統治者,最需要具備的兩項特質:徹底看清事物全貌的清明智慧,以及堅定不移、付諸行動的果決態度——這裡特別將此原則應用於司法審判之上,唯有敏捷果決的判斷,搭配洞察入微的清明智慧,兩者缺一不可,方能勝任裁決之責。若與第 54 卦「因衝動、位置失當而貿然推進」的警示對照來看,豐卦恰恰呈現了相反情況被推向邏輯頂點之後的樣貌——但《易經》一貫的審慎態度,也隨即為這份成功加上但書:壯盛豐盈本身,終究不是一個能夠恆常安穩棲身的終點,而僅僅是盈虛消長、循環不息過程當中的一個階段而已。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——烏茲別克(Uzbekistan)

  • 位置:位於中亞的內陸國家,也是全世界僅有兩個「雙重內陸國」之一(意即這個內陸國本身,四周邊境全部與其他內陸國相鄰,不與任何濱海國家接壤;另一個雙重內陸國是本系列已介紹過的列支敦斯登)。北側與西北側與哈薩克接壤,東側與東南側與吉爾吉斯、塔吉克為鄰,南側與阿富汗交界,西南側則與土庫曼相接。首都:塔什干(Tashkent)。
  • 領土:總面積約 447,400 平方公里——大致與瑞典(約 450,300 平方公里)相當,略大於美國加州(約 424,000 平方公里),約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 12 倍。是中亞五個「-斯坦」共和國中人口最多的國家。
  • 人口:截至 2020 年代中期,人口約 3,700 萬至 3,800 萬人,是中亞地區人口最多的國家,也是該地區人口結構最年輕、成長最快速的國家之一。
  • 宗教:絕大多數人口信仰遜尼派伊斯蘭教(比例遠高於 90%),反映出這片土地做為絲路沿線伊斯蘭學術重鎮長達數世紀的歷史背景;該國憲法明定為世俗國家,境內仍存在一小群俄羅斯東正教少數族群,主要集中於境內的俄裔人口之中。
  • 經濟:歷史上曾以蘇聯時代的棉花單一作物經濟為主體(一度被稱為該地區的「白色黃金」),如今經濟結構正逐步朝天然氣、黃金開採、紡織業,以及以撒馬爾罕(Samarkand)、布哈拉(Bukhara)、希瓦(Khiva)等古絲路名城為核心、快速成長的觀光產業多元發展。自 2016 年起,該國推動一連串大幅度經濟改革——包括貨幣自由化、減少棉花產業中的強迫勞動情事,以及對外國投資敞開大門——與獨立初期數十年間較為封閉、由國家全面主導的經濟模式,形成顯著分野。
  • 歷史:兩千餘年來始終是古絲路的重要交會樞紐,撒馬爾罕與布哈拉更是中亞地區歷史最悠久、持續有人居住至今的古城之列;曾為亞歷山大大帝的軍隊所征服(當時屬古粟特〔Sogdiana〕地區的一部分),其後歷經一連串波斯語系與突厥語系帝國先後統治。14 世紀在帖木兒(Tamerlane)治下迎來黃金時代,以撒馬爾罕為中心的帖木兒帝國,成為當時舉世聞名的藝術、建築與科學重鎮(帖木兒之孫兀魯伯〔Ulugh Beg〕更在當地建造了那個時代最偉大的天文台之一)。19 世紀遭俄羅斯帝國征服;1924 年成為蘇聯加盟共和國之一的烏茲別克蘇維埃社會主義共和國,並於 1991 年蘇聯解體之際獲得獨立。獨立後的烏茲別克,在伊斯蘭·卡里莫夫(Islam Karimov)威權統治下長達四分之一個世紀(1991 至 2016 年);其於 2016 年辭世,為該國此後逐步邁向政治與經濟開放的階段開啟契機。
  • 現任領導人:總統沙夫卡特·米爾濟約耶夫(Shavkat Mirziyoyev),自 2016 年上任以來,推動一系列經濟自由化措施,並謹慎地與中亞鄰國重新展開外交往來;然而批評人士仍認為,該國政治體制本質上依然帶有威權色彩,選舉競爭的實質開放程度相當有限。
  • 語言:烏茲別克語(Uzbek)屬突厥語系卡爾魯克語支(與維吾爾語關係密切),是該國唯一的官方語言,獨立後已由西里爾字母改採拉丁字母書寫。俄語在商業、政府部門與都市生活中,仍廣泛做為通用語言使用;塔吉克語(屬波斯語系)則在撒馬爾罕與布哈拉一帶,由為數不少的少數族群所使用。

6)網路迷因介紹——哭泣喬丹(Crying Jordan,麥可喬丹哭泣迷因)

  • 名稱/起源:「Crying Jordan」(哭泣喬丹)是一則反應圖迷因,取材自蓋帝圖像(Getty Images)攝影師強納森·丹尼爾(Jonathan Daniel)所拍攝的一張照片——籃球傳奇人物麥可·喬丹(Michael Jordan)於 2009 年奈史密斯籃球名人堂(Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame)動人入選演說中落淚的畫面。在拍攝當下,這其實是一個因驕傲與喜悅而落淚的時刻,而非悲傷或痛苦的瞬間。
  • 如何走紅:約在 2012 年至 2015 年間,網路使用者開始將喬丹那張淚流滿面、五官糾結的招牌表情,合成到各種因難堪失敗或落敗而出糗的運動員、球隊與公眾人物的照片之上。這股風潮在 2015 年 NFL 與 NBA 的休賽季期間迅速擴散開來,當時體育媒體與 Twitter、Instagram 上的帳號(甚至有專門帳號整個以「哭泣喬丹」合成圖為主題)將其發展成一個非正式的年度玩笑傳統——被稱為「Crying Jordan Season」(哭泣喬丹季)——幾乎套用在該年度所有知名的體壇崩盤事件之上。
  • 意義/使用情境:這則迷因藉由將喬丹的哭泣臉孔合成到某個人物、球隊或機構的照片上,嘲諷其遭逢的失敗、失誤或難堪的落敗經歷,將對方塑造成「最徹底的輸家」。其幽默效果有部分正是建立在刻意的反諷之上:原始照片捕捉的其實是一個充滿驕傲與喜悅的勝利時刻,而非悲傷,因此這則迷因所承載的負面意涵,其實完全顛覆了原始照片的真實脈絡——這種「脈絡完全翻轉」的手法,與其他經典的反應圖迷因有著相似的運作邏輯。
  • 變體/演化:網路上流傳著無數把這張哭臉合成到運動員身體、球衣乃至雜誌封面上的 Photoshop 模板;非正式的「哭泣喬丹沙皇」(Crying Jordan Czar)排行榜與淘汰賽制,更成為每年 NFL/NBA 休賽季票選年度最難堪體壇事件的固定傳統;其使用範圍後來也逐漸跳脫體育領域,擴散至網路上泛用的「幸災樂禍反應」情境。
  • 目前狀態:雖已過了 2015 至 2016 年病毒式擴散的高峰期,但至今仍是 2010 年代體壇網路文化中廣為人知的經典符號,偶爾仍會在重大體壇崩盤事件發生時被懷舊式地重新翻出使用,或在回顧「Photoshop 反應圖迷因黃金年代」的文章中被引用。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——盤索里(판소리,韓國敘事說唱)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:盤索里(Pansori)是韓國傳統的敘事說唱藝術形式,由一名歌手(sorikkun,「唱者」)獨自演唱,搭配一名鼓手(gosu)以桶狀的「鼓」(buk)伴奏。19 世紀時,學者兼贊助者申在孝(Sin Jae-hyo)對這門藝術的傳承與規範化貢獻卓著,一般認為正是他編輯、整理了現存曲目,使其定型為今日通行的標準文本形式。
  • 時代/背景:大約於 17 世紀左右,發源自韓國西南部全羅道地區的平民百姓之間,根源可追溯至當地薩滿信仰的儀式性歌唱,以及口傳民間敘事傳統;最初是在露天市集為一般庶民大眾演出,其後也逐漸發展成為受兩班貴族階層青睞的精緻藝術形式,使其成為朝鮮王朝時期少數能夠跨越階級界線、廣受各階層觀眾喜愛的傳統藝術形式之一。
  • 劇情/內容概述:盤索里過去曾有為數眾多的敘事曲目,但流傳至今、完整保存下來的「madang」(完整劇目)僅剩五部:《春香歌》(Chunhyangga,描述一位貴族公子與地位低下女子之女之間,一段跨越階級藩籬、考驗忠貞的愛情故事)、《沈清歌》(Simcheongga)、《興夫歌》(Heungbuga)、《水宮歌》(Sugungga)與《赤壁歌》(Jeokbyeokga)。每一部都是篇幅浩大的長篇敘事——單場演出往往長達數小時——融合口白敘述(aniri)、歌唱段落(chang),以及帶有程式化風格的肢體表演與扇子動作(ballim)。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:其最大特色,在於一種被稱為「通聲」(tongseong)的獨特粗獷嘶啞唱腔,傳統上須經過多年極其艱苦的嗓音訓練(相傳包括連續數小時在瀑布旁引吭高歌)才能練就,刻意將嗓音磨練得粗糙渾厚,進而發展出一種飽含深沉情感重量的共鳴音色。鼓手負責設定該曲目的節奏循環(jangdan),並透過口頭吆喝的鼓勵性插話(chuimsae,例如「얼씨구!」〔eoulssigu!〕或「좋다!」〔jota!〕)點綴全場演出——鼓手在此扮演的,並非單純打拍子的角色,而是與歌手一同,積極主動地共同形塑整場演出的情感節奏與起伏。
  • 文化/歷史意義:盤索里普遍被視為「恨」(han)——一種韓國文化中特有的、深沉且往往難以化解的悲愁與眷戀情感美學——最純粹的藝術表現形式之一;同時,它也是少數真正能夠橫跨社會階級、同時遊走於市井庶民與宮廷貴族觀眾之間的韓國傳統藝術形式,這一點在其他傳統韓國藝術中相當罕見。
  • 影響與傳承:盤索里是「唱劇」(changgeuk)的直接源頭——這是一種於 20 世紀初發展而成、由多位表演者共同演出盤索里故事的歌劇化改編形式;其影響力也延伸至現代韓國電影與音樂領域,最著名的例子即是林權澤(Im Kwon-taek)於 1993 年推出、廣受讚譽的電影《西便制》(Sopyonje),描述一個以流浪賣藝維生的盤索里演唱世家的故事。聯合國教科文組織(UNESCO)已於 2003 年將盤索里宣布為「人類口述與非物質遺產傑作」,並於 2008 年正式將其列入《人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄》。
  • 現況/如何觀賞:如今盤索里仍透過南韓國立國樂院(National Gugak Center)以及各地專門的盤索里傳承機構,持續獲得積極保存與傳承;技藝精湛的表演大師(myeongchang)可獲官方正式認定為「重要非物質文化財」保有者(即韓國所稱的「人間文化財」/「人間國寶」);盤索里至今仍定期於南韓各地,以傳統形式與當代/實驗性舞台改編等多元形式持續公演。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、ich.unesco.org

8)看片方法——動作匹配剪輯 Match on Action(第 41 課)

動作匹配剪輯(match on action)是指:在一段連續進行中的肢體動作正中央進行剪接,並將剪接點精準對應該動作的運動軌跡與力道時機,使得動作看起來能夠跨越剪接點、完全無縫地延續下去——即使前後兩個鏡頭在角度、距離或鏡頭焦段上截然不同,甚至可能來自完全不同的拍攝場次、機位設置,或由不同的替身演員拍攝、再於剪接時拼接成一段看似渾然一體的流暢動作。此手法不同於第 11 天所教的「跳接對位與圖形匹配剪輯」(Match Cut & Graphic Match)——後者是透過兩個鏡頭之間共享的圖形形狀或運動向量來銜接,目的正是要凸顯剪接動作本身,將其呈現為一次刻意為之的視覺呼應/韻腳;動作匹配剪輯則恰恰相反,是要把剪接點藏進動作本身之中,盡可能讓剪接的存在感降到最低、幾乎讓人察覺不到。它也不同於第 9 天的「連戲剪輯與 180 度線規則」,後者處理的是對話場景中,如何在兩位面對面說話的角色之間交替剪接,同時維持一致的攝影機軸線與方向,而非在單一連續動作「進行中」剪接。發現動作匹配剪輯時,可以檢視:剪接點是否真的做到逐格精準對位(真正的「匹配」),還是留下了一絲突兀、不連貫的跳動感(這樣一來便會落入另一種已介紹過的技巧「跳接」);這個手法主要是用於「補充鏡位」——單純為了在動作進行中變換景別或角度、讓場面在不打斷流暢度的前提下保持視覺上的動態感——還是帶有更進一步的表現企圖,例如透過在剪接點刻意裁減幾格畫面,壓縮或加劇動作給人的速度感與急迫感;以及畫面中呈現的「單一連續動作」,究竟是真的一鏡到底、不間斷拍攝完成的真實事件,還是其實是由多個分開拍攝的鏡頭/特技動作,經由精心剪接、拼湊而成,唯有在剪接完成後才顯得渾然一體的錯覺。

實例演練:《法櫃奇兵》(Raiders of the Lost Ark,1981,導演:史蒂芬·史匹柏〔Steven Spielberg〕)——祕魯古廟中那場經典的巨石追逐戲。當印第安納·瓊斯(Indiana Jones)沒命似地從滾動而來的巨石前狂奔逃命時,這段場面在巨石本身的遠景鏡頭、印第瓊斯全身入鏡的中景奔跑鏡頭,以及貼近地面的低角度鏡頭之間快速交替剪接——總計多達數十次剪接,每一次剪接點都精準對應他奔跑的步伐節奏與前衝力道,因此儘管攝影機的位置與距離不斷變換,他的這段狂奔在觀眾眼中,仍讀取為一段完全沒有中斷、渾然一體的連續動作。這項技巧在此同時發揮了兩種效果:一方面提供了足夠豐富多變的鏡位涵蓋範圍,得以從多個角度充分展現巨石駭人的龐大體積與迫在眉睫的致命威脅;另一方面,逐格精準對位在他奔跑步伐上的剪接手法,又讓這場逃命戲的動能與節節升高的緊迫感,自始至終完全沒有被打斷——證明了「不斷變換攝影機位置」與「維持一段連續、步步升高的動作流暢感」這兩者,其實並不互相牴觸,只要每一次剪接,都精準地對位在動作本身之上即可。
