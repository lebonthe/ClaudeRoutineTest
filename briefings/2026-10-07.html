3) Spanish Lesson — Day 83: Seguir + Gerund (still doing something)

Seguir + a gerund (-ando / -iendo) means "to keep doing" or "to still be doing" something that began earlier: Sigo trabajando = "I'm still working." Seguir is an e→i stem-changing verb with a spelling rule in the first person: sigo, sigues, sigue, seguimos, seguís, siguen; gerund siguiendo. Related patterns: seguir sin + infinitive ("still not doing"): Sigo sin entender; seguir + adjective ("still in a state"): Sigue enfermo; and seguir meaning "to follow / continue along": Sigue todo recto. It overlaps with todavía / aún (Day 74) and with continuar + gerund, which is more formal.

  • ¿Sigues trabajando? — Are you still working?
  • Sigo sin terminar el informe. — I still haven't finished the report.
  • Mi abuela sigue viviendo en Tainan. — My grandmother still lives in Tainan.
  • Sigue todo recto y gira a la derecha. — Go straight ahead and turn right.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 19: 達生 (Dá Shēng) — "Mastering Life"

The next chapter after "Perfect Happiness" in the Outer Chapters (外篇). It asks how a person can fully understand life and fate, and answers mostly through stories of skill. It opens with Lie Zi asking the border guard Guan Yin how the perfect person can walk under water without choking and tread fire without being burned. Guan Yin replies that this is not cleverness, skill or courage but guarding one's pure vital breath (qi) and keeping one's nature whole.

A drunk man who falls from a carriage may be bruised but not killed, because his spirit is intact: he did not know he was riding and did not know he fell, so fear never entered him. Confucius meets a hunchback catching cicadas with a sticky pole as effortlessly as picking them up; the man explains that he trained by balancing balls on the pole until his focus was unbroken, so that "though heaven and earth are vast and the ten thousand things many, I attend only to the cicada's wings." A ferryman tells Yan Hui that skilled swimmers handle the whirlpool as if it were dry land, and that archers who play for tiles shoot well, for belt buckles nervously, and for gold clumsily: the skill is the same, but concern for the prize divides the mind.

The woodcarver Qing makes bell stands so marvelous people think spirits made them. His method: he fasts to still his heart, first forgetting reward and rank, then praise and blame, finally his own body; then he enters the mountain forest, finds the tree whose natural form already fits the stand, and "matches Heaven with Heaven." King Xuan's cockfight trainer Ji Xingzi spends forty days: at ten days the bird is still vain and proud, at twenty it still reacts to sounds and shadows, at thirty it still glares fiercely, and at forty it is "nearly like a wooden chicken," its virtue complete, so that other cocks turn and run. Finally, Confucius watches a man swim calmly in the churning waters below a thirty-rèn waterfall; the man says he enters with the swirl and leaves with the surge, following the way of the water without selfishness.

Takeaway: The highest skill is not strain but self-forgetting: when attention is undivided and one fits one's nature to the nature of the task, even danger and competition stop being obstacles.

  • Location & territory size: A transcontinental country: about 97% of its land is in Anatolia (Asia Minor) and a small part, East Thrace, is in southeastern Europe, separated by the Bosphorus, Sea of Marmara and Dardanelles.   • History: Home to some of the world's oldest monuments (Göbekli Tepe, around 9500 BC; Çatalhöyük), the Hittites and Troy, then Greek, Persian and Roman rule. Constantinople became the Roman/Byzantine capital in 330 AD. The Seljuk Turks won at Manzikert in 1071, opening Anatolia to Turkic settlement; the Ottoman state emerged around 1299 and Mehmed II took Constantinople in 1453; the empire peaked under Suleiman the Magnificent in the 16th century and ended after World War I. After the War of Independence (1919-23), Mustafa Kemal Atatürk proclaimed the Republic on Oct 29, 1923 (the 103rd anniversary is three weeks away). Multi-party politics began in 1946, it joined NATO in 1952, and military coups occurred in 1960, 1971 and 1980. The Justice and Development Party (AKP) has governed since 2002.
  • Current leaders (last verified; not re-checked today): President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan (prime minister 2003-14, president since 2014, re-elected in 2023); Vice President Cevdet Yılmaz.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Planking

  • Name: Planking (also "the Lying Down Game").
  • Origin / history: The idea of lying rigid, face-down, in an unusual public place and photographing it is credited to two friends in England who say they began it in the 1980s-90s and called it the "Lying Down Game" (the dates in various accounts differ). It became a web trend when photos were posted on Facebook groups around 2008.
  • How it became popular: It exploded in Australia in 2010-11, when Facebook pages, photo contests and news coverage turned it into a competition for the most absurd location (rooftops, car bonnets, escalator rails, ledges). Celebrities and sports stars joined, and it spread to the UK, US and Asia in 2011.
  • Meaning & usage: The point is the photo: a stiff, straight body held rigid like a plank, laid across an incongruous place, and a deadpan caption. It was a low-cost, low-skill social-media game ("anyone can do it").
  • Variations / evolution: "Owling" (crouching like an owl), "batmanning" (hanging upside-down), "horsemanning" (headless-horseman pose), "coning" and "Tebowing" (2011), all following the "named pose + weird location" template. Later came the "Harlem Shake" and the "Ice Bucket Challenge," which belong to the same "do it, film it, tag a friend" lineage.
  • The dark side: Because the best photos required risky spots, the craze produced injuries and at least one widely reported death in Australia in 2011 (a man who fell from a balcony), which led to public warnings and sharp media backlash.
  • Current status: It faded after 2012 and is now a nostalgia reference for early-2010s social media. Today the word "plank" mostly means the core-strength exercise, which is unrelated to the meme.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 69: Weather & Landscape as Emotion (Pathetic Fallacy)

The method: Ask what the weather, light and landscape are "feeling." The critic John Ruskin coined "pathetic fallacy" (1856) for the habit of giving nature the emotions of the person looking at it, as in storms for anger or sunshine for joy. In film, directors use rain, fog, snow, heat or an empty landscape to externalize a character's inner state or to state a theme without dialogue. The strongest uses either match the mood (rain at a funeral) or deliberately contradict it (a sunny day at a tragedy), which creates irony.

How to analyze a scene: (1) Name the weather and time of day and compare it with the previous scene; is it new or recurring? (2) Ask whose emotion it expresses: a character's, the audience's, or the film's own commentary. (3) Check whether it matches or contradicts the mood. (4) Look at how it is photographed: backlit rain, diffuse fog and high-contrast sunlight each change the feel. (5) Listen for whether sound design (thunder, wind, rain on a roof) works as the scene's score. (6) Notice when the weather changes, since the shift often marks the turn in the story.

Worked example — Blade Runner (1982, dir. Ridley Scott), Roy Batty's death on the rooftop: The whole film takes place in a permanently dark, rainy Los Angeles, so the rain is a standing symbol of a wearying, wet world. In the finale, the dying replicant Roy Batty (Rutger Hauer) sits on a rooftop in the downpour, holding a dove and a nail he drove through his own hand to keep from seizing up. His speech ("I've seen things you people wouldn't believe... all those moments will be lost in time, like tears in rain") makes the rain his own tears, and the rain literally washes away his memories. As he says "Time to die" and releases the white dove into the sky, the rain, the dove and the shaft of light turn the weather from oppressive to mournful and almost sacred. Watch how Scott keeps Roy's face backlit and wet, so the water and the face become one image.

Compare: Singin' in the Rain (1952) reverses the logic: Gene Kelly dances in a downpour because he is happy, so the rain contradicts its usual meaning. Seeing the two together shows that the technique works whichever way the weather is bent.

4) 莊子 — 第 19 篇:達生(Dá Shēng)—「通達生命」

〈至樂〉之後的下一篇,屬於外篇。它探討人如何徹底通達生命與命運,答案主要透過一連串「技藝」的故事來講。開篇是列子問關尹:至人為什麼能潛行水底而不窒息、踏火而不灼熱?關尹回答,這不是聰明、技巧或勇氣,而是守住純粹的氣、保全本性。

醉酒的人從車上摔下,或許受傷卻不致死,因為他的神是完整的:他不知道自己坐在車上,也不知道自己摔下,恐懼根本進不了心。孔子遇到駝背的人用黏竿捕蟬,輕鬆得像撿東西一樣;那人說他先練習在竿頭疊放圓丸直到專注不散,於是「雖天地之大,萬物之多,而唯蜩翼之知」。擺渡人告訴顏淵,善游的人對漩渦如履平地;又說以瓦片為賭注射箭能射得巧,以帶鉤為賭注就緊張,以黃金為賭注就笨拙:技巧一樣,但對獎賞的掛念使心分散。

木匠梓慶做的鐻(樂器架)精妙到人們以為是鬼神所作。他的方法是齋戒以靜心,先忘掉賞賜爵祿,再忘掉毀譽,最後忘了自己的身體;然後進山林,找到形體天然就合於鐻的樹木,「以天合天」。周宣王的鬥雞訓練師紀渻子訓了四十天:十天時雞仍虛驕恃氣,二十天仍會對聲音與影子起反應,三十天仍怒目而視,四十天「幾矣」,「望之似木雞」,德已全備,別的雞見了都轉身逃走。最後,孔子看到有人在三十仞高瀑布下的激流中悠游,那人說他隨著漩渦入水、隨著湧浪出水,順著水的道,不帶私心。

重點:最高的技藝不是用力,而是忘我:專注不被分散,讓自己的本性與事物的本性相合,連危險與競爭也不再是阻礙。

5) 國家/地區介紹 — 土耳其(Türkiye / Turkey)

  • 位置與面積:橫跨歐亞的國家:約 97% 的國土在安那托利亞(小亞細亞),一小部分(東色雷斯)位於東南歐,以博斯普魯斯海峽、馬爾馬拉海與達達尼爾海峽相隔。與希臘、保加利亞、喬治亞、亞美尼亞、亞塞拜然(納希切萬)、伊朗、伊拉克與敘利亞接壤,面臨黑海、愛琴海與地中海。面積約 78.4 萬平方公里,約為台灣(3.6 萬平方公里)的 21-22 倍、日本的約 2 倍、比美國德州略大,也是昨天介紹的希臘的近 6 倍。
  • 人口:約 8,500-8,600 萬,約為台灣的 3.7 倍。最大城市伊斯坦堡(都會區約 1,500-1,600 萬,是歐洲最大的都會區之一);首都是安卡拉(約 500-600 萬)。
  • 宗教:約 99% 的人口登記為穆斯林,多數為遜尼派(哈乃斐學派),另有人數不小的阿列維派少數(估計差異大,約 10-20%),以及少數基督徒與猶太人。國家自 1920-30 年代改革起正式採世俗主義,但宗教在公共生活中的角色比 20 世紀中葉更大。
  • 語言:土耳其語(突厥語系,自 1928 年凱末爾改革起改用拉丁字母,取代阿拉伯文字);庫德語為人數眾多的少數語言,另有阿拉伯語、扎扎語等。
  • 經濟:G20 成員,GDP 約 1.3-1.4 兆美元,支柱包括汽車與電子製造、紡織成衣、營建與觀光(每年 5,000 萬以上遊客);自 1995-96 年起與歐盟有關稅同盟。里拉貶值與極高的通膨(2022 年高峰約 85%,之後仍居高)是長期問題。
  • 歷史:擁有世界最古老的遺址之一(哥貝克力石陣,約西元前 9500 年;恰塔霍裕克),還有西台人與特洛伊,後來歷經希臘、波斯與羅馬統治。330 年君士坦丁堡成為羅馬/拜占庭首都。1071 年塞爾柱突厥人在曼齊克特獲勝,開啟突厥人遷入安那托利亞;鄂圖曼國家約在 1299 年興起,1453 年穆罕默德二世攻下君士坦丁堡;帝國在 16 世紀蘇萊曼大帝時期達到巔峰,第一次世界大戰後瓦解。經過獨立戰爭(1919-23),穆斯塔法·凱末爾(阿塔圖克)於 1923 年 10 月 29 日宣布共和國成立(三週後是 103 週年)。1946 年開始多黨政治,1952 年加入北約,1960、1971、1980 年發生軍事政變。公正與發展黨(AKP)自 2002 年起執政。
  • 現任領導人(最後確認;今日未重新確認):總統雷傑普·塔伊普·艾爾多安(2003-14 年任總理,2014 年起任總統,2023 年連任);副總統傑夫代特·伊爾馬茲。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 平板撐/Planking

  • 名稱:Planking(又稱「Lying Down Game」,臥倒遊戲)。
  • 起源/歷史:在不尋常的公共場所筆直僵硬地面朝下趴著並拍照,這個點子被歸功於英格蘭的兩位朋友,他們說自己在 1980-90 年代就開始玩,並稱之為「Lying Down Game」(各種說法的年份不一)。約 2008 年起,照片被貼到 Facebook 社團,才成為網路潮流。
  • 如何爆紅:2010-11 年在澳洲爆發,Facebook 粉絲頁、照片比賽與新聞報導把它變成比「誰的地點最荒謬」的競賽(屋頂、車前蓋、電扶梯扶手、窗台)。名人與運動明星加入,2011 年擴散到英國、美國與亞洲。
  • 意義與使用情境:重點在照片:像木板一樣僵直的身體,橫放在格格不入的地方,配上一本正經的說明。它是低成本、低技巧的社群遊戲(「人人都能玩」)。
  • 變體/演變:「Owling」(像貓頭鷹蹲著)、「batmanning」(倒掛)、「horsemanning」(無頭騎士姿勢)、「coning」和「Tebowing」(2011),都沿用「命名姿勢 + 怪地點」的模板。之後的「哈林搖」與「冰桶挑戰」屬於同一個「做、拍、標記朋友」的系譜。
  • 黑暗面:因為好照片需要危險的地點,這股熱潮造成不少受傷,2011 年澳洲還有至少一起廣受報導的死亡事故(一名男子從陽台墜落),引發公開警告與媒體強烈反彈。
  • 目前狀態:2012 年後逐漸退燒,現在是 2010 年代初社群媒體的懷舊梗。今天「plank」一詞多半指核心肌群運動「平板支撐」,與迷因無關。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《偉大的莫臥兒》Mughal-e-Azam(1960,導演 K. 阿西夫)

  • 關鍵人物:導演 K. 阿西夫;出資製片夏普爾吉·帕隆吉(Shapoorji Pallonji);作曲納烏沙德,作詞沙基爾·巴達尤尼;主演普里特維拉吉·卡普爾(阿克巴大帝)、迪利普·庫馬爾(薩利姆王子)、馬杜芭拉(阿娜卡莉)、杜爾加·科特(約達巴伊王后)。卡薩克舞編舞為拉楚·馬哈拉吉,古典名歌手巴德·古拉姆·阿里汗為配樂演唱了兩首歌。
  • 年代/背景:來自孟買的印地-烏爾都語史詩電影,取材於阿娜卡莉的傳說(1922 年因蒂亞茲·阿里·塔吉的劇作而廣為人知)。1940 年代開始拍攝的第一版因印巴分治而中斷,影片重拍後歷經約十年製作,於 1960 年 8 月上映,以奢華預算與完美主義聞名。
  • 劇情:在阿克巴大帝的莫臥兒宮廷裡,薩利姆王子愛上宮廷舞者阿娜卡莉。阿克巴認為這門婚事有辱王朝,把她囚禁;薩利姆反抗父親並發動戰爭。影片以曖昧的方式結尾:阿克巴在一項秘密協議中饒了阿娜卡莉一命,她從此消失於歷史。故事是愛情與責任、個人自由與帝國權力的衝突。
  • 風格與技法:龐大的佈景與數千名臨時演員出演戰爭場面;莊重、富詩意的烏爾都語對白;植根古典拉格(raga)的配樂。核心段落是馬杜芭拉在鏡宮(Sheesh Mahal)演唱的〈Pyar Kiya To Darna Kya〉,鏡子將她的身影成倍反射,成為倔強的宣言。影片大部分以黑白拍攝,首映版加入少數彩色段落。
  • 文化意義與影響:它在當時成為印度票房最高的電影,並保持約 15 年,直到 1975 年的《火燒》(Sholay)。它被視為寶萊塢「黃金年代」的里程碑,也是奢華歷史史詩的典範。
  • 現今如何欣賞:2004 年有完整上色、數位修復版在影院重新上映(之後推出家用影音與串流,各地區上架情況不同)。大型舞台音樂劇《Mughal-e-Azam: The Musical》(導演費羅茲·阿巴斯·汗)於 2016 年在孟買首演,並曾在印度及海外巡演。

8) 看片方法 — 第 69 課:天氣與風景作為情緒(感情誤置/Pathetic Fallacy)

方法:問問天氣、光線與風景在「感受」什麼。評論家約翰·羅斯金在 1856 年創造「pathetic fallacy」(感情誤置)一詞,形容把觀看者的情緒賦予自然的習慣,例如風暴象徵憤怒、陽光象徵喜悅。在電影裡,導演用雨、霧、雪、酷熱或空曠的風景,把角色的內心狀態外化,或不靠對白就點出主題。最有力的用法不是配合情緒(葬禮上下雨),就是刻意反其道而行(悲劇發生在晴天),製造反諷。

如何分析一場戲:(1) 說出天氣與時間,和前一場戲比較;是新的還是反覆出現?(2) 問它表達誰的情緒:角色的、觀眾的,還是電影自己的評論。(3) 檢查它與情緒是相符還是相反。(4) 看它如何被拍攝:逆光的雨、漫射的霧與高對比的陽光,感受各不相同。(5) 聽音效設計(雷聲、風聲、雨打屋頂)是否就是這場戲的配樂。(6) 留意天氣何時改變,因為轉變往往標示著故事的轉折點。

實例 —《銀翼殺手》(Blade Runner,1982,雷利·史考特)羅伊·巴提在屋頂的死亡:整部片發生在永遠昏暗多雨的洛杉磯,因此雨是這個令人疲憊、潮濕世界的固定象徵。在結尾,垂死的複製人羅伊·巴提(魯格·豪爾)坐在大雨中的屋頂,手裡抱著一隻鴿子,還有一根他自己釘穿手掌、防止身體僵直的釘子。他的獨白(「我見過你們人類無法相信的事……所有這些時刻都將消逝在時間裡,就像雨中的淚水」)把雨變成他自己的眼淚,而雨也真的沖走了他的記憶。當他說出「該死了」並把白鴿放向天空時,雨、鴿子與那道光柱讓天氣從壓迫轉為哀悼,甚至帶有幾分神聖。留意史考特讓羅伊的臉始終逆光且被雨淋濕,使水與臉合成同一個畫面。

對照:《萬花嬉春》(Singin' in the Rain,1952)反轉了這個邏輯:金·凱利因為快樂而在傾盆大雨中跳舞,雨與它慣常的意義相反。把兩部片放在一起看,就知道這個手法不論往哪個方向扭轉天氣都有效。
