3) Spanish Lesson — Day 37 (10 min, completes the "Si" conditional system alongside Day 36)

Day 36 formalized the counterfactual "Type 2" conditional (Si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional simple) and explicitly flagged its real/likely counterpart, "Type 1," for a future lesson — today delivers it. Type 1 conditional sentences describe situations that are real, likely, habitual, or genuinely open in the future (not hypothetical or contrary to fact): the si-clause always takes the present indicative — never the subjunctive in any form — and the result clause can take the present indicative (a general rule or habit), the future simple (Day 23, a specific one-time promise or prediction), or the imperative (Day 25/26, direct advice or a command). This is the sharpest possible contrast with yesterday's lesson: same connector word "si," completely different grammatical logic, chosen entirely by whether the speaker is describing something real/possible or something hypothetical/false.

  • Si llueve mañana, no salimos. — If it rains tomorrow, we won't go out. (present indicative result: a general plan/rule)
  • Si terminas la tarea, te llevaré al cine. — If you finish your homework, I'll take you to the movies. (future simple result: a specific one-time promise)
  • Si tienes hambre, come algo. — If you're hungry, eat something. (imperative result: direct advice)

Grammar note: the si-clause in every Type 1 sentence uses the present indicative (llueve, terminas, tienes) and nothing else — never si llueva, never si tendrías. The three possible result-clause moods aren't interchangeable filler; they signal different nuances (habitual rule vs. one-time future promise vs. direct command), but all three share the same si-clause requirement. Mixing Type 1's present-indicative si-clause with Type 2's conditional-simple result clause (or vice versa) is a common but ungrammatical hybrid — the two systems taught on Days 36 and 37 must not be blended.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 37: 家人 (Jiā Rén) — "The Family" (The Clan) (Wind above, Fire below — Xùn over Lí). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the direct line from yesterday's Hexagram 36: "傷於外者必反於家,故受之以家人。" ("One who is injured from without must inevitably turn back toward the home; hence Míng Yí [Darkening of the Light] is followed by Jiā Rén [The Family].") Where Hexagram 36 showed King Wen and Jizi concealing their inner brilliance to survive a hostile outer world, Hexagram 37 picks up exactly where that retreat leads: back to the household, the one sphere where order can still be safely built and preserved. The Judgment is compact: "家人,利女貞。" ("The Family: favorable for the woman to be steadfast and correct.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) expands this into one of the Yijing's most quoted statements on social order: "家人,女正位乎內,男正位乎外,男女正,天地之大義也。家人有嚴君焉,父母之謂也。父父,子子,兄兄,弟弟,夫夫,婦婦,而家道正;正家而天下定矣。" ("In the family, the woman holds her correct place within, the man his correct place without; that man and woman are each correct in their place is the great principle of Heaven and Earth. The family has its stern rulers — this refers to the parents. When the father is truly a father, the son truly a son, the elder brother truly an elder brother, the younger brother truly a younger brother, the husband truly a husband, and the wife truly a wife, then the way of the family is correct; and when the family is set right, the whole world is thereby settled.") This is the same logic later formalized by Confucius as the "rectification of names" (正名) — that social harmony depends on each role being genuinely lived up to, not merely held by title — and the Tuan's leap from "correct family" directly to "the world settled" makes explicit the classical Chinese political idea that the household is the smallest working unit of the state, and that no larger order can be built on top of a disordered one. The Image commentary draws a more private, personal-conduct lesson from the same trigram picture: "風自火出,家人;君子以言有物而行有恆。" ("Wind issues forth from fire: this is the image of The Family. Thus the superior person's words have substance, and their conduct has constancy.") Wind (Xùn) naturally arises from the heat of fire (Lí) below it — an image of influence radiating outward from a well-tended inner source, exactly as a household's spoken values and habitual conduct radiate outward, by example rather than decree, into wider society. Read together with yesterday's Míng Yí, the pair forms a complete arc: light that must retreat and hide itself under an oppressive outer world (36) finds, in the very next hexagram, the one place where that inner clarity can be rebuilt into a stable, self-sustaining order (37) — the family not as a retreat from responsibility, but as the foundation from which any larger order must ultimately be built.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Qatar (卡達)

  • Location: A small peninsula jutting north from the Arabian Peninsula into the Persian Gulf, bordering only Saudi Arabia to the south, with the United Arab Emirates and Bahrain nearby across the water and Iran facing it from across the Gulf.
  • Territory: About 11,571 km² — roughly 32% the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), meaning Taiwan is a little over three times larger; comparable in area to the US state of Connecticut.
  • Population: Approximately 3 million, but only around 10-12% are Qatari citizens — the large majority are foreign migrant workers, mostly from South and Southeast Asia, drawn by the construction, energy, and services sectors.
  • Religion: Islam is the official state religion, followed by the large majority of citizens (predominantly Sunni, with significant Wahhabi/Salafi influence); the large non-citizen expatriate population also privately practices Hinduism, Christianity, Buddhism, and other faiths, though public worship outside Islam is tightly restricted.
  • Economy: One of the highest GDP-per-capita figures in the world, built overwhelmingly on liquefied natural gas (LNG) and oil exports — Qatar shares the world's largest natural gas field (the North Field/South Pars) with Iran, and its state-owned QatarEnergy is one of the planet's largest LNG exporters. The sovereign wealth fund Qatar Investment Authority recycles hydrocarbon revenue into global investments, and the state has pursued economic diversification (finance, aviation via Qatar Airways, sports/tourism) under its "Qatar National Vision 2030" plan, partly accelerated by hosting the 2022 FIFA World Cup.
  • History: Long under the influence of regional and Ottoman powers, Qatar came under British protection in the early 20th century; the Al Thani family, ruling locally since the mid-19th century, became the recognized ruling dynasty. The discovery of oil in the 1940s transformed a small pearling-and-fishing economy, and Qatar gained full independence from Britain in 1971. In 1995, then-Crown Prince Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani took power from his father in a bloodless palace coup and drove Qatar's rapid modernization and global media/sports profile (including founding Al Jazeera); he abdicated peacefully in 2013 in favor of his son. From 2017-2021, Qatar was subjected to a land, air, and sea blockade by Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain, and Egypt over accusations of supporting terrorism and overly close ties to Iran and Turkey — a rift resolved by the January 2021 Al-Ula agreement.
  • Current leader: Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, head of state since his father's abdication in June 2013.
  • Language: Arabic is the official language; English is very widely used in business, education, and daily life given the large expatriate workforce.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Drakeposting" (Drake Hotline Bling Meme)

  • Name: "Drakeposting," also known simply as the "Drake meme" or "Drake Hotline Bling meme."
  • Origin/history: The template is built from two screenshots of Canadian rapper Drake in the music video for his 2015 single "Hotline Bling" (directed by Director X): in one, Drake grimaces and turns away with a dismissive hand gesture; in the other, he smiles and points approvingly toward the camera.
  • How it became popular: The video premiered in October 2015, and by early-to-mid 2016 Twitter and Instagram users had cropped the two contrasting shots into a stacked two-panel (later four-panel) comparison format; it spread rapidly because the "rejecting/approving" structure could be dropped onto virtually any two-option comparison with zero editing skill required, and it was further amplified by countless parody edits of the dance moves in the original video.
  • Meaning/usage: The top panel (Drake recoiling/rejecting) is paired with a labeled option the poster wants to mock or dismiss, and the bottom panel (Drake pointing/approving) is paired with the option being endorsed instead — used to express a preference, make a joke about bad vs. good decisions, or satirize hypocrisy by pairing a rejected "sensible" option against an approved "self-indulgent" or absurd one.
  • Variations/evolution: Expanded into four- and six-panel versions for more complex comparisons; countless "reskins" swap in other reacting figures while preserving the reject/approve structure (a lineage that also fed into later hierarchical-comparison formats such as "Expanding Brain"); ironic variants show Drake reacting identically to both panels to mock false equivalence; the two source stills are also widely used standalone as reaction images outside the comparison format entirely.
  • Current status: Still in casual, everyday use as of 2026 as one of the internet's most durable "comparison" template formats, alongside Distracted Boyfriend and Trollface — a decade after the original video's release it functions as much as a recognizable "classic" meme grammar as a fresh joke vehicle, regularly remixed rather than retired.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Kunqu Opera (崑曲, Kūnqǔ)

  • Name/key figures: Kunqu (崑曲, also Kunju 崑劇), literally "Kunshan tune," named for its birthplace of Kunshan (崑山) near Suzhou in Jiangsu province. Its refined singing style is credited to musician Wei Liangfu (魏良輔), who codified the "Kunshan melody" (崑山腔) in the mid-16th century; its single most celebrated work, "The Peony Pavilion" (牡丹亭), was written by playwright Tang Xianzu (湯顯祖, 1550-1616), often called China's Shakespeare-era dramatic master.
  • Era/background: Kunqu crystallized in the mid-to-late Ming dynasty (16th century) and, for roughly three centuries, was the dominant, most prestigious form of Chinese theatre among the scholar-official literati class — until the 19th century, when the rising, more populist Peking opera (Jingju) eclipsed it in popularity, absorbing much of Kunqu's music theory and repertoire structure in the process.
  • Plot/content overview: Kunqu's repertoire draws on classical Chinese poetry, romance, and history; "The Peony Pavilion" tells of Du Liniang, a young woman who falls in love with a scholar in a dream, dies of lovesickness, and is ultimately resurrected by the power of that love — a famously supernatural, deeply lyrical plot built almost entirely around interior longing rather than external action.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Sung in a slow, highly ornamented, melismatic style accompanied principally by the bamboo transverse flute (曲笛), rather than the bowed jinghu fiddle central to Peking opera; librettos are drawn directly from classical shi/ci poetic forms, prized as much for literary reading as for stage performance; movement is minimalist and exquisitely stylized, and plays follow the multi-act "chuanqi" (傳奇) structure.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely called "the mother of a hundred operas" (百戲之母), since nearly every later regional Chinese opera form — including Peking opera itself — borrowed its melodic theory, role-type conventions, and repertoire from Kunqu; UNESCO named it a "Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity" in 2001, the first Chinese performing art so recognized.
  • Influence and legacy: Its vocal music and role-type system became the shared technical foundation nearly every later Chinese opera genre built upon; the form nearly died out by the mid-20th century (disrupted by war and the Cultural Revolution, with only a handful of troupes surviving), but was revived through state patronage, and more recently through novelist Pai Hsien-yung's (白先勇) celebrated 2004 "Young Lovers' Edition" (青春版) of The Peony Pavilion, which introduced Kunqu to a new, younger, cross-strait audience.
  • Current status/where to experience it: A handful of professional Kunqu troupes remain active across Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Shanghai, Beijing, and Hunan (China's "seven major Kunqu companies"), performing at dedicated theatres and festivals; the "Young Lovers' Edition" has toured internationally, including Taiwan, keeping the form's most famous work in active circulation even as it remains an endangered but actively preserved UNESCO heritage art.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, ich.unesco.org (UNESCO)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Adaptation Analysis: Comparing a Film to Its Source Material (Lesson 23)

Adaptation analysis is the practice of evaluating a film not by asking "how faithful is it to the book?" but by asking what its specific changes — omissions, additions, point-of-view shifts, and ending changes — reveal about what cinema as a medium can do that prose cannot, and about what the filmmakers actually cared about preserving. "Fidelity" to plot events is the least useful yardstick, since film and prose are fundamentally different storytelling technologies. Three things to look for: first, point-of-view translation — a novel's interior narration (a character's private thoughts) has no direct film equivalent, so adaptations must externalize psychology into something visual (performance, space, editing) or cut it; second, deliberate omissions and additions — what a film cuts from its source, or invents that wasn't there, usually signals its own thematic priorities rather than mere runtime economy; third, altered endings — a changed climax often reveals a difference in what the film believes its story is really about, or a concession to what the visual medium's audience expects from a payoff.

Worked example: Jaws (1975, dir. Steven Spielberg), adapted from Peter Benchley's 1974 novel of the same name. Benchley's novel weaves in two significant subplots absent from the film entirely — an affair between Chief Brody's wife Ellen and oceanographer Matt Hooper, and a subplot involving the mafia's financial leverage over Mayor Vaughn — both of which Spielberg cut outright, narrowing the story into a nearly pure survival/monster-movie structure focused on the three men and the shark. That narrowing wasn't purely artistic: the mechanical shark ("Bruce") built for filming malfunctioned constantly, forcing Spielberg to withhold it from view for most of the film's first two acts, relying instead on point-of-view shots from beneath the water, floating yellow barrels, dorsal-fin cutaways, and John Williams's two-note ostinato to signal the shark's presence — an enforced solution that most critics now consider more effective than fully showing the creature would have been, since imagination fills in a scarier shark than special effects could. The ending changes too: in the novel, the shark is repeatedly harpooned and simply drowns from exhaustion, sinking beneath the boat in a comparatively anticlimactic death; the film instead gives Chief Brody an explosive, crowd-pleasing kill shot — firing a rifle into a scuba tank lodged in the shark's mouth — trading the novel's more "realistic" ending for a purely cinematic, cathartic genre payoff. Comparing the two versions side by side shows how adaptation is a series of active creative choices (sharpened focus, effects-driven suggestion over depiction, and a bigger climactic beat), not merely a technical transcription of the book onto film.

3)西班牙文課程 — 第 37 天(10 分鐘,與第 36 天共同完成整套「Si」條件句系統)

第 36 天正式系統化了「與現在事實相反」的第二類條件句(Si + 虛擬式過去未完了式 + 條件式簡單式),並明確預告了它「真實/有可能發生」的對應版本——第一類條件句——留待未來課程,今天就要正式教授。第一類條件句用來描述真實、有可能發生、習慣性,或對未來仍然開放的情境(不是假設性、也不是與事實相反的):Si 子句一律使用直陳式現在式——絕對不能使用任何形式的虛擬式——而結果子句則可以是直陳式現在式(表達一般性規則或習慣)、未來簡單式(第 23 天教過,表達特定的、一次性的承諾或預測),或是命令式(第 25、26 天教過,表達直接的建議或命令)。這與昨天的課程形成最鮮明的對比:同樣都用連接詞「si」,文法邏輯卻完全不同,關鍵完全取決於說話者描述的,究竟是真實/可能發生的情境,還是假設性/與事實相反的情境。

  • Si llueve mañana, no salimos. — 如果明天下雨,我們就不出門。(結果子句用直陳式現在式:表達一般性的計畫/規則)
  • Si terminas la tarea, te llevaré al cine. — 如果你做完功課,我就帶你去看電影。(結果子句用未來簡單式:表達特定的一次性承諾)
  • Si tienes hambre, come algo. — 如果你肚子餓,就吃點東西吧。(結果子句用命令式:表達直接的建議)

文法筆記:每一句第一類條件句的 Si 子句,都只能使用直陳式現在式(llueve、terminas、tienes),絕對不能是其他形式——不能說 si llueva,也不能說 si tendrías。結果子句可能出現的三種語氣/時態並不是可以隨意替換的填空,它們各自傳達不同的細微差異(習慣性規則 vs. 特定的未來承諾 vs. 直接命令),但這三種結果子句都共同建立在同一個「Si 子句用直陳式現在式」的規則之上。把第一類條件句「Si 子句用直陳式現在式」的規則,和第二類條件句「結果子句用條件式簡單式」的規則混在一起使用,是常見卻不合文法的錯誤混搭——第 36 天與第 37 天所教的這兩套系統,絕對不能混用。

4)易經卦象

第 37 卦:家人 (Jiā Rén) ——「家庭、家族」(巽上離下,風在上、火在下)。〈序卦傳〉直接承接昨天的第 36 卦而來:「傷於外者必反於家,故受之以家人。」(在外遭受損傷的人,必然會轉而返回家中;所以〈明夷〉之後接著是〈家人〉。)昨天的明夷卦描繪周文王與箕子隱藏自己內在光明、以求在充滿敵意的外部世界中倖存的景象;家人卦則正好承接這條退路的終點:回到家庭之中——這是唯一仍能安全建立與維繫秩序的領域。〈卦辭〉簡潔明瞭:「家人,利女貞。」(家人卦:適宜女子堅守正道、保持節操。)〈彖傳〉則將這層意涵,擴展成《易經》中被引用次數最多的社會秩序論述之一:「家人,女正位乎內,男正位乎外,男女正,天地之大義也。家人有嚴君焉,父母之謂也。父父,子子,兄兄,弟弟,夫夫,婦婦,而家道正;正家而天下定矣。」(在家庭之中,女子端正地立於內,男子端正地立於外;男女各自安於其正確的位置,這正是天地間最大的道理。家庭之中自有其威嚴的主事者,指的就是父母。當父親真正盡到父親的本分,兒子真正盡到兒子的本分,兄長真正盡到兄長的本分,弟弟真正盡到弟弟的本分,丈夫真正盡到丈夫的本分,妻子真正盡到妻子的本分,家庭的倫常秩序就端正了;而家庭一旦端正,天下也就因此安定了。)這與後來孔子所提出的「正名」思想,其實是同一套邏輯——社會的和諧,取決於每個角色是否真正活出其應有的本分,而不僅僅是掛著那個名分——〈彖傳〉從「端正家庭」直接跳躍到「天下安定」的論述方式,正是明白揭示了中國古典政治思想中的核心信念:家庭是國家最基本的運作單位,若無法在這個最小單位上建立秩序,就無從在其上建立任何更大規模的秩序。〈象傳〉則從同樣的卦象結構中,推導出更貼近個人日常修養的教訓:「風自火出,家人;君子以言有物而行有恆。」(風,是從火中自然生發出來的:這就是家人卦的象。因此君子說話要言之有物,行事要保持恆常一致。)巽卦(風)自然生發自其下離卦(火)的熱力——象徵著影響力,是從一個經營良好的內在源頭向外自然擴散,正如一個家庭所秉持的言語價值與日常行為習慣,會以身教而非命令的方式,自然向外擴散、影響到更廣大的社會。將這一卦與昨天的明夷卦合看,兩者構成了一個完整的循環:光明被迫在充滿壓迫的外部世界中退守、隱藏自身(第 36 卦),緊接著在下一卦裡,便找到了唯一能將這份內在清明重新建立成穩定、能夠自我維繫的秩序的地方(第 37 卦)——家庭並非逃避責任的避風港,而是任何更大秩序,最終都必須以它為根基才能建立起來的地方。

5)國家/地區/勢力介紹 — 卡達(Qatar)

  • 位置:卡達是一個從阿拉伯半島向北突出、深入波斯灣的小型半島國家,陸地邊界僅與沙烏地阿拉伯(南側)相接,阿拉伯聯合大公國與巴林則隔海相望於附近,伊朗則位於波斯灣對岸。
  • 領土規模:國土總面積約 11,571 平方公里——約為台灣陸地面積(約 36,193 平方公里)的 32%,換算下來台灣的面積是它的三倍多一些;面積大致與美國康乃狄克州相當。
  • 人口:約 300 萬人,但其中僅約 10-12% 為卡達公民——絕大多數人口是外籍移工,主要來自南亞與東南亞,受建築、能源與服務業等產業吸引而來。
  • 宗教:伊斯蘭教為官方國教,絕大多數公民信奉伊斯蘭教(以遜尼派為主,深受瓦哈比/薩拉菲派思想影響);人數龐大的外籍勞動人口私下也信奉印度教、基督教、佛教及其他宗教,不過伊斯蘭教以外的公開宗教活動受到相當嚴格的限制。
  • 經濟:擁有全球數一數二的人均 GDP,經濟結構絕大部分建立在液化天然氣(LNG)與石油出口之上——卡達與伊朗共同擁有全球最大的天然氣田(北方氣田/南帕爾斯氣田),其國營企業卡達能源(QatarEnergy)是全球規模數一數二的液化天然氣出口商。主權財富基金「卡達投資局」(Qatar Investment Authority)將石化收益投入全球投資組合,而該國也依循「2030 年國家願景」計畫推動經濟多元化(金融、以卡達航空為代表的航空業、體育與觀光),2022 年主辦國際足總世界盃更進一步加速了這項進程。
  • 歷史:卡達長期受周邊區域勢力與鄂圖曼帝國影響,20 世紀初成為英國的保護國;自 19 世紀中葉起便在當地執政的阿勒薩尼家族(Al Thani),則成為受公認的統治王朝。1940 年代發現石油,徹底改變了這個原本以採珠與漁業為主的小型經濟體,卡達於 1971 年正式脫離英國獨立。1995 年,時任王儲的哈馬德·本·哈利法·阿勒薩尼(Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani)透過一場不流血的宮廷政變,自父親手中奪權,推動卡達快速現代化,並大幅提升其在全球媒體與體育領域的能見度(包括創辦半島電視台〔Al Jazeera〕);他於 2013 年和平退位,將王位傳給兒子。2017 至 2021 年間,卡達曾遭沙烏地阿拉伯、阿聯、巴林與埃及以「支持恐怖主義、與伊朗及土耳其關係過於密切」為由,實施陸海空全面封鎖——這場外交風波最終在 2021 年 1 月透過「阿拉烏拉協議」(Al-Ula agreement)化解。
  • 現任領導人:埃米爾(國家元首)塔米姆·本·哈馬德·阿勒薩尼親王(Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani),自 2013 年 6 月其父退位後即位至今。
  • 語言:阿拉伯語為官方語言;由於外籍勞動人口眾多,英語在商業、教育與日常生活中也被廣泛使用。

6)網路迷因介紹 —「Drakeposting」(Drake 拒絕/點頭比較迷因)

  • 名稱:「Drakeposting」,亦簡稱為「Drake meme」或「Drake Hotline Bling meme」。
  • 起源/歷史:這套模板取材自加拿大饒舌歌手 Drake 於 2015 年單曲《Hotline Bling》音樂錄影帶(導演為 Director X)中的兩張截圖:其中一張畫面裡,Drake 皺眉別過頭去,並做出帶有拒絕意味的手勢;另一張畫面裡,他則面帶微笑,朝鏡頭做出讚許的指向動作。
  • 如何走紅:這支音樂錄影帶於 2015 年 10 月首映,到了 2016 年上半年,Twitter 與 Instagram 使用者便開始將這兩張對比鮮明的截圖,裁切拼接成上下堆疊的雙格(後來演變為四格)比較圖格式;由於這種「拒絕/讚許」的結構幾乎不需要任何編輯技巧,就能套用到任何一組二選一的比較情境上,因此迅速蔓延開來,而原始音樂錄影帶裡的舞步動作,也被大量二創改圖進一步帶動了它的傳播熱度。
  • 意義/使用情境:上方畫面(Drake 退縮、拒絕的姿態)搭配貼文者想要嘲諷或否定的選項,下方畫面(Drake 用手指讚許的姿態)則搭配貼文者實際上支持的選項——常用來表達個人偏好、拿「壞決定 vs. 好決定」開玩笑,或是透過將「被拒絕的、明智的」選項與「被讚許的、放縱或荒謬的」選項並列對比,來諷刺某種偽善。
  • 變體/演變:衍生出四格甚至六格版本,用來呈現更複雜的多方比較;也出現無數套用其他角色、但保留「拒絕/讚許」核心結構的「換皮」改圖(這套邏輯後來也影響了如「Expanding Brain(腦容量遞增哏圖)」這類階層式比較迷因的發展);另有一種反諷變體,讓 Drake 對上下兩個選項做出完全相同的反應,藉此嘲諷「假平等/偽對等」的邏輯謬誤;這兩張原始截圖本身,也常常完全脫離比較圖格式,單獨作為反應圖廣泛使用。
  • 現況:截至 2026 年,這個哏圖仍在日常網路生活中被隨性地使用著,與「分心男友」、「酸民臉」並列為網路上最長壽的「比較型」哏圖模板之一——距離原始音樂錄影帶發行已過十年,如今它的功能更接近一套眾所公認的「經典」哏圖語法,而不只是拿來製造新笑點的工具,不斷被重新混搭使用,而非逐漸淡出。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹 — 崑曲(Kūnqǔ)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:崑曲(又稱崑劇),字面意思是「崑山腔調」,得名自其發源地——江蘇省蘇州附近的崑山。其精緻的演唱風格,一般歸功於音樂家魏良輔,他在 16 世紀中葉將「崑山腔」系統化定型;而崑曲最著名的代表作《牡丹亭》,則出自劇作家湯顯祖(1550-1616)之手,他常被譽為與莎士比亞同時代、中國戲劇史上的大師級人物。
  • 時代背景:崑曲成形於明代中後期(16 世紀),此後大約三百年間,一直是士大夫文人階層心目中最主流、最具聲望的中國戲曲形式——直到 19 世紀,更貼近庶民大眾、日益興盛的京劇後來居上,取代了崑曲的流行地位,並在這個過程中大量吸收了崑曲的音樂理論與劇目結構。
  • 劇情概述:崑曲的劇目取材自中國古典詩詞、愛情故事與歷史演義;《牡丹亭》講述少女杜麗娘在夢中與一名書生相戀,因相思成疾而香消玉殞,最終憑藉這份愛情的力量得以復生的故事——情節帶有濃厚的超自然色彩,且極為抒情,幾乎完全建立在內心思念之上,而非外在的行動情節。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:演唱風格緩慢、裝飾音極為繁複、講究拖腔轉音,主要伴奏樂器是曲笛(橫吹竹笛),而非京劇核心的拉弦樂器京胡;唱詞直接取材自中國古典詩、詞等文學形式,其文學性之高,甚至可單獨作為案頭文學閱讀,而不僅止於舞台演出;身段動作極簡而高度程式化、精雕細琢,劇本則遵循多幕式的「傳奇」結構。
  • 文化/歷史意義:崑曲素有「百戲之母」之稱,因為幾乎所有後起的中國地方戲曲劇種——包括京劇本身在內——都曾借鑑其曲調理論、角色行當成規與劇目內容;聯合國教科文組織(UNESCO)於 2001 年將崑曲列為「人類口述與非物質文化遺產代表作」,是首個獲此殊榮的中國表演藝術形式。
  • 影響與傳承:崑曲的演唱音樂與角色行當體系,成為幾乎所有後起中國戲曲劇種所共同承襲的技術根基;這項藝術形式一度在 20 世紀中葉瀕臨失傳(受戰亂與文化大革命衝擊,僅剩少數劇團倖存),後來透過國家扶植得以復興,近年更因小說家白先勇於 2004 年製作、備受讚譽的《牡丹亭》「青春版」,成功將崑曲介紹給新一代、橫跨兩岸的年輕觀眾。
  • 現況/觀賞方式:目前江蘇、浙江、上海、北京與湖南等地,仍有少數專業崑劇團持續活躍演出(即中國「七大崑劇院團」),在專門劇場與各類藝術節中登台;《牡丹亭》「青春版」也曾多次赴海外巡演,包括台灣在內,讓這部崑曲代表作至今仍持續在舞台上流通——儘管崑曲整體而言仍是一項瀕危、但受到積極保護的聯合國教科文組織文化遺產。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、ich.unesco.org(聯合國教科文組織)

8)電影賞析方法 — 改編分析:比較電影與原著素材(第 23 課)

改編分析,指的並非追問「這部電影對原著有多忠實?」,而是去檢視電影做出的具體改動——刪減、增添、觀點轉換與結局變動——這些改動究竟透露出電影這個媒介能做到、但文字散文做不到的哪些事,以及創作者真正在意、想要保留下來的究竟是什麼。「情節忠實度」是最不中用的評判標準,因為電影與散文本質上是完全不同的敘事技術。分析時要留意三件事:第一是「觀點的轉譯」——小說的內心獨白(角色私密的內在思緒)在電影裡沒有直接對應的手法,因此改編作品必須把心理狀態外化成某種可見的東西(表演、空間、剪輯),否則就只能刪掉;第二是「刻意的刪減與增添」——電影從原著中刪去了什麼、或增添了原著沒有的情節,通常反映的是這部電影自身的主題優先順序,而不僅僅是為了節省片長;第三是「結局的變動」——結局的改動,往往透露出電影對「這個故事真正在講什麼」有著不同的理解,或是對視覺媒介觀眾所期待的「爽度」做出的妥協。

實例演練:《大白鯊》(Jaws,1975,導演:史蒂芬·史匹柏 Steven Spielberg),改編自彼得·班奇利(Peter Benchley)1974 年出版的同名小說。班奇利的原著小說,交織了兩條電影裡完全沒有出現的重要支線——警長布洛迪的妻子艾倫(Ellen)與海洋學家胡珀(Matt Hooper)之間的婚外情,以及黑手黨對市長沃恩(Mayor Vaughn)財務施壓的支線——史匹柏將這兩條支線全數刪除,把整個故事收斂成幾乎純粹的「生存/怪物電影」結構,聚焦在三名男子與鯊魚之間的對抗。這種收斂並不單純出於藝術考量:拍攝時打造的機械鯊魚(暱稱「布魯斯」Bruce)故障連連,迫使史匹柏在電影前三分之二的篇幅裡,幾乎完全不讓鯊魚露臉,轉而依靠水面下的主觀鏡頭、漂浮的黃色浮桶、背鰭特寫鏡頭,以及約翰·威廉斯(John Williams)那兩個音符構成的固定音型配樂,來暗示鯊魚的存在——如今大多數影評人都認為,這個被迫而生的解決方案,反而比直接呈現鯊魚全貌更具效果,因為觀眾自己想像出來的鯊魚,遠比特效做得出來的更加恐怖。結局也做了改動:原著小說裡,鯊魚被反覆用魚叉刺中,最終因體力耗盡溺斃,悄悄沉入船底,是相對平淡收尾的死法;電影則讓布洛迪警長迎來一記爆炸性、大快人心的擊殺鏡頭——朝卡在鯊魚嘴裡的水肺氣瓶開槍——用一個純粹屬於電影語言、宣洩情緒的類型片高潮,取代了原著小說較為「寫實」的結局。將這兩個版本並列比較,可以清楚看出「改編」其實是一連串主動的創作抉擇(讓焦點更集中、以特效驅動的暗示取代直接呈現、營造更強烈的高潮橋段),而不僅僅是把小說技術性地搬上銀幕而已。
