4) Daodejing (道德經) — Chapter 14: 視之不見 (Shì zhī bù jiàn) — "Looked At, Yet Not Seen"

  • Context: The I Ching and Zhuangzi sequences are used up, so this section continues through the Daodejing in order. Yesterday was Chapter 13.
  • Text (Wang Bi edition): 視之不見,名曰夷;聽之不聞,名曰希;搏之不得,名曰微。此三者不可致詰,故混而為一。其上不皦,其下不昧,繩繩不可名,復歸於無物。是謂無狀之狀,無物之象,是謂惚恍。迎之不見其首,隨之不見其後。執古之道,以御今之有。能知古始,是謂道紀。
  • Meaning: The Way can't be grasped by any one sense: what we look at but can't see is "yi" (夷, the colourless), what we listen for but can't hear is "xi" (希, the soundless), what we reach for but can't hold is "wei" (微, the subtle). These three can't be pursued separately, so they blend into one. It isn't bright above or dark below; it is endless, unnameable, and returns to "no thing" — a form without form, an image without thing, "indistinct and elusive" (惚恍). Meet it and you see no head; follow it and you see no back. Holding to the ancient Way lets you steer present things and understand the beginning — "the thread of the Way" (道紀).
  • Link to earlier chapters: Chapters 1 and 4 said the Way can't be named; Chapter 11 found usefulness in emptiness; Chapter 14 now says why: the Way is beyond each separate sense, so naming and grasping fail. (Interpretation from general knowledge; the wording varies between editions, for example the silk-manuscript texts.)

5) Country Spotlight — Costa Rica (哥斯大黎加)

  • Location & size: Central America, between Nicaragua (north) and Panama (south), with the Caribbean to the east and the Pacific to the west. About 51,100 km², roughly 1.4 times Taiwan (about 36,000 km²) and a little larger than Slovakia, smaller than West Virginia (about 62,800 km²).
  • Population: about 5.1-5.2 million (the 2022 census counted roughly 5.1 million); capital San José, whose metro area holds around a third of the people.
  • Religion: Roman Catholicism is the constitutional state religion (roughly half the population is Catholic), with a large and growing evangelical Protestant minority; religious freedom is protected.
  • Language: Spanish (official); Limonese Creole English on the Caribbean coast; a handful of Indigenous languages such as Bribri and Cabécar.
  • Economy: upper-middle-income, GDP about US$ 90-100 billion (approximate), GDP per capita around US$ 18,000-19,000 (approximate). Since the 1990s it has shifted from bananas and coffee to high-tech and medical devices (Intel, Boston Scientific and others operate in free-trade zones), plus pineapples and tourism; ecotourism is a major earner. The currency is the colón.
  • History: Spanish colony from the 16th century, one of the poorest and most remote parts of the empire; independent from Spain with the rest of Central America in 1821, and fully independent after leaving the Central American Federation in 1838. After a brief civil war in 1948, José Figueres Ferrer abolished the army on 1 December 1948, and the 1949 constitution made that permanent, so spending went to education and health instead. President Óscar Arias won the 1987 Nobel Peace Prize for the Central American peace plan.
  • Current leader: President Laura Fernández Delgado, sworn in on 8 May 2026 as the 50th president (term 2026-2030, the second woman in the office after Laura Chinchilla); she won the Feb 1, 2026 election in the first round with about 48% and pledged a hard line on crime. Her predecessor Rodrigo Chaves stays in government as minister of the Presidency and Finance (per news reports).
  • Fun facts: The country holds roughly 5% of the world's species on a tiny share of its land; around a quarter of its territory is protected; the everyday phrase pura vida ("pure life") works as hello, goodbye and "all good". It has relied heavily on renewable electricity (hydro, geothermal, wind) for years.

6) Internet Meme — The Oscars Slap / "Will Smith Slap" (2022)

  • Name: "The Slap" (also "Will Smith slapping Chris Rock", "Keep my wife's name out your mouth").
  • Origin: At the 94th Academy Awards on March 27, 2022 (Dolby Theatre, Los Angeles), presenter Chris Rock joked about Jada Pinkett Smith's shaved head, comparing it to "G.I. Jane 2". Will Smith walked on stage, slapped Rock, returned to his seat and shouted for Rock to keep his wife's name out of his mouth. About 40 minutes later Smith won Best Actor for King Richard and apologised to the Academy in his speech.
  • How it spread: It was broadcast live and went viral within minutes, with the uncensored audio muted on some US feeds, so clips and quotes circulated on social media in the first hours. Photoshopped "Will Smith slapping ___" images, the hand-swing freeze frame and the "keep my wife's name out your mouth" line became templates within a day.
  • Meaning & usage: The image stands for a sudden, shocking reaction, a punchline that "goes too far", or an unexpected consequence; users edit the slap onto video-game characters, mascots and everyday objects. The audio line is quoted when someone is annoyed by a joke about their partner.
  • Aftermath and variations: Smith apologised publicly on March 28, resigned from the Academy on April 1, and was banned from Academy events for ten years on April 8, 2022. Rock addressed it in his 2023 Netflix special Selective Outrage. The meme later became shorthand for other awards-show moments.
  • Current status: The original meme peaked in spring 2022; today it is used mostly as a retro reaction and each March at Oscar time. (Dates and wording are from general knowledge; no live source was checked today.)

7) Film / Performing Arts — Ikiru (生きる, "To Live", 1952, dir. Akira Kurosawa)

  • Name & creators: Ikiru, produced by Toho; directed by Akira Kurosawa, who co-wrote it with Shinobu Hashimoto and Hideo Oguni. Lead actor Takashi Shimura plays Kanji Watanabe; the score is by Fumio Hayasaka.
  • Era & background: Postwar Japan, a society rebuilding itself under a vast bureaucracy. Kurosawa made it between Rashomon (1950) and Seven Samurai (1954), shifting from period drama to a contemporary story about an ordinary clerk.
  • Plot overview: Watanabe is a mid-ranking city official who has spent about 30 years stamping papers. He learns he has stomach cancer and a few months to live. He tries drinking and nightlife with a writer he meets, then follows a young colleague, Toyo, whose energy makes him want a purpose. He returns to his office and pushes through the long-stalled petition to turn a polluted lot into a children's park. The final act is his wake, told in flashbacks as his colleagues argue about what drove him, and promise to change but quickly slide back to old habits. We last see him at night, singing "Gondola no Uta" ("Life is short") on a swing in the park in the snow.
  • Style & technique: A two-part structure: the first half follows his search; the second half jumps past his death into the wake, which removes him from the screen and lets others reconstruct him. Kurosawa uses a narrator at the start, long dialogue in the wake, and Shimura's small, contained performance; the deep-focus compositions of the bureaucracy and the long wake scene are widely studied.
  • Significance & legacy: Frequently ranked among the great films about mortality and meaning; the park scene is an iconic image of finding purpose late. It was remade as the British film Living (2022, screenplay by Kazuo Ishiguro, starring Bill Nighy), which earned Oscar nominations for Nighy and Ishiguro.
  • Where to experience it: Available on home video, with restorations released by the Criterion Collection; revival cinemas screen it regularly. Check current streaming availability in your region. (From general knowledge; no live source checked.)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 97: Reading Visual Comedy (Staging, Timing and the Gag)

  • What it is: Comedy is built from the same craft as drama, but its tools are timing, geography (where things are in the frame) and escalation. To analyse a gag, ask: what is the setup, what does the audience expect, what actually happens, and how does the film control the moment of release?
  • How to analyse: (1) Find the setup: what rule or expectation is planted (a danger, a routine, a wrong assumption)? (2) Watch the staging: is everything needed for the gag visible in one frame, so we can see the cause and the victim at once? (3) Count the beats: comedy often runs setup, repeat, then twist (the "rule of three"). (4) Check the cut: does the film hold a static wide shot, which "proves" there is no trickery, or cut to a reaction to tell us how to feel? (5) Study the performer: a deadpan face lets the situation carry the joke, while a big reaction signals it. (6) Ask what the gag reveals about character.
  • Worked example — Steamboat Bill, Jr. (1928, dir. Charles Reisner, starring Buster Keaton), the falling house front: In the cyclone climax, Keaton's character stands in the street as the whole front wall of a building tips toward him. (a) Setup: a long storm sequence has destroyed everything around him, so the audience expects him to be crushed. (b) Staging: a single static wide shot shows the wall, the open attic window and Keaton at once, so there is no cut to hide a trick; reportedly the wall weighed around two tons and the window cleared him by only a few inches, with his foot placed on a marked spot. (c) Performance: he doesn't react at all, he only looks puzzled and glances up at the sky before the wall hits, which makes his obliviousness the joke. (d) Payoff: the wall falls and the window frame passes around him, and the film holds the shot after it lands, letting the audience laugh at their own held breath. Why it works: the shot's honesty (real danger, no cut) turns suspense into comedy, and the stillness of the star shows his character, a person who survives by sheer luck and calm.
  • Practice: Pick any comedy scene. Write the setup in one sentence, count how many shots the gag takes and whether one of them is a static wide, and note whether the performer reacts or stays deadpan.

4) 道德經 — 第 14 章:視之不見(Shì zhī bù jiàn)

  • 背景:易經與莊子已全部用完,本單元依序介紹道德經。昨天是第 13 章。
  • 原文(王弼本):視之不見,名曰夷;聽之不聞,名曰希;搏之不得,名曰微。此三者不可致詰,故混而為一。其上不皦,其下不昧,繩繩不可名,復歸於無物。是謂無狀之狀,無物之象,是謂惚恍。迎之不見其首,隨之不見其後。執古之道,以御今之有。能知古始,是謂道紀。
  • 大意:道無法被任何單一感官抓住:看而看不見的叫「夷」(無色),聽而聽不到的叫「希」(無聲),摸而摸不著的叫「微」(幽微)。這三者無法分開追究,所以混而為一。它的上面不明亮,下面不昏暗,連綿不絕、無法命名,又回到「無物」的狀態,是沒有形狀的形狀、沒有物體的形象,稱為「惚恍」。迎著它看不到頭,跟著它看不到尾。把握古老的道,來駕馭現今的事物,能知道原始的開端,這就是「道紀」(道的綱紀)。
  • 與前面章節的關係:第 1 章與第 4 章說道無法命名;第 11 章在「空」中看見用處;第 14 章接著說明原因:道超越各個感官,所以命名與抓取都會失敗。(以上為依一般常識的詮釋;各版本文字略有差異,例如帛書本。)

  • 後續與變體:史密斯 3 月 28 日公開道歉,4 月 1 日辭去影藝學院會籍,4 月 8 日被禁止出席學院活動十年。洛克在 2023 年的 Netflix 特別節目《Selective Outrage》中談到此事。此迷因後來成為其他頒獎典禮事件的代稱。
  • 目前狀態:原始迷因在 2022 年春季達到高峰;現在多半當作復古反應圖,並在每年三月奧斯卡時節重新出現。(日期與用語依一般常識,今日未查證即時來源。)

7) 電影 / 表演藝術 — 《生之慾》(生きる / Ikiru,1952,導演黑澤明)

  • 名稱與創作者:《生之慾》(Ikiru,字面意思「活著」),東寶出品;導演黑澤明,與橋本忍、小國英雄共同編劇。主演志村喬飾演渡邊勘治;配樂為早坂文雄。
  • 時代背景:戰後的日本,社會在龐大官僚體制下重建。黑澤明在《羅生門》(1950)與《七武士》(1954)之間拍攝此片,從古裝劇轉向描寫一位現代平凡公務員的故事。
  • 劇情概述:渡邊是市政府的中階官員,三十年來每天蓋章、傳遞文件。他得知自己罹患胃癌,只剩數個月生命。他先和認識的小說家一起縱情夜生活,後來跟隨年輕同事小豐,她的活力讓他渴望有所作為。他回到辦公室,推動長期擱置的陳情案,把一塊汙染的空地變成兒童公園。最後一段是他的守靈夜,以倒敘呈現,同事們爭論他究竟為什麼改變,並承諾改革,卻很快回到老習慣。我們最後一次看到他,是在雪夜的公園裡坐在鞦韆上,唱著〈ゴンドラの唄〉(「人生苦短」)。
  • 風格與技法:兩段式結構:前半部追隨他的探索;後半部越過他的死亡直接跳到守靈夜,讓他退出銀幕,由旁人拼湊出他的樣子。黑澤明在開頭使用旁白,守靈夜有大量對話,志村喬的表演內斂克制;官僚體系的深焦構圖與守靈夜的長場景常被拿來研究。
  • 意義與影響:常被列為關於死亡與生命意義的偉大電影;公園那場戲是「遲來的意義」的經典畫面。2022 年被英國重拍為《生之慾》(Living,凱茲·石黑一編劇、比爾·奈伊主演),奈伊與石黑一獲奧斯卡提名。
  • 欣賞方式:有家庭影音版本,標準收藏(Criterion Collection)有修復版;重映戲院也常放映。請查詢你所在地區目前的串流平台。(依一般常識,未查證即時來源。)

8) 看片方法 — 第 97 課:解讀視覺喜劇(調度、節奏與「笑點」)

  • 是什麼:喜劇與劇情片使用相同的技藝,但工具是節奏、空間配置(畫面中東西在哪裡)與層層升高。分析一個笑點時要問:鋪陳是什麼、觀眾預期什麼、實際發生什麼,以及影片如何控制「釋放」的那一刻?
  • 如何分析:(1) 找出鋪陳:埋下了什麼規則或期待(一個危險、一個慣例、一個錯誤的假設)?(2) 看調度:笑點所需的一切是否都在同一畫面中,讓我們同時看到原因與受害者?(3) 數拍子:喜劇常是鋪陳、重複、再轉折(「三的法則」)。(4) 看剪接:影片是用靜止的大全景來「證明」沒有作假,還是切到反應鏡頭告訴我們該怎麼感受?(5) 觀察表演者:面無表情讓情境自己製造笑點,誇張反應則在提示笑點。(6) 問這個笑點透露了角色的什麼。
  • 實例分析 —《小艇比爾》(Steamboat Bill, Jr.,1928,導演查爾斯·賴斯納,主演巴斯特·基頓),倒下的房屋前牆:在颱風高潮戲中,基頓飾演的角色站在街上,整棟建築的前牆朝他倒下。(a) 鋪陳:長時間的暴風雨段落已經摧毀了他周圍的一切,觀眾預期他會被壓扁。(b) 調度:單一的固定大全景同時呈現牆壁、敞開的閣樓窗與基頓,沒有切鏡頭掩蓋把戲;據稱那面牆重約兩噸,窗框只差幾英寸就擦過他,他的腳站在預先標記的位置。(c) 表演:他完全沒有反應,只是一臉困惑地抬頭看天,然後牆就倒下,他的渾然不覺就是笑點。(d) 爆點:牆倒下,窗框從他身邊穿過,影片在牆落地後繼續停留,讓觀眾笑自己剛才屏住的呼吸。為什麼有效:鏡頭的誠實(真實的危險,沒有剪接)把懸念轉成喜劇,而明星的靜止展現了角色:靠運氣與冷靜活下來的人。
  • 練習:挑任何一段喜劇場景。用一句話寫下鋪陳,數出笑點用了幾個鏡頭、其中是否有靜止的大全景,並記下表演者是有反應還是面無表情。
  • 附註:第 6-8 節來自一般常識,今日未查證即時來源;特技細節為一般流傳的說法。
