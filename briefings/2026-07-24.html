3) Spanish Lesson — Day 15 (10 min, builds on Day 14)

Day 14 introduced the present progressive (estar + gerundio) for actions happening right now. Today's new concept flips the sentence structure: the verb gustar ("to like/to please") and the indirect object pronouns (me, te, le, nos, les) that go with it — one of the trickiest but most essential structures in Spanish.

  • ¿Te gusta la música? (teh GOOS-tah lah MOO-see-kah) — Do you like music?
  • Sí, me gusta mucho el rock. (see, meh GOOS-tah MOO-choh el rock) — Yes, I really like rock.
  • A mi hermano le gustan los videojuegos. (ah mee er-MAH-noh leh GOOS-tahn lohs bee-deh-oh-hoo-EH-gohs) — My brother likes video games.

Grammar note: gustar works "backwards" compared with English — the thing being liked is the grammatical subject, and the person doing the liking is an indirect object. That's why the verb is gusta (singular) when one thing is liked (la música, el rock) but gustan (plural) when more than one thing is liked (los videojuegos) — the verb agrees with what's liked, not with who likes it. The indirect object pronoun (me/te/le/nos/les) always comes first, and an optional "a + person" (a mi hermano) can be added up front for clarity or emphasis, especially with le/les, which are ambiguous on their own.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Nepal

  • Location: A landlocked country in South Asia, wedged along the southern slopes of the central Himalayas between China (Tibet Autonomous Region) to the north and India on the other three sides.
  • Territory: About 147,516 km² — roughly four times the size of Taiwan (~36,200 km²), a little larger than Bangladesh, and comparable in area to the US state of Illinois. It contains eight of the world's ten highest peaks, including Mount Everest (Sagarmatha), the world's tallest, on its border with Tibet.
  • Population: Approximately 30 million people, with substantial ethnic and linguistic diversity across its steep altitude gradient — from tropical lowlands (Terai) to high alpine valleys.
  • Religion: Predominantly Hindu (roughly 81%), with significant Buddhist (~9%), Muslim, Kirat (indigenous), and Christian minorities; Nepal was the world's only constitutionally declared Hindu Kingdom until it became a secular state after the monarchy's abolition in 2008.
  • Economy: One of South Asia's lower-income economies, heavily dependent on remittances from millions of Nepali migrant workers abroad (which make up roughly a quarter of GDP), alongside agriculture, tourism (trekking and mountaineering around Everest and the Annapurna circuit are major draws), and largely untapped hydropower potential from its steep river systems.
  • History: Unified into a single kingdom by Prithvi Narayan Shah in 1768. The hereditary Rana family effectively ruled as autocratic prime ministers from 1846 to 1951, keeping the Shah kings as figureheads, until a 1951 revolution restored royal power. A decade-long Maoist insurgency (1996–2006) killed some 17,000 people before a peace accord; the monarchy was formally abolished in 2008, making Nepal a Federal Democratic Republic.
  • Current leader: President Ram Chandra Poudel serves as head of state (since 2023), a largely ceremonial role, while executive power sits with the Prime Minister — a post that has changed hands frequently amid Nepal's fractious multi-party coalition politics.
  • Language: Nepali is the official language and lingua franca, but the constitution recognizes dozens of mother tongues — over 120 languages are spoken nationwide, including Maithili, Bhojpuri, Tamang, and Newar.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Distracted Boyfriend"

  • Name: "Distracted Boyfriend" (also called "Disloyal Man," 分心男友).
  • Origin/history: Sourced from a 2015 stock photograph by Spanish photographer Antonio Guillem, part of a series depicting a man walking with his girlfriend while turning to admiringly ogle a woman passing by — his girlfriend visibly appalled. The photo sat unremarked in stock libraries for two years before meme culture discovered it.
  • How it became popular: It broke out on Twitter and Reddit in August 2017 after users began overlaying abstract concept labels on the three figures, turning a generic stock photo into an endlessly reusable template for "being tempted away from a current commitment by something newer and shinier." It spread explosively within weeks, becoming one of the defining meme formats of 2017–2018.
  • Meaning/usage: The boyfriend represents "me" or "people in general"; his girlfriend represents whatever the subject *should* be loyal to (an old habit, a prior choice, an existing tool or belief); the passing woman represents the tempting new alternative. Used across every imaginable context — programming languages, diets, political allegiances, sports fandoms — as pure structural shorthand for "attention shifting to something new."
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned countless labeled remixes, "meta" versions (memes about the meme itself), and even real-world recreations/parodies; drew brief controversy in 2023 when Antonio Guillem and his stock agency reportedly sent takedown/licensing notices over unauthorized commercial reuse of the image, a rare case of a meme's original source asserting rights over derivative works.
  • Current status: Largely a "legacy" template by the 2020s — rarely used for fresh content, but persistently cited in meme-history retrospectives and "greatest memes of all time" lists as one of the most recognizable reaction-image formats ever produced.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Citizen Kane (1941)

  • Name: Citizen Kane (1941).
  • Key figures: Directed by, co-written by, and starring Orson Welles (then 25 years old, making his feature directing debut), co-written by Herman J. Mankiewicz, and shot by cinematographer Gregg Toland, whose technical innovations are as central to the film's legacy as Welles' direction.
  • Era/background: Produced at RKO Pictures under an unprecedented contract giving Welles near-total creative control — a rarity for a first-time director in the Hollywood studio system.
  • Plot overview: A newsreel reporter investigates the meaning of dying newspaper tycoon Charles Foster Kane's last word, "Rosebud," piecing together Kane's life through fragmented, often contradictory flashbacks and testimony from those who knew him — a life loosely modeled on media magnate William Randolph Hearst.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Pioneered extensive deep-focus cinematography (keeping foreground, middle ground, and background all in sharp focus simultaneously), dramatic low-angle shots that reveal ceilings (forcing custom-built sets), stark chiaroscuro lighting, overlapping naturalistic dialogue drawn from Welles' radio background, a fractured nonlinear multi-perspective narrative structure, and innovative use of miniatures and matte paintings to expand its scale on a modest budget.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as one of the greatest films ever made — it topped the British Film Institute's Sight & Sound critics' poll for fifty consecutive years (1962–2002). Its release was overshadowed by attempts from Hearst himself to suppress and discredit the film, given its unflattering parallels to his own life.
  • Influence and legacy: Its deep-focus visual style and nonlinear storytelling shaped generations of filmmakers, from Stanley Kubrick and Martin Scorsese to David Fincher, and it remains a fixture of virtually every film-school curriculum as the foundational text for teaching cinematic technique.
  • Current status: Preserved in the US Library of Congress National Film Registry (as part of its inaugural 1989 class), available via multiple home-video and streaming platforms (including a Criterion Collection 4K restoration), and regularly revived in retrospective theatrical screenings worldwide.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Mise-en-scène (Lesson 1)

This is the first lesson in a progressive, never-repeating series teaching transferable ways of watching and analyzing film — think of it as a toolkit that builds week over week, the same way the Spanish lessons build up grammar. We start with the most foundational lens of all: mise-en-scène (French for "placing on stage").

The method: Mise-en-scène covers everything the director has deliberately arranged within the frame, before the camera even starts moving or a single cut is made: set/production design, props, costume, lighting placement, and — critically — where actors are positioned and how they move relative to each other and the space (blocking). To analyze mise-en-scène, train yourself to pause on a single shot and ask: What has been placed here, and why? Who is near the camera and who is far away? Who is in sharp focus and who is soft or hidden? What does the physical arrangement of people and objects reveal about power, relationships, or psychological state — independent of anything a character says?

Worked example — Citizen Kane (dir. Orson Welles, 1941), the "snow globe" negotiation scene: Young Charles Kane's parents and the banker Mr. Thatcher sit at a table inside a cabin, in the foreground of the shot, finalizing the legal arrangement that will take the boy away to be raised elsewhere and hand control of his fortune to Thatcher. Visible through the cabin's window, in the deep background of the very same shot, the boy plays obliviously in the snow. Thanks to Gregg Toland's deep-focus cinematography, all three planes — the adults negotiating in the foreground, the middle-ground room, and Charles playing outside in the background — remain in sharp focus simultaneously, with no cut required to show us both halves of the tragedy at once.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Spatial distance as emotional/power distance — the people deciding Charles's fate are large and close to camera; Charles himself, the person most affected, is small, distant, and pushed to the literal background of the decision about his own life. (2) Frame-within-a-frame — the cabin window forms a rectangle around the boy, turning him into a picture, almost a photograph or memory, even as the scene is still unfolding — foreshadowing the nostalgic "Rosebud" longing that drives the entire film. (3) Staging as storytelling — nothing about this needs to be said in dialogue; the arrangement of bodies and depth alone communicates that Charles is being decided for, not with. Once you can see mise-en-scène this way, you start noticing it in every film: it's the layer beneath cinematography (how the camera captures the frame) and editing (how frames are cut together) — you have to know what's deliberately placed in a shot before you can appreciate how it's being shot or cut.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第十五天(10 分鐘,銜接第十四天內容)

第十四天學了現在進行式(estar + 現在分詞),用來描述「此刻正在發生」的動作。今天的新概念會把整個句子結構「反過來」:動詞 gustar(喜歡/使人喜愛)以及與它搭配使用的間接受詞代名詞(me, te, le, nos, les)——這是西班牙文中最容易搞混、卻也最核心的句型之一。

  • ¿Te gusta la música?(貼 估斯達 拉 木西卡)—— 你喜歡音樂嗎?
  • Sí, me gusta mucho el rock.(西,梅 估斯達 木秋 誒爾 rock)—— 對,我很喜歡搖滾樂。
  • A mi hermano le gustan los videojuegos.(阿 米 誒爾馬諾 雷 估斯壇 洛斯 逼喋喔虎誒勾斯)—— 我哥哥(弟弟)喜歡電動遊戲。

文法重點:gustar 的邏輯跟中文/英文「喜歡」剛好「相反」——句子的文法主詞其實是「被喜歡的東西」,而「喜歡的人」則變成間接受詞。這就是為什麼被喜歡的東西只有一個時(la música、el rock),動詞要用單數的 gusta;而被喜歡的東西是複數時(los videojuegos),動詞就要用複數的 gustan——動詞是跟著「被喜歡的東西」變化,而不是跟著「喜歡的人」變化。間接受詞代名詞(me/te/le/nos/les)永遠放在動詞前面;而「a + 人」(如 a mi hermano)則是可加可不加的補充說明,通常用來釐清或強調到底是「誰」喜歡,尤其 le/les 本身意思模糊,常需要靠這個補充說明清楚。

  • 歷史: 1768 年由普里特維·納拉揚·沙阿(Prithvi Narayan Shah)統一建國。1846 年至 1951 年間,拉納家族(Rana)世襲把持首相大權、實質獨裁統治,沙阿國王僅為虛位元首,直到 1951 年一場革命恢復王權。1996 年至 2006 年間長達十年的毛派叛亂造成約 1.7 萬人死亡後簽署和平協議;2008 年正式廢除君主制,尼泊爾自此成為聯邦民主共和國。
  • 現任領導人: 總統拉姆·錢德拉·包德爾(Ram Chandra Poudel,自 2023 年就任)擔任國家元首,職權多屬儀式性質;實權則掌握在總理手中——由於尼泊爾多黨聯合政治紛擾不斷,總理職位更迭頻繁。
  • 語言: 尼泊爾語為官方語言與全國通用語,但憲法承認數十種母語——全國共使用超過 120 種語言,包括邁蒂利語、博傑普爾語、塔芒語與內瓦爾語等。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、en.wikipedia.org

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「分心男友」(Distracted Boyfriend)

  • 名稱: 「分心男友」(Distracted Boyfriend,又稱「花心男友」)。
  • 起源/歷史: 出自西班牙攝影師 Antonio Guillem 於 2015 年拍攝的一組付費圖庫照片,畫面中一名男子與女友同行時,卻回頭色瞇瞇地盯著擦身而過的另一名女子,女友則露出震驚不滿的表情。這張照片在圖庫網站沉寂了兩年,才被迷因文化「發掘」。
  • 如何爆紅: 2017 年 8 月,網友開始在照片中三個人物身上疊加抽象概念標籤,將這張普通的圖庫照片變成一個可無限重複使用的萬用模板,用來表達「被更新、更有吸引力的事物吸引,而背離原本應該忠於的對象」。這個概念在短短數週內爆炸性擴散,成為 2017 至 2018 年最具代表性的迷因格式之一。
  • 意義與使用情境: 男友代表「我」或泛指的「人們」;女友代表原本應該被忠誠對待的對象(舊習慣、先前的選擇、既有的工具或信念);擦身而過的女子則代表誘人的新選項。幾乎可以套用在任何情境——程式語言、飲食習慣、政治立場、球隊粉絲——純粹作為「注意力被新事物吸引」的結構性比喻。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出無數加上標籤的改編版本、「迷因中的迷因」(關於這個迷因本身的二創),甚至有真人重現/戲仿版本;2023 年一度引發小風波——據報導 Antonio Guillem 與其圖庫代理商對未經授權的商業性重製發出下架/授權要求,是少數迷因原始素材來源方主張衍生作品版權的案例。
  • 目前現況: 到了 2020 年代已大致成為「經典/懷舊」模板——鮮少再被用於製作全新內容,但在各種迷因史回顧文章與「史上最佳迷因」清單中,仍持續被提及,是最具辨識度的反應圖(reaction image)格式之一。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《大國民》Citizen Kane(1941)

  • 名稱: 《大國民》(Citizen Kane,1941)。
  • 關鍵人物: 由奧森·威爾斯(Orson Welles,時年僅 25 歲,此片是他首部執導的長片)自導、共同編劇並主演,共同編劇為赫爾曼·J·曼凱維奇(Herman J. Mankiewicz),攝影指導則為葛雷格·托蘭(Gregg Toland)——其攝影技術上的創新,與威爾斯的導演成就同樣是這部片流傳後世的關鍵。
  • 時代背景: 由雷電華電影公司(RKO Pictures)出品,威爾斯獲得當時罕見的近乎全面創作自主權——對一位初執導筒的新人導演而言,在好萊塢片廠制度下極為少見。
  • 劇情概述: 一名新聞紀錄片記者試圖調查臨終前的報業大亨查爾斯·福斯特·肯恩(Charles Foster Kane)最後遺言「玫瑰花蕾」(Rosebud)的真正含意,透過認識肯恩的各方人物之破碎、甚至互相矛盾的回憶片段,逐步拼湊出他的一生——其角色原型隱約影射媒體大亨威廉·藍道夫·赫斯特(William Randolph Hearst)。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 大量運用深焦攝影(讓前景、中景、背景同時保持清晰對焦)、刻意呈現天花板的戲劇性低角度鏡頭(因此片場天花板須特別搭建)、強烈明暗對比的打光風格、取材自威爾斯廣播經驗、自然而重疊交錯的對白處理、破碎且多重視角交錯的非線性敘事結構,以及巧妙運用微縮模型與繪景(matte painting),在有限預算下擴展畫面的視覺規模。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 普遍被認為是影史最偉大的電影之一——曾連續五十年(1962 至 2002 年)蟬聯英國電影協會《視與聽》(Sight & Sound)影評人票選影史最佳影片第一名。上映當時,由於劇情與赫斯特本人生平的不利對應,曾遭赫斯特本人企圖打壓、抹黑,一度蒙上陰影。
  • 影響與傳承: 其深焦視覺風格與非線性敘事手法,深刻影響了史丹利·庫柏力克(Stanley Kubrick)、馬丁·史柯西斯(Martin Scorsese)、大衛·芬奇(David Fincher)等橫跨數個世代的導演,至今仍是幾乎所有電影學校課程中,教授電影技巧的基礎範本。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 已被美國國會圖書館「國家影片登記部」(National Film Registry)列入保存名單(1989 年首批入選),可透過多個家庭影音與串流平台觀賞(包括 Criterion Collection 發行的 4K 修復版),並經常在世界各地的電影回顧展中重新上映。

8) 看片方法 — 場面調度 Mise-en-scène(第一課)

這是「如何看電影」系列的第一課——這是一套會逐週累積、絕不重複的看片方法/影評視角訓練,概念上就像西班牙文課程逐步累積文法一樣,持續建立起分析電影的工具箱。我們從最根本的一個切入點開始:場面調度(mise-en-scène,法文原意為「安排上台」)。

方法說明: 場面調度指的是導演在攝影機開始移動、甚至在剪接發生之前,就已經刻意安排好、存在於「畫面之內」的一切:場景/美術設計、道具、服裝、燈光的擺放位置,以及最關鍵的——演員彼此之間的站位與相對移動方式(走位/調度,blocking)。想分析場面調度,可以訓練自己在某個鏡頭上暫停,問自己:這個畫面裡刻意放了什麼、為什麼這樣放?誰靠近攝影機、誰又離得很遠?誰的焦點清晰、誰模糊或被遮擋?人物與物件的物理配置,在角色開口說話之前,已經透露出哪些關於權力關係、人際關係或心理狀態的訊息?

實例示範 ——《大國民》(導演:奧森·威爾斯,1941)「雪中協商」一幕: 年幼的查爾斯·肯恩的父母與銀行家柴契爾先生(Mr. Thatcher),坐在小屋內畫面前景的桌邊,正在敲定一紙法律文件——這份文件將把男孩帶離家鄉、交由他人撫養,並把他龐大財產的控制權交給柴契爾。就在同一個鏡頭的深景處,透過小屋的窗戶,可以看見男孩渾然不知情地在雪地裡玩耍。多虧葛雷格·托蘭的深焦攝影技術,前景協商中的大人、中景的房間,以及背景中在雪地玩耍的查爾斯——這三個景深層次「同時」保持清晰對焦,完全不需要剪接,就能在同一個畫面裡,把這場悲劇的兩個面向一次呈現出來。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)空間距離即情感/權力距離——正在決定查爾斯命運的大人們,體型巨大且貼近鏡頭;而真正受影響最深的查爾斯本人,卻顯得渺小、遙遠,被推擠到這場攸關自己一生的決定的「畫面背景」之中。(2)畫框中的畫框——小屋的窗戶恰好在男孩四周形成一個矩形邊框,把他變成一張畫、幾乎像一張照片或一段回憶,即便劇情此刻仍在進行中——這也預示了貫穿全片、驅動整個故事的「玫瑰花蕾」式懷舊渴望。(3)調度即敘事——這一切完全不需要透過台詞說出口;光是人物身體位置與景深的安排,就已經傳達出查爾斯的命運是被「代替他」決定,而非「與他」共同決定。一旦學會用這種方式觀察場面調度,你會開始在每一部電影裡都注意到它——它是「攝影(鏡頭如何捕捉畫面)」與「剪接(鏡頭如何被剪在一起)」之下更基礎的一層:你得先看懂畫面裡刻意安排了什麼,才能真正欣賞鏡頭是「如何拍」、又是「如何剪」的。
