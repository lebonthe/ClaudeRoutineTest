3) Spanish Lesson — Day 32 (10 min, builds on Day 31)

Days 27 through 31 completed the full WEIRDO framework for when the present subjunctive is triggered: Wishes, Emotions, Impersonal expressions, Recommendations, Doubt, and Ojalá. Today opens a new (and separate) major subjunctive trigger category that WEIRDO doesn't cover at all: adverbial clauses of time — cuando (when), en cuanto / tan pronto como (as soon as), hasta que (until), and antes de que (before) — where the same conjunction can take either the subjunctive or the indicative depending on whether the action described is a still-pending, not-yet-real future event, or an already-real habitual/past fact.

  • Te llamaré cuando llegue a casa. — I'll call you when I get home. (future, not yet real → subjunctive)
  • Cuando llego a casa, siempre ceno con mi familia. — When I get home, I always have dinner with my family. (habitual routine, already real → indicative)
  • Voy a esperar aquí hasta que termines la tarea. — I'm going to wait here until you finish the homework. (pending → subjunctive)
  • Debes salir antes de que empiece a llover. — You should leave before it starts to rain. (always subjunctive, see grammar note)

Grammar note: cuando, en cuanto, tan pronto como, and hasta que all follow the same rule — use the subjunctive when the clause describes something that hasn't happened yet from the speaker's point in time (a future or still-pending event), and use the indicative when the clause describes a habitual, generally true, or already-completed action (Ayer, cuando llegué a casa, mi hermano ya estaba durmiendo — indicative, because it already happened). Antes de que is the one outlier in this group: it always takes the subjunctive, in every tense, with no indicative counterpart, because "before X" inherently frames X as not-yet-real relative to the main clause's action, even when the whole sentence is about the past (that past-tense version needs the imperfect subjunctive, a tense not yet taught — today's examples stay in the present/future to keep the pattern clean). This time-clause category is structurally distinct from all six WEIRDO triggers: WEIRDO is about the *attitude* toward an event (wishing, doubting, feeling); this category is about *when* an event falls relative to the moment of speaking.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 32: 恆 (Héng) — "Duration" / "Perseverance" (Thunder above, Wind below — Zhèn over Xùn). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary draws the direct line from yesterday's Hexagram 31: "夫婦之道不可以不久也,故受之以恆;恆者,久也。" ("The way of husband and wife cannot but endure for a long time; hence it is followed by Héng. Héng means to endure, to last long.") Where Xián (咸) pictured the initial mutual attraction and courtship between man and woman, Héng pictures what must follow if that union is to mean anything: not a single spark of feeling, but the capacity to sustain it. The Judgment reads: "恆,亨,利貞,无咎,利有攸往。" ("Duration: success. Perseverance furthers. No blame. It is favorable to have somewhere to go.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) explains the image's inner logic: "恆,久也。剛上而柔下,雷風相與,巽而動,剛柔皆應,恆。恆亨利貞,久于其道也。天地之道,恆久而不已也。利有攸往,終則有始也。日月得天而能久照,四時變化而能久成,聖人久於其道而天下化成。觀其所恆,而天地萬物之情可見矣。" ("Héng means to endure long. The hard [trigram] is above and the soft [trigram] is below; thunder and wind work together — Xùn is compliant and moves, hard and soft both respond to each other: this is Héng. That duration brings success, perseverance, and freedom from blame comes from long constancy in one's proper way. The way of Heaven and Earth is to endure long without ceasing. 'Favorable to have somewhere to go' means that an ending is always followed by a new beginning. The sun and moon, in accordance with Heaven, are able to shine for a long time; the four seasons, through their changes, are able to bring things to completion over and over; the sage, by abiding long in his proper way, transforms and completes the world. Observe what it is that a thing takes as its constant, and the true condition of all things under heaven and earth can be seen.") The Image commentary gives the ethical instruction: "雷風,恆;君子以立不易方。" ("Thunder and wind: this is Héng. Thus the superior person stands firm and does not change his direction.") Read together, the three texts distinguish Héng's "duration" from mere stubbornness or stasis: true constancy is not standing still but continuing to move correctly along one's proper course — like thunder and wind, which are both constant motion, or like the sun, moon, and seasons, which endure precisely because they keep changing in a fixed, recurring pattern rather than freezing in place. Notably, Héng is also one of only four hexagrams in the entire 64 (alongside 63 and 64, and its own predecessor 31) to be discussed together as a structurally linked "pair" whose meanings are explicitly built on each other rather than standing alone.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Madagascar (馬達加斯加)

  • Location: An island nation in the Indian Ocean off the southeastern coast of Africa, separated from Mozambique by the roughly 400 km-wide Mozambique Channel; it is the world's fourth-largest island (after Greenland, New Guinea, and Borneo).
  • Territory: Approximately 587,041 km² — about 16.2 times Taiwan's land area (~36,193 km²), and roughly comparable in size to France and Belgium combined, or slightly larger than the US state of California plus Arizona. Madagascar physically separated from mainland Africa around 160 million years ago, and roughly 90% of its wildlife (including all lemur species) is endemic, found nowhere else on Earth.
  • Population: Approximately 30 million (2023 est.), one of the fastest-growing populations in the Indian Ocean region, with roughly two-thirds living in rural areas dependent on subsistence agriculture.
  • Religion: Roughly half the population identifies as Christian (split between Catholic and Protestant), with a large share continuing to practice indigenous Malagasy ancestor-veneration traditions (including "fady," localized taboos tied to specific families, clans, or regions) either alongside or blended with Christianity; a Muslim minority of roughly 7% is concentrated mainly in coastal and northern areas with historical Arab and Comorian trade links.
  • Economy: Predominantly agricultural — Madagascar supplies an estimated 80% of the world's natural vanilla — alongside mining (sapphires, and nickel/cobalt from the large Ambatovy mine) and a growing textile/garment export sector; despite its extraordinary biodiversity-driven tourism potential, it remains one of the world's poorest countries by GDP per capita, with chronic political instability a persistent drag on investment.
  • History: Uniquely among African nations, Madagascar was first settled not by mainland Africans but by Austronesian seafarers arriving from the Indonesian archipelago roughly 1,350-1,500 years ago (with later waves of Bantu African, Arab, and South Asian migration and trade), which is why the Malagasy language is Austronesian rather than African in origin. Various regional kingdoms unified into the Kingdom of Madagascar under the Merina dynasty in the early 19th century; France colonized the island in 1896-97, and Madagascar regained independence in 1960. Since then it has weathered repeated political crises, including a 2009 coup that first brought current leader Andry Rajoelina to power.
  • Current leader: President Andry Rajoelina, who first seized power via the 2009 coup, was elected president in 2018, and was re-elected in a disputed 2023 vote that the opposition boycotted amid eligibility disputes over his acquisition of French citizenship.
  • Language: Malagasy (the sole official indigenous language, and notably the only Austronesian language spoken entirely within Africa) and French (a co-official language retained from the colonial era, widely used in government, business, and higher education).
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov/the-world-factbook

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Kermit Sipping Tea" / "But That's None of My Business"

  • Name: Widely known as "Kermit Sipping Tea" or by its signature caption, "But That's None of My Business."
  • Origin/history: The image originates from a 2014 Lipton tea advertising campaign, a photograph of Kermit the Frog (the Muppet) holding and sipping a cup of Lipton tea. Once the photo escaped its original ad context and began circulating on Tumblr and Twitter, someone paired it with the now-iconic caption "But that's none of my business," turning an innocuous product photo into a reaction image for delivering a pointed, judgmental observation while performing exaggerated nonchalance.
  • How it became popular: The combination of a beloved, wholesome children's-TV character and a deliberately petty, gossipy caption created a comic incongruity that spread rapidly across Tumblr, Twitter, and Instagram through 2014 and 2015, quickly becoming one of the most recognizable reaction-image templates of that meme era, versatile enough to attach to virtually any observation a poster wanted to make while feigning detachment.
  • Meaning/usage: Deployed as a caption or reply to accompany a subtly shady, gossipy, or "shade"-throwing remark about someone else's behavior, delivered with mock innocence — the joke is the gap between the pointedness of the observation and the surface-level claim of not caring. The meme also cemented "sipping tea" as slang for gossiping, feeding directly into the now-mainstream expression "spill the tea" (share the gossip).
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned "Evil Kermit," a separate but related derivative meme pairing regular Kermit with a black-hooded "Dark Kermit" (originally an unrelated Muppet-show villain image) representing Kermit's inner devil/temptation, used for jokes about giving in to one's own bad impulses; also produced numerous "sipping tea" GIF variants across other characters, and helped drive "spill the tea" into mainstream, non-meme usage in entertainment journalism and everyday slang.
  • Current status: The original image is now a dated but still widely understood reaction-meme classic; unusually for a 2014-era meme, its associated slang ("spill/sip the tea" for gossip) achieved lasting, independent life in mainstream English well beyond internet-meme circles, giving it more longevity than most memes from its cohort.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Wayang Kulit (Indonesian Shadow Puppetry)

  • Name/creators: Wayang kulit ("shadow" + "hide/leather"), the traditional shadow-puppet theatre of Java and Bali, Indonesia; like most folk-court performing traditions, it has no single author, having evolved over centuries through generations of puppeteer-performers called dalang.
  • Era/background: Its roots likely predate Java's Hindu-Buddhist period, possibly connected to older indigenous ancestor-spirit rituals, but the tradition as known today crystallized once Hindu-Buddhist kingdoms (flourishing roughly the 9th-15th centuries) brought the Indian epics into Javanese culture. When Islam spread through Java in the 15th-16th centuries, wayang kulit adapted further rather than disappearing — puppet designs became more elongated and stylized, partly to navigate religious sensitivities around figural human imagery, while the stories and performance structure survived largely intact.
  • Plot/content overview: A traditional performance runs a single continuous night, dusk to dawn (roughly 7-9 hours), narrating episodes (lakon) from the Ramayana or Mahabharata, or from local Javanese Panji romances. Woven through the "high" Sanskrit-epic material are the punakawan — clown-servant characters (Semar, Gareng, Petruk, Bagong) unique to the Javanese versions of these stories — who break register to deliver comic relief, folk wisdom, and often pointed contemporary social or political satire.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Each puppet is an intricately carved and painted flat figure cut from buffalo or goat hide, with jointed rod-controlled arms, held up against a taut white cotton screen (kelir) backlit originally by an oil lamp (blencong, now often electric). This creates two simultaneous, distinct audiences: those seated on the shadow side see only the puppets' silhouettes in motion, while the dalang and those seated with him see the puppets' full painted color and craftsmanship directly. The dalang works alone, voicing every character with a distinct pitch and speech register, narrating transitions, and simultaneously cueing the gamelan orchestra using a wooden knocker (cempala) held between his toes — all while improvising extensively within the fixed lakon.
  • Cultural/historical significance: For centuries, wayang kulit served as a primary vehicle for transmitting Hindu epic literature, Javanese cosmology and court philosophy, and later Islamic-era moral teaching, to a largely non-literate audience; it functioned simultaneously as elite courtly art and as popular village entertainment, with the clown characters historically providing a sanctioned outlet for social and political commentary that direct speech could not.
  • Influence and legacy: UNESCO proclaimed Indonesian wayang a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity in 2003 (inscribed on the Representative List in 2008); it influenced related shadow-theatre traditions elsewhere in Southeast Asia (Malaysia's own wayang kulit, and the nang shadow-puppet traditions of Thailand and Cambodia), and its visual vocabulary continues to shape Indonesian batik motifs, fine art, and modern theatre and animation.
  • Current status/where to experience: Still actively performed at ceremonies, weddings, and cultural festivals across Java and Bali, though attendance among younger urban audiences has declined relative to television and digital media; dedicated conservatories such as ISI Surakarta continue training professional dalang, and government and UNESCO-linked heritage programs actively support the tradition's continuation, with shortened performances now common at festivals and for tourists alongside the traditional all-night form.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, ich.unesco.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Aspect Ratio & Screen Format as a Storytelling Device (Lesson 18)

Lesson 8 (Framing & Composition) covered how a director arranges subjects and space within a given frame. Today's lens zooms out one level further, to a variable most viewers never consciously register: the shape of the frame itself — its aspect ratio, the ratio of width to height — and how filmmakers use that shape as a storytelling tool independent of what's actually staged inside it.

The method: Aspect ratio choices carry inherent connotations before a single shot is composed. A boxier, narrower ratio (1.33:1/4:3 "Academy," the standard through the early 1950s) emphasizes verticality and confinement, well suited to intimate or claustrophobic character drama. A wider ratio (1.85:1 standard widescreen, or 2.35:1/2.39:1 anamorphic "Scope") opens up horizontal space, suited to landscapes, epic scale, and staging several characters or a wide environment across the frame at once. Most films commit to one ratio throughout, so the choice mainly signals genre and tone from the outset — but the most advanced use of the technique is a deliberate mid-film change in aspect ratio, letting the frame's shape itself mark a shift in time period, memory, or emotional register, doing narrative work usually reserved for costume, color grading (Lesson 5), or camera movement (Lesson 15).

Worked example — The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014, dir. Wes Anderson, cinematography by Robert Yeoman): The film nests three narrative time layers inside a frame story, and Anderson assigns each layer its own aspect ratio: the 1985 and 1968 "present-day" framing scenes use standard 1.85:1 widescreen; a brief 1960s-set middle layer uses 2.35:1 anamorphic widescreen; and the 1932 core narrative — Zero's account of working as a lobby boy under the concierge M. Gustave, which makes up the bulk of the film — is shot in the boxy, near-square 1.37:1 Academy ratio, deliberately evoking the visual format of films actually made in that era.

What to look for and why it works: (1) The ratio itself functions as an instant time-stamp — the moment the frame's shape changes, the audience registers "we've moved to a different time period" before a single line of dialogue, costume, or set detail confirms it, accomplishing in one visual cue what would otherwise require expository text or dialogue. (2) The chosen ratio reinforces a technique from an earlier lesson rather than working in isolation — the narrower 1.37:1 frame used for the 1932 story leaves less horizontal room to work with, which makes Anderson's famously precise, centered, symmetrical compositions (the subject of Lesson 8) read as even more deliberate and doll's-house-like, since there is less room on either side for anything to feel accidental. (3) It trains you to notice a device most viewers only feel, not see — audiences unfamiliar with aspect ratio as a concept typically describe the effect as "this part of the movie feels different" without being able to say why; naming the aspect-ratio shift converts a vague impression into an analyzable, describable choice — precisely the skill this lesson series is building toward.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第三十二天(10 分鐘,銜接第三十一天內容)

第二十七天到第三十一天,已經完整教完虛擬式現在式的整套 WEIRDO 觸發架構:希望動詞、情感表達、非人稱表達、建議/請求動詞、懷疑/否定,以及 Ojalá。今天要開啟一個全新、且完全不屬於 WEIRDO 範圍的重要虛擬式觸發類別:時間副詞子句——cuando(當……的時候)、en cuanto／tan pronto como(一……就……)、hasta que(直到……為止),以及 antes de que(在……之前)——這些連接詞會依句子描述的動作,究竟是「尚未發生、還不真實」的未來事件,還是「已經成真」的習慣性動作或過去事實,而在虛擬式與直陳式之間切換。

  • Te llamaré cuando llegue a casa. — 我到家後就會打電話給你。(未來、尚未發生 → 虛擬式)
  • Cuando llego a casa, siempre ceno con mi familia. — 我每次到家,都會和家人一起吃晚餐。(習慣性、已成真的事實 → 直陳式)
  • Voy a esperar aquí hasta que termines la tarea. — 我會在這裡等,直到你把作業寫完。(尚未發生 → 虛擬式)
  • Debes salir antes de que empiece a llover. — 你應該在開始下雨之前離開。(永遠使用虛擬式,詳見文法重點)

文法重點:cuando、en cuanto、tan pronto como 與 hasta que 都遵循同一條規則——如果子句描述的是「從說話者當下時間點來看,尚未發生」的未來或懸而未決的事件,就要用「虛擬式」;如果子句描述的是「習慣性、普遍成立,或已經完成」的動作,就要用「直陳式」(例如:Ayer, cuando llegué a casa, mi hermano ya estaba durmiendo——昨天我到家的時候,我弟弟已經在睡了,這裡用直陳式,因為事情已經發生過)。Antes de que 是這組連接詞中唯一的例外:無論哪個時態,永遠使用虛擬式,沒有對應的直陳式版本,因為「在 X 之前」這個結構,本質上就是把 X 定位成「相對於主句動作而言,尚未成真」的事件,即使整句話講的是過去發生的事也是如此(這種過去語境下的版本需要用到「過去未完成虛擬式」,一個尚未教過的時態——為了保持今天規則的清晰度,例句先只停留在現在/未來時態)。這一類時間副詞子句,和之前六個 WEIRDO 觸發類別在結構上完全不同:WEIRDO 探討的是說話者對一件事的「態度」(希望、懷疑、感受);而這一類探討的則是一件事「相對於說話時間點,發生在什麼時候」。

4) 易經卦象

第三十二卦:恆卦(Héng)——「恆久」/「持恆」(上雷下風,震上巽下)。〈序卦傳〉直接點出了與昨天第三十一卦的承接關係:「夫婦之道不可以不久也,故受之以恆;恆者,久也。」(夫婦相處之道,不可不長久維繫,因此接續的是恆卦;恆,意即長久。)如果咸卦(咸)描繪的是男女之間最初相互吸引、彼此求偶的一刻,恆卦所描繪的,則是這段結合若要有意義,接下來必須具備的能力——不是一時的感應火花,而是能夠讓這份感應長久持續下去的本事。卦辭寫道:「恆,亨,利貞,无咎,利有攸往。」(恆久之卦:亨通,正固有利,沒有過錯,適合有所作為、勇於前行。)〈彖傳〉進一步解釋了這一卦的內在邏輯:「恆,久也。剛上而柔下,雷風相與,巽而動,剛柔皆應,恆。恆亨利貞,久于其道也。天地之道,恆久而不已也。利有攸往,終則有始也。日月得天而能久照,四時變化而能久成,聖人久於其道而天下化成。觀其所恆,而天地萬物之情可見矣。」(恆,意即長久。剛爻在上,柔爻在下,雷與風相互配合;巽卦柔順而能動,剛與柔彼此相應,這就是恆。恆能亨通、正固而無過錯,正是因為長久堅守正道所致。天地運行之道,正是恆久不止息。「利有攸往」意指一件事的終結,正是另一件事的開始。日月依循天道運行,因此能夠長久照耀;四季透過不斷變化,才能一次又一次地成就萬物;聖人長久堅守正道,天下因而得以教化成就。觀察一件事物以何為恆常不變之道,天地萬物的真實情狀便可由此得見。)〈象傳〉則補上這一卦的德行教誨:「雷風,恆;君子以立不易方。」(雷與風,這正是恆卦的象徵;因此德行高尚的人,應當立身堅定,不輕易改變自己的方向。)綜合這三段文字可以看出,恆卦所謂的「恆久」,並非單純的停滯不動或固執不變,而是能夠持續沿著正確的方向前進——就像雷與風本身都是持續運動的力量,又像太陽、月亮與四季,正是因為不斷依循固定循環的方式變化,才能長久維持下去,而不是靜止凝固。值得一提的是,恆卦也是六十四卦當中,少數幾個會與前一卦(第三十一卦)被視為結構上緊密相連、意義彼此建立在對方基礎之上的「成對卦」之一(另一組類似的成對卦是最後兩卦,第六十三與第六十四卦)。

(第三十二次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)、第二十一卦 噬嗑(Shì Kè)、第二十二卦 賁(Bì)、第二十三卦 剝(Bō)、第二十四卦 復(Fù)、第二十五卦 无妄(Wú Wàng)、第二十六卦 大畜(Dà Chù)、第二十七卦 頤(Yí)、第二十八卦 大過(Dà Guò)、第二十九卦 坎(Kǎn)、第三十卦 離(Lí)、第三十一卦 咸(Xián)、第三十二卦 恆(Héng)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 馬達加斯加(Madagascar)

  • 位置: 位於印度洋、非洲大陸東南方外海的島國,與莫三比克之間隔著寬約 400 公里的莫三比克海峽;是全世界第四大島(僅次於格陵蘭、紐幾內亞與婆羅洲)。
  • 領土面積: 約 587,041 平方公里——約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 16.2 倍,大小大約相當於法國加上比利時的面積總和,或略大於美國加州加上亞利桑那州。馬達加斯加約在 1.6 億年前便與非洲大陸分離,島上約 90% 的野生動物(包括所有種類的狐猴)都是特有種,全球其他地方都看不到。
  • 人口: 約 3,000 萬人(2023 年估計),是印度洋地區人口成長最快的國家之一,約三分之二人口居住於鄉村地區,以自給自足的農業為生。
  • 宗教: 約一半人口信仰基督教(天主教與新教各占一部分),同時仍有相當比例的人口延續馬達加斯加本土的祖靈崇拜傳統(包括「fady」,一種因特定家族、氏族或地區而異的地方禁忌),這些傳統或與基督信仰並存,或彼此融合;穆斯林人口約占 7%,主要集中在沿海與北部地區,與歷史上阿拉伯及葛摩商貿往來有關。
  • 經濟: 以農業為主——馬達加斯加供應了全球約八成的天然香草,另外還有礦業(藍寶石,以及來自大型 Ambatovy 礦場的鎳與鈷)與逐漸成長的紡織/成衣出口產業;儘管擁有得天獨厚的生物多樣性觀光潛力,以人均 GDP 計算,馬達加斯加仍是全球最貧窮的國家之一,長期的政治不穩定持續拖累投資意願。
  • 歷史: 馬達加斯加在非洲國家中相當獨特——最早定居島上的並非非洲大陸族群,而是約 1,350 至 1,500 年前,從印尼群島一帶橫渡而來的南島語族航海者(後續又有非洲班圖族、阿拉伯與南亞移民與貿易往來的多次浪潮),這也是為什麼馬達加斯加語(Malagasy)屬於南島語系、而非非洲語系的原因。19 世紀初,各地區王國逐漸統一,由梅里納王朝(Merina)建立馬達加斯加王國;法國於 1896-97 年將其納為殖民地,馬達加斯加於 1960 年恢復獨立。此後歷經多次政治危機,包括 2009 年一場政變,現任領導人拉喬利納(Andry Rajoelina)便是透過這場政變首度掌權。
  • 現任領導人: 總統安德里・拉喬利納(Andry Rajoelina),他先於 2009 年透過政變奪權,2018 年當選總統,並於 2023 年一場爭議不斷的選舉中連任——反對陣營以其取得法國國籍是否影響參選資格為由,抵制了這場選舉。
  • 語言: 馬達加斯加語(唯一的本土官方語言,也是全球唯一完全在非洲境內通行的南島語系語言)與法語(殖民時期遺留下來的共同官方語言,廣泛用於政府、商業與高等教育)。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov/the-world-factbook

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「喝茶的科米」(Kermit Sipping Tea)/「But That's None of My Business」

  • 名稱: 廣為人知的名稱是「喝茶的科米」(Kermit Sipping Tea),或以其經典標語「But That's None of My Business」(但那不是我的事)稱之。
  • 起源/歷史: 這張圖片源自 2014 年立頓(Lipton)紅茶的一則廣告,拍的是布偶明星「科米青蛙」(Kermit the Frog)手持並啜飲一杯立頓紅茶的照片。這張照片脫離原始廣告情境後,開始在 Tumblr 與推特上流傳,後來有人為它配上了如今已成經典的標語「But that's none of my business」(但那不是我的事),讓這張原本無害的商品照片,轉變成一張可以搭配「一邊語帶批判地評論他人、一邊故作事不關己」的萬用反應圖。
  • 如何爆紅: 一個深受喜愛、形象純真的兒童節目角色,搭配一句刻意小題大作、帶點八卦意味的標語,兩者之間的反差製造出強烈的喜劇效果,2014 到 2015 年間迅速在 Tumblr、推特與 Instagram 上瘋傳,很快成為那個世代最具代表性的反應圖範本之一——因為它幾乎可以套用在任何一個「一邊裝作事不關己、一邊發表尖銳評論」的場合上。
  • 意義與使用情境: 常被用來搭配一句語帶諷刺、帶點八卦或「酸言酸語」意味、針對他人行為的評論,並刻意裝出一副無辜、事不關己的樣子——笑點就在於「評論本身有多尖銳」和「表面上宣稱毫不在意」之間的落差。這個迷因也讓「sipping tea」(啜茶)一詞,正式成為「講八卦」的代稱,直接催生出如今已廣泛流通的說法「spill the tea」(爆料、講八卦)。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出另一個相關但獨立的迷因「邪惡科米」(Evil Kermit)——把一般版本的科米,搭配一個原本出自布偶秀、與科米無關的「黑袍暗黑版科米」角色配對,象徵科米內心的另一個自己/心中的誘惑,常用來調侃「明知不該卻還是向壞念頭妥協」的情境;此外也衍生出大量套用其他角色的「啜茶」GIF 變體,並帶動「spill the tea」這個詞徹底走入主流,廣泛出現在娛樂新聞報導與日常口語俚語之中,早已超出迷因文化的範圍。
  • 目前現況: 這張原始圖片如今是一個過氣但仍廣為人知的經典反應圖迷因;不同於多數 2014 年代誕生的迷因,它衍生出的俚語(用「spill/sip the tea」代稱講八卦)已經在主流英語中獨立存續下來,遠遠超出網路迷因圈的範疇,讓它比同期的多數迷因更具生命力。
    資料來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 印尼皮影戲(Wayang Kulit)

  • 名稱/創作者: Wayang kulit(「wayang」意指影子/戲劇,「kulit」意指皮革),是印尼爪哇島與峇里島的傳統皮影戲劇場形式;如同大多數民間宮廷表演藝術傳統,它並沒有單一創作者,而是歷經數個世紀、由一代又一代稱為「dalang」的操偶師/表演者共同發展而成。
  • 時代背景: 其根源可能早於爪哇的印度教-佛教時期,或許與更古老的本土祖靈祭祀儀式有關,但如今所知的皮影戲傳統形式,是在印度教-佛教王國興盛時期(約 9 至 15 世紀)、將印度史詩文學帶入爪哇文化之後才逐漸成形。當伊斯蘭教在 15 至 16 世紀傳入爪哇時,皮影戲並未因此消失,反而進一步調整適應——偶身設計變得更加拉長、風格化,部分原因是為了迴避宗教對人形圖像的敏感度,但故事內容與演出結構大致保留了下來。
  • 劇情概述: 傳統演出從黃昏連續進行到天亮(約 7 至 9 小時),敘說取自《羅摩衍那》或《摩訶婆羅多》的劇目(lakon),或是取自爪哇本地的班吉(Panji)傳奇故事。在這些「高雅」的梵文史詩題材之間,穿插著爪哇版本獨有的丑角僕人角色群「Punakawan」(塞馬爾 Semar、加倫 Gareng、彼特魯 Petruk、巴貢 Bagong)——他們常會跳脫原本的敘事語氣,插入喜劇橋段、民間智慧,乃至帶有針對性的當代社會或政治諷刺。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 每個偶都是以水牛皮或山羊皮精雕細琢、彩繪而成的扁平人形,手臂裝有可活動的關節、以竿子操控,豎立在一面拉緊的白色棉布幕(kelir)前方,幕後原本以油燈(blencong)打光,現今多已改用電燈。這樣的設計創造出兩種截然不同、同時存在的觀眾視角:坐在影子一側的觀眾,只能看到皮偶移動的黑影輪廓;而坐在操偶師這一側的觀眾(包括 dalang 本人),則能直接看到皮偶完整的彩繪與工藝細節。dalang 通常獨自一人,以不同音調與口音為每個角色配音、負責劇情轉場的口白,同時用夾在腳趾間的木製敲擊棒(cempala)指揮甘美朗樂團(gamelan)伴奏——所有這一切都是在固定劇目框架下,大量即興發揮完成的。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 數百年來,皮影戲一直是向普遍不識字的大眾傳遞印度教史詩文學、爪哇宇宙觀與宮廷哲學,以及後來伊斯蘭時期道德教誨的重要媒介;它同時身兼宮廷精緻藝術與鄉村通俗娛樂兩種身分,而劇中的丑角角色,歷來也提供了一個「直接言論做不到」的、受社會認可的社會與政治評論出口。
  • 影響與傳承: 聯合國教科文組織於 2003 年將印尼皮影戲列為「人類口述與非物質文化遺產代表作」(2008 年正式列入代表名錄);其影響力擴及東南亞其他地區的皮影戲傳統(包括馬來西亞自身的皮影戲,以及泰國與柬埔寨的皮影戲 nang 傳統),其視覺語彙也持續形塑印尼的蠟染(batik)圖案、視覺藝術,以及現代劇場與動畫創作。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 至今仍在爪哇與峇里島各地的慶典、婚禮與文化節活動中積極演出,不過相較於電視與數位媒體,年輕都市族群的觀賞人口已有所下滑;像 ISI Surakarta 這類專門的藝術學院,仍持續培訓專業 dalang,政府與聯合國教科文組織相關的文化保存計畫也積極支持這項傳統延續下去,如今在藝術節與觀光場合,搭配傳統整夜演出形式的縮短版演出也已相當常見。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、ich.unesco.org

8) 看片方法 — 畫面比例與銀幕格式作為敘事工具 Aspect Ratio & Screen Format as a Storytelling Device(第十八課)

第八課「取景與構圖」談的是導演如何在既定的畫面框架內安排主體與空間。今天的分析視角要再往外拉一層,聚焦在一個大多數觀眾根本不會意識到的變因:畫面本身的形狀——也就是「長寬比」,畫面寬度與高度的比例——以及導演如何運用這個形狀本身作為敘事工具,而與畫面裡實際安排了什麼內容無關。

方法說明: 長寬比的選擇,在任何一顆鏡頭被構圖之前,就已經自帶特定的意涵。較窄、較方正的比例(1.33:1／4:3「學院比例」,是 1950 年代初期以前的標準規格)強調垂直感與侷限感,適合親密或帶有壓迫感的角色劇情片。較寬的比例(1.85:1 標準寬螢幕,或 2.35:1／2.39:1 變形寬螢幕「Scope」)則能開展出更多水平空間,適合呈現壯闊風景、史詩規模場面,或是一次在畫面中安排多位角色或廣闊環境。大多數電影會從頭到尾維持同一種長寬比,因此這個選擇主要在開場就為整部片定下類型與調性——但這項技巧最進階的運用方式,是在片中刻意改變長寬比,讓畫面形狀本身標記出時代、記憶或情緒調性的轉換,完成通常要靠服裝、色彩分級(第五課)或運鏡(第十五課)才能達成的敘事工作。

實例示範——《歡迎來到布達佩斯大飯店》(The Grand Budapest Hotel,2014,導演魏斯・安德森 Wes Anderson,攝影指導羅伯特・約曼 Robert Yeoman): 這部電影在一個框架故事中,嵌套了三層敘事時間軸,而安德森為每一層都分配了專屬的長寬比:1985 年與 1968 年的「現在時空」框架場景,採用標準的 1.85:1 寬螢幕;一段簡短的 1960 年代中間層敘事,採用 2.35:1 變形寬螢幕;而構成全片主體的 1932 年核心敘事——齊羅(Zero)講述自己在禮賓員古斯塔夫先生(M. Gustave)手下擔任門房學徒的故事——則採用方正、近乎正方形的 1.37:1 學院比例,刻意呼應那個年代真正拍攝的電影所使用的視覺格式。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)長寬比本身就成了一種即時的時間標記——畫面形狀一改變,觀眾在任何一句台詞、服裝或場景細節出現之前,就已經意識到「我們換到了不同的時代」,這個視覺線索一步到位,取代了原本需要靠說明性台詞或文字才能達成的效果。(2)選定的比例強化了前面課程教過的技巧,而不是單獨存在的選擇——用於 1932 年故事的較窄 1.37:1 畫面,留給水平方向的空間更少,這反而讓安德森招牌式的精準、置中、對稱構圖(第八課的主題)顯得更加刻意、更像玩具屋一般的精緻感,因為畫面兩側幾乎沒有多餘空間可以讓任何東西顯得隨意或偶然。(3)這項技巧訓練你去注意一個大多數觀眾只能「感覺到」、卻「看不出來」的手法——不熟悉長寬比概念的觀眾,通常只會形容「這部分的電影感覺不太一樣」,卻說不出原因;能夠準確指出長寬比的轉換,就是把一種模糊的印象,轉化成一個可以分析、可以清楚描述的具體選擇——這正是這一系列課程一直在培養的能力。
