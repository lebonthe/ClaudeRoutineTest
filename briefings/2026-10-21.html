3) Spanish Lesson — Day 97: Empezar a / Ponerse a / Dejar de / Terminar de ("Starting, Stopping, Finishing")

Empezar a (or comenzar a) + infinitive means "to start doing": Empiezo a trabajar a las nueve. Ponerse a + infinitive is "to set about, to get down to", with a sudden or determined feel: Se puso a llorar. Dejar de + infinitive is "to stop, quit": Dejé de fumar (not to be confused with plain dejar, "to leave/let", Day 79). Terminar de + infinitive is "to finish": Terminé de leer = I finished reading, while Dejé de leer = I stopped reading. Spelling note: the yo preterite of empezar is empecé (z becomes c). All four need the preposition (a / de) and then the infinitive, never -ing (Day 86).

  • ¿Cuándo empezaste a estudiar español? — When did you start studying Spanish?
  • Empecé a estudiarlo hace tres meses. — I started three months ago.
  • ¿Ya dejaste de ver la tele en inglés? — Have you stopped watching TV in English?
  • Todavía no, pero ya terminé de leer mi primer libro. — Not yet, but I've already finished reading my first book.
  • Ahora me pongo a practicar con un amigo. — Now I'm getting down to practicing with a friend.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 33: 天下 (Tiānxià) — "All Under Heaven" (the final chapter)

The closing chapter of the traditional 33-chapter text (Miscellaneous Chapters, Ch. 33), and the last piece in this daily series. Unlike the rest of the book, it is not a collection of fables but an essay-style survey of the schools of thought of the Warring States period, which many scholars read as an epilogue or even a later editor's work (that attribution is debated). It opens by lamenting that "the arts of governing the world are many" and that each school thinks its own is the best, then asks where the one true Way has gone. It speaks of an ancient unity of the Way and describes the ideal of the "sage within, king without" (內聖外王), and says the Way has now been torn apart: each school grasps one corner, like the ears, eyes, nose and mouth that cannot take the place of one another (the original image is that each sense organ has its use but none can communicate with the others).

It then reviews, in order, the Mohists (Mozi and Qin Guli, praised for effort but criticized for making life too hard for ordinary people), Song Xing and Yin Wen, Peng Meng, Tian Pian and Shen Dao (who "abandon knowledge and self" and drift with things), Guan Yin and Lao Dan (Laozi, praised highly: "regard weakness and softness as the outer shell, and emptiness that harms nothing as the core"), and then Zhuang Zhou himself. Zhuangzi's self-portrait: he thought the world was too muddled for serious speech, so he used "goblet words" (卮言), "weighty words" (重言) and "imputed words" (寓言) (compare Chapter 27), "wandered alone with the spirit of heaven and earth without looking down on the ten thousand things", and his writing, though strange and winding, "does no harm" (其書雖瑰瑋而連犿無傷也).

The chapter ends with Hui Shi, Zhuangzi's friend and debating partner, and the "Ten Theses" of his school of names (the largest is "the Great One, so large nothing is outside it; the smallest is the Small One, so small nothing is inside"; "a stick one foot long, cut in half each day, is never exhausted in ten thousand generations"; "the shadow of a flying bird has never moved"), together with paradoxes attributed to other debaters. The author admires Hui Shi's talent ("his books fill five carts") but judges that he chased things instead of the Way, comparing him to a mosquito or gadfly laboring in vain.

Takeaway: Every school holds a corner of the truth and is dangerous when it forgets the rest; and the book that began by soaring above the world ends with a sober map of the arguments that fill it. It joined the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes (later Yugoslavia) in 1918, was divided between Germany, Italy and Hungary in World War II, then became a republic in socialist Yugoslavia, the most prosperous one. After a December 1990 plebiscite (about 88% for independence), it declared independence on June 25, 1991, and fought the brief "Ten-Day War" with the Yugoslav army; the army withdrew within months and the EU recognized it in January 1992. The poem "Zdravljica" by national poet France Prešeren (1844) supplies the national anthem's text.

This is Janša's fourth term.
  • Language: Slovene, a South Slavic language written in the Latin alphabet; it keeps the rare "dual" grammatical number (a separate form for exactly two of something) and has about 2.5 million speakers worldwide. Italian and Hungarian are co-official in ethnically mixed municipalities.
  • Culture & trivia: Lake Bled and its island church, the Postojna and Škocjan caves (the Karst region gave its name to karst landscapes everywhere), a strong beekeeping tradition (the Carniolan honey bee), and world-class sport: cyclists Tadej Pogačar and Primož Roglič and basketball star Luka Dončić.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "OK Boomer"

  • Name: "OK Boomer" (also "Okay boomer").
  • Origin / history: A dismissive retort aimed at older people, especially baby boomers (born roughly 1946-1964), who lecture younger people or dismiss their concerns. Scattered uses of "boomer" as a pejorative appear years earlier on forums such as Reddit and 4chan, and the exact first use of the phrase is disputed. It broke out in late 2019, helped by a viral TikTok-era clip of an older man complaining about younger generations, to which commenters answered "OK boomer".
  • How it became popular: Within weeks in autumn 2019 it was everywhere on TikTok, Twitter and Reddit, then in mainstream media (the New York Times ran an explainer in late October or early November 2019). On November 4, 2019, New Zealand MP Chlöe Swarbrick, then 25, answered a heckler with "OK boomer" in Parliament while speaking on the Zero Carbon Bill, which turned it into a global headline. A US radio host, Bob Lonsberry, called it "the N-word of ageism" in a widely mocked tweet, which fed the backlash-and-spread cycle.
  • Meaning & usage context: It means roughly "I'm done engaging with that condescending, out-of-touch view." It is used for dismissal rather than argument, usually about climate, housing costs, debt and work culture. It can be playful between friends or sharp in a political fight; critics say it is ageist, and "boomer" can be used for an attitude rather than an age.
  • Variations / evolution: "OK Zoomer" and "OK doomer" came back as comebacks, "boomer" spawned "Boomer Remover" (a bitter joke during COVID-19) and "Karen"-like type names, and the phrase was printed on T-shirts and mugs, with trademark applications reported. Language-specific versions exist in many languages.
  • Current status: Past its peak (late 2019 to early 2020) and now often used ironically or as a nostalgic marker of 2019, though "boomer" as a label for outdated attitudes remains in common use. (Details are from general knowledge and approximate; not verified against a source today.)

7) Film / Performing-Arts Spotlight — Un Chien Andalou (1929, dir. Luis Buñuel, co-written with Salvador Dalí)

  • Name & key figures: Un chien andalou ("An Andalusian Dog"), a silent short film of about 16 minutes by Spanish director Luis Buñuel and painter Salvador Dalí, starring Pierre Batcheff and Simone Mareuil, with Buñuel himself as the man with the razor.
  • Era / background: Made in France in 1929, in the circle of the Paris surrealists, and paid for largely with money from Buñuel's mother. Buñuel and Dalí wrote the script in about a week by trading dreams and images (Buñuel's dream of a cloud slicing the moon, Dalí's dream of a hand crawling with ants), under the rule that no idea or image that could be explained rationally or psychologically would be accepted.
  • Content overview: There is no plot in the usual sense. Intertitles such as "Once upon a time" and "Eight years later" suggest a story but connect nothing. A man sharpens a razor, a cloud slices across the moon, and the film cuts to a razor slitting a woman's eye; a man drags pianos loaded with dead donkeys and priests; ants swarm from a palm; a severed hand lies in the street; a man chases a woman through a room. The images are linked by association and dream logic, not by cause and effect.
  • Artistic style & techniques: Surrealist "automatism" in film; the famous eye shot used a dead animal's eye (reportedly a calf's) in place of an actor's; graphic matches (the moon and the eye, the ants and the armpit hair) create meaning by visual rhyme rather than story; and the discontinuous editing denies continuity of time, place and character. A soundtrack of Wagner's Tristan und Isolde and Argentine tangos was added, chosen by Buñuel, for later screenings.
  • Cultural / historical significance: One of the founding works of surrealist cinema, and an early demonstration that film could work like a dream or a poem. Its opening shock is among the most famous images in film history. Buñuel later said he had wanted to provoke the audience and feared a riot at the premiere, and was surprised when the Paris audience applauded.
  • Influence & legacy: It made Buñuel's name; he followed it with L'Âge d'or (1930) and a long career including Los Olvidados, Viridiana and Belle de jour. Its dream-logic and shock imagery echo in the work of David Lynch, music videos and experimental animation, and the "eye cut" is a common reference in film school teaching.
  • Current status / where to experience it: Preserved and widely restored; it is regularly shown in film-history courses and museum programs, appears in home-video collections of Buñuel's work, and is often available free online because of its age and varying copyright status (availability varies by region and was not checked today). It is short enough to watch before breakfast, with a warning for the eye shot.
  • Notes: All details are from general knowledge and recalled from memory; they were not verified against a source today.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 83: Casting as Meaning (Star Persona and Meta-Casting)

What it is: Casting is not just "who fits the part". Every actor arrives with a public history: their earlier roles, their off-screen life, their reputation and their physical presence. Filmmakers can use that history as an extra layer of meaning, either by matching it (a known tough guy plays a tough guy and the audience brings its trust) or by subverting it (the nice star plays a killer, and the shock is amplified). When the casting directly comments on the actor's own real life or career, it is called meta-casting. This differs from Lesson 12 (performance analysis, which looks at how an actor plays a moment) and the earlier costume lesson: here you analyze the choice of who is in the frame and what the audience already knows about them.

How to analyze: (1) Before you watch, note what you know about each lead actor: famous roles, genre, reputation, public image. (2) Ask whether the role confirms or contradicts that image, and what each choice buys the film (instant trust, irony, shock, nostalgia). (3) Look for scenes or lines that seem to talk about the actor's real past, and for cameo appearances by real people playing themselves. (4) Notice what the film borrows from outside the frame (archive footage, a real director's name, an old film clip). (5) Ask how the film would change with a different, unknown actor in the role: that gap is the meaning added by casting. (6) Watch for what changes for a viewer who does not know the actor's history, so you can judge whether the film still works on its own.

Worked example — Sunset Boulevard (1950, dir. Billy Wilder): The fading silent-film star Norma Desmond is played by Gloria Swanson, who really was a major silent-era star who had left the screen when sound came. Her butler Max von Mayerling is played by Erich von Stroheim, who really was a celebrated silent-era director and who directed Swanson in Queen Kelly (1929), an expensive, unfinished film. In the screening-room scene, Norma shows Joe Gillis (William Holden) one of her old films: the clip is real footage from Queen Kelly, with Max running the projector, so the audience watches Swanson as she was and Von Stroheim, the man who once directed her, serving her in the same room. Her line that "we didn't need dialogue, we had faces" is spoken by a woman whose own career had been ended by the arrival of talkies. Elsewhere Cecil B. DeMille plays himself on the Paramount set, and Buster Keaton, Anna Q. Nilsson and H.B. Warner appear as Norma's bridge partners, "the waxworks". The film leans on the audience's knowledge that these are not actors pretending to be forgotten stars; they are forgotten stars. In the last scene Norma, descending the staircase, says "All right, Mr. DeMille, I'm ready for my close-up", and the line works partly because we know that Swanson is performing the end of a real era. (Details are recalled from memory and general knowledge; check the film for exact wording.)

Try it: Next time you watch a film, before the opening credits finish, write down the lead actor's best-known role. Then ask at the end whether the film used that image, bent it, or broke it, and what the change was worth.

Empezar a(或 comenzar a)+ 原形動詞表示「開始做」:Empiezo a trabajar a las nueve(我九點開始工作)。Ponerse a + 原形是「動手做、投入」,帶有突然或下定決心的語氣:Se puso a llorar(她哭了起來)。Dejar de + 原形是「停止、戒掉」:Dejé de fumar(我戒菸了)(別和單純的 dejar「離開、讓」混淆,Day 79)。Terminar de + 原形是「做完」:Terminé de leer = 我讀完了,而 Dejé de leer = 我不讀了。拼字提醒:empezar 的 yo 簡單過去式是 empecé(z 改成 c)。四者都要先接介系詞(a / de)再接原形,不能接 -ing(Day 86)。

  • ¿Cuándo empezaste a estudiar español? — 你什麼時候開始學西班牙文的?
  • Empecé a estudiarlo hace tres meses. — 我三個月前開始學的。
  • ¿Ya dejaste de ver la tele en inglés? — 你已經不再用英文看電視了嗎?
  • Todavía no, pero ya terminé de leer mi primer libro. — 還沒,不過我已經讀完第一本書了。
  • Ahora me pongo a practicar con un amigo. — 現在我就來跟朋友練習。

4) 莊子 — 第 33 篇:天下(Tiānxià)— 〈天下〉(最後一篇)

傳統 33 篇本的最後一篇(雜篇第 33 篇),也是這個每日系列的最後一篇。與全書其他篇章不同,它不是寓言集,而是一篇概覽戰國時期各學派的論述體文章,許多學者視之為全書的後序,甚至認為出自後來編者之手(此點有爭議)。開頭感嘆「天下之治方術者多矣」,各家都自以為最好,然後追問「古之所謂道術者,果惡乎在?」。文中談到古代道的整體,描述「內聖外王」的理想,並說如今道術被撕裂,各家只握住一個角落,就像耳、目、鼻、口各有所明,卻不能互相取代(原文意象是各種感官各有用處,但彼此不能相通)。

接著依序評述墨家(墨翟、禽滑釐,稱讚其勤儉刻苦,但批評讓一般人生活太苦)、宋鈃與尹文、彭蒙、田駢與慎到(「棄知去己」、隨物而轉)、關尹與老聃(對老子評價極高:「以濡弱謙下為表,以空虛不毀萬物為實」),最後談到莊周自己。莊子的自畫像:他認為天下沉濁,不能用莊重的話說,於是用「卮言」「重言」「寓言」(參見第 27 篇),「獨與天地精神往來,而不敖倪於萬物」,他的書雖然「瑰瑋」而婉轉曲折,「無傷也」(其書雖瑰瑋而連犿無傷也)。

全篇以惠施作結,惠施是莊子的朋友與論辯對手,名家的「歷物十事」(最大的是「至大無外,謂之大一;至小無內,謂之小一」;「一尺之捶,日取其半,萬世不竭」;「飛鳥之影,未嘗動也」),並附上其他辯者的悖論。作者欣賞惠施的才華(「其書五車」),卻評他追逐外物而不得道,把他比作蚊虻徒勞。

重點:每個學派都握著真理的一角,忘了其餘部分時就危險;而這部從飛越天地開始的書,最後以一張冷靜的地圖,標出填滿世界的各種爭論。(篇章細節與引文來自一般知識,為意譯,今日未對照原文查證,引用前請核對來源。)系列說明:隨著這一篇,33 篇已全部介紹完畢,莊子系列結束;第 4 項接下來要改成什麼,請見 ROUTINE_LOG.md 的 Run Notes。

5) 國家 / 地區 / 勢力介紹 — 斯洛維尼亞(Slovenia)

  • 位置與面積:中歐小國(常被形容為阿爾卑斯山、潘諾尼亞平原、狄那里克山脈與亞得里亞海的交會處),西鄰義大利,北接奧地利,東北是匈牙利,南方與東方是克羅埃西亞,擁有約 47 公里的短短亞得里亞海岸線。面積約 20,300 平方公里(一般知識):約為台灣(36,200 平方公里)的 56%,與威爾斯(約 20,800 平方公里)相近,略小於美國紐澤西州(約 22,600 平方公里),約為奧地利的四分之一。最高峰是特里格拉夫山(2,864 公尺),出現在國旗與國徽上。

  • 現任領導人:總統娜塔莎·皮爾茨·穆薩爾(Nataša Pirc Musar)(以禮儀性質為主的國家元首,2022 年 12 月就任)。政府由總理楊內茲·楊薩(Janez Janša)領導,在 3 月大選未產生多數後,於 2026 年 5 月 22 日由國會選出;第 16 屆政府於 2026 年 6 月 4 日任命,為中右翼聯合政府(SDS、NSi、SLS、Focus、Democrats),在 90 席中掌握 43 席,並獲 Resni.ca 的 5 席支持。這是楊薩的第四個任期。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「OK Boomer」

  • 名稱:「OK Boomer」(也寫作 "Okay boomer")。
  • 起源 / 歷史:一句針對年長者,尤其是嬰兒潮世代(約 1946-1964 年出生)的不屑回嘴,用於他們對年輕人說教或無視其擔憂時。「boomer」作為貶義詞的零星用法早在數年前就出現在 Reddit 與 4chan 等論壇,這句話的確切首次使用時間有爭議。它在 2019 年底爆紅,契機之一是一段 TikTok 時代的爆紅影片:一位年長男子抱怨年輕世代,留言者以「OK boomer」回應。
  • 如何流行:2019 年秋天數週之內,它席捲 TikTok、Twitter 與 Reddit,接著進入主流媒體(《紐約時報》在 2019 年 10 月底或 11 月初刊出解說)。2019 年 11 月 4 日,紐西蘭國會議員克洛伊·斯瓦布里克(Chlöe Swarbrick,當時 25 歲)在國會就《零碳法案》發言時,以「OK boomer」回應一位插話者,使它成為全球頭條。美國電台主持人鮑勃·朗斯伯利(Bob Lonsberry)在一則被廣泛嘲諷的推文中稱它是「年齡歧視的 N 字」,助長了反彈與擴散的循環。
  • 意義與使用情境:大意是「我不想再理會那種居高臨下、不合時宜的看法」。用於駁斥而非論證,通常圍繞氣候、房價、債務與職場文化。朋友之間可以是玩笑,政治爭論中可以很尖銳;批評者認為這是年齡歧視,而「boomer」也可以指一種態度而非年齡。
  • 變體 / 演變:「OK Zoomer」與「OK doomer」成為反擊,「boomer」衍生出「Boomer Remover」(COVID-19 期間的苦澀玩笑)與類似「Karen」的類型標籤,這句話被印在 T 恤與馬克杯上,並有商標申請的報導。許多語言也有各自的版本。
  • 目前狀態:已過高峰期(2019 年底至 2020 年初),現在多半用於反諷或作為 2019 年的懷舊標記,不過「boomer」作為過時觀念的標籤仍常使用。(細節來自一般知識,為概述,今日未對照來源查證。)

7) 電影 / 表演藝術介紹 — 《安達魯之犬》(Un Chien Andalou,1929,導演路易斯·布紐爾,與薩爾瓦多·達利共同編劇)

  • 名稱與關鍵人物:《安達魯之犬》(Un chien andalou,意為「一隻安達魯西亞的狗」),約 16 分鐘的默片短片,由西班牙導演路易斯·布紐爾與畫家薩爾瓦多·達利共同創作,主演為皮耶·巴切夫(Pierre Batcheff)與西蒙·馬雷伊(Simone Mareuil),布紐爾本人飾演拿剃刀的男人。
  • 年代 / 背景:1929 年在法國拍攝,處於巴黎超現實主義者的圈子,資金主要來自布紐爾母親的錢。布紐爾與達利以交換夢境與意象的方式,在大約一週內寫成劇本(布紐爾夢見一片雲切開月亮、達利夢見一隻爬滿螞蟻的手),並訂下規則:凡能用理性或心理學解釋的想法或意象一律不採用。
  • 內容概要:沒有一般意義上的情節。「很久很久以前」「八年後」之類的字幕暗示故事,卻什麼都沒有連接起來。一個男人磨剃刀,一片雲橫切過月亮,接著鏡頭切到剃刀劃開一個女人的眼睛;一個男人拖著載滿死驢與神父的鋼琴;螞蟻從手掌湧出;一隻斷手躺在街上;一個男人在房間裡追逐女人。畫面靠聯想與夢的邏輯相連,而不是因果。
  • 藝術風格與技法:電影中的超現實主義「自動書寫」;著名的眼睛鏡頭用的是死去動物的眼睛(據說是小牛的),而非演員的眼睛;圖形匹配(月亮與眼睛、螞蟻與腋毛)靠視覺的押韻而非故事來產生意義;不連貫的剪輯否定了時間、地點與角色的連續性。後來的放映版本加上了布紐爾選的華格納《崔斯坦與伊索德》與阿根廷探戈配樂。
  • 文化 / 歷史意義:超現實主義電影的奠基作之一,也是電影可以像夢或詩一樣運作的早期示範。開場的震撼是電影史上最著名的畫面之一。布紐爾後來說,他本來想挑釁觀眾,擔心首映會引發騷動,結果巴黎觀眾卻鼓掌,讓他感到意外。
  • 影響與遺產:它讓布紐爾成名;他接著拍了《黃金時代》(L'Âge d'or,1930),並展開漫長的生涯,包括《被遺忘的人們》(Los Olvidados)、《薇麗蒂安娜》(Viridiana)與《青樓怨婦》(Belle de jour)。它的夢境邏輯與震撼意象在大衛·林區的作品、音樂錄影帶與實驗動畫中都有迴響,「切眼」也是電影學校教學中常見的引用。
  • 目前狀態 / 如何欣賞:已被保存並廣泛修復;常在電影史課程與博物館節目中放映,收錄於布紐爾作品的家庭影音合集,並因年代久遠與版權狀況不一,常可在網路上免費觀看(各地情況不同,今日未查證)。短到可以在早餐前看完,但眼睛的鏡頭請先有心理準備。

8) 看片方法 — 第 83 課:選角即意義(明星形象與後設選角)

它是什麼:選角不只是「誰適合這個角色」。每位演員帶著一段公眾歷史:先前的角色、銀幕外的生活、名聲與身形氣質。電影人可以把這段歷史當作額外的意義層次,要嘛吻合它(知名硬漢演硬漢,觀眾帶著信任進場),要嘛顛覆它(好人形象的明星演殺手,衝擊因此放大)。當選角直接評論演員自己真實的人生或事業時,稱為後設選角(meta-casting)。這與第 12 課(表演分析,看演員如何演一個時刻)和先前的服裝課不同:這裡分析的是「誰在畫面裡」這個選擇,以及觀眾已經知道的關於他們的事。

如何分析:(1) 看片前,寫下你對每位主演的認識:知名角色、類型、名聲、公眾形象。(2) 問這個角色是在證實還是違背那個形象,各自為電影買到什麼(立即的信任、諷刺、震撼、懷舊)。(3) 找出似乎在談論演員真實過去的場景或台詞,以及真人本人客串的演出。(4) 注意電影從畫面外借用了什麼(檔案影像、真實導演的名字、舊電影片段)。(5) 想像換成一位不知名的演員演這個角色,電影會怎樣改變:那個差距就是選角增加的意義。(6) 留意不認識該演員歷史的觀眾,會看到什麼不同,藉此判斷電影本身是否仍然成立。

實例分析 —《日落大道》(Sunset Boulevard,1950,導演比利·懷德):過氣的默片明星諾瑪·戴斯蒙由葛洛莉亞·史璜森(Gloria Swanson)飾演,她本人真的是默片時代的大明星,有聲電影來臨後離開了銀幕。管家麥克斯·馮·梅爾林由艾瑞克·馮·史卓海姆(Erich von Stroheim)飾演,他本人真的是備受推崇的默片導演,並曾執導史璜森主演的《凱利女王》(Queen Kelly,1929),一部昂貴而未完成的電影。在放映室那場戲中,諾瑪給喬·吉利斯(威廉·霍頓飾)看她的舊電影:那段片段是《凱利女王》的真實影像,麥克斯操作放映機,於是觀眾同時看到年輕時的史璜森,以及曾經導演過她的人在同一個房間裡服侍她。她那句「我們不需要對白,我們有臉」(「We didn't need dialogue, we had faces」)出自一位事業被有聲電影終結的女人之口。此外,賽席爾·B·戴米爾(Cecil B. DeMille)在派拉蒙片廠親自演出自己,巴斯特·基頓(Buster Keaton)、安娜·Q·尼爾森與 H.B. 華納則以諾瑪的橋牌搭子「蠟像」現身。電影倚賴觀眾知道:他們不是在假扮被遺忘的明星,他們就是被遺忘的明星。最後一場戲,諾瑪走下樓梯說「好了,戴米爾先生,我準備好特寫了」(「All right, Mr. DeMille, I'm ready for my close-up」),這句台詞之所以有力,部分原因是我們知道史璜森正在演出一個真實時代的結束。(細節來自記憶與一般知識;確切台詞請以電影為準。)

試試看:下次看電影時,在片頭字幕結束前,先寫下主演最知名的角色。結束時再問:電影是沿用、彎折還是打破了那個形象,這個改變值多少?
