3) Spanish Lesson — Day 96: Echar de menos / Extrañar / Hacer falta ("Missing Someone, Needing")

Echar de menos (Spain) and extrañar (Latin America) both mean "to miss": Te echo de menos / Te extraño, with "I" as the subject and the missed person as the direct object (add personal a before a person). Hacer falta works like gustar: the thing needed is the subject (Me hace falta tiempo, Me haces falta = "I need you around"). In Spain, extrañar can also mean "to strike as odd": Me extraña que no llame (subjunctive).

  • ¿Echas de menos a tu familia? — Do you miss your family?
  • Sí, la extraño mucho. — Yes, I miss them a lot.
  • A mí me hace falta mi casa. — I really miss (need) my home.
  • Yo también te echo de menos. — I miss you too.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 32: 列禦寇 (Liè Yùkòu) — "Lie Yukou"

The tenth of the Miscellaneous Chapters (雜篇, Ch. 32), named for the Daoist sage Liezi (Lie Yukou). It is a collection of short anecdotes, many about reputation, wealth and how to see a person truly. It opens with Liezi turning back halfway to Qi after eating at a noodle stall where the owner served him ahead of everyone: the sage sees that his inner glow has leaked out and attracts people; his teacher Bohun Wuren later finds a crowd of shoes at his door, leans on his staff, and leaves without a word, a warning against letting one's inner state show.

The chapter's famous lines: "The clever toil and the wise worry; the incapable seek nothing, they eat their fill and wander, drifting like an unmoored boat" (巧者勞而知者憂,無能者無所求,飽食而敖遊,泛若不繫之舟). In another story, Cao Shang returns from the king of Qin with a hundred carriages and mocks Zhuangzi's poverty; Zhuangzi replies that the king's physician gave one carriage for lancing a boil and five for licking a hemorrhoid, "the lower the work, the more carriages", and tells Cao Shang to go away. Zhuangzi also turns down a ruler's offer of office by comparing it to a sacrificial ox dressed in embroidery and led to the temple, who would give anything then to be an orphan calf. A third tale: Zhu Pingman spends three years and his family's fortune learning to slay dragons and finds that there are no dragons to slay, a joke on skills with no use. Another, the boy who dives and brings up a thousand-gold pearl from beneath the black dragon's chin, is told that it was only possible because the dragon was asleep.

The chapter ends with Zhuangzi on his deathbed. When his disciples want a lavish funeral, he answers that heaven and earth are his coffin, the sun and moon his jade discs, the stars his pearls and all things his mourners; if left in the open he would feed the crows and kites, buried, he would feed the ants, so why rob one to feed the other? It is the book's final comment on life and death, followed by the closing chapter 33.

Takeaway: Be wary of cleverness that attracts attention, of gain that depends on luck, and of skills for a world that does not exist; wander light, like a boat that is not tied to anything. 5) Country / Region Spotlight — Estonia

  • Location & territory size: The northernmost Baltic state, on the eastern shore of the Baltic Sea, bordering Latvia to the south and Russia to the east, with the Gulf of Finland to the north (Helsinki is about 80 km across the water from Tallinn).   • Religion: One of the least religious countries in the world: roughly 70% report no religious affiliation, with Orthodox Christians (about 16%) and Lutherans (about 8%) the largest groups (approximate, 2021 census).

Under the 1939 Molotov-Ribbentrop pact it was occupied by the Soviet Union in 1940, by Nazi Germany from 1941, and again by the USSR from 1944, with mass deportations in 1941 and March 1949. The "Singing Revolution" (1987-1991) included a 1988 song-festival gathering of about 300,000 people, and the 1989 Baltic Way human chain linked Tallinn, Riga and Vilnius. Independence was restored on August 20, 1991. It joined the EU and NATO in 2004 and the Schengen area in 2007.

  • Language: Estonian, a Finnic language of the Uralic family, closely related to Finnish and unrelated to Latvian or Lithuanian; it has 14 grammatical cases and no grammatical gender. Russian is widely spoken, and English is common among the young.

7) Film / Performing-Arts Spotlight — The Magic Flute (Die Zauberflöte, 1791, Mozart / Schikaneder)

  • Name & key figures: The Magic Flute (K. 620), an opera in two acts with spoken dialogue (a German Singspiel), music by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart and libretto by Emanuel Schikaneder, who also ran the theater and played the first Papageno.
  • Era / background: Premiered on September 30, 1791 at Schikaneder's popular suburban Theater auf der Wieden in Vienna, only about two months before Mozart's death on December 5, 1791. It was written for a mixed audience, not a court, and in German rather than Italian, a sign of the growing German-language theater.
  • Plot overview: Prince Tamino is given a magic flute and sent by the Queen of the Night to rescue her daughter Pamina from the sorcerer Sarastro, accompanied by the bird-catcher Papageno, who gets magic bells. The story turns when Tamino learns that Sarastro is not a villain but the leader of a brotherhood of light, and that the Queen is the real threat. Tamino and Pamina pass trials of silence, fire and water together, while Papageno finds his Papagena.
  • Artistic style & techniques: A mix of comic folk-style songs (Papageno's "Der Vogelfänger bin ich ja"), solemn ritual music (Sarastro's "O Isis und Osiris"), and showpiece coloratura: the Queen of the Night's "Der Hölle Rache" reaches a high F. The overture and trial scenes use counterpoint and trombone chords for a sacred effect; the use of the number three recurs in chords, characters and trials.
  • Cultural / historical significance: Both Mozart and Schikaneder were Freemasons, and the work is widely read as filled with Masonic and Enlightenment symbolism: light over darkness, reason, brotherhood. It is also criticized today for its portrayal of women and of the character Monostatos, and productions often address this.
  • Influence & legacy: One of the most performed operas in the world, it helped establish German-language opera, was praised by Goethe, and inspired later composers. Ingmar Bergman's 1975 Swedish-language film version is a famous adaptation, and the Queen of the Night aria has become a pop-culture shorthand for vocal virtuosity.
  • Current status / where to experience it: It is a staple of opera houses worldwide, often performed in family-friendly and abridged versions, and its recordings and Bergman's film are widely available (availability varies by region and was not checked today). Many houses live-stream or broadcast it to cinemas.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 82: Repetition with Variation (The Rule of Three)

What it is: A filmmaker shows the same situation, image, line or gesture more than once, changing a few details each time. The viewer does not need to be told what changed: the first instance sets a pattern, the second confirms it, and the third (or a break in the pattern) delivers the point. It is one of the cheapest and most powerful ways to show time passing, a relationship changing, or a joke building. It differs from foreshadowing (Lesson 20: a detail planted for a later payoff), the leitmotif (Lesson 30: a recurring musical theme) and the bookend (Lesson 66: just the opening and closing image); here the structure itself is repeated several times, and the meaning lives in the differences.

How to analyze: (1) Find the repeated element (a place, a composition, an action, a line, a prop). (2) List what stays fixed (the frame, the table, the sound of a morning) and what varies (clothes, distance, tone, who speaks first). (3) Ask what the variation implies: time, growth, decay, escalation, or a gag. (4) Count the repetitions and ask what the last one does: confirm, pay off, or break the pattern. (5) Check the cut between instances: is it a quick whip pan, a dissolve, or a hard cut that hides time? (6) Ask why the director chose repetition over a line of dialogue saying the same thing.

Worked example — Citizen Kane (1941, dir. Orson Welles), the breakfast-table sequence: In about two minutes the film shows the decay of Charles Foster Kane's first marriage to Emily through a series of breakfast scenes at the same table. The fixed elements are the table, the two of them facing each other, the breakfast and a rhythm of lines; the variable elements are everything else. Early on they sit close and share affectionate, interrupted talk; later the camera pulls back, the table seems to grow, their clothes and mood change, the talk becomes short and sour, and at the end they sit at opposite ends of the long table in silence, each reading a rival newspaper. The transitions are fast whip pans that carry us across years, and Bernard Herrmann's music (a waltz that returns in a different tempo and mood each time) varies along with the picture. Nothing in the scene says "the marriage failed"; the repeated structure and the widening distance say it. (Details are from general knowledge and recalled from memory; check against the film.)

Try it: Next time a film shows the same location, gesture or line twice or more, write down one thing that stays the same and one that changes, and decide what the change means.

4) 莊子 — 第 32 篇:列禦寇(Liè Yùkòu)

《雜篇》第十篇(雜篇第 32 篇),以道家人物列子(列禦寇)為篇名,是由許多短故事組成的集子,多與名聲、財富、如何真正看清一個人有關。開頭是列子前往齊國、走到半路又折返:他在麵攤吃飯,老闆竟搶先招呼他,顯示他的內在光芒外洩、引人注目;他的老師伯昏瞀人後來看到他門外擠滿鞋子,拄著手杖站了一會兒,一句話也沒說就離開,提醒人不要讓內在的狀態外露。

篇中的名句:「巧者勞而知者憂,無能者無所求,飽食而敖遊,泛若不繫之舟」。另一則故事裡,曹商從秦王那裡帶著一百輛車回來,嘲笑莊子貧窮;莊子回答說,秦王的醫生替人切開膿瘡可得一輛車,舔痔瘡的得五輛車,「所治愈下,得車愈多」,要曹商快走開。莊子也曾拒絕君王請他做官,把當官比作披著繡衣、被牽進太廟的祭牛,到那時想做一頭孤獨的小牛也不可得了。第三則:朱泙漫花了三年、耗盡家產學習屠龍之術,結果世上沒有龍可屠,諷刺沒有用武之地的技能。另一則:一個孩子潛入深淵,從黑龍的下巴下取得價值千金的珍珠,有人告訴他,這是因為龍正在睡覺才得以成功。

全篇以莊子臨終的故事作結。弟子想厚葬他,莊子回答說,天地是他的棺槨,日月是他的玉璧,星辰是他的珠寶,萬物都是送葬者;若棄在野外會被烏鴉老鷹吃,埋在土裡會被螻蟻吃,為什麼要奪走這個去給那個,偏心至此?這是全書對生死的最後評論,之後便是最後一篇第 33 篇。

  • 位置與面積:波羅的海三國中最北的一國,位於波羅的海東岸,南接拉脫維亞、東接俄羅斯,北邊隔著芬蘭灣(與赫爾辛基相距約 80 公里水域)。面積約 45,300 平方公里(一般知識):約為台灣(36,200 平方公里)的 1.25 倍,比丹麥(約 43,000 平方公里)與瑞士(41,300 平方公里)略大,擁有 2,000 多個島嶼(最大的是薩雷馬島與希烏馬島)。
  • 人口:約 137 萬(近似值),約為台灣 2,300 萬人的 6%,是歐盟人口最少的國家之一。首都塔林約 45 萬人,第二大城塔爾圖是大學城。約 69% 為愛沙尼亞族,約四分之一為說俄語者(近似值)。
  • 宗教:是世界上宗教信仰最淡薄的國家之一:約 70% 表示沒有宗教信仰,最大的宗教群體是東正教徒(約 16%)與路德宗(約 8%)(近似值,2021 年人口普查)。
  • 經濟:2011 年加入歐元區、2010 年加入經合組織;屬高所得經濟體,人均 GDP 約 3 至 3.2 萬美元(近似值)。以數位國家聞名:幾乎所有公共服務都在線上、X-Road 資料交換平台、2005 年起的網路投票,以及 2014 年推出的電子居民計畫。Skype 就是在那裡開發的,Bolt 等新創讓它成為歐洲新創密度最高的國家之一。企業所得稅只對分配出去的盈餘課徵。國防支出占 GDP 比例在北約名列前茅。
  • 歷史:古代芬蘭語系民族;13 世紀被丹麥人與德意志十字軍征服(塔林成為漢薩同盟城市雷瓦爾);1561 至 1710 年屬瑞典,之後屬俄羅斯帝國直到 1918 年。1918 年 2 月 24 日宣布獨立,贏得獨立戰爭,並於 1920 年簽訂《塔爾圖條約》。1939 年《莫洛托夫-里賓特洛甫條約》後,1940 年被蘇聯占領,1941 年起被納粹德國占領,1944 年起再度由蘇聯統治,期間在 1941 年與 1949 年 3 月發生大規模流放。「歌唱革命」(1987-1991)包括 1988 年約 30 萬人參加的歌唱節聚會,1989 年「波羅的海之路」人鏈連結塔林、里加與維爾紐斯。1991 年 8 月 20 日恢復獨立;2004 年加入歐盟與北約,2007 年加入申根區。
  • 現任領導人:總統烏勒·馬迪塞(Ülle Madise),2026 年 9 月 2 日由國會首輪投票(71 票)選出,2026 年 10 月 12 日宣誓就職,接替阿拉爾·卡里斯。總理為克里斯滕·米哈爾(2024 年 7 月就任;今天未重新查證,請對照最新來源確認)。
  • 語言:愛沙尼亞語,屬烏拉爾語系的芬蘭語支,與芬蘭語關係密切,與拉脫維亞語和立陶宛語無關;有 14 個格,沒有語法性別。俄語使用普遍,年輕人多會英語。

7) 電影 / 表演藝術介紹 — 《魔笛》(Die Zauberflöte,1791,莫札特 / 席卡內德)

  • 名稱與主要人物:《魔笛》(K. 620),兩幕歌劇,帶有對白(德語歌唱劇 Singspiel),音樂由沃夫岡·阿瑪迪斯·莫札特創作,劇本由埃馬努埃爾·席卡內德(Emanuel Schikaneder)撰寫,他同時經營劇院,並飾演首位帕帕基諾。
  • 時代與背景:1791 年 9 月 30 日在席卡內德位於維也納郊區、受平民歡迎的維登劇院(Theater auf der Wieden)首演,距莫札特 1791 年 12 月 5 日去世僅約兩個月。它為一般觀眾而非宮廷而寫,使用德語而非義大利語,象徵德語劇場的興起。
  • 劇情概要:夜之女王給了王子塔米諾一支魔笛,派他去從魔法師薩拉斯特羅手中救出她的女兒帕米娜,同行的有捕鳥人帕帕基諾,他得到一組魔法鈴鐺。劇情的轉折是:塔米諾發現薩拉斯特羅其實不是惡人,而是光明兄弟會的領袖,真正的威脅是夜之女王。塔米諾與帕米娜一起通過沉默、火與水的試煉,帕帕基諾則找到了他的帕帕基娜。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:融合了詼諧的民謠風歌曲(帕帕基諾的〈Der Vogelfänger bin ich ja〉)、莊嚴的儀式音樂(薩拉斯特羅的〈O Isis und Osiris〉)與炫技的花腔:夜之女王的〈Der Hölle Rache〉唱到高音 F。序曲與試煉場景使用對位與長號和弦營造神聖感;「三」這個數字在和弦、角色與試煉中反覆出現。
  • 文化與歷史意義:莫札特與席卡內德都是共濟會會員,這部作品被廣泛解讀為充滿共濟會與啟蒙運動的象徵:光明戰勝黑暗、理性、兄弟情誼。今天它也因對女性與摩諾斯塔托斯這個角色的刻畫而受到批評,製作團隊常會處理這一點。
  • 影響與遺產:是世界上上演次數最多的歌劇之一,幫助確立了德語歌劇,受到歌德的讚賞,並啟發了後來的作曲家。英格瑪·柏格曼 1975 年的瑞典語電影版是著名的改編,夜之女王的詠嘆調也成為流行文化中形容聲樂高超技巧的代名詞。
  • 現況 / 哪裡可以欣賞:是世界各地歌劇院的常備劇目,常有親子版或精簡版演出,錄音與柏格曼的電影也很容易取得(可用性因地區而異,今天未查證)。許多歌劇院會直播或在電影院轉播。

8) 看片方法 — 第 82 課:重複與變奏(三次法則)

這是什麼:導演把同一個情境、畫面、台詞或動作呈現不只一次,每次改變一些細節。觀眾不需要有人說明改變了什麼:第一次建立模式,第二次確認模式,第三次(或打破模式)才交出重點。這是表現時間流逝、關係變化或笑話累積最便宜也最有力的方式之一。它不同於伏筆(第 20 課:為日後回收而埋下的細節)、主導動機(第 30 課:反覆出現的音樂主題)與首尾呼應(第 66 課:只有開頭與結尾的影像);這裡是結構本身重複數次,意義存在於差異之中。

如何分析:(1) 找出重複的元素(地點、構圖、動作、台詞、道具)。(2) 列出什麼固定不變(畫面、桌子、早晨的聲音),什麼在變(衣著、距離、語氣、誰先開口)。(3) 問這些變化暗示什麼:時間、成長、衰敗、升高,或是一個笑點。(4) 數一數重複幾次,問最後一次做了什麼:確認、回收,還是打破模式。(5) 觀察每次之間的轉場:是快速的甩鏡、溶接,還是隱藏時間的硬切。(6) 問導演為什麼選擇重複,而不是用一句台詞說出同樣的意思。

實例 —《大國民》(Citizen Kane,1941,導演奧森·威爾斯),早餐桌段落:影片在約兩分鐘內,用一連串在同一張桌子上的早餐場景,呈現查爾斯·福斯特·凱恩與第一任妻子艾蜜莉婚姻的衰敗。固定的元素是桌子、兩人面對面、早餐與台詞的節奏;變化的則是其他一切。一開始兩人坐得很近,親密而被打斷的談話;後來鏡頭拉遠,桌子彷彿變長,衣著與心情改變,對話變得簡短而尖酸,到最後兩人隔著長桌兩端沉默地坐著,各自讀著對立的報紙。轉場是快速的甩鏡,帶我們跨越多年,伯納·赫曼的音樂(一首每次以不同速度與情緒回來的華爾滋)也隨畫面一起變奏。場景裡沒有任何一句說「婚姻失敗了」;重複的結構與越拉越大的距離就說出來了。(細節出自一般知識,憑記憶回想;請對照影片查證。)

練習:下次電影把同一個地點、動作或台詞出現兩次以上時,寫下一件不變的事和一件改變的事,並判斷這個變化代表什麼。
