3) Spanish Lesson — Day 12 (10 min, builds on Day 11)

Day 11 introduced regular -ar verbs (hablar, trabajar, estudiar). Today's new concept: the present tense of regular -er and -ir verbs — the other two verb families, covering verbs like comer (to eat), beber (to drink), vivir (to live), and escribir (to write). For "yo/tú/él" (the forms covered so far), -er and -ir verbs actually share the exact same endings.

  • ¿Qué comes? (keh KOH-mehs) — What are you eating?
  • Como arroz con pollo, y bebo agua. (KOH-moh ah-RROHS kohn POH-yoh, ee BEH-boh AH-gwah) — I'm eating rice with chicken, and I'm drinking water.
  • Vivo en Taipéi y escribo cartas a mi familia. (VEE-boh ehn tai-PEI ee ehs-KREE-boh KAHR-tahs ah mee fah-MEE-lyah) — I live in Taipei and I write letters to my family.

Grammar note: drop -er/-ir to get the stem (com-, beb-, viv-, escrib-), then add -o (yo), -es (tú), or -e (él/ella) — identical endings for both families at this stage; they only start to diverge once we reach the plural forms (nosotros/vosotros), which come later.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 12: 否 (Pǐ) — "Standstill" (Stagnation) (Heaven over Earth). This hexagram is the direct reversal of yesterday's Hexagram 11 (泰, Peace): heaven sits above and continues rising away, while earth sits below and continues sinking away, so the two never meet and all exchange between them ceases. This is the image of a time of stagnation, discord, and disconnection between high and low — when the small-minded and unworthy advance while the great and superior withdraw. The counsel: in such a closed-off time, the superior person does not compromise their integrity to court favor with those in power, nor seek reward or recognition; instead they withdraw into quiet self-cultivation, conserve their strength, and wait — for like all cycles, standstill is temporary and will in time give way to renewed openness and progress.

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Brunei

  • Location: A small sultanate on the northern coast of the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia, almost entirely enclosed by the Malaysian state of Sarawak and facing the South China Sea.
  • Territory: About 5,765 km² — roughly the size of the island of Bali, and about one-sixth the land area of Taiwan (~36,200 km²).
  • Population: Approximately 450,000 people, one of the smallest national populations in Southeast Asia.
  • Religion: Islam (Shafi'i Sunni) is the official state religion and the Sultan also serves as the head of the Islamic faith in Brunei; Buddhist and Christian minorities exist, mostly among the ethnic Chinese and indigenous communities.
  • Economy: Overwhelmingly built on oil and natural gas exports (via a long-standing joint venture with Shell), giving Brunei one of the highest GDP-per-capita levels in Asia and funding an extensive welfare state with no personal income tax; the government has been pushing diversification into Islamic finance, halal industries, and tourism to reduce hydrocarbon dependence.
  • History: Once the seat of a powerful maritime sultanate that controlled much of Borneo and parts of the Philippines in the 15th-16th centuries, Brunei's territory shrank drastically under European colonial pressure before becoming a British protectorate in 1888. Oil discovered in 1929 transformed its fortunes, and Brunei achieved full independence from Britain in 1984.
  • Current leader: Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah, on the throne since 1967 — one of the longest-reigning monarchs in the world — rules as an absolute monarch, also holding the posts of Prime Minister, Finance Minister, and Defence Minister.
  • Language: Malay is the official language; English is widely used in business and education, alongside Chinese and indigenous Dayak languages.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "This Is Fine"

  • Name: "This Is Fine," also known as the "This is fine dog."
  • Origin/history: Originates from a 2013 webcomic strip titled "On Fire" by cartoonist KC Green, part of his webcomic series Gunshow. It depicts an anthropomorphic dog sitting calmly at a table in a room fully engulfed in flames, saying "This is fine."
  • How it became popular: A cropped panel of the comic — the dog with a cup, flames all around — began circulating on Twitter, Reddit, and Tumblr as a reaction image in the mid-2010s, and exploded in popularity during the turbulent 2016 US election news cycle, where it became a go-to shorthand for "everything is obviously falling apart, and yet."
  • Meaning/usage: Used ironically to caption or react to a situation that is visibly disastrous while the poster (or someone depicted) pretends everything is under control — common in commentary on politics, finance, tech outages, or a chaotic workday, e.g. captioning a crashing stock chart or a broken build with "this is fine."
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned an animated GIF version (the dog and room slowly melting), countless character-swap edits (replacing the dog with other franchises' mascots), sequel panels by KC Green revisiting the dog's fate, and adoption into sticker packs and mainstream news commentary as shorthand for collective denial.
  • Current status: More than a decade after its creation, "This Is Fine" remains in active, everyday use — one of the rare 2010s reaction memes that has settled into the internet's permanent vocabulary rather than fading out.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第十二天(10 分鐘,銜接第十一天內容)

第十一天學了規則 -ar 動詞(hablar、trabajar、estudiar)。今天的新內容是規則 -er 與 -ir 動詞現在式——另外兩組動詞家族,涵蓋 comer(吃)、beber(喝)、vivir(住)、escribir(寫)等動詞。在目前學到的「我/你/他」這幾個人稱上,-er 與 -ir 動詞其實共用完全相同的字尾變化。

  • ¿Qué comes?(克誒 摳梅斯)—— 你在吃什麼?
  • Como arroz con pollo, y bebo agua.(摳摸 阿羅斯 空 波悠,伊 貝波 阿瓜)—— 我在吃雞肉飯,而且我在喝水。
  • Vivo en Taipéi y escribo cartas a mi familia.(比波 恩 台北 伊 誒斯克里波 卡爾塔斯 阿 米 法米利亞)—— 我住在台北,而且我寫信給我的家人。

文法重點:去掉字尾 -er/-ir 拿到詞幹(com-、beb-、viv-、escrib-),再加上 -o(我)、-es(你)或 -e(他/她)——這個階段兩組動詞的字尾完全相同,要到之後學到複數人稱(我們/你們)時才會出現差異。

4) 易經卦象

第十二卦:否卦(Pǐ)——「否閉」(上乾下坤)。此卦正好是昨天第十一卦(泰,太平)的反轉:天在上持續上升遠離,地在下持續下沉遠離,兩者無法相遇,彼此之間的交流因而完全斷絕。這正是上下閉塞不通、小人得勢而君子退隱的意象——渺小、卑劣者向前得勢,而剛健、尊貴者則選擇隱退。此卦的啟示是:身處這樣閉塞不通的時代,君子不會為了討好當權者而犧牲自己的原則,也不追求獎賞或名聲;而是選擇隱退、韜光養晦、蓄積實力,靜待時機——因為如同所有的循環一樣,否閉終究只是暫時的,終將再度轉為通暢與進步。

5) 國家/地區介紹 — 汶萊(Brunei)

  • 位置: 位於東南亞婆羅洲(Borneo)島北岸的小型蘇丹國,幾乎完全被馬來西亞砂拉越州(Sarawak)包圍,面向南海。
  • 領土面積: 約 5,765 平方公里,大約與峇里島(Bali)相當,約為台灣(約 36,200 平方公里)面積的六分之一。
  • 人口: 約 45 萬人,是東南亞人口最少的國家之一。
  • 宗教: 伊斯蘭教(沙斐儀學派遜尼派)為國教,蘇丹同時也是汶萊伊斯蘭信仰的最高領袖;境內華人與原住民族群中也存在佛教與基督教少數族群。
  • 經濟: 高度依賴石油與天然氣出口(透過與殼牌石油長期合資經營),使汶萊擁有亞洲數一數二高的人均 GDP,並支撐起不課徵個人所得稅的完善福利制度;政府正推動伊斯蘭金融、清真產業與觀光業等多元化政策,以降低對石化能源的依賴。
  • 歷史: 汶萊曾是 15 至 16 世紀稱霸一方的海上蘇丹國,勢力範圍涵蓋大半個婆羅洲與部分菲律賓地區;在歐洲殖民勢力壓迫下領土大幅縮減,並於 1888 年成為英國保護國。1929 年發現石油徹底改變其國運,汶萊於 1984 年正式脫離英國、完全獨立。
  • 現任領導人: 蘇丹哈山納爾·博爾基亞(Hassanal Bolkiah)自 1967 年即位至今——是全球在位時間最長的君主之一——以絕對君主身分統治,同時兼任首相、財政部長與國防部長。
  • 語言: 馬來語為官方語言;英語廣泛使用於商業與教育場合,此外亦有華語與當地達雅族(Dayak)語言。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、en.wikipedia.org

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 「This Is Fine」(這樣很好)

  • 名稱: 「This Is Fine」,亦稱「這樣很好小狗」(This is fine dog)。
  • 起源/歷史: 出自漫畫家 KC Green 於 2013 年發表的網路漫畫《On Fire》,為其漫畫系列 Gunshow 的其中一篇。畫面中一隻擬人化小狗,坐在一間烈火熊熊燃燒的房間裡的桌前,平靜地說「This is fine」(這樣很好)。
  • 如何爆紅: 這幅漫畫中「小狗手拿杯子、四周烈焰包圍」的截圖片段,在 2010 年代中期於 Twitter、Reddit 與 Tumblr 上開始被當作反應迷因(reaction image)流傳,並在 2016 年美國總統大選這段動盪的新聞週期中爆紅,成為形容「情況明顯正在崩壞,但大家還是若無其事」的代表用語。
  • 意義與使用情境: 用來諷刺性地為明顯災難性的情況配文或做出反應,而發文者(或畫面中人物)卻假裝一切都在掌控之中——常見於政治、財經、科技當機或混亂工作日的評論中,例如用來為暴跌的股市線圖或當機的程式配上「this is fine」字樣。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出動畫 GIF 版本(小狗與房間逐漸融化)、大量將小狗替換成其他作品角色的改圖、KC Green 本人後續繪製、交代小狗命運的續篇漫畫,以及被收錄進貼圖包與主流新聞評論中,成為形容集體否認現實的代名詞。
  • 目前現況: 誕生超過十年後,「This Is Fine」至今仍被頻繁、日常地使用——是少數已經沉澱為網路「永久詞彙」、而非隨時間淡出的 2010 年代反應迷因之一。
