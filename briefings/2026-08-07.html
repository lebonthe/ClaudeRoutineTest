3) Spanish Lesson — Day 29 (10 min, builds on Day 28)

Day 28 added the subjunctive's second major trigger family — doubt, denial, and impersonal possibility. Today adds a third family that works on a different logic entirely: emotional reaction. Unlike doubt, an emotion-trigger sentence can embed a fact the speaker is completely certain of and still require the subjunctive — because the subjunctive here isn't marking uncertainty, it's marking that the clause is commentary/reaction rather than new asserted information.

  • Me alegra que vengas a la fiesta. — I'm glad (that) you're coming to the party.
  • Siento que no puedas venir tú. — I'm sorry (that) you can't come.
  • Es una lástima que llueva hoy. — It's a shame it's raining today.
  • Me sorprende que ella no sepa la noticia. — It surprises me that she doesn't know the news.

Grammar note: common emotion triggers include alegrarse de que (to be glad), sentir que (to feel sorry/regret that — not to be confused with sentir + noun, "to feel [a sensation]," which takes no subjunctive), sorprender que (to surprise), es una lástima que (it's a shame), and temer que (to fear). Several of these (sorprender, encantar, molestar) reuse the "backwards" gustar-pattern grammar from Day 15 — an indirect object pronoun (me/te/le) plus a verb that agrees with whatever follows, except today what follows is an entire subjunctive clause instead of a noun. Spanish pedagogy commonly groups all subjunctive triggers under the mnemonic WEIRDO — Wishes, Emotions, Impersonal expressions, Recommendations, Doubt, Ojalá. Day 27 covered W, Day 28 covered D (and part of I), and today covers E; the remaining categories will follow on subsequent days.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 29: 坎 (Kǎn) — "The Abysmal" / "Water" (Water above, Water below — the only hexagram formed by doubling a single trigram twice over into a "pit within a pit"). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary explains the transition from yesterday's Hexagram 28: "物不可以終過,故受之以坎。坎者,陷也。" ("Things cannot remain in a state of excess forever, hence Dà Guò is succeeded by Kǎn. Kǎn means 'a pit, a sinking into danger.'") This follows logically: something stretched to the point of "great exceeding" cannot sustain that extremity indefinitely — it collapses into a moment of real danger. The Judgment reads: "習坎:有孚,維心亨,行有尚。" ("The Abysmal, repeated: if you are sincere, you have success in your heart, and whatever you do succeeds.") The hexagram's structure makes the danger explicit and doubled: each of its two identical Water trigrams (☵) already places one solid line trapped between two broken lines — a pit — and stacking two of them creates a pit beneath a pit, danger recurring rather than a single isolated hazard. The Image commentary reads: "水洊至,習坎;君子以常德行,習教事。" ("Water flows on and on, reaching its goal — this is Kǎn, repeated. Thus the superior person acts with consistent, unwavering virtue and practices his affairs like a habitual teaching.") Traditional commentary draws its key image from water's own nature: water flows into every low place and pit it meets, yet it never loses its own essential nature or overflows its own truth in the process — the classical formula is "水流而不盈,行險而不失其信" (water flows without ever needing to fill itself to the brim, moving through danger without losing its own trustworthiness). Read together, the hexagram teaches that danger, when it recurs rather than being a one-time event, is best met not by a single heroic act but by cultivated, habitual sincerity and steady practice — the same disciplined constancy repeated every time the pit reappears, exactly as water repeats its own nature through every low place it passes through.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Jordan (約旦)

  • Location: A country in the Levant/Middle East, bordered by Syria to the north, Iraq to the northeast, Saudi Arabia to the east and south, and Israel and the Israeli-occupied West Bank to the west, with a short coastline on the Gulf of Aqaba (Red Sea) at its southern tip — its only sea access.
  • Territory: Approximately 89,342 km² — a little smaller than Portugal, and roughly 2.5 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²); terrain ranges from the Jordan Valley (part of the Great Rift Valley, including the shore of the Dead Sea, Earth's lowest point on dry land) to highland plateaus and the eastern desert, which makes up roughly 75% of the country.
  • Population: Approximately 11.5 million (2024 est.). A majority of citizens are of Palestinian origin, and Jordan additionally hosts one of the highest refugee-to-population ratios in the world, including large registered Syrian and Iraqi refugee populations concentrated partly in camps such as Za'atari.
  • Religion: Sunni Islam is the official state religion and the faith of roughly 95% of the population; Christians make up a small but historically significant minority (roughly 2-4%, mostly Greek Orthodox), tracing continuous roots back to the earliest Christian era and enjoying constitutionally protected status and reserved parliamentary seats.
  • Economy: An upper-middle-income, resource-poor economy with very limited oil and, more critically, some of the world's lowest per-capita freshwater availability — water scarcity is a chronic strategic constraint on growth. Key sectors include services, tourism (Petra, the Dead Sea, Wadi Rum), phosphate and potash mining/export, and information technology; the country relies significantly on remittances from Jordanians working abroad and on foreign aid from the US and Gulf states, and carries persistently high public debt and unemployment.
  • History: Part of the Ottoman Empire until World War I; became the British-administered Emirate of Transjordan in 1921 under Emir Abdullah I, a Hashemite prince descended from the Sharif of Mecca and, through that line, from the Prophet Muhammad; gained full independence in 1946 as the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. Jordan controlled and later annexed the West Bank from 1950 until losing it to Israel in the 1967 Six-Day War. In 1994 Jordan signed a peace treaty with Israel — only the second Arab state to do so after Egypt — and has since played a recurring mediating/stabilizing role in regional diplomacy, alongside close military and security cooperation with the US and Western allies.
  • Current leader: King Abdullah II bin Al-Hussein, reigning since 1999 after succeeding his father, the long-ruling King Hussein; Crown Prince Hussein bin Abdullah is heir apparent. Day-to-day government runs through an appointed Prime Minister and Cabinet operating under the King's substantial constitutional authority over foreign policy, the military, and senior appointments.
  • Language: Arabic is the official language (Jordanian dialect in everyday speech); English is widely used in business, higher education, and tourism.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov/the-world-factbook

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — 藍瘦香菇 ("Lán Shòu Xiāng Gū" / "Blue-Thin Mushroom")

  • Name: 藍瘦香菇 (lán shòu xiāng gū), literally "blue," "thin," and "mushroom" strung together — three unrelated words whose only real function is to sound, when read aloud, almost identical to the actual intended phrase, "難受,想哭" (nánshòu, xiǎng kū — "[I feel] sad, [I] want to cry").
  • Origin/history: In October 2016, a young man from Nanning, Guangxi (mainland China), surnamed Wei, uploaded a short, earnestly emotional video lamenting a breakup, saying "難受,想哭" in his strong regional accent. Guangxi Mandarin speakers often do not distinguish the initial consonants n/l or the sounds sh/s the way Standard Mandarin does, so his pronunciation of "難受想哭" came out sounding almost exactly like "藍瘦香菇" to listeners — an accidental, dialect-driven homophone.
  • How it became popular: The clip's unintentionally comic mismatch between the speaker's genuine heartbreak and the absurd, nonsensical literal phrase it sounded like caused it to spread explosively across Weibo, WeChat, and other Chinese platforms within days of upload, arriving in a cultural moment already primed for short dialect-driven catchphrase memes (a wave that would recur the following year with Taiwan's 傻眼貓咪, referenced in yesterday's briefing as a direct successor in the same meme lineage). It was quickly picked up by international outlets, including Western press, as a case study in Chinese internet virality.
  • Meaning/usage: Used — usually half-jokingly rather than to express genuine deep sorrow — to say "I'm sad, I want to cry," typically about something mildly disappointing rather than a real tragedy; saying "我藍瘦香菇" ("I'm blue-thin-mushroom") became a lighthearted, self-deprecating way to complain.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned mushroom-shaped plush toys and stickers, remix songs and parody videos built on the original clip, and entered the broader family of mid-2010s Chinese "accidental dialect homophone" memes; frequently cited alongside 黑人問號 (Black Guy Question Mark) and 無言薯條 (Wordless Fries) as a defining trio of that meme generation, and explicitly named as a direct lineage predecessor of 傻眼貓咪 in the historical record.
  • Current status: Largely a nostalgia reference by 2026 rather than an actively trending phrase — still widely recognized by anyone who was online in the Chinese-language internet in 2016, occasionally revived ironically ("好久沒說藍瘦香菇了" — "haven't said this in ages"), and now studied as a textbook example of how a real dialect accident, rather than a deliberate joke or image, can become a viral catchphrase.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, zh.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Metropolis (1927, dir. Fritz Lang)

  • Name/creator(s): Metropolis, directed by Fritz Lang, with a screenplay by Thea von Harbou (Lang's wife at the time) adapting her own novel of the same name; starring Brigitte Helm in a dual role as Maria and her robot double, alongside Gustav Fröhlich, Alfred Abel, and Rudolf Klein-Rogge as the inventor Rotwang.
  • Era/background: Produced by Germany's UFA studio during the Weimar Republic and released in 1927, at the time the most expensive film ever made. Its Berlin premiere was a critical and commercial disappointment, and distributors heavily re-cut the film for international release, destroying much of its original footage; for decades it circulated only in truncated, sometimes reordered versions until a nearly complete 16mm print surfaced in a Buenos Aires film museum in 2008, enabling a 2010 restoration close to Lang's original vision.
  • Plot/content overview: In a starkly divided future city, a leisured elite lives in tower-top luxury while an enslaved worker class toils underground to keep the machines running. Freder, son of the city's ruler Joh Fredersen, falls for Maria, a worker who preaches peaceful reconciliation between society's "head" (the planners) and "hands" (the workers), mediated by "the heart." Fearing her influence, Fredersen has the inventor Rotwang build a robot double of Maria to discredit her and incite the workers into self-destructive rebellion, nearly flooding the underground city and drowning the workers' own children before Freder and the real Maria intervene.
  • Artistic style and techniques: The definitive visual template for German Expressionist science fiction — towering Art Deco/Gothic skyscraper miniatures combined with live actors in single shots via the Schüfftan process (a mirror-based special-effects trick), and geometric, unison mass-choreography of the worker crowds that treats them almost as a single moving organism. The robot Maria's design — an angular, gleaming humanoid form — became the visual ancestor of screen robots for the following century.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely credited as one of the first feature-length science-fiction films and a founding text of the genre's visual grammar; its stark "head/hands/heart" class-reconciliation allegory has also long been debated as politically muddled, or even proto-fascist in its implication that class conflict is best resolved by a unifying mediator rather than structural change — making the film as contested ideologically as it is celebrated visually.
  • Influence and legacy: Its visual DNA runs directly through Blade Runner's dystopian skyline, Star Wars (C-3PO's design was explicitly modeled on Robot Maria), Dark City, and countless dystopian cityscapes across film, games, and music video (Madonna's "Express Yourself" restages several of its images directly). In 2001, Metropolis became the first film ever inducted into UNESCO's "Memory of the World" register.
  • Current status/where to experience: The 2010 "complete" restoration (still missing an estimated five minutes of unrecovered footage) is the standard version now available on Blu-ray and major streaming platforms, and is regularly screened with live orchestral performances of Gottfried Huppertz's original score at festivals and repertory cinemas worldwide.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, moma.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Camera Movement (運鏡) (Lesson 15)

Lessons 1-14 covered what's inside the frame and how a film is built and edited — including two lessons that come close to today's territory but stop short of it: Lesson 3 (Cinematography & Lighting) was about the quality and direction of light, and Lesson 8 (Framing & Composition) was about how a shot is composed within a static frame. Today's lens asks a different question: what does it mean when the frame itself moves through space, and what is that movement telling you that a static shot couldn't?

The method: Camera movement has a small, learnable vocabulary, and the first job of this lens is simply naming precisely what's happening: a pan pivots the camera horizontally from a fixed point; a tilt pivots it vertically; a dolly or tracking shot physically moves the whole camera through space (toward, away from, or alongside the subject); a crane or jib shot adds sweeping vertical movement to that horizontal travel; a Steadicam or handheld shot moves the camera on a human operator's body, either smoothed out or deliberately left shaky; and a zoom is not camera movement at all — the camera stays physically still while the lens's focal length changes, which is why a zoom and a dolly can look superficially similar but read completely differently (a dolly changes your physical relationship to the space; a zoom just changes magnification). Once you can name the movement, ask three questions: (1) Is it motivated or unmotivated? Does the camera move because it's following a character's action, or does it move on its own initiative, asserting the director's presence as an implied narrator? (2) Does it reveal new spatial information, or is it purely expressive? A track that follows a character down a hallway they're walking anyway is doing different work than a track that glides forward with no character moving at all, injecting dread or anticipation the actors' own performance isn't providing. (3) Is it meant to be invisible or foregrounded? Classical Hollywood continuity style hides camera movement inside motivated action so viewers never consciously register it; other films make the movement itself the point, daring you to notice the camera as an authorial hand shaping the scene.

Worked example — Goodfellas (1990, dir. Martin Scorsese), the Copacabana tracking shot: Henry Hill takes Karen on a date, and rather than cutting between shots of them arriving, the film follows them for roughly three unbroken minutes in a single Steadicam tracking shot: down a side street, through the club's back kitchen entrance, past cooks and staff who greet Henry by name, through corridors and a bustling kitchen, and finally out into the crowded main showroom, where a table is produced from nowhere and set up for them right at the front, as the emcee name-checks Henry from the stage.

What to look for and why it works: (1) The unbroken duration is the argument, not just a technical flex — because there is no cut, the audience never gets a moment of distance from the experience; we glide through the club exactly as effortlessly and continuously as Henry does, which means the seduction of "having pull" in the mob world is delivered to us physically, through unbroken movement, before a single line of dialogue states it. (2) The movement is fully motivated (it follows Henry and Karen's actual walk) yet still deeply expressive — it shows real spatial information (the literal back-door route into a club regular patrons wait in line for) while simultaneously functioning as pure seduction, which is why this scene is a favorite example of how "motivated" and "expressive" camera movement aren't opposites but can operate at the same time. (3) The camera's fluid privilege mirrors the film's whole moral logic — the ease with which the camera (and Henry) bypasses every obstacle other people have to queue or pay for is exactly the intoxicating shortcut-to-power the film is about to spend its whole running time complicating and eventually punishing.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第二十九天(10 分鐘,銜接第二十八天內容)

第二十八天新增了虛擬式的第二組主要觸發情境——懷疑、否定,以及非人稱的可能性表達。今天則要加入第三組完全不同邏輯的觸發情境:情感反應。和「懷疑」不同的是,情感觸發詞造出的句子,即使子句內容是說話者百分之百確定的事實,依然必須用虛擬式——因為這裡的虛擬式標記的不是「不確定」,而是「這個子句是評論/情緒反應,而不是在陳述一項新資訊」。

  • Me alegra que vengas a la fiesta. — 我很高興你會來派對。
  • Siento que no puedas venir tú. — 我很遺憾你不能來。
  • Es una lástima que llueva hoy. — 今天下雨真可惜。
  • Me sorprende que ella no sepa la noticia. — 她不知道這個消息讓我很驚訝。

文法重點:常見的情感觸發詞包括 alegrarse de que(感到高興)、sentir que(對⋯感到遺憾——別和 sentir + 名詞「感覺到某種感受」搞混,那種用法不需要虛擬式)、sorprender que(讓⋯感到驚訝)、es una lástima que(⋯真可惜),以及 temer que(擔心/害怕⋯)。其中好幾個(sorprender、encantar、molestar)沿用了第十五天學過「反過來」的 gustar 句型文法——用間接受詞代名詞(me/te/le)搭配一個要跟著後面內容變化的動詞,只是今天後面接的不再是名詞,而是一整個虛擬式子句。西語教學上常把所有虛擬式觸發詞歸納成 WEIRDO 這個口訣——Wishes(希望)、Emotions(情感)、Impersonal expressions(非人稱表達)、Recommendations(建議)、Doubt(懷疑)、Ojalá(希望詞)。第二十七天教了 W,第二十八天教了 D(以及部分 I),今天教的則是 E;剩下的類別將在接下來的課程中陸續補完。

4) 易經卦象

第二十九卦:坎卦(Kǎn)——「重重險陷」/「水」(上坎下坎,是唯一由同一單卦重複兩次疊成、形成「坑中之坑」的卦象)。〈序卦傳〉解釋了從昨天第二十八卦到今天的轉折:「物不可以終過,故受之以坎。坎者,陷也。」(凡事都不可能永遠處於過度的狀態,因此大過卦之後,便是坎卦。坎,意即陷落、危險。)這在邏輯上順理成章:被拉伸到「大過」極限的事物,終究無法一直維持在那樣的極端狀態——最終會陷落進真正的險境之中。卦辭寫道:「習坎:有孚,維心亨,行有尚。」(重重險陷之卦:只要心懷誠信,內心便能亨通,所行之事也必有所成就。)這一卦的卦象結構,把「危險」表現得既明確又加倍:兩個相同的坎卦(☵)單卦,每一個本身就是一條陽爻被困在兩條陰爻之間、形成一個「坑」,而兩個坑上下相疊,便構成了坑中有坑——象徵的是反覆出現的危險,而不只是一次性的孤立災難。〈象傳〉補充道:「水洊至,習坎;君子以常德行,習教事。」(水不斷流動、持續向前抵達目的地,這正是重重坎卦的象徵;因此君子應當以恆常不變的德行行事,並像日常教學一樣反覆練習自己該做的事。)傳統注疏在此特別取用水本身的特性作為核心意象:水流入所遇到的每一個低窪與坑洞,卻始終不失去自身的本質,也不會在這個過程中溢出自己的誠信——經典的說法是「水流而不盈,行險而不失其信」(水流動時,從不需要填滿到滿溢才肯繼續前進;即便行經險境,也絕不失去自身的誠信)。綜合來看,這一卦教導的是:當危險是反覆出現、而非一次性事件時,最好的應對方式並非仰賴單一次的英雄式壯舉,而是靠涵養出來的、習慣性的誠信與穩定的日常實踐——每次坑洞再度出現時,都用同樣有紀律的恆常心態去面對,正如水每經過一處低窪,都會重複展現自己不變的本質。

(第二十九次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)、第二十一卦 噬嗑(Shì Kè)、第二十二卦 賁(Bì)、第二十三卦 剝(Bō)、第二十四卦 復(Fù)、第二十五卦 无妄(Wú Wàng)、第二十六卦 大畜(Dà Chù)、第二十七卦 頤(Yí)、第二十八卦 大過(Dà Guò)、第二十九卦 坎(Kǎn)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 約旦(Jordan)

  • 位置: 位於中東黎凡特地區,北鄰敘利亞、東北鄰伊拉克、東側與南側鄰接沙烏地阿拉伯、西側鄰接以色列與以色列占領下的約旦河西岸;南端在阿卡巴灣(紅海)擁有一小段海岸線,是其唯一的出海口。
  • 領土面積: 約 89,342 平方公里——略小於葡萄牙,約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 2.5 倍;地形涵蓋約旦河谷(屬於「東非大裂谷」系統的一部分,包括死海沿岸——地球陸地上海拔最低點)一直到高原台地與東部沙漠,沙漠地形約占全國面積的 75%。
  • 人口: 約 1,150 萬人(2024 年估計)。多數公民具有巴勒斯坦裔血統,約旦同時也是全世界難民占總人口比例最高的國家之一,境內收容大量登記在案的敘利亞與伊拉克難民,部分集中於札塔里(Za'atari)等難民營。
  • 宗教: 遜尼派伊斯蘭教為官方國教,信眾約占人口 95%;基督徒是人數雖少但歷史意義重大的少數族群(約占 2% 至 4%,多屬希臘東正教),其信仰淵源可追溯至基督教最早期,並享有憲法保障的地位與國會保留席次。
  • 經濟: 屬於中高所得、天然資源匱乏的經濟體,石油蘊藏極為有限,更關鍵的是其人均淡水供應量位居全球最低之列——水資源短缺是長期制約經濟成長的戰略性難題。主要產業包括服務業、觀光業(佩特拉古城、死海、瓦地倫沙漠)、磷酸鹽與鉀鹽礦業出口,以及資訊科技業;該國高度仰賴海外約旦僑民匯款,以及來自美國與波灣國家的外援,長期背負偏高的公共債務與失業率。
  • 歷史: 第一次世界大戰前為鄂圖曼帝國轄地;1921 年成為英國託管的「外約旦酋長國」(Emirate of Transjordan),由哈希姆家族的阿卜杜拉一世埃米爾統治——該家族出身麥加謝里夫(Sharif of Mecca),據傳系出先知穆罕默德一脈;1946 年正式獨立,成為「約旦哈希姆王國」。約旦曾於 1950 年至 1967 年間統治並吞併約旦河西岸,直到 1967 年「六日戰爭」中被以色列奪走。1994 年,約旦與以色列簽署和平協議,成為繼埃及之後第二個與以色列建交的阿拉伯國家,此後在區域外交上屢屢扮演調停與穩定局勢的角色,並與美國及西方盟邦維持密切的軍事與安全合作關係。
  • 現任領導人: 國王阿卜杜拉二世・本・胡笙(King Abdullah II bin Al-Hussein),自 1999 年即位,接續其父、長年在位的胡笙國王;胡笙王儲(Crown Prince Hussein bin Abdullah)為法定繼承人。日常政務由國王任命的總理與內閣負責運作,而國王本身則在外交政策、軍事與高階人事任命上,擁有相當大的憲法權力。
  • 語言: 官方語言為阿拉伯語(日常口語使用約旦方言);英語則廣泛使用於商業、高等教育與觀光領域。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 藍瘦香菇("Lán Shòu Xiāng Gū")

  • 名稱: 「藍瘦香菇」——字面上是「藍色」「瘦」「香菇」三個毫不相干的詞湊在一起,唯一的功能就是唸出來的音,幾乎與真正想表達的那句話「難受,想哭」一模一樣。
  • 起源/歷史: 2016 年 10 月,一位來自中國廣西南寧、姓魏的年輕人,上傳了一段感情真摯、為分手而傷心的短片,用濃厚的廣西口音說出「難受,想哭」。由於廣西口音的普通話,常常無法像標準普通話那樣清楚區分聲母 n/l,以及 sh/s 的發音,他說出的「難受想哭」,聽在其他人耳裡幾乎就像是「藍瘦香菇」——這是一起道地的方言口音意外造成的諧音哏。
  • 如何爆紅: 影片中說話者真摯的傷心情緒,與聽起來卻是這句荒謬、毫無意義的字面短語之間的強烈落差,產生了意料之外的喜感,上傳短短幾天內便在微博、微信等中國社群平台上迅速瘋傳;當時的網路文化氛圍,本就已經對這類「方言諧音流行語」有所期待(隔年台灣爆紅的「傻眼貓咪」,正如昨天簡報所提及,便是同一系列流行語脈絡下的直接後繼者)。這起事件很快也被國際媒體(包括西方媒體)報導,視為中國網路爆紅現象的經典案例。
  • 意義與使用情境: 通常帶著半開玩笑的語氣使用,而非真的表達深沉的悲傷,用來說「我很難過,想哭」,多半是形容一些讓人有點失望、而非真正悲劇性的小事;說「我藍瘦香菇」逐漸變成一種輕鬆、自我調侃式的抱怨方式。
  • 變體與演變: 衍生出香菇造型的絨毛玩偶與貼圖、以原始影片為基礎的翻唱歌曲與惡搞影片,並成為 2010 年代中期中國「方言諧音意外爆紅」系列流行語的一員;經常與「黑人問號」、「無言薯條」並列,被視為那個世代最具代表性的三大流行語之一,而且在歷史脈絡紀錄中,也被明確指出是「傻眼貓咪」的直接源頭之一。
  • 目前現況: 到了 2026 年,這句流行語大致已成為一種懷舊指涉,而非仍在流行中的用語——但只要是 2016 年就活躍於華語網路圈的使用者,大多仍認得這個詞,偶爾也會被拿出來帶點反諷意味地重提(「好久沒說藍瘦香菇了」);如今更常被當作教科書等級的案例來研究:一起真實的方言口音意外,而非刻意設計的笑話或圖片,是如何演變成全民流行語的。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、zh.wikipedia.org

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《大都會》(Metropolis,1927,導演佛列茲・朗 Fritz Lang)

  • 名稱/創作者: 《大都會》,導演佛列茲・朗(Fritz Lang),劇本由朗當時的妻子特雅・馮・哈波(Thea von Harbou)改編自她自己的同名小說;由布莉姬・海爾姆(Brigitte Helm)一人分飾女主角瑪莉亞與其機器人分身兩角,另有古斯塔夫・弗洛里希(Gustav Fröhlich)、阿弗雷德・阿貝爾(Alfred Abel),以及飾演發明家羅特旺(Rotwang)的魯道夫・克萊恩-羅格(Rudolf Klein-Rogge)主演。
  • 時代背景: 由德國 UFA 電影公司於威瑪共和時期製作,1927 年上映,是當時史上製作成本最高的電影。柏林首映口碑與票房皆令人失望,發行商為求國際發行,大幅刪剪影片,原始拷貝大量佚失;此後數十年間,這部片只能以殘缺、甚至順序被打亂的版本流通,直到 2008 年在布宜諾斯艾利斯一間電影博物館發現一份幾近完整的 16 毫米拷貝,才使 2010 年得以完成一次接近導演原始構想的修復版。
  • 劇情概述: 故事發生在一座階級對立鮮明的未來城市:享受奢華生活的上層階級居住在高聳的塔樓之中,被奴役的勞工階級則在地底終日操勞,維持機器運轉。城市統治者約翰・弗雷德森之子費德爾,愛上了勞工瑪莉亞——她宣揚社會的「頭腦」(規劃者)與「雙手」(勞工)之間,應由「心」來調和、達成和平共存。弗雷德森擔心瑪莉亞的號召力,命發明家羅特旺打造一個瑪莉亞的機器人分身,企圖抹黑她並煽動勞工發動自我毀滅式的暴動,幾乎導致地底城市遭水淹沒、勞工自己的孩子險遭滅頂,所幸費德爾與真正的瑪莉亞及時介入化解危機。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 確立了德國表現主義科幻電影的標準視覺範式——運用「舒夫坦技法」(Schüfftan process,一種利用鏡子將模型與真人演員合成在同一鏡頭中的特效技術),打造出聳立的裝飾藝術/哥德式摩天樓縮小模型場景,並以幾何式、整齊劃一的群眾調度手法,將勞工群眾拍得幾乎像是一個單一的移動生命體。機器人瑪莉亞稜角分明、閃閃發亮的人形設計,更成為往後一整個世紀螢幕機器人形象的視覺始祖。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 普遍被認為是最早的科幻長片之一,也是奠定該類型視覺語彙的開創性作品;其「頭腦/雙手/心」的階級調和寓言,長期以來也被質疑政治立場曖昧不清,甚至帶有法西斯傾向——因為劇情暗示階級衝突最好的解方,是仰賴一位居中調停者,而非結構性的改變,使這部電影在視覺成就備受推崇的同時,也始終伴隨意識形態上的爭議。
  • 影響與傳承: 其視覺基因直接延續到《銀翼殺手》的反烏托邦天際線、《星際大戰》(C-3PO 的造型明確是以機器人瑪莉亞為藍本設計)、《極光追殺令》(Dark City),以及無數電影、電玩與音樂錄影帶中的反烏托邦城市景觀(瑪丹娜《Express Yourself》MV 便直接重現了片中多個經典畫面)。2001 年,《大都會》成為史上第一部入選聯合國教科文組織「世界記憶名錄」的電影作品。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 2010 年完成的「近乎完整」修復版(估計仍缺少約五分鐘未尋回的片段),是目前藍光光碟與各大串流平台上通行的標準版本,全球各大影展與經典重映戲院也經常搭配戈特弗里德・胡珀茲(Gottfried Huppertz)原創配樂的現場管弦樂團演出一同放映。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、moma.org

8) 看片方法 — 運鏡 Camera Movement(第十五課)

第一到十四課教了如何解讀畫面內的元素、電影如何被建構與剪輯——其中有兩堂課的內容與今天相近,但切入點並不相同:第三課「攝影與燈光」談的是光線的品質與方向,第八課「取景與構圖」談的是靜態畫面內部的構圖安排。今天的分析視角則要問一個不一樣的問題:當畫面本身在空間中移動時,這代表了什麼?這樣的移動,能告訴你哪些靜止的鏡頭無法傳達的訊息?

方法說明: 運鏡有一套精簡、容易掌握的詞彙,這個分析視角的第一步,就是先準確辨認出正在發生的是哪一種運鏡:橫搖(pan)是攝影機固定在原地、朝水平方向轉動;直搖(tilt)是朝垂直方向轉動;推軌鏡頭(dolly/tracking shot)是整台攝影機在空間中實際移動(靠近、遠離,或跟隨主體平行移動);吊臂鏡頭(crane/jib)則是在水平移動之外,再加上垂直方向的大幅度擺動;史坦尼康(Steadicam)或手持攝影是攝影機架在操作員身上隨之移動,可以刻意拍得平穩流暢,也可以刻意拍得晃動不安;而變焦(zoom)其實根本不算是運鏡——攝影機本身完全靜止不動,只是鏡頭的焦距改變——這正是為什麼變焦和推軌鏡頭表面上看起來有點像,實際感受卻完全不同(推軌鏡頭改變的是你與空間的實際相對關係,變焦則只是單純放大畫面)。能準確辨認出運鏡類型之後,接著問三個問題:(1)這個運鏡是「有動機的」還是「無動機的」? 攝影機的移動,是因為在跟隨某個角色的動作,還是攝影機自行主動移動,彰顯出導演作為隱藏敘事者的存在感?(2)它揭露了新的空間資訊,還是純粹出於情緒表達? 跟拍一個原本就要走過某條走廊的角色,和一個完全沒有任何角色移動、鏡頭卻自顧自向前滑行的運鏡,做的是完全不同的工作——後者注入的懼怕或期待感,並非來自演員的表演本身。(3)這個運鏡意圖讓觀眾「察覺不到」,還是刻意「凸顯出來」? 古典好萊塢的連戲剪輯風格,習慣把攝影機的移動隱藏在有動機的動作之中,讓觀眾幾乎意識不到攝影機正在移動;但也有些電影刻意把運鏡本身變成重點,大膽地讓你注意到攝影機是導演塑造這場戲的一隻「作者之手」。

實例示範——《四海好傢伙》(Goodfellas,1990,導演馬丁・史柯西斯 Martin Scorsese)的科帕卡巴納夜總會推軌鏡頭: 亨利・希爾帶著凱倫約會,電影並沒有用剪接來呈現兩人抵達的過程,而是用一鏡到底、長達約三分鐘的史坦尼康推軌鏡頭全程跟拍:穿過一條側街,從夜總會後方的廚房入口進入,經過認得亨利、與他打招呼的廚師與工作人員,穿越走廊與忙碌的廚房,最後走進擠滿賓客的主秀場——服務生像變魔術一樣憑空生出一張桌子,直接擺在舞台最前排,司儀甚至在台上點名歡迎亨利。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)不間斷的鏡頭時長本身就是論點,而不只是技術上的炫技——正因為全程沒有剪接,觀眾完全沒有機會抽離這段體驗,我們就這樣跟著亨利一起毫不費力、連續不斷地滑行穿越整個夜總會,這意味著「在黑幫世界裡吃得開」這種誘惑感,是透過不間斷的鏡頭運動,直接以身體感受的方式傳遞給觀眾,而不是靠任何一句台詞說出來的。(2)這個運鏡完全「有動機」(跟隨著亨利與凱倫實際的行走路線),但同時又極具表現性——它一方面呈現了真實的空間資訊(那條連常客都得排隊等候的後門捷徑),另一方面卻又同時發揮著純粹誘惑觀眾的功能,這正是這場戲之所以經常被拿來當作範例、說明「有動機」與「表現性」的運鏡並非互斥、而是可以同時並存的原因。(3)攝影機這種暢行無阻的特權,正呼應了整部電影的道德邏輯——攝影機(以及亨利本人)輕鬆繞過每一個其他人都得排隊或付費才能通過的障礙,這正是電影接下來要花上整部片時間,去逐漸複雜化、最終加以懲罰的那種令人陶醉的「權力捷徑」誘惑。
