3) Spanish Lesson — Day 66 (10 min, Verbal Periphrases: Acabar de / Llevar + Gerund / Volver a)

Steps outside the ser/estar contrast just completed on Day 65 to fill another foundational everyday-usage gap that has been used implicitly since the very first lessons but never formally named: three fixed "verbal periphrases" (a conjugated verb + fixed preposition/connector + infinitive or gerund, functioning together as one meaning-unit, the same grammatical family as Day 13's ir a + infinitive and Day 14's estar + gerund) that let Spanish express, with a single compact structure each, three concepts English and Chinese each handle with entirely separate vocabulary: acabar de + infinitive (conjugated in the present, "to have just done" something — the mirror image of Day 13's forward-looking ir a, this one points backward to an action that ended essentially at the moment of speaking); llevar + a length of time + gerund ("to have been doing" something for a given duration, continuing up to and including the present moment — the natural, everyday-preferred Spanish alternative to a literal present-perfect-progressive translation, with the fixed question form ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas + gerund?); and volver a + infinitive ("to do again," repurposing volver's literal "to return" into a marker of repetition, functionally interchangeable in most contexts with adding otra vez or de nuevo to the plain verb). The three periphrases answer three independent questions — did this just end? how long has this been going on? is this happening again? — and can freely coexist in a single conversation without overlap or conflict.

  • Lo siento, acabo de cenar. ¿Qué tal mañana? — Sorry, I just had dinner. How about tomorrow?
  • ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas viviendo en Taipéi? — Llevo cinco años viviendo aquí. — How long have you been living in Taipei? — I've been living here for five years.
  • ¿Vas a ver esa película otra vez? — Sí, voy a volver a verla este fin de semana. — Are you going to watch that movie again? — Yes, I'm going to watch it again this weekend.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 2: 齊物論 (Qíwù Lùn) — "On the Equality of Things" / "Discussion on Making All Things Equal"

The second chapter of the Inner Chapters (內篇), following directly from yesterday's Essay 1 (逍遙遊, "Free and Easy Wandering") and widely regarded by scholars as the single most philosophically dense and difficult chapter in the entire Zhuangzi. It opens with Ziqi of Nanguo (南郭子綦), seated in a trance-like state so complete that his disciple asks whether a person's body can really become like withered wood and the mind like dead ashes; Ziqi replies that he has just "lost himself" (吾喪我) — a deliberate first move signaling that the chapter's entire argument requires first suspending the ordinary, ego-bound "I" that insists on judging things from a single fixed vantage point. Ziqi then explains the famous distinction between the "pipes of Earth" (地籟, wind rushing through the ten thousand different hollows and cavities of trees and rocks, each producing its own distinct, equally valid sound purely as a function of its own particular shape) and the deeper "pipes of Heaven" (天籟, the underlying process that lets each hollow blow its own note without there being any single "correct" note or a piper standing behind it dictating the sound) — an image Zhuangzi uses to argue that every individual perspective, opinion, and claim of "right and wrong" (是非) arises the same way: naturally, out of the particular "shape" of the speaker's own situated position, with no perspective entitled to stand outside all the others as the sole neutral judge. The chapter then turns this cosmological image into a sustained argument about language and debate: if two people argue and one wins, does that prove the winner is right and the loser wrong? Zhuangzi says no — it only proves one person was better at arguing in that moment, since any third party asked to judge would themselves be trapped inside their own "this" (是) and "that" (彼), unable to adjudicate from a truly neutral position; "this" and "that," "life" and "death," "possible" and "impossible" are shown to be mutually defining, relative pairs rather than fixed absolutes, a state of affairs Zhuangzi calls "walking two roads at once" (兩行) and resolves only by reaching what he calls the "pivot of the Way" (道樞), the still point at the center of a wheel of shifting relative perspectives, from which opposites are seen as aspects of one single, larger process rather than truly separate things worth going to war over. The chapter's most celebrated passage, and the most quoted single image in the entire text, closes it: Zhuangzi dreams he is a butterfly, flying about pursuing its own pleasures, entirely unaware that he is Zhuangzi; he wakes with a start, now unmistakably and solidly Zhuangzi again — but is left unable to say for certain whether he is a man who dreamed he was a butterfly, or is now a butterfly dreaming he is a man. Zhuangzi names this boundary-dissolving transformation "the transformation of things" (物化), and the image has served, for over two thousand years since, as Chinese philosophy's single most enduring shorthand for the fluidity of identity and the porousness of the line ordinarily drawn between dream and waking, self and other, one perspective and the next.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — New Zealand

  • Location & territory size: New Zealand is an island nation in the southwestern Pacific Ocean, roughly 2,000 km southeast of Australia across the Tasman Sea, consisting of two main landmasses (the North Island and South Island) plus numerous smaller islands. Total land area is approximately 268,021 km², making it comparable in size to the United Kingdom (~243,610 km²) or the US state of Colorado, and roughly 7.4 times the land area of Taiwan (~36,197 km²) — despite that size, its population is barely a fifth of Taiwan's, giving it one of the lowest population densities of any developed nation.
  • Population: Approximately 5.3 million (2026 estimate), heavily concentrated in and around Auckland (the largest city, home to roughly a third of the national population) and the capital, Wellington, at the southern tip of the North Island; Māori, the indigenous Polynesian people, make up around 17-18% of the population, alongside significant Pacific Islander and Asian minorities in a country that has become increasingly ethnically diverse over recent decades.
  • Religion: Increasingly secular — "no religion" has been the single largest response category in recent censuses (over half the population), with Christianity (chiefly Anglican, Catholic, Presbyterian, and various Pentecostal/evangelical denominations) the largest remaining religious grouping at roughly a quarter to a third, alongside smaller Hindu, Muslim, Buddhist, and Māori-spiritual communities.
  • Economy: A small, highly-developed, trade-dependent economy built on agriculture and dairy exports (Fonterra, a farmer-owned cooperative, is one of the world's largest dairy exporters), tourism, wine, and a globally significant film/screen-production industry (Weta Workshop and Weta FX, based in Wellington — nicknamed "Wellywood" — have supplied effects work for productions from The Lord of the Rings to numerous Hollywood blockbusters); a founding CPTPP member with an independently floating currency, the New Zealand dollar (NZD).
  • History: Settled by Polynesian voyagers (the ancestors of the Māori) around the 13th century CE, among the last major landmasses on Earth to be settled by humans; first European contact came with Dutch explorer Abel Tasman in 1642 and British explorer James Cook in 1769, followed by escalating British colonization. The 1840 Treaty of Waitangi, signed between the British Crown and numerous Māori chiefs, remains the country's founding constitutional document and the central, still-contested reference point for Māori-Crown relations, land rights, and reparations disputes to this day. New Zealand became a self-governing Dominion in 1907 and achieved full legislative independence upon adopting the Statute of Westminster in 1947; it pursued a distinctive nuclear-free foreign policy from the 1980s onward (barring nuclear-armed or nuclear-powered vessels from its ports, a stance that strained ANZUS relations with the US for years) and maintains close economic and defense ties with Australia (the Closer Economic Relations agreement) and its Five Eyes intelligence-sharing partners.
  • Current leadership: Prime Minister Christopher Luxon, leader of the center-right National Party, has governed since November 2023 atop a three-party coalition with the libertarian ACT Party and Winston Peters's New Zealand First; King Charles III remains the ceremonial head of state as King of New Zealand, represented domestically by a Governor-General. New Zealand's next general election is due later in 2026 under its standard three-year electoral cycle.
  • Language: English is the dominant everyday language, alongside two other official languages: Te Reo Māori (the indigenous Polynesian language, subject to ongoing revitalization efforts after historical suppression) and New Zealand Sign Language — making New Zealand one of relatively few countries to grant a sign language full official-language status.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Star Wars Kid

  • Name & origin: In November 2002, 14-year-old high school student Ghyslain Raza, of Trois-Rivières, Quebec, Canada, filmed himself alone in a school studio room swinging a golf-ball retriever like a lightsaber, mimicking fight choreography from Star Wars: Episode I – The Phantom Menace's Darth Maul. Raza never intended the tape to be seen by anyone; he accidentally left the videotape behind at school, where classmates discovered it, digitized it, and began circulating it among students before it was uploaded to the internet under the file name "Jackass_starwars_funny.wmv."
  • How it became popular: The clip spread rapidly through peer-to-peer file-sharing networks in early 2003, at a moment when consumer video-sharing was still a novelty — years before YouTube existed (founded 2005) — making it one of the very first pieces of user-generated video content to achieve mass, spontaneous internet virality, widely cited as among the earliest "viral videos" in the modern sense of the term.
  • Meaning & usage context: Unlike most entries in this series, the meme's core "meaning" is inseparable from its harm: the video's sudden, unwanted exposure subjected Raza, then still a minor, to intense, sustained mass ridicule, harassment, and what he has since described as severe psychological distress, dropping out of school and undergoing psychiatric treatment during the worst of it. He has since become widely referred to as one of the earliest, most prominent documented cases of a private individual's life being upended by involuntary internet virality — a reference point frequently invoked in discussions of cyberbullying, consent, and the ethics of sharing footage of identifiable, non-consenting individuals, a theme this routine also flagged in yesterday's Bed Intruder Song/Antoine Dodson entry.
  • Variations & evolution: The original clip was remixed thousands of times with added lightsaber sound effects, music, and CGI lightsaber blades edited over the golf-ball retriever, format experimentation that some historians credit as an early forerunner of today's remix-culture meme conventions; "Star Wars Kid" entered general internet vocabulary as shorthand for anyone whose embarrassing private moment goes viral against their will.
  • Current status: Raza, now an adult with a law degree, has since spoken publicly (including at Canadian universities and in broadcast interviews) about the long-term effects of cyberbullying and his path toward reclaiming his own narrative, transforming a meme long treated as pure comedy into one now more commonly cited as a foundational cautionary case study in internet-ethics and digital-wellbeing discussions.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Parasite (기생충, 2019, dir. Bong Joon-ho)

  • Creator & era: Directed by Bong Joon-ho and co-written with Han Jin-won, released in South Korea in May 2019 (premiering at the Cannes Film Festival that month), the film arrived as the latest entry in Bong's genre-blending filmography, which already included Memories of Murder, The Host, Snowpiercer, and Okja.
  • Plot overview: The impoverished Kim family, living in a cramped semi-basement apartment, schemes its way one member at a time into the employment of the wealthy Park family — as English tutor, art therapist, driver, and housekeeper respectively — each posing as an unrelated, highly qualified professional and quietly engineering the dismissal of the Parks' existing staff to make room. The plan unravels when the Kims discover the Parks' previous housekeeper's husband has been secretly living, undetected for years, in a hidden bunker beneath the house, triggering an escalating chain of deception, violence, and class-driven catastrophe that erupts in full at the Parks' young son's birthday garden party.
  • Artistic style & technique: The film deliberately shifts genre registers mid-story — opening as a wry con-artist comedy before pivoting, at a precise turning point (a former housekeeper's late-night return and doorbell ring), into psychological thriller and eventually violent tragedy — a structural genre-pivot frequently cited in film-school analysis. Production designer Lee Ha-jun built the Park family's house from scratch specifically to encode class hierarchy spatially: the wealthy live in an elevated, sunlit modernist home reached by ascending stairs, while the Kims' home is a semi-basement (banjiha) that floods during a rainstorm the wealthy family experiences only as a pleasant excuse to camp in their garden — rain functioning as a single weather event with two entirely opposed class meanings within the same story.
  • Cultural & historical significance: Parasite won the Palme d'Or at the 2019 Cannes Film Festival by unanimous jury decision (jury president Alejandro González Iñárritu), then became the first non-English-language film in Academy Awards history to win Best Picture, sweeping four Oscars in total (Best Picture, Best Director, Best Original Screenplay, and Best International Feature Film) at the 2020 ceremony.
  • Influence & legacy: Widely credited, alongside the subsequent global success of Korean television (most notably Squid Game), with cementing the international "Hallyu" (Korean Wave) cultural export beyond music and television into prestige cinema; its structural mid-film genre pivot and spatial class-encoding via vertical architecture are now standard reference points taught in film-analysis curricula worldwide, directly relevant to today's Section 8 lesson on multi-perspective narrative structure.
  • Where to experience it today: Widely available on streaming platforms (availability varies by region and rotates over time) and in a Criterion Collection home-video edition; a continued staple of retrospective and repertory theatrical screenings worldwide.

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Multiple/Conflicting Subjective Narration ("The Rashomon Effect") (Lesson 52)

A new analytical lens distinct from Day 51's Frame Narrative/Nested Diegesis (which concerns how many layers of storytelling are nested inside one another, regardless of whether any layer's content is disputed) and from Day 6's Narrative Structure (which addresses achronological sequencing of a single agreed-upon set of events): this method asks whether a film presents the same single event more than once, through the conflicting, self-interested, or simply incompatible subjective accounts of different characters who each witnessed or participated in it — and, crucially, whether the film ultimately adjudicates which version is "true," or deliberately withholds that judgment to make a point about the unreliability of memory, self-interest, or perspective itself. Check how many distinct retellings the film stages, whether the visual style (lighting, blocking, framing) shifts to mark each teller's distinct psychological coloring of the same physical space, whether the discrepancies are minor filigree or flatly, irreconcilably contradictory, and whether the film ultimately supplies a stable "objective" baseline version at all or leaves the audience permanently without one.

Worked example: Rashomon (1950, dir. Akira Kurosawa) — the film that gave this technique its name, structured around four wholly incompatible eyewitness/participant accounts (a bandit, a samurai's wife, the murdered samurai himself via a medium, and a woodcutter) of the same rape-and-killing incident in a forest, each recounted in flashback to a magistrate and, in an outer frame, retold again by the woodcutter and a priest sheltering from rain at a ruined city gate. Kurosawa shoots each version with a subtly different visual and performative register (the bandit's account stages himself as a swaggering, fearless warrior; the wife's account recasts her as a fainting, victimized figure; the samurai's account, delivered through supernatural mediumship, frames his own death as an honorable suicide) — the same forest clearing, the same three bodies of evidence, restaged with just enough physical inconsistency between versions (a dagger's exact fate, who actually struck the fatal blow) that no version can simply be averaged into a composite truth. Crucially, Kurosawa's camera itself never adopts a single "objective" fifth vantage point to settle the matter — even the woodcutter, ostensibly the outer-frame narrator best positioned to know, is later revealed to have lied by omission about his own role, undercutting any temptation to treat his account as the neutral baseline the other three deviate from. The film's lasting legacy is the term "the Rashomon effect" itself, now used across law, psychology, and journalism to describe any situation in which multiple credible witnesses to one event produce fundamentally irreconcilable accounts of it.

3)西班牙文課程——第 66 天(10 分鐘,常用動詞短語:Acabar de / Llevar + 現在分詞 / Volver a)

跳出第 65 天剛完成的 ser/estar 對照,補上另一個從最早幾堂課就已在對話中默默使用、卻從未正式命名解說過的基礎缺口:三個固定的「動詞短語」(perífrasis verbales,一個變化動詞+固定介係詞/連接詞+不定詞或現在分詞,合起來當成一個意義單位使用,與第 13 天的 ir a + 不定詞、第 14 天的 estar + 現在分詞屬於同一個文法家族),讓西班牙文能各自用一個精簡結構,表達英文/中文各自需要用完全不同詞彙才能表達的三個概念:acabar de + 不定詞(現在式變化,「剛做完」某事——與第 13 天面向未來的 ir a 恰成鏡像,這個結構回頭指向一個幾乎與說話當下同時結束的動作);llevar + 一段時間 + 現在分詞(「已經做」某事多久了,動作持續至說話當下仍未結束——這是西語口語中,比逐字翻譯「現在完成進行式」更自然、更常用的說法,固定疑問句型為 ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas + 現在分詞?);以及 volver a + 不定詞(「再次」做某事,把 volver 字面上「返回」的意思,轉用為表示動作重複發生,在多數語境下,與直接在動詞後加上 otra vez 或 de nuevo 可以互換使用)。這三個動詞短語分別回答三個彼此獨立的問題——這件事剛結束了嗎?這件事已經持續多久了?這件事是不是又發生了一次?——因此可以自由並存於同一段對話中,彼此毫不衝突。

  • Lo siento, acabo de cenar. ¿Qué tal mañana? —— 抱歉,我剛吃完晚餐。明天怎麼樣?
  • ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas viviendo en Taipéi? — Llevo cinco años viviendo aquí. —— 你在台北住多久了?——我已經在這裡住五年了。
  • ¿Vas a ver esa película otra vez? — Sí, voy a volver a verla este fin de semana. —— 你要再看一次那部電影嗎?——對,我這個週末要再看一次。

4)莊子——第 2 篇:齊物論(Qíwù Lùn)——「使萬物歸於齊一之論」

《莊子》內篇第二篇,緊接昨日第 1 篇〈逍遙遊〉之後,學界普遍認為這是整部《莊子》中哲學density最高、也最難解讀的一篇。文章開頭,南郭子綦端坐,進入一種極度出神的狀態,深沉到他的弟子忍不住問道:難道一個人的形體真能像枯木、心靈真能像死灰嗎?子綦回答,他剛剛「吾喪我」——這個開場刻意先點出:要理解本篇的整套論證,必須先放下那個堅持要從單一固定立場評判萬物的、以自我為中心的「我」。子綦接著解釋著名的「地籟」與「天籟」之別:地籟,是風吹過樹木與岩石上千千萬萬個形狀各異的孔竅時所發出的聲響,每個孔竅單純依其自身形狀,便各自發出獨特卻同樣「有效」的聲音;而更深一層的「天籟」,則是讓每個孔竅都能各自吹出自己的音調、卻沒有任何一個「正確」音調、也沒有任何一位吹奏者站在背後決定聲音的那個根本歷程——莊子藉此主張,每個人的觀點、意見與「是非」判斷,其實都是以同樣的方式產生的:自然而然地,源自說話者自身所處位置的特定「形狀」,沒有任何一個觀點有資格凌駕於其他觀點之上、自居為唯一中立的裁判。文章接著把這個宇宙論意象,轉化為一場關於語言與辯論的持續論證:若兩人辯論,其中一方勝出,這是否就證明勝者為是、敗者為非?莊子的答案是否定的——這只證明了那一刻某一方比較擅長辯論,因為就算找來第三方仲裁,這第三方本身依然被困在自己的「是」與「彼」之中,無法真正站在中立立場做出裁決;「此」與「彼」、「生」與「死」、「可」與「不可」,都被證明是彼此相互定義的相對概念組,而非固定不變的絕對真理——莊子稱這種狀態為「兩行」(同時走在兩條路上),並主張唯有抵達他所謂的「道樞」——一個位於相對觀點之輪中心、靜止不動的樞紐點——才能將對立的兩端,視為同一個更大歷程的兩面,而非真正值得為之爭戰的分裂事物。全文最為人稱道、也是整部《莊子》中被引用最頻繁的單一意象,出現在篇末作結:莊子夢見自己化為一隻蝴蝶,翩翩飛舞、自得其樂,渾然不覺自己是莊周;夢醒之後,他確確實實、清清楚楚又是莊周了——卻再也無法確定,究竟是「莊周夢見自己變成蝴蝶」,還是此刻其實是「蝴蝶夢見自己變成了莊周」。莊子將這種消融了界線的轉化,稱為「物化」——兩千餘年來,這個意象始終是中國哲學中,用來表達「身分認同的流動性」以及「夢與醒、自我與他者、此一觀點與彼一觀點之間那條界線其實模糊不清」這個核心思想時,最歷久不衰的一句總結。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——紐西蘭(New Zealand)

  • 地理位置與領土規模:紐西蘭是位於南太平洋西南部的島國,隔著塔斯曼海,位於澳洲東南方約 2,000 公里處,由兩個主要島嶼(北島與南島)以及眾多較小島嶼組成。國土總面積約 268,021 平方公里,與英國(約 243,610 平方公里)或美國科羅拉多州相當,約為台灣(約 36,197 平方公里)陸地面積的 7.4 倍——儘管國土面積如此,人口卻僅約為台灣的五分之一,使其成為已開發國家中人口密度最低的國家之一。
  • 人口:約 530 萬人(2026 年估計值),主要集中於最大城市奧克蘭(Auckland,約佔全國人口三分之一)及其周邊地區,以及位於北島南端的首都威靈頓(Wellington);原住民毛利人(Māori,南島語族玻里尼西亞裔)約佔總人口 17-18%,此外太平洋島民與亞裔族群比例也相當可觀,使紐西蘭近數十年來種族組成日趨多元。
  • 宗教:世俗化程度日益提高——近幾次人口普查中,「無宗教信仰」始終是單一佔比最高的選項(超過總人口一半),基督宗教(主要為聖公會、天主教、長老會,以及各類五旬節/福音派教派)則是剩餘宗教人口中最大宗,約佔四分之一至三分之一,此外還有規模較小的印度教、伊斯蘭教、佛教與毛利傳統信仰社群。
  • 經濟:屬小型、高度已開發、高度依賴貿易的經濟體,以農業與乳製品出口(恆天然〔Fonterra〕,一家由酪農共同持有的合作社,是全球最大乳製品出口商之一)、觀光業、葡萄酒產業,以及具全球指標地位的電影/影視特效產業(位於威靈頓、暱稱「威萊塢」的維塔工作室〔Weta Workshop〕與維塔數位特效〔Weta FX〕,曾為《魔戒》系列及眾多好萊塢大片提供特效製作)為支柱;為 CPTPP(跨太平洋夥伴全面進步協定)創始會員國,採獨立浮動匯率的紐西蘭元(NZD)。
  • 歷史:約於西元 13 世紀由玻里尼西亞航海者(即毛利人的祖先)首度定居,是地球上少數最晚被人類定居的主要陸地之一;歐洲人最早接觸紀錄,分別為 1642 年荷蘭探險家亞伯・塔斯曼,以及 1769 年英國探險家詹姆士・庫克,其後英國殖民力度逐漸加深。1840 年簽署的《懷唐伊條約》(Treaty of Waitangi),由英國王室與眾多毛利部族酋長共同簽訂,至今仍是紐西蘭的立國憲政文件,也是毛利人與王室之間土地權利、賠償爭議至今仍持續角力的核心參照依據。紐西蘭於 1907 年成為自治「自治領」(Dominion),並於 1947 年採納《西敏寺法規》後取得完整立法獨立地位;自 1980 年代起奉行獨樹一格的「無核」外交政策(禁止核武裝或核動力船艦進入其港口,此舉一度使其與美國的紐澳美安全條約〔ANZUS〕關係緊張多年),並與澳洲(透過「更緊密經濟關係協定」)及五眼聯盟情報夥伴維持密切的經濟與國防合作關係。
  • 現任領導人:總理克里斯多福・拉克森(Christopher Luxon),中間偏右的國家黨(National Party)黨魁,自 2023 年 11 月起領導由自由意志派的行動黨(ACT Party)與溫斯頓・彼得斯(Winston Peters)領導的紐西蘭優先黨(New Zealand First)組成的三黨聯合政府;查爾斯三世國王仍為紐西蘭名義上的國家元首,在國內由總督代表行使職權。依紐西蘭標準三年一屆的選舉週期,下一次大選預計於 2026 年稍晚舉行。
  • 語言:英語是日常生活中最主要使用的語言,另有兩種官方語言:毛利語(Te Reo Māori,原住民玻里尼西亞語言,歷經歷史上的壓抑後,如今正持續推動語言復振工作)以及紐西蘭手語——使紐西蘭成為少數賦予手語完整官方語言地位的國家之一。

6)網路迷因介紹——星際大戰小子(Star Wars Kid)

  • 名稱與起源:2002 年 11 月,來自加拿大魁北克省三河市(Trois-Rivières)、時年 14 歲的高中生吉斯蘭・拉扎(Ghyslain Raza),獨自一人在學校的攝影棚教室裡,拿著一支高爾夫球撿球器當作光劍揮舞,模仿《星際大戰首部曲:威脅潛伏》中達斯・魔(Darth Maul)的打鬥招式,並將過程錄影下來。拉扎原本從未打算讓任何人看到這捲錄影帶;他不慎將錄影帶遺留在學校,被同學發現後數位化,並開始在學生之間流傳,最終被人以「Jackass_starwars_funny.wmv」的檔名上傳到網路上。
  • 如何走紅:這支影片在 2003 年初,透過點對點(P2P)檔案分享網路迅速擴散,當時消費者等級的影片分享仍屬新奇事物——距離 YouTube 成立(2005 年)還有好幾年——使其成為現代意義上「病毒式影片」最早期的案例之一,也是使用者自製影音內容首度大規模、自發性在網路上瘋傳的先例之一。
  • 意義與使用情境:與本系列大多數條目不同,這個迷因的核心「意義」與其造成的傷害密不可分:影片突如其來、未經同意的曝光,讓當時仍是未成年人的拉扎,承受了長期且高強度的大規模嘲弄與網路霸凌,他本人後來形容那段時期經歷了嚴重的心理創傷,一度輟學並接受精神科治療。他後來被廣泛視為最早、也最具代表性的紀錄案例之一,說明一名普通人的生活,如何因非自願的網路瘋傳而徹底翻轉——這個案例也經常被引用於網路霸凌、當事人同意權,以及未經同意散播可辨識個人影像等倫理議題的討論中,與本例行公事昨日介紹的「床上入侵者之歌/安東尼・多德森」條目,主題上遙相呼應。
  • 變體與演進:原始影片後來被大量混音重製,加上光劍音效、配樂,並用電腦動畫將高爾夫球撿球器改成發光劍刃,這類格式實驗,部分網路文化史學者認為正是今日「混音再創作」迷因慣例的早期先驅;「星際大戰小子」一詞後來也進入日常網路用語,泛指任何一個人尷尬的私人時刻違反本人意願而爆紅的情況。
  • 現況:如今已成年、並取得法律學位的拉扎,近年來公開發聲(包括在加拿大各大學演講及接受媒體專訪),談論網路霸凌對他造成的長期影響,以及他如何一步步奪回自己人生敘事主導權的歷程;這個長期以來被單純視為喜劇素材的迷因,如今更常被引用,作為網路倫理與數位身心健康討論中一則具有奠基意義的警世案例。

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《寄生上流》(기생충,Parasite,2019,導演:奉俊昊)

  • 創作者與時代背景:由奉俊昊執導,並與韓進元(Han Jin-won)共同編劇,於 2019 年 5 月在坎城影展首映後,於南韓正式上映;本片是奉俊昊融合多重類型的創作生涯最新代表作,此前已有《殺人回憶》、《駭人怪物》、《末日列車》、《玉子》等作品。
  • 劇情概述:住在狹窄半地下室公寓、生活貧困的金家一家人,一個接一個透過設局,分別以英文家教、藝術治療師、司機、管家的身分,混入富裕的朴家擔任雇工,各自偽裝成互不相識、資歷優異的專業人士,並悄悄設計讓朴家原有的雇工遭到解僱,好挪出空缺。當金家一家人發現,朴家前任管家的丈夫,竟已在這棟房子地下的一處隱密地下室裡,神不知鬼不覺地躲藏多年後,整個計畫開始失控,一連串的欺瞞、暴力與階級衝突不斷升級,最終在朴家年幼兒子的生日花園派對上徹底爆發。
  • 藝術風格與技法:本片刻意在劇情進行到一半時,大幅轉換類型基調——開場是一齣詼諧機智的騙徒喜劇,接著在一個精準的轉折點(前任管家深夜返回按下門鈴)之後,急轉直下變為心理驚悚片,最終演變為充滿暴力的悲劇——這個結構性的類型轉折,經常被電影學校的分析課程引用討論。美術指導李夏俊為本片量身打造了朴家的房子,刻意運用空間設計來編碼階級位階:富裕的朴家住在需拾階而上、採光明亮的現代主義樓房中,而金家的住所則是一間會在暴雨中淹水的半地下室(banjiha)——同一場暴雨,對富裕家庭而言,不過是搭帳篷露營的浪漫藉口,對金家而言卻是滅頂之災——同一個天氣事件,在同一則故事裡,承載著兩種完全相反的階級意涵。
  • 文化與歷史意義:《寄生上流》在 2019 年坎城影展,由評審團主席艾利謝多・崗札雷・伊納利圖(Alejandro González Iñárritu)領軍的評審團一致通過,榮獲金棕櫚獎;隨後在 2020 年奧斯卡頒獎典禮上,成為奧斯卡史上第一部榮獲最佳影片獎的非英語電影,一舉囊括最佳影片、最佳導演、最佳原創劇本、最佳國際影片共四項大獎。
  • 影響與傳承:本片與其後《魷魚遊戲》等南韓影劇作品在全球的成功,共同被視為將「韓流」(Hallyu)文化輸出,從音樂與電視劇進一步鞏固延伸至高規格藝術電影領域的關鍵指標;其劇情中段的類型轉折手法,以及透過垂直建築設計編碼階級意涵的空間敘事手法,如今已是全球電影分析課程的標準教材案例,與今日第 8 項單元「多重觀點敘事結構」的課程主題直接相關。
  • 今日可觀賞管道:目前廣泛可於各串流平台觀看(依地區與授權時效而異),並有 Criterion Collection 發行的家用影碟版本;至今仍是全球各地回顧展與經典重映影展的常客片單。

8)看片方法——多重/矛盾的主觀敘事(「羅生門效應」)(第 52 課)

與第 51 課的「框架敘事/嵌套敘事層」(關注一部電影嵌套了多少層敘事,而不論任一層的內容是否受到質疑)以及第 6 課的「敘事結構」(處理單一、各方公認無爭議的一組事件如何以非線性順序呈現)皆截然不同的全新分析角度:這項技法探討的是,一部電影是否針對同一起單一事件,透過不同角色各自目睹或親身參與該事件後,彼此矛盾、各懷立場、甚至根本互不相容的主觀敘述版本,重複呈現不只一次——更關鍵的是,電影最終是否會裁定哪一個版本才是「真相」,還是刻意懸而不決、藉此傳達關於記憶不可靠、自身利益考量,或觀點本身根本不可靠的論點。判斷重點包括:電影一共安排了幾種不同版本的重述;視覺風格(燈光、走位、構圖)是否隨敘述者不同而改變,藉此標記每位敘述者對同一個物理空間各自不同的心理色彩;版本之間的差異只是細微枝節,還是根本無法調和的直接矛盾;以及電影最終是否真的提供了一個穩定的「客觀」基準版本,或是刻意讓觀眾永遠得不到這樣的答案。

實例演練:《羅生門》(1950,導演:黑澤明)——這項技法的命名正是源自本片,全片架構圍繞著四段完全互不相容的目擊者/當事人證詞(一名盜賊、一名武士的妻子、透過靈媒代為發言的死去武士本人,以及一名樵夫),敘述森林中同一起強暴與殺人事件,各自在公堂上以回憶倒敘方式陳述,並在外層框架中,由這名樵夫與一位在殘破城門下躲雨的僧侶再次轉述。黑澤明為每個版本,分別採用了微妙不同的視覺與表演調性拍攝(盜賊的版本,將自己塑造成趾高氣昂、無所畏懼的勇士;妻子的版本,將自己改寫成暈厥無助、飽受欺凌的受害者;武士透過通靈超自然方式陳述的版本,則將自己的死亡定調為光榮的自盡)——同一片森林空地、同一批物證,卻在每個版本中被重新搬演,彼此之間存在著恰到好處、足以讓觀眾無法簡單「取平均值」拼湊出單一真相的具體矛盾(一把匕首的確切下落、究竟是誰真正給出了致命一擊)。關鍵在於,黑澤明的鏡頭本身,從未採取任何第五種「客觀」視角來一錘定音地解決這起懸案——就連表面上最有資格知曉真相、身處外層框架的敘述者樵夫,後來也被揭露曾因隱瞞自己涉入的角色而說謊,徹底打消了觀眾想把他的說法當作其餘三個版本所偏離的「中立基準」的念頭。本片流傳至今最深遠的影響,正是「羅生門效應」這個詞彙本身——如今廣泛應用於法律、心理學與新聞學領域,用來描述任何一種情況:多位皆屬可信的目擊者,針對同一事件,卻各自提出根本無法調和一致的說法。
