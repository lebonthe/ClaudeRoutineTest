3) Spanish Lesson — Day 36 (10 min, builds directly on Day 35)

Day 35 previewed, but only briefly, the "Si + imperfect subjunctive, + conditional" sentence pattern used to talk about things contrary to present fact. Today formalizes it as Type 2 conditional sentences (contrary-to-present-fact "if" sentences): the si-clause always takes the imperfect subjunctive taught yesterday (Day 35), and the result clause always takes the conditional simple taught back on Day 24 — never the other way around, and never the present subjunctive or future tense in either clause. The two clauses can appear in either order; a comma is used only when the si-clause comes first. This pattern is reserved specifically for hypothetical or counterfactual present situations (I don't have a fan, I'm not the boss, she didn't ask), sitting in contrast to the "real/likely" Type 1 conditional (si + present indicative + present, future, or imperative) which students will meet in a future lesson.

  • Si tuviera más tiempo, aprendería a tocar el piano. — If I had more time, I would learn to play the piano. (I don't have more time)
  • Si fueras el jefe, ¿qué cambiarías primero? — If you were the boss, what would you change first? (you aren't the boss)
  • Ella hablaría contigo si tú se lo pidieras. — She would talk to you if you asked her to. (result clause first, no comma; you haven't asked)

Grammar note: the si-clause is never conditional and never present subjunctive — only imperfect subjunctive belongs there in this pattern (si tuviera, not si tendría or si tenga); the result clause is never subjunctive — only the conditional simple belongs there (aprendería, not aprendiera). Getting the two tenses swapped between clauses is the single most common learner error with this structure.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 36: 明夷 (Míng Yí) — "Darkening of the Light" (Earth above, Fire below — Kūn over Lí). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary pivots directly off yesterday's Hexagram 35: "進必有所傷,故受之以明夷。夷者,傷也。" ("Advance must inevitably bring about some injury; hence Jìn [Progress] is followed by Míng Yí [Darkening of the Light]. Yí means injury.") Where Hexagram 35 pictured the sun climbing ever higher above the earth, spreading its light without limit, Hexagram 36 is its structural mirror image and cautionary correction: the same light now sinks below the earth's surface and is wounded or obscured. The Judgment is spare and severe: "明夷,利艱貞。" ("Míng Yí: favorable to remain steadfast amid hardship.") The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) supplies the reasoning together with two illustrative historical sages: "明入地中,明夷。內文明而外柔順,以蒙大難,文王以之。利艱貞,晦其明也。內難而能正其志,箕子以之。" ("The light has entered into the earth: this is Míng Yí. Inwardly there is brilliance, outwardly there is gentle compliance, and by this one can withstand great hardship — King Wen did so. 'Favorable to remain steadfast amid hardship' means to dim one's own light. Amid inner difficulty yet still able to keep one's resolve correct — the Viscount of Ji did so.") Both figures invoked lived under the tyrannical last king of the Shang dynasty: King Wen concealed his own ambition and endured years of unjust imprisonment without ever revealing his true resolve, while Jizi, a loyal minister, is traditionally said to have feigned madness to survive a court that punished honest counsel with death — both men deliberately dimming their outward light precisely in order to protect the inward flame. The hexagram's structure enacts this directly: Kūn (Earth, docile receptivity) sits above Lí (Fire/Light, inner clarity) — light buried beneath the surface rather than rising freely above it, an image of brilliance forced into concealment by a hostile, oppressive environment. The Image commentary draws the lesson outward, for anyone who must govern or guide others under difficult conditions: "明入地中,明夷;君子以蒞眾,用晦而明。" ("The light has entered into the earth: this is the image of Míng Yí. Thus the superior person, in overseeing the multitude, uses an appearance of obscurity while remaining inwardly clear-sighted.") Read together with yesterday's Jìn, the pairing frames a complete cycle: light that rises and is openly rewarded (35) can be followed, without warning, by light that must retreat and protect itself (36) — genuine integrity, the hexagram insists, is proven not by how brightly one shines in favorable times, but by how much inner clarity survives intact through times when shining openly would be dangerous.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Liechtenstein (列支敦斯登)

  • Location: A microstate in the Alps of Central Europe, sitting along the Rhine river valley between Switzerland (to its west and south) and Austria (to its east) — one of only two "doubly landlocked" countries in the world (a landlocked country entirely surrounded by other landlocked countries), the other being Uzbekistan.
  • Territory: About 160 km² — one of the smallest sovereign states on Earth, only a little larger than Washington, D.C. (~177 km²) and roughly the same size as the city of San Francisco; just 0.44% of Taiwan's land area (~36,193 km²), meaning Taiwan is over 225 times larger.
  • Population: Approximately 40,000.
  • Religion: Predominantly Roman Catholic (around 70-75%), which remained the constitutionally established state religion until a 2012 amendment ended that official status; a notable Protestant minority and a growing share of non-religious and other-faith residents make up the rest, reflecting a significant foreign-born workforce.
  • Economy: One of the highest GDP-per-capita figures in the world, built on an unusual mix of high-end precision manufacturing (Hilti power tools and Ivoclar Vivadent dental products are both headquartered there) and international financial services — historically a byword for banking secrecy and favorable tax treatment, though it substantially reformed its banking-transparency rules after 2008-era OECD and EU pressure. More companies are registered in Liechtenstein than it has citizens.
  • History: Formed in 1719 when Holy Roman Emperor Charles VI united the lordship of Schellenberg and the county of Vaduz into the Principality of Liechtenstein, purchased outright by the wealthy Austrian House of Liechtenstein — a family that reportedly did not set foot in the territory for over a century after buying it. It became fully sovereign in 1806 with the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire, later joined the German Confederation until 1866, adopted permanent neutrality after the abolition of its own army in 1868, and entered a currency and customs union with Switzerland in the 1920s (it still uses the Swiss franc today) — a partnership that let it remain neutral and unoccupied through both World Wars.
  • Current leader: Prince Hans-Adam II has been reigning monarch and head of state since 1989, though since 2004 he has delegated almost all day-to-day governing authority to his son and heir, Hereditary Prince Alois, who serves as Prince Regent. Day-to-day government is headed by Prime Minister Brigitte Haas, who took office in 2025 as Liechtenstein's first woman to hold the post.
  • Language: German is the sole official language, spoken locally in a distinct Alemannic dialect.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Surprised Pikachu"

  • Name: "Surprised Pikachu" (also called "Surprised Pikachu Face" or "Shocked Pikachu").
  • Origin/history: The image is a screenshot from the very first season of the Pokémon anime — Episode 10, "Bulbasaur and the Hidden Village," which originally aired June 3, 1997. In the scene, a wild Bulbasaur breathes in Ash's Butterfree's sleep powder attack and blows it straight back, knocking Butterfree out with its own move; Pikachu, visible in the corner of the frame reacting to this twist, pulls a wide-eyed, slack-jawed expression of shock.
  • How it became popular: The still frame sat unremarked for over two decades until a Tumblr post on September 26, 2018 repurposed it as a reaction image; within weeks it exploded across Twitter and Reddit as the punchline image for jokes built around a setup that leads to an entirely foreseeable outcome that a character (or the poster) nevertheless reacts to with exaggerated shock.
  • Meaning/usage: Used to mock predictable-but-denied cause and effect — the classic format is a short setup ("quits job in a huff" / "gets fired for showing up late every day"), a foreseeable consequence, and then the Surprised Pikachu image standing in for someone's theatrically overdone shock at a result they should have seen coming; it has become general-purpose internet shorthand for "well, obviously" across sports commentary, politics, and workplace humor alike.
  • Variations/evolution: Endless four-panel "setup → predictable consequence → surprised Pikachu" templates across every subject imaginable; recolored and cropped versions of the still image; crossover edits substituting other characters' faces into the same pose while keeping the caption format intact; also widely used as a standalone reaction sticker/GIF on Discord, Twitter, and messaging apps without any accompanying joke template.
  • Current status: Still in vigorous everyday use as of 2026 — one of the more durable "evergreen" reaction-image formats to emerge from the 2018 meme cycle, deployed as a live, current-events reaction rather than only nostalgically, nearly thirty years after the original animation aired.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, screenrant.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Taiwanese Opera (歌仔戲, Gezaixi / Koa-á-hì)

  • Name/key figures: Gezaixi (歌仔戲, Mandarin) or Koa-á-hì (Taiwanese Hokkien) — literally "song-drama" — is not attributed to a single creator; it coalesced organically in the Yilan (宜蘭) region of northeastern Taiwan in the early 20th century, and its modern popularization is closely associated with mid-20th-century television troupes, most famously that of star performer Yang Li-hua (楊麗花) and, later, Ye Ching (葉青) and the still-active Ming Hwa Yuan (明華園) troupe.
  • Era/background: It emerged in the 1900s-1920s under Japanese colonial rule (1895-1945), evolving out of local Hokkien-language "gezai" folk-ballad singing traditions brought over by earlier Han Chinese settlers from Fujian, absorbing performance conventions from Chinese opera forms such as Beiguan (北管) theatre along the way; it survived Japan's late-1930s-40s "kōminka" assimilation campaign, which suppressed Chinese-language theatre, and was revived after 1945.
  • Plot/content overview: Stories are typically drawn from Chinese historical romances, folk legends, scholar-and-beauty (才子佳人) romances, and martial/loyalty tales, performed through stock role-types broadly analogous to Chinese opera's sheng/dan/jing/chou categories, usually centered on loyalty, filial piety, romantic longing, and the triumph of moral justice.
  • Artistic style and techniques: Sung in vernacular Taiwanese Hokkien (not Mandarin) using signature melodic modes such as "七字調" (Qizi Diao, the "seven-character tune") and "都馬調" (Dou Ma Diao); blends spoken dialogue, sung arias, and stylized gesture and movement adapted from Peking-opera-style conventions, with elaborately embroidered costumes and, in modern staged productions, ambitious lighting and stage effects. Its use of vernacular Hokkien and folk-ballad-rooted melody, rather than Mandarin and the more codified conventions of Peking opera, is its clearest artistic fingerprint.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as the only major traditional Chinese-language opera form to have originated within Taiwan itself rather than being imported from the mainland, making it a core marker of distinctly Taiwanese cultural identity — a significance sharpened by 20th-century political tensions over Taiwanese versus mainland-Chinese identity. Its survival through Japanese colonial-era suppression, and later through Kuomintang-era Mandarin-only broadcast policy that discouraged Hokkien-language media, marks it as a case of grassroots-level linguistic and cultural resilience.
  • Influence and legacy: Television gezaixi in the 1960s-80s — especially the Yang Li-hua and Ye Ching troupes' productions — became some of the most-watched programming in Taiwanese broadcast history, helping keep spoken Taiwanese Hokkien culturally vital during a period of Mandarin-only policy; it continues to shape Taiwanese popular culture and has inspired modernized hybrid stagings, notably the Ming Hwa Yuan troupe's elaborate lighting- and pyrotechnic-driven productions.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Still actively performed today, both as outdoor temple-fair performances (廟會外台戲) common during temple deities' birthday festivals, and as professional indoor theatre by troupes such as Ming Hwa Yuan; Taiwan's National Center for Traditional Arts (國立傳統藝術中心) in Yilan is dedicated to preserving and promoting the form, and it remains a regular presence in Taiwanese television specials and government-supported cultural-heritage programming.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, ncfta.gov.tw (National Center for Traditional Arts)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Production Design & Art Direction: World-Building Through Sets and Space (Lesson 22)

Production design (sometimes split into the broader "art direction") is the discipline of designing the built physical world a film's story takes place in — sets, locations, architecture, and props — and it is distinct from three lessons already taught in this series: it is not costume (Day 12's lesson on clothing characters), not cinematography or colour grading (how light and colour are captured, not what physical objects exist to be lit), and not the broader umbrella of mise-en-scène (Day 1's lesson, which covers everything staged in frame together). Three things to look for: first, verticality and scale as power hierarchy — who is housed in monumental, soaring spaces and who is confined to cramped or low-lying ones tells an audience exactly where power sits before a single line of dialogue explains it; second, cultural bricolage — an invented world's culture or technology is rarely built from nothing, but from blending recognizable real-world reference points together in new combinations, letting audiences intuitively "read" an invented society's history through familiar fragments; third, texture and decay as unspoken backstory — whether a space looks pristine, retrofitted, worn down, or outright abandoned communicates a world's economic and political history without a line of dialogue ever stating it.

Worked example: Blade Runner (1982, dir. Ridley Scott; production design by Lawrence G. Paull, concept design by Syd Mead) — the opening flying-car descent into the Los Angeles cityscape. The towering, pyramid-like Tyrell Corporation headquarters looms high above the city, its monumental, Mayan-revival-inflected architecture bathed in warm, isolated light — while the street level far below is a chaotic bricolage of neon signage in mixed Japanese, Chinese, and English script, hissing steam vents, and umbrella-carrying crowds moving through perpetual rain and grime. The sheer vertical distance the camera travels between these two levels of production design establishes the film's entire class-stratified dystopian society before a word of dialogue explains it — and Syd Mead's "retrofitted future" design philosophy (old structures patched over with new technology rather than replaced outright) tells the audience this is a world defined by economic decline layered underneath technological advance, not a clean utopia.

3)西班牙文課程 — 第 36 天(10 分鐘,直接承接第 35 天)

第 35 天曾經稍微預告過「Si + 虛擬式過去未完了式,+ 條件式簡單式」這種用來談論「與現在事實相反」情況的句型,今天要正式將它系統化,定名為「第二類條件句」(與現在事實相反的假設句):Si 子句一律使用昨天(第 35 天)教過的虛擬式過去未完了式,而結果子句則一律使用第 24 天教過的條件式簡單式——順序不能顛倒,而且兩個子句都絕對不能使用現在式虛擬式或未來式。兩個子句的先後順序可以互換;只有當 Si 子句放在句子前半時,才需要用逗號隔開。這個句型專門用來表達假設性、或與現在事實相反的情境(我沒有扇子、你不是老闆、她並沒有拜託),與「真實/有可能發生」的第一類條件句(Si + 直陳式現在式 + 現在式、未來式或命令式)形成對比,第一類條件句將留待未來課程介紹。

  • Si tuviera más tiempo, aprendería a tocar el piano. — 如果我有更多時間,我會去學彈鋼琴。(其實我沒有更多時間)
  • Si fueras el jefe, ¿qué cambiarías primero? — 如果你是老闆,你會最先改變什麼?(你其實不是老闆)
  • Ella hablaría contigo si tú se lo pidieras. — 如果你開口拜託她,她會願意跟你談。(結果子句在前,不加逗號;你其實還沒開口拜託)

文法筆記:Si 子句絕對不能用條件式,也不能用現在式虛擬式——這個句型裡 Si 子句的位置只能放虛擬式過去未完了式(si tuviera,而不是 si tendría 或 si tenga);結果子句則絕對不能用虛擬式——這個位置只能放條件式簡單式(aprendería,而不是 aprendiera)。把這兩個子句該用的時態搞反,是這個句型初學者最常犯的錯誤,沒有之一。

4)易經卦象

第 36 卦:明夷 (Míng Yí) ——「光明受損、隱晦」(坤上離下,地在上、火在下)。〈序卦傳〉直接承接昨天的第 35 卦而來:「進必有所傷,故受之以明夷。夷者,傷也。」(前進必然會帶來某種損傷,所以〈晉〉之後接著是〈明夷〉。夷,就是損傷的意思。)昨天的晉卦描繪太陽不斷升高、光芒無限擴散的景象;明夷卦則正好是它結構上的鏡像與警示性修正:同一道光明如今沉入地平線之下,受到傷害或遮蔽。〈卦辭〉簡短而嚴峻:「明夷,利艱貞。」(明夷卦:在艱難處境中,適宜堅守正道、保持節操。)〈彖傳〉進一步說明其中道理,並舉出兩位歷史賢者為例:「明入地中,明夷。內文明而外柔順,以蒙大難,文王以之。利艱貞,晦其明也。內難而能正其志,箕子以之。」(光明沉入地中,這就是明夷卦。內在保有文采光明,外表卻表現柔順恭謹,因此得以承受重大的患難——周文王就是這樣做的。「利艱貞」指的是刻意收斂、隱藏自己的光明。身處內部的患難之中,卻仍能堅守自己端正的心志——箕子就是這樣做的。)這兩位被引用的人物,都曾身處商紂王暴政之下:周文王隱藏自己的雄心壯志,在遭受多年不公不義的囚禁期間,始終沒有洩露自己真正的意志;而身為忠臣的箕子,傳統上則被認為是靠著佯裝瘋癲,才得以在一個以死刑懲罰直言進諫的朝廷中倖存下來——兩人都刻意收斂自己對外顯露的光芒,正是為了保全內在那一點不滅的火苗。這一卦的卦象結構直接呼應了這層意涵:坤卦(地,象徵柔順包容)覆蓋在離卦(火、光明,象徵內在的清明)之上——光明被埋藏於地表之下,而非自由地向上升起,描繪出一幅光明才華被迫隱藏於險惡、充滿壓迫的環境之中的畫面。〈象傳〉則將這層教訓,進一步推展到任何身處逆境、卻仍需治理眾人或引領他人的處境上:「明入地中,明夷;君子以蒞眾,用晦而明。」(光明沉入地中,這就是明夷卦的象。因此君子在治理眾人時,表面上運用隱晦不顯的姿態,內心卻仍保持清明洞察。)將這一卦與昨天的晉卦合看,兩者構成了一個完整的循環:光明上升、並公開獲得肯定與獎賞的階段(晉卦),隨時可能毫無預警地轉為光明必須退守、自我保護的階段(明夷卦)——這一卦想強調的是,真正的操守與正直,並非取決於順境時能發出多麼耀眼的光芒,而是取決於在那些「公開發光反而危險」的時刻裡,內在的清明究竟能完好無損地保存下多少。

5)國家/地區/勢力介紹 — 列支敦斯登(Liechtenstein)

  • 位置:列支敦斯登是位於中歐阿爾卑斯山區的一個袖珍小國,沿萊茵河谷地坐落於瑞士(西側與南側)與奧地利(東側)之間——是全世界僅有的兩個「雙重內陸國」之一(意指一個內陸國,其周邊鄰國本身也全部都是內陸國),另一個是烏茲別克。
  • 領土規模:國土總面積約 160 平方公里——是全球最小的主權國家之一,僅略大於美國首都華盛頓特區(約 177 平方公里),與美國舊金山市面積相當;僅約為台灣陸地面積(約 36,193 平方公里)的 0.44%,換算下來台灣的面積是它的 225 倍以上。
  • 人口:約 4 萬人。
  • 宗教:絕大多數信奉羅馬天主教(約 70-75%),天主教在憲法上曾是官方國教,直到 2012 年修憲後才終止此官方地位;其餘人口則包含相當比例的新教徒,以及逐漸增加的無宗教信仰與其他信仰人口,反映出當地相當可觀的外籍勞動人口組成。
  • 經濟:擁有全球數一數二的人均 GDP,產業結構是相當獨特的組合:高階精密製造業(喜利得〔Hilti〕電動工具與義獲嘉〔Ivoclar Vivadent〕牙科產品皆總部設於此)與國際金融服務業並重——歷史上該國一直是銀行保密制度與租稅優惠的代名詞,不過在 2008 年前後歷經經濟合作暨發展組織(OECD)與歐盟的壓力後,已大幅改革其銀行透明度規範。登記在列支敦斯登境內的公司數量,甚至超過該國的公民人數。
  • 歷史:1719 年,神聖羅馬帝國皇帝查理六世將謝倫堡領地(Schellenberg)與瓦杜茲伯爵領地(Vaduz)合併,建立列支敦斯登公國,並由富裕的奧地利貴族列支敦斯登家族(House of Liechtenstein)整體買下——據傳這個家族在買下這塊土地後,超過一百年間都未曾親自踏上這片土地。1806 年隨著神聖羅馬帝國解體,列支敦斯登取得完全主權地位;此後曾加入德意志邦聯直到 1866 年;1868 年廢除自己的軍隊後,採行永久中立政策;並於 1920 年代與瑞士簽訂貨幣與關稅同盟協議(至今仍使用瑞士法郎)——這層夥伴關係,讓它得以在兩次世界大戰期間都維持中立、未遭占領。
  • 現任領導人:漢斯-亞當二世親王(Prince Hans-Adam II)自 1989 年起即為在位君主與國家元首,但自 2004 年起,他已將幾乎所有日常執政權力,授權給他的兒子暨王位繼承人、世子阿洛伊斯親王(Hereditary Prince Alois)以「攝政親王」(Prince Regent)身分代為執行。日常政府事務則由 2025 年就任的總理布莉姬特·哈斯(Brigitte Haas)領導,她是列支敦斯登歷史上首位女性總理。
  • 語言:德語為唯一官方語言,當地慣用一種獨特的阿勒曼尼方言(Alemannic)。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、en.wikipedia.org

6)網路迷因介紹 —「驚訝皮卡丘」(Surprised Pikachu)

  • 名稱:「Surprised Pikachu」(驚訝皮卡丘),又稱「Surprised Pikachu Face」或「Shocked Pikachu」。
  • 起源/歷史:這張圖片截自《寶可夢》動畫第一季第 10 集《妙蛙種子與隱藏村莊》(Bulbasaur and the Hidden Village),原始首播日期為 1997 年 6 月 3 日。劇情中,一隻野生妙蛙種子吸入了小智的巴大蝶所使出的「睡粉」招式,並直接將睡粉反吹回去,結果巴大蝶反被自己這招擊昏;畫面角落的皮卡丘目睹這個逆轉場面,露出瞪大雙眼、下巴驚訝掉落的表情。
  • 如何走紅:這一幕靜止畫面在動畫首播超過二十年後,才於 2018 年 9 月 26 日被一則 Tumblr 貼文重新挖出,當作反應圖使用;短短數週內便在 Twitter 與 Reddit 上爆紅,成為一種固定笑點格式的代表圖像——用來搭配「明明有跡可循、結果卻還是表現出誇張驚訝反應」這類劇情鋪陳的哏圖。
  • 意義/使用情境:用來嘲諷「明明是可預期的因果關係,當事人卻裝作毫不知情」的情境——經典格式是先鋪陳一個小故事(例如「一氣之下辭職」或「每天遲到結果被開除」),接著呈現顯而易見的後果,再放上「驚訝皮卡丘」這張圖,代表某人對一個理應早就預料到的結果,表現出誇張到近乎做作的震驚;如今已成為社群網路上通用的萬用哏,廣泛應用於體育評論、政治議題與職場幽默等各種情境,意思近似「這不是廢話嗎」。
  • 變體/演變:衍生出無數套用各種主題的「鋪陳 → 可預期後果 → 驚訝皮卡丘」四格漫畫模板;原始靜止畫面的各種重新上色與裁切版本;將其他角色的臉套用進同一個姿勢、但保留原本標題格式的混搭改圖;也常見完全不搭配任何笑話文字模板,單純作為 Discord、Twitter 與各類通訊軟體上的獨立反應貼圖或 GIF 使用。
  • 現況:截至 2026 年,這個哏圖仍在日常網路生活中被大量使用——是 2018 年迷因週期中少數幾個格外長壽、「常青」的反應圖格式之一,即便距離原始動畫首播已將近三十年,如今仍被當作即時、貼合時事的反應圖使用,而不只是懷舊性質的引用。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、screenrant.com

7)電影/表演藝術介紹 — 歌仔戲(Gezaixi / Koa-á-hì,台灣本土戲曲)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:歌仔戲(閩南語又稱 Koa-á-hì)——字面意思是「歌謠戲曲」——並非由單一創作者發明,而是在 20 世紀初於台灣東北部宜蘭地區自然孕育形成;其在現代的普及,則與 20 世紀中葉的電視歌仔戲劇團密切相關,其中又以巨星楊麗花的劇團最為知名,其後還有葉青,以及至今仍活躍演出的明華園劇團。
  • 時代背景:約於 1900 至 1920 年代,在日本殖民統治時期(1895-1945)發展成形,源自更早期由福建移居台灣的漢人所帶來的閩南語「歌仔」民謠說唱傳統,並沿途吸收了諸如北管戲曲等中國戲曲表演形式的元素;歌仔戲一度撐過了日本在 1930 年代末至 1940 年代推行的「皇民化運動」(該運動壓制漢語戲曲演出),並於 1945 年之後重新復甦。
  • 劇情概述:劇情題材通常取自中國歷史演義、民間傳說、才子佳人式的愛情故事,以及俠義忠孝故事,以近似中國戲曲生、旦、淨、丑等固定角色行當的方式演出,主題大多圍繞忠誠、孝道、兒女情長,以及善惡有報、正義終將伸張等核心價值。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:以台灣閩南語(而非國語)演唱,使用「七字調」與「都馬調」等代表性曲調;融合口白對話、演唱曲目,以及借鑑自京劇風格的程式化身段與動作,搭配色彩繽紛、刺繡精美的戲服,現代舞台製作更加入了精心設計的燈光與舞台特效。它最鮮明的藝術印記,正是使用在地閩南語演唱、並以紮根於民間歌謠的曲調為主,而非國語演出、也不採用京劇那套較為程式化、貴族氣息濃厚的表演成規。
  • 文化/歷史意義:普遍被認為是唯一一種真正起源於台灣本土、而非從中國大陸傳入的重要漢語戲曲劇種,因此成為台灣本土文化認同的核心象徵之一——這層意義在 20 世紀圍繞「台灣認同」與「中國認同」的政治張力下更顯突出。歌仔戲撐過日本殖民時期的壓制,以及後來國民政府時期「國語運動」對閩南語廣播的限制,足以說明它是一種扎根於庶民階層、韌性十足的語言與文化存續案例。
  • 影響與傳承:1960 至 1980 年代的電視歌仔戲——尤其是楊麗花與葉青兩大劇團的製作——曾是台灣廣播電視史上收視率最高的節目之一,在「國語運動」盛行的年代裡,協助維繫了台灣閩南語在文化上的活力;歌仔戲至今仍持續形塑台灣的流行文化,並啟發了諸如明華園劇團融合絢麗燈光與火藥特效的現代化混合式舞台製作。
  • 現況/觀賞方式:歌仔戲至今仍活躍地演出著,無論是常見於各地廟宇神明聖誕慶典的「廟會外台戲」,或是由明華園等劇團帶來的專業室內劇場演出,都仍持續進行;位於宜蘭的「國立傳統藝術中心」則致力於保存與推廣這項傳統藝術,而歌仔戲也仍定期出現在台灣的電視特別節目與政府支持的文化資產推廣活動之中。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、ncfta.gov.tw(國立傳統藝術中心)

8)電影賞析方法 — 美術設計與世界構築:透過場景與空間說故事(第 22 課)

美術設計(production design,有時再細分出「藝術指導」art direction 一詞)是指設計電影故事所在的「實體建構世界」——包括場景、拍攝地、建築與道具——這個分析角度,與本系列已經教過的三堂課有明顯區別:它不是服裝(第 12 課教的角色穿著)、不是攝影與調色(關注的是光線與色彩如何被拍攝下來,而不是畫面裡究竟存在哪些實體物件供光線照射)、也不是更廣義的「場面調度」(第 1 課教的概念,涵蓋畫面中所有共同呈現的元素)。分析時要留意三件事:第一是「垂直落差與規模,作為權力階層的象徵」——誰住在宏偉、高聳入雲的空間裡,誰又被侷限在低矮擁擠的空間裡,這件事本身就已經在任何台詞說明之前,明確告訴觀眾權力究竟掌握在誰手上;第二是「文化拼貼」——一個虛構世界的文化或科技,很少是憑空創造出來的,而是將現實世界中觀眾熟悉的參照物,以新的方式重新組合拼接而成,讓觀眾能夠憑直覺「讀懂」這個虛構社會背後的歷史脈絡;第三是「材質與斑駁,作為未曾言明的背景故事」——一個空間看起來是嶄新光鮮、拼裝改造、飽經風霜,還是徹底遭到遺棄,這些細節本身就足以傳達出這個世界的經濟與政治歷史,完全不需要靠任何一句台詞說明。

實例演練:《銀翼殺手》(Blade Runner,1982,導演:雷利·史考特 Ridley Scott;美術設計:勞倫斯·G·保羅 Lawrence G. Paull;概念設計:席德·米德 Syd Mead)——開場飛天車緩緩降入洛杉磯城市景觀的畫面。高聳如金字塔般的泰瑞公司(Tyrell Corporation)總部矗立於城市上空,其帶有馬雅復興風格色彩的宏偉建築,沐浴在一片溫暖而孤立的光線之中;而遙遠的街道地面層,則是一片由混雜著日文、中文與英文招牌的霓虹燈光、嘶嘶作響的蒸氣排放口,以及在永無止盡的雨水與髒污中撐傘穿行的人群所拼貼而成的混亂景象。攝影機在這兩層美術設計之間所跨越的巨大垂直落差,早在任何一句台詞說明之前,就已經建立起這部電影整個階級分明的反烏托邦社會樣貌——而席德·米德「拼裝改造式未來」(retrofitted future,舊建築被新科技拼裝改造、而非直接汰換重建)的設計理念,則向觀眾傳達出:這是一個經濟持續衰敗、卻又疊加著科技持續進步的世界,而不是一個乾淨整潔的烏托邦未來。
