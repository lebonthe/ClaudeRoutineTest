3) Spanish Lesson — Day 19 (10 min, builds on Day 18)

Day 18 covered the "u-stem" irregular preterites (tener, estar, poder). Today introduces a sibling irregular family that shares similar logic but with one extra twist: the "j-stem" preterites — decir (to say/tell), traer (to bring), and conducir (to drive) — whose stems end in the consonant j, which swallows the "i" of the regular -ieron ending.

  • ¿Qué te dijo el jefe? (keh teh dee-HOH el HEH-feh) — What did the boss tell you?
  • Me dijo que trajera los documentos mañana. (meh dee-HOH keh trah-HEH-rah lohs doh-koo-MEHN-tohs mah-NYAH-nah) — He told me to bring the documents tomorrow.
  • ¿Trajiste el cargador? (trah-HEES-teh el kar-gah-DOHR) — Did you bring the charger?
  • Sí, y también conduje toda la noche para llegar a tiempo. (see, ee tahm-bee-EHN kohn-DOO-heh TOH-dah lah NOH-cheh PAH-rah yeh-GAHR ah tee-EHM-poh) — Yes, and I also drove all night to arrive on time.

Grammar note: decir→dij-, traer→traj-, and conducir (and every other -ducir verb: traducir, producir, reducir...)→conduj- all take the same endings as the u-stem family (-e, -iste, -o, -imos) — except in the ellos/ellas/ustedes form, where the "j" swallows the expected "i": dijeron, trajeron, condujeron — not "dijieron." This is the one true exception to the -ieron ending taught on Day 16, and it applies to every preterite stem ending in "j."

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 19: 臨 (Lín) — "Approach" (Earth over Lake). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary bridges directly from yesterday's hexagram: "蠱者事也,有事而後可大,故受之以臨,臨者大也" — Gǔ (Hexagram 18, "Work on What Has Been Spoiled") means having concrete matters to attend to; only once decay has been actively remedied can something grow truly great, hence Lín ("Approach," carrying the sense of "greatness" or "growing great") follows Gǔ. The trigram image is a lake beneath open earth — water rising to meet, or the earth drawing near and overlooking the lake below — evoking the image of a superior approaching, drawing near to, or overseeing what lies beneath. The Judgment reads: "臨:元亨,利貞。至于八月有凶。" ("APPROACH has supreme success. Perseverance furthers. When the eighth month comes, there will be misfortune.") The Image adds: "澤上有地,臨;君子以教思无窮,容保民无疆。" ("The earth above the lake: the image of APPROACH. Thus the superior man is inexhaustible in his will to teach, and without limits in his tolerance and protection of the people.") Classical commentary reads the "eighth month" warning as a caution against complacency: influence and favorable circumstances that are currently waxing will inevitably wane in time, so the superior man is urged to use a period of ascending strength to teach and to nurture others without limit, rather than to grow careless while things are going well.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Singapore

  • Location: A city-state in Southeast Asia at the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula, separated from Malaysia by the narrow Straits of Johor to the north and facing Indonesia's Riau Islands across the Singapore Strait to the south.
  • Territory: About 734 km² today (continually growing through land reclamation from roughly 581 km² at independence in 1965) — smaller than New York City, and only about 1/50th the size of Taiwan (~36,200 km²), making it one of the smallest sovereign states by area despite being an economic and financial powerhouse.
  • Population: Roughly 6.04 million (2026), among the highest population densities in the world at over 8,000 people per km²; a multiethnic society of Chinese (~75%), Malay (~15%), and Indian (~7%) descent, plus a large expatriate workforce.
  • Religion: Highly diverse — Buddhism (~31%), Christianity (~19%), no religion (~20%), Islam (~15%), Taoism (~9%), and Hinduism (~5%), reflecting its multiethnic population and constitutionally secular governance.
  • Economy: One of the world's leading financial and trade hubs, with nominal GDP per capita among the highest globally; built on entrepôt trade, Changi Airport (a major regional aviation hub), oil refining, electronics manufacturing, and biotechnology, underpinned by sovereign wealth funds GIC and Temasek Holdings that invest the state's reserves globally.
  • History: Founded as a British trading post in 1819 by Sir Stamford Raffles; grew into a Crown colony and major regional port; occupied by Japan from 1942 to 1945; joined the Federation of Malaysia in 1963, then separated to become fully independent in 1965 under founding Prime Minister Lee Kuan Yew, who drove its rapid transformation into one of Asia's "Four Tigers" economies.
  • Current leader: Prime Minister Lawrence Wong, in office since May 15, 2024 (Singapore's fourth PM, succeeding Lee Hsien Loong), alongside President Tharman Shanmugaratnam, the ceremonial head of state since September 14, 2023.
  • Language: Four official languages — English (the main working language of government, business, and education), Malay (the ceremonial national language), Mandarin Chinese, and Tamil.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, pmo.gov.sg, britannica.com

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Bernie Sanders' Mittens

  • Name: "Bernie Sanders Sitting" / "Bernie's Mittens," widely just called the Bernie mittens meme.
  • Origin/history: Photographed at Joe Biden's US presidential inauguration on January 20, 2021, Vermont Senator Bernie Sanders sat alone, arms folded, wearing a bulky brown-patterned parka and handmade wool mittens (crafted from recycled sweaters by a Vermont schoolteacher, Jen Ellis) — a practical, unglamorous outfit chosen for a socially-distanced outdoor ceremony during the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • How it became popular: The photo went viral on social media within hours of the ceremony; internet users rapidly photoshopped the seated, mitten-clad Sanders into countless unrelated scenes — famous paintings, movie stills, historical photographs, and everyday locations — turning it into one of the biggest viral image templates of 2021.
  • Meaning/usage: Represents someone appearing unbothered, out of place, or pragmatically unfussed amid a formal or elaborate occasion — the "not here for the pageantry" archetype, often used humorously to insert a deadpan, grumpy-but-relatable bystander into any scene.
  • Variations/evolution: Endless photoshop variations placed Bernie into everything from Renaissance paintings to red-carpet events to fictional universes; Sanders' own campaign later sold an official version of the image on sweatshirts, with proceeds donated to charities including Meals on Wheels, raising over $1.8 million.
  • Current status: Largely dormant as an active meme format today, but remembered as one of the defining viral images of the early 2020s and periodically resurfaces as nostalgic reference material.

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Bunraku (文樂)

  • Name: Bunraku (文楽), also known by its older name Ningyō Jōruri (人形浄瑠璃, "puppet jōruri").
  • Key figures/creators: Developed in Osaka in the late 17th century; playwright Chikamatsu Monzaemon (近松門左衛門, 1653–1725), often called "Japan's Shakespeare," wrote many of its most enduring works (including The Love Suicides at Sonezaki); chanter Takemoto Gidayū founded the Takemoto-za theatre in 1684, giving rise to the gidayū narrative-chanting style still used today.
  • Era/background: Flourished during the Edo period alongside Kabuki as popular entertainment for Osaka and Kyoto's merchant-class townspeople (chōnin).
  • Plot/content overview: Its repertoire spans jidaimono (historical/period pieces about samurai and political intrigue) and sewamono (domestic dramas about ordinary townspeople, frequently tragic love stories, including double-suicide plays).
  • Artistic style and techniques: Each puppet — roughly two-thirds to full human size — is manipulated in full view of the audience by three visible puppeteers: the omozukai (master puppeteer, who controls the head and right arm and appears unmasked in formal dress), the hidarizukai (left arm), and ashizukai (feet and legs), the latter two hooded in black (kurogo) to signal their intended "invisibility." A single narrator, the tayū, voices and chants every character's lines plus all narration, accompanied by the three-stringed shamisen.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Designated a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2003; deeply intertwined with Kabuki, which adapted many of its most famous plays directly from Bunraku scripts.
  • Influence and legacy: Its uncanny expressiveness — puppets that convey subtle emotion despite visible human operators — has influenced puppetry and theatre worldwide, including the visible-manipulator technique used in stage productions such as War Horse.
  • Current status: Still actively performed today, primarily at the National Bunraku Theatre in Osaka and the National Theatre in Tokyo, sustained by professionally trained performers even as audiences remain smaller than during its Edo-period peak.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, unesco.org, ntj.jac.go.jp

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Colour (Lesson 5)

Lesson 1 covered mise-en-scène, Lesson 2 editing, Lesson 3 cinematography and lighting, and Lesson 4 sound design and score. Today's lens is colour — one of the most consciously "designed" layers of a film, and one of the easiest to read once you know what to look for.

The method: Start by identifying the film's dominant palette (Is it warm or cool? Saturated or desaturated? Does one or two colours recur as a motif?), then ask what that palette is doing narratively rather than just decoratively. Look for moments where colour is used sparingly against an otherwise restrained or monochrome backdrop — a single accent colour functions like a spotlight, forcing the eye toward whatever it touches and often encoding symbolic meaning onto it. Track whether the palette shifts across the film's acts (a saturation drop signalling a character's decline, a warm-to-cool shift marking a turn in mood or fortune), and notice when a colour choice recurs later in the film as a deliberate callback, letting a single visual motif carry meaning across a long stretch of runtime without a word of dialogue.

Worked example — Schindler's List (1993, dir. Steven Spielberg; cinematography by Janusz Kamiński), the girl in the red coat: Spielberg shot nearly the entire film in black and white, evoking newsreel-like documentary authenticity appropriate to depicting the Holocaust. The one sustained exception is a small girl in a red coat, glimpsed wandering alone through the chaos of the Kraków ghetto liquidation — the single splash of colour in an otherwise monochrome sequence, drawing both Oskar Schindler's attention within the story and the audience's attention watching it, amid overwhelming, near-incomprehensible horror. Later in the film, the same red coat reappears — now in a black-and-white pile of clothing being removed from a mass grave, silently confirming the girl's fate without a line of dialogue, in the scene widely identified as the turning point of Schindler's own moral awakening.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Colour as spotlight — an isolated splash of colour against a desaturated or monochrome frame automatically draws the eye, making it an efficient tool for directing attention without a camera move or a cut. (2) Colour as symbolic compression — singling out one small girl in colour lets the film represent the incomprehensible scale of genocide through one legible, emotionally graspable figure, rather than through abstraction or statistics. (3) Colour as a long-distance callback — the coat's reappearance, unchanged in hue but radically changed in context, carries narrative information (the girl's death) purely through visual recognition across a real gap in screen time, with no dialogue required. This builds on Lessons 1-4: mise-en-scène is what's arranged in the frame, editing is how frames are sequenced, cinematography/lighting is what frames look like, sound/score is what a scene feels like beneath what you see, and colour is what a single visual detail can be made to mean.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第十九天(10 分鐘,銜接第十八天內容)

第十八天學了「u 字根」不規則過去式(tener、estar、poder)。今天要介紹的是邏輯相近、但多了一個小變化的另一組不規則家族——「j 字根」過去式:decir(說)、traer(帶來)、conducir(開車),這三個動詞的字根都以子音 j 結尾,而這個 j 會把常規 -ieron 字尾裡的「i」吞掉。

  • ¿Qué te dijo el jefe?(給 貼 迪厚 誒爾 黑費)—— 老闆跟你說了什麼?
  • Me dijo que trajera los documentos mañana.(咩 迪厚 給 特拉黑拉 羅斯 多庫門多斯 馬娘那)—— 他叫我明天把文件帶來。
  • ¿Trajiste el cargador?(特拉黑斯貼 誒爾 卡爾嘎多爾)—— 你有帶充電器嗎?
  • Sí, y también conduje toda la noche para llegar a tiempo.(西,伊 譚碧恩 空杜黑 托達 拉 諾切 帕拉 耶嘎爾 阿 提恩波)—— 有,而且我還開了一整晚的車才準時到。

文法重點:decir→dij-、traer→traj-,以及 conducir(還有其他所有 -ducir 結尾的動詞,如 traducir、producir、reducir 等)→conduj-,這三者都套用與 u 字根家族相同的字尾(-e、-iste、-o、-imos)——唯獨在 ellos/ellas/ustedes(他們)形態時,「j」會把原本預期的「i」吞掉:dijeron、trajeron、condujeron,而不是「dijieron」。這是第十六天所學 -ieron 字尾規則裡唯一真正的例外,凡是字根以「j」結尾的過去式動詞都適用。

第十九卦:臨卦(Lín)——「臨近」(上坤下兌,澤上有地)。〈序卦傳〉直接銜接了昨天的卦象:「蠱者事也,有事而後可大,故受之以臨,臨者大也」——蠱卦(第十八卦,「腐敗待整」)意味著有具體的事務需要處理;唯有積極整治腐敗之後,才能真正走向壯大,因此臨卦(意涵「臨近」,也帶有「壯大」之意)接續蠱卦而來。卦象描繪的是澤水下方、大地之上——象徵大地逼近俯視下方的澤水,亦即尊者親臨、逼近、俯視在下者的意象。卦辭寫道:「臨:元亨,利貞。至于八月有凶。」〈象傳〉補充道:「澤上有地,臨;君子以教思无窮,容保民无疆。」古代注疏將「至于八月有凶」這句警語,解讀為對自滿的提醒:眼前正在增長的影響力與有利局勢,終將隨時間消長,因此君子應當把握勢力上升的階段,竭盡全力教化、扶育他人,而不是在情勢順遂時鬆懈大意。

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 新加坡(Singapore)

  • 位置: 位於東南亞馬來半島最南端的城市國家,北隔狹窄的柔佛海峽與馬來西亞相望,南側則隔著新加坡海峽與印尼的廖內群島相望。
  • 領土面積: 目前約 734 平方公里(自 1965 年獨立時約 581 平方公里,持續透過填海造陸擴張)——面積比紐約市還小,僅約台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的五十分之一,是全球面積最小的主權國家之一,卻是舉足輕重的經濟與金融重鎮。
  • 人口: 約 604 萬人(2026 年),人口密度高居全球前列,每平方公里逾 8,000 人;是華人(約占 75%)、馬來人(約 15%)、印度裔(約 7%)組成的多族裔社會,另有龐大的外籍工作者族群。
  • 宗教: 宗教相當多元——佛教(約 31%)、基督教(約 19%)、無宗教信仰(約 20%)、伊斯蘭教(約 15%)、道教(約 9%)、印度教(約 5%),反映其多族裔人口組成與憲法規定的世俗治理原則。
  • 經濟: 全球首屈一指的金融與貿易樞紐之一,名目人均 GDP 位居世界前列;經濟建立在轉口貿易、樟宜機場(區域重要航空樞紐)、煉油、電子製造與生技產業之上,並由主權基金 GIC 與淡馬錫控股(Temasek Holdings)在全球投資國家儲備作為後盾。
  • 歷史: 1819 年由萊佛士爵士(Sir Stamford Raffles)建立為英國貿易據點;逐漸發展為英國直轄殖民地與區域重要港口;1942 至 1945 年間曾遭日本占領;1963 年加入馬來西亞聯邦,後於 1965 年脫離獨立,由開國總理李光耀主政,帶領新加坡迅速轉型為亞洲「四小龍」經濟體之一。
  • 現任領導人: 總理黃循財(Lawrence Wong),自 2024 年 5 月 15 日就任(新加坡第四任總理,接替李顯龍),另有總統尚達曼(Tharman Shanmugaratnam),自 2023 年 9 月 14 日起擔任儀式性國家元首。
  • 語言: 官方語言共有四種——英語(政府、商業與教育的主要工作語言)、馬來語(儀式性的國家語言)、華語(Mandarin),以及泰米爾語。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 桑德斯的毛線手套(Bernie Sanders' Mittens)

  • 名稱: "Bernie Sanders Sitting"(桑德斯坐姿迷因)或 "Bernie's Mittens",通稱「桑德斯手套迷因」。
  • 起源/歷史: 2021 年 1 月 20 日,美國總統喬・拜登就職典禮上,佛蒙特州參議員伯尼・桑德斯獨自坐著、雙臂交疊,身穿厚重的棕色花紋防寒外套與手工毛線手套(由佛蒙特州一位小學老師 Jen Ellis 用回收毛衣改製而成)——這身樸實無華的裝扮,是為了因應 COVID-19 疫情期間戶外保持社交距離的典禮所做的實用選擇。
  • 如何爆紅: 典禮結束後數小時內,這張照片便在社群媒體上瘋傳;網友迅速將坐姿、戴著毛線手套的桑德斯合成進無數不相干的畫面中——從名畫、電影劇照、歷史照片到日常場景無所不包,使其成為 2021 年最具代表性的病毒式圖片模板之一。
  • 意義與使用情境: 象徵一個人在正式或盛大場合中顯得毫不在意、格格不入,卻又務實從容——一種「不在乎排場」的原型形象,常被幽默地套用,把一個面無表情、有點不耐卻很有共鳴感的旁觀者插入任何畫面中。
  • 變體與演變: 網友持續產出各種合成版本,把桑德斯放進文藝復興名畫、紅毯活動、虛構世界等場景中;桑德斯本人的競選團隊後來也推出官方版周邊商品(印有該畫面的運動衫),所得捐贈給包括「送餐到家」(Meals on Wheels)在內的慈善機構,募得超過 180 萬美元。
  • 目前現況: 如今已不再是活躍的迷因格式,但仍被視為 2020 年代初期最具代表性的病毒式圖片之一,偶爾會以懷舊致敬的方式被重新提起。

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 文樂(Bunraku)

  • 名稱: 文樂(ぶんらく,Bunraku),舊稱人形淨瑠璃(にんぎょうじょうるり,Ningyō Jōruri,「人偶淨瑠璃」)。
  • 關鍵人物: 17 世紀末發展於大阪;劇作家近松門左衛門(1653–1725)常被譽為「日本的莎士比亞」,寫下多部至今仍廣為演出的經典作品(包括《曾根崎心中》);說唱藝人竹本義太夫於 1684 年創立竹本座劇場,開創了至今仍沿用的「義太夫節」說唱風格。
  • 時代背景: 江戶時代與歌舞伎並行興盛,是大阪、京都商人階層(町人)的重要通俗娛樂。
  • 內容概述: 劇目涵蓋時代物(以武士與政治鬥爭為主題的歷史/古裝劇)與世話物(描寫市井小民日常生活的家庭劇,常見悲劇性的愛情故事,包括殉情雙亡的劇碼)。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 每具人偶——大小約為真人的三分之二到與真人相當——皆由三名操偶師在觀眾面前公開操作:主遣い(主操偶師,操控頭部與右手,不戴面罩、身著正式服裝現身)、左遣い(操控左手)與足遣い(操控雙腳),後兩者身披黑色頭罩(黑衣,即「黒子」),象徵他們刻意保持「隱形」。唯一一位說唱者「太夫」負責唱唸所有角色台詞與旁白,並由三味線伴奏。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 2003 年被聯合國教科文組織列為人類非物質文化遺產;與歌舞伎關係密不可分,後者許多知名劇目都直接改編自文樂劇本。
  • 影響與傳承: 其獨特的表現力——即使操偶師清晰可見,人偶仍能傳達細膩情感——影響了全世界的偶戲與劇場創作,包括舞台劇《戰馬》(War Horse)所採用的「操偶師可見」技法。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 至今仍持續演出,主要據點為大阪的國立文樂劇場與東京的國立劇場,由專業訓練的表演者傳承至今,儘管觀眾規模已不如江戶時代全盛時期。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、unesco.org、ntj.jac.go.jp

8) 看片方法 — 色彩 Colour(第五課)

第一課教了場面調度,第二課教了剪輯,第三課教了攝影與燈光,第四課教了音效與配樂。今天要看的鏡頭是色彩——電影裡最「刻意設計」的層面之一,一旦掌握訣竅,也是最容易解讀的一環。

方法說明: 首先辨認全片的主色調(是暖色系還是冷色系?飽和還是去飽和?是否有一兩種顏色反覆出現、形成母題?),接著問這個色調在敘事上——而不只是裝飾上——發揮了什麼作用。留意色彩在整體克制或近乎單色的背景中被謹慎、少量使用的時刻——單一的強調色就像一道聚光燈,強迫觀眾的視線聚焦其上,也往往為其賦予了象徵意義。追蹤色調是否隨劇情進展而改變(飽和度下降暗示角色走向衰敗、暖色轉為冷色標誌情緒或命運的轉折),並留意某個顏色選擇是否在片尾刻意重現、形成呼應,讓單一視覺母題得以在很長的片長跨度中,不靠任何台詞就傳遞意義。

實例示範 ——《辛德勒的名單》Schindler's List(1993,導演:史蒂芬・史匹柏 Steven Spielberg;攝影:雅努什・卡明斯基 Janusz Kamiński),紅衣女孩一幕: 史匹柏幾乎全片採用黑白攝影,營造出如新聞紀錄片般的真實感,呼應大屠殺題材的沉重份量。片中唯一持續出現的例外,是一名穿著紅色外套、獨自在克拉科夫猶太區清算行動的混亂中遊蕩的小女孩——在整段近乎單色的畫面裡,這是唯一一抹色彩,不僅在劇情中吸引了奧斯卡・辛德勒的目光,也吸引了觀眾在銀幕外的注意力,在難以言喻、令人難以承受的恐怖景象之中格外醒目。片子後段,同一件紅色外套再度出現——這次出現在一堆從集體墓穴中搬出、以黑白呈現的衣物堆裡,不需要任何台詞,便無聲確認了這名女孩的命運,這一幕也被廣泛視為辛德勒自身道德覺醒的轉捩點。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)色彩作為聚光燈——在去飽和或黑白畫面中出現的一抹孤立色彩,會自動吸引觀眾視線,是一種不需要運鏡或剪接就能引導注意力的高效手法。(2)色彩作為象徵性的濃縮——把焦點放在一名身著彩色的小女孩身上,讓電影得以透過一個具體、情感上容易理解的個體,來呈現大屠殺這種難以理解的巨大規模,而不必訴諸抽象概念或統計數字。(3)色彩作為跨越時間的呼應——外套色調本身沒有改變,但脈絡已徹底轉變,單靠視覺辨識,就在銀幕時間出現明顯間隔的情況下,傳遞了關鍵的敘事資訊(女孩已死),完全不需要台詞。這堂課承接第一至四課:場面調度是畫面之內安排了什麼,剪輯是畫面如何被排列剪接,攝影與燈光決定畫面看起來是什麼樣子,音效與配樂決定一場戲在畫面之下「感覺」起來是什麼樣子,而色彩,則決定了單一視覺細節可以被賦予什麼樣的意義。
