3) Spanish Lesson — Day 22 (10 min, builds on Day 21)

Day 21 introduced the present perfect (pretérito perfecto compuesto: haber + participle) for past actions still tied to the present. Today adds the pluscuamperfecto (past perfect) — the same haber-plus-participle structure, but built from haber's imperfect forms instead of its present-tense forms, used to describe an action that had already happened before another past action or point in time.

  • Cuando llegué a la fiesta, ya se habían ido todos. — When I arrived at the party, everyone had already left.
  • Nunca había viajado a España antes de este año. — I had never traveled to Spain before this year.
  • ¿Habías estudiado español antes de tomar esta clase? — Had you studied Spanish before taking this class?
  • Ya habíamos comido cuando llamaste. — We had already eaten when you called.

Grammar note: the pluscuamperfecto is built from haber's imperfect conjugation (había, habías, había, habíamos, habían) + past participle (the same -ado/-ido forms, and the same irregulars — hecho, dicho, escrito, visto, puesto, vuelto — taught on Day 21). The key conceptual shift from Day 21: the present perfect anchors a past action to now ("I have eaten" = relevant to the present), while the pluscuamperfecto anchors a past action to another point further back in the past ("I had eaten [before X happened]") — it always implies, or explicitly states, a second, more recent past-tense reference event. This is structurally identical to English "had done," which makes it one of the more intuitive tenses for English speakers, but it still requires care with word order: unlike English, nothing may be inserted between haber and the participle (no "había ya comido," only "ya había comido" or "había comido ya").

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 22: 賁 (Bì) — "Grace" (Mountain above, Fire below). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary bridges from yesterday's hexagram: "物不可以苟合而已,故受之以賁;賁者,飾也" — things cannot simply come together through raw, unadorned force alone (as Hexagram 21, Biting Through, achieved by removing an obstruction); once union is secured, it needs form, pattern, and beauty to hold and be sustained, hence Bì ("Grace," literally "ornament" or "adornment") follows Shì Kè. The trigram image is fire at the foot of the mountain — flickering light illuminating a still, solid mass from below, suggesting culture and refinement (wén, 文) applied to a stable substance. The Judgment reads: "賁:亨。小利有攸往。" ("Grace has success. In small matters it is favorable to undertake something.") Grace/adornment is explicitly framed as a force suited only to minor, refining matters — not to be relied upon for great, decisive undertakings, since beauty of form is never a substitute for substance. The Image adds: "山下有火,賁;君子以明庶政,無敢折獄。" ("Fire at the foot of the mountain: the image of GRACE. Thus the superior man proceeds in ordinary civil affairs with clarity, but dares not use this alone to decide criminal lawsuits.") Commentators read this as a caution: clarity and refinement (fire/culture) are excellent for administering everyday matters, but weighty judicial decisions demand more than surface elegance or persuasive presentation — a direct thematic counterpoint to yesterday's Shì Kè, which was precisely about the firm, unadorned application of justice.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Nigeria

  • Location: West Africa, on the Gulf of Guinea coast of the Atlantic Ocean; borders Benin to the west, Niger to the north, Chad to the northeast (across Lake Chad), and Cameroon to the east.
  • Territory: About 923,768 km² — roughly 25.5 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), larger than Venezuela, and more than twice the size of California; it is Africa's 14th-largest country by area but by far its largest by population.
  • Population: Roughly 235 million (2026 estimate) — Africa's most populous country and the world's 6th most populous, projected by UN demographers to become the world's 3rd most populous nation (behind only India and China) by around mid-century.
  • Religion: Almost evenly split — roughly 50% Muslim (concentrated in the north) and 45% Christian (concentrated in the south), with a small remainder practicing indigenous traditional religions; this near-50/50 divide is a defining feature of the country's politics, federal power-sharing conventions, and periodic sectarian tension.
  • Economy: Africa's largest economy by nominal GDP (alternating with Egypt and South Africa depending on currency swings), and Africa's largest oil producer and an OPEC member, though oil's share of GDP has shrunk relative to services and agriculture; home to "Nollywood," the world's second-largest film industry by output volume, and to Lagos's fast-growing fintech scene (unicorns Flutterwave and Paystack), while still grappling with high inflation, a volatile currency (the naira), and fallout from 2023's fuel-subsidy removal.
  • History: Home to ancient societies including the Nok culture (c. 1000 BC), the Benin Empire, the Oyo Empire, and the 19th-century Sokoto Caliphate; the British colonial administrator Lord Lugard amalgamated the separate Northern and Southern Protectorates into a single colony in 1914, a merger of vastly different regions and peoples whose consequences still shape Nigerian politics. Independence came in 1960; a 1967-1970 civil war (the Biafran War) followed an attempted secession by the largely Igbo southeast, killing up to roughly 2 million people, mostly from famine, and remains one of the 20th century's deadliest conflicts. A long era of military coups and dictatorships gave way to civilian democratic rule in 1999, launching the ongoing "Fourth Republic."
  • Current leader: President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, in office since May 29, 2023.
  • Language: English is the official language (a colonial legacy that also serves as the practical lingua franca across ethnic lines); over 500 indigenous languages are spoken, with Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo the three largest.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, britannica.com

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — Grumpy Cat

  • Name: "Grumpy Cat" — real name Tardar Sauce.
  • Origin/history: Tardar Sauce was born April 4, 2012, in Morristown, Arizona, a female cat whose feline dwarfism and pronounced underbite gave her face a permanent scowl.
  • How it became popular: On September 22, 2012, her owner's brother posted a photo to Reddit titled "Meet Grumpy Cat," and it went viral almost overnight — the striking contrast between her perpetually disapproving expression and the internet's usual appetite for "cute" cat photos was the core of its appeal.
  • Meaning/usage: Used as a reaction image/caption format to express annoyance, disapproval, or blunt pessimism toward virtually any statement or situation, typically paired with deadpan, sarcastic one-liners ("I had fun once. It was awful.").
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned an unusually large commercial empire for an internet meme — books, a 2014 Lifetime TV movie ("Grumpy Cat's Worst Christmas Ever"), a video game, plush merchandise, and a Friskies-branded "Grumppuccino" coffee tie-in; her owners even won an approximately $710,000 judgment in 2018 against a beverage company for unauthorized use of her image and the "Grumppuccino" name, an unusually concrete legal precedent for meme-image commercial rights. She also held the Guinness World Record for "Most Popular Cat on the Internet."
  • Current status: Tardar Sauce died on May 14, 2019, of complications from a urinary tract infection. The meme is now a settled "classic" of the 2012-2014 golden age of reaction-cat/image-macro memes — still widely recognized and referenced, though no longer generating new commercial content.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, knowyourmeme.com

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Flamenco

  • Name: Flamenco.
  • Key figures/creators: A folk art form with no single creator, shaped historically by Andalusia's Roma/Gitano communities; landmark modern figures include guitarist Paco de Lucía (credited with revolutionizing and modernizing flamenco guitar technique in the 20th century), his frequent creative partner, singer Camarón de la Isla, dancer/choreographer Carmen Amaya, and legendary early cantaora La Niña de los Peines.
  • Era/background: Emerged in Andalusia, southern Spain, drawing on a fusion of Andalusian, Romani/Gitano, Moorish/Arabic, Sephardic Jewish, and Castilian folk traditions; first documented in the late 18th century, with its classic "Golden Age" occurring in the mid-to-late 19th century, centered on the café cantante performance venues.
  • Content overview: Not narrative in the conventional sense — built from three interlocking elements: cante (singing, often raw and anguished), toque (guitar playing), and baile (dance), traditionally accompanied by palmas (rhythmic handclapping) and jaleo (vocal interjections of encouragement); the most intensely emotional vocal style is called cante jondo ("deep song").
  • Artistic style and techniques: Percussive footwork (zapateado), intricate, sculptural arm and hand movement (braceo), complex rhythmic cycles known as compás (frequently a 12-beat pattern), and structured improvisation — performers work within a rigid traditional framework while inventing freely inside it.
  • Cultural/historical significance: A defining emblem of Andalusian and Spanish cultural identity worldwide, despite its origins among a historically marginalized and persecuted Roma/Gitano population; inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2010.
  • Influence and legacy: Shaped genres well beyond Spain, including jazz fusion (Paco de Lucía's collaborations with John McLaughlin and Al Di Meola) and the broader "nuevo flamenco" movement; remains central to Spain's cultural tourism and soft-power export.
  • Current status: Actively performed today in tablaos (dedicated flamenco venues) across Spain, especially Seville, Jerez de la Frontera, and Madrid, at international touring shows, and at major festivals such as Seville's biennial Bienal de Flamenco; continues evolving through fusion with pop, electronic, and hip-hop influences among younger artists.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, ich.unesco.org

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Framing & Composition (Lesson 8)

Lessons 1-7 covered mise-en-scène, editing, cinematography/lighting, sound, colour, narrative structure, and blocking/staging. Today's lens is framing and composition: the deliberate choice of what the camera includes, excludes, and how it arranges everything within the rectangle of the frame — symmetry versus asymmetry, perspective, negative space, and where the eye is pulled first.

The method: Ask what sits at the exact center of the frame versus its edges, and whether the composition is symmetrical (evoking order, control, or unnatural rigidity) or asymmetrical (evoking imbalance, tension, or naturalism). Look for one-point perspective (a single vanishing point drawing the eye deep into the frame, often used to create geometric order or to trap a character within a rigid space), negative space (empty areas that isolate or diminish a subject), and what happens when a shot's established symmetry or perspective is suddenly broken — a break in composition is rarely accidental, and usually signals that the world of the film, or a character's control over it, has just been disrupted.

Worked example — The Shining (1980, dir. Stanley Kubrick), Danny's tricycle rides through the Overlook Hotel corridors: Kubrick shot Danny's tricycle-riding scenes with a specially rigged low, child's-eye-level camera (mounted on a modified wheelchair platform) tracking directly behind him in perfectly centered one-point perspective, the hotel's identical patterned carpet and symmetrical hallway walls converging toward a single vanishing point ahead. The soundtrack alternates between the loud rattle of wheels on wood flooring and sudden near-silence as he rolls onto carpet, a sonic pattern that keeps the audience's nerves on edge purely through the anticipation of the next transition. This rigid, inhuman symmetry visually encodes the Overlook itself as a geometrically perfect, emotionally dead space — until Danny rounds a corner and the shot's established symmetry is abruptly broken by the sudden, static appearance of the Grady twins standing shoulder-to-shoulder in the hallway, dead center in the frame. Because the twins themselves are staged with eerie bilateral symmetry, the composition doesn't resolve the tension by breaking symmetry outright — it doubles down on an even more unnatural, doll-like symmetry, which is precisely why the moment reads as uncanny rather than simply surprising.

What to look for and why it works: (1) Centered one-point perspective as a control mechanism — perfectly symmetrical corridors converging on a single vanishing point make physical space itself feel engineered and inescapable, mirroring the hotel's psychological grip on its inhabitants. (2) Sound-image counterpoint — pairing the composition's visual rigidity with an unpredictable, alternating sound pattern (loud wheels/silence) shows how framing rarely works in isolation; here it directly reinforces Lesson 4's sound-design lens. (3) Symmetry escalated, not broken, at the moment of horror — rather than shattering the frame's order to signal danger, Kubrick intensifies the symmetry into something uncannily doll-like, proving that a break in expectation, not just a break in form, is what actually unsettles an audience. This builds on Lesson 1 (mise-en-scène) and Lesson 7 (blocking): where blocking tracked how bodies move and relate to each other over time, framing and composition asks how the camera's own rectangle organizes everything — moving or still — inside it.

3) 西班牙語課程 — 第二十二天(10 分鐘,銜接第二十一天內容)

第二十一天介紹了現在完成式(pretérito perfecto compuesto:haber + 過去分詞),用來表達與現在仍有關聯的過去動作。今天要新增過去完成式(pluscuamperfecto)——結構同樣是「haber + 過去分詞」,但這次 haber 要用它的過去未完成式(imperfecto)變化,而非現在式變化,用來描述「在另一個過去動作或時間點之前,就已經發生」的動作。

  • Cuando llegué a la fiesta, ya se habían ido todos. —— 我到派對時,大家都已經先離開了。
  • Nunca había viajado a España antes de este año. —— 在今年之前,我從來沒去過西班牙。
  • ¿Habías estudiado español antes de tomar esta clase? —— 你在上這堂課之前,學過西班牙文嗎?
  • Ya habíamos comido cuando llamaste. —— 你打電話來的時候,我們已經吃過飯了。

文法重點:過去完成式是由 haber 的過去未完成式變化(había、habías、había、habíamos、habían)+ 過去分詞(與第二十一天教過的規則 -ado/-ido 形式,以及 hecho、dicho、escrito、visto、puesto、vuelto 等不規則形式完全相同)組成。與第二十一天最關鍵的觀念差異在於:現在完成式是把過去動作錨定在「現在」這個時間點(「我已經吃過飯了」與現在有關),而過去完成式則是把過去動作錨定在「另一個更早的過去時間點」之前(「我〔在某事發生之前〕已經吃過飯了」)——它一定隱含,或明確提及,另一個時間點更近的過去式事件作為參照。這個結構其實與英文的「had done」完全對應,對母語為英文的學習者來說相對直覺,但仍要留意語序:與英文不同,haber 與過去分詞之間不能插入任何字詞(不能說「había ya comido」,只能說「ya había comido」或「había comido ya」)。

4) 易經卦象

第二十二卦:賁卦(Bì)——「文飾 / 優雅」(上艮下離,山下有火)。〈序卦傳〉銜接了昨天的卦象:「物不可以苟合而已,故受之以賁;賁者,飾也」——萬物不能僅僅依靠粗糙、未經修飾的強力便草率結合(如同第二十一卦噬嗑,是靠咬除阻礙來完成結合);一旦結合已然達成,便需要形式、條理與美感,才能使其得以維繫長久,因此賁卦(字面意義為「文飾」「裝飾」)接續噬嗑卦而來。卦象描繪的是山腳下燃著火——搖曳的光,由下方照亮一座靜止而厚實的山體,象徵將「文」(人文、修飾)施加於穩固的實質之上。卦辭寫道:「賁:亨。小利有攸往。」(意為:賁卦象徵亨通,但只適合在小事上有所作為。)這裡明確點出,文飾/優雅這股力量,只適用於細微的、修飾性的事務,不可倚賴它去成就重大、決定性的事業,因為外在形式的美,終究無法取代內在的實質。〈象傳〉補充道:「山下有火,賁;君子以明庶政,無敢折獄。」(山下有火,是賁卦的象徵;因此君子藉此清明地處理日常政務,卻不敢僅憑此便斷定刑獄訴訟。)歷代注疏將此解讀為一種告誡:清明與文采(火/人文)極適合用來處理日常事務,但攸關重大的司法判決,所需要的遠不只是表面的優雅或具說服力的包裝——這恰好與昨天噬嗑卦所談、剛毅果決、未經修飾地施行正義的主題,形成鮮明的對照與呼應。

(第二十二次介紹的卦象——已記錄於 ROUTINE_LOG.md 避免未來重複。目前已介紹:第一卦 乾(Qián)、第二卦 坤(Kūn)、第三卦 屯(Zhūn)、第四卦 蒙(Méng)、第五卦 需(Xū)、第六卦 訟(Sòng)、第七卦 師(Shī)、第八卦 比(Bǐ)、第九卦 小畜(Xiǎo Chù)、第十卦 履(Lǚ)、第十一卦 泰(Tài)、第十二卦 否(Pǐ)、第十三卦 同人(Tóng Rén)、第十四卦 大有(Dà Yǒu)、第十五卦 謙(Qiān)、第十六卦 豫(Yù)、第十七卦 隨(Suí)、第十八卦 蠱(Gǔ)、第十九卦 臨(Lín)、第二十卦 觀(Guān)、第二十一卦 噬嗑(Shì Kè)、第二十二卦 賁(Bì)。)

5) 國家/地區/政治實體介紹 — 奈及利亞(Nigeria)

  • 位置: 位於西非,瀕臨大西洋的幾內亞灣;西與貝南接壤,北與尼日相鄰,東北隔查德湖與查德為鄰,東側與喀麥隆接壤。
  • 領土面積: 約 923,768 平方公里——約為台灣(約 3.62 萬平方公里)的 25.5 倍,面積大於委內瑞拉,超過美國加州的兩倍;以面積而言僅是非洲第 14 大國,但人口規模卻是非洲之冠。
  • 人口: 約 2.35 億人(2026 年估計)——是非洲人口最多的國家,也是全球人口第 6 多的國家;聯合國人口學家預估,到本世紀中葉左右,奈及利亞人口將躍居全球第 3 多(僅次於印度與中國)。
  • 宗教: 幾乎呈現五五對分的局面——約 50% 信奉伊斯蘭教(主要集中於北部),約 45% 信奉基督教(主要集中於南部),其餘少數人口信奉本土傳統宗教;這種近乎對半的宗教分布,是形塑該國政治、聯邦權力分配慣例,以及間歇性教派緊張情勢的關鍵特徵。
  • 經濟: 以名目 GDP 計算為非洲最大經濟體(視匯率波動,與埃及、南非互有消長),同時是非洲最大產油國、OPEC 成員國,不過石油占 GDP 比重已隨服務業與農業成長而相對下降;奈及利亞也是「奈萊塢」(Nollywood)——按產量計算全球第二大電影產業——的所在地,以及拉哥斯(Lagos)蓬勃發展的金融科技新創聚落(獨角獸企業 Flutterwave、Paystack 均出自於此)的據點,但同時仍面臨高通膨、貨幣(奈拉)劇烈波動,以及 2023 年取消燃油補貼後續衝擊等挑戰。
  • 歷史: 境內曾孕育多個古代文明,包括諾克文化(Nok culture,約西元前 1000 年)、貝南帝國、奧約帝國,以及 19 世紀的索科托哈里發國(Sokoto Caliphate);英國殖民官員盧加德勳爵(Lord Lugard)於 1914 年將原本各自獨立的北、南保護地合併為單一殖民地,這場合併涉及差異極大的地區與族群,其後果至今仍持續形塑著奈及利亞的政治局勢。1960 年獨立;1967 至 1970 年間,以伊博族為主的東南部地區企圖分離,引爆比亞法拉內戰(Biafran War),造成約 200 萬人死亡(多數死於饑荒),是 20 世紀死傷最慘重的衝突之一。此後歷經長期軍事政變與獨裁統治,直到 1999 年才重返文人民主體制,開啟至今仍在運作的「第四共和」時期。
  • 現任領導人: 總統博拉・阿赫邁德・提努布(Bola Ahmed Tinubu),自 2023 年 5 月 29 日就任至今。
  • 語言: 官方語言為英語(殖民時期遺留下來的語言,同時也是跨族群溝通的實質通用語);境內另有超過 500 種本土語言,其中豪薩語(Hausa)、約魯巴語(Yoruba)與伊博語(Igbo)為三大主要語言。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、britannica.com

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 生氣貓(Grumpy Cat)

  • 名稱: 「Grumpy Cat」(生氣貓)——本名塔達醬(Tardar Sauce)。
  • 起源/歷史: 塔達醬於 2012 年 4 月 4 日出生於美國亞利桑那州莫里斯敦(Morristown),是一隻患有貓侏儒症、且下顎明顯前凸的母貓,這些特徵讓牠的臉部表情永遠呈現一副「生氣臭臉」的模樣。
  • 如何爆紅: 2012 年 9 月 22 日,飼主的兄弟在 Reddit 上發布一張標題為「Meet Grumpy Cat」(認識一下生氣貓)的照片,幾乎一夕爆紅——牠那永遠一臉不悅的表情,與網路上向來偏好「可愛貓咪照」的氛圍形成強烈反差,正是這個迷因爆紅的核心魅力所在。
  • 意義與使用情境: 常被當作反應圖/圖說格式使用,用來表達對幾乎任何說法或情境的不耐煩、不認同,或直白的悲觀態度,通常搭配一本正經、語帶諷刺的一句話吐槽(例如「我曾經開心過一次,那次很糟」)。
  • 變體與演變: 催生出對一個網路迷因而言異常龐大的商業版圖——出書、2014 年 Lifetime 電視電影《生氣貓的最慘耶誕節》(Grumpy Cat's Worst Christmas Ever)、電玩遊戲、絨毛玩偶周邊,以及與 Friskies 貓食品牌合作推出「Grumppuccino」聯名咖啡飲品;飼主團隊甚至在 2018 年一場官司中,因一家飲料公司未經授權使用其肖像與「Grumppuccino」名稱,獲判約 71 萬美元賠償金,是網路迷因肖像商業權利中少見、具體可循的法律先例。牠也曾獲金氏世界紀錄認證為「網路上最受歡迎的貓」。
  • 目前現況: 塔達醬已於 2019 年 5 月 14 日,因泌尿道感染併發症過世。這個迷因如今已是 2010 年代 2012 至 2014 年「反應貓/圖片迷因」黃金時期公認的「經典」代表——至今仍被廣泛認得與引用,但已不再持續產出新的商業內容。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、knowyourmeme.com

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 弗拉明戈(Flamenco)

  • 名稱: 弗拉明戈(Flamenco)。
  • 關鍵人物: 作為一種民間藝術形式,弗拉明戈並非出自單一創作者之手,而是由安達魯西亞(Andalusia)的羅姆人/吉普賽(Gitano)社群在歷史中共同形塑而成;具代表性的現代人物包括吉他手帕可・德・盧西亞(Paco de Lucía,20 世紀公認徹底革新並現代化弗拉明戈吉他技法的人物)、與他長期合作的歌手卡馬隆・德拉伊斯拉(Camarón de la Isla)、舞者暨編舞家卡門・阿瑪雅(Carmen Amaya),以及早期傳奇女歌者「梳子女孩」拉尼尼亞・德洛斯佩內斯(La Niña de los Peines)。
  • 時代背景: 起源於西班牙南部的安達魯西亞地區,融合了安達魯西亞、羅姆/吉普賽、摩爾/阿拉伯、西班牙猶太(Sephardic)以及卡斯提亞民間傳統;最早在 18 世紀晚期留下文字記載,19 世紀中後期則迎來以「café cantante」(咖啡演唱館)為核心表演場域的經典「黃金時期」。
  • 內容概述: 並非傳統敘事性的藝術形式,而是由三個彼此交織的元素構成:cante(歌唱,往往原始而充滿悲愴)、toque(吉他演奏)與 baile(舞蹈),傳統上搭配 palmas(節奏性拍手)與 jaleo(即興的鼓舞吶喊);其中情感最為深沉激烈的歌唱風格,稱為 cante jondo(「深歌」)。
  • 藝術風格與技巧: 具有強烈節奏感的踏步(zapateado)、精緻如雕塑般的手臂與手部動作(braceo)、被稱為 compás 的複雜節奏循環(常見為 12 拍一循環的結構),以及在嚴謹傳統框架內自由發揮的即興表演。
  • 文化/歷史意義: 儘管起源於歷史上長期遭受邊緣化與迫害的羅姆/吉普賽族群,弗拉明戈如今已成為安達魯西亞乃至整個西班牙在全球最具代表性的文化象徵之一;於 2010 年被聯合國教科文組織(UNESCO)列入《人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄》。
  • 影響與傳承: 影響範圍遠遠超出西班牙本土,包括爵士融合樂(帕可・德・盧西亞與約翰・麥克勞夫林〔John McLaughlin〕、艾爾・帝・梅歐拉〔Al Di Meola〕的合作)以及更廣泛的「新弗拉明戈」(nuevo flamenco)音樂運動;至今仍是西班牙文化觀光與軟實力輸出的核心元素之一。
  • 現況與觀賞管道: 如今仍活躍於西班牙各地的 tablao(弗拉明戈專門表演場所),尤以塞維亞(Seville)、赫雷斯・德拉弗龍特拉(Jerez de la Frontera)與馬德里最為興盛,也常見於國際巡演節目,以及如塞維亞兩年一度的「弗拉明戈雙年展」(Bienal de Flamenco)等大型藝術節;新一代藝人持續將其與流行、電子與嘻哈元素融合,推動這項藝術形式不斷演變。
    資料來源:en.wikipedia.org、ich.unesco.org

8) 看片方法 — 取景與構圖 Framing & Composition(第八課)

第一到七課教了場面調度、剪輯、攝影與燈光、音效、色彩、敘事結構,以及走位與空間調度。今天的分析視角是取景與構圖(Framing & Composition):攝影機刻意選擇畫面中要「納入什麼、排除什麼」,以及如何在畫面矩形範圍內安排一切元素——對稱與不對稱、透視、留白(negative space),以及觀眾視線最先被引導到哪裡。

方法說明: 觀察畫面正中央擺放了什麼、邊緣又擺放了什麼,並判斷這個構圖是對稱(通常暗示秩序、掌控,或不自然的僵硬感)還是不對稱(通常暗示失衡、張力,或寫實自然感)。留意單點透視(single vanishing point,單一消失點將視線引導至畫面深處,常用來營造幾何秩序感,或將角色困在一個僵硬的空間裡)、留白(空曠的區域用來孤立或弱化某個主體),以及當一個鏡頭原本建立好的對稱或透視感突然被打破時會發生什麼——構圖上的破壞很少是偶然的,通常正是在暗示電影世界本身、或角色對其掌控力,正在此刻遭到瓦解。

實例示範 ——《鬼店》The Shining(1980,導演:史丹利・庫柏力克),丹尼騎三輪車穿越「全景飯店」走廊的場景: 庫柏力克以特別搭建、貼近孩童視線高度的低位攝影機裝置(架設於改裝的輪椅平台上),緊跟在丹尼身後,以完美置中的單點透視拍攝他騎三輪車的畫面,飯店裡花紋一致的地毯與左右對稱的走廊牆面,全部朝畫面深處的單一消失點匯聚而去。配樂則在車輪碾過木地板的喧鬧聲響,與滾上地毯後近乎全然的寂靜之間反覆交替,這種純粹透過「等待下一次轉換」所營造出的聲音節奏,讓觀眾的神經始終緊繃。這種僵硬、近乎非人的對稱感,將全景飯店本身視覺化為一個幾何上完美無瑕、卻情感全然死寂的空間——直到丹尼轉過一個轉角,鏡頭原本建立好的對稱感被猛然打破:葛雷迪家的雙胞胎姊妹突然靜止地並肩出現在走廊正中央,畫面完全置中。然而,由於這對雙胞胎本身的站位又呈現出一種詭異的左右對稱,這個構圖並非透過「打破對稱」來化解張力——反而將對稱感推向另一種更不自然、如人偶般的極致,而這正是這一刻之所以令人毛骨悚然、而非僅僅是「嚇一跳」的真正原因。

該注意什麼、為什麼這樣的手法有效:(1)置中單點透視作為一種掌控機制——完美對稱、朝單一消失點匯聚的走廊,讓物理空間本身顯得如經過精密設計、且無從逃脫,恰恰映照出這間飯店對其居住者心理上的牢牢掌控。(2)聲音與畫面的對位手法——將構圖上的僵硬感,搭配聲音上難以預測、忽大忽小交替出現的節奏,顯示取景與構圖很少能單獨發揮作用;此處它直接呼應了第四課所教的音效設計視角。(3)恐怖時刻選擇「加強對稱」而非「打破對稱」——庫柏力克並未透過打破畫面秩序來預示危險,反而將對稱感推向更詭異、如人偶般的極致,證明真正令觀眾不安的,往往不只是形式上的破壞,而是「預期被打破」本身。這堂課承接第一課(場面調度)與第七課(走位):走位探討的是身體隨時間如何移動、彼此如何互動,取景與構圖問的則是,攝影機這個矩形畫框本身,如何組織框內一切——無論靜止或移動的元素。
