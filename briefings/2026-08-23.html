3) Spanish Lesson — Day 44 (10 min, direct object pronouns)

Today's lesson steps outside the tense/mood track to fill in a foundational gap: direct object pronouns lo/la/los/las, which replace a noun that directly receives a verb's action (answering "what?" or "whom?"). The forms track the gender and number of the noun being replaced, not the speaker: el libro (masc. sing.) → lo, la llave (fem. sing.) → la, los libros (masc. pl.) → los, las llaves (fem. pl.) → las. Placement follows two rules: before a conjugated verb (Lo tengo, No las encuentro), or — when paired with an infinitive or gerund (as in Day 13's ir a + infinitive or Day 14's present progressive) — either before the whole verb group or attached to the end of the infinitive/gerund (Las voy a comprar / Voy a comprarlas; Estoy comprándolas, where an accent mark is now required to preserve the original stress). The lesson also directly contrasts today's direct object pronouns with Day 15's indirect object pronouns: me/te/nos are shared by both, but the third person differs — indirect object is always le/les regardless of gender, while direct object must match gender and number as lo/la/los/las, including for people (La conozco, not the regional Spain-only "leísmo" variant Le conozco).

  • ¿Tienes mi libro? — Do you have my book?
  • Sí, lo tengo aquí. — Yes, I have it here.
  • ¿Y las llaves del coche? — And the car keys?
  • No las encuentro. — I can't find them.

4) I Ching Hexagram

Hexagram 44: 姤 (Gòu) — "Coming to Meet" (Heaven above, Wind below — Qián over Xùn). The Xugua (Sequence) commentary pivots directly off yesterday's Hexagram 43: "夬者,決也;決必有遇,故受之以姤;姤者,遇也。" ("Guài means resolute breakthrough; after a resolute breakthrough there must inevitably be an encounter, hence it is followed by Gòu [Coming to Meet]; Gòu means to meet.") The hexagram's structure mirrors 43's in reverse: a single yielding (yin) line has now appeared at the very bottom, rising to meet five firm (yang) lines above it — the image of a small new force, having just entered from below, encountering an established order it may either serve or subvert. The Judgment reads: "姤,女壯,勿用取女。" ("Coming to Meet: the maiden is powerful; do not take such a maiden in marriage.") This warns that the newly arisen yin force, though still small, is inherently vigorous and quick to grow — the metaphor cautions against binding oneself permanently to a force whose ultimate character and intentions are not yet known. The Tuan (Commentary on the Judgment) elaborates: "姤,遇也,柔遇剛也。勿用取女,不可與長也。天地相遇,品物咸章也。剛遇中正,天下大行也。姤之時義大矣哉!" ("Gòu means encounter — the yielding meets the firm. 'Do not take such a maiden' means one cannot remain joined to her for long. When Heaven and Earth meet, all things display themselves and flourish. When firmness meets what is central and correct, the great Way spreads throughout the world. Great indeed is the meaning of the time of Gòu!") The Image commentary draws the lesson for conduct: "天下有風,姤;后以施命誥四方。" ("Wind moving beneath heaven: this is the image of Coming to Meet. Thus the sovereign issues commands and proclaims them to the four quarters.") Read together with Hexagram 43, the pair completes a full cycle: a decisive breakthrough that resolutely removes what should not remain (43) is inevitably followed by new, unavoidable encounters (44) — and the wisdom of the time lies in recognizing early whether a new contact is Heaven-and-Earth-like fruitful meeting or a small force that, left unchecked, will grow to overturn the very order that decisive action just secured.

5) Country / Region / Faction Spotlight — Suriname (蘇利南)

  • Location: Northern coast of South America, on the Atlantic Ocean; bordered by Guyana to the west, French Guiana to the east, and Brazil to the south.
  • Territory: About 163,820 km² — roughly 4.5 times the size of Taiwan (~36,193 km²), and comparable in area to the US state of Georgia (~153,910 km²) or Tunisia (~163,610 km²); despite this, it is the smallest sovereign country in South America by both area and population, and over 90% of its territory remains dense, largely intact Amazonian rainforest.
  • Population: Roughly 618,000 (one of the smallest national populations in South America), heavily concentrated in and around the capital, Paramaribo.
  • Religion: One of the most religiously diverse countries in the Americas as a direct result of its indentured-labor colonial history: roughly 48% Christian (Catholic, Protestant, and Moravian denominations), about 22% Hindu, and around 14% Muslim, alongside smaller communities practicing indigenous, Afro-Surinamese (Winti), and other traditions.
  • Economy: Historically built on bauxite/alumina mining and gold mining, alongside rice and banana agriculture; substantial offshore oil discoveries since around 2020 (led by TotalEnergies and partners) have made deepwater petroleum development the country's biggest near-term economic prospect, with first oil production anticipated later this decade. The economy remains vulnerable to commodity-price swings and has required IMF-backed fiscal support in recent years.
  • History: Originally home to Arawak and Carib indigenous peoples; colonized by the English in the 17th century, then ceded to the Dutch under the 1667 Treaty of Breda — in exchange for Dutch-held New Amsterdam, which England kept and renamed New York. As Dutch Guiana, its plantation economy (sugar, coffee) ran on enslaved African labor; after abolition in 1863, Dutch planters imported indentured laborers from India (Hindustani) and the Dutch East Indies (Javanese), producing today's uniquely diverse ethnic mix alongside Afro-Surinamese Maroon descendants of escaped slaves. Suriname gained independence from the Netherlands in 1975; a 1980 military coup brought Dési Bouterse to power, whose regime carried out the December 1982 executions of opposition figures. Bouterse later returned as elected president (2010-2020) despite an in-absentia Dutch drug-trafficking conviction, and was separately convicted of murder for the 1982 killings; he died in December 2024 while a fugitive from that sentence.
  • Current leader: President Jennifer Geerlings-Simons, elected by the National Assembly following the May 2025 general election and inaugurated in July 2025 — Suriname's first female president, leading a coalition government.
  • Language: Dutch is the sole official language, making Suriname the only Dutch-speaking sovereign nation in South America; Sranan Tongo (an English-based creole) serves as the widely used lingua franca, alongside Sarnami Hindustani, Javanese, Surinamese Hakka, and various Maroon and indigenous languages reflecting the country's colonial and immigration history.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, cia.gov World Factbook

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — "Is This a Pigeon?"

  • Name: "Is This a Pigeon?"
  • Origin/history: Taken from a single frame of the 1991 Japanese anime "The Brave Fighter of Sun Fighbird" (太陽の勇者ファイバード), in which the android character Ginga, still learning about the human world, mistakes a butterfly for a pigeon and asks, sunglasses-clad and pointing, "Is this a pigeon?"
  • How it became popular: The screenshot circulated in a smaller way on imageboards for several years after being unearthed from the obscure 1990s series, but it exploded into mainstream popularity in September 2018 after a captioned edit went viral simultaneously on Twitter and Reddit, quickly becoming one of the most recognizable reaction-image templates of that year.
  • Meaning/usage: Used to mock a person, group, or institution confidently and earnestly misidentifying something obvious — typically satirizing willful or out-of-touch misunderstanding, such as corporations misreading genuine criticism as praise, or one generation misjudging another's behavior; the joke is Ginga's sincere confusion, not malice.
  • Variations/evolution: Spawned an enormous "Is this a [X]?" edit template applied across politics, fandom-specific in-jokes, and tech/business commentary, with Ginga's bewildered, sunglassed pose becoming a stand-alone recognizable icon independent of the original 1991 show it came from.
  • Current status: A "classic" or "dead" template in meme-culture terms — no longer actively spreading in new forms, but still widely recognized and periodically revived in nostalgia compilations and "iconic memes of the 2010s" retrospectives; considered one of the defining reaction formats of its era.
    Sources: knowyourmeme.com, en.wikipedia.org

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Do the Right Thing (1989, dir. Spike Lee)

  • Name/key figures: Do the Right Thing, written, directed, and produced by Spike Lee, who also stars as Mookie; cinematography by Ernest Dickerson; jazz score by Bill Lee (Spike Lee's father); Public Enemy's "Fight the Power," commissioned for the film, became its recurring anthem.
  • Era/background: Released in 1989 and set across a single, sweltering summer day in the Bedford-Stuyvesant neighborhood of Brooklyn, New York; made amid heightened real-world racial tension in the city, drawing directly on incidents like the 1986 Howard Beach attack and the 1989 killing of Yusuf Hawkins.
  • Plot/content overview: Chronicles escalating tension between the Black residents of a Brooklyn block and Sal (Danny Aiello), the Italian-American owner of the neighborhood's longtime pizzeria, and his two sons, as the heat and accumulated grievances build across the day toward the police chokehold killing of Radio Raheem and the riot that follows, in which Mookie throws a trash can through Sal's storefront window.
  • Artistic style and techniques: A deliberately hyper-saturated, warm red-and-orange color palette conveys the day's oppressive heat; canted "Dutch" camera angles and a direct-to-camera montage of racial-slur monologues break the fourth wall; an ensemble of street-corner characters functions as a Greek chorus; the film pointedly refuses a tidy resolution, closing on two seemingly contradictory epigraphs — one from Martin Luther King Jr. on nonviolence, one from Malcolm X on self-defense — without endorsing either.
  • Cultural/historical significance: Widely regarded as one of the most important American films about race; controversial on release, with some critics predicting (wrongly) that it would incite theater riots; the Radio Raheem chokehold scene gained renewed, painful resonance after Eric Garner's 2014 death in police custody, which involved the same "I can't breathe" refrain.
  • Influence and legacy: Established Spike Lee as a major American auteur and influenced generations of Black filmmakers; selected for preservation in the US National Film Registry by the Library of Congress in 1999 as "culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant"; "Fight the Power" became a standalone cultural and protest anthem independent of the film.
  • Current status/where to experience it: Widely available on streaming and home video, taught extensively in film and cultural-studies curricula, and regularly revisited in critical retrospectives on police violence and racial justice in America.
    Sources: en.wikipedia.org, loc.gov National Film Registry

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Sound Bridge (J-Cut & L-Cut) (Lesson 30)

A sound bridge is an edit where audio deliberately overlaps a picture cut, so that sound from one scene either arrives before its own picture does (a J-cut, named for the shape the offset audio/video blocks make on a nonlinear editing timeline) or lingers on after its picture has already ended (an L-cut). This is distinct from Day 4's broader Sound Design & Score (the overall diegetic/non-diegetic mix and score as emotional cue), Day 2's Editing & Montage (Kuleshov-style meaning built through shot juxtaposition), and Day 11's Match Cut (a purely visual rhyme across a cut, with no audio component) — a sound bridge specifically makes a transition between two scenes feel causally or emotionally continuous rather than abrupt, either smoothing over a hard cut or generating anticipation and dread by letting the audience hear the next scene before they can see it. Three things to check: whether the overlapping sound is diegetic (part of the story world) or non-diegetic (score/narration); whether it functions as a J-cut (audio arrives early, building anticipation for what's coming) or an L-cut (audio lingers, letting an emotional beat carry forward into the next image); and whether the bridge serves narrative economy (skipping over dead time invisibly) or a jarring, ironic contrast between what is heard and what is then seen.

Worked example: Apocalypse Now (1979, dir. Francis Ford Coppola) — the opening sequence, where the thudding sound of helicopter rotor blades begins over jungle-and-napalm imagery and continues unbroken as the picture cuts to Captain Willard alone in his Saigon hotel room. The sound bridge fuses the war outside with the war already inside Willard's head before the picture even confirms the location change, and it sets up the sequence's famous visual match cut moments later, when a slow-spinning ceiling fan's blades visually echo the helicopter rotor whose sound never actually stopped — making the transition feel like one continuous psychological event rather than two separate scenes stitched together by an editor.

3)西班牙文課程——第 44 天(10 分鐘,直接受詞代名詞)

今天的課程暫時跳出時態/語氣的學習軌道,回頭補上一塊基礎但先前未正式教過的文法:直接受詞代名詞 lo/la/los/las,用來取代動作「直接」施加的對象(回答「什麼?」或「誰?」)。這四個代名詞的形式跟著「被取代的名詞」的陰陽性與單複數走,而非跟著說話者:el libro(陽性單數)→ lo、la llave(陰性單數)→ la、los libros(陽性複數)→ los、las llaves(陰性複數)→ las。放置位置有兩個規則:放在已變化動詞之前(Lo tengo、No las encuentro);或是遇到第 13 天「ir a + 原形動詞」、第 14 天現在進行式這類組合時,可以放在整組動詞最前面,也可以黏在原形動詞/現在分詞字尾(Las voy a comprar / Voy a comprarlas;Estoy comprándolas,此時需加重音符號以維持原本重音)。課程也直接對比了今天的直接受詞代名詞與第 15 天教過的間接受詞代名詞:me/te/nos 兩者共用,但第三人稱不同——間接受詞不分陰陽性、永遠是 le/les,直接受詞則必須依陰陽性、單複數選出 lo/la/los/las,即使取代的是「人」也一樣(La conozco,而非西班牙地區方言現象 leísmo 的 Le conozco)。

  • ¿Tienes mi libro? —— 你有我的書嗎?
  • Sí, lo tengo aquí. —— 有,我這裡有它。
  • ¿Y las llaves del coche? —— 那車鑰匙呢?
  • No las encuentro. —— 我找不到它們。

4)易經卦象

第 44 卦:姤 (Gòu) ——「相遇」(天在上,風在下——乾上巽下)。《序卦傳》從昨天的第 43 卦直接接續下來:「夬者,決也;決必有遇,故受之以姤;姤者,遇也。」(夬就是決斷、突破之意;決斷突破之後,必然會有新的相遇發生,因此夬卦之後接的是姤卦;「姤」就是相遇之意。)這一卦的卦象結構與第 43 卦恰成鏡像:如今最下方出現了一條柔弱的陰爻,正逐漸上升、與上方五條剛強的陽爻相遇——象徵一股剛從底層冒出的新生力量,面對一個既有的秩序,可能選擇順服其中,也可能伺機顛覆它。卦辭寫道:「姤,女壯,勿用取女。」(姤卦:這位女子十分強盛有力;不宜娶這樣的女子為妻。)這句話警示:這股新生的陰性力量雖然此刻還很微小,但本質上生猛而容易迅速壯大——以婚姻為喻,提醒人不宜貿然與一股尚未看清其最終性格與意圖的力量長久綁定。《彖傳》進一步解釋:「姤,遇也,柔遇剛也。勿用取女,不可與長也。天地相遇,品物咸章也。剛遇中正,天下大行也。姤之時義大矣哉!」(姤,就是相遇之意,是柔弱之爻與剛強之爻相遇。「不宜娶這樣的女子」,是說不能與她長久相守。天與地彼此相遇,萬物才得以顯現、蓬勃生長。剛強之德與居中守正相遇,大道才能通行於天下。姤卦所蘊含的時機之意義,是何等重大啊!)《象傳》則點出實際行事的教訓:「天下有風,姤;后以施命誥四方。」(風行於天下之間,這就是姤卦的卦象。因此君王藉此頒布政令,昭告四方。)若將第 43 卦與第 44 卦合觀,恰好構成一個完整的循環:當一場決斷性的突破,果斷地排除了不該存續的事物之後(第 43 卦),新的、無可迴避的相遇必然隨之而來(第 44 卦)——而這個時刻真正的智慧,就在於及早分辨眼前這場新的相遇,究竟是如天地交會般帶來蓬勃生機的良緣,還是一股若不加以節制、終將壯大到足以推翻剛剛才辛苦守住之秩序的隱患。

5)國家/地區/政體介紹——蘇利南(Suriname)

  • 位置:位於南美洲北部海岸,面向大西洋;西鄰蓋亞那,東接法屬圭亞那,南邊與巴西接壤。
  • 領土:約 163,820 平方公里——約為台灣(約 36,193 平方公里)的 4.5 倍,面積與美國喬治亞州(約 153,910 平方公里)或北非國家突尼西亞(約 163,610 平方公里)相近;儘管如此,蘇利南在面積與人口上都是南美洲最小的主權國家,國土超過九成仍是密布、大致完整未受破壞的亞馬遜雨林。
  • 人口:約 61.8 萬人(南美洲人口最少的國家之一),高度集中於首都巴拉馬利波周邊。
  • 宗教:由於其獨特的契約勞工殖民歷史,蘇利南是美洲宗教組成最多元的國家之一:基督教(天主教、新教及摩拉維亞教派)約佔 48%,印度教約佔 22%,伊斯蘭教約佔 14%,另有較小規模的原住民信仰、非裔蘇利南「溫蒂教」(Winti)等傳統宗教社群。
  • 經濟:傳統上以鋁土礦/氧化鋁開採與金礦開採為經濟支柱,並輔以稻米與香蕉農業;自 2020 年前後起,由法國道達爾能源(TotalEnergies)等公司主導的大規模外海石油發現,已使深海石油開發成為該國近期最重要的經濟前景,首批石油產出預計於本世紀末前展開。整體經濟仍高度受原物料價格波動影響,近年曾需要國際貨幣基金組織(IMF)提供財政支援。
  • 歷史:最初為阿拉瓦克族與加勒比族原住民的居住地;17 世紀由英國殖民,後於 1667 年《布雷達條約》中割讓予荷蘭——荷蘭則以此換得原本由荷蘭持有、英國願意保留並更名為「紐約」的新阿姆斯特丹。作為「荷屬圭亞那」,其種植園經濟(蔗糖、咖啡)長期仰賴非洲奴隸勞動力;1863 年廢除奴隸制後,荷蘭莊園主轉而自印度(印度裔,Hindustani)與荷屬東印度群島(爪哇裔)引進契約勞工,造就了今日蘇利南獨特多元的族群組成,加上逃亡奴隸後裔「馬龍人」(Maroon)社群。蘇利南於 1975 年脫離荷蘭獨立;1980 年一場軍事政變將德西·包特瑟(Dési Bouterse)推上權力核心,其政權於 1982 年 12 月處決多位反對派人士。包特瑟後來以民選身分重返總統職位(2010-2020),儘管他曾在荷蘭因毒品走私罪遭缺席判決,並另因 1982 年處決事件被判謀殺罪成立;他於 2024 年 12 月以在逃之身分去世。
  • 現任領導人:總統珍妮佛·吉爾林斯-西蒙斯(Jennifer Geerlings-Simons),於 2025 年 5 月大選後由國民議會選出,並於 2025 年 7 月就職——為蘇利南首位女性總統,領導聯合政府執政。
  • 語言:荷蘭語為唯一官方語言,使蘇利南成為南美洲唯一以荷蘭語為官方語言的主權國家;通用的共通語則是以英語為基礎的克里奧爾語「蘇利南通用語」(Sranan Tongo),另有薩爾納米印度斯坦語、爪哇語、蘇利南客家話,以及多種馬龍人與原住民語言,反映該國殖民與移民歷史的多元交織。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、cia.gov 世界概況(World Factbook)

6)網路迷因介紹——「Is This a Pigeon?」(這是鴿子嗎?)

  • 名稱:「Is This a Pigeon?」(這是鴿子嗎?)
  • 起源/歷史:取自 1991 年日本動畫《太陽的勇者 Fighbird》(太陽の勇者ファイバード)中的一格畫面,劇中仍在學習人類世界的機器人角色銀河(Ginga),誤把一隻蝴蝶認作鴿子,戴著墨鏡、手指向蝴蝶問道:「這是鴿子嗎?」
  • 如何走紅:這張截圖最初僅在圖片論壇小範圍流傳多年,直到這部冷門的 1990 年代作品被重新挖掘出來;2018 年 9 月,一個加了字幕的改編版本同時在 Twitter 與 Reddit 上爆紅,迅速成為當年最具代表性的反應圖模板之一。
  • 意義/使用情境:用來嘲諷某人、某群體或某機構,以極其自信、認真的態度誤認顯而易見的事實——通常用來諷刺一種「明知故犯」或「狀況外」的誤解,例如企業將真實的批評誤讀為讚美,或某個世代誤判另一個世代的行為;笑點在於銀河那份真誠的困惑,而非惡意。
  • 變體/演化:衍生出龐大的「Is this a [X]?」改圖模板,被廣泛套用於政治議題、粉絲圈內哏,以及科技/商業評論等各種情境;銀河那副困惑、戴墨鏡的姿態,也演變成一個獨立於 1991 年原作之外、廣為人知的迷因圖示。
  • 目前狀態:以迷因文化的角度而言已算是「經典」或「退流行」的模板——雖不再持續衍生新變體,但依然廣受辨識,並經常在懷舊迷因合輯或「2010 年代經典迷因」回顧文章中被重新提及;被視為該年代最具代表性的反應圖格式之一。
    來源:knowyourmeme.com、en.wikipedia.org

7)電影/表演藝術介紹——《為所應為》Do the Right Thing(1989,導演:史派克·李)

  • 名稱/關鍵人物:《為所應為》(Do the Right Thing),由史派克·李(Spike Lee)編劇、執導、監製,並親自飾演男主角穆基(Mookie);攝影指導為厄尼斯特·迪克森(Ernest Dickerson);爵士配樂由史派克·李之父比爾·李(Bill Lee)創作;公眾之敵合唱團(Public Enemy)為本片量身打造的〈Fight the Power〉,成為全片反覆出現的主題曲。
  • 時代/背景:1989 年上映,故事發生於紐約布魯克林貝德福—斯泰弗森特(Bedford-Stuyvesant)社區、酷熱難耐的一整個夏日;拍攝當下正值紐約市種族關係緊張升溫之際,直接取材自 1986 年霍華德海灘(Howard Beach)攻擊事件與 1989 年尤瑟夫·霍金斯(Yusuf Hawkins)遇害等真實事件。
  • 內容概述:描繪布魯克林某街區的黑人居民,與義大利裔美籍披薩店老闆薩爾(丹尼·愛羅飾)及其兩個兒子之間,隨著全片一整天的酷熱與長年累積的怨懟持續升溫,最終引爆警方以鎖喉方式導致「收音機拉希姆」(Radio Raheem)喪命的悲劇,並引發暴動,穆基最終將垃圾桶砸向薩爾店面的櫥窗。
  • 藝術風格與技巧:刻意採用高飽和度的暖紅、橙色調,傳達整日酷暑逼人的氛圍;運用傾斜的「荷蘭角」攝影機角度,以及一段打破第四面牆、角色直視鏡頭輪流唸出種族歧視用語的蒙太奇;街角眾生相般的角色群像,發揮如古希臘悲劇歌隊(Greek chorus)般的評論功能;全片刻意拒絕提供一個乾淨俐落的結論,片尾並列兩段看似矛盾的引言作結——一段引自馬丁·路德·金恩談非暴力,另一段引自麥爾坎·X談自我防衛——並未明確支持任一方。
  • 文化/歷史意義:普遍被視為美國電影史上探討種族議題最重要的作品之一;上映當時頗具爭議,部分影評人甚至(錯誤地)預測本片將引發戲院內的暴動;片中「收音機拉希姆」遭鎖喉致死的場景,在 2014 年艾瑞克·加納(Eric Garner)因遭警方壓制致死、同樣留下「我無法呼吸」遺言的事件後,重新獲得令人痛心的現實共鳴。
  • 影響與傳承:奠定史派克·李作為美國重要電影作者的地位,並影響了後續數個世代的黑人電影創作者;1999 年由美國國會圖書館選入國家影片登記表(National Film Registry),認證其具有「文化、歷史或美學上的重要意義」;〈Fight the Power〉一曲更超越電影本身,成為獨立流傳的文化與抗議運動代表歌曲。
  • 現況/如何欣賞:目前可透過多數串流平台與家庭錄影帶市場輕易觀賞,長年是電影研究與文化研究課程的教材,並在探討美國警察暴力與種族正義議題的影評回顧文章中經常被重新提及。
    來源:en.wikipedia.org、loc.gov 美國國家影片登記表

8)看片方法——聲音橋接:J-Cut 與 L-Cut Sound Bridge(第 30 課)

「聲音橋接」(sound bridge)是一種刻意讓聲音跨越剪接點、與畫面不同步重疊的剪輯手法:若聲音提前於自己所屬畫面出現(稱為 J-cut,因為在非線性剪輯軟體時間軸上,音軌與視軌錯開的區塊形狀恰似字母 J),或是聲音在自己所屬畫面結束後仍持續延續(稱為 L-cut,區塊形狀恰似字母 L)。這與第 4 天較廣義的「音效與配樂」(整體的音源設計與配樂作為情緒提示)、第 2 天的「剪輯與蒙太奇」(透過庫勒雪夫式的鏡頭並置產生意義),以及第 11 天的「跳接對位」(純粹視覺層面、不涉及聲音的畫面呼應)皆不相同——聲音橋接的獨特功能,是讓兩個場景之間的轉場感覺具有因果或情感上的連續性,而非生硬的切斷:它可以用來柔化一次硬切,也可以讓觀眾「先聽見」下一場戲、藉此營造期待或不安的預感。可以檢視三個重點:重疊的聲音是屬於故事世界本身的「劇情內聲音」(diegetic),還是配樂/旁白這類「劇情外聲音」(non-diegetic)?它是屬於「音先於畫」、營造期待感的 J-cut,還是「音延續於畫之後」、讓情緒餘韻延伸到下一個畫面的 L-cut?這座橋接,究竟是為了敘事效率(不著痕跡地跳過無關緊要的時間),還是為了製造一種「聽到的」與「接下來看到的」之間充滿反諷意味的強烈對比?

實例演練:《現代啟示錄》(Apocalypse Now,1979,導演:法蘭西斯·福特·柯波拉)——開場段落中,直升機螺旋槳的沉重聲響先於畫面出現在叢林燃燒的凝固汽油彈畫面上,並在畫面剪接至威拉德上尉獨自身處西貢旅館房間時仍持續不斷。這段聲音橋接,早在畫面確認場景已經切換之前,便已將「外在的戰爭」與「威拉德內心早已存在的戰爭」融為一體;而稍後那個著名的視覺跳接對位——緩緩旋轉的天花板電風扇扇葉,在畫面上呼應著那始終不曾真正停止的直升機螺旋槳聲響——正是建立在這段聲音橋接之上,使得整個轉場感覺像是一段連續不斷的心理事件,而非剪接師拼接起來的兩個獨立場景。
