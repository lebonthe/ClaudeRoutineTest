3) Spanish Lesson — Day 85: Soler & Habit Expressions (usually, used to, be accustomed to)

Soler (o→ue: suelo, sueles, suele, solemos, soléis, suelen) + infinitive means "usually do"; in the imperfect (solía) it means "used to do". It is used almost only in these two tenses. Related: acostumbrar a + infinitive (formal), estar acostumbrado/a a (be used to), and acostumbrarse a (get used to).

  • ¿Qué sueles hacer los domingos? — What do you usually do on Sundays?
  • Suelo levantarme tarde y desayunar con mi familia. — I usually get up late and have breakfast with my family.
  • Mi abuela solía hacer tamales los domingos. — My grandmother used to make tamales on Sundays.
  • Yo ya estoy acostumbrado a madrugar. — I'm already used to getting up early.

4) Zhuangzi — Essay 21: 田子方 (Tián Zǐfāng) — "Tian Zifang"

The next chapter of the Outer Chapters (外篇), named for its opening figure Tian Zifang, a teacher at the court of Marquis Wen of Wei. When the Marquis praises another man's wisdom, Tian Zifang says the man is a humble neighbor, and the Marquis comes away feeling that his own court is only "dirt and clay" and that his own ruler's robes bind him. The chapter keeps returning to one question: how can a person recognize and live by the Way (Dao) without display?

The best-known scenes: Wen Boxue Zi (溫伯雪子) passes through Lu and refuses to see the Lu gentlemen who know ritual but "know little about knowing the heart"; when Confucius does meet him, the two do not speak, and Confucius's students ask why. Confucius replies: "With one glance the Way is already there (目擊而道存矣); there was no room for words." In another scene, Yan Hui tells Confucius that when the Master walks he walks, when the Master runs he runs, but when the Master "races without raising dust" Yan Hui can only stare behind him. Confucius answers that nothing is more tragic than a dead heart (哀莫大於心死), and a dead body is second to it: the real problem is that time and change run on in front of us, and only an alert, flowing mind keeps up.

Lao Dan, just after washing his hair, sits so still he looks like a dried tree, and says he is "letting his mind wander at the beginning of things" (遊心於物之初). Sunshu Ao is made prime minister three times without joy and dismissed three times without regret; asked how, he says he does not know whether the rank belongs to him or to the office, and so there is nothing to be happy or sad about. In the archery scene, the master archer Lie Yukou shoots perfectly with a cup of water balanced on his elbow, but Bohun Wuren stands with his heels hanging off a cliff and says that is only "shooting that shoots"; the true man is not shaken when he looks down at a ten-thousand-fathom abyss. The chapter closes with Duke Yuan of Song looking for painters: the one who arrives late, strips off his robe and sits cross-legged without ceremony is the only "true painter."

Takeaway: Real mastery is not display or rank; it is a settled, flowing mind that is not moved by honors, losses, or heights. (Chapter details are from general knowledge of the text, not from today's search.)

5) Country / Region Spotlight — Kyrgyzstan (Kyrgyz Republic, 吉爾吉斯)

  • Location & territory size: A landlocked mountain country in Central Asia, bordered by Kazakhstan (north), Uzbekistan (west and southwest), Tajikistan (south) and China's Xinjiang (east). Area about 199,900 km², roughly 5.5 times Taiwan (36,200 km²) and close to Belarus (about 207,600 km²) or Senegal. About 90% of the land lies above 1,500 m; the Tian Shan range and Pobeda Peak (7,439 m) dominate, and Issyk-Kul is one of the world's largest alpine lakes.
  • Population: About 7.2 million (Knoema, 2025), around 30% of Taiwan's population, in a country five times its area. The capital and largest city, Bishkek, has about 1.1 million people. The population is young; most are ethnic Kyrgyz, with Uzbek and Russian minorities.
  • Religion: Predominantly Muslim (about 90%, mostly Sunni of the Hanafi school, mixed with older Tengrist and shamanic customs); small Russian Orthodox and other Christian communities. The state is constitutionally secular.
  • Language: Kyrgyz (a Turkic language) is the state language and Russian the official language; Kyrgyz now uses Cyrillic, with periodic debate about switching to Latin script.
  • Economy: Lower-middle income, about US$17.5 billion GDP and about US$2,400 GDP per capita in 2024 (Knoema). It depends on gold (the Kumtor mine), agriculture and herding, re-export trade (the huge Dordoi bazaar in Bishkek) and worker remittances from Russia. President Japarov claims real GDP growth of 11.1% in the first seven months of 2026; I could not verify that independently. Currency: the som.
  • History: The Kyrgyz were originally a people of the Yenisei region of Siberia who migrated southward to the Tian Shan over centuries. Their national identity is tied to the epic Manas, one of the longest oral epics in the world (over 500,000 lines in some versions). The region was a Silk Road corridor, then was absorbed by the Russian Empire in the 1860s-1876; the 1916 uprising was crushed, and the Kirghiz SSR was created in 1936. Independence came on Aug 31, 1991. Revolutions toppled presidents in 2005 (the "Tulip Revolution"), 2010 and 2020, which makes Kyrgyzstan unusual in Central Asia for its turbulent politics.
  • Current leaders: President Sadyr Japarov, in office since January 2021 (confirmed by today's search). The Cabinet chairman is listed as Adylbek Kasymaliev by an aggregator; the search also mentioned a Cabinet swearing-in on Sept 9, so this post may have changed and was not independently confirmed.
  • Culture note: The flag shows a yellow sun with 40 rays (for the 40 tribes of Manas) over the tunduk, the crown of a yurt. The nomadic sport kok-boru (a horseback game with a goat carcass) and the World Nomad Games come from here.

6) Internet Meme Spotlight — The Dancing Baby ("Baby Cha-Cha", 1996)

  • Name: The Dancing Baby, also called "Baby Cha-Cha" or "Oogachaka Baby," one of the earliest known viral internet phenomena.
  • Origin / history: A short 3D animation of a diapered baby doing a cha-cha, created in 1996 by Michael Girard and Robert Lurye at the software company Unreal Pictures as a demo for the character-animation plug-in Character Studio (for Autodesk/Kinetix 3D Studio MAX). The animation was an example of realistic human motion, not intended as a joke.
  • How it became popular: Developers and artists shared the animation file by email, and it spread across early web pages, mailing lists and office computers in 1996-97. Its fame jumped when it appeared as a recurring hallucination in the TV series Ally McBeal in 1998, and a version set to the Blue Swede song "Hooked on a Feeling" (the "ooga-chaka ooga-ooga" chorus) became the standard form.
  • Meaning & usage: It carries no deeper meaning; it was shared because it was strange, new and fun in the days of dial-up modems. Today it is a nostalgic symbol of "web 1.0" and the first time many people received a viral file by email.
  • Variations / evolution: Countless remixes, dancing-baby GIFs and the same character in other contexts. Do not confuse it with the unrelated 2007 "Let's Go Crazy" video of a toddler dancing, which became the copyright case Lenz v. Universal.
  • Current status: A retro internet-history icon. It is referenced in retrospectives of early viral content (its animation is more than 30 years old), but is no longer in active use as a meme. (Details are from general knowledge, not from today's search.)

7) Film / Performing Arts Spotlight — Oedipus Rex (Sophocles, c. 429 BC)

  • Key figures: The Athenian playwright Sophocles (c. 496-406 BC), one of the three great Greek tragedians with Aeschylus and Euripides. Oedipus Rex (Oedipus Tyrannus) was first performed in Athens at the City Dionysia festival in the Theatre of Dionysus.
  • Plot overview: Thebes is struck by a plague, and the oracle says it will end only when the murderer of the former king, Laius, is found. Oedipus, the king, vows to find him, quarrels with the blind prophet Tiresias who tells him he himself is the killer, and suspects a plot by his brother-in-law Creon. Step by step, the testimony of his wife Jocasta, a Corinthian messenger and an old shepherd reveals that Oedipus was Laius's abandoned son, that he unknowingly killed his father at a crossroads and married his mother. Jocasta hangs herself; Oedipus blinds himself with her brooch pins and goes into exile.
  • Style & technique: Masked actors (only three speaking actors, playing all roles), a chorus of Theban elders who sing and dance between episodes, verse dialogue, and a tightly built plot that unfolds almost in real time as a detective story. Its signature technique is dramatic irony: the audience already knows the myth, so every line Oedipus speaks in confidence carries a second, terrible meaning.
  • Significance: Aristotle's Poetics used it as the model tragedy, citing its peripeteia (reversal), anagnorisis (recognition) and hamartia (error of judgment). The play is a landmark of Western theatre for exploring fate versus free will and the cost of seeking truth. Sigmund Freud later named the "Oedipus complex" after it.
  • Influence & legacy: Adapted by Seneca, Corneille and Stravinsky (the opera-oratorio Oedipus Rex, 1927), and filmed by Pier Paolo Pasolini in Edipo Re (1967). It remains a staple of drama curricula and of the repertoire worldwide.
  • Where to experience it today: It is regularly staged by national theatres and universities, including summer productions at the ancient Theatre of Epidaurus in Greece (about 12,000+ seats, famous for its acoustics) in the Athens Epidaurus Festival, and in film versions such as Pasolini's. (Details from general knowledge, not from today's search.)

8) Film-Appreciation Method — Lesson 71: Scene Structure — Turning Points & Value Shifts

The method: Treat each scene as a small story with a goal, conflict and a turning point. A well-built scene begins with a character wanting something, meets resistance, and ends in a different emotional or situational state than it began (a "value shift," for instance from hope to rejection, safety to danger, control to loss). Screenwriters and editors often "enter late and leave early": they cut the greeting, the setup and the goodbye and join the scene already in motion. A scene with no change is usually filler.

How to analyze a scene: (1) Name the main character's goal at the start. (2) Mark the beats (small shifts of tactic) as the characters push and pull. (3) Find the turning point: the line or action after which things cannot return to how they were. (4) State the value at the beginning and end (e.g. confident → humiliated) and ask what changed. (5) Check where the scene starts and ends: what was cut? (6) Ask what the scene sets in motion for the rest of the film.

Worked example — The Social Network (2010, dir. David Fincher, written by Aaron Sorkin), the opening bar scene: Mark Zuckerberg (Jesse Eisenberg) and Erica Albright (Rooney Mara) sit in a Harvard bar. There is no hello; we enter mid-conversation, with rapid-fire dialogue that leaps between final clubs, SAT scores and China. Mark's goal is to impress and stay in control of the conversation; Erica's is to be heard and eventually to leave. Each beat shifts the power: his boasting, her patient correction, his insults. The turning point is her line that he will spend his life thinking girls dislike him because he is a nerd, "when it will be because you're an asshole," after which she leaves. The value shifts from a self-assured Mark to a rejected, humiliated one, and the scene ends not on a goodbye but on his stunned face, then cuts to him running through the Harvard campus to his dorm to blog angrily, which launches the site that leads to Facemash and Facebook. Everything that follows in the film comes from this one rejection.

3) 西班牙文課 — 第 85 天:Soler 與習慣表達(通常、以前總是、習慣於)

Soler(o→ue:suelo, sueles, suele, solemos, soléis, suelen)+ 原形動詞表示「通常會……」;用未完成式(solía)則是「以前常常……」。這個動詞幾乎只用在這兩個時態。相關說法:acostumbrar a + 原形(較正式)、estar acostumbrado/a a(已經習慣)、acostumbrarse a(逐漸習慣)。

  • ¿Qué sueles hacer los domingos? — 你星期天通常做什麼?
  • Suelo levantarme tarde y desayunar con mi familia. — 我通常很晚起床,和家人吃早餐。
  • Mi abuela solía hacer tamales los domingos. — 我奶奶以前星期天都會做塔馬雷。
  • Yo ya estoy acostumbrado a madrugar. — 我已經習慣早起了。

4) 莊子 — 第 21 篇:田子方(Tián Zǐfāng)

〈山木〉之後的下一篇,屬於外篇,以開頭的人物田子方命名,他是魏文侯身邊的老師。魏文侯稱讚另一個人的智慧,田子方說那只是他的一位謙遜鄰居,文侯聽完覺得自己的朝廷不過是「泥土」,自己的君王服飾反成了束縛。全篇不斷回到同一個問題:人如何不張揚地認識並依「道」而活?

最有名的幾段:溫伯雪子路過魯國,拒見那些懂禮儀卻「不知人心」的魯國君子;後來他與孔子見面,兩人卻一句話也沒說,孔子的弟子問原因,孔子說:「目擊而道存矣,亦不可以容聲矣。」另一段,顏回對孔子說,夫子走他也走,夫子快跑他也快跑,但夫子「奔逸絕塵」時,他只能瞠目在後。孔子回答說,最大的悲哀是心死(哀莫大於心死),人死還在其次:真正的問題是時間與變化不斷往前奔,只有清醒而流動的心才跟得上。

老聃剛洗完頭,靜坐得像一棵枯木,說他正在「遊心於物之初」。孫叔敖三次當上宰相不喜,三次被免職不憂;被問原因時他說,他不知道官位屬於他還是屬於職位,因此沒什麼好喜或憂的。射箭一段,列禦寇能射得極準,手肘上還放著一杯水;但伯昏無人站在懸崖邊、腳跟懸空,說那只是「射之射」;真人面對萬仞深淵,神氣不變。全篇結尾,宋元君要找畫師:最後才到、解開衣服盤腿而坐、毫無禮數的那位,才是「真畫者」。

重點:真正的功夫不在炫耀或名位,而是一顆安定、流動的心,不因榮辱得失或高處而動搖。(本篇內容細節來自對原文的一般知識,而非今日搜尋。)

5) 國家/地區介紹 — 吉爾吉斯(Kyrgyzstan / 吉爾吉斯共和國)

  • 位置與面積:位於中亞的內陸山地國家,北鄰哈薩克、西與西南鄰烏茲別克、南鄰塔吉克、東鄰中國新疆。面積約 199,900 平方公里,約為台灣(36,200 平方公里)的 5.5 倍,接近白俄羅斯(約 207,600 平方公里)或塞內加爾。約 90% 的土地海拔在 1,500 公尺以上;天山山脈與勝利峰(7,439 公尺)是主要地景,伊塞克湖是全球最大的高山湖泊之一。
  • 人口:約 720 萬(Knoema,2025),約為台灣人口的 30%,卻住在 5 倍大的國土上。首都兼最大城市比什凱克約 110 萬人。人口年輕,以吉爾吉斯族為主,另有烏茲別克族與俄羅斯族少數群體。
  • 宗教:以穆斯林為主(約 90%,多為遜尼派哈乃斐學派,並混合更古老的騰格里信仰與薩滿習俗);另有少數俄羅斯東正教與其他基督徒。憲法規定為世俗國家。
  • 語言:吉爾吉斯語(突厥語系)為國家語言,俄語為官方語言;吉爾吉斯語目前使用西里爾字母,不時有改用拉丁字母的討論。
  • 經濟:中低收入,2024 年 GDP 約 175 億美元、人均約 2,400 美元(Knoema)。依賴黃金(庫姆托爾礦)、農牧業、轉口貿易(比什凱克龐大的多爾多伊市集)與俄羅斯勞工匯款。總統加帕羅夫聲稱 2026 年前七個月實質 GDP 成長 11.1%;我無法獨立驗證。貨幣:索姆。
  • 歷史:吉爾吉斯人原是西伯利亞葉尼塞河一帶的民族,數百年間逐步向南遷至天山。民族認同與史詩《瑪納斯》密切相關,那是世界上最長的口傳史詩之一(某些版本超過 50 萬行)。此地是絲路走廊,1860 年代至 1876 年被俄羅斯帝國併吞;1916 年的起義被鎮壓,1936 年成立吉爾吉斯蘇維埃社會主義共和國。1991 年 8 月 31 日獨立。2005 年(「鬱金香革命」)、2010 年與 2020 年的革命推翻了總統,使吉爾吉斯成為中亞政治最動盪的國家。
  • 現任領導人:總統薩迪爾·加帕羅夫(Sadyr Japarov),自 2021 年 1 月就任(今日搜尋已確認)。內閣主席由一個彙整網站列為阿迪爾別克·卡西馬利耶夫(Adylbek Kasymaliev);搜尋同時提到 9 月 9 日有內閣宣誓就職,因此該職位可能已有異動,未能獨立確認。
  • 文化小註:國旗是紅底、中央有一個 40 道光芒的黃色太陽(象徵瑪納斯的 40 個部族),太陽中間是蒙古包頂的「通迪克」(tunduk)。遊牧運動「馬上叼羊」(kok-boru)與世界遊牧民族運動會都源自這裡。

6) 網路迷因介紹 — 跳舞的寶寶(Dancing Baby / “Baby Cha-Cha”,1996)

  • 名稱:跳舞的寶寶(The Dancing Baby),又稱「Baby Cha-Cha」或「Oogachaka Baby」,是最早的網路瘋傳現象之一。
  • 起源與歷史:一段穿著尿布的寶寶跳恰恰的簡短 3D 動畫,1996 年由 Michael Girard 與 Robert Lurye 在軟體公司 Unreal Pictures 製作,是角色動畫外掛 Character Studio(適用於 Autodesk/Kinetix 的 3D Studio MAX)的示範範例,用來展示逼真的人體動作,而不是為了搞笑。
  • 如何爆紅:開發者與美術人員透過電子郵件分享這個動畫檔,1996-97 年間在早期網頁、郵件列表與辦公室電腦間流傳。1998 年它在影集《Ally McBeal》中成為反覆出現的幻覺,名氣大增;配上 Blue Swede 的歌曲〈Hooked on a Feeling〉(「ooga-chaka ooga-ooga」副歌)的版本成為標準形式。
  • 意義與用法:沒有更深的意義;在撥接上網的年代,它因為奇怪、新鮮又好玩而被分享。如今它是「Web 1.0」的懷舊象徵,也是許多人第一次用電子郵件收到瘋傳檔案的記憶。
  • 變體與演變:無數混音、跳舞寶寶 GIF,以及同一角色出現在其他情境。請勿與無關的 2007 年幼兒跳舞影片〈Let's Go Crazy〉混淆,後者成為 Lenz v. Universal 著作權案。
  • 現況:是復古的網路歷史圖騰。回顧早期病毒式內容時仍會被提起(動畫已超過 30 年),但已不再作為活躍的迷因使用。(細節來自一般知識,而非今日搜尋。)

7) 電影/表演藝術介紹 — 《伊底帕斯王》(索福克勒斯,約西元前 429 年)

  • 主要人物:雅典劇作家索福克勒斯(Sophocles,約西元前 496-406 年),與埃斯庫羅斯、歐里庇得斯並稱希臘三大悲劇詩人。《伊底帕斯王》(Oedipus Tyrannus)首演於雅典的酒神節(City Dionysia),在酒神劇場上演。
  • 劇情概要:底比斯遭瘟疫侵襲,神諭說只有找出前任國王萊厄斯的兇手,瘟疫才會結束。國王伊底帕斯發誓要找出兇手,與盲眼先知提瑞西阿斯爭吵(先知說他自己就是兇手),並懷疑是姻兄克瑞翁的陰謀。隨著妻子約卡斯塔、一名科林斯使者與一位老牧羊人的證詞,真相一步步揭露:伊底帕斯是萊厄斯被遺棄的兒子,他在三岔路口不知情地殺了父親、娶了母親。約卡斯塔上吊自盡;伊底帕斯用她的胸針刺瞎雙眼,自我放逐。
  • 風格與技法:戴面具的演員(只有三位說話的演員,分飾所有角色)、在各場之間歌唱舞蹈的底比斯長老歌隊、詩體對白,以及結構嚴密、幾乎以即時推進的偵探式情節。標誌性技法是戲劇性反諷:觀眾早已知道神話,因此伊底帕斯每一句自信的話都帶有第二層可怕的意義。
  • 意義:亞里斯多德的《詩學》把它當作悲劇的典範,舉出其「突轉」(peripeteia)、「發現」(anagnorisis)與「過失」(hamartia)。此劇探討命運與自由意志,以及追求真相的代價,是西方戲劇的里程碑。佛洛伊德後來以它命名「伊底帕斯情結」。
  • 影響與傳承:被塞內卡、高乃依與史特拉汶斯基(歌劇清唱劇《伊底帕斯王》,1927)改編,並由帕索里尼拍成電影《伊底帕斯王》(Edipo Re,1967)。至今仍是戲劇課程與全球劇場的經典劇目。
  • 今日可在哪裡欣賞:各國國家劇院與大學經常演出,包括夏季在希臘古埃皮達魯斯劇場(約 12,000 個以上座位,以音響效果聞名)舉行的雅典與埃皮達魯斯藝術節演出,也可看帕索里尼等電影版本。(細節來自一般知識,而非今日搜尋。)

8) 看片方法 — 第 71 課:場景結構 — 轉折點與價值轉換

方法:把每一場戲當成一個小故事,有目標、衝突與轉折點。結構良好的場景從角色想要某樣東西開始,遇到阻力,最後以不同於開頭的情感或處境結束(「價值轉換」,例如從希望到被拒、從安全到危險、從掌控到失去)。編劇與剪接師常「晚進場、早離場」:剪掉問候、鋪陳與道別,直接在場景進行中切入。沒有任何改變的場景通常是贅肉。

如何分析一場戲:(1)說出主角在開頭的目標。(2)標出節拍(beat,即雙方推拉過程中策略的小轉變)。(3)找出轉折點:說完那句話或做完那個動作後,事情再也回不去。(4)寫下開頭與結尾的價值(例如自信 → 受辱),並問:改變了什麼。(5)檢查場景從哪裡開始、哪裡結束:什麼被剪掉了?(6)問這場戲為全片接下來的發展啟動了什麼。

實例分析 — 《社群網戰》(The Social Network,2010,導演大衛·芬奇,編劇亞倫·索金),開場酒吧戲:馬克·祖克柏(傑西·艾森柏格飾)與艾芮卡·艾柏特(魯妮·瑪拉飾)坐在哈佛的酒吧裡。沒有打招呼,我們從對話中途進場,台詞快速地在終極俱樂部、SAT 成績與中國之間跳躍。馬克的目標是留下好印象並掌控對話;艾芮卡的目標是被聽見,最終離開。每一個節拍都讓權力轉移:他的自誇、她耐心的糾正、他的侮辱。轉折點是她說他這輩子會以為女生不喜歡他是因為他是書呆子,「其實是因為你是個混蛋」,然後離開。價值從自信滿滿的馬克轉為被拒絕、受辱的馬克,場景結束的不是道別,而是他錯愕的臉,接著切到他穿過哈佛校園跑回宿舍、憤怒寫部落格,啟動了通往 Facemash 與臉書的網站。電影後續一切都源於這次被拒絕。
